# Vladimir Samusenko is invited as NOVA product owner; capabilities are drafted one file per person before grooming

Decided by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-21-dutch-mill-on-nova-and-native-engine.md|Oct 21 2026 Dutch Mill review]] (8:02 AM, Russian), after the room agreed to drop the browser and write capabilities before code — [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-21-nova-drops-the-browser-for-a-native-engine-no-code-before-capabilities-and-mvp1-is-a-stable-nova-releasing-six-texas-games.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dutch_Mill_on_NOVA_and_Native_Engine-October-21-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Dutch_Mill_on_NOVA_and_Native_Engine-October-21-2026.md]].

*(Quotes come from an unpunctuated Russian speech-to-text transcript, kept word for word.)*

## What kind of product owner

- **Needed.** Val: someone must be assigned as product owner — "человек, который административно может охватить… Весь скоп этой платформы". Irina asked who holds it in hand; Pavel first said the task should go to everyone interested. Val: "Нам нужно… Продакт онор… И чисто продукт тонер" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dutch_Mill_on_NOVA_and_Native_Engine-October-21-2026.md]].
- **Not a developer.** "не забудьте продукт онор и девелопер это конфликтные позиции." Biratka: then he must not write code on this project; Val: "Я бы не хотел бы, чтобы он писал код в этом проекте. А все люди на этом митинге будут писать код." Leshkevich and Biratka know the subject well and Val will not pull them off it [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dutch_Mill_on_NOVA_and_Native_Engine-October-21-2026.md]].
- **The model is Klimovich.** Val: a product owner works well like [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] — "Он выполняет этот респонсобилоти очень хорошо". Irina: with Andrei, he wrote, then they discussed and corrected; Val: "в этом заключается вся ценность этой конфликтности" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dutch_Mill_on_NOVA_and_Native_Engine-October-21-2026.md]].
- **A thinker, not an administrator.** "мы же не ищем администратора, мы ищем мыслящего человека… у нас есть клод, который может администрировать эту работу." Someone who says "нам нужны такие по белоце. Вот я думаю так." Knowing the subject too well may even hurt — "есть определенный конвекшен уже" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dutch_Mill_on_NOVA_and_Native_Engine-October-21-2026.md]].

## Who was weighed

- **Leshkevich** — Pavel proposed him "Как самого молодого"; he said "Окей". Not taken: Val wants developers to code [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dutch_Mill_on_NOVA_and_Native_Engine-October-21-2026.md]].
- **Biratka** — Val: "саша был архитектором", not a product owner; Pavel wants him for architecture decisions [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dutch_Mill_on_NOVA_and_Native_Engine-October-21-2026.md]].
- **Pavel himself** — he feared he would write the capabilities worse than Anton, Sasha or Igor; Val: "Тоже неправильно" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dutch_Mill_on_NOVA_and_Native_Engine-October-21-2026.md]].
- **[[wiki/people/alexei-khamin.md|Alexei Khamin]]** — Val raised him twice; Pavel: "Нет… я думаю, что нам нужен продуктоер. Тот, кто эту тему хорошо знает"; Irina: "Я не вижу" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dutch_Mill_on_NOVA_and_Native_Engine-October-21-2026.md]].
- **Klimovich himself** — Pavel: possible, if he has the time; Val: "у него нету диапазона"; Irina: "Андрей занимается транзакшн… не надо его портить" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dutch_Mill_on_NOVA_and_Native_Engine-October-21-2026.md]].
- **Others named.** Igor named Ivan ("Ваня шпак") and Dmitriy Khandov (Val: "Нет"). Irina asked why [[wiki/people/sebastian.md|Sebastian]] had been given capabilities to write (by context — **inference**); Val: he will not fit [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dutch_Mill_on_NOVA_and_Native_Engine-October-21-2026.md]].
- **From outside.** Val would take someone from outside if anyone has a candidate. Biratka: an outsider means a lag of 2–3 weeks; Leshkevich: but a fresh look; both agreed searching takes long [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dutch_Mill_on_NOVA_and_Native_Engine-October-21-2026.md]].

## The ruling — Samusenko

- **Named.** Val raised "Влад… фронт энд дизайна" — Irina and Pavel identified him as [[wiki/people/vladimir-samusenko.md|Vladimir Samusenko]]. Pavel: a better candidate than Khamin. Irina: in recent work he came in carefully, then quite actively. Val: "Самусенко самый лучший кандидатура" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dutch_Mill_on_NOVA_and_Native_Engine-October-21-2026.md]].
- **Support him.** A written instruction — a persona of what a product owner is — so he understands what is wanted, and help so he does not fail; "Если его поддержать, то мы можем себе приобрести хорошего продуктора." Val: "я его не знаю. Я только так как бы сбоку" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dutch_Mill_on_NOVA_and_Native_Engine-October-21-2026.md]].
- **Backup.** If Samusenko does not work out, Val would propose Biratka "только тогда как более архитектор" — "но это нехорошая идея". Pavel's idea of making Biratka product owner only for the build-out and then returning him to development was turned down: Val wants someone who grows with NOVA [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dutch_Mill_on_NOVA_and_Native_Engine-October-21-2026.md]].
- **Availability.** Samusenko is on a short vacation and returns Monday Oct 26 (Irina: "Он в понедельник вернётся 26") [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dutch_Mill_on_NOVA_and_Native_Engine-October-21-2026.md]].

## How the capabilities are drafted (Pavel's method; Val agreed)

- **Each writes his part by Monday.** Use cases, personas, capabilities: "нам нужно начинать с us case… Нам нужна персона… Нам нужно кейпобилоти" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dutch_Mill_on_NOVA_and_Native_Engine-October-21-2026.md]].
- **Local for only a couple of hours.** Leshkevich asked whether to push to Jira or keep it local. Val: "Локально можно работать пару часов, после этого надо заливать" — another handshake [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dutch_Mill_on_NOVA_and_Native_Engine-October-21-2026.md]].
- **One file per person, then AI merges.** Pavel feared overlapping capabilities would create "много мусора". Val: there will be garbage, but the product owner runs grooming, as now with Klimovich. Pavel: with Klimovich it was different — he gave a big list and the team added what he missed. His proposal: a new project in the repository ("в gri" as heard; the notes: a new repo), each opens a file with his capabilities; at a meeting they go through each, AI removes the extra and combines; after a preliminary grooming a common list is fixed and AI enters it into Jira. Val: "Ну давайте сделаем повашему… Пусть будет повашему." Irina: less garbage in the main project [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dutch_Mill_on_NOVA_and_Native_Engine-October-21-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Dutch_Mill_on_NOVA_and_Native_Engine-October-21-2026.md]].
- **The week's plan.** Val: this week the team writes; Samusenko comes Monday and is invited to the role; Pavel prepares the description; "Во вторник тогда вы начинаете делать grouming capobile thes на MVP один для nor." Pavel: "Я согласен" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dutch_Mill_on_NOVA_and_Native_Engine-October-21-2026.md]].

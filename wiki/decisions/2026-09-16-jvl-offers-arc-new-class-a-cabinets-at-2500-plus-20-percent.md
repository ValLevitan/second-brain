# JVL offers ARC new Class A cabinets at $2,500 plus 20% — an open offer, not a deal

**Status in one line: put on the table and not accepted.** At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-16-arc-gaming-cashless-suite-demo-and-class-a-cabinet-counter-offer.md|Sept-16 2026 10:12 AM meeting]] at JVL's Lawrenceville office, [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] made ARC's CEO [[wiki/people/david-sloan.md|David Sloan]] a specific counter-offer against ARC's existing [[wiki/topics/georgia-class-a-machines.md|Class A]] arrangement with **Primero**. Sloan neither accepted nor refused: "**Let me just think on that**," and then "**Send me a message.**" **Treat this as an open offer, not a deal** — the derived notes say so in those words [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]].

Sloan raised the subject himself: "I like the technology. It's what about **Class A? Let's go into the work.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]]

## The offer, verbatim

> "Would you consider **scrapping your games and giving me twenty-five hundred for the cabinet and twenty percent**?… Cabinet **complete**. Complete. **Cabinet game complete. Twenty percent.** And twenty percent, and **you scrap your cabinet. I'll take your cabinet. I'll give you mine. I just want to make sure that I'm replacing it. I'll take yours, take possession of it. I'll scrap yours. I'll give you mine, brand new. You'll give me twenty-five hundred dollars plus twenty percent.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]]

So: **$2,500 per complete new cabinet with the game in it, plus 20%, with JVL taking possession of ARC's existing cabinets and scrapping them.** *(What the 20% is a percentage **of** is never said on this call. Read against the Primero structure described minutes earlier in the same conversation, it is a **revenue share** — that reading is an **inference**, not a quoted term. No contract length, no minimum volume, no start date and no territory were discussed.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]]

## What it is priced against — ARC's Primero deal, from ARC's CEO

The clearest statement of these terms anywhere in the corpus, given by Sloan in answer to Val's questions [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]]:

- **ARC's own cabinet: $1,000 plus 10%.** "every gamepad, and basically **ten percent. We use our own cabinets. It's ten percent.**" And what the $1,000 buys: "**I pay a thousand dollars for the software. I'm using my piece of equipment.**"
- **Primero's cabinet: 20%, and an up-front figure Sloan would not swear to.** "**We use their cabinets twenty, but we haven't had to use their cabinets.**" Pressed on whether the $1,000 still applies: "**The current one right now is if I use my cabinet, it's a thousand bucks. If I use their cabinet, I don't remember if it's a thousand dollars up front, but I think it still is, in twenty percent.**" **The up-front figure on the their-cabinet variant is uncertain in the source and is not asserted here.**
- **ARC has never used Primero's cabinets.** "We haven't taken either yet… **We've got a warehouse full of refurbished cabinets. We could utilize, so we've been going that instead.** Get some back out in the street. So **I haven't worried about that second part of the deal.**"
- **What a cabinet costs ARC: $2,500** — "**It cost me twenty-five hundred**" — which Val totalled back to him as "**twenty-five hundred plus a thousand, thirty-five hundred.**" *(Sloan's preceding words, "It's a steal for you," are garbled in context and are not interpreted.)*

**Val will not compete on price, and said so plainly:** "**Price-wise, I cannot touch your deal with [Primero]. They're subsidizing a crazy amount of money.**" *(Rendered "Premiere" here and "Romero's" a page later; the corpus carries this manufacturer as **Primero** and the derived notes read both as Primero.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]]

## Why Sloan did not take it

- **He would rather keep his own box and pay less.** "**I'd rather give up 10% use my box.** That's one of the things. I might open it. I might remove some of that equipment" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]].
- **And he had already been half-considering Primero's cabinet for a particular site.** "Let me just think on that because **I might have had part of my reason for using [Primero]'s cabinet was I had another place. I think I was going**" *(the sentence breaks off)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]].
- **He left performance-based room, without a band.** Asked twice whether the deal is flexible: "Is there anything that if I… **feel like I have games that perform better**… **You have a game that's doing forty bucks a day, and I get one that's doing sixty. I'll pay more for the sixty for sure**"; and "**I like to have some variety, so there is some flexibility, depending on how much that is a little bit**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]].
- **Val's own limit on generosity:** "**And I will also be buying the market, literally buying the market. But it depends how much I need to subsidize**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]].

## The number the whole deal turns on is still missing

Val asked directly — "**What's the realistic average per day now per machine?**" — and got "**Right now, I can't say it's a lot.**" Val then supplied his own working figure out loud, uncorrected: "**Thirty bucks. Ten percent is three dollars.**" Sloan's reply describes Primero's position rather than confirming the number: "**There's no cost to them at that point. I pay a thousand dollars for the software. I'm using my piece of equipment, so they're getting three bucks continuously. It's a good return for those games.**" Val's rejoinder: "**the development cost never ends. They have to continuously make games unless they stop making games**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]].

**So the $30/day is Val's assumption, not ARC's figure** — and every percentage above is being weighed against it.

## What ARC has actually committed to: nothing

**An owner correction, recorded on the derived notes and dated 2026-09-16.** The notes had flagged that Val told an internal alignment call the same day that this operator "**basically gave me an order for Class A machines**," and that nothing in this recording is an order. Val's answer, quoted in the notes: "**I agree with your record, however, I played it to my advantage.**" The stronger framing to his own engineers was **deliberate** — he used it to move the engineering work along. The notes then rule: **this record, not the alignment call, is what ARC actually committed to — nothing yet — and no ARC Class A order should be carried into any forecast** [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]].

This is consistent with the pattern the corpus already holds for this account: on Sept 9 Val told his own team he had "a verbal handshake" with ARC and corrected it the next day as a matter of speech ([[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-arc-account-exclusivity-sought-and-not-obtained.md]]). **Twice now, the internal version of an ARC conversation has run ahead of the room.**

## What Val committed to, and what he pointedly did not

- **He will build the redesigned Class A machine either way.** "My machine is very expensive, and **I need to redesign it. I can redesign it by New Year.**… I have to redesign math. I have to redesign graphics. **That's not a big deal. But I have to redesign hardware**… it's **too expensive. It just can't fit into the bulk market. So I will do it. There's no issue.**" And explicitly not on ARC's tab: "**I need it for others. It's not on your account. You're not responsible for it**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]].
- **He sold the partnership rather than the price.** "**If we make a deal, I will rush the covenant through and I will make your games. And I will go into new markets with you. I'm a very, very good, reliable partner. And I do things on a handshake and I stick with it**" *("rush the covenant through" is as transcribed; the derived notes read it as rushing **development**)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]].
- **No exclusivity of any kind was asked for on this call** — neither the account exclusivity Sloan refused on Sept 9 nor the location-level transactional exclusivity. Recorded as an absence [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]].

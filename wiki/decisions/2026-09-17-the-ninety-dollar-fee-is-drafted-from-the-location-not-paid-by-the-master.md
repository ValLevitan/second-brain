# The $90 a month is drafted from the location, not paid by the master

Settled between [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] and the visiting master licensee of [[wiki/organizations/dynamic-gaming.md|Dynamic Gaming]] at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-17-dynamic-gaming-georgia-master-licensee-and-cashless-pitch.md|Sept 17 2026, 3:17 PM meeting]]. It is a deal term for one customer, not a change to the standing offer on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-free-ports-and-pos-with-every-machine-bought.md]].

## What Val first said

"**The connection fee, we're not charging location for connection fee. We're charging you for connections.**" He also tied credit to the master's backing: "**if we are collecting money, we want to make sure that you stand behind us. Otherwise, we we can't give credit.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dynamic_Gaming_Georgia_Master_Licensee_and_Cashless_Pitch-September-17-2026.md]]

## The master's refusal

"**That that will not fly with me as a master. Even if you provide me thirty thousand dollars… I cannot be paying for hundred and thirty, hundred and fifty locations, ninety dollars a month.**" His model is how the broker he uses already works — "**the brokerage Ugo is charging. They are charging to the location**" — and how Fiery Pay handles a failed payment: "**If the EFT did not clear, fire turns off the machine… Fire turns back on the machine.**" Val warned him it would put him "**at marketing disadvantage**" because other operators will pay the fee; he answered with his seven-year location contracts. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dynamic_Gaming_Georgia_Master_Licensee_and_Cashless_Pitch-September-17-2026.md]]

## What was agreed

- **JVL bills the location.** He: "**as long as you are able to charge ninety dollars monthly to that location**" — Val: "**Then we have a deal. I I can charge whoever you want.**" The mechanism: "**They'll give you a voided check. You can draft every month ninety dollars from there.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dynamic_Gaming_Georgia_Master_Licensee_and_Cashless_Pitch-September-17-2026.md]]
- **Non-payment stops the service.** He: "**if they stop paying the fee, we stop providing the service**"; Val: "**I can turn it. No issue.**" But Val added that he would call first — "**I'm not gonna turn it off. I'm gonna call you**" — and that JVL rarely, if ever, uses a kill switch: "**I could probably count on my fingers when we… turn all this off… We never turn off.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dynamic_Gaming_Georgia_Master_Licensee_and_Cashless_Pitch-September-17-2026.md]]
- **No equipment contract.** Asked "**Sign any contract… since I got the equipment**", Val: "**No contract.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dynamic_Gaming_Georgia_Master_Licensee_and_Cashless_Pitch-September-17-2026.md]]
- **No special price.** Asked "**Do I have a better deal than them?**", Val: "**We will help you get rid of your machines and taxes, but we… can't offer discounts… We're on a mission to buy the market.**" ("taxes" reads as **Texas** in context.) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dynamic_Gaming_Georgia_Master_Licensee_and_Cashless_Pitch-September-17-2026.md]]
- **Data confidentiality, offered by Val.** "**I will gladly sign the contract of non-disclosure, and if you want, I can destroy this information upon request.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dynamic_Gaming_Georgia_Master_Licensee_and_Cashless_Pitch-September-17-2026.md]]

The derived notes record the same terms in their decisions list ("JVL bills the location directly ($90/month, voided check and pre-authorised debit), not the master", "No equipment contract and no lock-in period", "Pricing is standardised post-show") [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Dynamic_Gaming_Georgia_Master_Licensee_and_Cashless_Pitch-September-17-2026.md]].

## How this sits against other rulings

- **Same pattern as Diamond, Sept 10.** Another master refused to guarantee card-clearance debt for his locations — see [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-10-diamond-will-not-guarantee-card-clearance-debt.md]]. Here the refusal covers the **monthly fee** too. *Observation, not a source claim: two Georgia masters in a week declined to stand behind their locations' payments to JVL.*
- **No exclusivity was asked.** On Sept 9 Val asked Rao for JVL exclusivity in the location and a three-year location contract ([[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-rao-takes-the-bundle-with-jvl-exclusive-in-the-location.md]]); the Aug-31 ruling ties transactional equipment to location exclusivity ([[wiki/decisions/2026-08-31-transactional-equipment-only-with-location-exclusivity.md]]). **Nothing in this meeting mentions exclusivity**, and "no contract" was said without any condition. Recorded side by side; nothing reconciles them.
- **Credit per location.** Earlier in the meeting Andrei said JVL "**can set up a cap per location**", and the example given was "**Fifteen thousand**"; the master said he would stand behind a location only when he asks JVL to extend it more credit. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dynamic_Gaming_Georgia_Master_Licensee_and_Cashless_Pitch-September-17-2026.md]]

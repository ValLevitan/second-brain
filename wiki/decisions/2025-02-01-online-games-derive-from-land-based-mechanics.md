# Online games derive from land-based mechanics on a separate production line

**Decision (Val, Feb 1 2025):** JVL builds **game mechanics for the land-based business first**, because that is where JVL makes money; online games are **not ported** from land-based builds but produced on a **separate production line** that reuses only the **mechanics + math**. [[raw/recap_with_andrei_klimovichonlineecho212025_2025-02-01.md]]

## Rationale and detail

- Val: "our principle of mechanics is land-based necessities... we make money on land-based, so we need mechanics for land-based." He explicitly refused to adopt online-market mechanics requirements or to take Andrei Klimovich's list of currently-popular online mechanics, saying he already knows what to build from casino reports and his people. [[raw/recap_with_andrei_klimovichonlineecho212025_2025-02-01.md]]
- **Porting does not work** — the only common elements between a land-based and an online game are the **mechanics and the math**; resolution, visual effects, framing and style differ. So a **separate branch / production line** will make online games specifically, from inception to deployment, once a mechanic is ready. [[raw/recap_with_andrei_klimovichonlineecho212025_2025-02-01.md]]
- **Cadence goals:** ~**one online game per week** (a goal, subject to scaling/QA/deployment bottlenecks) and ~**one land-based game per month** (more graphics-intensive). Getting JVL's game mechanics to "catch up with the market" is expected to take until **end of 2025**. [[raw/recap_with_andrei_klimovichonlineecho212025_2025-02-01.md]]
- Related priority ruling in the same call: **home solution (ECHO) = priority #1, website = priority #2**, online kept alive via a cheap account manager on existing integrations. See [[wiki/topics/echo-home-gaming-device.md]]. [[raw/recap_with_andrei_klimovichonlineecho212025_2025-02-01.md]]

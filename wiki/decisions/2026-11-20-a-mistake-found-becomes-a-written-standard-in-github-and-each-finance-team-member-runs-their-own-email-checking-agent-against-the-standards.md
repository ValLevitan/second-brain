# A mistake found becomes a written standard, and each finance-team member runs their own email-checking agent (Nov 20 2026)

Set by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] on the finance call "Account Balance - ACH Authorization Attempt", Friday Nov 20 2026, about 3:32–3:50 PM Eastern, in English with Spanish translation ([[wiki/meetings/2026-11-20-finance-team-ach-authorization-delay-and-standards-agents.md|stub]]), with [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda Giorgi]], [[wiki/people/igor-donis.md|Igor Donis]], [[wiki/people/erika-gonzalez.md|Erika Gonzalez]] and [[wiki/people/laura.md|Laura Escorcia]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Finance_Team_ACH_Authorization_Delay_and_Standards_Agents-November-20-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Finance_Team_ACH_Authorization_Delay_and_Standards_Agents-November-20-2026.md]]. We apologize for this mistake. Our normal practice is response to customer requests immediately" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Finance_Team_ACH_Authorization_Delay_and_Standards_Agents-November-20-2026.md]].
- The customer is not named on tape; Felipe's opening words "Megan Kent, the GA coin" are **unclear** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Finance_Team_ACH_Authorization_Delay_and_Standards_Agents-November-20-2026.md]].
- **Val's response.** "the only people that don't make mistakes are those that never do anything." What drives him "crazy" is people not owning mistakes, so he has "to become an investigator"; "what I love about this is quick and clear, our mistake, we screwed up" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Finance_Team_ACH_Authorization_Delay_and_Standards_Agents-November-20-2026.md]].

## The ruling

- **Fix it now, by hand.** "let's fix the immediate problem that we have. Let's do it manually as soon as possible." Who does it is not named [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Finance_Team_ACH_Authorization_Delay_and_Standards_Agents-November-20-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Finance_Team_ACH_Authorization_Delay_and_Standards_Agents-November-20-2026.md]].
- **Concept 1 — standards.** "Standards need to be filed in GitHub. We have knowledge base repository called standards. So when we make mistakes and we discover these mistakes, we write a standard. We filed the standard into GitHub." Whenever "oh, we missed it… why? Because… It wasn't in our… set of standards… So we want to update it" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Finance_Team_ACH_Authorization_Delay_and_Standards_Agents-November-20-2026.md]].
- **Concept 2 — your own agent.** Each person needs "your own routines, skills, agents that go through your e-mail on a regular basis", searching the email while referring to the standards; as new standards appear, the agent relies on them. "had we had those standards written down, and had you guys have those agents traversing through your mailboxes… the agent would have said, you missed that e-mail" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Finance_Team_ACH_Authorization_Delay_and_Standards_Agents-November-20-2026.md]].
- **It is yours, not Val's.** "You're not catering to me, you're catering to you… It's your agent, not mine." Frequency is "your style" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Finance_Team_ACH_Authorization_Delay_and_Standards_Agents-November-20-2026.md]].
- **Loose instructions work better.** "the less you direct an agent, the better the agent works. If you put an agent into a very tight description, it doesn't work so well." Example prompts: "traverse through my emails and see, maybe I have forgotten something"; or see "what is the usual issue that I might be missing, or something that Val reminds me of all the time" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Finance_Team_ACH_Authorization_Delay_and_Standards_Agents-November-20-2026.md]].

In one line: **a missed step is written down once as a standard, and each person's own agent reads their email against the standards so the next miss is caught.**

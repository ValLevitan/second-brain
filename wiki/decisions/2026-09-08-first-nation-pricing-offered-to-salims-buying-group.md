# "First Nation pricing" offered to Salim's buying group

[[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]]'s answer at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-08-dixie-amusement-bundle-offer-and-card-economics.md|Sept-8 2026 Dixie visit]] to the standing Georgia problem recorded since Aug 31 — **how JVL gets in front of a group of master licensees at once** rather than one at a time. He took the structure from a precedent [[wiki/people/salim-khambawala.md|Salim Khambawala]] had just described to him, and offered it back in the same breath. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]

## What was offered

> Val: "**Help me secure all your friends in one custom deal, and I'll give you First Nation prices.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]

And the term itself, as he defined it on the spot: "**You could sell at the same price, but not cheaper than to us. First nation price.**" Earlier in the same exchange: "You know what it's called in law. Actually, there is a legal term for this. **It's called First Nation pricing. In America, in law, there is such a clause.**" *(That is Val's own characterisation of the term's legal standing; **no source in the corpus corroborates it** and none is asserted here.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]

So the commitment is a **price floor for the group, not a discount** — JVL may sell the same terms to anybody, but nobody gets better terms than the group that commits first.

## The 2015 precedent it copies

Salim's account, unprompted, of the last time this was done to him — by the man who then held the **IGT** distribution for Georgia: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]

- **Who.** "who got me involved was **Lee Hunter from IGT**. He was the original owner. So when he brought IGT in **2015**, he was at the expo… he is like **the founding father of this industry**." Val's read on hearing it: "**He commands respect.**"
- **The ask.** "These people around you, and **get them to buy hundred equipment**." Salim: "So what are you offering?" Hunter: "I don't know, **five hundred dollars**."
- **Salim's condition, which is the whole idea.** "Okay, five hundred or five dollars or a thousand dollars. I don't care. Five hundred dollars, **but if you sell it to this group, it has to be the best price. Nobody could be below or over.**"
- **What it produced.** "**Twelve master license, fifteen maybe, around twelve or fifteen master license. We formed the group. We gave an order, but there were twenty more asking, 'What is Dixie buying?'**… **But we won the same pricing.**"

Val's aim in copying it is the one he had already named that afternoon — "**Everyone will come. That's why I'm trying to buy my way through**" — and his sizing of who is worth convening: "if you give me your business exclusively, what happens to the other masters? **They'll go crazy. They'll give me their business.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]

## Why Salim is the only person who can do it

- **He has run the group before, and lets it lapse.** Recorded from Aug 31 on [[wiki/people/salim-khambawala.md]]: "one year, two years, I do it, then I let it go because it's too much work for me."
- **He says a small core will follow him without asking.** "**A couple of my close friends, if I tell them to jump, they don't ask why. They say how.** Once I run that idea with them." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]
- **And most of the market will follow the followers.** His own numbers: "there is only **190 master licenses** out there, but only **80 operators**"; "from even that **sixty**, I would say **twenty would talk what we are talking. The rest forty is monkey see, monkey do** type of mindset." Val's gloss: "It's always the **eighty-twenty rule** in everything." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]
- **This is the route Val had already concluded was the only one that works.** On Aug 31 he ruled out dinners and open houses and said "**let me talk to your friends. You can only influence your friends. If you personally say, look, let's meet here for one hour, they'll come.**" See [[wiki/people/salim-khambawala.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]

## What is not settled

Salim neither accepted nor refused. He said he needed to talk to his people first — "Unless I have not taken him, because **I need to talk to him to understand**" — and asked for roughly two weeks, framing the whole thing as next year's business: "**In preparing for next year, we can start talking, start thinking, and see what we come up with.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]

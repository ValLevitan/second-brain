# AKS tests the full JVL transactional system at two new locations before year end; JVL installs the first location

**Decided:** October 14 2026, at the JVL Georgia office — [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-14-aks-amusements-transactional-demo-two-location-test-and-financing.md]]. **By whom:** [[wiki/organizations/aks-amusement.md|AKS Amusement]] (speaker labels only; which AKS person is not marked), with [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] and [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda]] for JVL [[raw/Meeting_Notes_AKS_Amusements_Transactional_Demo_Two_Location_Test_and_Financing-October-14-2026.md]].

**Status note:** the intent is agreed; the two locations are **not yet named**. AKS said it would try to answer the next day. This page stays active until a later source names the sites or drops the test.

## What was decided

- **AKS wants the test, with data.** An AKS speaker: "I would like to get this into a location, and again, with thirty days of data." Another: "No, two new locations. So we already bought the two machines from you, and we bought two more where we could then set up this whole, whole setup and test it". The aim is "by the end of the year, hopefully, we have 30, 30 plus days of the data", and it must work first time: "we don't want it to be where you know it's not working, functioning properly." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AKS_Amusements_Transactional_Demo_Two_Location_Test_and_Financing-October-14-2026.md]]
- **The package.** Felipe: "we can commit to the two you already have plus two for this location, right?" AKS: "We want the entire package." Val: "so it will be two machines plus two machines plus four. Yeah. Transactional." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AKS_Amusements_Transactional_Demo_Two_Location_Test_and_Financing-October-14-2026.md]]
- **JVL installs the first location.** Asked "Is it possible for you guys to do a test install?", Val: "we will install your first location. We will send our people to install your first location." Felipe added that Andrei would be there for the first installation, "And Sebastian, our other engineer" — that is, [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] and [[wiki/people/sebastian.md|Sebastian]] (the Sebastian link is an **inference** from "our other engineer" brought to Georgia). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AKS_Amusements_Transactional_Demo_Two_Location_Test_and_Financing-October-14-2026.md]]
- **Answer by the next day.** An AKS speaker: "I'll try to get you an answer by tomorrow… We'll find two locations that are good locations". Val: "I strongly advise you to take that deal." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AKS_Amusements_Transactional_Demo_Two_Location_Test_and_Financing-October-14-2026.md]]

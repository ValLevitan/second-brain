# Ship 100 best machines from Mexico to Georgia; scrap the rest after audit

## What was decided

Ship the **100 best-condition machines** (selected by Miguel from the ~330–390 in the Mexico warehouse) to Georgia now, in **two 53-foot trucks by ground**, door-to-door [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]].

## Key points

- Packing ~**$45/machine**; ATLAS quoted **$12,000** for the two trucks; Mary to get competing quotes from BOOMER and ASIMEX [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]].
- Preferred routing: **ASIMEX** Mexico City → Laredo, then **ATLAS** Laredo → Toronto/Georgia; quote the two legs separately [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]].
- Check with ASIMEX (contact **Alejandro**) whether export taxes paid on the way into Mexico can be rebated on export [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]].
- **Truck #3** should carry mostly Altera and Altera V machines [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]].
- After shipment: Miguel sorts the warehouse (garbage vs. keep), orders a scrap container, and calls in **external auditors** to count the write-off machines **before** scrapping, so Val can properly write them off inventory. Val framed this as an order, not a request [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]].

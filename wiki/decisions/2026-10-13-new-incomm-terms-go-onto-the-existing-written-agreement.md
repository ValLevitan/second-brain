# New InComm–JVL terms go onto the existing written agreement

Decided on the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-13-incomm-prize-play-reset-agreement-scope-and-cash-dispense.md|Oct-13 2026 phone call]] between [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] and Jason of [[wiki/organizations/incom.md|InComm]] (read as Jason Hall — **inference**). The relationship side of the same call is on [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-13-incomm-and-jvl-reset-the-relationship-and-launch-on-prize-play-first.md]].

## What was decided

- **Work from the paper.** Jason: "We should just go back to what's on paper, and any of the pieces that you want to negotiate, let's just get it introduced onto that agreement, and negotiate from there." Later: "we probably just need to refresh the agreement and get the new items you want to discuss on there." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Prize_Play_Reset_Agreement_Scope_and_Cash_Dispense-October-13-2026.md]]
- **One template for both.** Val: "Send me the agreement you are referring to. I want to make sure we work off the same template." Jason: "I'll zip that over to you right now." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Prize_Play_Reset_Agreement_Scope_and_Cash_Dispense-October-13-2026.md]] The notes record this as proposed by Jason, with Val asking for the document [[raw/Meeting_Notes_InComm_Prize_Play_Reset_Agreement_Scope_and_Cash_Dispense-October-13-2026.md]].

## What goes onto it

- **The $300,000 daily cap (raised by Jason).** "There's a three hundred thousand daily cap amount… what this is saying is you're gonna wire us as soon as you have three hundred thousand dollars or more outstanding. And if you are planning to roll out your solution across a high number of locations, I think you'll hit that amount pretty quickly." He asked Val to think it through "based on your settlement timing with the locations". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Prize_Play_Reset_Agreement_Scope_and_Cash_Dispense-October-13-2026.md]] *(This is a cap in JVL's own agreement with InComm. It is not the $5,000 default daily cap per location told to a customer on Sept 15 — see [[wiki/organizations/incom.md]].)*
- **Val's items from John.** Val said he had talked with John (read as [[wiki/people/john-houseal.md|John Houseal]] — **inference**) about InComm guaranteeing "those transactions that have not been paid", and about InComm paying JVL "half a percent of all the money being held in accounts". Jason: "I know about that last one." Val called the guarantee time sensitive, "because I'm going to be clearing a lot of transactions". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Prize_Play_Reset_Agreement_Scope_and_Cash_Dispense-October-13-2026.md]]
- **A broader talk later.** Jason also spoke of "a broader conversation with how you want to have a bigger piece of the market in the settlement in the brokerage". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Prize_Play_Reset_Agreement_Scope_and_Cash_Dispense-October-13-2026.md]]

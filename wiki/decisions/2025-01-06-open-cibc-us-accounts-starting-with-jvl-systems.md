# Open CIBC US accounts, starting with JVL Systems Inc.

## What was decided

JVL will open US-dollar business accounts at CIBC for its three companies, done one at a time in succession, beginning with **JVL Systems Inc.** (the Florida US company) as the most important. [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]]

## Who and when

- Decided on the Jan 6, 2025 CIBC account-setup call, with [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] leading and [[wiki/people/monica.md|Monica]] on the call; [[wiki/people/inna-levitan.md|Ina]], Val, and Monica will be the three signers. [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]]
- CIBC contacts: [[wiki/people/tony.md|Tony]] (relationship), Michelle Tumby (retiring), and [[wiki/people/taylor.md|Taylor]] (ongoing primary contact). [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]]

## Why

- JVL needs a US account so US clients can pay by ACH at no cost, which is cheaper and easier for them than the current setup; funds can then be wired to Canada. [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]]
- Accounts will be opened one at a time; Taylor can reuse the signers' ID across all three, but needs articles of organisation and the EIN letter for each company. [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]]

## Open items before opening

- CIBC to confirm whether **Belarus** is a prohibited country for US wires. [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]]
- CIBC to confirm whether its "cash management online" platform can interface with JVL's accounting system (Microsoft Business Central / migrating from Navision), the way JVL's RBC API does today. [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]]

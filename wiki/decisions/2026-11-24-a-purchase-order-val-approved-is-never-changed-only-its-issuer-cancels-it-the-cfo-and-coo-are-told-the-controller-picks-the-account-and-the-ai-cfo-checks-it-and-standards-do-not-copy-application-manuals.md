# A purchase order Val approved is never changed; only its issuer cancels it; standards do not copy application manuals (Nov 24 2026)

## How it came up

- **The case.** On the [[wiki/meetings/2026-11-24-accounting-team-ai-show-and-tell.md|Nov-24 1:05 PM accounting call]] [[wiki/people/mary.md|Mery Villavicencio]] showed that she gave Claude the GitHub standards and asked whether they cover a purchase order created with the wrong G/L account — "el caso que vimos con la compra de la PC de Antón. La compra fue creada este días atrás por Mikhail. Se usó una cuenta contable este que correspondía a materiales, debiendo ser una cuenta de gasto." The standards do not say what to do once a PO is approved and received. [[wiki/people/igor-donis.md|Igor Donis]] gave the right account and, since it was a credit-card purchase, it was fixed at the accounting level [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Accounting_Team_AI_Show_and_Tell-November-24-2026.md]]. "Mikhail" = [[wiki/people/mikhail-perelman.md|Mikhail Perelman]] by the purchasing context (**inference**); which Anton is not said.
- **The question.** Mery: "¿Qué deberíamos hacer, abrir la orden de compra, cancelarla y emitir una nueva orden de compra con la cuenta correcta o se debería modificar solamente a nivel contable?" Before Val joined, she and [[wiki/people/laura.md|Laura Escorcia]] also said this should be in the standard, and that the people who use the standards should have a say [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Accounting_Team_AI_Show_and_Tell-November-24-2026.md]].

## What Val ruled

Not on tape. Per the notes' dispatch log, Val first had an email drafted at 5:04 PM ET asking [[wiki/people/lev-rudky.md|Lev Rudky]] (Cc Vaselisa, who is to follow up) to write a standard on fixing a purchase order approved with the wrong account; at 5:24 PM ET the draft was rewritten after **Val's rulings in chat** [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Accounting_Team_AI_Show_and_Tell-November-24-2026.md]]:

1. **Add to the purchase-order standard:** nobody changes a purchase order after Val approves it; only the person who issued it may cancel it; the CFO and COO are told of every cancelled purchase order; the controller decides the right account and the AI CFO verifies it; no Business Central click steps in the standard [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Accounting_Team_AI_Show_and_Tell-November-24-2026.md]].
2. **A new short standard:** standards do not duplicate application user manuals; for "how do I" questions, people point their own AI at the application's online user manual first, then ask MIS or IT [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Accounting_Team_AI_Show_and_Tell-November-24-2026.md]].

Val sent the email to Lev on Nov 25 2026 at 3:16 PM ET, Cc Vaselisa [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Accounting_Team_AI_Show_and_Tell-November-24-2026.md]].

In one line: **an approved purchase order is frozen; a wrong one is cancelled by its issuer, with the CFO and COO told; standards say who decides, not where to click.**

# Credits on the buttons instead of currency, and the maths on two games changes

**Decided by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] on the evening of Sept 8 2026 and reported the next morning** at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-09-september-resource-plan-and-flex-port-readiness.md|Sept 9 7:02 AM call]] — the page is dated to when the decision was taken, per §11, and cites the Sept-9 sources that record it.

## The change

[[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]] asked what the maths wishes were — "ты там что[-]то с математикой… какие[-]то пожелания[?] [В] кредитах отображать[?]" — and Val answered: "[п]ожелания очень простые. **Вместо Того, чтоб отражать на кнопках валюту, я хочу отражать кредиты**" — instead of showing currency on the buttons he wants credits shown [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_September_Resource_Plan_and_FlexPort_Readiness-September-9-2026.md]].

And the second half: "**[[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Ефим]] сказал, что ему нужно поменять математику на 2 играх** и сказал, что это **14[ часов] работы**" — Efim said the maths on two games has to change, and estimated it at fourteen hours. Val confirmed the scope when Irina checked it: "2 игры он сказал надо поменять математику" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_September_Resource_Plan_and_FlexPort_Readiness-September-9-2026.md]]. *(The duration is rendered "14:00 работы" in the transcript — a clock-time artifact of the Russian "14 часов"; the derived notes read it as "about 14 hours of work" [[raw/Meeting_Notes_September_Resource_Plan_and_FlexPort_Readiness-September-9-2026.md]].)*

## The point of the page: it is settled, and someone thought it was not

Irina reported the opposite understanding — "**[о]н сказал, что решение ещё не принято**[,] поэтому если решение принято, то [пусть] делает" — Efim had told her the decision was not yet taken [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_September_Resource_Plan_and_FlexPort_Readiness-September-9-2026.md]].

Val closed it flatly: "**[р]ешение… было принято вчера в 21:00**[.] Я ему написал, чтобы он делал математику" — decided the previous evening at 9:00 PM, and he had written to Efim to start [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_September_Resource_Plan_and_FlexPort_Readiness-September-9-2026.md]]. Pavel accepted it on the call: "[х]орошо[,] принято, да?" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_September_Resource_Plan_and_FlexPort_Readiness-September-9-2026.md]].

**The delivery date follows from the estimate:** "Я ему вчера сказал, чтоб он начинал это делать. Это значит, **до 11 числа он должен вам сдать**" — on that basis it reaches the team by September 11 [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_September_Resource_Plan_and_FlexPort_Readiness-September-9-2026.md]].

## Why it matters now

Val raised it immediately after saying the V3 product has to be thoroughly tested for the Eclipse submission around September 15 — see [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]] — so the maths change lands inside that window. Game-maths ownership sits with Efim Podvoiski; the broader maths-container direction is on [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-18-nova-math-container-per-game-set.md]].

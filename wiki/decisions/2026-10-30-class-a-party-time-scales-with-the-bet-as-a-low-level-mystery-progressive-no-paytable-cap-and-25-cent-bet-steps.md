# Class A party time scales with the bet; no paytable cap; 25-cent bet steps on screen (Oct 30 2026)

Worked out at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-30-game-development-class-a-party-time-and-colombia-refresh.md|Oct 30 2026, 2:00 PM game-development meeting]] (English) between [[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael Gottlieb]], [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim Podvoiski]] and [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], with [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena Zhukova]] present [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]]. It changes the party-time economics set on [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-08-class-a-party-time-may-start-on-the-paid-spin-after-free-spins-and-lions-riches-is-the-single-target.md|Oct 8]], where each party-time win was "50 bucks a pop". Background: [[wiki/topics/georgia-class-a-machines.md]].

## 1. Free spins and party time — the flow (Michael, Efim)

- **Why party time follows free spins.** Michael: "because you can only win 50 bucks in free games before you get win capped… we were going to have the player after free games is over going to party time." The flow was walked through: "what if the player cashes out? So that would disable it… what if the player changes bets… That would disable that potentiality" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **Once, not twice, after free spins.** Michael's standing rule: "if you get party time, you'll get it twice at minimum… twice is $100." Coming out of free spins there may not be enough money for two, so party time may trigger "maybe once after free spins." Efim thought one might disappoint; Michael: the player was win-capped at $50 and "on the next spin, they get thrown another $50" — "the surprise and delight of hitting party time once." Val: "Seems, seems, seems logical." Michael: "all of this is theoretical" until the games run [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **Base game.** Val asked whether the base game awards anything; Michael: "we'll definitely award wins in the base game… water to help get you… across the desert to the oasis," but "a meaningful amount of Rtp" sits in the $50 wins. Val: "it's all about getting the party time. Base is not important" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].

## 2. The frequency problem, and Val's market facts

- **Frequency depends on the bet.** Val asked "What is the win frequency?" Michael: "Haven't determined that yet." Efim: "it depends on your bid, because the higher the bid, the more often it happens, because it's always $50, so… it's not scaled" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **Why $50 at all?** Val: "why do you have to give $50 on the twenty-five cent bet?" Michael: "That's what our competitors are doing. When you hit that bonus round, you win 50 bucks. That's the lowest win that I saw," and "you don't want to be less than" the competitor next to you. He already planned "a conversation about 10, 20, 30, 40, and $50 wins and basically tie the win levels to the bet levels" if low bets felt dead, but wanted to "start with that $50 and see what it feels like" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **Val's position.** "most people don't play on maximum bet"; "I would rather go for smaller wins on a per win basis, increase the frequency of wins"; "3 * 10 or 1 * 50? Three times 10 is better." Michael's own thought experiment agreed: "$10 three times in a row… gets your endorphins pumping and you're in the event for a longer period of time" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **The competitor machine does not earn (Val).** "We know that the competitive machines doesn't really make money." The industry is excited "about the potential of unregulated Class A machine entering the market", not about what the machine makes; Michael: "It bypasses the tax." Val: locations are unlimited because the machines are unregulated — "right now the market is 47,000 machines, but Class A, if it's properly manufactured, may take it to 100,000 machines." But "some of the locations make $6 a day per machine, and some… Forty dollars a day… The disparity between 6:00 [6] and 40 is huge. And nobody will dive into a business at such spread… It will be very, very cautious market." Operators "don't find the play in particularly interesting." Michael: "this is new information. This is new data for me." Val on the law: Class A was meant for "crane type of amusement devices… to limit the maximum win," and nobody foresaw the workaround of "multiple awards" — "we're kind of linking a chain of single games into a party time." His verdict on the competitor game: "it does not have legs" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]]. The notes name the competitor as Primero; the transcript has Michael say "the model that's been put forth by Primero isn't working" [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **What players bet.** Val: "a player will not be betting $5 or $6… the player really feels comfortable at around a dollar… a dollar to $1.50." Michael: "Agreed. No question." Val: "Why cater to a player at $5 bet if we… already know that we need to cater to a player at a dollar?" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]]
- **Play evidence from the Georgia trip.** Michael: Val "popped 50, 50, 50… 250 bucks in one session"; Val: "I want 800, I want $800" (heard — read as "won"; **unclear**). Michael: "I pumped $800 through those things"; free spins triggered once and paid "like 30 bucks"; a person betting low next to Val lost money. Michael: "We're failing if the… experience at 5 bucks a spin is significantly superior to the experience at… the lower bet levels" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].

## 3. The ruling — a low-level mystery progressive

- **Michael turns.** "the model that's been put forth by Primero isn't working. So there's no reason to chase what isn't working." JVL already does the same thing elsewhere: "we do it in the Hold and Respin Bonus… You can win the lower level progressive multiple times." Thought experiment: "if we set it 5 bet levels, 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, I'm just being arbitrary… even if you're a 25 cent player, you can pop party time once or twice and win 10 or $20." Then: "Okay, Val, I'm with you," and after Val's market facts, "Okay, I'm sold… if you're telling me the stuff they're doing is not working, there is no argument to be made. This is an opportunity." Val: "that I know factually" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **The model.** "treat it like a mystery progressive… a low-level mystery progressive." The value "goes up as you bet more. 10, 20, 30, 40, 50," like progressive values. **Within one party-time session every win has the same value:** "You don't want the values all over the place. You want them fixed… either all those wins are 10 bucks or all those wins are 20 bucks" — so the player learns "I go into party time. I got a $10 win. Let's see how many $10 wins I can get" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **Where the money comes from.** "shave off the top end of the pay table a little bit… and then use that money to give it back to the player via party time by popping party time at… 10 bucks a pop" — the high-pay symbols hit less often, party time more often [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **Free spins gain value too.** Lower party-time wins "give free spins some legitimacy"; with $50 party-time wins and a hard $50 free-spins ceiling, "Free Spins is less than. It is not what you're chasing" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **Val's "two games in one" and his mixed-value idea, declined.** Val: "if we don't breach the $50 maximum win, we should use the casino logic, the native casino logic of bet and awards… The party time only kicks in as a loophole for larger awards. I look at it as two games in one." Efim: "Yeah, me too, actually." Val also floated "assorted wins that are below 50… five, five, 15, 50, and 50" linked into one win; Michael: "adding a level of complexity that is perhaps unnecessary." Val: "I'm not a game designer… Don't read this as a proposal. This is a pure conversation" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **Efim's open maths.** "$50 is natural cup and you can win more than $50, but it's still $50… You artificially make a win of $10 exactly? Find the combinations that gives always $10 or… twelve dollars here, fifteen dollars here… We would have to think about it." Efim's own view: a good game is possible on small bets, where the $50 cap does not bite; party time "allows players who bet at least $2.00 to have a meaningful game." Michael: "it becomes 3D chess for [Efim] mentally"; at 25 cents "you have to have a multiple of 25 cents that makes sense" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **Michael on JVL's games.** Lions Riches "will play better… You'll hit it more often"; "A cabinet making $4 a day is not a cabinet I'm concerned about"; the competitor's art and presentation are "really bad" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].

## 4. No paytable cap at higher bets

- **What the competitor does.** Val: "you know how you increase your bet and the odds? Paytable doesn't change anymore." Michael: "It caps… whether you're 4 bucks a spin or 6 bucks a spin, the top award is green." Val: "isn't it insulting?… It's insulting and it's stupid." Michael: "It doesn't give you a reason to bet higher." Efim: "We will not do it," and later "we definitely will avoid capping the Paytable this way or that way" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **Val on the competitor's bet buttons.** "they made another mistake by imitating the bad [bet] buttons as they have in Georgia co-op [COAM]"; "if you want to have five bet buttons, they should be lower bet… start with 25 cents and go maybe to $2.50"; later: "This is not a Georgia coam machine… Five bed [bet] buttons from 50 cents to $2.50. You're fine." Val's maximum bet on the competitor machine: "$6, I think, or $6.50. I don't even know where they got $6 from" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **Street business, not a casino (Val).** "This is not a casino. This is a street business." High rollers "occupy two, three machines" to show status; "I know street business." On copying competitors: "For the past 40 years, I've been dealing with the same question. Why do our competitors do something? And how often do we say at the end, well, that was stupid… if I cannot explain it, it doesn't mean that I have to copy it" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **Efim's data point.** "some data from Jacob from a few years ago": "quarter of the money on the beds [bets] comes from beds from three to $5… 28%, 29%, something like this." Val: maybe that is why the competitor designed it so, "but it still doesn't cater to logical explanation." Which Jacob is not stated (see Notes on the stub) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].

## 5. Bet steps of 25 cents on screen, the Aristocrat/IGT way

- **What the market does.** Michael: competitors bet "in 25 cent increments, starting at 25… 25, 50, 75, a dollar, a dollar 25, a dollar 50"; Georgia COAM, Aristocrat and IGT all use on-screen "up and down arrows for your bet levels"; "IGT Aristocrat and Light and Wonder are all doing the bet up and down on the screen" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **Val's doubt, then agreement.** Val: "I think I steered you the wrong way to do the Aristocrat type of button… I think it's a mistake." Michael: "50,000 Elvis fans can't be wrong." Val then: "I think what we're doing is correct, and I steered you the wrong way," and a garbled line about the V-deck ("I think our VDEC is devil" — heard, **unclear**). He kept one reservation: "I don't believe that this is what makes Aristocrat make money… I don't think that changing it specifically because our machine will be making more money." Michael: "I agree with that point. I'm just talking about the flow, reducing friction." This reverses Val's Sept-4 theory on the Sept-4 page — see [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-04-georgia-v4-mechanical-button-deck.md]] (Oct-30 update) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **The zero-out reason (Michael, answering Efim's "why would somebody bet 6.75").** In Class A "you're earning awards. It doesn't go to your credit meter… I now have a fractional number of credits left." With 25-cent steps "I can basically set my bet to the amount of money that I have left" — e.g. $1.75 — and "zero yourself out" instead of cashing out small change; "you need a certain minimum number to get a gift card… There's even a charge for the stupid gift card." Bluntly: "To get every last nickel out of the player." Efim: with $1.75 you could bet $1.50 then 25 cents, "But yeah, easier to bet once and makes more sense for the player." Val: "That part, yes." The cash-out kiosk on the trip "didn't even work" — a 30-digit number typed on screen for a gift card (Michael, about his companion) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **Set it and forget it.** Michael: "by having the buttons, it's the piano. I think it invites people to jerk around. It probably reduces velocity of play… I saw set it and forget it." Val: "What is the compelling argument here is that set it and forget it" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **The cost (Efim).** "there is a big cost of making all this intermediate bed [bet] levels, because math is different… it's testing." Five natural bets "25, 50, dollar, two, two and a half" mean five maths; "If you have 25 cents increment, you have 10 maths instead of five, if you max bet to [2.]50" — "twice as much work… adding more tests to do more ways to make a mistake" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **The call.** Michael: "I think we should do it the Aristocrat way, the IGT Aristocrat way… I think we'd put ourselves in harm's way if we don't do it for Class A." Efim: "I am not judging… I trust your vision." Val accepted [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **V-deck on the Class A cabinet.** Val: "if we wanted to get rid of the V deck and just use buttons. That is an advantage." Michael: "If you were building the cabinet and saying, do we have VDEC or don't, I wouldn't spend the money on it for Class A. I don't… see any advantage or upside to it." Val: "all you need is 1 slam button." Michael: "a part can't fail that doesn't exist." Not decided [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].

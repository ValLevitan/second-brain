# A part price comes only from a vendor quotation in procurement@jvl.ca; the GitHub–MSBC link is never cut (Nov 27 2026)

Taken on a ~1 h 38 min call on Friday Nov 27 2026 between [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] and [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi Peari]] (mostly Russian, some English; many lines carry the speaker label "Room", so who said them is taken from context, as the notes also do) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_FlexPort_GitHub_MSBC_Link_and_Vendor_Quote_Rule-November-27-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Genadi_FlexPort_GitHub_MSBC_Link_and_Vendor_Quote_Rule-November-27-2026.md]]. It is the day after the [[wiki/decisions/2026-11-26-a-bom-has-two-sources-of-truth-the-product-description-and-the-github-data-contracts-mery-orders-from-msbc-revision-1-now-and-genadi-finishes-the-data-contracts-for-revision-2.md|Nov-26 two-sources-of-truth ruling]], and works through a real FlexPort part. ## 1. The price rule (dictated by Val to Claude)

- **What triggered it.** Claude's table showed two prices for the 5-inch LCD (MON-480X854-5I-TS-1): "it's picking up две цены какие-то, 4.34 или 24.30" (Val) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_FlexPort_GitHub_MSBC_Link_and_Vendor_Quote_Rule-November-27-2026.md]]. Val asked Claude: "what is the source of truth for these prices?" Claude's answer, pasted into the chat by Val: "Neither number has a vendor quote or invoice behind it that I can find" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_FlexPort_GitHub_MSBC_Link_and_Vendor_Quote_Rule-November-27-2026.md]].
- **Where each number came from** (Claude's answer as pasted) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_FlexPort_GitHub_MSBC_Link_and_Vendor_Quote_Rule-November-27-2026.md]]:
  - **$4.34** — the item-card cost. It comes from 2 receipts of 30 units each, posted Dec 31 2025 and Jan 1 2026; "The vendor on those receipts is JVLCAN01, which is JVL Labs, not Startek. So it is an internal transfer cost between the two JVL companies, not a supplier price." One receipt was reversed by a credit memo.
  - **$24.30** — the price on PO P_ORD000000502 to Startek, dated Sep 15 2026, entered by purchaser "MP": 70 units at $24.30 = $1,701 plus $220 freight, $1,921 in total. In procurement@jvl.ca, Mery asked for payment of $1,921 on Oct 28, Nov 9 and Nov 25; it was still pending on Nov 25. "There is no Startek quote or invoice in procurement@ and no posted invoice in MSBC."
  - Val's reading: "Когда мы отсылали это, э, эти детали, мы поставили цену липовую" (when we sent these parts we put a fake price).
- **Why Val liked the finding.** "То есть нету факта… если ты не видишь корреспонденции от вендора или инвойса или чего-то такого, значит, ты не можешь просто так enter numbers" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_FlexPort_GitHub_MSBC_Link_and_Vendor_Quote_Rule-November-27-2026.md]].
- **The rule, as Val typed it into the chat** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_FlexPort_GitHub_MSBC_Link_and_Vendor_Quote_Rule-November-27-2026.md]]:
  - "None of these is ever a source of truth. Memorize it for the future."
  - "The only source of truth is a direct Vendor quote that can only be sourced from the procurement@jvl.ca email box"
  - It may come "in a form of a casual email or an official quotation - in which case, a casual email can only be considered as an indicative price - not final."
  - "A final price that can enter MSBC must always be supported by a vendor's quotation emailed to on their letterhead to our procurement@jvl.ca"
  - Spoken next (partly Room-labelled): "If you cannot locate a quotation, this means we can not have a quote in MSBC hence bring those irregularities to my attention."
  - To Genadi: "Я хочу, чтобы вы его запомнили… потому что это будет являться нашим modem of operation" ("modem" as heard; mode of operation).
- **"None of these"** = the item-card cost, our own PO price and the receipts just traced; the notes state it as "MSBC item costs, our own PO prices and receipts are never a source" [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Genadi_FlexPort_GitHub_MSBC_Link_and_Vendor_Quote_Rule-November-27-2026.md]].
- **The re-run.** Claude rebuilt the dashboard on the rule: "0 of 14 parts have a vendor quotation and 11 have MSBC costs with no quotation" (screen description). Val: "Zero деталей with vendor quotation. Но это может быть просто надо подтянуть emails" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_FlexPort_GitHub_MSBC_Link_and_Vendor_Quote_Rule-November-27-2026.md]]. Genadi's check — does procurement hold the whole history and every quote? — "Конечно" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_FlexPort_GitHub_MSBC_Link_and_Vendor_Quote_Rule-November-27-2026.md]].

## 2. The GitHub–MSBC link is never cut

- **The starting point.** Val took the FlexPort assembly PT1-M05-B10-12-01 ("Я назову это short PT1"; spoken as "PT1-M05-V10-12-01"). Genadi: a PO is not issued for the assembly but for "all all parts, которые under this assembly". Today the assembly's BOM has no link to GitHub — "Линк есть только на флат" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_FlexPort_GitHub_MSBC_Link_and_Vendor_Quote_Rule-November-27-2026.md]]. Claude, reading MSBC: "PT1-M05-B10-12-01 is an assembly. JVL builds it from 20 parts, so you don't buy it as one item. Its own item card has no vendor either"; "None of the 20 parts has a vendor on its item card" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_FlexPort_GitHub_MSBC_Link_and_Vendor_Quote_Rule-November-27-2026.md]].
- **Val's reasoning.** If the link is cut, "MSBC ничего не знает, про какие-то изменения"; a change in GitHub would have to be reported by hand. Kept, it sees what changed in the flat list — and when a new supplier is chosen, the assembly must know to order from it [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_FlexPort_GitHub_MSBC_Link_and_Vendor_Quote_Rule-November-27-2026.md]].
- **The ruling.** Building the assembly, freezing it and saving it into MSBC — "Вот этот шаг неправильный"; "link it to MSBC. Не copy-paste. а link it. to MSBC." "а за основу нужно браться. GitHub." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_FlexPort_GitHub_MSBC_Link_and_Vendor_Quote_Rule-November-27-2026.md]]. A "Room" line says "А вчера я думал, что можно" — the notes read it as Val noting that the day before they had thought the link could be cut [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Genadi_FlexPort_GitHub_MSBC_Link_and_Vendor_Quote_Rule-November-27-2026.md]].
- **How, not settled.** A "Room" line: "Как это можно линк? Я еще не знаю." Val's idea: a standing query, for example when a PO is made, where Claude checks only "чтобы part numbers на каждую деталь в assembly совпадала с теми же part numbers в GitHub" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_FlexPort_GitHub_MSBC_Link_and_Vendor_Quote_Rule-November-27-2026.md]]. Genadi's alternative is a lock: "Если я хочу что-то изменить, я должен unlock, потому что должен быть reason for this, right? Какой-то ECN" — then lock it back. Val: "я не знаю, что такое лог… Но мы говорим о том, что линк постоянно должен сохраняться" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_FlexPort_GitHub_MSBC_Link_and_Vendor_Quote_Rule-November-27-2026.md]].
- **Genadi's worry.** "Проблема в том, что вся чё- цены находятся в MSBC." Val: Claude works in seconds, so checking every item is no burden [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_FlexPort_GitHub_MSBC_Link_and_Vendor_Quote_Rule-November-27-2026.md]].

# The ramp counts units of equipment, not locations

On Sept 9 2026 [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] set last-quarter volume twice, to two different audiences, in two different sets of figures. **Both sets are recorded here side by side; neither source mentions the other and nothing in either resolves them into one plan.** What both share is the unit of measure and the disclaimer.

## The figures as given

**To the Minsk engineers, [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-09-september-resource-plan-and-flex-port-readiness.md|7:02 AM]]** — counted as *installations* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_September_Resource_Plan_and_FlexPort_Readiness-September-9-2026.md]]:

| When | Figure (Val's words) |
|---|---|
| October 1 | "1 [ин]ст[ол]" — one installation |
| during October | "[з]а октябрь мы делаем 10 инст[ол]ейшен" |
| from November 1 | "[с] 1 ноября мы делаем 200 installat[ions]" |
| during November | "[в] ноябре мы делаем 500 instal[l]ations" |

The derived notes read the same passage as "one installation on October 1, ten installations during October, 200 from November 1, and 500 during November" [[raw/Meeting_Notes_September_Resource_Plan_and_FlexPort_Readiness-September-9-2026.md]].

**To the North-America group, [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-09-october-rollout-staging-and-unit-ramp.md|8:43 AM]]** — counted as *units* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_October_Rollout_Staging_and_Unit_Ramp-September-9-2026.md]]:

| When | Figure (Val's words) |
|---|---|
| October | "hundred units, total" |
| November | "November, five hundred units" |
| December | "December, a thousand units. It's giving you an exponential" |

The derived notes read the same set as "100 units in October, 500 in November, 1,000 in December… about seven units per location" [[raw/Meeting_Notes_October_Rollout_Staging_and_Unit_Ramp-September-9-2026.md]].

He restated the November and December numbers before closing: "November, I'm looking for **500 units**. In December… **a thousand units**. So you guys would have to schedule it in a way that it could work out" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_October_Rollout_Staging_and_Unit_Ramp-September-9-2026.md]].

## What a "unit" is

- **Not a location.** "I will explain what unit means, **not locations**… I mean combined units of ports and pay and IQ all bundled together. So let's say it will be… the machine. So everything together" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_October_Rollout_Staging_and_Unit_Ramp-September-9-2026.md]].
- **About seven per location.** After a colleague corrected him downward — "[t]hat's why I said it's an average. It's less" — Val landed on: "it will be **six ports, one IQ, one pay, and one game machine. So seven units**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_October_Rollout_Staging_and_Unit_Ramp-September-9-2026.md]]. *(The arithmetic of the sentence sums to nine; Val says "seven". Recorded as spoken, not corrected.)*
- **In Russian he said the same thing and admitted he had not settled the measure.** "Инсталле[йшенс] я не говорю лакейшнов[,] я говорю единиц[ —] в каком[-]то измерении… я ещё пока не понимаю, в каком измерении… я не говорю, что это будет 200 лаке[й]шинов, я говорю, что это будет **единицы нашего оборудования**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_September_Resource_Plan_and_FlexPort_Readiness-September-9-2026.md]].

## Val labelled the numbers as intent, not forecast — in both meetings

- Russian: "[я] специально эти цифры говорю, конечно, они не будут такие большие, но я специально говорю, чтоб вы понимали, о чём я говорю" — the numbers are deliberately overstated so the team understands what he means [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_September_Resource_Plan_and_FlexPort_Readiness-September-9-2026.md]].
- English: "**I'm not saying that this is how it will be**… I'm just giving you a scope" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_October_Rollout_Staging_and_Unit_Ramp-September-9-2026.md]].

So this is a **pace-setting instruction**, and it is recorded as one. It is not a sales forecast and no source treats it as a commitment.

## Why the pressure — Val's commercial case

Stated in full in the engineering call and recorded here because the ramp is its consequence [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_September_Resource_Plan_and_FlexPort_Readiness-September-9-2026.md]]:

- **JVL is the only company making both halves.** "[Е]динственная компания, которая выпускает машину игральную и транзак[шнл] сами. Притом транзак[шнл] мы выпускаем… и IQ и flex pay слим и флекс порты сами."
- **The window is short and can close without warning.** "[Т]олько что рынок открылся на транзакционный [эквипмент,] его нигде нету. Это уникальн[о], но это долго длиться не будет… [в] один день выключат[,] выключат свет… просто выключат[,] клик[,] всё остановится."
- **Vertical machines are scarce and the V43 earns.** "[В]ертикальных машин очень мало на рынке, то есть это уникальная позиция. Всем нужна вертикальная машина на лок[ей]шине" — and the machine is new, "по проли[ми]нер[и] тестом делаем деньги… потому что если машина не делала деньги, то мы бы не смогли запаковать это всё вместе."
- **Hence the mobilisation and the pledge.** "[М]не с сегодняшнего дня до декабря нужно мобилизировать людей по максимуму. Я за всё заплачу всем. Никто в обиде не останется… И в декабре мы сможем отдыхать[,] в конце декабря. Но сейчас мне нужно больше, чем максимум[ —] мне нужны вещи, которые мы никогда не могли сделать." *(The commitment to pay for the overtime is recorded as a stated commitment; **no rate, amount or named-person compensation appears in the source** — §15.8.)*

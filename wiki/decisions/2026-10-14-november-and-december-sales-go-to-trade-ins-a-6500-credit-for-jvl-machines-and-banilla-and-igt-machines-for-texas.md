# November and December go to trade-ins

Agreed by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] and [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda]] at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-14-sales-q4-trade-in-plan-and-transactional-momentum.md|Oct-14 5:33 PM sales call]]. Val: "**So we agreed on the marching orders. Focus for November, December, is the six thousand five hundred dollar trading.**" Felipe: "**Agreed. And the trading for Banilla and IGTs and different Texas**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sales_Q4_Trade_In_Plan_and_Transactional_Momentum-October-14-2026.md]].

## What was decided

- **Fill the two months with trade-ins.** Val: "**Fill November and December with trade and deals.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sales_Q4_Trade_In_Plan_and_Transactional_Momentum-October-14-2026.md]]
- **JVL machines first, at $6,500.** Val: "**First, I would focus on JVLs. Those that are at six thousand five hundred will buy at six thousand five hundred**". His reasons: "**We clean up our own market. There is no residual inventory. We get a chance to convert them or to refurbish them, and I can package the deal and send them to South Dakota or to Nebraska.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sales_Q4_Trade_In_Plan_and_Transactional_Momentum-October-14-2026.md]]
- **Two packages to offer.** Val: "**one package is this machine with transactional equipment. Another package is if you wanna take the [trade] in, we give you [$6,500] against the purchase of a new machine.**" The transcript says "six to five hundred dollars"; the notes read it as the $6,500 trade-in [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sales_Q4_Trade_In_Plan_and_Transactional_Momentum-October-14-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Sales_Q4_Trade_In_Plan_and_Transactional_Momentum-October-14-2026.md]].
- **Banilla and IGT machines go to Texas.** Val: "**I'm going to take your machine. I'm going to ship it to Texas, and I'm going to send my [kits] for conversion. So my [kits] are twenty five hundred. So your machine is roughly fifteen hundred dollars.**" ("kids" in the transcript; the notes read "conversion kit".) The Texas side is on [[wiki/projects/texas-used-machine-distribution.md]] (Oct 14, 5:33 PM) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sales_Q4_Trade_In_Plan_and_Transactional_Momentum-October-14-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Sales_Q4_Trade_In_Plan_and_Transactional_Momentum-October-14-2026.md]].
- **Parts from incoming machines are crushed.** Val: "**Take the validators and printers out and crush them.**" Then: "**monitors, whatever it is, just disassemble it into parts and crush. And we don't need any boards or any of that stuff.**" Felipe: "**There were only a few validators that we were able to use.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sales_Q4_Trade_In_Plan_and_Transactional_Momentum-October-14-2026.md]]

## Why Val wants this now

- **He expects no other deals before year end.** Val: "**I do not see you making any more deals with anybody. Those that will take machines now, initially, they'll wait. They will sit and wait.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sales_Q4_Trade_In_Plan_and_Transactional_Momentum-October-14-2026.md]]
- **The transactional install should pull buyers.** Val: "**when transactional equipment goes and people see that it's working, they'll line up for the deal.**" Felipe: once people know "**they could get it for free if they buy a game. We're gonna sell**", if they commit "**in writing**" before the end of the year. See [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-free-ports-and-pos-with-every-machine-bought.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sales_Q4_Trade_In_Plan_and_Transactional_Momentum-October-14-2026.md]].
- **The scrapping story.** Felipe mentioned "**videos of the claw destroying machines**". Val: "**It's just a front. I'm using it as a front, so people feel good about selling it to Texas.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sales_Q4_Trade_In_Plan_and_Transactional_Momentum-October-14-2026.md]]
- **A pool of old JVL machines exists.** Val: "**You said there's a large quantity of JVLs, right? They're trying to sell.**" Felipe: "**from that chat, it was just twenty-five.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sales_Q4_Trade_In_Plan_and_Transactional_Momentum-October-14-2026.md]]

## How it sits against earlier trade-ins

- **ARC (Oct 13).** The day before, Val agreed a trade-in with ARC for Texas, paid as credit — [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-13-arc-trades-in-old-machines-for-texas-as-credit-on-2027-purchases.md]]. On Oct 14 he described it to Felipe as about 200 IGT machines at about $1,500 (Oct-14 note on that page).
- **Narayani (Aug 26).** An earlier, smaller trade-in of three D27 machines — [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-26-narayani-three-machine-d27-trade-in-pilot.md]].
- *(Inference: the $6,500 JVL trade-in and the Texas trade-in are two separate offers — one for JVL's own machines, one for other makers' machines. The source treats them as two lines of the same plan.)*

## Oct 15 2026 — the other end of the trade-in flow: 20 refurbished machines offered to an operator

The next evening, at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-15-blue-sky-gold-rush-refurbished-machines-offer-and-transactional-demo.md|Blue Sky / Gold Rush meeting]], Val described what JVL does with machines it takes in: "From other operators, let's say somebody wants to upgrade to this machine, they bring us this machine… We take it in, we fully refurbish it here, put the new software in, and yes, we can sell it cheaper. But it's a very limited quantity. Right now we have 20 machines." They carry "a ninety-day warranty" and come without a topper [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]].

- **The bid.** The operator asked for all 20 at "Around the nine thousand mark" over "twelve months". Val: "I don't think that I can meet 9,000" — some units "Have broken monitors, have broken touchscreens… burnt out PC boards" — and he floated fourteen or sixteen monthly payments instead. JVL is to check the machines one by one and answer by Oct 19–20 (commitment held on [[wiki/organizations/blue-sky-gold-rush.md]]) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]].
- **The rules Val set:** refurbished machines do not get the show special, and show-special machines are not financed — [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-15-refurbished-trade-in-machines-are-sold-apart-from-the-show-special-and-show-special-machines-are-not-financed.md]].
- *Inference:* the Oct-14 plan's "refurbish them… package the deal and send them to South Dakota or to Nebraska" and this Georgia offer are two outlets for the same kind of stock. Whether the 20 machines here are JVL machines taken at $6,500 is not said.

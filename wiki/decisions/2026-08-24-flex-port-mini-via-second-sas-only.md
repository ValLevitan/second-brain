# Build Flex-Port-Mini via the second SAS port only, at a ~$45 build budget

At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-24-flex-port-mini-capabilities.md|Aug 24 2026 Flex-Port-Mini session]] (Russian), [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]], [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi Peari]] and [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]] designed a stripped-down port. Val opened by asking whether the cost drops if you connect only to SAS — "Я понимаю, что можно удешевиться, если подключать только к сассу, правильно?" — and closed by fixing the budgets [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Design-August-24-2026.md]]. Device-catalog substance is on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]]; the commercial model is [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-24-free-hardware-fixed-monthly-fee-model.md]].

## What was decided

Val's closing statement: "Я останавливаюсь на том, что мы делаем. Флекс спорт мини. С бюджетом $45. И флекс порт. С бюджетом $250. И прокладываем… Индивидуальные провода к каждой машине. Через с протоколом как 1 стадия." — I settle on: we build Flex-Port-Mini with a $45 budget, and Flex-Port with a $250 budget, and we run individual wires to each machine, on the [existing] protocol, as stage 1 [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Design-August-24-2026.md]].

- **Connect only through the machine's second SAS port.** This removes the bill-acceptor in-line wiring that Pavel and Genadi called the most problematic part of today's port — Pavel: "которая самая проблемная часть здесь." Cash still enters through the operator's existing bill acceptor as before ("Кэш поступает на машину как обычно"); the Mini only loads credits over SAS and reads vouchers [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Design-August-24-2026.md]].
- **Machines without a second SAS port are out of scope.** Val: "мы уходим от машин, которые не имеют… Секон сас… на сегодняший год это гдето 15 20% рынка… то гдето 10 15000 машин сегодня… всё равно они за дватри года умрут" — roughly 15–20% of the market, ~10–15,000 machines today, expected to die off within two to three years. Anyone needing a full conversion can still use the existing Flex-Port [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Design-August-24-2026.md]].
- **Build budget: ~$45 for the Mini.** Val pushed low first — "мы всё равно можем вложиться в $20, да?" and then "В $30 мы можем вложиться?", to which Genadi initially agreed ("Да в 30 да"); Genadi had himself floated "нам надо продукт сделать за $25" earlier in the call. Once he costed the scanner and the memory he reversed: "все равно мы вложимся в 40. Мы не вложимся в 30," landing on "40 плюс минус 5… я бы сказал что $40. 40 45, да." Val then put it directly — "я пока не могу понять это $30 ни мы не попадаем, а в 40 мы попадаем" — Genadi: "40 это уже более реально"; Val: "Ну 50 ещё более реальный." **$45 became the working budget** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Design-August-24-2026.md]].
- **Include a minimal, cheap, non-touch information display.** Val wanted no screen at all ("нам не нужен экран"); Genadi argued for "без тачскрина самый простой маленький экранчик" at "$5" so the unit can show simple messages, and Pavel identified the concrete need: on cash-out the player presses cash-out on the machine and "надо, чтоб ктото ему сказал, поднесите этикет" — something has to tell them to present the ticket. Genadi: "Вот это нужен экранчик для этого." Val accepted at that price. Pavel priced an OLED he had bought at about "доллар 50" and thought China would be under a dollar [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Design-August-24-2026.md]].
- **Stage-1 networking is wired CAT5 / RS-485 on the existing protocol; wireless is a later experiment.** Val: "Мне наоборот нравится через форе, потому что провода уже лежат на всех лакенах" — I prefer CAT5 because the wiring is already run at every location. Pavel: wires are the most reliable and "может быть нам пока пойти с проводами даже для запуска всего," with mesh networking as an experiment afterwards. Val's summary: local network needed, buildable by hardware or wireless, "у нас нету экспириенса нам нужно только хайдвай потом уже делать 2 шагом какойто вайлес" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Design-August-24-2026.md]].
- **Do not cooperate with or reverse-engineer Mutha Goose's RS-485; build JVL's own board and use SAS.** Pavel raised reverse-engineering as an alternative, then rejected it: "Нам лучше с ним не кооперироваться вообще," and on JVL's own board over SAS, "нам не надо мы мы мы свой сделаем лучше, нам не нужен их протокол." His technical objection to the Mutha-Goose route: the electromagnetic-counter cash-in/cash-out signals "очень ненадёжны по сравнению с тем, что сас нам даёт" — very unreliable compared with what SAS gives. Val agreed. Val also noted the vendor is unlikely to hand over the protocol; Pavel: "тогда у них никто не будет покупать мутогуз" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Design-August-24-2026.md]].
- **No partial loading and no partial cash-out.** Val: "Все деньги, которые мы вложили в 1 раз… У нас нету порций." Pavel drew the consequence: "если у нас нету частичного зачисления, то любые деньги выведены из машины можно отоваривать." The playable / redeemable money split happens only at an intelligent device (Flex IQ or Flex Recycler), never at the port — Val: "это только при поднесении на флекс i kl на какойто интеллигент девайс. Не на не на порт." The existing Flex-Port's "load 10, 20 or all" dialog is dropped: "это вообще фичер не нужна" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Design-August-24-2026.md]].

## Costs quoted in the session

- **Scanner engine: $11 today** (Genadi: "у нас сканер стоит сегодня… $11… Этот энжин"), and it needs its own interface board, which JVL makes itself; Genadi put the board at around "fiftin" [$15] and quoted the scanner-plus-interface stack at figures between $15 and $20 during the discussion. He described the scanner as good quality, not a cheap Chinese part [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Design-August-24-2026.md]].
- **Flash memory is the other cost driver.** Genadi said even 32 [MB] "будет стоить наверное, $10, если не больше." The team had already told [[wiki/people/evgeniy-alekseev.md|Alekseev]] to cut the flash by a factor of four in a future redesign. Pavel floated using a processor with internal flash only and dropping over-the-air software updates; Val rejected that: "это не хороший режим" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Design-August-24-2026.md]].
- **Today's full Flex-Port: Genadi put it "в районе 140 $150" including scanner and screen**; the board alone had cost **$45** before memory prices rose ("Этот сам борт стоил $45. Но после Того, как увеличилась цена памяти…"). Val had guessed ~$200. Val's saving math: at a $150 difference across 6 units he first said "тысячу долларов на локе" then corrected to "$900 на окей" — about $900 per location; at a $100 difference, "$600 на локэйшен" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Design-August-24-2026.md]].
- **Flex IQ bill of materials: ~$210** (Genadi: "Мы считали Белов, материал, по моему, был $210" — fully assembled, with enclosures) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Design-August-24-2026.md]].

## Rejected networking options

- **WiFi — rejected.** Val: "Вай фай не пройдёт." Genadi's objections: an antenna would have to be fitted (and possibly stuck on the outside) in every machine model, and "это уже не будет $30… ты это всё равно $10 будет в любом случае" — the module alone adds about $10. He thought fitting WiFi may be no easier than pulling CAT5, "может даже вай фай сложнее установить." Pavel's separate objection: WiFi still needs a central point that must see every machine directly, and a machine turned metal-case-forward probably will not see it [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Design-August-24-2026.md]].
- **LTE / cellular modem per port — too expensive.** Val floated it as "обалденно тупая идея"; Pavel: "модем всё равно не бесплатный… ну 15 минимум," Genadi: "$20" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Design-August-24-2026.md]].
- **Bluetooth mesh — cannot be estimated.** Pavel raised it for reliability (a neighbour node relays around a blocked machine), but "мы с этим никогда не работали" and, asked how confident he was, "Нет никаких идей, как это оценить даже." He noted having seen that certain Chinese chips use a protocol he recalled as **ESP-NOW** with about **500 m** of line-of-sight range [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Design-August-24-2026.md]].
- **NFC instead of QR — rejected.** Val: NFC would confine the scheme to phones, and physical NFC cards are "очень дорогие" at roughly a dollar each, which turns it into a player-tracking system; "это невозможно" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Design-August-24-2026.md]].
- **Protocol note:** on the daisy-chained RS-485 variant Pavel warned WSS will not run over it — "там WS не пойдёт, там будет уже какойнибудь протокол типа модбас" — and Modbus would need an encryption layer added, which he thought Claude could probably propose or that Modbus may already have an encrypted variant ("Я не не знаю"). If CAT5 is run to each machine individually, the existing WSS protocol can stay: "мы можем остаться с WSS протоколом, который у нас сегодня есть. И нам очень мало надо что делать." Existing WSS work was written by an Evgeny — Pavel: "для дабл ю s там какието наработки уже есть то что Евгении писали"; resolved by §4 context (FlexPort/FlexPay integration software) to [[wiki/people/eugine-yavorski.md|Eugine Yavorski]], though the transcript names no surname [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Design-August-24-2026.md]].

## Open questions

- Is the $40–45 build cost actually achievable? Memory sizing is unconfirmed — Genadi: "это надо оценить количество памяти" — [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Design-August-24-2026.md]]
- Bluetooth-mesh viability is unknown; nobody on the call has worked with it and it cannot be estimated — [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Design-August-24-2026.md]]
- What happens if an operator refuses to disconnect Mutha Goose? Genadi: then new wiring is needed anyway — "если я не отключаются, тогда все равно новые ставить" — [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Design-August-24-2026.md]]
- Genadi wants daisy-chain support so the operator only has to unplug from Mutha Goose and plug into JVL ("Из бута гус воткнул, изъяла борт, воткнул к нам"); Pavel countered that daisy-chaining needs two Ethernet ports or RS-485, and that any break or unpowered node on an RS-485 chain kills the whole network — unresolved — [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Design-August-24-2026.md]]

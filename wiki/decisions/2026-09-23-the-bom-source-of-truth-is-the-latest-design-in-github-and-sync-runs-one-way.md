# The BOM's source of truth is the latest design in GitHub, and the sync runs one way

Stated by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-23-texas-flex-slim-release-street-cabinet-and-bom-source-of-truth.md|Sept 23 2026 11:05 AM alignment call]] (Russian) with [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi Peari]]. **Status note:** active as Val's ruling, but **Genadi did not accept the BOM half** and the conversation was cut off; Val took it to a separate meeting [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Texas_Flex_Slim_Release_Street_Cabinet_and_BOM_Source_of_Truth-September-23-2026.md]]. **Sept 24 update:** Val read the rule out from his written note, and said the BOM itself is **built in BC, automatically**, from GitHub as the source data. How to do that is still open research; Genadi still said he could not see the gain — see [[#sept-24-2026--the-written-rule-read-out-and-the-bom-built-in-bc-from-github-data|Sept 24]] below. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Hardware_Repo_BOM_Source_of_Truth-September-24-2026.md]] **Sept 30 update:** the promised wider meeting was held with Nathan and Lev. Agreed: GitHub holds **one flat list** of every part (designed or supplier-spec'd) with its revisions — Genadi: "Great". Still contested: whether the **grouping into assemblies** is automated from that flat list (Val) or the BOM is simply built in MSBC with the item database and history in GitHub (Genadi: "I really don't see the advantage here"). Nathan will **experiment in phases, items first**, in the development MSBC instances, after checking how others do it. Val ended the meeting early and took Genadi aside — see [[#sept-30-2026--one-flat-list-agreed-the-grouping-still-contested-nathan-experiments-items-first|Sept 30]] below. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GitHub_to_MSBC_BOM_Flat_List_Bridge-September-30-2026.md]] **Sept 30, 12:04 PM:** that follow-on with Genadi — titled "How to get from Solid Works mechanical design tool to a flat list of parts" — began and was parked after two minutes, with nothing discussed ([[wiki/meetings/2026-09-30-solidworks-flat-parts-list-parked.md]]) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Solid_Works_Flat_Parts_List_Parked-September-30-2026.md]]. The Oct-1 meetings on the flat BOM continue this thread; the timeline is open.

## How it came up

Val asked where the **GitHub ↔ MSBC synchronization** project stands; he had had no time with [[wiki/people/nathan-carver.md|Nathan]] ("у нас не было времени общаться с нейтаном вообще") and did not know what was needed. Genadi said he had written Nathan his vision: that the sync lets Val have an AI chief-operations assistant over everything. Val corrected the premise: "могу своего AICO направить на gedхаб также удачно даже может быть и легче" — he can point his AI assistant at GitHub just as well, maybe more easily, and does not need the sync with Microsoft BC for that [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Texas_Flex_Slim_Release_Street_Cabinet_and_BOM_Source_of_Truth-September-23-2026.md]].

## The two positions

- **Genadi — build the BOM in Business Central.** "Сегодня все цены находятся в м из би си." It is convenient in the ERP because "если изменяется ревизия, оно сразу уходит во все rileated itames"; all orders go out of MSBC; a requisition is easier there. He could not picture BOM trees in GitHub: "если у нас есть. Флекс у него есть там 10 модулей разных… я могу это Excel файлы держать разные XL файлы, которые потом merch. Как это сделать?" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Texas_Flex_Slim_Release_Street_Cabinet_and_BOM_Source_of_Truth-September-23-2026.md]]
- **Val — drawing first, then part number, then MSBC.** "нет чертежа нету пагнала" — no drawing, no part number. You design in SolidWorks; the finished parts go into GitHub, which keeps versions and history; then the part-number list is carried into MSBC, a supplier is attached, quotations come in, a new supplier gets a vendor card, and only then does a purchase order go out. "версионность деталей конструкции деталей лучше всего удобней держать в кидхабе, потому что там поддерживается версионный случай всего." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Texas_Flex_Slim_Release_Street_Cabinet_and_BOM_Source_of_Truth-September-23-2026.md]]

## The ruling

- **One source of truth; two-way sync rejected.** Genadi asked whether the sync works both ways. Val: "Обе стороны это обе стороны обе стороны плохо, когда работает. Должен быть wan sorrs of chers" — there must be one source of truth — "Иначе у вас будет ту сорси". Genadi: "Хорошо." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Texas_Flex_Slim_Release_Street_Cabinet_and_BOM_Source_of_Truth-September-23-2026.md]]
- **The BOM's source of truth is GitHub.** Genadi asked it straight — "Mсбиси или гетхап." Val: "Китхаб конечно, потому что это последняя версия дизайна." The BOM in MSBC is "уже финальный результат", derived from the design versions. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Texas_Flex_Slim_Release_Street_Cabinet_and_BOM_Source_of_Truth-September-23-2026.md]]
- **The reason Val gave is about culture:** "вы не думаете в м с биc у вас нету мысли в MSBC у вас перед вами дизайн и вы работаете с дизайном и я бы не хотел бы это менять, потому что это правильная культура." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Texas_Flex_Slim_Release_Street_Cabinet_and_BOM_Source_of_Truth-September-23-2026.md]]

## How it sits against Aug 12

On Aug 12 the same two people agreed "GitHub is the source of design truth", with a one-way sync robot from GitHub into BC that rewrites the assembly BOM when a revision is tagged — see [[wiki/topics/business-central-erp-implementation.md]] (Aug 12) and [[wiki/topics/github-release-management-pipeline.md]] (Aug 12). Sept 23 restates that direction. What is new is that Genadi now says he builds the BOM in BC and questions keeping BOM trees in GitHub. This is recorded as an **evolution with a live disagreement**, not as a contradiction: the two sources describe the same plan at different dates, and the Sept-23 source shows it is not yet agreed in practice. It also narrows the **June-25** "Business Central is the single source of truth" rule for design data, as Aug 12 already did (inference). ## Sept 30 2026 — one flat list agreed; the grouping still contested; Nathan experiments, items first

The wider meeting Val had promised on Sept 23 — [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-30-github-to-msbc-bom-flat-list-bridge.md|Sept 30 2026, 10:00 AM, "GitHub_to_MSBC_BOM_Integration"]] (English) — with Genadi, [[wiki/people/nathan-carver.md|Nathan Carver]] and [[wiki/people/lev-rudky.md|Lev Rudky]]. All quotations [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GitHub_to_MSBC_BOM_Flat_List_Bridge-September-30-2026.md]]. The two rules that *were* settled on this call have their own pages: [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-30-engineers-never-change-msbc-and-administration-never-changes-github.md]] and [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-30-a-new-part-number-is-assigned-by-claude-from-the-standard-in-github-and-pushed-to-msbc-once.md]].

- **Val's objective, as he set it.** Genadi keeps updating drawings and supplier specs; a change to anything "that is part of a bill of material" becomes a new revision in GitHub ("Github is very good at creating revisions"); "Costs, bill of materials are updated. In Msbc. This is where we want to arrive."
- **Genadi's line, unchanged.** "I fully support first part of your description when we keep all the engineering information in Github, including… Parts, specifications, drawings, designs of the printed circuit boards, mechanical designs… No questions… About the second part of the bill of material, I cannot comprehend how it's possible, in what format we can keep all the bill of materials in Github." He pointed at the structures JVL has "in the vision and the Msbc"; Val: "Navision was designed 50 years ago, got obsolete 30 years ago, and was taken offline 20 years ago… let's not say, how do I take my Excel and do something else?"
- **Flat, not structured — Val's capability wording.** "We're capable of assembling a raw bill of material or raw list of materials." Genadi: "Structurized." Val: "I'm not sure that it needs to be structurized. We just need a list of some sort… I think it has to be a flood [flat]." Genadi: "if it's flat, how you will be able to transfer it to Msbc?" Val: "That's a different capability. We have to then be able to transfer. A flat list to Msbc."
- **Nathan's framing: a build script.** "whatever structure is needed to maintain. That within Github and then have a build that translates that into a data structure that can be pushed to Msbc. So there may be a middle step that is automated."
- **What Genadi actually does, in Val's account.** "Genadi says there is 550 nominations that will go into assembling one machine. Some of it will be two each, some will Require 50 each. But a flat list is 550 nominations." Step two is the quantities; step three is "the process and priority by which all of the flat list comes together." Genadi: "Not only, no… It's very important what you're missing" — in production, MSBC needs sub-assemblies ("they might be assembling, for example, monitor assembly. It has to be this assembly in the Msbc").
- **Where they agreed and where they did not.** Val: "in Github, it is all flood information… It gets grouped, compiled in inside of Msbc. But that grouping, the step, could be a step of automation. It doesn't have to be a manual step." Genadi: "If you keep all the database of the flat list in Github. Perfect. No argue. But why you need to keep in Github? You take two of these and three of these. You can keep it in Msbc. You keep all the revision, all the history in Github." Val: "I am saying the same thing, except… that it does not have to be a manual process." Later Genadi, to Nathan: "what rule you will apply for converting… If each bill of material is different, how you can create the rule? Each product is different… You need two structures to be similar, right? Then it's easy to synchronize." Nathan: "Correct." Genadi: "I really don't see the advantage here, but… Maybe I don't understand."
- **The flat list itself — agreed.** Val: "Github has a list, one flat list of everything either designed by you that comes out from SolidWorks or comes out from a supplier which you spec, which you approved, which a supplier must. Provide the drawing for… if there are revision, then it gets substituted into revision… a flat list is kept in Github." Genadi: "Yes, yes, includes in the revision. Yes" — "Great." On the ten-locks example he had raised: "We have 10 part numbers in Github for different logs. This is, I think, this is doable. I mean, this is basically what we have today. It just will be kept in the Github."
- **Phases.** Genadi: "I would go with… Few iterations. We'll start with the database to keep in Github. And then, next step would be to deal with all the bill of materials." Nathan: "So start with just maintaining items and updating those first before going into bill of materials… I can experiment in phases and figure out what we can and can't do." Genadi: "Maybe we should check our best practices before we start the deep investigation… we don't want to be first in this investigation." Nathan: "Yeah, I agree with that." His method: "take an existing BOM and Msbc. trace it back to all of its items and come up with a way, okay, what would this BOM look like in Github?… how do we make it maintainable? And not just add complexity." He needs "examples or actual actual BOM structures that you're using now. What does flat versions look like" and will use "development Msbc instances".
- **How Genadi gets from SolidWorks to a BOM today.** "Design tool, it doesn't give me the bill of material… I do not have in the SolidWorks source of all fasteners, all the tapes, locks, wires. I have strictly mechanical make parts" — off-the-shelf parts are kept out because "it's very heavy… I need a powerful computer". "Whatever I have in SolidWorks, when I do a new part, I already assigned the number… Then, when I enter it after the project is done, manually enter this number. In Msbc. If it's not part of the design… like, I don't know, washer. And I know we need 6 washers. Just put in the Msbc, I add the instance. This part number multiply 6. It's not during the design, it's during the building bill of material."
- **How it ended.** Val: "I think this meeting is not productive. I need to let Lev and Nathan go because I'm just burning their hours. And I will switch to a different meeting with Genadi." The derived notes record the BOM question as "Considered and still open… Not resolved" [[raw/Meeting_Notes_GitHub_to_MSBC_BOM_Flat_List_Bridge-September-30-2026.md]].

# Diamond's field auditor will capture JVL settings data

The first time in the corpus an operator agrees to **collect field data for JVL, by hand, for nothing** — no fee, no contract, no data-purchase agreement. Agreed at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-10-diamond-amusements-machine-performance-and-bundle-pilot.md|Sept-10 2026 Diamond visit]], and carried in the derived notes as a standing undertaking — "**Diamond field auditor — capture settings / QR data from JVL machines during compliance rounds — ongoing.**" [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

## Why Val asked

He had spent an hour failing to explain [[wiki/organizations/diamond-amusement.md|Diamond]]'s per-location rankings ([[wiki/topics/slot-game-design-principles.md]], Sept 10), and rejected the reports Diamond was reading them off: "**the financial data that we pull off the [Intralot]… is not reliable… [Intralot] generates erroneous reports.**" What he wants instead: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

> "what helps me is the **popularity report, setting reports**. This what I need… for my machines, it's either **QR code on the older machines or the modems on the newer machines**."

And the reason he thinks Diamond should want it too: "**you and I [are] in the same boat. If you want good performance of machines, you need to understand how all machines are set, and you need to understand which games are popular and which are not. Not machines, but games.**" He acknowledged the limit — it only covers JVL's own cabinets: "It helps me with my machines. At least I get reliable data." Sean: "**within your own, but you're not seeing comparison within the location.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

## What was agreed

**Diamond's field auditor picks it up as part of rounds he already makes.** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

> "we've got a **field auditor** guy that works in our staff. **All he does is compliance checks**, right? But he's also like **physically working every machine**, so he's not there for technical service. So he has a different priority, right? So for him to **take a minute, pull up this menu screen or whatever, take a screenshot**… that's really important."

Val had tried to give Diamond an exit and was waved off — "I'm afraid to burden your people with scanning QR codes… **physical interaction with the machine**" — Diamond: "**We're in the locations anyway, so [it's] not particularly that big of a deal.**" Val's answer is the sentence the arrangement exists for: "**Would be a great help for us. I mean, we're operating blind, so that would be a fantastic help.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

**Scope: the whole JVL fleet, not just the new cabinets.** Asked whether he wanted it off the older games or only the new set — "**I'm interested in all of the games.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

**It extends something already running.** Diamond already sends a QR capture **every two weeks** for the two new machines — "That's right. That's what we're doing now with the two new ones", and a technician: "[K]oloski sends over a QR code to him every two weeks." *(The name is garbled and is not identified; recorded as spoken.)* The complaint attached to it: "**nobody else does that, so we have no other way to pull the other data.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

## Why this is a different animal from the data buy-back

JVL's standing programme pays an operator **$337 per machine per year on a three-year contract** for modem data ([[wiki/topics/machine-data-repurchase-program.md]]). **None of that appears here** — no fee, no term, no contract, no anonymisation clause, no confidentiality language. This is manual capture, by the customer's own staff, as a favour, off a QR code. It continues the run of customer meetings since Aug 26 in which the reporting is pitched and **the repurchase programme is not offered** (see that page) — and it is the first in which Val asks the operator to supply the data instead of offering to buy it. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

It is also the second time in two days that Val asks an operator for settings rather than revenue. On Sept 9 he told [[wiki/people/narasimha-rao-namburi.md|Rao]] "**Don't send me the report. Send me the settings** of the competitor machines" — and got a promise. Here he asks for JVL's own settings, and gets a standing process. **The difference matters: Rao's offer covered competitors' machines, Diamond's covers only JVL's.** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]]

## Open questions

- **Does this ever reach the competitors' machines?** Val's own statement of what he needs is "**you need to understand how ALL machines are set**", and he conceded in the same breath that he cannot get it: "I need exactly what you need, but **you cannot get it. None of the manufacturers will give you.**" The agreement covers JVL cabinets only, and Sean named the gap — no in-location comparison. **Nothing in the source bridges it.** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

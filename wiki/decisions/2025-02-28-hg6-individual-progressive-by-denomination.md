# Individual progressive per denomination on HG-6 CPU kits

Reported on the Feb 28 2025 game-design review: [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel]]'s engineering group confirmed it is **easier to add a progressive level for HG-6 CPU kits by denomination**, so JVL is no longer limited to running the progressive only on the **maximum** denomination. [[raw/Game design part 1 - Feb-28-2025.md]]

## What changed

- The earlier plan added a banner and grayed out the progressive on non-max denominations; that is **no longer required**. [[raw/Game design part 1 - Feb-28-2025.md]]
- The engineering commitment to have this done in **two to three weeks** includes an **individual progressive setup for each denomination** — which is what JVL wanted and also what Columbia (Colombia) needs, so it serves more than one jurisdiction. [[raw/Game design part 1 - Feb-28-2025.md]]
- Not a proportional/fractional allocation and **not** the "jackpot rush" model — these are **individual progressives by denomination**. Smaller denominations get proportionately smaller progressives (dependent on setup, since it is a mystery progressive), and the **higher progressive always sits on the highest enabled denomination** to incentivize players to play max denom. [[raw/Game design part 1 - Feb-28-2025.md]]

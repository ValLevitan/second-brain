# Nothing on Class A until the January legislation is heard

> **Read with the Sept-11 entry below.** One day after this was said, Val put Class A product development on NOVA into the schedule. Whether that contradicts this position or sits outside it is **not resolved** — see `## Open contradictions`. Do not read this title alone as current state.

Stated by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] in the room at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-10-georgia-lottery-coam-manufacturer-introduction.md|Sept 10 2026 Georgia Lottery meeting]], twice, and left as his closing position. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]]

## What he said

- **The timing.** "**I appreciate the fact that I would have to watch out for profitability and possibly the legislation will change in January. I doubt very much that I can do anything until January. So it will probably hear the legislation before even doing anything.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]]
- **The sequence, spelled out.** "**But my intention is to bring it here before the legislation, have this conversation prior to legislation, then probably hear the legislation, understand where you stand on it, and then make a decision.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]]
- **The reason he is being careful at all** is not the law but the relationship: "**this relationship is important to me because this is what's keeping me in the business**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]].
- He also put the boundary of his own interest on the record: "**I don't want to get into legislation[;] that's above my pay grade**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]].

The Lottery's own answer — that building Class A is lawful today and does not damage the relationship — is on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-10-the-lottery-tells-jvl-class-a-manufacturing-is-lawful-today.md]]. So this is a wait he chose, not a wait he was told to take.

## How this sits against the ARC commitment

Ten days earlier, at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-31-arc-gaming-transactional-demo-class-a.md|Aug-31 ARC Gaming demo]], Val told [[wiki/people/georgios-zazanis.md|George Zazanis]] "**George, I will have a deal within three months. Three months. Give me three months. January. We're gonna do business**" — see [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-31-class-a-deal-with-arc-within-three-months.md]].

Both statements point at January, but they point at different things. On Aug 31 January was the **deadline for a deal**; on Sept 10 January is the **earliest point at which he will decide anything**, and only after hearing the bill. Nothing in this source mentions ARC, Zazanis or the three-month promise, so the two are **not reconciled here** — recorded as an evolution of Val's stated posture, with the tension left visible.

## The staff's counter-consideration, which he did not answer

A staff member offered the market-level argument against early entry, and Val's reply was to change the subject: "**The more manufacturers that get into this market between now and the end of April, where they end up positioning this, the more attention it creates for the legislature. The more urgency they're going to feel to act to make a change. It could be really great if it stays the way it is for the next year.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]]

Full record: [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-11-georgia-class-a-v43-is-built-on-nova-not-rage.md]]. **Two readings, and this wiki picks neither:**

- **Compatible.** "Anything" on Sept 10 means entering the market and committing commercially; Sept 11 is engineering work, and the February-1 target sits *after* the January session, so the decision point he reserved is untouched.
- **Incompatible.** He said he doubts he can do anything until January, and the next day a product he had not authorised anyone to build is scheduled, staffed and started — before January.

**Neither source mentions the other**, neither mentions the regulator on the other's side, and nothing in the corpus reconciles them. Per §11 this is recorded, not resolved. **Note also** that the Sept-11 meeting never mentions the Lottery, the missing rulebook, certification or the legislature at all — see [[wiki/topics/georgia-class-a-machines.md]] (Sept 11). **Queued for the owner.**

# Competitor-machine access at Diamond goes ahead through the head tech, without waiting for Sean

**Decided:** September 14 2026, around 12:51 PM Eastern, on a ~4-minute call. **By:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], answering a JVL colleague who is **never named in the recording** (see [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-14-competitor-field-reconnaissance-and-class-a-visits.md|the meeting stub]] for what is and is not known about him). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Competitor_Field_Reconnaissance_and_Class_A_Visits-September-14-2026.md]]

## What was decided

JVL's field visits to [[wiki/organizations/diamond-amusement.md|Diamond Amusement]] locations go ahead the next morning, arranged by Diamond's head tech, **without** first getting the owner's sign-off.

The colleague put the choice to Val plainly: "I was just wondering if you wanted me to get **Sean's okay** or otherwise **not do it**. I just wanted to, but she said **you should coordinate with me**. So why don't we go at it right now?" Val's answer was one line — "**This is absolutely perfect.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Competitor_Field_Reconnaissance_and_Class_A_Visits-September-14-2026.md]]

- The ask put to Tara was **"at least three locations, one tier two, something you recommend visiting"** — the tier-two site being her own recommendation. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Competitor_Field_Reconnaissance_and_Class_A_Visits-September-14-2026.md]]

## The real object of the visits

Not JVL's own cabinets — the rival manufacturers' machines sitting beside them:

> "**Very important. We need access to the machines, the competitors' machines. Take a few screenshots, get some information.** She said, '**that's fine.**'" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Competitor_Field_Reconnaissance_and_Class_A_Visits-September-14-2026.md]]

*(Inference: the competitors meant are the manufacturers Diamond runs alongside JVL — IGT, Aristocrat, Light & Wonder, Novomatic on the Feb-2026 benchmark — but the Sept-14 source names no manufacturer and none is asserted.)* [[raw/Diamond_Shawn_Fellows_interview-February-9-2026.md]]

**Why this matters, and why now.** It is the direct field follow-through on the question [[wiki/organizations/diamond-amusement.md|Diamond]] left open on Sept 10: *why does JVL rank behind Aristocrat and Light & Wonder in Diamond's own locations?* Val's answer there was a **settings** hypothesis — default bet, RTP, displayed progressive — which can only be tested by reading the competitors' own menu screens. The same week [[wiki/people/narasimha-rao-namburi.md|Rao]] had been asked for exactly the same artefact from his side: "the **settings** of the competitor machines and **photographs of their bet buttons**." Screenshots off a competitor's interface are the evidence that hypothesis needs. *(The causal link is an inference; the Sept-14 source states the purpose but not the reasoning.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]]

## The second, separate objective — Class A

Val added an errand that is not about Diamond at all: "I need him to see the **Class A machines** as a separate matter or in addition." The location is one JVL already knows — "**that's the Mexican restaurant**." "Him" is [[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael Gottlieb]], in Georgia that week. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Competitor_Field_Reconnaissance_and_Class_A_Visits-September-14-2026.md]]

Val moved it forward a day, correcting himself mid-sentence:

> "I recommend for you to go there on Wednesday, because by any chance **those machines are not there anymore**, we would have to find a new location for Wednesday. **Sorry, I meant to say you should go there on Tuesday, tomorrow. Tuesday**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Competitor_Field_Reconnaissance_and_Class_A_Visits-September-14-2026.md]]

The reasoning is the risk, not the convenience: go Tuesday so that if the machines have gone, Wednesday is still free to find a replacement. See [[wiki/topics/georgia-class-a-machines.md]].

## The backup, ordered regardless

Val told him to line up [[wiki/people/narasimha-rao-namburi.md|Rao]] in parallel even if Diamond confirms: "I think you should **reach out to Rao just in case. Put something in place for Wednesday. Because the more he sees, even if the machines are not open, the more he understands.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Competitor_Field_Reconnaissance_and_Class_A_Visits-September-14-2026.md]]

He can drive himself** unless you guys want to drive together. **It doesn't matter to me**" — with Michael due at the office that morning anyway.

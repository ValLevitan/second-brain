## 2. The terms the customer sees — "due upon use of service"

- **The question.** Klimovich: the invoice "from JVL to a location… has net 10 days"; processing "may take more than one day… 5, seven days. So can we agree on the term, like keep it 10? Or make it 5, 7" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Payment_Hub_Invoice_Terms_Template_and_First_Install-November-24-2026.md]].
- **Val's answer.** "The term should say due on due on use of service… No terms due upon use of service. Period. No 10 days, no one day, no three hours, no half an hour." "the fact that we may factually grab the money a bit later, that's our issue. But it's not a subject of their obligations. They are obligated to pay upon use of service… Nothing to discuss" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Payment_Hub_Invoice_Terms_Template_and_First_Install-November-24-2026.md]].

## 3. Interest — an internal setting, day 7

- **Two different terms (Nathan).** "the customer is expected to have the money there, day of service. MSPC [MSBC] uses the terms to know when to apply interest. And so we can have a different term in MSPC for that purpose. And then on the receipt we send them, we don't use the internal value" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Payment_Hub_Invoice_Terms_Template_and_First_Install-November-24-2026.md]].
- **Day 7.** Val: "The interest applies, I don't remember, either five or seven days… I think we decided, we decided on seven days, if I recall correctly. I'm going by my memory." Nathan: "we can update MSPC to be 7. Right now it's 10, we can update it to 7." Val: "payment due upon the use of service, so they have to pay right away. And interest accumulates starting seven days and on." Later: "Terms is 7 days… interest rates start being charged seven days after, but the client never sees that it's a seven-day term… These are internal settings in accounting" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Payment_Hub_Invoice_Terms_Template_and_First_Install-November-24-2026.md]].
- **Remove the terms field.** Klimovich: "we can just remove terms field from this type of invoice, and they will not see this seven days over there. It's clean and easy." Val: "With me, it would be fine" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Payment_Hub_Invoice_Terms_Template_and_First_Install-November-24-2026.md]].
- **Can BC do it?** Lev: "I will have to look into it because the invoice is posted and then… Interest rates start accruing after seven to 10 days… Or is it a separate charge… the original issued invoice to modify it, it's I don't think it's possible." Val: "at this stage, we don't have an answer… I'm almost 90% sure that it's possible", because interest is usually charged once an account is "past the terms" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Payment_Hub_Invoice_Terms_Template_and_First_Install-November-24-2026.md]].
- **The footer and the rate.** Klimovich, showing "the one from yesterday's test": "Now it shows terms. So as we've agreed, we can remove this field"; the footer "needs to contain a proper… Numbers… those numbers that Val approved previously, like 45% annual interest. Split by dates" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Payment_Hub_Invoice_Terms_Template_and_First_Install-November-24-2026.md]]. The Nov-20 call recorded 35% a year — see Open contradictions.
- **A bounced payment.** Lev asked what happens if the withdrawal finds no funds. Nathan: "the agreed upon workflow initially is to have the customer… pay by credit card or pay some other way with JVL. And then the future is the system has capabilities to re-initiate or whatever we agree upon" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Payment_Hub_Invoice_Terms_Template_and_First_Install-November-24-2026.md]].

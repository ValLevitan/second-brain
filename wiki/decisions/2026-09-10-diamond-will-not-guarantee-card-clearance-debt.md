# Diamond will not guarantee card-clearance debt

At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-10-diamond-amusements-machine-performance-and-bundle-pilot.md|Sept-10 2026 warehouse visit]] [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] put the non-payment problem to [[wiki/people/sean-fellows.md|Sean Fellows]] the way he had put it to [[wiki/people/salim-khambawala.md|Salim Khambawala]] on Aug 31 and Sept 8. **This is the second time in the corpus a Georgia master licensee is asked to back the broker's exposure, and the second refusal** — with one difference: Sean also gave Val something. The derived notes record the outcome the same way: "**Diamond will not act as reseller or guarantor for card-clearance debt.** The relationship stays arm's length; JVL's exposure sits between JVL, the processor and the location. Shawn will take a call before anything is shut off." [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

## The question, as Val framed it

> "we act as a broker. For card clearance, so we front our money. So we we clear transactions. **So we front our money to the clearing house.** Okay. **If the location doesn't pay us, we're out of money. So we swipe location through an ACH** automatically, but the location could say, '**I don't have money.**' In the meantime, we cleared the card, so **we're out of money. What do you suggest we do?**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

## What was decided, on Diamond's side

**1. Call first.** Val: "before we shut off the system, what do you want me to do? **Call you?**" — Sean: "**Absolutely, call me, because we have a different relationship with them**… **I've done this multiple occasions.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

**2. But no money, and no reseller role.** Asked "You'll give me the money?" — "**No, because I'm not going to be a reseller**"; and again, "that's why **I don't want to be a reseller**." Val did not argue, because he holds the same view of resale: "**I don't make money on resale**… **It's stupid… No interest.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

**3. The liability sits between JVL, the bank/processor and the location — not with Diamond.** "ultimately, that's going to be… funding the merchant account, right? So **your contract and the financial liabilities between the bank or processor and the location. No, we're not.**" Val's counter — "Okay, **but I am part of it with your location**" — got no concession. Where it ends if nothing works: "**At that point, you're forced into a litigation claim.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

**4. Diamond wants to be told about a bad location, and says it already watches for one.** "we want to be **a good partner and steward with you. It's your money**, right? And **if we feel like it's a problematic location or we see something that's alarming, we're going to let you know as well.**" His account of how they already do it: "when we start seeing **odd anomalies in the trends and cycles, we reach out to them**… because that's our lifeline… I explain to them all the time: **you're a store owner. The games are part of your income. They're all of my income.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

## The reason — the same one Diamond used to refuse InComm years ago

> "**I'm sure you see a business model that makes sense for us. It made no sense. We looked at this years ago when we first rolled out the pilots, and even with [InComm], we didn't want to get in the middle of it** because the bigger problem we have, **the relationship we have with the location, is so fragile that we didn't want some outside financial relationship to ultimately collapse this relationship. I don't want to lose ten thousand dollars.**" — Val: "Or or a hundred," — "**Even a thousand. It's not good business.**" And the principle: "**it's better for us to have an arms-length relationship.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

*(Rendered "Incon" in the transcript; read as [[wiki/organizations/incom.md|InComm]] from context — an inference. This is the first source in the corpus in which an operator says he **already declined** the same arrangement from InComm, and it dates that refusal only as "years ago… when we first rolled out the pilots".)*

## The shut-off lever: a third independent voice says the Lottery will object

The mechanics Val described are **word for word** the refinement he and [[wiki/organizations/incom.md|InComm]] agreed on Sept 2 — the machine stays on, only the money stops — and Sean reached the same objection from his own side without having heard it. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

> Sean: "**here's the problem: lotteries, lottery. We can't shut machines down.**" — Val: "**you're not shutting machines down. The machines are up and running.**" — Sean: "**They just can't take the money.**" — Val: "Right. **And cannot process the payment**… **And cannot award a winner.**"

He conceded the mechanism and kept the objection: "**I mean, we could. You could shut them off. I still think lottery['s] probably going to take [issue]**." *(The clause is rendered "going to take seven" and breaks off; the word is not recoverable and none is asserted.)* Val's answer was the one he has given all month: "**To be honest with you, they could do whatever they want. I will shut the game off.**" Sean did not dispute the liability — "**No, I get it. Because you've got a lot of… you've got liability. You've already pre-funded.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

**That is now three independent sources putting the regulatory cost of the shut-off on the customer JVL needs to sell it to** — InComm (Sept 2), Salim Khambawala (Sept 8), Sean Fellows (Sept 10) — none of whom is recorded hearing the others. See [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-31-transactional-equipment-only-with-location-exclusivity.md]]. ## The exposure, sized by the operator — the first weekend-risk arithmetic in the corpus

Sean volunteered where the real risk sits, and Val agreed on the spot ("**You're dead on**", "**That's the most active play**"): [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

- **The window is the weekend, because settlement is slow.** "**where your risk comes in is on long weekends or weekends in general because processing runs over seventy-two hours**… **So that's your highest risk exposure.**" On the bank cycle: "depending on how the bank processes, **most of them are three days**, right? Because it's Friday… Friday, two o'clock, and then it hits again Monday morning / Monday afternoon."
- **The worked example.** "**Thirty thousand dollars a week in redemption. Fifteen, fifteen thousand that goes back in replay within the system. That's fifteen thousand dollars of exposure risk.**" Val: "**Per day. Three days. Forty-five thousand.**"
- **Against a margin of ten dollars.** Val, immediately: "**let's say you issue ten cards a day. So I made ten dollars per day.**" That is the Aug-31 size-of-claim argument restated by its author to a second operator — a dollar a card against thousands of dollars of exposure. See [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]].

## Open questions

- **What actually happens when a location fails to pay?** Sean will take a call, refuses any financial backstop, says Diamond cannot shut machines down under lottery rules, and expects the Lottery to object if JVL does it. Val says he will disable the payment function anyway. **Untested — no location has yet been put on the arrangement.** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

**§15.8:** clear — broker exposure, settlement timing and per-card margin only; no named-person compensation.

# Stay on the committed transactional pipeline (May 18 2026)

Ruled by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-05-18-ga-trip-recap-kiosk-intel.md|May-18 GA trip recap]], after [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] asked whether a standalone kiosk is needed at all (a phone e-wallet tapped on an RFID reader at the machine could do the job). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GA_Trip_Recap_Kiosk_Intel-May-18-2026.md]]

## The ruling

- **Focus on the products in the pipeline.** "I would like to focus on the products that we're bringing into the market right now… that we are committed to and are in the pipeline." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GA_Trip_Recap_Kiosk_Intel-May-18-2026.md]]
- **Why.** "we cannot switch on a dime… without further investigation, without further trial of some sort, Or speaking to operators" — and "commit to another development is extremely expensive. And it will not expedite penetration of the current product." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GA_Trip_Recap_Kiosk_Intel-May-18-2026.md]]
- **The test for reopening it.** A new product is only a timely topic if someone says the current product "is not applicable… There's no demand for it. We made a mistake by making this product." Andrei declined to say that after a three-day trip and reframed his points as **risks** against the current product. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GA_Trip_Recap_Kiosk_Intel-May-18-2026.md]]
- **The products named.** Andrei listed the FlexPay kiosk, the POS and FlexPort; Val added the **Recycler** "because it's a committed product that's being designed now." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GA_Trip_Recap_Kiosk_Intel-May-18-2026.md]]

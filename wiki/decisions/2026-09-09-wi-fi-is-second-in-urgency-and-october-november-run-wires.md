# Wi-Fi is second in urgency — October and November run wires

Two halves of one position, stated by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] in both Sept 9 2026 meetings: **what gets sacrificed** (the engineering call) and **what is done instead** (the rollout meeting).

## Wi-Fi is the thing to give up

[[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]] asked directly — "[В]ал[,] планы по вайфай[:] на флекспорт и на флекспей переход на вайфай[.] [К]акие тут сроки, когда мы это хотим сделать?" — and Val answered with a word he flagged as deliberate: "[э]то может быть во 2… во 2 срочность… [с]пециально использовал слово **срочность**. Я понимаю, что это не… правильный язык, но я специально это использовал" — second in *urgency*, not second in priority [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_September_Resource_Plan_and_FlexPort_Readiness-September-9-2026.md]]. The derived notes summarise the ruling as "WiFi migration for FlexPort and FlexPay is second in urgency — it is the piece to sacrifice for other functionality" [[raw/Meeting_Notes_September_Resource_Plan_and_FlexPort_Readiness-September-9-2026.md]].

Pavel drew the operational conclusion and Val confirmed it twice: "это то, чем можно сейчас пожертвовать [в] пользу другой функциональности" — "Да. Да" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_September_Resource_Plan_and_FlexPort_Readiness-September-9-2026.md]].

**Val's reason is a claim about the installation rate:** "я рассчитываю, что… аксел[е]ре[й]ш[н] будет… [т]ам первые 10 инст[ал]ейшен… они займут столько же, сколько последующие 100. Столько же времени" — he expects the first ten installations to take as long as the following hundred, so wiring cost is front-loaded and temporary [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_September_Resource_Plan_and_FlexPort_Readiness-September-9-2026.md]].

This sets the sequencing for the Wi-Fi work assigned on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-07-andrei-bosik-drives-the-wi-fi-work-and-the-process-chain.md]] and specified on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-07-wi-fi-module-on-flex-port-with-an-external-router.md]] — the work continues, but it is not what October depends on.

## What is done instead: wires

- **Everything is SAS, on wires, in parallel.** "[W]e need to cease talking about non-SAS connection. **Everything is SAS connection.** I'm going there. In October and maybe November, we will only be installing wires. Wires will run in parallel… that means **one wire per device**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_October_Rollout_Staging_and_Unit_Ramp-September-9-2026.md]]. The SAS-only direction itself was ruled on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-07-flex-port-goes-sas-and-jvl-drops-the-old-machine-segment.md]].
- **All ports connect only to "the second fastboard".** "[L]et's make another assumption that all of our ports will only be connected to the second fastboard" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_October_Rollout_Staging_and_Unit_Ramp-September-9-2026.md]]. *(Recorded verbatim. In the Russian call the same morning Val put it as "флег[с]пор[т] должен подключаться к сас 2" — Flex-Port connects to SAS 2 [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_September_Resource_Plan_and_FlexPort_Readiness-September-9-2026.md]] — and the second-SAS-port convention is already on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]]. Whether "second fastboard" is the same thing is not stated in either source; not resolved here.)*
- **Do not raise Wi-Fi with customers.** "[W]e only need them for the first quarter, just to get that thing going. Because after that, we will have everything in Wi-Fi. **Don't talk about Wi-Fi to customers.**" A colleague added "Not yet" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_October_Rollout_Staging_and_Unit_Ramp-September-9-2026.md]].

## Who lays the wire

Val's hiring instruction was specific and exclusionary: "you want to get **electricians**, not technicians, not programmers, not from university, not from a college — trade, electrical trade. You want to get a **junior electrical tradesman with an electrical certificate**." His reason: "[t]hey are the best to lay wires because all you need is to lay wires. **Laying wires is the longest thing.** But the electricians lay wires the best. They're the fastest. They're the most efficient" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_October_Rollout_Staging_and_Unit_Ramp-September-9-2026.md]].

They are needed only through the first quarter, because after that everything moves to Wi-Fi [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_October_Rollout_Staging_and_Unit_Ramp-September-9-2026.md]].

For the installations themselves Val proposed splitting the two people he named: "[w]hat I suggest we do for every installation is send **[[wiki/people/sebastian.md|Sebastian]] in one direction and [[wiki/people/anibal.md|Anibal]] in the other**." A colleague said he has "another guy just in my back pocket if I need to, but I don't want to use him" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_October_Rollout_Staging_and_Unit_Ramp-September-9-2026.md]].

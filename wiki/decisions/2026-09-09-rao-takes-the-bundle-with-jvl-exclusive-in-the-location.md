# Rao takes the bundle, with JVL exclusive in the location

The first time in the corpus that the free-hardware bundle is put to a **distributor/installer** rather than to a master operator, and **the first time the [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-31-transactional-equipment-only-with-location-exclusivity.md|Aug-31 location-exclusivity condition]] is actually stated as a term of a live offer to a named counterparty.** Put by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] to [[wiki/people/narasimha-rao-namburi.md|Rao]] at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-09-rao-machine-performance-and-exclusivity-offer.md|Sept-9 2026 10:04 AM meeting]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]].

## The offer

- **One machine paid for; everything transactional free.** "we will **bundle one machine plus the rest of the equipment for free, completely for free, no cost**… **All the operator needs to pay is the cost of one machine.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]]
- **The price and the composition, stated by a JVL colleague and not corrected:** "The machine is a **fifteen-game software**… We're charging **fifteen thousand seven hundred and fifty dollars**… It will come with **one port for that machine and five ports, five additional**, which is a half of six." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]]
- **The derived notes read the free side as the machine's own port + five additional ports + one IQ point-of-sale unit + one Pay unit.** The transcript supports the ports directly; the IQ and Pay half is garbled in it — a colleague says "**The kiosk is part of the deal, but in October we will start with [IQ]**. It will cover all the functionality, and later we will still choose" (rendered "iTunes"), and Val's next line is "**Pay or next page. Yeah, next page**". The composition used here is the notes'. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]]
- **Val valued the giveaway at about $15,000, out loud, twice, with the arithmetic spoken loosely both times:** "this is **eight thousand dollars**… This is like **six, seven, plus eight, fifteen thousand dollars**. We're giving you away worth of fifteen thousand dollars"; and a minute later "**Six plus eight, fourteen. Yeah, fifteen thousand dollars.**" **Recorded as spoken, not repaired.** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]]
- **The rationale, unchanged since Sept 2:** "this offer came together **post show, post trade show, after we spoke to a number of people like yourself**… **And this offer is based on buying the market.**" And the sales theory in one line: "You know what the **best price in the market is? Free.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]]
- **No monthly fee was quoted.** The **$90 per location per month** that has been attached to this bundle in five previous customer meetings ([[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-free-ports-and-pos-with-every-machine-bought.md]]) **does not appear anywhere in this source** — neither raised nor withdrawn. Recorded as an absence, not a change. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]]

## What Val asked in return — and this is the exclusivity condition, applied

Three things, stated separately and all in the room [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]]:

1. **Rao's undivided attention to JVL for at least two years, and he stops selling Access.** "Can I get your **undivided attention to JVL**? Focus solely on JVL… I don't want you to be selling access or anything else. But **dedicate yourself to us for at least two years**, solely focused on JVL."
2. **JVL exclusive in the location** — and the reason is exactly the Aug-31 one, restated to a customer for the first time in these words: "**if there is another system present in location, we will never get paid. We can't collect our money. We have to be able to be exclusive in the location.**"
3. **A three-year contract.** "I want to be honest with you. **We have to have three-year contract with the location or with the operator**"; refined a beat later to the location — "**with the location, yeah. And even they sign, otherwise we cannot collect.** I mean, **we give in so much away that somehow somehow we need to collect it back.**" Rao's answer: "**Operator is not going to sign. Location may sign.**"

**This is dated evidence on the Aug-31 open question about whether exclusivity is sellable — it is not a resolution of it.** Rao agreed to the *deal* (below) but said nothing about whether his locations will sign a three-year exclusive, and warned separately that Access can only leave "organically." See [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-31-transactional-equipment-only-with-location-exclusivity.md]].

## Rao's answer

- He asked for time — "I'm going to think about it and work it out" — then, pressed directly by Val ("You're in"), said: "**I'm in.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]]
- **Two conditions of his own:** the installation entity kept separate from the rest of his business ("I want to do this **installation entity separate**"), and no forcing Access out ("**I cannot force access to go away**… It will happen organically" — Val: "Agree") [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]].
- **The start date and the shape of the pilot:** "**My start date is the first of October**"; "Let's plan **five locations for October. Only five locations**"; "Let's aim it for **installation in the first week of October**." That is the customer-side counterpart of [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-first-transactional-installation-planned-for-october-1.md]], set the same morning inside JVL [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]].

## Val's advice to liquidate the Access stock, and he named it aggressive himself

> "**Five hundred thousand. This is the best time to get rid of it.** Instead of installing them, install us… **Sell it to somebody who doesn't know about this deal**… **Tomorrow there will be no market for access. If we are giving it away for free, then Access cannot sell it.** It's just a matter of time until the entire market knows that we're giving it away." And the alternative: "if you have receivables with access, tell him you know what. **Take it back. I cannot pay for it. Empty out your warehouse.**"

Val's own coda: "**I know it's aggressive.**" And the pressure argument he put next to it: "**if you don't agree, you'll be competing with somebody who's giving his equipment for free. Why would you want to be on the other side?**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]]

## Also settled in the room

- **Internal testing comes before any installation.** A JVL colleague: "we will do some **in-house testing next week**"; "The roadmap is that we need to do internal testing… **We make it polished. Only after that are we ready to install.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]]
- **JVL will carry the first installations itself.** "From our side, we will be **ready to support those five locations in October**… We will support your team with all the learning curve of installing them. **Me personally, I'll be at the first couple of locations.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]]
- **A fully enclosed top-mount box on two posts, offered on the spot**, for locations whose cabinets are wooden or have no base plate so nothing has to be cut: "we can give you a **top mount box**… fitting remotely in **a fully enclosed box**… We'll give you **two posts, mounting posts, and through one of the hollow posts** we'll fit the wire." Val flagged it as improvised — "I just had a quick idea. I'm sorry for interruption." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]]
- **The dual-SAS / single-SAS question was decided in this same meeting**, on Rao's insistence, and it pulls against a JVL ruling made two days earlier — [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-jvl-supports-both-dual-sas-and-single-sas-and-not-mudagoose.md]].
- **An October test location where all machines are dual source.** A JVL colleague: "In October, if it's doable, **we would prefer to start with a location where all the machines are dual source**… Just for test." Rao: "**There already all of them do it.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]]
- **Rao's earlier single-game machine gets upgraded to the fifteen-game version.** A colleague: "The one I gave you that you bought… **one game. Not the one that you ordered. That is already completed**… but the one that you bought before… **We need to upgrade that one. 15 game.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]]

## The reversal at the end: the deal may not have to be packaged at all

Late in the meeting Val stopped the thread the whole session had been built on [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]]:

> "we all got **laser focused on packaging the deal**. But for a guy like [Rao], he **does not necessarily need to package the deal**. He can go and install this and **resell this to whoever he resells it to**. He sells Access system. He can sell JVL system. If an operator doesn't want to buy a machine and he wants to buy ports and… that's fine."

He returned to it twice more: "as a rule, we should accept that **we don't have to force the marriage between the two. We can absolutely sell it as a standalone unit**"; and, in the recording's closing seconds, to the room after Rao had left: "**Are we all in agreement that we don't have to package the deal?**"

**It was not answered.** The only reply on the transcript is a colleague's "Well, from today beginning," followed by Val's own "**I will consider this package deal under the schools level**" *("schools level" is garbled and is left as spoken; the derived notes read it the same way)* — and the recording ends mid-sentence. Recorded as an open question below and cross-referenced on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-free-ports-and-pos-with-every-machine-bought.md]].

# Do not discount into the Georgia market — hold the price, extend the terms, and add value instead

**Decided by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] on September 16 2026**, out loud and at length, on [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-16-jvl-transactional-ecosystem-and-class-a-market-strategy.md|the afternoon strategy session]] — and stated **against** a direct objection from JVL's games lead, who argued the opposite in the same conversation. It is the doctrine behind the price positions catalogued on [[wiki/topics/jvl-machine-pricing-strategy.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JVL_Transactional_Ecosystem_and_Class_A_Market_Strategy-September-16-2026.md]].

The derived notes record the same two rulings in their decisions table — "**Do not discount in this market — hold price and extend terms; add value (POS, kiosk, security package) instead of cutting the number**" and "**Class B machine to be positioned as expensive and to look expensive; Class A as mass market, cost-fought**", both attributed to Val [[raw/Meeting_Notes_JVL_Transactional_Ecosystem_and_Class_A_Market_Strategy-September-16-2026.md]].

## The objection it answers

The games lead made the economical-alternative case first, from a location he had been taken round by [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]]: a seven-machine location will hold "**a couple of aristocrat… a couple of light and wonder… a couple of IGT. That gets you four or five machines**", and the jockeying happens for the rest. His conclusion: "**having an economical offering from JVL might make a lot of sense, as opposed to something that's extremely expensive**", and — the sharp end of it — "**I'm not convinced that somebody's going to pull out an IGT name and put a JVL name in its place**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JVL_Transactional_Ecosystem_and_Class_A_Market_Strategy-September-16-2026.md]].

*(Several manufacturer names in that passage are garbled in the transcript and are **left as transcribed, not upgraded** — see the meeting stub.)*

## The ruling, in Val's own argument

**1. Nobody has to displace anybody.** "**First of all, nobody needs to pull out an IGT to put JVL, even if the first three are already determined, and JVL isn't that first three, still there are an average six machines in the location.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JVL_Transactional_Ecosystem_and_Class_A_Market_Strategy-September-16-2026.md]]

**2. What sells here is a new shape, not a better game.** "**we're dealing with a tightly knit community of Indian and Pakistanis, who are extremely gossipy, and they want to have every new toy in the market. The dual screen machine is not a new toy… And game design, no matter what it is, it's an extremely marginal business. To move from number three to number one is not easy… We need a new shape of a machine, a new looking machine**" — so that "**a guy in the location sees it and gets it. The other guy doesn't even care whether the machine is good or not. He needs to have it that shape.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JVL_Transactional_Ecosystem_and_Class_A_Market_Strategy-September-16-2026.md]]

**3. Therefore price is not the lever.** "**whether I sell it a discount or at a high price, people need to have it. I may as well sell it a high price. Why would I discount it?**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JVL_Transactional_Ecosystem_and_Class_A_Market_Strategy-September-16-2026.md]]

**4. Terms are the lever, because operators buy on cash flow.** "**every decision that an operator makes is a cash flow decision. It's not a price decision, and the cash flow decision depends on terms rather than price. So I'd rather extend my terms than cut down the price.**" And the reason a high price is needed *in order* to extend terms: "**If I need to extend the terms, then better I have high enough price to quickly cover my boom, and then the rest let it trickle down through financing.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JVL_Transactional_Ecosystem_and_Class_A_Market_Strategy-September-16-2026.md]]

**5. If a discount becomes unavoidable, discount from a high price — or add a thing instead of cutting the number.** "**if I'm already forced to discount, I better discount from a higher price than a lower price. And if I'm already discounting from a higher price instead of lowering the price… [I] will add a toy like a new POS system or a new kiosk or a security package**" — his worked example being a store surveillance package. "**Discounting or lowering the price in the market is not a good strategy.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JVL_Transactional_Ecosystem_and_Class_A_Market_Strategy-September-16-2026.md]]

**6. And the split by category.** "**I want a machine that is more expensive and looks expensive for Class B. For Class A, that's a different story. Class A is a mass market**" — where the rule inverts entirely: "**we're fighting for every, for every, literally for every ten dollars.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JVL_Transactional_Ecosystem_and_Class_A_Market_Strategy-September-16-2026.md]]

## The bundle arithmetic said out loud in the same conversation

Before the pricing argument, the games lead put the give-away pitch in round numbers: "**For each machine, we give you a complete set of transactional equipment for your location. If you buy a machine for sixteen thousand, we give you thirty thousand dollars' value for your location.**" Val's answer was that the transaction fee is not the point — "**Two dollar fee. We don't really care. We're willing to give it up. We make money on monthly recurring fees, but I'm after slightly different thing. I want to have that cash**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JVL_Transactional_Ecosystem_and_Class_A_Market_Strategy-September-16-2026.md]].

*(**Both figures are spoken by the games lead as a way of framing the offer, not quoted as JVL's price list.** The corpus's actual run of quoted machine prices — $15,750 and the costed giveaway — is on [[wiki/topics/jvl-machine-pricing-strategy.md]]; **the $16,000 / $30,000 pair is recorded here as said, and is not reconciled with it.** The "two dollar fee" is likewise as spoken, with no product named.)*

## Open questions

- **Is this a new rule or a restatement?** The corpus already carries a Sept-23 quoting-and-discount policy and the Aug-21 "dollars, not discounts" alternative on [[wiki/topics/jvl-machine-pricing-strategy.md]]. **None of those sources and this one mention each other**, and nothing here reconciles the doctrine with the $15,750-plus-free-hardware bundle actually being quoted in the field — [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JVL_Transactional_Ecosystem_and_Class_A_Market_Strategy-September-16-2026.md]]
- **The objection was never answered on its own terms.** The games lead's point was about *which* machines get displaced in a full location; Val's answer was that displacement is unnecessary. Both men moved on. Recorded as unresolved — [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JVL_Transactional_Ecosystem_and_Class_A_Market_Strategy-September-16-2026.md]]

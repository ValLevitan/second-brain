# Defer all test-tool purchases (not this year) (2025-01-15)

**Decision:** Val ruled that JVL will not buy any test-automation tool this year, favouring the tools already in place (e.g. X-Ray) and open-source options for now. [[raw/integration_and_testing_procedures_january_15_2025.md]]

**Decided by:** Val, on the test-automation checkpoint call with the QA consultant. [[raw/integration_and_testing_procedures_january_15_2025.md]]

## Rationale

- Val: there are so many other priorities that by the time JVL needs a tool it will likely be cheaper or a newer tool will exist; "very skeptical about buying a tool at this stage." [[raw/integration_and_testing_procedures_january_15_2025.md]]
- The consultant accepted the budget direction but cautioned there are consequences to waiting — it builds "technical debt," and starting small sooner is better than starting big later. [[raw/integration_and_testing_procedures_january_15_2025.md]]
- Val wants to settle the testing architecture (technical and process) first, before spending. See [[wiki/topics/test-automation-strategy.md]]. [[raw/integration_and_testing_procedures_january_15_2025.md]]

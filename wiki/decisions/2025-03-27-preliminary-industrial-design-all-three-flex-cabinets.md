# Design all three Flex cabinets as one family before detailing

On the Mar 27 2025 call [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] and [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi]] agreed to change the sequence of the Flex cabinet work: rather than push the **curved-monitor ("curve") machine** straight into detailing, [[wiki/people/igor-starovoytov.md|Igor Starovoytov]] (the industrial designer) is to first produce a **preliminary industrial design across all three machines** and assemble them into one visual "family," and only then detail the curve machine while the others follow. Genadi agreed and said this is now "priority number one." [[raw/Flex-S-design-March-27-2025.md]]

This extends the Feb 27 street-cabinet direction ([[wiki/decisions/2025-02-27-flex-street-cabinet-design-direction.md]]), which covered the single curve machine. [[raw/Flex-S-design-March-27-2025.md]]

## The direction

- **Two-pass process.** Genadi described JVL's normal flow: a general **industrial-design** pass first (configuration, layout, overall look) until Val says "I'm happy generally"; only then the **detailing ("деталировка")** — cutting the plastics and forming the shapes — because reworking after detailing is expensive. Genadi is under time pressure and wants to avoid detailing the curve machine only to change it once the other two are designed. [[raw/Flex-S-design-March-27-2025.md]]
- **Design the three as a family.** Val wants Genadi to assemble all three machines and give them to Igor to combine "into one family / one heap"; the three named machines are the **curve** machine, the **243** (today's machine, described as "completely different"), and a machine Val calls **"Dnavi" / "D-navi"** — he proposes carrying the same design onto it. (Machine names transcribed from Russian; "Dnavi" spelling unclear.) [[raw/Flex-S-design-March-27-2025.md]]
- **Prepare three variants, then converge.** Plan: Igor prepares three preliminary variants for review; once ~70–80–90% agreed, Genadi switches to finishing only the curve machine while the others are known to be nearly stable, then builds the rest. [[raw/Flex-S-design-March-27-2025.md]]
- **Amplifier box on top of the base unit.** Val praised the redesign that mounts the "amplifier box" on top of the base unit as elegant and a "level up." Idea: give it a **removable/interchangeable front cover** so one node can be swapped per model, with different handles per machine. Genadi to prepare three variants of this unit. [[raw/Flex-S-design-March-27-2025.md]]
- **Monitor angles (preliminary idea).** Val wants the **lower monitor** kept or laid more horizontal (angle increased a couple of degrees toward the player) and the **upper monitor** (currently near-vertical, ~2°) tilted more toward the player (~5°). His reasoning: the old fear that a player-facing top monitor makes players feel something will fall on them doesn't apply here because the panel/deck is deep and this is a **street machine**. [[raw/Flex-S-design-March-27-2025.md]]
- **Remove the light/speaker fixture.** Val wants the "фонарь" (light fixture with a speaker) removed from the top and replaced with something flat and elegant; no topper is needed on a street machine. Its height was discussed as roughly 4 inches but the measurement exchange is garbled/unclear. The top bracket ("кронштейн") at the very top is removable, leaving only the stand; Val wants its placement reworked. [[raw/Flex-S-design-March-27-2025.md]]
- **Handles.** Val feels the handle thickness hasn't been felt out — on the longer distance the horizontal handles to the monitor may look too thin **aesthetically** (not structurally; the handles are plastic and non-load-bearing). He wants a relief, drawing, or text added to them and the handle model changed. [[raw/Flex-S-design-March-27-2025.md]]

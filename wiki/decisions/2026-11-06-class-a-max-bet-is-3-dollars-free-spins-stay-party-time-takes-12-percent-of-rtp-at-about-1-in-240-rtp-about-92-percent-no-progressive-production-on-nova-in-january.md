# Class A: max bet $3; free spins stay, party time 12% of RTP at ~1 in 240; RTP ~92%; no progressive; vertical, on the Georgia COAM base; production on NOVA in January (Nov 6 2026)

Worked out at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-11-06-game-development-class-a-party-time-max-bet-and-qr-wallet.md|Nov 6 2026, 2:04 PM game-development meeting]] (English) between [[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael Gottlieb]], [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] and [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim Podvoiski]], with [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena Zhukova]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. It continues [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-30-class-a-party-time-scales-with-the-bet-as-a-low-level-mystery-progressive-no-paytable-cap-and-25-cent-bet-steps.md|Oct 30]] (bet-scaled party time, no paytable cap, 25-cent steps) and settles the top bet left open there. Background: [[wiki/topics/georgia-class-a-machines.md]].

## 1. Party time — the first target (Michael, with Efim)

- **Casino maths plus party time.** Michael: "As we discussed last week, Val, we're going to have wins that are less than $50"; given "your feedback about what they're doing isn't working. Primero, combined with what your experience was, what my experience was, we are going to go in a very different direction, which is we're going to keep the casino math intact, but then we're going to add the party time feature on" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. Two purposes: "if we need to get the player more than 50 bucks, but the other will be kind of like a low level mystery progressive" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. "what we're calling party time, what they call winning streak" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]].
- **The numbers.** "Efim came to me with some hit frequencies that were way, way, way out there for party time… to treat it more like only in case of needing the 50 bucks. And now let's pull the whole thing in. So our initial shot across the bow is we're going to have 12% of RTP dedicated to party time, and it will hit half as often as free spins, roughly. So free spins are one and 120, Party time will happen 1 and 240"; "if you try and make it more frequent than that, it's going to be problematic and it's not going to feel special"; "I've never done this before. This is a very different type of mechanic compared to… a generic mystery progressive, which tends to be quite a bit further out there" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]].
- **After capped free spins, silently.** "if you get win capped at 50 bucks after free spins is over, on the subsequent spin, base game spin, we're going to put you in party time. We're not going to tell you, but we're going to put you in party time"; "We're still going to trigger party time after free spins on higher bet levels" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. The cap is on the whole round: Val — "maximum you could win is 50"; Michael — "Exactly right, in total"; Efim — "In total, in total"; Val — "for all 7 free spins" — "Yes" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. How many free spins: "Depends on the game… 10, 10" (Michael); Efim: "in line three, just 7 three spins, for example" (heard) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]].
- **The cap bites only above $1.** Efim: "this cup, it's important only on beds above $1.00. Because, with $1.00, most of the principal's outcomes will be naturally below $50"; Michael: "if you're telling me this is a street player and they're going to be betting a dollar or less on average, it'll just be fine" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]].
- **Still to tune.** "The real magic is the question of frequency, EV, making sure that free spin still has some guts and some punch to it… You want to balance that against hit frequency and EV of party time… that's going to take Efim and I some period of time to figure out" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. Michael also plans to "rob those top end pays a little bit… to feed party time to make it happen more frequently"; Val: "I don't mind you robbing the top pay" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]].

## 2. Free spins stay — Val's challenge and Michael's answer

- **The challenge.** Val: "I really like the concept of party… party time. I'm not sure that eating up valuable payout on free spin is needed. Maybe not to have free spins and increase frequency of party party time" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]].
- **The answer.** Michael: "One of the rules of slot machines is you take their money very fast and you give it back to them very, very slowly. That feeds time on device"; free spins giving $50 "can be a two, three minute experience. That's two or three minutes of time on device and a reward to the player"; "Party time does not really have entertainment value. It has surprise and delight when it happens, but… the message comes up, boom"; "A slot machine should have two things to chase, sometimes three, but you have to have two"; "If we got rid of free spins and we only had party time… we're basically screwing ourselves the way Primero did… When you were looking at Primero game, what were you chasing?… Why aren't you chasing free spins? One, it never happened. And 2, if it does happen, you're win capped. Well, we're going to have it happen far more frequently than Primero to solve problem number one, and then we're going to put you in party time right after free spins to solve problem #2" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. Val: "Solid argument, clever"; Michael: "if I'm speculating on something, I'll absolutely tell you I'm speculating, but this, to me, I think it's foundational to the experience" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]].
- **No pick bonus for party time.** "you wouldn't want to throw them into a pick bonus because then it would just become quicksand or swimming in oatmeal"; the design is "the slower pacing and choreography of free spins, and then you blend it with this bam of party time" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. He cited "the match 3 progressive we just designed. That is not a kaboom… It is a time on device interactive bonus" (the Nebraska Match-3) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]].

## 3. The maximum bet — $3

- **Positions.** Val: "What's the maximum bet? We touched on this subject quite exhaustively last time." Michael: "I think 5 bucks." Val: "I recommend not more than three." Michael: "you're going to set your bet level on the screen, set it and forget it. Why would you cripple us and not allow somebody to bet more if they wanted to?" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]
- **Val's reasons.** (1) The visual: "When you look at the table and you bet 25 cents, the odds are fine. 50 cents, the odds change, they're still fine. But when you get to over $3, all you see is that 50 bucks cup [cap]. On every, every odds. And that is not. Clever"; "I just don't like the visual connotation of increasing your bet while you're seeing the top win [capped]. Visually, it's unattractive" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. (2) The market: "the average bet in the state of Georgia is about $1.20, if I'm not mistaken" — Efim: "A little bit more, alright"; the competitor's top is "about $6.50"; "I don't see a purpose of having five and $6 bets"; "being in the business for a long time, watching the player, I don't see a use of… Four and five and $6 [bet] button" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. (3) The regulator: "The regulator and the lottery corporation also will see this as a limiting bet machine… here's a limited bet machine. It will cost cheaper for an operator. It has less regulation. So the whole connotation of, well, this is different"; "because we're largely keeping class 3 math. It may be even more interesting than… Class B machine. if we get the math right"; "I'm giving you my vision as a… business executive… I'm not a designer" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]].
- **Michael's case for higher bets, then the yes.** "there are people that like to play at that level. And if they play at that level, they don't get effed because they get party time. You experienced it yourself. You won 150, 200 bucks in one party time session. And that takes the sting out of that $50 win cap because cash is king"; the top pays hit rarely anyway — "perception versus reality" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. On the decision: "I'm not going to necessarily object. I look at things purely from a standpoint of… what's the potential upside, what's the potential downside?"; Val named the downside himself: "A player complains, the operator comes to us and says, give me my $6 [button]… We want higher bet level. Give me, give it to me… Okay, so here you have it" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. Michael: "This reduces the amount of work for [Efim] because it's going to be 25 cent a bet increments. So if we max out at 3 bucks instead of 5 bucks, that's 8 increments you don't have to deal with" — Efim: "True" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. Val: "we're not leaving money on the table. I can guarantee it to you. At least for a couple of years… Let the competition come out with some… better bets and the market says, look, they're doing it. We need to do it as well. We'll come back. We'll do it. It's not a big deal" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. Michael: "I just wouldn't want to cripple ourselves compared to our competitors… But if you feel like $3 makes this more… palatable and digestible for the environment that we're operating in, it allows you to create a story on a couple of different levels… Start at the intended outcome and work backwards… in the back room between all of us, we all know that these players are betting a buck or they're betting $2, then fine. We're not putting ourselves in harm's way at $3… if you want to do it, let's do it." Val: "I think 3 [bucks] is fine" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. (Michael's aside that "there can always be a whale somewhere" and his casino observations are opinion, not recorded further.)

## 4. RTP, progressive, cabinet, base game, skill

- **RTP.** Val: "What's the RTP that we're going for?" Michael: "We haven't made a decision on that necessarily… I was assuming we were going to do the same as what we normally do in Georgia." Val: "About 92 percent… Total RTP, 92%, yeah." Michael: "Okay, well then" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]].
- **No progressive.** Val asked about "the progressing"; Michael: "There isn't one, no progressives… It's the 50, the $50 win cap [eats] it all up, basically. So we just stay away from that. Party time is the replacement for the progressive. And by the way, by the time we're done paying for free spins and party time, there's no money left over for progressive anyway" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]].
- **Vertical.** Val: "that's a vertical machine, am I correct?" Michael: "Yes" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]].
- **The base is the Georgia COAM game.** Val asked whether the transitions and effects are like Class 3; Lena: "No, it's the same over to [Georgia]"; Michael: "We're using Georgia [COAM] as the base for this"; "They're the same, it's the same, it's just the skill element"; the presentation of the pick is "similar, but it's different… but it's still the same mechanic" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]].
- **The skill element — and where the rule comes from.** Val: "But this game does not have skill. It's the typical class 3." Efim: "It does." Michael: "It does have skill… It has picked the two symbols that match" — later corrected: "It's still choose the larger coin… We randomly choose… whether it's going to be on the left or the right. And then your job is to choose the larger coin. That's the skill" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. Val: "Why does why does it mean skill?" Michael: "regulatory requirement. I was under the impression that it was required." Lena: "They had it to just reference this reference company they have it." Michael: "It's Class A skill" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. After the break Val: "Just wondering if the regulation talks about… Skill in Class A machines." Michael: "Yes, the stuff that you sent and that Juan sent absolutely referred to skill." Val: "it was [Juan] who sent, it wasn't me… Can you forward it to me? Not now, but after the meeting." Michael: "Yes" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. Near the end Michael showed the document: "This was the thing that we got sent when you guys went out and did the whole thing as to what Class A is. What makes a machine Class A? It refers to things like kiddie rides, skeeball, claw machines, pinball, pool, table. So that all sounds like. Skill[] to me"; Val: "where is it coming from?" Michael: "This was sent. By you… you sent it on 831… it's Claude's interpretation of Class A regulations. But clearly it's skill because… kiddie rides, skeeball, and claw machines… you can't just have it as a full-on fire hose, regular old slot"; Efim: "No… Mr. Lyashenko does not rely on it" (heard; the notes: "marked do not rely on it") [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. "Juan" here is [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]] by the Georgia context — **inference**. The team's belief that Class A needs skill rests on that Aug-31 Claude reading; it feeds the standing question on [[wiki/topics/georgia-class-a-machines.md]] (is the skill element real or a fiction).

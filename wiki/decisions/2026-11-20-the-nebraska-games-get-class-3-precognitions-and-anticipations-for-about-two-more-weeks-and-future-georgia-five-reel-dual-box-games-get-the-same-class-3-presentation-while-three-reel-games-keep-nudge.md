# Nebraska gets precognitions and anticipations; Georgia five-reel dual-box games get Class 3 presentation, three-reel keep nudge (Nov 20 2026)

Proposed by [[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael Gottlieb]] and accepted by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-11-20-game-development-nebraska-precogs-class-a-plan-and-promo-credits.md|Nov 20 2026, ~12:02 PM game-development meeting]] (English), with [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim Podvoiski]] and [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena Zhukova]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Nebraska_Precogs_Class_A_Plan_and_Promo_Credits-November-20-2026.md]]. Project context: [[wiki/projects/nebraska-skill-market.md]] and [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]].

## 1. Nebraska — add what the Class 3 product has

- **Why.** Michael: "I have been playing the Nebraska build and I determined that I think we should really add the additional production value that we have had in our class 3 product… into the Nebraska product." The first release ran on "an extremely accelerated schedule, get it done, knock it out, get the dual box in"; now, with the second release, "what we do not have and what we should have, and we have the assets, it's the asset integration that needs to get done, except for Wildfire Ranch, which does not have a precog" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Nebraska_Precogs_Class_A_Plan_and_Promo_Credits-November-20-2026.md]].
- **What the two effects are.** "the precognition is a graphical effect that happens before something good happens. It gets you excited"; "the anticipations are instead of the Free spins, symbols hitting, boom, boom, boom, they go boom, boom, and then there's a wind-up on the third one before it hits" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Nebraska_Precogs_Class_A_Plan_and_Promo_Credits-November-20-2026.md]].
- **Cost.** Michael: "a little bit of a schedule implication… is it about two weeks worth of work?" Lena: "Yes, about it, because not only for wildfire, but also for new line cities [Lions Riches], we need to create… new graphics, we did not do because we are not doing in Georgia." Michael later: "We still need to do one for Wildfire Ranch. We need to do one for Lions Riches"; "It is going to extend the schedule a couple of weeks, but I definitely think it's a worthwhile endeavor"; the effects "will follow us through because… we'll take some of those games to Columbia [Colombia]" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Nebraska_Precogs_Class_A_Plan_and_Promo_Credits-November-20-2026.md]].
- **The performance link.** Val: "Nebraska, Nebraska is not performing, so our games are not interesting." Michael: "that's exactly one of the reasons why I thought we needed to do this… especially Fiery Ice… they're flat in terms of presentation value"; "I don't expect those Nebraska games to be lighting the world on fire, but I am surprised as to how poor they're performing"; with this and "the match 3 progressive, which is a newer superior progressive to that Golden Husk progressive pick… it should definitely make it a superior product" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Nebraska_Precogs_Class_A_Plan_and_Promo_Credits-November-20-2026.md]].

## 2. Why Georgia has no precognition — and what changes

- **The nudge.** Asked "why don't we do precognition in Georgia?", Lena: "Because of nights" (heard; nudge by context), Michael: "Because of Nudge." Michael: "You trigger with a stack on reel 3 in order to initiate whatever the event is, as opposed to reels 2, three, and four… That's a class 3 behavior. What we had to do because of nudge is on like the center reel, you'd have… two out of the three symbols that you need, and then you would have to nudge to create a full stack of that trigger symbol on Reel 3" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Nebraska_Precogs_Class_A_Plan_and_Promo_Credits-November-20-2026.md]].
- **Five-reel Georgia games.** Michael: "when we do 5 real [reel] games in the future in Georgia, we'll be using dual box, and then with using dual box, we can do all the stuff, and we will. That's the plan. We'll have anticipations, we'll have precogs, and do all the class 3 behaviors"; "not necessarily the math for Georgia, But the presentation layer for Georgia will be more like a class 3" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Nebraska_Precogs_Class_A_Plan_and_Promo_Credits-November-20-2026.md]].
- **Three-reel games keep nudge.** "For the three real games, they're still doing nudge. Our competitors are still doing nudge, and I think we should do nudge for three reels, because that's just the, I would call it expected behavior"; "midnight train… is a nudge mechanic and people like it… So I don't think we should deviate from that" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Nebraska_Precogs_Class_A_Plan_and_Promo_Credits-November-20-2026.md]].
- **Nebraska is different.** Lena: "in Nebraska, even for three reels… we are doing dual boxes, right?" Michael: "100%, yes, absolutely" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Nebraska_Precogs_Class_A_Plan_and_Promo_Credits-November-20-2026.md]].
- **Felipe.** Michael: "as we get closer, I will check back in with Felipe as we're at the juncture where that final decision needs to be made"; "I checked with Felipe recently and he still agrees with… With that approach" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Nebraska_Precogs_Class_A_Plan_and_Promo_Credits-November-20-2026.md]]. See [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md]].
- **Val's answer.** "The fact that you are… tackling Nebraska and I'm glad that we're moving with more Class 3 feature reach [rich] designs in Georgia is the right move for us"; Michael: "I would agree"; Val: "I'm happy, I'm happy with that" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Nebraska_Precogs_Class_A_Plan_and_Promo_Credits-November-20-2026.md]].

The derived notes record both decisions ("Add precognitions and anticipations to the Nebraska games; schedule moves about two weeks"; "Georgia: 5-reel games with dual box get Class 3 presentation; 3-reel games keep nudge") [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Game_Development_Nebraska_Precogs_Class_A_Plan_and_Promo_Credits-November-20-2026.md]]. Their line "Val: Nebraska is not performing; he is glad" misreads the transcript: Val's "I'm glad" is about the Class 3 move in Georgia, not about Nebraska's performance.

# A new set of regulations for the Passport and RIS, produced from a meeting transcript

[[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]]'s main point at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-11-technician-complaints-and-new-capabilities-workshop.md|Sept 11 2026 10:33 AM call]]: "майпоинс заключается в том, что нам нужно… Сделать сет огню рекламас, как мы видим, чтобы работал паспорт и arase" — my point is that we need to make a set of new regulations for how we see the passport and RIS working [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Technician_Complaints_and_New_Capabilities_Workshop-September-11-2026.md]].

## The frame he put around it first: the system is not at fault, the requirements moved

He was explicit that this must not turn into retrospective fault-finding [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Technician_Complaints_and_New_Capabilities_Workshop-September-11-2026.md]]:

- "сегодняшняя система, так как она построена… построена, правильно… По требованиям, которые были на тот момент, когда мы её строили. Но требования радикально поменялись" — today's system is built correctly, against the requirements that existed when we built it; but the requirements changed radically.
- "я не хотел бы, чтоб мы вдруг стали в позицию, что о, что мы пропустили, как мы не то сделали, почему мы не сделали так, как нужно было делать?" — I would not want us to end up in the position of "what did we miss, what did we do wrong, why didn't we do it the way it should have been done".
- "мы сделали всё правильно… По рекламентам 10 лет тому назад" — we did everything right, by the regulations of ten years ago. *(Earlier in the same passage he hedges the age of the design himself — "Так не помню, лет 20 тому назад или 10" — twenty years ago or ten. **Both are recorded; neither is preferred.**)*
- **And what stopped now:** "нам нужно адресовать новый рекламент рынка" — we need to address the market's new regulation.

## Where his own work stopped, and why

This is the clearest account in the corpus of Val running the process chain himself and abandoning it one step from the end [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Technician_Complaints_and_New_Capabilities_Workshop-September-11-2026.md]]:

> "Я сделал прозу. Я сделал архитектуру дизайн… Ну такой широкий, очень такой поверхностный. Оттуда вырисовал кипа билоти… Но не дошел до регламента."
> — I wrote the prose. I did the architecture design — a broad one, a very shallow one. From it I drew out the capabilities. But I did not get as far as the regulations.

"Просто не хватило времени" — simply not enough time. And the reason he did it at all: "я хотел знать, как это сделано сегодня. И что нужно поменять?… какие кейподелать их надо поменять, что надо новое сделать?" — I wanted to know how it is done today and what needs to change: which capabilities have to change, and what new ones are needed [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Technician_Complaints_and_New_Capabilities_Workshop-September-11-2026.md]].

His conclusion: "мы находимся на том уровне, где нужно делать новые кейпопило" — we are at the level where new capabilities have to be made [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Technician_Complaints_and_New_Capabilities_Workshop-September-11-2026.md]].

**This is the same chain ruled on Sept 7 and re-affirmed on Sept 10–11** — prose and architecture → capabilities → regulations carrying the requirements and acceptance criteria — run by the principal on his own material. See [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-07-andrei-bosik-drives-the-wi-fi-work-and-the-process-chain.md]] and [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-11-acceptance-criteria-live-in-the-regulations-not-the-capabilities.md]].

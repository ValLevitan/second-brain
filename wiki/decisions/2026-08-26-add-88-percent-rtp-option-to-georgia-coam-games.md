# Add a lower (~88%) RTP option to the 15 Georgia COAM games and fold it into the Sept-1 release

On the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-26-alignment-planning-nova-september-releases.md|Aug 26 2026 alignment call]] [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]] raised a gap that blocks the pricing direction Val had just described, and Val ruled it into the September 1 Georgia release on the spot.

## What was decided

- **A lower RTP option — Irina named 88% — is to be added to the Georgia COAM games that do not have one, and the Sept-1 release waits for it.** Val: "и добавить в этот релиз" (and add it to this release). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alignment_Planning_NOVA_and_Sept_Releases-August-26-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Alignment_Planning_NOVA_and_Sept_Releases-August-26-2026.md]]
- **[[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim Podvoiski]] does the work**, and [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina]] contacts him the same day and then puts the build on test. Irina: "нам нужно взять ефима доделать этот процент на вот эти 15 игр, где есть, где нету" — take Efim to finish this percentage on these 15 games, where it exists and where it doesn't. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alignment_Planning_NOVA_and_Sept_Releases-August-26-2026.md]]
- **Val's sizing of the task: "не часы, но это не месяца"** — not hours, but not months either; he expects it to be a fairly light job for Efim. Irina's own estimate was that after Efim adds it, it goes on test, "максимум… Следующая неделя будет через неделю" (at most next week, or the week after). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alignment_Planning_NOVA_and_Sept_Releases-August-26-2026.md]]

## Why

- **The current Georgia setting is too generous to hit the netwin Val wants.** Irina stated the existing configuration: the Georgia games run at **90, 92 or 94 percent**, plus **2% or 2.5% on the progressive** — "это игры 90, 92, 94 процента плюс 2 процента, либо 2 с половиной процента у прогрессива." To set a lower percentage the games need an 88% option that some of them simply do not have today. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alignment_Planning_NOVA_and_Sept_Releases-August-26-2026.md]]
- This is the enabling step for Val's location-based RTP / minimum-bet thinking from the same call — lower RTP and a higher minimum bet in Atlanta, the current settings in the periphery. That thinking itself was **parked, not decided** (see [[wiki/topics/slot-game-design-principles.md]], Aug-26 section); only the 88% option was ruled in.
- Irina also reported both September products are effectively finished — "оба продукта, они уже достаточно… готовые, то есть там осталось минимально" — so the percentage work is the remaining content item rather than a schedule risk. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alignment_Planning_NOVA_and_Sept_Releases-August-26-2026.md]]

# GLI certification of Omni runs on JVL-written white papers; GLI's proposal waits until GLI has read them

## The decision

- **Who and when.** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-01-28-gli-omni-platform-certification-kickoff.md|Jan-28 2026 GLI kickoff]] for the Omni platform, with [[wiki/people/dan-lewis.md|Dan Lewis]] of [[wiki/organizations/gli.md|GLI]] agreeing ("Okay."). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]
- **The method.** Fewer talks, more documents. Val: "I would like to minimize it, so I do like the idea of us initiating the white paper, you assessing the white paper, and reflecting on it, and then suggesting something else." He took each paper as a JVL undertaking: "I'm taking it as JVL's undertaking, to provide you with the white paper." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]
- **The papers and list JVL owes GLI** (set in the same meeting): a list of all peripherals, marked where already approved, so all are tested at one time; a white paper on how the math and RNG are built in the new framework; a white paper on how GLI can plug into JVL's ecosystem and get an output; and a white paper on testing the setup screens. Owners and detail on [[wiki/organizations/gli.md]] (Jan 28 2026). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]
- **GLI's commercial proposal waits.** When Deborah Oxnem offered a proposal, Val: "No, until we get closer and until you understood our white paper, only then provide us with the proposal." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]
- **One channel.** Val asked GLI to send follow-up questions through him and Juan, not the whole group: "my preference is not to pollute mailboxes so juan and myself… will decide how to disseminate the information". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]

## Rationale

- Val wanted to start early, while there is time: "I want to make sure that everybody understands the scope, the requirement, the objective, and all of that. And we have plenty of time right now". He said a written paper that GLI reviews is "much more efficient" than rounds of talk. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]
- On testing everything at once: "it's always cheaper to test it at one." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]

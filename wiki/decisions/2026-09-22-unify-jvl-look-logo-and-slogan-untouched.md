# Unify JVL's look — no rebrand; logo and slogan untouched

Decided by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] on the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-22-howard-tikka-brand-realignment-and-collateral.md|Sept-22 2026 call]] with [[wiki/people/howard-tikka.md|Howard Tikka]], who is to propose how to do the work. The notes record it as two decisions by Val: logo and slogan off the table, and no rebrand [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Howard_Tikka_Brand_Realignment_and_Collateral-September-22-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Howard_Tikka_Brand_Realignment_and_Collateral-September-22-2026.md]].

## What was decided

- **One uniform look for everything JVL does.** "We need to get a uniform. Style and look for everything that we do. From stationery, whether it's printed or digital, to paper stock if it's a printed medium, to business cards, to trade shows." Later: "From advertising to style guides to everything." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Howard_Tikka_Brand_Realignment_and_Collateral-September-22-2026.md]]
- **Trade-show clothing is in scope.** "I need help with putting attire together. Just a trade show attire. We don't use suits anymore. It's loose shirts and pants." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Howard_Tikka_Brand_Realignment_and_Collateral-September-22-2026.md]]
- **The logo and slogan stay.** Asked if he wanted to revamp "the logo and look and everything", Val said: "No logo. Logo cannot be touched. The slogan cannot be touched. It has so much meaning for me. It's my father's company, so I'm not going to revamp any of that." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Howard_Tikka_Brand_Realignment_and_Collateral-September-22-2026.md]]
- **Incremental, not a big leap.** Howard offered the choice between small tweaks and "changing colors, changing looks, changing how you communicate". Val: "I don't think that we need it. JVL has a tremendous name and history, and people remember it." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Howard_Tikka_Brand_Realignment_and_Collateral-September-22-2026.md]]

## Why

- **No one owned it for years.** "for many years. There was no chef in the kitchen, so everything got disintegrated and disjointed and lost… Just need to collect the pieces and put it together." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Howard_Tikka_Brand_Realignment_and_Collateral-September-22-2026.md]]
- **The old look still works.** "Nothing changed. Colors didn't change. Fonts didn't change… I need to bring it back together into one package." The look "is actually very nice and simple. But it needs to be collected, dusted, rearranged." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Howard_Tikka_Brand_Realignment_and_Collateral-September-22-2026.md]]
- **Staff will take it up.** "when we create consistency and present the whole consistent look to members of my company, they will happily embrace it." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Howard_Tikka_Brand_Realignment_and_Collateral-September-22-2026.md]]
- **New shows ahead.** Val tied it to showing the product at c-store, home and design trade shows: "I need to collect the look again… that's the first point." See [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-31-convenience-store-trade-shows-as-a-channel.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Howard_Tikka_Brand_Realignment_and_Collateral-September-22-2026.md]].

*(Which slogan is meant is not said on the call. The wiki records "We Take Our Games Seriously" as JVL's slogan in an earlier document — see [[wiki/organizations/jvl.md]]. Linking the two is inference.)*

Howard to send a proposal; his commitment is on [[wiki/people/howard-tikka.md]]. Val: "Think it through. See how many hours you have around and let me know." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Howard_Tikka_Brand_Realignment_and_Collateral-September-22-2026.md]]

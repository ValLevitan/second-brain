# Mould without the screen first, and a fallback if the printer is not installed

The hardware half of the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-09-october-rollout-staging-and-unit-ramp.md|Sept 9 2026 8:43 AM rollout meeting]]. [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] set the priority order for [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi Peari]], read the stock position out loud, and pushed back on the room's anxiety about it.

## Genadi's priority order

"[Genadi is] working right now on changing the mold. **Making another mold, removing the screen, and changing the mold.** Providing this, **that's his number one priority**. His other priority is to **install the stan[d]s. All of the internal… components, harnesses**, everything to ensure that we can accommodate for all the purposes" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_October_Rollout_Staging_and_Unit_Ramp-September-9-2026.md]].

## Brackets are done and gone to the manufacturer

Asked about brackets, Val cut it off: "we don't need these brackets. **This is already done today and sent to the manufacturer**… So now all we need to do is to receive it here," and again a moment later: "[h]ere is already done. **It's past tense.** There is nothing to talk about" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_October_Rollout_Staging_and_Unit_Ramp-September-9-2026.md]].

That is the state of the critical path set five days earlier on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-04-brackets-and-harnesses-from-china-and-a-metal-order-for-one-hundred.md]] — where the brackets and harnesses were the one thing not covered, and Val was openly sceptical that Genadi would release the drawings on time. **On Sept 9 he reports them released and sent.** *(Neither Sept-9 source says which manufacturer, or whether "sent to the manufacturer" means the Chinese shop reached through [[wiki/people/mikhail-perelman.md|Mikhail Perelman]]; not asserted here.)*

## The printer is the open risk, and it has a fallback

"[I'm] focusing on this, but **I'm not sure that the actual printer will arrive installed. It's a question mark.** So if that doesn't work, then we'll put these out. **What's the panic? I don't understand. We… know that both do not work together. So it's either installing this or this. If this ready, and that is not ready, we'll install this. And this already ordered. It's fine**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_October_Rollout_Staging_and_Unit_Ramp-September-9-2026.md]].

The fallback only works because of the one-unit-per-location limit — FlexPay and Flex-IQ cannot go in together anyway, so swapping which one goes in costs nothing. See [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-one-transactional-unit-per-location-until-the-wi-fi-iteration.md]].

A colleague's reaction, recorded because it is the room's own assessment: "[t]he question will disappear. Good question. **The chances are very good**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_October_Rollout_Staging_and_Unit_Ramp-September-9-2026.md]].

## The stock position, as stated in the meeting

Quoted as spoken; the sentence is elliptical and the units are the speaker's own [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_October_Rollout_Staging_and_Unit_Ramp-September-9-2026.md]]:

| Item | Val's words |
|---|---|
| Plastics | "[p]lastic, we have **a hundred**" |
| Hardware | "hardware we have **twenty**" |
| Plastic for the Pay unit | "[f]or its plastic, we have **unlimited**" |
| Screens | "[s]creens, I think we have **a hundred**" |
| Boards | "[b]oards we're redesigning right now, but it will **come in two weeks**" |

**And his answer to the panic:** "[d]on't forget that **you don't need it on the first of October. You need five and ten and four**" — the October 1 installation needs a handful of units, not the full run. A colleague: "[g]ood, good point" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_October_Rollout_Staging_and_Unit_Ramp-September-9-2026.md]]. *(What the three numbers each count is not stated in the source and is not inferred here.)*

# Competitor games are studied for new mechanics, not maths; the method is first proved on JVL's own games

Set at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-30-ai-competitor-game-teardown-and-mechanics-library.md|Oct 30 2026 teardown review]] (8:03–8:49 AM Eastern, Russian) by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], with [[wiki/people/evgeny-kharetski.md|Evgeny Kharetski]], [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]], [[wiki/people/igor-metelski.md|Igor Metelski]] and [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]]. It is the Friday competitor session set on Oct 24 for Kharetski's hypothesis test — [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-18-evgeny-kharetski-owns-a-voice-ai-game-design-assistant-with-a-first-pass-reviewed-oct-25.md]] (Oct-30 update).

## What Kharetski tested

- **Source: free YouTube videos.** He asked Claude to break down competitor games from videos in open access: first Aristocrat (a blogger playing a penguin game; the same blogger has a wolf game), then IGT and "блюбери" (heard). Claude found more videos itself — "почти 300 минут", then "234 по четырем играм"; Aristocrat's Timber Wolf ("тимберлф") three times, two from one channel and one from another [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]].
- **What comes out.** The mechanics for sure: how the bonuses work, how the game looks, the grids, some animations — for example the penguin that keeps walking in from the screen edge and appears on the reels as if breaking ice with a stick — and the symbols [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]].
- **Maths only roughly.** Claude wrote "свою математическую модель" that runs each bonus about 20,000 times and builds probabilities from that; Kharetski later called it "самописная модель" (what he said about how informative it is is garbled — **unclear**) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]]. The artifact was shared in the meeting chat [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]].
- **Long videos.** Claude cut an hour-long video into frames, one every few seconds ("по моему 4 секунды"); where the player balance changed sharply, it went back over that stretch in detail [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]].
- **The prompt.** He first pulled everything on this topic out of the previous meeting's transcript and gave it to a new session, with YouTube as the source for now [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]].
- **A capture list.** He also had Claude write a first version of a file for the person who takes the video-capture box to other companies' machines: what to film and how many games to play. Val read it out: 10 seconds of the attract screen, then 5 seconds of each help/rules page; Kharetski: order helps but is not critical [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]]. Val: he can also play a machine for money, then go through its demo ("show") mode and record all the bonuses [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]].

## The ruling — mechanics, not maths

- **How much play.** Pavel: walking through every bonus gives little; playing the game for some time gives more, and AI may then get the mechanics and even the percentages; a test on JVL's own games against their par sheets would show the minimum play time for, say, 95 percent confidence [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]]. Irina: on JVL's maths they run 4 million games, twice (8 million) for a quick check; if there are doubts, 2 × 400 million, and compare the error [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]].
- **Val:** "Нам нам математика не нужна, нам нужна механика" — "как работает вся игра" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]]. Kharetski: with video of about 1,000 games a bonus will come up, and show mode can call bonuses on purpose; Val: even a full day on one game is possible [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]]. The notes record "focus on mechanics, not math" as Val's decision [[raw/Meeting_Notes_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]].

## Why JVL does this (Val, Pavel)

- **Pavel:** to see whether competitors have something JVL's games lack — for example an interactive character who climbs out from behind the reels, does nothing, yet gives the feeling that someone plays against you; Val: "или тебе помогает" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]].
- **Val:** Aristocrat, Light & Wonder ("лайтен вон") and IGT move game mechanics first; JVL picks them up years later and wants to do it "на лету". JVL cannot set mechanics — it lacks their exposure to markets, players and casinos, "и у нас никогда их не будет, у нас их через 30 лет тоже не будет". So JVL must pick up new mechanics faster, name them correctly, and turn them into capabilities with acceptance criteria as a request to game designers [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]].
- **Not copying.** Val: the aim is not to copy machines but to understand what the industry needs and do it JVL's way — interpret a mechanic in JVL's style on JVL's framework, then write capabilities, requirements and acceptance criteria [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]]. Pavel called it a scientific approach; Val: "систем[ный]" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]].
- **In JVL's words.** Val asked whether Claude can describe a new mechanic in words, to JVL's templates and language; Kharetski: "Да я думаю легко." Igor: from a written mechanic the team could build a working bonus with placeholder scenes, define the code contracts (scene elements, their names, animations), and learn what the bonus needs from the maths [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]]. The framework side is on [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-29-the-new-engine-is-fully-described-and-split-into-mvp-1-2-3-with-schedules-and-a-test-environment-before-code-and-anton-igor-and-alexander-build-it-together.md]] (Oct-30 note).

## Val's plan — which games, and the steps

- **Only JVL's markets.** Val: JVL does not need all mechanics or all games; what it needs is easily found through a report he can get or buy; he will say which games, and takes on finding where to film them — "от меня выйдет этот файл" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]]. Pavel: start from the most in-demand games; Val: in JVL's markets — not Australia, not Macau, not even Las Vegas, which has "свой микроклимат" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]].
- **The steps (Val).** Find the new mechanics ("Может быть… 2 механики"); add each to JVL's database of mechanics, with a glossary and a taxonomy; bring the framework to where games can be built with it; find all titles with that mechanic that are profitable in JVL's markets; film them and confirm they match the mechanic; split them by theme and replace the themes with ones already recognized in JVL's markets ("те же волки там. Девушка ещё ктото там") [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]].

## First: prove the method on JVL's own games

- **Kharetski's test.** He takes one current JVL game and breaks it down from video with a Claude session that knows nothing about JVL's code; in parallel he builds that game's wiki from the code, with its mechanics; then the two are compared. "Если оно плюс минус совпадает, значит… способ рабочий." A good match also gives an exact list of what to film on competitor machines [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]]. Compare the code-first wiki of [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-24-code-not-confluence-is-the-source-of-truth-and-claude-builds-a-wiki-per-old-game-from-the-svn-code.md]].
- **Which games.** Irina: use the GA-COAM machine ("гку ам"; the notes read GA-COAM) that carries three different products at once — three different progressives — and take one game from each, for different mechanics; Val agreed. Val: remember those games run finite maths, not random maths; Kharetski: the maths does not matter, the mechanics do [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]].
- **When.** Val: a week on the calendar; Kharetski: next Friday; Irina: the 6th [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]] — Friday Nov 6 2026 [[raw/Meeting_Notes_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]].
- **JVL's own mechanics in words.** Val: JVL has no prose description of its own mechanics yet, but one can be made; Kharetski: it belongs in the wiki discussed the day before [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]]. Val then asked that the mechanics of JVL's newer games be written up — "сначала у нас идёт вики" — covering more than one game, "хотя бы 2 механики"; he first named Igor Metelski, then Kharetski proposed doing it with his test. Who owns it is not settled (the notes ask Val to confirm) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_AI_Competitor_Game_Teardown_and_Mechanics_Library-October-30-2026.md]].

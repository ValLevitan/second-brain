# Close the JVL Mexico operation

**Decision (Val, Mar 24 2025):** wind down and dissolve JVL's Mexico operation. Announcement to be made **the week after the third machine shipment departs** (that shipment leaving on the Friday); Val will announce closure and machine-withdrawal to commence **immediately**, with a **maximum six months to dissolve** the company — staff paid for those months. [[raw/financial_alka_alignment-march-24-2025.md]]

## Rationale

- Mexico is "throwing money out of the window"; Val had been patient but the market no longer justifies it. This follows the same Latin-America wind-down logic as Peru (already closing) — see [[wiki/people/anibal.md]] and the market update Val gave on [[wiki/meetings/2025-03-23-doug-business-update.md]] (Mexico had an unfavourable tax audit/penalty; JVL "milked the cow until it died"). [[raw/financial_alka_alignment-march-24-2025.md]] [[raw/doug_update_jvl-march-23-2025.md]]
- Colombia is **kept** — it has a strong local manager and keeps generating cash, booked as JVL Canada revenue. [[raw/doug_update_jvl-march-23-2025.md]]

## Mechanics

- **Early-close bonus:** staff keep the balance of their six months' pay as unencumbered "bonus" if they close faster (close in one month → keep five months' pay; close in five → one). This incentivises a quick wind-down. [[raw/financial_alka_alignment-march-24-2025.md]]
- Machines are being **repatriated to Canada** (two shipments gone, a third departing Friday, leaving ~100 machines); refurbished machines route onward to Puerto Rico. [[raw/financial_alka_alignment-march-24-2025.md]] [[raw/doug_update_jvl-march-23-2025.md]]
- A **local accounting firm** handles the company wrap-up, so the Mexico accountant **Erica** and a Mexico subcontractor **technician** are let go (nothing left to do). Erica gets ~six months' paid notice; Val handles that conversation himself. [[raw/financial_alka_alignment-march-24-2025.md]]
- Finance tasks are reshuffled off Erica onto Laura/Mary — see [[wiki/topics/jvl-finance-back-office.md]]. [[raw/financial_alka_alignment-march-24-2025.md]]

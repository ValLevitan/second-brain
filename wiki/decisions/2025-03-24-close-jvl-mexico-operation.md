# Close the JVL Mexico operation

**Decision (Val, Mar 24 2025):** wind down and dissolve JVL's Mexico operation. Announcement to be made **the week after the third machine shipment departs** (that shipment leaving on the Friday); Val will announce closure and machine-withdrawal to commence **immediately**, with a **maximum six months to dissolve** the company — staff paid for those months. [[raw/financial_alka_alignment-march-24-2025.md]]

## Tightened Apr 7 2025 — 30 days, ~$20k budget (current state)

On the Apr 7 finance call ([[wiki/meetings/2025-04-07-finance-recurring-alka-hector.md]]) Val rejected Alka/Hector's six-month Mexico cash-flow outlook: "Six months cannot happen… The office must be closed in 30 days" — telephone lines and services terminated with it. [[raw/finance_recurring-april-7-2025.md]]

- **Scope of the wind-down:** "They have 98 machines or 100 machines in locations. I want these machines picked up and shipped… they all fit into one truck" — about a month of work including shipping. **Miguel and Camilo** (both still on payroll, "two strong men" who have been servicing locations) collect, package and ship the machines; ~**$10,000** of receivables to be pursued (assumed 50/50 over two months). [[raw/finance_recurring-april-7-2025.md]]
- **The closure budget:** ~**$8,000 for April + ~$11,000 for May** (operating expenses net of expected collections) — "You're only going to get $20,000. End of story" — target close **~May 15**. Alka to write a short narrative summary for Val's sign-off, then circulate the budget to Mexico. [[raw/finance_recurring-april-7-2025.md]]
- **Severance stays discretionary and off the forecast:** Val will still "be very fair with people that are fair with me" on the earlier six-months-salary intent, but **at his discretion only** — he refused to have it in the forecast or in writing ("if it already circulates in the company, somehow it will leak") and will send **a final letter stating that if the deal is not closed by May, all my obligation is null and void.** [[raw/finance_recurring-april-7-2025.md]]
- **Kept off the Mexico budget entirely** (Val's direct responsibility, not the local team's business): legal expenses, taxes, **SAT settlement cases**, legal fees — the forecast including them is "purely for you [Val]" to plan cash flow. [[raw/finance_recurring-april-7-2025.md]]

*(Evolution, not contradiction: the Mar-24 "maximum six months" ceiling is superseded by the 30-day order; the early-close bonus logic below is consistent with it — closing faster preserves the discretionary payout.)*

## Apr 15 2025 — the tax audit blocks legal dissolution (current state)

On the Apr 15 wind-down call with external Mexican accountant [[wiki/people/alejandro.md|Alejandro]] (via interpreter [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda]] — [[wiki/meetings/2025-04-15-mexico-orderly-wind-down-alejandro.md]]), a distinction emerged between **stopping operations** and **legally dissolving the company**: [[raw/mexico_jvl_orderly_wind-down-apr-15-2025.md]]

- **Operations vs legal closure.** Val stated he "will probably close down all the operations within the next 60 days," but Alejandro warned the company **cannot be fully/legally closed** until the **SAT tax audit** — running "a little more than a year" with no certain end — concludes: his estimate to fully dissolve is **"a year, year and a half," but uncertain** (it depends on the authority, not JVL). (Refines Val's own Apr-7 "office closed in 30 days" operational order — the machine-pickup/office shutdown is a separate, shorter track from legal dissolution; recorded as Val's later restatement, not a contradiction.) [[raw/mexico_jvl_orderly_wind-down-apr-15-2025.md]]
- **Legal-representative liability.** Bankrupting the company would not sever exposure: a foreign company must keep a **legal representative**, and the tax authority "will be able to continue trying to find the legal representative" for any assessed tax credit, since someone must answer for it. Normally the representative stays liable for a period even after bankruptcy; **unlinking them needs a lawyer's strategy** (the audit lawyers could propose a mechanism). [[raw/mexico_jvl_orderly_wind-down-apr-15-2025.md]]
- **Staffing after operations stop.** Once operations halt completely (no income/expenses), accounting reduces to **filing declarations "in zeros,"** doable externally — so **all direct JVL Mexico employees can be terminated**, keeping only the **lawyers** (audit) and an **external accountant** (zero filings) until the audit closes. Until the last operations end (~one–two months), the **internal bookkeeper** stays. A JVL manager (Igor — [[wiki/people/igor-donis.md|Igor Donis]] by finance context, inference) had already told Alejandro that JVL would absorb part of the registration/accounting work, leaving him mostly the tax and payroll pieces. [[raw/mexico_jvl_orderly_wind-down-apr-15-2025.md]]
- **Post-closure retainer + payment routing.** Alejandro to propose a **yearly standby retainer** (near-zero day-to-day work; audit follow-up + zero filings) and email Val a figure the same day. Val: payments will come **from the United States directly** (no Mexico office to pay him), and Alejandro should **deal with Val directly, not through anyone else**. [[raw/mexico_jvl_orderly_wind-down-apr-15-2025.md]]

*(Consistent with the earlier decision: legal fees, taxes and SAT-settlement cases were already flagged as Val's direct responsibility off the local Mexico budget. This call fills in the mechanism and timeline for that tail.)*

## May 1 2025 — restatement to Lev (current state)

On the [[wiki/meetings/2025-05-01-action-list-review-lev-rudky.md|May-1 action-list review]], Val restated the wind-down to [[wiki/people/lev-rudky.md|Lev]]: JVL will **completely stop any physical operation within 30 days**, but the company **legally continues to exist — probably until the end of the year** — filing **nil returns** with no operation. Inventory: **all machines returned except 98** that are "being picked up now," to be loaded on a truck within ~30 days and reach Canada within ~60 days, then sorted (some cannibalized, some into inventory). (Consistent with the Apr-7 30-day order and the Apr-15 operations-vs-legal-dissolution distinction; the "98 machines" refines the earlier "98 or 100.") [[raw/action_list_review_lev_rudky-may-1-2025.md]]

## Rationale

- Mexico is "throwing money out of the window"; Val had been patient but the market no longer justifies it. This follows the same Latin-America wind-down logic as Peru (already closing) — see [[wiki/people/anibal.md]] and the market update Val gave on [[wiki/meetings/2025-03-23-doug-business-update.md]] (Mexico had an unfavourable tax audit/penalty; JVL "milked the cow until it died"). [[raw/financial_alka_alignment-march-24-2025.md]] [[raw/doug_update_jvl-march-23-2025.md]]
- Colombia is **kept** — it has a strong local manager and keeps generating cash, booked as JVL Canada revenue. [[raw/doug_update_jvl-march-23-2025.md]]

## Mechanics

- **Early-close bonus:** staff keep the balance of their six months' pay as unencumbered "bonus" if they close faster (close in one month → keep five months' pay; close in five → one). This incentivises a quick wind-down. [[raw/financial_alka_alignment-march-24-2025.md]]
- Machines are being **repatriated to Canada** (two shipments gone, a third departing Friday, leaving ~100 machines); refurbished machines route onward to Puerto Rico. [[raw/financial_alka_alignment-march-24-2025.md]] [[raw/doug_update_jvl-march-23-2025.md]]
- A **local accounting firm** handles the company wrap-up, so the Mexico accountant **Erica** and a Mexico subcontractor **technician** are let go (nothing left to do). Erica gets ~six months' paid notice; Val handles that conversation himself. [[raw/financial_alka_alignment-march-24-2025.md]]
- Finance tasks are reshuffled off Erica onto Laura/Mary — see [[wiki/topics/jvl-finance-back-office.md]]. [[raw/financial_alka_alignment-march-24-2025.md]]

# Class A maths stays predetermined; Lions Riches party time ~1 in 240 at 10–60× the bet; the maths tasks must show in Jira before the meeting (Nov 13 2026)

Worked through at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-11-13-game-development-class-a-party-time-math-and-colombia-vertical-games.md|Nov 13 2026, 12:10 PM game-development meeting]] (English) between [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael Gottlieb]], [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim Podvoiski]] and [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena Zhukova]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]]. It continues [[wiki/decisions/2026-11-06-class-a-max-bet-is-3-dollars-free-spins-stay-party-time-takes-12-percent-of-rtp-at-about-1-in-240-rtp-about-92-percent-no-progressive-production-on-nova-in-january.md|Nov 6]] ($3 top bet, party time ~1 in 240, production on NOVA in January). Background: [[wiki/topics/georgia-class-a-machines.md]].

## 1. Predetermined, not random

- **The question.** Val: "Are Giorgi or Cohen math [Georgia COAM maths, reading], is it a finite math or is it a like a random math?" Efim: "Finite, you mean predetermined outcome? Yes" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]].
- **Why.** Efim: "it's easier to have it predetermined because we can do some interesting things with masks that's hard to achieve naturally." Michael: "There are specific outcomes that we want the player to experience, and it's far easier to do that with predetermined math than actual true class 3 math." Val: "So we want it to be predetermined." Efim and Michael: "Yes" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]]. Efim's example: with random maths "you can never promise players" who go to free spins at least some win, "like 5 beds [bets]"; "sometimes they just get zero… and this terminal mass [predetermined maths], we can do it" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]].
- **Does it feel worse?** Val: "Doesn't random math feel better for a player?" Michael: "No, no, not necessarily… probabilities are probabilities, right? And RTP is RTP"; he named "Blueberry" (heard) using some predetermined maths in "Shark's Lock and Devil's Lock" (heard). Whether Aristocrat uses it — nobody knew [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]].
- **The South Dakota lesson.** Michael: "we took Liberty spins to South Dakota… because we couldn't do predetermined math, we took an approach that you thought was going to work and it didn't. And the game ended up being dead last in the rankings. So we've made some substantial changes to it for rodeo spins for South Dakota." Val: "South Dakota prohibits predetermined, they only want random" — Efim "Yes", Michael "Correct"; "we converted it into random, assuming that we can succeed, but we failed" — both "Yes" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]]. See [[wiki/projects/south-dakota-vlt-market.md]] (Nov 13).
- **Class A.** Val: "But class A, you will be doing random." Efim: "No"; it "cannot be a purely random period because it's has this partial time [party time], this predicted outcome" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]].
- **The rule.** Val: "So our preference would always be predetermined math if the law permitted." Efim: "Yes" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]].
- **Dual box, not Nudge.** Michael: "because we're going to dual box for Class A, it will be superior to Nudge"; with Fiery Ice "we created a class 3 product and then had to reverse engineer it to support nudge"; "Nudge on three reel is okay… but nudge on five reel is not as compelling"; the Class A five-reel games will "look exactly like a class 3 game". Caveat: "because we don't have progressives, we're not doing Fiery Ice and Jackpot Rush for Class A yet. Someday we will." Efim: "Less artificial for sure" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]].

## 2. Party time testing — Lions Riches first

- **Where it sits.** Michael: South Dakota "D2703, Nova, all of that concept work is done. IP 1190, Georgia Class A, V43, the concept work is done"; the party-time testing "would be underneath IP 1190. It's IP 1193" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]].
- **How.** "Efim has a simulator, so we're playing the simulator and observing the behaviors of party time triggers"; it runs in autoplay and counts rounds, free-spin rounds and party-time starts. Efim: "The numbers are there, but how it actually works" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]].
- **What they test — three legs of the stool.** "does party time happen often enough? And when it does happen, does it… pay enough"; free spins must still pay well, and "making sure that we still have a base game. Because those Primero games don't have base games"; party time "takes RTP away from the base game conceivably in theory and from free spins", so "Where do we have to pull from to get the stool level?" — without one feature overpowering the rest, so players are not "just slamming waiting for party time" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]].
- **Lions Riches done.** "of the six games, one of them we believe we're in good shape… With Lion's Riches" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]]. The values, as worked out with Val: party time triggers about 1 in 240 (Michael: "240"; "For Lions Riches specifically, yeah"); "Our maximum bet is now 3 bucks"; Efim first said 60 to 200, then corrected: "Four of multiplier is 10 to 60"; Val: "Ten to 60, so you will weigh in somewhere between 30… And 180" — Efim: "Yes" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]]. Michael's template idea, given as an example of a baseline: "one in 240 with a minimum of two party time rounds when party time triggers at $10 per party time at a dollar bet, that feels good" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]].
- **Rodeo Spins next.** "a weird bird because it's a single line game with a fourth reel modifier"; "Rodeo Spins has a multiplier on Real 4 that's a 5x multiplier. So bang, you're wind capped"; so "we're going to bolt party time onto the back end" of the re-spin feature, as with free spins, "to keep the ride going" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]].
- **Not every game in depth.** Efim: "Maybe we don't need… to pay the same attention to all six games, but first three probably or four"; Michael: "because then we'll have some baseline" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]].
- **Why now.** Michael: "from a risk management standpoint… you want to front load your risk"; party time "is the most important element and aspect of what Class A is" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]].

## 3. JVL's house numbers — Val asks, Michael answers

- Val asked whether every game should have the same party-time rate; Michael: "Not necessarily, because all the games are different"; "the party time hit frequency is partially derived from the free spins hit frequency, and the free spins hit frequency varies on a game-by-game basis" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]]. Val: "still be somewhere between. Two 100 and 300"; Efim: "not radically different"; Michael: "theoretically. But we'll see" — if a game is "too much of A hammer", party time is pulled in [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]].
- Val: "I'm trying to establish in JVL's rules and what's our house rule for designing games? What's the hit frequency for… each of those parts". Michael: "it varies on a game to game basis, but I would say… one and 120 for free spins", and 1 in 240 "For party time" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]].
- The base game has no single number: "It's a wind [win] distribution curve" — what share of the base-game EV goes to winning two, three times the bet and so on. Efim showed one: for the 2-to-4 interval "14% of RTP goes into these bins"; the grey line is a game "which is well accepted by players" (name heard as "Lyashenko", **unclear**), and "I tried this party time not to go too far from this" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]]. Michael: the charts are "helpful, but I don't find them to be definitive. You have to play the game"; "the most important thing… that mountain at 10 times your bet and above, we're achieving that" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]].

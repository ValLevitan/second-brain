# The new perceived-persistence games are vertical, not dual screen: Fly Me to the Moon, a Colombian carnival game and Treasure Island (Nov 13 2026)

Settled at the end of the [[wiki/meetings/2026-11-13-game-development-class-a-party-time-math-and-colombia-vertical-games.md|Nov 13 2026, 12:10 PM game-development meeting]] (English) by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] with [[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael Gottlieb]] and [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena Zhukova]], [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim Podvoiski]] present [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]]. It follows [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-30-georgia-gets-a-v4-release-with-perceived-persistence-games-and-colombia-gets-6-to-12-class-3-games-on-nova-before-december-2027.md|Oct 30]] (a Georgia V4 with perceived-persistence games; a Colombia refresh of Class 3 games on NOVA).

## Where Colombia stands

- Val: "For class 3 in Colombia. Did you get all the answers, Michael?" Michael: "I'm still waiting for some more feedback from Martha, but I've got enough to know what I think we should do. And I have a preliminary list of games. The greatest challenge is going to be the perceived persistence. games" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]]. "Martha" is [[wiki/people/marta.md|Marta]] by the Colombia context (**inference**, as on Oct 30).
- **What the popular games look like.** Above the reels "basically it's objects. It's firecrackers. Just think 3 strands of firecrackers or three gold pots with accumulations of gems, 3 treasure chests" — no "big dominant striking image of a person or an animal"; and the most successful competitors' "art style is… very cartoony and not photorealistic". Lena: "a minimalistic cartoon style" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]]. Val: "why would I care?… You're not designing games for me"; Michael: "no surprises is good" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]].
- **How hard.** Every firecracker "needs to be animated and lit", with "zillions of these false hits"; "one of these games is… the equivalent of 6 to 8, doing 6 to 8 regular games in terms of complexity"; a competitor game had "30 pages of help screens", against "three or four pages" for a regular slot; "all the phases are going to take longer" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]]. Michael and Efim's reading of the market: "it's all just hold and re-spin… It's more complexity than innovation"; the draw is "the sense of accumulation of trying to fill the screen" — "a hell of a lot of entertainment value, not a crazy amount of pay" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]].

## The ruling — vertical

- Michael: "You are Nostradamus, Val, because you predicted a couple years ago that everything was going to go vertical. Everything has gone vertical"; "The challenge in Columbia is that you want dual screen" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]].
- Val: "No, no, no, no, no, I don't want dual screen. I don't care what screen… I don't care which screen." Michael: "Okay, then we want vertical… Then it's vertical from here on out. I thought that you had D27s that you wanted to put new software in." Val: "I mean, I have D27s, but it doesn't matter. It's very easy to convert our hardware" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]].
- Lena: "And then we can easily use the same assets for Georgia" (the rest of her line is garbled); "it's much better for this type of games to be on vertical screen." Michael: "Right, okay, it will be vertical then. It will absolutely be vertical" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]].

## The first three games (Michael)

"I'm not saying we're going to be able to do all three of these in the next go around… but I have a vision for where we're going" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]]:

1. **Fly Me to the Moon** — "based upon the Midnight Train game at Aristocrat" (earlier in the same passage he called it "that Midnight Express that I saw while I was in Georgia"); he already has a preliminary design; perceived persistence: "Yes"; "very specifically directed toward the Georgia market" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]].
2. **A Colombian carnival game** — "Three strands of firecrackers above the real bundle, cartoony graphics", "red, yellow, and green, which are the colors of the carnival"; the name will be the carnival's city ("I can't even pronounce the bar barquanilla", heard; the notes: Barranquilla); modelled on "Baozu, Baozu Festival, one of the popular Aristocrat games"; meant to be "embraced by the Colombians, but at the same time could conceivably work in Georgia as well, because this is going to be a monster"; "very specifically directed to the Columbia market" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]].
3. **Treasure Island** — "a gem game because everybody is doing this", "three treasure chests"; "a universal" theme, "because nobody else is really doing it", with "a real pretty female pirate… but she's going to look cartoony" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]].

He also showed "Spooky Link, which is basically an evolution of Mo Mummy" and "Aristocrats chili game" as examples of the style [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Math_and_Colombia_Vertical_Games-November-13-2026.md]].

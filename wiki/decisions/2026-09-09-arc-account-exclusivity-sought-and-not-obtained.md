# Val asks ARC's CEO for the account — and the exclusivity does not come

**Titled to the outcome, not to what was said in the room.** An hour after the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-09-arc-gaming-backend-displacement-negotiation.md|Sept-9 4:35 PM meeting]] with [[wiki/people/david-sloan.md|David Sloan]], [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] told his own team he had "a verbal handshake" and that **every location would have a JVL machine**. **Val corrected that on 2026-09-10: it was a matter of speech and an attempt to talk exclusivity into being; it did not work, and JVL will not have all of Sloan's business.** The correction is recorded in both derived notes, and this page carries the corrected state. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Georgia_Exclusivity_Bet_and_Sebastian_Reassignment-September-9-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

## What Val put on the table

The bundle, in its ARC-CEO composition — another of the run of compositions tracked on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-free-ports-and-pos-with-every-machine-bought.md]]: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

- **The machine is the only paid item, at $15,750.** "we came out with a with a very good machine and it's making money. We know that it's making money, so the machine is **price competitive. Fifteen, seven, fifty.**"
- **Free with it: a kiosk, a point of sale, and five more ports.** "with that, we give away a **kiosk, a point of sale, and five more ports**, which go into other machines. So we're **virtually converting the entire location to us.**"
- **Val valued the giveaway at $15,000** — the same figure he used with [[wiki/people/narasimha-rao-namburi.md|Rao]] that morning and with [[wiki/organizations/lucky-coin.md|Lucky Coin]] that afternoon: "**You're buying a machine for fifteen seven fifty, and I'm giving you fifteen thousand dollars worth of equipment.**"
- **The arithmetic of a whole location.** "**Five machines other than five ports plus our machine, so six right now. You have six on average. So we convert your entire park of machines in the location. We put our POS, we put our kiosk, and we control all the cards.**"
- **What he wants to see:** "**I would love to see one machine per location.** Co-op machine, and I would like to see my kilos at the point of sales in my port." *("kilos" is the transcript's rendering of kiosk.)*
- **No monthly fee was quoted.** The **$90 per location per month** attached to this bundle in earlier customer meetings **does not appear in this source** — neither raised nor withdrawn. Recorded as an absence.

## The exclusivity condition, stated as a hard technical requirement

Not by Val — by the JVL colleague explaining the FlexPort, as a property of the product rather than a negotiating position: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

> "It's an **AFT device**. Think of it as a slot AFT device. It fits into an **AFT window**. It connects to **SAS two port**. So we **emulate an AFT device. But we must be exclusive in the location. Otherwise, it doesn't work for us.**"

Sloan's reply is a single word — "Exclusive for what?" — and Val's answer, across the speaker boundary, is "**The card.**" Nothing further was negotiated on it in the recorded portion. This is the [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-31-transactional-equipment-only-with-location-exclusivity.md|Aug-31 location-exclusivity condition]] restated to a new counterparty, and the **first time in the corpus it is presented as a technical constraint of the FlexPort rather than as a commercial term**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

## What Sloan said back

- **He ruled the whole account out, before Val had asked for it in those words.** "I'm buying." / "**I don't think you can buy my entire business.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]
- **And he gave the reason — he already has several systems.** "But I have **M3K** in the field now as well. **Bill has X and out**, and I've got a **number of other point of sale systems out there as well.** I don't go up to the point of sale anymore, so I have different systems. **I'm not against what you're saying.**" *("M3K" and "X and out" are flagged unresolved in the derived notes and are **not** read as any known entity here.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]
- **Val's reply was to ask for a share instead of the whole:** "**How much of a business can I get?**… I'm buying. **I've made a lot of deals already. I'd like to get yours.**" No figure was reached in the recorded portion. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

## What Val told his own team an hour later, and the correction

At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-09-georgia-exclusivity-bet-and-sebastian-reassignment.md|5:31 PM debrief]], pressed by a colleague on whether JVL should hang its hat on one supplier, Val answered with the ARC relationship: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Exclusivity_Bet_and_Sebastian_Reassignment-September-9-2026.md]]

> "But **I have a verbal handshake from a CEO of Lucky Bucks. Every location will have our machine, and they're number two in the state. So we will have immediate recognition.** The CEO, by the way, is ex-Bali guy… Maybe you know him, **Dave Sloan.**"

**The correction, ruled by Val on 2026-09-10:** the "verbal handshake" was *a matter of speech* and Val's attempt to secure exclusivity — **it did not work, and JVL will not have all of Sloan's business.** What is working is the broader agenda of winning the account, and Val rates the ARC meeting **positive overall**. The spoken line overstates what was agreed and is recorded here as rhetoric, not as a commitment. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Georgia_Exclusivity_Bet_and_Sebastian_Reassignment-September-9-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

*(Two things in that quote are settled by the same ruling and one is not. **"Lucky Bucks" is the former name of [[wiki/organizations/arc-gaming.md|ARC]]** — one company, renamed — and **ARC is the state's number two operator**; both are Val's 2026-09-10 rulings. **"ex-Bali guy" remains flag-only** — the derived notes read it as *ex-Bally*, the manufacturer, and explicitly say it has **not** been ruled on. Not asserted here.)* [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Georgia_Exclusivity_Bet_and_Sebastian_Reassignment-September-9-2026.md]]

## How this sits against the standing exclusivity ruling

**This is the account-level exclusivity, not the in-location one** — the same distinction drawn on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-val-asks-lucky-coin-for-its-entire-business.md]] earlier the same day. Both were asked for on September 9; **both are now unanswered or refused**: Lucky Coin's answer is due Tuesday Sept 15, and ARC's chief executive said outright that his whole business is not purchasable. The *in-location* condition, by contrast, was stated to Sloan as a product constraint and drew no objection in the recorded portion. Dated evidence on [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-31-transactional-equipment-only-with-location-exclusivity.md]]; **nothing here resolves that page's open questions.** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]]

## The condition Val attached to proceeding at all

Twice, and it was never closed — he offered to withdraw if the kiosk vendor is already on the same ground. Detail on [[wiki/people/bill.md]]:

> "Again, **if Bill is on it and you're friends with Bill, then I shouldn't be here.** That's the way it's done. **I'm not convinced of it.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

Sloan neither confirmed nor denied it: "That he's looking at different things because I've asked" and "**I think he is working on a solution.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

# The Billy Liu mutual NDA is agreed — Clause 12 deleted, Clause 8 time-boxed

Settled in about four minutes on the evening of **September 15 2026**, at the start of the long [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-15-billy-liu-playpay-nda-and-georgia-float-economics.md|Val–Billy evening session]]. [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] had run [[wiki/people/billy-liu.md|Billy Liu]]'s draft mutual NDA through **Claude Code** before the meeting and came back with two objections. **Billy accepted both on the spot, without argument** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Mutual_NDA_Renegotiation-September-15-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Mutual_NDA_Renegotiation-September-15-2026.md]].

This is the instrument that makes the rest of the evening possible. Everything commercial that followed — the float economics, the pre-funding risk, the AFT shut-off leverage — was said under it, and Billy said so explicitly two hours later, asking Val not to repeat his dispenser idea: "**We signed an NDA. We're going to keep it. I want to do business with you**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]].

## What was agreed

### 1. Clause 12 is deleted

Val's objection, in full: "**So I put your mutual NDA into Claude Code, and Claude Code knows a lot about what I'm doing. You have Clause Twelve. It's non-conventional and non-compete, and it prohibits me. Any third party not enter in any agreement or work with any third party in the lottery, gaming, casino, or [teleady] industry, introduce or not introduce. I cannot agree with that.**"

Billy's answer: "**I agree with you… Delete clause twelve… We're not. I'm not here to tie you down. I'm here to share information with you so that you can make sense. So get rid of the clause.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Mutual_NDA_Renegotiation-September-15-2026.md]]

*(The fourth industry in that list is transcribed "**teleady**". It was **put to Val on 2026-09-16 and he does not know what it is**; the derived notes direct that it be left as transcribed and not guessed. The summary half of those same notes glosses it as "telelottery" — **that gloss is not adopted here.**)* [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Mutual_NDA_Renegotiation-September-15-2026.md]]

**Why it mattered to Val** is visible across the whole of that fortnight: he was simultaneously talking to [[wiki/organizations/incom.md|InComm]], to [[wiki/organizations/digital-cash-processing.md|Digital Cash Processing]] and to a string of Georgia operators. A clause barring him from any agreement with any third party in lottery, gaming or casino would have shut all of it.

### 2. Clause 8 — perpetual confidentiality — gets an end date

Val: "**And clause eight is out of reviews forever**" — Billy: "**You want to put a date on it? Fine**" — Val: "**I'm okay. Confidentiality within the normal period. I think it should not bother.**" **No specific period is named in the source**; "the normal period" is as far as it goes [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Mutual_NDA_Renegotiation-September-15-2026.md]].

### 3. The three-year initial term stands

"**Closed three-year, which is your initial term. I'm okay with it, and I believe it.**" *(The first word is as transcribed and is not interpreted.)* Unchanged, and accepted by Val without a counter [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Mutual_NDA_Renegotiation-September-15-2026.md]].

### 4. Effective date corrected to September 15 2026

Val edited the template live: "**I'm changing the date. I'm setting the effective date September fifteenth, and I'm changing the year from twenty four to twenty six.**" So Billy's draft carried a **2024** effective date before the meeting [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Mutual_NDA_Renegotiation-September-15-2026.md]].

### 5. The agreement is mutual, and both men checked it

Val: "**I told him, 'Is this mutual, indeed?'**" — Billy: "**If you read, there's a mutual part.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Mutual_NDA_Renegotiation-September-15-2026.md]]

## The counterparty name, and the one thing not settled cleanly

Val asked outright: "**What's the name of the company that the NDA is for?**" Billy answered with a name the recording renders "**Late payments. Late payments.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Mutual_NDA_Renegotiation-September-15-2026.md]]

That is almost certainly **[[wiki/organizations/play-payments.md|Play Payments]]** — the same recording renders his email domain "late pay" in a separate passage, and the derived notes flag that garble [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Mutual_NDA_Renegotiation-September-15-2026.md]]. **It is read that way here as an inference, not as a corrected quote.** It matters more than it looks: later the same evening Billy separated his two companies for the first time — the Georgia Lottery licence and the machine-side hardware sit in [[wiki/organizations/surfnote.md|SurfNote]], which is wholly his, while Play Payments includes his brother. **Which entity actually signed is not established by any source.**

He also gave his name for the document letter by letter: "**Bill, B-I-L, Ming, M-I-N-G. Last name Liu, L-I-U.**" **This is the first time in the corpus the surname comes from the man himself** rather than from a derived note [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Mutual_NDA_Renegotiation-September-15-2026.md]].

## How it gets signed

Val had the document in **DocuSign** and did not want to use it: "**the iPhone doesn't have the latest form. I have it in DocuSign now. Do you know how to finish DocuSign?**" Billy waved it off — "**You don't have to. Send the document via email**" and "**Send it, and I'll show you how**" — and then coached Val through filling and saving the PDF on the spot: "**You can fill out the top one**… **You can save it after you fill in those blanks. If you can save it as a PDF, then this will handle it**… **Where the company is, you change the company**… **Signature page. You could change to your PDF also**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Mutual_NDA_Renegotiation-September-15-2026.md]].

# Lucky Coin declines exclusivity — and opens a kiosk pilot instead

**Kyle Shepherd's answer to the [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-val-asks-lucky-coin-for-its-entire-business.md|Sept-9 ask for Lucky Coin's entire business]] is no to exclusivity, and no to nothing else.** He phoned [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] on **Wednesday September 16 2026 at 2:42 PM Eastern** ([[wiki/meetings/2026-09-16-lucky-coin-exclusivity-declined-and-pilot-opening.md|the six-minute call]]). The answer had been due **Tuesday September 15** [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Lucky_Coin_Exclusivity_Declined_and_Pilot_Opening-September-16-2026.md]].

> "as much as I appreciate your offer, the the exclusivity. Really, what I'm hung up on. **I just don't know that I want to put myself in a box like that. As fast as the technology is changing in this state**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Exclusivity_Declined_and_Pilot_Opening-September-16-2026.md]]

## What he offered instead

Two things, in his own sentence — and he was explicit that nothing else about the offer bothers him [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Exclusivity_Declined_and_Pilot_Opening-September-16-2026.md]]:

- **More JVL machines worked into the route.** "so we're happy to kind of work there to **try to work some more JVLs into our route**. Like, because I really do appreciate the offer that you make."
- **A kiosk trial in one or two locations.** "I'm even willing to **try your PLS, your kiosk in a location or two**, and help work." *(The transcript renders a product name as "PLS" immediately before "your kiosk"; the derived notes read the offer as a trial of the kiosk and say nothing about a second product. Left as transcribed — **unresolved garble**, not upgraded to any known JVL product name.)* [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Lucky_Coin_Exclusivity_Declined_and_Pilot_Opening-September-16-2026.md]]

**No scope, no commercial terms and no date were discussed for either.** [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Lucky_Coin_Exclusivity_Declined_and_Pilot_Opening-September-16-2026.md]]

## Why the answer took a week — and what it says about Sept 9

- **It is a family business, and the size of the offer took it out of his sole hands.** "the unfortunate reality of my situation is **we are a family business**, and some decisions, like a lot of most decisions, Val, **I can make on my own. But when somebody starts making offers, like you were making offers, I wanted to talk it over with Curtis and Barry and those guys** and kind of take some time with them before I made a call" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Exclusivity_Declined_and_Pilot_Opening-September-16-2026.md]].
- **He would have said the same thing a week earlier.** "**I probably would have given you this. Answer when we met last week**" — i.e. the Sept-9 delay bought Lucky Coin consultation time, not a change of mind [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Exclusivity_Declined_and_Pilot_Opening-September-16-2026.md]].
- **The pressure was not the problem.** "**That doesn't bother me at all. The way that we've spoken so far doesn't turn me off at all. I don't feel like you've been too aggressive or too pushy**"; and "**I feel like we have a little chemistry, so we'll keep working together and we'll make something go**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Exclusivity_Declined_and_Pilot_Opening-September-16-2026.md]].
- *(Who **Barry** is, is not established anywhere in the corpus. Kyle's Sept-9 consultation circle was **Curtis** — his brother — and **Aaron**, his technician. Barry is either a fourth person or a mis-hearing; the derived notes flag it as needing Val's confirmation before any contact record is created. **Left unresolved — no page created, no match forced** (§4).)* [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Lucky_Coin_Exclusivity_Declined_and_Pilot_Opening-September-16-2026.md]]

## Val's side: an apology, and a deliberate step back

Val accepted the refusal on the call and offered to work around the exclusivity — "**And if it's only the exclusivity that it's an issue, then I'm happy to work with you. And if something else bothers you, then yeah, bring it up and we'll work around it**" — after naming his own style as the friction [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Exclusivity_Declined_and_Pilot_Opening-September-16-2026.md]]:

> "I know that **my ask was a lot. I was aggressive. Maybe I was too aggressive and shocked you in a way. I realize it, and that's probably a force of my character, and it's at my own fault.** So I fully understand the hesitation… **I'm fast. I think fast. I, you know, I do things with shake hand commitment, and it bothers a lot of people.**"

**Then he parked the pursuit, by choice** — the second decision on this call [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Exclusivity_Declined_and_Pilot_Opening-September-16-2026.md]]:

> "**I'm not gonna pursue this business. I'm gonna leave it in your court** because I feel that it's you that needs to wrap your mind around the whole thing… **Let it sit, and you do things at your pace. I'm here for you.** I'd love to do business with you. Whenever you're ready… You know my number. Direct."

Kyle's close: "**I will let you know**… [as] **soon as we come up with all the details**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Exclusivity_Declined_and_Pilot_Opening-September-16-2026.md]].

## What this does and does not settle

- **The account is not lost.** What died is the exclusivity, not the relationship: a non-exclusive route expansion plus a kiosk pilot is live, and by Val's own choice the next move belongs to Kyle [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Lucky_Coin_Exclusivity_Declined_and_Pilot_Opening-September-16-2026.md]].
- **This is evolution, not contradiction (§11).** Sept 9 left the answer open and due Sept 15; Sept 16 supplies it. The Sept-9 page is annotated and its titular ask marked dead; nothing there is deleted.
- **The Sept-9 bundle's fate is untouched by this call.** The free per-location equipment, the locally stationed engineer and the free replacement of Lucky Coin's ten-to-twelve existing kiosks were all priced against winning the whole account. **Nothing was said on Sept 16 about what stays on the table** — recorded as an absence, an open question below [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Lucky_Coin_Exclusivity_Declined_and_Pilot_Opening-September-16-2026.md]].
- **How Val reported it internally the same afternoon.** The derived notes quote him on that day's later strategy recording — "**Kyle Shepard is back online. He called me. He's afraid of exclusivity. Everything else works**" — and use it to confirm the counterparty's identity, recorded as confirmed by Val on 2026-09-16 [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Lucky_Coin_Exclusivity_Declined_and_Pilot_Opening-September-16-2026.md]].

**Second Georgia operator in eight days to refuse account-level exclusivity.** [[wiki/organizations/arc-gaming.md|ARC Gaming]] refused it on Sept 9 — "I don't think you can buy my entire business" ([[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-arc-account-exclusivity-sought-and-not-obtained.md]]) — and Lucky Coin refuses it here for a different stated reason: not the size of the account but the **speed of technology change in the state**. *(The pairing is an observation across two pages, labelled as an **inference**; neither source mentions the other.)*

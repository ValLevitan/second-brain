# The Class A software supports two cash set-ups — bill acceptor + printer, or two QR scanners with the kiosk ticket as the e-wallet; scanners are Val's launch goal (Nov 6 2026)

Set by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] with [[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael Gottlieb]] at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-11-06-game-development-class-a-party-time-max-bet-and-qr-wallet.md|Nov 6 2026, 2:04 PM game-development meeting]] (English), with [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim Podvoiski]] and [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena Zhukova]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. It answers the question Val left on Sept 25 — can the Class A cabinet do without a printer ([[wiki/topics/georgia-class-a-machines.md]]).

## 1. What Val wants

- **No printing.** Class A has two meters — Michael: "Credits, you have credits and awards. So you put credits in the machine and you win awards" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. Val: "the competition has a bill acceptor and a printer. I'd like to avoid it… I really don't want to print the ticket every time. So I'd like to have an e-wallet for awards and e-wallet for [credits]. Same meters. But instead of a print[er]… I'd like to put money on the wallet. And then I'd like to take money from the wallet… The user interface has to be there" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]].
- **The ticket is the wallet.** Michael asked, "Is this an app on your phone?" Val: "No, it's a ticket that we print… You insert $20 into a… kiosk. and you get a $20 worth of e-wallet ticket. You come with the ticket to the machine and you scan the QR code or barcode"; the use case: "The player walks into a location, inserts $20 into a [kiosk]. Get the ticket. and then walks up to the machine and treats this ticket at all times as an electronic wallet. Imagine this ticket is just a paper carrier or plastic carrier. It doesn't matter. And all it has is a QR code or a barcode. That carries temporary value" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. Michael's worry — "in order for them to convert the awards back into credits, we're going to make them have to get up from the machine and go to a kiosk?" — is answered by the second reader: "You present the same ticket to the same barcode reader. The machine goes to zero. and the money transfer on that barcode"; Michael: "So I have to scan the QR code twice… to put the awards onto the QR code ticket, and then… to use that cash to put more credits in the machine"; Val: "Correct" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]].
- **Two readers, where the printer and the bill acceptor sit.** "I'm going to put two QR codes [readers], one on the left, one on the right. One will be a cash out QR code, another one is cash in QR code"; "Two scanners, one slightly to the left and one slightly to the right. To the left mimics a printer. But instead of printing the paper, it pays out. So you put your ticket and it scans your QR code and zeros the machine. Now… the money is on the ticket. You… present it to a QR code on the right. And that acts as though you're inserting a ticket into a bill acceptor. And that frees up the credits"; physically, "in this physical space on the left… where the printer is supposed to sit physically. I'll mount an optical reader in there. And instead of a bill acceptor in that window, I will mount a second… Optical reader" — "just like a normal casino Tito machine" in layout [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]].

## 2. The ruling — both set-ups in the software

- Val: "physically it could have two combinations, either two… Optical readers or a printer and a [bill acceptor]… So if the market will be accepting optical readers, then we'll go with optical readers. If the market will reject optical readers, we have a way back with a printer and a bill acceptor." Michael: "So the software should support both ways of doing it, is what you're saying." Val: "Yes, correct." Michael: "So that's going to be in the setup menu. You'll be able to set up whether you're choosing bill acceptor and printer or QR scanners… if you use bill acceptor ticket printer, it'll behave just like we've already designed it. And if not, you'll have this text that says, scan to add credits, scan to redeem awards. And then that last screen, Lena, will have to come up with a new screen to tell the player" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]].
- **Which ships first.** Michael: "And these will initially be deployed with the scanner first… We don't know yet." Val: "To be honest with you, that's my goal. I would like to do that… I would like to introduce a new product to the market." Michael: "the hope is to have this when we deploy, but we'll basically have two supported configurations" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. Val: "That sets these machines apart as well" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]].

## 3. The screens and wording

- **Why on-screen prompts.** Michael: "the player is trained to take a physical bill and put it into a bill acceptor. So I'm just wanting to communicate to the player, perhaps via a large overlay on the main game screen that says, scan QR code to add credits with an arrow. Pointing down to where the reader is… and then when you hit collect and it says do you wish to collect and you hit yes, it'll say scan QR code to… redeem awards with an arrow pointing over to the left" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]].
- **The words.** Val floated "scan to add or scan to redeem" and "receive awards"; Michael: "you're not receiving credits, you're adding credits… you've already received your credits when you took your cash and you put it on the QR code thing"; Val: "you're right"; final: "Scan to add credits and then scan to redeem. Awards… I hit collect. Do you really want to redeem $150 awards? I hit yes, scan to redeem awards"; "two large boxes. Scan to redeem awards, with maybe an arrow pointing to where it's located, and then scan to add credits. An arrow on the right"; Val: "Yeah, I like that" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. The confirmation itself does not change — "it simply says, would you like to collect $150 cashable awards?… the collect mechanism is just going to be through an e-wallet"; the "continue playing" message (today "print the ticket, remove the ticket, and then insert it back into the machine") needs its own phrasing — Val: "instead of remove the ticket, scan" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. Michael: "the programmers have to tell us what the behavioral characteristics are" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]].
- **The V-deck.** Val: "that provides us with an ability not to have a [V-deck]. But if you have a [V-deck], I suggest to have a supporting graphics for where the windows are" — today the V-deck shows "an arrow out for a printer and an arrow in for the [bill acceptor]"; Lena: "On real machines, we just don't show it on the Windows Build because we don't have real device"; Val: "Messages or illustration, maybe an illustration is good enough… a barcode with red… Like a strobe light going through… like in the store"; Michael: "a QR code representation"; "we can put a physical hand attached to the piece of paper, Lena… hold the ticket here to scan" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]]. Whether the Class A cabinet keeps a V-deck is still open (Oct 30).
- **Partial amounts.** Efim asked whether the whole ticket always goes to credits; Val: "we have an ability right now to put less than the whole amount, but don't forget that it slows down the transaction… it's not a great thing"; Efim: "it's good for the player, not good for the [operator]" (heard); Val: "you want the whole transaction right away. And you wanna make it fast" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Class_A_Party_Time_Max_Bet_and_QR_Wallet-November-6-2026.md]].

# The bonus step is built from templates now; LGS is to keep statistics in a database on the machine (Nov 24 2026)

Agreed at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-11-24-nova-standup-bonus-step-templates-and-lgs-statistics-database.md|Nov 24 2026 NOVA stand-up]] (Russian, 6:16 AM, 26 minutes), led by [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]]. The bonus-step ruling is [[wiki/people/anton-leshkevich.md|Anton Leshkevich]]'s and Pavel's; the LGS statistics direction came from [[wiki/people/alexander-biratka.md|Alexander Biratka]] and [[wiki/people/evgeny-kharetski.md|Evgeny Kharetski]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]]. Val was not present. Context: [[wiki/topics/nova-platform-architecture.md]] and the [[wiki/decisions/2026-11-23-nova-is-the-one-name-ip-481-is-the-root-initiative-owned-by-biratka-one-weekly-mvp-spans-hardware-system-and-studio-and-maths-and-sound-go-into-the-brief-agent.md|Nov-23 NOVA rulings]].

*(Quotes come from an unpunctuated Russian speech-to-text transcript, kept word for word; garbles are kept as heard.)*

## 1. The studio's bonus step — templates now, general steps later

- **Where the studio stands.** Leshkevich built the step that builds the base game ("колеса бэкграунд и так далее") yesterday; today he tests it and makes the review changes, then the installer, then — if time allows — auto-updates for the app [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]].
- **Biratka's question.** "Бонусная игра может быть очень разная" — reels, a select, something else — "Какогото универсального шага, наверное, у нас не получится" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]].
- **Leshkevich.** "Скорее всего, это будет какойто… Шаблон, который будет поддерживать такие достаточно общие фичи"; "Я не хочу полностью просить ai, чтобы генерировал это всё дело" — he fears it would be "Неэффективен" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]].
- **[[wiki/people/igor-metelski.md|Igor Metelski]].** They already made a bonus with another mechanic: the brief holds the bonus-screen requirements and the bonus-mechanic requirements; the bonus is made first on template scenes, then the resources are converted by the contracts. The template set grows over time. In one step: "мы сразу делаем экраны бонусы, мы сразу делаем транзишн, мы сразу делаем сообщения для этого бонуса… а потом уже пакуем. Это в сцены по контрактам" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]].
- **Biratka's general idea.** Steps like "создание ресурса" or "создаёт сцену" from a ready set of templates (base scene, select bonus, gamble, transition), joined into a step path per game type — "если мы, например, создаём какойнибудь рулетку, то у нас совсем другие шаги и другие шаблоны" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]].
- **The ruling.** Leshkevich: "Можно сделать так, но в будущем сейчас… Сейчас это будет слишком сложно, и только замедлитно". Pavel: "Давайте по принципу 80, 20"; "давайте нижние яблоки соберем. С дерева, потом будем верхнее досаживать." Igor: today the tool is special for slots, and Val's requirement is that it leads a person "по всем этапам создания" of slot games. Biratka: "Но Киева транскрипте останется, потом вернемся" ("Киева" — read as "идея", **inference**) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]].

## 2. Engine, textures and games

- **Daniil's pull request.** Green in the library; Leshkevich: "мержи Да да сам мержи". He and Biratka agreed how machine state passes between the engine and LGS (task heard as "л г с 1353") [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]].
- **Texture format.** Daniil asked which format textures are converted to for the new engine. Leshkevich: most likely WebP, he will check and write after the meeting; "У нас пока есть всего пи и п нг sapport." Biratka: WebP was chosen because it gave "самый компактный размер текстур именно в размере к качеству" and supports an alpha channel. Biratka offered a performance study; Igor: "если упримся производительность Путин думать" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]].
- **Igor's four games.** Agents spent a whole day fixing his games; he has four (heard: "твинбайсон колеочек 300 сети карнавал и сапфер стар" — the notes: Twin Bison, one **unclear**, Carnival, Sapphire Star). They come from different products with different progressives; his LGS "так работать не умеет", so he waits for the LGS fixes [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]].
- **LGS fixes and repositories.** Kharetski: the LGS fixes run, "300 плюс камитов" on top; for testing "нам нужны все" four games in repositories, each following the same contract as the first. Igor: "я запрошу репозиторий и все позаливаю" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]].
- **A real LGS in simulation mode.** Kharetski: "мы ж вчера говорили, мы вроде как решили, что нам… В нашей студии лжес не нужен. Игры будут на моках запускаться"; so he builds a container where a real LGS runs "без… Бил валидаторов… в режиме симуляции", so new game logic can be checked [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]]. Where that was decided is not in the wiki's sources.

## 3. RK packages and the installer

- **Firmware packages for RK.** Pavel is making the build output ready update packages: old machines have a firmware-update folder ("фервая апдейта"), and he is making it work for RK boards [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]].
- **RK installer build.** Pavel: built in the same repository (integration), as a separate platform. Kharetski: you choose the platform to build ("Пока там f seven 1000 и… ещё рука"); his side is "плюс минус все готово" and he needs Pavel's go; if Igor delivers the game repositories first, he can build "как мультигейм" for the current platform [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]].

## 4. Jira rename and LGS statistics

- **NOVA-2 → NOVA done.** Biratka: "большим переименованием в джиро новадва на нова… около трёхсот правок", also fixing places that still said Omni [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]]. This carries out the Nov-23 "нова и всё" ruling.
- **A statistics capability for LGS.** Biratka, after talking with Kharetski: LGS has no statistics collection now; it needs "довольно… Гибкий инструмент"; which statistics "мы, наверное, ещё обсудить должность ефимом"; "мы должны это хранить долговременно. Соответственно, нам нужна база данных", then send it to "наш хаб" for analysis. A full database in LGS means modernising it — "например, к спрингу. Или к чемуто подобному женя проведёт анализ" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]].
- **Storage becomes a buffer.** Biratka: write to storage, which is fast, then "Потихонечку сливать в базу" in one transaction; this protects against power loss rather than adding risk. Kharetski: "сторож превращается практически в буфер, Кроме какихто важных каунтов" kept across reinstalls [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]].
- **Pavel's two conditions.** Build it so an SSD can be added easily and mounted automatically (Biratka: in addition, not instead); and check it does not break GLI rules — if the hard disk dies or is replaced, "это мы, возможно, нарушаем. Правило". Kharetski: give Claude the code and the idea; Pavel: "100% да нам надо скормить этот жела и 11" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]]. Pavel also: storage must allow adding and removing games on the fly without reformatting [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]].
- **History of a removed game.** Biratka: must history be kept after a game is removed — "это вопрос под желай"; idea: an "архив" tab with history "за последний там год, например". Kharetski: screenshots in history are generated by the game, so a fully removed game cannot redraw them; Pavel: maybe no screenshots, or the cloud-screenshot mechanism if there is internet [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]].
- **Main requirement.** Biratka: "Мы хотим собирать много статистики и ложить её, отправлять её на хапну наш. Это основное требование"; freeing storage is a side technical choice [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]]. Earlier: on Nov 11 Val asked which metrics NOVA and Galaxy must collect ([[wiki/topics/nova-platform-architecture.md]]).
- **Daniil's next task.** Biratka will give Daniil the demo-mode menu ("демомодом нашим, ну то есть менюшкой") [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]].
- **Transcripts through Microsoft 365.** Kharetski: Claude with his Microsoft 365 connector "легко подключается, и у него очень большие права" and pulls a meeting's transcript when asked [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nova_Standup_Bonus_Step_Templates_and_LGS_Statistics_Database-November-24-2026.md]]. Thread: [[wiki/topics/ai-assisted-development.md]] (Nov 24).

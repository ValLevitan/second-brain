# Georgia machines go out at 92%, and the progressive tops are $2,400 / $2,400 / $1,500

Two linked settings rulings from the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-25-georgia-progressives-and-class-a-lions-riches.md|Sept 25 2026 2:32 PM game-development call]]. The RTP ruling is [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]]'s. The progressive values were brought by [[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael Gottlieb]], backed by [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim Podvoiski]], and accepted by Val. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GA_Progressives_and_Class_A_Lions_Riches-September-25-2026.md]]

## 1. The RTP — 96.5% was a mistake, 92% for now

**What happened.** Michael opened the call with it: "**Georgia Vertical 2 was deployed at 92 plus 4.5 for the progressive. So those machines were deployed at 96.5, which doesn't do your hold a whole lot of favors.**" He said a player can feel it: "**you can feel the difference between 96.5 and 92.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GA_Progressives_and_Class_A_Lions_Riches-September-25-2026.md]]

**Val's ruling.** "**I agree that we cannot be at 96.5% because we will never get to our net win. It just never. And eventually we're going to get kicked out.**" Michael: "**Should have never been there**", "**No, that was a mistake.**" Then Val: "**So, 92 percent. Should it be 93? Should it be 91? I don't know. But 92 is good enough right now. Let's put the machines out there.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GA_Progressives_and_Class_A_Lions_Riches-September-25-2026.md]]

**What is not said.** Whether the 92% includes the progressive's share, or is the base game with a progressive on top, is **not stated**. Nobody knows how 96.5% happened. Val: "**I honestly don't know how the F they got to 96.5%.**" Efim: a document "**originated somewhere**" was used (the line is garbled). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GA_Progressives_and_Class_A_Lions_Riches-September-25-2026.md]]

**The check that goes with it.** Val asked Efim to spot-check the RIS actually installed on a machine: "**send me the RAS that you actually installed yesterday on the machine, spot check it**" ("RAS" = RIS, by context). The reason: "**we are doing maximum we can for a small company with [expletive] QA right now.**" (the word is masked in the transcript) The commitment is on [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GA_Progressives_and_Class_A_Lions_Riches-September-25-2026.md]]

*Context, not a contradiction:* on Sept 10 Val told an operator that JVL sets its machines "**between ninety-two and ninety-four**" — see [[wiki/topics/slot-game-design-principles.md]] (Sept 10). That figure and the 96.5% deployment can both be true if 92% was the base and the 4.5% progressive sat on top. The sources do not say. Recorded side by side.

## 2. The progressive top awards

**The values.** Michael: "**we are going to go with 2400 top award for Match 3, 2400 top award for Fiery Ice, and 1500 top award for Jackpot Rush.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GA_Progressives_and_Class_A_Lions_Riches-September-25-2026.md]]

**Why these numbers, in Michael's words:**

- **The market has no single answer.** "**Progressives are all over the map. There is not one-size-fits-all.**" Some competitors show **$5,000** top awards; IGT runs discrete progressives per game at "**300 bucks, 400 bucks**". IGT's Jackpot-Rush-style product has a top award "**a little bit higher**" than JVL's.
- **Two of the three cannot go higher.** There had been "**a discussion, Val, about bumping up that match three**", but "**Fiery Ice and Jackpot Rush, those are fixed. Those top awards, they can't go any higher.**" So Match 3 stays close to them, so that one does not hurt the others: "**a comparatively fair horse race.**"
- **Match 3 will grow fast anyway.** "**All six games will feed into that match three.**"
- **Felipe's field reports.** Some locations ask to reset the progressive "**when it gets to 8,000 bucks**", which "**is not something they're supposed to do**"; at 8,000 it goes to an overflow bucket. (A later line says "88,000" — most likely a garble of 8,000, inference.) And "**Felipe has not had issues or complaints or any negative feedback about where our progressive levels are right now.**"
- **"Do no harm."** "**I just don't think we're in a position to go off and do anything drastic based upon instinct and gut.**"

[[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GA_Progressives_and_Class_A_Lions_Riches-September-25-2026.md]]

**Efim:** "**something in the middle, because they don't have data to support going into any specific direction… very far.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GA_Progressives_and_Class_A_Lions_Riches-September-25-2026.md]]

**Val accepted it.** "**I agree with everything… We agree that we don't have enough data. We agree that do no harm. We agree that we need multiple progressives.**" He called the competitor stories "**hearsay, it's gossip… It's not conclusive.**" And the way out: "**data is coming… for the first time… we'll be putting out machines with models**" — "a big, big, humongous breakthrough". ("models" as spoken; the notes read it as "modems". Not asserted.) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GA_Progressives_and_Class_A_Lions_Riches-September-25-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_GA_Progressives_and_Class_A_Lions_Riches-September-25-2026.md]]

*Against the Sept 10 record:* the $2,400 default JVL top progressive, set against the $6,000 an operator reported for the big makers, is on [[wiki/topics/slot-game-design-principles.md]] (Sept 10). This ruling keeps $2,400 for two of the three progressives and sets Jackpot Rush lower.

## Oct 13 2026 — the RTP as Val told it to Intralot and the Lottery

At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-13-intralot-machine-lockouts-test-lab-and-route-partnership.md|Oct-13 meeting at Intralot]] Val gave the same problem in round numbers: "**Our RTP is roughly at ninety - five percent, and competitors' RTP is between ninety and ninety - two percent. That amounts for approximately forty percent of net win.**" And: "**We'll adjust it, of course.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Intralot_Machine_Lockouts_Test_Lab_and_Route_Partnership-October-13-2026.md]]

This fits the Sept-25 ruling (move down toward 92%); it is **not** a new figure for the fleet. "Roughly ninety-five" is a spoken round number, against 96.5% deployed on Sept 25 — recorded side by side, not resolved.

## Oct 14 2026 — "by mistake… 95 percent", told to a customer

At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-14-aks-amusements-transactional-demo-two-location-test-and-financing.md|Oct-14 AKS meeting]] Val told AKS: "by mistake, in another revision of games, we set our RTP to 95 percent, where competitors were between 90 and 92 percent", so that in "seven hours a day… our game had to play almost double the amount of time" for the same net win. "We fix the RTP problem." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AKS_Amusements_Transactional_Demo_Two_Location_Test_and_Financing-October-14-2026.md]]

Like Oct 13, this is a spoken round number (95) against the 96.5% recorded on Sept 25; it is new only in calling the setting a mistake to a customer and in saying it is already fixed. Whether the fleet is now at 92% is not stated.

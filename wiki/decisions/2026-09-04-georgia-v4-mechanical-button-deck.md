# Georgia V4 — mechanical button deck, bet up/down defaulting to a dollar, dual box in place of nudge

Worked out in the second block of the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-04-naming-convention-standards-and-nova-conversion.md|Sept 4 2026 afternoon game-development meeting]], after [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]] asked whether JVL keeps the V-deck long term ("Primero has a mechanical button deck. Are we going to keep the V deck long term…?"). Val: "this provokes another conversation… many conversations, they're not easy, they're difficult conversations. But I'm glad you asked." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]]

**Scope: V4, not the current release.** Val: "I'm not asking for right now. Let's call it V4." Michael then read the package back: "Georgia will call it V4, mechanical button deck, bet up and down on the dashboard, default of $1, make the dashboard larger, and then use the dual box probably in a similar fashion that we just designed" — with the dual box "in place of nudge" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]].

## The theory behind it — Val's V-deck-as-a-piano argument

Val started from the commercial question, not the design one: "**I love VDEC. But I'm analyzing why is our machine making less money than aristocrat**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]].

- **What he observed touring Georgia locations.** "Aristocrat also has a mechanical… deck… with one slam button. An[d] aristocrat has this small scale on the main screen that increases or decreases the bet." They **default to a dollar** (Juan corrected Val's "start at a dollar" to "they default to a dollar"; Val accepted the correction) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]].
- **His hypothesis.** "when you play our machine, like it's natural to start from the left… the smallest button… And that's almost kind of… We almost like question, do you really want to go and spend $4 or $3? Like it becomes scary… we are almost counterproductive and discouraging people, like almost warning, you're entering a danger zone." Put in one image: "**our deck is a beautiful, gorgeous piano. And an aristocrat deck is no choice. Keep on slamming.**" And on the V-deck's own prominence: "our slam button, although the largest button on the market, it's still, percentage-wise, much smaller than the entire… V deck with prominently displayed dollar value saying, schmuck, you're about to bet 5 bucks" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]].
- **Val flagged it as untested, repeatedly.** "I never thought about it. I'm thinking about it for the first time. I'm making a theory out of it, but it might be total BS. I have no idea." And "these are my theories, so I don't know. Not really sure that it's true" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]].
- **Michael supplied the mechanism and the arithmetic.** "if you put 20 bucks in the machine and it's at a buck, it is just as confusing and it requires just as much effort to up your bet as it does to go down… there's a far higher probability that the player will sit there and play at a dollar, won't bet up, won't bet down. And that is a superior mathematical solution than our player who can play the piano." On the defaults: Aristocrat defaults to a dollar, JVL to the lowest — Efim supplied "25 here", Michael: "25. Okay, that's **four to one**." And the abstraction argument: "that up and that down, it's more abstract… our V deck glaringly shows you, do you want to bet five US dollars… it creates friction, it creates hesitancy, it creates second guessing." Val: "good choice of words" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]].
- **Juan's field evidence, both ways.** Some manufacturers let dollars display on buttons and let the operator toggle it: "actually seeing a dollar, seeing $5. Seeing $3, you're able to know how much you're about to spend, and you may spend less." Aristocrat's Georgia cabinet is an **unretired Class 3 cabinet called the Mars X** that always had mechanical buttons, so the choice may be opportunistic rather than researched. And per [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]], reporting from Georgia the previous week, "light and wonder sales are down a little bit because people are breaking their VDEX… true or false, I don't know" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]].
- **Michael's argument for just doing it.** "you have to believe that our aristocrat has infinite money, infinite research analysis… If they're coming into this market with a blank panel and a play button, that has to mean something." And when Efim proposed testing a partial swap on some machines in one release: "**That's a very expensive test.** I think the greater question… is what's the downside of changing it and doing it the way Val is saying? And if Aristocrat's #1 in the marketplace, I just don't see what the downside is." Efim's concession: "At least if you are making a mistake in this direction, it's mistakes that everybody will be making soon." Efim's own unanswered question stands: do we actually know Aristocrat's **average bet** is higher, rather than just their number of plays? Juan: "we only know that they're currently number one in the market, but we don't have any data to support that" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]].
- **There is also a bill-of-materials saving.** Michael asked how expensive the V-deck is against a panel with one button; Val: "**Probably 120 our cost.**" Michael: "that's a lot of money." Juan added that Class A is more price-sensitive than Class B in Georgia. Val also saw a commercial play in the reversal itself: "if we take our VDAC out and put buttons[,] then the operators may say, finally, we convinced you" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]].

## What was agreed, and what was dropped

- **Agreed for V4:** mechanical button deck (borrowing Aristocrat's design idea — Val: "let's replace the VDAC. Let's borrow the design idea from Aristocrat. I hope we're not making a mistake because I just love VDEC… but I've learned to understand that whatever I love is not necessarily good"); bet up/down on the dashboard with a **$1 default**; the **dual-box skill feature in place of the nudge arrows**; and a **14–15 game selection screen** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]].
- **The bet levels themselves do not change.** Juan: "we keep all the bets that we have today, six or seven buttons a theme. We don't change any of that. It's just that you're not directly going to the bet. You've got to go up or down to select your bet" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]].
- **Dropped: the larger dashboard.** Michael objected — "we have an entire library of games now that we've built using the existing dashboard" — and Val conceded at once: "I'm happy to forego that. Not an issue." Lena agreed with Michael. Juan had separately noted that per Felipe there were already complaints about players reaching the top nudge arrow on a V43, and enlarging the dashboard would push everything up [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]].
- **Rejected for the current Georgia release: switching the buttons from dollars to credits.** Val recommended doing it even in this release; Efim said it cannot be done because "the mass[maths] is done in… such a way that there are different denominations in some games, not one [cent] denomination." Val asked whether it was worth pulling one, two or three games out; Juan warned "what happens if they're the top two games?", Efim named **Crazy Spins** as a top game and Michael named **Rodeo Spins** as "our number one performer by a substantial margin." Michael's verdict: "I would not suggest making that change, Val. I think what you've put forth are some interesting theories… But I think that's a pretty substantial change without more data." Val: "Fair enough, fair enough" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]].
- **Parked for a few weeks before anything is built.** Michael: "there's lots of work that Lena and I have to do with both Nebraska and South Dakota right now. So it might help to just park these issues for a few weeks and let it have a little oxygen, get some further feedback… we don't have to make a decision on this this very second." Val agreed [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]].

# One JV entity, 50/50, with a special allocation of depreciation; JVL invoices the real $4,000 (Sept 30 2026)

Proposed by [[wiki/people/larry-sharon.md|Larry Sharon]] and accepted by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] on the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-30-larry-sharon-single-entity-jv-and-100-machine-pilot.md|Sept-30 8:42 AM call]]. Quotations from [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Larry_Sharon_Single_Entity_JV_and_100_Machine_Pilot-September-30-2026.md]]. It replaces the Entity A / Entity B structure of Sept 28 and the $12,000 note of Sept 29 on [[wiki/projects/transactional-equipment-depreciation-investor-deal.md]].

## What was decided

- **One entity, not two.** "We actually don't even need two entities. We just need one entity because we because in in an LLC we can we can allocate depreciation separate from separate from profits and revenue. So we really just need one entity in which… it's like a JV structure." Later: "Super simple structure, one entity, both both of us in a JV, fifty fifty. We make you know a special allocation for. For depreciation, and that's it. There's, there's nothing. Everything else is just pure, purely simple" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Larry_Sharon_Single_Entity_JV_and_100_Machine_Pilot-September-30-2026.md]].
- **The allocation can change each year.** "if the business starts working, we're not going to be the only ones that need depreciation… we'll be able to allocate the depreciation in the partnership agreement, which, by the way, we could change every year to match those figures, so that so that when we are taking depreciation, you can take advantage of it as well." He expects his side to need "a lot more depreciation than you" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Larry_Sharon_Single_Entity_JV_and_100_Machine_Pilot-September-30-2026.md]].
- **JVL invoices $4,000, not $12,000.** "you invoice us 4K instead of 12." And, when Val worried about having to book a $15,000 invoice at JVL: "Why fifteen?… I won't have an invoice for fifteen. It will never happen." Then: "I was thinking about it in terms of inflated values. But we don't have to inflate the values. We can use the real four thousand dollar value… Four thousand dollars invoice per machine. You get four thousand dollars… you book and pay taxes off… of exactly what you received" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Larry_Sharon_Single_Entity_JV_and_100_Machine_Pilot-September-30-2026.md]].
- **No note between the partners and JVL.** The $12,000 "note" of Sept 28–29 is gone; the only note contemplated is a bank's, later, repaid "from inside the company" (see [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-30-a-100-machine-trial-first-then-bell-bank-seacoast-and-possibly-incomm-finance-the-rest.md]]) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Larry_Sharon_Single_Entity_JV_and_100_Machine_Pilot-September-30-2026.md]].
- **A Florida LLC, formed once the papers are agreed.** "once we agree, I'll have Gabe open up a Florida LLC and start drawing up a partnership agreement." Val: "That's fine." If the company needs a licence, "we'll get a license for that company" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Larry_Sharon_Single_Entity_JV_and_100_Machine_Pilot-September-30-2026.md]].
- **Val's acceptance.** "Unbelievably. Smart… I would have not come up with this"; "Very smart, very clever, very simple. No complications. No gymnastics whatsoever"; on the real-value invoice, "Even better… We don't have to do. Any Of that" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Larry_Sharon_Single_Entity_JV_and_100_Machine_Pilot-September-30-2026.md]].

The derived notes record the same ruling as "One JV entity (Florida LLC), Val and Larry 50/50, with a special allocation of depreciation" and "JVL invoices the JV at the real $4,000 per machine; no inflated values" [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Larry_Sharon_Single_Entity_JV_and_100_Machine_Pilot-September-30-2026.md]].

## Why

- **Larry's own logic on the write-off.** "If you're billing me at four and I'm and I'm paying four and I'm taking four in depreciation, you have to understand my my my logic here" — either the money is written off and the business works, or "эти деньги подаюте в ИРС" (the money goes to the IRS anyway). The depreciation on the real price is enough [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Larry_Sharon_Single_Entity_JV_and_100_Machine_Pilot-September-30-2026.md]].
- **It removes the three Sept-29 problems at once.** Problem A (how the partners' half comes back from JVL) disappears because there is no debt payment to JVL to route back; Problem B (the note must be repaid within the ten-year machine life) disappears with the note; Problem C (JVL taxed on an invoice it has not been paid) disappears because JVL books "exactly what you received". *(Inference from the structure; Larry said only "Everything else is just pure, purely simple.")* The problems themselves are on [[wiki/projects/transactional-equipment-depreciation-investor-deal.md]] (Sept 29).
- **Val's "I got infected" line.** "And I got infected by. So did I." — both had been thinking in inflated values since the Sept-28 lunch [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Larry_Sharon_Single_Entity_JV_and_100_Machine_Pilot-September-30-2026.md]].

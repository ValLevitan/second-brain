# Order only limited retrofit parts until machines can be assessed

## What was decided

For the incoming used machines, JVL will order parts cautiously rather than committing to a full parts order up front. Specifically:

- Order about **50** of the 30-inch "Hammerhead" monitors now (for the Maple/Altera HH machines). [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]]
- Order roughly **100–200 CPU boxes/kits** (Val settled on about 200), since CPU kits are needed regardless. [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]]
- Order buttons and plates for **all** the Altera machines so they can be rebuilt quickly. [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]]
- Hold off on the big metal overlay/decorative parts for Maple; consider cheaper local "brushed" metal later if needed. [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]]

## Who and when

- Decided by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] with [[wiki/people/mikhail-perelman.md|Misha]] on the Jan 7, 2025 retrofit call. [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]]

## Why

- Val does not want to spend money on parts (each 30-inch monitor is ~$250 plus a controller, ~$300 total) that might be wasted if the Maple machines turn out not to be worth rebuilding. Ordering only 50 monitors carries no real worry. [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]]
- The plan is to rebuild one good machine out of several, reassess as containers arrive, and re-order only what is genuinely short. Altera HH tops (30-inch) will mostly be dropped and the machines converted to standard units. [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]]

# Website releases lead with the online-games module; ECHO uses Amazon Brand Registry, not its own site

On the Nov 6 2024 website-alignment call, [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] and [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] settled a priority dispute about the new JVL website. (As-of Nov 2024; recorded from a source staged 2025-03-29 — see the date note on [[wiki/meetings/2024-11-06-website-architecture-and-design.md]].) [[raw/business_architecture_and_the_website_design_for_online_2025-03-29.md]]

## What was decided

- **The website's first releases are the online-games module** (landing page, game collection, per-game profiles, contact forms), not the ECHO/e-commerce module. Val had earlier asked to flip the priority to ECHO/Amazon support, but conceded once two things were clear: the online module's specification was already essentially finished ("готовы для отправки на контрактором"), and ECHO's needs can be met without a website at all (next point). [[raw/business_architecture_and_the_website_design_for_online_2025-03-29.md]]
- **ECHO is presented through Amazon Brand Registry / A+ content instead of a JVL-built site** for now: Andrei argued the Brand Registry page can tell the JVL story, carry contacts and rich product content, and receive purchased ad traffic directly — "используя амазоновскую платформу и сделать такой же сайт" — cheaper and faster than building a comparable-quality landing site. Val agreed ("я был не в курсе дела, что Амазон сайт позволит нам сделать все эти нюансы... давайте так пойдем"); Andrei to send reference examples of strong Brand Registry pages. [[raw/business_architecture_and_the_website_design_for_online_2025-03-29.md]]
- **Fixed-price contract scope:** Andrei proposed contracting releases 1–3; Val trimmed it — fix-price **releases 1–2 first**, see how the contractor works, "если мне нравится, продолжим." Later releases get estimated after more detailed elaboration. [[raw/business_architecture_and_the_website_design_for_online_2025-03-29.md]]
- **Art-direction gate:** phase one delivers an art vision with up to **three rounds of feedback**; if still unsatisfactory, JVL may terminate, paying only for the art-direction work done. Val asked to pre-agree the hours cap for that stage ("чтобы мы знали точно сколько нам стоит наш развод"). [[raw/business_architecture_and_the_website_design_for_online_2025-03-29.md]]

The full release ladder and the "final frontier" self-service vision are on [[wiki/projects/jvl-corporate-website.md]].

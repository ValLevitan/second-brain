# JVL ships games and transactional equipment to Effingham, because J&J evaluates everything there

**Decided:** September 17 2026, on a nine-minute phone call ([[wiki/meetings/2026-09-17-jj-gaming-nebraska-entry-and-effingham-evaluation.md|7:59–8:08 AM ET]]) between [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]], driving, and [[wiki/people/sam-westgate.md|Sam Westgate]] of [[wiki/organizations/jj-operator.md|J&J]]. **By whom:** Sam stated the process; Val committed to it inside the same minute, without being asked to. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_J_and_J_Gaming_Nebraska_Entry_and_Effingham_Evaluation-September-17-2026.md]]

## What was decided

**Everything J&J evaluates goes to Effingham, Illinois — for every state, Nebraska included.** In Sam's words: products "get shipped to our office here… in **Effingham**", where J&J goes through them "with our CEO", and "**that was all evaluation for Nebraska or anywhere else that matters done here in Effingham**." Val could come down and demo, ship and talk by phone, "or whatever you prefer to do." *(The sentence "What we typically do when evaluating products is they get shipped to our office here" sits under **Val's** speaker label in the transcript but is plainly Sam's — a label bleed across the turn; the derived notes attribute it to Sam and so does this page.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_J_and_J_Gaming_Nebraska_Entry_and_Effingham_Evaluation-September-17-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_J_and_J_Gaming_Nebraska_Entry_and_Effingham_Evaluation-September-17-2026.md]]

**Val chose the maximal option — all three at once.** Not ship, and not visit, but ship *and* staff *and* present:

> "**Illinois. Absolutely. I will ship games to Illinois, and I will ship the transactional equipment to Illinois. I'll send my guys to set it up. They'll set it up… Fully functioning setup for Nebraska, and I'll come down. I'll come down myself for presentation. That's the only chance I'm asking for. I'm not asking for any [favors].**"

[[raw/Meeting_Transcript_J_and_J_Gaming_Nebraska_Entry_and_Effingham_Evaluation-September-17-2026.md]]

So the shape of the commitment is: **Nebraska-approved games plus the transactional equipment, shipped to Illinois, installed by JVL's own technicians as a working Nebraska configuration, with Val presenting in person.** **No date, no cost, no equipment list and no headcount are stated in either source**, and none is inferred here. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_J_and_J_Gaming_Nebraska_Entry_and_Effingham_Evaluation-September-17-2026.md]]

## Why — the rationale in the room

- **The evaluation is centralised and Val cannot route around it.** Nebraska business is decided in Illinois. Val had been trying to reach J&J "for a Nebraska business" and was pointed at Sam by [[wiki/people/sean-fellows.md|Sean/Shawn Fellows]] over dinner the previous evening. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_J_and_J_Gaming_Nebraska_Entry_and_Effingham_Evaluation-September-17-2026.md]]
- **Val opened with the weak start rather than hiding it.** "**Full disclosure: our original reiteration… [did] not show good results, but if you understand JVL's history, we will deliver a game that makes money. We need to find a good partner who can help us with the project.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_J_and_J_Gaming_Nebraska_Entry_and_Effingham_Evaluation-September-17-2026.md]]
- **The man on the phone is not the evaluator.** "**I'm not the one you need to convince. Our CTO evaluates everything**" — so a shipped, working, in-person demonstration is the only way to reach the people who decide. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_J_and_J_Gaming_Nebraska_Entry_and_Effingham_Evaluation-September-17-2026.md]]

## What Val pitched alongside it

The transactional product, argued from his own history rather than from a spec: he **owned CashCode**, **sold it in 2006 to Crane National Vendors, "CPI now. Later they bought MEI afterwards"**, and **before that sale CashCode designed "Bill to Bill", a recycling bill acceptor**. JVL's transactional product "is built around the bill recycler and an **AFT**", with point of sale optional, and "**we can operate in either eWallet or TITO.**" He closed on standards credibility: "**I sat on the board of directors for Gaming Standards Association in Las Vegas for six years.**" *(The recording renders CashCode as "Cashcoat" four times and correctly twice; the canonical spelling is used here per §4.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_J_and_J_Gaming_Nebraska_Entry_and_Effingham_Evaluation-September-17-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_J_and_J_Gaming_Nebraska_Entry_and_Effingham_Evaluation-September-17-2026.md]]

**And the Georgia variant, in answer to Sam's cash-out challenge:** "**In Georgia we have a slightly different product. We have a payment kiosk, a [point of] sale and an AFT display. It's a modification in Georgia.**" *(The transcript reads "a sale"; the derived notes gloss it to "a point of sale". The gloss is plausible from the parallel Nebraska pitch above but is **the notes' word, not the recording's**.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_J_and_J_Gaming_Nebraska_Entry_and_Effingham_Evaluation-September-17-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_J_and_J_Gaming_Nebraska_Entry_and_Effingham_Evaluation-September-17-2026.md]]

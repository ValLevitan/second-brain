# Keep "JVL Dollars" as designed — printed, serialized barcode cards, not an account credit

At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-21-post-goam-show-recap.md|Aug 21 2026 post-GAMOA recap]], [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]] said the JVL-dollars pitch confused customers at the show — "It's going to cost 950, but since you will get JVL dollars for this, you can spend it on this. It was a little bit confusing" — and proposed packaging a credit per purchase instead. [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] added that "maybe the term JVL dollars doesn't ring the bell. So maybe if we call it just a credit line, it should be easier for people to understand." [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] rejected both and defended the design in detail [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_IQ_Georgia_Recap_and_JVL_Dollars-August-21-2026.md]].

## What was decided

- **Keep the name and the model.** Val: "It's good. It's good. JVL dollars. It's good." On the naming logic: "Why JVL? Because we are called JVL. That's our brand… And why dollars? Because it's better than rubles" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_IQ_Georgia_Recap_and_JVL_Dollars-August-21-2026.md]].
- **Physical, printed, serialized, barcoded.** Val: "we give them dollars, we print it, serialize it, and we say, here's a value. Hold it in your hands. This is yours. With an expiry date." He costed the card itself at "a dollar or two to print it," with a barcode [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_IQ_Georgia_Recap_and_JVL_Dollars-August-21-2026.md]].
- **Redeemable against any JVL product, within 12 months.** Val: "They could buy whatever they want. It's physically JVL money… they need to spend it within 12 months of purchase," and elsewhere "where they could use it within 365 days. For any of the JVL products." Juan confirmed the same credit can buy Flex ports — Val: "To buy anything they want" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_IQ_Georgia_Recap_and_JVL_Dollars-August-21-2026.md]].
- **Deliberately NOT booked as an account credit.** Juan asked whether it appears as a credit on the customer's account; Val: "No, it will not. They will keep it as dollars and they will bring it to us as, I'll pay you 5000 plus here is another 3000 in JVL dollars." His accounting reason: "it doesn't show up as a liability on my books. The moment I book a credit, it shows up as a liability. This, there is no liability" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_IQ_Georgia_Recap_and_JVL_Dollars-August-21-2026.md]].
- **Verification via a simple database.** Andrei asked who verifies the dollars on redemption; Val: "Yes, of course. Of course, I'll create an easy database. Scan, scan the barcode, and that's it" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_IQ_Georgia_Recap_and_JVL_Dollars-August-21-2026.md]].

## Rationale — dollars instead of discounts, and a reason to meet a decision-maker

- **A discount is permanent; dollars are not.** Val: "if I sell ports for $1000, that's expensive, but if I give $1000 in JVL money… then it's smart. Then I'm creating good revenue and I'm driving people back to JVL. The moment I give discounts, I could never bring the price back. I'm done" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_IQ_Georgia_Recap_and_JVL_Dollars-August-21-2026.md]].
- **It is a relationship instrument, not a price instrument.** Andrei's question was whether JVL ever meets the actual payer rather than a technician. Val: "I actually think that maybe it opens a door to a CFO and somebody higher up who you say, look… your company has earned 20,000 JVL dollars. I just want to bring it by." Andrei: "It definitely creates an additional reason to meet in person." Val's framing: "It's a caring package that you want to carry to somebody. It's A handshaking procedure. It's eye to eye communication." He imagined 20 wrapped packages of $1,000 each in a JVL bag, as "beautiful platinum or black metal card… something that is heavy" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_IQ_Georgia_Recap_and_JVL_Dollars-August-21-2026.md]].
- **Borrowed, not invented.** Val: "it's not my original idea. There is actually a very successful restaurant chain in Miami" that opens sales of restaurant money "every November… They only sell it for 15 days. And they create their entire revenue for the year from this particular purchase," because buyers use the write-off and send their customers. "Not a gift card, dollars. A restaurant dollar." (The chain is not named in the source.) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_IQ_Georgia_Recap_and_JVL_Dollars-August-21-2026.md]]
- **It only works on top of a machine that earns.** Val: "Of course, if your machines don't make any money, then of course there is nothing to talk about. Nobody would want your dollars." He tied it to the release "that it's now we know for sure that it's making north of $200 per day" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_IQ_Georgia_Recap_and_JVL_Dollars-August-21-2026.md]]. (That $200 figure is disputed later the same evening — see the daily-hold contradiction on [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]].)
- **Andrei came around.** "I'm totally following you and I agree with this idea. And I'm really happy that we've talked it through because now we're on the same page. I'm quite sure that initially this idea sounded good, but now we understand why it is good" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_IQ_Georgia_Recap_and_JVL_Dollars-August-21-2026.md]].

## Not every deal gets dollars

- Val: "Not always. If the business is booming and everybody wants the product, why would I give dollars?" He floated timing it instead — "maybe I should do dollars only in November? Or maybe during the show, or maybe during introduction of a new product… a new software, a new machine" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_IQ_Georgia_Recap_and_JVL_Dollars-August-21-2026.md]].

## Use as a machine-sales lever

- Later the same evening (recording 2) Felipe proposed using JVL dollars with **Raul** at **Texas Play**, "a decision maker for a lot of companies on what to place in their locations," who personally looks after around 10 masters. Val's counter-shape was to sell **Flex ports carrying JVL cash**, so the cash pulls a machine sale: at a hypothetical $2,000 of JVL cash against a $13,000 machine, "13,000, I have 2000 in cash. Why don't I buy JVL for 11,000?" — "This way we're getting two things in the market" instead of discounting one [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Machine_Sales_Strategy_Post_GOAM-August-21-2026.md]]. Pricing angle on [[wiki/topics/jvl-machine-pricing-strategy.md]].

# A Class A deal with ARC within three months

Made in the room, unprompted, by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-31-arc-gaming-transactional-demo-class-a.md|Aug 31 2026 ARC Gaming demo]], moments after [[wiki/people/georgios-zazanis.md|George Zazanis]] confirmed that manufacturers are free to partner with operators on Class A. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

## What was decided

- **The commitment, verbatim.** Val: "Manufacturers like me are allowed to make partnerships with you." The next two turns are split across speaker labels — "George, you have." / "**A deal. Seriously.**" — and read together as Val's line, *"George, you have a deal."* Then, on the clock, unambiguously under Val's label: "**George, I will have a deal within three months. Three months. Give me three months. January. We're gonna do business.**" Zazanis: "**We're gonna kill it.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **The derived notes record it as a decision**, in Val's own framing: "Val: Class A partnership deal with ARC within three months (by January)." [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **The deal shape Val is aiming at** is the one ARC already runs with Primero and described in the same conversation: the operator supplies the cabinet, the manufacturer supplies the game, and they share revenue — **not** a machine sale. Mechanics on [[wiki/topics/georgia-class-a-machines.md]] and [[wiki/organizations/arc-gaming.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

**A dating note, recorded not reconciled.** Val said "three months" and "January" in the same breath. Three months from August 31 2026 is **end of November**; January is four to five months out. The derived notes resolve it to "~3 months (January)". Both readings are left visible; the target is not treated as precise. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

## Why now

- **The window is closing.** Val's own stated reason for speed: "**This business, you gotta act very, very quickly because the market fills up.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **The precedent he is reasoning from** is the German AWP market, where two firms took an unlimited-licence, limited-payout category nobody expected to earn and ended up with ~150,000 machines. Detail on [[wiki/topics/georgia-class-a-machines.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **The product exists.** Val drew the conclusion in the room — "That opens up for us a huge market for coffee machines" (transcribed as written; read as **countertop** machines from context — an inference) — which is the same answer JVL reached in February 2026 when Class A first surfaced as a threat: offer the countertop / Echo units as Class A machines rather than fight the category. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **The counterparty wants competition on its own floor.** ARC is explicitly non-exclusive with Primero, is tired of shipping the same six games, and told Val "More manufacturers come. It's a plus." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

## What it is not

- **It is not a signed deal, and Zazanis is not the signer.** He said so himself: "with the current situation, I think it's better to not make this agreement with me. **I'm not the guy.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **It is not underwritten by any evidence that JVL games will perform in Class A.** Asked whether they would, Zazanis: "People don't believe that it would be good. **It's fifty-fifty. If you ask, nobody knows.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **JVL has not read the regulations.** They were requested in the same meeting and had not arrived. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

## Sept 9 2026 — Val asked ARC's CEO for the handshake and did not get one

At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-09-arc-gaming-backend-displacement-negotiation.md|Sept-9 4:35 PM meeting]] Val put the Class A question to the man [[wiki/people/georgios-zazanis.md|Zazanis]] had ruled himself out in favour of — ARC's CEO [[wiki/people/david-sloan.md|David Sloan]] — and asked for the commitment directly: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

> "**If I put my energy, I will only put it if you say, 'I'll do it.' So do we have a handshake deal?**"

**He did not get an answer.** Sloan's reply was about difficulty, not commitment — "**I don't think it's that hard to engineer. I think it's just pretty**" — and Val finished the thought himself: "Pretty simple. **It's the meter**… I think we've **done much harder stuff than this.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

**So the open question below — who on ARC's side signs — is now *asked of the right person and still unanswered*, not resolved.** What the same meeting did add is that the category is under-performing for ARC by its CEO's own account ("It's not doing great"), that ARC is placing machines opportunistically, and that the licence is the real reason ARC is in it — all on [[wiki/topics/georgia-class-a-machines.md]]. **None of it withdraws or amends the three-month commitment**, which is untouched by this source. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

## Sept 10 2026 — the same January, meaning something else

Ten days after the commitment, at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-10-georgia-lottery-coam-manufacturer-introduction.md|Sept-10 meeting with the Georgia Lottery]], [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] described his Class A position to the regulator — and January had changed role. It is no longer the deadline by which a deal lands; it is the earliest point at which he will decide anything at all [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]]:

> "**I doubt very much that I can do anything until January. So it will probably hear the legislation before even doing anything.**"

> "**my intention is to bring it here before the legislation, have this conversation prior to legislation, then probably hear the legislation, understand where you stand on it, and then make a decision.**"

[[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]]

**Neither ARC, Zazanis, Sloan nor the three-month promise is mentioned anywhere in the Sept-10 source**, so nothing there withdraws or amends this commitment. But the two positions do not sit comfortably together, and this page should not be read as if they did: on Aug 31 January was a **deal date**, on Sept 10 it is a **decision date after hearing a bill**. Recorded as an evolution of Val's stated posture with the tension left visible, not reconciled. The Sept-10 position has its own page: [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-10-no-class-a-build-until-the-january-legislature-is-heard.md]].

*(Note also what the Sept-10 meeting settled in JVL's favour: the Lottery says manufacturing Class A is lawful today and would not damage the relationship — [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-10-the-lottery-tells-jvl-class-a-manufacturing-is-lawful-today.md]]. So the wait is a commercial and legislative judgement, not a regulatory block.)*

## Sept 16 2026 — a date for the hardware, a concrete offer, and still no handshake

At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-16-arc-gaming-cashless-suite-demo-and-class-a-cabinet-counter-offer.md|Sept-16 10:12 AM meeting]] Val gave ARC's CEO a **third** version of the timing — this one about the machine rather than the deal or the decision [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]]:

> "My machine is very expensive, and I need to redesign it. **I can redesign it by New Year.**… I have to redesign math. I have to redesign graphics. That's not a big deal. **But I have to redesign hardware**… **So I will do it. There's no issue.**"

- **The build is now explicitly unconditional on ARC.** "**I need it for others. It's not on your account. You're not responsible for it.**" That removes ARC as the reason for the work while leaving the promise to ARC standing [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]].
- **A concrete deal shape finally went on the table** — $2,500 per new complete cabinet plus 20%, JVL scrapping ARC's own — **and was not accepted**: "Let me just think on that." See [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-16-jvl-offers-arc-new-class-a-cabinets-at-2500-plus-20-percent.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]].
- **The open question below — who at ARC signs — is not answered.** No handshake was asked for or given on Sept 16; the next contact is **October 15 2026** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]].
- **And Val's own record of what ARC has committed to is: nothing.** Having told an internal call the same day that ARC "basically gave me an order for Class A machines," he confirmed on 2026-09-16: "**I agree with your record, however, I played it to my advantage**" [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]].

**The Aug-31 / Sept-10 strain above is neither resolved nor deepened by this.** "New Year" is a hardware-redesign date, not the deal date of Aug 31 nor the hear-the-legislation decision point of Sept 10. All three are left standing side by side.

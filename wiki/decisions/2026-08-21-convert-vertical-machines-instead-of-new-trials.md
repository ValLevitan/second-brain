# Convert under-performing verticals free instead of new-machine trials; Bulldog is the exception

At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-21-post-goam-show-recap.md|Aug 21 2026 post-GOAM recap]] (recording 2), [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]] argued that "for meaningful volume in Georgia, structured trials are still necessary," recommending disciplined 60-day trials for most new prospects. [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] pushed back hard on giving away brand-new hardware [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Machine_Sales_Strategy_Post_GOAM-August-21-2026.md]]. Sales-pipeline substance is on [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]].

## What was decided

- **Bulldog Gaming gets a 90-day trial — approved without hesitation, but as an exception.** Felipe relayed **Brian Deaton**'s ask: return the 5 machines that froze in the field in mid-October so that with a 90-day trial he "can make decisions in January, buy those five and place an order for the real quantity." Val: "the answer for Bulldog is yes, but my point is that it needs to be selective and it needs to be well thought of… But will the answer be the same for somebody else? I don't think it should be" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Machine_Sales_Strategy_Post_GOAM-August-21-2026.md]].
- **Preferred play instead of a trial: convert their existing vertical machines to the new software for free, conditioned on the modem.** Val, on the master who says JVL games make no money: "let me take the machines that you're not making money with. I will convert them. For free. So, you could see that the new software is making money. But under one condition, that I will install my cell modem in there. So this way, it doesn't cost me anything. I don't have to manufacture a new machine. The software upgrade is for free" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Machine_Sales_Strategy_Post_GOAM-August-21-2026.md]]. Modem ruling: [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-21-cell-modem-on-every-machine-from-september-1.md]].
- **Move warm and hot customers on terms and discounts, not trials.** Val: "Can, can we move with the with the warm, warm and hot? Could we move? With terms, but not a trial. That would be a better solution for us." Felipe agreed: "not all of them are trial material… most of them should be treated as non-trial material" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Machine_Sales_Strategy_Post_GOAM-August-21-2026.md]].
- **Probably do not approach cold prospects at all.** Val: "a lot of people should be left alone and in the beginning and not approached… And the question should be, should we even approach cold? Probably not, probably not" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Machine_Sales_Strategy_Post_GOAM-August-21-2026.md]].
- **Classify the pipeline by temperature and by market influence.** Val credited Felipe for "rating who is cold and who is warm and who is hot," then asked for a second axis: "can we classify them as… Market movers and shakers out of the warm and hot? Yes, for example, Bulldog is a fantastic example. They are market creators. If Bulldog is happy, people will be listening to it" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Machine_Sales_Strategy_Post_GOAM-August-21-2026.md]].
- **Add the convertibility column to the sales sheet.** Juan asked Felipe to "add a column letting us know which master does have a vertical cabinet"; Felipe agreed [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Machine_Sales_Strategy_Post_GOAM-August-21-2026.md]].

## Rationale

- **Giving things away is not selling.** Val: "It's very easy to give away things. You know, if I put an idiot, an idiot in the market… and I will say, do me a favor, call everybody and give them machines for free. He will do it." And: "you haven't sold by giving things away" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Machine_Sales_Strategy_Post_GOAM-August-21-2026.md]].
- **A conversion proves the software at zero hardware cost** and lets JVL make the earnings comparison directly: "okay, you're making X money right now. Look how much money you're making with the new software." Val called that "a constructive conversation… a conversation that appeals to my ear" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Machine_Sales_Strategy_Post_GOAM-August-21-2026.md]].
- **Conversions spread by word of mouth.** Val: "when we start converting people from one to the next to the next… they share information. They get to talk to each other. So the more that we convert… the happier they are, the more they talk" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Machine_Sales_Strategy_Post_GOAM-August-21-2026.md]].
- Val also confirmed trials were never forbidden, only unjustified: "I even told you, yes, you can go and trial things. I don't wanna see it, but you can go and do it. If you show me how you're gonna create trial into how you create sales into trial, go do it… I just don't see the necessity in it" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Machine_Sales_Strategy_Post_GOAM-August-21-2026.md]].

## Limits of the play, surfaced in the same conversation

- **Not every unhappy master has a convertible cabinet.** When Val proposed converting for "Feeling Lucky" / "feeling amusement," Felipe asked "How is the D27 going to convert to the new software?" — Val conceded: "I see. He doesn't have a vertical machine. So he should tell me that. So which ones have vertical machines that are not making money?" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Machine_Sales_Strategy_Post_GOAM-August-21-2026.md]].
- **A conversion already done did not fully land.** Felipe had already converted **Z2**'s Peach One to Diamond Link: "I told him that he's happier. He might not be the happiest." Z2 asked for a call back on **September 24** (he travels the 25th); Felipe wants to be cautious because Z2 "was difficult to collect from at some point" — though everything owed has now been collected [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Machine_Sales_Strategy_Post_GOAM-August-21-2026.md]].
- Felipe's position on trials generally: "I don't want to push trials, but it should be our last resource in negotiation after I offer X&Y" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Machine_Sales_Strategy_Post_GOAM-August-21-2026.md]].

# Slot and amusement machine parts come in under HTS 9504.30.00.60, about 20% all-in — not the vendor's steel code (Nov 24 2026)

Decided by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] on a call with [[wiki/people/mary.md|Mery Villavicencio]], Tuesday Nov 24 2026, 3:57 PM Eastern, about 39 minutes, English ([[wiki/meetings/2026-11-24-mery-hongjin-ddp-costs-and-slot-parts-tariff-code.md|stub]]); Teams title "RE: Additional information for P_ORD000000584 - DDP Costs - Hongjin Hongkong" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Mery_Hongjin_DDP_Costs_and_Slot_Parts_Tariff_Code-November-24-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Mery_Hongjin_DDP_Costs_and_Slot_Parts_Tariff_Code-November-24-2026.md]].

## The rule

- Val: "The rule will say that we import, specifically, we import parts for slot machines and amusement machines under tariff number 9504.30.00.60. Which is game parts? And the total tariffs should be approximately 20 percent. All in, tariff duties all in. Section 301, section 232, all of it." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Mery_Hongjin_DDP_Costs_and_Slot_Parts_Tariff_Code-November-24-2026.md]]
- **When it fits.** The material does not matter ("steel, plastic, or anything else, as long as it's parts accessory"); the parts must be made only or mainly for these machines — Claude's examples on screen: "cabinet frame, door, build validator bracket, or real housing" (bill validator bracket, reel housing). The tariff covers slot machines ("bell type machines") and amusement machines. Val's argument: "I cannot use it to make windows. I cannot use it to make cars… I can only use them for slot machines" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Mery_Hongjin_DDP_Costs_and_Slot_Parts_Tariff_Code-November-24-2026.md]].
- **The numbers.** Under the vendor's code 7326.90.86.88 (steel articles) from China, "For every $10… you will be paying approximately $8" — about 80%; under game parts, "approximately $2.05, 20% versus 80%" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Mery_Hongjin_DDP_Costs_and_Slot_Parts_Tariff_Code-November-24-2026.md]].
- **Checked against the schedule.** Claude's fact check, read out by Val: "I checked all three points in the official US Tariff Schedule, Chapter 99 version of November 10, 2026", broken down by Section 301 and Section 232 [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Mery_Hongjin_DDP_Costs_and_Slot_Parts_Tariff_Code-November-24-2026.md]].
- **A standard.** "I'm going to make it a rule standard right now and I will post it in GitHub." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Mery_Hongjin_DDP_Costs_and_Slot_Parts_Tariff_Code-November-24-2026.md]]

In one line: **a part made only for a slot or amusement machine is a game part (9504.30.00.60, ~20%), not steel (~80%) — whatever the vendor's paper says.**

A favor de Hongjin de 66.56" — so "debería trabajar el didipicos con Hongjin" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Accounting_Team_AI_Show_and_Tell-November-24-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Accounting_Team_AI_Show_and_Tell-November-24-2026.md]]. (The stated 66.56 does not equal the 10 USD gap between the two totals — **unclear** as heard.) **Replaced the same afternoon** by this ruling, per the notes [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Accounting_Team_AI_Show_and_Tell-November-24-2026.md]].
- **At 3:57 PM.** Mery got a freight-only quote from FedEx and used Claude for duties and taxes; Val read her figure as about 99% ("99… Almost 100"); her spoken figure is garbled [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Mery_Hongjin_DDP_Costs_and_Slot_Parts_Tariff_Code-November-24-2026.md]]. Val asked why FedEx — "it's probably the most expensive" — and Mery offered an Atlas freight quote [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Mery_Hongjin_DDP_Costs_and_Slot_Parts_Tariff_Code-November-24-2026.md]].
- **Where the code came from.** The HTS code was on the vendor's invoice, and Mery asked Claude to check it with the invoice attached. Val: "vendor can elect any code"; "Claude is programmed to agree with you"; the invoice probably says "metal parts", not "parts for a slot machine", and "metal is extremely expensive from China" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Mery_Hongjin_DDP_Costs_and_Slot_Parts_Tariff_Code-November-24-2026.md]]. His first guess was "approximately 35% duty, not 99" before he ran the check [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Mery_Hongjin_DDP_Costs_and_Slot_Parts_Tariff_Code-November-24-2026.md]].
- **The broker.** Mery offered to send it to "our customs broker, Val Mark" (name as heard); Val: "No… You have to do your own research" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Mery_Hongjin_DDP_Costs_and_Slot_Parts_Tariff_Code-November-24-2026.md]].
- **How Val got there.** About "15 minutes of investigation", with Claude, in steps: what code 7326908688 means; the duty from China; the total cost on a $10 part (about 80%); then "Is there a specific tariff number that covers parts made out of steel specifically for slot and amusement coin operated machines?"; then a demand to fact-check against the regulations [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Mery_Hongjin_DDP_Costs_and_Slot_Parts_Tariff_Code-November-24-2026.md]].

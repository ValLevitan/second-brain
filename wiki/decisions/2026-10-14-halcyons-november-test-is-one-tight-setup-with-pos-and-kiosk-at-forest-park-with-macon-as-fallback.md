# Halcyon's November test: one tight setup with POS and kiosk, Forest Park first, Macon as fallback

**Decided:** October 14 2026, over breakfast — [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-14-yash-patel-halcyon-2027-plan-forest-park-test-and-texas-profit-share.md]]. **By whom:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] and [[wiki/people/yash-patel.md|Yash Patel]] of [[wiki/organizations/halcyon-amusements.md|Halcyon Amusements]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Yash_Patel_Halcyon_2027_Plan_Forest_Park_Pilot_and_Texas_Profit_Share-October-14-2026.md]].

## What was decided

- **Yash offered test sites.** "I've got some legacy locations that I can test stuff out at if you'd like. Whether if that's software or the redemption system or whatever it may be, happy to help." Val: "I would like to… Test one setting for you. One one setup for you." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Yash_Patel_Halcyon_2027_Plan_Forest_Park_Pilot_and_Texas_Profit_Share-October-14-2026.md]]
- **November, at Forest Park.** Yash first named Macon, "closer to where my warehouse is". Val: "Can we schedule for November." Yash: "November we might be able to do Forest Park… It's south of the airport." Then: "if we want to do November, we can do it at Forest Park. Let me just call the guy, see how he feels about it." Val: "Okay, let's try for November." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Yash_Patel_Halcyon_2027_Plan_Forest_Park_Pilot_and_Texas_Profit_Share-October-14-2026.md]]
- **Macon is the fallback.** "If not, I know we can definitely do one in Macon. I've got another location coming up in Macon in January." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Yash_Patel_Halcyon_2027_Plan_Forest_Park_Pilot_and_Texas_Profit_Share-October-14-2026.md]]
- **Tight scope, with POS and kiosk.** Val: "I want to frame my testing very tightly… I don't want to go too broad right now", and "I would like to test it with the POS and the kiosk." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Yash_Patel_Halcyon_2027_Plan_Forest_Park_Pilot_and_Texas_Profit_Share-October-14-2026.md]]
- **Second SAS port.** Val said the machines need a second SAS port; Yash: "I think most of the games in there will have second SAS." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Yash_Patel_Halcyon_2027_Plan_Forest_Park_Pilot_and_Texas_Profit_Share-October-14-2026.md]]

## The site

- A gas station and convenience store, "a fairly big store itself on the inside", with a laundromat and a barber shop next door — "a lot of foot traffic. So we're able to get a lot of testing done." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Yash_Patel_Halcyon_2027_Plan_Forest_Park_Pilot_and_Texas_Profit_Share-October-14-2026.md]]
- **Not open yet.** "They already applied for the games, but they're waiting on the business license, the city business license. As soon as that comes in, the lottery will give them the print date for their COE license." Yash: "I'll have to buy a new game for that location anyway." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Yash_Patel_Halcyon_2027_Plan_Forest_Park_Pilot_and_Texas_Profit_Share-October-14-2026.md]]

## How it sits against Sept 17

On Sept 17 Val and Yash agreed **one JVL machine plus the transactional package, first week of November**, in a new location, with the state not named — [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-17-halcyon-takes-one-machine-plus-the-free-transactional-package-first-week-of-november.md]]. The Oct-14 test is also November, also one new location, and Yash again says he will need a new game for it. **Inference:** this is the same install, now with a site in Georgia. Neither source says so, and the Oct-14 recording does not mention the Sept-17 terms (free equipment, the $90 fee). Both pages stay active and are cross-linked.

# Sebastian takes the RIS MVP1 capabilities into Jira

Ruled by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] at the end of the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-28-ris-mvp1-capabilities-walkthrough-and-jira-handover.md|Sept-28 11:07 AM RIS review]], once [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]] had read out the nine capabilities ("That's what I have, Val." — "Fantastic."). All quotations from [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_RIS_MVP1_Capabilities_Walkthrough_and_Jira_Handover-September-28-2026.md]].

## What was decided

- **Into Jira, linked.** "So what we want now is the capabilities to be entered in Jira. And we want… requirements to be linked."
- **Sebastian owns it.** "Sebastian, you know how to do it, right?" — [[wiki/people/sebastian.md|Sebastian]]: "Yes, sir, I know." Val: "Okay, so it's on you. Work with Pavel directly. You're taking over from now." And: "you manage Jira. Make sure that the capabilities are in Jira, requirements are linked to the capabilities."
- **Felipe steps back.** "I want to free up Felipe for other things." "Felipe will help you in the background".
- **Pavel directs and plans.** "Pavel direct you what to do, and then on the other side, Pavel will create features and schedule resources and work." ([[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]].)
- **Irina, acceptance criteria — no autotests.** "Irina will manage acceptance criteria, and her department will create automated testing for." [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina]]: "No." Val: "Well, no, no, sorry… The automated testing will not be created on this. That's fine."

## How it sits against Sept 25

On Sept 25 Val said Felipe, as product owner, would hand over to Sebastian, "And Sebastian will then manage the product in the field", and that Pavel would assign resources and negotiate features for each capability ([[wiki/decisions/2026-09-25-felipe-acts-as-product-owner-for-the-ris-rework.md]]). Sept 28 carries this out and adds the Jira work to Sebastian's part. This is **evolution**, not a contradiction.

*(Inference, not stated: "no automated tests on this" sits beside the Sept-22 ruling that autotests go inside the existing capabilities — [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-22-autotests-go-inside-the-existing-capabilities-and-the-first-e2e-test-is-a-one-hour-micro-step.md]]. That ruling is about the transactional MVP 1; the Sept-28 source names only the RIS work. Nothing there is changed.)*

The derived notes record the hand-over and the no-autotest correction as decisions by Val, which matches the transcript [[raw/Meeting_Notes_RIS_MVP1_Capabilities_Walkthrough_and_Jira_Handover-September-28-2026.md]].

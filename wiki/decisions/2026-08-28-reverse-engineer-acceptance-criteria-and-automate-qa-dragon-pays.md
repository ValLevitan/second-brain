# Reverse-engineer requirements and acceptance criteria from the ported Dragon Pays game and try to automate its QA (one to two weeks)

Ruled by Val at the end of the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-28-dragon-pays-nova-conversion-review.md|Aug 28 2026 conversion review]], after [[wiki/people/igor-metelski.md|Igor Metelski]] demonstrated the Dragon Pays port and the group had argued about whether animation can be specified in prose at all. Val's own framing of why this matters: the port itself is now fast, so **QA is the bottleneck**, and if it is not solved the speed gained is worth nothing.

## What was decided

- **Proceed with the Rage→NOVA migration, as settled.** Val: "Все переводить, как будто мы мы решили и идем вперед" — migrate everything, as if we have decided and are going forward. This closes the debating loop that ran through the [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-03-no-en-masse-rage-to-nova-migration.md|Aug-3]] and [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-18-rage-nova-migration-experiment-review.md|Aug-18]] sessions; it does **not** change which product ships first (see [[wiki/topics/nova-platform-architecture.md]], Aug-26 section). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dragon_Pays_Nova_Conversion_Review-August-28-2026.md]]
- **Igor runs the QA experiment on the game he just ported, but role-playing as if someone else had ported it.** Val's setup: "давайте скажем, что Игорь это не ты переделывал игру, а ктото для тебя сделал, а ты работаешь к его и допарению" — pretend the game was converted by someone else and you are the QA department. The method: take the prose and the code, construct the overall architecture, come back to the capabilities, and from prose + code + capabilities write the requirements **in reverse**, "Включая акцептон критерии" (including acceptance criteria) — then try to drive testing off them automatically. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dragon_Pays_Nova_Conversion_Review-August-28-2026.md]]
- **Timebox: one week, two weeks maximum**, with a review meeting Val schedules for **Friday September 4 2026** (Irina supplied the date, Val confirmed it). Val: "давайте максимум срок 2 недели, но у меня какаято надежда… чтото вы сделаете за неделю." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dragon_Pays_Nova_Conversion_Review-August-28-2026.md]]
- **A second game is ported to NOVA in parallel, assigned to [[wiki/people/anton-leshkevich.md|Anton Leshkevich]]** (transcribed "Антон лишкевич" / "ришкевич" / "вишкевич"). Pavel proposed it — a second game is needed before the multi-game **game-select screen** can be tested at all — and noted Anton "не занят на 100%" (not fully loaded). Val: do it in parallel, and have Anton pick up Igor's work so he adds his own improvements and then shares the experience back. Val's stated by-product: "у нас тогда будет хорецкие метельские вишкевич… у нас разойдутся знания" — the knowledge then spreads across Kharetski, Metelski and Leshkevich. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dragon_Pays_Nova_Conversion_Review-August-28-2026.md]]
- **It should be a line game, not Calle Ocho.** Irina asked whether the second game should be a line game rather than "кена/кино"; Val: "я бы сейчас я бы делал бы лайн с геймс" — line games now — noting Leshkevich already did the Keno one. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dragon_Pays_Nova_Conversion_Review-August-28-2026.md]]
- **Standing rule restated: anything that becomes a standard goes into the company standards repository.** Val: "если из этого вырастают какието стандарты, то стандарты надо добавлять… Это репозиторий стандартов." His reason is the distinction between a developer learning something and **JVL** learning something — "мы говорим, что ни девелопер чемуто научился. А что j чемуто научился? Это 2 разные вещи" — and once a previous developer's standard is in the repository it becomes visible to Claude for every developer after ("Они для Клода становятся прозрачными"). He said he will himself keep a **matrix of which developer created which standards**, and that a developer who has created none is a "единоличник" (a lone operator). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dragon_Pays_Nova_Conversion_Review-August-28-2026.md]]

## The QA argument behind it

- **Val's position: the Rage→NOVA transition is the ideal moment to write the requirements and acceptance criteria properly** and keep the prose in the repository next to the code, because then Claude can generate and run the tests itself against the acceptance criteria — "при позитории занести прозу… которые понятно клоду он может… Протестировать, сделать интуинтесты" — and because the code already exists, the requirements can be recovered from it rather than invented. He noted he had already done this superficially for the RGS ("для араэс") and, reading it back, "помоему попадало правильно." He also said the theory is not his invention — "я бы от этой теории не хотел бы отходить, потому что все её толкают" (everyone is pushing it) — and that it needs testing, not more theory. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dragon_Pays_Nova_Conversion_Review-August-28-2026.md]]
- **Val on the stakes:** if JVL cannot reach that in QA, "все фишенцы, с которой мы здесь достигли, они равны нулю" — all the efficiency achieved is equal to zero, because "мы можем двигаться только… as fast" as the slowest part. Pavel supplied the word: "Боттлмек" (bottleneck). Val: "А если мы сейчас решим эту проблему? То мы можем двигаться очень быстро." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dragon_Pays_Nova_Conversion_Review-August-28-2026.md]]
- **Pavel's objection — animation cannot be specified frame-by-frame, because video playback is not deterministic.** Neither the capability, nor the requirement, nor the acceptance criteria will ever say what must be on screen pixel-by-pixel in frame 1, 2, 3. His technical reason: the code "всего лишь воспроизводит видео, причём он не гарантирует, что он отобразит вообще 2 кадр" — it merely plays a video and does not guarantee it will display even the second frame; frames are taken as fast as they can be taken and dropped otherwise. "Это не детерминированная система." [[wiki/people/evgeny-kharetski.md|Evgeny Kharetski]] added that layout may be relative rather than absolute. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dragon_Pays_Nova_Conversion_Review-August-28-2026.md]]
- **Pavel's counter-proposal (his own words, "самая грубая идея" — the crudest idea):** run the original game on a real machine, give Claude access to it, and have it compare **event by event, not frame by frame** ("не покадрово, а по крайней мере по событийно"), against what it built for the new platform. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dragon_Pays_Nova_Conversion_Review-August-28-2026.md]]
- **Where the two agreed:** Pavel — "любые поведенческие тесты можно и 100% покрыть… что там чтото произошло, есть какойто отклик… по статусам всё это можно покрывать тестами 100%" — behavioural/functional tests (something happened, there is a response, status checks) can be 100% covered. Irina supplied "функциональные." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dragon_Pays_Nova_Conversion_Review-August-28-2026.md]]
- **Val cut the theory short:** "давайте сейчас чтото очень много теорий. Давайте мы опустимся в практику… Давайте, чтобы Игорь опустился в это, и он нам скажет, что работает, что не работает." He cited what he reads on Twitter/X — people claiming 70%, 90%, some 100% automated — while allowing "понятно, что это может быть просто болтовня" (it may just be talk). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dragon_Pays_Nova_Conversion_Review-August-28-2026.md]]

# Val proposes taking 50% of Play Payments — not accepted, not refused

The last ninety minutes of a very long **September 2 2026** ([[wiki/meetings/2026-09-02-billy-liu-playpay-partnership-deep-dive.md|the evening meeting with Billy Liu]]) ended with [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] making a partnership proposal on the spot to [[wiki/people/billy-liu.md|Billy Liu]] of [[wiki/organizations/play-payments.md|Play Payments]]. **Nothing was signed and nothing was agreed**; the title states the proposal, and the status line below states where it stands [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

**Status:** `active` as a live proposal only. The 50% has been **offered, not accepted**; Billy's answer was neither yes nor no. Every term below is Val's proposal or Billy's counter-condition, not a settled agreement [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

## The proposal, in Val's words

> "**You want me to write a contract? Why don't you sleep on it? If you like it, let's meet next week. I'm back here on Tuesday. I get fifty percent of your company… I'm driving you right into the market.**"

Billy's reply was not a refusal: "**And you know what? No one was there when I was driving it myself.**" Val closed the evening on it — "**Let's do a celebration dinner next week. Sign the agreement**" — and, minutes earlier, "**Let's meet again next week. You'll think about it. But I'm after one billion dollars**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

## The arithmetic Val built it on — and why it does not hold together

He arrived with the number from that day's [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-02-incomm-machine-payments-and-recycler-standoff.md|InComm lunch]] and worked forward out loud: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]

1. **The share is half a percent, and InComm already pays him that.** "The reason I'm asking is because **I had a meeting with [InComm], and that's how much they share. Half a percent**"; Billy confirmed his own is "**Half a percent, more or less, because they're tiers.**"
2. **Half a point out of a ~3% card fee is a sixth of the take.** "**out of three percent, they will pay you half a point, which is one sixth. They'll pay you fifteen percent. Out of their hundred percent, they'll pay you fifteen percent, which is equal to half a point.**"
3. **The market is $5 billion.** "**Last year, GLC processed five billion dollars in payouts.**"
4. **Twenty percent of it is a billion.** "**If you get twenty percent of the market, you'll be conducting one billion dollars.**"
5. **And the conclusion he stated twice:** "**That's one hundred fifty million dollars**… A year."

**The derivation is internally inconsistent and is recorded, not repaired.** Steps 1–2 make the share **half a percent of transacted volume** (which on $1 billion is $5 million); step 5 applies the **fifteen percent** from step 2 to the whole $1 billion instead. Val also says "not twenty percent" in the middle of asserting twenty percent. The figures are quoted exactly as spoken; **no corrected total is asserted here**, and the derived notes likewise carry the $150M figure without showing the working [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

**Billy did not endorse it.** "**I doubt I'd be that successful**"; and, to the ambition itself, "**You're very ambitious. I like it.**" He could not answer what he earns on a billion transacted: "**I don't know the answer because I have to calculate**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

**The parallel Val drew about his own position is the reason he wants the equity:** "**My agreement is the same with [InComm]. [InComm] gives me 0.5 percent of the processing fee, but I don't have to split my 0.5 percent. Here I'm splitting my 0.5 percent with you**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

## What Val put on the table

- **The hardware, free.** "**I will give away these units for free. It's a good tactic**… these are expensive units. **We sell them for a thousand bucks.** I'm selling you a brand new machine. **This machine is sixteen thousand dollars. I'm going to give you one machine plus six of these units plus Flex IQ for free for sixteen thousand dollars. You don't pay a penny more.**" The split: "**One unit goes into my machine. Plus, we have five more units.**" This is the same giveaway offered to a named operator that morning at a stated list price of $15,750 — see [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-free-ports-and-pos-with-every-machine-bought.md]], where both figures are recorded side by side [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Connected over the second SAS port.** "**All of these units are installed and connect to SAS protocol**… We could connect into a bill acceptor. We take the bill acceptor out… **But in this case, this is not important because this will go to SAS. SAS two.**" And a financing hint dropped without detail: "**I may even rent these [units] or lease these [units].**" *("humans" is the transcript's rendering of "units" in that sentence.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]
- **Distribution speed, which he named as the only real asset either side has.** "**However, you have the magic solution. But tomorrow, everybody will have this magic solution. There is no magic in this solution. The magic is in speed. That's why I'm meeting with you today, because the magic is in speed.**" And the test he has to pass at the door: "**You have to walk into location where location says, 'Do you have it?' I say yes. You have it? I say yes. How much does it cost? Nothing. Zero. That's how it needs to happen. Otherwise, it's not going to happen.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]
- **And the legal gap PlayPay fills.** Val demonstrated JVL's own flow and pointed at the one step that does not work — see [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]] (Sept 2, evening). Reaching the cash-redemption screen he said plainly: "**So that is illegal… Right now. What is legal now, with your application**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

## The two things they disagreed about

### 1. Cash at the redemption terminal

**Val wants the terminal to pay cash for a fee.** "**I'm interested because I came to spend cash. So when you say send it to location, I want you to say, would you like to redeem it for cash? And then charge five dollars and redeem it for cash.**" His reason is not convenience but the grey market: if the store cannot earn on a legitimate redemption it will buy the card off the player at a discount and "**kind of create a homegrown economy**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

**Billy's position is that his two-step push already does this lawfully** — "**As long as there's a two-step process, as long as you just don't hand the cash over, you're not breaking the law**" — and therefore that JVL does not need a cash device at all: "**If you're dispensing cash, why do you need to dispense anything else?**"; "**Cash is what everybody wants. If this is the glorified holy grail, why else?**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]

Val's answer was about the seller's position, not the player's: "**I want to be able to walk into a location and say, 'I do this, and I do this,' and it doesn't cost you anything**… Otherwise, **the location has a choice**," and duplicating what is already there "**confuses the market and invites [the] location to create a black market**." Billy conceded the friction but not the point: "**I got it. There might be some pushback on it.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]

### 2. Who gets the front-end fees

**Val: the store owner.** "**I'll tell you, you and I should give front fees to the location**… **Give it to location because you want to disintegrate them from black market**"; restated plainly: "**the front fees, in my opinion, need to be given away to the store owner.**" His economic case for it is that a variety store nets about two percent — "**you sell a million, you make two percent**" — so a few dollars a transaction is real money to it, and the people running those stores "**have nothing to lose**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

**Billy: that is not how the market works today.** "**Except [InComm] doesn't share fees to the store**… **They share with the MLHs**" — the master licence holders, not the locations [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

**Val's own framing of where he wants to sit, and it settles which half of the stack he is buying into:** "**I should be doing the backend. This is where my money is.**" *(A long aside in this passage about which ethnic groups will and will not run a variety store is the speaker's own characterisation, is not knowledge about the business, and is not recorded.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]

## Billy's counter-condition: exclusivity — and Val would not give it

Billy attached one condition of his own, unprompted: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]

> "**You gonna think about it? It'll be an exclusive thing, right? You're not gonna offer [InComm] out of those things. You're not gonna dispense a physical card.**"

**Val's answer was not a yes:** "**But I don't know. This is a big ask. Maybe even impossible.**"

This is the single most consequential open item on the page, because JVL is committed in the opposite direction on three counts, all recorded before this meeting:

1. **[[wiki/decisions/2026-08-26-certify-only-flex-iq-and-flex-port-with-incomm.md]]** — JVL's two products are being certified *with InComm*.
2. **[[wiki/decisions/2026-08-27-flex-iq-multiple-clearinghouses-selectable-per-device.md]]** — Flex-IQ was specified precisely so an operator can pick a clearing house per device, and that capability was being sold to customers as "a choice" as recently as that same morning ([[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-free-ports-and-pos-with-every-machine-bought.md]]).
3. **The card dispenser is part of the slim unit JVL sells** — "card dispenser, printer, bill acceptor, and scanner" ([[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]], Sept 2, Lucky Coin).

And it runs head-on into the *other* exclusivity demand made of JVL the same day: at lunch [[wiki/organizations/incom.md|InComm]] told Val the partnership would struggle if he put cash functionality near COAM ([[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-hold-the-recycler-back-but-sign-no-undertaking.md]]). **Two counterparties, one day, each asking JVL to close a door to the other. Nothing here reconciles them** — the derived notes flag exactly this and recommend settling it before any contract [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

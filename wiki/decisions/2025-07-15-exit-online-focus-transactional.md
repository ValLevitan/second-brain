# Exit the online-gaming business; focus on transactional products and ECHO amusement

**Decision (Val, mid-July 2025):** JVL stops actively pursuing the **online-games / RGS** business and redirects effort to (a) **transactional products** — the products tied to the claim kiosk, ticket/coin dispensers, "topper" dispenser, and POS — and (b) the **ECHO amusement** device sold through Amazon. Val: "я принял решение от онлайн отойти, пока я не готов с этим" and directs Andrei to wind down the online spend and let go of the people time/money is going to. [[raw/Alignment with Andrei Klimovich.md]]

## Rationale

- **Can't compete online.** "Я не вижу, что мы можем эффективно конкурировать на этом рынке, и там это просто будет мешать." Reaching **$1M/year online** would require sinking in a lot of money, whereas the ECHO/amusement business Andrei runs can bring "действительно чистых … какой-то миллион долларов год." [[raw/Alignment with Andrei Klimovich.md]]
- Val sees **potential in transactional products** and is "ускоряя развитие транзакционного продуктов" (accelerating them), while ECHO-amusement Amazon sales are small but steady and "можем повторить сейлс." [[raw/alignment-planning-catch-up.md]]

## Scope / what "wind down" means

- **No new integrations.** Andrei is told **not to take on any new pep-lab / online integrations** and not to put the technical team on them; in-flight integrations already close to done **may be finished**, but nothing new is to be chased ("не надо бежать от этого, просто не надо к этому стремиться"). [[raw/Alignment with Andrei Klimovich.md]]
- **Release the online contractors.** Andrei confirms "мы уже расстались" with the online experts/people Val was paying. [[raw/Alignment with Andrei Klimovich.md]]
- The online piece is explicitly **parked, not declared dead** — Val frames it as "пока я не готов с этим" (not ready *yet*). Recorded as `active` because the direction (stop pursuing online, pour into transactional + amusement) is current; revisit if a later source revives online. [[raw/Alignment with Andrei Klimovich.md]]

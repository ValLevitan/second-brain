# Pursue the MG Oil / South Dakota route opportunity

## What was decided

JVL will meet with MG Oil to pursue the South Dakota route-machine opportunity. Val told Aaron to "make it your point to meet with them," pending an introduction from a contact named Maurice. [[raw/jira_value_stream_distribution_infrastructure_january_7_2025.md]]

## Who and when

- Decided by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] with [[wiki/people/aaron.md|Aaron]] on the Jan 7, 2025 Jira/2025-plan call. Aaron is waiting on the introductory contact from Maurice. [[raw/jira_value_stream_distribution_infrastructure_january_7_2025.md]]

## Why (the market case)

- A market recap for South Dakota shows about 3,000 old machines in the market performing about 58% worse than newer machines. MG Oil controls most of South Dakota and, if an introduction goes well, could give JVL access to about 200 locations. [[raw/jira_value_stream_distribution_infrastructure_january_7_2025.md]]
- Old machines make roughly $52–54/day versus about $100/day for a new one. Val's math: a $12,000 machine financed over 2 years is ~$6,000/year, about $16/day — easily covered by the performance gap. [[raw/jira_value_stream_distribution_infrastructure_january_7_2025.md]]
- MG Oil is well off (oil and gas stations) and, already running some old machines, should be open to the performance difference. [[raw/jira_value_stream_distribution_infrastructure_january_7_2025.md]]

## Related commitment

- Separately, Aaron confirmed he will still go out to get the machine up and running in the lab on the 16th of the month. [[raw/jira_value_stream_distribution_infrastructure_january_7_2025.md]]

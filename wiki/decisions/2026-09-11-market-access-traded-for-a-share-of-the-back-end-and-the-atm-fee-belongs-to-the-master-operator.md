# Market access for a share of the back end — and the ATM fee is the operator's

Stated by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] to an outside payment processor at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-11-digital-cash-processing-card-solution-and-cash-float-partnership.md|Sept 11 2026 11:07 AM call]] with [[wiki/organizations/digital-cash-processing.md|Digital Cash Processing / ICP Inc.]]. It is a **position, not an agreement** — the counterparty's model was the opposite and nothing was settled [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]].

## The refusal

Asked directly whether carrying their card is what they want from him — [[wiki/people/rick-beer.md|Rick Beer]]: "**To offer our card, yes**" — Val answered [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]:

> "To offer your card, yeah. **I'm not making any money on it. By making very little money on it, so I'm looking at the other side of the business. In order for me to handle somebody's card, I wanna, I wanna have part of that, of that profit, because this is where this is where the money is at.**"

And the benchmark he used, which is the sharpest statement in the corpus of why he is looking past the incumbent at all: "**InComm doesn't offer any profit sharing on the other side. So why would I do InComm.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]

## The trade he proposes instead

> "my job is to sell my hardware to operators. If I'm successful in selling my hardware, then we get into transactional business. So **my goal in speaking to people like yourselves is to be on your side of the business. In exchange, in exchange for providing you with the access to the market.**"
> [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]

- **Hardware is sold once, and that does not interest him.** "**So I'm in the business what of selling hardware once. Doesn't really attract me too much, so I need… to be able to share a profit of cash of that your side.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]
- **They do not buy or place JVL hardware — operators do.** "**you don't need to put my hardware anywhere. Masters put my hardware**… **I'm not keeping it a secret. I'm just saying that you don't need to purchase my hardware. My hardware is purchased by masters.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]
- **The closing statement of intent:** "**My goal is to derive… my profit at… the end. From managing cash.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]

## The ATM fee goes to the master operator — and Val gives it away as opinion

This is the part that surprised the counterparty, because it costs JVL money rather than earning it [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]:

> "**The ATM you will not be sharing with me. I believe that ATM money need to go to master operator. Okay. In my opinion, I'm only giving you my opinion. It doesn't mean my opinion doesn't mean much. You could buy into it or you could dismiss it.**"
>
> "**in my opinion, master will buy ATMs, place ATMs in locations just like he does with slot machines, and because he invests money into ATMs, he should be the beneficiary of the process.**"

**Their counter-position, stated plainly and not reconciled:** "**We want to offer a solution to just share revenue on the ATM two dollars side or something like that. That's all. We don't want to be a hardware guy. We just want to be a processor side, and people distribute our stuff.**" Val's reply: "**I don't understand. Why do you want to compete on pricing?**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]

## How this sits with the rest of the corpus

- **It is consistent with Sept 2 and Sept 10.** "I should be doing the backend. This is where my money is" (to [[wiki/people/billy-liu.md|Billy Liu]], Sept 2); "money is not in issuing a card; it's in managing cash" (to the Georgia Lottery, Sept 10); "my goal is to get into the card business" (to Diamond, Sept 10). Those quotations and citations are on [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]].
- **The ATM-fee-to-the-operator rule is new here**, and it is the counterpart of the "front fees to the store" instruction he gave Billy Liu on Sept 2 — give the visible fee away down the chain, keep the invisible back-end margin. Neither source mentions the other.
- **One thing sits awkwardly against the record and is NOT resolved here.** "InComm doesn't offer any profit sharing on the other side" is hard to square with what Val told Billy Liu nine days earlier — "**[InComm] gives me 0.5 percent of the processing fee**" — and with InComm's own "somewhere to start would be like fifty basis points" at the Sept-2 lunch. The two may be about different things (a processing-fee share versus a share of the float and back-end) and **the source does not say**. Both statements stand; see [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]] and [[wiki/organizations/incom.md]].

## Open questions

- **Does "profit sharing on the other side" mean something different from the half-percent processing share Val says InComm already gives him?** Asserted both ways nine days apart, by the same person, and never reconciled in any source — [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]
- **Is there a deal shape at all with a processor that wants the ATM fee?** Val's model gives that fee to the operator and takes the back end; theirs takes the ATM fee and gives away the hardware. Both stated, neither moved — [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]

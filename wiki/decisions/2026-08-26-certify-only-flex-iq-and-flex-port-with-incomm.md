# JVL certifies only two products with InComm for the next 12 months — Flex IQ and Flex Port

On the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-26-incomm-skill-pay-atm-guidance.md|Aug 26 2026 InComm follow-up]] **Jason Hall** asked JVL to state every product it intends to bring to the Georgia market "say, in the next 6 to 12 months" and to certify the UX of each one against InComm's requirements. Val answered on the spot. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]

## What was decided

- **Two products, for 12 months from that day:** "**In the foreseeable future, 12 months from today, we're seeing two products, Flex IQ and Flex Port**" — Flex IQ being "the tabletop POS", Flex Port being mounted **on the outside** of a machine (Val first said "inside", [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan]] corrected him and Val accepted the correction). Val added the escape clause "unless the road expedites, but you will be the first one to know." (Val Levitan) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]
- **The ATM is confirmed as a real JVL ambition but explicitly gated on licensing.** Val: "I'm confirming that we are working on an ATM. Let's call it ATM Redemption Service. This cannot be, I repeat, cannot be implemented. Without us being licensed as an ATM operator. Which requires a bank." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]
- **The banking track has not moved.** "We are contacting **Harris Bank** and we're in touch with them. But aside from my initial conversation, nothing happened." *(Val says "Harris Bank"; the bank JVL was registering its ATM with as of the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-12-incomm-skill-pay-recycler-strategy.md|Aug-12 call]] is [[wiki/organizations/herring-bank.md|Herring Bank]] in Texas, also an InComm suggestion. Probably the same bank, misspoken or mis-transcribed — flagged, **not** asserted; queued for the owner.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]
- **The certification mechanics:** for each product JVL sends InComm **UX screenshots and a video of the full operation**, and InComm reviews and certifies the UX. [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] owns sending them and said he can do the POS one "in a matter of day". Any later change to the POS interface follows the same loop — **video recording + a test receipt + a test card**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]

## The posture Val took

- **No law-breaking, and the reason given in scale terms.** "We are not in the business of breaking the law… because it's going to cost us probably our business and **43 year of tedious work**. So we're not going to jeopardize it over a potential market in Georgia. But yes, we are quite keen on the idea of becoming an ATM manufacturer." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]
- **Val's counter-argument to InComm** (recorded as his argument, not as a settled fact): the ATM business exists the moment an ATM-usable card is issued, because "every single location has an ATM machine"; JVL's standalone ATM "resolves the issue of **cost**, not legitimacy, not legality, because it's already there", specifically by "**Removing the aspect of a fill**." He also found it "a little bit confusing why your card can be accepted on ATM by a manufacturer XYZ. and not by a manufacturer called JVL." Jason's reply: "I don't have a good answer for you, but that's kind of the point is that there is not a good answer for that yet from the lottery or from the legislature." The constraint InComm actually imposed is on [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-26-no-cash-from-coam-redemption-device-for-skill-pay.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]
- **Jason's framing of why he asked:** "we have to be sure everything we're doing… meets our understanding of the regulatory guidance", and — bluntly — "obviously, the cash recycler was a sore spot. And we just have to be sure that we don't risk our card programs for anything that could be seen as problematic." He was otherwise complimentary: "I think you guys have some great ideas and have put together some good stuff." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]

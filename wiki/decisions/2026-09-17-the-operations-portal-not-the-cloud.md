# "The cloud" is retired as a name — the back-office is called the operations portal

A short naming ruling taken in the last three minutes of the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-17-transactional-mvp1-skillpay-multi-product-and-device-network-security.md|Sept 17 2026 8:02 AM call]] (Russian), and the reason for it is a working-tool reason, not a branding one.

## What was agreed

[[wiki/people/andrey-bosik.md|Andrey Bosik]] asked for it while handing a signal-quality display to the back-office side: "**Правда, давайте это вместо клауда както называть[,] это я не знаю, только не ц р м**" — let us call this something other than "the cloud", anything but CRM. [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] agreed in one word — "**Подходит**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Transactional_MVP1_SkillPay_Multi_Product_and_Device_Network_Security-September-17-2026.md]].

**The reason is the AI assistant.** Bosik's own explanation: "**То у меня просто клод потом… не очень хорошо понимают, о чём была речь**" — when he later feeds the material to Claude, the assistant does not understand well what was meant. The team writes its requirements with Claude ([[wiki/decisions/2026-09-15-the-week-goes-to-non-functional-requirements-and-monday-september-21-is-the-deadline.md]]), so an ambiguous in-house word costs them twice — once in the meeting and once in the tool [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Transactional_MVP1_SkillPay_Multi_Product_and_Device_Network_Security-September-17-2026.md]].

**What the thing actually is**, in Klimovich's identification: the back-office [[wiki/people/dmitri-chudinov.md|Chudinov]] had shown "in the morning", "**где мы управляем локациями, создаем продакт[ы]**" — where locations are managed and products are created. That is the same surface the multi-product decision lands on ([[wiki/decisions/2026-09-17-jvl-supports-multiple-incomm-products-not-a-fixed-pair.md]]) and the same dashboard Klimovich took requirement 1410 into ([[wiki/decisions/2026-09-17-bosiks-network-list-splits-between-mvp1-and-mvp2.md]]) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Transactional_MVP1_SkillPay_Multi_Product_and_Device_Network_Security-September-17-2026.md]].

Bosik closed it as a standing convention, not a one-off: "**Давайте это тогда везде называть… мы называем это опешн[…] повтор**" — let us call it that everywhere [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Transactional_MVP1_SkillPay_Multi_Product_and_Device_Network_Security-September-17-2026.md]].

## The name itself is recorded as the notes' reading, not as heard

The transcript garbles the chosen name — "**опешн даже повтор ещё**" — and no clean rendering of it exists anywhere on the recording. The derived notes read it as **"operations portal"** and that reading is used here and on the pages that link to this one [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Transactional_MVP1_SkillPay_Multi_Product_and_Device_Network_Security-September-17-2026.md]]. **Two parts of the ruling are certain and are not reconstructions:** the word "cloud" is dropped, and the thing is *not* to be called a CRM. The positive name is the notes' gloss of a garble.

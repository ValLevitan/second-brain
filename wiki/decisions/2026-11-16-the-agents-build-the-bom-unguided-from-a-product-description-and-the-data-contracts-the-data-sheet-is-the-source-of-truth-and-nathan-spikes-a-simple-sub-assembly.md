# The agents build the BOM unguided, from a product description and the data contracts only (Nov 16 2026)

Ruled by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] in two calls on the afternoon of Monday Nov 16 2026: first with [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi Peari]] (4:05–4:33 PM Eastern, Russian; [[wiki/meetings/2026-11-16-genadi-unguided-bom-test-and-standards.md|stub]]), then with [[wiki/people/nathan-carver.md|Nathan Carver]] (4:40–5:02 PM Eastern, English; [[wiki/meetings/2026-11-16-nathan-unguided-bom-spike-from-data-contracts.md|stub]]). Nathan agreed; Genadi agreed to the test but said he is skeptical. It is the next step on the BOM thread sequenced at [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-23-the-bom-source-of-truth-is-the-latest-design-in-github-and-sync-runs-one-way.md]].

## Why Val called Genadi

- Val's worry was the procurement and BOM integration — whether a bill of materials can be built from data contracts and put into Microsoft Business Central. He could not tell, "Ни у нейтана понять не у вас понят работает ли" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_Unguided_BOM_Test_and_Standards-November-16-2026.md]]; "Покербанк" / "кюрман" / "Купила матирую" are garbles — of procurement and bill of material by the notes' reading [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Genadi_Unguided_BOM_Test_and_Standards-November-16-2026.md]].
- Genadi: helping procurement and building the BOM are "2 вещи, они разные немножко" — procurement does not care how the BOM is made, only "лишь бы он был солит правильный и стейбл. Не менялся никогда". He urged Val to run the AI-COO "В полную мощь" now, even unfinished, to watch inventory and catch errors [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_Unguided_BOM_Test_and_Standards-November-16-2026.md]].

## The split of work

- Val agreed but said the AI-COO is in his hands: "я не прошу никакой помощи от вас в этом". He split the work in two: "Моя часть это административная часть прокюрмента. И помощь aic o. А ваша часть это?… Дейтащитз, чтобы был правильным Белов материал" ("Спящий" before it is read as specs — **inference**) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_Unguided_BOM_Test_and_Standards-November-16-2026.md]]. Genadi: "Окей, я понял, что вы всё держите под контролем" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_Unguided_BOM_Test_and_Standards-November-16-2026.md]].
- The administrative side is on [[wiki/topics/business-central-erp-implementation.md]] (Nov 16) — purchase orders issued without a G/L account, so the cost goes to expenses, not inventory [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_Unguided_BOM_Test_and_Standards-November-16-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Genadi_Unguided_BOM_Test_and_Standards-November-16-2026.md]].

## The test Val wants (with Genadi)

- **Theory, not yet tried.** "это же всё, теория правильно, а теорию я не попробовал. Я прошу, чтобы нейтон и вы попробовали эту теорию." It works well in development; he is not sure it works in hardware engineering ("в Харькове инженере" — read as hardware engineering, **inference**). He wants to check it on one small sub-assembly: can a BOM be assembled from the data contracts? "В теории похоже, что можем, но интрактес мы никогда это не делали" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_Unguided_BOM_Test_and_Standards-November-16-2026.md]].
- **What Genadi has.** "Мой агент делает безошибочно все правильно" — it sorts the data contracts by category and adds the documentation he points it to; "всё готово полностью весь билов материал по категориям порул лежит". Val checked that this is a flat parts list, not a BOM; Genadi: yes. About half the parts are in so far — "Ну не всё фифти пашент лицея" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_Unguided_BOM_Test_and_Standards-November-16-2026.md]].
- **Standards written.** In the hardware repository the categories are folders (Val: "Андрей сказал, давайте делать полдер… Мы согласились на фолдер"; "Андрей" = [[wiki/people/andrey-bosik.md|Andrey Bosik]] by the Oct-21 ruling, **inference**). There is a standard for how the folders are laid out and a separate one for how part numbers go inside them; asked whether they are written down and in the standards repository, Genadi: "Да да, абсолютно". He tells the MSBC agent to export a category's part numbers to GitHub "по стандарту", and it asks him for what is missing [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_Unguided_BOM_Test_and_Standards-November-16-2026.md]].
- **Val's steps.** Exporting from MSBC is "читинг", done only to save hand entry. The test: pull everything into GitHub and polish it to the standards; "Потом мы убираем с м с би си тот старый Белов материал… создай нам белок матери, как будто бы в MSBC его никогда не было"; then compare the two BOMs [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_Unguided_BOM_Test_and_Standards-November-16-2026.md]].
- **Who has the ball.** Genadi: part numbers work in both directions, "Как и в эту сторону, так и в ту сторону"; on the BOM itself "я не знаю, потому что я всё приготовил. Нейтан получил… сейчас у него мяч он он сказал, что он это проверит… У меня нет ответа на ваш вопрос" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_Unguided_BOM_Test_and_Standards-November-16-2026.md]].
- **Do not tell the AI how.** Val: "вы хотите сказать ai, как нужно создать Белов материал… вся моя теория заключается в том, что… не говорить ai, как это делать, а чтобы попросить air сделать пилот mandy. Из ДЕЙТА contract"; "пилот mandy" = a bill of material, **inference**. Genadi: "я скептика насчёт этого я не понимаю… по какой логике он… будет руководствоваться"; also "это идея на самом деле… тут очень много нюансов" — with a picture of the product it might start to reason, but he does not see how to give it the logic. Val: "моя теория заключается в том, что он умнее, чем я"; if he is wrong, "мы просто потратим время" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_Unguided_BOM_Test_and_Standards-November-16-2026.md]].

## What was ruled (with Nathan)

- **Nathan's first answer.** "So we have logic to do that": he is working on "the structure of the bomb in GitHub", then running it on the contracts; syncing up to MSBC is already possible [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nathan_Unguided_BOM_Spike_from_Data_Contracts-November-16-2026.md]]. Contracts must state the interfaces — the screw size and dimensions, the board's mounting standard [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nathan_Unguided_BOM_Spike_from_Data_Contracts-November-16-2026.md]]. His plan was "a procedure, a function of some kind that can… match up and align contracts", with the agent for edge-case searching only — "we shouldn't need the agent to identify compatible parts" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nathan_Unguided_BOM_Spike_from_Data_Contracts-November-16-2026.md]]. The notes record this as considered and replaced [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Nathan_Unguided_BOM_Spike_from_Data_Contracts-November-16-2026.md]].
- **Unguided.** Val: "I'm trying to avoid any sort of template. I would like to leave. The agent or group of agents free, absolutely free without any instructions how to assemble a machine. Only based on data contracts." The only guide is "a verbal description" of what the product is; "this is an ATM machine or this is a gaming machine". Nathan: "Unguided assembly of a bill. OK." Val again: "These agents have to be unguided. They cannot go there with predetermined view of Genadi"; "The only guiding principle, this is an ATM machine… You're not assembling a garbage compactor" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nathan_Unguided_BOM_Spike_from_Data_Contracts-November-16-2026.md]].
- **A group of agents with a loop.** "I need an agent that writes the spec. I need an auditor. I need an assembler", and "a looping learning process" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nathan_Unguided_BOM_Spike_from_Data_Contracts-November-16-2026.md]].
- **The data sheet is the truth.** When a build fails the agents must say why — a missing data sheet, or a wrong one the supplier sent — and chase the supplier. "the exit is the data contract, but the entry point is the data sheet"; "we only fall on, do I have the right data sheet?… that's the source of actual knowledge, the correct data sheet" — then Val can hold the supplier to it [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nathan_Unguided_BOM_Spike_from_Data_Contracts-November-16-2026.md]].
- **Pretend the product never existed.** "pretend that we don't have a bill of material in the MSBC. This product never existed". The aim is substitution and supplier work "within minutes through an agent", not Genadi in 3D design. Val's reason for confidence: "Developers don't guide agents anymore", and end-to-end testing is done that way too [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nathan_Unguided_BOM_Spike_from_Data_Contracts-November-16-2026.md]].
- **Nathan agreed.** "that answers a question that I was bouncing around with Genadi. So I understand my starting point". He had already had the agent "review the design materials and write the contracts for that part… without any" help. He expects the agent to ask clarifying questions and perhaps offer "three different alternatives"; a human then refines [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nathan_Unguided_BOM_Spike_from_Data_Contracts-November-16-2026.md]].
- **The spike.** Val: "Let's assemble A sub-assembly of any sort, whatever is easy to assemble" — so Genadi can see it. His example is a power supply: the agent knows only that it is a power supply and is given flat parts — boards, filter, receptacle — and builds the enclosure. Nathan: "spike out a workflow where we can ask the agent for something simple" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nathan_Unguided_BOM_Spike_from_Data_Contracts-November-16-2026.md]].

In one line: **the agents get only a short product description and the data contracts; no template from anyone; a failed build must point to a missing or wrong data sheet; first proof is one easy sub-assembly.**

## Update, Nov 18 2026 — the spike is done: a power-supply sub-assembly built through questions; Genadi calls it incredible; the next spike is a supplier loop

At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-11-18-nathan-bom-agents-supplier-data-loop.md|Nov-18 4:36 PM call]] (English) with Val and Genadi [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nathan_BOM_Agents_Supplier_Data_Loop-November-18-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Nathan_BOM_Agents_Supplier_Data_Loop-November-18-2026.md]]:

- **Result.** Nathan ran several simulations with "3 scoped agents… an auditor and an assembler and a spec writer" plus an agent playing a non-expert user. With no technical direction — only the design purpose, where the data contracts are, a flat parts list and the interfaces needed — the assembler built a power-supply sub-assembly, asked questions over several rounds (answers were simulated), found contracts still missing, and iterated with the auditors' findings [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nathan_BOM_Agents_Supplier_Data_Loop-November-18-2026.md]].
- **Reactions.** Val: "I'm ecstatic about… the result." Genadi: "more than half of the answers, they are in the product requirements"; "No, it's incredible" — after his Nov-16 skepticism [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Nathan_BOM_Agents_Supplier_Data_Loop-November-18-2026.md]].
- **Next.** Val ruled the next spike: no answers or hints from people; the agent finds the part's supplier, asks for the missing data, chases it, updates the data contracts and re-assembles until no human is needed; Genadi is asked only when a supplier is unknown, once — [[wiki/decisions/2026-11-18-the-next-bom-spike-is-a-self-learning-supplier-loop-the-agent-finds-the-supplier-asks-for-the-missing-data-updates-the-data-contracts-and-reassembles-with-no-human-answers.md]].

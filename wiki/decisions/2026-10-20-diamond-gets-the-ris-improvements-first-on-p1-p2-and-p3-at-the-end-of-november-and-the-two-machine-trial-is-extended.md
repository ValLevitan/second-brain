# Diamond gets the RIS improvements first on P1, P2 and P3 at the end of November; the two-machine trial is extended

Set at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-20-diamond-amusement-ris-improvements-release-preview.md|Oct 20 2026, 1:11 PM visit to Diamond Amusement]] by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] and [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda Giorgi]], with several Diamond people (one named Tara) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusement_RIS_Improvements_Release_Preview-October-20-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Diamond_Amusement_RIS_Improvements_Release_Preview-October-20-2026.md]]. The notes shown were the clean draft settled that morning — [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-20-the-ris-improvements-release-notes-are-settled-point-by-point-and-the-tasks-are-to-be-rewritten-to-match-them.md]].

*(Plaud recording; speaker labels mix people. Val is mostly "Speaker 2", Felipe mostly "Speaker 3"; Diamond lines are attributed only to "Diamond" [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Diamond_Amusement_RIS_Improvements_Release_Preview-October-20-2026.md]].)*

## Why the visit

Val: "Remember last time I was here. We had a conversation, and I told you that I that I would put everything on hold, and I will implement the changes that you wanted… These are the changes that we're going to be implementing if you approve, and **our tentative delivery date is end of November**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusement_RIS_Improvements_Release_Preview-October-20-2026.md]] This follows the [[wiki/organizations/diamond-amusement.md|Sept-10 warehouse visit]], where Diamond's technicians listed their complaints.

## What Felipe walked them through

- **Battery:** "anytime you change the battery, you will not have to do the hard reset… It won't go in critical."
- **Software upgrade:** if the cabinet keeps the same games, peripherals, bet buttons and denominations, the upgrade "will reload the previous settings"; no settings file from Felipe — "That's that's a big big help right there" (Diamond).
- **Printer:** adding or removing a printer becomes "just a switch on off in the settings menu"; Diamond can do it themselves, with no password and no new settings. Asked about swapping a bad printer for the same type, Felipe: "In theory, yes. If you have the right firmware on the new printer".
- **Lease mismatch:** Felipe had a call "Yesterday" from a Diamond tech (heard "Kowalski") whose machine's lease had expired — the machine "that I gave you a dongle to… for the upgrade". After the release the machine "will remember the previous lease… it will tell you, hey, you must do the upgrade… so it does not shut off."
- **Peripheral replacement** (printer, bill acceptor, "even a monitor"): no more QR scan at the next hard reset; it registers at the next lease scan. Val: "it's transparent to you… So that is completely gone." Felipe: "No more passport pictures and stuff."
- **Machines awaiting an upgrade:** "machine receives temporary lease renewal and displays upgrade pending… your machine will continue function." Val: "we can install any… reasonable duration of time here. Let's say you tell me, give me a thirty to sixty days, I'll upgrade it. We'll give you sixty days."
- **Critical error:** the yellow screen (Val's example: "Error 700") "will identify the cause of the error", so a tech can diagnose from a photo "So you don't have to go and come back and go again."
- **Lease renewal:** "sometimes scanning three or four or five codes is… too much. Now one scan does it all."
- **Cash-out tab:** "a dedicated tab… for cash out", so a Saturday-night cash-out is not overwritten by Monday; it is for cash-outs that are not TITO (heard "MudaGoose cash" — **unclear**); "the TITO cash outs, tab is already there."

All from [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusement_RIS_Improvements_Release_Preview-October-20-2026.md]].

## The rollout (the decision)

- **P1, P2 and P3 first, end of November.** Felipe: "we're planning on releasing the new builds with these changes for P1, 2, and 3 last week of November, maybe or yeah, the end of November… The ones that we have more off out there… and then start with all the rest." *(P1–P3 read as the Peach 1–3 builds Diamond runs — **inference**; the source says only "P1, 2, and 3".)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusement_RIS_Improvements_Release_Preview-October-20-2026.md]]
- **One-time recommission.** "it will have to be a new software, and this will require a decommission and recommission, but only this time. Moving forward, when we upgrade the software, the settings will be… Preloaded, if nothing changes." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusement_RIS_Improvements_Release_Preview-October-20-2026.md]]
- **Then everyone, and modems.** Val: "eventually in a short time, we'll roll it to everybody. And we also adding cell modems to the machines. We pay for the data… So a lot of these things will be moved to a cell modem." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusement_RIS_Improvements_Release_Preview-October-20-2026.md]]
- **The two trial machines.** Val: "I understand that we're extending the trial for the two machines, and we are changing the software for these two machines. Right." A Diamond person tied one of them to the tech's update ("He did the update already"). Val: "we personally expect between ten to thirty percent improvement in net win" from that software, "Plus we did a number of tweaks", with new games added [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusement_RIS_Improvements_Release_Preview-October-20-2026.md]].

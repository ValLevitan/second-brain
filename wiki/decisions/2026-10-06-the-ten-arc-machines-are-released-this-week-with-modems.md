# The ten ARC machines go out this week, with modems

Approved by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] when [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda]] asked, at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-06-felipe-delivery-forecast-arc-release-and-modem-notice.md|Oct-6 1:41 PM call]]. The customer is [[wiki/organizations/arc-gaming.md|ARC Gaming]].

## What was said

- **The check.** Felipe: "I talked to Georgios about the check from Arc… He said that he reached out to accounting and it should be ready for pickup next week." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Felipe_Delivery_Forecast_ARC_Release_and_Modem_Notice-October-6-2026.md]] (Georgios = [[wiki/people/georgios-zazanis.md|Georgios Zazanis]], ARC's COO, by context.)
- **The ask and the answer.** Felipe: "am I okay to release the 10 games for Arc this week?" Val: "Yes, absolutely." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Felipe_Delivery_Forecast_ARC_Release_and_Modem_Notice-October-6-2026.md]]
- **Modems.** Val: "These machines to ARC are all going out with modems." Felipe: "they're already tested. I've seen them and they all have modems, yes." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Felipe_Delivery_Forecast_ARC_Release_and_Modem_Notice-October-6-2026.md]]
- **Earlier machines.** For machines already released, Felipe: "we will upgrade them and add the modem to it", with "the latest software that was just approved on Monday… build 4333 is the new build version. So that build, all machines will be upgraded. I'm telling this to customers." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Felipe_Delivery_Forecast_ARC_Release_and_Modem_Notice-October-6-2026.md]] *(Monday = Oct 5 2026, by the calendar.)*

The notes add that the release is due this week, by Oct 9 [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Felipe_Delivery_Forecast_ARC_Release_and_Modem_Notice-October-6-2026.md]].

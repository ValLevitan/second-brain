# Hold the cash recycler back from anything InComm-associated — but sign no undertaking not to build it

The confrontation half of the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-02-incomm-machine-payments-and-recycler-standoff.md|Sept-2 2026 InComm lunch]], and the hardest thing said to JVL about its Georgia product plans in the corpus. It is the **evolution** of the [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-26-no-cash-from-coam-redemption-device-for-skill-pay.md|Aug-26 no-cash ruling]] — same red line, moved further out — plus a new dispute about putting anything in writing [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].

## What InComm said, and how far it has moved since Aug 26

- **The line is now the whole partnership, not just one device.** "we believe… it would be **unwise at this point to push ATM functionality closer to [COAM]**"; and if JVL proceeds, "that's your product line. You know, if that's what you really believe in and want to do, and that's fine. **We would just struggle to see our partnership**… because we want to keep those issues separate" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].
- **The fallback Val offered on Aug 26 — two separate boxes — was tested again and rejected again, at ten paces.** Val: "I don't have to issue your card from my ATM… I could have a **station that dispenses your card. A separate station**… that will be **ten steps away** from this ATM." The answer: "**We don't believe that would be received very well in the current climate.**" *(On Aug 26 the same fallback was refused on a **same-entity** test — "if it's operated by the same entity. JVL. We're not going to be okay with that." Sept 2 refuses it on **climate**, without the entity qualifier. That is a widening, not a contradiction; both are recorded.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]]
- **What InComm says it is actually objecting to.** Not the ATM transaction — "If you're certified as an ATM, it's going to process the transaction no matter what" — but the pairing: "**What we're taking issue with, what we're highlighting as a potential target on our back, is if that same ATM is actually issuing our product out of the ATM. No other ATM on the market issues** [it]. And that's what we're talking about: **separating it from the COAM space**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].
- **The stated motive is risk, not competition.** "We don't want to risk InComm's license for this program, which is **a small part of InComm's business**"; and explicitly, "**I don't want you to work with my competitors. I'm just saying we can't do that in association with our products**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].
- **The risk named is press, not enforcement.** "technically, I think it's **brilliant**, right? And I think it solves a lot of problems… **If we did this today, though, I think we would attract a lot of media. Very quickly. So that's the risk that I just can't** [take]… It's **more of a risk assessment** than saying yes or no" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].
- **It is expressly conditional and expressly temporary.** "**If the lottery were to say something to clarify that point and say no, there's no problem here**… I would say now **we should see what Joe [Kim] respond[s] in writing**, because that will likely trigger [it]." And on the horizon: "**Maybe three years from now, it's different**… I think things will change. This year, no" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Why the climate hardened, in InComm's account.** "there's been **significant interaction between [InComm] and the lottery, [the] Governor's office, the Attorney General's office**, and I think maybe where the market was two, three, four years ago has really **zeroed in on where the battle lines are**"; "the regulators are a lot more tuned in… and there's been **written record** now." The trigger at the show, and the two things InComm believes [[wiki/people/joe-kim.md|Joe Kim]] said in his speech that afternoon, are on his page [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].
- **InComm is not the regulator and said so.** "we're not regulating this. The lottery's there"; "Maybe you're right, and I'm wrong, and this is totally fine, and it'll work… **I'm not the regulator either**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].

## What Val decided

- **He accepted the constraint without conceding the argument.** "So, **if you are telling me… we're not going to allow you to do it, then it leaves me with no choice but not to do it.** What can I do? I can't do it" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].
- **He restated the intent behind the product, twice, on the record.** "please keep in mind **this is not about politics, and this is not about beating the law. This is about me capitalizing on technology, and my goal is to sell technology. My goal is not to beat the legislation.**" And: "I don't think about legislation. I don't think about politics. I don't think about the governor… **There is no other intent**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].
- **He did not accept the asymmetry.** "I find it difficult to understand. You allowed for your ATMs, you can do it, but **these ATMs cannot do it**" — the same objection he made on Aug 26 about manufacturer XYZ versus JVL, still unanswered [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].
- **He stated his grievance plainly, and it is a concrete factual claim.** Two years ago, one-on-one with **John**, InComm validated and pushed the investment *(the transcript says only "John"; read as [[wiki/people/john-houseal.md|John Houseal]], the InComm lead Val dealt with through 2025 — an inference from role, not stated here)*: "it was John. With me, **face to face, excited. That's a great product. Go for it**… He said, 'Mastercard. If not, I have a second route with Visa.'" The consequence: "**you made me spend a lot of money, a lot of money, on building something that you're now saying that it's not going to go**… I spent it on a one-on-one meeting, where you [validated] the whole business model with me and **sanctioned it**." The apology offered: "on behalf [of InComm], apologise for whatever was said and now isn't being said, but I think that's **the nature of Georgia's regulatory evolution**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].
- **He closed conciliatory and said why.** "I think the great idea between us is to love each other. **I am more of a lover than a fighter**… I think we should **work towards the business instead of shutting down the business**." And the calculation underneath it, as spoken: "when push comes to shove, **for every regulator in the state of Georgia doesn't want me to stink up their honey. So the best for me is to make money**… **Nobody wants me to be loud and ugly, which I have no intention** [of being]" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].

## The letter — refused, and replaced with a question

InComm's wrap-up attached a written condition to JVL's other products: "**if you write me a note and say you're not complying with recycling associated with the [Skill Pay] card until further notice**, we'll get the other kiosk and the POS up and running" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].

**Val refused, and drew a distinction he repeated several times** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]]:

- He objected to the condition itself first: "I didn't realize that **my condition of activating my business was based on the note that I need to write**… Wait, is this a new requirement now?"
- The distinction: "**I have not submitted any of the features that are questionable to you**… So **you can only question features that I submitted**, unless I'm lying to you, which I'm not"; and "the scope of your legal engagement with me is to ensure that **the screens that I presented are genuine screens and they do the function that I'm saying** that they will."
- Applied to the two different asks: "'Can you validate these are not associated?' — **Yeah, you should ask that question. I will tell you no**… But now you're asking me, 'Please say that you will not be doing this.' **I won't say that.**"
- And the warning attached: "when you ask your questions, **consult your legal counsel because I have one in house too**."

**The compromise landed the other way round: InComm writes the question.** "I've asked for the… screenshots and everything, and what I am going to do is **reply back to that and say we confirm the features that we saw on the floor of the show are not associated with this solution**." Val: "**that would be a fair question for you to ask** instead of me writing a note." InComm's reason for needing anything at all: "**some of this is for my internal facing explanation. It's not just me and Adam. It's our general counsel and our legal regulatory engagement**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].

Val's parting note on how he expects to answer it: "So I'm seeing screens here. Do I understand correctly that **you cannot combine it with [an] ATM function in one thing**… And I will say, yeah, correct, absolutely. **But you know, for every lawyer, there's another lawyer. For every argument, there's another argument**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].

## How this sits against the Aug-26 pair

- **[[wiki/decisions/2026-08-26-no-cash-from-coam-redemption-device-for-skill-pay.md]] — evolution, not contradiction.** Aug 26 said no cash out of a redemption device and no coordinated kiosk+ATM pair from the same entity. Sept 2 keeps that and extends it to a **separate device ten steps away**, and raises the stated penalty from "we'd stop offering our products" to "we would struggle to see our partnership." The Aug-26 page remains `active` and is annotated with this update; its titular claim still holds [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].
- **[[wiki/decisions/2026-08-31-transactional-equipment-only-with-location-exclusivity.md]] — a new regulatory cost appears.** When Val described removing MudaGoose, rewiring the location and shutting the terminal off for non-payment, the reply was: "**you may get an angry letter from the lottery about that**… you landed upon one of the very few things they will enforce, which is **keeping the games on**. And ultimately, they probably wouldn't cite you. **They would probably cite the MLH**" — the master licence holder. Val's refinement, agreed to: "the equipment is on. **They just can't process the wins**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].
- **[[wiki/decisions/2026-08-26-certify-only-flex-iq-and-flex-port-with-incomm.md]] holds.** Nothing on Sept 2 changed the 12-month two-product declaration, and the ATM remains gated on JVL being licensed as an ATM operator.

## The collision worth keeping in view

Three positions taken in one week do not fit together, and the derived notes flag it for Val explicitly [[raw/Meeting_Notes_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]]:

1. **InComm** says the partnership struggles if JVL puts ATM/cash functionality anywhere near COAM (this page).
2. **[[wiki/people/billy-liu.md|Billy Liu]] ([[wiki/organizations/play-payments.md|PlayPay]])** — the same day — wants exclusivity: no InComm, no physical card dispensing.
3. **Flex-IQ's multiple-clearinghouse capability** ([[wiki/decisions/2026-08-27-flex-iq-multiple-clearinghouses-selectable-per-device.md]]) was specified precisely so an operator could pick a provider other than InComm.

Recorded as the notes state it; nothing here reconciles them.

**The primary source for point 2 is now in the corpus.** Billy Liu made the demand himself that evening, six hours after this lunch ended: "**You gonna think about it? It'll be an exclusive thing, right? You're not gonna offer [InComm] out of those things. You're not gonna dispense a physical card.**" Val did not agree to it — "**But I don't know. This is a big ask. Maybe even impossible**" — so the collision is live but neither side of it has been conceded. The full proposal it was attached to is on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-val-proposes-fifty-percent-of-play-payments.md]]; the meeting is [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-02-billy-liu-playpay-partnership-deep-dive.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

## Sept 9 2026 — told to a customer: the Pay unit will not be re-opened for a recycler, and what replaces it is not an ATM

A week later, at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-09-lucky-coin-service-burden-and-exclusive-partnership.md|Lucky Coin shop visit]], [[wiki/people/kyle-shepherd.md|Kyle Shepherd]] asked for the modularity promise to be confirmed in front of his technicians — "**that is modular in the sense that if these regulations change here, we need to open that thing up and put in… [a] recycler or any other piece of equipment**" — and [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] said no. **This is the first customer-facing statement in the corpus that the recycler is not coming into the device at all.** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]]

> "let me be clear. **If you have to dispense a card and put a recycler, it's not gonna fit. So you'll have to remove the bill acceptor. You'll have to remove the dispenser. I don't think it's gonna happen, and I cannot tell you what it is, and I'm not gonna discuss it with you. But I believe something else will happen. And I believe that you will leave it the way it is, and you will have another piece of equipment.**"

- **The physical reason is capacity, not policy** — the recycler cannot coexist with the card dispenser and the front-load bill acceptor in the same unit [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]].
- **And whatever replaces it is a separate purchase, and Val does not think it is an ATM.** Asked whether a second box would also come free if the law changes: "I don't think the law has anything to do with it. But **if you were to have an ATM machine, that will be additional piece of equipment**"; "he will have to [buy that separate]. And **I don't believe it will be exactly an ATM. We have an idea of how it needs to be done, but I don't think it should be an ATM**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]].
- **Kyle accepted the answer:** "I think the **second piece of equipment is the answer as well**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]].

**How this bears on the first open question below, without answering it.** "Does 'hold it back' mean not building it, or building it and not selling it into Georgia COAM?" — Sept 9 says something narrower and new: **the recycler will not be a field upgrade to the shipped Pay unit**, and Val expects "something else" instead, which he refused to describe. That is a product-architecture statement, not a development-status one, so the question stays open. It also **pulls against the Sept-2 modularity promise made to the same customer** — [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]]'s "if you decide to add additional features, you can **upgrade, not replace**," and Val's own "you're not losing anything… **the same form-fit device**." Both stand; **the two sources do not mention each other and nothing here reconciles them.** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]]

## Sept 16 2026 — Val's own account of the standoff, and an operator telling him to break it

On a **12:50 PM phone call** with [[wiki/people/jamie-boyden.md|Jamie Boyden]] ([[wiki/meetings/2026-09-16-jamie-boyden-call-recycler-advice-and-class-a-recruitment.md|the call]]), Val narrated the whole recycler episode to someone outside JVL for the first time in the corpus — and got the opposite advice to the one he has been following [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Jamie_Georgia_Class_A_Opportunity_and_Market_Outlook-September-16-2026.md]].

**His own telling of what happened:**

> "**I reached out to Joe Kim. I showed all the cards that Georgia introduced. InComm… They introduced a conversion to an ATM card. I manufactured a recycler that would take these cards and dispense cash. When Joe Kim saw it, he shot in his pants. He just went ballistic.**"
> [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Jamie_Georgia_Class_A_Opportunity_and_Market_Outlook-September-16-2026.md]]

- **Both parties asked him to stop, and both asked *after* they had seen it.** "**InComm politely asked me, 'Please not do not make it, do not put it in the market.' And so I met with Georgia Lottery… And they also politely told me it would be nice if you wouldn't do**" — and, to Jamie's "**You went ahead and did it**": "**No. Post fact. They asked me post fact after they saw it.**" *(This is the **first statement in the corpus that the Georgia Lottery itself made the same request**. Which Lottery meeting he means is **not stated on the call** and is not asserted here.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Jamie_Georgia_Class_A_Opportunity_and_Market_Outlook-September-16-2026.md]]
- **He says the regulator is now retreating from its own card.** "**the whole Georgia Lottery now is backpedaling on the card, on the ATM card, and the whole industry is upside down.**" *(Val's characterisation, on a phone call; no Lottery source in the corpus says this.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Jamie_Georgia_Class_A_Opportunity_and_Market_Outlook-September-16-2026.md]]
- **And he states his own legal reading out loud, which he had not done before.** Jamie: "**It's illegal under the current law, is it not?**" Val: "**That's how I interpret it. That's how InComm interprets it. Everybody's confused. I think Joe Kim is confused.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Jamie_Georgia_Class_A_Opportunity_and_Market_Outlook-September-16-2026.md]]

**The advice he was given, and did not answer.** Jamie told him to do the opposite of holding back: "**Don't you want to force the issue? Sell a bunch, force the issue, and then they have to change the rates. It's going to open up. It could open up the door to make Georgia a VLP market**" — with the political case behind it (Kemp's departure; Jamie's estimate of "**over sixty percent in favor of cash gaming in Georgia**"; "**if you get the catalyst going to force change, could be kind of interesting**"). **Val answered only with a joke — "It could make Georgia great again" — and moved the conversation to Class A. He took no position either way on the call** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Jamie_Georgia_Class_A_Opportunity_and_Market_Outlook-September-16-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Jamie_Georgia_Class_A_Opportunity_and_Market_Outlook-September-16-2026.md]].

**Nothing here changes the decision.** No source shows Val acting on the advice; the Sept-2 hold stands, and the new question it raises is recorded below rather than resolved.

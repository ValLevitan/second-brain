# Georgia COAM Class A is built on NOVA, not RAGE

Taken by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-11-rage-sunset-and-class-a-on-nova.md|Sept 11 2026 1:00 PM game-development call]], against [[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael Gottlieb]]'s stated objection, with [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim Podvoiski]] siding with Val and [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena Zhukova]] — who had already started the RAGE version — accepting the change. Michael then withdrew the objection in the room. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]

## How it came up — work had already started on RAGE

Val put the Class A platform on the table himself: "**Georgia Class… AV43 will also be on Nova**", and Lena repeated it back — "**Georgia Com Class A will be on Nova**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]. ("Georgia Com" is the transcript's rendering of Georgia COAM.)

What the team had actually been doing was the opposite. Lena: "**We thought we are going to do it based on current Georgia V3 release, taking existing games like Line Switches, Bison, all the games we have, six slot games from Georgia V3**", with Michael adding "**and make whatever changes are required to make them compliant with Class A**". Lena had already begun: "**I started to work on this, like all this changing in dashboard, adding like extra dialogues**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]].

## Val's ruling and his three reasons

> "**I, I, I don't want to do it on Rage. It's really a bad idea to do it on Rage.**"
> [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]

Asked "Why would you say that?" he gave three [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]:

- **"Well, Rage needs to end. I mean, the development… Environment is not what we want."**
- **"Supporting 2 platforms is just… Not possible."**
- **"I mean, hardware related issues that, you know, very difficult to fix on rage."** — "And we need, we need to end rage."

And the closing image, which is the clearest statement of the sunset in the corpus: "**We need to end on a on a happy note… Rage has served so well to us for us. Let's just now park it. Kiss it, say our goodbyes, and move on.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]

## Michael's objection — and why he dropped it

- **His objection was that this is one derivative of finished work.** "**I thought the… directive that I was looking to operate under was to basically get us a Class A version of Georgia V43 V3 with six lines and ways titles done as quickly as possible**" — and "**it would seem that trying to migrate the entire thing to Nova just for one derivative version of something we've already finished in Rage. Seems like a lot of additional work that doesn't benefit us.**" He also put the general rule: "**inherently switching over platforms takes longer than doing something in the native platform**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]].
- **Efim's counter carried it.** "**I agree with Val in this case. We… don't see all the changes that we need to do in the framework, but they might be substantial and doing anything in this rage framework, something new, it will. Be a lot of work that will be wasted**" — plus the evidence from the migration experiments: "**the conversion of the assets is proven that it is proven that this can be done relatively easily… in the cases when we converted Shkapcova games**", and the long view, "**even if you spent a little bit more time preparing it then… [i]n Nova, this we will have a more solid product and more life in future for this efforts.**" He was honest about his confidence: "**it might be wrong, it might be fifty-fifty, but in the long run, in any case, we'll better have an answer in… nov[a]**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]].
  - *(Two phrases in Efim's turn are garbled and are recorded as spoken, not repaired: "because we don't want to kill the rage soon" — which reads against the sense of his own argument — and "Shkapcova games", the converted title set, which matches no game name elsewhere in the corpus. Both are queued rather than interpreted.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]
- **Val's own argument was speed, not theory.** "**I'm seeing that they work with incredible speed and no[va]… I could never visualize the speed that they work at. It's just, it's unbelievable. It's, it's ours, literally ours. It's not, it's not months, weeks. It's not, it's ours… It's mind-boggling.**" ("ours" = *hours*, from context.) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]
- **The concession, verbatim.** Michael: "**No objections, then let's get the first six lines and ways games brought over to brought over to Nova.**" Earlier in the same turn: "**Okay, then they can take the first, take the six lines and ways games from V3… Yeah, if it's easier. I mean, that's totally valid. I just, I didn't realize that the level of modifications we were talking about were so invasive**… **if you're feeling like this is a good place to cut, then then so be it.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]

Lena's closing formulation of the product name: "**Okay, so it's going to be Class A, V43, Nova.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]

## The date — January 1 proposed, February 1 landed, and it is only a goal

This is the one part of the decision with numbers in it, and all four steps happened in about ninety seconds [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]:

1. **Lena asked for a date** — "**can you give me some sense of due date or something, because we need to schedule it. Even if we can convert like symbols and backgrounds, it doesn't mean we can convert other elements.**"
2. **Val: "Yeah, January 1st."**
3. **Lena's objection was Nebraska, not Class A** — "**then Nebraska, we will not finish it by the end of the year if we will do it in parallel**", and "**I would rather finish Nebraska and then move whole team to NOVA projects.**" Val accepted at once: "**Nebraska takes the precedence**", "**I think I can work with that. Sure, I can work with that.**" Her capacity statement, put to her by Val and confirmed: focused on Nebraska only, with full resources, she needs **101 days**.
4. **Val: "So then, February 1st?"** — and Lena took it as a goal only: "**Let's keep it as a… goal, but once we start to work… closer with this… transition resources from R[age] Studio to Nova, we will see what is transitioning… well, what assets will need to be… adjusted or something like this and then I can give you more… educated evaluation, more reasonable opinion about if it's possible to do by February 1st.**"

**No year is spoken for either date.** Given the meeting sits on 11 September 2026, both fall in **2027**; the derived notes read them that way. Recorded as the source has them, with the year marked as the wiki's inference. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]

**Why Lena's 101 days has slack in it, in her own words:** people are away — Dima's vacation starts the following week, Anastasiya takes two weeks, Natalia is also going — and the person hired to replace Julia has been let go, so a new interview round and a fresh learning curve are ahead. *(The source also carries a one-line performance judgement about that unnamed individual; it is HR material under prohibition #8 and is not reproduced here.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]

Sequencing Class A behind Nebraska did **not** mean waiting for it:

- **Line Switches becomes the live test bed.** Michael: "**as I said to you yesterday, Lena, let's take L[ine Switches] and use that as the test bed for our Class A product. It'll be Nova. And… theory is great, but doing, let's just do it.**" Lena: "**That's what I'm going to do.**" Her reason it is not wasted effort: "**I will work on design for this line… just because design will be the same for Nova, Rage Studio, I mean design of this dialogues and dashboard.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]
  - **Which game this is, is unclear and is not resolved here.** The transcript renders Michael's naming as "**Lions Riches**" and Lena's, twice, as "**line switches**" / "**Line Switches**". Both are real JVL titles in this corpus — Lion's Riches, and Line Switches (IP998) on [[wiki/projects/south-dakota-vlt-market.md]]. The derived notes read every instance as **Line Switches**. Recorded as the notes' reading, with the disagreement visible; not guessed at. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]
- **The new NOVA workflow gets started in parallel with Nebraska.** Lena: "**what we planned once Dima is back from vacation, we planned to already start to talk to Biratko and other developers about**… we want to work a little bit ahead, so we will start to work about our new workflow in N[ova], how we are going to do it. So, in parallel of developing Nebraska, we will already… start to discuss our new workflow.**" And: "**maybe we will even start experiments with assets transition from [Line Switches] before even before full development.**" ("Biratko" = [[wiki/people/alexander-biratka.md|Alexander Biratko]]; "Jima" in the transcript = Dima.) - **The written concepts are re-pointed at NOVA immediately.** Val: "**can you please change… [t]he description in the concept and game design, so we know that we are cruising with Nova, please.**" Michael: "**Hundred percent, totally**", and later, "**I've already updated all the game concepts.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]

## Nebraska is confirmed, again, as the last RAGE release — but no sunset DATE is set

Val checked it in the room and got it from all three: "**Nebraska is still on R[age], am I correct?**" — Lena "Yes", Efim "Yes", Michael "Mmh" — then "**That's the last release that we're making on Rage.**" Michael: "**Yep.**" Lena: "**Yes, that was Juan.**" Efim: "**Hopefully.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]

This **confirms** the same claim already recorded from the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-04-naming-convention-standards-and-nova-conversion.md|Sept-4 call]] ("Nebraska is the last stage, the last release for OnRage") — evolution by restatement, not a new fact. See [[wiki/projects/nebraska-skill-market.md]].

**No calendar date for the RAGE sunset appears in either source.** The only quantities given are Lena's ~101 days of Nebraska work with a full team and the Feb-1 *goal* for Class A on NOVA. Anyone reading this page for "when does RAGE stop" should read: *when Nebraska ships*, which this source does not date.

**And no NOVA → GLI date appears either.** GLI is not mentioned once in either source. The owner's standing position — NOVA's GLI submission is **unscheduled** — is untouched here; do not read the February-1 product goal as a certification date. See [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-01-first-nova-product-south-dakota-vlt-2.md]].

## Open contradictions

- **Does Class A work starting now sit against Val's own Sept-10 "nothing until January"?** On **Sept 10 2026**, in front of the Georgia Lottery, Val said "**I doubt very much that I can do anything until January. So it will probably hear the legislation before even doing anything**" — recorded on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-10-no-class-a-build-until-the-january-legislature-is-heard.md]]. On **Sept 11 2026**, one day later, he directs Class A product work to begin: the platform is chosen, Lena starts the Line Switches design, Efim is asked for the behaviour write-up, and Michael is sent to record a Class A machine the following week. The two can be read as compatible — Sept-10 is about *entering the market* and deciding, Sept-11 is about *building the software*, with delivery targeted **after** January — or as incompatible, because work Val said would wait is under way before January. **Neither source mentions the other. Not resolved here.** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]
- **This is a build into a regulatory vacuum, and nobody in the room said so.** The day before, the Georgia Lottery told Val there is no book of Class A regulations, no central accounting system and therefore no certification — see [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-10-the-lottery-tells-jvl-class-a-manufacturing-is-lawful-today.md]] and [[wiki/topics/georgia-class-a-machines.md]]. Neither the regulator, the rulebook nor certification is mentioned anywhere in this meeting; the only compliance reference is Michael's "**make whatever changes are required to make them compliant with Class A**", with the requirements themselves undefined. Recorded as an absence in the source, not as a decision. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]

# The bundle put to Dixie — and the $70,000 of installation Val took back

The first **hard commercial offer** JVL has put to [[wiki/organizations/dixie-amusement.md|Dixie Amusement]], made in the room at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-08-dixie-amusement-bundle-offer-and-card-economics.md|Sept-8 2026 visit]]. [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] opened it without preamble: "**Did you align yourself with anybody in transactional equipment here? Why not align yourself with me? Let's talk. Let's see. I can make you a deal that you can't refuse.**" [[wiki/people/salim-khambawala.md|Salim Khambawala]]'s first reply was the sceptic's: "**Everything is for free. Free. There's nothing free, like your dad said.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]

## The offer, as stated in the room

- **Buy one machine, everything else is free.** "We're introducing a **new machine, vertical, with 15 games** in it. **For every one machine, I'll give you everything else for free.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]
- **Five additional ports plus a counter unit.** "You get this machine. With this building, **you'll get five more of these**. And I'll get a key or a **stander behind the counter**. You'll get one of those, and you'll get one of this. **At no cost.** This has, so you understand. It has an **optical reader**, it has a **bill acceptor**, and it has a **ticket printer**. All different… **No gimmick, no gimmicks.**" *(Which physical device each "this" refers to is recoverable only in outline — the room is full of hardware being pointed at. The derived notes read the package as five free FlexPorts plus a free counter-top/behind-the-counter unit.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]
- **$90 per location per month, all in.** "**What is the monthly fees? Ninety bucks. Per location.**" Salim checked it back — "All the equipment, buy the machine, ninety dollars a month" — and asked what was inside it. TITO: "**Everything is our own.**" The servers: "**Back end server at the location. We have a mini server on the location and a backup server in the cloud**"; "**It's included in the ninety dollars.**" No third party. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]
- **Operator intelligence is free now and priced later.** "The only thing that we're giving away for free now is the **operator intelligence, which is optional, but we eventually will start charging for it.**" What it is: "the entire intelligence of your operation, **from collection to service to popularity reports**… **machine number one has not played for three days. Send the technician**… **the cash door was open** at such and such time… **your bill acceptor's acceptance rate on the first insertion is falling to sixty five percent. Time to clean your bill acceptor** … **Delivered directly to your cell phone.**" See [[wiki/topics/machine-data-repurchase-program.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]

**The machine's daily number was stated twice and the two figures do not agree.** Val: "By the way, this machine. **Do you know how much it makes? You told me last time. Two hundred ninety-four dollars.**" Two minutes later Salim, asking his own question: "that **this machine makes three hundred and ninety something dollars**." Neither man reconciles them and nothing here picks one. *(The $294 is the same order as the "about two hundred ninety dollars per day" Val quoted Salim on Aug 31 — see [[wiki/organizations/dixie-amusement.md]].)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]

## The reaction — "it's unbearable," then a hand pulled back

Salim's verdict on the price was one word: "**It's Unbearable.**" *(The derived notes read this as **unbeatable**; the wiki records both the word spoken and the notes' reading, and does not correct the transcript.)* Val went straight for the close — "**Do we shake hands on this?**" — and got "**Okay.**" Then Salim stopped himself: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]

> "We just talked about it. **We are on the upper, upper casing. I don't want to shake hands and then back out.**"

*("Upper casing" is flagged unclear in the derived notes and is not interpreted here.)* Val's read of the pause: "**You smell a good deal when you hear it. You smell a good deal. You count money faster than I can.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]

**The benchmark he measured it against:** "the **cheapest I've seen is thirteen dollars per month per machine**. Where another one I've seen is a **dollar per day per machine**, which is far on the high side, and thirteen I think was on the low side." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]

## Installation — priced, dangled, and explicitly withdrawn

This is the sharpest thing in the meeting and the reason this page exists as a decision rather than a note.

1. **Val asked who pays for the wiring** when a location swaps systems: "You will remove your **MudaGoose** from the location, and you need to do the wiring. **Who does the wiring?**" Salim: the master pays. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]
2. **Salim priced it.** "**A company like me, who has my own employee, per hour, my math is it costs me around 150 bucks per hour. Two guys going out.**" Val did the arithmetic aloud: "**150 times five hours. It's about a thousand bucks per location.**" Locations: "**Sixty to seventy.**" Val: "**So that's another seventy thousand.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]
3. **Val dangled it.** "**Remember that seventy thousand. So if I told you that you don't have to pay seventy thousand, can we make the deal today?**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]
4. **And took it back, cleanly, the moment Salim started negotiating against it.** "**I didn't. I didn't say anything about installation. Let's just be clear. I didn't promise anything.**" Salim: "You didn't say, but you want me to negotiate." Val: "**No. I just asked the question. That's all I did. I asked how much it costs.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]

**So the $70,000 is not on the table**, and the derived notes state it in those words. Salim's counter-argument for why it would cost more than $1,000 anyway: distance. "if it's in **Waldo**, it starts which is **four hours**. People say I only calculated five hours total, and now four-hour drive is one way, and now four-hour drive, and plus, now I have to get my people. **Hotel** up there." *("Waldo" is flagged in the notes as probably **Valdosta**; not corrected here.)* [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]

## What the JVL side concluded afterwards

The last two minutes are Val and one JVL colleague, alone, and they are the clearest statement of what JVL thinks it just sold: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]

- **The configuration:** "**Five, six, four plus I Q and kiosks.**" *(Recorded verbatim; the three numbers are not explained anywhere in the source and are not interpreted here.)*
- **Card dispensers are in every kiosk in this deal.** "Card dispenser was **printer, bill receiver and scanner only. But all of the kiosks have the card dispenser.**"
- **One machine.** "And **he buys game machine. One.**"
- **A contract will be needed.** "We didn't discuss, but yes, **there will be a contract definitely.**"
- **He is comfortable with the recurring side.** Colleague: "he's perfectly fine with paying monthly." Val: "**No issue with that. No issue with the service.**"

## How this sits against JVL's other rulings

- **It is the [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-free-ports-and-pos-with-every-machine-bought.md|Sept-2 free-hardware offer]] in a sixth composition.** Sept 2 (Lucky Coin) was six ports plus a terminal, ~$7,000 of hardware, $15,750 machine. Sept 4 (internal) was one Flex-Slim + one Flex-IQ + five Flex-Ports, $12,820, against $15,750. **Sept 8 (ARC, morning)** was machine + kiosk + Flex-IQ + five additional FlexPorts. **Sept 8 (Dixie, afternoon)** is machine + five ports + a counter unit, with kiosks in the debrief. All stand side by side; **no source reconciles any two of them.** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Vertical_Screen_Bundle_Offer_and_David_Intro-September-8-2026.md]]
- **No machine price was quoted.** Unlike Sept 2 and Sept 4, the machine's price never enters the Dixie conversation — only its daily earnings. See [[wiki/topics/jvl-machine-pricing-strategy.md]].
- **Exclusivity was never asked for**, and the remote shut-off that the [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-31-transactional-equipment-only-with-location-exclusivity.md|Aug-31 exclusivity ruling]] depends on was raised — and **refused by the operator**. That evidence is recorded on that page.

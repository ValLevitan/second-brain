# Location invoice due on its date, 35% simple daily interest from day 7; monthly connection fee to the master at net 15 (Nov 20 2026)

Set by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-11-20-andrei-prod-test-2-billing-runbook-and-invoice-terms.md|Nov 20 2026, ~10:02 AM "PROD test 2 with InComm kickoff" call]] (English, with some Spanish), called by [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] to close the open items "for our prod test 2 and actually the going live with our first real location" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Andrei_PROD_Test_2_Billing_Runbook_and_Invoice_Terms-November-20-2026.md]]. These are customer billing terms (company economics), not anyone's pay.

## 1. The goal and where the billing module stands

- **Klimovich's target.** "in a perfect world, I would expect that we go to the location next week, let's say Wednesday or Thursday, and we are sure that everything in the back end is ready. Like the invoices are being issued, they have been sent, the wallet is being paid, money go all the way like from income [InComm], sorry, from JVL to income, from location to JVL"; "we've already postponed the release a couple of times with our customer" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Andrei_PROD_Test_2_Billing_Runbook_and_Invoice_Terms-November-20-2026.md]].
- **Nathan's two runbooks.** [[wiki/people/nathan-carver.md|Nathan Carver]]: "I have two runbooks, one for deployment and verification of configuration, and then the second is to recover the invoice from our first test run. So those two things have to happen" before another test or "any invoice through the system." He sent them "last month"; "Since that time, I've been reprioritized to other JVL work"; he needs feedback that they "will work" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Andrei_PROD_Test_2_Billing_Runbook_and_Invoice_Terms-November-20-2026.md]].
- **What QA could test.** [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]]: "our QA tested only until the moment when the real money is actually started to move"; real cards and real invoices going "through the workflow… and up to Business Central" is "impossible to test on QA." Nathan: "the module has gone to QA, but we haven't integrated with Incom and Duala [Dwolla] yet" — Pavel: "Yes" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Andrei_PROD_Test_2_Billing_Runbook_and_Invoice_Terms-November-20-2026.md]].
- **Why the first invoice stuck.** Nathan: "MSBC didn't have the proper configurations, which I've gone through with Igor and we've agreed upon those." The fix: services "that would allow a signal from Payment Hub to reprocess invoices that have failed for non… banking reasons" (insufficient funds is "a different matter"); the runbook re-starts payment for that invoice "through Diwala [Dwolla]" so it is sent and "finish[es] its journey flowing through MSPC [MSBC]." Doing it "will actually be a kind of an initial integration test… with Duala and MSPC"; "then you would be, in my opinion, ready to run another end-to-end." Which module sends the signal: "not sure which module the team decided" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Andrei_PROD_Test_2_Billing_Runbook_and_Invoice_Terms-November-20-2026.md]].
- **Bank reconciliation is last and not built.** Nathan: "the reconciliation with CIBC. That still needs to be integrated with the bank… it's been deprioritized on my list of work. But we can change that"; "You can go without that if you only have a few, right? But we want to get to full automated reconciliation so that there's nobody in MSBC having to do the reconciliation" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Andrei_PROD_Test_2_Billing_Runbook_and_Invoice_Terms-November-20-2026.md]]. See [[wiki/organizations/cibc.md]].
- **Dependencies.** Pavel: "I do not see any dependency on other services except for Business Central, dual [Dwolla] and location building [billing]"; Nathan: the service contracts between Payment Hub (or Location Manager) and Location Billing — "We need to make sure that that handshake is good" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Andrei_PROD_Test_2_Billing_Runbook_and_Invoice_Terms-November-20-2026.md]].
- **The Saturday review.** Pavel offered his team "tomorrow" ("we were working on our weekends"); Nathan: "let's do it early tomorrow"; Pavel later: "I already created the meeting… at 9 A.m.?" — Nathan: "Yes, that looks good." Pavel "will ask Dima and Serge to go through this document to be prepared for tomorrow's call." Klimovich's summary: "Nathan and Pavel, you meet tomorrow, you go through all the opened items, and then you just provide us with expected dates" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Andrei_PROD_Test_2_Billing_Runbook_and_Invoice_Terms-November-20-2026.md]]. "Dima" / "Serge" (and the heard "Tsimanov Chudzinau and Tsimanov") = [[wiki/people/dmitri-chudinov.md|Dmitri Chudinov]] and [[wiki/people/sergey-tsymanov.md|Sergey Tsymanov]] — **inference** from the back-end context and the same day's speaker labels "Dzmitry Chudzinau" / "Sergey Tsimanov" on the 9:02 AM call.

## 2. The location invoice — terms

- **Wording.** Klimovich: the invoice mock-up's latest version is done; "wordings need to be approved by Igor and Lev, and if it's all good, it should be implemented on a Business Central site, so it will be generated exactly in this way as we approve it" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Andrei_PROD_Test_2_Billing_Runbook_and_Invoice_Terms-November-20-2026.md]].
- **Due on the invoice date.** Klimovich: "based on Val's feedback, we are not providing 10 days net or anything like that. So it works immediately. So the date is issued, it is the date when we expect the funds to be received." Val: "invoice payment is on the date of invoices not negotiable" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Andrei_PROD_Test_2_Billing_Runbook_and_Invoice_Terms-November-20-2026.md]].
- **Interest must be written down.** [[wiki/people/igor-donis.md|Igor Donis]] spoke in Spanish; [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda]] translated: Igor cannot change the terms in the system himself — "Lev would be the person responsible to making those changes agreed by Val"; and "if the invoice is not paid on due date, it will generate interest… should be somewhere in the invoice or in the contract with the customer" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Andrei_PROD_Test_2_Billing_Runbook_and_Invoice_Terms-November-20-2026.md]]. Val: "We don't have any agreements in place yet. So as a point of interest, it should be decoupled from… The date when the payment is due" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Andrei_PROD_Test_2_Billing_Runbook_and_Invoice_Terms-November-20-2026.md]].
- **Day 7, 35%, simple daily.** Nathan: MSBC "can apply interest based on the term you set on the invoice", but it does not see the money until the ACH transaction has finished. Val: "probably 3 days, right?" Nathan: "it could be up to five days, depending on if we hit holiday or weekend." Val: "if you want to be safe, let's pick seven days and start your interest count. The interest is daily… Based on 35% per year, divided the number of days, non-compounded… So it's a simple daily interest." Klimovich later to Igor: "this interest should start with day 7, not immediately" — Igor: "Exactly" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Andrei_PROD_Test_2_Billing_Runbook_and_Invoice_Terms-November-20-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Andrei_PROD_Test_2_Billing_Runbook_and_Invoice_Terms-November-20-2026.md]].
- **Standard layout, approved content, no customization.** [[wiki/people/lev-rudky.md|Lev Rudky]]: "there are no problems to create an appropriate payment term in the system. It's practically immediate. But in terms of invoice layout… Why can't we use the existing layout like the standard layout?" Klimovich: "We can… It's a question only about the content… Description of the service, terms of payment, all this stuff." Asked when, Igor wanted to review the final version with Lev and send it; Val: "No, no… we don't have time… One hour, one hour, get back to me in one hour"; Lev: "If it requires customizations, I won't be able to tell you"; Val: "No customization… Present an invoice to Andrei in one hour." Igor: "After this meeting, Blev [Lev] and I meeting" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Andrei_PROD_Test_2_Billing_Runbook_and_Invoice_Terms-November-20-2026.md]].
- **The contract.** Klimovich: "I will also send a message to Monica, who is working on the contract, that it should be stated there too" — Val: "Yes, Sir" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Andrei_PROD_Test_2_Billing_Runbook_and_Invoice_Terms-November-20-2026.md]]. "Monica" = [[wiki/people/monica.md|Monica]] by her contracts/compliance role (**inference**).

## 3. The monthly connection fee — a new recurring invoice

- **The gap.** Klimovich: "the monthly fee, which slipped under our radar… we don't have a specific invoice type right now, which will be billed on a monthly basis for amount of $90." Val: "The amount depends on what we charge. The description should be a reoccurring monthly connection service"; a recurring invoice "is just a matter of setup in Microsoft Business Central" ("I suspect, I don't know for sure, subject for less [Lev's] confirmation" — reading **inference**) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Andrei_PROD_Test_2_Billing_Runbook_and_Invoice_Terms-November-20-2026.md]].
- **When and how much.** Val: "This invoice has to be produced at the same day, on the first day of the month"; for part of a month, "if the connection service was rendered only for one day, it would take 90 days [dollars] divided by the number of days in the month." The start marker is "date of installation… We need that marker reflected in Microsoft Business Central. From there, we count number of full number of days, 24 hours… minimum billing period is 24 hours" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Andrei_PROD_Test_2_Billing_Runbook_and_Invoice_Terms-November-20-2026.md]].
- **Different terms for the operator.** Klimovich asked whether interest is the same 35% and seven days; Val first said "Same, same, pay, pay on invoice", then: "Forgive me, we're charging the operator different. Everything changes to… net 15 days and interest starts at day 31" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Andrei_PROD_Test_2_Billing_Runbook_and_Invoice_Terms-November-20-2026.md]]. Whether the 35% simple daily rate also applies after day 31 was not restated (the notes give only "net 15, interest from day 31") [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Andrei_PROD_Test_2_Billing_Runbook_and_Invoice_Terms-November-20-2026.md]].
- **Always the master?** Felipe: "Are we always charging the master Val?… I don't think that's the case." Val: "I'm 99% sure that this is the case. If it's not, we'll meet again and we'll adjust again" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Andrei_PROD_Test_2_Billing_Runbook_and_Invoice_Terms-November-20-2026.md]].
- **Keep the payer choice in the requirements.** Klimovich: "on the payment hub side, there is already capabilities and requirements describing exactly the way how you described it right now, with additional features that we can choose, either it's master or location, but we can simplify it, make master only for now." Val: "if that feature is already in, keep it… Keep, keep it." Klimovich: "It's not implemented, it's described in the document" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Andrei_PROD_Test_2_Billing_Runbook_and_Invoice_Terms-November-20-2026.md]].
- **The mock-up.** Klimovich will make "the same mock-up with the content for the invoice"; to Felipe's "mock-up you're doing is for master exclusively", he answered "it should be business type agnostic. So it just goes to a specific pay[er], someone who will pay for it" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Andrei_PROD_Test_2_Billing_Runbook_and_Invoice_Terms-November-20-2026.md]].

## How it sits against earlier pages

- **July 20 2026 — COD, not Net 7.** The Payment Hub was set up in Business Central with COD terms because the invoice and the draw happen the same day ([[wiki/topics/business-central-erp-implementation.md]], July-20 section). "Due on the invoice date" keeps that rule; the late interest from day 7 is new. **Evolution.**
- **Sept 17 2026 — Dynamic Gaming: the $90 is drafted from the location, not paid by the master.** That was one customer's deal ([[wiki/decisions/2026-09-17-the-ninety-dollar-fee-is-drafted-from-the-location-not-paid-by-the-master.md]]). Here the default payer is the master, and the master-or-location choice stays in the Payment Hub requirements. Felipe's doubt fits the Sept-17 and Oct-15 pattern of masters asking JVL to bill the location (**inference**). Recorded side by side; not a contradiction, because the Nov-20 rule was set as a default and Val promised to adjust if wrong.
- **Oct 9 2026 — a real transaction in production before the first real location.** PROD test 2 is that step for the new version; its commitment stays open — see [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-09-a-real-transaction-is-run-in-production-on-the-new-version-before-the-first-real-location.md]] (Nov-20 note).
- **Oct 14 2026** — a five-day payment window was sought with InComm ([[wiki/organizations/incom.md]]); the "up to five days" ACH time here is the same order of size (**inference**; different flows).

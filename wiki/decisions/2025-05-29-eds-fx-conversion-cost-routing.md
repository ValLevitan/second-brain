# Split the missed EDS currency-conversion charge 50/50, but re-route payments to avoid it

On the May 29 2025 call between [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]], [[wiki/people/eliana.md|Eliana]] and her daughter/translator [[wiki/people/sofia.md|Sofia]] ([[wiki/meetings/2025-05-29-eliana-fx-conversion.md]]), Val and Eliana settled how to handle a bank charge for **converting funds US↔Colombian pesos** that both sides had missed when forming their [[wiki/organizations/eds-colombia.md|EDS]] agreement. [[raw/Eliana-May-29-2025.md]]

## What was decided

- **The charge was a mutual omission, so it is shared 50/50.** Val: it "cannot be a responsibility of one party only" — negligible on small amounts but "substantial" when measured across millions in transactions, and it was never negotiated. [[raw/Eliana-May-29-2025.md]]
- **Preferred fix — restructure the payment path to avoid the conversion entirely:** **JVL pays Eliana into her US (Miami) bank account**, and **Eliana's company pays [[wiki/organizations/elevate.md|Elevate]] directly** — removing the extra US↔Colombia conversion step. [[raw/Eliana-May-29-2025.md]]
- **Elevate hasn't formally approved this** (Julián raised it with "the man from Elevate" and it was rejected), **but** Val judges Elevate is very unlikely to actually reject an incoming payment (they want to be paid). If Elevate does reject it, the extra conversion cost reverts to the **50/50** split. [[raw/Eliana-May-29-2025.md]]
- **Implementation:** Eliana will meet her Miami accountant to structure the consignments (funds can't all be consigned to her account at once; and to decide personal vs company account). [[raw/Eliana-May-29-2025.md]]

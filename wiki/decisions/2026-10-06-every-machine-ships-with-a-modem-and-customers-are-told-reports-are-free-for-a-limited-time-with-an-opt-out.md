# Modem standard on every machine; free reports for a limited time; opt-out by message

Ruled by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-06-felipe-delivery-forecast-arc-release-and-modem-notice.md|Oct-6 1:41 PM call]], after [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda]] asked how to present the modem.

## What was said

- **Felipe's question.** "we did an agreement and a discount for Arc for the… For the modem, right? For the other masters, I'm not even talking about the modem. I just say that modem will help you get better information about your machines." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Felipe_Delivery_Forecast_ARC_Release_and_Modem_Notice-October-6-2026.md]]
- **Standard.** Val: "this is our standard packaging, everything's coming out with the model" (modem). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Felipe_Delivery_Forecast_ARC_Release_and_Modem_Notice-October-6-2026.md]]
- **If a customer objects.** Val: "they could say, look, I don't want to share anything, disconnect the modem. It says, sure, of course, no problem." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Felipe_Delivery_Forecast_ARC_Release_and_Modem_Notice-October-6-2026.md]]
- **Tell them.** Val: "we should notify them… We're evolving, the machines are coming out with modems. And for now, for a limited period only, we will offer all the reports to you for free." Felipe: "And they could opt out of modem." Val: "we should say you could opt out of the service… By opting out, please send us a message." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Felipe_Delivery_Forecast_ARC_Release_and_Modem_Notice-October-6-2026.md]]

## How it sits against Aug 21 — evolution

On Aug 21 2026 Val ruled that every machine gets the modem from Sept 1, but it is **activated only** for customers who sign the data-sharing agreement — [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-21-cell-modem-on-every-machine-from-september-1.md]]. On Oct 6 the modem is standard, the customer is told, and the customer must **opt out** by message. The install-on-every-machine part stands. The opt-in condition is replaced by an opt-out. This is **evolution**: the newer ruling is current. The Aug-21 page is annotated.

**Oct 14 2026 — how Val put it to a customer.** At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-14-yash-patel-halcyon-2027-plan-forest-park-test-and-texas-profit-share.md|Halcyon breakfast]] Val told Yash Patel: "I'm installing modems in the new machines… All the information is confidential, strictly, but they will all have modems inside." Yash welcomed it — "That's fantastic idea… More manufacturers will be doing that" — and gave a reason from his own route: a competitor's first games in Georgia did well but had many physical faults, so he pulled them; with remote data, "they would've been able to figure out… what the issue is." No opt-out or free-report offer is mentioned on that recording. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Yash_Patel_Halcyon_2027_Plan_Forest_Park_Pilot_and_Texas_Profit_Share-October-14-2026.md]]

How the free reports sit with the $337-per-year data buy-back ([[wiki/topics/machine-data-repurchase-program.md]]) is not said in the source.

**Oct 14 2026 — the notice given in person to AKS.** At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-14-aks-amusements-transactional-demo-two-location-test-and-financing.md|Oct-14 AKS meeting]] Val: "we installed here in the new machine, and you will have it in yours, is cellular modem with the data plan. I mean, we pay for it." JVL will see which games are popular and drop weak ones in later versions. Asked about automatic updates: "The law prohibits us to do anything automatic with the machine… We can only collect data one way. We cannot talk to the machine." Felipe: "For a period of time, it will be at no cost… you will be able to receive these reports." Val: "You could opt out if you want." This carries out the Oct-6 notice for one customer, face to face; the length of the free period is still not given [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AKS_Amusements_Transactional_Demo_Two_Location_Test_and_Financing-October-14-2026.md]].

**Oct 15 2026 — the zero-revenue alert, and used machines without a modem.** At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-15-blue-sky-gold-rush-refurbished-machines-offer-and-transactional-demo.md|Oct-15 Blue Sky / Gold Rush meeting]] Felipe said "we're adding an LTE modem", and Val put the main benefit as an alert: "It will tell you that nobody puts money that day in the machine… We could say this machine has zero revenue." How often is up to the customer — "You could do it hourly. You could do it daily" — "And we pay for the data." Val on the 20 refurbished trade-in machines: "The used machines don't have it." Opt-out and the free period were not mentioned [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]].

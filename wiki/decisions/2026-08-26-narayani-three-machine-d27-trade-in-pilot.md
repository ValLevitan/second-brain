# Narayani pilot: 3 D27s traded at $5,000 each, with the 25-machine version conditioned on transactional equipment

> **Status, as of Sept 1 2026: still `active`, but the headline number is no longer settled and nothing has been signed.** At the follow-up demo six days later Val restated the negotiation as "**you asked for five thousand and I agreed to three thousand**… **We shall make a deal somewhere in between five and three**," which is not what the Aug-26 record below says was agreed. The decision is **not** marked superseded, because no source says the Aug-26 terms were withdrawn — the two accounts of the same negotiation simply disagree, and both are recorded under `## Open contradictions`. Read the title's "$5,000" as the Aug-26 claim, not as a live price. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

## What was decided

- **A three-machine pilot swap, not the whole warehouse.** Val was the one who cut it down: "One should trade in two or three machines… You don't have to trade in 25. Trade in two three, then you'll see." Answer: "We can do that." The derived notes record the outcome as a **pilot of 3 machines**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]
- **$5,000 of trade-in credit per D27 cabinet, built as $4,000 + $1,000.** Val: "Top notch dollar, this is top notch as an exception, four thousand dollars per machine. But I will do an exception. I will do four thousand plus one thousand for that contract. I'll give you five thousand dollars for this deal only for the three machines, but under condition that you will sign the contract for three year… data purchase." Vishal: "**Fine.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]
- **Against a new D43 at $14,500.** Val gave the new-machine price as "14-5"; the old cabinets are **D27s** Narayani bought at $13,200. The notes record the swap as "D27 trade-in at $5,000 each ($4,000 + $1,000 data contract) against new D43s at $14,500, machines equipped with cellular modems." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]
- **A three-year data-purchase contract at $337/year/machine is part of the price, not an extra.** Full terms on [[wiki/topics/machine-data-repurchase-program.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]
- **The full 25-machine swap is parked behind a condition.** Vishal offered to swap all ~25 at the same price and place them in his best locations as marketing — "If I swap everything, right? I'm gonna install it in all of my locations… You will have more visibility in the market with the new game… think of this as a marketing strategy for your place." Val's counter-condition: "I want something else. I want you to install my transactional equipment in your location… And then I'll make twenty-five machine deal with you because then it's okay, because I am getting something out of [it]." Vishal did not agree and did not refuse — "Let me come look at it first. Let me go look at it. If it makes sense, I'll get to it." The notes record the 25-machine deal as **parked** pending the demo. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]

## How the number was reached

The negotiation is worth recording because Val ran it deliberately rather than arriving at a list price. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]

- **Opening position: $2,000.** "We usually offer $2,000 for a cabinet. Name your price, I want to make you happy. It's only 25 games. But be fair with me because I'm looking for a long-term relationship. Don't screw me, be fair and I'll facilitate this conversion."
- **Val handed over the pen.** "Give me a pen. Give me a piece of paper… You're a fair man. You write the deal. I will honor the deal that you will write." Vishal declined to write it and instead worked from the machine price, proposing he pay a $1,500 difference against the $2,000 credit; Val walked him back — "Two thousand I'm giving you. You're paying twelve five."
- **The counter was $7,000.** "We paid what thousand dollars? Thirteen two. Can you buy it for seven thousand? I am asking. See, don't let me do this." Val: "The answer is no, but I want you to try it."
- **Val's justification for refusing the higher number** was the cabinet's age and earned return: "you had the machine for what, three years, four years? So it paid. It paid plenty of money," and later "You're selling me a four-year-old machine that paid you four times over. You're selling it to me for five thousand dollars."
- **The close was a cash-conversion argument**, not a price argument: "those machines are sitting here for me" / "And doing nothing. And you can turn it into cash."

## Why the transactional condition

Val was explicit that the 25-machine price only works if JVL gets distribution for its transactional products in return. The bundle he described as included — a **POS terminal at the attendance counter**, a **port mounted in each machine** ("like an extra screen… has an optical reader QR code"), and the future **ATM Recycler** pending licensing — he valued at "**Each unit is one thousand dollars**… I'm including all of this in the price. This is an incredible value." He also pressed for installation rather than evaluation: "Just install it. Don't show it." Device detail on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]

## Sept 1 2026 — the follow-up demo: no deal, a different price, and a different count

At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-01-narayani-akshar-mega-hit-demo-and-trade-in.md|Sept-1 demo]] the deal was supposed to close. It did not, because [[wiki/people/vishal-patel.md|Vishal Patel]] did not come — [[wiki/people/neel.md|Neel]] and [[wiki/people/jai.md|Jai]] attended and cannot commit. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

- **The price is open again, and Val set the band himself.** "**I think you asked for five thousand and I agreed to three thousand.** I think we're quite a bit apart, twenty six and twenty one. But the most important thing for me is when that deal is, because I need to move the game to the secondary market. If it's later, then I am not sure that I can take the game. **We shall make a deal somewhere in between five and three.**" *(The figures "twenty six and twenty one" are unexplained in the source. In context they most likely refer to **cabinet counts** — the Aug-26 record says ~25, Neel now says 21 — but nothing in the transcript says so, and no reading is asserted here.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]
- **The count came back as 21, with the version still unchecked.** "Did you say you have 21 games in the warehouse? Which games is that? Is that dual screen?" — "**We have two of those original ones, but I might be using some of them**," and later "I would have to look at the [exact] version at the end." Condition: "**The games are good. I guess they have some minor software errors**… **They're fully functioning.** Some of them show the battery things on the screen." Val: "The software, I don't care, I don't worry, but they're fully functioning." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]
- **Val's urgency is a real outside deal, and it has a cost attached.** "**I have a secondary market for them**, so whatever discount we agreed upon, I need to be able to get it from the secondary market… I need to know exactly how many games… **It will cost me roughly a thousand dollars per game to recondition it.** Now I have secondary market. **Tomorrow I don't know.**" And at the close: "**We're discussing a deal in a different state**, so if we are taking your trade in, then I'll move that trade into another deal. **That's why I'm rushing you to make that deal**… **Otherwise I will take somebody else's deal.**" *(The other state is not named.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]
- **The offer to go to them.** [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]]: "**Val will be here next week. If [Vishal] would like to meet to close this deal, we're more than happy to go to your office. We just need to pull the trigger when you say.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]
- **The transactional condition was not tested.** The equipment was demonstrated, but the men who saw it cannot decide, so the ~25-machine question went back to Vishal unanswered. See [[wiki/organizations/narayani-amusements.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

## Open contradictions

**What was actually agreed on Aug 26 2026 — $5,000 per cabinet, or $3,000?** Two accounts of the same negotiation, six days apart. Recorded, not resolved.

- **Aug 26, in the room:** Val offers "**four thousand plus one thousand for that contract. I'll give you five thousand dollars for this deal only for the three machines**," conditioned on the three-year data contract, and Vishal answers "**Fine.**" The derived notes of that meeting record the outcome as "D27 trade-in at **$5,000 each** ($4,000 + $1,000 data contract)." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]
- **Sept 1, at JVL's office:** Val describes the same negotiation as unfinished and the positions as still apart — "**I think you asked for five thousand and I agreed to three thousand**… **We shall make a deal somewhere in between five and three.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]
- **Neither source reconciles them.** Two things are worth noting without resolving anything: Val prefaces the Sept-1 version with "**I think**," and the men he says it to — Neel and Jai — **were not at the Aug-26 meeting**, so nobody in the room could correct him. Nothing in either source says the Aug-26 terms were withdrawn, re-negotiated or superseded. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

# ARC finishes the current deal first, then buys at full price

Stated by [[wiki/people/georgios-zazanis.md|George Zazanis]], COO of [[wiki/organizations/arc-gaming.md|ARC Gaming]], to [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-13-arc-zazanis-lunch-trade-in-and-2027-volume.md|Oct-13 lunch]]. The derived notes list the first and third points as decisions made by George [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]].

## What was decided

- **No 65 machines before Christmas.** Val: "**can you take the sixty-five machines before Christmas or not?**" George: "**No, we don't have the locations to put them. No way.**" Storing them in ARC's warehouse came up and was not agreed: "**I have to convince our guys.**" George also noted JVL had been late: "**And you guys were delayed.**" Val: "**We were.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]]
- **Finish the current deal first.** "**For the time being, we have to finish that 60… So far, so good. Meaning that the machines are performing very well, and everybody wants to put them out.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]]
- **After the promotion, full price.** Val: "**I don't ask me for the same deal I gave you.**" George: "**when we reach at the point that we say, guys, the promotion finished now… Then it's gonna be full price. Whatever is your full price at the moment.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]]
- **A new JVL model is a new deal.** "**If tomorrow, next month, you come up with a new machine, this not part of this deal. The new machine is a new machine, guys. We made this deal with the current one.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]]

## 2027 volume — George's estimate

- **ARC's total.** "**If we go with today's numbers, anywhere between 120, 150 machines total**", from "**Eight, nine**" suppliers [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]].
- **JVL's share.** "**We can put eight machines per month from me**" (said first in Portuguese, "Oito máquinas por mês"). Val called it a conservative estimate; George: "**it's not a conservative; it's going to be very accurate… Not only to buy, but to stock**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]].
- **Why not faster.** A new team now decides where machines go: "**before I was responsible of… deciding the locations that the machines go. Now there is a different team that decides this, and they do it slower than me.**" And cash: "**I cannot do so much at the same time. I have to get some money back… the capital is not unlimited.**" Machines are placed "**progressively**" so they do not sit in the warehouse [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]].
- **What Val asked for.** "**I would be very happy with ten machines a month… Full price… And if you can influence maybe another one or two operators, that would help me a great deal.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]]
- **George on JVL as a supplier.** "**Your price and overall the services are very good… you guys are in a very good position.**" Compare ARC's Aug-27 view that JVL "always had service issues" — see [[wiki/organizations/arc-gaming.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]].

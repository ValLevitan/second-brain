# JVL 2025 strategic plan adopted

## What was decided

JVL adopted a written **2025 strategic plan** built around competing as a small, fast, agile player against larger rivals [[raw/jvl_strategic_plan_and_vision_2025_2025-01-08.md]].

## The four strategic pillars

1. **Games Development & Production** — rapid, market-specific variants (e.g. the Fortune Fu Paws series), Florida-market games, HHR games [[raw/jvl_strategic_plan_and_vision_2025_2025-01-08.md]].
2. **Distribution Infrastructure** — multi-market deployment (Puerto Rico, Georgia); Flex-Pay, Flex-Port, and a Mobile Administration App [[raw/jvl_strategic_plan_and_vision_2025_2025-01-08.md]].
3. **Core Technology** — the OMNI Platform, Binary Acceptance Criteria, Test-360 automated-testing architecture [[raw/jvl_strategic_plan_and_vision_2025_2025-01-08.md]].
4. **DevSecOps** — migration to Microsoft Business Central and GIT, identity management, secrets server, on-premise infrastructure optimization [[raw/jvl_strategic_plan_and_vision_2025_2025-01-08.md]].

## Execution

- Val is personally grooming the plan into a working document tied to JIRA initiative-level tasks, aiming to review it with the team around Monday **2025-01-13** [[raw/jira_value_stream_distribution_infrastructure_january_7_2025.md]].
- The JIRA value stream is named to match: Games, Distribution Infrastructure, Core Tech, and DevOps [[raw/jira_value_stream_distribution_infrastructure_january_7_2025.md]].

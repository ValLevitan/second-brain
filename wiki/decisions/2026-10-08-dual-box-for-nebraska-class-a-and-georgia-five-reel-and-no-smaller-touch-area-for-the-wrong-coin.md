# Dual box for Nebraska, Class A and Georgia 5-reel; no smaller touch area for the wrong coin

Taken by [[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael Gottlieb]] at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-08-game-design-class-a-party-time-and-dual-box.md|Oct 8 2026, 12:00 PM game-design meeting]] (English), with [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena Zhukova]] and [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Design_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Dual_Box-October-8-2026.md]]. The dual box is the "pick the larger coin" skill overlay; its history is on [[wiki/topics/slot-game-design-principles.md]].

## Efim's idea, rejected

[[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim Podvoiski]] looked at the select-a-coin feature in Nebraska and Class A and suggested making the wrong choice harder — "Make a box actually a touch box smaller". Michael: "I don't think so. I think we're already on the edge of what is appropriate, and I wouldn't want to give anybody any reason to make a video that they could post on YouTube." And: "these players know… Exactly how to play these games… they have no difficulty whatsoever making the correct decision. So I don't think we're doing ourselves any favors by deviating from what kind of the norm is" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Design_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Dual_Box-October-8-2026.md]].

## Where the dual box sits

- **Val's idea, credited.** "originally we had the dual box and it sat higher… Val is 100% right. Having it lower right above the button panel, that's exactly where it should be", because the arm movement is more natural and less tiring, "especially if you're elderly" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Design_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Dual_Box-October-8-2026.md]].
- **Nebraska keeps its current panel.** Lena: "for Nebraska, we are keeping it as it is. So we are not making it like a small panel." Michael: "that'll be for class A" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Design_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Dual_Box-October-8-2026.md]].
- **Both markets get the dual box.** Val asked if it was only for Class A. Michael: "No, no, we're going to have dual box in Nebraska and in Class A. It's just the Class A implementation is more similar to what our competitors are doing, which is the location of it" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Design_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Dual_Box-October-8-2026.md]].

## Georgia

Val: "Would we consider doing it for Georgia as well?" Michael: "We are, absolutely… We are going to do it." The open choice is "5 reel dual box, three reel nudge, or whether we just do dual box across the map", because competitors use nudge on 3-reel and dual box on 5-reel. "we know out the gate that the five reels are going to be dual box." For 3-reel he suspects nudge feels like completion with only 9 symbols on screen, and cites an Aristocrat "midnight train" game that nudges from worthless to valuable symbols. On 5-reel, dual box removes the nudge compromise for hold-and-respin games and Jackpot Rush, so games can play like the Class 3 version; "Firey Ice is going to play better". He is "still trying to understand why Aristocrat and IGT and WMS are still, or Light and Wonder are still doing Nudge for three reelers", and once saw both in the same cabinet. Val: "My personal opinion that dual box is the same here" (garbled; the notes read it as Val favouring dual box). Michael: "It's good, it's very, very good, I agree." The 3-reel choice will be revisited "when we have to pull that trigger" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Design_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Dual_Box-October-8-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Game_Design_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Dual_Box-October-8-2026.md]].

## Update — Oct 23 2026: Nebraska keeps the larger box; the box now comes before the rack

At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-23-game-development-dual-box-rule-and-colombia-class-3.md|Oct-23 2:00 PM game-development meeting]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Dual_Box_Rule_and_Colombia_Class_3-October-23-2026.md]]:

- **Nebraska keeps its larger dual box (confirms Oct 8).** Val asked why Nebraska does not use the same see-through selection box as Class A. Michael: in Nebraska "our competitors are doing the larger dual box implementation… I think it's actually easier to understand… there's less probability of making a mistake"; the Class A look follows that market. "I think they're market appropriate implementations." Nobody, Juan included, has said the Nebraska dual box is a problem — "Do no harm." Class A gets semi-transparent "Liquid Glass" dialog boxes — [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-23-class-a-is-the-first-nova-game-project-ahead-of-south-dakota-d27-and-val-keeps-that-priority.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Dual_Box_Rule_and_Colombia_Class_3-October-23-2026.md]]
- **New timing rule.** On a line win the dual box comes up first and the win is racked only after the choice; on a Match 3 progressive or free-spins trigger the box reads "to access bonus". The same approach goes to Class A. This changes **when** the box appears, not **where** — [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-23-nebraska-dual-box-comes-before-the-rack-and-reads-to-access-bonus-when-a-bonus-is-triggered.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Dual_Box_Rule_and_Colombia_Class_3-October-23-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Game_Development_Dual_Box_Rule_and_Colombia_Class_3-October-23-2026.md]]

# Val discloses the InComm displacement intent to ARC's CEO

The first time in the corpus that **JVL's intent to displace [[wiki/organizations/incom.md|InComm]] as the back-end processor is stated out loud to anyone outside JVL.** Said by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] to [[wiki/people/david-sloan.md|David Sloan]] at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-09-arc-gaming-backend-displacement-negotiation.md|Sept-9 2026 4:35 PM meeting]], unprompted, immediately after describing the bundle. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

## What was said

Twice, in almost the same words. First, as the last clause of the bundle description: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

> "we give away a kiosk, a point of sale, and five more ports, which go into other machines. So we're virtually converting the entire location to us. **On the back end, we're looking to replace [InComm].**"

Then, minutes later, with the confidentiality frame attached: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

> "**I'm buying the business and the hope that I can replace [InComm]. This is highly confidential. We don't have a confidentiality agreement, but you're a senior executive, so I can trust you. Let's just not talk about it. By the way, George does not know about our intent to replace [InComm]. He's the only one who knows.** You're the only one in the industry who knows, and the reason I'm telling you. And the reason you know is because **if I get your account, it's a big deal.**"

*(The passage contradicts itself as transcribed — "George does not know… He's the only one who knows" — and the derived notes read the whole turn as: George does not know, and Sloan is the only person in the industry who does. **Recorded with the garble visible; the notes' reading is labelled as theirs.**)* [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

And, earlier, as the sharp half of the question Val used to test the kiosk relationship: "**Aside from these kiosks, I'm looking at taking the business from [InComm].**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

## What the decision actually is

Three things were decided in the same breath, and only the first is a commercial position: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

1. **The strategic intent itself** — the free hardware is not the product. The hardware converts the location; the back end is what JVL is buying its way toward. This is the first source in which the giveaway thread and the processor thread are joined out loud into one plan.
2. **To disclose it to a counterparty, with no NDA in place**, on the stated ground that the man is a senior executive. Val named the absence of an agreement himself rather than being caught by it.
3. **To withhold it from [[wiki/people/georgios-zazanis.md|Zazanis]]**, ARC's own COO and JVL's existing relationship at the company — so the CEO now holds something his COO does not.

**§15.8:** clear — commercial strategy and processor economics only; no named-person compensation.

## The timeline behind it: buy it or build it

Sloan asked how quickly JVL could get there. Val's answer separates the two routes and commits to neither: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

> "I don't know. **If I want to go with somebody else, the solution is available to me now. But I don't know if that somebody else is the right solution.**" … "**If I have to do it myself, then half a year.**"

Sloan's reply — "**We thought there was somebody as well**" — is left as spoken; the source names no partner on either side. The unnamed partner is **not** identified in either source and **no candidate is asserted here**. The corpus's nearest live partnership thread is [[wiki/organizations/play-payments.md|Play Payments]] (see [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-val-proposes-fifty-percent-of-play-payments.md]]), but nothing in this meeting connects the two. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

## Why Sloan's account is the one Val wanted to hear it

Sloan did the size-of-the-prize arithmetic himself, unprompted, and reached a number per day — detail and verbatim figures on [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]]. His own conclusion: "**It's a great business model**," followed immediately by asking how fast Val could deliver it. That is the reaction the disclosure was made to produce. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

## What it cost, measured after the fact

Val confirmed on 2026-09-10 that the exclusivity he was reaching for **was not obtained** — see [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-arc-account-exclusivity-sought-and-not-obtained.md]]. The derived notes draw the consequence plainly: the confidential disclosure, made with no NDA, "**bought less than it was offered against**." Val's own overall assessment of the meeting is nonetheless **positive**, because the broader agenda of winning the account is working. Both readings are recorded. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

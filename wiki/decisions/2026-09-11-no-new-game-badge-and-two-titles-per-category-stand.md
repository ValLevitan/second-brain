# No "new game" badge, and two titles per category stand

Two separate home-screen questions [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] raised at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-11-rage-sunset-and-class-a-on-nova.md|Sept 11 2026 1:00 PM game-development call]]. [[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael Gottlieb]] refused both, Val said plainly that he disagreed, and both stand as they were. They are recorded together because the same authority principle closed each one. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]

## 1. How many games on the home screen — two per category stands

- **Val's probe.** Whether Georgia V3 could show **all fifteen** games. He reported the memory objection as answered: "**And Pavel and Irina don't see an issue with it**", against [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim]]'s "**Should be it should be tested, but most likely it cannot**" and Michael's "**That's what somebody said last Friday.**" Val was explicit it was not a direction: "**I don't know. I'm asking. I'm asking. I'm probing.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]
- **Michael's refusal was categorical.** "**No, absolutely not**" / "**I think it's an awful idea**", for two reasons: [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan]] "**had said many, many, many times that he needs additional games that are not enabled for flexibility purposes. Different locations may react to different games differently**" — the worked example being "**they don't like Crescent City Carnival. You disable Crescent City Carnival and you enable Rodeo Queen**" to refresh a location — and "**the paradox of choice. You give the player too many choices, they go into overwhelm.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]
- **Val pushed back on the certainty, not the answer.** "**Why do you say so categorically?**"; his own tested band is "**anywhere between… Six and 12 is kind of a tested… environment and accepted environment**", and whether 14 or 15 is overwhelming is "**questionable. I wouldn't be so categorical about… it being it wrong.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]
- **Efim put a number on the optimum, and disclaimed it.** "**I think that 12 is not optimal already**… **my guess is optimum is about like 6 to 9 somewhere and 12 is acceptable**", then "**it's in my opinion, it's not, I cannot confirm it in any way, scientifically.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]
- **Val's real question was the category depth** — "**I'm questioning whether two games per category is a good choice**… **maybe we should have four games at least per category.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]
- **Michael's answer, which won it: shipping four burns titles for nothing.** He traced it back to [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]]'s rejection of the earlier any-bet Jackpot Rush work — "**Felipe said, I don't want this. And he cited that the games were too similar**" — and concluded "**I think we're burning titles for no justifiable reason… I don't think that there's enough differentiation between the Jackpot Rush titles to justify that… giving them two Jackpot Rush titles to choose from, if you like that style of game, two is perfectly adequate.**" The same argument for Fiery Ice: "**the hold and re-spin mechanic does remain consistent amongst all the titles.**" The alternative he proposed instead is rotation on evidence: "**if in the overall earnings of the V3 release, we find that one of the Jackpot Rushes is just bottom of the barrel, well, we disable that one. We enable a different Jackpot Rush game.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]
- **His evidence for why a re-skin is worth keeping in reserve.** "**sometimes the same damn game in blue, makes three times the money than the one in red does. And nobody can explain it. But we saw it at Bally with the different, you know, blazing sevens, this and wild red, wild blue**" — and a folk explanation he offered without endorsing it, that the local football team's colours were red. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]
- **Val closed it without being convinced.** "**Okay, let's go with that. I don't know whether it's right or wrong, but let's go with it.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]

*(The memory question itself was never settled — see Open questions. It is moot for now only because the home screen is not changing.)*

## 2. No "new game" badge — not even for three days

- **Val's question**, after working through what a player sees when a location swaps titles overnight ("**The same interface, but the icons change**"): "**Do we have, let's say, new game stamp, or do we have something in advertising?**" Michael: "**No. No, we don't have anything along those lines.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]
- **Val asked whether that was an omission or a position.** "**Is it because we didn't have time to think about it, or is it because we are set, we have an opinion that we shouldn't be advertising new game?**" Michael: "**Yes, we have an opinion that we shouldn't be advertising new game. It doesn't. It's just not a thing.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]
- **The reason, in one line:** "**Players don't care if a game is new or not, they care if it has progressives**… **And they care if it pays.**" On Val's fallback of a three-day flag — "**Not, not even, not even for the first three days**" — Michael: "**Clutter**", "**I don't think it makes any difference. I've never seen anybody do it**", "**I think it's just noise and a distraction.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]
- **The case he built around it was marketing simplification.** The old "new and improved" grocery-shelf layering, then the Apple case study — "**When Steve Jobs came back to Apple, they had like 14 different Macintoshes**… **he killed all of them except for three**" — car makers cutting model counts, and the constraint that actually applies: "**we have very, very limited real estate. So with the incredibly small amount of real estate that you have, what is it that you want to communicate and you want to sell?**" He named his own aesthetic — "**the minimalism of Tesla**" — and Val disputed how widely it appeals. Fuller treatment on [[wiki/topics/slot-game-design-principles.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]
- **Efim agreed, on timing rather than principle.** A passer-by who sees a new game on the attraction advertisement "**might try it**", and whether that happens the day of the swap or a week later "**I think it doesn't**" matter. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]
- **Lena added what the player does get:** "**during attraction mode, you will see the big logo and characters and full-screen video of all games.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]

## The authority principle, in Val's own words

This is the part worth keeping. He dissented, on the record, and then explicitly declined to use his position [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rage_Sunset_and_Class_A_on_Nova-September-11-2026.md]]:

> "**Look, I'm trying to develop our own philosophy, right or wrong. And I'm hearing things from you I disagree with, but I want us to have our own philosophy. And I'm not in charge of the philosophy, and I'm not setting it up. So I don't want to tell you what to do, but I'm raising questions that… To me, sound logical. I disagree that we shouldn't show new game at least for three days period. But I don't direct the design, so… That's it.**"

This restates, in a new context, the authority line already recorded from May 14 2026 — that lineup decision authority sits with Juan and Michael, not Val ("I will never say no to something that you should be saying yes or no") — see [[wiki/projects/south-dakota-vlt-market.md]]. Treated as **consistent, not new**.

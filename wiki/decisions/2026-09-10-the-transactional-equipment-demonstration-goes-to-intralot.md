# The transactional equipment demonstration goes to Intralot

Agreed between [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] and [[wiki/organizations/georgia-lottery-corporation.md|Georgia Lottery]] COAM staff at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-10-georgia-lottery-coam-manufacturer-introduction.md|Sept 10 2026 introductory meeting]]. Small in itself, but it is the first time JVL's transactional line gets a **named place to be inspected by the regulator** after the August show demonstration that caused the trouble — see [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]] and [[wiki/people/joe-kim.md|Joe Kim]]. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]]

## How it was settled

- **Val offered.** "**Would you like to see my equipment here? Should I set it up for you?**" — and, on cost, "**Much cheaper for me to bring it here and set it up for you here**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]].
- **The staff steered it to Intralot, tied to a second want.** "**I know that [Leaf] has worked with Bill at [Intralot] quite a bit, and we've got an older version of the cabinet, and we had inquired if we could get an updated version of the product… So if you wanted to… kind of kill two birds with one stone. There, we could actually accept that technology at Intralot as well as a setup**" *(the transcript renders the company "Interlock" here and "Intralot" elsewhere in the same passage; the name at the start of the sentence, rendered "Leaf", is not interpretable and no person is asserted)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]].
- **Val refused to read between the lines.** "**You prefer for me to set it up at Intralot or here? I… don't read between the lines. I… need a binary answer.**" The answer was the lab-space and heat problem: "**Yeah, yes. We've got limited space… we put too many games in there; gets real hot. So we… do have Intralot… host a lot of the prototypes**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]].
- **The decision.** Val: "**I'll set it up with Intralot… And you're welcome. You're welcome to look at it… we're dealing with Bill. He's great**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]].

## Two small facts recorded in the same exchange

- **Val is not going to G2E.** Asked "**Are you going to be at G Two E?**" he answered "**I can't afford it**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]].
- **The Lottery missed JVL's booth at the Southern Amusement Expo** and apologised for it: "**my apologies for not making it by the booth at the Southern Amusement Expo. We had a couple of things that we lined up, and then we had a presentation coming in, so we were a little pressed for time this year**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]].

## Update, Oct 13 2026 — at Intralot: the transactional equipment needs no testing there; the updated cabinet is still wanted

At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-13-intralot-machine-lockouts-test-lab-and-route-partnership.md|Oct-13 meeting at Intralot]] Val asked directly about Flex-IQ, the kiosk and Flexport: "**Do we need to test this here? Is it something that we need to bring in here, test it, set it up?**" The answers [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Intralot_Machine_Lockouts_Test_Lab_and_Route_Partnership-October-13-2026.md]]:

- [[wiki/people/christos-tzoumaras.md|Chris]] ([[wiki/organizations/intralot.md|Intralot]]): "**We don't connect with these gears. We have nothing to do.**" Val: "**Intralot has nothing to do.**"
- The Lottery representative: "**That's all outside of the game.**" Val's summary: "**My point is that no, I don't have to test anything. I don't have to bring anything, right?**"

So the equipment needs **no testing** at Intralot (**evolution**). Whether the Lottery still wants to **see** it set up there — the Sept-10 offer — was not raised; the commitment below stays open but is now in doubt. The Lottery representative did repeat the cabinet request: the prototype in the lab "**is the old tabletop**", and "**if we can get that upgraded… that will certainly help**". That need is now folded into the ten-machine lab test — [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-13-ten-jvl-machines-go-into-intralots-lab-now-without-waiting-for-the-lottery.md]].

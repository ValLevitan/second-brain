# No private calls with the AI PM; it should speak at the meeting

Set by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-18-flex-port-sas-igt-lock-and-emulator-requirements.md|Oct 18 2026, 7:00 AM call]] (Russian), on a proposal from [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]], who agreed [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex-Port_SAS_IGT_Lock_and_Emulator_Requirements-October-18-2026.md]]. The notes list it as Val's decision, Pavel agreeing [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Flex-Port_SAS_IGT_Lock_and_Emulator_Requirements-October-18-2026.md]]. The whole AI PM picture is on [[wiki/projects/ai-project-manager-and-company-second-brain.md]].

## How the AI PM is doing (Pavel)

- It creates few tasks so far and sends questions from the meeting to the chat — things the team must not forget. Of useful tasks it made "может там 45 было за за эту неделю" (heard "45"; the notes read it as a handful — **unclear**) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex-Port_SAS_IGT_Lock_and_Emulator_Requirements-October-18-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Flex-Port_SAS_IGT_Lock_and_Emulator_Requirements-October-18-2026.md]].
- Val: "мы специально его так консервативно настроили." Pavel: "мы пока не хотим, чтобы у нас был вал из 100 задач"; it opened tasks where people clearly said a task is needed [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex-Port_SAS_IGT_Lock_and_Emulator_Requirements-October-18-2026.md]].
- A good sign: some people now ask for the text transcript of a recorded meeting "я сам создам задачи по этому митингу" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex-Port_SAS_IGT_Lock_and_Emulator_Requirements-October-18-2026.md]].

## The proposal and the ruling

- **Pavel's idea.** Each participant already gets text from the PM tied to his own work and questions. A next step: let anyone call the PM privately and dictate things about his questions, "чтоб не тратить общее время" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex-Port_SAS_IGT_Lock_and_Emulator_Requirements-October-18-2026.md]].
- **Val: no.** Private talks would leave "вакуум… между всеми"; when each person says at the meeting which task he needs, "тогда все своим ухам это ловят." Pavel: "Согласен… если все начнут уходить… в личное общение с пимом… трудно будет поймать что чтото они всётаки наговорили не то." Val: "Поэтому да, я в эту сторону не пошёл бы." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex-Port_SAS_IGT_Lock_and_Emulator_Requirements-October-18-2026.md]]
- **The direction instead.** Val: let the PM talk at the meeting and ask its own questions — e.g. it has collected questions over the week and asks Andrei, Vadim, Sergey in turn whether a task should be opened, or whether it understood correctly. Pavel: the PM already personalises questions; the next step is to add voice and, as the last item of the meeting, give the PM the floor for a summary and the clarifying questions or proposals it has [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex-Port_SAS_IGT_Lock_and_Emulator_Requirements-October-18-2026.md]].
- **A two-week look-back.** Val: the PM may keep "максимум за 2 недели" and say that a topic came up two or three times at those meetings "но задача на эту тему нету" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex-Port_SAS_IGT_Lock_and_Emulator_Requirements-October-18-2026.md]].
- **Voice.** Val: "Андрей занимается войс… И обещал помоему к 8 ноября это сделать." Pavel: "Да." ("Андрей" = [[wiki/people/andrey-bosik.md|Andrey Bosik]], the AI PM owner — **inference**; he was not present.) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex-Port_SAS_IGT_Lock_and_Emulator_Requirements-October-18-2026.md]]

Val closed: "Окей, хорошо, я доволен, куда мы идём." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex-Port_SAS_IGT_Lock_and_Emulator_Requirements-October-18-2026.md]]

# Engage a Poland/Belarus contractor for the FlexPay discovery phase (Nov 24 2024)

On the Nov 24 2024 online-strategy update, [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] told [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] he had **selected a contractor for the FlexPay project** and planned to "shake hands" with them the following Monday, request the contract, send mockups, and start signing. [[raw/andrei_klimovich_update_report_online_strategy1_2025-03-29.md]]

## What was decided

- Proceed with a selected contractor: a **~5-year-old company with a legal entity in Poland and a development team in Belarus**. [[raw/andrei_klimovich_update_report_online_strategy1_2025-03-29.md]]
- Their relevant experience: a **mobile app for monitoring industrial refrigerators** (many real-time parameters pushed to a phone) and a **delivery-service custom cabinet/app** (order dispatch, address-book management). [[raw/andrei_klimovich_update_report_online_strategy1_2025-03-29.md]]
- Scope the **discovery phase at ~$8,000**, producing a **detailed specification + mockups** (and possibly an interactive Figma prototype) to serve as the basis for a later **fixed-price** build project. [[raw/andrei_klimovich_update_report_online_strategy1_2025-03-29.md]]
- Next step: schedule broader **stakeholder meetings** so responsibilities/availability are aligned before the contractor starts. [[raw/andrei_klimovich_update_report_online_strategy1_2025-03-29.md]]

## Context and open thread

This is the earliest recorded step on the **FlexPay mobile app** track — see [[wiki/topics/flexpay-mobile-app-and-service-advocates.md]]. **Inference / flag:** by Jan 2025 the wiki shows FlexPay work being carried by JVL's own embedded engineers, and the mobile-app persona work (Jan 13 2025) described the app as low-urgency and subcontracted; whether this Nov-2024 external discovery contractor was actually engaged, delivered, or was later superseded is **not confirmed by any later source** — recorded as active as-of Nov 24 2024 and flagged (see Owner Decisions).

# Georgia V4 with perceived-persistence games; Colombia gets 6–12 Class 3 games on NOVA before December 2027 (Oct 30 2026)

Set by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] at the end of the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-30-game-development-class-a-party-time-and-colombia-refresh.md|Oct 30 2026, 2:00 PM game-development meeting]] (English), on questions from [[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael Gottlieb]] and [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena Zhukova]], with [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim Podvoiski]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].

## Georgia V4

- **The question.** Michael: "Are we planning on bringing this new curve cabinet to the Georgia show in the fall and is there another software release that should go with that? Basically V43 or… V4? Because we've got V3 right now" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **The ruling.** Val: "no doubts that there will be another Georgia software. It must be." Michael: "I have it planned… I think what the games are going to be. There's a couple of more ambitious products in there, some perceived persistence, which is a space that we need to get into." Val: "I am all for it… I'm calling it large format games. Similar to what you guys did with Bison, but perceived persistence is absolutely the right way to go." Michael later: "they'll be a Georgia V4 release. I've got that blocked out and we know what… the titles are" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]]. The cabinet question (curved cabinet at the show) was not answered on the transcript.
- **Earlier.** V4 was a Jira placeholder with a preliminary game list on Oct 8 — [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-04-georgia-v4-mechanical-button-deck.md]] (Oct-8 update) and [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]] (Oct 8). Oct 30 confirms the release will happen; it does not set a date. **Evolution.**

## Colombia — the refresh

- **Why now.** Val: "I would love to do something in Columbia [Colombia] to renew our Park of Games because… our contracts expire December 2017, 2027 [heard; read as December 2027]. So the entire install base need to be refreshed" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]]. This dates the "end of 2027" deadline of [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-23-jvl-stays-out-of-casino-class-3-and-a-colombia-refresh-on-nova-starts-from-what-earns-there-now.md|Oct 23]].
- **What.** Val: "Bison and wolves brought into that market in class 3. And the lions" — "take your updated games for Georgia and made them into Class Three" for Colombia. Michael: "Six game pack, 12 game pack. Don't know yet." Val: "Minimum 6, maximum 12" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **Platform and hardware.** Lena: "Should it be Nova release or?" Val: "Nova, Nova, Nova." The machines: "Most of the machines horizontal though. Dual Screens." Michael asked if the cabinets have V-decks; Val: yes (heard "All the decks") [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **Maths.** Michael: "did we do Columbia specific math because their denom is different than American denom?" Efim: "No, no." Michael: "Just class 3 math." Efim agreed (a garbled word follows — **unclear**) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **Game mix — left to Michael and Lena.** Michael floated "2 releases… each release… like 6 lines and waves games, 2 Jackpot Rush games, and two Fiery Ice games", as in Georgia. Val: "I'm the wrong person to talk to on this." Michael: "we'll just come up with a list of games and we'll do the games"; dual-screen versions of Jackpot Rush and Fiery Ice are coming. Val said "Fiery ice product did not do well in Columbia"; Michael: Fiery Ice never went there — "Diamond Link did not do well in Columbia"; Val accepted ("Not done, yeah") [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **A Colombian theme?** Michael wants "market insight" to do "something more tailored specifically to Columbia in the same way that we did… Calle Ocho… for the Cubans" — not sure Colombians care for Cuba; "I want to be in alignment with what's cool in Columbia" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].
- **The data Michael has.** "I got a list, Val, of the top performing games in Columbia… pub crawl is actually pretty darn popular, which is not a theme that I think makes any sense" — perhaps a local pub or cantina theme on the same maths; "the fruit games are super popular down there." Val: "used to be… I think they outlive themselves. My personal… opinion." Efim collected the data "for the last two years"; Michael asked for "just the last six months to see if… the fruit games fall to the bottom of the list"; Efim worried the set would be "too small" but agreed: "I'll do it. No problem. Easy." Michael: "a number of these games could use some updated art" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]].

## How to ask the market — Martha

Val: "I would suggest having a meeting with Martha, but if you have a set of questions that you want her to answer… She is quite binary, like digital, very structured… Give her a list of questions, have her reply. Give her another list of verification questions. She will go research and she'll reply. Her answer will be factual based on the research. It will not be an opinion." She will take the questions to "casino operators" and report "I've collected replies from 5 casino operators." Michael will also try to "connect with her on a human level" about what is popular there. Val: "Good luck to you" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Class_A_Party_Time_and_Colombia_Refresh-October-30-2026.md]]. By the Colombia context this is [[wiki/people/marta.md|Marta]] — **inference**; the transcript gives no surname. Personal matters in this passage are not recorded.

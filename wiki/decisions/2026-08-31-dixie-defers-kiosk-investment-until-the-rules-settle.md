# Dixie Amusement will not buy kiosks or transactional equipment until the rules settle

An **operator-side** decision, stated to [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] in person at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-31-dixie-amusement-kiosk-wait-and-see.md|Aug-31 2026 visit]]. It is recorded here because it is the clearest statement in the corpus of **why Georgia operators are not buying the product category JVL is building**, and because the reasoning — not the refusal — is the useful part. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]

## What was decided

[[wiki/people/salim-khambawala.md|Salim Khambawala]] of [[wiki/organizations/dixie-amusement.md|Dixie Amusement]] put it in one sentence: "**because of all these reasons, I decided not to invest and wait out.**" The notes record it as "a deliberate wait until the regulator, lawmakers and manufacturers settle, so he does not pay two or three times." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]

This is a hold, not a rejection of JVL: he is simultaneously running **two JVL machines on trial** and accepted their $290/day average without argument (see [[wiki/organizations/dixie-amusement.md]]). What he will not buy is **redemption/transactional equipment**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]

## The three reasons, in his order

**1. The regulator will not give a clear answer, and has an interest in not giving one.** "they are not giving clear answers… **day by day they talk more and make it more muddier**… **The worst reason is they know that's where they make funds. So when they [find] someone, they get fined. And they don't want to pick sides of anyone.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]

**2. He has watched this exact cycle before, and paying twice is the predictable outcome.** On the 2013 law: "I don't think the lottery [moved fast] in **2013** when they implemented this law. **It took them at least two years to give us a clear** [answer]." On today: "**Gift cards got effective July first. Still, they are [arguing] cash with ATM, cash with no ATM. It's okay. It's not okay.**" And the conclusion: "**my personal idea of not investing… And now I invest. Then again I have to invest. Then again I. That's what happened in 2013.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]

**3. No vendor — JVL included — has a finished product.** "it's still unclear from the lottery side, from the regulatory side, and from the distributor or the manufacturer side… **my general idea to everyone, whoever is making a kiosk, nobody has yet figured out an end-to-end system.**" He qualified it as a market judgment rather than a verdict on JVL specifically: "**So I've seen all your system, and I've seen everybody's system, not just yours.**" His diagnosis of the mismatch: "**Technology, I think, runs faster than the law**… Everyone has it. You name it. Your company has it. IGT has it… **But which direction to implement it?**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]

## The political timeline behind the hold

This is the part of the meeting that dates the decision. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]

- **The gift card began as a pilot five years ago, explicitly to get cash out.** "For this gift card, we started a **pilot five years ago**. The intent of that gift card was to eliminate [cash] **because the Georgia Constitution doesn't allow it**." He restates the intent unambiguously later: "**five years ago we told them that the reason we wanted a gift card is not to have cash.**" *(The first rendering of the word is "tax" in the transcript; the second passage settles it as **cash**.)*
- **The sequence he gives:** "we talked about it five years before that. Then five years later, we got a pilot program. **Three years ago, we did a pilot program with [InComm]. Two years ago, we put it in the law** [i.e. "**two years ago in 2024, when they regulated it**"]. **And now, July first, it got effective.**"
- **Lawmakers and regulators are annoyed but silent, because of the election.** "**Now those lawmakers and regulators are kind of pissed, but they are not talking right now**… because it's an election. **Midterm election is in November.** Currently they are all looking for funds or votes. **They are not looking for trouble.**"
- **January is when the bill comes due.** "**What happens in January is going to be a tough one for us to answer them**" — the question being why the industry asked for gift cards to remove cash and then went straight back to cash. "**Again, so now we are at square one. I think that's going to be a big bridge to cross. Nothing is impossible.**"
- **The Lottery is not the body that can hurt him.** "**the lottery is just the regulator. They are not the law maker or the enforcer. The enforcer is going to be GBI, the local police department, the local sheriff department.** If they get their hands into it and say… the constitution which they are there to protect and enforce, we see it's [breached]… **It's an uphill battle to fight the state and the attorney general.**" See [[wiki/people/joe-kim.md]] for the Lottery's own posture over the same weeks.
- **On the Lottery's public position so far:** Val pressed — "JVL is showing money coming out. So [Joe], come and see your system, the lottery guy." Salim: "**But he spoke about it. He wasn't clear.** I think he's going to come out… You give them one line, and you talk to five attorneys, and you pay those five attorneys, and tell them how you want that line to be read, and he'll read it that way."
- **What would end the hold, in his own words:** "**once… the final product comes out from the regulator, then the whole industry is going to pick one.**"

## What it means for JVL

- **It is not price resistance and not a product objection**, so a discount or a demo does not move it. The unblocking event is external — a clear regulatory line — and he expects the fight over it in **January 2027**.
- **It is consistent with what JVL already knew but had only second-hand.** Felipe reported on Jul 7 2026 that operators, Salim included, were "moving away from kiosks toward something simpler"; this is the same position stated first-hand and with its reasoning. See [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]
- **Val did not argue the hold.** He agreed with the diagnosis — "**From [the] number side, regulator is one. Manufacturers to develop their solutions. I agree**" — and spent the rest of the meeting on the economics instead (see [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]] and [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-31-transactional-equipment-only-with-location-exclusivity.md]]).

# NOVA: one name, IP-481 as root, one weekly MVP, maths and sound in the brief (Nov 23 2026)

Taken at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-11-23-nova-sync-ip-481-root-and-weekly-mvp-plan.md|Nov 23 2026, ~8:14 AM "NOVA sync meeting"]] (Russian; ~91 minutes), the first working session after the [[wiki/decisions/2026-11-21-biratka-becomes-nova-2-architect-and-coordinator-one-closed-jira-project-no-work-without-capabilities-a-studio-for-non-developers-by-january-15-texas-on-nova-about-february-1-and-georgia-games-on-rk-by-friday.md|Nov-21 Saturday rulings]] made [[wiki/people/alexander-biratka.md|Alexander Biratka]] architect [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]]. Architecture context: [[wiki/topics/nova-platform-architecture.md]].

## 1. What the project is, and the end goal

- **Biratka's overview.** The project is for "создания. И выпуска игр" as a flow, in two parts: the games, and the "Систему, которая помогает нам выпускать эти игры" — tools that move a game "от создания идеи до конечного имиджа". The flow starts with "Агента Евгения, который выпускает бриф"; the brief goes into "студии Антона", which shows step by step what exists and what is missing and ends with a game running "прямо в нашем движке". Still to finish: pieces in LGS, system libraries and the engine; the same on "более дешёвую р к платформу"; and in parallel "набор игр для теста в Техасе, либо в джорджии" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]].
- **Val's end goal.** Street-operation machines have a mobile modem; JVL reads which game parameters work best in a territory, builds new games from those signals, sends them to the lab ("клипс или challine" — read by the notes as Eclipse or GLI), and after approval sends them "Через модом отправлять их обратно… конкретные машины в конкретной лоте". After install the machine must check the lab's signed checksum: "нам нужно проверить этот чек сам после установки" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]]. This restates the end goal on the Nov-21 page.
- **Components, not only games.** [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]]: a single component can be released alone — for a new printer type "нам никто не мешает выпустить Только этот фикс, относящийся к принтеру", leaving the games as they are; an extra point for the architecture [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]].
- **Test "add a game" early.** [[wiki/people/igor-metelski.md|Igor Metelski]]: there are at least two games of one class to try adding a game; Biratka: "обновление игр надо проверить как можно раньше", because it pulls changes "и с рисом, и с LG so… Со статистикой" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]]. No owner was named.
- **Secret inside the company.** Val: "работа, которой вы занимаетесь, она абсолютно секретная внутри компании… другие люди не должны иметь эту информацию" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]].

## 2. The rulings on names, Jira and the MVP

- **"NOVA", not "NOVA-2".** Biratka: rename in Jira "просто нова, потому что путаница сейчас… Давайте нова и всё" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]].
- **IP-481 is the root.** Pavel: "корням всей работы предлагается использовать IP 481" (NOVA Gaming Platform Land-based); all capabilities and MVPs go under it; no objections; Val: "принято". Pavel: it "Находится под встрем IP 90" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]]. Whether the games themselves go under IP-481 — Val: "Давайте этот вопрос, оставим открытый пока" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]].
- **Owner and team.** Val, "для река": the initiative owner is Alexander Biratka, and the team is Igor, Evgeny and Anton; Irina and Biratka added Daniil [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]]. The notes: "Team: Igor, Evgeny, Anton, Daniil" [[raw/Meeting_Notes_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]].
- **One MVP for everything, named NOVA-MVP-1.** Pavel offered two ways — separate MVPs per product, or one MVP with a date ("1 декабря. Например") holding the hardware platform, system work and the studio. Val: "У меня такой подход больше нравится"; Pavel: it lets them filter all tasks by the MVP. Val: the name is "нова тире MVPT… нового мвпи один" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]]. No date was fixed [[raw/Meeting_Notes_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]].
- **Small and often.** Val: "MVP заканчивался. Ну, желательно в течение недели… не набирайте, чтоб у вас был большой выпуск"; testing will be a bottleneck. Pavel's picture of MVP 1 for the hardware platform: a base game on the new engine, with today's services and LGS, on "рк… 35, 76" — about a week [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]].
- **The January 15 limit.** Val: from January 15 an outside senior producer ("Вне командные синерпродюсер", read by the notes as senior producer) starts, so the studio must be good enough for a person to start work [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]]. Same date as Nov 21.
- **The studio is tested as its own product.** Biratka: "Он будет тестироваться так как и другие продукты"; Val: "это понятно, что должно быть так" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]].
- **Meetings.** The team meets Val again "В понедельник"; Kharetski: "каждый день… мы команду собираемся" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]].

## 3. Maths and sound belong in the brief

- **Maths is missing from the pipeline.** Biratka: the pipeline lacks "Математики. И не хватает звуков". Igor: "математику нужно иметь как можно раньше" — it sets the symbols, the pays and the bonuses; until now the team only worked with games that already had maths. Igor: "Дефим говорит, что нет" (that AI can write it) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]]. See [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md]].
- **For now, the library.** [[wiki/people/anton-leshkevich.md|Anton Leshkevich]]: use "готовую библиотеку моделей", because at first "Большая часть игр будут рискины", and check in parallel whether maths can be made on the fly. Val: "Я я не верю, что математику нельзя написать" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]].
- **Where it goes.** Igor: "входные данные для студии это уже готовый бриф", so this is the brief agent's stage; Val agreed. [[wiki/people/evgeny-kharetski.md|Kharetski]]: the agent "Не не делает брифы с математиками"; Val: "это одна из вещей, которые нужно доделать в первую очередь". Biratka: the agent should ask "есть ли у вас математика, либо её прицепить, либо предложить разработать" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]]. No owner was said aloud; the notes put it on Kharetski pending Val's confirmation [[raw/Meeting_Notes_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]].
- **Sound into the brief.** Val: "добавить звуки вбрыв", and ask at design time whether a sound is needed and of what kind. Igor can add to the existing Georgia briefs when a sound starts, its name and maybe its length, but not its character — Kharetski: "Чистоклот не умеет слушать" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]].
- **A sound standard.** Val: today's sounds are final and approved — "влад, который наш звук, инженер" works smoothly — so pull them all out and "Стандартизировать точно так, как игр успешно сделал со всеми fraims", then put them into Evgeny's brief [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]].
- **A sound step in the studio.** Biratka: a list of the game's sounds, where they play, a way to play them and to move one earlier or later [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]]. Val told Anton not to start on sound: Igor first makes the standard, then Evgeny puts it in the brief [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]].

## 4. MVP 1 for the studio — narrow, plus a self-install page

- **Narrow but working.** Anton wanted to try Igor's 3D-model-plus-video method on symbols. Val: "Давайте мы сделаем MB один, который с узким применением, но работающим." Biratka: keep simple shader symbols and build the base scene; Anton: "Хорошо, тогда я займусь базовой сценой" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]].
- **A new designer installs alone.** Anton's plan: log in with a JVL account, download the studio, it installs what it needs, and a guide explains the steps. Val would not accept a Dropbox link and a phone call: "Но это так не должно быть… вот пришёл новый дизайнер. Вот иди вот у нас есть сайт." Biratka: maybe a GitHub page [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]].
- **Keep the know-how closed.** Val: "Нас будут пытаться скопировать люди не лояльные". Ideas: compiled tools instead of readable scripts — "чёрный ящик" (Biratka); lock the tool to a JVL account (Kharetski); hide the generated prompt (Igor). Biratka to Anton: "когда будешь разрабатывать, думай о безопасности" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]].

## 6. Val's commissioning bot (Georgia COAM)

Val raised it as new to this team. Commissioning a COAM machine to the Intralot computer is a phone call between a technician and an Intralot engineer; it takes "гдето минут 20" per Intralot and about two hours per JVL's people. He wants an AI bot on JVL's side to do the call, hand back to the technician if needed, and send a report. JVL should not design the voice technology: "У меня есть минимум одно предложение… И коз звонка $4 в час"; he knows "как минимум 3" such companies; JVL does the integration. "Я уже разговаривал с интерлотом, и эта идея очень нравится." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]]. No one was assigned [[raw/Meeting_Notes_NOVA_Sync_IP-481_Root_and_Weekly_MVP_Plan-November-23-2026.md]]. Earlier ruling: [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-21-pavel-frolov-is-product-owner-of-the-georgia-commissioning-chatbot-and-andrey-bosik-develops-it.md]] (Nov-23 note); [[wiki/organizations/intralot.md]].

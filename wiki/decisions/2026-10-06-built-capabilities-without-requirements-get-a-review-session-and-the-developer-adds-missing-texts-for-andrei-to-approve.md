# Built capabilities without requirements: a review session, and missing texts added by the developer

Settled by [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]] and [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]] with [[wiki/people/vladimir-samusenko.md|Vladimir Samusenko]] at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-06-daily-standup-ris-georgia-branch-and-critical-error-text.md|Oct-6 Minsk stand-up]] (Russian).

## The question (Irina)

What to do with things already built that have no requirements: either QA writes them and Pavel reviews and is the author, "Или мы там чегото ждём и пытаемся кудато эти тесты положить в сторону, а потом к ним возвращаться." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Daily_Standup_RIS_Georgia_Branch_and_Critical_Error_Text-October-6-2026.md]]

## The answer

- **A session.** Pavel: make a list; "Можем сессию сделать просто пройтись по этим сделанным кап обилитис". Irina: Vladimir writes what he lacks, "и потом мы вместе с пашей посмотрим и примем эти требования. Мы не будем откладывать в сторонку." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Daily_Standup_RIS_Georgia_Branch_and_Critical_Error_Text-October-6-2026.md]]
- **Text gaps are easy; design and translation gaps are not.** Vladimir: text requirements "это окей, это решаем"; the problem is missing design and translations. His example is test 69: differences between the requirements and the screens. The test fails because it has nothing to compare some texts with. The map of English and Spanish texts covers everything "Кроме 4 помоему кнопок". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Daily_Standup_RIS_Georgia_Branch_and_Critical_Error_Text-October-6-2026.md]]
- **Chudinov's way.** His team built and marked the file where the requirements or mock-ups need more work. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Daily_Standup_RIS_Georgia_Branch_and_Critical_Error_Text-October-6-2026.md]]
- **The rule for texts.** Vladimir: "я самостоятельно добавляю эти тексты в дата текст извожу там задачу на Андрея смотри. Вот я добавил эти тексты, иди проверь." Pavel: "Да, все верно." Pavel had already said that everything the team writes itself should go to Andrei so he can update it. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Daily_Standup_RIS_Georgia_Branch_and_Critical_Error_Text-October-6-2026.md]]

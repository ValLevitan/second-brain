# A 100-machine trial first; then the banks, and possibly InComm (Sept 30 2026)

**Standing (owner ruling 2026-10-05): this page records a working PROPOSAL, not a decision.** The 100-machine trial and the bank path belong to the same Sept-30 proposal; nothing is decided. Owner ruling 2026-10-05: still under consideration — no decision made yet. `status: active` is kept only because nothing has superseded the proposal; it is not an agreed ruling.

Agreed between [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] and [[wiki/people/larry-sharon.md|Larry Sharon]] on the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-30-larry-sharon-single-entity-jv-and-100-machine-pilot.md|Sept-30 8:42 AM call]]. ## What was decided

- **The trial.** Val: "let's have a small test, small run, because we will have disappointments and there will be things that we're not thinking about, and I don't want to be standing in front of you, John, and and Eddie, and. Make excuses"; "fifty, a hundred. That's not going to kill you or me or anybody." Larry: "I think a hundred machines is is a good proof of concept… a big enough sample set that what could have went wrong would have went wrong, and we would have fixed it already." Val: "Let's call it for the sake of this deal. Let's call it a 100 machine trial." Larry: "That's fine" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Larry_Sharon_Single_Entity_JV_and_100_Machine_Pilot-September-30-2026.md]].
- **More if JVL can.** Val: "I just want to make sure about 100 machines. We have a handshake agreement. We're ready to go." Larry: "We don't even need a handshake agreement. If you can do 50, 100, 150, 200"; "if there's an ability to do 125 or 150 or whatever the number is, let's just commit to doing that number. Because the more we put out, the better our business case is." Earlier: "if you could put out tomorrow a hundred machines, we'll gladly find us a hundred machines. If you put out five hundred, we'll we'll put out five hundred" (the word "find" is as transcribed; "fund" is the plain reading — **inference**) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Larry_Sharon_Single_Entity_JV_and_100_Machine_Pilot-September-30-2026.md]].
- **Then the banks.** "after that, we have a proof of concept. I will take it upon myself to then go to a financial institution. Like with the ones that I'm working with now, like Bell Bank or Sea Coast or whatever the case is, and I'll show them the business and make the case for traditional financing, like just like I do with my rail cars… Our side will put up, you know, thirty percent of the money. Thirty - three percent of the money, whatever it is, we want you guys to finance the rest. We pay the notes. The the the note is repaid from from inside the company." Worked example: "we want to buy a million dollars worth of machines, and we're gonna put down three hundred thirty - three thousand. The bank finances the other six hundred sixty - six thousand. JVL receives the full million and books its profits and pays its taxes" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Larry_Sharon_Single_Entity_JV_and_100_Machine_Pilot-September-30-2026.md]].
- **If the banks say no, nothing changes.** "If it works, that we get to capture the same depreciation that we're capturing on the railcars. If it doesn't work and the banks hate it, we just keep going the way that we're. Going" — the partners keep financing machines themselves [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Larry_Sharon_Single_Entity_JV_and_100_Machine_Pilot-September-30-2026.md]].
- **Possibly InComm as well.** "there's no reason why why when we're going to traditional banks why we can't also go to income [InComm] and say, look… we've put out a hundred machines, two hundred machines, whatever it is. We want to put out two thousand, five thousand, seven thousand more machines. We're willing to give you first right of refusal on a further on a future acquisition of this business. Would you be interested in financing this business?… at a normal interest rate with with the opportunity to to buy it in the future." And: "Tell them what the idea is in terms of becoming the financial tool booth. I think they can help there as well." Val added that InComm is not the only option: "At least, at least two more parties" (see [[wiki/organizations/incom.md]], Sept 30) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Larry_Sharon_Single_Entity_JV_and_100_Machine_Pilot-September-30-2026.md]].
- **Val's own view of the need for financing.** "I actually don't think that we need to finance this business past one thousand machines. Not machines, installations. I think the business will finance itself." Larry: "that's even better" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Larry_Sharon_Single_Entity_JV_and_100_Machine_Pilot-September-30-2026.md]].

The derived notes record "100-machine trial as the proof of concept; more if JVL can deploy more" and "After the proof, Larry approaches banks (Bell Bank, Seacoast) and possibly InComm with a first right of refusal" [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Larry_Sharon_Single_Entity_JV_and_100_Machine_Pilot-September-30-2026.md]].

## Why

- **Risk kept small, by Val's wish.** "I really don't want you to take any risks with me. I hate to bring people that may lose money with me." The worst case, in Russian: the money is frozen and they cannot move — "это немного денег" (it is not much money); or it takes more years than expected to get the money back. Larry: "Yeah, that's fine. That happens" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Larry_Sharon_Single_Entity_JV_and_100_Machine_Pilot-September-30-2026.md]].
- **A sample the banks will believe.** "we could then confidently go to a financial institution and say, 'Okay, guys, look, hundred machines, good sample set, we've worked out the kinks, it's running, we're collecting monthly recurring revenue. This is our plan going forward.'" And: "The, the faster we move, the The better we look" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Larry_Sharon_Single_Entity_JV_and_100_Machine_Pilot-September-30-2026.md]].
- **Zero down, if guaranteed.** Val's add-on: tell the bank "I'm guaranteeing that one million dollars in the back end. You put zero down. The bank fronts all the money." The rest of that line is garbled ("complete ride of four million… That's also not true"); figure not asserted [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Larry_Sharon_Single_Entity_JV_and_100_Machine_Pilot-September-30-2026.md]].

# A mini-distributor tier — percentages not yet decided

**Owner ruling 2026-10-18:** "the exact percentage is still being discussed. These percentages are just place holders — for discussion only. No final decision yet." This covers the Oct-6 figures (about 8% / 20%), the 9% / 20% Felipe recalled on Oct 14, and the figure he offered a distributor contact. None is a decided rate.

Agreed by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] and [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda]], before the recording started, and restated by Val at the start of the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-06-felipe-delivery-forecast-arc-release-and-modem-notice.md|Oct-6 1:41 PM call]].

## What was said

Val: "You also said that you have Padma. who is a mini distributor, and we agreed that we should have a definition in our distribution system, or in our taxonomy, I should say, should cover a definition of a mini distributor, and where a full distributor gets Twenty percent discount, and many distributors should probably get 8% discount, and we both agree to that." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Felipe_Delivery_Forecast_ARC_Release_and_Modem_Notice-October-6-2026.md]] ("many distributors" is read as "mini distributors", as in the notes [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Felipe_Delivery_Forecast_ARC_Release_and_Modem_Notice-October-6-2026.md]].)

- The 8% is "probably" — a working figure, not a fixed rate.
- No one was named to write the definition; the notes mark the owner as needing Val's confirmation [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Felipe_Delivery_Forecast_ARC_Release_and_Modem_Notice-October-6-2026.md]].
- Padma has no page. Whether he is the Padma who was [[wiki/people/vishal-patel.md|Vishal Patel]]'s technician is **unclear**.

# Scrap unusable machines with an auditor count for write-off

## What was decided

After the 100 machines ship, Miguel will sort the Mexico warehouse — scrap on one side, Altera machines on the other — order a **scrap container** for the machines that cannot be restored, and call in **external auditors** to count the scrapped machines so Val can properly **write them off** the inventory list. [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]]

## Who and when

- Given as a direct order (not a request) by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] to [[wiki/people/miguel.md|Miguel]] via [[wiki/people/mary.md|Mary]] on the Jan 8, 2025 call. Val stressed "it's an order how to do things," not a question. [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]]

## Important clarifications

- The auditors are **external** personnel — **not** Igor and Erika (JVL staff). [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]]
- Val explicitly stated JVL has **no issue with the tax department**; the auditor count is for a proper inventory write-off before scrapping, not to resolve a tax problem. (Miguel had raised a tax/"Zunat" concern and a push to change the whole warehouse; Val had stopped the warehouse-change idea.) [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]]

## Why

- Val does not want to waste time packing and shipping garbage machines; scrapping the unrestorable ones cuts cost and clears the warehouse. [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]]

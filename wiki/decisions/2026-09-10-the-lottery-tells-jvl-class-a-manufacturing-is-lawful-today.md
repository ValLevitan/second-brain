# The Lottery says Class A manufacturing is lawful today — with named limits

Given to [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] by [[wiki/organizations/georgia-lottery-corporation.md|Georgia Lottery]] COAM staff at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-10-georgia-lottery-coam-manufacturer-introduction.md|Sept 10 2026 introductory meeting]] in the COAM Hearing Room in Atlanta. It is the **first direct regulator answer** in the corpus to the question JVL has been circling since February 2026 — see [[wiki/topics/georgia-class-a-machines.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]]

**Attribution limit:** the recording labels speakers by number, so these are the Lottery staff collectively, not identifiable individuals [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]].

## The question, and the answer

Val asked it three ways and refused to leave it rhetorical:

- "**I'm not a politician, and I'm not here for politics… I'm here just to make money. Is it legal or not?**" — "**So yes, it currently is.**"
- "**Can I make it or can I not.**" — "**For now, yes. We are hearing some things that the [legislature] will address it, possibly in January, and next session. But currently, it is clear.**"
- "**So I'm here to ask you a question: If I make Class B, will I ruin this? Sorry, Class A. Will I ruin this relationship?**" — "**Okay. Got it.**" Then: "**as far as harboring any ill will or anything like that, you're playing within the rules.**"
- The only qualification on JVL's side: "**Now we may ask questions. Yes… because you have that paradigm of regulation and innovation. So… there's always going to be some… ebb and flow to that. So we certainly may ask questions.**"

## What the answer does NOT come with

This is the part that matters most for [[wiki/topics/georgia-class-a-machines.md]], which had carried "what do the Class A regulations actually say?" as its top open question since August 31:

- **No rulebook.** Val: "**And is there a book of regulations for Class A that I can read? So because I haven't… been able to locate one.**" No document was offered [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]].
- **No central system and no certification.** "**it's very much a different comparison when you compare it to B's because the A's are not hooked up to a central accounting system, so there's no certification**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]].
- **A licence regime that was, until about a year ago, no regime at all.** "**before it was on the Class A side, all it was was if you had a pulse and you could pay your licensing fee, you get a license… So we didn't even think about this approach until about a year ago or so. But we started hearing… this was coming up, and it was a curveball**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]].
- **A request they cannot enforce.** "**We ask for… specifications. You know, even though legally we don't have to get them, it'd be nice to see them**", and "**From a technical aspect, we would want to see how the machine functions**", followed a turn later (a different numbered speaker) by "**Truly a Class A? Does it violate the law?**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]]
- **An explicit expiry.** "**Just… I understand that our understanding may change with the legislation.**" Val, accepting it: "**I am not relying on you for legislation. Of course I understand**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]].

## The commercial frame Val put on the record in return

- **JVL is not chasing Class A customers.** "**So I'm not going after A customers. I'm going after B customers. And I'd like to do it within a frame of law… I like it to be B customer because it's regulated customer. It has a license and… it allows me to financially prove myself better by dealing with the big customers because they have a lot to lose**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]]. This is recorded separately as JVL's stated position; a staff member had prompted it — "**your B customers are quite different than your A customers… you are really kind of marginalizing the bees**."
- **His own view of where the category should end up.** "**From my… experience with legislation, I spent many years legislating… And being on regulatory side of things from 2000 to 2006, my personal opinion, and this is only my opinion, I think it should be regulated, and it should be legislated, and it should be taxed**" — and that it would be "**Phenomenal addition to the business overall… additional influx of healthy cash**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]].
- **Why he came in person at all.** "**that's why I feel very comfortable sitting in this room and discussing this ahead of time. What I don't like to do is to discuss it post-fact. I think that's just bad manners**… **I just don't want to be coming into your backyard and shitting all over the place**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]].

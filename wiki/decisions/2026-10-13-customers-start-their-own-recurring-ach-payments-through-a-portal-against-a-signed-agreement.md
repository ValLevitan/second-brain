# Customers start their own recurring ACH payments through a portal (Oct 13 2026)

**Decided:** October 13 2026, by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], on the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-13-bank-of-america-cashpro-business-central-source-of-truth-and-customer-ach-portal.md|Bank of America CashPro call]] (3:32 PM Eastern). It is a requirement JVL put to [[wiki/organizations/bank-of-america.md|Bank of America]]; the bank has not yet said whether it can meet it. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Bank_of_America_CashPro_Business_Central_Source_of_Truth_and_Customer_ACH_Portal-October-13-2026.md]]

## What was decided

- **The customer starts the payment.** "We want ACH to be initiated by the customer. That's the whole point of ACH, and customers are willing to do that." Val stressed: "Emphasis is made on customers, not suppliers. Customers pay us." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Bank_of_America_CashPro_Business_Central_Source_of_Truth_and_Customer_ACH_Portal-October-13-2026.md]]
- **Against a signed agreement, as a recurring payment.** "We send an agreement. They have an agreement to pay a specific amount per month… The agreement is executed. Then they go to our banking side and start recurring payments… against this particular agreement or invoice." His example: "you will be paying us three hundred dollars monthly for the next twelve months. You need to go here. This is how you log in." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Bank_of_America_CashPro_Business_Central_Source_of_Truth_and_Customer_ACH_Portal-October-13-2026.md]]
- **JVL does this today in QuickBooks.** "I know that this is possible because I'm doing it through QuickBooks right now… Then Bank of America should have that feature." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Bank_of_America_CashPro_Business_Central_Source_of_Truth_and_Customer_ACH_Portal-October-13-2026.md]]
- **Every payment method, each fee told in advance and charged to the customer.** "Every option that is available for payment, I would like to offer." Card today carries "three percent or two and a half percent, whatever the credit card is charging us"; the same is to apply to wire and ACH: "So we advise the customer in advance." The wire and ACH amounts he gave ("X amount of dollars", "fifty dollars") were examples, not set fees. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Bank_of_America_CashPro_Business_Central_Source_of_Truth_and_Customer_ACH_Portal-October-13-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Bank_of_America_CashPro_Business_Central_Source_of_Truth_and_Customer_ACH_Portal-October-13-2026.md]]

Brooks read it as a portal: "You need a portal for them to be able to go in." He will look for a solution with Connie. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Bank_of_America_CashPro_Business_Central_Source_of_Truth_and_Customer_ACH_Portal-October-13-2026.md]]

## How it sits against earlier sources

On Sept 8 the open question was whether JVL should bring its daily customer **ACH debits** (run today by a third party) in-house to Bank of America at all ([[wiki/decisions/2026-09-08-bofa-scope-prior-day-reporting-ach-debits-by-file.md]]). Oct 13 adds a different model for some customers — the customer pushes the payment through a portal. Whether this replaces, sits beside, or is unrelated to the debit flow was not said. Read as a new requirement, not a contradiction (inference).

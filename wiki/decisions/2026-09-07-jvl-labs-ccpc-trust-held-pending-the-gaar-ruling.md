# The JVL Labs CCPC trust is held until the GAAR position is clear

Ruled on the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-07-departure-tax-trust-and-bad-debt.md|Sept 7 2026 tax counsel call]] by **[[wiki/people/vitaly-timokhov.md|Vitaly Timokhov]]**, the tax lawyer [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] has retained. It is his own professional hold on his own proposal — not something Val refused. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]

## The problem it solves

- **[[wiki/organizations/jvl-labs.md|JVL Labs]] has to remain a CCPC.** Vitaly: "this corporation has to remain CCPC, Canadian Control[led] Private Corporation." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]
- Vitaly opened by assuming [[wiki/people/inna-levitan.md|Inna]] was a *director* and Val a *shareholder*; **Val corrected him — "Inna is the owner."** (That matches the April-2025 corporate structure chart already on [[wiki/organizations/jvl-labs.md]]: Inna is sole director and 91% shareholder.) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]
- **If control of JVL Labs passes outside Canada, it ceases to be a CCPC**, and the [[wiki/topics/sred-tax-credit.md|SR&ED]] benefit goes with it: "the provincial portion of [SR&ED] is not available, and the federal portion is available only to the extent to offset the profits, which you don't have." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]
- **What that is worth:** Vitaly worked from "about $1,000,000 worth of tax credits back… it's a major cash flow," and later called losing it "quite disastrous." He attributed the figure to what Val had told him and was explicit he had **not looked at the tax returns** — recorded as his working number, not a verified one. *(It does not line up cleanly with the figures already on [[wiki/topics/sred-tax-credit.md]] — a ~$150k modelled SR&ED inflow alongside a separate ~$600k media credit. Not reconciled here; see the note on that page.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]

## The proposed structure (drawn, not executed)

A **trust resident in Canada**, with a Canadian trustee, **holding the shares of JVL Labs**. Vitaly's own characterisation: "I wouldn't say standard structure, but okay, it's a structure is a little bit aggressive." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]

The Canadian-residency argument he would run for the trust: it is still controlled in Canada, "directors are Canadians who are basically long-term employees, and it's properly managed out of Canada… it still remains a Canadian enterprise." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]

## Why it is on hold — GAAR

- **The rule.** Vitaly described the **General Anti-Avoidance Rule ("GAAR"; rendered "GAR" throughout the transcript)**: if a taxpayer receives a benefit in the course of a transaction or arrangement, the benefit is denied where the transaction was done for avoidance purposes. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]
- **The case.** He named a case he dated to **2023**, transcribed as ***"Polisat"*** *(spelling exactly as transcribed; not verified against any court record — do not assume a real-world case name)*. It has **not yet gone to the Tax Court**. The **same structure** was used there, "way more aggressively," by a **US public corporation**, and **CRA alleged anti-avoidance**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]
- **His source.** Vitaly contacted **the lawyer running the case at the large international law firm handling it** *(the firm name is garbled in the transcript — rendered "Northern" — and is NOT asserted here)*, who confirmed **GAAR is one of the issues raised by the Crown** and that it **will be litigated in mid-October**, and suggested they speak **after the hearing, probably by the end of October**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]
- **Why he cannot simply proceed.** "Once there's a Tax Court decision on the merits of application of [GAAR], I am bound by that decision. As a lawyer, I'm bound by that decision." If the court holds the structure is caught, putting it in place "would automatically impose a **50% penalty** on you, and this is not something that I would like to do." So: "I'll have to wait." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]
- **His own read, separate from the hold.** "I personally don't think the Crown would challenge it" — Val's situation being much less aggressive than a US public corporation's — "but I still would like to gain a little bit of comfort before I can give you the structure." He also noted "there are only a few people who know about this case," himself among them. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]
- **If the court rules against the structure:** "most likely we'll have to find a workaround." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]

## Related timing judgment — no tax-law change expected before year end

Asked whether Canadian tax law might change before year end, Vitaly said he does **not** expect changes this year: changes normally arrive with the **annual federal budget**, the regular budget is expected only next year, and "the government is too busy right now." He allowed in the same breath that a **mini-budget** is possible — it happened about two years earlier — while still not expecting one. Both halves recorded as spoken. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]

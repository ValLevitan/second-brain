# Machine-retrofit and parts-procurement plan

## What was decided

Val and Misha agreed how to rework the machine inventory and what parts to order now, deliberately keeping early orders limited until the machines are inspected [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]].

## Key points

- **Altera / Altera HH:** order buttons/plates for all Alteras so they can be converted quickly; convert Altera HH into standard Altera (drop the 30-inch top, re-plastic the main and top monitors). These go to **Puerto Rico** [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]].
- **Altera V:** prepare game boxes; likely also to Puerto Rico [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]].
- **Maple (~230 units):** the hard case — depends on sourcing scarce 30-inch (Hammerhead) monitors; likely finished and sent to **Georgia**, then on to a grey market (possibly Texas) [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]].
- **Order now:** ~**50** 30-inch monitors and ~**200** CPU boxes/kits — a limited buy to avoid spending on parts that may be wasted before inspection [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]].
- Hold off on metal overlays; possibly use local brushed parts later [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]].

## Rationale

JVL has no new production scheduled, so early 2025 is the ideal window to rework the ~335 machines in stock. Target: finish and ship the rework backlog by **mid-April 2025**, then switch back to normal production [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]].

# JVL go-forward: test the liquidators, then offer the other holders a nominal buyout (Nov 10 2026)

Proposed by [[wiki/people/alan.md|Alan Litwack]] and accepted by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] on a ~32-minute call ([[wiki/meetings/2026-11-10-litwack-jvl-go-forward-noteholder-buyout-and-liquidation-test.md]], Nov 10 2026, ~4:01 PM Eastern) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Litwack_JVL_Go_Forward_Noteholder_Buyout_and_Liquidation_Test-November-10-2026.md]]. Funding context: [[wiki/topics/jvl-2026-funding-crisis-capital-call.md]].

*Speaker note:* the Plaud recording labels speakers "Speaker 1/2/3" and mixes them. The derived notes read Speaker 1 as mostly Val and Speaker 2 as mostly Alan ("likely; Val closes with 'Thanks, Alan'") [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Litwack_JVL_Go_Forward_Noteholder_Buyout_and_Liquidation_Test-November-10-2026.md]]. Who said what below follows that reading and is an **inference** where the labels are mixed.

## 1. Val's position

- "My position on this is that I would like everybody to stay." But "if nobody stays, I personally am not willing to carry anybody… So I don't want any baggage" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Litwack_JVL_Go_Forward_Noteholder_Buyout_and_Liquidation_Test-November-10-2026.md]].
- He is "very much open for them investing money pro rata with me." The other holders are "very wealthy people", so "It's an issue of not trusting that the company will ever turn around" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Litwack_JVL_Go_Forward_Noteholder_Buyout_and_Liquidation_Test-November-10-2026.md]].
- His worry: "something concerns me is that Boris works for a competitor", so if JVL dissolves, [[wiki/people/boris.md|Boris]] "may step in and say, 'I'll take it over'" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Litwack_JVL_Go_Forward_Noteholder_Buyout_and_Liquidation_Test-November-10-2026.md]].
- Even Val hesitates to put money in: "the only thing that may change is if the current product clicks" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Litwack_JVL_Go_Forward_Noteholder_Buyout_and_Liquidation_Test-November-10-2026.md]].

## 2. The numbers, as spoken (company debt, rounded on the call)

- **The notes.** Asked about "The prosperity notes" (heard; the notes suggest "probably 'priority notes'"), Val: "What's the total? Eleven." Of the eleven, "Probably three million is mine" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Litwack_JVL_Go_Forward_Noteholder_Buyout_and_Liquidation_Test-November-10-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Litwack_JVL_Go_Forward_Noteholder_Buyout_and_Liquidation_Test-November-10-2026.md]].
- **Val's money.** "My money is twenty four million"; Alan: "No, it was twenty seven", then "twenty four and eleven is thirty five." Val adds secured interest "approximately two point eight" plus interest owed to him. Alan: "So we're talking well over thirty million dollars" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Litwack_JVL_Go_Forward_Noteholder_Buyout_and_Liquidation_Test-November-10-2026.md]].
- **Liquidation value.** Val: if he marketed it, "I could get somewhere between five and ten million"; the cost of liquidating, "Probably half of it." Alan: "maybe if they're lucky, twenty-five cents on the dollar"; "There's nothing for the shares. The shares clearly have no value" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Litwack_JVL_Go_Forward_Noteholder_Buyout_and_Liquidation_Test-November-10-2026.md]].
- **How Val's own money ranks against the other holders** was also discussed (an earlier pro-rata arrangement and an example split). Those repayment figures are **not recorded (§15.8)**.

## 3. What a liquidation would look like

- **Insolvency.** Alan: insolvency is either assets below liabilities, "Or you don't have money to continue as a going concern. So if you don't put money in… You're insolvent"; then "some liquidator, trustee, receiver, somebody like that has to go in and shut down the company in an orderly way" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Litwack_JVL_Go_Forward_Noteholder_Buyout_and_Liquidation_Test-November-10-2026.md]].
- **Boris cannot run it.** "He can't do this. He has no capacity. All he can do is say, 'If you're liquidating it, I'll buy.'" Val can outbid him (the example given: "I'll pay another five hundred thousand dollars"). Proceeds pay debts, then "first it would go to the note holders, which includes you, And then it would go to the shareholders" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Litwack_JVL_Go_Forward_Noteholder_Buyout_and_Liquidation_Test-November-10-2026.md]].
- **Regulatory cost.** Alan: "a lot of the casinos, etc., are not allowed to buy from an entity that's in liquidation"; buying from the holders avoids "losing, whatever regulatory registrations you have." Val: "all my licenses are in the U.S."; Alan: a buy needs a new company [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Litwack_JVL_Go_Forward_Noteholder_Buyout_and_Liquidation_Test-November-10-2026.md]].
- **Assets.** Inventory "In in the state of Georgia, in Atlanta… In the warehouse", which Val says "has a zero value"; it sits in the US subsidiary. JVL has no office: "People work from home." Val: "The financial statement is absolute disaster" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Litwack_JVL_Go_Forward_Noteholder_Buyout_and_Liquidation_Test-November-10-2026.md]].
- **Tax credits.** Val lent JVL money "against media tax credit and SR&EDs. That was received, and I took the money back." He will apply for new ones: "In total, it will be around eight hundred thousand." He would advance money "only against shares and tax and media tax credit" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Litwack_JVL_Go_Forward_Noteholder_Buyout_and_Liquidation_Test-November-10-2026.md]]. See [[wiki/topics/jvl-2026-funding-crisis-capital-call.md]] for the earlier CRA refund that secured the August bridge.

## 4. The plan

1. **A current balance sheet.** Alan asked for an up-to-date one; Val: "Sean Riley can do it" ([[wiki/people/sean-riley.md]]). Alan: "this is the same one we'll probably end up showing the shareholders and note holders" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Litwack_JVL_Go_Forward_Noteholder_Buyout_and_Liquidation_Test-November-10-2026.md]].
2. **Run the shutdown numbers** — liquidation cost, leases, termination payments, liquidator fees — and "think up some number you think Boris might be prepared to pay"; an adviser "Not a PricewaterhouseCoopers or somebody, but somebody who does know what they're doing" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Litwack_JVL_Go_Forward_Noteholder_Buyout_and_Liquidation_Test-November-10-2026.md]].
3. **Test the liquidators.** Talk to a few trustees and receivers so JVL can say "none of them will take this without upfront money, and I'm not prepared to put the upfront money." Val: "Anybody you show to will puke." Alan: "It may take a few days" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Litwack_JVL_Go_Forward_Noteholder_Buyout_and_Liquidation_Test-November-10-2026.md]].
4. **Then the offer.** Go back to noteholders and shareholders: "you're essentially going to get nothing." In the words Alan scripted for Val, Val would put money in only for himself — "I'm not going to do it if I only own a piece" — and "take everybody else out for some nominal amount", paid over time, "With no personal guarantees", and "if I do manage to turn it around… I will share ten percent or something with everybody else." Val: "Yeah, that's the plan. I like it" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Litwack_JVL_Go_Forward_Noteholder_Buyout_and_Liquidation_Test-November-10-2026.md]].
5. **An insolvency partner first.** Alan: "I'm not an insolvency lawyer… I want to set up a call with one of my partners who does this." The call is set for Thursday at eleven: "It's you and I and John Leslie" (name as transcribed; the notes flag the spelling as unverified) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Litwack_JVL_Go_Forward_Noteholder_Buyout_and_Liquidation_Test-November-10-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Litwack_JVL_Go_Forward_Noteholder_Buyout_and_Liquidation_Test-November-10-2026.md]].

Val's close: "the plan is good. The plan is established… Subject to various comments, but the more or less the track is clear" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Litwack_JVL_Go_Forward_Noteholder_Buyout_and_Liquidation_Test-November-10-2026.md]].

An option Alan raised first and Val turned down: offer to buy the holders out "for some not so big amounts" straight away. Val: "the moment I pronounce that phrase that I'm willing to buy you out for peanuts… They will say why peanuts and what kind of peanuts?" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Litwack_JVL_Go_Forward_Noteholder_Buyout_and_Liquidation_Test-November-10-2026.md]].

# Invest in a Flex sound and power-supply redesign

On the Mar 27 2025 call [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] approved [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi]]'s plan to redesign the Flex machines' **power distribution** and **audio** (amplifier + speakers), saying he is "for it with hands and feet." The current sound is described as "ugly" / unpleasant and there is "no concept of live sound"; the goal is clean industrial power and high-fidelity "live sound." [[raw/Hardware_redesign-march-27-2025.md]]

## Power side

- Redesign the **power distributor** in the power block — it is all old — to make it cheaper (Genadi estimates ~40–50% cheaper) and cleaner. [[raw/Hardware_redesign-march-27-2025.md]]
- Replace the very old **power regulators** with newer, more powerful ones and add **filtration** so the block delivers clean ("industrial") power, because the new machines draw more current. [[raw/Hardware_redesign-march-27-2025.md]]
- Replace the **converters** that step the input down to **5V and 12V** for distribution to the sockets (input transcribed "2,4 вольта" — almost certainly **24V**, recorded as unclear). [[raw/Hardware_redesign-march-27-2025.md]]

## Audio side

- The current **analog amplifier** on the Flex is not rated for the load; the **subwoofer** may lack power. Plan: raise the power supply (needs rerouting), adjust **gain**, and improve the internal **routing of the sound**. An **audio engineer** will run an initial test and then give recommendations. [[raw/Hardware_redesign-march-27-2025.md]]
- Install higher **high-fidelity speakers**. [[raw/Hardware_redesign-march-27-2025.md]]

## Cost

- The subwoofer JVL puts in a machine today costs **$10**. Val is willing to spend **$200** total for board, power, speakers and enclosure combined. Genadi estimates even the best components would run only ~**$60**; he mentioned a good-quality speaker (Val: two of them at ~$20 would be fine). [[raw/Hardware_redesign-march-27-2025.md]] (Component-cost figures, not §15.8 compensation — recorded.)

# Unlimited Amusement takes one pilot location, and Val asks for a month-by-month rollout schedule instead of a volume commitment

At the **September 15 2026** visit ([[wiki/meetings/2026-09-15-unlimited-amusement-mega-hit-blaze-v3-and-bundle-pitch.md]]), [[wiki/organizations/unlimited-amusement.md|Unlimited Amusement]] answered the Mega Hit Blaze V3 + free-transactional-hardware bundle the way most Georgia masters in this run have: **one location, see how it performs, then talk.** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] accepted it on the spot and then asked for something the corpus has not recorded him asking any other operator — **a forward rollout calendar, on supply grounds rather than sales grounds.** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Unlimited_Amusement_Mega_Hit_Blaze_V3_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pitch-September-15-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Unlimited_Amusement_Mega_Hit_Blaze_V3_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pitch-September-15-2026.md]]

## The customer's reason — the market spreads location to location

Not caution for its own sake. Their stated model of how demand travels in Georgia: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Unlimited_Amusement_Mega_Hit_Blaze_V3_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pitch-September-15-2026.md]]

> "One location because it's like **diamond link**… we buy this sort of our good one location. **If it's work, then people next door and they ask for this machine or this game there.** We can start with one location to see how [it's] working."

> "That location, all surrounding maybe **ten, fifteen, twenty miles**, people go for this thing. **They will call other location.** … like **diamond link**. **When I put diamond link two places, then I buy from you more** because everyone sending… other master too because **JVL have like a double screen**."

So the pilot is being used as advertising, and the precedent they cite is a JVL product they already bought ([[wiki/topics/jvl-machine-lineup.md|Diamond Link]]).

## Val's counter — the supply argument, not a sales push

He conceded the pilot immediately — "**so go ahead and try one location**", then "**Sure, try one location, but I suggest for you, friend, a suggestion**" — and asked for the calendar: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Unlimited_Amusement_Mega_Hit_Blaze_V3_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pitch-September-15-2026.md]]

> "We're making the deals that we offer to you. **We're making it with all operators, so it will spread. You will have difficulty securing inventory. You will have difficulty rolling machines out.** … **Program the rollout to all of your locations on a monthly basis ahead of time so we can prepare inventory.**"

**The reason he gives is the offer's own success.** The free-hardware bundle ([[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-free-ports-and-pos-with-every-machine-bought.md]]) is being put to every Georgia master, so the constraint he is warning about is one JVL is creating itself — and he says so plainly in the same breath. The derived notes summarise it as "**supply, not sales pressure**." [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Unlimited_Amusement_Mega_Hit_Blaze_V3_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pitch-September-15-2026.md]]

**The answer was a maybe, and Val let it go:** "**We can help. We prepare. Talk, and I will let you know. No pressure.**" — Val: "**You do what you need to do.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Unlimited_Amusement_Mega_Hit_Blaze_V3_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pitch-September-15-2026.md]]

## How this sits against the rest of the Georgia run

- **It is the same outcome as [[wiki/organizations/diamond-amusement.md|Diamond Amusement]] five days earlier, reached from the opposite direction.** At Diamond, Val talked a customer *down* from ten machines to two ([[wiki/decisions/2026-09-10-diamond-buys-two-machines-instead-of-trading-the-fleet.md]]); here the customer arrives at one by himself and Val does not push the number up — he pushes on the **schedule** instead. *(The comparison is drawn here; neither source mentions the other.)*
- **The inventory-scarcity argument is new as a closing instrument.** Earlier meetings in the run closed on exclusivity asks (Rao, Lucky Coin, ARC — [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-rao-takes-the-bundle-with-jvl-exclusive-in-the-location.md]], [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-val-asks-lucky-coin-for-its-entire-business.md]], [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-arc-account-exclusivity-sought-and-not-obtained.md]]) or on pilot terms. **No exclusivity is asked for at all in this meeting** — recorded as an absence, not a withdrawal.
- **It lands against a real production constraint.** The October rollout staging and unit ramp were being scheduled internally the week before ([[wiki/meetings/2026-09-09-october-rollout-staging-and-unit-ramp.md]]), so "prepare inventory" is not only a sales line; but **no source connects the two**, and the connection is flagged here as an inference.

# Sergey's Georgia work is research in short spikes, reported through Klimovich

Settled on [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]]'s [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-05-sergey-vysotsky-georgia-location-opt-in-list-and-research-spikes.md|first call with Sergey Vysotsky]] (Oct 5 2026, 10:06 AM, Russian). The transcript has no speaker labels; attribution follows the derived notes and the context [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Sergei_Vysotski_Georgia_Location_Opt_In_List_and_Research_Spikes-October-5-2026.md]].

## What Sergey said

- **The task he got.** [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Klimovich]] ("Andrei", by context) sent him a list of Georgia locations — "около семи тысяч, даже более локаций" — with the aim of finding contacts and getting consent so JVL could contact them [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sergei_Vysotski_Georgia_Location_Opt_In_List_and_Research_Spikes-October-5-2026.md]].
- **What he did.** "Я нашел там около пятидесяти процентов этих локаций какие-то контакты, либо телефоны, либо адреса электронной почты." Most are not decision-makers — often a gas station's number from Google Maps [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sergei_Vysotski_Georgia_Location_Opt_In_List_and_Research_Spikes-October-5-2026.md]].
- **What he will not do.** Calls, outreach, email threads, trips and networking: "я не могу такой работой заниматься постоянно". What he can do: quick analysis, automation, sorting clients by type, and AI tools [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sergei_Vysotski_Georgia_Location_Opt_In_List_and_Research_Spikes-October-5-2026.md]].
- **Not a COAM specialist.** He said he does not know the nuances of how Georgia operators work with locations [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sergei_Vysotski_Georgia_Location_Opt_In_List_and_Research_Spikes-October-5-2026.md]].

## The rulings

1. **No sales work for him.** Val: "Окей, я все понял. Вам ничего такого делать не надо." If outreach is ever needed, Val wants a service or an AI engine for it — see [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-05-jvl-does-not-reach-out-to-georgia-locations-and-aims-for-inbound-calls-to-its-ai-voice.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sergei_Vysotski_Georgia_Location_Opt_In_List_and_Research_Spikes-October-5-2026.md]].
2. **Short spikes, not a big block.** Sergey proposed short iterations ("работать с итерациями, скажем, недельными") checked with Klimovich, instead of a large open-ended block, because "это скорее R&D больше". After each step they decide the next step or stop. Val: "Это называется spikes… Это абсолютно нормально." And: "мы можем просто остановиться… и сказать: окей, мы попробовали, ничего не получилось." Sergey: "Логично окей… меня это устраивает" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sergei_Vysotski_Georgia_Location_Opt_In_List_and_Research_Spikes-October-5-2026.md]]. *(The size of a spike in hours is billable time and is not recorded — §15.8.)*
3. **Results go through Klimovich.** Sergey keeps his research in a separate Google Drive with restricted access and offered Val the link. Val: "давайте сначала вы пройдите через Andrei, если там будет что-то полезное, я уже потом тоже." Sergey will message Klimovich after the call to agree the next steps [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sergei_Vysotski_Georgia_Location_Opt_In_List_and_Research_Spikes-October-5-2026.md]].

Both sides said they have no clear plan yet. Val: "Я как-то вижу некоторые вещи на тридцать процентов, на шестьдесят процентов, но я до конца ни одной вещи не вижу" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sergei_Vysotski_Georgia_Location_Opt_In_List_and_Research_Spikes-October-5-2026.md]].

The derived notes record the same four decisions (no sales work; no outreach in JVL's name; short spikes reviewed with Andrei; reporting through Andrei) [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Sergei_Vysotski_Georgia_Location_Opt_In_List_and_Research_Spikes-October-5-2026.md]].

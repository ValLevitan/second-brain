# Val writes the capital-call letter; Litwack writes the dilution part (Sept 29 2026)

Agreed by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] and [[wiki/people/alan.md|Alan Litwack]] on the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-29-litwack-capital-call-letter-and-transactional-equipment-write-off-model.md|Sept-29 11:33 AM call]]. Quotations from [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alan_Litwack_Capital_Call_Letter_to_Partners-September-29-2026.md]] unless marked.

## What was decided

- **A written letter goes to all partners.** "I will send a message to all the partners, written letter to all the partners." It will say that after the trade show Val is "moderately optimistic that the situation will turn", encourage them to put money in, and "provide a top-level budget of what I think JVL needs" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alan_Litwack_Capital_Call_Letter_to_Partners-September-29-2026.md]].
- **The ask is $1–2 million.** "I will be asking for… somewhere between one and two million dollars" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alan_Litwack_Capital_Call_Letter_to_Partners-September-29-2026.md]].
- **Who writes what.** Val did not know how to put the dilution on paper ("The only thing I don't know how to verbalize is the portion of dilution"). Alan: "you write the part that you want to say, and I'll write the other part." Val: "Agreed" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alan_Litwack_Capital_Call_Letter_to_Partners-September-29-2026.md]].

## Why (the rationale on the call)

- **Where JVL stands.** "It seems like JVL will be moving back into a healthy regime, like healthy capital." About a month earlier Val had "multiple discussions… with the partners. Some of them did not even reply to me. The rest who replied did not have any appetite for putting more money in" — and "with all fairness, I didn't sound very optimistic to them." After the trade show "the financial situation still has not changed, and we still need capital. But it looks like… JVL might be turning the corner" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alan_Litwack_Capital_Call_Letter_to_Partners-September-29-2026.md]].
- **Alan's dilution logic.** There is still debt owing, so "at this point there's no equity in JVL after that debt… if money is going in, it would dilute the hell out of all the common shares." Money put in now "should end up… once you get past the debt, with almost all of the equity." The letter therefore gives every partner "the same opportunity as you, in the proportion that you are already, to put the money in… if they don't, you understand, but you're essentially going to dilute the crap out of them" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alan_Litwack_Capital_Call_Letter_to_Partners-September-29-2026.md]]. This is the same same-offer protection Alan set out on Jul 10 and Jul 15 2026 (see [[wiki/topics/jvl-2026-funding-crisis-capital-call.md]], History — July 2026).
- **Val's expectation.** "I think that the partners will not pay. I think that they will not contribute" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alan_Litwack_Capital_Call_Letter_to_Partners-September-29-2026.md]].
- **Alan's framing of the message.** "you're saying we need the money to turn the corner, and that you have faith. You're willing to put it in, and you're willing to give everyone the same opportunity, and that way they can't complain if they chose not to exercise it" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alan_Litwack_Capital_Call_Letter_to_Partners-September-29-2026.md]].

## How it fits the funding arc

In August the working instrument was a bridge loan (pref debt secured by the CRA refund) with a warrant-holder notice, not a cash-call letter ([[wiki/topics/jvl-2026-funding-crisis-capital-call.md]], Aug 12–14). On Sept 29 Val is back to a written capital-call letter with a $1–2M ask. Whether the letter replaces, or sits beside, the August loan offer is **not said on the call** — recorded as evolution of the approach, not a contradiction. The derived notes record the same decision in the same words [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Alan_Litwack_Capital_Call_Letter_to_Partners-September-29-2026.md]].

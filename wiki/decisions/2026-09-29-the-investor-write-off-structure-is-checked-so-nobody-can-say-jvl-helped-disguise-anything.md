# The write-off structure gets checked so nobody can say JVL helped disguise anything (Sept 29 2026)

Advised by [[wiki/people/alan.md|Alan Litwack]] and accepted by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] ("Okay, understood") on the second segment of the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-29-litwack-capital-call-letter-and-transactional-equipment-write-off-model.md|Sept-29 call]]. Quotations from [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alan_Litwack_Transactional_Equipment_Tax_Write_Off_Model-September-29-2026.md]].

## The model being checked

Val's telling to his lawyer: a friend with a lot of cash pays JVL the $4,000 cost of the transactional equipment, takes the write-off on the $15,000 retail price "immediately that year", and owes the balance over time; JVL uses the cash to build the equipment and places it with Georgia operators "for free, essentially, in exchange for a five-year contract at hundred fifty dollars a month", with "no profit participation". Full detail on [[wiki/projects/transactional-equipment-depreciation-investor-deal.md]] (Sept 29) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alan_Litwack_Transactional_Equipment_Tax_Write_Off_Model-September-29-2026.md]].

## The ruling

- **Val's first position: it is the investor's problem.** "The tax write-off is for him, not for me. I don't care how he does his stuff… I don't want to research his issue and spend money on it" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alan_Litwack_Transactional_Equipment_Tax_Write_Off_Model-September-29-2026.md]].
- **Alan's condition.** "you don't want to be accused of participating in a… So we got to look at it enough just to make sure that nobody can say you did anything wrong." And: "whether it doesn't work at the end of the day doesn't matter to me as long as nobody in the IRS can say you helped them disguise something… Just means we have to, you know, structure it better" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alan_Litwack_Transactional_Equipment_Tax_Write_Off_Model-September-29-2026.md]].
- **Val accepts.** "Okay, understood. All right. Okay. Thank you" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alan_Litwack_Transactional_Equipment_Tax_Write_Off_Model-September-29-2026.md]].
- **How the check happens.** Alan: "You'll have to explain it to me better, and I'll have to get some of my U.S. people, you know, who can look at how… that tax write-off works" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alan_Litwack_Transactional_Equipment_Tax_Write_Off_Model-September-29-2026.md]].

The derived notes record the same decision ("The structure must be checked enough that no one (IRS) can say JVL helped disguise anything; the deal must be structured better") [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Alan_Litwack_Transactional_Equipment_Tax_Write_Off_Model-September-29-2026.md]].

## What Alan did and did not accept on the economics

- He accepted the payment shape once he saw it as deferred payment: "You're essentially… lending him. He's paying you over time"; "As long as at some point you're getting the full fifteen thousand dollars, so whether it's the same amount every month or you do it based on how much goes through the machine… That's fine" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alan_Litwack_Transactional_Equipment_Tax_Write_Off_Model-September-29-2026.md]].
- His worry was the investor's standing. "Is he the operator of the casino?" — Val: "No… Legally, he may have to because he needs an active business in order to write the entire amount." The machines "will be in various locations in the state of Georgia", run by "a third-party operator". Alan: "So he just paying somebody to operate a business for him" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alan_Litwack_Transactional_Equipment_Tax_Write_Off_Model-September-29-2026.md]].

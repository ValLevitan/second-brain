# ARC trades in old machines for Texas, as credit on 2027 purchases

Agreed in principle at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-13-arc-zazanis-lunch-trade-in-and-2027-volume.md|Oct-13 lunch]] between [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] and [[wiki/people/georgios-zazanis.md|George Zazanis]] of [[wiki/organizations/arc-gaming.md|ARC Gaming]]. The derived notes: "agreed in principle as a handshake deal. Quantity and price still open" [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]].

## What was decided

- **Val's offer.** He asked for ARC's pain points and named one: "**your IGT machines. If I take them on trade-in**". He would "**put my box into IGT machine and ship it to Texas**" and sell it to an operator, through "**a relationship with Georgia operator, who's got a route in Texas**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]].
- **George's answer.** "**This is gonna help us tremendously.**" He had pitched the idea to other manufacturers: "**I didn't know you guys do this. Or you're willing to do it because I have pitched these to other manufacturers.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]]
- **Paid as credit, not cash.** George: "**can we do this repurpose, and you give me credit for the next buy-in for next year… I will have to commit as well to buy myself.**" Val: "**I'll give you credit. We'll figure it out the price.**" Val: "**this is for one year credit**"; George: "**we will use it immediately**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]].
- **A handshake, with no one else involved.** Val: "**I don't want anybody involved… This is going to be a handshake deal.**" The machines ship straight to a different warehouse: "**I have a warehouse in Austin, Texas.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]]
- **Condition.** "**board, I don't care. Board could be garbage. …but monitor, buttons, touch screen, cabinet… When you turn it on, it has to come on.**" George wanted an outside person to assess the cabinets; Val said no need — but "**if let's say twenty percent is shit, I'm going to tell you**", and then ARC gives a touch screen or monitor [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]].
- **Timing.** The trade-in comes after ARC finishes the current deal ("**when we finish at 60, let's say 60. Whatever is the number, trade in whatever we have**") — see [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-13-arc-finishes-the-current-deal-first-and-buys-at-full-price-after-the-promotion.md]]. Val wants Texas moves fast, in small batches: "**If you have ten machines ready, ten machines**"; "**five, ten**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]].

## Quantity and price — not agreed

- **Quantity.** Val: "**don't shock me with five hundred machines**". George: "**We're talking about sixty, seventy, eighty, hundred, hundred fifty, hundred for two hundred seventy-five, maybe.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]]
- **Price.** Val: "**in Texas… they're not gonna pay more than three four thousand dollars per machine. And don't forget that I need my board and I need some labor.**" Then: "**now you told me fifteen hundred sounds like a logical price**" — and "**Give or take, give or take. Let me see what we have again, because there are so many different type of cabinets.**" Who said "$1,500" first is unclear in the labels; the notes give it to George [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]].

## Context

- **ARC's old fleet.** George: about half the machines in Georgia's locations were made "**six to seven years ago, average**". IGT asked ARC for about **$300,000** to replace the logic boxes on **50** machines. His framing: "**Three hundred thousand to Val Levitan to give me brand new machine that performs better, or three hundred thousand to [IGT]**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]].
- **Spare game boards.** George has "**hundreds**" of old game boards; he has already found another way to send them to Texas. Val will pull boards from trade-in machines and sell them to a parts company [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]].
- **Texas risk.** Val: "**I heard that legislation will change in March… To make them illegal**", and "**right now there is 200,000 machines in Texas**". The wider Texas plan is on [[wiki/projects/texas-used-machine-distribution.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]].
- **An earlier swap.** On Aug 27 ARC described a one-to-one exchange of old machines at $6,000 each, which Val accepted — see [[wiki/organizations/arc-gaming.md]] (Aug 27). This trade-in is a separate, later deal, paid as credit [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Coffee_Fleet_Swap_and_Transactional_Pitch-August-27-2026.md]].

For twenty-seven, at fifteen hundred dollars.**" ("For twenty-seven" is unclear.) His argument for the seller: IGT wants "**sixty five hundred dollars**" for its kit, so "**That's eight thousand dollars perceived value that they get up front**", and "**I think that Arc will trade actually will trade with you about two hundred machines.**" The Banilla and IGT trade for Texas is now part of Felipe's November–December plan — [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-14-november-and-december-sales-go-to-trade-ins-a-6500-credit-for-jvl-machines-and-banilla-and-igt-machines-for-texas.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sales_Q4_Trade_In_Plan_and_Transactional_Momentum-October-14-2026.md]].

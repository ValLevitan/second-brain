# InComm's ruling — no cash out of a COAM redemption device for a Skill Pay card, and no coordinated kiosk+ATM pair from the same entity

This is the central outcome of the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-26-incomm-skill-pay-atm-guidance.md|Aug 26 2026 InComm follow-up]]. It is **[[wiki/organizations/incom.md|InComm]]'s** guidance to JVL, not a JVL choice — but it binds JVL's Georgia product roadmap, because as Val put it, "the moment you stop supplying cards to us, we're not going to be in business." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]

## What InComm ruled

- **No cash may come out of a redemption machine.** Adam Harlow, stated as the whole of it: "Our posture and positioning. As of now, is that **we do not want any cash coming out of the redemption machine**. That's it." And: "the simple, straightforward answer today is that **no cash comes out of the kiosks for a skill pay card**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]
- **Not even as two separate boxes, if JVL operates both.** Val tested this explicitly — two physically separate devices, "One is in one side of the room, and the next one is in the next side of the room", one dispensing the card and one being the ATM. Jason Hall: "For now, Val, we're going to say if it's **operated by the same entity**. JVL. We're not going to be okay with that." His concern is a **coordinated pair**: "to sell the Cohen Redemption kiosk and the ATM cash dispensing kiosk as a pair or a… product offering. It is not something we want to engage in right now." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]
- **What remains allowable:** a player physically handed a Skill Pay card who then converts it to cash at a separate, independent ATM. Juan put the case — "if you physically give the player a… skill pay card, and then he takes the card and converts it into cash at an ATM, that's an allowable transaction" — and both Jason ("Yes") and Adam ("Correct") confirmed it. Juan's illustration of the clean case was "a standalone Bank of America ATM… in the location who has nothing to do with the income side of things"; Jason declined to bless that specific framing ("I don't allow or disallow what people do with their strategy"). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]
- **Combining an ATM into a COAM kiosk would end the card supply.** Jason: "we are not interested in any of the Georgia COAM kiosks combining ATM functionality onto that machine at this time… We would stop offering our products to someone who tried in **2026 or 2027** to combine an ATM with a Georgia COAM redemption kiosk. Because we believe that would be a problem." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]
- **It is explicitly a time-bound posture, not a permanent rule.** Jason: "three to five years from now, who knows, but for the time being, that is not something we're…". Adam: "Two years from now, they may see things differently… until we get better clarity from the regulators." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]

## Why — the optics argument and Joe Kim's expo reaction

- **It is about optics, not payment rails.** Adam: "the nuance here is the device itself that kind of adds to the optics. So if a player walks away from the redemption device holding cash… that's seen as a **cash payout**. Even if it was run along… the appropriate rails on a certified ATM processor." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]
- **The trigger was [[wiki/people/joe-kim.md|Joe Kim]] at the expo the previous week.** Adam admitted InComm was "probably getting out over our skis a little bit, trying to speculate from Joe Kim's comments last week at the conference." What worried Kim, per Adam: demos of a card being put into a machine that was *also* a redemption machine and the machine "waving its wand and spitting out cash", and separately products "explicitly for ATM use only" — "basically a mechanism to turn your redemption directly into cash." Adam read him as "perturbed or fatigued at people trying to sort of skirt around his prior guidance", and said Kim is "going back to kind of sharpen that guidance." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]
- **Why InComm believes its own card is safe.** ATM access is "merely another feature of our product. It's not the exclusive feature… The user can still go on amazon.com and use our product", and there are **daily ATM withdrawal limits** that push players toward general spend "at Target, Kroger, wherever they want to shop." Adam did not believe InComm's product "was in his crosshairs last week." The ATM functionality **is live on the card at its September launch** — Jason, correcting Juan: "No, it'll be active on the card when it launches in September." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]
- **Juan's unanswered objection.** In most Georgia operations the master or location licence holder already owns both the kiosk and the ATM — and, Val added, the location owner is also the entity **InComm ships the cards to** ("They don't ship cards to JVL. JVL does not touch the cards. The master operator does not touch the cards. But the location owner… Gets the cards. Dispenses the cards and has an ATM network that they own, that they actually bought"). Juan: so how are they outside this network? Jason declined the argument — "I understand what you're trying to argue, Juan, and I'm not trying to say that or require that… this is my guidance to what you're trying to offer **in partnership with us**." Recorded as an unresolved objection. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]
- Val closed the topic without contesting it further: "All right, clear… No questions, no questions on that." Adam's own summary of the state of the guidance: "Clear as mud." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]

## Open questions

- When, if ever, will the Georgia Lottery ([[wiki/people/joe-kim.md|Joe Kim]]) issue guidance clear enough to permit ATM/cash-out tied to COAM redemption? InComm gave no single horizon — Jason Hall "three to five years from now, who knows", Adam Harlow "Two years from now, they may see things differently" — and both were firm it holds for 2026–27 — [[raw/Meeting_Notes_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]
- Does the same-entity rule reach the master/location licence holders who already own both the redemption kiosk and the ATM, and who receive the cards? Jason explicitly declined to say — [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]

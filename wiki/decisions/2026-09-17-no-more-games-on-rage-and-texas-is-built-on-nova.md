# Build no further games on RAGE — switch straight to NOVA, and do Texas on NOVA

Taken by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-17-kleimenychev-hire-nova-direction-and-planning-handover.md|Sept 17 2026 12:45 PM ET "General alignment" call]] (held in **Russian**), in answer to a point [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]] raised about the conversion experiment then running. [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]] was the third person on the call.

## How it came up — Pavel's double-conversion problem

Pavel's observation was practical: the experiment under way converts a **horizontal RAGE game into a vertical RAGE game**, which means the same games would later have to be converted **again**, out of RAGE into NOVA.

> "**Ну окей, ну единственное, что хотелось бы отметить, что вот то, что мы сейчас делаем, эксперимент это, по сути конвертация рейч горизонтальный в рейч вертикальной.**"
> "**И, возможно, нам потом понадобится конвертация рейч тех же самых игр речь в нова.**"
> [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Kleimenich_Hire_Nova_Direction_and_Planning_Handover-September-17-2026.md]]

("рейч" / "речь" are the transcript's renderings of **RAGE**; "нова" / "ново" of **NOVA**.)

## Val's ruling

> "**Всё я предлагаю да я предлагаю не делать никаких игр в речь.**"
> "**Я предлагаю сразу переключиться на нового и Техас сделать на ново.**"
> [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Kleimenich_Hire_Nova_Direction_and_Planning_Handover-September-17-2026.md]]

— build no games at all in RAGE; switch straight to NOVA; and build **Texas on NOVA**.

**Nobody answered.** After a pause of about nine seconds in the cue timings, Val took the silence as assent and closed the point himself: "**Я понял, что вы согласны, поэтому идём в этом направлении**" — I take it you agree, so we go in this direction — followed by "**У меня нету времени нету, время на полемику нету время на полемику**", there is no time for debate. He then moved to the general mood: "**Мы делаем шикарный бизнес**", "**Вещи начали двигаться**", "**Мы толкаем во всю силу**", "**Поехали?**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Kleimenich_Hire_Nova_Direction_and_Planning_Handover-September-17-2026.md]]

**Recorded as spoken:** neither Pavel nor Irina says yes to this item anywhere in the file. The agreement is Val's reading of a silence, stated by Val. The derived notes report the same thing — "He took the silence as agreement and said there is no time for debate" [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Kleimenich_Hire_Nova_Direction_and_Planning_Handover-September-17-2026.md]].

## What this settles, and what it deliberately does not

**Settles:** the **platform for the Texas product is NOVA**. That is the first platform choice for the vertical Texas work anywhere in the corpus — the [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-14-vertical-texas-starts-from-the-georgia-coam-vertical-games.md|Sept-14 decision]] that set the *starting material* for a vertical Texas product explicitly recorded that no platform choice had been stated. This supplies it.

**Settles:** the standing direction is that **no new game work starts in RAGE**. This is consistent with, and sharper than, the [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-03-no-en-masse-rage-to-nova-migration.md|Aug-3 ruling]] ("build **new** games on the new tools") and the [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-11-georgia-class-a-v43-is-built-on-nova-not-rage.md|Sept-11 ruling]] that Georgia Class A V43 goes on NOVA rather than RAGE.

**Does NOT settle — and nothing here should be read as settling:**

- **RAGE still has no sunset date.** Val's instruction is about what is *started*, not about when RAGE ends. No calendar date for a RAGE sunset appears in either source, and the standing position on [[wiki/topics/nova-platform-architecture.md]] — RAGE parked, no sunset date, Nebraska the last RAGE release — is unchanged by this call.
- **NOVA → GLI remains unscheduled.** GLI, certification and submission are not mentioned once in either source.
- **NOVA's known gaps are not addressed.** Three days earlier the same group recorded that development is **faster in RAGE today** and that **NOVA still cannot run several games in one shell** ([[wiki/topics/nova-platform-architecture.md]], Sept 14). Neither limitation is raised, answered or withdrawn on this seven-minute call. The direction is recorded as stated; the open capability questions stand.
- **The experiment already running is not cancelled in words.** Val says build no *games* in RAGE; he does not say to stop the horizontal→vertical RAGE conversion experiment Pavel had just described, and nobody asks. What happens to the work in flight is not stated.

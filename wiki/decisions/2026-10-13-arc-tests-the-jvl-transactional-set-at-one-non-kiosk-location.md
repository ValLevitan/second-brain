# ARC tests the JVL transactional set at one non-kiosk location

Agreed at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-13-arc-zazanis-lunch-trade-in-and-2027-volume.md|Oct-13 lunch]] between [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] and [[wiki/people/georgios-zazanis.md|George Zazanis]], COO of [[wiki/organizations/arc-gaming.md|ARC Gaming]]. The derived notes list it as a decision [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]].

## What was decided

- **The gap ARC wants filled.** ARC's locations have either a kiosk or a Pax device. For the ones that do not want a kiosk: "**for the locations that they do not want the kiosks, I need to have a solution. Today the solution that I give is the Pax device. But if there is an alternative, better solution, yours, I can upgrade it**" (George) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]].
- **What the test does.** The player moves money "**from one machine to the other as needed**", then cashes out at the IQ "**With the gift card**". MudaGoose comes out: "**we don't want MudaGoose**" / "**So we remove MudaGoose**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]].
- **The set.** "**So your average location has six machines.**" — "**Yes.**" Val: "**I will give you six [ports]. I will give you one IQ and one printer.**" Each machine needs a Flexport; Val recommends starting with newer machines. JVL must make Flexport plates, so ARC must say which machines are there [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]].
- **The order of steps.** George: "**I'll come one day to your office just to demo, to show it to me once again, end to end, very quickly, half an hour, one hour. And after that, I'll find one location that will agree, and we will test it. I'll get the buy-in from David.**" Val will send a technician to connect it [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]].
- **The candidate location.** "**Top tier location, 80k per month. So the guy is asking me for access… Instead of access, we put yours. It's exactly the same.**" The notes read "access" as a kiosk request. A later line, "This is a Light & Wonder", may describe the machines there; unclear. The location is not named [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]].
- **The fee.** "**Monthly fee. Ninety dollars per location… for all of the devices. Doesn't matter how many devices. But keep in mind, that's a monitoring fee.**" Val said the equipment may be rented under his family-office deal, or ARC could buy it at "a phenomenal price"; "**The test doesn't matter**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]].
- **The kiosks stay.** George: "**My baggage are with the kiosks**… But doesn't mean I'm not allowed to do something else." Val: "**Parallel. Leave the kiosks alone.**" This keeps the test clear of the kiosk lock-in on [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-31-arc-kiosk-board-swap-routed-through-the-ceo.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]].

## The broker part — "my selling point"

Val offered to sweep the location's account, act as broker and post to ARC's books: "**I will swipe operator's account, put money into intermediary account, put money into your account, and I will interface with your accounting program… [your] General Ledger.**" George, twice: "**This is my selling point**" — because ARC is the broker at many locations and it is hard work. But: "**I'm not sure if we're gonna end up with the accounting part… but the rest we're gonna do it.**" This is the Aug-31 cloud-processing pitch (see [[wiki/organizations/arc-gaming.md]]) now attached to a real test [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]].

## Why

No measure of success was set. George: "**I don't have a measurement for the success.**" He wants an option for non-kiosk sites that does "a little bit more" than the Pax [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]]. For JVL it is the first agreed field placement of the transactional set at ARC, which the Aug-31 open question "Does ARC pilot JVL's cloud processing, and where?" had left open — see [[wiki/organizations/arc-gaming.md]].

# Recycler MVP 1 player-flow rules: refunds, jams, one Texas balance, a cash-out fee, power loss (Nov 15 2026)

Agreed at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-11-15-recycler-mvp1-user-flow-escrow-jam-refund-and-cashout-fee.md|Sunday Nov 15 2026, 8:01 AM recycler review]] (Russian, about 56 minutes), the meeting set on Nov 12 for this day. [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] walked the team through the player screens and flows he had put into recycler MVP 1 in Jira, and the questions he had emailed the day before. In the room: [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/alexander-biratka.md|Alexander Biratka]] (label "Alexander Biratko"), [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]], [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Recycler_MVP1_User_Flow_Escrow_Jam_Refund_and_Cashout_Fee-November-15-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Recycler_MVP1_User_Flow_Escrow_Jam_Refund_and_Cashout_Fee-November-15-2026.md]]. It builds on [[wiki/decisions/2026-11-12-the-recycler-gets-its-own-mvp-1-on-the-production-server-by-thursday-november-19-and-its-mvp-2-a-week-later-scope-is-cut-never-the-date.md|the Nov-12 ruling]] (recycler MVP 1 by Thursday Nov 19, Texas, e-wallet only).

*(Quotes come from an unpunctuated Russian speech-to-text transcript, kept word for word; garbles are kept as heard.)*

## What MVP 1 is for Thursday

- **The player flow only.** Klimovich: he added "все леварь экраны и флоу пользовательские для риса айклера" to MVP 1; the admin screens "я добью, там до завтра". Biratka: for Thursday "у нас хотя бы пользовательский надо добить"; reports "немножко больше времени займёт"; his recycler work is done and the screens need to be "Отполировать" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Recycler_MVP1_User_Flow_Escrow_Jam_Refund_and_Cashout_Fee-November-15-2026.md]].
- **MVP 1 is not the first release to people.** Irina: "1 релиз мы это хотим, который уйдёт людям… а MVP один это кусочек, который мы сделаем до четверга." Klimovich summed it up: "Деньги берём волят и создаём деньги, выдаём воли ты, обновляем", then play "с подключением к портам" — Biratka: "Да к портам", so Klimovich can install it and walk it through [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Recycler_MVP1_User_Flow_Escrow_Jam_Refund_and_Cashout_Fee-November-15-2026.md]].
- **The flow PDF** sits in Jira beside the screens ("он там же приотачен") [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Recycler_MVP1_User_Flow_Escrow_Jam_Refund_and_Cashout_Fee-November-15-2026.md]].

## What was ruled

1. **Capability 341 names the wrong recycler.** It refers to "модель RT"; Klimovich: "У arqq 4 барабана у о р т 2 барабана". Biratka: "У нас 4… тот, который у нас стоит" — JCM ("у джимовского") makes both. Biratka owns the capability and will fix it ("Дай я поправлю конечно") [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Recycler_MVP1_User_Flow_Escrow_Jam_Refund_and_Cashout_Fee-November-15-2026.md]]. (RT = 2 drums, RQ = 4 drums — the transcript's "arq"/"arqq"/"аркэ" read as RQ, as in the notes.)
2. **Bills in: three paths.** The recycler holds one bill in escrow; Pavel: "через 30 секунд сама возвращается"; Biratka: with "холд холдхолд" about every 20 seconds it stays as long as needed. Val: "мы всегда съедаем всё, что нам дают" — every bill goes to the drums. On Cancel the player gets his bills back (Val's example: five twenties, "4 двадцатки обратно возвращаем", and what to do with the fifth is "технический вопрос" — Biratka). Biratka set out the paths and Val agreed ("Так точно"): Cancel → return the money; Print → print the ticket; no action → "Мы ждём таймаута и после этого не печатаем тикета записываем" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Recycler_MVP1_User_Flow_Escrow_Jam_Refund_and_Cashout_Fee-November-15-2026.md]].
3. **Not the word "unclaimed".** Pavel asked to drop it ("Очень сложное слово для произношения"); Val: "форфид это хорошее слово, но можно просто сказать… Отказ" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Recycler_MVP1_User_Flow_Escrow_Jam_Refund_and_Cashout_Fee-November-15-2026.md]].
4. **No paper: block money in first.** Pavel: warn first and do not let money go in; Biratka: "Это у нас уже сделано… в fp" (Flex-Pay) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Recycler_MVP1_User_Flow_Escrow_Jam_Refund_and_Cashout_Fee-November-15-2026.md]].
5. **A failed or jammed ticket cancels the transaction.** Val: the wallet is confirmed "только тогда, когда билет правильно напечатался". Klimovich first proposed going back to a "try again" screen so an attendant could clear the jam and reprint. Val: "вы очень комплектуете этот процесс" — jam, transaction ended, money back, tell the attendant "Приди фикс свой продукт и протестируй его", and the product goes out of service until fixed. Klimovich: "Упрощаем я поправлю, тогда сразу всё возвращаем. Отменяем создание тикета волюто" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Recycler_MVP1_User_Flow_Escrow_Jam_Refund_and_Cashout_Fee-November-15-2026.md]]. Klimovich said the IQ already counts a wallet only after the ticket prints; "Дим дима это проектировал" — which Dima is **unclear** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Recycler_MVP1_User_Flow_Escrow_Jam_Refund_and_Cashout_Fee-November-15-2026.md]].
6. **Texas: one balance.** Klimovich asked whether Texas has a handpay rule like Georgia; Val: "Нету нету нету". So the ticket and wallet show one amount, not "плейбл и reed demable" — Val agreed [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Recycler_MVP1_User_Flow_Escrow_Jam_Refund_and_Cashout_Fee-November-15-2026.md]]. Val added a reminder: promo credits in Georgia COAM must respect the handpay law — the player "не можешь это акешить, пока ты не набрал общее количество генс" (heard; handle), "потому что иначе мы нарушаем закон". Pavel's example: credit 21 for 20, "этот 21 это будет плейбл редима был будет только 20" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Recycler_MVP1_User_Flow_Escrow_Jam_Refund_and_Cashout_Fee-November-15-2026.md]]. Whether promo credits are in MVP 1 is open (Biratka: "если не готова, давай на MVP 2 перекинем") — see [[wiki/decisions/2026-11-12-the-texas-twenty-dollar-promo-bonus-is-one-dollar-per-twenty-with-machines-at-ninety-percent.md]].
7. **The cash-out fee.** Val wants fees defined in setup: "3 типа категорий по физ… и название давали, и процент устанавливали, из кого мы берём"; he does not yet know which fees, so the categories will not be used yet. He also said that on the IQ "должно быть 9 типов". His example: in Nebraska bank structures service the dispensers ("нересайтлеры, а просто. Диспенсеры") for "$65 в месяц", and some operators want to pass this to the player, for example "один процент" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Recycler_MVP1_User_Flow_Escrow_Jam_Refund_and_Cashout_Fee-November-15-2026.md]]. Biratka suggested taking the fee when money goes in; Klimovich: "я пришёл расстаться со своими деньгами, и вы с меня хотите взять за это процент? Нет, нет, я категорически против." Klimovich's proposal for MVP 1, accepted by Biratka ("Давай"): a fee on the redemption screen when the player takes money out, set per location; at zero "эта строка вообще не показывается"; otherwise the player sees it before agreeing [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Recycler_MVP1_User_Flow_Escrow_Jam_Refund_and_Cashout_Fee-November-15-2026.md]].
8. **Amounts below the smallest note stay on the wallet.** Biratka's example: $9 with fives — the player gets $5 and "$4 наиволить иди иди продолжай играть"; Pavel: "может продолжать играть" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Recycler_MVP1_User_Flow_Escrow_Jam_Refund_and_Cashout_Fee-November-15-2026.md]]. (Klimovich first called the left-over "наш заработок чистой воды"; Pavel noted that in casinos unclaimed money must go back to players as bonuses.)
9. **Payout wait: at least 90 seconds.** Klimovich proposed 30 seconds to take a dispensed note; Val: "как минимум 90 секунд минимум". If the note is not taken it is pulled back and the amount goes back to the wallet (Klimovich: "мы возвращаемся к его лету") [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Recycler_MVP1_User_Flow_Escrow_Jam_Refund_and_Cashout_Fee-November-15-2026.md]].
10. **Power cut with bills in.** Val: there is a GLI requirement to restore to the same stage; "Здесь надо ссылку сделать… Это помоему call ai leven" (heard; GLI-11). Pavel thought kiosks fall under a separate GLI document, "не 11". Biratka: the bill validator spits the escrow note out at start-up. Agreed: go back to the previous screen, give back the accepted bills, and the last note "не должна быть засчитана" (Klimovich). Biratka: "это важное требование, его бы как бы отдельно так действительно запечатлить"; Klimovich: "Пропишем" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Recycler_MVP1_User_Flow_Escrow_Jam_Refund_and_Cashout_Fee-November-15-2026.md]].
11. **Payout money is frozen first.** Klimovich's case: start a $50 payout, switch the recycler off, play the $50 at a machine. Biratka: the money must be marked as being paid out — "они должны быть сразу заморожены перед тем, как выдаваться"; Pavel and Klimovich agreed. A note that has come out is not returned to the wallet (Irina: "Мы их уже отдали, неважно, куда игроку в руки или на пол") [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Recycler_MVP1_User_Flow_Escrow_Jam_Refund_and_Cashout_Fee-November-15-2026.md]].
12. **RIS and Passport: not MVP 1, but before the first live install.** Val: "Я бы не хотел бы отказываться от рис и паспорта." RIS = "remond innation setup" (heard; Remote Installation Setup) — a USB stick, a QR code or the cell modem. Passport = the device's birth certificate: at manufacture the parts' serial numbers go into the Dallas chip. Pavel: the IQ has no Dallas (the board ID serves), but the recycler and Flex-Pay boards have one. Biratka: "давайте не выпи один"; Val: "Безусловно, не в MD один". Klimovich: "это чтото, что должно быть сделано до 1 инсталляции" — Irina "Да", Val "я соглашусь с этим" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Recycler_MVP1_User_Flow_Escrow_Jam_Refund_and_Cashout_Fee-November-15-2026.md]]. The IQ install planned for next week goes ahead without it: Val is "на 99%" sure the design will show many shortcomings, "Поэтому я не тороплюсь, устанавливая десятки или сотни" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Recycler_MVP1_User_Flow_Escrow_Jam_Refund_and_Cashout_Fee-November-15-2026.md]]. Background: [[wiki/topics/ris-remote-install-and-machine-states.md]].

## Advice, not a rule: refuse $100 bills

Pavel asked whether $100 bills are refused. Val: "сейчас надо запрещать их в любом случае" — about 20 years ago fake hundreds from Iran flooded the US, the central bank knew, so JVL did not take them; now "я тоже не рекомендую… это просто приглашать. Фальшивщиков". Klimovich: this is set on the recycler on site, not built in now; Val: "Да да да" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Recycler_MVP1_User_Flow_Escrow_Jam_Refund_and_Cashout_Fee-November-15-2026.md]].

## One note at a time, or a bundle?

Not known. Klimovich read in the RQ documents that it gives "по одной банкноте ждёт когда достали одну и тогда выдаёт следующую". Biratka at first: from his teardown "некуда сложить их стопочку". Val thinks JCM has an extra module that builds a bundle — in the past "пачка у них 30 купюр". Klimovich recalled "переписку Геннадия с дрессемом" (heard; with JCM, as the notes read it): the RQ cannot be upgraded for bundles, and a model "чуть ли не в 2 раза дороже" can. Val: "гену сюда можно подтянуть" — document the questions, take this meeting's transcript, and ask the JCM designers through [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi Peari]]; Biratka wants his own tests first [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Recycler_MVP1_User_Flow_Escrow_Jam_Refund_and_Cashout_Fee-November-15-2026.md]]. ("гену" / "Геннадия" = Genadi Peari — **inference** by name and his hardware role.)

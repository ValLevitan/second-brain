# Val approaches David Sloan directly — the introduction is made and Zazanis has stepped aside

The routing decision taken at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-08-arc-gaming-bundle-sanity-check-and-david-sloan-intro.md|Sept-8 2026 ARC Gaming call]]. It is the moment the [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-31-arc-kiosk-board-swap-routed-through-the-ceo.md|Aug-31 "route it through the CEO" ruling]] stops being a plan and becomes an actual introduction — though the proposal now travelling that route is the **Georgia bundle giveaway**, not the kiosk board-swap that ruling was about. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Vertical_Screen_Bundle_Offer_and_David_Intro-September-8-2026.md]]

## What was decided

**Val initiates contact with [[wiki/people/david-sloan.md|David Sloan]] himself, and the two of them set the time between them.** [[wiki/people/georgios-zazanis.md|Zazanis]]'s own account of what he has already done: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Vertical_Screen_Bundle_Offer_and_David_Intro-September-8-2026.md]]

> "Sorry, I didn't want to be in the middle of your discussions, right? So **I said to David, you know, Val wants to talk to you. He will reach out to you**, so you guys can schedule something based on your availability, both of you, right, when it's convenient. So **reach out to him. He's expecting you.** You know, a note, a call, a text, whatever, and you guys figure it out."

Val closed on it: "The date and time. **Thank you, George. I really appreciate it.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Vertical_Screen_Bundle_Offer_and_David_Intro-September-8-2026.md]]

## The question Val actually asked, and the answer he got

Val asked whether the offer was worth putting in front of the CEO at all — "For sure. I am not. **Should I even present it to David?**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Vertical_Screen_Bundle_Offer_and_David_Intro-September-8-2026.md]]

Zazanis's reply opens with "**I don't think so**" and then argues the opposite for the rest of the turn, so the two-word opening reads as a false start rather than a verdict — the derived notes take the turn as "it does not matter either way and there is nothing to lose." Recorded that way, with the wording visible: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Vertical_Screen_Bundle_Offer_and_David_Intro-September-8-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Vertical_Screen_Bundle_Offer_and_David_Intro-September-8-2026.md]]

> "**It doesn't matter at this point.** It doesn't matter because again, **the last three, four weeks we have seen. I have seen at least so many different solutions from from everybody. You have no idea. One more, it doesn't make any difference.** His eyes, and at the end of the day, **he might say, 'Oh my God, this is a good opportunity. Let me test it. Let me take it… Let's see how it works.'**"

And, flatly: "**You know, there is nothing to lose here. Absolutely nothing**," plus his read of the man — "**she's a very reasonable guy. I'm telling you, she's very reasonable guy**" *(a speaker slip about David Sloan, not a transcription error — the derived notes flag it as such)*. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Vertical_Screen_Bundle_Offer_and_David_Intro-September-8-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Vertical_Screen_Bundle_Offer_and_David_Intro-September-8-2026.md]]

## Why this matters against the Aug-31 ruling

- **Aug 31 established the route and the prohibition.** Zazanis blocked Val from calling the kiosk vendor ("Bill") directly and insisted "**Has to come from David. David has to tell Bill.**" See [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-31-arc-kiosk-board-swap-routed-through-the-ceo.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **Sept 8 supplies the introduction, and Zazanis removes himself.** "I didn't want to be in the middle of your discussions." That is new: on Aug 31 he was the channel; eight days later he is standing aside and Val owns the relationship. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Vertical_Screen_Bundle_Offer_and_David_Intro-September-8-2026.md]]
- **What is going up the route has changed.** The Aug-31 commitment — put the kiosk-conversion proposal together and route it via Sloan — is **not** discharged by this source, and the kiosk conversion is not mentioned at all on Sept 8. What Val asked about taking to Sloan is the bundle. Both are recorded; neither source reconciles them. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Vertical_Screen_Bundle_Offer_and_David_Intro-September-8-2026.md]]

## Sept 9 2026 — discharged, two days early, and it went further than the bundle

The contact happened on **September 9 2026 at 4:35 PM Eastern**, two days ahead of the follow-up date set on Sept 8 and without the intermediate scheduling exchange this decision anticipated. See [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-09-arc-gaming-backend-displacement-negotiation.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

**What Val actually put to Sloan answers this page's open question in one direction and leaves the other half untouched:** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

- **The bundle: yes.** The machine at $15,750 with a kiosk, a point of sale and five more ports free — [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-arc-account-exclusivity-sought-and-not-obtained.md]].
- **And something the Sept-8 call never contemplated: the back end.** Val disclosed JVL's intent to displace [[wiki/organizations/incom.md|InComm]], with no NDA — [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-val-discloses-the-incomm-displacement-intent-to-arcs-ceo.md]].
- **The kiosk board-swap: no.** It was **not raised at all**. The Aug-31 commitment that routes it through Sloan is **not** discharged by this meeting, and the friendship question it turns on was asked in a different form — whether Val would be stepping on [[wiki/people/bill.md|Bill]]'s toes — and got "A little bit, yeah."
- **[[wiki/people/georgios-zazanis.md|Zazanis]]'s "nothing to lose" read held up.** Sloan did not refuse the approach, gave Val 22 recorded minutes plus whatever followed the recorder's battery failure, and set a second meeting for September 16 — but he also said flatly that his whole business cannot be bought.

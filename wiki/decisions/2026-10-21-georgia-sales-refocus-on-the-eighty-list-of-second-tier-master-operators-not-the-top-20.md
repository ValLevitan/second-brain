# Sales effort is refocused on the "Eighty-List" of second-tier Georgia master operators, not the top 20

Stated by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] to [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda]] on the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-21-val-felipe-sales-refocus-on-the-eighty-list.md|Oct-21 11:55 AM call]], a seven-minute recording that starts mid-conversation. The derived notes list it as a decision by Val [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Sales_Outbound_Targeting_Mid_Tier_Operators-October-21-2026.md]].

## What Val said

- **The direction.** "I do not see a way out of this without … refocusing our efforts on the … Eighty-List list of operators." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sales_Outbound_Targeting_Mid_Tier_Operators-October-21-2026.md]]
- **Not the top 20.** "At this stage, I don't see a way of dealing with the top twenty as as a solution." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sales_Outbound_Targeting_Mid_Tier_Operators-October-21-2026.md]]
- **What the Eighty-List is.** The list of registered master operators in Georgia that form the second tier; each is about 20 locations, or about 100 machines. This definition is in the derived notes, given by Val the same day; it is not spoken on tape [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Sales_Outbound_Targeting_Mid_Tier_Operators-October-21-2026.md]].

## The market argument around it

- **Felipe's view.** Smaller operators "might be more inclined towards either a cheaper solution to upgrading the equipment or following what's hot in the market." He said everyone is getting terms from makers ("you buy five. They'll give you a discount"), that IGT "has been hit", and that "even Light & Wonder is not selling like hotcakes" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sales_Outbound_Targeting_Mid_Tier_Operators-October-21-2026.md]].
- **Val's view.** "Everybody knows what's hot in the market. It's Aristocrat." A small operator going to Aristocrat "will not get terms, and they will be asked to pay twenty-five thousand dollars"; he doubts small operators get terms. He called Felipe's view "a hypothesis. Maybe you're right. Maybe you're wrong" — "that's to be proven" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sales_Outbound_Targeting_Mid_Tier_Operators-October-21-2026.md]].

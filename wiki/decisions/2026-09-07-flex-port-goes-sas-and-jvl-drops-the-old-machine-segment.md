# Flex-Port goes SAS, and the old-machine segment is dropped

The opening ruling of the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-07-sas-aft-on-flex-port-and-remote-update.md|Sept 7 2026 10:02 AM engineers' alignment]] (Russian). [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] stated the purpose in his first sentence: "Обсудить подключение flex порта через sa[s] протокол и уйти от вот этой всей акробатики, которая подключает[ся]… через ка[унтеры]" — connect the Flex-Port through SAS and get away from all the acrobatics of connecting through counters and bill acceptors. His next line is "Флег спорт… напрямую к со[с]" *(the verb is garbled in the transcript; the sense — Flex-Port connects directly to SAS — is the one the derived notes give)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_SAS_AFT_on_FlexPort_and_Remote_Update-September-7-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_SAS_AFT_on_FlexPort_and_Remote_Update-September-7-2026.md]].

It is the execution follow-on to [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-04-port-the-smib-sas-module-into-the-flex-port-main-branch.md]] and to [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-flex-port-sas-universal-with-scanner-touch-vs-non-touch.md]].

## The commercial consequence, stated by Val himself

"Проект флекс[-]порт сда[ё]тся с[о] [SAS]. Мы уходим от этого гибкого подключения, и мы не будем адресовать ту часть рынка, которая сегодня стоит на старых машинах" — the Flex-Port project goes with SAS, the flexible connection is dropped, and JVL will not address the part of the market sitting on old machines [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_SAS_AFT_on_FlexPort_and_Remote_Update-September-7-2026.md]].

That segment is the one the wiki already carries an **open contradiction** about — Val at 15–20 % on Aug 24, Pavel at 10 % on Sept 2, Val at "90 % have two SAS ports" to InComm the same day (see [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]], Open contradictions). **Nothing in either Sept-7 source quantifies it**, so the contradiction is untouched here — Val names the segment but attaches no number to it. *(He quantified it the next day, to a customer — see the Sept-8 section below, which adds two further data points to the same contradiction.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_SAS_AFT_on_FlexPort_and_Remote_Update-September-7-2026.md]].

## Sept 8 2026 — the segment sized, out loud, to a customer

The day after this ruling, at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-08-dixie-amusement-bundle-offer-and-card-economics.md|Sept-8 Dixie Amusement visit]], [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] put the same reasoning to a Georgia master licensee — **the first time it is stated outside JVL** — and attached numbers to it, which the Sept-7 sources did not. He credited the position to the same-day internal work: "**We just spoke today, Andre and I, probably for two hours, to exhaustion. So honestly, we don't know what to do about it.**" *(He names "Andre" only; read as [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] from the commercial/product context — an **inference**, not stated. The two-hour session is not in any source in `raw/`, and it is not the Sept-7 10:02 engineers' call recorded above, which was the previous day.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]

**The position, stated to the customer:**

> "the question is, Andre and I were discussing today, probably for two hours on its solution. **We feel that this market will disappear within three years**… The market of single-[SAS] machines… **It will die within three years. It's already dying. So what's the point of trying to craft something that is dying? We're thinking maybe we shouldn't even support single-[SAS]. Just let it go, and connect to a second-[SAS] port.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]

**The arithmetic he gave, verbatim in outline:** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]

- Installed single-SAS base: "**our speculation is about ten thousand machines**."
- Market size: "**we're saying forty-seven thousand machines in the market.**"
- Replacement: "the rule of thumb everywhere in the world is **ten percent replacement. Let's say four thousand machines per year.**"
- Displacement: "**Seventy to eighty percent of that four thousand will be single-[SAS] machines, so that makes it thirty-three hundred machines per year.**"
- Runway: "**Out of ten thousand installed, fifteen thousand remaining. So between three to five years, it will all disappear.**" *(The "ten thousand installed, fifteen thousand remaining" clause is internally inconsistent and is recorded as spoken, not repaired. Note also that "within three years" and "between three to five years" are both said in the same passage.)*
- Revenue per single-SAS machine: "**the average is 108. So if the average is 108, we figure that single [SAS] is probably around 60 to 70 dollars per machine, on average, maybe less. I'm thinking more like 40.**"
- And the general principle, restated a minute later: "**there will always be technology that will always progress. That's why we're not sure that we should support single [SAS].**"

**Salim's independent confirmation on the regulatory half:** "**if it's single [SAS], lottery is not ready to let you check in**… **So it has to be dual [SAS].**" Val's answer was the device design, not the protocol — "**No. This device is intelligent. We connect it to counters and connect the bill acceptor to this device**" over "**pulse and serial**" — which is precisely the "acrobatics" this ruling drops. Both are recorded; neither source reconciles the two. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]

**Salim's own sizing points the opposite way, and is recorded as evidence on the open contradiction, not as agreement.** "Now I think **we are seven percent ahead, which is dual [SAS], and rest is still that single [SAS] market** where that will change." Seven percent dual-SAS is irreconcilable with Val's own "90 % of the market has two SAS ports" of Sept 2; both now sit in the contradiction held on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]

**Nothing here changes the ruling** — Val describes it as unsettled to a customer ("we don't know what to do about it," "we're thinking maybe," "we're not sure"), while the Sept-7 engineering decision states it flatly. The customer-facing hedging and the internal decision are recorded side by side. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_SAS_AFT_on_FlexPort_and_Remote_Update-September-7-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]

## What was decided

- **SAS goes into the current Flex-Port software, not a separate project.** [[wiki/people/evgeniy-alekseev.md|Evgeniy Alekseev]]: "это нужно вставить в текущий софт[ ]флэкспорт или это отдельный проект?" Val: "В текущий" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_SAS_AFT_on_FlexPort_and_Remote_Update-September-7-2026.md]].
- **No new menu; the user flow shifts only slightly.** [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]]: "зачем[?] там меню не надо. Единственное, что придётся… поменять немножко юзер[ ]флоу, то есть экраны, которые у нас отображаются. Ну незначительно, по сути всё примерно так же остаётся" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_SAS_AFT_on_FlexPort_and_Remote_Update-September-7-2026.md]].
- **One firmware supports every interface, selected by configuration.** Alekseev raised the problem: "у нас в машине может быть 3 интерфейса… [ … ,] ebd[s] и sas… И они должны поддерживаться одним ферм[варе]" *(the first of the three is not legible in the transcript and the derived notes do not name it either — only **EBDS** and **SAS** are readable; no third protocol is asserted here)*. Val could not answer ("я не могу на этот вопрос ответить"). Pavel supplied the shape and Alekseev confirmed it was his intent: "лучше в одном софте держать всё… мы уже вправе конфигурировать флекспорт, как ему дальше работать; если мы ему говорим, что ты работаешь по сас… то мы код, который там загружается, связанный с EBDS и [идентификаторами,] просто не грузим. И работаем исключительно по са[с]у. Ни каунтеры не считаем — или, если считаем, [то] не считаем их как элемент вывода денег, а просто… для статистики" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_SAS_AFT_on_FlexPort_and_Remote_Update-September-7-2026.md]].
- **Georgia runs over AFT, not TITO.** [[wiki/people/dmitri-chudinov.md|Dmitri Chudinov]] (credited "Dzmitry Chudzinau") asked and Pavel confirmed: "Протокол будет в [Джордже] — это через a[ ]ft мы будем проводить?" — "Да." Pavel on what that costs: "это другие команды, не те, которые были реализованы… в тестовой прошивке — там поддерживался режим тит[о]… а нам сейчас надо реализовать режим AFT. Транзакции" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_SAS_AFT_on_FlexPort_and_Remote_Update-September-7-2026.md]]. See [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]].
- **AFT and TITO are configured separately, and AFT is the one that is always there.** Val checked it twice, calling his own question stupid. Pavel: "насколько я понимаю, это на всех машинах [A]FT должен быть"; "[A]FT у нас отдельные галочки выбираются; тито… определяется, если у нас принтер подключен… там на странице тит[о] выбираются, какие параметры… для сас надо передать"; and "тит[о] может не быть на машинах." Chudinov: "это разные наборы команд — поддерживать / не поддерживать." Val's read-back: "[A]FT и тито это отдельно конфигурируется… отдельно на всех машинах" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_SAS_AFT_on_FlexPort_and_Remote_Update-September-7-2026.md]]. Pavel supplied the expansion of the acronym, rendered "Эдванс[е]танс трансфер" — a funds-transfer protocol — and Val repeated it back in a more degraded form [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_SAS_AFT_on_FlexPort_and_Remote_Update-September-7-2026.md]].
- **The physical connection is two cables.** [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] asked what hangs out of the machine; Pavel: "Питание и один… RS[-]232 кабель[,] всё" — power, plus one RS-232 cable. Power is a separate cable ("Отдельно, отдельно"). Asked by Klimovich whether every model on the market has enough power outputs, Val answered "Уверены… Все, все," and Pavel put the reason plainly: "12 вольт[,] я думаю, стандарт во всех машинах будет." Val added: "все класс[-]3 машины поддерживают дисплей. Это стандарт" *(he named a standards body after that, rendered "в джилаге" and not legible — not asserted)*. Val on commissioning: "машина сама должна найти[,] потому что это AFT конфигу[р]эйшн — то есть ты делаешь [e]nable на AFT" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_SAS_AFT_on_FlexPort_and_Remote_Update-September-7-2026.md]].

## Where the work stands, and who does it

- **Pavel already ran the experiment, on the Friday.** "Я в пятницу сделал эксперимент — то есть я взял эту прошивку, которую Евгений, 2 Евгения делали, сасовскую… Я попытался туда добавить вот этот вот AFT. Вот я думаю, что это надо закончить, то есть провести тестовые зачисления денег, снятие денег по [A]FT, и тогда… нам станет понятно… минимальный набор команд. И описать их тогда сможем" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_SAS_AFT_on_FlexPort_and_Remote_Update-September-7-2026.md]]. ("The two Evgenys" = Alekseev and [[wiki/people/eugine-yavorski.md|Eugine Yavorsky]], the pair named on the Sept-4 decision.)
- **Pavel takes the larger part of SAS; Alekseev does Wi-Fi first.** Pavel: "я наверное на себя возьму большую часть работы по сас имплементации… потому что Евгений… только в выходные сможет этим заниматься." Alekseev: "эту неделю я в отпуске не могу… там вай фай же ж ещё надо" — he is on vacation this week and has the Wi-Fi work. Pavel's judgement on priority: "вам как раз и вай фаем лучше заниматься — разводкой… придумать, какой у нас должен быть протокол… даём мы отсканировать этот QR код, расшифровать его и применить настройки и соединиться с вайфай сетью. Я думаю, что вот эта задача более приоритетная. А сас мы наверное здесь сможем более эффективно работать, потому что у нас несколько машин и несколько портов. Мы можем распараллелить работу" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_SAS_AFT_on_FlexPort_and_Remote_Update-September-7-2026.md]].
- **The meeting itself is the specification.** Alekseev asked for the changes to be written down somewhere; Pavel: "наш митинг для этого сейчас и есть — что мы озвучиваем, какие надо делать изменения. И потом в автоматическом режиме мы можем сгенерировать задачи или документ, описывающий эти изменения" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_SAS_AFT_on_FlexPort_and_Remote_Update-September-7-2026.md]].
- **An AFT command document is requested, matching the earlier TITO/SAS one.** Alekseev to Chudinov: "помните, вы делали нам документ вот этих команд, которые нужны для поддержки сас протокола[,] тит[о]?… давайте сделаем такой же документ для… [A]FT протокол[а]." Chudinov: "Помню конечно да," and suggested handing it to Claude across both projects at once — "чтобы эта штука занялся клод в рамках 2 проектов одновременно" — which he named as Flex-IQ and Flex-Port (Pavel first guessed Flex-Pay and Flex-Port) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_SAS_AFT_on_FlexPort_and_Remote_Update-September-7-2026.md]]. This is the same document family as the scope-of-record on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-04-port-the-smib-sas-module-into-the-flex-port-main-branch.md]].

*(Transcription note: Chudinov's first line addresses "Саша" while asking how the work will be split; later in the same call he addresses Pavel as "Паша". Read as a mishearing of "Паша" — flagged, not asserted.)*

## Open contradictions

**Whether JVL supports single-SAS machines at all (Sept 7/8 vs Sept 9 2026).** Two days after this ruling, [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] told a Georgia distributor the opposite, in a customer meeting, and changed position inside a single exchange. Recorded, **not resolved**; nothing here withdraws the ruling above.

- **Sept 7 (this page):** "**мы не будем адресовать ту часть рынка, которая сегодня стоит на старых машинах**" — JVL will not address the part of the market on old machines [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_SAS_AFT_on_FlexPort_and_Remote_Update-September-7-2026.md]].
- **Sept 8, to a master licensee — hedged, same direction:** "**We're thinking maybe we shouldn't even support single-[SAS]. Just let it go**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]].
- **Sept 9, 10:04 AM, to [[wiki/people/narasimha-rao-namburi.md|Rao]]:** "we have **no final decision whether we go dual [SAS] or single [SAS]**" → Rao: "you **definitely should go with dual plus single**… without [it] it's not going to work" → Val: "**you just made up our decision. We're going with both**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]].

**Why it is held open rather than treated as a plain evolution.** The statements are two days apart — the same period — and directly incompatible. But they are not like for like: Sept 7 is an internal engineering ruling about how the Flex-Port connects, Sept 8 is explicitly a thought in progress, and Sept 9 is a sales-room concession whose wording ("dual saws or single saws") is garbled enough that it is not certain Val is answering the same question the engineers asked. **No source mentions any other.** Full treatment on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-jvl-supports-both-dual-sas-and-single-sas-and-not-mudagoose.md]].

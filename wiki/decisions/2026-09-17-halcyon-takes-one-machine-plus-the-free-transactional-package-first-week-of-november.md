# Halcyon takes one JVL machine plus the free transactional package, first week of November — in new locations, not on the Axcess estate

**Decided:** September 17 2026, in person over breakfast in Roswell, Georgia ([[wiki/meetings/2026-09-17-halcyon-amusements-georgia-class-a-and-texas-route-partnership.md|8:49–10:19 AM ET]]). **By whom:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] and [[wiki/people/yash-patel.md|Yash Patel]] of [[wiki/organizations/halcyon-amusements.md|Halcyon Amusements]], agreed out loud and then restated twice at the door. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Halcyon_Amusements_Georgia_Class_A_and_Texas_Route_Partnership-September-17-2026.md]]

## What was decided

**One location, one JVL machine, the whole transactional package with it, first week of November.**

> Val: "**Let's open one location with one of my machines and transactional equipment.**" — Yash: "Okay. Cool." — Val: "**November.**" — Yash: "**We can do October. That's fine too. We can be ready by that. If you don't have it, November is fine.**" — Val: "**I will not be ready.**" — Yash: "**As soon as you are ready, I am ready.**" — Val: "**First week of November.**"

[[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Halcyon_Amusements_Georgia_Class_A_and_Texas_Route_Partnership-September-17-2026.md]]

**The customer offered the earlier date and JVL declined it.** That is worth holding: the constraint is JVL's readiness, not the operator's appetite. Yash's own closing remark was "**I'll need a new game by then anyway.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Halcyon_Amusements_Georgia_Class_A_and_Texas_Route_Partnership-September-17-2026.md]]

## The commercial terms, as Val restated them at the door

> "I don't want to confuse you. **You buy one machine. I'll give you the equipment for free… for the entire location, one game machine… and the rest of the equipment is free… Ninety dollars per month per location.** … That's the reoccurring charge **per location**." — Yash: "Okay. Per location. Okay, that's fine."

And the contents, from earlier in the meeting: "**you buy one machine, and with every machine, I give you transactional equipment to equip your entire location with the rest of the machines, with the screens and all that stuff. Everything… Including the kiosks… I'll give you point of sale… I'll give you kiosks, and I'll give you all the screens. And I'll give you the actual AFT.**" *("the actual AFT type of Smith" in the recording; the trailing words are garbled and nothing is substituted for them.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Halcyon_Amusements_Georgia_Class_A_and_Texas_Route_Partnership-September-17-2026.md]]

**A dated data point on the standing $90 question — per LOCATION.** The recurring charge is stated as **per location** three times in the closing exchange, with Yash confirming it back. Recorded as evidence, **not as a resolution** of the corpus-wide per-location-versus-per-machine question, which no source in this meeting addresses. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Halcyon_Amusements_Georgia_Class_A_and_Texas_Route_Partnership-September-17-2026.md]]

## Where it goes — and where it does not

**The existing estate is not available.** Yash's objection was concrete and he volunteered it: "**Oh, you can** [get my business]. **The only issue is I already have all the equipment from [Axcess] for my locations. I mean, I'm just gonna tell you the truth.**" Asked why he does not sell the Axcess equipment on, he answered "**If I get the money for it, I will.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Halcyon_Amusements_Georgia_Class_A_and_Texas_Route_Partnership-September-17-2026.md]]

**So the placement rule is two-part, and it is the operator's own counter-offer:**

1. **New locations get JVL.** "So what we can do is **I'm getting the new locations. So let's put your stuff in there.**"
2. **A couple of high-volume existing locations get JVL too, for the data.** Val: "**Why don't you try couples?**" — Yash: "**Let's try a couple. And what I'll do is, I'll put them in my high volume locations and see what happens there. Because the more data, the better.**"

[[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Halcyon_Amusements_Georgia_Class_A_and_Texas_Route_Partnership-September-17-2026.md]]

*(The recording renders the incumbent vendor "**Axis**" and "**access equipment**"; the derived notes read both as **Axcess**, which is already in this corpus as a large route operator and equipment supplier. The notes' reading is adopted in prose and the garbles survive only in quotation.)* [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Halcyon_Amusements_Georgia_Class_A_and_Texas_Route_Partnership-September-17-2026.md]]

## What sold it — the AFT argument, not the price

Val's price claim — "**I don't think anybody could beat my price**", answered with "No, he can't" — did **not** move the objection; the Axcess estate did not go away. What landed was the **AFT**, argued in three parts, each recorded on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]] and [[wiki/organizations/halcyon-amusements.md]]: promo credits that build the **operator's** customer database rather than the location's; promo credits that under SAS rules sit in the promo meter and **cannot be cashed out**; and **partial redemption** off the eWallet, which Val called "**a killer application**" for Georgia. Yash's reply to the customer-database argument: "**That's perfect. I was looking for a solution like that.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Halcyon_Amusements_Georgia_Class_A_and_Texas_Route_Partnership-September-17-2026.md]]

# Val asks the Lottery, in one letter, for a site controller or testing at Intralot

Settled at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-13-intralot-machine-lockouts-test-lab-and-route-partnership.md|Oct 13 2026 meeting at Intralot]] by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], on the advice of the [[wiki/organizations/georgia-lottery-corporation.md|Georgia Lottery]]'s representative (read as [[wiki/people/chris-boncek.md|Chris Boncek]] — **inference**). The notes list it among the meeting's decisions [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Intralot_Machine_Lockouts_Test_Lab_and_Route_Partnership-October-13-2026.md]]. Quotes from [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Intralot_Machine_Lockouts_Test_Lab_and_Route_Partnership-October-13-2026.md]].

## Why a letter

[[wiki/people/bill-avgeris.md|Bill]] of [[wiki/organizations/intralot.md|Intralot]]: access to the test environment goes "**only… to the certification labs and the lottery**", so it is the Lottery's call. The Lottery representative: "**go ahead and make that ask formally… I'm not prepared to give an answer today**."

Val's case is a precedent from another state: "**The South Dakota Lottery Corporation gave us access to Light and Wonder controller. We actually have Light and Wonder controller in our office. Why did they do it? Because they don't want to be hustled in the street. They want machines operating.**" And: "**a precedent has been established by other lotteries… and we are reputable enough to get that privilege.**" See [[wiki/projects/south-dakota-vlt-market.md]].

## How the ask was shaped

- **Val first wanted to ask for the site controller only** — "**I'm being very strategic about it**" — "**For a possession of a site controller, temporary or purchase or however**". He feared that asking for lab testing would weaken the bigger ask.
- **The Lottery representative advised both:** "**having the two options on the table. You know, certainly is better**". If JVL asks only for a controller and is refused, any later lab testing "**we've still essentially got to okay that**". And: "**we are probably having more of an appetite to do it in house**".
- **He was frank about the controller:** "**we're not in the habit of issuing site controllers to any manufacturer when there's issues. And that's because it is proprietary**", though a loan is "**maybe… a possibility**"; and "**historically we haven't done it**". Val: "**Yes, I will ask for exception.**"
- **Val's closing word:** "**I will follow up with the letter containing two two options.**"

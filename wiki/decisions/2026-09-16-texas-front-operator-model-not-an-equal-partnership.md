# Texas own-locations: a funded front-operator model, not an equal partnership — and no decision taken

**Decided by:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], speaking for himself and JVL.
**When:** September 16 2026, 6:11–6:48 AM ET, over breakfast in Lawrenceville, Georgia — [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-16-dannettee-hickman-texas-partnership-exploration.md]].
**To whom:** [[wiki/people/dannettee-hickman.md|Dannettee Hickman]], an independent route operator with three Texas locations.
**Status: active.** Two things were settled — the **shape** Val will accept for a Texas own-locations venture, and the **deliberate refusal to close** anything on the day. Neither has been superseded in the corpus as of the last ingested source.

This is a **new Texas thread**: JVL opening and running *its own* gaming locations, as against selling machines or transactional equipment into other people's. Sequenced with the rest of the Texas storyline on [[wiki/projects/texas-used-machine-distribution.md]].

## What was ruled

**1. Not an equal partnership, and Val said so before anything else.** The clearest statement of terms in this source:

> "**We will not be equal partners, and we will not be real partners.** When I'm saying partners, this is a stretch. It sounds good, but we're not. **I'm looking for somebody to be a front man to open up locations. I will not be splitting money with you fifty-fifty because it will be mainly my investment.** From that perspective, we need to understand what we're getting into." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dannettee_Hickman_Texas_Gaming_Partnership_Exploration-September-16-2026.md]]

The counterparty's role is explicitly **field-only**: "You'll just go and open up locations." Val keeps everything behind it — "**Back office is not my issue. Management is not my issue. I'm very, very good at it**… **Accounting runs automatically. Everything runs automatically now. I will know how much each machine makes before you wake up.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dannettee_Hickman_Texas_Gaming_Partnership_Exploration-September-16-2026.md]]

No percentage, capital amount, or any other commercial term was stated. **The split is asserted only as "not fifty-fifty."** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dannettee_Hickman_Texas_Gaming_Partnership_Exploration-September-16-2026.md]]

**2. JVL would build a purpose-made cheap Texas machine — one vertical screen.**

> "**I will design and manufacture an inexpensive machine with one vertical screen. Nothing fancy. I will put all my technology in it. I'm interested in spreading my own technology.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dannettee_Hickman_Texas_Gaming_Partnership_Exploration-September-16-2026.md]]

Said twice — earlier in the same conversation as "**I will manufacture for Texas an inexpensive machine, and I will become your partner.**" *(Inference, flagged as such: this is consistent with the vertical-format reads already in the corpus — the Sept-12 2026 "the market is vertical forty-three / forty-three, forty-seven" cabinet spec and the Sept-14 2026 ruling that a vertical Texas product be built from the Georgia COAM vertical games, [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-14-vertical-texas-starts-from-the-georgia-coam-vertical-games.md]]. **No source links them**; Val does not mention either on this recording, and this is recorded as a convergence, not as one plan.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dannettee_Hickman_Texas_Gaming_Partnership_Exploration-September-16-2026.md]]

**3. The model is own-or-lease locations, built one at a time, run clean — explicitly modelled on a Colombian route he watched being built.**

> "So **I prefer buying or having leasing, having less of a hustle, more focused operation, building one by one, clean operation, one by one. I don't know if legislations will work in our favor or not.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dannettee_Hickman_Texas_Gaming_Partnership_Exploration-September-16-2026.md]]

The template is a long anecdote about a friend in **Colombia** who built a machine route from nothing in the 1980s, rented spots, then "starts **buying up those spots when the leases come to an end**… **secures long term leases**", and in **2010 sold fifty percent of the business to BlackRock**, **retaining the real estate** and leasing it back to the buyer. Val was at the man's sixtieth birthday. *(He is never named. The machines he started with are rendered "**used valley slot machines**" in the transcript — an unresolved rendering, **not** upgraded to any manufacturer's name here.)* The full telling is on [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dannettee_Hickman_Texas_Gaming_Partnership_Exploration-September-16-2026.md]]

**4. The cash problem is answered by architecture, not by trust.** Against her objection that a game room needs a person in it and "**cash money makes people funny**," Val's model is "**With our system, nobody touches cash**" — kiosks, a "**proper cage**", an attendant, security cameras, supervisor management, "**You build your own brand.**" See [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dannettee_Hickman_Texas_Gaming_Partnership_Exploration-September-16-2026.md]]

**5. And the ruling that closes the meeting: no decision, on purpose.**

> "**We're leaving this conversation open. I prefer no decision than a decision.**"

Earlier, the same instruction to her: "**Don't give me any answers. We don't need to shake hands on anything. Let's leave it open. If a week from now you wake up and you think, yeah, maybe this is for you. I'm here. You don't have to commit to anything.**" Nothing was signed, agreed, priced or scheduled. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dannettee_Hickman_Texas_Gaming_Partnership_Exploration-September-16-2026.md]]

## Why Texas, in his own words

- **He opened with a warning, not a pitch.** "Just letting you know that **Texas is volatile**. From what I'm hearing, there are mixed reactions." The net-win spread he quoted is a dated data point in the standing Texas contradiction — see [[wiki/projects/texas-used-machine-distribution.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dannettee_Hickman_Texas_Gaming_Partnership_Exploration-September-16-2026.md]]
- **What attracts him is the ability to be the location.** "it seems to me that in Texas, **if you run a clean operation, then you're not going to get bothered**. And another thing, and **this is just gossip. I have no direct knowledge**. It seems that **you can open your own locations, like you don't have to be a part of somebody's location. I like that very much.**" *(His own hedge — "just gossip… no direct knowledge" — is kept because it is the epistemic status he himself put on it.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dannettee_Hickman_Texas_Gaming_Partnership_Exploration-September-16-2026.md]]

## The disclosure he attached to the offer

Val gave an unprompted description of himself as a business partner, framed as full disclosure before anyone commits, and immediately after setting out the unequal terms:

> "Just like you said, this is who I am. I'm telling you who I am. **I am a wolf. I take no prisoners. I will step all over you. I will absolutely kill you if you fuck around. No mercy. And I will do it with a competitor. I will do it with a friend. I will do it with anybody.**"

With the operating rules that follow from it: "**I never bullshit. I never lie. Because I have a bad memory. I don't remember what I said**"; "**I can get to the point within the first 15 minutes. My meetings are very short. Most people don't like it, so that's what you're getting**"; and the other side of the bargain, "**if you can open locations that make money, I will make things run**… **Flawless.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dannettee_Hickman_Texas_Gaming_Partnership_Exploration-September-16-2026.md]]

## The counterparty's answer, and Val's one reservation

She matched the disclosure with her own — she has always worked for herself, is "**not corporate**" and doubts she could be, keeps her own books, and "**would prefer to be out in the field, setting up locations.**" Detail on [[wiki/people/dannettee-hickman.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dannettee_Hickman_Texas_Gaming_Partnership_Exploration-September-16-2026.md]]

Val accepted all of that — "**I'm not trying to change who you are**" — and named the single thing he cannot assess:

> "You're not a corporate entity. You're not a back office person. You want to be in the front. **I don't know whether you can build a business or not, and I don't know how fast you can build a business. I don't know whether you can build a business from zero to something. That is my issue.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dannettee_Hickman_Texas_Gaming_Partnership_Exploration-September-16-2026.md]]

*(Recorded because it is the operative commercial risk in the deal and the reason no decision was taken — not as a personnel assessment. Flagged under §15.8 as a borderline call for the owner.)*

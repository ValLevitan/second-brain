# Halcyon Texas trial: two JVL machines and a recycler, Halcyon fronts the cost, profit 50/50

**Agreed in principle:** October 14 2026 — [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-14-yash-patel-halcyon-2027-plan-forest-park-test-and-texas-profit-share.md]]. **Between:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] and [[wiki/people/yash-patel.md|Yash Patel]] of [[wiki/organizations/halcyon-amusements.md|Halcyon Amusements]]. The notes: "Final yes waits on Yash's partners" [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Yash_Patel_Halcyon_2027_Plan_Forest_Park_Pilot_and_Texas_Profit_Share-October-14-2026.md]].

## What was proposed and accepted in principle

- **Val's opening.** "Would you be interested if I let's say went into partnership with you? In profit sharing in locations." Yash: "Sure… I'm open. Anything." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Yash_Patel_Halcyon_2027_Plan_Forest_Park_Pilot_and_Texas_Profit_Share-October-14-2026.md]]
- **The setup.** In a location of about six machines, "two of my games and one recycler… Two machines side by side, recycler in the middle." No bill acceptor and no printer. Val may add "a pop-up display in the back, just to segregate the two." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Yash_Patel_Halcyon_2027_Plan_Forest_Park_Pilot_and_Texas_Profit_Share-October-14-2026.md]]
- **Someone else's location.** Val: "you should go into somebody else's location." Yash: "Hackberry, we'll leave it alone. Let's go to somebody else's location." Val's aim: "to displace all other machines." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Yash_Patel_Halcyon_2027_Plan_Forest_Park_Pilot_and_Texas_Profit_Share-October-14-2026.md]]
- **Who pays and how profit is shared.** Val: "Instead of me making profit on machines, you pay the cost. So you front the cost of the machines, and we split operation fifty-fifty." Restated later: "nine thousand will come from you. Profit will split fifty-fifty… two machines plus the recycler." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Yash_Patel_Halcyon_2027_Plan_Forest_Park_Pilot_and_Texas_Profit_Share-October-14-2026.md]]
- **The cost, as a rough number.** Val: "roughly three thousand dollars per setup" — then "Three thousand per unit, right? Per unit. So you're talking nine thousand bucks… I anticipate that being my cost", with "some soft cost in it". One more machine is "another three thousand". Yash: "We're talking about numbers generally… I'm not holding it to it." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Yash_Patel_Halcyon_2027_Plan_Forest_Park_Pilot_and_Texas_Profit_Share-October-14-2026.md]]
- **Texas software, not Class III.** Val: "it's Texas software… It's not class three software… don't measure it against Class Three." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Yash_Patel_Halcyon_2027_Plan_Forest_Park_Pilot_and_Texas_Profit_Share-October-14-2026.md]]
- **If it fails.** Val: "I can convert it into Georgia COAM, and you can use it… my loss, your gift. I'll convert it for free… bring it to my shop." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Yash_Patel_Halcyon_2027_Plan_Forest_Park_Pilot_and_Texas_Profit_Share-October-14-2026.md]]

## How the player uses it (as Val described it)

The player puts money into the recycler, which "outputs a TITO looking" ticket. Each machine "has Flexport installed, and the optical reader reads it. You could play one machine or the other" — "It's your temporary wallet." To cash out, the player puts the ticket on the recycler's optical sensor and uses its touch screen: "Give me my cash," and "it gives you cash instantly." See [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Yash_Patel_Halcyon_2027_Plan_Forest_Park_Pilot_and_Texas_Profit_Share-October-14-2026.md]].

## What JVL supplies

Val: "I'll convert the machines that I took on trade here… I took on trade the D27, the horizontal screens, the dual screen, but I'll convert them into vertical. I will install the Texas software. And I will provide the recycler. So all of it is non-tested." Target: "mid to end January." Yash: "we'll leave it out there for a couple of months… And actually get some data." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Yash_Patel_Halcyon_2027_Plan_Forest_Park_Pilot_and_Texas_Profit_Share-October-14-2026.md]]

Val also said he will try his Flex for the trial: "flex is very expensive… But for experiment, it's fine." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Yash_Patel_Halcyon_2027_Plan_Forest_Park_Pilot_and_Texas_Profit_Share-October-14-2026.md]]

## Where it might go

Yash: the route runs from San Antonio to about an hour from the Louisiana border, and stores get bigger to the east, so "we might have to start east to west". Or **Nix**, "owned by Margaret Green that you met", "a really heavy volume location", about eight games [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Yash_Patel_Halcyon_2027_Plan_Forest_Park_Pilot_and_Texas_Profit_Share-October-14-2026.md]].

## How it sits against other Texas rulings

- **Sept 16 2026 — not a contradiction.** With a different counterparty, Val ruled out a 50/50 split for JVL-funded own locations — [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-16-texas-front-operator-model-not-an-equal-partnership.md]]. Here the operator funds the equipment, so the 50/50 split is a different model, not a reversal.
- **Sept 17 2026.** That afternoon Val spoke of "another operator for San Antonio" buying traded-in machines with Texas software and a recycler — [[wiki/projects/texas-used-machine-distribution.md]]. **Inference:** this trial fits that description; no source names the operator.
- **Oct 8 2026.** The D27 conversion to vertical sits beside the ruling that horizontal Texas games go to vertical NOVA — [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-08-texas-rage-games-convert-to-vertical-nova-unchanged-and-igor-ports-dutch-mill-first.md]]. **Inference** that this is the same conversion work; not stated.
- **D27 discontinued (Sept 4).** The D27 machines here are trade-ins, not new builds — [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-04-d27-discontinued-curve-c43-premium-line.md]].

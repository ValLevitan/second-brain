# Diamond buys two machines instead of trading the fleet

At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-10-diamond-amusements-machine-performance-and-bundle-pilot.md|Sept-10 2026 warehouse visit]] [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] asked [[wiki/organizations/diamond-amusement.md|Diamond Amusement]] to trade its **entire park** of JVL cabinets for the new vertical machine. [[wiki/people/sean-fellows.md|Sean Fellows]] refused the fleet deal and committed instead to **two machines in two different types of location**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

## The ask

> "would would you guys be interested in in changing your entire park to the brand new machine that's coming out now?" — and, when asked whether he meant an upgrade: "**Trade… I'm saying trade-in.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

Diamond has **74 JVL machines deployed in the field** ("So we have 74 JVLs deployed in the field right now"). The new machine is **vertical rather than dual-screen** — "No, it's different cabin. Vertical, vertical instead of dual screen" — and Val quoted it as "**fifteen seven, seven fifty**". He attached a free sweetener: the older units he called "**those four one nine whatever**" would be "**just upgrade[d]… with the modem and everything else, so you don't have to do reading or anything else. That's for free.**" *(The derived notes render the unit family as "419"; the corpus elsewhere carries a legacy Georgia build baseline as "1919" — see [[wiki/topics/egm-freezing-investigation.md]]. No identification is asserted.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

## The refusal, and the reason — which is commercial, not technical

> "**Probably not until we see more data**, because the problem you have is with the locations… So you have you have a player that uses that particular game, right? And so **the store across the street still has that same game because these stores compete with each other**… **How fast does the player adopt to the new game versus going across the street?**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

And flatly, when Val pushed tiers:

> "everything we do is data, so it's it's really going to be. **I'm not going to upgrade 74 games if that's what you're asking.** So, like you said, we could do potentially **incremental upgrades**. **But I need to know what you're talking about from a price standpoint.** And then if it doesn't, let's say **we do 10.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

**Val talked him down, not up.** "**Don't don't do ten. Do two.**" — an unusual move for the corpus, and the immediate precedent is his own Sept-2 advice to a different operator to pilot in a tolerant location rather than the best one ([[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-free-ports-and-pos-with-every-machine-bought.md]]). Timing: "We're submitting it for **September fifteenth**. Let's call it **October first**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

A Diamond technician had already proposed the same shape unprompted: "**Take take one or two of your pitch [peach] ones and replace them with vertical and see if it makes**…" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

## What was decided

**Two machines, two different location types**, taken immediately after Val's transactional-bundle pitch: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

> "on that note, I would say **definitely let's let's start with two. We'll do we'll buy two and and deploy two different types of locations.** Okay. Right. **That way, we get feedback from**…"

Earlier in the same passage, on the transactional set itself: "**Yeah, I mean, I'd like to try one.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

**The fleet trade-in is not dead, it is deferred behind three gates** — more field data, a written price, then possibly incremental upgrades. Nothing in the source closes it.

## How this sits against JVL's other Georgia deals

- **It is the smallest committed order in the three-week Georgia run, and the only one where Val argued the customer *down*.** The comparable asks — [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-rao-takes-the-bundle-with-jvl-exclusive-in-the-location.md|Rao]], [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-val-asks-lucky-coin-for-its-entire-business.md|Lucky Coin]], [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-arc-account-exclusivity-sought-and-not-obtained.md|ARC]] — were all for a machine in every location or the whole account. Here Val asked for the whole fleet and settled for two.
- **It is also the first *committed* machine order in that run.** Rao said "I'm in" but named no quantity; Lucky Coin's answer was due Sept 15; ARC's CEO refused. Diamond named a number and a deployment plan.
- **The trade-in price was never set.** See the open question below.

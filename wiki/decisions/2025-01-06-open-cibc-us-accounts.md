# Open CIBC US bank accounts, starting with JVL Systems Inc.

## What was decided

JVL will open US-dollar business accounts at CIBC US for its three companies — **JVL Systems Inc.**, **JVL Labs**, and **JVL Gameworks** — opened one at a time, **starting with JVL Systems Inc.** [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]].

## Key terms

- **Signers:** Ina, Val, and Monica [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]].
- Cross-border money movement (US ↔ Canada) is only by **wire or check** — there is no same-bank transfer across the border [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]].
- US credit cards go through CIBC's partner **Alon Financial**; Ina (US SSN + Florida address) is the likely primary guarantor [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]].
- Documents to provide: passports + Canadian driver's licenses for the three signers, articles of incorporation, EIN letters, and a completed business questionnaire per company [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]].

## Rationale

US clients can pay JVL into the US account cheaply and easily by ACH (no receiving fee), and funds can then be moved to Canada as needed [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]].

## Open items

- Confirm the CIBC platform can interface with JVL's accounting system (Microsoft Business Central) the way RBC's API does [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]].
- Confirm whether Belarus is a prohibited country for US wires [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]].

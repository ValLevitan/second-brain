# Rename the Flex POS away from "point of sale" (May 18 2026)

Agreed at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-05-18-ga-trip-recap-kiosk-intel.md|May-18 GA trip recap]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GA_Trip_Recap_Kiosk_Intel-May-18-2026.md]]

## Why

- **Operators hear "POS" as a store till.** [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] reported that operators he asked about POS "didn't express any enthusiasm": a location that already has a POS needs "a really, really strong motivation to change anything", and a POS is seen as the location owner's business, not the operator's. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GA_Trip_Recap_Kiosk_Intel-May-18-2026.md]]
- **Juan's question and Andrei's answer.** [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan]] asked whether "the name POS" is scaring them. Andrei: in "ninety percent of cases" a point of sale sells goods (a product library, gas-station integration), so operators ask "why should we care about that?" — even inside JVL, "I still got a feeling like we are developing… a point of sale system, but not gift card validation system." Juan: "Maybe, we want to revisit naming P O S to something as far from a point of sale device as possible." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GA_Trip_Recap_Kiosk_Intel-May-18-2026.md]]
- **What the device is.** Andrei asked whether the Flex POS's "first, and the only task… is to issue a gift card"; Juan: "Correct. It's not for selling anything in the store." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GA_Trip_Recap_Kiosk_Intel-May-18-2026.md]]

## The ruling

[[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]]: "should probably depart from naming it P, O S and name it something else." Juan: "let's all come up with names and I'll post it here… to see what we all collectively agree on." No new name is chosen on this call. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GA_Trip_Recap_Kiosk_Intel-May-18-2026.md]]

## What happened next

By the **May 25 2026** Flex-IQ walkthrough the product was being renamed **Flex-POS → Flex-IQ** (Val had asked Dima to rename everything to "IQ"). See [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]] (May-25). Who proposed "IQ" is not on record. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Transactional_Products_Review_FlexIQ-May-25-2026.md]]

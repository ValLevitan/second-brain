# JVL moves its South Dakota office to Nebraska

Taken by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] in the room at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-06-18-mg-oil-dana-giesey-rapid-city-visit.md|June 18 2026 MG Oil visit]] in Rapid City, after [[wiki/people/dana-giesey.md|Dana Giesey]] told him the office was not needed. [[wiki/organizations/mg-oil.md|MG Oil]] is effectively JVL's only South Dakota customer, so the office existed to serve one account.

## What was decided

The derived notes record it as a decision of the meeting — "**JVL will move its regional office from South Dakota to Nebraska**", with parts held in MG Oil's own warehouse cage and a technician travelling in monthly. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]

- **The regional office moves from South Dakota to Nebraska.** Val, closing the exchange: "**So we'll start moving the office to Nebraska.**" His reasoning: "**Nebraska has more customers** … having an office and a tech locally there **serves more purpose than serving one customer here. I would like to have an office there rather than here.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **Parts and spare machines stay in South Dakota — inside MG Oil's own warehouse.** "We're happy to build a stock here. **We can build a stock in your place** of course because you probably have a **cage. Separate cage in your warehouse.** Bring a bunch of things, put it in the cage." Spare machines are included, so a broken unit can be swapped rather than repaired on site. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **A technician travels in monthly from Nebraska.** "a tech would travel here **once a month** to do all the repairs, swapping whatever he needs to do, **putting it back in the cage or putting it in the truck and shipping it to Georgia**. **It's much easier than having an office and a dedicated person here.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]

## Why the customer agreed

Val asked directly — "**Do you want us to continue having an office here because we're thinking of moving our office? Because you are practically the only** [customer]" — and Dana's answer was no: "**I don't know that you really need to have an office here. As long as we have someone to call when we have a problem and figure it out**"; later, "**I don't think it would be a problem not having somebody in South Dakota. Nebraska is not that far away.**" What he asked for instead was "**Spare parts and somebody to answer on the phone** … **Somebody on the phone somewhere who knows the machine.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]

Dana's one hesitation was about losing technical depth locally. The passage is garbled and is quoted rather than interpreted: "We don't have somebody here **from the other side**. Most of the people who've dealt with it are in **Montana or Reno** now, where some of this stuff comes from. So I think that would be an issue to not have that **as long as there is a technical need for something**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]

## The lease, and the one condition

**JVL's South Dakota office is leased from Troy Erickson**, MG Oil's owner — and it is **month-to-month**. Dana: "He told me that the lease he did with you was just a **month-to-month** thing"; Val confirmed: "**It's a month-to-month thing.**" Val asked whether he should clear the move with Troy, and Dana undertook to raise it when Troy was back in town the next day. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]

## Later corroboration — and what it does not settle

A much later source confirms the **South Dakota office was in fact closed**: on the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-26-alignment-planning-nova-september-releases.md|Aug-26 2026 alignment call]] the Light & Wonder SAS test station that had sat in the South Dakota office was described as shipped out when that office closed — "**всё отправили в джорджию**" — to **Georgia**, not Nebraska. See [[wiki/projects/south-dakota-vlt-market.md]] (Aug-26). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alignment_Planning_NOVA_and_Sept_Releases-August-26-2026.md]]

**That confirms the closure but not the destination.** No source in the corpus states that a Nebraska office was actually opened, and the Aug-26 source sends the test bench to Georgia. The decision is therefore left **active** as recorded, with the Nebraska half unverified — recorded, not resolved.

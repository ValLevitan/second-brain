# InComm and JVL reset the relationship and launch on Prize Play first

Decided on the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-13-incomm-prize-play-reset-agreement-scope-and-cash-dispense.md|Oct-13 2026 phone call]] between [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] and Jason of [[wiki/organizations/incom.md|InComm]] (read as Jason Hall — **inference**).

## What was decided

- **The past is parked.** Jason did not want to discuss "who confused who, or who did what in the market". Val: "I'm going to park the subject of who said what and when… for now, I agree to park that subject." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Prize_Play_Reset_Agreement_Scope_and_Cash_Dispense-October-13-2026.md]]
- **Prize Play first.** Jason: "I intend to reply back to Andrei and just work through normal progression of getting that product certified and deployed." Later: "We can launch with Prize Play… it's a much simpler conversation", and "Let's just stay focused on Prize Play." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Prize_Play_Reset_Agreement_Scope_and_Cash_Dispense-October-13-2026.md]] The notes list this as decided by Jason, with Val agreeing [[raw/Meeting_Notes_InComm_Prize_Play_Reset_Agreement_Scope_and_Cash_Dispense-October-13-2026.md]].
- **Skill Pay with ATM access is a separate, later talk.** Jason: "We… are still engaged with various topics associated with Skill Pay and ATM access. So that's not a settled item." He said the lottery would introduce a rule that week, and InComm hopes for an outcome "that does allow us to bring skill play back into the conversation" (Skill Pay by context). Both agreed to talk it through in detail once the rule is out. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Prize_Play_Reset_Agreement_Scope_and_Cash_Dispense-October-13-2026.md]]
- **The reset, in Val's words:** "So as of this moment, let's press a reset button on our relationship and move forward." Jason: "That sounds wonderful to me. Agreed." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Prize_Play_Reset_Agreement_Scope_and_Cash_Dispense-October-13-2026.md]]

## Why — the two positions behind it

- **Val: JVL is a hardware provider, not a clearinghouse.** "JVL is a technology, hardware technology provider, and I think we're being confused with a clearinghouse." JVL transacts whatever card the market sets up. Since the market moved to debit cards only, JVL's device cannot dispense cash: "how would my product get to dispense cash if your InComm card does not provide that path?" Jason: "It's impossible. I think that's right." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Prize_Play_Reset_Agreement_Scope_and_Cash_Dispense-October-13-2026.md]]
- **Jason: cash dispense is the sensitive point.** "Your solutions are very elegant… really, the sensitive point is a device that is dispensing cash… I just can't let that be associated with my products." He added that "six months from now, maybe it changes". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Prize_Play_Reset_Agreement_Scope_and_Cash_Dispense-October-13-2026.md]]
- **Val: the dispenser is already out.** Andrei had already replied to Jason that JVL does not provide the feature, and Val had told Chris at the GLC that JVL is removing it. Later: "Right now at this stage, I have removed the capability of the dispenser. I removed it not to comply with you, but to comply with legislative interpretation at this point in time." See [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-hold-the-recycler-back-but-sign-no-undertaking.md]] (Oct-13 update). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Prize_Play_Reset_Agreement_Scope_and_Cash_Dispense-October-13-2026.md]]

## What the reset did not settle

- **Who may restrict JVL when the Skill Pay card returns.** Val: "the moment that Skill Pay card is back in the market, I would be introducing that feature… You can't restrict me from the market." Jason: "I can pull my card from a solution that we think is not compliant", and "I still have to retain the ability to certify a solution." Val will obey any legal or regulatory limit but "will fight it to the end" if the limit comes only from another private company. Jason: "I don't expect there to be a path where the COAM kiosk can dispense cash… in any capacity." Each side expects its own legal reading of the rule. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Prize_Play_Reset_Agreement_Scope_and_Cash_Dispense-October-13-2026.md]]
- **An accusation, half withdrawn.** Val said InComm was creating obstacles to approval of JVL's COAM ATM; Jason: "I don't think I'm doing anything." Val: "Maybe unfair. I should not have said it." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Prize_Play_Reset_Agreement_Scope_and_Cash_Dispense-October-13-2026.md]]
- **ATM certification.** Asked if JVL seeks it, Val: "If it's required, yes", and he prefers "certification over… litigation". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Prize_Play_Reset_Agreement_Scope_and_Cash_Dispense-October-13-2026.md]]

## How this sits against earlier items

- It is an **evolution** of the [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-hold-the-recycler-back-but-sign-no-undertaking.md|Sept-2 standoff]] and of InComm's [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-26-no-cash-from-coam-redemption-device-for-skill-pay.md|Aug-26 no-cash ruling]]: InComm's line on cash is unchanged; what is new is that both sides agree to launch on Prize Play and set the history aside.
- It follows JVL's wait for InComm's approval of JVL as a broker on Oct 9 ([[wiki/organizations/incom.md]], Oct 9).
- The terms side of the call is on [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-13-new-incomm-terms-go-onto-the-existing-written-agreement.md]].

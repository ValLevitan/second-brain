# Val asks Lucky Coin for its entire business

Made at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-09-lucky-coin-service-burden-and-exclusive-partnership.md|Sept-9 2026 1:31 PM visit]] to the [[wiki/organizations/lucky-coin.md|Lucky Coin]] shop, a week after the [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-free-ports-and-pos-with-every-machine-bought.md|Sept-2 free-ports offer]] to the same customer — and **materially larger than it**. [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] had spent the first half of the meeting listening to the technicians' complaints, then asked for everything [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]].

> "Gentlemen, how about this? **I'm here to ask for your business. I'm asking for your business. I'd like to have an exclusive business with you, where I have your entire business.** I will **move an engineer here that you can use for a limited time for the beginning**. And for you, I will **sweeten the pot by throwing a the [FlexPay] per location, which is a seven thousand nine hundred and fifty dollar piece of equipment**."

[[wiki/people/kyle-shepherd.md|Kyle Shepherd]]: "**That's a deal, Val.**" Val: "**But I want exclusive business.**" Kyle: "**That's my challenge**, and I understand where you're coming [from]."

## The offer, as Val regurgitated it in the room

Val restated it deliberately — "**Let me regurgitate what I'm offering**" — and Kyle checked each item back [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]]:

- **"You will pay for one machine, and I want one machine on every location"** — at **$15,750** *(rendered "Fifteen seventy" at this point in the transcript; Val had said "**fifteen seven fifty**" in full minutes earlier, and the derived notes read both as $15,750)*. Restated bluntly: "**Every location must have my machine.**" [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]]
- **Five additional ports** beyond the one in the machine — Kyle: "So one comes on the machine I'm buying **plus five more**." Val: "More."
- **One Flex-IQ** — "which is that little point of sale."
- **One Pay unit**, itemised: "equipped with **screen and touch screen**… the **optical reader, front-load bill acceptor**, and a **ticket** and a **card dispenser**" — Kyle adding "**and a ticket printer**." *(Rendered "one Pure" in the transcript; read as the Pay unit / FlexPay, which is what the rest of the sentence describes and what the derived notes give.)*
- **A FlexPay per location, free, valued at $7,950** — the sweetener above, and **the first time in the corpus that FlexPay's $7,950 figure is quoted to a customer.** It had previously appeared only as internal arithmetic (Sept 4, to [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi Peari]], as the Flex-Slim retail value) — see [[wiki/topics/jvl-machine-pricing-strategy.md]].
- **An engineer stationed locally for a limited time at the start.**
- **Total framed as "practically getting a fifteen thousand dollar gift per location"**, plus the engineer — the same ~$15,000 figure Val put on the giveaway to [[wiki/people/narasimha-rao-namburi.md|Rao]] three hours earlier the same morning ([[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-rao-takes-the-bundle-with-jvl-exclusive-in-the-location.md]]).
- **No monthly fee was quoted.** The **$90 per location per month** attached to this bundle at five previous customer meetings does **not appear in this source** — neither raised nor withdrawn. Recorded as an absence.

## The kiosk replacement, free

> "How many **[Gauselmann] Kiosk** do we have? I know we've got **ten or twelve** of them… which is a **[Gauselmann] kiosk**. I know the company intimately. **They are not going anywhere.** It's a piece of anchor. It's a metal. It's a **German or Austrian** where they manufacture piece of metal… **I'm replacing all of them by giving it to you for free. And if my kiosk doesn't work, bring your [Gauselmann] out on the location, and you'll be happy. You haven't lost anything.**"

*(The vendor is rendered "GV Kiosk", "GV Gewertheir", "Goselmann" and "Gewetter" in a single passage. The corpus carries this vendor as **Gauselmann** and separately as "Gewete / GWT (Austrian, part of Germany's Merkur group)" on [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]]; treating them as one vendor here is an **inference** from the German/Austrian metal-kiosk description, not stated in the source.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]]

**This supersedes the Sept-2 picture of the same customer's redemption stack.** On Sept 2 Kyle's answer to what works was "**EasyLink and GBT kiosk**. That's been the best combo that we found," with 30–40 % of locations carrying a kiosk. Sept 9 puts the kiosk count at ten or twelve and their maker as a German/Austrian manufacturer. The two are **not obviously the same vendor** and neither source mentions the other; the Sept-2 statement stays on [[wiki/organizations/lucky-coin.md]] and nothing here is asserted to replace it. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]]

## Why Val says he is asking now

- **He is putting people on the ground.** "**I'm moving [Andre] here. We're moving another engineer here. We're quite serious about the market.**" *(Only the first name is spoken; the corpus holds three distinct Andreis (§4) and this source gives no context to pick one. **Left unresolved.**)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]]
- **And the argument he thinks is unanswerable:** "Not only that, **I'll be designing to your spec.** Have you? When was the last time that **IGT or aristocrat came to you? Sat here with sandwiches**, and said, 'I'll be designing the system to your liking'? Can you tell me the date they were here?… **I've been in this business for forty-two years now.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]]
- **Kyle answered it honestly, and partly against Val.** He agreed the gesture is rare and meant a lot — "**I want you to understand that this means a ton**" — but named two firms that already do it: "I've had numerous conversations like with **[Fiery Pay]**, and they've considered a lot of the stuff that me and some other guys have said… **Bad Dog has done it with us a whole bunch of times**… the deck that they have with the **up and down buttons on the deck. Greg got that idea of talking to one of the players at my location**… **I don't know that it gets any more designed to our specs than that.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]]
  - *(That up/down button deck is the design JVL itself resolved to adopt five days earlier — see [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-04-georgia-v4-mechanical-button-deck.md]]. "Bad Dog" is a transcript rendering the corpus has never resolved to an entity, and "Greg" is a first name only; [[wiki/people/narasimha-rao-namburi.md|Rao]] separately named an older Bad Dog salesman called Greg the same morning. **Not matched — recorded as two unresolved mentions.**)*

## Kyle's blocker: his own technicians

Not the price and not the deal, by his own account — the testing history [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]]:

> "the issue there, Val, is that **we would be testing**, and so **we've done a whole bunch of testing the last couple years**… I'm afraid **if I tell these guys we're testing something else, they're gonna be outside my office with pitchforks and lanterns** trying to get me… **I've seen these guys test so many things the last couple years.** So I mean, **price wise, it definitely helps.**"

Val's answer was that he is not the only one being asked: "**You will not be the only one testing. From the time I spoke to you and until now, I've lined up a number of people.**" Kyle floated a middle path — "**Maybe maybe I can talk with the Chinese at one or two other places**" *(garbled; the derived notes read it as testing alongside one or two other places)* — and Val declined to constrain it: "You could try one. You could try zero. **You could try all of them.**" [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]]

## The answer is due Tuesday

Kyle asked for the afternoon — "**How about a couple hours?**… I want to talk to **Curtis**. I want to talk to **Aaron**. I want to talk to guys that are in this room." Val: "**I'm leaving tomorrow. I'm back on Monday night. How about you give me an answer on Tuesday?**" The reply, "Is that fair enough? Yes" / "More than fair," straddles a speaker boundary in the transcript; the derived notes read the agreement as Kyle's. **Sept 9 2026 was a Wednesday, so Tuesday is September 15 2026.** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]]

**This supersedes the Sept-2 expectation that Kyle would answer at the Sept-9 visit** ([[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-free-ports-and-pos-with-every-machine-bought.md]]) — he did not, and the offer he now has to answer is a bigger one.

## Rollout pace: half a year, stretched to eight months

- Val's opening ask, put by a JVL voice: "**Half a year**" for a machine in every location.
- Kyle's pushback is funding, not willingness: "I'm not trying to sound unappreciative, because you are doing me a big favor with the other equipment, **but I still have to come out of pocket for the machine itself**… if you're asking me to **buy fifty of them at sixteen thousand**, that's…"
- Val: "you can buy fifty because **you have thirty locations**." Kyle: "**North of forty.**"
- The landing: "if you feel uncomfortable with half a year, then we can do, let's say," / "**Eight months.** We'll talk about that part when we get in. Okay, **I'm comfortable going down that** road." *(The words "eight months" and "I'm comfortable" fall on the same side of a speaker boundary that the transcript almost certainly mis-splits; the derived notes read the eight months as Val's offer. Both sources agree the outcome is **eight months, with the detail deferred**.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]]

Note that Kyle prices the machine at "**sixteen thousand**" in his own arithmetic while Val quotes **$15,750**; neither corrects the other. See [[wiki/topics/jvl-machine-pricing-strategy.md]].

## How this sits against the standing exclusivity ruling

**This is a different exclusivity from the one [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-31-transactional-equipment-only-with-location-exclusivity.md|Aug 31]] ruled on.** That ruling is about being the only transactional system *in a location*, backed by a remote shut-off. What Val asked Lucky Coin for is **the operator's whole account** — all of its business, one JVL machine in every location. **No shut-off, no non-payment enforcement and no removal of a competing system was mentioned in this meeting at all.** Recorded as dated evidence on that page's second open question; neither resolves it. ## Open questions

- **Will Kyle accept?** Due Sept 15 2026. He called the FlexPay sweetener "a deal" and the price help real, and still would not commit — [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]]
- **Can the price gap against Aristocrat and Bad Dog be closed?** Kyle's lens: JVL at $15,750 against a new Aristocrat at **$18,000–19,000** and a new Bad Dog game at **$10,000–12,000** — "**for them, it's got to work better**… **It doesn't make sense everywhere for me. Whereas it might if it were a little less expensive.**" The bundle helps; he did not say it closes the gap — [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]]

Not reconciled — see [[wiki/organizations/lucky-coin.md]] — [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]]

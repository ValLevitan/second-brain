# D27 goes away; Curve (C43) becomes the premium line

Val's own agenda item in the third block of the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-04-naming-convention-standards-and-nova-conversion.md|Sept 4 2026 afternoon game-development meeting]]: "if I may go back to Georgia Market now. I think that we should **not offer any upgrades for D27 machines**. And the next trade show, we should show… 43 inch curve." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]]

## The lineup as stated

- **D27 is discontinued in Georgia** — "we will discontinue… D[27]", Michael: "the D27 goes away" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]].
- **V43 continues for about three years**, through versions 4, 5, 6 — "we're going to continue View 43, version 456 in Georgia"; Val: "I see[,] before the 43 for probably three years"; Michael: "we'll continue on with the V43 for three years, the C43 starting when we launch it to who knows when" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]].
- **D27 continues in Nebraska** — Val: "you're staying with D27 in Nebraska", Lena and Michael: "Yes"; the next product for concept and design is **South Dakota D2703** (horizontal, not vertical) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]].
- **C43 is a NOVA product and is introduced at the Georgia show.** Michael: "we're introducing C43. Yes, **C43 Nova will be introduced at the Georgia show. It'll be developed in Nova.**" Val: "Let's call it C for curve, C43" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]].

## Where the disagreement is — should Curve look different from V43

- **Val's ask:** "I would like to ask if curve graphic user interface and the whole presentation could be designed… differently from V43… Vertical. And curve. Should not look the same." Michael: "Why?" Val: "I don't know." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]]
- **Michael's objection:** "That's… basically a violation of branding 101 if we're trying to establish JVL branding comfort" — and "the one that we've just done hasn't even been deployed yet"; "It's possible, but it is extremely unconventional. Our competitors don't do it. Curve is just a different… the same as a vertical in the minds of…" He also noted the aspect ratio of Curve matches that of Vertical [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]].
- **Juan reframed it commercially, and that is the version that stuck:** "how can we do a **liquid glass plus** for the curve to make it appear as a premium, a more premium product?" and, plainly, "if we want to **sell Curve for $1,000 more**, how do I get there visually?" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]]
- **Lena's counter:** premium should come from the games, not the home screen — "they should be premium not only for the reason of difference in home screen, probably games different, like more premium games… some licensed games or whatever." Juan: "I don't think we're there yet for licensed games." Michael's resolution for the games question: C43 gets "more new games for that release and then we'll also have our tried and true, we'll have a blend… just like Aristocrat" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]].
- **Michael's landing summary:** "you have a V43 and a C43, we need to reimagine the interface in such a fashion that the C43 has a come on factor that says this is fresh, **this is more expensive**, this is new." Val: "More expensive, yeah." Left as a thinking item — "let us let us give some thought to it" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]].
- **Juan's implementation idea, unanswered:** "could the effect and the extra glitz and glitter be an on and off switch so that we only have one software and you're just enabling it for the cur[ve], disabling it for the V43 just as a cost reduction?" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]]

## Val's market reasoning — why a premium cabinet works in this market specifically

This is the fullest statement of Val's Georgia market read in the corpus, and it is the argument the Curve decision rests on [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]]:

- **The market is a status-symbol society.** The locations are C-stores, gas stations, variety stores, "dirty locations for the most part, poor service, machines are usually locked up in a small room or in between the boxes and cases," and "it's a Uber super duper Uber gossipy environment." A self-respecting store owner without the machine "is antiquated… you're less than" in the standing of the community. Val's illustration of the bad placement: a JVL machine put touching a tank-sized ice maker, where the player's chair blocks the ice-maker hood — "You don't want that spot. You want to be a jam of a location."
- **Who actually decides.** "**Sam doesn't buy machines. It's the operator that buys machines, and the operator will buy whatever Sam wants.** And Sam will put his choice of a machine in a premium location within the… location" — less dusty, more convenient, prime and exposed. (*"Sam" is Val's shorthand in this passage for the store owner.*)
- **What he is trying to build.** "I'm trying to create a visual candy"; "that curve silhouette… it's unusual"; "I want… Sam to come to… an association dinner and say… **I have what you don't have. Because I am Sam.**"
- **Michael's design vocabulary for the same idea:** "Lights, bezels… some serious visual presence"; a modern take on the old slot-machine **candle** — "one of those red dome police lights that when you get a jackpot… rotates around and flashes red"; or "a projector that projects the word winner onto the ceiling." He also predicted "Chrome might be back." Juan's caution: "these are 50% ****** locations. Your machines are blocked by some partial wall… so it's bling, but it's obscured… some of it could be wasteful." Val's fix: "chrome in the dark looks black, so it's not nice, but the shiny lights."
- **The bet-visibility observation that supports the whole theory.** From JVL's own showroom: "when you play your jackpot rush on any bet and you bet 25 cents and you have this ****** jackpots, little ones, and then somebody plays $4 and the two machines side by side, you know how much status it is[?] for people to see what you're be[tting]." Michael's corroboration: the big-bankroll players "lean back in their chair and they look around to see if people were watching their win."

## Val's crazy idea, pushed back on

"Maybe Curve should be a collection of **large format premium type of games**. Instead of collection of multiple games, it's a collection of only premium games." Michael disagreed on market direction: "I don't think that's where the market is headed based upon what our competitors are doing. If anything, they're going further and further back in their library to older and older, crappier looking games for these markets." Left as a thinking item, not a decision [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Naming_Convention_Standards_and_Nova_Conversion-September-4-2026.md]].

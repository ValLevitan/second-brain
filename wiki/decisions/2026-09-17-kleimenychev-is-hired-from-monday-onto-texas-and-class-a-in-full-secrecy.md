# Sergey Kleimenychev is hired from Monday onto Texas and Class A, in full secrecy

Taken by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-17-kleimenychev-hire-nova-direction-and-planning-handover.md|Sept 17 2026 12:45 PM ET "General alignment" call]] (held in **Russian**), with [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]] and [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]]. It is the follow-through on the plan Val stated the previous day — see the Sept-16 passage on [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md]], where he said he wanted to route the street-machine graphics work around JVL's graphics department, take one person in Minsk, and **bring "Сергей Клемёныч" back to work**, paired with a programmer and taught the AI coding tool.

> **⚠ §15.8 — most of this conversation was not ingested.** The seven-minute call is largely made up of **personal assessments of the person being hired** — his competence, temperament, character, domestic life, political leanings, prior-employment history and fitness to manage — voiced by more than one speaker, plus a **judgement about a second named person's mindset**. **None of that is recorded here or anywhere in the wiki.** This page carries the non-compensation business substance only. **No compensation material of any kind appears in either source** — no salary, rate, bonus, equity or engagement terms, and no paraphrase of any; there was nothing of that class to withhold.

## The instruction

Val framed it as conditional on the room's support, then gave it across three cues:

> "**Если вы готовы, если вам нравится, куда мы идём? Если вы поддерживаете, то направление, куда мы идём? Я советую климеча взять на работу с понедельника.**"
> "**И толкать направление Техас и класс эй.**"
> "**В полном в полном секрете.**"
> [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Kleimenich_Hire_Nova_Direction_and_Planning_Handover-September-17-2026.md]]

Three things, in order:

1. **Hire him from Monday.** The transcript says only "**с понедельника**" — from Monday. It names no calendar date. The next Monday after this Thursday call is **September 21 2026**, which is the date the derived notes give [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Kleimenich_Hire_Nova_Direction_and_Planning_Handover-September-17-2026.md]]; the date is **derived, not spoken**.
2. **Put him on the Texas and Class A direction** — "**толкать направление Техас и класс эй**", push the Texas and Class A direction. ("класс эй" is the transcript's rendering of the English *Class A*.) **Which Class A market is not stated** — the words "Georgia" and "COAM" do not appear in either source. Not inferred.
3. **Keep the work in full secrecy** — "**В полном в полном секрете.**"

Val addressed the instruction to the room in the plural ("если **вы** готовы… Я советую"), i.e. to both Pavel and Irina. The derived notes assign the hiring action to Pavel alone; the transcript does not.

## Val directs the work himself

> "**А командовать парадом буду я.**"
> [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Kleimenich_Hire_Nova_Direction_and_Planning_Handover-September-17-2026.md]]

— I will run the show. This is the reporting line the call establishes: the work is directed by Val, not by the person hired and not through a manager.

## The no-consultation rule

Pavel relayed a question from the candidate himself:

> "**И вот один из вопросов был у Сергея, а может ли он?**" … "**Обращаться к ленения с какими то вопросами в процессе этой работы или это полностью Тайная работа?**"
> [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Kleimenich_Hire_Nova_Direction_and_Planning_Handover-September-17-2026.md]]

— may he approach [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena]] with questions during this work, or is it fully secret work? ("ленения" is the transcript's garble of "Лене".)

Val's answer was immediate and unqualified:

> "**Даю ответ.**" … "**Что абсолютно нет.**"
> [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Kleimenich_Hire_Nova_Direction_and_Planning_Handover-September-17-2026.md]]

**Recorded:** Lena is **not** to be consulted on this work at any point. Val's stated reasoning was a **personal assessment of Lena**, which is **not ingested** (§15.8). Val did open that reasoning by asking the room not to misunderstand him and saying he does not think badly of her — that much is recorded because it bounds the ruling: the no-consultation rule is a secrecy instruction, not a statement against her.

## Risk posture agreed for the work

The one substantive non-personal exchange before the instruction was about accepting an uncertain outcome. Pavel put the condition:

> "**Мы не знаем, стопроцентный ли получится результат или нет, то эксперимент провести можно.**"
> [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Kleimenich_Hire_Nova_Direction_and_Planning_Handover-September-17-2026.md]]

— if we accept that we do not know whether the result will be 100%, the experiment can be run. Val's answer: "**100% только таксы и смерть.**" … "**Всё остальное.**" … "**Подлежит.**" … "**Проценту вероятности.**" — only taxes and death are 100%; everything else is subject to a percentage of probability. Pavel closed it with the scale limit he had already given the candidate: "**мы не собираемся делать 20 игр, а потом сказать, что всё плохо и всё в мусорку**" — we are not going to make 20 games and then say it is all bad and throw it in the bin. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Kleimenich_Hire_Nova_Direction_and_Planning_Handover-September-17-2026.md]]

**Note on scope:** the transcript never names *which* experiment or task this hire is for — "tasks of this kind" is as specific as it gets, and the 12:19 PM session that ended two minutes earlier is not referred to anywhere in the file. The assignment as stated is "Texas and Class A"; anything narrower would be an inference and is not made here.

# Refurbished trade-in machines are sold apart from the show special, and show-special machines are not financed

Said by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] to [[wiki/organizations/blue-sky-gold-rush.md|Blue Sky / Gold Rush]] at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-15-blue-sky-gold-rush-refurbished-machines-offer-and-transactional-demo.md|Oct-15 5:18 PM meeting]] at the JVL office. The derived notes list both points as decisions by Val [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]]. These are sales rules stated to one customer; no internal meeting confirmed them.

## What Val said

- **No financing on a machine already discounted by half.** "when we sell a machine at full price, we are receptive to some sort of financing. But when we have already discounted the machine by fifty percent, we are not receptive to financing. We will work something out, but it will depend. We also need to understand the full view." The "fifty percent" is the free transactional set given with each machine — see [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-free-ports-and-pos-with-every-machine-bought.md]] (Oct 15) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]].
- **The refurbished machines do not get the show special.** "We cannot offer a fifty percent discount on that. We don't want to mix two things up." And: "We can't tell you refurbish equipment plus the deal, 50 percent. That's not gonna work for us." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]]

## Why

- **JVL is "buying the market".** Val: "We're taking a financial hit. We're… actually going and buying the market… because we're behind, so we're coming into the market where people are already in the market, so we now have to recapture the market. We're giving things away." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]]
- **Refurbished stock is small and already cheaper.** "we have equipment that we take on trade-in… We take it in, we fully refurbish it here, put the new software in, and yes, we can sell it cheaper. But it's a very limited quantity. Right now we have 20 machines." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]]

## What the refurbished machines are

- **20 machines**, from trade-ins, "fully refurbished and in very good condition", with "a ninety-day warranty" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]].
- **No topper.** Felipe: "I don't think I have refurbished toppers. That would have to be new." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]]
- **No modem.** Val: "The used machines don't have it." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]]
- **Not all are in good shape yet.** Val: some "Have broken monitors, have broken touchscreens, have… burnt out PC boards. I need to understand what I'm putting inside of the machine and what my final cost will be." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]]

## How it played out in the same meeting

Financing was still on the table for the refurbished machines. The operator asked for all 20 at about $9,000 each over twelve months. Val: "So if I cannot meet your price per machine, I can probably meet your monthly payment", then "I don't think I can make nine thousand, but I'm thinking in the direction of maybe fourteen months or sixteen months payment." A deal needs a credit check, the company as guarantor and a bank reference. Detail on [[wiki/organizations/blue-sky-gold-rush.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]].

I don't see a problem with twelve months." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_AKS_Amusements_Transactional_Demo_Two_Location_Test_and_Financing-October-14-2026.md]] (as recorded on [[wiki/organizations/aks-amusement.md]]). On **Oct 15** he told Blue Sky / Gold Rush that on a machine already discounted by half "we are not receptive to financing", while adding "We will work something out, but it will depend."

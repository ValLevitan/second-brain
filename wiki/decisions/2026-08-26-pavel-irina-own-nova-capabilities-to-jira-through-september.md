# Pavel and Irina own the NOVA capabilities → MVP → Jira process through September; developers start on NOVA ~Oct 1

On the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-26-alignment-planning-nova-september-releases.md|Aug 26 2026 alignment call]] Val put a named pair of owners on the September work that stands between his "NOVA is the priority" direction and actual development. It makes concrete the Aug-21 directive recorded on [[wiki/topics/nova-platform-architecture.md]] ("October 1 = begin allocating resources to NOVA; September = polish the MVP capabilities for the first NOVA release").

## What was decided

- **[[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]] and [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]] jointly own** polishing the capabilities set, extracting the **MVPs** out of it, sketching them in **Jira**, and aligning the result across the whole company. Val: "Ложится на вас двоих?" … "Скетчу и ресурсы лежат на вас" … "вам это нужно… Алайн по всей компании. Со всеми согласовать." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alignment_Planning_NOVA_and_Sept_Releases-August-26-2026.md]]
- **The window is September**: "У вас на это есть сентябрь?" — they have September for it — with **developers handed work and starting on NOVA from about October 1**, when resources free up. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alignment_Planning_NOVA_and_Sept_Releases-August-26-2026.md]]
- **Sequencing is unchanged:** the two September 1 deliveries (SALTA/VLT2 and the V3 Georgia COAM release) go out first, then the September capability/MVP narrowing, then the Oct-1 development start. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alignment_Planning_NOVA_and_Sept_Releases-August-26-2026.md]]
- **They must also understand the constraints they are planning inside** — Val: "вам нужно понять наши лимитейнс наш скетжу, что мы должны delever" (our limitations, our schedule, what we must deliver). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alignment_Planning_NOVA_and_Sept_Releases-August-26-2026.md]]

## Why, and the communication demand attached to it

- Val said plainly that the NOVA move is turning out easier than feared **because Claude is doing much of it and the team is engaged**: "переход… На ново это не настолько сложно, как мы раньше думали… Клод делает это довольно успешно" and "наши люди заинтересованы использовать клодом… они подходят к этому с интересом и агрессивно, это быстро двигается." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alignment_Planning_NOVA_and_Sept_Releases-August-26-2026.md]]
- **The stated obstacle is not technical but communicative, and Val named it as their biggest problem.** "в этом у вас у двоих самая большая проблема. Вы или не умеете или не любите разговаривать? Из вас тяжело чтото вытащить. Хотя знание много." He wants an explicit education process — not just answering, but giving the background and the depth behind a decision: "должен быть agucation op prostass [education process]… Донести глубину этого решения." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alignment_Planning_NOVA_and_Sept_Releases-August-26-2026.md]]
- **The "one virtual company" framing.** Val told them to stop dividing the world into "Минск девелопмент и всё остальное" (Minsk development and everything else) — "У нас всё одно" — and that if they accept the company works virtually as one, they will see that nobody else will carry the message for them. Detail on [[wiki/topics/minsk-team-communication.md]] (Aug-26 section). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alignment_Planning_NOVA_and_Sept_Releases-August-26-2026.md]]

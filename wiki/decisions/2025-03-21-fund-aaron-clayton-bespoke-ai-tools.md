# Val to fund Aaron (and Clayton) to build bespoke AI tools

On the Mar 21 2025 "building tools" call, [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] and [[wiki/people/aaron.md|Aaron]] agreed the shape of an ongoing arrangement: **Val guarantees paid hours** for Aaron (and, through Aaron, [[wiki/people/clayton-chancey.md|Clayton]]) to build the bespoke AI tools Val wants, and Aaron/Clayton are free to **commercialise** whatever they build. [[raw/aaron_building_tools-march-21-2025.md]]

## What was decided / proposed

- **Guaranteed retainer of hours.** Val: "your hours are guaranteed — just give me the tools." He floated something like a few hours a day or ~5 hours/week, guaranteed payment, so Aaron can spend some days **building rather than talking** ("if you tell me you can't chat because you're building for me… I will not chat with you"). [[raw/aaron_building_tools-march-21-2025.md]]
- **Val keeps the tools; Aaron keeps the IP.** Val's framing ("my potatoes… your hamburgers"): Aaron builds Val's tools first, then may package and sell them as his own product — "I don't even care what you do with it, as long as I get my potatoes first and get to use them." [[raw/aaron_building_tools-march-21-2025.md]]
- **Bring in Clayton without Val managing anyone.** Val will fund Clayton's time via Aaron; Clayton gains a guaranteed retained customer and never has to interact with Val directly ("he'll never see me… he'll just see money come, and you get tools"). Val was explicit he **cannot manage developers** — they must be self-managed ("here's what to build, go build it"). See [[wiki/people/clayton-chancey.md]]. [[raw/aaron_building_tools-march-21-2025.md]]
- **First deliverable + delivery vehicle.** Val will get access to a separate GitHub space (a "**Foray**" space, plus Aaron's), not JVL and not personal; the first real tool is the improved **transcription tool** (eventually a desktop UI, speaker diarization, SQL storage, and an MCP interface). See [[wiki/topics/transcript-knowledge-base-tooling.md]] and the transcription section of [[wiki/topics/ai-assisted-development.md]]. [[raw/aaron_building_tools-march-21-2025.md]]

## Rationale

Aaron and Clayton have been building an enterprise-AI-architecture consultancy (an alternative to Aaron's prior firm CPRIME); Clayton struggles with customer retention, and Val — self-described as an ideal, loyal, non-flaky retained customer ("I am your number-one customer") who doesn't care how much they make — is a natural anchor client. Val ties this to his standing push to adopt AI tooling and hire self-managing developers. [[raw/aaron_building_tools-march-21-2025.md]]

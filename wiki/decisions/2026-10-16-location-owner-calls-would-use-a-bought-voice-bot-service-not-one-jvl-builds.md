# Location-owner calls would use a bought voice-bot service

Settled between [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] (Speaker 2) and [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] (Speaker 1) at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-16-klimovich-recap-of-the-vysotsky-call-location-owner-outreach.md|Oct-16 2:00 PM recap of the Vysotsky call]] (Russian). Speakers per the notes [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Andrei_Klimovich_Vysotski_Recap_Location_Owner_Outreach_Bot-October-16-2026.md]].

## Owner ruling (2026-10-18)

Val: the voice bot is intended for use **after JVL has received a qualified list of leads**. It calls owners already qualified, not a cold list. This is how it fits the Oct-5 "no outreach to locations" line — [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-05-jvl-does-not-reach-out-to-georgia-locations-and-aims-for-inbound-calls-to-its-ai-voice.md]].

## What was said

- **The question.** Val: of about seven thousand accounts, "мы сможем сделать надежные половина. Говоря, три тысячи аккаунтов. Как физически?" — how would they be contacted [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Andrei_Klimovich_Vysotski_Recap_Location_Owner_Outreach_Bot-October-16-2026.md]].
- **Buy, not build.** Val: "Это бот, который мы купим этот сервис, правильно? Этот сервис есть, мы не будем строить свой бот." Klimovich: "Да. Найти, настроить его, обучить в контексте говорить. И этот бот сам голосом будет звониться и общаться." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Andrei_Klimovich_Vysotski_Recap_Location_Owner_Outreach_Bot-October-16-2026.md]]
- **Who sets it up.** Val asked whether [[wiki/people/sergey-vysotsky.md|Sergey Vysotsky]] can set it up. Klimovich: yes — first find and choose, then set up; when they reach that step he will work it out, and if not, they will decide otherwise; "пока нет второго" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Andrei_Klimovich_Vysotski_Recap_Location_Owner_Outreach_Bot-October-16-2026.md]].
- **Plan B — a call center.** Klimovich: "Это отдельная история"; "Сейчас решения нет, понятно, куда копать." Call centers exist, but the approach would need even more care [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Andrei_Klimovich_Vysotski_Recap_Location_Owner_Outreach_Bot-October-16-2026.md]]. *(How callers would be matched to the owners is held — see below.)*

## How it moves earlier pages

- **Oct 14:** Klimovich said Sergey was to propose a bot that would start contacting locations — [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-05-sergey-vysotskys-georgia-work-is-research-not-sales-in-short-spikes-reported-through-klimovich.md]] (Oct-14 update). On Oct 16 the bot is a bought voice service. Evolution, not a contradiction.
- **Oct 5:** Val wanted "a service or an AI engine" if outreach is ever needed, and ruled out outreach by JVL or in its name — [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-05-jvl-does-not-reach-out-to-georgia-locations-and-aims-for-inbound-calls-to-its-ai-voice.md]]. A bought voice service fits the first point (**inference**). How it sits against the Oct-5 line that JVL "не можем автоматом" contact locations is the open question below.
- **Sergey's role.** On Oct 5 he said he would not do calls or outreach; choosing and setting up a tool is the automation work he said he does (**inference**). His time on JVL is now being cut — see his page (Oct 16).

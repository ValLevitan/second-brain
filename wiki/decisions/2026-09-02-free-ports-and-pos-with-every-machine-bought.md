# Six Flex ports and a terminal free with every machine bought, at $90 a month per location

**Status note (owner ruling 2026-10-05):** this decision **stands**. The Sept-29 idea of a lease at "$150 minimum" (see [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-29-pitch-the-transactional-equipment-as-a-lease-with-set-terms-and-a-buy-out-or-return-not-as-free.md]]) is a working proposal still under consideration — no decision has been made to replace "free" or "$90". (An earlier note on this page had marked those elements superseded; withdrawn.)

The first time in the corpus that the free-hardware model is quoted as a **priced, standing offer to a named operator**, and the first time it is made **conditional on buying a gaming machine**. Val put it to [[wiki/people/kyle-shepherd.md|Kyle Shepherd]] of [[wiki/organizations/lucky-coin.md|Lucky Coin]] at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-02-lucky-coin-service-and-upgrade-offer.md|Sept-2 2026 office visit]], and framed it in his own words as buying market share rather than selling equipment [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].

> "Today I am buying my way in. Today I'm buying my way in… We're physically buying our market."

## The offer

- **Buy the machine, get the transactional hardware for the location free.** "We will give you right now, give you **six of these units plus one of these. For free, for every location that you have, when you buy one of these** — but we will not give you this." The derived notes read the three items as **six Flex ports plus one POS / slim terminal free**, with the larger kiosk excluded [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Machine list price $15,750; about $7,000 given back; effective price just under $9,000.** "This machine is **fifteen seven fifty**… But I'm giving you **seven thousand dollars worth of discounts**, so you'll be buying it at **roughly just under nine thousand dollars**. It's a steal" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Ports are about $1,000 each, and the seventh onward is paid for.** "This piece of equipment is a thousand dollars approximately." Asked about a nine-machine location — "I gotta buy the other three" — Val: "You have to, and it's **a thousand dollars each**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Six ports is sized to the market average.** "You have approximately six machines per location, more or less, **five point nine machines**. I will supply these for free into all of your machines… your location will become **fully equipped**." The only physical work is brackets: "The only thing we'll need [is] brackets. If you could change brackets, you'll install it in every machine" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **$90 a month, per location, not per machine.** "**Ninety bucks… per location. Doesn't matter if it's three games or nine games**"; "We're not charging per game. We're charging per location. **You get all the reports. You get all the transparency.** Plus, our machines are connected through cell phone" — and, on the money, "[de]position on your account" automatically [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **What is not free: the larger slim kiosk, quoted at $5,000.** Asked its price — "How much is that? **Five thousand**" — "with the scanner, [the] bill [acceptor] and the dispenser, and the software that does it all." Val's advice was to buy it only where it pays: "**don't spend five thousand dollars now**… if you need it, do it in select locations," e.g. "my top ten percent of my route needs the kiosks" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Upgrade, don't replace.** [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]]: "if you decide to add additional features, you can **upgrade, not replace**." Val on the ATM half specifically: install the slim unit now without cash dispensing and "if we don't [get activated], that's fine… whether the ATM comes in a month or in a year or two years, as long as it comes once, it's fine. **You're not losing anything.** You're still with the same technology, the same product" — because it is "the **same form-fit device**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].

*(Deixis warning: the transcript is a room full of devices being pointed at — "this," "these," "that one" — so which unit each price attaches to is recoverable only in outline. What is unambiguous in the words themselves: **$15,750** machine, **~$7,000** of free equipment, **just under $9,000** net, **$1,000** per extra port, **$5,000** for the kiosk, **$90/month/location**. The arithmetic is self-consistent at six $1,000 ports plus one ~$1,000 terminal.)*

## What Val asked for in return

- **A pilot in a tolerant location, not the best one.** Kyle: "It has bugs. I'm sure it has bugs. That's going to stop me from putting it in my very best locations to start." Val agreed immediately — "Don't put it in your very [best]… **Put it in a tolerant location.** Work with us" — and offered to carry the risk himself: "We will wire the first location. We will **babysit** your first location, literally babysit… overnight. When we're ready, you're happy. Now, hands off, it's your baby. **But our deal will continue for your entire route**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **He named the trade openly.** "You'll be my **guinea pig**… I'm looking for operators that **could put up with me while I'm developing things**. So I'm giving it to you for free" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **And the strategic reason, stated to the customer's face:** "that's why I'm buying my way in. **When I have thirty percent of the market, this conversation would not happen**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **It removes the incumbent.** Among the things the free ports do: "It will make you remove your **[MudaGoose]**. It will replace [it] with this." *(Rendered "moose goose" in the transcript.)* That is the same removal the [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-31-transactional-equipment-only-with-location-exclusivity.md|Aug-31 exclusivity ruling]] asks for — here offered as a benefit of the giveaway rather than as a condition of sale [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Old cabinets come out and go to the secondary market.** "I want more of these machines in location, and I want less of the past machines in locations… **I'll help you deal with it. I'll find ways to replace it, move it to the secondary market.**" *(He names the cabinets to remove in a garbled phrase, "Take DelTeras out" — in context the **Altair** units he had said minutes earlier "need to come out"; an inference, not a corrected quote.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]]

## The machine being sold

Val's three claims for it, each stated as a Georgia first, and each recorded as **his sales claim**: "the machine that you are looking at right now has **three style jackpots**. This has never been done in Georgia. This particular machine comes with **fifteen games**… It has never been done in Georgia. This machine will come with a **built-in modem**, and this modem has never been done in Georgia." The derived notes call it "the new **triple-progressive** machine" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]]. The modem is the fleet-wide fitment ruled on [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-21-cell-modem-on-every-machine-from-september-1.md]]; the three-progressive package is on [[wiki/topics/slot-game-design-principles.md]].

## How this sits against JVL's other rulings

- **It is the [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-24-free-hardware-fixed-monthly-fee-model.md|Aug-24 free-hardware model]] at the top of its own range, plus a condition the Aug-24 ruling does not contain.** Aug 24 set "all the equipment free… a fixed monthly fee between 60 and 90 dollars" with the per-transaction dollar handed back to the operator. Sept 2 quotes **$90** — the top of that range — and attaches it to a **machine purchase**, which is new. Nothing in this source mentions handing the transaction dollar back, and nothing withdraws it [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **The $90/location figure matches what was quoted to a different operator the day before** — Air Gaming Group, Sept 1 — alongside Flex port ≈$1,000 and Flex-IQ $1,000. See [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]] (Sept 1).
- **$15,750 is quoted to a customer as an exact list price**, where the figures Val used with other Georgia operators in August are rounded and vary by context ($13,000 at the show, "just under sixteen" at Dixie, $16,000 in the Narayani notes). All of them sit on [[wiki/topics/jvl-machine-pricing-strategy.md]]; none is reconciled with another there, and this page does not reconcile them either.

# No casino Class 3; Colombia's refresh starts from what earns there now (Oct 23 2026)

Raised by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] at the end of the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-23-game-development-dual-box-rule-and-colombia-class-3.md|Oct 23 2026, 2:00 PM game-development meeting]] (English), with [[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael Gottlieb]], [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena Zhukova]] and [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim Podvoiski]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Dual_Box_Rule_and_Colombia_Class_3-October-23-2026.md]].

## Val's idea

- **Back to Class 3, for Colombia, on NOVA.** Val: "I'm thinking maybe… To go back to Class 3 for Columbia… In Nova, in Nova." He said NOVA could do "vertical or horizontal, like universal", that it "would be a very easy test bed for us", and that it "might give us the practice that we need for class 3" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Dual_Box_Rule_and_Colombia_Class_3-October-23-2026.md]].
- **Who gives product direction?** Val did not know who could give "Prod ownership direction" for Colombia. JVL has "a reliable person there", but "she has never been trained on product ownership… I think she will struggle with it." Michael asked whether she could list the products that are successful there now, with screenshots. Val: "Yeah, that she will be able to… She's a clever, clever woman… An engineer." Who she is is not named [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Dual_Box_Rule_and_Colombia_Class_3-October-23-2026.md]].

## Casino Class 3: no

- **Michael.** Asked if Val meant "regular class 3… Like Florida" rather than South Dakota and Nebraska, Val said yes. Michael: "I think it's suicide." Friends at competitors are doing very badly and "The situation is just continuing to deteriorate"; "Dozens and dozens of games are being released and not a whole lot is sticking"; casinos have capital limits, spent heavily on equipment in recent years on "10 year cycles", and will take a new game on test and send it "out the door" after 30 days [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Dual_Box_Rule_and_Colombia_Class_3-October-23-2026.md]].
- **Val agreed.** "If you want to be pragmatic, you're very correct." And: "Yes, stay focused on what we're doing. Stay nimble." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Dual_Box_Rule_and_Colombia_Class_3-October-23-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Game_Development_Dual_Box_Rule_and_Colombia_Class_3-October-23-2026.md]]
- **Where Michael sees growth.** "if people can't afford to go to Las Vegas to gamble, the street locations are only going to get more popular"; Georgia, Illinois and Texas: "Regional Gaming, I think, is going to continue to grow." He called Val's vision for the transactional product "genius". Val: "There you go, you resolved the issue." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Dual_Box_Rule_and_Colombia_Class_3-October-23-2026.md]]

## Colombia: yes, but start from the market

- **Michael's view.** If Colombian operators liked JVL machines before, JVL now has much more to offer — new art, mechanics, the Match 3 bonus; he asked whether Jackpot Rush ever went to Colombia (Lena: "No"), and "certainly fiery ice has never been there". "if there's an opportunity there to make money all day long. But as a test bed and proving ground for doing something else, I respectfully do" — cut off, read as a polite no (**inference**) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Dual_Box_Rule_and_Colombia_Class_3-October-23-2026.md]].
- **Why Colombia matters (Val).** "we're very well established in Columbia. Our business is ran by a very reliable individual… We have installed base that… We haven't touched, and that's that cow that keeps on giving milk… We need something for the end of 2027… If we don't have something by the end of 2027, we will lose that cow." Then: "Let's gently plan a class 3." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Dual_Box_Rule_and_Colombia_Class_3-October-23-2026.md]]
- **The ruling.** Lena asked (garbled: "a new available at any bed which we created for class three") whether to start from something already made for Class 3 — the notes read it as an existing Class 3 game. Val: "Let's not… Let's ask what's making money right now in Colombia. Let's start from that." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Dual_Box_Rule_and_Colombia_Class_3-October-23-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Game_Development_Dual_Box_Rule_and_Colombia_Class_3-October-23-2026.md]]
- **The machines.** Efim asked if there are new cabinets there. Val: "We have first version of Flex… So, we can upgrade it." Michael: "this would be Nova." Val: "Nova, because we're decommissioning… Rage completely." The machines are "our own machine by participating"; they have the V-deck button panel ("VDACs", "V-Dex" — **inference**). Michael: then JVL would upgrade them "with a new CPU, new graphics card" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Dual_Box_Rule_and_Colombia_Class_3-October-23-2026.md]].
- **First step.** Michael: find out which of JVL's own games are most popular and what players are moving to from competitors — "We just need to know what's going on down there… and we can build off of that." Val asked him for the questions in writing: "I'll open that dialogue." Michael offered to talk with her on Teams; Val: "I am happy to arrange a call." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Game_Development_Dual_Box_Rule_and_Colombia_Class_3-October-23-2026.md]]

# A metal mesh on standoffs covers the FlexPay Slim service bay, and damped legs replace the string stopper

**Decided:** September 17 2026, on the 1:47 PM call ([[wiki/meetings/2026-09-17-flexpay-slim-lid-critique-and-flexport-landed-cost.md]]). **By:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], to [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi Peari]]. **Language:** Russian.

Val had a physical **FlexPay Slim** (the meeting series is titled *Review Flex-Pay-Slim design and construction*) in front of him and read out a list of complaints about the **top lid that opens** and the space underneath it. He framed the whole list as observation rather than argument — "**я просто говорю, что я вижу**", I am just saying what I see — and gave the reason it matters: "**Мы начинаем установки всех этих вещей, это будет критики полно**", we are starting the installations of all these things, there will be plenty of criticism. His standing instruction for the unit was "**сделать это элегантно и красиво и индустриально**" — elegant, handsome and industrial [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Pay_Slim_Lid_and_Wiring_Critique-September-17-2026.md]].

## What was decided

**1. The exposed boards and wiring get a flat metal mesh on standoffs — not a box, not a plate.**

Val's objection: with the lid open the boards "**лежат плоско**", lie flat, alongside "**куча проводов**" / "**масса проводов**" — a heap of wires — and "**они не могут лежать открыто просто так**", they cannot simply sit open like that. Two reasons, both given: "**не очень эстетично**" and "**чтото может упасть**" — something can fall in. He added that the wires probably need shortening: "**провода наверно нужно короче сделать**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Pay_Slim_Lid_and_Wiring_Critique-September-17-2026.md]].

Genadi argued the area does not need covering at all, because it is already a service compartment: "**когда вы монитор закрываете, он же лок[ается], его не открывают[,] его только для сервиса**" — the monitor locks when closed and the bay is opened only for service — so "**провода сделать порядок**" and shorten them, and there is no need to close it in with another compartment. (His lines here are heavily garbled — "компактмен", "компаниям", "компарт" — the compartment reading is taken from the recurring stem and from the derived notes, and is a **reading**, not asserted.) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Pay_Slim_Lid_and_Wiring_Critique-September-17-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Flex_Pay_Slim_Lid_and_Wiring_Critique-September-17-2026.md]]

Val did not take the counter-argument. His specification, in his own words: "**Я бы не закрывал бы их компани[ей], я просто бы сделал бы такую сеточку металлическую… На ножках[,] на подставочках. Не коробку[,] я б не делал бы коробку[,] а плоскую такую сеточку металлическую**" — a flat metal mesh on little legs or standoffs, explicitly **not** a box, laid neatly over the area with the wires and boards underneath: "**уже внутри там были провода и борта, и всё остальное**". His cost-and-benefit line: "**она бы стоила нам буквально копейки, но эстетику придала просто колоссальны**" — it would cost us literally kopecks, but it would add colossally to the look. He also ruled out bending anything: "**Я бы не гнул бы ничего**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Pay_Slim_Lid_and_Wiring_Critique-September-17-2026.md]].

Genadi echoed it back in English — "**Мэш металлический[,] мэш такой**" — and Val chose mesh over the alternative he raised himself: "**или как металлический м[э]ш[,] или просто пластинку металлическую? Ну знаешь, мне больше нравится чем сама пластинка**" — mesh appeals to him more than a plain plate. Genadi: "**Окей… Понял**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Pay_Slim_Lid_and_Wiring_Critique-September-17-2026.md]].

*(No material, gauge, mesh size, fixing method or price is stated anywhere in the source beyond "metal" and "kopecks". Nothing further is asserted here.)*

**2. The taut string that holds the lid open is replaced by two damped legs near the hinge.**

The plastic lid is currently stopped by "**натянутая струна**", a tensioned string. Val: "**она мне очень не нравится[,] это это неправильно, это некрасиво**" — wrong and ugly. The replacement: "**Ближе туда к хен[д]жу поставить 2 ножки, которые… как бы лимитируют ход**" — two legs closer to the hinge that limit the travel — "**сделано оригинально и красиво**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Pay_Slim_Lid_and_Wiring_Critique-September-17-2026.md]].

They are to be damped rather than free: "**если мы уже делаем ножки, я б хотел, чтобы они были бы с пр[уж]ин[ой], чтобы когда ты отпускаешь эту крышку[,] на[ ]не[й] не ляпалась просто так**" — so the lid does not flop when released. Val then named the component he had in mind as "**air шок**" — "**может быть маленького размера можно такие air шок сделать?**" He accepted the cost: "**это на какието доллары увеличить стоимость, но элегантность… совершенно на другом уровне**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Pay_Slim_Lid_and_Wiring_Critique-September-17-2026.md]].

*(Two garbles in this passage are recorded, not repaired: "**с принды**" for the sprung/damped property, and "**поставить туда IT are такие supressers**", whose product name is not interpretable. Val's own word is **air shock**; the **gas** strut wording enters the call only later, from Genadi — see point 3. The derived notes render Val's request as "ideally small gas shocks", which flattens that difference; the Russian is kept above.)* [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Flex_Pay_Slim_Lid_and_Wiring_Critique-September-17-2026.md]]

**3. With a gas strut, no separate travel stoppers are needed — the strut sets the open height.**

Genadi's point, and the one piece of the mechanism he contributed: "**когда она поднимается, нам не нужно делать стоперы, потому что оно поднимется на высоту этого… газ[ ]лоудера**" — it rises to the height of the gas loader. Val agreed: "**Да да, хорошо**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Pay_Slim_Lid_and_Wiring_Critique-September-17-2026.md]].

**4. Self-opening versus merely not slamming is left to Genadi.**

Genadi asked which behaviour was wanted — "**вы хотите, крышка, чтобы она не [с]лам[ала], когда закрываешь[,] или чтобы она сама открывалась?**" — and offered to do both ("**болл своейс**", rendered; Val answered "**босс вэйс**"; both are read as *both ways*, an **inference** from the sound, not asserted). Val's answer was to take neither off the table [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Pay_Slim_Lid_and_Wiring_Critique-September-17-2026.md]]:

> "**И так и так красиво. Если вы сделаете, чтоб она сама открывалась плавно, это удобно и красиво[,] элегантно, но если вы не хотите… вы хотите, чтобы ты с силой открыл, а потом отпустил, но она не ляпалась[ — ]это хотя бы уже лучше. Я оставляю это для вас, как вы хотите.**"

He then gave the argument that leans toward self-opening: the lid was **deliberately made smooth with nothing to grip** — "**вы же специально сделали гладкую поверхность, чтоб не за что было дёрнуть**" — so "**лучше, чтобы она поднималась чуть**", better that it rises a little by itself [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Pay_Slim_Lid_and_Wiring_Critique-September-17-2026.md]].

**5. The front-connected wiring runs round to the back — restated, and confirmed not done.**

Val repeated a request he attributed to an **Andrei**: the wiring that connects at the front "**нельзя подключать[ ]впереди[,] их нужно запустить сзади… чтоб они шли по[ ]сзади и сзади уже подключались**". He believed it was already done — "**Помоему, что это уже сделано, если не ошиба[ю]сь**" — and Genadi corrected him: "**Ну не сделано, но…**" Val: "**Да да да, учтено**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Pay_Slim_Lid_and_Wiring_Critique-September-17-2026.md]].

## Already fixed before the call

The **glued ports come unstuck**. Val: "**порты, которые приклеенные, они отклеиваются**". Genadi: "**Да[,] знаю[,] это уже фикс[,] фикс**" — already fixed. Val's qualifying clause about which port — "**Который наташскрини, а не ната скриня не приклеенная**" — is garbled; the derived notes read it as *the one on the touchscreen side is not glued at all*, which is recorded here as **their reading**, not as a sourced fact [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Pay_Slim_Lid_and_Wiring_Critique-September-17-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Flex_Pay_Slim_Lid_and_Wiring_Critique-September-17-2026.md]].

## Genadi's closing reservation

He ended the topic with a service concern about the same wiring, not a design objection: "**Я с проводами немножко тяжело[,] на ремонт[ ]ли[,] это надо**" — the wiring is somewhat hard where servicing is concerned. He had also said, earlier and with no elaboration recorded, "**А я думал[,] что это серьёзно?**" — I thought this was going to be serious — the same shape as his Sept-14 reaction on being sequenced ([[wiki/decisions/2026-09-14-tito-printer-cases-come-before-the-street-cabinet.md]]). A one-word line, "**Вот и цвет**" (and the colour), opens no discussion in the source and is **not** treated as a colour ruling [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Pay_Slim_Lid_and_Wiring_Critique-September-17-2026.md]].

## Why it matters

This is the first source in the corpus where Val inspects a built FlexPay Slim and returns a **finish-and-fit** list. The programme context is the [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-04-brackets-and-harnesses-from-china-and-a-metal-order-for-one-hundred.md|first ten Slims]] and the ~50-unit request released on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-14-tito-printer-cases-come-before-the-street-cabinet.md|Sept 14]]; the same week Genadi is due to start the [[wiki/topics/low-cost-street-machine.md|street cabinet]] on **September 23**. Val's stated trigger — installations begin and the units will be judged in the field — is the same argument he used on Sept 16 to move the street machine's electronics to eye level ([[wiki/decisions/2026-09-16-street-machine-electronics-move-to-eye-level-and-the-monitor-opens-like-a-cupboard-door.md]]). **Inference, labelled:** the mesh-not-box ruling and the eye-level ruling both trade a small part cost for serviceability and appearance; the transcripts do not connect them.

## Update — Sept 23 2026: Val repeats it, and nothing is ordered yet

Six days later, on the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-23-texas-flex-slim-release-street-cabinet-and-bom-source-of-truth.md|Sept-23 alignment call]], Val asked again for the boards to be closed "крышкой да сеткой какойто" and the board harnesses made shorter: "обязательно закрыть это не это не годится так." Genadi said he had prepared a provision for it, "пока ничего не заказывали", and that a lid can be done. So the ruling stands; the cover was not yet ordered as of Sept 23.

# The accounting team shows its AI use every week; Val's Claude writes the next lesson; next step MCP, then agents (Nov 24 2026)

Set by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] on the call "Claude demos by the accounting team", Tuesday Nov 24 2026, 1:05 PM Eastern, about 58 minutes, mostly in Spanish with Val in English ([[wiki/meetings/2026-11-24-accounting-team-ai-show-and-tell.md|stub]]), with [[wiki/people/mary.md|Mery Villavicencio]], [[wiki/people/laura.md|Laura Escorcia]], [[wiki/people/igor-donis.md|Igor Donis]], [[wiki/people/cris-nacarino.md|Cris Nacarino]] and [[wiki/people/erika-gonzalez.md|Erika Gonzalez]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Accounting_Team_AI_Show_and_Tell-November-24-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Accounting_Team_AI_Show_and_Tell-November-24-2026.md]]. It follows the [[wiki/decisions/2026-11-20-a-mistake-found-becomes-a-written-standard-in-github-and-each-finance-team-member-runs-their-own-email-checking-agent-against-the-standards.md|Nov-20 ruling]] that the team learn routines, skills and agents.

## The format

- **AI only, your own experience.** "These sort of meetings to be. Your show and tell… you will tell your personal experience how you use AI. This is only dedicated to AI." "Nobody will interrupt you." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Accounting_Team_AI_Show_and_Tell-November-24-2026.md]]
- **Time.** First "15 minutes", then "Let's try it for 12 minutes", since there are five people and one hour [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Accounting_Team_AI_Show_and_Tell-November-24-2026.md]]. The demos took about 30 minutes ("I think we did it in. Probably 30 minutes") [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Accounting_Team_AI_Show_and_Tell-November-24-2026.md]].
- **Honesty over stories.** "At this stage, I am okay with you saying I don't use AI at this stage. Two weeks from now, if you still say I don't use AI, then you are probably not for JBL" ("JBL" = JVL, as heard); "Don't make anything out. My goal is to feel where you are" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Accounting_Team_AI_Show_and_Tell-November-24-2026.md]].
- **Val is not the teacher.** "I am not an expert on AI. I am not a coach." He hopes the team teaches each other, and that his AI, which knows what works for him, can "create a short lesson" each time, so each meeting lifts the team "one notch" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Accounting_Team_AI_Show_and_Tell-November-24-2026.md]]. The notes call it weekly [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Accounting_Team_AI_Show_and_Tell-November-24-2026.md]]; on tape Val says "I'll see you probably next week" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Accounting_Team_AI_Show_and_Tell-November-24-2026.md]].
- **Spanish, with live translation.** Val asked Mery to speak Spanish "so it's easier for them to understand"; he has "a translation that's running" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Accounting_Team_AI_Show_and_Tell-November-24-2026.md]].

In one line: **each person shows their real AI use; Val's Claude writes the next small step; the next big step is MCP, then agents.**

## Val's closing teaching

- **Two tricks.** Ask for the summary in "ESL. Type of English" — "it uses shorter sentences. With simpler, simpler words"; and highlight a paragraph and say "chew it up for me" — "chew it up means dissect. Break it down for me" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Accounting_Team_AI_Show_and_Tell-November-24-2026.md]].
- **From instructions to MCP.** "you've been using Claude as a glorified. instruction processor. You should start learning how to use Claude with MCP… You should also build useful agents… But that comes later." "For example, we have MCP for Business Central. So basically you could speak to Business Central and human language." When the MCP lacks something: "We can add use case… send this particular use case to a developer who will add this particular use case to an MCP" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Accounting_Team_AI_Show_and_Tell-November-24-2026.md]]. This matches [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-19-mary-asks-business-central-through-mcp-not-special-reports-and-nathan-adds-msbc-endpoints-from-ai-coo-use-cases.md]].
- **Artifact versus agent.** Igor asked if they are the same. Val: "No, artifact is final material that you produce. Agent is like. Like a server"; "you give agent a mission"; an agent needs "A skill" and "Guard rails" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Accounting_Team_AI_Show_and_Tell-November-24-2026.md]].
- **No rush on the words.** "I don't expect you to know it next week, but I want you to hear these things because I'm gonna continue repeating them"; they can learn "from YouTube in Spanish" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Accounting_Team_AI_Show_and_Tell-November-24-2026.md]].

# Ship the 100 best-condition machines from Mexico first

## What was decided

JVL will ship the **100 best-condition machines** out of the Mexico warehouse first, in **two 53-ft trucks by ground**, door-to-door to Georgia. A **third truck** later will carry mostly Altera and Altera V (and Echo) machines. [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]]

## Who and when

- Decided by Val on the Jan 8, 2025 call, with Miguel (warehouse, selected the 100 best) and Mary (translating). [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]]

## Details and costs

- Packing is about **$45 per machine**. [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]]
- ATLAS quoted **$12,000 door-to-door for the two trucks**, excluding export customs in Mexico and Canada. [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]]
- Routing: ASIMEX carries Mexico City to Laredo, then ATLAS carries Laredo to Georgia. Val wants the two legs quoted separately, and Boomer/Monoplus asked for comparison. [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]]
- Val asked Mary to check with Alejandro at ASIMEX about a possible **export tax rebate**, since tax was paid bringing the machines into Mexico. [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]]

## Why

- The 100 are the ready-to-move units; shipping them first frees space so Miguel can sort the rest of the warehouse. The 120 machines still installed at locations stay put for now. [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]]

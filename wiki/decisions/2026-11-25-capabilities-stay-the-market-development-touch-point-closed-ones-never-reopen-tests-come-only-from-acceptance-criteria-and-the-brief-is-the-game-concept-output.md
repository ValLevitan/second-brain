# Requirements and capabilities stay; a closed capability is never reopened; tests come only from acceptance criteria; the brief is the output of the game-concept stage (Nov 25 2026)

Agreed at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-11-25-design-brief-capabilities-requirements-and-game-concept-stage.md|Nov 25 2026, 7:59 AM "Design Guide to Capabilities list - REQ as part of brief and CAP" meeting]] (Russian, about 1 h 51 min of transcript) by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] with [[wiki/people/evgeny-kharetski.md|Evgeny Kharetski]], [[wiki/people/alexander-biratka.md|Alexander Biratka]], [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]], [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]] and [[wiki/people/andrey-bosik.md|Andrey Bosik]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]]. It follows the previous day's [[wiki/decisions/2026-11-24-a-game-is-one-capability-with-one-feature-branch-per-asset-opened-by-the-brief-agent-no-jira-inside-the-studio-and-project-number-and-repository-are-created-together.md|Nov-24 one-capability-per-game call]].

## 1. Why the meeting

- **Val opened with the Nov-24 call.** "Евгений сказал, что похоже, что есть всего одна кей побил эти." — one capability for the brief ("мы создаем игру"); the tool creates the features, each opens a feature branch, and the capability closes when all artifacts are made. Val: "у меня нету веских аргументов" against it, but the real subject became use cases and requirements and how they point at capabilities [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]].

## 2. Process first, standards after

- **Kharetski** said the Nov-24 scheme does not fit today's standards: requirements must be created, and "у каждой фичи должен быть дуедэйт" — "И как быть с этим непонятно?" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]].
- **Irina:** if the standards are wrong they will be rewritten — "я бы сейчас не смотрела на то не подгоняла под стандарты, а выработала правильную" scheme. **Val:** the same — "По согласию, когда мы согласимся по процессу, тогда я бы уже откорректировал бы стандарты." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]]

## 3. Biratka's proposals — not taken

- **The complaint.** At today's pace the structure "Это выглядит очень сложно и громоздко": one capability links about ten requirements, each with about ten acceptance criteria, the capability may meet only two of them, and it is unclear what to test; "мы тонем в в реквайрменсах" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]].
- **Proposal 1 — brief and features only.** "По факту у нас есть бриф и фичи, внутри которых будут асептанской критерия." The brief is a versioned specification in the repository; a meeting transcript goes to Claude, which "поправил спецификацию эту или бриф и у нас он новой версии уже лежит в репозитории", new features come from the diff, and Claude checks a pull request against the spec. His example: in Transactional a requirement needed a "мутагус" (heard; **unclear** what), then it disappeared and capabilities had to be redone [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]].
- **Proposal 2 — invert the hierarchy.** "Я хочу иерархию поменять" — the requirement is the parent and capabilities hang under it; test the requirement when all under it is done. **Proposal 3:** "Давайте их копировать просто." — copy requirements per product instead of reusing them [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]].
- **Why they were not taken.**
  - **Val — the capability is the touch point.** The product owner lives with the market ("он живёт в джорджии, он общается с клиентом"), writes use cases and requirements in plain words, defines capabilities and takes them to Pavel; developers turn them into features. "Поэтому его язык общения с вами базируется на юске и рекламу." "И побила цвета камень прикосновения между рынком и девелопом." Dropping capabilities means "у нас не будет камня прикосновения" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]].
  - **Val — Jira also plans non-developer work** (service, market research). "Может быть такое, что мы от жиры отойдём, ну я сплю и вижу, чтобы это случилось." But not now: "Давайте не будем пока нарушать этот шаг", or other movements he is trying to synchronise break [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]]. See [[wiki/topics/docs-as-code-and-atlassian-exit.md]] (Nov 25).
  - **Irina — requirements are reused.** "за нашу жизнь там было 1000 игр"; "Но там 90% будет уже Того, что было." Tests written for a requirement stay in the right place and prove a new version did not break the old acceptance criteria [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]].
  - **Bosik:** "Нас тоже не все устраивает, понимаешь, но лучше решение пока не придумали." **Val:** "меня это устраивает больше чем 2 года тому назад" — two years ago it was very bad. Biratka: better, but he wants it better still; "Я несколько предложений высказал, они все были отказаны." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]]
- **Result.** The structure stays: use cases and requirements with acceptance criteria on the market side, the capability as the touch point, features on the development side. Biratka did not agree (the notes) [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]].

## 5. The real problem — requirements that conflict

- **Pavel:** capabilities were split well, but each got its own requirements made separately — "В результате мы получили большой набор требований, которые из разных капбирити между собой пересекаются и могут нарушать друг друга." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]]
- **Irina:** this is what slows her — "Нет, потому что новое требование противоречит прошлому требованию"; "И вот я сегодня не не сдаю версию" because of it [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]].
- **Val:** requirements started in the middle of development, about a year to a year and a half ago, "мы начали вытягивать эти вещи с вакуума", with no base built over the years. "Сейчас поступила интересное предложение" — write requirements from the existing code, "По коду потому что код это сорс овчус" [source of truth], since the machines already exist [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]].
- **Pavel's agent.** Run an agent over all requirements, including non-functional ones, before development, so none overlap or contradict — "Ну там скажем, 80% таких ситуаций." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]]
- **Close automatically? Not decided.** Val: "Я бы закрывал на автомате, хочу объяснить почему, потому что легче потерять" and open a new one. Biratka: it would re-link them so "вообще ни черта не поймёшь". Irina: "То есть зачем прямо сразу это что то на автомате закрывать?" — a person reviews what the agent proposes [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]].
- **Val's standards parallel.** The more standards he writes and points his Claude at, "тем больше у меня возникает конфликтов" and gaps; the same happened with requirements. "У нас должен быть пару агентов" — he named an "origin агент" (heard) and a constant loop that grooms them [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]].

## 6. Acceptance tests come only from use cases, requirements and acceptance criteria

- **Val's ruling.** "Если Ирина направляет свой клод на готовый код, то Ирина это делает неправильно" — it can never find the error. Claude must be told plainly not to look at the code or the prose ("Luck at prouse and don not lock at cout", heard) and to use only use cases, requirements and acceptance criteria: "Вся изюминка заключается в том, чтобы тесты писали только согласно эксцептом" — then the gap between what was planned and what was built shows [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]].
- **Irina:** she checks against the acceptance criteria; Claude goes into the code only to find the hooks ("крючки"). **Biratka:** with today's structure "У клоды реально не хватает" data to do it another way [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]].
- **Contracts — open.** Pavel and Biratka: tests need contracts — hooks that bring the stand to a state; "Эмуляторы возможно, как работать с эмулятором?" Val: "Я должен задуматься, я должен задуматься насчет контрактов, это интересно." ## 8. A change means a new capability — a closed one is never reopened

- **Val:** a scope gap means "Окей, нам нужно открыть кейпобилоти, который фикс этот скоп гад"; edit the current requirement and its acceptance criteria and open a new capability for the fix. When Pavel suggested marking the requirement no longer applicable on the old capability: "Нет, нет, нет, нет, абсолютно нет, я не согласен, нет кей побил эти закрыт, умер закончен он никогда мы кей побил этим никогда не возвращаемся всё." Pavel: "Окей, принято." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]]
- **His example.** The Transactional board: revision 1 without some printing capabilities, revision 2 with a multiplexer for printing, then "Мы сделали 3 кейпобил эти борта" — the revision going to production, without the printer; each earlier revision's capability died [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]].
- **Amendment type.** Pavel: a type already exists, made "Андреем или Димой" (**unclear** which Andrei and which Dima) — "Есть понятие, как обилите есть понятие кополите амман" (heard: capability amendment) — maybe for exactly this case [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]].
- **Biratka's worry.** Old closed capabilities stay in Jira and Claude will follow them into wrong context — "Будет ходить ира, если ему сказать не ходи, он не будет, а если не сказать, зайдёт обязательно." Irina: the new capability links to the requirement ("Нет, мы линкуем только к требованию"), and closed items show the history of each version in the field — "Вот в джорджии 12 версия" is still installed [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]].

## 9. Back to the brief — one capability, with a doubt

- **Val:** "Скорее скорей скорее получается так, что нам" one capability is needed — "Вернее, по этому прифу нужно сделать set of aseds"; the design brief is the trigger to open one capability in Jira [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]].
- **His doubt.** To write a brief you must know what the market needs, and today that is described by use cases and requirements. Pavel: "По сути бриф это набор требований от рынка." What the brief describes: Biratka — the whole game function; Kharetski — "С механикой с бонусами, со всем"; Val — the story, how each artifact looks, the transitions, "я буду называть что физика игры" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]].

## 10. Val's four stages of the studio (agreed "as a concept")

- **Stage 1 (today):** "Франк энд инструмент, который позволяет дизайнеру копировать игры" — it holds the layers of today's games, and the designer can only repeat them with other stories and artifacts. **Stage 2:** add layers, timing diagrams, and the physics of transitions. **Stage 3:** take signals from the machines and make new stories from them. **Stage 4:** film other makers' machines, decode the video into JVL's layers, timing, geometry and transition physics, build the missing layers automatically and let the designer add stories. "То, что я сказал 3 пункт и 4 они могут местами поменяться." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]]
- Bosik: "Но как концепт, да?"; Biratka and Pavel: yes [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]]. Stage 4 is the video method of [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-30-competitor-games-are-studied-for-new-mechanics-not-maths-only-in-jvls-markets-and-the-video-method-is-first-proved-on-jvls-own-games.md]].
- **Signals instead of people (direction).** Machines send signals — maths, statistics, hardware; JVL turns them into a "Новый запрос рынка" (deliberately not "requirements"), then a design brief, and builds the game automatically — a self-learning loop; after that Val would leave Jira for this process and use GitHub. "Но мы ещё нет, мы ещё не там." Today the signals come from people — interviews, field visits, photos, video — so JVL needs a very strong product owner [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Design_Brief_Capabilities_Requirements_and_Game_Concept_Stage-November-25-2026.md]].

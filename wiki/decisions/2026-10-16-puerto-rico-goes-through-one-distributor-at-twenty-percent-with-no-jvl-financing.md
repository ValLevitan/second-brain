# Puerto Rico goes through one reliable distributor at a 20% commission on a ~$9,000 machine, with no financing or collecting by JVL

Stated by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] to [[wiki/people/david-villari.md|David Villari]] of [[wiki/organizations/bad-dog-zephyr.md|Bad Dog / Zephyr]], with [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda]] present, at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-16-david-villari-zephyr-puerto-rico-distribution-and-beta-test.md|Oct-16 breakfast]]. The derived notes list it as a decision by Val [[raw/Meeting_Notes_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]. These are terms Val set out for a partner not yet found; no partner has accepted them.

## What Val said

- **One reliable partner, and JVL does nothing on the island.** "I don't want to do anything in Puerto Rico. I want a reliable relationship, and I'll pay very large commission." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]
- **No financing, no chasing.** "And I don't want to finance. I don't want to do anything." At the end: "But you understand? I don't want to finance. I don't want to chase." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]
- **The number.** "I will pay twenty percent commission. It's a good commission. I want to get nine thousand dollars here minus twenty percent. I don't want to finance." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]
- **Why $9,000.** David put the Puerto Rico price under $10,000 ("nine thousand nine, about nine… we need to be in under ten for sure"); Val: "Let's call it nine thousand." It is the "American price" — the machine is picked up in the US and the buyer handles shipping. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]
- **What Val gives in return.** "I will develop games, and I will speak to one person to do whatever needs to be done." "I am extremely flexible. If I get a good relationship, if I get a serious person, I will work on games." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]
- **Transactional equipment too.** "I would like to push my transactional" — the port, the table POS and the kiosk; prices on [[wiki/topics/jvl-machine-pricing-strategy.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]

## Who the commission is for

David said the commission should go to the distributor partner who pushes the product, and that he wants nothing himself: "I don't want the commission. I'm just helping you out." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]] No partner has been chosen.

## David's pushback, in the same meeting

- **Financing falls on someone.** "That's going to be on the distributor. Then the distributor is going to pay you, and he's going to finance those guys. Nobody has the cash flow to buy a nine thousand dollars machine upfront." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]
- **The 20% must also cover the shipping.** "Twenty percent for the guy to pay for shipping, handling, and port dues… the port dues have to be figured out. I don't know how much that's worth. So the only way it makes sense is to do a whole container." A container holds "roughly 70 machines". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]
- **Test first.** David proposed one to three machines in a location for ninety days before any order — see [[wiki/projects/puerto-rico-expansion.md]]. Val agreed the game must play: "If it doesn't play" — "It doesn't make sense." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]

## How it sits against other rulings

- **Earlier Puerto Rico plans were for JVL's own operation** — a local office, a Ruta street-operator licence and a law firm (2025, on [[wiki/projects/puerto-rico-expansion.md]]). These terms instead hand the island to one outside distributor. Whether the 2025 own-operation plan is now dropped is **not stated** in this source.
- **Same stance as Georgia.** One day earlier Val told a Georgia operator that show-special machines are not financed — [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-15-refurbished-trade-in-machines-are-sold-apart-from-the-show-special-and-show-special-machines-are-not-financed.md]]. The two fit together (**inference**).

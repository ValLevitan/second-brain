# JVL does not reach out to Georgia locations; the target is inbound calls to its AI voice

Stated by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] on his [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-05-sergey-vysotsky-georgia-location-opt-in-list-and-research-spikes.md|Oct-5 call with Sergey Vysotsky]] (10:06 AM, Russian with some English). The transcript has no speaker labels; attribution follows the derived notes and the context [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Sergei_Vysotski_Georgia_Location_Opt_In_List_and_Research_Spikes-October-5-2026.md]].

## What Val said

- **The restriction.** "здесь очень важно понимать, что есть федеральные restrictions… поэтому мы как JVL это делать не можем и даже от нашего имени это делать нельзя" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sergei_Vysotski_Georgia_Location_Opt_In_List_and_Research_Spikes-October-5-2026.md]].
- **Not even after an opt-in.** "даже если компания opt-in, opt-in в то, что мы можем им позвонить, мы бы даже не хотели" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sergei_Vysotski_Georgia_Location_Opt_In_List_and_Research_Spikes-October-5-2026.md]].
- **The target model — inbound.** "я бы хотел построить так… чтобы звонки шли нам, наш AI голос принимал на себя звонов и qualify и закрывал клиента. То есть вот это уже финальный шаг" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sergei_Vysotski_Georgia_Location_Opt_In_List_and_Research_Spikes-October-5-2026.md]].
- **Physical contact versus automation.** A later passage (speaker not labelled) says JVL could physically call or come to the door and present its machines, but "Мы не можем автоматом… because that against the federal regulation" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sergei_Vysotski_Georgia_Location_Opt_In_List_and_Research_Spikes-October-5-2026.md]]. *This narrows the first point: the bar, as spoken, is on automated contact. The two lines are recorded as said, not reconciled.*

## The four parties, and what Val wants

Val laid out the parties around each location: the **location**; the **master operator**, who buys the machines, brings them and manages the locations; the **player**; and JVL as the fourth — "Мы manufacturer, который продает наши машины, мастер оператор. Не location." The location tells the operator what it wants, but often does not know [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sergei_Vysotski_Georgia_Location_Opt_In_List_and_Research_Spikes-October-5-2026.md]].

What he wants: "Мне нужно просто reliable opt-in list. Of locations. И мне нужно потом найти как - то каким - то образом, географическим или каким - то другим образом людей, которые most likely могут играть на этих локациях" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sergei_Vysotski_Georgia_Location_Opt_In_List_and_Research_Spikes-October-5-2026.md]].

The bottom-up path (players ask their store for JVL machines; the owners then ask their operator) was described on the call; the notes give it to Sergey [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Sergei_Vysotski_Georgia_Location_Opt_In_List_and_Research_Spikes-October-5-2026.md]]; in the transcript it opens "Как видит Andrei…" and ends "Путь идеальный" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Sergei_Vysotski_Georgia_Location_Opt_In_List_and_Research_Spikes-October-5-2026.md]].

## How it moves the Sept-22 plan

On Sept 22 Val's plan was that AI calls would reach location owners and get their consent for a contact list ([[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]], Sept 22). On Oct 5 the opt-in list is still the first goal, but JVL itself does not do the outreach, and the end state is inbound. *Whether this replaces the Sept-22 AI-call idea or only moves it away from JVL is not said — recorded as an evolution of the plan, with the open question on the project page.*

**Tier A hold (§15 #8 — legal material).** Val's description of how a third party might reach locations without naming JVL is **held for owner review** and not recorded, as the Sept-22 passage on lawful calling was held. **§15.8:** clear on this page. **§15 #9:** none.

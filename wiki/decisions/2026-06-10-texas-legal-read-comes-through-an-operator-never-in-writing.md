# JVL's Texas legal read comes through an operator, and never in writing

On the [[wiki/meetings/2026-06-10-stephen-fenoglio-texas-gaming-counsel.md|Jun 10 2026 introductory call]], [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] asked the Austin gaming lawyer [[wiki/people/stephen-fenoglio.md|Stephen Fenoglio]] to look at a JVL game and opine on its skill element — the thing [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]] had said he could do. Fenoglio said no in writing, yes in person, and Val took the route around it in the same breath. This is how JVL's Texas product gets vetted from here.

## What was settled

- **No binding written opinion for JVL, because of the Pace conflict.** "Because I represent Pace, I cannot give a binding legal opinion for any other company. I do look at games on behalf of operators and will make an observation, but **I can't put it in writing** because I have a conflict of interest in representing Pace. That's just kind of a nuance." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Stephen_Fenoglio_Texas_Skill_Gaming_Counsel-June-10-2026.md]]
- **The route JVL takes instead is through an operator.** Val: "So we can come to you through an operator." Fenoglio: "**I don't mind meeting with you and will give you the same opinion I would give the operator, but I am not going to put anything in writing.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Stephen_Fenoglio_Texas_Skill_Gaming_Counsel-June-10-2026.md]]
- **What JVL actually gets is a feature-level verbal read.** "And I'll give you a look. I think these features are good and I would definitely not have this feature." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Stephen_Fenoglio_Texas_Skill_Gaming_Counsel-June-10-2026.md]]
- **Val accepted it on the spot** — "I hear you understood. Let me keep in touch" — and did not press for a written opinion or raise finding conflict-free counsel. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Stephen_Fenoglio_Texas_Skill_Gaming_Counsel-June-10-2026.md]]

## Why it binds

The standard Fenoglio would be reading against is Texas's **pure-skill** test, not Nebraska's — "if the skillful player wins each and every time more than it costs to play, that's not a gambling device", and one percent of chance in the outcome is fatal. JVL's Texas product is an adaptation of its Nebraska build ([[wiki/projects/texas-used-machine-distribution.md]]), so the read is not a formality. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Stephen_Fenoglio_Texas_Skill_Gaming_Counsel-June-10-2026.md]]

## Confirmed in practice — Jun 23 2026

Thirteen days later, on the [[wiki/meetings/2026-06-23-texas-operators-reach-out.md|Jun-23 operator reach-out]], Val described the same rule to the two Texas operators and it was acted on rather than argued with: "I spoke to Stephen about it. **I asked him to look at my machine. He says he's got conflict of interest; he cannot look at another manufacturer. But if an operator presents my machine, he can definitely look at it.**" Jordan then offered to be that operator — he would text his Austin shipping address so JVL could send one machine — and said of Fenoglio "we've gone through fifty or more games together in the past fifteen years." The decision stays **active**. [[raw/Transcript_Texas_Operators_Reach_Out-June-23-2026.md]]

## The cost of it, named but not answered

The derived notes flag the consequence: if JVL needs a Texas opinion it can actually rely on, it needs separate counsel without the Pace conflict — and that was **not raised on the call**. Recorded as the notes-writer's observation, not as anything said by either participant. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Stephen_Fenoglio_Texas_Skill_Gaming_Counsel-June-10-2026.md]]

# Felipe does not quote trade-in prices; Val is in the middle of every trade-in deal (Nov 4 2026)

Set by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] on the [[wiki/meetings/2026-11-04-felipe-igt-trade-in-approach-and-arc-delivery.md|Nov-4 10:27 AM call]] with [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda]] (English) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Felipe_IGT_Trade_In_Approach_and_ARC_Delivery-November-4-2026.md]].

## What Felipe brought

- Felipe had spoken to an operator's "operations manager or tech manager, Ashley, about the trade-in. They have a bunch of the old IGT sitting in their warehouse and I said, hey, what are you doing with this? Let's trade them in for brand new flex." He wanted first "to learn how many machines they're willing to trade", and asked Val "what would be something that… would make them want to do it and would be good for us" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Felipe_IGT_Trade_In_Approach_and_ARC_Delivery-November-4-2026.md]]. The operator is not named on this recording; by the afternoon call it reads as [[wiki/organizations/inamax-ultra.md|Inamax / Ultra]] (Ashley, head of operations) — **inference**.

## The ruling

- **No number from Val, and none from Felipe.** Val: "I do not want to tell you this… it's an ingredient of many things, how many machines, how committed they are… How quickly do they need it? What else is involved? I need to be in the middle of this deal… I can decide on it on my own, by myself. You should assume that you don't know what it is, and… Always look me in" (heard; read as "always loop me in" — **inference**, the notes' reading) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Felipe_IGT_Trade_In_Approach_and_ARC_Delivery-November-4-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Felipe_IGT_Trade_In_Approach_and_ARC_Delivery-November-4-2026.md]].
- **Why — the role split.** Val: "I'm a professional prick and you're a professionally nice guy. And that's the difference between you and I… You cannot change your personality. I like you for who you are, and I use you for who you are. You need to remain you, and I need to remain me, and that's why you and I work together." Felipe: "seems that nice is not… Getting results, so I wanted to run it by you" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Felipe_IGT_Trade_In_Approach_and_ARC_Delivery-November-4-2026.md]].
- **What Felipe does at the meeting.** Felipe: "I will go anyway. I will have a conversation with him. How many games you have? I'm going to work with Val to give you the best deal we can possibly do." Val: "you should actually tell them, I will work my harness on your behalf. To lobby Val… To give you the best deal, you should also tell them that there is another large operator who is making this deal with Val… for all of his IGTs in the warehouse for 2027" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Felipe_IGT_Trade_In_Approach_and_ARC_Delivery-November-4-2026.md]]. The other operator is not named; by the Oct-13 handshake it is [[wiki/organizations/arc-gaming.md|ARC]] — **inference** — see [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-13-arc-trades-in-old-machines-for-texas-as-credit-on-2027-purchases.md]].
- **The Nebraska angle.** Felipe: "this opportunity is good for him, not only for… the brand new games here, but for Nebraska. Because remember, we have the opportunity to move in games to Nebraska. So even if he goes with refurbished, you know, we could work something out even better" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Felipe_IGT_Trade_In_Approach_and_ARC_Delivery-November-4-2026.md]]. See [[wiki/projects/nebraska-skill-market.md]] (Nov 4).
- **Outreach.** Felipe: "I've also shared a couple of text messages with those large companies that I know have a lot of games in their warehouse. I shared this with Christina Kaiser, who is very influential in Gamoa. She talks to everyone and to all the masters… Help me spread the word." Val: "this is indeed very good news. I'm very happy for you and I'm very happy for us" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Felipe_IGT_Trade_In_Approach_and_ARC_Delivery-November-4-2026.md]]. See [[wiki/organizations/gamoa.md]] (Nov 4).

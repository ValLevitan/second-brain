# Finish the Business Central rollout, then move off it to a simple, cheaper system

On the June 17 2025 GL chart-of-accounts call ([[wiki/meetings/2025-06-17-finance-gl-chart-of-accounts-structure.md]]), [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] restated a strategic direction for JVL's ERP: keep the current **Business Central** implementation deliberately simple, finish and run it, then replace it with a cheaper system. Continuation of [[wiki/topics/business-central-erp-implementation.md]]. [[raw/finance_gl_accounts_structure-june-17-2025.md]]

## What was decided

- **Business Central is overkill for JVL's business** — Val's framing: "It's shooting birds with a cannon… going hunting with a cannon. Absolutely unnecessary," especially as JVL closes down its Latin-American subsidiaries. The recurring Business Central / Microsoft-sponsorship expense is judged "absolutely unnecessary." [[raw/finance_gl_accounts_structure-june-17-2025.md]]
- **Do not proceed with the manufacturing component of BC** — a decision Val and [[wiki/people/lev-rudky.md|Lev]] had already made together (Val "always felt that way"). [[raw/finance_gl_accounts_structure-june-17-2025.md]]
- **Still implement, install and run BC anyway** — Val did not want to stop the rollout, because "it will offer us a learning curve for everyone." [[raw/finance_gl_accounts_structure-june-17-2025.md]]
- **Then move off it:** "right after, the next step, I'm going to ask Lev to find a simple software that we can run and move away from Business Central." So the whole GL/chart-of-accounts work is to keep simplifying with that exit in view. [[raw/finance_gl_accounts_structure-june-17-2025.md]]

## Rationale

- **AI will replace the software UI.** Val: "I do not see user interface or any user interface for software as a service valuable anymore. It will be replaced by AI, by human interface… I'm not saying next year, but in three years, there's no questions about it." So he does not want to invest heavily in a SaaS front-end that he expects to become obsolete. [[raw/finance_gl_accounts_structure-june-17-2025.md]]
- Lev's reaction (Val's phrase, "Lev is in shock now") was corrected by Lev: "I'm not in shock. I'm just on top of my mind to find a simple cost-effective solution. It's on the market." Val parked the choice of replacement as "a separate topic." [[raw/finance_gl_accounts_structure-june-17-2025.md]]

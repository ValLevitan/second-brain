## 4. Locking a BOM — one option, not final

- **Genadi's ask.** After a human check, a generated BOM is locked: "Чтобы никто его не мог менять, даже он сам." A variant for a customer (the same Flex "с перламутровыми пуговицами", i.e. with other buttons) becomes a second saved BOM; the person ordering sees two products and picks one, with no new generation each time [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_FlexPort_BOM_Revision_2_and_Vendor_Data-November-26-2026.md]]. He made the same point on Oct 13 ([[wiki/decisions/2026-10-13-the-agent-builds-the-bom-as-a-control-gate-taught-by-data-contracts-not-cad-and-genadi-finds-why-it-is-wrong.md]]).
- **Val.** "я не против", but "закрадывается… оптимизм… что есть э, лучшие варианты. Я просто еще не додумал их"; "как один из вариантов… лог, bill of material, он неплохой." In that option the checked BOM gets a dated revision number and is sent to Business Central — "Это делается через MCP, там нам нужен Nathan" — where the vendor side is joined to the part numbers [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_FlexPort_BOM_Revision_2_and_Vendor_Data-November-26-2026.md]]. **Tentative**, as the notes also mark it [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Genadi_FlexPort_BOM_Revision_2_and_Vendor_Data-November-26-2026.md]].

## 5. Vendor data — two sides that merge in MSBC

- **Who fills what.** Genadi: vendor data belongs to parts and contracts, at item level, not to the BOM. Val: engineering sends the part and the vendor name to Business Central; the vendor card — bank details, address, phone, contacts, deal terms — is filled "с административной стороны. не с инженерной"; "Это наполняет финансовая и procurement сторона" and lives its own life there. "И вот эти вещи должны merge" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_FlexPort_BOM_Revision_2_and_Vendor_Data-November-26-2026.md]]. In line with [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-13-business-central-is-the-only-source-of-truth-for-vendor-bank-details-and-wires-take-them-from-it-every-time.md]].
- **With Nathan more than Lev.** The merge is to be talked through "Меньше с Левой, чем с Нейтаном." Val: [[wiki/people/lev-rudky.md|Lev]] is careful and "хочет руками это закладывать. Руками это будет очень долго, мы только через три года сделаем что-то"; "Мне лучше допустить ошибки, чем а, э, делать это три года" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_FlexPort_BOM_Revision_2_and_Vendor_Data-November-26-2026.md]].
- **Part numbers.** Claude already assigns part numbers to items itself; Val: "Я вернусь к Part Numbers" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_FlexPort_BOM_Revision_2_and_Vendor_Data-November-26-2026.md]].
- **Vendor history from email.** The first quotes and the talks with a vendor sit in Genadi's mailbox ("Она в имейлах у вас где-то"). Val did not want to merge Genadi's own mailbox into procurement. His plan: Genadi points Claude at his mailbox to find every vendor he talked to about the parts now under `components` and move those emails to a separate folder ("Назовем его procurement export"); that folder then goes into the procurement mailbox, is groomed there, and Claude pulls out the vendor attributes and, "на каждого вендора", a "negotiation history". Genadi: "да, это, это легко… Я могу это сделать" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_FlexPort_BOM_Revision_2_and_Vendor_Data-November-26-2026.md]].
- **Why the history matters.** Genadi wants every quotation "построчно", so the agent can see "цена поднялась на 4%, на 2, на 30%" with nothing having changed — "ее никто, все теряют ее" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_FlexPort_BOM_Revision_2_and_Vendor_Data-November-26-2026.md]]. Today every new part goes through procurement; the parts in GitHub are already being bought; "в BC огромный месс", so grooming is needed (Room label; from context, Genadi) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_FlexPort_BOM_Revision_2_and_Vendor_Data-November-26-2026.md]].
- **Vendor attributes first.** Genadi wants the attribute list agreed before the email work, once Lev has finished: "надо сделать список атрибутов". The plan (Room label; the notes give it to Val): have Claude go into MSBC and mark the fields the vendor profile already offers, so the team can say which are needed [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_FlexPort_BOM_Revision_2_and_Vendor_Data-November-26-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Genadi_FlexPort_BOM_Revision_2_and_Vendor_Data-November-26-2026.md]]. Genadi has tried MSBC's item attributes: "он легко настраивается… я сделал там, допустим, атрибут ревизия" — but that is the item tab, not vendors. Val: part attributes too — "Нам это очень нужно, очень"; if MSBC has no vendor attributes, JVL adapts to how MSBC keeps vendor details [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Genadi_FlexPort_BOM_Revision_2_and_Vendor_Data-November-26-2026.md]].

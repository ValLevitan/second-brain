## Oct 13 2026 — what he said at Intralot

He was in the room for the first part only; after he left, Val said "**So the bad guy is gone. Now we can talk.**" Quotes from [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Intralot_Machine_Lockouts_Test_Lab_and_Route_Partnership-October-13-2026.md]]. Full meeting: [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-13-intralot-machine-lockouts-test-lab-and-route-partnership.md]].

- **On a site controller for JVL:** "**we're not in the habit of issuing site controllers to any manufacturer when there's issues. And that's because it is proprietary.**" But "**We can discuss it and come to an agreement where we can loan you a site controller. You know, maybe that's a possibility.**"
- **His advice on the ask:** put both options in one letter — "**having the two options on the table… certainly is better**" — and "**we are probably having more of an appetite to do it in house**" — [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-13-val-asks-the-lottery-in-one-letter-for-a-site-controller-or-testing-at-intralot.md]].
- **On the lab test now:** "**Yeah, I'm comfortable moving ahead with that now… It's a pressing problem**" — [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-13-ten-jvl-machines-go-into-intralots-lab-now-without-waiting-for-the-lottery.md]].
- **On the old prototype:** "**you guys have been kind enough to loan us a prototype for the lab here, but it's the old tabletop**… **if we can get that upgraded, you know, that will that will certainly help.**"
- **On the call center:** if the fix is just a reset, "**maybe we can educate the call center**" instead of telling technicians it is the wrong software.
- **On meters:** calling in the meters before a hard reset makes the million-dollar correction easier to process.
- **On promo credits:** "**Promo meters are not part of the current SaaS**" (= SAS) requirements; it "**has to fit within the confines of what version 1. 8 is right now**". Turning it on raises the question of inducements, and whether that is allowed "**if it's coming from… manufacturer to a player**". Asked if that is a conversation with Joe: "**I would say, yeah. Our legal representatives will probably give you some more clarity on that**" — see [[wiki/people/joe-kim.md]].
- **On the gift-card rules:** "**they're probably going to fine tune HB 353 in the spring**"; "**right now digital. Gift cards are okay**", but rules may stop them becoming "**a conduit for the cash**". Enforcement: "**we have to give them a warning by law initially**", then a violation. No reporting rule applies to JVL's transactional system: "**that's up to you**".
- **On JVL dropping the recycler:** "**I think that's a sigh of relief for many of the folks out there.**"
- **Who to watch in the legislature:** "**the regulated industries**", and "**Alan Powell is a very influential representative**", "**heavily involved with GAX**" (read as the GACS show) — see [[wiki/topics/georgia-class-a-machines.md]].

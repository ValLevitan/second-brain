# Mark Sinosich

Mark Sinosich is an **Australian gaming-industry contact of Val's, based in Sydney** — "been now 25 years in the casino industry" and a lifelong gamer (an office full of board games). In mid-2024 Val engaged him to explore whether a **sweepstakes casino** opportunity is real (see [[wiki/meetings/2024-07-02-mark-sinosich-sweepstakes-casinos.md]] and [[wiki/topics/online-games-rgs-distribution.md]]). [[raw/sweepstakes_casinos_with_mark_sinosich.md]]

## Background (as of Jul 2024 — History-era, pre-window source)

- Worked in **social slots**: he was with "the Fox Cup guys" (as transcribed) on a product called **Slots Vacation**, which at one stage ranked in the top 50 and made "a million a month" — mostly revenue that got pumped back into advertising/user acquisition, so "we as a company did not make much." [[raw/sweepstakes_casinos_with_mark_sinosich.md]]
- Described their tournament mechanic: during timed tournaments a game's feature paid **tournament points instead of money** (effective RTP cut ~30% below normal), the withheld amount funding the top-of-ladder prizes — and purchases roughly **doubled** during tournament hours. [[raw/sweepstakes_casinos_with_mark_sinosich.md]]
- His framing of free players: they "want to feel a sense of achievement … their win is not necessarily money related. It's leaderboard related" — cosmetics, badges, status, the "game around the game." [[raw/sweepstakes_casinos_with_mark_sinosich.md]]

## Position on the sweepstakes idea (Jul 2024)

He believes sweepstakes casinos are under-served ("there's not many ways to play for free and win real things … there's still an opportunity there with the right idea") but agrees the sticking point is the legal mechanism — how operators like **VGW** ("Chump a Casino," as transcribed) hand out redeemable "gold coins" without it being gambling; he committed to re-research that mechanism, while Val pressed the harder question of whether VGW is actually *profitable* (free cash), not just growing. [[raw/sweepstakes_casinos_with_mark_sinosich.md]]

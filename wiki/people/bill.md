# Bill (ARC's kiosk vendor)

The single largest obstacle between JVL and [[wiki/organizations/arc-gaming.md|ARC Gaming]]'s location estate, and — since September 9 2026 — also the reason [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] twice offered to walk away from the account. **No surname is given in any source.** He is described only by two people: ARC's COO [[wiki/people/georgios-zazanis.md|Georgios Zazanis]] on August 31, and ARC's CEO [[wiki/people/david-sloan.md|David Sloan]] on September 9. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

## What he is to ARC

- **He runs the kiosk company that supplies ARC.** Sloan, asked whether Bill is still involved: "**He's running a kiosk company.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]
- **They have been partners.** "**Bill and I have been partners.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]
- **And friends for years** — Val on Aug 31: "The Sloan… **Is friends with Bill. For years.**" Zazanis: "Maybe. Bill friends." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **The relationship, not the technology, is what blocks any JVL kiosk proposal at ARC.** Zazanis: "**Has to come from David. David has to tell Bill.**" Full ruling: [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-31-arc-kiosk-board-swap-routed-through-the-ceo.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

## How long Val has known him — two accounts that do not agree

- **Val, Aug 31:** "**I know him for thirty years**… He was a **president and CEO of a company called G. P. With an office in Long Island.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **Sept 9, the transcript's labelling:** "**I know Bill since the '90s**" sits under the **same speaker label as Val's own questions**, and Sloan's next line is "Bill and I have been partners." The derived notes attribute the '90s line to **Sloan** instead. Thirty years back from 2026 is the mid-1990s, which fits Val's Aug-31 statement exactly — so the transcript's labelling is the more consistent reading, but **the discrepancy is recorded, not resolved.** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]
- Sloan adds "**I know Bill from GPT**", which is flagged **unresolved** in the derived notes. It sits close to Val's "a company called G. P." — but the two are **not** asserted to be the same thing here. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

## What his kiosk actually does (Sept 9 2026)

- **Gift cards, plus one more product being added.** "**His kiosk here is to put the gift cards. He's putting a CVU in as well.**" Val asked what that is and got no answer on the tape. *("CVU" and Val's follow-up "What's ten suspension?" are both flagged unresolved in the derived notes and are **not** interpreted here.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]
- **Val rates the product highly, and said so to its customer.** "**Bill's kiosk is fantastic. I really, really think he's done a he's done a great job.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]
- **And Sloan rates the service.** "The good thing is Bill can do for me. **I can think of the phone to get anything changed on the kiosk.**" *(Transcribed as written; the derived notes read it as: he can call and get anything changed on the kiosk.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]
- **ARC has already paid for the kiosks**, which is the hinge of Val's argument — see below. Contrast the Aug-31 account of a **20-year** kiosk agreement a previous ARC CEO signed with his own company, vendor named as **EXIO**; neither source connects "Bill" to that name and **nothing is merged here**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

## Sept 9 2026 — Val used him as an honesty test, twice, and made him the condition of proceeding

At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-09-arc-gaming-backend-displacement-negotiation.md|Sept-9 4:35 PM meeting]] Val put the question to Sloan directly rather than working around it. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

**First, the soft version:** "I know that you are long-term friends with Bill, and I know that Bill supplied you with 170… **Is there anything else that you're bound by to build, or would I be would I be stepping on his feet?**" The answer — "**A little bit, yeah**" — falls **inside Val's own speaker block**, one of several places the Plaud labels mis-split turns; the derived notes read it as Sloan's. *(The "170" is transcribed bare with no unit; ARC's location count was given as "more than 200" on Aug 31 and Val had guessed "170 locations" then. **Not interpreted here.**)*

**Then the sharp version:** "**Aside from these kiosks, I'm looking at taking the business from [InComm]**… **Is Bill doing something of that nature?**" Sloan: "**That he's looking at different things because I've asked**," and later "**I think he is working on a solution.**"

**Val's own test, stated twice:**

> "if all that Bill has done is the kiosks, and he has paid—I mean, **you guys already paid for it**—and there's nothing else, **then I think you and I can talk about business. A lot of business.**"

> "Again, **if Bill is on it and you're friends with Bill, then I shouldn't be here.** That's the way it's done. **I'm not convinced of it.**"

Sloan's answer to both was the same non-answer: "**I'm not against what you're saying.**" The question was never closed — it is carried as an open commitment on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-arc-account-exclusivity-sought-and-not-obtained.md]].

**§15.8:** clear — vendor relationship and product description only; no compensation of any kind recorded for this person.

## Open questions

- **Is Bill building a back-end solution of the same nature as JVL's intent?** The answer decides whether Val proceeds at all. Sloan said he had asked and got "different things" and "working on a solution" — neither a yes nor a no. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]
- **Is Bill's company the "EXIO" of the Aug-31 twenty-year agreement?** Both accounts describe ARC's kiosk supplier, from two different ARC executives, eight days apart, with no name in common. **Not merged.** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]
- **Will Sloan ever carry a JVL proposal to him?** The friendship is what makes him the only route and the reason the ask may not be made — the question first recorded on [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-31-arc-kiosk-board-swap-routed-through-the-ceo.md]] and **still untouched**: the board-swap proposal was not raised at the Sept-9 meeting.

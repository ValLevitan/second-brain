# Anton (SMM / Meta-ads marketer)

Anton is a **social-media-marketing (SMM) contractor** based in **Minsk** whom [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] engaged to buy ads in the **Meta ecosystem (Facebook + Instagram)** and drive traffic to the [[wiki/topics/echo-home-gaming-device.md|ECHO]] Amazon page. He was found via networking — he already runs Instagram ads for the European high-end-medical-device (tonometer) company where Andrei's sister works. Suggested first-month **ad budget ~$500** to run ~10 ad variations. Geo-targeting to start in wealthy Florida areas. [[raw/recap_with_andrei_klimovichonlineecho212025_2025-02-01.md]]

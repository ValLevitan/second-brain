# Anton (SMM / Meta-ads marketer)

Anton is a **social-media-marketing (SMM) contractor** based in **Minsk** whom [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] engaged to buy ads in the **Meta ecosystem (Facebook + Instagram)** and drive traffic to the [[wiki/topics/echo-home-gaming-device.md|ECHO]] Amazon page. He was found via networking — he already runs Instagram ads for the European high-end-medical-device (tonometer) company where Andrei's sister works. [[raw/recap_with_andrei_klimovichonlineecho212025_2025-02-01.md]]

> **Different person from [[wiki/people/anton-leshkevich.md|Anton Leshkevich]]** (the JVL Minsk engineer, Omni/RGS/CD). Same first name only; this Anton is an outside marketing contractor. Recorded in Owner Decisions. [[raw/recap_with_andrei_klimovichonlineecho212025_2025-02-01.md]]

## What he does / terms

- Builds and runs the ad campaigns (targeting, creatives management, killing losing ads, analysing results); JVL supplies the visuals to his brief. Experienced in Meta; can refer Google/YouTube specialists; advises **against LinkedIn** (~10× Meta's cost, wrong for a consumer product). [[raw/recap_with_andrei_klimovichonlineecho212025_2025-02-01.md]]
- **Rate schedule:** ramped monthly engagement, ~30 h/month; personal rate figures **held per §15.8** (in the cited raw). Suggested first-month **ad budget ~$500** to run ~10 ad variations (ad spend, not comp). Geo-targeting to start in wealthy Florida areas. [[raw/recap_with_andrei_klimovichonlineecho212025_2025-02-01.md]]

## Confirmation (Feb 21)

- The Feb 21 recap confirms Anton is engaged as the **SMM expert from Minsk** running Meta ads, with **experience running campaigns for medical devices in Europe**, a **$500 initial test budget**, LinkedIn avoided for cost, and the same ramped rate schedule (figures held per §15.8). Meta campaigns to focus on **Florida**. [[raw/recap_meeting_andrei-February-21-2025.md]]

## Sept 22 2026 — his role restated: Meta only

On the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-22-digital-marketing-lead-and-georgia-demand-campaign.md|Sept-22 call]], [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Klimovich]] told Val who runs JVL's advertising. Anton is the Meta person: "Антон, который только метой занимается… он настраивает просто метарекламы". He keeps the ads showing, watches the balance, and bids on the ad exchange to win placements to a set strategy: "он играет со ставками на бирже, чтобы выигрывать рекламу по заданной стратегии". He does **not** create the ad creatives [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Marketing_Lead_and_GA_Demand_Campaign-September-22-2026.md]]. *(Resolved to this page by context — Meta ads. Klimovich's personal assessment of him is not recorded, §15.8.)*

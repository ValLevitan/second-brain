# Humberto Sarkis

Humberto Sarkis is Val's long-standing **Mexican corporate/liquidation lawyer**, engaged to close out [[wiki/organizations/jvl.md|JVL]]'s Mexico entity in an orderly, compliant way (name from the Apr-28 2025 recording, in which Val is in Miami/transit). Val: "From the beginning of my relationship, I put my faith into your hands." [[raw/jvl_mexico_wind-down-humberto_sarkis-apr-28-2025.md]]

## The wind-down conversation (Apr 28 2025)

- Sarkis framed two paths: **"fire and forget"** (do the basics, let it die) or **finish everything before liquidating**. Val chose the orderly path — treat employees fairly (full compensation + accumulated vacation), pay all supplier debt to zero, forgo minor receivables, no assets/equipment left in Mexico. Val compared it to how JVL "wound our Peruvian operation" the same way. [[raw/jvl_mexico_wind-down-humberto_sarkis-apr-28-2025.md]]
- Sarkis **recommends a full liquidation + dissolution of the entity** even though it has no assets, "so there's nothing left" to pursue. [[raw/jvl_mexico_wind-down-humberto_sarkis-apr-28-2025.md]]
- **The blocker is the tax situation** — "the stone in the shoe of JVL." An agreement with the Mexican tax authority is not yet formalized; Val believes he already accepted an offer (he referenced not wanting to spend "$60,000 or whatever we agreed to"). The tax matter is for **2021 or 2022**. Liquidating the tax liability is essential for legal dissolution and to **liberate Monica and Val from any remote repercussions** if the debt is unpaid. [[raw/jvl_mexico_wind-down-humberto_sarkis-apr-28-2025.md]]
- Val asked whether there is a **legal way to collapse the company and forego the penalty** (avoid the settlement) if the government is satisfied simply that JVL is out of business. Sarkis explained the mechanics: liquidating the entity goes through a **notary public**, must be **published in the newspapers three times in intervals**, and needs a **constancia (testament) from the tax authority that no taxes are owed** — and his concern is that settling only 2021/2022 may not clear the later years/audits. [[raw/jvl_mexico_wind-down-humberto_sarkis-apr-28-2025.md]]
- **Alternative he offered:** settle the taxes, then place the entity under **"suspension of activities" without liquidation**. He took it as homework to check with the notary whether dissolution is possible now, and to find "the easiest, cheapest way to finalize" (liquidate vs keep alive, whichever is cheaper). [[raw/jvl_mexico_wind-down-humberto_sarkis-apr-28-2025.md]]
- Payment: there are **about three outstanding invoices** to Sarkis; Val will **pay him directly from the US company** (not the Mexico office) and asked Sarkis to keep them in sight for the liquidation. Sarkis offered a **US account** for the transfer. [[raw/jvl_mexico_wind-down-humberto_sarkis-apr-28-2025.md]]
- Context Val gave: he continues to retain [[wiki/people/alejandro.md|Alejandro de la Rosa]] (accountant) at minimal cost until closure; he is isolating "strictly employee and office-related expenses versus my personal agreements with my personal consultants" and paying both Sarkis and de la Rosa directly from the US company. A "Fernando" had been following the tax matter more closely before. [[raw/jvl_mexico_wind-down-humberto_sarkis-apr-28-2025.md]]

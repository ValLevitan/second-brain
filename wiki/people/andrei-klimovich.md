# Andrei Klimovich

Andrei Klimovich runs JVL's **online-games / RGS commercial operations** — the distribution of JVL's online casino games through aggregators, market licensing, certification economics, and related product/marketing ventures Val oversees. He gives Val a weekly business recap; Val's standing instruction to him is to make proposals and drive the business, with Val approving spend where he sees a return. [[raw/Recap with Andrei Klimovich - January 30, 2025.md]]

**Distinct from [[wiki/people/andrey-bosik.md|Andrei (Andrey)]]**, the Minsk build-infrastructure/DevSecOps engineer. That Andrei is coached by Aaron on GitHub/Kubernetes/Jira; this Andrei Klimovich manages the commercial online-games business. They are treated as two different people (see Owner Decisions). [[raw/Recap with Andrei Klimovich - January 30, 2025.md]]

## What he manages (Jan 30 recap)

- **Online casino games via RGS / aggregators** — JVL's games are distributed through EveryMatrix (the "RGS Matrix" / "EveryMatrix" aggregator) to operators. See [[wiki/topics/online-games-rgs-distribution.md]]. [[raw/Recap with Andrei Klimovich - January 30, 2025.md]]
- **Certification economics and revenue-share strategy** — comparing labs (GLI vs RiskCherry) and pushing to re-negotiate revenue share (from ~5% up toward 8–10%) so certification costs pay back. [[raw/Recap with Andrei Klimovich - January 30, 2025.md]]
- **Online-sales hiring** — sourcing an account manager (a candidate from the Kindred holding, currently in the live-casino domain, found via LinkedIn). [[raw/Recap with Andrei Klimovich - January 30, 2025.md]]
- **An Amazon consumer device** — a gaming device marketed for man-caves / game rooms. A paid-ad test was starting ($100/day for 4 days) to measure click and purchase conversion against Helium-10 historical data (target ~$68 of ad spend per sale). Wholesalers/studios that furnish game rooms said the device is too expensive at its current price and declined to stock it; a discount-code/commission model was floated. (Scope unclear — see Owner Decisions.) [[raw/Recap with Andrei Klimovich - January 30, 2025.md]]
- **A data-collection project ("Norcross"/"North Cross")** — an MVP to find and collect data sources, possibly using Anthropic. A candidate developer, **Sergei**, would try a first free data-collection pass at ~8 hours/week; Val asked to see Sergei's CV first and flagged not wanting to pay for anyone's Anthropic learning curve. (See Owner Decisions — Sergei's situation includes a sensitive personal matter.) [[raw/Recap with Andrei Klimovich - January 30, 2025.md]]

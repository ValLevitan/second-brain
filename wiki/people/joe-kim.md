# Joe Kim (Georgia Lottery general counsel, COAM)

**Joe Kim is the general counsel for the Georgia Lottery who regulates COAM.** Identified by name and role by **Adam Harlow** of [[wiki/organizations/incom.md|InComm]] on the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-26-incomm-skill-pay-atm-guidance.md|Aug 26 2026 InComm follow-up]], in answer to Val asking outright "So, sorry, who is Joe Kim?" — "Joe Kim is the general counsel for the Georgia Lottery that regulates COM." He is external to JVL and, on the evidence so far, JVL has no direct relationship with him — he reaches JVL through InComm and through [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]]'s account of the trade show. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]

*(Adam renders the name once as "Joe Kemp" in the same transcript; "Joe Kim" is used consistently elsewhere, including by Val. Kept under "Joe Kim" with "Joe Kemp" as an alias.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]

## Why he matters to JVL

- **His reaction at the August 2026 Georgia expo is the direct cause of InComm's cash-out posture**, which in turn constrains JVL's Georgia product roadmap. Per Adam Harlow, what worried him on the show floor was demos of a card going into a machine that was *also* a redemption machine and the machine "waving its wand and spitting out cash", plus products that were **ATM-use-only** — "basically a mechanism to turn your redemption directly into cash." Adam read him as "perturbed or fatigued at people trying to sort of skirt around his prior guidance", and said he was "going back to kind of sharpen that guidance and hone it to be a bit better." Full consequence on [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-26-no-cash-from-coam-redemption-device-for-skill-pay.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]
- **Adam believed InComm's own product was not in his sights** last week, "after some conversations that we had with him." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]
- **The same person appears from JVL's side of the show, and there he is described as excited rather than alarmed.** On the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-26-alignment-planning-nova-september-releases.md|Aug-26 morning alignment call]] Val reported that "Главный юрист [лотереи] Джорджия" — the Georgia lottery's chief legal counsel — "заявил, что они взволнованы этим решением" (declared they are excited/agitated about this solution) after seeing JVL's transaction recycler, and Val was pleased by it: "мне нравится, когда, выступая главный юрист и он в шоке… Я доволен результатом." Pavel's second-hand version, from [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei]], was that the demo "фурор сделал" (caused a furore), that JVL has no ready answer and cannot sell the kiosks yet, but promised a legal version soon. *(That the Aug-26 morning "chief legal counsel" is Joe Kim is an **inference** from role and event — the name is not spoken on that call.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alignment_Planning_NOVA_and_Sept_Releases-August-26-2026.md]]
- Val's own framing of the regulator's underlying objection, from the same morning call: the state promotes lotteries as gambling and does not want direct cash payment — "они как бы не хотят, чтоб это было… Если мы напрямую выплачиваем деньги." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alignment_Planning_NOVA_and_Sept_Releases-August-26-2026.md]]

## Aug 31 2026 — an operator's read: he has spoken, and he was not clear

At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-31-dixie-amusement-kiosk-wait-and-see.md|Aug-31 Dixie Amusement visit]], [[wiki/people/salim-khambawala.md|Salim Khambawala]] — vice president of the state operators' association — gave the first operator-side assessment of him in the corpus. It is the reason Dixie is not buying redemption equipment (see [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-31-dixie-defers-kiosk-investment-until-the-rules-settle.md]]). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]

- **He has addressed the cash-out question publicly and settled nothing.** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] suggested inviting him to see JVL's machine ("JVL is showing money coming out. So [Joe], come and see your system, the lottery guy"). Salim: "**But he spoke about it. He wasn't clear. I think he's going to come out**" — followed by his view of how a regulator's single line gets turned into five readings: "**You give them one line, and you talk to five attorneys… and tell them how you want that line to be read, and he'll read it that way.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]
- **Salim's structural point: he cannot keep kiosks outside his remit.** "**That's where Joe Kim comes into factor. He first cannot say he is not the regulator on [kiosks] because it's coming**… **Them saying no, kiosk is not part of the [COAM], but it will become a part.**" He illustrated it with an operator's reductio — a weighing scale placed under a COAM machine, which does "something else besides a COAM machine which is being played" and so by the Lottery's own logic would fall outside the rules. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]
- **And the limit of his authority, which is the operators' real fear.** "**the lottery is just the regulator. They are not the law maker or the enforcer. The enforcer is going to be GBI, the local police department, the local sheriff department.** If they get their hands into it and say… the constitution which they are there to protect and enforce, we see it's [breached]… **It's an uphill battle to fight the state and the attorney general.**" On Salim's reading, a favourable Joe Kim ruling would therefore not settle the question. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]
- **Why regulators keep it vague at all, in his account:** "**they know that's where they make funds. So when they [find] someone, they get fined. And they don't want to pick sides of anyone. So it's always murkier and murkier.**" Recorded as one operator's characterisation, not as established fact about the GLC. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]

## Sept 2 2026 — what InComm believes he said in his speech at the show

A week after the Aug-26 account above, InComm gave Val a fuller version at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-02-incomm-machine-payments-and-recycler-standoff.md|Sept-2 lunch]]. It is still **InComm's reading of him**, not his words; the corpus still holds nothing he wrote or said directly [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].

- **What he noticed on the floor, itemised:** "what you saw at the show was **Joe Kim noticed your product. He noticed Billy Liu's product. He noticed other kiosk vendors that represented ATM functionality access** — they have ATM marketed on their kiosk. I don't think it's live, but their payline kiosk. And I think what Kim realized was: **I need to address this point.**"
- **The speech, and the two things InComm believes it said.** "did you see the speech that he gave that afternoon?… he says two things. **He was very confusing**, but… **we believe he said you cannot exchange the gift card product for cash** — so a hundred-dollar gift card for a hundred dollars cash with the location, some kind of handshake exchange or facilitated exchange, is not acceptable. **So an ATM transaction has been deemed to be different** — there's fees, there's a purchase transaction. And **the other thing we believe he said was he doesn't want to see workflows that restrict the redemption to a cash destination.**"
- **What that means in practice, per InComm:** "what he was sensitive to that he saw on the floor was **solutions that do a hop, skip, and a jump to ultimately give the player cash. He didn't like that**… generically, you just want to **keep those cash transactions away from the COAM Redemption space**." The speaker was careful about the limits of his own reading: "**I don't have all the wording worked out there, because we're not regulating this. The lottery's there.**"
- **His view of ATM access on the card itself is treated as settled and favourable:** the card's ATM function "**is considered lawful by Joe Kim, the lottery council**. Part of that is because **it's after the point of redemption and it's separate from COAM** interactive."
- **He is also the named unlock.** Asked what would change InComm's refusal of JVL's recycler: "**If the lottery were to say something to clarify that point and say no, there's no problem here**… I would say now **we should see what Joe [Kim] respond[s] in writing**, because that will likely trigger [it]." Consequence on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-hold-the-recycler-back-but-sign-no-undertaking.md]].
- **A dating oddity, recorded not resolved.** Explaining that InComm could not have foreseen the shift, the speaker said even John Houseal could not have predicted "**what [Joe Kim] would say in November**." The month does not fit the August 2026 show the rest of the passage is about, and no source explains it. Left as transcribed; **no date is asserted** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].

## Sept 10 2026 — his letter lands, and his own colleagues explain it to Val

At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-10-georgia-lottery-coam-manufacturer-introduction.md|Sept-10 meeting at the Georgia Lottery]] Val finally heard the Lottery's own reading, from the COAM staff rather than from InComm — but still **not from Kim himself**. The corpus still holds nothing he wrote or said directly. *(The recording labels speakers by number, so no line below is attributed to an individual.)* [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]]

- **There is a letter, it is recent, and Val read it over someone's shoulder.** "**I read that letter… The one from last week. Yeah, I was never more confused than reading this letter**" — and "**I read it on somebody's phone. It wasn't… addressed to me.**" The staff reaction to his confusion was "**Welcome to our world**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]].
- **The rule the letter is read as stating** is on [[wiki/organizations/georgia-lottery-corporation.md]] and [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]]: the gift card cannot be exchanged for currency **at the location where the prize is won**; what the holder does with it elsewhere is treated as outside the statute.
- **Val has asked to meet him three times and got a form reply.** "**I asked for a meeting with Joe. We sent an official letter to… My administrative assistant found a general inquiry. Sent… an email. Silence. We sent another message. Silence. Then we finally… third message and the reply was, 'We got your message. We directed it to an appropriate department'**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]].
- **The Lottery staff offered to fix that.** "**We can call. We'll be glad to give you a phone number and… email address**", and at the close "**Let me get you some contacts, and I'll email them to you, and we'll go from there.**" Val, on the person he has still never met: "**I never met him. I don't know how to get a hold of him. I would like to have a one-on-one with him**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]].
- **His recent comments are shaping InComm's product line.** On the two InComm cards: "**they've got the… black prize play card and then the white one that's got the ATM functionality. Based on… some of Joe's comments… here recently… They may continue with one product and kind of… [drop] the other product, but we'll… have to see**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]].
- **And someone is already working around the letter.** A staff member: "**I heard that InComm is still working on a product to… get around even Joe's recent letter**" — Val: "**So I'm not sure what it is, but I… am being approached by other people than InComm**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]]. *(Both halves of that exchange are spoken across adjacent numbered speakers; who said which is not recoverable from the recording.)*
- **His office's own account of the shift is that it is recent and awkward.** "**if we would have had this meeting a couple weeks ago, the answer… probably would have been a little different**", and "**our council's taking a different stance. So… that's just a recent update**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]].

## Sept 15 2026 — a competing processor's account: the letter's addressee, and whether Kim ever saw his product

At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-15-billy-liu-playpay-nda-and-georgia-float-economics.md|Sept-15 evening session]] [[wiki/people/billy-liu.md|Billy Liu]] gave [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] the **first non-InComm, non-Lottery account of the letter** in the corpus — from someone who competes with InComm and talks to Lottery staff directly. It is still not Kim's own words; the corpus still holds nothing he wrote or said first-hand. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]]

### What the letter is, per Billy

- **It is addressed to InComm — and he names the addressee.** "**It was addressed to John House[al], which is [InComm].**" *(The recording renders the surname "Household" and "Housell"; both fall under the existing aliases of [[wiki/people/john-houseal.md|John Houseal]], so this is alias resolution rather than a fresh guess.)* **This is the first source to name who the letter was written to**; on Sept 10 Val had only read it "on somebody's phone. It wasn't… addressed to me." See [[wiki/people/john-houseal.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]].
- **It reverses the previous letter.** "**The previous letter was to say that once… the redemption is finished, the lottery's responsibility is also finished**… **they're not going to enforce what happens to that card or how the player intends to use it… Therefore, ATM access is allowed. Push it to the bank account is allowed. The PayPal to them or whatever else.**" The new one: "**He no longer believes. He's kind of redacting what he had said in the previous letter. I don't understand how a legal counsel can change his mind.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]]
- **What it substitutes instead: the statute plus other enforcers.** "**House Bill Three Five Three stipulates that… the value of the gift card cannot be turned into currency… He mentions that in the letter**"; "**even though the lottery doesn't want to regulate it… law enforcement people might. He's alluding to the fact that we didn't say it was fine, we being the lottery. We just gave an opinion about enforcement, and since we're not going to enforce it, maybe **GBI** would**"; "**And then he mentions FinCEN, which you know, money laundering.**" Billy's reading of the motive: "**he's trying to scare us into believing that the law stands. Whatever the lottery believes doesn't matter.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]]
- **It does not actually ban anything, and Billy says so twice — after first saying it does.** Opening: "**the skill pay card is no longer an option. He cannot issue that anymore after this letter.**" Ten minutes later: "**In the letter, it doesn't say that you can't [issue]… it doesn't say that you no longer are, you cannot issue this skill pay card anymore. It doesn't say anything like that. But it does say that here's the law.**" Val put the reading plainly — "**basically saying John, you cannot issue**" — and Billy agreed it is only implied: "**That blue card. They're basically saying that. They're hinting. That's exactly how I read it.**" **Both of Billy's statements stand as spoken; the source does not reconcile them.** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]]
- **And it is as incomprehensible to him as it was to Val.** "**It's a very confusing letter. You're going to read it. I read it several times, and it's hard to understand.**" His standing name for it is "**a stupid letter**", and he says Lottery staff laugh when he calls it that: "**I ask them, according to this letter, I call it a stupid letter, and they laugh and say, 'Yeah.'**" The derived notes summarise the same posture: he "treats the Georgia Lottery's letter on this dismissively… and has offered to turn the feature off" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]]
- **His characterisation of the regulator as a whole:** "**the lottery is a bipolar regula[tor]. They change their minds**"; and on being told "not right now, we'll let you know" about switching off his card's bank/PayPal off-ramp, "**they're just always two-faced. They want you guessing all the time.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]]

**Is this the same letter Val read on Sept 10?** Billy's own dating wavers inside one sentence — "**Just this week, a few days ago. Not a few days ago. There is a new one**" — and the source never settles whether he means the letter already on this page or a further one. **Not resolved; recorded as an open question below.** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]]

## Sept 16 2026 — Val's own telling, to an outside operator: "he shot in his pants"

It is his **bluntest description of Kim's reaction in the corpus**, and the first told to someone outside the JVL/InComm/Lottery circle [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Jamie_Georgia_Class_A_Opportunity_and_Market_Outlook-September-16-2026.md]].

- "**I reached out to Joe Kim. I showed all the cards that Georgia introduced. InComm… They introduced a conversion to an ATM card. I manufactured a recycler that would take these cards and dispense cash. When Joe Kim saw it, he shot in his pants. He just went ballistic.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Jamie_Georgia_Class_A_Opportunity_and_Market_Outlook-September-16-2026.md]]
- **Asked why — "Is it because he didn't understand it, or because he thought it was legal?" — Val did not answer the question** and went to the consequence instead: "**the whole Georgia Lottery now is backpedaling on the card, on the ATM card, and the whole industry is upside down.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Jamie_Georgia_Class_A_Opportunity_and_Market_Outlook-September-16-2026.md]]
- **And he says plainly that he thinks Kim is lost:** "**That's how I interpret it. That's how InComm interprets it. Everybody's confused. I think Joe Kim is confused.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Jamie_Georgia_Class_A_Opportunity_and_Market_Outlook-September-16-2026.md]]

The consequence side of this — both InComm **and** the Georgia Lottery asking Val after the fact not to put the recycler in the market — is on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-hold-the-recycler-back-but-sign-no-undertaking.md]].

## Open contradictions

**Did Val show the recycler to Kim himself?** The Sept-16 telling reads that way — "**I reached out to Joe Kim. I showed all the cards**… **When Joe Kim saw it**" — while everything else in the corpus has Kim seeing JVL's equipment **on the show floor in August**, reaching JVL only through InComm and [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]], and Val stating on Sept 10 that he asked for a meeting and was brushed off. **Both stand; the phone-call wording is loose and no source settles whether "showed" means a meeting, a booth, or the product simply being seen.** Not resolved [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Jamie_Georgia_Class_A_Opportunity_and_Market_Outlook-September-16-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]

**Did Kim see Billy Liu's product at the August show?** Two accounts of the same event, from the two competitors, and they do not agree. Recorded, **not resolved**.

- **Sept 2 — InComm says he did.** At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-02-incomm-machine-payments-and-recycler-standoff.md|Sept-2 lunch]]: "**Joe Kim noticed your product. He noticed Billy Liu's product. He noticed other kiosk vendors that represented ATM functionality access.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]]
- **Sept 15 — Billy says he never came near the booth.** "**Joe Kim claims that he did not like what he saw at the show**… **'Did he stop by your booth?' I said, 'No.' In fact, I saw him from afar. I ran over there and I said, 'Hey, Joe, how's it going? I didn't know you would come to these things.'**… **No, he did not come by my booth. So whatever he didn't like, he couldn't have said, 'I don't like what you're doing.' He didn't see it. He didn't like what the other guys were doing.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]]

The two may be compatible if "noticed" means something looser than a booth visit, but **neither source says so and neither is preferred here**. What Billy does say Kim objected to is consistent with every other account: "**He says that he doesn't like the fact that this card can go straight to cash.**"

*(Also recorded: this is the **only** report in the corpus of anyone speaking to Kim face to face. Val has asked for a meeting three times and never met him.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]]

# Joe Kim (Georgia Lottery general counsel, COAM)

**Joe Kim is the general counsel for the Georgia Lottery who regulates COAM.** Identified by name and role by **Adam Harlow** of [[wiki/organizations/incom.md|InComm]] on the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-26-incomm-skill-pay-atm-guidance.md|Aug 26 2026 InComm follow-up]], in answer to Val asking outright "So, sorry, who is Joe Kim?" — "Joe Kim is the general counsel for the Georgia Lottery that regulates COM." He is external to JVL and, on the evidence so far, JVL has no direct relationship with him — he reaches JVL through InComm and through [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]]'s account of the trade show. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]

*(Adam renders the name once as "Joe Kemp" in the same transcript; "Joe Kim" is used consistently elsewhere, including by Val. Kept under "Joe Kim" with "Joe Kemp" as an alias.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]

## Why he matters to JVL

- **His reaction at the August 2026 Georgia expo is the direct cause of InComm's cash-out posture**, which in turn constrains JVL's Georgia product roadmap. Per Adam Harlow, what worried him on the show floor was demos of a card going into a machine that was *also* a redemption machine and the machine "waving its wand and spitting out cash", plus products that were **ATM-use-only** — "basically a mechanism to turn your redemption directly into cash." Adam read him as "perturbed or fatigued at people trying to sort of skirt around his prior guidance", and said he was "going back to kind of sharpen that guidance and hone it to be a bit better." Full consequence on [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-26-no-cash-from-coam-redemption-device-for-skill-pay.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]
- **Adam believed InComm's own product was not in his sights** last week, "after some conversations that we had with him." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]
- **The same person appears from JVL's side of the show, and there he is described as excited rather than alarmed.** On the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-26-alignment-planning-nova-september-releases.md|Aug-26 morning alignment call]] Val reported that "Главный юрист [лотереи] Джорджия" — the Georgia lottery's chief legal counsel — "заявил, что они взволнованы этим решением" (declared they are excited/agitated about this solution) after seeing JVL's transaction recycler, and Val was pleased by it: "мне нравится, когда, выступая главный юрист и он в шоке… Я доволен результатом." Pavel's second-hand version, from [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei]], was that the demo "фурор сделал" (caused a furore), that JVL has no ready answer and cannot sell the kiosks yet, but promised a legal version soon. *(That the Aug-26 morning "chief legal counsel" is Joe Kim is an **inference** from role and event — the name is not spoken on that call.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alignment_Planning_NOVA_and_Sept_Releases-August-26-2026.md]]
- Val's own framing of the regulator's underlying objection, from the same morning call: the state promotes lotteries as gambling and does not want direct cash payment — "они как бы не хотят, чтоб это было… Если мы напрямую выплачиваем деньги." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Alignment_Planning_NOVA_and_Sept_Releases-August-26-2026.md]]

## Aug 31 2026 — an operator's read: he has spoken, and he was not clear

At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-31-dixie-amusement-kiosk-wait-and-see.md|Aug-31 Dixie Amusement visit]], [[wiki/people/salim-khambawala.md|Salim Khambawala]] — vice president of the state operators' association — gave the first operator-side assessment of him in the corpus. It is the reason Dixie is not buying redemption equipment (see [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-31-dixie-defers-kiosk-investment-until-the-rules-settle.md]]). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]

- **He has addressed the cash-out question publicly and settled nothing.** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] suggested inviting him to see JVL's machine ("JVL is showing money coming out. So [Joe], come and see your system, the lottery guy"). Salim: "**But he spoke about it. He wasn't clear. I think he's going to come out**" — followed by his view of how a regulator's single line gets turned into five readings: "**You give them one line, and you talk to five attorneys… and tell them how you want that line to be read, and he'll read it that way.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]
- **Salim's structural point: he cannot keep kiosks outside his remit.** "**That's where Joe Kim comes into factor. He first cannot say he is not the regulator on [kiosks] because it's coming**… **Them saying no, kiosk is not part of the [COAM], but it will become a part.**" He illustrated it with an operator's reductio — a weighing scale placed under a COAM machine, which does "something else besides a COAM machine which is being played" and so by the Lottery's own logic would fall outside the rules. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]
- **And the limit of his authority, which is the operators' real fear.** "**the lottery is just the regulator. They are not the law maker or the enforcer. The enforcer is going to be GBI, the local police department, the local sheriff department.** If they get their hands into it and say… the constitution which they are there to protect and enforce, we see it's [breached]… **It's an uphill battle to fight the state and the attorney general.**" On Salim's reading, a favourable Joe Kim ruling would therefore not settle the question. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]
- **Why regulators keep it vague at all, in his account:** "**they know that's where they make funds. So when they [find] someone, they get fined. And they don't want to pick sides of anyone. So it's always murkier and murkier.**" Recorded as one operator's characterisation, not as established fact about the GLC. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]

## Open questions

- When, if ever, will he issue the sharpened guidance, and will it permit ATM/cash-out tied to COAM redemption? InComm treats its posture as time-bound but does not give one figure — Jason Hall said "three to five years from now, who knows", Adam Harlow said "Two years from now, they may see things differently"; both were firm that it holds for 2026–27 — [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Skill_Pay_ATM_Guidance-August-26-2026.md]]

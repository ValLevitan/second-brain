# Andrew Cashin

The man who did nearly all the talking on the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-11-digital-cash-processing-card-solution-and-cash-float-partnership.md|Sept 11 2026 exploratory call]] between JVL and [[wiki/organizations/digital-cash-processing.md|Digital Cash Processing / ICP Inc.]]. He describes himself as "**an outside guy**" rather than an owner of the business he is representing [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]].

*(Speaker attribution is Plaud's numbering plus the calendar invite: he is Speaker 3 and names himself at 00:33.)* [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]

## Background, as he gave it

- **Cash Systems → Global Cash Access → Everi → IGT.** "**We had a company years ago. It was called Cash Systems. That was grown when IPO. Eventually, our founder sold out, and I became CFO of that company… Continued on as a head of engineering for that and so on, and we sold out… eventually to Global Cash Access, which is now it became Everi, which is now IGT.**" *(The transcript rendered "Everi" as "Every"; the corrections applied to the raw file on 2026-09-11 are listed in the derived notes, which also record that a second "every" in the file is the ordinary word and was deliberately left alone.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]
- **A second telling, with a name and an ambiguity.** "**eventually I became CFO working with Rick Beer, a guy named Craig Potts, and Craig was the owner at the time.**" **Which company Craig Potts owned is not stated** — the sentence follows straight on from the Cash Systems account but also names his present colleague. *(Recorded, not resolved. "Web Publishing" appears in the same broken sentence and is not interpreted.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]
- **South Florida, and Arthur Andersen.** "**I'm a South Florida guy. I grew up in Broward County. I worked for Arthur Anderson CPA firm in One Biscayne Tower downtown Miami.**" This is what put him and Val on the same ground immediately — Val knew the building [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]].
- **He was calling from Mexico, where he has a kiosk company.** "**I'm sitting in Mexico right now, and I actually have a company in Mexico for with kiosks.**" That is what he asked for a separate follow-up about [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]].
- **He and Val have overlapping Las Vegas history.** Cashin worked at **United Coin** in Las Vegas and has known "Bill Nader" since about 1995; Val: "**Sure, I know United Coin, and I've done and I know Bill Madler from the days of GPT.**" *("Bill Madler" and "Bill Nadler" are both renderings in the same file; see [[wiki/people/bill.md]] for why the identification with the corpus's existing "Bill" is flagged and **not asserted**.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]

## What he came for, and what he ran into

His question was blunt and he asked it twice: "**does somebody want a card based solution without the MasterCard piece of it, or do we have to have a MasterCard piece?**" He said he genuinely does not know the legal difference between a card with a logo and one without, and that Bill Nader did not either [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]].

- **He relies on Bill Nader for regulatory guidance, and Val punctured it.** Asked whether he had spoken to the Georgia Lottery, Cashin: "**I've always relied on Bill Nader and those guys to guide.**" Val: "**I don't know if Bill Nader speaks to [the Lottery].**" Cashin: "**I don't know.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]
- **He reduced the coming Georgia rule to absurdity in one line**, and Val agreed with him. Told that a card-to-ATM transaction **in the same location** is to become illegal: "**So they're trying to say you got to… It's got to be some other location, whether or not it's ten feet away or what.**" Val: "**To me, it sounds absolutely funny.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]
- **He reads the regulator's motive as anti-operator; Val told him it is not about money at all.** Cashin: "**They're trying to create a law to say no, you can't… Because it's actually good for the operators.**" Val: "**Nobody cares about the operator. Nobody cares about the profit. These are not profit motivated issues. These are legislative issues.**" Cashin's closing position on it: "**they don't want to ruin the market.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]
- **He did not know the size of the market he had entered.** Guessing at "**the sixty eight hundred locations or sixty eight hundred machines**", he was given 7,400 locations, 47,000 machines and $5bn, and answered: "**Five billion. Didn't know that. B with the B.**" See [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]] (Sept 11) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]].
- **And he is spending ahead of revenue, by his own colleague's account.** "**Rick Beer just keeps thinks I just keep spending and they keep getting these products approved.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]

## His commercial position

He wants revenue share on the ATM fee and third-party distribution, and explicitly not hardware margin: "**we charge them the two dollars fee, or something… That's what we're looking for**"; "**We don't want to be a hardware guy. We just want to be a processor side, and people distribute our stuff.**" Val called it competing on price and refused it — the ATM fee belongs to the master operator. See [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-11-market-access-traded-for-a-share-of-the-back-end-and-the-atm-fee-belongs-to-the-master-operator.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]].

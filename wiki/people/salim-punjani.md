# Salim Punjani (Continental — Georgia, Texas and Virginia)

The operator behind [[wiki/organizations/continental.md|Continental]], and the customer who gave [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] the clearest account in the corpus of **why Georgia operators want a replay machine**. Val and [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] visited his facility on **September 2 2026** — [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda Giorgi]], through whom the derived notes say JVL reached him the day before, was stuck in traffic for the first half and is referenced but not recorded speaking in the second — see [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-02-continental-bifurcated-operations-and-vendor-lock-in.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]]

## How he runs the business

- **He owns both companies, and Continental came first.** Asked whether he was related to the two names — "with the north northeastern continent. Are you continental?" — he answered "no, I'm both," and "**So we started with continental.**" *(The second company's name is not clearly rendered anywhere in the transcript; the derived notes write "Continental / Northeastern Continental." Recorded as spoken rather than resolved.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]]
- **He came to this meeting through Felipe, over a complaint.** "**I was having issues with JVL, so I contacted Philip yesterday and talked to him.**" *(Transcript renders Felipe as "Philip"; the derived notes read the same line as Felipe.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]]
- **The out-of-state business is one person, on the road, running on referral.** "he runs my Texas and Virginia locations. **He's always on the road.** But who's looking for locations? **He does everything.** It's always the phone call. **It's one customer, one customer is happy calls the second one, second one calls us.**" *(The man is not named. §15.8 — no compensation is discussed for him anywhere in these sources.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Vendor_Lock_In_Dead_End-September-2-2026.md]]
- **Out-of-state, he leases rather than owns the locations.** Val: "But you operate your own locations." — "**We just lease these.**" — "You go from location to location and you lease it." — "**We have a team that goes from location.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]]
- **He judges the market to have no product moat, and says his own edge is reporting.** "**That's not a competitive name**… they have fusions, we have fusions… **They have same Omega cabinets, we have Gio Vega cabinets.** It's just where the customer sits. **There's no competitive advantage.** The only reason… people like us is… **because we have our online reporting.** So the person has four or five locations… they are able to make sure push these games to the right people." *("Gio Vega" is transcribed as written and is not resolvable from this source.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]]

## Why Georgia needs replay and Texas does not — his explanation, twice

This is the mechanism the whole Georgia transactional argument turns on, and he gave it unprompted in both segments. It is a **reporting artefact, not a player feature**. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]]

> "**Georgia, you need a replay**… In Georgia, we have to report that, right? So that's why they want a replay machine. Otherwise, every time cash comes out, it shows that there is so much cash out… **It's like hundred dollars, play ten, cash out ninety. Play ten, cash out eighty.** So the machine, **Georgia Lottery reports this as** … **hundred seventy or hundred ninety in and twenty out, which is wrong.** So Georgia Lottery is wrong… **So that's why people are asking for replay. In Texas, there is no authority that's asking us to do whatever you want.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]]

Restated in segment 2, with the store's exposure named: "currently **Georgia Lottery says hundred dollars in, ninety dollars out**… The customer takes same ninety bucks and puts it in machine number two… **so Georgia Lottery says you had one hundred and ninety dollars in**… So **170 out is the one that's bothering the stores a lot**… **If this was a replay, my guitar would be only 70 dollars.**" *("my guitar" is a garble; the derived notes read the passage as the real $70 versus the inflated $170.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Vendor_Lock_In_Dead_End-September-2-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Continental_Vendor_Lock_In_Dead_End-September-2-2026.md]]

And why **Access** is the one product that answers it: "**in access you can tap the card or you can insert the card and take machine to machine, and they provide a report.** So when Georgia law inspector comes to our new location, they will show… **It's only seventy. So no one else has been able to solve that except for access. The last one that I saw who solved that was Novomatic.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Vendor_Lock_In_Dead_End-September-2-2026.md]]

## What he buys, and why — Val's "dead end" read

- **In Georgia he buys only Aristocrat.** "**In state we only are buying right now aristocrat because that's the only one that's performing**"… "**It's the only one that people want to play.**" And the demand is from the trade, not the player — Val asked whether it is the locations or the players asking, and the answer was "**The locations are asking.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Vendor_Lock_In_Dead_End-September-2-2026.md]]
- **Nothing has matched Banilla out of state.** "**nothing has come far so far that comes close to Banilla.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]]
- **Val said it to his face.** "**Doesn't look like we can do business here.** No. Well, I mean, I I don't know. **It seems like your mind is set on aristocrat and access system.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Vendor_Lock_In_Dead_End-September-2-2026.md]]
- **He did not close the door.** "**At this point, aristocrat is doing**… **I'm not saying that's going to change.** Doesn't mean as the customer asks for more, I will. **I'll reach out to Filipe. Filipe is always in touch with me every 15 days.** He's like, Hey, Salim, do you need something? Can I get you? **Great guy. He's the one… who got JVL back in the market.**" And his own stated trigger: "**In the future, if there's a new JVL game that comes in that people want.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Vendor_Lock_In_Dead_End-September-2-2026.md]]
- **He volunteered that JVL verticals had come back from his locations.** Asked "Have you tested our games?" — "**Lately, we put some verticals which is coming back, and we have to replace them with aristocrats. When you walked in the back, you saw there were a couple of JVs just sitting out there**"… "**They're not many. They're just two, but they came back because they want to say what they want.**" Val did not push: "**I don't see anything else, but I believe it**"… "**I hear you. I hear you. Thank you very much.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Vendor_Lock_In_Dead_End-September-2-2026.md]]
- **He has seen JVL's transactional system and it did not move him.** Val: "You know that we have a better system." — "**Obviously, yes. I saw it in your demo.**" Asked "You don't want to use our system?" — "**We haven't tried it yet. We don't know the pricing point and everything.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Vendor_Lock_In_Dead_End-September-2-2026.md]]

## The one opening: refurbished machines for Texas

- **Georgia purchasing is shut; Texas is not.** "as long as… **they have the feature for the outer state**, like in Georgia. **Now we're not buying anymore. No, for Texas.** For Texas, yeah, refurbished again." Val: "**We can sell you refurbished machines. For taxes**" *(the transcript's rendering of Texas)*. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]]
- **Five or ten, not one.** "**if price is right, and you know we can try five or ten.** We'll put in there." Val: "You would try one." — "**No, the one is not going to give me**…" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]]
- **The benchmark is Banilla.** Val: "**Imagine if it does one hundred fifty percent of that.** Then we'll" — "**Replace it with Banilla, but… nothing has come far so far that comes close to Banilla.**" *(The derived notes read this as: at 150% of Banilla's performance he would replace Banilla with the new machine. The transcript's literal wording is "Replace it with Banilla"; the notes' reading is recorded as **the notes' reading**, not asserted.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]]

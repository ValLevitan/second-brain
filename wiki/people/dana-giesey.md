# Dana Giesey

**MG Oil's senior manager at its Rapid City headquarters** and [[wiki/organizations/jvl.md|JVL]]'s working contact at [[wiki/organizations/mg-oil.md|MG Oil]] — the company that is, in [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]]'s words on the visit, "practically the only" JVL customer in South Dakota. He reports to owner [[wiki/people/troy-erikson.md|Troy Erickson]] and does not make purchasing decisions himself. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]

*(Name note: the surname **Giesey** appears only in the derived notes and the source filenames — the transcript never says it. The corpus has carried him as bare "**Dana**" since May 2025, when Troy named him as the general manager who oversees MG Oil's gaming and would coordinate with JVL's product manager [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]]. [[raw/mg_oil_south_dakota_intro-may-13-2025.md]])*

## Background, in his own telling (June 18 2026)

- He **managed the Flying J truck stop** for MG Oil — "I managed the Flying J truck stop out here for **fifteen years** back in **nineteen ninety**" — running the truck stop, its restaurant and the hotel. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **He is the reason MG Oil is in gaming at all.** The truck stop had lottery machines placed by another vendor: "**Didn't take us long to figure out there was money to be made there.** So we took a few years to break into the business" — after which MG Oil began buying its own machines for its own locations. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- He ran the lottery route alongside the truck stop when it was **about three hundred machines**, then handed that off as MG Oil began buying other operators and moved to **oversee things at the headquarters office**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- On his tenure he says both "back in nineteen ninety" for the truck stop and "**I've worked for them for a long time since the eighties**." The two are not reconciled here; both are as spoken. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- He told Val he is **not an engineer** ("Somewhat, I'm not an engineer, no"). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]

## What he actually wants from JVL (June 18 2026)

This is the most useful thing in the source: Val offered to strip a feature out, and Dana asked for something narrower and more useful instead.

- **Keep the QR scan on initial setup; remove only the reload after a component change.** "As far as the **initial setup** of the machine, that's probably something we want to set **because we don't want people changing anything in there**" — and then, of the reload: "**That part I think we would want to remove.**" The reason the initial scan suits him is the same reason JVL built it: MG Oil does not want its own technicians altering settings either. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **What he is really trying to remove is the wait, not the QR code.** "The main thing we're trying to get around is **the wait. Waiting for that code.** When you get to that point, you have to **take a picture and call somebody and send them that picture, then wait for them to get something back to you.**" His own fix: give MG Oil **portal access** — "I can just log in… **I can hurry up, get done, get out of the location, get the player back.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **Parts were not the problem.** "And Park [parts] showed up fast. **The only holdup was just waiting on the codes** from the QR codes. That was the only slow part." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **He does not need JVL to keep a South Dakota office.** "I don't think it would be a problem **not having somebody in South Dakota. Nebraska is not that far away.**" What he needs instead: "**Spare parts and somebody to answer on the phone**, just in case we have questions. Somebody on the phone somewhere who **knows the machine**." That answer produced [[wiki/decisions/2026-06-18-jvl-moves-its-south-dakota-office-to-nebraska.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **He rates JVL's South Dakota man well.** Asked how "Dennis" was working out: "**Dennis has been great. He's always there when we need him. He's been responsive.**" (Read by context as [[wiki/people/denis-desmarais.md|Denis Desmarais]] — the transcript gives only the first name.) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]

## His read of the market and the regulator

- **On Val's transactional products** — recycling, the ticket-only QR design, the InComm prize card — his answer was consistent: good technology, but he doubts South Dakota would permit it. "Pretty handy thing to do, but I don't know. **I don't know if you could get it done in South Dakota or not.** … **It's tough to get them to change anything.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **On how slowly the state moves:** "**We've been trying to get the badland money increased for twenty years, and it goes nowhere.**" *("badland money" is a garble; the derived notes read the subject as the **payout limit**. That reading is the notes', not asserted here.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **The one idea he liked** was splitting a payout above the withholding threshold into two: "**I like that idea.**" See [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]] (Jun-18). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **On MG Oil's Nebraska business, he was candid that it is under-served:** "**It's a long way away from us. So I don't think we've probably given it the attention** — somebody local could do a better job because we got a lot going on in South Dakota." And, unprompted: "**I think he would sell that in Nebraska.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **On MG Oil's dependence on gaming:** "**we wouldn't be in the business if it weren't for the lottery.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **On succession, unprompted:** "**One of the things is that we're all getting older**" — and that the management team across the whole company is in Troy's age group. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]

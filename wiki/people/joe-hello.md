# Joe Hello (Texas operator)

A Texas operator who reviewed a JVL skill game in August 2026 and wants to place it in his locations. He is one of the two Texas operators Val was introduced to through [[wiki/people/stephen-fenoglio.md|Stephen Fenoglio]] in June 2026. The fuller Texas story is on [[wiki/projects/texas-used-machine-distribution.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Hello_Games_Texas_Skill_Payout_Wording-August-11-2026.md]]

**Name and identity (§4, resolved by context).** The Aug-11 transcript calls him only "Joe" ("Thank you, Joe"), and never names his company. The derived notes file the call as "Hello Games", from an Aug-21 calendar event "JVL_Hello_Games_Follow-up_call" with attendee "Joe Hello". [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Hello_Games_Texas_Skill_Payout_Wording-August-11-2026.md]] The wiki already records a Texas operator "Joe Hello" / "Joe Hollow", named by the Jun-10 derived notes as "**Joe Hello** (Capitol Amusement, Austin)", introduced to Val by Fenoglio on June 17 [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Stephen_Fenoglio_Texas_Skill_Gaming_Counsel-June-10-2026.md]]. This Joe is taken to be the same man — an **inference from context**, not stated in the Aug-11 transcript: he is a Texas operator, his reviewer is by context Fenoglio, and the notes' calendar pairing names "Joe Hello". **"Hello Games" is the notes' label only; the company name is not reconciled** (see Open questions). "Joe" alone also names [[wiki/people/joe-kim.md|Joe Kim]] of the Georgia Lottery — a different person.

## Aug 11 2026 — the skill-game review and the payout wording

On the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-11-joe-hello-texas-skill-game-payout-wording.md|Aug-11 call]] (~12:23 PM ET, ~7 min, English) with [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]]. §15.8: clear — product, legal and commercial talk only; Val's small talk about his personal life is not recorded. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Hello_Games_Texas_Skill_Payout_Wording-August-11-2026.md]]

- **He likes the game.** "I love the game; the game looks great." He had gone through the documents JVL sent. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Hello_Games_Texas_Skill_Payout_Wording-August-11-2026.md]]
- **His one concern: the ability to adjust payout amounts**, which he read in the specs and manuals. Val: "I Think it refers to the slot portion of the game… a skill element you cannot adjust… But if you want, we can take the wording out." Joe: that wording "might hold you up if that had to be, you know, questioned in court… that would be the only hiccup." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Hello_Games_Texas_Skill_Payout_Wording-August-11-2026.md]]
- **His read on Texas.** "As far as it being one hundred percent skill, I can't see anything that would keep it from not being able to operate in Texas. In my opinion." He still wants a reviewer's "final say" — see the name note on the Texas project page (Aug 11). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Hello_Games_Texas_Skill_Payout_Wording-August-11-2026.md]]
- **He offered to buy; Val told him not to.** Joe: once cleared, "I'll go ahead and purchase it from you and get it out on the street." Val: "you don't have to purchase it from me uh, Go ahead and test it… I am looking for a large business. I don't care about selling one machine." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Hello_Games_Texas_Skill_Payout_Wording-August-11-2026.md]]
- **Locations.** He has "several places" that would benefit from something different "besides a Banilla or a pace game". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Hello_Games_Texas_Skill_Payout_Wording-August-11-2026.md]]
- **Install.** His own crew can place the machine; if they have issues, JVL's phone tech-support contact can walk them through it — Val named him as [[wiki/people/sebastian.md|Sebastian]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Hello_Games_Texas_Skill_Payout_Wording-August-11-2026.md]]

## Aug 21 2026 — the follow-up: "don't invest in Texas until after the session", and a free October install

On the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-21-joe-hello-texas-market-entry-follow-up.md|Aug-21 follow-up call]] (7:00 PM ET, ~34 min, English) with [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] and fellow Texas operator Jordan Barberio. The transcript labels Joe "Speaker 1"; he is identified by context (Val: "Enough, Joe, you gave me an honest answer"). The Texas-wide read is on [[wiki/projects/texas-used-machine-distribution.md]] (Aug 21). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Hello_Games_Texas_Market_Entry_Followup-August-21-2026.md]]

- **The reviewer is booked.** Joe has "Steve" booked to look at the game in depth; "He'll be back around I think September twentieth". Steve already has the manual and the how-to-play material. By context this is [[wiki/people/stephen-fenoglio.md|Stephen Fenoglio]] (see that page, Aug 21). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Hello_Games_Texas_Market_Entry_Followup-August-21-2026.md]] *(The derived notes split "Steve Camillo" and "Fenoglio" into two people and again label Joe's company "Hello Games" from the calendar event; neither is in the recording.)* [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Hello_Games_Texas_Market_Entry_Followup-August-21-2026.md]]
- **He likes the game.** "The graphics are amazing… it outdid all the other ones" at the convention; "it's very fast. There is no delay." He asked if there are more than the six games on the board he was sent; Val: "We have plenty of games I I just didn't want you to drown in games." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Hello_Games_Texas_Market_Entry_Followup-August-21-2026.md]]
- **His honest advice: wait.** "I would not invest a lot of money in Texas until after the session. Or we get a clear picture of what we have ahead of us." Skill games' survival "could go either way; it's fifty fifty", and his own business is "looking at other options for Texas than skill right now". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Hello_Games_Texas_Market_Entry_Followup-August-21-2026.md]]
- **He asked JVL to help fund the association's fight.** "we're always looking for money for our association to help fund this battle… Your your company, too." No answer from Val is recorded. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Hello_Games_Texas_Market_Entry_Followup-August-21-2026.md]]
- **He wants the transactional equipment.** He has "several locations that would work with your equipment", named bingo halls ("they do have attendance"), and one bingo hall with "twenty five, I believe, sweepstakes games". He asked for more detail and a four-way email: "All four of us on an email would be great, and we can get the conversation started about that equipment for Texas. I think that would be a game changer." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Hello_Games_Texas_Market_Entry_Followup-August-21-2026.md]]
- **He set the install date.** "Let's try to get this scheduled, like the first week of October. After Steve is back, and we can have him sit down with us while we have the install." Val: "So so first week of October it is then." Val asked who his product manager should deal with; the answer reads as Joe himself ("You look at me directly"). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Hello_Games_Texas_Market_Entry_Followup-August-21-2026.md]]

**§15.8:** clear — market, legal and product talk only.

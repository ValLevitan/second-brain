# Arkady Batson

Arkady Batson handles JVL's **sales orders, invoicing and shipping on the Canada side** — the person who prepares invoices and arranges shipment when JVL sells a machine or parts out of Canada. His surname ("Бэтсон" / Batson) surfaced on the Apr 18 2025 [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Klimovich]] update. [[raw/andrei_klimovich_update-apr-18-2025.md]]

## Role (Apr 18 2025)

- Was preparing **two invoices** with Klimovich for the revived home-version orders (Birmingham Vending Company, Alabama; and one bound for Virginia) and had earlier shipped **two spare parts** to a customer. [[raw/andrei_klimovich_update-apr-18-2025.md]]
- Val corrected a process error Arkady had made: on a **direct third-party sale** Arkady listed **JVL's own customs broker** on the sales order/invoice — JVL's broker should be used **only** for inter-company transfers or JVL's own imports; a third-party buyer must clear through **their own** broker, or JVL wrongly eats the fees. [[raw/andrei_klimovich_update-apr-18-2025.md]]
- Advised on the Burger King repair economics — that a monitor swap should replace the whole **"sandwich"** (touchscreen glued to a 20-year-old monitor can't be peeled once dried; gaskets harden; plastic frame is cheap), saving only ~$100–120 to reuse the frame — and reported that decommissioned old Georgia machines share the same CPU as the old units, a possible parts source. See [[wiki/organizations/burger-king.md]]. [[raw/andrei_klimovich_update-apr-18-2025.md]]
- Going forward: **Klimovich supplies Arkady all invoice information and Arkady generates it through the system** for **Canada-origin** sales, and arranges Canada shipping ("there's an established shipping system there"); **US-origin** sales route to [[wiki/people/lev-rudky.md|Lev]] instead until BC is fully installed. [[raw/andrei_klimovich_update-apr-18-2025.md]]

## Apr 28 2025 — Canada-side assembly/manufacturing (Business Central)

On the BC status call ([[wiki/meetings/2025-04-28-business-central-labs-tariffs-status.md]]) Arkady ("Arcadi"/"Acadia") is the person who **assembles and ships** on the JVL Labs (Canada) side — he receives purchase orders (from Sebastian/Elizabeth), picks, and ships intercompany. Val wants him confined to **picking and shipping**, with the posted (irrevocable) **invoice triggered at shipment**, rather than Arkady issuing invoices or shipping on a reversible sales order. He would run the **light manufacturing module** in Canada (as "Alec" once did in Navision). See [[wiki/topics/business-central-erp-implementation.md]]. [[raw/business_central_update-apr-28-2025.md]]

## Nov 3 2025 — the rushed 335-machine shipment; the "never checks" trait

On the [[wiki/meetings/2025-11-03-machine-qc-checklist-release-form.md|Nov-3 QC meeting]] Val used Arkady as the case study for JVL's lost production discipline: when tariffs on Canada loomed, Arkady's team **tossed the sub-assemblies together and shipped 335 machines to Georgia as fast as possible**, and the loose-speaker defect slipped through. Val's blunt read — **Oleg (Hmyrov)** used to hand-check every machine and trained the assemblers who kept doing so, but no one trained Arkady, who **"never checks anything himself."** Context for his Canada-side assembly/shipping role. See [[wiki/topics/slot-machine-production-test-procedure.md]] (Nov-3). [[raw/Machine Quality Control Checklist and a Release form.md]]

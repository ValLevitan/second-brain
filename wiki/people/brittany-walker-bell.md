# Brittany Walker Bell

Brittany Walker Bell is JVL's **official point of contact at the Nebraska Department of Revenue** — Juan reported (Mar 24 2025) that she will handle both the **entity-creation / tax** side and the **technical** side of JVL's Nebraska entry. She clarified points in the regulations for JVL (e.g. around whether a machine is required to connect to a system). [[raw/games_markets-march-24-2025.md]] See [[wiki/projects/nebraska-skill-market.md]].

(Name transcribed "Brittany Walker Bell" on Mar 24 and "Brittany Waters" on May 15; spelling flagged for confirmation — see Owner Decisions.)

## History

- **May 15 2025 — overruled by her boss.** On [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan]]'s Nebraska visit he was introduced to **Brittany's boss, the attorney**, who apologized that "she should have never spoken about the game, she's not qualified for that" — retracting her earlier verbal "your game looks fine" assessments. The attorney then ruled that JVL's **nudge does not qualify as skill**, forcing JVL to drop nudge for Nebraska. See [[wiki/projects/nebraska-skill-market.md]].

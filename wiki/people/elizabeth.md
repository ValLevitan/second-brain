# Elizabeth

Elizabeth is a JVL **full-time engineering technician** — a university-degree engineer, not an accountant ("she actually doesn't know anything about accounting… she is an engineer"). She previously worked for JVL's **Colombian entity as a field-service technician**, speaks Spanish, and now handles **service, parts and replacements for the Georgia market**, where local Georgia technicians come to her. Val had mistakenly thought she "is just an accountant." [[raw/business_central_update-apr-28-2025.md]]

## Role in the Business Central / order-flow model (Apr 28 2025)

- On the Business Central status call ([[wiki/meetings/2025-04-28-business-central-labs-tariffs-status.md]]), Val worked out who should create which documents. Elizabeth is one of **three engineers who place orders** — with [[wiki/people/sebastian.md|Sebastian]] (Florida) and "Andrei" (Florida). [[raw/business_central_update-apr-28-2025.md]]
- Val's ruling: Elizabeth should **place purchase orders** into JVL Labs (e.g. to receive Flex machines in Georgia) — **not issue sales orders** (she is "right now issuing sales orders. She should not be" — sales orders are for customer-facing people: [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]], Andrei, or [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]]). She and Sebastian would put purchase orders to [[wiki/people/arkady-batson.md|Arkady]], who assembles and ships intercompany. [[raw/business_central_update-apr-28-2025.md]]
- She is a current BC/Systems user — Lev cited getting occasional "how to fix that" requests from Elizabeth. She would need a BC user license (~$1,000–1,200/user/year) and a short training seminar (with [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Gennady]] lecturing on parts/diagrams) to place orders and identify part numbers. [[raw/business_central_update-apr-28-2025.md]]

## June 24–26 2025 — heads manufacturing in Georgia

When JVL decided to move manufacturing/assembly from Canada to Georgia ([[wiki/decisions/2025-06-24-move-manufacturing-from-canada-to-georgia.md]]), Val named **Elizabeth to head up manufacturing** at the new Louisville warehouse — responsible for **warehousing, inventory control, and work scheduling** (logistics stays with [[wiki/people/mary.md|Mary]]). Val confirmed the choice with [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]]: "she is definitely the right person," a serious young engineer, "technically very inclined… dedicated," on "a different level" than [[wiki/people/arkady-batson.md|Arkadi]] — "which is what we need." She will need schematics/instructions and will interact with the engineering team. Val had worked with her only at a distance ("she worked for me for a long time, but I never worked with her personally"). [[raw/procurement_relocation-june-24-2025.md]] [[raw/Alignment_procurement_manufacturing_transfer-june-26-2025.md]]

## Sept 22 2025 — South Dakota service onboarding

On the Val↔[[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi]] electro-mechanical alignment ([[wiki/meetings/2025-09-22-electro-mechanical-alignment-genadi.md]]), Genadi is onboarding Elizabeth onto the **service/reference material for the South Dakota machines** (10 machines, sealing/labeling tasks). Her hurdle is **English**, so she follows the technical group via the team chat with instant translation, and Genadi will screen-share and walk her (and [[wiki/people/albertini.md|Albertini]]) through the material. Val coached Genadi that she isn't ignoring him — she processes and asks only when she has a real question. [[raw/alignment_elctro-mechanical-september-22-2025.md]]

## Nov 3 2025 — preparing to assemble 100 machines in Georgia

Discussed (not present) on the [[wiki/meetings/2025-11-03-machine-qc-checklist-release-form.md|Nov-3 QC meeting]]: Elizabeth is **preparing to assemble 100 machines** in Georgia. Val praised her as a degreed engineer who treats the work as a career and does it seriously; she had personally checked the ~10 machines shipped to one location, but JVL's lack of a final whole-machine QC step let the hidden loose-speaker defect through. [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi]] is to travel to Georgia ~2 weeks to work hands-on with her on the problem sub-assemblies and set up a proper QC/release process. See [[wiki/topics/slot-machine-production-test-procedure.md]] (Nov-3). [[raw/Machine Quality Control Checklist and a Release form.md]]

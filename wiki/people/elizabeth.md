# Elizabeth

Elizabeth is a JVL **full-time engineering technician** — a university-degree engineer, not an accountant ("she actually doesn't know anything about accounting… she is an engineer"). She previously worked for JVL's **Colombian entity as a field-service technician**, speaks Spanish, and now handles **service, parts and replacements for the Georgia market**, where local Georgia technicians come to her. Val had mistakenly thought she "is just an accountant." [[raw/business_central_update-apr-28-2025.md]]

## Role in the Business Central / order-flow model (Apr 28 2025)

- On the Business Central status call ([[wiki/meetings/2025-04-28-business-central-labs-tariffs-status.md]]), Val worked out who should create which documents. Elizabeth is one of **three engineers who place orders** — with [[wiki/people/sebastian.md|Sebastian]] (Florida) and "Andrei" (Florida). [[raw/business_central_update-apr-28-2025.md]]
- Val's ruling: Elizabeth should **place purchase orders** into JVL Labs (e.g. to receive Flex machines in Georgia) — **not issue sales orders** (she is "right now issuing sales orders. She should not be" — sales orders are for customer-facing people: [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]], Andrei, or [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]]). She and Sebastian would put purchase orders to [[wiki/people/arkady-batson.md|Arkady]], who assembles and ships intercompany. [[raw/business_central_update-apr-28-2025.md]]
- She is a current BC/Systems user — Lev cited getting occasional "how to fix that" requests from Elizabeth. She would need a BC user license (~$1,000–1,200/user/year) and a short training seminar (with [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Gennady]] lecturing on parts/diagrams) to place orders and identify part numbers. [[raw/business_central_update-apr-28-2025.md]]

# Louis Glenn

Louis Glenn is a **partner / investor in JVL**. Val called him second (after Steve Muzzo) during the August 2026 cash-call round. Unlike some other partners, Louis says he **has nothing to do with the Trump project** — he came into JVL "for strictly commercial reasons" and out of belief in Val ("it's you who I believe in"). Full context on [[wiki/topics/jvl-2026-funding-crisis-capital-call.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JVL_Liquidity_Crisis_Cash_Call_Louis_Glenn-August-8-2026.md]]

> **Transcript note.** The Aug-8 call has no speaker labels and at least one other person was on Louis's side, so some remarks the summary attributes to Louis may belong to that second participant. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_JVL_Liquidity_Crisis_Cash_Call_Louis_Glenn-August-8-2026.md]]

- **Current focus — modular precast.** Louis says he has "tens of millions of dollars tied up at modular precast" and is watching that investment with everything he has ("twenty-six hours of a twenty-four-hour day"), leaving no bandwidth for a JVL cash call. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JVL_Liquidity_Crisis_Cash_Call_Louis_Glenn-August-8-2026.md]]
- **"Good with books."** Val said Louis is good with books and offered him full access, even to the computer. Louis asked for the **last 3–4 years of year-end financials** — he hasn't seen JVL financials in 3–4 years — both to review the business and as **evidence for a tax loss**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JVL_Liquidity_Crisis_Cash_Call_Louis_Glenn-August-8-2026.md]]
- **Declined the cash call.** He is out — all his capital is committed to modular precast. He was empathetic and respectful, offering phone calls and introductions wherever they might help. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_JVL_Liquidity_Crisis_Cash_Call_Louis_Glenn-August-8-2026.md]]
- **A prior venture — "Integra" / curtain wall.** In the same call, a participant on Louis's side recounted having to walk away "two years ago" from **Integra** (a curtain-wall project) rather than put good money after bad — it cost him **$7 million**, and "Louis took a hit with me on that one." (Attribution uncertain — no speaker labels; recorded as stated.) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JVL_Liquidity_Crisis_Cash_Call_Louis_Glenn-August-8-2026.md]]

## Leads Louis offered JVL

- He knows the **Mississaugas of the Credit** well and understands (not yet public) that **three more casinos** will go into the **Niagara** area — years away, but Canadian buyers might prefer a Canadian product. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JVL_Liquidity_Crisis_Cash_Call_Louis_Glenn-August-8-2026.md]]
- The **son of Andre Kapinski** (who did the license for the first Niagara casino) has a venture **similar to JVL's transactional prepaid-card product**; Louis will find out more — a **merger** could be at hand. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JVL_Liquidity_Crisis_Cash_Call_Louis_Glenn-August-8-2026.md]]

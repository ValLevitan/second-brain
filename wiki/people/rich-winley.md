# Rich Winley (Texas and Pennsylvania operator; JVL Texas prospect)

**Rich Winley is an Atlanta-based gaming operator with a game route in Texas and a skill-game room in Pennsylvania.** He met JVL at G2E in October 2025 and had lunch with [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] in Miami on **January 8 2026**. There he asked about a JVL dealership in Texas, and the two agreed to test JVL machines in three Texas locations first. He is the thread behind much of JVL's early Texas testing. [[raw/G2E_juan_martinez_report-october-10-2025.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rich_Winley_Miami_Lunch_Texas_Distribution_Pitch-January-8-2026.md]]

On **January 27 2026** he gave Val, Michael Gottlieb and Juan Martinez an hour-long briefing on the Georgia COAM market, drawn from his years as a Georgia route operator. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rich_Winley_Georgia_COAM_Market_Intel-January-27-2026.md]]

## His businesses, as he described them (Jan 8 2026)

All from the lunch recording [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rich_Winley_Miami_Lunch_Texas_Distribution_Pitch-January-8-2026.md]]:

- **Georgia, past.** He and a partner held a Georgia **master licence**; the partnership ended ("We broke up"). Their model was to "build our portfolio and sell it to a large fund". *(The partner's name is garbled in the transcript and is not recorded.)*
- **Why he stepped back from Georgia.** New locations cost too much: "if you take a brand new location nowadays in Georgia, $100,000 cash, and then you put seven, eight games in there", plus Christmas bonuses and paying again at contract renewal. *(The transcript gives the game price as "$18,000,000"; the notes read it as $18,000 per game.)*
- **Miami, past.** He once had "nine game runs down here", and left as the laws "with Seminoles" made it "a little too risky".
- **Texas, now.** "I got an operator that runs the route for me." He said "Most operators don't know I'm an operator."
- **Pennsylvania, now.** One skill-game room. Whether skill games are legal there was waiting on a court opinion; he put the state at "80,000 games and I don't see it going anywhere." He also said "My Pennsylvania room is self-sufficient."
- **Gift cards (a Bahamas partner).** "I'm a partner with a company out in the Bahamas to bring the technology." The product is a digital-first gift card under the **Visa** brand: the player keeps one card "like it's a player's card", reloads it at a kiosk card reader or in an app from a debit card. He pitched it against InComm, which he said has no digital side. He has "a team in place" and a salesperson in Georgia. See [[wiki/organizations/incom.md]].
- **Ties to other makers.** He is "pretty close" to Banilla and named "my financing machine, Banilla" as his biggest obligation. He holds a **distribution agreement with Pace-O-Matic** (which state it covers is not said). See [[wiki/organizations/pace-o-matic.md]].
- **Before gaming.** He ran a marketing agency and a coding school, and writes code (JavaScript, Python, Ruby on Rails, Node.js).

## Jan 8 2026 — the Miami lunch

Stub: [[wiki/meetings/2026-01-08-rich-winley-miami-lunch-texas-distribution-pitch.md]]. All from the transcript [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rich_Winley_Miami_Lunch_Texas_Distribution_Pitch-January-8-2026.md]]:

- **His idea: a JVL dealership in Texas.** "What do you think about us setting up a JVL dealership in Texas." He already has "a distributor in Georgia". He thought "modestly I would say we sell through a thousand" machines, with the question being how many Pace-O-Matic machines JVL could replace.
- **What Texas locations need.** "There's not a location that I know of in Texas that doesn't take, they won't take games unless the operator has a kiosk." Game rooms run a Pyramid system or TITO; convenience stores, smoke shops and gas stations run Pyramid. Operators in Texas: "about 100 guys across the state" that matter — "I know at least 30 of them". Game rooms prefer splits like "70-30… 80-20" with the room taking the larger share. See [[wiki/organizations/pyramid-technologies.md]].
- **Val's answer: focus.** Val pressed him to stop running several businesses and "focus on JVL, JVL hat, deliver what you promise". His conditions: "I am not prepared to give JVL to somebody who's not focused. But I am prepared to work with somebody who is focused." Rich: "if the deal is proper, then yeah… But I have to figure out the other things that I'm doing."
- **What the role would be.** Val: pay "should definitely be attached to a total revenue that we generate", and Rich would help shape the product — "thinking of new generation of the product, thinking what features it needs… communicating with engineers". *(No figures recorded — §15.8.)*
- **The test of trust.** Val: "if I cannot give you a performing game… and you cannot move a performing game then we're not for each other." Rich: "if I can't move 100% I wouldn't want the relationship either."
- **The test plan.** Rich: "I got three there… let's go ahead and start testing them out." Val: "We will give you the printer integration"; for the three machines "all I need to implement is the QR code." Val: "We'll test the machines calmly, quietly… There will be no decisions made before we know what it is."
- **The operator meeting.** Rich planned to show the game at a Texas meeting of "50 operators, 60 operators". Val warned him to make an agreement first, or "you're just going to disclose my machine… So at least get paid for your services."
- **A QR-code worry.** Rich had heard from operators that players photograph the QR code, which counts up from "001", and scan through it to rob the machine. He would "ask them about that". Val agreed it is not a good system. See [[wiki/organizations/pyramid-technologies.md]].
- **Marketing ideas.** Instead of a trade-show booth he once got a luxury shop to host a free private shopping evening for Georgia operators ("They talk about it for years"); "The Wisconsin guys" rent Caesars cabanas instead of showing at AMOA in Vegas.
- **Georgia.** Both men talked about why JVL is out of favour in Georgia (Val's own view is on [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md]]). Their remarks about named people are not recorded (§15.8).
- **Next steps.** Rich: "I'm gonna come up with a scenario… and meanwhile we'll get the test out." Val will come to Texas; Rich offered to meet in Austin and take him to San Antonio. Rich: "We'll be in touch next week."

- **Now.** "I'm running in Texas now." He says he has never operated a JVL machine: "I've never run it."
- **What he offered to do.** Call [[wiki/people/sean-fellows.md|Sean Fellows]] first ("he'll give it to me raw"); try to get Bad Dog's numbers; find "friendly locations" where JVL can watch real players; and call any operators on JVL's customer list whom he knows personally. See Open commitments.
- **His next visit.** He is to see Val "on the 13th" (the month is not spoken; February is an inference from the date).

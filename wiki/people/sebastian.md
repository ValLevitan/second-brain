# Sebastian

Sebastian is a JVL field-service technician who installs and services machines in the field. He is based in **Florida** and originally from **Colombia**. [[raw/Alignment_outstanding_subjects_2025-02-19.md]]

## Role

- On the Feb 19 alignment call, Val and Juan discussed **sending Sebastian to South Dakota** to service the lab machine — Val stressed at length how hostile the winter trip is (Florida/Colombia climate, a one-day journey via Miami→Dallas→Sioux Falls plus a 3-hour drive) so the Minsk team appreciates the effort involved. His trip would replace the CPU board/assembly on the frozen machine and swap the Gen 5 printer for an Epic 950 for Lonnie to test. See [[wiki/projects/south-dakota-vlt-market.md]]. [[raw/Alignment_outstanding_subjects_2025-02-19.md]]
- **Field verification:** Sebastian confirmed the machine's fan was blowing inwards (correct from the factory) during the mystery-freeze investigation. [[raw/Alignment_outstanding_subjects_2025-02-19.md]]
- On the **Florida CPU-kit** machine, Sebastian made a setup mistake with **show mode** (it was left checked), and he had to connect dual monitors before selecting single-monitor to reproduce the customer's desired single-screen look. [[raw/Alignment_outstanding_subjects_2025-02-19.md]]

The transcript does not give Sebastian's last name.

- **≈Mar 22 2025:** Val cited "Sebastian, the technician that's coming in" as his salary benchmark while sizing Julian Puerta's planned US employment (figures held §15.8) — indicating Sebastian was being brought in/employed by JVL US around this time (same Sebastian by context: Colombian JVL technician in the US; recorded as an inference). [[raw/meeting_with_julian_puerta_texas_eds.md]]

- **Apr 3 2025 — SD printer troubleshooting:** on the Epic-printer call ([[wiki/meetings/2025-04-03-sd-printer-and-mh1-settings.md]]) Sebastian was "on the phone" with Lonnie troubleshooting an **Epic 950** that wouldn't power up in the South Dakota lab (the other units worked). See [[wiki/projects/south-dakota-vlt-market.md]]. [[raw/tech_and_settings_issues-juan_martinez-april-3-2025.md]]

## Apr 28 2025 — Business Central order role; ex-Colombia engineer

On the BC status call ([[wiki/meetings/2025-04-28-business-central-labs-tariffs-status.md]]) Val placed Sebastian as one of three engineers who place orders — he should **place purchase orders** (internal, to JVL Labs/[[wiki/people/arkady-batson.md|Arkady]]), not sales orders. Val confirmed his background: a **full-time engineering technician** in Florida who **used to work for JVL's Colombian entity as a field-service engineer** ("he knows everything inside out"), speaks Spanish, and is currently light on installations.

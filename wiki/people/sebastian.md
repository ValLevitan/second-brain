# Sebastian

Sebastian is a JVL field-service technician who installs and services machines in the field. He is based in **Florida** and originally from **Colombia**. [[raw/Alignment_outstanding_subjects_2025-02-19.md]]

## Role

- On the Feb 19 alignment call, Val and Juan discussed **sending Sebastian to South Dakota** to service the lab machine — Val stressed at length how hostile the winter trip is (Florida/Colombia climate, a one-day journey via Miami→Dallas→Sioux Falls plus a 3-hour drive) so the Minsk team appreciates the effort involved. His trip would replace the CPU board/assembly on the frozen machine and swap the Gen 5 printer for an Epic 950 for Lonnie to test. See [[wiki/projects/south-dakota-vlt-market.md]]. [[raw/Alignment_outstanding_subjects_2025-02-19.md]]
- **Field verification:** Sebastian confirmed the machine's fan was blowing inwards (correct from the factory) during the mystery-freeze investigation. [[raw/Alignment_outstanding_subjects_2025-02-19.md]]
- On the **Florida CPU-kit** machine, Sebastian made a setup mistake with **show mode** (it was left checked), and he had to connect dual monitors before selecting single-monitor to reproduce the customer's desired single-screen look. [[raw/Alignment_outstanding_subjects_2025-02-19.md]]

The transcript does not give Sebastian's last name.

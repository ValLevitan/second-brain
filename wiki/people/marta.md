# Marta (Automated Technologies)

Marta (also transcribed "Martha") is a JVL-connected businesswoman in Colombia. She "has a company, her own company that she has had for many years **outside of JVL**" called **Automated Technologies**, which "distributes bill acceptors" — the phrasing implying her main work is *with* JVL. Val calls her "very, very competitive, professional … in everything she does." [[raw/meeting_with_julian_puerta_texas_eds.md]]

# Stephen Fenoglio

An Austin, Texas gaming lawyer — outside counsel to the Texas amusement and music operators' trade association (**AMOT**) and one of roughly fifteen outside counsel to [[wiki/organizations/pace-o-matic.md|Pace-O-Matic]]. He is the source of the clearest account in the corpus of how Texas law treats skill games, and the route by which JVL got its first Texas operator introductions. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Stephen_Fenoglio_Texas_Skill_Gaming_Counsel-June-10-2026.md]]

**Name resolution.** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] and the transcripts call him "Steven" and "Steve"; he is written *Fenolio* once in his own quoted speech and appears in the [[wiki/meetings/2026-06-05-amot-texas-market-scouting.md|Jun-5 2026 AMOT report-back]] only as an unidentified Texas attorney rendered "Steve Brunoglio" / "Stephen Pinoglu" [[raw/Meeting_Notes_AMOT_Texas_Market_Scouting-June-5-2026.md]]. The [[wiki/meetings/2026-06-10-stephen-fenoglio-texas-gaming-counsel.md|Jun-10 call]] supplies the correct name, and the [[wiki/meetings/2026-06-23-texas-operators-reach-out.md|Jun-23 operator call]] confirms it — Val there says "I spoke to Stephen about it. I asked him to look at my machine. He says he's got conflict of interest." All garbles are recorded as aliases only. ## Background, in his own account (Jun 10 2026)

- **About thirty-one years in the gaming space; licensed forty-one years.** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Stephen_Fenoglio_Texas_Skill_Gaming_Counsel-June-10-2026.md]]
- **Outside counsel to the Texas amusement and music operators' trade association since about 1995–96** — he names it "the trade association, amusement music operators. Texas" and later calls AMOT "the primary trade association". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Stephen_Fenoglio_Texas_Skill_Gaming_Counsel-June-10-2026.md]]
- **He works with the lobbying team on legislation nearly every session but is not a registered lobbyist.** His own term is "near lobbying": "draft bills and amendments, craft arguments, negotiate language, et cetera." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Stephen_Fenoglio_Texas_Skill_Gaming_Counsel-June-10-2026.md]]
- **Designated an expert witness in gaming law by two different Texas district courts.** His self-assessment: "I guess I am considered one of the experts on gaming law in Texas." He also handles some **oil, gas and utility** matters. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Stephen_Fenoglio_Texas_Skill_Gaming_Counsel-June-10-2026.md]]
- **He represents a number of operators throughout the state** and offered to introduce Val to the **president of AMOT** and to good operators he represents. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Stephen_Fenoglio_Texas_Skill_Gaming_Counsel-June-10-2026.md]]

*(The [[wiki/projects/texas-used-machine-distribution.md|Texas project page]]'s Jun-12 2026 entry refers to "the attorney who runs the Texas association", whom Val was meeting that Sunday evening. On this source he is AMOT's **outside counsel**, not its head — the two descriptions probably point at the same man, but that identification is an inference, not stated in either source.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Stephen_Fenoglio_Texas_Skill_Gaming_Counsel-June-10-2026.md]]

## What he told Val about Texas law

The full market read — the pure-skill test, the county patchwork, market size and the two manufacturer business models — is on [[wiki/projects/texas-used-machine-distribution.md]] (Jun-10). The two lines that matter most:

- **Texas is a pure-skill state, not a predominant-skill state.** "Texas is a pure skill game state, not predominant skill, so it's a little different." He knew the Nebraska framework Val offered as JVL's analogue and drew the distinction himself. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Stephen_Fenoglio_Texas_Skill_Gaming_Counsel-June-10-2026.md]]
- **The test he can send an opinion on:** "if the skillful player wins each and every time more than it costs to play, that's not a gambling device." And its hard edge: "if it's ninety nine percent skill, but one percent chance in determining the outcome in Texas, that would be considered an illegal gambling device." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Stephen_Fenoglio_Texas_Skill_Gaming_Counsel-June-10-2026.md]]

## The Pace-O-Matic conflict — what he will and will not do for JVL

- **He is one of "probably fifteen lawyers who are on outside council" for [[wiki/organizations/pace-o-matic.md|Pace-O-Matic]]** (transcribed "Panthematic"; the derived notes read it as Pace-O-Matic and Fenoglio says "Pace" plainly later in the same passage). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Stephen_Fenoglio_Texas_Skill_Gaming_Counsel-June-10-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Stephen_Fenoglio_Texas_Skill_Gaming_Counsel-June-10-2026.md]]
- **No written opinion for another manufacturer.** "Because I represent Pace, I cannot give a binding legal opinion for any other company. I do look at games on behalf of operators and will make an observation, but I can't put it in writing because I have a conflict of interest in representing Pace." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Stephen_Fenoglio_Texas_Skill_Gaming_Counsel-June-10-2026.md]]
- **What he will do — a verbal read, same content, through an operator or direct.** Val: "So we can come to you through an operator." Fenoglio: "I don't mind meeting with you and will give you the same opinion I would give the operator, but I am not going to put anything in writing." What that read sounds like: "I'll give you a look. I think these features are good and I would definitely not have this feature." Recorded as a decision: [[wiki/decisions/2026-06-10-texas-legal-read-comes-through-an-operator-never-in-writing.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Stephen_Fenoglio_Texas_Skill_Gaming_Counsel-June-10-2026.md]]

## How he advises operators

- **Avoid the bad counties rather than fight them.** "So you have a patchwork; there are certain counties where you should not operate, unless you want a criminal case." His advice to operators and to Pace is to stay out of them — "There is plenty of fertile ground… you should be placing your games in and not have to spend fifty to one hundred thousand dollars on a criminal case." "And some people take the advice and some don't." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Stephen_Fenoglio_Texas_Skill_Gaming_Counsel-June-10-2026.md]]
- **He sorts operators into two kinds.** "There are really good operators, multi-generational like you are that are not going to take an unnecessary risk." And the others, whom he has also represented: they "will just operate as long as we can if we get popped", relying on him afterwards — to whom he says "Well, just make sure you got plenty of money." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Stephen_Fenoglio_Texas_Skill_Gaming_Counsel-June-10-2026.md]]

**§15.8:** clear. No fee, rate or retainer of his is stated anywhere in either source, and none is recorded here. The "fifty to one hundred thousand dollars" figure is his estimate of what defending a criminal case costs an **operator** — a market cost, not his compensation. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Stephen_Fenoglio_Texas_Skill_Gaming_Counsel-June-10-2026.md]]

He told Val he takes a summer sabbatical: **"I'll be leaving July eighth and will not be returning till the end of September"**, out of Austin and "in the mountains". That set the deadline on any Austin visit. He also checked flights during the call and reported **one Southwest Airlines nonstop from Miami to Austin**, adding that he did not know about other airlines; Val believed there was a nonstop from Toronto but not from Miami.

# Igor (industrial designer)

Igor is JVL's **industrial / product designer** for the Flex machine cabinets — the person building and finishing the 3D cabinet renderings and proposing how the plastics, panels, and monitor mounts look. On the Feb 27 2025 Flex V42/C design review, Val directed that the team "let Igor finish the design," discuss the speaker panel with him, and "let our industrial designer just play with it a little bit."

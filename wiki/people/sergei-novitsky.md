# Sergei Novitsky

Sergei Novitsky is a JVL **field/hardware technician** who sets up and troubleshoots the slot machines by hand. [[raw/flex_port_hardware_update-may-7-2025.md]]

- On the May 7 2025 FlexPort call he was the one who **got two prepared slot machines running** after others could not: over the prior days [[wiki/people/evgeniy-alekseev.md|Alekseev]] (Saturday, with [[wiki/people/dmitri-chudinov.md|Dima Chudinov]]) and Novitsky had spent roughly 20 work-hours failing to bring them up; Novitsky finally raised them into games on the Tuesday. His written diagnosis (read aloud by Irina): "Плохой валидатор. Принтер видит и даже ругается, что нет бумаги. Провода Харнесса убиты. Я там подергал-поерзал, вроде заработало. Машины поднялись в игры." (bad validator, printer complained of no paper, harness wires dead — he jiggled them and it worked). [[raw/flex_port_hardware_update-may-7-2025.md]]
- He confirmed on the call that the two slot machines run games on the **Georgia 1.14** software, with the bill acceptor, printer and SAS host checked; he had **not** connected them to FlexPay (not in his task). [[raw/flex_port_hardware_update-may-7-2025.md]]
- Val floated sending him to **Miami** to set up a machine after Toronto (Val then said it was a joke). [[raw/flex_port_hardware_update-may-7-2025.md]]

Val's take: Novitsky "с опытом знает, как с машинами работать" — the experienced pair of hands who could do in ~10 minutes what the others couldn't. [[raw/flex_port_hardware_update-may-7-2025.md]]

- **May 8 2025:** on the next-day TITO standup he undertook to perform the **FlexPay software update to version 154** ("Сергей Новицкий обещал помочь, сделает это") so [[wiki/people/eugine-yavorski.md|Yavorski]] could work with the latest TITO commands. See [[wiki/topics/flexpay-mobile-app-and-service-advocates.md]]. [[raw/flex_pay_port_hardware_update-may-8-2025.md]]

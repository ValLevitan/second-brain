# Hector

Hector is a member of JVL's **finance back-office** whom Val wants narrowed down to a pure **controllership** role — "controlling responsibilities, nothing else." He is described as **detailed and meticulous** at reviewing vendor bills. [[raw/financial_alka_alignment-march-24-2025.md]] Managed via [[wiki/people/alka-sood.md|Alka Sood]]; see [[wiki/topics/jvl-finance-back-office.md]].

## Role (Mar 24 2025)

- Val wants Hector to move his **vendor-bill / vendor-review activity to Mary** and keep only controlling functions. The four tasks still to transfer to Mary: **preparing the schedule of vendor payments; matching vendor bills to POs; monitoring system reports matching POs to packing slips; and following up on discrepancies between vendor quotes and invoices.** [[raw/financial_alka_alignment-march-24-2025.md]]
- The controlling work Val wants to keep with Hector: **weekly cash-flow forecast**, monthly financial reports (Jan/Feb/Mar), **systematic balance-sheet reconciliation**, bank-account reconciliation, ledger accuracy, and regulatory-filing preparation. Val wants a fixed **wish-list of reports on set days** as the way to hold him accountable. [[raw/financial_alka_alignment-march-24-2025.md]]
- **System access:** Val wants Hector limited to only what he needs, but for audit/controlling he needs review access across **finance, customer invoicing, banking and procurement** (read vs write distinction discussed). [[raw/financial_alka_alignment-march-24-2025.md]]
- The task transfer had been stalled because Hector "felt exposed with Igor leaving"; once Igor was gone Val pushed the handover. (Which Igor is unresolved — see Owner Decisions.) [[raw/financial_alka_alignment-march-24-2025.md]]

## Apr 28 2025 — removed from creating purchase orders (segregation of duties)

On the BC status call ([[wiki/meetings/2025-04-28-business-central-labs-tariffs-status.md]]) Val ruled Hector should be **removed from creating purchase orders** in both Systems and Labs — "he is the controlling arm for me… his job is only to ensure that there is a match between all the control documents." A PO creates a liability, so it belongs to the requesters ([[wiki/people/mary.md|Mary]] for non-BOM; Sebastian/Elizabeth internally), not the controller. Reinforces the Mar-24 controllership-only scope. See [[wiki/topics/business-central-erp-implementation.md]]. [[raw/business_central_update-apr-28-2025.md]]

## May 7 2025 — Val moves to replace him with a Miami fractional controller

On the recurring finance call ([[wiki/meetings/2025-05-07-finance-recurring-alka-stabilization.md]]) Val told [[wiki/people/alka-sood.md|Alka]] plainly he does not believe JVL can keep working with Hector: he needs a controller **physically near him**, and Hector is "too far" (remote) and "lacking North American polish" — "don't try to change an individual … we need to replace them." He gave Hector a two-month chance (one month elapsed, "the needle" hadn't moved). The replacement should be a **Miami-based fractional controller** (near where Val spends most time; manufacturing stays in Canada with quarterly visits) — experienced, formally educated, "at the sunset of their career," ~1,000 hours/year. Alka to write a job profile and begin interviewing. In the interim Hector keeps the controllership work: he is to build the five-category weekly cash-flow template and **validate the monthly balance-sheet reconciliations** (Laura does the legwork). See [[wiki/topics/jvl-finance-back-office.md]]. [[raw/financial_review-may-7-2025.md]]

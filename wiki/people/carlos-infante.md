# Carlos Infante

Carlos Infante is a **gaming lawyer at [[wiki/organizations/estrella.md|Estrella]]**, the Puerto Rico law firm JVL interviewed on Apr 10 2025 for its Puerto Rico entry ([[wiki/meetings/2025-04-10-estrella-law-firm-carlos-infante.md]]). [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan]] and [[wiki/people/igor-nazarenka.md|Igor]] "spoke very highly" of him and had given him JVL's background before the call. [[raw/estrella_law_carlos_infante_puerto_rico-april-10-2025.md]]

## What he told JVL (Apr 10 2025)

- **Gaming Commission state:** the gaming law was amended at the end of the previous year but the machinery was in flux; the new government team "were just approved when Juan and Igor were visiting Puerto Rico," so there is now "a ratified and an actual nominated and confirmed head of the gaming commission," whose priority is to **increase the number of in-route slot machines allowed** — including publishing a list of licensed clients (addressing Val's complaint that PR customers are "in hiding"). The agency's working team survived the government change, ending the prior four-years pattern of the commission team changing "like four times." [[raw/estrella_law_carlos_infante_puerto_rico-april-10-2025.md]]
- **Temporary licence:** his firm has gotten temporary licences from the same regulator before — in **sports betting**, not yet slot machines — and expects the Commission is open to one for JVL's market test, though "it will take some negotiating." Even with a temporary licence, the full legal/background licensing process still runs; the temporary path just skips waiting for the final compliance/technology steps. **Machine manufacturers/importers/sellers need their own licence in PR** (separate from operators' licences), and a temporary licence is likely needed even to clear machines from customs into the market legitimately. [[raw/estrella_law_carlos_infante_puerto_rico-april-10-2025.md]]
- **Tax pitch:** PR is US legal system but "considered a foreign tax jurisdiction" — no federal income tax; territory income tax "about 30%" reducible to **4% via decrees**; property-tax and capital-gains incentives; US tariff regime applies (incl. against China). [[raw/estrella_law_carlos_infante_puerto_rico-april-10-2025.md]]
- **Committed:** engagement letter (via NINTEX) + a written temporary-licence cost/time estimate to Val **by Monday** (~Apr 14). [[raw/estrella_law_carlos_infante_puerto_rico-april-10-2025.md]]

## Engagement negotiation (May 5 2025 — [[wiki/meetings/2025-05-05-estrella-engagement-agreement-negotiation.md]])

- On the follow-up call Carlos took Val's ~20 requested changes to Estrella's draft engagement agreement, calling most of them reasonable or already Estrella's standard practice, and agreed to counter after JVL's [[wiki/people/monica.md|Monica]] grooms the marked-up document. He works with his partner **Jose**. [[raw/estrella_engagement_agreement_negotiations-may-5-2025.md]]
- Confirmed the fee structure: a **flat fee** for obtaining the licences, **hourly** for additional work, and a **flat fee** for the corporate-structure creation. He noted Estrella's "**sundown rule**" (respond before the next sunrise). [[raw/estrella_engagement_agreement_negotiations-may-5-2025.md]]
- Will attend the **SBC Summit Americas** in Miami (May 13–16) and invited any JVL attendee to meet in person; Val flies home to Toronto on May 13. [[raw/estrella_engagement_agreement_negotiations-may-5-2025.md]]

# Lucie (customs / international-trade advisor)

Lucie (rendered "Lucy" in the transcript) is a **customs / international-trade specialist** introduced to [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] on **June 11 2025** — brought in by the lawyer [[wiki/people/alan.md|Alan]] — to advise on JVL's **Canadian customs (CBSA) tariff classification** problem on China-origin cabinet metal. Surname and firm are **not stated** in the source; a colleague named **Lydia** is referenced as able to give "more of the particulars." [[raw/tariffs_lucie_alan_intro-june-11-2025.md]]

## The matter she was engaged on (June 11 2025)

- Alan had already explained JVL's products to her; she said that to study the classification JVL must give **precise product descriptions** — classification "is determined by what their primary use is," with rules about general-use vs specific-use goods. [[raw/tariffs_lucie_alan_intro-june-11-2025.md]]
- The core issue Val put to her: **formed, painted sheet-metal cabinet parts imported from China** are being taxed at the **25% steel/aluminum tariff**, when Val argues they should fall under the **slot-machine-component subcategory** (which is tariff-exempt) because a bent, hole-punched cabinet part "cannot be repurposed for anything else." Full substance on [[wiki/topics/tariffs-and-customs-compliance.md]]. [[raw/tariffs_lucie_alan_intro-june-11-2025.md]]
- Her firm appears to have **both Canada and US reach** — Alan noted Canadian customs decisions are not public (unlike the US), so they sometimes look at how the US treated a matter under the **harmonized system**, and "reach out to our U.S. colleague." She was left to decide whether US-side people are needed. [[raw/tariffs_lucie_alan_intro-june-11-2025.md]]
- Immediate asks: Val to send her the **HS classification number currently used** and product descriptions; she and Alan to look at whether JVL can also pursue a **duty-drawback / transitional export waiver**, mindful of **new (April 2025) CBSA rules for release of goods before payment of customs**. JVL has a **CBSA reply due June 16** — the fallback is to tell CBSA that JVL "retained lawyers" and request an extension. [[raw/tariffs_lucie_alan_intro-june-11-2025.md]]

## June 18 2025 — brought in her US colleague Bruce Halen

- On the [[wiki/meetings/2025-06-18-tariff-bruce-halen-us-customs.md|June-18 tariff call]] Lucie brought in her US colleague [[wiki/people/bruce-halen.md|Bruce Halen]] (the firm's Detroit customs specialist) to analyze the US-side treatment of JVL machines — confirming her firm's cross-border reach flagged on June 11. Bruce led the substance (HS 9504, Section 301/Geneva/IEEPA tariffs, substantial-transformation origin). See [[wiki/topics/tariffs-and-customs-compliance.md]]. [[raw/tariff-June-18-2025.md]]

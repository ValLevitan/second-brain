# Vishal Patel

**Vishal Patel** heads **[[wiki/organizations/narayani-amusements.md|Narayani Amusements]]**, a large **Georgia coin-operated-amusement-machine** route, and is a former high-volume JVL customer, described by [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda]] as "a younger fellow." He is the subject of the six-minute car briefing Felipe gave [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] on the way to a customer visit on **August 26 2026** ([[wiki/meetings/2026-08-26-vishal-patel-pre-meeting-briefing.md]]), and then the counterparty in the visit itself ([[wiki/meetings/2026-08-26-narayani-amusements-trade-in-negotiation.md]]). [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Vishal_Patel_Pre_Meeting_Briefing-August-26-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Vishal_Patel_Pre_Meeting_Briefing-August-26-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]

The "History with JVL" and "business model" sections below are **Felipe's account as of Aug 26 2026**, given to brief Val before the meeting; that part is a single source and Felipe repeatedly flags his own uncertainty about it. The Aug-26 1:00 PM sections further down come from the meeting itself.

## History with JVL

- **He was once a very large buyer.** "They bought many cabinets from us. I think this must have been twenty three twenty four… he started buying several cabinets at a time, ten at a time, twenty at a time. So it was it was a very, very good customer of ours." The **2023–24 dating is Felipe's own hedged recollection** ("I think this must have been"). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Vishal_Patel_Pre_Meeting_Briefing-August-26-2026.md]]
- **How the relationship started.** Felipe reached him through JVL's technician network: "I got his contact or I was able to do business with him through one of our tech. Tech friends, **Podpunk**. Uh, Other one I told you **Dave** when he brought him over, and he introduced me and everything." "Podpunk" is **unclear** — it is not a name that appears anywhere else in the corpus and is very likely a speech-to-text garble; it is **not** resolved here. The derived notes compress the same passage to "introduced through a tech friend (via Dave)". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Vishal_Patel_Pre_Meeting_Briefing-August-26-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Vishal_Patel_Pre_Meeting_Briefing-August-26-2026.md]]
- **He has not bought in a long while.** "he hasn't purchased in a while." He still holds JVL equipment: "he likes your product, but I understand they have some JVLs in the warehouse. That's the last time. I heard." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Vishal_Patel_Pre_Meeting_Briefing-August-26-2026.md]]
- **Service, not sales, is what keeps the account alive.** The most recent interaction was a repair batch: "They actually brought last time? I think ten cabinets for repair. We repaired them in house, got them all ready and then they picked them up. We upgraded all all the software that they bring." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Vishal_Patel_Pre_Meeting_Briefing-August-26-2026.md]]

## The business model Felipe reads — buy small routes, grow them, sell them on

Felipe is explicit that he does **not** fully understand it: "However I don't I don't under clearly understand what his business model is." His working read: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Vishal_Patel_Pre_Meeting_Briefing-August-26-2026.md]]

- "Wants to grab locations, make them a bit profitable and sell… get them small, make them grow and then sell them **four times the price**. You know with machines included."
- The buyers he names are the market's consolidators: "a lot of the cabinets that I have sold to him, I have reconfigured or upgraded or changed or fixed **for United Gaming**. **United has bought a lot of his routes**, uh **[[wiki/organizations/diamond-amusement.md|Diamond Amusement]]** and others. He's sold a lot, a lot of his locations."
- **Consequence for JVL's own records:** because the cabinets keep changing hands with the locations, "It's been very hard for us to know what date still keep. From what they originally bought from us" — i.e. JVL cannot reliably tell which of the machines Vishal originally bought are still his. (The sentence is garbled in the transcript; the sense — that JVL cannot track what he still holds — is clear, the exact wording is not.) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Vishal_Patel_Pre_Meeting_Briefing-August-26-2026.md]]

**Why this matters beyond one account (inference, labelled as such):** the same dynamic explains a recurring line of JVL work — reconfiguring and upgrading cabinets JVL originally sold to one operator so they can run for whoever bought that operator's route. Felipe states the pattern for this account; the generalisation is mine.

[[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Vishal_Patel_Pre_Meeting_Briefing-August-26-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Vishal_Patel_Pre_Meeting_Briefing-August-26-2026.md]]
- **Padma**, his former technician, has left: "Now he's Padma is no longer working with him. He's working, New text, and I think I think he's a not a fixed technician, but they have one of these tech service companies" — i.e. a **third-party tech service firm** now covers him rather than an in-house tech. The firm is **not named** in the source. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Vishal_Patel_Pre_Meeting_Briefing-August-26-2026.md]]

## How the Aug-26 meeting was set up

Felipe's pitch to get the appointment: "I spoke to [Vishal] right now and I said you know. **The owner of JVL is here.** There's some very interesting products I want to talk to you about… **Games, but transactional products.** Do you have a moment to meet us? And then he said yes." So the visit was framed from the start as a **transactional-products** call (the Flex line) as much as a games call. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Vishal_Patel_Pre_Meeting_Briefing-August-26-2026.md]]

Felipe also navigated to the location by **Lucky Bucks** as a landmark — "You know what we'll just go to. Lucky Box and and then we'll take it from there. So it's very close to Lucky Box." ("Lucky Box"/"Lucky Bugs" are the transcript's usual garbles of **Lucky Bucks**). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Vishal_Patel_Pre_Meeting_Briefing-August-26-2026.md]]

## Aug 26 2026, 1:00 PM — the meeting itself

The visit the briefing was preparing for. Full account across [[wiki/organizations/narayani-amusements.md]] and [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-26-narayani-three-machine-d27-trade-in-pilot.md]]; what belongs to him personally: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]

**Question settled: he heads Narayani Amusements.** The briefing left open whether he owns or runs Narayani, because neither briefing source said so. The negotiation's derived notes name the attendee "**Vishal Patel (Narayani Amusements)**", give the meeting location as "**Narayani Amusement (customer's office)**" and title it "**Vishal Patel's office**" — and in the recording he answers for the whole route in the first person ("we have around nine hundred machines on field"), sets the terms, and closes the deal, with colleagues present who defer to him. Treated as settled. **One precision kept:** no source in this batch uses the word *owner* of him in relation to Narayani, and his own word for the business is consistently "we". [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]

- **He is the decision-maker, and he negotiates from the location's point of view.** His stated case against buying JVL was not price but demand: "every location that we have they ask for aristocrats… **So nobody's really asking for [JVL] right now**," and "at the end of the day, It's the location who who asks we have to give them what they ask for." He is also willing to say the opposite in the same breath — "**I would love to buy JVLs**" — which is what Val worked with.
- **He took Val's contradiction without taking offence.** Val: "Would you like me to agree with you or disagree with you? … I think that you're wrong. My opinion. You could kick me out of here. It's your place." He did not.
- **He declined to write his own deal, then anchored high anyway.** Offered the pen — "You write the deal. I will honor the deal that you will write" — he deflected ("I don't want you to lose money either"), then asked **$7,000** per traded-in D27 against Val's $2,000 opening, prefacing it with "See, don't let me do this."
- **He tried to convert the pilot into a route-wide deal on the spot**, arguing JVL should want it as marketing: "If I swap everything… I'm gonna install it in all of my locations… You will have more visibility in the market with the new game… think of this as a marketing strategy for your place." Val's answer was the transactional-equipment condition, and Vishal's answer to that was "**Let me come look at it first.**"
- **He accepted the three-year data contract without argument** — "Fine" — and repeated it unprompted: "I'll sign up for the three year contract with you if you want. It's not a problem." See [[wiki/topics/machine-data-repurchase-program.md]].
- **He wants the big manufacturers checked.** "Right now, they're trying to monopolize the market so badly… I was getting all the big masters together. I'm like, Let's make a syndicate together and show these guys that they cannot dominate the market."
- Cell numbers were exchanged at the end, and he agreed to the **Sep 1, 1:00 PM** JVL-office demo. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]

# Pavel (Ukrainian outsourcing-studio owner)

Pavel is the **owner of a Ukrainian game-development / outsourcing studio** and a long-time contact of [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] — they have known each other **more than 10 years** and once worked together on a mobile game. Andrei uses him as a market source on the outsourcing/RGS business. He is an **external party, not a JVL employee**, and is distinct from [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]] (the JVL Minsk engineer). [[raw/andrei_klimovich_update_report_online_strategy1_2025-03-29.md]] [[raw/online_strategy_brainstorm_with_andrei_klimovich_decembe_2025-03-29.md]]

**Identity note (inference):** the Dec-2024 brainstorm names "Pavel" and ties him to the studio (Andrei helped him seek investment ~2 years before joining JVL and kept his materials). The Nov-24 report's unnamed "friend in Ukraine… the owner… known 10+ years" is inferred to be the same Pavel. [[raw/online_strategy_brainstorm_with_andrei_klimovich_decembe_2025-03-29.md]]

## His studio's business model (as reported by Andrei)

- Sells games on a **flat-fee** basis (roughly **$15k–$25k** a game; a MegaWays-style clone ~$25k, a simpler slot ~$15k) with **no revenue share** — the operator owns the game outright. [[raw/andrei_klimovich_update_report_online_strategy1_2025-03-29.md]]
- Historically also charged a **fixed ~$500/game/month RGS-operation fee**. Over the ~6 months before the Dec-2024 talk, operators stopped agreeing to pay RGS fees, so Pavel now **bundles RGS into the game price** and deploys the RGS on the **operator's own server** (the operator pays hosting) rather than billing for it. [[raw/online_strategy_brainstorm_with_andrei_klimovich_decembe_2025-03-29.md]]
- Once his games are in an operator's collection, he later gets **direct integration** and earns a **~7%** share on his own titles (no aggregator cut). [[raw/andrei_klimovich_update_report_online_strategy1_2025-03-29.md]]
- He shares this information openly because Andrei says their business models differ and he does not see JVL as a direct competitor. [[raw/andrei_klimovich_update_report_online_strategy1_2025-03-29.md]]

## Cost model shared with Andrei

- Andrei estimated Pavel's **net labour cost of a ~$25k game at roughly $9,000** (about **830 hours** across all roles), implying a margin near **~67%**. [[raw/online_strategy_brainstorm_with_andrei_klimovich_decembe_2025-03-29.md]]
- **§15 held:** Andrei also had a ~2-year-old **list of Pavel's staff and their individual salaries** (by role). Those per-role compensation figures are **not ingested** (HR/compensation material, §15.8) — flagged for owner review. [[raw/online_strategy_brainstorm_with_andrei_klimovich_decembe_2025-03-29.md]]

# Mark (JVL US state-tax advisor)

An external **US tax advisor** at JVL's US accounting firm who led the May 27 2025 review of JVL Labs' Georgia state-tax position ([[wiki/meetings/2025-05-27-georgia-tax-review-jvl-labs.md]]). Surname not stated. [[raw/finance_tax_review_for_2023_2024-on_may-27-2025.md]]

- Owns the **Georgia nexus / apportionment** analysis: explained market-based income sourcing, the pro-forma federal return → per-state apportionment, and the **voluntary disclosure agreement** (three-year lookback, penalties waived but interest due). [[raw/finance_tax_review_for_2023_2024-on_may-27-2025.md]]
- Ran the "back-of-the-envelope" Georgia tax math (~5.19% effective; ~$160k for 2023, similar for 2024) and confirmed the foreign-tax credit is "pretty close to a dollar-for-dollar credit" in Canada — but deferred Canadian-side advice to [[wiki/people/sean-riley.md|Sean Riley]]. [[raw/finance_tax_review_for_2023_2024-on_may-27-2025.md]]
- Works with **Ashley** ([[wiki/people/ashley-tax-advisor.md]]) at the same firm; the prior-year Georgia question had been discussed with "Daniel," who then "went missing." [[raw/finance_tax_review_for_2023_2024-on_may-27-2025.md]]

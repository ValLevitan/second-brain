# John Houseal

John Houseal is **InComm's product-development lead for route gaming** and the InComm executive who leads the relationship with JVL (the gift-card / COAM prize-redemption payment products). Surname confirmed on the Apr-3 2025 "JVL Payment Options" recording; the July 1–2 2025 in-person meeting fills in his background and remit. [[raw/incomm_transcript_gacoam_payment_options-april-4-2025.md]] [[raw/incomm_product_discussion-july-1-2025-1.md]]

## Background (as told July 1 2025)

- Not an engineer — **degree in accounting**, never practised; early career in technology at research universities and at **Deloitte** (helping universities maximise their federal-research indirect-cost rate, which he automated with software). [[raw/incomm_product_discussion-july-1-2025-1.md]]
- Joined **InComm ~six years ago (≈2019)** when InComm bought the startup he was an executive at — a "miserably failing" lottery-innovation startup (selling lottery at ATMs, gas stations, gift cards) that burned ~**$110M in eight years**, funded by ~10 of the richest families in America, and had been partnered with **Blackhawk** (InComm's chief rival — "like the Hatfields and the McCoys"). InComm outbid Blackhawk 5× to buy it. [[raw/incomm_product_discussion-july-1-2025-1.md]]
- At InComm his official title is **product development**, but he oversees the **Vanilla Direct** US cash network (~100,000 retail locations, 3–5 transactions/second, routing cash into banks/wallets/sportsbooks — ~70–80% of his work), so he negotiates with Visa/MasterCard/banks/retailers on licensed money movement. His specialty is "building new products for new markets and finding ways for InComm to make more money." [[raw/incomm_product_discussion-july-1-2025-1.md]]
- Got into route gaming ~four years ago when **Nick Papadopoulos** pulled him into the Georgia gift-card work; learned the market working with the largest masters (Excel, United, Intermax, J&J, Magnolia, Eagle Global). [[raw/incomm_product_discussion-july-1-2025-1.md]]

## Role in the JVL relationship

- Leads the InComm product/market side across the Feb–Jun 2025 calls and the July in-person session; on Jun-20 Val flagged him as the person to ask on failure-mode design "since InComm has seen many markets and isn't a competitor." See [[wiki/organizations/incom.md]] and [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]]. [[raw/review_flex-POS_use_cases.md]] [[raw/incomm_jvl_alignment-june-23-2025.md]]
- Confirmed InComm is JVL's **partner, not competitor**, but that the products JVL needs (SkillPay / card-back-into-play) genuinely aren't finalised yet. [[raw/incomm_jvl_alignment-june-23-2025.md]]
- **Aug 6 2025 Prize-Card confirmations:** in reply to a call from Val, John confirmed **all Prize Cards will be barcoded by year-end** and are **"not rechargeable for now"** — the two answers that let JVL drop the Flex-Port swipe reader and keep e-Wallet as the cash-out route. See [[wiki/organizations/incom.md]] and [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]]. [[raw/Flex-Port-use_case-August-6-2025.md]]

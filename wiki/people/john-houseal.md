# John Houseal

John Houseal is **InComm's product-development lead for route gaming** and the InComm executive who leads the relationship with JVL (the gift-card / COAM prize-redemption payment products). Surname confirmed on the Apr-3 2025 "JVL Payment Options" recording; the July 1–2 2025 in-person meeting fills in his background and remit. [[raw/incomm_transcript_gacoam_payment_options-april-4-2025.md]] [[raw/incomm_product_discussion-july-1-2025-1.md]]

**Current status — left InComm; Sept 25 2026 was his last day (first-hand).** On the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-25-incomm-john-exit-and-blue-card-hold.md|Sept-25 phone call]] with Val, John said: "**Today's my last day with InComm. I quit.**" He has a new job and is "staying in the industry", but "**I can't share anything now… In the next week, I can.**" The recording gives no surname; this page reads him as Houseal by context (inference; see Sept 25 below) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_John_Exit_and_Blue_Card_Hold-September-25-2026.md]]. Where he went is **not confirmed**: only [[wiki/people/billy-liu.md|Billy Liu]]'s second-hand Sept-22 account names **Bulldog** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Virtual_Wallet_Pay_Tag_Plan-September-22-2026.md]].

## Background (as told July 1 2025)

- Not an engineer — **degree in accounting**, never practised; early career in technology at research universities and at **Deloitte** (helping universities maximise their federal-research indirect-cost rate, which he automated with software). [[raw/incomm_product_discussion-july-1-2025-1.md]]
- Joined **InComm ~six years ago (≈2019)** when InComm bought the startup he was an executive at — a "miserably failing" lottery-innovation startup (selling lottery at ATMs, gas stations, gift cards) that burned ~**$110M in eight years**, funded by ~10 of the richest families in America, and had been partnered with **Blackhawk** (InComm's chief rival — "like the Hatfields and the McCoys"). InComm outbid Blackhawk 5× to buy it. [[raw/incomm_product_discussion-july-1-2025-1.md]]
- At InComm his official title is **product development**, but he oversees the **Vanilla Direct** US cash network (~100,000 retail locations, 3–5 transactions/second, routing cash into banks/wallets/sportsbooks — ~70–80% of his work), so he negotiates with Visa/MasterCard/banks/retailers on licensed money movement. His specialty is "building new products for new markets and finding ways for InComm to make more money." [[raw/incomm_product_discussion-july-1-2025-1.md]]
- Got into route gaming ~four years ago when **Nick Papadopoulos** pulled him into the Georgia gift-card work; learned the market working with the largest masters (Excel, United, Intermax, J&J, Magnolia, Eagle Global). [[raw/incomm_product_discussion-july-1-2025-1.md]]

## Role in the JVL relationship

- Leads the InComm product/market side across the Feb–Jun 2025 calls and the July in-person session; on Jun-20 Val flagged him as the person to ask on failure-mode design "since InComm has seen many markets and isn't a competitor." See [[wiki/organizations/incom.md]] and [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]]. [[raw/review_flex-POS_use_cases.md]] [[raw/incomm_jvl_alignment-june-23-2025.md]]
- Confirmed InComm is JVL's **partner, not competitor**, but that the products JVL needs (SkillPay / card-back-into-play) genuinely aren't finalised yet. [[raw/incomm_jvl_alignment-june-23-2025.md]]
- **Aug 6 2025 Prize-Card confirmations:** in reply to a call from Val, John confirmed **all Prize Cards will be barcoded by year-end** and are **"not rechargeable for now"** — the two answers that let JVL drop the Flex-Port swipe reader and keep e-Wallet as the cash-out route. See [[wiki/organizations/incom.md]] and [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]]. [[raw/Flex-Port-use_case-August-6-2025.md]]

## Jul 9 2026 — the ATM-registration introduction

On a short call, Val asked John how JVL could get **ATM registration** to launch its bill-recycler. John confirmed **InComm has no ATM registrations of its own and does not touch the money-services business (MSB)**, so it cannot sponsor JVL, and offered to introduce **Maya Fuentes** — an ATM Association of the US board member and a personal friend of John's, not an InComm employee — as the registration expert (she becomes JVL's [[wiki/organizations/herring-bank.md|Herring Bank]] cash-management contact the next day). See [[wiki/organizations/incom.md]] and [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]] (Jul-9). [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Bill_Recycler_ATM_Registration-July-9-2026.md]]

## Sept 10 2026 — Val names him, to the regulator, as the relationship that went political

Describing to [[wiki/organizations/georgia-lottery-corporation.md|Georgia Lottery]] staff how the InComm relationship had deteriorated, [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] identified where it started: "**my initial relationship was with John, John [Houseal], and that was a great relationship in the beginning. And now he is… too political, and it's just… weird.**" *(The transcript renders the surname "Hasil"; recorded as an alias.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]]

It sits in a longer complaint — "**Lately, very aggravated. It was… very different two years ago**" — and Val's account of a lunch with three InComm people that was "**extremely political**". The Lottery's only reply was to agree about the period, not about the person: "**weird is one way to describe it. We're definitely in… a uncomfortable period of time.**" Full context on [[wiki/organizations/incom.md]] (Sept 10). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Lottery_Class_A_Guidance_and_Prize_Card_Compliance-September-10-2026.md]]

*This is Val's characterisation, recorded as such — no InComm source in the corpus responds to it.*

## Sept 22 2026 — reported move to Bulldog, as told by a competitor

On the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-22-billy-liu-playpay-virtual-wallet-pay-tag-plan.md|Sept-22 Val–Billy call]], Val opened with "**How did you get the news about John?**" Billy's account [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Virtual_Wallet_Pay_Tag_Plan-September-22-2026.md]]:

- **Where he went:** Bulldog. Billy's reason came down to Bulldog being able to take him on; the pay side of that answer is withheld (§15.8). *(Bulldog is read as the Georgia operator Bulldog on [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]] — an inference; the call gives no full name.)* On what Bulldog offers him: "**Bulldog is big… Bulldog has many states… They have a close loop program. They can issue close loop cards. Retail cards.**" Val's own question — "**what is Bulldog's plan for John?**" — got no clear answer [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Virtual_Wallet_Pay_Tag_Plan-September-22-2026.md]].
- **His InComm role, as Billy put it:** "**John was the head of the Coin Card division. He was there since the pilot program.**" *(Both the division name and the pilot are Billy's; the corpus has no other source for them.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Virtual_Wallet_Pay_Tag_Plan-September-22-2026.md]]
- **He opposed the Skill Pay push.** "**when InComm made that announcement that they were going to go forward with the skill pay, John was irate**"; "**InComm just went against all logic, reason, and wanted to push it through**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Virtual_Wallet_Pay_Tag_Plan-September-22-2026.md]]. See [[wiki/organizations/incom.md]] (Sept 22).
- **He had proposed a tie-up with Billy's company:** Billy providing a virtual card and InComm a physical one — "**if we could join forces**" — which Billy read as "**a takeover tactic**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Virtual_Wallet_Pay_Tag_Plan-September-22-2026.md]].
- **Why this is read as Houseal:** an InComm "John" running its card business in Georgia since the pilot matches this page (inference). Billy never gives a surname [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Virtual_Wallet_Pay_Tag_Plan-September-22-2026.md]].

**§15.8 exclusion:** Billy's estimate of John's yearly worth, and the private job conversations between the two men, are **not recorded** [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Virtual_Wallet_Pay_Tag_Plan-September-22-2026.md]].

## Sept 25 2026 — his last day at InComm, told to Val directly

Val phoned him after hearing he had left. All from [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_John_Exit_and_Blue_Card_Hold-September-25-2026.md]] unless marked.

- **The role change.** "**Today's my last day with InComm. I quit. I resigned about a month ago. Month or two ago, August twenty-first, I think.**" He said he told InComm six months earlier that he was leaving "**and that they should start planning for the transition**", and that the focus since has been "**getting a team in place that can take over behind me**". (A name in that line, "Iron Jason", is garbled and not resolved.)
- **Where next: not said.** "**I have a new job**"; asked if he is staying in the industry: "**I am**". He could not share more yet. This first-hand account confirms the **exit**; it neither confirms nor denies Billy's **Bulldog** report of Sept 22 [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Virtual_Wallet_Pay_Tag_Plan-September-22-2026.md]].
- **His own account of his weight at InComm:** "**The thing that people don't know is that InComm got into this business largely because of me.**" His view of why the Blue Card is on hold is on [[wiki/organizations/incom.md]] (Sept 25).
- **No new product from him.** Asked if JVL should expect something: "**I don't have anything right now, Val… I'm trying to let InComm figure out what they're going to do with themselves.**"
- **The relationship stays open.** He keeps the same phone number, and Val proposed a coffee in Atlanta the week of October 12; John "should be around" but must check his travel.
- **Why this is read as Houseal (inference):** an InComm John who led its entry into this market and who discussed JVL's machine with Val two years ago matches this page; no surname is spoken.

**§15.8 exclusion:** the personal reason John gave for leaving concerns his pay and is **not recorded** here [[raw/Meeting_Notes_InComm_John_Exit_and_Blue_Card_Hold-September-25-2026.md]].

# Albertini

Albertini is a JVL **first-line support advocate** for the Georgia market. As of Sept 22 2025 he handles **60–70% of incoming support calls** — a shift from earlier, when calls routed through [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]]. [[raw/alignment_elctro-mechanical-september-22-2025.md]]

Val's characterization: technically capable, polite, "European style," but not very driven ("he doesn't overwork himself… but he's polite and will help you"). Val plans to move Felipe to escalation/last line and put Albertini onto more quotas through the ticketing/CRM tool he wants to implement (transcribed "roses" — name garbled), so first-line volume runs through Albertini with [[wiki/people/elizabeth.md|Elizabeth]] supporting the technical side.

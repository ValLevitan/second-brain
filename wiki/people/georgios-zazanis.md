# Georgios "Zaza" Zazanis

**COO of [[wiki/organizations/arc-gaming.md|ARC Gaming]]**, a Georgia COAM master operator. He met [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] for the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-31-arc-gaming-transactional-demo-class-a.md|Aug 31 2026 transactional demo]], a candid ~1h 41m session he closed by being thanked for exactly that — Val: "thank you for being sincere. I appreciate it." [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

The notes put him at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-27-arc-coffee-fleet-swap-and-transactional-pitch.md|Aug-27 coffee at ARC]] — the calendar attendee was "Georgios Zazanis" — and label him Speaker 3. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Coffee_Fleet_Swap_and_Transactional_Pitch-August-27-2026.md]] The recording never names him. What ARC said that morning is on [[wiki/organizations/arc-gaming.md]] (Aug 27). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Coffee_Fleet_Swap_and_Transactional_Pitch-August-27-2026.md]]

## Why "Zaza"

He explained the nickname himself: "My nick because George Zazanis, so everybody called me Zaza. There were so many George Zazanis… I [knew] like six Georges, George. They call me Zaza. **To distinguish between**." The Greek naming custom behind it — sons named for grandfathers — he also set out: "Continue the family name. Of course, my son… is the seventh or the eighth… generation that carries the name." This is recorded because it is the reason the name is ambiguous, not as biography. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

## What he controls, and what he does not

The single most useful thing this meeting establishes about him is the shape of his authority.

- **He runs the operation** — daily InComm reconciliation, ACH swipes, exposure thresholds, the decision whether to shut a non-paying location down, the field install backlog. All of that he describes in the first person. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **He does not control the kiosk contract**, and said so unprompted: "There are some relationships built, but it's **beyond my reach and my capabilities**." On being overruled: "if I say this sucks, the other guy says George. **It is what it is. Find the solution.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **He keeps looking anyway.** "it's not a secret. I never like that, so I always try to find an alternative. But this doesn't prevent me from looking for other options." Val's read, and warning: "I've been in business for many, many years, and when you say it is what it is, I understand what it means… Just don't lose your job, George. You ask too many questions." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **He is not the deal-maker on Class A either.** "with the current situation, I think it's better to not make this agreement with me. I'm not the guy. The guy is only the deal, not to make the deal." (Transcribed as written; the sense is that the agreement has to be struck above him.) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

On Oct 13 2026 he said it plainly: "**When I was at Intralot**", "**when I was in Intralot, I met him**", and "**I knew everybody from Intralot**". Val calls Christos, who joins the Nov 9 game, "**your ex-colleague**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]]. *(History: on Aug 31 the transcript had "when I used to work at Intelec", and whether "Intelec" was Intralot was left open. The Oct-13 source answers it: the reading as Intralot is now well supported.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **He joined ARC after the kiosk agreement was signed** and after the PAX agreement was made: "There is an agreement there. Very nice agreement. I made before I came, just when I joined the company," and on the Primero partnership, "I have no idea, by the way, why it was before me." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **He knows the competitive field selectively:** "Some of them very well. Some others I've never seen in my life." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

## How he reads the market

His market assessments are the substance JVL took away; they are recorded on [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]] (Aug 31 2026) and on [[wiki/people/sean-fellows.md]]. The through-line is that he ranks operators by **honesty and political access**, not by size — asked who JVL should build a relationship with, his first answer was simply "Somebody who's honest."

On how manufacturers game the industry reports, he was blunt and first to the point: "I guarantee you, this is wrong. There is no way. There is absolutely no way… I see it from our data." His own defence is placement discipline: "That's why we place them here one, here two, here three. So we don't put them all in the top location." Val's version of the same mechanism — a new manufacturer's small fleet sits in the best locations, so its average is flattering — is on [[wiki/topics/machine-data-repurchase-program.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

## Sept 8 2026 — his read of the Georgia transactional market, given as counsel

Val called him eight days later ([[wiki/meetings/2026-09-08-arc-gaming-bundle-sanity-check-and-david-sloan-intro.md|Sept 8, 11:09–11:18 AM]]) explicitly for judgement rather than business — "I also want to hear your opinion **because I consider that you're a smart man**" — and named his two worries: making "a **stupid mistake**" in a market he is new to, and "**giv[ing] something away, and just cheapen[ing] the brand**." Zazanis answered all of it in market terms. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Vertical_Screen_Bundle_Offer_and_David_Intro-September-8-2026.md]]

- **"There is always room for everything in this market."** His opening and his conclusion. On why now: "we saw that happened this day with **that false thing about the gift card, the ATM**. This, the other, **this is the right opportunity to put new stuff**… because **it's a mess**, right? I'm telling you, it's a mess. There's so many different things out there. **It's the right time to put one more thing on top of everything else.**" *(The "false thing about the gift card, the ATM" is flagged as unclear in the derived notes and is not interpreted here.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Vertical_Screen_Bundle_Offer_and_David_Intro-September-8-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Vertical_Screen_Bundle_Offer_and_David_Intro-September-8-2026.md]]
- **Georgia is still a cash market and penetration is under ten percent — from any vendor.** "it's **not easy** to because **the market still is cash**… the majority of the [locations] they take cash… **If you go to [InComm] and if you have [InComm] to give you data overall for the Georgia market, they would say not even let's say ten percent of the market is using any kind of equipment**, right? **Any kind doesn't matter from where they are.**" *(The transcript writes "income" for InComm and "vacations" for locations; the notes flag both.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Vertical_Screen_Bundle_Offer_and_David_Intro-September-8-2026.md]]
- **Which cuts both ways, and he said so.** "**So that means that at the same time there is room for growth**, right? The market is very brand new to any kind of equipment. You know, very few stores has a solution, any kind of solution, **but at the same time, it's very difficult to say if they want one or they don't want one.**" And from inside his own estate: "**Look, not even all locations we have** … **Not all of them want something. Some of them they want nothing. They care less. Nothing, no solution.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Vertical_Screen_Bundle_Offer_and_David_Intro-September-8-2026.md]]
- **Val's counter, which Zazanis did not answer:** "the location could **continue doing what they're doing, but instead of paying cash, they could redeem it on the card and then buy a card. It's much much better for them.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Vertical_Screen_Bundle_Offer_and_David_Intro-September-8-2026.md]]
- **He did not answer the brand-cheapening question at all.** Val raised it himself and Zazanis moved on. Recorded as open on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-free-ports-and-pos-with-every-machine-bought.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Vertical_Screen_Bundle_Offer_and_David_Intro-September-8-2026.md]]
- **He made the CEO introduction and removed himself from it.** "I didn't want to be in the middle of your discussions." Ruling: [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-08-val-approaches-david-sloan-directly-with-the-bundle-offer.md]]; the man himself on [[wiki/people/david-sloan.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Vertical_Screen_Bundle_Offer_and_David_Intro-September-8-2026.md]]

*(A separate figure from the same call belongs to Val, not to Zazanis: "**At least seventy percent of locations still operating with the PACS system**" — the notes read "PACS" as **PAX**. It is recorded on [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]] because it is Val's assertion, made unchallenged.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Vertical_Screen_Bundle_Offer_and_David_Intro-September-8-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Vertical_Screen_Bundle_Offer_and_David_Intro-September-8-2026.md]]

Misha is JVL's retrofit and technical lead. He and Val plan how to rebuild the used gaming machines coming in from other markets.

## Role

- Misha and Val worked through the plan for retrofitting the incoming machines, deciding what parts to order and how to rebuild each machine type. [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]]

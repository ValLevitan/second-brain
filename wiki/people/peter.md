# Peter (former JVL Corp CFO/CEO)

Peter is a **former shareholder and executive of the old JVL Corp** who surfaces in JVL's 2025 loan reconciliation. Surname is **not stated** in the sources. [[raw/finance_loans_review_sean_alka-june-13-2025.md]]

- **Role in the 2006 restructuring.** When JVL restructured from **JVL Corp** into **JVL Labs** in 2006 — after JVL lost a **copyright court case in Pennsylvania** and **2011-680 Ontario** stepped in as secured creditor to wipe out the unsecured creditors — Peter was the **CFO and CEO** and **led the restructuring**. [[raw/finance_loans_review_sean_alka-june-13-2025.md]]
- **Loan-to-equity (unresolved on the books).** Peter had a loan of **~$150,000** to JVL; Val gave him **shares instead of the loan, gratuitously**, but the loan was **never reclassified** off the balance sheet, so JVL still shows both the shares and the ~$150k liability. On the June-13 2025 loan review, Val directed that this be corrected (a debt-forgiveness journal entry) — **but only after consulting the lawyer [[wiki/people/alan.md|Alan Litwack]]**, because **Peter is now disputing** the conversion. Val believes he "may have a claim against Peter and Boris on this." Full context on [[wiki/topics/jvl-corporate-loans-and-shareholder-financing.md]]. [[raw/finance_loans_review_sean_alka-june-13-2025.md]] [[raw/finance_loan_summary_detailed_review_alka-june-13-2025.md]]

Paired throughout with [[wiki/people/boris.md|Boris]] (the other former shareholder whose loan was likewise converted to shares).

# Dan Lewis (GLI)

Dan Lewis is a technical and testing person at [[wiki/organizations/gli.md|GLI]], the lab that certifies JVL's machines and games.

## Role

- **Jan 2025.** Named among GLI's technical/testing people. He measures math testing, the most labour-intensive stage of Class 3 approval, at close to 20 hours. (That source says only "Dan"; he is taken to be Dan Lewis because both sources place a Dan in GLI's technical and testing role — an inference.) [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]]
- **Jan 28 2026 — Omni certification kickoff.** At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-01-28-gli-omni-platform-certification-kickoff.md|GLI kickoff]] he spoke for GLI's testing side. His passages are often garbled in the transcript. What is clear: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]
  - He agreed to test all peripherals in one pass: "We're not going to do a whole new test all over again." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]
  - He asked for a JVL paper on how the RNG is called: "That's how the RNG is called, so it makes it easier." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]
  - He wants GLI to work from JVL's own ecosystem: "I want to have an ecosystem and have your ecosystem and generate this rather than somebody making it." Any deviation would then flag something to look at. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]
  - He described GLI tools that compare screen images with a reference image: "We give it an image of what a chair is supposed to be on the screen and it will compare to that." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]
  - He accepted Val's white-paper method ("Okay."). See [[wiki/decisions/2026-01-28-gli-omni-certification-runs-on-jvl-white-papers.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]

# David Sloan (CEO, ARC Gaming)

**Chief executive of [[wiki/organizations/arc-gaming.md|ARC Gaming]]** — full name given in the derived notes as **ARC Gaming & Technologies** — the Georgia COAM master operator whose COO is [[wiki/people/georgios-zazanis.md|Georgios "Zaza" Zazanis]]. He matters because he is the **only route** by which any JVL proposal can reach ARC's inherited kiosk arrangement, and since **September 8 2026** he is a direct JVL counterparty rather than a name on a routing note. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Vertical_Screen_Bundle_Offer_and_David_Intro-September-8-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

**Since September 9 2026 he speaks for himself in the corpus.** The Aug-31 and Sept-8 record was entirely second-hand — everything below the "How Zazanis reads him" heading is still what two other people said about him. The [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-09-arc-gaming-backend-displacement-negotiation.md|Sept-9 4:35 PM meeting]] is the first source in which he is a participant. **His name is never spoken in that transcript**: Plaud labels speakers by number, and Val confirmed the counterparty on 2026-09-10, with the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-09-georgia-exclusivity-bet-and-sebastian-reassignment.md|5:31 PM debrief]] an hour later naming "**Dave Sloan**" as the CEO he had just met. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Exclusivity_Bet_and_Sebastian_Reassignment-September-9-2026.md]]

## Why everything has to go through him (Aug 31 2026)

- **He is the only person who can instruct the kiosk vendor.** Zazanis, refusing to let Val approach the vendor ("Bill") directly: "**Has to come from David. David has to tell Bill. Bill, you need to work with[them] on this solution.**" The full ruling is [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-31-arc-kiosk-board-swap-routed-through-the-ceo.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **And he is a long-standing friend of that vendor** — which is what makes him the only viable route and, simultaneously, the reason the request may never be made. Val: "**The Sloan… Is friends with Bill. For years.**" Zazanis: "Maybe. Bill friends." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **He overrules his own COO on the subject.** Zazanis's account of what happens when he objects: "if I say this sucks, the other guy says George. **It is what it is. Find the solution.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-08-arc-gaming-bundle-sanity-check-and-david-sloan-intro.md|Sept-8 call]] Zazanis made the introduction and removed himself from the middle of it: "**I said to David, you know, Val wants to talk to you. He will reach out to you**… So **reach out to him. He's expecting you.**" Val initiates, by "a note, a call, a text, whatever," and the two agree the date and time between themselves. ## Sept 9 2026 — in his own words

### What he says about himself

- **He is close to closing the sale of a business.** "Getting really close to closing this business." *(Personal matters removed — owner ruling 2026-09-28.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]
- **He has a second business in Chicago, where he once had one of the largest outlets and then sold it.** "I got another business in…" / "**We had one of the largest outlets there one time.**" *(The sold-it line falls on the other speaker label, one of several places the Plaud labels mis-split turns here; the derived notes read the whole passage as his.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]
- **He holds equity but does not own the operations.** "But **I have some equity. Most of them are privately owned.**" That is the first statement in the corpus about his stake in ARC — and it bounds what he can personally commit. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]
- **Background:** Val told his own team the same evening that the CEO "**is ex-Bali guy**." The derived notes read this as *ex-Bally*, the manufacturer, and **explicitly say it has not been ruled on** — so it is recorded as a flag, not as biography. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Georgia_Exclusivity_Bet_and_Sebastian_Reassignment-September-9-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Georgia_Exclusivity_Bet_and_Sebastian_Reassignment-September-9-2026.md]]

### How he negotiates

- **He does the other side's arithmetic himself.** Handed the outline of JVL's back-end ambition, he worked out the size of the prize out loud and unprompted — two dollars on the card, four-fifty on the back-end transaction, 47,000 machines, five cash-outs a machine a day, eighty percent control — and landed on "**It's a million dollars.**" Then: "**It's a great business model**," followed immediately by "**how quickly**" JVL could get there. Verbatim figures on [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]
- **He does not say no, and he does not say yes.** Three separate asks got the same shape of answer: on stepping on [[wiki/people/bill.md|Bill]]'s toes, "**A little bit, yeah**"; on the proposal generally, "**I'm not against what you're saying**"; on a Class A handshake, a remark about engineering difficulty instead of an answer. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]
- **He drew one hard line, unprompted.** "**I don't think you can buy my entire business**" — and gave the reason: he already has M3K in the field, Bill's kit, "and I've got a **number of other point of sale systems out there as well.**" *("M3K" is flagged unresolved in the derived notes.)* That line is why the exclusivity ask failed — see [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-arc-account-exclusivity-sought-and-not-obtained.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]
- **His competitive instinct is to give away his own margin to undercut.** On the ATM fee a player pays to cash a prize card: "**I would probably more valuable need not take my part of that fee and give it back to the customer and not charge them, because I know I'm my competitive advantage. I'm charging less than the next guy, so I'll give up three dollars to gain twenty dollars.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

### What Val entrusted him with

Val disclosed JVL's intent to displace [[wiki/organizations/incom.md|InComm]] on the back end — with **no confidentiality agreement in place**, on the stated ground that "**you're a senior executive, so I can trust you**" — and told him that [[wiki/people/georgios-zazanis.md|Zazanis]], ARC's own COO, does not know. Full ruling: [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-val-discloses-the-incomm-displacement-intent-to-arcs-ceo.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

## Sept 16 2026 — the second meeting: he watches the demo, then opens the Class A negotiation himself

[[wiki/meetings/2026-09-16-arc-gaming-cashless-suite-demo-and-class-a-cabinet-counter-offer.md|Sept 16 2026, 10:12–10:41 AM]], at JVL's Lawrenceville office rather than his own place as agreed. The commercial outcome is [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-16-jvl-offers-arc-new-class-a-cabinets-at-2500-plus-20-percent.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]]

- **The "not against it" answer again — a third time, on a third subject.** On the equipment: "**No, I like it. I said I'm not against trying it out**"; "**I like to see him take a look for sure**… **I'm not against it; I like the technology.**" On the cabinet offer: "**Let me just think on that.**" On next steps: "**Send me a message.**" **Nothing he said on this call is a yes or a no** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]].
- **He put Class A on the table himself**, at the end of the demo: "It's what about **Class A? Let's go into the work.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]]
- **He gave his supplier's commercial terms openly** — $1,000 for the software plus 10% on his own cabinet, 20% on the supplier's — and just as openly refused the one number Val needed: asked the realistic daily average per machine, "**Right now, I can't say it's a lot.**" He let Val's "**thirty bucks**" stand without confirming it [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]].
- **He prices by performance, not by list.** "**You have a game that's doing forty bucks a day, and I get one that's doing sixty. I'll pay more for the sixty for sure**" — and "**there is some flexibility, depending on how much that is a little bit**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]].
- **He would rather keep his own boxes than take new ones at a higher share.** "**I'd rather give up 10% use my box**" — with a warehouse of refurbished cabinets behind the preference [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]].
- **He argues against cashless while being sold cashless.** "**Play-wise, it doesn't do me good. I'd rather have cash because we generate more**" — because a player handed cash "**more likely… put[s] it back into the machine**" and starts chasing. He has cashless locations only because a venue's corporate policy requires it, and may convert them back. Detail on [[wiki/organizations/arc-gaming.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]].
- **The Jamie connection, from his side.** "**He's a wonderful man. I put him in business in Illinois. Now he's buying locations, cafes**… **I'm the one who dragged him in there**" — into South Dakota. Val entered Georgia through Lucky Bucks, "**through Jamie**," is having dinner with him the following Monday, and says he **invested** in Jamie's business and is happy with the investment. He now has a page — **[[wiki/people/jamie-boyden.md|Jamie Boyden]]**, created from the phone call Val took at 12:50 the same day, which corroborates the Illinois route and the South Dakota history from Jamie's own mouth [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Jamie_Georgia_Class_A_Opportunity_and_Market_Outlook-September-16-2026.md]].
- **His diary, which sets the next date:** he leaves **Saturday for G2E**, then **away for two weeks from Wednesday**, back on **the 13th** and wanting "**a couple days to get my**[desk in order]" — hence **October 15** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]].
- **Nothing was said about [[wiki/people/bill.md|Bill]], the kiosk vendor, the board swap or the InComm disclosure** — the whole Sept-9 back-end thread went unmentioned [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]].

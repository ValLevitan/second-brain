# Kyle Johnson (embedded-systems engineer; event-recorder candidate)

**Kyle Johnson is an embedded-systems engineer whom [[wiki/people/nathan-carver.md|Nathan Carver]] put forward to [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] for JVL's machine events recorder ("black box").** Val: "I asked Nathan, if he knows somebody who… can not only adhere to these standards… Also… enforce this sort of a culture in my company. And he thought that you might be a good person for that." They met on **April 3 2026** ([[wiki/meetings/2026-04-03-kyle-johnson-event-recorder-recruitment-intro.md]]). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Kyle_Johnson_Event_Recorder_Recruitment_Intro-April-3-2026.md]]

He is **not** [[wiki/people/kyle-shepherd.md|Kyle Shepherd]] of Lucky Coin, a Georgia operator. The two are different people.

## The role offered (Apr 3 2026)

All from the transcript [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Kyle_Johnson_Event_Recorder_Recruitment_Intro-April-3-2026.md]]:

- **The job.** Design the events recorder. There is no architecture yet: "if you're asking me, if there is an architectural design yet, no There isn't". Asked if he could do the architecture himself, Kyle said: "I can do it as long as we have a basic understanding of the requirements and goals". The technical need is on [[wiki/topics/egm-freezing-investigation.md]] (Apr-3 2026).
- **Part-time, around his full-time job.** Val: "we will uh. Work around around your capacity… with Nathan for example, He tells me that he can give me X number of hours per week, and we cater to that". No hours were agreed.
- **Not on a deadline, but not open-ended.** Val: "this event recorder is not schedule critical device because I can snap it in when it's ready… I don't want it to be a five-year project".
- **Time zones.** He is in Arizona; the engineers are at "zero plus three". Val: "there's gonna be a nine hour time difference." Kyle: "I'll have to figure that out".
- **Working with the team.** Nathan's advice: "you'll have to drive a lot of that… they won't necessarily initiate with you unless you are actively pushing for it." Kyle said he has worked that way before.
- **Val's condition.** "if I start going with you, I like to know that there is longevity there… If something doesn't feel comfortable, Just let me know and we can either fix it or we just not going to go for it."

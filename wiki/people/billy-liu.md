# Billy Liu

External. Founder and principal of [[wiki/organizations/play-payments.md|Play Payments]], the Atlanta company behind the **PlayPay** wallet and the **PayApp** app. He first met JVL on the record on **September 2 2026** (he is first *named* earlier — Feb 16 2026, see History), when he spent roughly an hour and a half at the JVL Georgia office demonstrating his product to [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] and left with an offer on the table for half his company ([[wiki/meetings/2026-09-02-billy-liu-playpay-partnership-deep-dive.md]], [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-val-proposes-fifty-percent-of-play-payments.md]]) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

**The surname is now confirmed from the man himself.** Asked on **September 15 2026** how he wanted his name on the NDA, he spelled it out: "**Bill, B-I-L, Ming, M-I-N-G. Last name Liu, L-I-U.**" The derived notes for that session render it **Bill Ming Liu** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Mutual_NDA_Renegotiation-September-15-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Mutual_NDA_Renegotiation-September-15-2026.md]].

*(Until Sept 15 the surname rested on the derived notes alone — the Sept-2 transcript addresses him only as "Billy" and never spells it. That is now superseded; the earlier position is kept here so the provenance is visible.)* [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]

**He owns two companies, not one — established Sept 15 2026.** [[wiki/organizations/surfnote.md|SurfNote]] is the Georgia-Lottery-licensed, machine-side company and is **100% his**; [[wiki/organizations/play-payments.md|Play Payments]] is the payments company, came later, and "**has my brother involved. The two of us.**" No earlier source draws this line, and [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-val-proposes-fifty-percent-of-play-payments.md|Val's Sept-2 offer for half the company]] was made without it [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]].

## Identity — the "Billy Luke" question (inference, not confirmed)

The corpus has carried a competitor called **"Billy Luke"** since the [[wiki/meetings/2026-04-21-transactional-financial-model.md|Apr-21 2026 transactional competitive-landscape session]]: [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]] described him as "**the Asian guy, Billy Luke**… he has a product ready for the market where **he talks directly to banks. There's no in between**," someone both Val and [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan]] had already met; Juan's objection then was that "**Billy Luke is not a hardware manufacturer**, so he's got to get this stuff from somebody else," and that no machine talks to a phone by design [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_transactional_financial_model-April-21-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_transactional_financial_model-April-21-2026.md]].

Every element of that description fits the man in the Sept-2 room: a payments company that holds its own banking licence (hence "directly to banks, no in between"), no hardware of its own, and a product that needs NFC or a display on the machine before it can work — which is exactly what he asked JVL for. **Read here as the same person, with "Billy Luke" recorded as an alias. Neither source says so, no owner ruling exists, and this is labelled an inference; queued for the owner.** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]

## Background, as he gave it

- **PhD in theoretical physics.** Asked "What's your education?" — "**I have a PhD in theoretical physics.**" *(Val teases him twice afterwards with "master's degree" and "Ph.D. in quantitative physics"; the PhD in theoretical physics is Billy's own statement.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]
- **He wrote code for the company that made Atlanta a payments town.** His schoolfriend **Steve Hughes** got into MIT, could not afford it, and instead "built this gateway, **merchant processing gateway company called Nova Information Systems**… **They supplied First Union Bank. First Union became Wachovia and became Wells Fargo.**" Billy's own part: "in the early days when my buddy needed help, **I wrote code to help him and I understood the processing, the gateway, things**." Hughes is retired and out of contact except at holidays [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].
- **His claim for why Atlanta matters, offered as the frame for everything else:** "**This city processes seventy percent of the world's plastic. Debit or credit, seventy percent is done here**… Nobody knows that because it's behind the scenes." Recorded as **his assertion**; no source in the corpus corroborates it [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].
- **He helped fund and lobby [[wiki/organizations/gamoa.md|GAMOA]] into existence, fifteen years ago.** "In the early days with this lobbying of gift cards, I was part of it. **I donated money to get GAMOA going, and I even did some lobbying on my own. That dates back 15 years.** Georgia became what it became" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].
- **He holds a patent on NFC "tap to fund".** Raised unprompted, as something to marry to JVL's ports: "**What if we marry? I have a patent on that NFC**… For these types of things. NFC on, **we call it tap to fund**"; and when Val said a QR code on the phone would do, "**Doesn't matter. NFC is what we made to load credits**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].
- **He and JVL have talked before.** "remember our cart system and the whole management system? We had that discussion. **At that time, payments was at the back of my mind, but since it didn't materialize, I wasn't ready to disclose it**" — so an earlier JVL conversation exists that no source in the corpus records. He also saw Val at the Georgia show: "**I saw you walking around. I was always here**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].
- **On AI, he is a sceptic.** "AI is nice, it's useful, **but only for very specific tasks**… **to replace a human person for every task, I think it's not there yet**," and on agents specifically, "I think it's an excuse for techies to spend time… **We're not there yet.**" He names two Silicon Valley products his friends built, rendered "**Sim AI**" and "**Scale AI**". *(Speaker attribution wobbles across this passage in the transcript — the line "I use Anthropic… Claude Code" sits in Val's turn — and no claim here is attributed to Billy beyond the AI-scepticism he states in his own labelled turns.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]

## Sept 15 2026 — the second meeting: where he comes from, and how he negotiates

The [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-15-billy-liu-playpay-nda-and-georgia-float-economics.md|Sept-15 evening session]] ran nearly four hours, and about half of the first hour was the two men getting to know each other.

### Biography, as he gave it

- **South American trade links.** He still trades in South America — "**I still have friends and people in the business that buy stuff from Chile**" — and he speaks Spanish ("You speak Spanish? **Yes.**") [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Mutual_NDA_Renegotiation-September-15-2026.md]].
- **The trade came from his father:** "**My dad sold a lot of products: Taiwanese boards, electronics, whatever you need**" — which is where the hardware side of his business starts: "**You learn electronics by doing. My father, I grew up in that business, so electronics came kind of naturally**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Mutual_NDA_Renegotiation-September-15-2026.md]].
- **Norcross is the point, not an accident.** "**I grew up in Georgia. Believe it or not, when people said whatever they say about gaming as a whole, Norcross, Georgia, is the hotbed.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Mutual_NDA_Renegotiation-September-15-2026.md]]
- **Berkeley, and the Las Vegas job he turned down.** Recruited as a young man to go to Las Vegas and design games, he refused: "**I said, 'Oh no, I'm not a designing game kind of guy. I want to be a scientist. I'm going to Berkeley to become a physicist, and I did.'**" *(Consistent with the "PhD in theoretical physics" he gave on Sept 2, above. The recruiters are transcribed "**Sid Red**"/"**Cy Red**", "**Steve Shafer**" and "**Joe Caminca**", with a company rendered "**Dade East Pimples**" — all **put to Val on 2026-09-16 and unverified; left as transcribed and not to be guessed.**)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Mutual_NDA_Renegotiation-September-15-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Mutual_NDA_Renegotiation-September-15-2026.md]]
- **His family ran machines in Georgia before any of this.** "**When he owned ninety-five percent of this market, my family operated. We had over a thousand machines out, and we were the only company in Georgia that didn't use [Cadillac] machines.**" The "he" is **Mike Mackey**, whom Billy returns to twice — "**the sheer arrogance of Mike Mackey back when he had ninety-five percent of the marketplace.**" *(No page exists for Mike Mackey and none is created on this alone; "Cadillac machines" is as transcribed and is not interpreted.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Mutual_NDA_Renegotiation-September-15-2026.md]]

*(Personal matters removed — owner ruling 2026-09-28.)*

### He settled the NDA in four minutes and gave away both points

Val came with two objections; Billy conceded both without a counter — "**I'm not here to tie you down. I'm here to share information with you so that you can make sense. So get rid of the clause**" — and then coached Val through the paperwork. Terms on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-15-mutual-nda-agreed-clause-12-deleted-and-clause-8-time-boxed.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Mutual_NDA_Renegotiation-September-15-2026.md]].

He treats it as binding and invoked it once Val started describing an unreleased product: "**We signed an NDA. We're going to keep it. I want to do business with you**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]].

### On Class A — he has already converted some titles

"**What are your thoughts about class A? I actually converted some of my titles to class A because why not**… **Just some customers are asking for it, and I converted some of it just to see what it'll do.**" His read of why operators want it: "**Don't have to share with the lottery, or don't have the scrutiny. They're fine with it. It doesn't matter how much money [it] makes. It's just… the idea that they don't want to share with the regulator that could turn you off at any moment.**" And his forecast: "**It's a minority. Most people want to make money. But I think it's short lived.**" See [[wiki/topics/georgia-class-a-machines.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Mutual_NDA_Renegotiation-September-15-2026.md]].

### His posture towards the regulator — the substantive split with Val

- **He talks to Lottery staff often and does not take the letter seriously.** "**I speak to them often. I ask them, according to this letter, I call it a stupid letter, and they laugh and say, 'Yeah.'**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]]
- **He has already offered to switch the contested feature off and been told to wait.** His app can push funds off the Visa card "**send it back to my bank account, send it to PayPal**"; "**I asked them, Do you want me to turn that off?**" — "**Not right now. We'll let you know.**" His conclusion about the Lottery: "**they're just always two-faced. They want you guessing all the time.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]]
- **Val disagreed with the whole approach and Billy conceded on the spot.** The money-laundering argument and Billy's "**I'm totally in agreement. I just wanted to hear someone else… hear me out**" are on [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]]. The derived notes call it "a real difference in approach between the two parties" and record it as **unresolved** [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]].

### He still refuses to deal with InComm

Thirteen days after telling Val they would kick him to the curb, the position is harder: "**I don't have a relationship [with InComm]. You did, or you were planning to, right?**… **That son of a bitch wants to talk to me, but I say no**… **We don't do business with the devil. Because you already know that he's the billion-dollar gorilla.**" Val's reply was to distance himself from the characterisation — "**I'm not sure where you get this information**" — and Billy dropped it: "**Let's leave it alone.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]]

He also remembered a number from the Sept-2 meeting that no page carries: "**You said last time that [InComm] was willing to give you 2.5 percent. Where is that… coming from?**" *(The line is transcribed twice, once as "2.5 percent" and once as "point two point five percent" — **the two are not the same number and neither is asserted.** Val did not confirm either; his answer was about the broker's clearing dollar, on [[wiki/organizations/incom.md]].)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]]

## His view of InComm

The most useful thing he gave JVL for free was a competitor's read of [[wiki/organizations/incom.md|InComm]], on a day when Val had spent lunch being told by InComm itself that the partnership was at risk ([[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-hold-the-recycler-back-but-sign-no-undertaking.md]]).

- **They approached him to partner, and he expects to be cut out.** "**They want to partner up, and I said, 'Partner up with you. I'm gonna get fucking kicked to the curb.' They're very difficult people to deal with.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]
- **He agreed with Val's 1.5-cards-a-day figure.** Val: "**Average today, 1.5 car a day.**" Billy: "**Average. Exactly. It's horrible.**" *(The figure is Val's, carried over from the InComm lunch that same day; Billy confirms it rather than supplying it independently — the source is garbled around his first attempt, "Also, bents one car a day." Recorded on that basis.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]
- **He says InComm shares its fees with master licence holders, not with stores.** Against Val's argument that front fees should go to the store owner: "**Except [InComm] doesn't share fees to the store**"; Val: "They share with me"; Billy: "**They share with the MLHs.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]
- **And thirty years of plastic is their position, not his.** "[InComm is] a you know. **They've been doing this for thirty years, offering physical cards**" — the sentence he uses to explain why Visa pushed him the other way [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

## How he handled Val's proposal

He neither accepted nor refused. To "I get fifty percent of your company. I'm driving you right into the market," his reply was "**And you know what? No one was there when I was driving it myself**" — and earlier, to Val's $150M projection, "**I doubt I'd be that successful**" and "**You're very ambitious. I like it.**" His one condition, raised on his own initiative, was exclusivity: "**It'll be an exclusive thing, right? You're not gonna offer [InComm] out of those things. You're not gonna dispense a physical card.**" The terms and Val's answer are on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-val-proposes-fifty-percent-of-play-payments.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

His assessment of Val, volunteered mid-argument: "**Val, you're the brightest person that I've met in the gaming business because you calculate every goddamn thing**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

## Sept 22 2026 — the third conversation: John, the refused card, and the pay tag

A [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-22-billy-liu-playpay-virtual-wallet-pay-tag-plan.md|4:04 PM call]], a week after Sept 15. The product and market substance is on [[wiki/organizations/play-payments.md]], [[wiki/organizations/incom.md]] and [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]] (all Sept 22) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Virtual_Wallet_Pay_Tag_Plan-September-22-2026.md]].

- **He and InComm's John are friends, and he keeps it quiet.** "**John and I have been having some private conversations. We're friends**"; "**I don't want to broadcast that because we're very competitive.**" Val: "**I had no idea that you were friends.**" The John is read as [[wiki/people/john-houseal.md|John Houseal]] (inference) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Virtual_Wallet_Pay_Tag_Plan-September-22-2026.md]].
- **The InComm approach he turned down came from John.** John had proposed a virtual-plus-physical card tie-up; Billy called it "**a takeover tactic**" and says "**that was initiated by John**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Virtual_Wallet_Pay_Tag_Plan-September-22-2026.md]]. This is the approach behind his Sept-15 "**That son of a bitch wants to talk to me, but I say no**" above.
- **His answer to Val's legal question is to switch two features off** — the push-back to a bank account and the bank-debit-card route — leaving the wallet and a reloadable virtual Visa. Detail on [[wiki/organizations/play-payments.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Virtual_Wallet_Pay_Tag_Plan-September-22-2026.md]].
- **On plastic cards he is a realist, not a convert.** "**The physical card has been in existence since the '80s. I can't change what's already there… I can only offer my system as a reloadable product**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Virtual_Wallet_Pay_Tag_Plan-September-22-2026.md]]. By the end of the call both men expected to dispense cards at the start — which sits against his own Sept-2 condition, "**You're not gonna dispense a physical card**" (see [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-val-proposes-fifty-percent-of-play-payments.md]]) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Virtual_Wallet_Pay_Tag_Plan-September-22-2026.md]].
- **G2E.** "**Since you're not going to G2E, I have some meetings planned**"; he does not enjoy it any more — "**sometimes it's unnecessary evil**." Both agreed not to wait for the show: "**We can always talk whenever there's more information**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Virtual_Wallet_Pay_Tag_Plan-September-22-2026.md]].

**§15.8 exclusion:** his estimate of John's yearly worth and his private job talks with John are **not recorded** [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Virtual_Wallet_Pay_Tag_Plan-September-22-2026.md]].

# Yuri Zakharenko

Yuri Zakharenko is a JVL Minsk staff member (matches "Zaharenkov, Yury" on the [[wiki/topics/minsk-staff-roster.md|Minsk roster]]). In mid-April 2025 Val singled him out to be trained to control JVL's Jira/requirements work with AI tooling.

## Role & current status

- On Apr 16 2025 Val had [[wiki/people/andrey-bosik.md|Andrei]] install Aaron's **Jira MCP** and **VS Code (in Russian)** on Yuri's machine, and start training him to read a story-level task description, reverse it into a feature, and check whether the feature matches — "Я хочу, чтобы Юрий начал контролировать это." [[wiki/people/dmitri-kovalchuk.md|Dmitri]] had already shown Yuri a simple MCP prompt and is continuing to teach him. [[raw/it_automation_schemas_secrets_server-apr-16-2025.md]]
- Same day, in the Omni daily, Val listed Yuri among those getting the Jira MCP installed and asked that he be invited to the QA integration-test training meeting. [[raw/daily_omni_progress-april-16-2025 (Val Levitan's conflicted copy).md]]

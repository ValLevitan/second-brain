# Igor Starovoytov

Igor Starovoytov is a **contractor industrial designer** for JVL (owner ruling 2026-07-13). He does the Flex machine-cabinet industrial design, and is sometimes engaged via [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi Peari]] (hardware). (Owner correction is its own citation; the owner notes his contractor record should be in the JVL Obsidian **Contractors** records.)

## Role

- On the Feb 27 2025 Flex V42/C cabinet design review, Val directed the team to "let Igor finish the design," discuss the speaker panel with him, and "let our industrial designer just play with it" — i.e. this industrial designer. See [[wiki/decisions/2025-02-27-flex-street-cabinet-design-direction.md]] and [[wiki/meetings/2025-02-27-flex-v42-c-design-review.md]]. [[raw/Flex_V42_C_design_evolution_review_2025-02-27.md]]

- On the Mar 27 2025 Genadi call, Val wants "Igor" to take all three Flex machines (curve, 243, "Dnavi") and **combine them into one visual family**, doing a **preliminary industrial design across all three before detailing** the curve machine. Resolved to this industrial-design Igor by the cabinet/product-design context (per §4). See [[wiki/decisions/2025-03-27-preliminary-industrial-design-all-three-flex-cabinets.md]]. [[raw/Flex-S-design-March-27-2025.md]]

Distinct from [[wiki/people/igor-nazarenka.md|the mobile-application Igor]] and [[wiki/people/igor-donis.md|Igor Donis]] (Financial Controller). The "Igor, our design(er)" who worked on the **ECHO landing page** (Feb 1, per Andrei Klimovich) could be this industrial/product designer or the mobile-app Igor — left flagged, resolve by context.

## Apr 16 2025 — dispensing-tower industrial design

In Val's Apr-16 call with Genadi Peari, Igor was assigned to draw the industrial design of the new self-serve card-dispensing tower — a simple, aesthetically pleasing design in real dimensions, gray/milky plastic with no lighting, a 7-inch FlexPort-style screen in front, shown both as a standalone unit and standing between machines — due by ~Wednesday/Thursday. Genadi to prepare the models/reference for him first; Val noted Igor works Friday/Saturday/Sunday. [[raw/fkex_pay_evolution-apr-16-2025.md]] See [[wiki/decisions/2025-04-16-dispensing-tower-first-magnetic-stripe.md]].

## May 1–2 2025 — dispensing-tower / POS design iterations

On the two Val↔Genadi design calls ([[wiki/meetings/2025-05-01-flex-tower-spatial-design-review.md]], [[wiki/meetings/2025-05-02-flex-tower-pos-form-fit-design.md]]) Igor's industrial-design proposal for the card-dispensing tower was reviewed and reshaped: the group dropped the detachable-head concept in favour of a separate two-hood POS reusing the same FlexPort electronics. Val was to hand Igor all the models so he could draw over the weekend, and had a **~6-hour design session with Igor** the evening of May 2. See [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]]. [[raw/flex_tower_spacial_design_review-may-1-2025.md]] [[raw/flex_tower_pos_form_fit_design-may-2-2025.md]]

## May 20 2025 — tower/dashboard design iteration continues

On the May-20 Val↔Genadi industrial-design call ([[wiki/meetings/2025-05-20-flex-tower-industrial-design.md]]) Igor's designs were reviewed third-person: Val judged the current FlexPort dashboard bracket a temporary "conversion" and wants Igor pushed toward a solid, integrated, forward-looking design harmonized with the FlexTower (Val preferred Igor's third variant direction). Igor's CAD continues while Genadi drives the tower toward an orderable state. See [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]]. [[raw/industrial_design_update-may-20-2025.md]]

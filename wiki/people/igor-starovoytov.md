# Igor Starovoytov

Igor Starovoytov is a **contractor industrial designer** for JVL (owner ruling 2026-07-13). He does the Flex machine-cabinet industrial design, and is sometimes engaged via [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi Peari]] (hardware). (Owner correction is its own citation; the owner notes his contractor record should be in the JVL Obsidian **Contractors** records.)

## Role

- On the Feb 27 2025 Flex V42/C cabinet design review, Val directed the team to "let Igor finish the design," discuss the speaker panel with him, and "let our industrial designer just play with it" — i.e. this industrial designer. See [[wiki/decisions/2025-02-27-flex-street-cabinet-design-direction.md]] and [[wiki/meetings/2025-02-27-flex-v42-c-design-review.md]]. [[raw/Flex_V42_C_design_evolution_review_2025-02-27.md]]

- On the Mar 27 2025 Genadi call, Val wants "Igor" to take all three Flex machines (curve, 243, "Dnavi") and **combine them into one visual family**, doing a **preliminary industrial design across all three before detailing** the curve machine. Resolved to this industrial-design Igor by the cabinet/product-design context (per §4). See [[wiki/decisions/2025-03-27-preliminary-industrial-design-all-three-flex-cabinets.md]]. [[raw/Flex-S-design-March-27-2025.md]]

Distinct from [[wiki/people/igor-nazarenka.md|the mobile-application Igor]] and [[wiki/people/igor-donis.md|Igor Donis]] (Financial Controller). The "Igor, our design(er)" who worked on the **ECHO landing page** (Feb 1, per Andrei Klimovich) could be this industrial/product designer or the mobile-app Igor — left flagged, resolve by context.

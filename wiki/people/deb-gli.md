# Deb (Deborah Oxnem, GLI account manager)

Deb is JVL's account manager at GLI (Gaming Laboratories International). She visited Val in person for the January 7, 2025 meeting.

**Name.** The Jan 2025 source gives her name only once, in a joke she tells about herself ("they're like, Deb, you can't have any fun..."), so it was first treated as tentative [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]]. The Jan 28 2026 transcript labels the GLI speaker who opens the call and offers GLI's proposal as **"Deborah Oxnem"** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]. Taken as the same person by context (GLI's client-facing contact who runs the relationship) — an inference, but a strong one.

## Role and background

- She is fairly new to GLI and is the client-facing account manager for JVL. [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]]
- She flew in from Las Vegas specifically to meet Val in person; she was the only client visit on that trip. [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]]
- She lives in Las Vegas, having earlier lived in Wisconsin and Minneapolis. Before GLI she worked at "Valley Corporate", latterly in a marketing role that was not her preference. She has spent her adult life in gaming, starting as a blackjack dealer. [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]] *(§15.8: a family matter she mentioned is not recorded.)*
- She relays JVL's questions to GLI's technical people (Patrick, Dan, Jason) and promised to take Val's automation and taxonomy questions back to them. [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]]

## Jan 28 2026 — the Omni certification kickoff

- She opened and led the GLI side of the [[wiki/meetings/2026-01-28-gli-omni-platform-certification-kickoff.md|Omni certification kickoff]], thanking JVL for "sending all the documentation. This was a great way to kick off the year." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]

This led to the one-pass peripherals list. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]
- She offered to prepare GLI's commercial proposal. Val deferred it until GLI has read JVL's white papers — see [[wiki/decisions/2026-01-28-gli-omni-certification-runs-on-jvl-white-papers.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]
- She asked whether follow-up questions should go to the whole group; Val asked that they go through him and Juan. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]

# Ryan O'Donnell (Double Down Gaming; GAMOA board member)

A Georgia COAM master licensee — small route, deliberately high-volatility — who also sits on the board of the state operators' association. He visited the JVL office in Lawrenceville on **September 1 2026**; see [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-01-double-down-gaming-visit.md]]. His route is on [[wiki/organizations/double-down-gaming.md]]; the association intel he gave is on [[wiki/organizations/gamoa.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]

He matters to JVL twice over: as a buyer JVL has only just started selling to, and as a route into the association board — the same access problem [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] had put to [[wiki/people/salim-khambawala.md|Salim Khambawala]] the day before.

## What he says about himself

- **"We're a small operator."** *(The line sits inside a block the transcript labels with Val's speaker number, immediately after Val's "I don't know anything about the company" — a plain mis-split; the derived notes read it as Ryan's self-description, "Small operator, deliberately volatility-driven," and that reading is followed here.)* And volatility is a choice, not an accident: "**I'm on the side of I'd rather gamble more myself. Not meaning I'll play, but I'm willing to let them run at ten dollars a hand. I'm willing to run at ten or twelve thousand dollar jackpots because I think I'm just a volume. I think the more money that goes in the machine, the better.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **On the association board seat:** "**A lot of people don't want to do this**… **I'm only doing this because I'm really enjoying it and I'm just keep getting volunteered for stuff.**" Val's reply: "this is probably the worst… job in the world." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **He was not on the board when the current manufacturer representative joined:** "He was on the board when I got there. He's been there for a long time." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **He is on the receiving end of JVL's show feedback, not the giving end** — the "best show that you put out so far" praise in this meeting comes from the JVL/exhibitor side of the table and is addressed to him as an association board member; it is recorded on [[wiki/organizations/gamoa.md]]. *(That direction of travel is read from the speaker labels plus the exhibitor's "for us, and this is our six… year" — an inference, flagged.)*

## The one product change he keeps asking for: adjustable jackpot reset values

This is his recurring ask and the clearest product signal in the meeting.

- **The problem.** "The feedback that I constantly get, depending on the product, is when somebody **hits a jackpot and takes ten grand**, they win that ten thousand, and then **it resets to twenty four hundred**. It's like they really love to be able to **set it at eight thousand again and let them chase that**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **Why it costs him money.** "say if we have these three games here, and they're different manufacturers, and they're all at ten thousand. This one hits. **Now nobody's playing this one for a while.** Then they're just chasing [the others]." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **What he wants is a reset floor, not a different top:** "it doesn't have to hit at ten. Like whatever the math is between eight and ten, the money going in between twenty four and ten and eight and ten. **If I could get that, that'd be great.**" *(The middle clause is garbled; recorded as spoken.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **This sits against a standing JVL requirement.** The GA-COAM v2 product-manager persona ruled in May 2026 that **progressive settings cannot be changed on the fly** — see [[wiki/topics/persona-driven-design-and-orchestration-platform.md]] and [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]]. Nothing in this source addresses that rule; noted here so the tension is visible rather than lost. *(The link between the two is this wiki's observation, not a statement in any source.)*

## Where he stands on Class A — against it

Ryan is the first Georgia operator in the corpus to argue **against** Class A in his own words — the February 2026 record has the association board fearing cannibalisation, but only second-hand through [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]]. He spoke one day after [[wiki/people/georgios-zazanis.md|George Zazanis]] of [[wiki/organizations/arc-gaming.md|ARC Gaming]] made the case for it. The mechanics and the full argument are on [[wiki/topics/georgia-class-a-machines.md]]; in short:

- "**in our opinion, it's a waste of time. It's skirting the rules that are in place. It's trying to find a vulnerability and a loophole and exploit it.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- He separates his own view from the board's. Asked whether that is the united view of the operators: "**Not all. It's my view.**" Asked whether it is a board view: "**We don't have a specific thing.**" Earlier in the same exchange he had said "We're not in favor of them." Both recorded; not reconciled. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]

## What he wants from the industry, and from JVL

- **The two unsettled things.** "**The A's and the ATMs are kind of too big things. We need to get settled. Otherwise, it's a little murky.**" *("too" transcribed as written; read as "two.")* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **He wants to skip cards entirely and go to digital reloadable wallets.** "**I'm hoping to avoid the whole thing and just keep it all digital.** That's the solution that works best before these ATM cards get a problem." And: "**I'm hoping that these kiosks and the interfaces start just going digital, reloadable, like in and out of your own wallet.**" He referenced "a rule change in July" as "the big thing" and thought "the law is kind of opening itself up to that reloadable option." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **He did like the ticket-scanning part.** "**I like the ticket thing**… Scanning the ticket instead of using the validator." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **He asked whether JVL has its own card processor.** "Do you have an in-house solution for being the carrier, like your own e-card processor?" Val: "**Our own e-cards? We don't.**" Ryan's reason: at the show he had seen vendors offering exactly that — "**it would be a [JVL] card or whatever you want to call this system, smart play card system, and it would be digital.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **He passed, without closing the door.** "**Now I'm gonna pass right now. I've just got one place coming up that I'm gonna try to see. But I always want to stay abreast. So this is informative.**" Val: "We're here for you. We're not going anywhere." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **He asked for recurring face-to-face time with industry people** — the derived notes read this as Ryan's request of Val: he "is in town every week, considers this market important, and asked for recurring face-to-face time with industry people he can learn from." The transcript's words are "**I'm actually in town every week. This market is quite important for me. I'm trying to understand it and to learn. Meetings with industry experts are important for me, and I'm hoping you could spare a little time for face-to-face time with me.**" *(The speaker labels in that four-minute segment are not reliable and the line would also fit Val; the derived notes' reading is followed here and the ambiguity is flagged.)* [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Double_Down_Gaming_Venue_Selection-September-1-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Venue_Selection-September-1-2026.md]]

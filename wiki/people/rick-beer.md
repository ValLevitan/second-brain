# Richard "Rick" Beer

President and founder of [[wiki/organizations/digital-cash-processing.md|Digital Cash Processing]], introduced on the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-11-digital-cash-processing-card-solution-and-cash-float-partnership.md|Sept 11 2026 call]] by [[wiki/people/andrew-cashin.md|Andrew Cashin]]: "**Rick Beer is the president of the company, and he started this company. I'm going to say in 1996**, okay, and it's gone through different iterations… **Basically, been doing this payment processing business for a long time.**" The same introduction ends "**he resides up in Minnesota**" — *the antecedent is not explicit and the derived notes read it as him; recorded as their reading* — and he was on the phone [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]].

## What he says his company holds

He spoke twice, and both contributions are structural rather than commercial. The first is the licence and rails statement — registered payment facilitator in the US, own issuing party and switch, ATM driving and processing in the US, sub-merchant boarding, financial institutions mostly in the East Caribbean — quoted in full on [[wiki/organizations/digital-cash-processing.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]].

## The float answer he would not give, and the one he did

Val asked him the question the whole meeting turned on — what can be made on holding the money, all in, per hundred dollars per year, including processing fees, monthly charges and unused funds at the end of the term [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]].

- **He declined to guess:** "**Some research and some projections. I wouldn't be able to answer that, but that's a good question. I mean, we… can run some exercises and put some projections together.**"
- **And he put the legal limit ahead of the commercial one:** "**then we'd have to look from the legal side… have to hold money for so long… can we even in the FBO that we have with our sponsor bank… And if so, for how long? And then after so much time… regulations that are basically a depository institution, so we have to consider all that. It just undercuts a lot of it.**"
- **What he did supply is the mechanism** — FBO account under their control, a safe and easily liquid investment, fee taken and money released when the cardholder draws cash, ATM interchange bypassed where they drive the ATM themselves. Full quotations on [[wiki/organizations/digital-cash-processing.md]]; the commercial reading on [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]] (Sept 11).
- **And his account of how the incumbent earns it:** "**That's the card issuer. They're going to make interchange… and then… depending on what they do with the funds and how they invest it, obviously you can make money there. So… they just needed to have a liquid solution.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]

**He also agreed with Val's one general rule about the product class**, immediately and without qualification. Val: "in any market, [a] card solution is not… useful if it doesn't lead to cash." Rick: "**I would agree with that.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]

**And he asked the question that made Val state his position:** "**What's the financial opportunity with our offering versus existing offerings like Fiery Pay?**" — which drew "**InComm doesn't offer any profit sharing on the other side. So why would I do InComm?**" See [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-11-market-access-traded-for-a-share-of-the-back-end-and-the-atm-fee-belongs-to-the-master-operator.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]].

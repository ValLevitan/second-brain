# Doug Skrepnek

Doug Skrepnek is a **lender / investor to JVL** to whom Val owes money (the transcript is a periodic update call between them about JVL's ability to repay). Surname **owner-confirmed** (2026-07-14) — the transcript says only "Doug"; he also appears in JVL's corporate loan records as Doug Skrepnek. [[raw/doug_update_jvl-march-23-2025.md]]

- **His own business is real estate.** He describes a tough real-estate/commodities market, expects a housing downturn "like we haven't seen since 1989–91," and is building a **nine-building residential project in Somerton** (tallest five storeys; a 3–4-year build). [[raw/doug_update_jvl-march-23-2025.md]]

## May 26 2026 — "turning the corner", and status quo until September 1

A short call ([[wiki/meetings/2026-05-26-doug-skrepnek-deferral-turning-the-corner.md]]). Val gave a careful business update and asked, in effect, to keep paying as he is. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Doug_Skrepnek_Deferral_and_Turning_the_Corner-May-26-2026.md]]

- **Val's framing:** "I'm very cautious in how I'm gonna say this. Um I think we're at the juncture of turning the corner." Reasons: markets outside Georgia "are starting to show results, and we're starting to see sales"; the gaming product was rolled out "in two more markets" and orders are coming. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Doug_Skrepnek_Deferral_and_Turning_the_Corner-May-26-2026.md]]
- **Georgia:** "we are still doing poorly, but it seems like we found the reason"; Val believes "by August, we're going to probably prove to be back in the market again." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Doug_Skrepnek_Deferral_and_Turning_the_Corner-May-26-2026.md]]
- **The financial (transactional) product:** at "alpha test"; beta "with operators in locations" probably in July; "we're going to roll it out in the first ten locations by July. First." Prime focus Georgia; markets Georgia, Texas, Nebraska; into 2027 with "Georgia, Nebraska, South Dakota and Texas" for both the gaming and the financial product. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Doug_Skrepnek_Deferral_and_Turning_the_Corner-May-26-2026.md]]
- **Cash:** "We are physically strained with cash and we're operating on fumes", with a stronger cash position hoped for toward year-end and, into 2027 "if everything materializes", "much stronger position than we have ever been". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Doug_Skrepnek_Deferral_and_Turning_the_Corner-May-26-2026.md]]
- **The business model, as Val put it to a lender:** "we were selling;. We're not operating machines… we're not participating in profit"; the transactional product should carry recurring revenue and a high margin — "the moment our revenue gets over eleven million dollars Canadian, we are truly printing money." Doug's point that building machines to sell ties up capital: Val agreed "That will have an effect… this affects us for a year, but not more." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Doug_Skrepnek_Deferral_and_Turning_the_Corner-May-26-2026.md]]
- **The sale horizon:** Doug asked whether the plan to sell within "three to five years" still stands; Val: "Nothing changed for me." Doug's view that by 2028 Val "may not be in a position to liquidate" — Val: "very sober thinking." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Doug_Skrepnek_Deferral_and_Turning_the_Corner-May-26-2026.md]]
- **The ask and the outcome.** Doug summed it up as "hey, I can do what am doing now. That's all I can do." Val: "let's die arise to have another call on September first… maybe I will not do the full amount or the full increase. Maybe I could do partial increase. I want to give you more money." The call closed on "until then, it's status quo" (speaker labels mix on this line). Val also said he would follow up by email with Jacob ("I'll try to do it by friday, but maybe maybe over the weekend") — most likely [[wiki/people/jacob-kriszenfeld.md|Jacob Kriszenfeld]] (by context). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Doug_Skrepnek_Deferral_and_Turning_the_Corner-May-26-2026.md]]

## Aug 7 2026 — the cash-call phone call

Val called Doug first, before any paper went out, to tell him JVL is out of operating cash and he must make a **~$1M cash call**. Doug did not doubt the books ("the books aren't the issue") and steered Val away from a bare cash-call letter — some partners might respond badly after years of silence — toward **short-term "pref debt"** that sits in front of everyone and is secured by JVL's approved CRA refund, with a pay-direction so the lender is repaid automatically when the refund lands. Doug offered to **co-fund** it (illustratively ~$300K each to bridge ~90 days) and, asked directly, confirmed he would participate as pref debt. Full context on [[wiki/topics/jvl-2026-funding-crisis-capital-call.md]]. [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-07-funding-crisis-cash-call-doug-skrepnek.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_JVL_Funding_Crisis_Cash_Call_Doug_Skrepnek-August-7-2026.md]]

- Doug recommended the **CRA complaints-department** route to speed the refund (he got a dated payment letter ~5 weeks after complaining; Val was paid within 5 days last year). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JVL_Funding_Crisis_Cash_Call_Doug_Skrepnek-August-7-2026.md]]
- Doug's read on the other partners: the Muzzo family likely won't respond, Joe DiTomasso "had choice words" ~18 months ago, and Steve would say "do what you got to do" but not answer a cash call. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JVL_Funding_Crisis_Cash_Call_Doug_Skrepnek-August-7-2026.md]]
- **Decision:** no cash-call letter yet — Val takes the weekend to weigh the pref-debt bridge and call Doug when ready. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_JVL_Funding_Crisis_Cash_Call_Doug_Skrepnek-August-7-2026.md]]

## Aug 12 2026 — structuring the bridge loan

The Aug-7 weekend-thinking discharged into a follow-up call ([[wiki/meetings/2026-08-12-financial-forecast-bridge-loan-doug-skrepnek.md]]) where Val brought a two-year cash forecast and the two settled the **bridge-loan (pref-debt) structure**. Full economics on [[wiki/topics/jvl-2026-funding-crisis-capital-call.md]] (Aug 12); Doug's part: [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Financial_Forecast_Bridge_Loan_Doug_Skrepnek-August-12-2026.md]]

- **Doug does bridge loans for a living and can close in ~14 days once he has the paper.** Structure he proposed: preferred debt in front of shares and other debt, **secured by the approved CRA SR&ED refund** ("I'd rather have that than their house") with a legally mandated pay-direction (CRA pays → JVL sends the cheque straight to the debtor). Pricing: **4% fee + 10% annual interest**, open, repayable on 30 days' notice; a facility up to ~$2M, drawable ~$600K/quarter, repayable in full when the credit lands. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Financial_Forecast_Bridge_Loan_Doug_Skrepnek-August-12-2026.md]]
- **Possible co-lenders:** Val, Doug, and Doug's contact **Ray Marks** (illustratively ~$400K each ≈ $1.2M). Doug endorsed Val charging the company the same 4% + 10% on Val's own $400–500K injection ("more than fair"). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Financial_Forecast_Bridge_Loan_Doug_Skrepnek-August-12-2026.md]]
- **Doug's gating condition:** he won't commit until he sees the **CRA approval** ("As soon as you send that to me, I'm good"); Val is sending it plus the monthly forecast. §15.8/§15.9 clear — loan-structure and company-forecast economics only; no named-person compensation and no account/tax identifiers recorded. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Financial_Forecast_Bridge_Loan_Doug_Skrepnek-August-12-2026.md]]

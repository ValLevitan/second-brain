# Vitaly Timokhov

Vitaly Timokhov is the **cross-border tax lawyer** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] retained in 2026. The derived meeting notes place him at **Gardiner Roberts** and describe him as cross-border tax counsel. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]] He first appears in this wiki on the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-07-departure-tax-trust-and-bad-debt.md|Sept 7 2026 call]], by which point he had already **sent the retainer** and begun pulling information from Val's accountants. ## The three corporate projects (Sept 7 2026)

Vitaly summarised his corporate workstream as three projects, "each of which will actually give you a benefit, long-term benefit": [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]

1. **Save the tax credits.** Work out whether the Canadian-resident trust over [[wiki/organizations/jvl-labs.md|JVL Labs]] will work — "if not, what will work?" — to preserve CCPC status and with it roughly **$1 million a year** in tax credits. Currently **on hold**: [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-07-jvl-labs-ccpc-trust-held-pending-the-gaar-ruling.md]]. See [[wiki/topics/sred-tax-credit.md]].
2. **Bad-debt utilisation.** Establish whether the **$26 million loan from 680 to JVL** is a bad debt and how to use it. See [[wiki/topics/jvl-corporate-loans-and-shareholder-financing.md]].
3. **Move the JVL US subsidiaries out of the corporation** to [[wiki/people/inna-levitan.md|Inna]] personally, "so there's no future double taxation, and you can sort of transfer that business to United States." Explicitly **only under consideration** — "I'm thinking about it. I'm not saying that that's going to be the case." See [[wiki/organizations/jvl.md]].

## How he works

- **Timetable.** "We have until the October the 30th to complete the whole work," with **2025 filable whatever else happens**. He picked September deliberately because accountants are back from vacation and not yet busy. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]
- **He pushes the accounting work back to the accountants.** On working with **Yasha** — the accountant he will call to line up scope, named **Yasha Bushuev** in the derived notes [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]] — "he will ask me to do 90% of work. I'll push like a little bit back to him, all the accounting work, just to make sure that… he bears some responsibility. I will ask him to prepare all the drafts and I will then review." Vitaly keeps the legal paperwork and the specific instructions for himself. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]
- **He was blunt about the financial statements he had been given.** Of the pack [[wiki/people/sean-riley.md|Sean Riley]] sent: "I'm asking information and he sends me like this [expletive] financial statement… I cannot do my work based on this. But anyway, I'll tell him that." Earlier in the call: "he gave me financial statements with a total BS." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]
- **He tests his own positions against a peer group.** He sits in a **Bay Street tax group** that meets regularly — "we kind of ask stupid questions once in a while from each other… there's some fairly sophisticated guys out there" — and said he would put the GAAR question to them. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]
- **He volunteers where he is guessing.** He repeatedly labelled his own statements as assumptions ("it's an assumption, it's not a fact"), accepted correction without argument when Val contradicted him, and closed a subject the moment it was settled. He also asked Val to send him the transcript of the call. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]
- **Contact cadence.** Val asked to hear from him by the end of the week; Vitaly: "I will keep in touch with you all the time." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]

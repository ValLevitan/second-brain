# Julian Puerta

Julian Puerta is JVL's man in **Colombia**. A meeting-notes header lists him as "Julian Puerta — Colombia JVL" [[raw/julian_puerta_and_peter_new_imigration_the_2025-03-29.md]], and on a long call with Val he speaks as an insider of the **EDS** operation in Colombia ("our operation"), where JVL's machines run. [[raw/julian _puerta_nebraska-march-29-2025.md]] Per Val on the Texas call, JVL **hired him "a year ago … to work for JVL in Colombia"** (≈ spring 2024) with the standing plan "to transfer you to the US"; meanwhile he consults for [[wiki/organizations/eds-colombia.md|EDS]] on a monthly retainer, managing all its operations day-to-day (retainer figure held §15.8). [[raw/meeting_with_julian_puerta_texas_eds.md]] As of late March 2025 Val is recruiting him for a **US role** — the Texas distributorship build-out ([[wiki/projects/texas-used-machine-distribution.md]]) and days later the Nebraska / upper-Midwest market.

## The Texas / EDS-distributor plan (≈Mar 22 2025)

On the weekend call before the Nebraska pitch ([[wiki/meetings/2025-03-22-julian-puerta-texas-eds-distribution.md]]), Julian presented his **Texas market research** (learned largely from his Miami-based distributor friend **Diana Safra**) and Val laid out the plan: Julian relocates to the US as a JVL employee and builds the used-machine distribution through EDS, with Julian and [[wiki/people/eliana.md|Eliana]] splitting EDS's ~20% distributor share 50-50 — full terms on [[wiki/projects/texas-used-machine-distribution.md]]. Val was blunt about the condition: "either through distributor or direct it still has to be through you … if you're not running, I am not interested." Julian accepted a lower JVL salary during the build-out as fair ("it would not be fair to keep getting the same amount of money for doing a remote job" — figures held §15.8) and planned a trip: Pennsylvania week, Miami (meet Val "on the 9th", plus Diana Safra), Texas if the agenda allows. [[raw/meeting_with_julian_puerta_texas_eds.md]]

## The Nebraska / US recruitment (Mar 29 2025)

- Val pitched Julian on moving to the **Nebraska / South Dakota** region, describing it frankly: cold and snowy, "perfect for business," entertainment there is hunting and fishing, wealthy states (South Dakota oil, Nebraska "huge farming"). Julian's response: "for me, living in Nebraska would be just fine. I don't care." (The transcript has no speaker labels; attributions here are by context.) [[raw/julian _puerta_nebraska-march-29-2025.md]]
- **Terms:** Val said he would keep **the same proposal he had earlier given Julian for Puerto Rico** "for that area, too" — both men admitted they no longer remember the proposal's details, only that it was "very generous." Val is to **text Julian the basics**; Julian asked for **four or five days** to "draw some numbers and come back." [[raw/julian _puerta_nebraska-march-29-2025.md]]
- *Inference:* this recruitment matches the open staffing gap recorded on [[wiki/projects/nebraska-skill-market.md]] — Val wanted one service person to cover the Nebraska / South Dakota / North Dakota tri-state (Mar 24) but hadn't decided whom to appoint.

[[raw/julian _puerta_nebraska-march-29-2025.md]]

[[raw/julian _puerta_nebraska-march-29-2025.md]]

## History

- **≈May 2024 (date inferred from content; pre-window source):** the long pre-hire negotiation ([[wiki/meetings/2024-05-15-julian-puerta-mexico-negotiation.md]]). At the time Julian ran the **[[wiki/organizations/en-grande.md|En Grande]]** Ruta operation in Medellín and kept a **five-person crew in Mexico City** on his own payroll (a location-contact/commercial man, two technicians, an office manager/accountant, an accountant/administrator — modest total cost, figures held; "ten years… five years" tenures) maintaining "mainly maples," plus a rented warehouse and a lawyer/accountants/logistics person "on the side." He pitched Val on splitting his time across En Grande, a Mexico JVL role, and a Ruta-Mexico venture with **Guillermo Santillán** (Producciones Móviles, one of only two Mexican licences allowing remote/small-venue casino operation; built ~20,000 terminals that sit "getting rusted" after copying the idea, ~500 barely working); Val refused the split — "it's very difficult to sit one ass on two chairs," "I do not want to share a failure and I don't believe in your success [on Ruta]" — demanding full-time focus and floating a Mexico casino push (300 machines by October on Eliana's redirected money). Compensation asks/counters were negotiated (held §15.8); no agreement closed. [[raw/julian_puerta_jvl_mexico.md]]
- His stated deal history: Val sold the En Grande Echo machines at ~10% margin "chiseled … to eight percent" by Julian, who says he pushes every deal to the limit for whichever company's "hat" he wears. [[raw/julian_puerta_jvl_mexico.md]]
- Mexico market knowledge he brought (≈May 2024): operators becoming buyers rather than participation-only; Baja California the fastest-growing state (~61% post-pandemic) with Nuevo León the other top-revenue region; his target groups Logran(d), Codere, Caliente, Golden Lion Entertainment (~180–200 machines placeable); Caliente ~7,300 slots in ~20–28 locations; Logran backed by an American fund. [[raw/julian_puerta_jvl_mexico.md]]

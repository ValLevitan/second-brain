# Joseph DeTommaso

Joseph DeTommaso is an **external private lender/creditor** to JVL — the holder of a loan [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] has been repaying over several years. On the **July 11 2025** call Val phoned him for a courtesy business update and to ask for current financial statements "for the file here," describing him as someone he had not spoken with "a couple of years." The relationship is a lender↔borrower one: Val volunteers company performance and loan status, and Joseph asks only for financial statements and to meet in person. [[raw/update_joseph_detommaso-july-11-2025.md]]

## The loan (as of Jul 11 2025)

- JVL paid Joseph **$50,000 a month up to and including December 2024**. [[raw/update_joseph_detommaso-july-11-2025.md]]
- Because 2025 revenue was expected to fall sharply (legislative changes in the principal market plus "two more serious competitors"), and because JVL had cash on hand and did not want interest to keep accruing, they **prepaid the remaining principal in advance** — the remaining balance was **~$585,000** ("plus minus $10,000"), paid off (Val believed) in **February 2025**. The **entire principal of the loan has now been repaid in full**. [[raw/update_joseph_detommaso-july-11-2025.md]]
- The **accumulated interest** on the loan over the years — Val clarified he means **interest on all loans — is ~$2.9 million**. [[raw/update_joseph_detommaso-july-11-2025.md]] *(Inference, not asserted: this ~$2.9M closely matches the ScrapNeck off-books accrued-interest figure of $2,912,553 booked at June-13 year-end — see [[wiki/topics/jvl-corporate-loans-and-shareholder-financing.md]]; whether Joseph's loan is one of the schedule's named notes is an open question, queued, not resolved.)*
- JVL expects to **restart repayment of this loan as of January 2026**. [[raw/update_joseph_detommaso-july-11-2025.md]]

## Contact / relationship notes

- Joseph and Val had not spoken in roughly a couple of years; Val apologized for not meeting face-to-face and said he wants to **come see Joseph in person** after his vacation to "go over a few things." [[raw/update_joseph_detommaso-july-11-2025.md]]
- Val told him the best contact is **SMS/call to his mobile**, or the **corporate email val@jvl.ca** — Val "doesn't check my leviton.me email too often." [[raw/update_joseph_detommaso-july-11-2025.md]]
- Both agreed "the market is dead for everyone now." [[raw/update_joseph_detommaso-july-11-2025.md]]

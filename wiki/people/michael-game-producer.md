# Michael (game producer)

Michael is a **game producer / designer on JVL's game side**, working closely with [[wiki/people/juan.md|Juan]] and reviewing the design team's output. Created as a distinct page under a descriptive title because the transcripts give only the first name.

> **Identity flagged (see Owner Decisions).** Prior batches flagged a "Michael" and asked whether he is the same person as [[wiki/people/misha.md|Misha]] (the used-machine retrofit/technical lead). This batch shows Michael clearly acting as a **game producer/designer** (commenting on comp art, game math, help screens, alongside Juan) — a role distinct from Misha's retrofit work — which is why he is recorded separately, not merged. Confirm identity before merging.

## Role

- On the Feb 21 2025 game-design review Michael is treated as the **producer/designer** whose sign-off (with Juan) matters: e.g. work is "approved by Michael and Juan," and he defended the industry-standard progressive design against Val's objections (min-bet eligibility, the "deceptive but standard" jackpot framing). See [[wiki/topics/slot-game-design-principles.md]]. [[raw/game_design_meeting_february_21_2025.md]]
- He is a target play-tester for the Miranda→math ("mass") test tool (running the game with placeholders to check the math). [[raw/game_design_meeting_february_21_2025.md]]
- On the Feb 26 branching-workflow coaching, Michael is the reviewer who should comment **only at pull-request time**, not on unfinished branch work; Aaron praised him as "so tactful" a commenter. He and Juan are to be rolled into the Git structure **last**, after the four core designers are comfortable (~30 days). [[raw/Evolution_of_Migration_to_GitHub_for_Graphic_designers_2025-02-26.md]]

## Game-design review (Feb 28)

- Michael reviewed the **FireEyes Wink** attract-mode build ("Mike" liked it) and led the **bet-up incentive** resolution: although on Feb 21 he argued *against* glamorising higher bets, here he proposed the concrete mechanism the group adopted — the **"jackpot rush" flame on bet buttons 3/4/5** (growing flame + a unique sound), without any textual claim to the player. He kept restating his **"bankroll philosophy"** caution (many players stay at min-bet regardless; don't make the min-bet player feel "less than"). See [[wiki/topics/slot-game-design-principles.md]]. [[raw/Game design part 1 - Feb-28-2025.md]]
- On the **minimum bet**, Michael pushed to raise Fortune Foo Paws' minimum from 60 to at least **70 credits** (70/88 are "the new norms"), and framed reserving higher bets for the next cabinet. Val ceded final creative/visual control of the game to Michael ("it's Michael"), positioning himself as a contributing partner. Michael is to show V-Deck **creative** for the bet-up transformation. [[raw/Game design part 1 - Feb-28-2025.md]] [[raw/Game design part 2 - Feb-28-2025.md]]
- Referenced (Feb 28 Git check-in): Michael and [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim]] are jointly writing the **Game Design Document (GDD)** in Confluence — not yet committed to the repo. See [[wiki/topics/docs-as-code-and-atlassian-exit.md]]. [[raw/Git_progress_check_in_2025-02-28.md]]

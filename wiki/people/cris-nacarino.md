# Cris Nacarino

**Cris Nacarino** is a member of JVL's **finance back-office** (see [[wiki/topics/jvl-finance-back-office.md]]) who works accounts-payable and the general-ledger / chart-of-accounts effort for **JVL Systems and JVL Labs**. She is a standing participant in the 2026 finance working sessions alongside [[wiki/people/igor-donis.md|Igor Donis]] and [[wiki/people/laura.md|Laura Escorcia]], with [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda Giorgi]] translating English↔Spanish.

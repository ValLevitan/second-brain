# Cris Nacarino

**Cris Nacarino** is a member of JVL's **finance back-office** (see [[wiki/topics/jvl-finance-back-office.md]]). She joined as a Peru-based bookkeeper/accountant under [[wiki/people/alka-sood.md|Alka Sood]] in 2025 and by 2026 works accounts-payable and the general-ledger / chart-of-accounts effort for **JVL Systems and JVL Labs**, a standing participant in the 2026 finance working sessions alongside [[wiki/people/igor-donis.md|Igor Donis]] and [[wiki/people/laura.md|Laura Escorcia]], with [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda Giorgi]] translating English↔Spanish. Transcripts spell her name both **Chris** and **Cris**; the two were confirmed by the owner (2026-08-10) to be one person. [[raw/financial_alka_alignment-march-24-2025.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_GL_Accounts_Systems_Labs-August-3-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Chart_of_Accounts_Alignment_Systems_Labs-August-5-2026.md]]

Part of the Peru-based staff kept on after the Peru office closure (moved to remote/subcontractor arrangements; compensation specifics held §15.8). [[raw/Finance - Alka.md]]

## Role

- **Aug 3 2026 (GL working session):** Cris explained that most JVL Labs *purchases* were done in Business Central (by "Michael"), but most Labs *payments* — to contractors/vendors, from the Canadian bank — were recorded in Navision, so there is more sitting in Navision than Val had assumed. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_GL_Accounts_Systems_Labs-August-3-2026.md]]
- **Aug 5 2026 (chart-of-accounts alignment):** one of the three finance people (with Igor and Laura) whose joint agreement is now required before any proposed account change is accepted. Val also asked her to show progress on the **EDS reconciliation** received from Sandra Carmona. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Chart_of_Accounts_Alignment_Systems_Labs-August-5-2026.md]]

## History

- **Mar 24 2025:** the busiest of the finance team (~90%); ~20% of her load to be shifted to Laura. [[raw/financial_alka_alignment-march-24-2025.md]]
- **Apr 15 2025 — the commission test:** Alka gave her the task of **calculating Felipe's sales commission** (pulling data from several systems into a spreadsheet template). "She turned it around on time. And it was accurate" — and she caught returned customer checks and adjusted the commission herself rather than posting mechanically. It took her ~2 hours where Alka needed ~4 ("I said to Grace when she did the sales commission" — "Grace" read as an ASR slip for Cris in the same anecdote; labeled inference). Alka on Val's question whether she could be his next controller: **"Yes"** — with the caveat that the language barrier would be a problem. Val: "I always hear wonderful things about Chris." Also slated for analytics work and (per the February plan) the sales-tax and commission calculations. [[raw/finance_update-apr-15-2025.md]] [[raw/Finance - Alka.md]]
- **2025 finance back-office (Sep–Oct):** thorough, but the language barrier meant Alka often worked through Igor to translate; as procurement shifted to JVL Systems (US) and Mary came off A/P, Cris's Systems payment proposals grew (Mary trained her). Workload readings tracked on [[wiki/topics/jvl-finance-back-office.md]]. [[raw/finance_recurring_meeting-september-9-2025.md]] [[raw/finance_recurring_meeting-october-7-2025.md]]

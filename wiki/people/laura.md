## Role

- **Apr 7 2025:** receiving the last small piece of [[wiki/people/hector.md|Hector]]'s day-to-day accounting handover "this week." [[raw/finance_recurring-april-7-2025.md]]

I don't know what that is" — she is "the only person that I'm not really sure of what's keeping them busy," and Alka will spend time with her this month, talking **directly with translation** rather than through Hector ("I get the story, the right story … My trust level is low" — about translated versions, not Laura). Val floated splitting part of Hector's controllership checks between Laura and Chris for cross-control. [[raw/finance_update-apr-15-2025.md]]

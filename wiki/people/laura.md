# Laura

Laura is a member of JVL's **finance back-office** (managed via [[wiki/people/alka-sood.md|Alka Sood]]; see [[wiki/topics/jvl-finance-back-office.md]]). She does the **local accounting for the Colombian operation**. [[raw/financial_alka_alignment-march-24-2025.md]]

## Role

- **Mar 24 2025:** ~26% utilised, mostly Colombia local accounting; assigned the credit-card reconciliation and ~20% of Chris's load. [[raw/financial_alka_alignment-march-24-2025.md]]
- **Apr 7 2025:** receiving the last small piece of [[wiki/people/hector.md|Hector]]'s day-to-day accounting handover "this week." [[raw/finance_recurring-april-7-2025.md]]
- **Apr 15 2025:** on Alka's new scope-of-work control sheet Laura shows ~81% utilisation, but only because Alka credits her "for spending half her time on doing the Colombian accounting. I don't know what that is" — she is "the only person that I'm not really sure of what's keeping them busy," and Alka will spend time with her this month, talking **directly with translation** rather than through Hector ("I get the story, the right story … My trust level is low" — about translated versions, not Laura). Val floated splitting part of Hector's controllership checks between Laura and Chris for cross-control. [[raw/finance_update-apr-15-2025.md]]

- **Apr 14 2026:** owns the GA-COAM accounts-receivable regularization — on the brief [[wiki/meetings/2026-04-14-accounts-receivables-ga-coam.md|Accounts Receivables GA-COAM]] wrap-up (with Val and [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]]) she was directed to finish regularizing the AR reports (statements of account) and to work hand-in-hand with [[wiki/people/lev-rudky.md|Lev Rudky]] to extract **native statements of account directly from Business Central**. Continues her Apr-10 receivables-cleanup ownership on [[wiki/topics/jvl-finance-back-office.md]] (Apr 14 2026). - **Jun 29 2026:** handled the **MSII customer refund** ([[wiki/meetings/2026-06-29-msii-refund-situation.md]], with Val and [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]]) — she is to attach the customer statement and file a **"request approval" for return of funds** in the system for Val to approve electronically, then issue the payment; directed to **work with [[wiki/people/lev-rudky.md|Lev Rudky]]** in case the refund-approval step is not yet configured. See [[wiki/topics/jvl-finance-back-office.md]] (June 29 2026). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MSII_Refund_Situation-June-29-2026.md]]

At the Aug-3 [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-03-gl-accounts-systems-labs.md|GL working session]] and Aug-5 [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-05-chart-of-accounts-alignment-systems-labs.md|chart-of-accounts alignment]] Laura (with Igor Donis and Cris Nacarino) argued for preserving GL traceability — keep and rename existing accounts rather than build a company from scratch (which took "Daniel" ~8–10 months to configure and re-link), because "every dollar has a history"; she flagged that Navision reversals are technically painful.

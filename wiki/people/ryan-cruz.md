# Ryan Cruz

Ryan Cruz is the **president and co-owner** of **[[wiki/organizations/nts-nebraska-technical-services.md|NTS (Nebraska Technical Services)]]**, the largest gaming route operator in the Omaha, Nebraska market — a family-owned business he runs with his sister Sarah. [[wiki/people/denis-desmarais.md|Denis Desmarais]] met him and Sarah on JVL's behalf and reported back to [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] on May 29 2025. [[raw/denis_ntc_nebraska-may-29-2025.md]]

## What is known (Denis's report, May 29 2025)

- **Owner/president of NTS**, a family business (Denis compared it to JVL: mother-and-father-started, family-owned). His sister **Sarah** is co-owner. NTS is familiar with JVL — they used to buy JVL countertop video games "back in the days." [[raw/denis_ntc_nebraska-may-29-2025.md]]
- NTS holds **~80–85% market share in Omaha** (the eastern Nebraska market); the western market past Lincoln they cover in **partnership with [[wiki/organizations/jj-operator.md|J&J]]**. [[raw/denis_ntc_nebraska-may-29-2025.md]]
- Ryan **lives in Lincoln**, where NTS grew its share **from zero to ~35% over the last two years** and is still growing aggressively. [[raw/denis_ntc_nebraska-may-29-2025.md]]
- Very interested in taking on a JVL product line and in the deal for **~300 refurbished machines from South America**; Denis described him and Sarah as "very, very nice people" and was unusually excited about the partnership. [[raw/denis_ntc_nebraska-may-29-2025.md]]

# Byron Booth

**A board member and lobbyist of [[wiki/organizations/arc-gaming.md|ARC Gaming]].** [[wiki/people/georgios-zazanis.md|George Zazanis]]: "**Our lobbyist, one of our board members, Byron Booth.**" The surname is said only once, in this Oct-13 source [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]].

## What the sources say

- **Aug 27 2026.** At the coffee at ARC, an ARC speaker said he "**might bring Byron**" to JVL's office. Reading this Byron as Byron Booth is an **inference** — see [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-27-arc-coffee-fleet-swap-and-transactional-pitch.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Coffee_Fleet_Swap_and_Transactional_Pitch-August-27-2026.md]].
- **Oct 13 2026, at Intralot.** [[wiki/people/christos-tzoumaras.md|Chris]] of [[wiki/organizations/intralot.md|Intralot]]: "**Byron… is a board member for [ARC]. And he was our CEO for years, and he was the lobbyist also for Intralot.**" And: "**We know Byron for twenty years.**" No surname is given there; the link is an **inference** from the ARC board role [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Intralot_Machine_Lockouts_Test_Lab_and_Route_Partnership-October-13-2026.md]].
- **Oct 13 2026, ARC lunch** ([[wiki/meetings/2026-10-13-arc-zazanis-lunch-trade-in-and-2027-volume.md|1:00 PM]]). The board owns Ark… there is a fund, and the fund has board members, and Byron sits on the board. The same fund… they are the ones that they are behind United.**" He also "**knows… very, very well**" the Wyoming lottery director [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Zazanis_Lunch_Trade_In_and_2027_Volume-October-13-2026.md]].

# Denis Desmarais

Denis Desmarais ("Dennis" in the transcript) is a gaming-industry veteran and old acquaintance of [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] from **Montreal**, now living in **Florida**, whom Val is courting for the planned **South Dakota / Nebraska border office** ([[wiki/meetings/2025-04-05-denis-desmarais-south-dakota-nebraska.md]]). He also appears (as a name only) in the 2026 JVL contractor roster's "not yet in the wiki" list ([[wiki/topics/jvl-contractor-roster.md]]) — this is his first transcript appearance. [[raw/denis_desmarais_south_dakota_nebraska-April-5-2025.md]]

## Background (his own account, Apr 5 2025)

- Left Montreal "a while back," lives in Florida. [[raw/denis_desmarais_south_dakota_nebraska-April-5-2025.md]]
- **Moncton, New Brunswick track record:** ran an office there "for Daniel for five years" — started alone, grew to three–four people, **first year ~$800,000 in sales**, and "when I left the office five years later, we were doing $2.5 million." Val: "It's very similar to how we started State of Georgia… you're very familiar with the job." [[raw/denis_desmarais_south_dakota_nebraska-April-5-2025.md]]
- Florida work dried up: "the gaming industry in Florida pretty much died" after the **Florida Gaming Commission Control** was formed ~two years earlier ("initiated by the Seminoles… to eliminate illegal gambling") and cease-and-desist letters went to the game rooms — "a lot of instability and a lot of risk to stay open here in Florida." He now does "a bit of everything." [[raw/denis_desmarais_south_dakota_nebraska-April-5-2025.md]]
- Familiar with northern climate/lifestyle (Quebec-like), which Val flagged as a fit for South Dakota. [[raw/denis_desmarais_south_dakota_nebraska-April-5-2025.md]]

## Market intelligence he offered

- **Texas:** "a bunch of mixed gaming depending on the jurisdiction… by county" — sweepstakes, skill, slot machines. He has two long-time Canadian friends operating there (one from "Tepford Mines" as transcribed — possibly Thetford Mines, QC, inference — and one from Montreal; one in San Antonio, one in Dallas–Fort Worth); the bigger one runs **~2,000 class-3 slot machines** unregulated ("a renegade") across the state. [[raw/denis_desmarais_south_dakota_nebraska-April-5-2025.md]]
- **His brother runs a sweepstakes system** with exactly one customer (who operates pool tables, jukeboxes and sweepstakes). Denis explained the sweepstakes legal model: internet-cafe style — the player buys internet time / a product / makes a donation (e.g. $20 → 2,000 free entries), a small share (~10%, e.g. $2 of $20) goes to a charity such as a war-vet association, and because the entries are free there is no "consideration" — defeating the three gambling elements (consideration, prize, chance). [[raw/denis_desmarais_south_dakota_nebraska-April-5-2025.md]]
- Pennsylvania/Wyoming: he confirmed skill games are present in Pennsylvania and that **Wyoming is a skill market as well**. [[raw/denis_desmarais_south_dakota_nebraska-April-5-2025.md]]

## The candidacy

- Denis: "if you have any interest for me, I can relocate. That's something I'm willing to consider." Val: "I am looking for somebody to take a job in… South Dakota and Nebraska" — a **one-or-two-man mixed-use office right on the border** (commercial front office + small warehouse to prepare machines), servicing operators across **Nebraska, South Dakota, North Dakota and Wyoming**; purely sales/service ("no vertical integration" in SD), in the Georgia service-first style ("Mr. Operator, what can I do for you?"). This continues the Mar-24 tri-state coverage gap and parallels the Mar-29 Julian Puerta pitch — see [[wiki/projects/nebraska-skill-market.md]]. [[raw/denis_desmarais_south_dakota_nebraska-April-5-2025.md]]
- The call was interrupted and rescheduled for **1:30 pm** the same day; Val to send the invite. Whether the follow-up happened is not in this source. [[raw/denis_desmarais_south_dakota_nebraska-April-5-2025.md]]

## Nebraska market development — the NTS meeting (May 29 2025)

By late May Denis was doing on-the-ground Nebraska market development for JVL and reported an "unusually exciting" Omaha meeting ([[wiki/meetings/2025-05-29-denis-nts-nebraska.md]]). [[raw/denis_ntc_nebraska-may-29-2025.md]]

- He met **[[wiki/organizations/nts-nebraska-technical-services.md|NTS (Nebraska Technical Services)]]** — owners **[[wiki/people/ryan-cruz.md|Ryan Cruz]]** and sister Sarah — the largest Omaha operator (~80–85% share), which already knew JVL's countertops, is dropping Bank Shot, and is interested in a JVL product line and the ~300 refurbished South-American machines. Denis: "it takes a lot to excite me… this really was a positive meeting." [[raw/denis_ntc_nebraska-may-29-2025.md]]
- Val directed him to line up sample-machine shipping and an **open, recorded on-site walkthrough** of the location business architecture (with Val-prepared questions) for early the following week, after Lincoln. Denis uses a recorder that auto-transcribes and syncs to his laptop. [[raw/denis_ntc_nebraska-may-29-2025.md]]

## Jun 16 2026 — how two Nebraska customers see him, and Val saying out loud that he is going

On Val's Nebraska field trip both operators he called on that morning knew Denis by name, and the two readings of him are very different. (The replacement decision itself was taken on Jun 5 — see [[wiki/projects/nebraska-skill-market.md]].)

- **The name.** [[wiki/people/rick-rees.md|Rick Rees]] at [[wiki/organizations/jj-operator.md|J&J Gaming]] asked Val how to say it — "I'm sure I'm pronouncing that wrong. Is it Dennis, or is it…" — and Val answered "**It's [a] French name.**" This is direct support for the corpus's long-standing reading that the transcripts' "Dennis" is **Denis**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JJ_Gaming_Nebraska_Site_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **J&J rate him well.** Rick: "As far as our relationship, **he's good. I like him**… He does [help], as much as we need him. I think **my technical guys probably work with him more than I do**. But yeah, he **answers anything and responds very quickly** to them." Rick also said Denis and one other JVL person "**both came out on the first seven and went to the locations with the guys and put them in**" *(quoted as transcribed — "the first seven" is garbled and is not interpreted; the second JVL person's name is rendered only as "One", and no name is asserted — see the note on [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md]]).* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JJ_Gaming_Nebraska_Site_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **He sold the Funambulist account.** At [[wiki/organizations/funambulist-gaming.md|Funambulist Gaming]], Heather Ackerman confirmed the company bought JVL machines and named Denis unprompted: "**Dennis has been here many times.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **Val told that customer, in the room, that Denis is being replaced.** "We're **replacing Dennis** here, so **Dennis will be gone**. We're bringing somebody else." Heather's response was to ask, later, "So Dennis is moving on to different pastures." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **The framing Val used, and which Heather adopted.** Val's pitch line — "**I make all the decisions in the company, so I can address it. Dennis, for example, cannot**" — uses Denis as the foil for his own authority. Heather put herself in the same category: "**like me, I'm like Dennis. I cannot — but I know who to get.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]

- **He had been in Nebraska that week and had just left for Florida.** In the [[wiki/meetings/2026-06-16-jj-gaming-bank-shot-and-nebraska-withholding.md|closing segment]] of the J&J visit, Rick asked "You have a meeting with **Dennis** tomorrow? In Canada, or is he still back down in **Florida**?" Val: "**He was here, but he just left to Florida.**" *(Inference by context: "Dennis" here is Denis Desmarais — Rick had discussed him by that name earlier the same day as JVL's Nebraska field rep. The transcript gives only the first name and no role, so the identification is flagged, not asserted.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JJ_Gaming_Bank_Shot_and_Nebraska_Withholding-June-16-2026.md]]

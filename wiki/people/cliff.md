# Cliff (JCM)

Cliff is JVL's contact at **[[wiki/organizations/jcm.md|JCM]]** (a sales/technical rep; surname not given in the source). He walked Val through JCM's bill-recycler product line on the May 20 2025 evaluation call. [[raw/JCM_meeting_Cliff__bill_recycler-may-20-2025.md]]

## Role

- On the May-20 call he laid out the trade-offs between JCM's recyclers — the end-of-life **iPro-RC**, the replacement **UBA Pro-RC** (friction-feed), and the twin-drum **UBA Pro RT** (Mylar-tape) — explained the ID-003/ID-008 protocols, and offered his software-engineering team's help if JVL adopts ID-008. He took actions to send RT/RQ brochures with dimensions, get pricing on the RT and UBA Pro-RC, provide the updated ID-003 protocol, and verify the MTBF figures Val questioned. See [[wiki/organizations/jcm.md]] and [[wiki/meetings/2025-05-20-jcm-bill-recycler-eval.md]]. [[raw/JCM_meeting_Cliff__bill_recycler-may-20-2025.md]]

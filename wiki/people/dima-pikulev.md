# Dima Pikulev

Dima Pikulev is a **low-level embedded / Linux contractor** JVL uses for board bring-up work. He is effectively semi-retired: Val says he only gives a small amount of time and "doesn't work because he needs to… he doesn't even want to work," so Val prefers **not** to depend on him. [[raw/alignment_elctro-mechanical-september-22-2025.md]]

## Role

- **June 26 2025 — new board is missing interfaces.** [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi]] reported that a new board **"Dima Pikulev" worked on** is built for an "account-top board" and is **missing some interfaces** for full machine work (detail unclear at the time). See [[wiki/topics/omni-core-advancement-and-game-templating.md]]. [[raw/OMNI_schedule_grooming-june-26-2025.md]]
- **Sept 22 2025 — candidate to rebuild the RockChip Linux.** On the electro-mechanical alignment, Pikulev came up as one option to **rebuild the Linux** for the cheap Chinese RockChip POS board so JVL's usual USB-image flashing works — but Val was reluctant (limited availability), and noted Pikulev **had not finished the "forward"/firmware for the core-control system** JVL is already waiting on for Flex. See [[wiki/meetings/2025-09-22-electro-mechanical-alignment-genadi.md]] and [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]]. [[raw/alignment_elctro-mechanical-september-22-2025.md]]

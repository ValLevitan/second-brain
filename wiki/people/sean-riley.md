## May 12 2025 — year-end call (met Val in person)

On the year-end finance call ([[wiki/meetings/2025-05-12-year-end-finance-sean-riley.md]]) Sean advised Val and [[wiki/people/alka-sood.md|Alka]] on claiming the **JVL Peru** ~$2.5M receivable as a **bad-debt write-off** on the JVL Labs books. Key points he gave: the loss can be claimed once Peru is **insolvent** (ceased ops, no assets) — 2024 if that happened by Dec 31 2024, else 2025; CRA support = proof Peru was officially wound up; JVL Labs' **filing deadline is June 30**; and he has consolidated JVL numbers only **up to 2023** (will send them so Val can prepare 2024). He confirmed **Nick** filed the US extensions and clarified the away-schedule (Nick off ~2 weeks, Sean off the week after). Detail on [[wiki/topics/jvl-finance-back-office.md]]. ## May 30 2025 — Georgia foreign-tax-credit call ([[wiki/meetings/2025-05-30-finance-georgia-taxes.md]])

Sean joined Val, [[wiki/people/alka-sood.md|Alka]] and a colleague ("Eno") to explain the **Canadian foreign-tax-credit** side of JVL Labs' Georgia exposure (the follow-up to the [[wiki/meetings/2025-05-27-georgia-tax-review-jvl-labs.md|May-27 US-side review]]). His key points: the FTC is **non-refundable** — usable only against **future Canadian tax within 5 years** — so with JVL Labs' ~$14M carry-forward losses (20-year life), the Georgia tax paid now is effectively out of pocket for years; keep JVL **Systems ~breakeven** via transfer pricing so it owes no US federal tax and Labs' Georgia profit stays low; time the **Peru** write-off (2024 vs 2025) to reduce the Georgia base; **Mexico** losses sit in the Mobi holdco (no Georgia offset). This answered Val's May-27 open question (does the credit become cash?): not refundable, only recoverable. See [[wiki/topics/jvl-finance-back-office.md]]. [[raw/finance_taxes_georgia-may-30-2025.md]]

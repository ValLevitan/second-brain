# Jamie Boyden (Illinois route operator; Val's route into Georgia)

**Jamie is an Illinois gaming-route operator and the man through whom [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] entered the Georgia market.** He is also, on Sept 16 2026, the person Val tried to recruit to operate [[wiki/topics/georgia-class-a-machines.md|Class A machines]] in Georgia.

**On the surname.** The only name spoken anywhere on the phone recording is **"Jamie"**. The derived notes give the full name as **Jamie Boyden** and record it as "**Confirmed by Val 2026-09-16**" — an owner confirmation captured inside the source, not a name the recording contains. The page is kept under that form with "Jamie" as an alias [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Jamie_Georgia_Class_A_Opportunity_and_Market_Outlook-September-16-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Jamie_Georgia_Class_A_Opportunity_and_Market_Outlook-September-16-2026.md]].

**His company is not named in any source in this corpus.** Not on the recording, not in the notes, not by Sloan. Recorded as an open question below rather than guessed at.

- **[[wiki/people/david-sloan.md|David Sloan]] (CEO, [[wiki/organizations/arc-gaming.md|ARC Gaming]]) put him into business in Illinois** and pulled him into South Dakota: "**He's a wonderful man. I put him in business in Illinois. Now he's buying locations, cafes**… **He's doing pretty good. I'm happy. I'm the one who dragged him in there**" — and on the difficulty of that market, "**the licensing is more difficult… you got to get [a] liquor license in every little jurisdiction**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Cashless_Suite_Demo_and_Class_A_Cabinet_Counter_Offer-September-16-2026.md]].

## His business, as he described it himself (Sept 16 2026)

All from the phone call [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Jamie_Georgia_Class_A_Opportunity_and_Market_Outlook-September-16-2026.md]]:

- **Scale.** "**We're still in the fifties, but we've got you know line of sight to seventy**" Illinois locations — and "**We're not far from being the largest operator in Illinois.**"
- **Not cafés.** "**they're more than just cafe… ninety ninety five percent revenue is from gaming and five to ten percent revenue is from liquor.**"
- **Deliberate defensive mix.** "**We have some neighborhood bars. We're diversifying our mix a bit because we're more defensive. We're also getting a broader player pool. We're loyalty based as well.**"
- **Pipeline and expansion.** "**probably twelve locations in the pipeline, or looking outside of Illinois. There are a couple of markets in Nebraska, and also a couple of footprints for Wisconsin and West Virginia.**"
- **His two-hub thesis:** "**the Plains and Midwest could be one hub, and the Northeast Atlantic Coast as another hub.**"
- **His own grade for the business:** "**You guys are great. We're good. We've got some work to do to be great, but we're good**" — followed by "**Out of Illinois, our next steps. We make sure we spend our money wisely. We build out a platform.**"

*(§15.8 hold — see the note at the foot of this page: he also described a management reshuffle at his company, splitting one senior role in two and looking for a multi-unit retail operator. The individual it concerns, the role change and the performance reasoning he gave for it are **not recorded here**.)*

## What he told Val about Georgia (Sept 16 2026)

Val called this "**to get your opinion**", and the opinion was the opposite of caution [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Jamie_Georgia_Class_A_Opportunity_and_Market_Outlook-September-16-2026.md]]:

- **Force the regulatory issue.** "**So you know how this goes. Don't you want to force the issue? Sell a bunch, force the issue, and then they have to change the rates. It's going to open up. It could open up the door to make Georgia a VLP market.**" *(**"VLP"** is as transcribed; the derived notes read it as **VLT**. Recorded as the transcript has it, with the notes' reading flagged as their inference.)* [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Jamie_Georgia_Class_A_Opportunity_and_Market_Outlook-September-16-2026.md]]
- **He checked the legality first, and Val did not dispute it.** "**And it's illegal under the current law**"; "**It's illegal under the current law, is it not?**" — Val: "**That's how I interpret it. That's how InComm interprets it. Everybody's confused.**"
- **The political read.** "**With Kemp's departure, we could get a** [change]… **he was not a fan of gaming… but would if the people** [voted] — "**if it got out of the… legislature, he would allow the… populace in Georgia to vote on it and cash gaming. I think sentiment is like over sixty percent in favor of cash gaming in Georgia. So if you… get the catalyst going to force change, could be kind of interesting.**" *(The 60%+ figure is **Jamie's own estimate**, given with "I think"; no source is named.)*

## What he said about Class A — and what Val asked him to do

- **He already knows the category and names who is behind it:** "**Oh yeah I know. Well that's Mike pushing those.**" No surname is given, and **"Mike" is not matched to anyone in this corpus** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Jamie_Georgia_Class_A_Opportunity_and_Market_Outlook-September-16-2026.md]].
- **His verdict on JVL building them:** "**Then I'd go for it. They're cheaper machines too, cheaper to make, cheaper to administer.**" On the size of it — Val's "**tens of thousands of machines that could go into the market**" drew "**You could certainly make millions**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Jamie_Georgia_Class_A_Opportunity_and_Market_Outlook-September-16-2026.md]].
- **Val's actual ask — bring him back to Georgia to operate it:** "**So I thought of bringing you back here again and operating it. And I could create a killer. I could make a killer price. I can actually change the**" — Jamie completing it: "**the economics**." **No terms of any kind were discussed** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Jamie_Georgia_Class_A_Opportunity_and_Market_Outlook-September-16-2026.md]].
- **His reaction was urgency:** "**It sounds like something that may not, you may not want to wait till October.**" Val held the date anyway [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Jamie_Georgia_Class_A_Opportunity_and_Market_Outlook-September-16-2026.md]].

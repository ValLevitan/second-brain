# James Reble

James Reble is JVL's day-to-day contact at Elevate, a **specialty lender** that financed a gaming operator's purchase of JVL machines and took a pledge on them (owner-confirmed 2026-07-13; Elevate is **not** a JVL customer — see [[wiki/organizations/elevate.md]]).

## Role

- Called Val to reconcile Elevate's EDC financing approval against what JVL is actually invoicing. Their original EDC approval covered about 700 machines described as "video lottery terminals," and James wanted to confirm JVL's current invoice (different unit prices and Flex model descriptions) is consistent with that approval. [[raw/james_reble_elevate_january_14_2025.md]]
- Val explained the price and description differences are just a function of hardware configuration and software (more screens, more games = higher price), but it is the same type of product — a video lottery terminal. [[raw/james_reble_elevate_january_14_2025.md]]
- James has a colleague who is Elevate's CFO (was supposed to join the call but could not be found). [[raw/james_reble_elevate_january_14_2025.md]] That CFO is **Scott Rambeau**, who called Val the next day about the pledge/item numbers (identification inferred — both are Elevate's CFO around the same dates). See [[wiki/people/scott-rambeau.md]]. [[raw/elevate_convo_w_scott_rambeau_january_15_2025.md]]
- He confirmed the plan: payment for the December shipment (the third 56-machine voucher) going out that week, with three more shipments to come, still targeting end of January / early February. He would release the final tranche once Mary shares the paperwork. [[raw/james_reble_elevate_january_14_2025.md]]

See [[wiki/organizations/elevate.md]] and [[wiki/organizations/edc.md]].

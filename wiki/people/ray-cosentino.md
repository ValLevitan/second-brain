# Ray Cosentino

Ray Cosentino is a senior executive of the Georgia master operator [[wiki/organizations/inamax-ultra.md|Inamax (Powered by Ultra)]], a JVL customer. The 2026 operator briefing lists him as **CFO**. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]] His exact office is disputed: a rival operator called a "Ray Constantino" the CEO on Aug 31 2026. That dispute is recorded, not resolved, on the org page. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

## Background (his own account, Feb 11 2026)

- He worked in the **Illinois casino business for 14–15 years**, then moved to the **lottery side with Intralot** (transcribed "Incelot"), which handled lottery contracts across **15 US states**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]
- He started as a **technician in the casino** and later worked "on the data analytics side in the casino world". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]
- He runs Inamax's performance data himself: he imports Intralot data, adds data from independent locations still on **Pulse**, and his own "SaaS" data (as transcribed), and works it all in **Tableau**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]
- Inamax's engineering team (13–16 people, mostly software) reports "fifty fifty, between myself and the owner of our company, Nick". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]
- Asked whose decision a trade-in would be, he said: "That'd be mine." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]

What he told JVL about the market, JVL's machines and the trial is on [[wiki/organizations/inamax-ultra.md]] (Feb 11 2026). The meeting stub is [[wiki/meetings/2026-02-11-inamax-ray-cosentino-georgia-market-trial.md]].

# Kenny Patel (Epic Amusement)

Owner of [[wiki/organizations/epic-amusement.md|Epic Amusement]], a large Georgia COAM master licensee. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]] Felipe has long described him as hard to deal with and hard to reach. [[raw/To-Do_Georgia_meetings-February-18-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_GA_COAM_Inventory_Pipeline_Review-July-7-2026.md]] Not to be confused with **Mike Patel** of GAMOA, who is mentioned at the same Aug-25 lunch as a separate person. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]

**Name.** The Aug-25 transcript calls him "Kenny" only (once "Kitty", a mishearing, and once "Chris Kenny"); the surname is from the derived notes and the calendar title. ## Background, as he told it (Aug 25 2026)

- In the industry "almost fourteen fifteen years"; before that "in a hotel business". He rented Epic's office on the terms he was first offered and has kept it since. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]
- In the past "me and Josh All Star was together" — consistent with the Allstar partnership another operator described on Sept 17 (see [[wiki/people/yash-patel.md]]). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]
- He calls himself "a stubborn guy": he refuses one brand (transcribed "IDD"/"IDT", not resolved) over an old distributor refusal. "It's my term or no terms." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]

## His positions at the Aug-25 visit

Full account on [[wiki/organizations/epic-amusement.md]] and [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-25-epic-amusement-kenny-patel-visit.md]].

- **Players want to handle cash.** "They like to put their money into machines manually", and the bill acceptor must stay — "It's not going to work in Georgia" without it. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]
- **The ATM cost falls on someone.** A normal store ATM costs about $3,000 and dispenses only $20 bills; JVL's recycler would cost $9,000–12,000, and existing ATM contracts at stores will be hard to cancel. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]
- **Compliance is what sells.** Georgia locations fear lottery citations and suspensions, so he is "leaning towards" a counter system that produces redemption reports for inspectors. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]
- **He called the idea the future.** He compared Val's plan to a cashier-less store: "that's a future… The other companies… is just a band-aid", and asked to see it working at JVL's office. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]

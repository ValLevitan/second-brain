# Salim Khambawala (Dixie Amusement; VP of the Georgia operators' association)

The **master licensee** behind [[wiki/organizations/dixie-amusement.md|Dixie Amusement]] (~70 Georgia COAM locations, south-side Atlanta), an officer of the state operators' association, and the operator JVL treats as the market's product tester and a route into the association board. [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] met him at his office/warehouse on **August 31 2026** — see [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-31-dixie-amusement-kiosk-wait-and-see.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]

*Name spelling is unsettled in the corpus: the Aug-31 derived notes write **Salim Khambawala**; the May-2025 source writes **Selim Kwambuwala**; Felipe's 2026 notes write only **Salim**. Kept under the most recent rendering with all variants as aliases, pending an owner call.* [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]] [[raw/felipe_georgia_market_update-may-29-2025.md]]

## Association roles

- **Vice president of the state association, in his own words (Aug 31 2026):** "So **I'm currently the vice president of the state association**, and I also do a buying group." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]
- **He identifies the body as GAMOA and stresses it is the only non-profit in the room.** "[InComm] is [a] for profit company. **JVL is for profit. IGT is for profit.** Sky is everybody for profit. **But GAMOA is not for profit. We wear [the] GAMOA head because we do this for our passion and the industry we live. Nobody pays us.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]
- **He sits on a four-person executive board.** Val: "How many people are executive? Four people." Salim: "Four executives." The board was due to meet **that same week — Wednesday, Thursday and Friday** — to debrief the trade show: "**it's like a debriefing of the whole. What we saw, what we didn't see, what we liked, what we didn't like, where did we go wrong.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]
- **He was brought onto the board about ten years ago** by the Fiery Pay principal and by "**Adam**" — surname rendered "**Adam Faus**" by Val in the same exchange. *(The corpus carries an **Adam Faust**, United Gaming's ex-CFO now at Skillmaster Pro, from Feb 2026 — whether these are the same person is **not stated in this source** and is left unresolved rather than merged.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]
- **On the association's next show:** the venue is changing and getting bigger — "We are also going to change venues… **Too big. So now, Georgia convention**" — with next year's planning starting immediately. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]

They mainly bought [altars]**" *(product name garbled and not resolvable from this source)*. ## How JVL gets in front of the group — and what he ruled out

Val asked directly: "**How do I sit down with you and your colleagues in the same room? Can I organize? Can I invite people? Can I invite a group?**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]

- **Dinners: no.** "**Dinner? I don't think so because it never works out.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]
- **Open houses: lukewarm, and Val closed the door himself.** Salim: "Open house… whenever they do it, everyone comes in." Val: "**People are tired of open houses**," and Salim agreed — "**Everybody's tired.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]
- **Val's reason: JVL is no longer a novelty.** "**Novomatic is new for you. That's why you would go to an open house. JVL is a known entity. We've been here for five years, so I don't see that happening.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]
- **The only route Val named as workable is Salim convening his friends personally.** "**But let me talk to your friends. You can only influence your friends. If you personally say, look, let's meet here for one hour, they'll come. Otherwise, I don't see it. I'm a realist.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]

## His reading of the Georgia regulatory position

The wait-and-see ruling this produced is on [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-31-dixie-defers-kiosk-investment-until-the-rules-settle.md]]. The reasoning is his, and it is the most complete operator-side account of Georgia's political mechanics in the corpus. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]

- **Why regulators keep it murky.** "**The worst reason is they know that's where they make funds. So when they find someone, they get fined. And they don't want to pick sides of anyone. So it's always murkier and murkier.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]
- **The Lottery regulates; someone else enforces.** "**the lottery is just the regulator. They are not the law maker or the enforcer. The enforcer is going to be GBI, the local police department, the local sheriff department.** If they get their hands into it and say, hey, the constitution which they are there to protect and enforce, we see it's [breached]… **It's an uphill battle to fight the state and the attorney general.**" See [[wiki/people/joe-kim.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]
- **On whether a kiosk is even in scope, he told the regulator's own argument back as a reductio.** "One guy said, 'What if I put a scale, a weighing scale?… **What if I put a scale under my machine? And every person who comes in first stands on it and weighs himself before he starts playing? Does it mean that it's not a [COAM]? It's a weighing scale?**'… **Lottery says it's not a part of it because it's doing something else besides a COAM machine which is being played**… Them saying no, kiosk is not part of the coin, **but it will become a part**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]
- **How the industry keeps its legal ground, in his telling.** "in **2003**, the [COAMs] were banned from Georgia. We were taken out… **It took us ten years to build that relation into… the legislation**… Then you have to keep pushing it in a way to make it more legit and more strong. **So every year we have to go back to them during legislation and talk to them. Even if we don't have anything to ask, we still have to keep it.**" And the standing risk: "**they sign one executive order and say, 'Hey, these [machines] are illegal; they are gambling.' And if the governor… signs it, [the] charter goes.**" His conclusion on the lawmakers themselves: "**Some of these guys don't even understand business. They've never had a business, but they run and sit at the capital and make decisions for us**… So **we have to take them on dinners and educate them.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]

## His view of the competitor field

- **His count of the market:** "There are many, many players in the market. **I think fifteen of them that we counted who are dealing with [kiosks] and processing and all that stuff.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]
- **Names he offered as introductions:** "**I can talk to Angela. I can talk to this Bob Perez, which does two in one. Then there is another company named Genesis**… **Access**… **all of them I know.**" *(No companies are attached to Angela or Bob Perez in this source; no pages created for them.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]
- **On a German kiosk vendor, which Val identified as a Gauselmann company:** Val — "That's a [Gauselmann] company… They call it [GeWeTe] from Germany" and "**It's a very overpriced product.**" Salim: "**Very overpriced.** But when I asked them, I said, 'Do you want this processing?'… He said, 'No, please.' We only said yes." *(The transcript renders these "Goselman" and "Gewetter"; the corpus has carried the German cash-dispenser maker as **Gauselmann / GeWeTe** since June 2025 — treating them as the same firm is an **inference**.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]
- **On Light & Wonder's posture:** "they said **they just want to sell kiosks. They don't want anything else. No monthly from the location or the master license or [InComm] or anything. But I think they are using [InComm].**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]
- **On TITO, which JVL built and he is stepping away from:** "**a year ago, everyone was talking about TITO, and you guys came up with TITO. But then the issues I hear from TITO is service.**" Felipe's answer in the room: JVL advertised "with TITO, without TITO, no problem," but always said TITO would mean more service calls and jams outside a casino setting — "**we strongly recommend our solution, which is jamless.**" Val's read of why it persists: "**the operators want to keep their dual setups.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]

## The Fiery Pay introduction

- **He is friends with the Fiery Pay principal and offered to open the door:** "**he's a good friend of mine. Him and Adam brought me to the board ten years ago. But I can talk to him**… **maybe I'll talk to them, so Adam, work with them. With them, I know for sure I have zero risk.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]

And I said I can't sign a non-disclosure agreement because we're a technology company. We're working on the same thing**… **And that's how we fell apart.**" *Nineteen days earlier the same impasse was recorded with the refusal running the other way — see the contradiction below.* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]
- **Salim's view of NDAs, offered as reassurance:** "**It's only challenging if you do something wrong and go to a judge, or else it's a piece of paper you can shred, right? It's the relation. I understand you are a big company. You don't want to take any risk about signing.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]
- **What Val would want from them:** "**with our device, if Fiery Pay is interested in processing our cards, we will manufacture that for us.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]

- **Jul 7 2026 (Felipe's note):** "Salim (**formerly VP at Gomoa**, now with Dixie)" — i.e. no longer holding the office. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_GA_COAM_Inventory_Pipeline_Review-July-7-2026.md]]

Recorded; **not** resolved, because "former president" and "current vice president" are not the same claim and neither source addresses the other.

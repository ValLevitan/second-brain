# Neel

**Neel** runs **operations** for [[wiki/organizations/akshar-gaming.md|Akshar Gaming]], and makes purchasing decisions for **Ananta Amusement** as well. He was one of the two men who came to the JVL Georgia office on **September 1 2026** in place of [[wiki/people/vishal-patel.md|Vishal Patel]] — see [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-01-narayani-akshar-mega-hit-demo-and-trade-in.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

**The spelling.** Val asked directly — "Sorry, I keep forgetting your name. **Neil. Neil.** Sorry, Neil. **Spells like American Neil?**" — and the answer in the transcript is "I have it's a **N E L**," with Val adding "**Double E.**" The derived notes write **Neel** throughout, which is the form used here; "Neil" — the only variant the transcript uses — is recorded as an alias. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

## His role

- **Everything operational, for both companies.** "**I handle all the operations, all the manufacturers**… **I look after the inventory, the revenues.**" Val's summary — "So operations manager" — went unchallenged. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]
- **He and [[wiki/people/jai.md|Jai]] make the purchasing decisions** for Akshar and Ananta both — "So you make purchasing decisions for both companies." "**Yes.**" — but the deal itself still routes through Vishal: "**Since [Vishal] isn't here, we need to go back and explain everything to him.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]
- **Not family.** Asked whether he is related to Vishal: "**Not directly. Same city**"; and to Val's "Second cousin of the third sister" — "**Not that. Just came to know.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

## What he actually contributed

The most valuable content in the meeting was his own, unprompted account of the card system his locations already run, walked through step by step with Val: the one-time ~$5,000 install for six machines, the **$13.30 per machine per month**, the complete removal of MudaGoose and re-wiring, the persistent plastic player card, and why gift cards still have to come from a separate provider. The full account is on [[wiki/organizations/akshar-gaming.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

He also supplied the two live constraints JVL did not have before:

- **The EasyLink play-through limitation as a player complaint**, not a spec sheet item — "**I don't want to play a full five hundred**" — and the fact that they are **removing EasyLink from one of their two locations**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]
- **The InComm location credit ceiling**, which the derived notes read as $3,000–$6,000 a week against locations paying out $50,000 — "**They need something with a higher payout.**" See [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

And he is the source of the **name change** and the **company structure** — Akshar Gaming as of late August 2026, Ananta Amusement as Vishal's separate ~400-machine company — which is the evidence behind the open contradiction on [[wiki/organizations/akshar-gaming.md]] and [[wiki/organizations/narayani-amusements.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

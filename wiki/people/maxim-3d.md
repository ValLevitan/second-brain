# Maxim (3D artist)

Maxim is a **3D artist in Minsk** whom [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] engaged to build a **high-poly 3D model of the [[wiki/topics/echo-home-gaming-device.md|ECHO]]** device, so promo images/video can be rendered at any angle and resolution instead of doing photo shoots. His day job is 3D artist at **Wargaming**; he also runs a small firm doing industrial design to order. [[raw/recap_with_andrei_klimovichonlineecho212025_2025-02-01.md]]

- His firm made a fully-3D promotional video (no source photos, only design/CAD files) for the tonometer company where Andrei's sister works, priced at **$5,000** turnkey; JVL only needs the model + renders, so cheaper. Could later be used for JVL's land-based slot machines too. [[raw/recap_with_andrei_klimovichonlineecho212025_2025-02-01.md]]

## Confirmation (Feb 21)

- The Feb 21 recap reconfirms Maxim (3D artist from **Wargaming**) is engaged for **high-poly product renders** of ECHO and the slot machines, to avoid expensive photoshoots. A **5,000-ruble** video is priced but not needed immediately; the high-poly model is the priority. [[raw/recap_meeting_andrei-February-21-2025.md]]

# Jacob Kriszenfeld

Jacob Kriszenfeld is an **external finance contact who works with [[wiki/people/doug-skrepnek.md|Doug Skrepnek]]** on JVL's bridge-loan / warrant-holder matter. He first appears **on tape** on the Aug-14 2026 call as the person pressing Val on **when the approved CRA refund actually pays**, because that timing is what turns the refund into real security for a bridge loan. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_CRA_Refund_Bridge_Loan_Participation-August-14-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_CRA_Refund_Bridge_Loan_Participation-August-14-2026.md]]

- **Role on the call.** He wanted to validate CRA's stated **August 31, 2026** assessment date — ideally by speaking to a CRA agent or seeing a screenshot from Val's CRA online account — and raised the risk that CRA could open a review and hold the money. He also proposed filing a CRA **service complaint** (a route he says he has used successfully), which Val explained does not apply before an assessment exists. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_CRA_Refund_Bridge_Loan_Participation-August-14-2026.md]]
- **He can bring capital — but Val declined a group.** Jacob said he has a **group that could raise the capital**; Val refused flatly ("I don't want anybody else in my business"), clarifying he would deal only with Doug directly if at all. Jacob framed it as Doug assembling a group that Val would not report to; Val still declined but kept the door open for Doug or Jacob to contribute personally, even on the last day. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_CRA_Refund_Bridge_Loan_Participation-August-14-2026.md]]
- **Affiliation (from the meeting notes, not the call).** The processing notes attribute Jacob to **Intellect Capital Corp** as the point of contact on the Skrepnek relationship, sourced from a separate handover document rather than this transcript — recorded here as an *inference/attribution*, not stated on the call.

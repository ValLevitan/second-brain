# Rick Rees

**Nebraska Operations Manager for [[wiki/organizations/jj-operator.md|J&J Gaming]]**, the route operator whose new Omaha office [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] toured on **June 16 2026** ([[wiki/meetings/2026-06-16-jj-gaming-nebraska-site-visit.md]]). He is Val's operational counterpart in Nebraska and the gateway to J&J's corporate decision-makers in Illinois. He placed himself precisely: "**I am Nebraska's operations people, but I have bigger** [above me]." [[raw/Meeting_Notes_JJ_Gaming_Nebraska_Site_Visit-June-16-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JJ_Gaming_Nebraska_Site_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]

*(He was previously recorded in prose on [[wiki/organizations/jj-operator.md]] with a note to promote him to a person page if he recurred with a real role. The June-16 transcript supplies a full career history, his reporting line and his own operating judgements, so the page is created here.)*

## Career (his own account, Jun 16 2026)

- **Started with J&J in 2013, in Illinois, as a technician** — "one of these guys putting machines together and servicing them." The company had set its first gaming machines in **2012**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JJ_Gaming_Nebraska_Site_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **Transferred within Illinois first**, opening a **new office in a new territory** and running it for about **three years**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JJ_Gaming_Nebraska_Site_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **Then back to Springfield**, "which is where I'm originally from," where he **operated that office for about five years**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JJ_Gaming_Nebraska_Site_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **Moved to Nebraska in "September of last year"** — September 2025, relative to this June-2026 conversation. He arrived to a small operation: J&J had been in Nebraska "about three years" running "just a small office… three employees and we took care of fourteen locations," before the state began regulating the market more closely. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JJ_Gaming_Nebraska_Site_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]

## What he runs and what he does not

- **He runs Nebraska operations** — the facility, the ~500-location Nebraska route, and the **14-person cash-collection team** (seven two-man routes a day, ~70 locations a day). Operational detail is on [[wiki/organizations/jj-operator.md]] (Jun-16 2026). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JJ_Gaming_Nebraska_Site_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **He does not handle purchasing or payment terms.** Asked who gives J&J terms: "That I don't deal with. **That all gets taken care of from my corporate office.**" Machine-buying decisions belong to **James Jansen** in Illinois — "he does all the analytics… he's the one that basically says, 'We're going to buy that or no.' So **it's all based on numbers**." **Jeff Harwick** is "overall our technical." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JJ_Gaming_Nebraska_Site_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **He reports his reporting line honestly under sales pressure.** When Val pressed for the decision-maker, Rick named both men and offered to make the call: "I'll call them. I'll talk to them and see, give them what you've given me and see if they're interested." Asked (jokingly) whether he would describe Val as a lunatic: "I won't say it like that, but I'm going to say **this guy has this great system that he feels would increase productivity in our day-to-day**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JJ_Gaming_Nebraska_Site_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **He is candid about the limits of his own knowledge** — on Iowa: "I haven't really dove into Iowa because **I am trying to get the process figured out before I really get crazy**"; on machine-level analytics he was open about not being sure what a column meant ("This one, I am not a hundred percent sure on"). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JJ_Gaming_Nebraska_Site_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]

## His read on Val's pitch (Jun 16 2026)

- **Receptive to the cash-recycling kiosk, cautious on the machine share.** On the recycler: "if it works as flawlessly as you say it is, I mean that could be **seamless**." But on Val's stated aspiration to take 100% of the transactional-terminal business and 50% of the machines: "**The fifty percent of the machine is going to be the difficult part, I think.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JJ_Gaming_Nebraska_Site_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **He asked for something he could take upstairs** — "What kind of information can you give me that I can present to the market?" — and Val answered with the offer of a demo. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JJ_Gaming_Nebraska_Site_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **On sharing J&J's performance data:** he would not hand it over himself but would ask — "I can ask them to share the data with you. That's all I can say: this gentleman would like some data. This is why. Can you share it with him?" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JJ_Gaming_Nebraska_Site_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **On what J&J would want from a JVL Nebraska office:** "I would want **parts availability, technical support**… replacements for machines, parts that we need to request — be it monitors or buttons… keeping that stuff on hand," plus **training**. He noted J&J has not had to order many parts from JVL so far. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JJ_Gaming_Nebraska_Site_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]

## His assessment of JVL's field rep

Asked how [[wiki/people/denis-desmarais.md|Denis Desmarais]] ("Dennis") was doing, Rick was positive: "As far as our relationship, he's good. **I like him**… He does [help]. As much as we need him. I think **my technical guys probably work with him more than I do**. But yeah, he **answers anything and responds very quickly** to them." He also asked Val how the name is pronounced ("I'm sure I'm pronouncing that wrong"); Val: "It's [a] French name." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JJ_Gaming_Nebraska_Site_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]

## His read on Bank Shot and on progressives (Jun 16 2026, closing segment)

In the [[wiki/meetings/2026-06-16-jj-gaming-bank-shot-and-nebraska-withholding.md|short closing segment]] after the tour, Rick gave the fullest account in the corpus of the **Bank Shot** business and why it is dying — the substance is on [[wiki/organizations/american-amusements.md]]. Two judgements belong to him personally:

- **He is removing the equipment, not defending it.** "Bank shot is **another one of our very low performing equipment**"; "for the most part, **I've probably taken more bank shots out this year**." And on whether the business can be revived: "**I don't think there is anymore.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JJ_Gaming_Bank_Shot_and_Nebraska_Withholding-June-16-2026.md]]
- **Progressives no longer decide the Nebraska market.** Asked by Val whether "progressivism" is still important there: "**I don't think it is as much anymore.** It was great when that was the only thing going on. But now, I think it's about **being able to know what the win is — on site**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JJ_Gaming_Bank_Shot_and_Nebraska_Withholding-June-16-2026.md]]

## The one product question he put to Val (Jun 16 2026)

The only direct ask Rick made of JVL all day, and the reason the withholding problem is now a Nebraska product requirement (recorded on [[wiki/projects/nebraska-skill-market.md]], Jun-16 2026). The derived notes read this question as **directly blocking J&J's willingness to take payouts above $1,199 on JVL equipment in Nebraska**. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_JJ_Gaming_Bank_Shot_and_Nebraska_Withholding-June-16-2026.md]]

- **Can a JVL machine take a W-9 at the terminal?** "That is **one question I'll ask you**. Your machine that doesn't fill itself — those machines like **Easy Links** have what's called **casino software**… that allows you to **fill out the W nine right at the terminal**. That way, we **don't actually have to go out there and pay the customer or get any information from them**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JJ_Gaming_Bank_Shot_and_Nebraska_Withholding-June-16-2026.md]]
- **He is candid that Nebraska's rules defeat him.** "**Nebraska is just weird. I am still having trouble wrapping my head around exactly what of the regulations.**" On the proposed state debt-check database he was blunt about the consequence for his own staff: "**You imagine how pissed off people are going to be with that?**" — and on whether J&J is resisting it, he could only assume: "I would assume that **our legal team is trying to keep it from happening**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JJ_Gaming_Bank_Shot_and_Nebraska_Withholding-June-16-2026.md]]

## The steakhouse lunch — the objection, and the man (Jun 16 2026)

Val took Rick to lunch after the visit ([[wiki/meetings/2026-06-16-jj-gaming-steakhouse-lunch-rick-rees.md]]). **The raw transcript's speaker labels are inverted** — everything attributed to Rick here has the documented swap applied; see the stub. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_JJ_Gaming_Steakhouse_Lunch_Rick_Rees-June-16-2026.md]]

- **He restated the reporting-line limit, and then raised the objection that actually matters.** "**You got a lot of people to impress, not just me. I'm step one.** But especially for a system that **you have nowhere else. So how are you testing it?**" Val answered straight — "**This will be my first test**" — and Rick came back twice, unhurried and without pressing: "I'm not going to say it's impossible, but **being the first is not always the best**", then again, "**Being the first isn't always the best.**" He did not withdraw it and Val did not answer it. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JJ_Gaming_Steakhouse_Lunch_Rick_Rees-June-16-2026.md]]
- **He confirmed the local P&L is his.** Asked whether the bottom line is his responsibility: "Not completely — we still have people we answer to." Asked again, narrowed to locally: "**Yes. I am responsible for what happens here**"; and on profit, "**Yes. And what I do and how I manage it**"; and hiring, "**Yes.**" This is the hook Val then used (see [[wiki/topics/jvl-operating-principles.md]], Jun-16 2026). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JJ_Gaming_Steakhouse_Lunch_Rick_Rees-June-16-2026.md]]
- **He reconfirmed the introduction.** Closing the lunch: "**I'll get you to the right people**" and "**I'll get in touch with James and Jeff**"; asked by Val whether he would circulate the email, "**Absolutely.**" *(The transcript gives only the first names; the surnames **Jansen** and **Harwick** come from the derived notes and from the morning session.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JJ_Gaming_Steakhouse_Lunch_Rick_Rees-June-16-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_JJ_Gaming_Steakhouse_Lunch_Rick_Rees-June-16-2026.md]]
- **His loyalty to J&J is the thing that makes him useful, and he says so.** Asked whether he would move again if the company opened a new state: "if they came to me today and said, hey, **North Carolina's open up**… **I would go.**" Why: "**They're great company. They've always taken care of me**… I've been able to **better myself through the business**… **I'm not just a number. We're not just numbers to them.**" And the access: "**I can call the CEO of the company.**" Val named it in the room — "**You have a direct line to the decision maker, which is an important thing.**" *(His accompanying remark about the company's pay is withheld under §15.8.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JJ_Gaming_Steakhouse_Lunch_Rick_Rees-June-16-2026.md]]

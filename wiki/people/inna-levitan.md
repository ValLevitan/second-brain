# Inna Levitan

Inna is the majority owner of the JVL group's holding companies. Canonical name confirmed by Val as **Inna Levitan** (owner ruling 2026-07-13); the transcript renders it "Ina"/"Ena"/"Enaville," kept as aliases for search.

## Role

- **Sole director** of **[[wiki/organizations/jvl-labs.md|JVL Labs Inc.]]** (the Canadian parent) and of the sibling holding company **JVL Mobi, Inc.** [[raw/jvl_corporate_structure_2025-04.md]]
- Owns **91%** of both JVL Labs and JVL Mobi; the two minority partners are Peter Guterres (4.5%) and Boris Itskov (4.5%) in each. Because JVL Labs is the parent that owns JVL Systems, JVL Gameworks, JVL Colombia and 2574038 Ontario (and 95% of JVL Gaming Peru), her ownership of the group flows through JVL Labs. [[raw/jvl_corporate_structure_2025-04.md]]
- She is one of the three signers on the planned CIBC US accounts, together with Val and Monica. [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]]
- The bank noted she could be the primary on a US business credit card. [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]]
- She signed and paid JVL Systems' 2024 California franchise-tax bill in March 2026 and its 2025 filing in June 2026. (source: Corporate Registry — JVL Systems Identity & Tax IDs)

## History

- **Jan 2025:** Val described Inna simply as owning "91% of the companies", without distinguishing the holding structure. [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]] The April 2025 structure chart refines this: she owns 91% at the **JVL Labs / JVL Mobi holding level**, and it is JVL Labs (not Inna directly) that owns the operating companies. [[raw/jvl_corporate_structure_2025-04.md]]

## Sept 7 2026 — her shareholding and JVL Labs' CCPC status

On the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-07-departure-tax-trust-and-bad-debt.md|Sept-7 2026 call]] with cross-border tax lawyer [[wiki/people/vitaly-timokhov.md|Vitaly Timokhov]], her shareholding turned out to be the single point the whole [[wiki/topics/sred-tax-credit.md|SR&ED]] question turns on. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]

See [[wiki/organizations/jvl.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]

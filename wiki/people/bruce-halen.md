# Bruce Halen (US customs / trade lawyer)

Bruce Halen is a **US customs / international-trade lawyer** — the "most experienced US knowledge and customs colleague" of [[wiki/people/lucie.md|Lucie]] at her firm, based in the firm's **Detroit office**, where US customs is his **primary area of practice**. He joined the [[wiki/meetings/2025-06-18-tariff-bruce-halen-us-customs.md|June-18 2025 tariff call]] to advise [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] on the US-side treatment of JVL slot machines. [[raw/tariff-June-18-2025.md]]

## What he advised (June 18 2025)

- **Slot machines classify under HS heading 9504** at a **duty-free ordinary tariff**; the extra duties come from other programs. [[raw/tariff-June-18-2025.md]]
- **Section 301** (2018, first Trump administration; finding of unfair Chinese trade practices) adds **7.5%** for Chinese-origin slot machines. [[raw/tariff-June-18-2025.md]]
- The April-2025 **"Geneva Agreement"** (not yet written or issued by executive order) references a **~55%** additional tariff on Chinese goods — for a slot machine roughly **37.5%** total (a 30% component + the 7.5% 301). The **30% IEEPA "fentanyl" component was struck as unconstitutional by the Court of International Trade** and is under appeal (Federal Circuit hearings ~end of August; possibly to the Supreme Court; ~a year to a final decision). [[raw/tariff-June-18-2025.md]]
- **Taiwan:** a **32%** reciprocal tariff (announced April 10) delayed to **July 9**, not being charged in the meantime, dependent on negotiations. **South Korea:** a US free-trade agreement, mostly duty-free. [[raw/tariff-June-18-2025.md]]
- **Country of origin = the last substantial transformation.** "Simple screwdriver assembly is not substantial transformation," so assembling Chinese/Taiwanese/Korean parts in Canada may not make the machine Canadian-origin; customs may apply an **essential-character** test (his electric-motor/side-view-mirror example). He found **no CBP country-of-origin rulings on slot machines**; the only definitive route is a **ruling request** (NY ruling division — slow, sometimes conflicting). A customs query arrives as a **CF-28** request for information. [[raw/tariff-June-18-2025.md]]
- His honest conclusion: **no certainty is achievable** now or within months, given the shifting tariff landscape. [[raw/tariff-June-18-2025.md]]

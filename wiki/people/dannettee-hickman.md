# Dannettee Hickman

An independent gaming-route operator based in the American South-East / South. Val first met her (in the corpus) at an **Aug 28 2026** JVL office drop-in — [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-28-danette-texas-operator-office-visit.md]] — and, as of **September 16 2026** — [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]]'s candidate to be the **on-the-ground front operator** for a JVL-funded plan to open and run its own gaming locations in **Texas**. See [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-16-dannettee-hickman-texas-partnership-exploration.md]] and [[wiki/projects/texas-used-machine-distribution.md]].

Nothing was agreed. Both sides deliberately left it open — see [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-16-texas-front-operator-model-not-an-equal-partnership.md]].

## Profile (as of Sept 16 2026)

- **Eleven years in gaming.** Asked how long she had been in the industry: "Like no, no. I think **eleven years**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dannettee_Hickman_Texas_Gaming_Partnership_Exploration-September-16-2026.md]]
- **Twelve years a truck driver before that, as an owner-operator.** "I had my own truck, and I drove about **twelve years**. It wasn't as glamorous as I thought it was" — and she ran others: "I **owned my own trucks, and I had other owner operators**." Her truck was a show truck, "a very beautiful **Kenworth**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dannettee_Hickman_Texas_Gaming_Partnership_Exploration-September-16-2026.md]]
- **She runs three businesses and keeps the books in-house** — for "**all three of my businesses**, and there are mistakes. It's not that serious because it's just us." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dannettee_Hickman_Texas_Gaming_Partnership_Exploration-September-16-2026.md]]
- **She has always worked for herself, and says so as a warning.** "Honestly, just full disclosure. **I am a nightmare. I am used to working for myself. I've always worked for myself.** Conformity that way is a little different… **I'm not corporate, exactly, and I'm not sure that I could be.** The I would not want to partner with someone that expects me to be like that." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dannettee_Hickman_Texas_Gaming_Partnership_Exploration-September-16-2026.md]]
- **What she does want to do: the field, not the back office.** "**I am diligent. I work. I would prefer to be out in the field, setting up locations.**" Asked directly whether back office was her thing: "I don't. That stuff is much harder for me." On reliability: "**I am reliable as they come. If I say I'm going to do something, I do it, and I do it within the time frame I set.**" On how her work looks while in progress — she used to lay tile and brick — "it looks like complete chaos. But then you have this beautiful fireplace, floor or countertop." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dannettee_Hickman_Texas_Gaming_Partnership_Exploration-September-16-2026.md]]
- **What she is actually chasing is stability.** "I make money in all these different states, but **it's not stable money, and I need it to be stable**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dannettee_Hickman_Texas_Gaming_Partnership_Exploration-September-16-2026.md]]

## Her operating objection to the plan — staffing and cash

Told Val that opening game rooms is hard for a specific reason: "here's what has to happen: **you have to have somebody to work in it. Always**… If you open a little place for games, **it's all cash money, and cash money makes people funny. So you have to really bet** [vet] **people**." Val's answer was that JVL's architecture removes the problem — "**With our system, nobody touches cash**" — kiosks, a "**proper cage**", an attendant, security cameras, supervisor management, "**You build your own brand.**" Her reply: "That's what you do. And that's what you're interested in doing." See [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dannettee_Hickman_Texas_Gaming_Partnership_Exploration-September-16-2026.md]]

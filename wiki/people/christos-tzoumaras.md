# Christos "Chris" Tzoumaras (Intralot executive)

**An executive at [[wiki/organizations/intralot.md|Intralot]] who set out Intralot's plans for the route business to [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] on October 13 2026**, and with whom Val had dinner that evening. On the tape he is only "**Chris**" (Val's goodbye: "**Okay, Chris. I'll see you… tonight**"). **The full name "Christos Tzoumaras" comes from the derived notes**, which say Speaker 4 is "taken to be Christos Tzoumaras". Treat the surname as **not confirmed** [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Intralot_Machine_Lockouts_Test_Lab_and_Route_Partnership-October-13-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Intralot_Machine_Lockouts_Test_Lab_and_Route_Partnership-October-13-2026.md]].

He is a different person from the Georgia Lottery's "Chris" who was in the same room for the first part of the meeting — see [[wiki/people/chris-boncek.md]].

## Oct 13 2026 — Intralot's route plans

Plaud Speaker 4, per the notes' speaker map. Quotes from [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Intralot_Machine_Lockouts_Test_Lab_and_Route_Partnership-October-13-2026.md]]. Full meeting: [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-13-intralot-machine-lockouts-test-lab-and-route-partnership.md]].

- **Intralot's position in Georgia:** "**very difficult and delicate because we only have one customer, the Georgia Lottery**." He waited until the Lottery representative had left to talk about routes.
- **He laid out the group and the gap** — Soo Kim's ownership, Bally's Interactive, a sports-betting purchase, and "**what is missing is the route business**". Details on [[wiki/organizations/intralot.md]].
- **He named the target states:** Nebraska, Ohio, Wyoming, Idaho, maybe Arkansas, Puerto Rico and the Dominican Republic. He is "**very close friends**" with the Wyoming lottery director.
- **He wants two partners:** "**I need a partner for the route, and I need a partner for the machines.**" Revenue share, a cap, or equity in the manufacturer are all open.
- **He was careful to say it is not settled:** "**Nothing is signed**", "**This is conceptual.**" His board wants a 2027 plan.
- **He sees JVL as the game maker in a "full solution"** for lotteries: "**We have this game manufacturer called JVL that has good software**", plus Intralot and a repair depot (Renova).
- **During the lockout part he backed JVL's side of the numbers gap:** "**it's more than what is reported… to us, basically, and that's what I think is happening.**"

## Background he mentioned

- He and Val knew each other before: "**I remember I was with Banti with United for a few years, and your numbers were…**", and later "**you remember I always liked you from Bandy**". The notes read "Banti/Bandy" as Bunty of [[wiki/organizations/united-gaming.md|United]] — **inference** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Intralot_Machine_Lockouts_Test_Lab_and_Route_Partnership-October-13-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Intralot_Machine_Lockouts_Test_Lab_and_Route_Partnership-October-13-2026.md]].
- "**We know Byron for twenty years**" — Byron, an [[wiki/organizations/arc-gaming.md|ARC]] board member and, per Chris, Intralot's former CEO and lobbyist [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Intralot_Machine_Lockouts_Test_Lab_and_Route_Partnership-October-13-2026.md]].

# Narasimha Rao Namburi (Georgia distributor and installer)

The **distributor and installer who currently sells and installs the Access transactional system in Georgia**, and the counterparty [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] spent nearly two hours trying to move onto JVL exclusively at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-09-rao-machine-performance-and-exclusivity-offer.md|Sept-9 2026 10:04 AM meeting]] at JVL. The derived notes give his full name as **Narasimha Rao Namburi** and his role as **Owner/CEO, techs at PLAY LLC**; the transcript never states either — he is addressed only as "Rao" (and variously as "Raul", "Raúl", "Rallo" and "Lao" by the same speakers in the same file) [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]].

*(Name note: the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-08-dixie-amusement-bundle-offer-and-card-economics.md|Sept-8 Dixie visit]] closed with "Tomorrow we have 10 a.m." for a counterparty rendered "**Rio, Rau**" — the same meeting, one day out, under two more garbles. All variants are recorded as aliases; the wiki writes **Rao**.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]

## His operation, in his own numbers

All from Sept 9 2026, spoken across the room and partly restated back to him by Val; **figures are as spoken and the transcript is unpunctuated in places** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]]:

- **350 locations put on Access in about three to three and a half months.** Val's restatement — "You put three hundred fifty locations with access in three months… hundred days, for three hundred fifty" — and Rao's own version: "We put every month hundred. Every day we put four or five."
- **Three to four crews, three installations a day each** (Val's reconstruction, which Rao did not contest): "You probably have three, three, three to four crews… they do three installations per day."
- **Install times:** "For machine, generally, it takes **thirty minutes**. For **IGT and Aristocrat**, you need **three to five**" *(minutes; the unit is not spoken)*. A six-machine location is "**at least three hours** just to install. More than that, it depends."
- **About $2,500 per install, on reselling the equipment** — Val: "Twenty-five hundred, I think, around… Reselling equipment"; "On each install, you make twenty-five hundred dollars."
- **The monthly side is not a business, and he is losing on it.** "That is not a good business. Monthly, I couldn't do that"; "masters are **not paying the monthly fee** either"; and plainly: "because of the subscription, I see all the money I'm making. Like mostly, **I'm losing a little bit right now** because of subscription." His own conclusion: "Collecting money from monthly for location is painful… **That's why I don't want to really do it.**"
- **His stock and his exposure:** "it's **250,000**. I need to collect and pay. I bought for **1.5 million** now. **Cash**. I paid." His on-hand Access stock is put at **$500,000** — but **by Val, not by Rao**: asked "How many," Val answers "**Five hundred thousand. This is the best time to get rid of it**," Rao replies "**No**" *(the transcript does not make clear whether he is disputing the figure or the advice)*, and Val later scripts the sales call for him — "I have **five hundred thousand dollars worth of equipment**. Would you like to buy it at my cost?" **The figure is Val's and is recorded as such.**
- **A distributor's answer to who decides what goes in a location.** Val put it as a question — "I find it difficult to believe that location really dictates what to put… in general, **seven out of ten**, you will probably go with your solution. Am I correct?" — and moved straight on; **Rao's answer is not on the transcript**.
- **His 200 Access locations "lined up" are not new installations.** Asked whether they could be JVL instead: "No, it's already inherited a long time ago… **Those masters have access already previously.**"
- **He only sells in Georgia.** Asked whether he could take 65 machines JVL is receiving on trade from an operator, and where else he sells: "**Only here.**"

## What he told JVL about the market

- **The market is close to saturated and JVL is late.** "it would have come here like **two years ago**. It would have been nice. **It's already almost saturated.** If we start now, by the time we pick up, it's saturated." Val agreed on the timing and declined the conclusion — the exchange is on [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]] (Sept 9) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]].
- **He cannot force Access out, and said so before agreeing to anything.** "I will work around. **I cannot force access to go away. It's not going to be that simple. It will happen organically.**" Val: "Agree" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]].
- **On why the Georgia card market shifted, he named a person rather than a company.** "**Absad is the main guy. Absad is the crazy guy**" — against a JVL colleague's "I think income had a lot to do with it too, by targeting locations." Rao: "I think Absad is not income." *(The corpus carries the Fiery Pay principal as "Afshad"/"Avshad"; "Absad" is read here as the same name, an **inference** from context, not stated in the source.)* See [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]] (Sept 9) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]].
- **Locations never pay, and he expects they never will.** "Before you install it, when you install it, I'm going to get a sign and a document. Then think about getting money. **But after you install it, the money is not there.**" Val's matching view — "The location is used to getting shit for free… **They will extort their way to getting it for free**" — is on [[wiki/topics/jvl-operating-principles.md]] (Sept 9) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]].
- **Mounting is a real field problem on his route.** "They have **wooden** [cabinets] still… They **don't have plate at all**. What we do? **We need to cut it.** That's a complicated location to cut." Val's answer — a fully enclosed top-mount box on two posts — is on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-rao-takes-the-bundle-with-jvl-exclusive-in-the-location.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]].
- **His locations are Spanish-speaking, and the operators are not.** Val asked for a Spanish-speaking attendant for the first location; Rao's own framing of the trade — "Most of the population in this business belongs to **one community from Indian Pakistan**," with the players "can be anybody, **not Indian-Pakistani exclusively**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]].

## The performance dispute — his side

His position is that **JVL machines do not earn enough**, and he held it for most of the meeting. Val's counter-case, and the physical/configuration explanation he insisted on, are on [[wiki/topics/slot-game-design-principles.md]] (Sept 9) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]].

- **A Savannah location where JVL sits behind Novomatic.** Rao: "**JVL, Reddah, Nomadic fighting**… they are again updated. **It's called rocket rush**." The location is "in Savannah" and Rao's crews did not install it — "He went there and installed themselves. We didn't even go." *(The ranking language is garbled — "It became second" is said of an unclear subject — and is recorded as spoken. What is unambiguous is Val's reply: "Take me to that location, and I will be first," and later "here's the location. Here's eight machines. **JVL is last.**")*
- **Progressive size is what a player looks at.** Pointing at the machines in the room: "That is **six thousand** and IGT"; "Even that is **twenty-four hundred**. This goes to **six thousand**." Val restated it back: "IGT right here says six thousand" — Rao: "**Eight thousand?**" — Val: "It says eight thousand, and **this one is a thousand**. That the player will gravitate to that."
- **His crews do not think about machine placement at all, and he said so against his own interest.** "While they're dropping you from the truck, they put in a line. **They never have priority**… Because I put my personally in **thousand places, I never check**… **never came into my mind.** This come from the truck trailer. Goes first machine from the trailer goes to first place, second place."
- **But he rates the cabinet.** "The best cabinet, good-looking cabinet. The best cabinet is what we have highlighted. It's very smooth, **better than anybody else's**."
- **What he wants instead of an argument:** "What we need to improve? **How we can get the man and the people to play JVL.**"

## What he committed to

He asked for time first — "I'm going to think about it and work it out" — and later, when Val put it to him directly ("You're in"), answered: "**I'm in.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]]

His two conditions, in his own words: "I want to do this **installation entity separate** from this cable itself, not for anything else. That's all" — and the warning above that Access will only leave organically [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]].

The deal itself is on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-rao-takes-the-bundle-with-jvl-exclusive-in-the-location.md]].

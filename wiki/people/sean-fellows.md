# Sean Fellows

**Sean Fellows** (transcript title spells it **"Shawn Fellows"**) is the **CEO/owner of [[wiki/organizations/diamond-amusement.md|Diamond Amusement]]**, a Georgia COAM master/terminal operator. JVL treats him as the **sole decision-maker** for the account — he ran the Feb 9 2026 partnership meeting without bringing anyone below him. [[raw/Diamond_Shawn_Fellows_interview-February-9-2026.md]] [[raw/Exec_recap_Diamond_Amusement-February-9-2026.md]]

## Background

- In his **38th year** in the industry; started in traditional amusements and has consistently run distributed routes. [[raw/Diamond_Shawn_Fellows_interview-February-9-2026.md]]
- **Instrumental in the legislation** that established Georgia's current COAM regulatory structure, and active in state/national industry advocacy since the late 1990s; has been **president of GAMOA for ~two years**. Felipe frames JVL's support of that association as part of the relationship. [[raw/Diamond_Shawn_Fellows_interview-February-9-2026.md]] [[raw/Exec_recap_Diamond_Amusement-February-9-2026.md]]
- Long history with JVL: recalled buying early **JVL countertops** from **Bob Whitehead** and operating **Cash Code** equipment. [[raw/Diamond_Shawn_Fellows_interview-February-9-2026.md]]

## Role in the Feb 9 2026 partnership

- Presented Diamond's own market data (competitive benchmark, saturation, RTP tactics, gift-card readiness — detailed on [[wiki/organizations/diamond-amusement.md]]) and responded positively to JVL's data-sharing proposal, committing to deploy JVL machines across low/mid/premium tiers, share performance data, provide historical baselines, and facilitate field visits. [[raw/Diamond_Shawn_Fellows_interview-February-9-2026.md]]
- Val judged there is "a deal to be made with Sean" but that Sean **sidestepped** the question of how much business JVL could win by pricing machines at $1 — smirking and moving on rather than answering — which Val read as politics on Sean's side. [[raw/Exec_recap_Diamond_Amusement-February-9-2026.md]]
- First surfaced (as "Sean") on the May 29 2025 Georgia update, when Felipe installed Diamond's early "Peach One" test units and met Sean and his tech. [[raw/felipe_georgia_market_update-may-29-2025.md]]

## Jan 27 2026 — an operator's first call

On the [[wiki/meetings/2026-01-27-rich-winley-georgia-coam-market-intel.md|Jan-27 Georgia briefing]], former route operator [[wiki/people/rich-winley.md|Rich Winley]] named him as the first master he would call for JVL. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rich_Winley_Georgia_COAM_Market_Intel-January-27-2026.md]]

- **Why him.** "somebody like calling it Sean Fellows, he's for a long time… He understands the difference in features. He understands the difference in payouts." Later: "Sean's growing, but a lot of these other guys aren't growing", and "He's all about the relationship… He's not trying to take your head off."
- **What Rich expected.** "Let's start with Sean, and he'll give it to me raw." On a trade-in deal with report access: "Somebody like Sean will give it to you." Rich said he knows him "personally done business".
- **Identification.** The transcript names him "Sean Fellows" once and "Sean Phillips" once; Juan's reply names "Diamond", and Juan then asks how the games are set up at Diamond. So this is read as the Diamond Sean by context. Rich undertook to call him the next day — tracked on [[wiki/people/rich-winley.md]]. No source says whether that call led to the Feb 9 meeting.

## Aug 31 2026 — "the most politically connected in the state of Georgia"

At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-31-arc-gaming-transactional-demo-class-a.md|Aug-31 ARC Gaming demo]], [[wiki/people/georgios-zazanis.md|George Zazanis]] — COO of a rival master — was asked by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] which operator JVL should build a relationship with. His answer was this man, and he made it emphatic. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

- Val knew him already and had the same read on his manner: "Yes. I had a conversation with him. **He's very, very quiet.**" Zazanis: "one thing you know about him, **as quiet as he is, he's the most politically connected in the state of Georgia today**, at least to my eyes. **By far, he's been seen with every single politician that exists in this state.**"
- The recommendation was exclusive, not one of a list: "**If you need to choose one guy, choose this guy.**" And, dismissing the association route: "**Forget [GAMOA], forget everybody there.** [He is] today for me, the guy."
- With a caveat about what the access actually buys: "He doesn't say he might give you business, and **he's very expanding. He's becoming very big.**"
- A first-hand instance from Zazanis's previous employer: "when I used to work at [Intelec], **he was bringing the staff of the office from the governor**. The guy has seen him coming to the [Intelec] office a couple of times."
- His only other named endorsement, alongside this one, was **Bulldog Gaming** — "So [Sean] Fellows and [Bulldog]" — recorded on [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]] (Aug 31 2026).

**Identification — an inference, labelled.** The Aug-31 transcript never spells the name correctly: it renders it "Shonfello", "Sonfellows" and "shunned fellows", and the derived notes write "**Shonfellows — the most politically connected operator in Georgia**". No company is attached to the name in either source. This page reads it as **Sean Fellows of [[wiki/organizations/diamond-amusement.md|Diamond Amusement]]** on three grounds: the phonetic match, the description as a Georgia *operator* of growing size, and the political profile, which fits a man this wiki already records as instrumental in the legislation that created Georgia's COAM structure and as a ~two-year GAMOA president. **It is not stated in the source** and is flagged for owner confirmation. ## Sept 10 2026 — two hours across a table: how he negotiates, and the career he laid out

The [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-10-diamond-amusements-machine-performance-and-bundle-pilot.md|Sept-10 2026 warehouse visit]] is the longest first-hand record of him in the corpus. *(Plaud splits the Diamond side across several speaker numbers — his own lines land under at least two — so nothing below is attributed to him unless the surrounding exchange makes it unambiguous.)* [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

### How he answers an offer

- **Data first, every time.** "**everything we do is data**, so it's really going to be. **I'm not going to upgrade 74 games if that's what you're asking**… we could do potentially **incremental upgrades. But I need to know what you're talking about from a price standpoint.**" Full outcome on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-10-diamond-buys-two-machines-instead-of-trading-the-fleet.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]
- **His objection to a fleet swap is about the player, not the machine.** "you have a player that uses that particular game… and **the store across the street still has that same game because these stores compete with each other**… **How fast does the player adopt to the new game versus going across the street?**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]
- **He will take the risk on the product and not on the money.** He committed to two machines the same afternoon — "**definitely let's start with two**" — and to trying the transactional set — "**I'd like to try one**" — while refusing any exposure to card-clearance debt: "**I'm not going to be a reseller**… **it's better for us to have an arms-length relationship.**" See [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-10-diamond-will-not-guarantee-card-clearance-debt.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]
- **The reason he gives for protecting the location relationship is the sharpest statement of it in the corpus:** "**the relationship we have with the location is so fragile that we didn't want some outside financial relationship to ultimately collapse this relationship. I don't want to lose ten thousand dollars**" — and, offered bigger numbers, "**Even a thousand. It's not good business.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]
- **He teaches the location its own economics.** "I explain to them all the time: **you're a store owner. The games are part of your income. They're all of my income.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]
- **He did the weekend-exposure arithmetic for Val unprompted** — 72-hour processing, three-day bank cycles, "$30,000 a week in redemption… **fifteen thousand dollars of exposure risk**" — the first operator-side sizing of the broker's risk in the corpus. On the same decision page.

### The career, in his own telling

- **Atlanta, and a partnership that ended badly.** "at that time I **built a company here in Atlanta with Larry Simmons**"; "**He and I had an awful fallout, two thousand, two thousand and one.** Sorry to hear that. Just… things happen in business." Val's reply: "**it's hard to find a good partner.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]
- **Scale of that earlier business: about 9,000 machines across four states.** "**Number two and number three counties in Georgia combined — they're still small. If you combine them, and then we built South Carolina, Alabama, and Texas — all together, it's about nine thousand machines.**" Nothing left in South Carolina now — "**No. All done with it.**" He also names "**Hess Technology**… out of Texas" as a firm he worked with, and says the company was "**very successful**" and "**was sold off**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]
- **He has lived through two state shutdowns, and links them causally.** Val compared Georgia to South Dakota; the answer: "**that put the businesses out in 1999 and 2000, and then those machines largely flooded into Georgia, and that forced Georgia to shut down by 2002. So I've lived through two states being shut down.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]
- **The Bob Whitehead memory, corroborated a second time.** "you guys, when you all came in the countertop business, back with the old blue… **the blue blasters. Bob Whitehead** was his name, right?" — Val: "**Whitehead and Moore**… you remember Moore" — "**Moore forever, but Bob was our sales guy.**" This is the same recollection recorded from Feb 9 2026, now dated and detailed. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]] [[raw/Diamond_Shawn_Fellows_interview-February-9-2026.md]]

*(**Years-in-the-business — recorded, not resolved.** Early in this visit a Diamond principal says "**I'm in the business for 43 years. I started with my dad. I was 17**", while a different speaker in the same exchange says "**I'm 63**" — two people. The Feb-2026 interview has Sean in his **38th year**, seven months earlier. Because the Plaud labels split the Diamond side unreliably, **this page does not assert that the 43-year line is his**; the derived notes likewise attribute it only to "a Diamond principal". Both figures stand as spoken.)* [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]] [[raw/Diamond_Shawn_Fellows_interview-February-9-2026.md]]

### On Class A, and on Primero

His read is negative and structural, and it is the fifth operator account on [[wiki/topics/georgia-class-a-machines.md]]: "**the Class A, what everybody's calling these AV games, is to me… problematic. It wasn't what was intended, and I don't know that the players are really going to migrate.**" On deployment he and Val agreed it is "**spotty**." On Primero specifically: "**It is a classic**… it **doesn't have the RTP, the distribution of prizes. Everything is… old processes**", and "**they had to take the progressive off.**" He expects the regulator to address it in "**the next four to six months**", and his account of how the grey area formed is on that page. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Diamond_Amusements_Machine_Performance_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pilot-September-10-2026.md]]

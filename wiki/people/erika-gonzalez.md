# Erika Gonzalez

Erika Gonzalez is a member of JVL's finance team. She speaks Spanish and English [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Finance_Team_ACH_Authorization_Delay_and_Standards_Agents-November-20-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Finance_Team_ACH_Authorization_Delay_and_Standards_Agents-November-20-2026.md]]. Team context: [[wiki/topics/jvl-finance-back-office.md]].

## Nov 20 2026 (~3:32 PM) — she owns the late reply to an ACH authorization request

On the [[wiki/meetings/2026-11-20-finance-team-ach-authorization-delay-and-standards-agents.md|Nov-20 finance call]] she explained, for the team, that a customer's ACH authorization request was answered late: "the delay was due to an internal oversight on our site. We did not review the ACH authorization request promptly… We apologize for this mistake. Our normal practice is response to customer requests immediately" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Finance_Team_ACH_Authorization_Delay_and_Standards_Agents-November-20-2026.md]]. Val praised the clear admission ("what I love about this is quick and clear, our mistake") and set the rule that mistakes become standards, checked by each person's own email agent. She confirmed she has JVL's Claude and Claude Code subscription ("Yes, it's correct"), translated part of Val's message into Spanish, and said "Yes, I get it" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Finance_Team_ACH_Authorization_Delay_and_Standards_Agents-November-20-2026.md]]. Record and her weekend learning commitment: [[wiki/decisions/2026-11-20-a-mistake-found-becomes-a-written-standard-in-github-and-each-finance-team-member-runs-their-own-email-checking-agent-against-the-standards.md]].

## Nov 24 2026 (1:05 PM) — collections work with Claude

At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-11-24-accounting-team-ai-show-and-tell.md|Nov-24 accounting show-and-tell]] (Spanish) she said Val gave her six months to help with collections, working mostly with Laura. She downloads the daily movements of the Bancolombia accounts by hand, since Claude cannot get bank access; Claude formats them to Val's standard and checks them, and she uploads them to the SharePoint folder Val gave her. She also uses Claude for task priorities, collection-letter templates, a customer payment calendar, sorting email by customer, the ACH format and a morning brief linked to her email, and never lets it send or act without her review [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Accounting_Team_AI_Show_and_Tell-November-24-2026.md]]. Personal matters not recorded.

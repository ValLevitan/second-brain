# Juan

Juan works on JVL's commercial side, its relationship with the game-testing lab GLI, and international operations/expansion.

## Role

- Juan is named alongside Val as the JVL people who deal with GLI; the GLI representative mentioned that "neither Juan or myself" would be at the amusement trade show. [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]]
- Anibal reported to Juan on preparing Peru machines for shipment to Toronto, and Juan was on the call discussing the Peru office closure and Anibal's possible move to Puerto Rico. See [[wiki/projects/puerto-rico-expansion.md]]. [[raw/anibal_call_011325_meeting_notes_2025-01-14.md]]

- Juan also weighs in on game creative direction: on the Jan 16 game-design review, Val notes that "Juan and I are in agreement" on giving the Crescent City Carnival woman character a nondescript ethnicity so the game reflects the Florida market's players. [[raw/game_design_january_16_2025.md]]

## South Dakota and Puerto Rico (Jan 22–29)

- **South Dakota (Jan 22):** Juan is the field/regulator-facing lead on the SD VLT effort — he visited the lab, relayed the regulator's printer preferences (Epic 950) and the unapproved-software allowance, coordinated with Light & Wonder and Lonnie, and did test-play. See [[wiki/projects/south-dakota-vlt-market.md]]. [[raw/sd_vlt_recap-jan-22-2025.md]]
- **Puerto Rico (Jan 29):** Juan narrated Igor's Puerto Rico status document in English on the progress call and is expected to help vet the incoming law-firm quotes before they reach Val. See [[wiki/projects/puerto-rico-expansion.md]]. [[raw/Puerto Rico progress report - January 29, 2025.md]]

- **Game-design review (Feb 6):** feedback was requested from Juan (he could not view live) on the Nico-cat topper — the promotional text ("hold and re-spin feature with up to three bonuses") and the low-angle Nico-cat topper image. See [[wiki/topics/jvl-machine-lineup.md]]. [[raw/Game design meeting - February 6, 2025.md]]

## Outstanding-subjects alignment (Feb 19)

- Juan is the field/customer-facing lead across the market releases. Val credited him with building a good **rapport with Lonnie** (the SD lab lets JVL pre-test unapproved software as an exception, not an obligation — to be handled with care), discussed sending [[wiki/people/sebastian.md|Sebastian]] to South Dakota, and worked through the Georgia dual-SAS/third-printer-port design and the Florida CPU-kit ("Florida Ruta") single-monitor requirements. See [[wiki/projects/south-dakota-vlt-market.md]] and [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]]. [[raw/Alignment_outstanding_subjects_2025-02-19.md]]

## Flex cabinet review + Incom (Feb 27)

- **Kiosk-repurpose idea (Flex V42/C review):** Juan brainstormed that since the payment **kiosk uses the same CPU as a Flex machine**, JVL could pitch masters that at end-of-life the kiosk converts into a standalone machine (remove dispenser/card reader + software upgrade + Georgia software) — "not a paperweight." See [[wiki/topics/jvl-machine-lineup.md]]. [[raw/Flex_V42_C_design_evolution_review_2025-02-27.md]]
- **Incom commissioning call:** Val designated **"Juan Martinez"** as JVL's **first point of contact** for the Incom payment-processor relationship (Val stepping to the background for executive/legal aspects, with [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel]] as Director of Engineering).

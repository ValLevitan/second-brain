# Scott Rambeau

Scott Rambeau is the CFO of Elevate, the finance company involved in the JVL machine sale. He introduced himself to Val as CFO "since day one" of Elevate. [[raw/elevate_convo_w_scott_rambeau_january_15_2025.md]]

(The transcript spells the surname "Rambo"; recorded here under the form used by the owner, "Rambeau", with both kept as aliases. Name spelling flagged as unclear.) [[raw/elevate_convo_w_scott_rambeau_january_15_2025.md]]

## Role

- Describes his job as "the one making sure they pay us" — i.e. he oversees the repayment of Elevate's loan tied to the JVL machines. [[raw/elevate_convo_w_scott_rambeau_january_15_2025.md]]
- Called Val to confirm the item numbers on the machines because Elevate took a pledge on the video terminals as collateral for a loan (a "second loan"), and Scott needs the pledge agreement to describe the correct hardware. The original collateral was secured using an old quote dated May 11, 2022 with item number "C10-VLOT-TERM-RU"; Scott wanted to confirm whether that identifier still matches the current Flex SD27 / V43 machines. Val confirmed it is a different hardware+software combination, so Scott expects to amend Elevate's pledge agreement. [[raw/elevate_convo_w_scott_rambeau_january_15_2025.md]]
- Offered to have Elevate revise its invoicing to add a software description or add "video lottery terminal Flex SD27" wording if that helps JVL. [[raw/elevate_convo_w_scott_rambeau_january_15_2025.md]]
- Explained why Elevate is comfortable lending in this industry: gaming operators almost never default because their gaming license (the "right to print money") is at risk if they get into litigation, and a company cannot be sold without its license. [[raw/elevate_convo_w_scott_rambeau_january_15_2025.md]]

# Howard Tikka

External designer in **Phoenix, Arizona** ("We're still in Arizona, still in Phoenix") who did JVL's brand work about thirty years ago and is being asked to do it again [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Howard_Tikka_Brand_Realignment_and_Collateral-September-22-2026.md]]. The derived notes call him a "brand and design consultant"; the transcript does not use that label [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Howard_Tikka_Brand_Realignment_and_Collateral-September-22-2026.md]].

## Relationship with Val and JVL

- **Known to Val for about 31 years.** Howard: "I would have been ninety five… So thirty one years" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Howard_Tikka_Brand_Realignment_and_Collateral-September-22-2026.md]].
- **He did JVL's look once before.** Howard: "I did this once. How many years ago? Twenty-five, thirty years." Val: "Probably thirty years ago. Nothing changed. Colors didn't change. Fonts didn't change." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Howard_Tikka_Brand_Realignment_and_Collateral-September-22-2026.md]]
- **He also worked on CashCode's image.** Howard: "it kind of reminds me when we started out with CashCode and how we took everything apart and put it all back together." Val: "CashCode, we had to create an image. We had to create a direction." (CashCode's history with Val is on [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md]].) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Howard_Tikka_Brand_Realignment_and_Collateral-September-22-2026.md]]
- **Why Val chose him.** Val: "you know me so well, and you know the styles, and you know the company. I would probably find it least painful to deal with it through you than to start explaining about the industry." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Howard_Tikka_Brand_Realignment_and_Collateral-September-22-2026.md]]

## The Sept-2026 brand engagement

On the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-22-howard-tikka-brand-realignment-and-collateral.md|Sept-22 call]] Val asked him to unify JVL's look without touching the logo or slogan — see [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-22-unify-jvl-look-logo-and-slogan-untouched.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Howard_Tikka_Brand_Realignment_and_Collateral-September-22-2026.md]].

- **His read of the brief.** "I think the pieces are there. I think it just needs to be pulled together and create some consistency." He wants to talk more "about the booths and what you're looking at, and what sizes" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Howard_Tikka_Brand_Realignment_and_Collateral-September-22-2026.md]].
- **His time is limited.** "I don't have a huge amount of free time available. I'm working from home twice a week and in the office the other times. I can squeeze things in here and there, an hour here, two hours there." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Howard_Tikka_Brand_Realignment_and_Collateral-September-22-2026.md]]
- No fee or rate was discussed [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Howard_Tikka_Brand_Realignment_and_Collateral-September-22-2026.md]].

# Maria Sanchez

Maria Sanchez is a **Colombian trade/customs lawyer** whose firm has represented JVL before (name from the Apr-30 2025 recording). Val engaged her again — as an **advisor/"quarterback," not as legal counsel** — to help him respond to a Canadian customs (CBSA) trade-compliance inquiry on machine components imported from China. [[raw/cbsa_maria_sanchez-april-30-2025.md]]

## Prior matter — the Canada-origin case

- Her firm ran a long origin dispute for JVL: it **started in 2017**, and in **2023** the sentence confirmed that JVL's **gaming machines were originated in Canada** (the customs authority had declared they were *not*). She noted this prior case has **nothing to do with** the new CBSA matter. [[raw/cbsa_maria_sanchez-april-30-2025.md]]

## The CBSA China-import matter (Apr 30 2025)

- JVL received an email (dated **April 24**) from **Canadian Customs (CBSA), a "Trade Compliance Validation"** regarding merchandise imported from **China into Canada**, obtained via the **CARM system**. The email only asks JVL to confirm the **contact person (name, title, mailing address)** who handles customs documentation; CBSA will then send a letter about past importations. See [[wiki/topics/tariffs-and-customs-compliance.md]]. [[raw/cbsa_maria_sanchez-april-30-2025.md]]
- Sanchez's read (from the Colombian/international-law perspective): these are ordinary customs requests that "sometimes end up in nothing"; the firm can help draft responses and flag risks arising from international law, **hourly / at a fixed rate**, but **cannot advise on Canadian law or give a reliance opinion** — a disclaimer she repeated until Val cut it off ("I already put it in writing that I'm not hiring you as lawyers… I'm simply needing a quarterback"). [[raw/cbsa_maria_sanchez-april-30-2025.md]]
- Process agreed: the firm runs a **conflict check** (today/tomorrow) and **sends a proposal Friday** with an hourly rate; on acceptance they open the case. Val will answer the initial CBSA email himself, set up a **common drive** for the documents, and use the firm's English-speaking staff + a **researcher** when the substantive information requests arrive. A colleague, **"Marina" (Mariana)**, was asked to check whether a Canada–China free-trade agreement exists. [[raw/cbsa_maria_sanchez-april-30-2025.md]]

## May 1 2025 — "final negotiation" to retain the firm as file-manager

On the [[wiki/meetings/2025-05-01-action-list-review-lev-rudky.md|May-1 Lev review]] (a day after the call above), Val described being in **"final negotiation for hiring the same law firm that represented us in Colombia"** — Colombian international-trade/tariff specialists who won JVL's earlier case — to act as a proactive **"file manager,"** preparing an easily-defensible dossier "just in case somebody comes and asks questions." Explicitly **not litigation** (a licensed firm would handle that if needed); Val is "putting money aside for litigation" separately. This is the same firm/matter as the Apr-30 CBSA engagement above.

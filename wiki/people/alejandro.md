# Alejandro

Alejandro is an **external Mexican accountant / tax advisor** whom Val brought onto the Apr 15 2025 orderly-wind-down call for [[wiki/organizations/jvl.md|JVL]] Mexico (introduced through interpreter [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda]]). His surname is not stated in the transcript. [[raw/mexico_jvl_orderly_wind-down-apr-15-2025.md]]

## Role in the Mexico wind-down (Apr 15 2025)

- Alejandro walked Val through the legal/accounting reality that JVL Mexico **cannot be fully/legally closed soon** because a **tax authority (SAT) audit has been running "a little more than a year"** with no certain end — his estimate to fully dissolve is **"a year, year and a half," but uncertain** because it depends on the tax authority, not JVL. [[raw/mexico_jvl_orderly_wind-down-apr-15-2025.md]]
- He explained that if Val simply bankrupts the company, the tax authority "will be able to continue trying to find the **legal representative** of JVL" for any tax credit assessed — a foreign company must keep a legal representative, who normally stays liable for a period even after bankruptcy; **unlinking the legal representative needs a lawyer's strategy** (he suggested the lawyers already handling the audit could propose a mechanism). [[raw/mexico_jvl_orderly_wind-down-apr-15-2025.md]]
- Once operations stop completely (no income, no expenses), the accounting collapses to **filing "declarations in zeros,"** which he can handle externally — so JVL can **terminate all direct Mexico employees** and keep only the **lawyers** (audit) and an **external accountant** (zero filings) until the audit closes. Until the last operations end (est. one–two months), the **internal bookkeeper** should stay. [[raw/mexico_jvl_orderly_wind-down-apr-15-2025.md]]
- He agreed to propose a **yearly standby retainer** for his services (near-zero day-to-day work, mostly audit follow-up + zero filings) and to **email Val a figure the same day**. Val told him payments would come **from the United States directly** (no Mexico office to pay him), and to **stay in contact with Val directly, not through anyone else**. [[raw/mexico_jvl_orderly_wind-down-apr-15-2025.md]]

## Apr 28 2025 — surname confirmed ("de la Rosa"); retained through closure

On the Apr-28 Mexico wind-down call with lawyer [[wiki/people/humberto-sarkis.md|Humberto Sarkis]], Val named him **"Alejandro de la Rosa"** — confirming the surname (consistent with the JVL Mexico accountant `adelarosa@dlrcontadores.com.mx`). Val "continue[s] employing Alejandro de la Rosa… at a really minimal cost" until JVL Mexico is closed, paying him **directly from the US company** (not the Mexico office).

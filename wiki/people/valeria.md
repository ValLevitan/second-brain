# Valeria (ECHO social-media / SMM contractor)

Valeria is a **social-media / SMM contractor** signed by [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] in **June 2025** for the [[wiki/topics/echo-home-gaming-device.md|ECHO]] consumer go-to-market. Recommended through mutual acquaintances; Andrei had not worked with her before but found her credible and **fully autonomous**. [[raw/echo_sales_marketing_progress_report-june-18-2025.md]]

## Background (June 18 2025)

- **Belarusian**, now living in **Vilnius, Lithuania** (long out of Belarus). [[raw/echo_sales_marketing_progress_report-june-18-2025.md]]
- Her main role is SMM for the **Fund of Sport Solidarity** (a Belarusian athletes-in-exile initiative); she also runs social accounts for a **European tattoo-salon network** and a **sports-nutrition store**. No prior experience with premium/expensive products, but strong command of the mechanics. [[raw/echo_sales_marketing_progress_report-june-18-2025.md]]

## Scope on ECHO (June 18 2025)

- **First priority:** run **Facebook and Instagram** organically — posts, photos, correct copy, measuring which posts convert — to **grow the follower audience** and refine how JVL talks to it. She can also make **basic arts** for FB/Instagram herself. She already drafted a rough **media plan**; the following week they concretize it. [[raw/echo_sales_marketing_progress_report-june-18-2025.md]]
- **Second priority:** after the website blog launches, do **SEO** — write ECHO/countertop/bartop articles so the JVL site becomes a concentrated store of arcade/countertop content and surfaces in search. [[raw/echo_sales_marketing_progress_report-june-18-2025.md]]
- **Third priority (lower):** "arcade marketing" — reviving old ECHO threads on Facebook communities/Reddit/forums, signalling that ECHO is back and JVL supports it. [[raw/echo_sales_marketing_progress_report-june-18-2025.md]]
- She is distinct from [[wiki/people/anton-smm.md|Anton]] (paid Meta ad-campaign setup): Valeria does organic content/community, Anton does the paid-ads auctions. [[raw/echo_sales_marketing_progress_report-june-18-2025.md]]

## Sept 16 2025 — influencer sourcing + Abfluence accuracy check

On Andrei's [[wiki/meetings/2025-09-16-echo-marketing-influencer-sourcing.md|Sept-16 marketing update]], Valeria was delegated the **horses vertical** for influencer sourcing (~4 sub-groups, same brand→collaborator method as the guns work), and is **testing the accuracy** of the **Abfluence** influencer-database tool during its 48-hour trial (checking a sample against other tools). Andrei described her as **detail-oriented** and likely a **designer by training** — she studied at the **European Humanities University (EGU/ЕГУ)**, known for a strong design faculty. **§15.8:** her monthly cost / hours-rate (raised while weighing the Abfluence subscription against her labour) is **held** — not recorded. See [[wiki/topics/echo-home-gaming-device.md]] (Sept-16). [[raw/echo_distribution_marketing_update-september-16-2025.md]]

# Sam Westgate

A principal at **[[wiki/organizations/jj-operator.md|J&J]]** (named in the sources as *J&J Ventures* and *J&J Ventures Gaming*), and — on the evidence below — [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]]'s longest-standing personal relationship into that company. He first enters this corpus in **May 2025** as a Nebraska branded-kiosk lead, and becomes the **door into J&J's Nebraska evaluation** on **September 17 2026**.

## Who he is, as the sources have him

- **May 29 2025 — first sighting.** [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]] surfaced Sam Westgate as a **J&J Ventures** principal, met in Georgia **through Magnolia Gaming**. Sam's company had a **large operation in Nebraska** (and some Illinois), had been testing kiosks, wanted "something of its own," and Sam was interested in JVL **making or branding a kiosk** for it across several markets. Val said he knew Sam Westgate and asked Felipe for his number. [[raw/felipe_georgia_market_update-may-29-2025.md]]
- **A roughly thirty-year commercial relationship, in Val's telling.** Describing Sam to another operator the same morning as the call below: "**I've done business with Sam for 30 years. Buying my equipment when I was selling countertops… Then I owned a company called CashCode… So Sam was my customer. Anyway, I'm dealing with him now on Nebraska.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Halcyon_Amusements_Georgia_Class_A_and_Texas_Route_Partnership-September-17-2026.md]]
- **Who pointed Val back at him.** Over dinner on **Sept 16 2026** [[wiki/people/sean-fellows.md|Sean/Shawn Fellows]] of [[wiki/organizations/diamond-amusement.md|Diamond Amusement]] told Val to call Sam: Val, recounting it, "I said, 'Look, I'm trying to meet with J and J for a Nebraska business,' and he says, 'Why don't you call Sam Westgate?'… he says, 'Yeah, I mean he is the top guy there. He's in charge.'" Val had **not realised Fellows knew J&J**. *(The transcript renders the name "Sean Fellis"; the derived notes read it as Shawn Fellows of Diamond Amusements, matching the man Val met on Sept 16 — an identification made in the notes, adopted here. That Sept-16 dinner itself is **not in this corpus**.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_J_and_J_Gaming_Nebraska_Entry_and_Effingham_Evaluation-September-17-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_J_and_J_Gaming_Nebraska_Entry_and_Effingham_Evaluation-September-17-2026.md]]

## Sept 17 2026 — "I'm not the one you need to convince"

The nine-minute call ([[wiki/meetings/2026-09-17-jj-gaming-nebraska-entry-and-effingham-evaluation.md|7:59–8:08 AM ET]]) is the corpus's only direct recording of him. Two things stand out: he **did not evaluate anything himself**, and he **volunteered the process instead of a verdict**.

- **He set out J&J's evaluation process, unprompted.** Everything J&J looks at "gets shipped to our office here… in **Effingham**", where it is gone through "with our CEO", and "**that was all evaluation for Nebraska or anywhere else that matters done here in Effingham**." Val could come and demo, ship it and talk by phone, "or whatever you prefer to do." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_J_and_J_Gaming_Nebraska_Entry_and_Effingham_Evaluation-September-17-2026.md]]
- **He named himself out of the decision.** "**I'm not the one you need to convince. Our CTO evaluates everything.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_J_and_J_Gaming_Nebraska_Entry_and_Effingham_Evaluation-September-17-2026.md]]
- **And named the buyer.** "**A gentleman by the name of Derek Bremley is also involved and handles equipment purchasing for Nebraska and Georgia, not Illinois.**" *(The spelling comes only from the recording; the derived notes flag it as unverified and possible as Bramley or Bromley.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_J_and_J_Gaming_Nebraska_Entry_and_Effingham_Evaluation-September-17-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_J_and_J_Gaming_Nebraska_Entry_and_Effingham_Evaluation-September-17-2026.md]]
- **He asked one commercial question of his own — about Illinois.** "**Is this available in Illinois as well? As far as your transactional product.**" Val's answer was that JVL's whole push is Georgia and that it is "anxious to put it in every market"; Sam pushed straight back — "**How does it apply in Georgia when there's no cash out in Georgia?**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_J_and_J_Gaming_Nebraska_Entry_and_Effingham_Evaluation-September-17-2026.md]]
- **He offered to carry it up.** "**Text me your contact information, or you can email it, and I will meet with our CTO when I get to the office, which will be in about an hour, and I'll have him call you back**"; then, at the end, "**Send me that information. I will walk it over to our CEO and have him give you a call.**" *(He names the **CTO** in the first offer and the **CEO** in the second; both are recorded and neither is preferred.)* Val was driving and could not write, so Sam agreed to text first. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_J_and_J_Gaming_Nebraska_Entry_and_Effingham_Evaluation-September-17-2026.md]]
- **The footprint he listed**, in the sentence that follows "Our CTO does not cover all states": "**Whether it's Montana or Nebraska or Vegas or Pennsylvania or Illinois or Georgia, and of course we're in Iowa too.**" *(The derived notes read the list as **J&J's footprint**. The transcript's own sentence attaches it to the CTO's coverage and is garbled; the state list is sourced, the framing is the notes'.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_J_and_J_Gaming_Nebraska_Entry_and_Effingham_Evaluation-September-17-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_J_and_J_Gaming_Nebraska_Entry_and_Effingham_Evaluation-September-17-2026.md]]

## His read on Georgia cash payouts — reported second-hand, and used as evidence

Ninety minutes later, at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-17-halcyon-amusements-georgia-class-a-and-texas-route-partnership.md|Halcyon breakfast]], Val used Sam as his authority for the view that a national operator will not touch cash payouts in Georgia:

> "I said, Sam, I have this product which in Nebraska will work very well for you. He says, Yeah, yeah, yeah, I love it. Bring it in… He says, **Do you have it for Chicago?** I said… **I don't have a license yet**… He says, 'Where are you concentrating right now?' I said, 'Well, I'm putting a lot of attention to Georgia.' He says, '**But in Georgia, it's illegal.**'… **My point is that Sam on the phone tells me that it's illegal**… this is the national company… **The last thing that they want is to get busted.**"

[[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Halcyon_Amusements_Georgia_Class_A_and_Texas_Route_Partnership-September-17-2026.md]]

**Two divergences between Val's retelling and the recording it retells**, recorded because the same morning supplies both: Sam's question in the recording is about **Illinois**, not **Chicago**; and the recording contains **no line in which Sam says Georgia is illegal** — the Georgia exchange in the recording ends with Sam asking how the product applies "when there's no cash out in Georgia", and Val answering with the Georgia kiosk variant. Val's account may draw on an earlier conversation the corpus does not hold. Neither version is preferred.

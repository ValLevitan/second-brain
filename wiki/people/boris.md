# Boris (former JVL shareholder)

Boris is a **former shareholder of the old JVL Corp** who appears in JVL's 2025 loan reconciliation, paired with [[wiki/people/peter.md|Peter]]. Surname is **not stated**. [[raw/finance_loans_review_sean_alka-june-13-2025.md]]

- **Loan-to-equity (unresolved on the books).** In the 2006 JVL Corp → JVL Labs restructuring (after the Pennsylvania copyright case, with 2011-680 Ontario stepping in as secured creditor), Boris — like Peter — had a loan of **~$150,000** that Val converted to **shares gratuitously**, but the loan was **never reclassified** off the balance sheet. On the June-13 2025 review, Val directed the correction (a debt-forgiveness entry) after consulting the lawyer [[wiki/people/alan.md|Alan Litwack]]; Peter is disputing the conversion and Val believes he "may have a claim against Peter and Boris." Full context on [[wiki/topics/jvl-corporate-loans-and-shareholder-financing.md]].

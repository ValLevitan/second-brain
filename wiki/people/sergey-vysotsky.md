# Sergey Vysotsky

Sergey Vysotsky is a **digital-growth specialist** who works with [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]]. Klimovich described him to [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] on Sept 22 2026 as the person "который за диджитал гроуз вообще отвечает" — responsible for digital growth overall [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Marketing_Lead_and_GA_Demand_Campaign-September-22-2026.md]].

## Role (as Klimovich described it, Sept 22 2026)

- **Strategy, not ad set-up.** He can work through Google "во всех его вариантах", but "непосредственно настройка рекламных… компании он профессионально не занимается". He "может выстроить целую стратегию" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Marketing_Lead_and_GA_Demand_Campaign-September-22-2026.md]].
- **Current work: ECHO growth.** "сейчас он помогает нам с ростом эко" — see [[wiki/topics/echo-home-gaming-device.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Marketing_Lead_and_GA_Demand_Campaign-September-22-2026.md]].
- **Background, as stated:** a product manager who worked with Klimovich at an earlier company, and at "одной из самых крупных в Белоруссии мобильных компаний. Аполлон, который потом Apple купила". This is Klimovich's account and is not checked here [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Marketing_Lead_and_GA_Demand_Campaign-September-22-2026.md]].
- **Based in Warsaw** ("Сейчас в Варшаве") [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Marketing_Lead_and_GA_Demand_Campaign-September-22-2026.md]].

## Engagement terms (as stated)

- He works with JVL **part-time** ("на полставки") and runs a separate startup of his own in parallel [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Marketing_Lead_and_GA_Demand_Campaign-September-22-2026.md]].
- By their arrangement he stays **at least two more months**, then it is reviewed: "ещё 2 месяца он точно с нами, потом пересмотрим" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Marketing_Lead_and_GA_Demand_Campaign-September-22-2026.md]].
- If he leaves, Klimovich said he will hand over and can recommend people: "он мягко передаст всё… он сделает переход", and "он останется на связи" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Marketing_Lead_and_GA_Demand_Campaign-September-22-2026.md]].

# Irina Shishko

Irina Shishko is a JVL **Minsk workshop/shop-floor coordinator** — the person the engineers go to when they need machines, parts and a working station organised in the Minsk shop. She reconciles to the Minsk-payroll name **"Shyshko Iryna"** (see [[wiki/topics/minsk-staff-roster.md]]), who gains a page here on first appearing in a transcript. ## Role

- On the May-1 dispensing-tower design call, [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] told [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi]] that to get the workshop to prepare a working station for [[wiki/people/evgeniy-alekseev.md|Evgeny]] (machines, printers, bill acceptors, spare parts) the person to contact is Irina Shishko — describing her as the one who "**глотку перегрызет и сделает что нужно**" (will bite through throats and get it done), in contrast to the helpful-but-passive [[wiki/people/alexei-khamin.md|Khamin]]. Val offered to open a chat with her. [[raw/flex_tower_spacial_design_review-may-1-2025.md]]

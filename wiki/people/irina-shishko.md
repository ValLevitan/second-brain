# Irina Shishko

Irina Shishko is a JVL **Minsk workshop/shop-floor coordinator** — the person the engineers go to when they need machines, parts and a working station organised in the Minsk shop. She reconciles to the Minsk-payroll name **"Shyshko Iryna"** (see [[wiki/topics/minsk-staff-roster.md]]), who gains a page here on first appearing in a transcript. [[raw/flex_tower_spacial_design_review-may-1-2025.md]]

## Role

- On the May-1 dispensing-tower design call, [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] told [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi]] that to get the workshop to prepare a working station for [[wiki/people/evgeniy-alekseev.md|Evgeny]] (machines, printers, bill acceptors, spare parts) the person to contact is Irina Shishko — describing her as the one who "**глотку перегрызет и сделает что нужно**" (will bite through throats and get it done), in contrast to the helpful-but-passive [[wiki/people/alexei-khamin.md|Khamin]]. Val offered to open a chat with her. [[raw/flex_tower_spacial_design_review-may-1-2025.md]]

## May 28–29 2025 — named on two engineering calls (two-Irinas flag)

The transcripts of the **May-28 jackpot-treatment call** ([[wiki/meetings/2025-05-28-jp-jackpot-class3-georgia-coam.md]]) and the **May-29 e-Wallet/TITO review** ([[wiki/meetings/2025-05-29-ewallet-tito-flexpay-implementation.md]]) both name **"Irina Shishko" / "Ирина Шишко"** as an attendee — but in a **QA / meter-interpretation / release-process** role (jackpot-meter questions on the 28th; firmware-version naming, test-vs-production folders and build-numbering on the 29th). That substance historically belongs to [[wiki/people/irina-makarych.md|Irina Makarych]] (the QA/build Irina), not to a shop-floor coordinator. Recorded as transcribed here **without reconciling** — this reinforces the strained two-Irinas split and is **queued Tier A** for an owner ruling on whether these engineering-meeting "Irina Shishko" mentions are actually Makarych.

# Mary

Mary coordinates JVL's Mexico and international operations and translates between Val (English) and Spanish-speaking staff.

## Role

- Mary translated Val's instructions to Miguel on the Mexico shipment call, and Val asked her to stop checking whether Miguel understood each sentence and simply assume he doesn't and translate. [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]]
- Val asked her to send him two separate shipping quotes (ASIMEX Mexico City to Laredo, and the Laredo-to-Toronto leg). [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]]
- In the retrofit discussion, Val said Mary could find out from the Mexico side whether the Maple machines had had their CPU boards removed before shipping. [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]]
- On the Elevate financing call, Val said James Reble would wait for "Mary" to share the shipment paperwork before releasing the final payment tranche. [[raw/james_reble_elevate_january_14_2025.md]]
- On the Anibal call, "Mery" is credited with suggesting Anibal's relocation from Peru to Puerto Rico. [[raw/anibal_call_011325_meeting_notes_2025-01-14.md]]
- **Feb 2025 (Alka task-map call, date inferred):** with the Peru office closing she stays on remotely (moving to subcontract; see [[wiki/topics/jvl-finance-back-office.md]]); Val's target load for her is **~15–20 h/week of finance** plus logistics, deliberately keeping slack — "Mary is the type of a girl that you could throw her at anything … eager to learn … she doesn't say no. And she's responsive" (she answered a units query instantly, with pictures). She is also the suggested conduit for notifying Floor (Peru) of her termination. [[raw/Finance - Alka.md]]

The retrofit transcript spells her name "Мэри" (Mary); she is also spelled "Mery" elsewhere. Her last name is not stated (the 2026 contractor roster's **Mery Villavicencio** is the likely match — see [[wiki/topics/jvl-contractor-roster.md]]).

## Apr 15 2025 (scope sheet)

- On Alka's control sheet Mary reads ~**50% utilised**; Hector "is still training Mary, though. Mary is learning slowly. And she's nervous. So he's slowly handing over the reins to her more and more." [[raw/finance_update-apr-15-2025.md]]

## Apr 28 2025 — POs for non-BOM items; assigned to the tariff/HS subject

On the BC status call ([[wiki/meetings/2025-04-28-business-central-labs-tariffs-status.md]]) Val's role model has **Mary create purchase orders for non-BOM items** (e.g. GLI / administrational), keeping [[wiki/people/mikhail-perelman.md|Michael]] focused on purchasing bill-of-material. Val also said he is **assigning Mary closely** to the HS-code / tariff / international-trade-attorney subject — see [[wiki/topics/tariffs-and-customs-compliance.md]]. [[raw/business_central_update-apr-28-2025.md]]

## June 26 2025 — logistics stays with Mary; relays Anibal's move

On the [[wiki/meetings/2025-06-26-procurement-manufacturing-transfer-alignment.md|manufacturing-transfer alignment]] Val confirmed **incoming and outgoing logistics stay on Mary** (not moved to Elizabeth, who owns warehousing/inventory/scheduling). Mary in Peru **advised [[wiki/people/anibal.md|Anibal]] he's moving to Georgia** (Atlanta) to assemble Echo machines. On the accounting side she is one of "the same team" (with Hector) working the Georgia system. See [[wiki/decisions/2025-06-24-move-manufacturing-from-canada-to-georgia.md]].

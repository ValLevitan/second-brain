# Miguel

Miguel manages JVL's warehouse in Mexico. He handles the machines stored there and prepares them for shipping.

## Role

- Miguel selected the 100 machines in the best condition to ship first from the Mexico warehouse. [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]]
- He gave the packing cost (about $45 per machine) and confirmed the shipping arrangements. [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]]
- He mainly speaks Spanish; Mary translated Val's instructions to him during the call. [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]]
- Val gave Miguel a direct order: after shipping the 100 machines, separate the scrap machines from the Altera machines, and call in external auditors to count the scrap so Val can write it off inventory. [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]]

His last name is not stated.

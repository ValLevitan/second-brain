# Kyle Shepherd (Lucky Coin)

**Kyle Shepherd runs [[wiki/organizations/lucky-coin.md|Lucky Coin]]**, a third-generation Georgia COAM route of about 50 locations and 250 machines, together with his younger brother Curtis. He is an existing JVL customer and the purchasing decision-maker: asked who decides what to buy, "it's me. We're still a family business, so I run the business" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].

*(Spelling: the derived notes give **Kyle Shepherd** and cite the calendar invite for the Sept-2 meeting; the transcript renders the spoken introduction "Kyle Shepard," which is also how the wiki carried him from Jun-24 and Jul-6 2026. Kept under Shepherd with Shepard as an alias — not resolved by the owner.)* [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]]

## Who he is

- **Third generation in the business.** His maternal grandfather (surname **Jones**) started the route in the early 1990s; his father joined a few years later; his mother owns the company today. Kyle was "probably 10ish when they really got going. And that's all I've ever done since I was big enough to chase them around and help [count] money… **Everything went into rubber bands of fifties**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **His brother Curtis** is his partner in running the route; the hardest service complaints reaching Kyle come from Curtis. Kyle runs a monthly internal "board meeting" with his own people — "I get my guys in the room together once a month, and just kind of make sure we're on the same page" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].

## How he deals with JVL

- **He gave the service criticism straight when asked, having prepared it.** He had put the question to his own monthly meeting the day before specifically because he was coming to JVL. The full account is on [[wiki/organizations/lucky-coin.md]]; his headline was "we have **more service issues out of our JVL games than we do most others**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **He separates the criticism from the relationship.** "We haven't pulled the JVLs off our route yet… I'm happy to work with you guys any way that I can… I don't expect you to fix them overnight. But if we can see that there's a real attempt to make it better… then I'm happy to keep trying these JVLs" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **He volunteers, and says his own people give him grief for it.** "My brother, my guys… they get upset with me because I volunteer for this stuff all the time. But man, I would love to help you… I don't know anything about programming, but I would love to work with you on the **operating side** of this" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **He offered access to his technicians without being asked, and warned Val what he would hear.** "If ever you're interested in talking to my brother or my technicians or my route guys… **they're going to tell you exactly what they're dealing with.**" Val took it on the spot; the office visit was set for **Wednesday September 9, 8:30 AM** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **He was clear that the technology argument has to survive contact with his technicians.** On JVL's remote-configuration controls: "That's a great feature. **When that technology starts to make it difficult for my technicians to work on it, is when that becomes an issue for us as an operator**" — and, on why the conversation was worth having, "it's important that you know how my side of the business works, right? So that you can design a game that has the technology that you want to have, but **also is easy for us to operate**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **He and Val disagreed, unresolved, on whether a frozen machine goes unnoticed.** Val's case is that a frozen machine still looks alive, the player moves on without complaining, and "we end up with the machine sitting there for half the time not functioning, and nobody knows about it." Kyle rejected it from experience: "I get those calls on your JVLs **regularly**… I would have to [have] one of your games or any other game on my route go **more than a day** being down without me knowing about it." He conceded only that it may not be real-time. Val's counter: "a day is a lot" — and he wants to know "the instant it happens." See [[wiki/topics/egm-freezing-investigation.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **On JVL's fraud-control reason for locking down components, he took the point and then declined the feature.** Told that the reset requirement exists to stop a technician swapping a new bill validator for a used one, he answered "I never considered that angle" and "I can tell you with a very high degree of confidence that me personally have not faced any" — at which Val offered to remove it: "then you don't need that feature, and we should remove it" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **What he actually wants to buy is cash.** "If it could spit out cash, I'd throw that in every one of my locations" — while separately predicting the competing ATM-style products will "create a real problem with legislators in Georgia" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].

## Sept 9 2026 — he made good on the offer, and then had to answer a much bigger ask

The visit he offered on Sept 2 happened a week later at his own shop ([[wiki/meetings/2026-09-09-lucky-coin-service-burden-and-exclusive-partnership.md|Sept 9, 1:31 PM]]). His technicians' testimony is on [[wiki/organizations/lucky-coin.md]]; what he himself did in the room is here [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]].

- **He ran the comparison question himself, in front of Val.** He put it to his own technicians — "Compared to all of our other games… would you say that JVLs require an **average** amount of attention, service calls from us, or **above average** or **below average**?" — and then supplied the arithmetic behind their "above average": "**We have a relatively small number of them on our route, and we spend proportionately a greater amount of time on them** than we do on a lot of other games that we have" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]].
- **He separated the service problem from the product again, as on Sept 2.** "They look great, **players love them**. I can tell you that from our route. **When they're working, they get played**"; and the one fix that would change things: "**if you could get rid of the decommission recommission thing, that would help a ton**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]].
- **The real obstacle he named is price, and he gave the whole comparison unprompted.** "I don't have a clue. I'm nobody to tell you what to charge people. Just from my perspective. If I'm looking at buying your new game for [fifteen seven fifty]… or **I can get a new aristocrat for eighteen or nineteen**… now I can put so many aristocrats in a location… Can get a **new Bad Dog game for ten or twelve thousand bucks**. So **for them, it's got to work better**… **where do I get the most bang for my buck**? And that's not to say that the JVLs don't play because they do. **A few of them that do really well.** I just have to be honest with you that **that's a hurdle**… **It doesn't make sense everywhere for me. Whereas it might if it were a little less expensive**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]].
- **His blocker on the exclusivity offer is his own people's patience, not the economics.** "we've done **a whole bunch of testing the last couple years**… **I'm afraid if I tell these guys we're testing something else, they're gonna be outside my office with pitchforks and lanterns** trying to get me." He was explicit that he had not refused: "**I haven't given up on it, Val**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]].
- **He said plainly what the visit itself was worth to him** — and simultaneously named two competitors who already do it. "**I want you to understand that this means a ton. The fact that you're willing to sit here and listen to us and hear us and… try to work with us**"; against "I've had numerous conversations like with [Fiery Pay]… **Bad Dog has done it with us a whole bunch of times**… **I don't know that it gets any more designed to our specs than that.** Um, **but it doesn't happen often, Val. It really doesn't**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]].
- **He corrected Val's count of his own route: "North of forty"** locations, against Val's "you have thirty locations" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]].
- **He does not read the reports himself.** "**He's the numbers guy.** I don't. So **I have a numbers guy that brings the numbers to me.** So yes, I do look at statistics" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]].
- **And he did not take the modular promise on trust** — he pushed Val on whether the Pay unit can be opened up for a recycler if Georgia's rules change, "just so for these guys' sake, because they weren't in your office." Val's answer was no; see [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-hold-the-recycler-back-but-sign-no-undertaking.md]] (Sept 9) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]].

The offer he had to answer is on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-val-asks-lucky-coin-for-its-entire-business.md]]; his answer, a week later, is below.

## Sept 16 2026 — he says no, in a six-minute phone call he placed himself

He rang Val on **Wednesday Sept 16 at 2:42 PM**, a day after the deadline ([[wiki/meetings/2026-09-16-lucky-coin-exclusivity-declined-and-pilot-opening.md|the call]]; the decision is [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-16-lucky-coin-declines-exclusivity-and-opens-a-kiosk-pilot.md]]) [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Lucky_Coin_Exclusivity_Declined_and_Pilot_Opening-September-16-2026.md]].

- **He refused the exclusivity and nothing else.** "as much as I appreciate your offer, the the exclusivity. Really, what I'm hung up on. **I just don't know that I want to put myself in a box like that. As fast as the technology is changing in this state**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Exclusivity_Declined_and_Pilot_Opening-September-16-2026.md]].
- **He came with a counter, not just a no:** more JVL machines worked into the route, and "**your PLS, your kiosk in a location or two**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Exclusivity_Declined_and_Pilot_Opening-September-16-2026.md]].
- **He explained his own decision limits — this is the clearest statement of them in the corpus.** "**we are a family business**, and some decisions, like a lot of most decisions, Val, **I can make on my own. But when somebody starts making offers, like you were making offers, I wanted to talk it over with Curtis and Barry and those guys** and kind of take some time with them before I made a call." *(On Sept 2 he called himself the purchasing decision-maker — "it's me… so I run the business." The two are not in conflict on their own terms: routine buying is his, an offer of this size is not. Recorded as **evolution/qualification**, not a contradiction, and the Sept-2 statement stands.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Exclusivity_Declined_and_Pilot_Opening-September-16-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]]
- **The extra week did not change the answer.** "**I probably would have given you this. Answer when we met last week**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Exclusivity_Declined_and_Pilot_Opening-September-16-2026.md]].
- **He volunteered that Val's manner was not the problem**, unprompted and while cutting in: "**I don't mean to cut you off. That doesn't bother me at all. The way that we've spoken so far doesn't turn me off at all. I don't feel like you've been too aggressive or too pushy**"; "**I feel like we have a little chemistry, so we'll keep working together and we'll make something go**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Exclusivity_Declined_and_Pilot_Opening-September-16-2026.md]].
- *(**Barry**, named in that consultation circle, matches nobody in the corpus — his Sept-9 circle was Curtis and Aaron. Left unresolved, §4; the derived notes ask for Val's confirmation first.)* [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Lucky_Coin_Exclusivity_Declined_and_Pilot_Opening-September-16-2026.md]]

**Corroborated from a second recording made five minutes later.** On [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-16-jvl-transactional-ecosystem-and-class-a-market-strategy.md|the 2:53 PM strategy session]] Val told a JVL colleague: "**Also, one more news. Kyle Shepard is back online. He called me. He's afraid of exclusivity. Everything else works.**" He called Kyle's counter "**a smaller, smaller portion, test it, try it… absolutely civilized and frugal approach**", repeated the apology he had made on the call — "**I said I was a little bit aggressive in my offering**" — and added, unprompted, "**I actually appreciate the fact that he called and had a very decent conversation.**" An hour later he generalised from it: "**at the end a decision is made, just like Kyle made a decision. You could give any deal you want, but first they'll try it**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_JVL_Transactional_Ecosystem_and_Class_A_Market_Strategy-September-16-2026.md]].

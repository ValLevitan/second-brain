He is the day-to-day "JJ" of the Peru operation. *(Spelling note: the owner rendered the surname "Catillo"; the earlier-ingested transcripts and the common form render it "Castillo" — kept as "Castillo" with "Catillo" as an alias, pending owner confirmation.

# Anibal

Anibal runs JVL's operations in Peru. As of **June 2025 he is moving to Georgia (Atlanta)** to be JVL's dedicated Echo/amusement-countertop assembler — superseding the earlier plan to relocate him to Puerto Rico (now in History below). His last name is not stated.

## Current status — moving to Georgia (June 24–26 2025)

- When JVL decided to move manufacturing to Georgia ([[wiki/decisions/2025-06-24-move-manufacturing-from-canada-to-georgia.md]]), Val had **[[wiki/people/mary.md|Mary]] in Peru advise Anibal he's moving to Georgia** (Atlanta), to be the **dedicated individual who assembles Echo machines** — Val's "Porsche one-team" model (a dedicated line producing one product all the time, not mass production), fitting the growing Echo business. [[raw/procurement_relocation-june-24-2025.md]] [[raw/Alignment_procurement_manufacturing_transfer-june-26-2025.md]]

## Sept 9 2025 — payroll setup (finance)

On the [[wiki/meetings/2025-09-09-finance-recurring-alka.md|Sept-9 finance call]], [[wiki/people/alka-sood.md|Alka]] raised how to pay Anibal while his payroll setup with [[wiki/people/monica.md|Monica]] is pending (contractor from Labs vs Systems). Val noted that Anibal was **transferred to JVL Systems from JVL Peru**, and asked that he and Monica review his papers together. [[raw/finance_recurring_meeting-september-9-2025.md]]

## History — Role and the earlier Puerto Rico plan (Jan 2025)

- Told Juan he was preparing machines in Peru for shipment to Toronto. [[raw/anibal_call_011325_meeting_notes_2025-01-14.md]]
- JVL's Peru office is set to close at the end of February (no exact date given). [[raw/anibal_call_011325_meeting_notes_2025-01-14.md]]
- His potential relocation to Puerto Rico was discussed; Anibal mentioned Mery's suggestion. His main concern was support for the move — salary and living arrangements. [[raw/anibal_call_011325_meeting_notes_2025-01-14.md]]
- With the Peru office closing in late February, Anibal wants no gap in work and would like to start in Puerto Rico as soon as possible. [[raw/anibal_call_011325_meeting_notes_2025-01-14.md]]

See the project page [[wiki/projects/puerto-rico-expansion.md]] for the (now-superseded) PR context.

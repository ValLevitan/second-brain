# Troy Erikson

Owner of [[wiki/organizations/mg-oil.md|MG Oil]], the largest video-lottery-terminal (VLT) route operator in South Dakota, and JVL's prospective anchor customer / beta-test partner for the [[wiki/projects/south-dakota-vlt-market.md|South Dakota VLT market entry]]. He was introduced to [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] on a **May 13 2025** call — the introduction JVL had been waiting for since the Jan-7 decision to pursue MG Oil ([[wiki/decisions/2025-01-07-pursue-mg-oil-south-dakota.md]]). [[raw/mg_oil_south_dakota_intro-may-13-2025.md]]

- **Based in South Dakota** most of the time; MG Oil has an office in **Sioux Falls** (the state's largest city, where most operators are, east of the Missouri River) and one in **Rapid City** on the west end. Troy effectively runs the business himself. [[raw/mg_oil_south_dakota_intro-may-13-2025.md]]
- Told Val he **saw JVL's machines** (the **V43 cabinet and the D27**) at the South Dakota state lottery office about a month earlier, during a meeting there with "Norm and the boys." [[raw/mg_oil_south_dakota_intro-may-13-2025.md]]
- **Dana** is Troy's **general manager**, overseeing all of MG Oil's gaming; Troy will pass Dana's contact to JVL so Dana and JVL's product manager can coordinate. Val named **[[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]]** as the JVL product manager who will handle the relationship. [[raw/mg_oil_south_dakota_intro-may-13-2025.md]]

## The beta-test offer (May 13)

- Troy wants MG Oil to be JVL's **beta-test site** once JVL clears the lottery/GLI steps: **~4 A-plus locations** — one in **Rapid City** (west, mountain time), one or two in **Sioux Falls** (east, central time), and **Pierre + Fort Pierre** in the center (to catch a known time-zone mismatch between the machine and the state) — **one machine per location**, run **14–21 days**. If the beta goes well, MG Oil would then order **10–100 machines** at an agreed price. [[raw/mg_oil_south_dakota_intro-may-13-2025.md]]
- Val agreed to **ship six machines** to Troy's office. Troy also keeps one in his back room in **demo mode** so the ~50 casino managers who visit daily can play the games and form opinions before a beta. [[raw/mg_oil_south_dakota_intro-may-13-2025.md]]
- Corroborating detail (matches the [[wiki/projects/south-dakota-vlt-market.md|SD project]]'s May-1 note): Troy heard JVL's new SD office employee **starts May 19** and will look for a **2,000–3,000 sq ft office/warehouse**, ideally near the Nebraska border. [[raw/mg_oil_south_dakota_intro-may-13-2025.md]]

## June 18 2026 — described second-hand by his general manager, in his absence

Troy did not attend the [[wiki/meetings/2026-06-18-mg-oil-dana-giesey-rapid-city-visit.md|June-18 2026 Rapid City visit]] — he had "**been gone all week**." Everything below is [[wiki/people/dana-giesey.md|Dana Giesey]]'s account of him, given to Val in the room. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]

- **He is buying the company from its founder.** "**Troy's in the process, I think basically of buying the company from his dad.**" The founder is named here as **Martin**, a former Standard Oil rep. Company detail on [[wiki/organizations/mg-oil.md]] (Jun-18). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **He lives in Rapid City** and is in and out of the head office rather than at a desk — "he's **in and out**", and "I think he's coming back to town sometime this evening." *(A closing line, "He spends two hours a day here, but he's not here every day," sits immediately after Kevin Laird is introduced and may describe either man; **it is not attributed to Troy here**.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **He is the buyer, and he is waiting for data.** MG Oil was taking delivery of the last of ten JVL machines that week, and "**He told me he'd like to see sixty to ninety days of numbers come in before he decides on future purchases.**" Dana could not commit to anything further. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **He is JVL's landlord in South Dakota.** The office lease Troy did with JVL is **month-to-month**, which is what let Val decide on the spot to move it — see [[wiki/decisions/2026-06-18-jvl-moves-its-south-dakota-office-to-nebraska.md]]. Dana undertook to raise the move with him the next day. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **He is one of the three decision-makers for MG Oil's Nebraska business** (with Robert "Whitey" Tolliver and Mike Kotab), and Dana believes **he would sell that Nebraska operation** to the right buyer. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **Temperament, as Dana put it** — the line is garbled but follows directly on Val asking about Troy: "he's kind of [a] guy that is… **I can't get too worked up about anything.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **Val asked to meet him** and was told to email him directly and offered **Sioux Falls** as an alternative meeting place if Val were ever in that part of the state. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]

# Daniel (EDS family principal)

Daniel is the **head of the family that runs [[wiki/organizations/eds-colombia.md|EDS]]**, the Colombian gaming operation that runs JVL machines. In mid-2025 he "has taken active role in running day-to-day company now" and wanted to get familiar with the accounting of the two JVL–EDS financing deals. Val described the family relationship as "20 years maybe, 15 years… not one error, ever. And not one conflict, ever." Daniel is also **a lawyer** and speaks both English and Spanish (his English "rusty," he apologised, but was fully understood). [[raw/finance_eds_jvl_alignment-july-1-2025.md]]

- He said he "was in the 90% of the company negotiation" but stepped back from the EDS/machine-financing details; he had spent the prior two weeks (up to 2 a.m. the morning of the call) reconstructing the business flow from information he and **[[wiki/people/eliana.md|Eliana]]** held, and now understands it. [[raw/finance_eds_jvl_alignment-july-1-2025.md]]
- On the call he verified a recent ~**$39,048** wire had landed in EDS's US (Bank of 10 / "PNC") account and undertook to remit ~$39,000 onward to [[wiki/organizations/elevate.md|Elevate]], plus a Colombian-side transfer via [[wiki/people/marta.md|Marta]]. [[raw/finance_eds_jvl_alignment-july-1-2025.md]]
- Warm, family framing throughout ("my mamá te ama… nuestra familia te aprecia"). Val proposed an **annual review** of the payments to give Daniel comfort going forward. [[raw/finance_eds_jvl_alignment-july-1-2025.md]]

*(Surname not stated. The family also employs [[wiki/people/eliana.md|Eliana]] as the EDS decision-maker and retains [[wiki/people/julian-puerta.md|Julian Puerta]] as operations consultant.)*

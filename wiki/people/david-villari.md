# David Villari

Sales lead at the machine maker [[wiki/organizations/bad-dog-zephyr.md|Bad Dog / Zephyr]] — "I run the sales for them." He met [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] and [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]] over breakfast on **Oct 16 2026** and offered to connect JVL with a distributor in Puerto Rico [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]. Meeting: [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-16-david-villari-zephyr-puerto-rico-distribution-and-beta-test.md]].

## Background (his own account, Oct 16 2026)

- **Banilla.** "I worked for Banilla for seven years, eight years." "I ran their sales department, but Texas, Pennsylvania and Virginia were my big markets." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]- **Riken (2024).** He started a company "called Riken" to give grey-market operators the game-performance reporting that regulated markets have ("Money in, money out, location"). The partner's R&D "took too long… I gave it two years", so "I got out". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]
- **Bad Dog from January 2026.** "that's when I started working with Bad Dog in January 2026." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]

## Same man as the Sept-9 "David Milani"? (inference)

On Sept 9 2026 Val asked [[wiki/people/narasimha-rao-namburi.md|Rao]] for a direct line to a younger Bad Dog salesman, "David Milani", also said as "David. Vilari. With a V", whom Rao called "Very close to access, too". Rao's garbled account of his past ("Bandel", then "Raikon security") fits Banilla and Riken [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]]. Reading "Milani" / "Vilari" as David Villari is an **inference** (name, employer and history match). On that reading, Val's Sept-9 commitment to reach him was met at the Oct-16 breakfast — see [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]].

## What he told JVL (Oct 16 2026)

- **Puerto Rico** — the market size, licence rules, price band, shipping terms and the people who can distribute or place games. All on [[wiki/projects/puerto-rico-expansion.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]
- **His own company** — owner, staff, Puerto Rico entry, Fiery Pay work. On [[wiki/organizations/bad-dog-zephyr.md]].
- **Banilla is losing ground**, in his view: "They just lost their largest buyers in Missouri. Missouri shut down… 33% of their business, just cut off overnight", and in Pennsylvania "as of October 13… you can't even have the equipment in the location". He expects those machines to move to Texas, which "erodes sales". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]
- **Grover and Palisades.** Grover ran "a route pull tab business. Charitable gaming", about "ten thousand machines in the field"; it was sold to Light & Wonder, and its owner Garrett Blackwell "started a new company called Palisades", which is registered in Georgia and plans to sell skill games there. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]
- **Cabinets.** "nobody plays duals anymore except for Aristocrat and IGT… verticals still play better than duals." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]
- **Val's CashCode past.** David linked CashCode to CPI ("What did they turn into when they bought? CPI.") and named an Andreas who "used to work for JVL for CashCode". Who Andreas is, is **unclear**; not linked. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]

## His offer

He will call his Puerto Rico contacts and put Val and Felipe "in contact with the right guy", suggests a small 90-day test, and wants nothing for himself: "I don't want the commission. I'm just helping you out." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]] His open commitments are listed on [[wiki/projects/puerto-rico-expansion.md]].

# Jai

**Jai** runs **orders and the service-call department** for [[wiki/organizations/akshar-gaming.md|Akshar Gaming]], and shares the purchasing decisions for **Ananta Amusement** with [[wiki/people/neel.md|Neel]]. He came to the JVL Georgia office with [[wiki/people/neel.md|Neel]] on **September 1 2026** in place of [[wiki/people/vishal-patel.md|Vishal Patel]] — see [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-01-narayani-akshar-mega-hit-demo-and-trade-in.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

Val greeted him by name at the top of the recording — "Hey, hi. Good to see you again. **Jay**, I'm Val" — so this was not their first meeting. **Both spellings are in the transcript itself:** it renders him "Jay" when Val speaks to him, and "**Jai**" when [[wiki/people/neel.md|Neel]] says "**Me and Jai will decide**." The derived notes use **Jai** throughout, which is the form used here; "Jay" is recorded as an alias. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

## His role

Asked directly what he does: "**I'm handling all the orders**… Sending the **EFPs** and all the other things, **all the service calls, the whole service call department. If his customer needs something, they have to call the service line number.**" *("EFPs" is transcribed as spoken and is **not** resolved — it matches nothing in the corpus.)* Val's follow-up — "So the decision, the technical decision, is made between the two of you" — got "**Most of the time**," qualified by Vishal's sign-off. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

Day-to-day service calls themselves are **outsourced to Angie**, who used to work for the company and now runs her own service-call team — detail on [[wiki/organizations/akshar-gaming.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

**Attribution caveat.** The transcript gives both him and Neel the same speaker label, so apart from the two exchanges above — where Val addresses each man in turn — **no line in the meeting is attributed to him here**. The substantive content of the visit is recorded as Neel's, following the derived notes.

# Puerto Rico expansion

JVL's early-2025 move to stand up an operation in Puerto Rico, targeting the route market first.

## Current state (as of January 2025)

- **Market focus:** the route market (machines in neighborhoods, stores, gas stations) rather than casinos [[raw/anibal_call_011325_meeting_notes_2025-01-14.md]].
- **Office/company setup:** Igor handling registration paperwork and legal steps; expected in 2–3 months [[raw/anibal_call_011325_meeting_notes_2025-01-14.md]].
- **Staffing:** Anibal (currently Peru) is the likely person to run it, pending salary/living terms; the Peru office closes end of February 2025 [[raw/anibal_call_011325_meeting_notes_2025-01-14.md]].
- **Trial:** five test machines being prepared before the office officially opens [[raw/anibal_call_011325_meeting_notes_2025-01-14.md]].
- **Licensing:** operations depend on getting the required licenses; readiness expected late March / April 2025 [[raw/anibal_call_011325_meeting_notes_2025-01-14.md]].

## Machine supply

- Altera and Altera V machines from the retrofit program are earmarked for Puerto Rico — see [[wiki/projects/machine-inventory-refurbishment.md]] and [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]].
- Puerto Rico is also named as a distribution market in the 2025 strategy [[raw/jvl_strategic_plan_and_vision_2025_2025-01-08.md]].

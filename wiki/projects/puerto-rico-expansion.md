# Puerto Rico expansion

JVL's plan to open an operation in Puerto Rico, tied to the closure of its Peru office. Puerto Rico is one of JVL's main land-based expansion targets for 2025 (the route market).

## Plan and timeline (as of Jan 13 2025)

- Igor is handling the paperwork and legal steps to register the company and set up the office; expected in about 2–3 months. [[raw/anibal_call_011325_meeting_notes_2025-01-14.md]]
- Initial focus is the **route market** — machines in neighborhoods, stores, and gas stations — rather than casinos. [[raw/anibal_call_011325_meeting_notes_2025-01-14.md]]
- Five machines are being prepared as a trial before the office officially opens. [[raw/anibal_call_011325_meeting_notes_2025-01-14.md]]
- Operations depend on getting the required licenses; readiness anticipated by late March or April. [[raw/anibal_call_011325_meeting_notes_2025-01-14.md]]

## Val's foreign-market vendor structure (a standing principle)

Val directed the Puerto Rico setup to follow the structure he uses in Mexico, Colombia and Peru: [[raw/Puerto Rico progress report - January 29, 2025.md]]

- Keep the **resident agent separate** from the lawyer (combining them "never works"); keep the **customs agent separate** too (customs + freight can be combined).
- Engage the **first** relationships with a resident agent and a freight/customs broker; use their referrals to find a **frugal, local, well-entrenched corporate lawyer and corporate accountant** (not specialists).
- Keep a small retainer only for the day-to-day corporate lawyer (Mexico example: ~$1,500–2,000/month) and a regular accounting firm (used for overflow); **specialists** (tax lawyer, tax accountant) are engaged per-need, never on retainer.
- Ask the five specialist firms only for the **gaming-licensing** work (operation / possession / sale of gaming devices) and strip out corporate, resident-agent and customs modules from their quotes.
- Possible referral source for a Puerto Rico contact: **Asimex** (JVL's Mexico freight/customs firm). [[raw/Puerto Rico progress report - January 29, 2025.md]]

## The market, from Val's site tour (Mar 24 2025)

After touring Puerto Rico locations, Val described the route market as "insanely large" and broke the island into **three distinct markets**: [[raw/games_markets-march-24-2025.md]]

- **Casinos** — only in resort hotels, taxed and regulated by the government (which does the physical cash **drop**, not the casino). **21 casinos with north of 5,000 machines.** [[raw/games_markets-march-24-2025.md]]
- **OTB / horse tracks** — an **exclusive contract held by [[wiki/organizations/scientific-games.md|Scientific Games]]**; thousands of small OTB outlets, upwards of **5,000 machines** (SciGames VLTs + generic third-party VLTs, all SciGames-managed), currently **tax-free**. [[raw/games_markets-march-24-2025.md]]
- **Route market** — largely unregulated; by any account **70,000–100,000 illegal machines** (old Atari-style units and copied Chinese machines, e.g. a copied WMS "Zeus/Kronos"). The government's stated goal is to **regulate, tax and standardise**, starting with a first tranche of **25,000** machines, then further tranches of ~25,000. Games often need to be **bilingual (Spanish)** for neighbourhoods outside San Juan. [[raw/games_markets-march-24-2025.md]]
- Machines that "cash out" do so via **pulse-metered POS** systems (like Georgia's "mother goose") rather than a true system. Val plans to go back within **two weeks**; [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan]] to travel too (while also returning to Georgia). [[raw/games_markets-march-24-2025.md]]

## Law-firm selection — the Estrella intro call (Apr 10 2025)

Val and [[wiki/people/monica.md|Monica]] (JVL in-house counsel) met the PR firm **[[wiki/organizations/estrella.md|Estrella]]** — [[wiki/people/carlos-infante.md|Carlos Infante]] plus his colleague Jose ([[wiki/meetings/2025-04-10-estrella-law-firm-carlos-infante.md]]). Igor and Juan were on the invite but Val asked them not to join ("we deal with lawyers privately"; they had briefed the firm beforehand). [[raw/estrella_law_carlos_infante_puerto_rico-april-10-2025.md]]

- **Val's stated posture:** the gaming-law change is an opportunity but "Puerto Rico is not an easy place to do business"; the law "has not really become a law yet"; and clients are opaque — "it's difficult to find the actual clients. They're in hiding for some reason … we were introduced to one … just one individual." So: "we would like to spend as little money as possible on testing waters … create a minimum legal structure." He found Estrella's proposal "doesn't look unreasonable." [[raw/estrella_law_carlos_infante_puerto_rico-april-10-2025.md]]
- **Regulator news (Carlos):** the new gaming-commission head is now nominated and confirmed (approved "when Juan and Igor were visiting Puerto Rico"); the commission's priority is increasing allowed in-route slot machines, including publishing **a list of the actual licensed clients** — which would fix the client-opacity problem (evolves the Feb-12 stuck "Ruta operators list" ask). [[raw/estrella_law_carlos_infante_puerto_rico-april-10-2025.md]]
- **The route agreed:** phase 1 market assessment with **no PR entity** (a US company visiting isn't doing business there; no resident agent needed until incorporation; a local business address / shared-space suffices later); phase 2 the **temporary licence** — needed because in PR **machine makers/importers/sellers are licensed** separately from operators, and likely needed even to clear machines through customs; phase 3 full licence. Entity choice (local company vs authorizing the Florida US entity) deferred — "mainly an issue of taxes"; initial contracts can sit on the Florida company with assignment clauses. Machines would ship **from the state of Georgia** and "need to remain our ownership" for re-export; Val to get the **tariff/classification code from the customs broker** (he had a customs-broker call the same day) for Estrella's import check — PR is a separate customs/treasury clearance from the mainland and some equipment needs special licences. [[raw/estrella_law_carlos_infante_puerto_rico-april-10-2025.md]]
- **First employee plan:** "our plan is to bring a first employee within three months … an engineer whose job is to … technically facilitate placement of the machines" — a **Peruvian citizen with US residence** (identity not named; consistent with the Jan-13 Anibal-relocation idea — inference, not confirmed). At the assessment stage he can visit without visa formalities. [[raw/estrella_law_carlos_infante_puerto_rico-april-10-2025.md]]
- **Tax pitch** (Carlos/Jose): no federal income tax; ~30% territory tax reducible to **4% by decree**; property-tax/capital-gains incentives; US tariffs (incl. China) apply. [[raw/estrella_law_carlos_infante_puerto_rico-april-10-2025.md]]
- **Marching orders:** Estrella to send the engagement letter (via their **NINTEX** e-sign platform; correspondence to Monica) and the temporary-licence estimate + delivery time **by Monday**; Val to see the customs broker and reconvene the following week. [[raw/estrella_law_carlos_infante_puerto_rico-april-10-2025.md]]

# Puerto Rico expansion

JVL's plan to open an operation in Puerto Rico, tied to the closure of its Peru office. Puerto Rico is one of JVL's main land-based expansion targets for 2025 (the route market).

## Plan and timeline (as of Jan 13 2025)

- Igor is handling the paperwork and legal steps to register the company and set up the office; expected in about 2–3 months. [[raw/anibal_call_011325_meeting_notes_2025-01-14.md]]
- Initial focus is the **route market** — machines in neighborhoods, stores, and gas stations — rather than casinos. [[raw/anibal_call_011325_meeting_notes_2025-01-14.md]]
- Five machines are being prepared as a trial before the office officially opens. [[raw/anibal_call_011325_meeting_notes_2025-01-14.md]]
- Operations depend on getting the required licenses; readiness anticipated by late March or April. [[raw/anibal_call_011325_meeting_notes_2025-01-14.md]]

## Staffing

- The Peru office is set to close at the end of February (no exact date given). Anibal, who runs Peru, may relocate to Puerto Rico; his concern is support for the move (salary, living arrangements), and he wants no gap in work. Mery suggested the relocation. See [[wiki/people/anibal.md]], [[wiki/people/igor.md]], [[wiki/people/mary.md]]. [[raw/anibal_call_011325_meeting_notes_2025-01-14.md]]

## Follow-ups noted

- Confirm the exact Peru office closure date; settle Anibal's salary and living conditions; complete and submit the Puerto Rico licensing documents; prepare and ship the first five test machines; keep Anibal updated. [[raw/anibal_call_011325_meeting_notes_2025-01-14.md]]

Note: Puerto Rico is also flagged as a priority in the FlexPay mobile-app discussion and was an earlier retrofit destination for Altera machines — see [[wiki/topics/jvl-machine-lineup.md]] and [[wiki/topics/flexpay-mobile-app-and-service-advocates.md]].

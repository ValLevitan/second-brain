# South Dakota VLT market entry

JVL's effort to enter the South Dakota video-lottery-terminal (VLT) route market. It began with the MG Oil opportunity (Jan 7) and, by late January, was in lab testing with the South Dakota lottery ahead of a first field release. [[raw/jira_value_stream_distribution_infrastructure_january_7_2025.md]] [[raw/sd_vlt_recap-jan-22-2025.md]]

## Market opportunity (Jan 7)

- **MG Oil** controls most of South Dakota and could give JVL access to ~200 locations via an introduction; JVL decided to pursue it. See [[wiki/decisions/2025-01-07-pursue-mg-oil-south-dakota.md]]. [[raw/jira_value_stream_distribution_infrastructure_january_7_2025.md]]

## Lab testing and certification path (Jan 22)

- The regulator/lab contact **Lonnie** is testing JVL's machine in the South Dakota lab. He agreed to accept **unapproved / experimental software** for pre-testing, which lets JVL iterate and reduce GLI costs before submitting final software to GLI. (Lonnie's exact affiliation — regulator vs. Light & Wonder lab — is unclear; see Owner Decisions.) [[raw/sd_vlt_recap-jan-22-2025.md]]
- Lonnie becomes tied up with lottery projects from **Feb 10**, so JVL has until ~Feb 9 to get software tested; the target is an urgent GLI approval and a first release around **Feb 1**, to be in the field by Feb 10. [[raw/sd_vlt_recap-jan-22-2025.md]]
- Val's caution: don't exhaust Lonnie's goodwill by over-using the unapproved-software favour. [[raw/sd_vlt_recap-jan-22-2025.md]]
- The system in the SD lab is a **Light & Wonder** (SAS) accounting system; JVL asked Light & Wonder for the lab system logs, and uses a **SAS emulator in Minsk** to reproduce ticket printing. [[raw/sd_vlt_recap-jan-22-2025.md]]

## Product issues to resolve for South Dakota

- **Printers:** the market's #1 printer is the **Transact Epic 950**, followed by FutureLogic/"Paycheck" printers; JVL must support the Epic 950. A **FutureLogic Gen 5** vs **Gen 2** discrepancy exists (software was written for Gen 2). See the printer decision [[wiki/decisions/2025-01-22-support-epic-950-printer-for-south-dakota.md]] and [[wiki/topics/jvl-machine-lineup.md]]. [[raw/sd_vlt_recap-jan-22-2025.md]]
- **Game-shutdown countdown bug:** the end-of-day "game about to be turned off" countdown (a lottery requirement) was wrongly triggering whenever the door was opened / the service menu was entered — a SAS-event handling issue to find urgently. [[raw/sd_vlt_recap-jan-22-2025.md]]
- **Volume:** operators want very quiet machines (Midwest venues where "you could hear a pin drop"); add an option to lower volume to zero. [[raw/sd_vlt_recap-jan-22-2025.md]]

### South Dakota market intel (from Troy, May 13)

- The SD market **replaces ~400 machines per year** across all manufacturers; it is a **mature market**. [[raw/mg_oil_south_dakota_intro-may-13-2025.md]]
- Competitors: **IGT** and **AGT** (both "very good companies"; **Grand Vision shares the AGT licence**), and **Grover** (the weakest — "people aren't buying anymore"). The **Signature** platform is still the best-played machine; Grand Vision's newer platform is weaker than Signature. **VLCs** are the old legacy machines being retired. [[raw/mg_oil_south_dakota_intro-may-13-2025.md]]
- A **10-machine location** typically holds two old VLCs plus a mix of AGT/Grand Vision, IGT and Grover; for a beta, MG Oil would pull the weakest machine (an old VLC or a Grover) and drop the JVL unit into an A-plus spot. [[raw/mg_oil_south_dakota_intro-may-13-2025.md]]

## May 7 2026 — the SD/NE/TX vertical roadmap sequenced: Rage through 2026, stagger content, defer Wolfpack-vs-Bison

On the [[wiki/meetings/2026-05-07-vertical-roadmap-omni-transition.md|May-7 vertical-roadmap call]] ([[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]], [[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael Gottlieb]], [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]], [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena Zhukova]], [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim Podvoiski]]) the Apr-9 "vertical for SD/NE/TX" next-initiative got a working plan. The platform-transition side (Rage→Omni timing, three-cabinet FP7 Omni V1) is on [[wiki/topics/omni-core-advancement-and-game-templating.md]] (May 7 2026). - **The vertical product = the Georgia liquid-glass vertical, adapted per market.** Juan: the Georgia vertical (liquid-glass cabinet) is the basis for all three — **South Dakota gets it as-is**, **Nebraska a slight variation**, and **Texas tracks with Nebraska**. The Nebraska/Texas version differs from SD in exactly two ways: **no Keno** and a **dual box (secondary skill)** — but that version needs a separate cabinet submission to the regulator, adding time. All three verticals ship on **Rage Studio through end of 2026** (Omni is infeasible in that window); SD needs only **one net-new game asset, Solar King**, everything else reused from the Georgia Jackpot Rush vertical work. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_vertical_roadmap_and_omni_transition-May-7-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_vertical_roadmap_and_omni_transition-May-7-2026.md]]
- **Stagger the content, don't drop 20 games at once.** Michael's math for the SD vertical: 3 Keno (Black Hills, Cool Cat, Solar) + 6 Lines-and-Ways + 5 Jackpot Rush + 6 Fiery Ice = **20 games**. His recommendation, accepted by Val and Juan ("not necessary"): release **2 Jackpot Rush + 2 Fiery Ice** per vertical release and pace the rest into the next SD/NE vertical release. Open feasibility question left standing: can the SD cabinet even support 20 simultaneous pay tables — tied to the unresolved SD "keno pay-tables maximum supportable game" question. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_vertical_roadmap_and_omni_transition-May-7-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_vertical_roadmap_and_omni_transition-May-7-2026.md]]
- **Wolfpack-vs-Twin-Bison ordering deferred to Georgia data.** Wolfpack is in the SD/NE vertical content with **Twin Bison** as additional content, but Juan refused to lock which leads until Georgia performance data lands — "if Bison is doing phenomenal, lead with something proven and make Wolfpack the second vertical release." Lena confirmed Wolfpack is in scope but **Bison is not in the Georgia asset set** (so the ordering only matters for SD/NE). Michael accepted the deferral. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_vertical_roadmap_and_omni_transition-May-7-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_vertical_roadmap_and_omni_transition-May-7-2026.md]]
- **D27 next on SD — not urgent.** Adding Keno via the next release "buys another year" before SD needs a new D27 product; no rush within the next ~nine months. Texas is the fastest entry (no regulator approval needed), so the sequence works backwards from Texas into Nebraska while SD goes through its lab/regulator path early next year. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_vertical_roadmap_and_omni_transition-May-7-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_vertical_roadmap_and_omni_transition-May-7-2026.md]]
- **Nebraska 9-day data discounted as inconclusive** — the field-performance comparison Juan ran (9 days before vs after install) is not apples-to-apples (the "before" window held two weekends vs one after, and the "after" had several zero-play days); [[wiki/people/denis-desmarais.md|Denis Desmarais]] (the SD/Nebraska field/border-office man — resolves by context) drives 3h + 2h to the two Nebraska sites to pull data, and Juan needs him to explain mechanically why several machines showed zero play (install timing, machines down, or a watchdog-reboot failure — Juan doubts the last). Val: "basically inconclusive." Nebraska specifics live on [[wiki/projects/nebraska-skill-market.md]]. §15.8 clear — market/product/machine economics only, no named-person compensation.

# JVL Insights — AI-driven company knowledge base (2024 proposal)

**JVL Insights** was a proposed internally-developed initiative to build a **company-wide, AI-driven knowledge base** — aggregating, categorizing and sharing JVL's "tribal knowledge" (what experts hold in their heads but never document) so employees could find accurate information, avoid duplicate effort, and "spend less time in meetings." The draft frames it under the goal **"ONE-JVL - ONE SOURCE OF TRUTH!"** — the same one-source-of-truth principle later codified in the JVL Constitution ([[wiki/topics/jvl-operating-principles.md]]). The initiative was scoped to span **six months, January to June 2024**, staffed by a mix of employees and consultants. Whether it was actually executed is not shown by any source in the wiki — it reads as the earliest ancestor of the meeting-transcript knowledge-base work Aaron and Val ran in Mar 2025 ([[wiki/topics/transcript-knowledge-base-tooling.md]]) and, ultimately, of this second brain. [[raw/jvl_insights_the_aidriven_knowledge_base_draft_2_2025-03-29.md]]

## Scope (from the draft)

- **Functional:** data aggregation/categorization from internal sources; robust search; real-time access; role-based access and custom views per persona; collaboration features; integration with existing systems — **Adobe, MS Business Central, the Atlassian suite (JIRA, Confluence, Xray)**, **ADP Payroll** (for onboarding/training), and reporting via **OLAP, Tableau and Excel**; mobile access for field employees. [[raw/jvl_insights_the_aidriven_knowledge_base_draft_2_2025-03-29.md]]
- **Non-functional:** performance, scalability, security/encryption, usability, reliability, regulatory compliance, interoperability, backup/recovery, multilingual support. [[raw/jvl_insights_the_aidriven_knowledge_base_draft_2_2025-03-29.md]]
- **Personas served:** product developers/designers, marketing & sales, customer support, management, IT, HR, finance/operations, R&D, compliance/legal, remote/field employees (LatAm), new hires, international partners. [[raw/jvl_insights_the_aidriven_knowledge_base_draft_2_2025-03-29.md]]

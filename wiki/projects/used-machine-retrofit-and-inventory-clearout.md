# Used-machine retrofit and inventory clear-out

A body of work to clear out JVL's stored used machines — mostly held in the Mexico warehouse and in Georgia — by rebuilding the good ones for sale into markets like Puerto Rico, Peru, and Colombia, and scrapping the rest. Led by Val, with Misha on the technical/retrofit side and Miguel and Mary handling Mexico.

## Why now (the window)

- JVL has no new production to run through to mid-February, so people would otherwise sit idle; Val sees this as the ideal time to rework the stored inventory. [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]]
- The plan is to rework the whole inventory (~335 machines cited) by around April and then switch back to normal production by mid-April. [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]]

## The fleet and where it goes

- Machine list totals around 460 lines. Roughly: ~230 Maple, 88 Altera / Altera HH, plus Altera V, Echo, and some already-stripped units. [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]]
- Altera and Altera V are expected to go quickly to Puerto Rico; Maple is the awkward group likely routed to Georgia and then a grey market (possibly Texas). Peru is a market where the coin inserts don't need changing. [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]]

## Key decisions and open issues (Jan 7)

- **Decision:** order 50 of the 30-inch "Hammerhead" monitors and about 200 CPU boxes/kits now; do not over-order Maple parts until the containers arrive and can be assessed. See [[wiki/decisions/2025-01-07-order-limited-retrofit-parts.md]]. [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]]
- Order buttons/plates for all the Alteras so they can be rebuilt fast. For Altera HH, drop the 30-inch top and convert to a standard unit (swap the top plastic and main-monitor plastic). [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]]
- 30-inch monitors are the "sensitive" sourcing problem: a former supplier (Angus/Akira) can no longer supply; they appear on Alibaba but quality is uncertain. [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]]

## The Mexico shipment (Jan 8)

- **Decision:** ship the 100 best-condition machines first, in two 53-ft trucks by ground. See [[wiki/decisions/2025-01-08-ship-100-best-machines-from-mexico.md]]. [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]]
- After the 100 ship, Miguel is to sort the warehouse: scrap on one side, Altera/Altera V/Echo on the other (for a third truck), and call external auditors to count the scrap so Val can write it off inventory. See [[wiki/decisions/2025-01-08-scrap-and-write-off-unusable-machines.md]]. [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]]
- Counts (Jan 8): ~450 machines total in Mexico, 120 installed at locations, ~330 in the warehouse; after shipping 100, about 230 remain. Packing ~$45/machine. [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]]

## Refurbishing plan (Mar 27)

On the Mar 27 Genadi call ([[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi]] + Val), the rebuild plan firmed up. [[raw/refurbishing_genadi_planning-march-27-2025.md]]

- **Order Altera / Altera V plates + buttons.** Agreed with [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan]] on the panels; order plates and the **8 buttons** (same layout for Altera, Altera V, and Flex S — same buttons, different physical arrangement). [[raw/refurbishing_genadi_planning-march-27-2025.md]]
- **Inventory counts (from [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]]'s list):** Georgia has **17 Altera + 27 Altera V** (the rest are Maples); the **Toronto warehouse** has **24 Altera + 11 Altera V**; more machines are coming from **Mexico** with **no devices** (bill acceptors, ramps and installation must be ordered) — Val states the Mexico figure inconsistently in the same call, first "~100 more … in addition" then "~200 from Mexico" (both recorded verbatim; the exact count is unclear). Val counts ~**140–160 machines excluding Maple**; he values each machine at ~**$4,000–5,000**. Quantities are small ("limited supply," not hundreds). [[raw/refurbishing_genadi_planning-march-27-2025.md]]
- **Maple is mostly scrap.** Maple is in terrible condition; only ~**25–30%** (about **35 of ~90–114**) are reusable — strip the monitor and side panels for reuse, discard the rest; the machines are all different (some with pads, some without). Maple **might go to Nebraska**. [[raw/refurbishing_genadi_planning-march-27-2025.md]]
- **Why now — keep workers busy.** From **Oct 2025 to June 2026** JVL must produce **thousands of FlexPay** plus some Flex S, so the team will be very busy — but until ~July/August there is little to do. Rather than pay people for idle time (they were sent on vacation for lack of work), Val would rather have them refurbish machines, even if it means ordering buttons/panels or putting ~$50k more into inventory and boxing/shelving the finished units. [[raw/refurbishing_genadi_planning-march-27-2025.md]]
- **Next steps:** Genadi to write a **summary for Val and "Arkady"** (Arkady to make the decision, Val as judge; "Arkady" identity unclear — see Owner Decisions), prepare a **button layout** for Maple, an **order grid** for Altera/Altera V (new layout) + buttons, give the **key mapping to the programmers**, and start restoring. [[raw/refurbishing_genadi_planning-march-27-2025.md]]

## Toronto keeps the refurbishing while production moves to Georgia (June 24–26 2025)

When JVL decided to move manufacturing/assembly from Canada to Georgia ([[wiki/decisions/2025-06-24-move-manufacturing-from-canada-to-georgia.md]]), the refurbishing work stayed in **Toronto**. Val's plan: leave the Toronto shelving as-is and load all **Maple and Altera** units onto it for **refurbishing there**, then ship the finished units to **Texas or Nebraska**; only the **Echo** and **Flex** production/stock moves to the new Louisville, Georgia warehouse.

# Machine inventory refurbishment & redeployment

Early-2025 effort to clear and rework JVL's stock of machines (held in Mexico and Georgia) and redeploy them to markets, using the production lull as the window to do it.

## Current state (as of January 2025)

- Mexico warehouse holds roughly **330–390** machines, plus ~120 on locations; Georgia holds additional stock [[raw/shipment_of_machines_from_mexico_january_8_2025.md]] [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]].
- Machine types in play: Altera, Altera HH, Altera V, Maple (~230), Echo [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]].
- Target: finish reworking and shipping the backlog by **mid-April 2025**, then resume normal production [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]].

## Two linked workstreams

- **Shipment out of Mexico** — see [[wiki/decisions/2025-01-08-ship-100-machines-mexico-to-georgia.md]].
- **Retrofit / parts procurement** — see [[wiki/decisions/2025-01-07-machine-retrofit-plan.md]].

## Redeployment intent

- Altera / Altera V → Puerto Rico.
- Maple → Georgia, then likely grey market (possibly Texas) [[raw/retrofit_january_7_2025.md]].

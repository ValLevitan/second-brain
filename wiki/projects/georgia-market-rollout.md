## Dual SAS + third dedicated printer port

- Georgia previously required **dual SAS** (two independent SAS ports on two DB9 connectors, primary + secondary, with the primary controlled by the Georgia Lottery Corporation / "GLC") — implemented last year. [[raw/Alignment_outstanding_subjects_2025-02-19.md]]
- Now that pay-kiosks (relying on the TITO model) need an on-board printer, JVL is adding a **third dedicated printer port**: a new DB9 connector (a **harness**, not a "jumper") inside the CPU box, driven by a **hardware-abstraction-layer library** (Pavel corrected Val that "firmware" is wrong — the printer's firmware sits on the printer; JVL's side is a HAL library). Two printer library types: **Gen 2 / Gen 5 (JCM) + nano-optics** share one communication language; **Epic** uses a different one. [[raw/Alignment_outstanding_subjects_2025-02-19.md]]
- Design intent (Juan/Val): the third COM port is **always the printer**, defaulted so operators don't reassign SAS ports by mistake (SAS 1 must stay primary per Georgia rules; SAS 2 can be reassigned only on a COM 1 malfunction). Pavel to remove/relabel the printer connection in the SAS-port selection UI so the wrong port can't be picked. [[raw/Alignment_outstanding_subjects_2025-02-19.md]]

## SAS events 51/52 compliance scare (Apr 14)

On the Apr 14 call ([[wiki/meetings/2025-04-14-sas-51-52-georgia-coam-interpretation.md]]; Val, [[wiki/people/ivan.md|Ivan]], [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina]] translated by [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel]], + the American Georgia commercial colleague), Eclipse's **Steve** and GLC's **Chris** flagged that JVL doesn't support **SAS event 51 (hand-pay) and 52 (hand-pay reset)** — mandatory in the SAS 1.8 spec (the transcript says both "from 2003" and "from 2023"; the older date is the spec vintage). [[raw/sas_51_52_requirement_interpretation-april-14-2025.md]]

- **Why it "broke":** Georgia COAM has **no jackpot/hand-pay in practice** — no slot attendant exists to clear a lock, and winnings pay out by **"hands"** (1 hand = 5 spins = 5 credits = **$5**; you can only cash out amount × hands, the rest stays on the machine). Eclipse's Steve used to test 51/52 by setting a **cash-out limit** (e.g. $20), playing to exceed it (5 hands = $25 > $20) so a hand-pay window popped, then turning the key. After JVL **added TITO + AFT support**, that limit can still be set but "will not produce 51 and 52 events over the SAS." Irina: JVL always submitted these hand-pay conditions as **"not applicable"** for Georgia; the concrete bug is that the machine **issues 51 but never sends 52, and keeps re-sending 51**. [[raw/sas_51_52_requirement_interpretation-april-14-2025.md]]
- **The fear:** JVL may have been technically **out of compliance since 2003** even though Eclipse approved and GLC accepted every prior Peach submission — "a delicate conversation." Plan: talk to **Steve first**, then GLC. [[raw/sas_51_52_requirement_interpretation-april-14-2025.md]]
- **Val's reinterpretation (the fix):** jackpot, hand-pay and cash-out are **parallel, not sequential, conditions** — "a hand pay is a jackpot that cannot be paid automatically." His "cup of coffee with a donut" illustration: an operator sets a jackpot/lockup limit (e.g. $1,000) purely so a **buzzer fires (51)** and someone turns the key **(52)** — nothing else changes. So JVL should **allow setting a lockup limit and auto-clear it in the lab** to emit both 51 and 52 and pass certification, even though no operator uses it in the field (no real jackpot exists federally). The team agreed this is a **re-interpretation** ("we misunderstood the requirement," which applies to **all markets**, not just Georgia) and that supporting the jackpot limit is effectively a **new requirement** to write for the COAM product. Class-3 difference recorded: Class 3 has a **$1,200 IRS lockup limit** and does emit 51/52, but on key-turn the jackpot is paid by the house/system; in Georgia the money must return to the **credit meter** for the hands calculation. [[raw/sas_51_52_requirement_interpretation-april-14-2025.md]]
- **FlexPay large-win handling (noted in passing):** if a win exceeds the **$1,000 gift-card max**, funds move to the temporary wallet → FlexPay and card issuance produces **two or more cards** (one $1,000, one for the remainder) — assuming enough hands to cash out. [[raw/sas_51_52_requirement_interpretation-april-14-2025.md]]

## Class-3-vs-COAM meter interpretation — Seminole-lab retest (May 21 2025)

A certification-testing call ([[wiki/meetings/2025-05-21-ga-coam-class3-meters-seminole-retest.md]]; Val, [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]], [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]], the QA engineer "Vlad", [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]]) that extended the SAS 51/52 thread into a broader worry: JVL may be reading Georgia's metering rules **wrong at the root**. (The transcript renders "meter" as **"mitra/MITRA"**, "Georgia COAM" as **"GCOM"/"Georgia COEM"/"Georgia QAM"**, and "Seminole" as **"seminal"** — corrected here, flagged.) The trigger was an **Eclipse** query on the **Total Jackpot Credits** meter, which JVL's Georgia product returns as **0**. [[raw/sas_intricacies_ga-coam-may-21-2025.md]]

- **The root-cause reframing (Val).** Georgia COAM "**was born from Class 3 SAS protocol**" — a "sort of gymnastics to fit Class 3 standard into Georgia COEM" done at the *legislative* level. JVL's mistake was reading the Georgia COAM regulation directly "without any regard to Class 3," when it should interpret **Class-3 first**, then how Georgia COAM fits into it. Correct meters must still **increment** as in Class-3; the hand-pay ("hands") restriction is "just a nuance," not a reason a meter reads 0. [[raw/sas_intricacies_ga-coam-may-21-2025.md]]
- **Jackpot / key-to-credit definitions.** A jackpot is "**any value that is greater than the jackpot limit that we have enabled on the machine**" — with no limit set there is no jackpot (so the meter never fires). Example: limit **$900**, a **$901** win → the machine **locks up** and is cleared by an attendant's **jackpot reset key**. Normally the money then **disappears from the machine** (handled by the system, not the credit meter); the SAS feature **"key-to-credit"** instead moves it to the **credit meter** — a feature JVL supports "**only for C3 class machines. For example, MegaHeat, FireEyes**," **not** for COAM products. Georgia has no jackpots defined today, which is why the Total Jackpot Credits meter gives 0. Adding a jackpot-credit limit "the same as on other products" is doable but "a big alteration," because Georgia's **hands** restriction complicates paying a progressive win. [[raw/sas_intricacies_ga-coam-may-21-2025.md]]
- **Irina's "two steps back."** [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]] raised — and Val had privately shared — the deeper doubt that even JVL's **Class-3 meters may be interpreted incorrectly**; the group agreed to first **redefine/verify the Class-3 meters against the protocol**, then derive the Georgia COAM behaviour. Hand-pay and K2/credit meters were clarified as **different meters** (a hand-pay meter is not the K2/credit-limit meter). [[raw/sas_intricacies_ga-coam-may-21-2025.md]]
- **The undertaking — parallel meter retest.** [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan]] (named "the best expert on Earth" for Class-3 meter interpretation) to **retest meter behaviour on a Class-3 "Mega Hit One" T27/D27 machine at the Seminole ("seminal") lab**, using the **GLI-approved MH1 Class-3 software** JVL had installed there, while **Vlad runs the same software in parallel at JVL** — same version both sides — documenting all jackpot/credit-transfer metering scenarios. [[raw/sas_intricacies_ga-coam-may-21-2025.md]]
- **Lab-subordination / compliance stance.** Val stressed JVL is "**subservient to a laboratory**": if the lab rules JVL wrong, "they can come back, check the previous approvals and revoke them. This will automatically freeze all our games. Instantaneous." He judged the earlier Eclipse pass as luck ("we have good relations, plus this Pavel from Eclipse is not very deep"). Current posture: "**Right now we are compliant. We are behaving as though we're compliant. We are doing our own internal investigations just in case.**" [[raw/sas_intricacies_ga-coam-may-21-2025.md]]
- **Scheduling.** Next call set for **Wed May 28 at 7am** to hear Vlad's findings (Vlad is unavailable from ~May 29 for about a week); an alternative Mon **June 2** was floated, subject to Seminole-lab availability. [[raw/sas_intricacies_ga-coam-may-21-2025.md]]

## Jackpot processing — Class-3 "constitution" vs Georgia-QAM "legislation" (May 28 2025)

The May-28 reconvene ([[wiki/meetings/2025-05-28-jp-jackpot-class3-georgia-coam.md]]; Val, [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]], the SAS jackpot/meter expert **[[wiki/people/vladislav-gurin.md|Vladislav Gurin]]** — "Vlad", [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]], and [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]] (owner-confirmed 2026-07-18: meter/QA interpretation → Shishko)) walked jackpot/meter behaviour and produced Val's architectural ruling. The decision-grade content is on [[wiki/decisions/2025-05-28-class3-meters-constitution-georgia-parallel-logic.md]]. [[raw/jp_treatment-may-28-2025.md]]

- **The mechanics clarified.** No jackpot limit → all wins post to **total coin-out**; a jackpot limit (typical US **$1,200**, the IRS value) makes wins ≥ the limit a **hand-pay**. A separate **key-to-credit limit** must be **less than** the jackpot limit and needs a value; a win above the jackpot limit goes **entirely to hand-pay** regardless of key-to-credit. Whether JVL's Seminole test posted to the correct meters is still to be confirmed against the iView/Bally logs (JVL doesn't write meters to its trace logs — noted as a new requirement). [[raw/jp_treatment-may-28-2025.md]]
- **The ruling.** JVL "made Georgia QAM fit" by altering Class-3 meter behaviour — wrong, and a latent compliance risk (if an operator lawfully set a jackpot limit, JVL "would immediately fall out of compliance"). ## Jackpot / key-to-meter scope gaps found — the reconvene (June 10 2025)

The ~13-days-later reconvene of the May-28 jackpot call ([[wiki/meetings/2025-06-10-jackpot-meter-sas-scope-gaps.md]]; Val, [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]], the SAS/meter expert **[[wiki/people/vladislav-gurin.md|Vlad]]**, [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]] (owner-confirmed 2026-07-18: meter/QA → Shishko), [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel/Pasha]], and an implementer "Zhenya"). Trigger: during JVL's **PH1 submission**, **Eclipse** noted that JVL's **jackpot limit** — unlike other machines — **can't be changed for lab testing** (JVL confirmed the jackpot limit "does not function the way it functions in Class 3" for Georgia COAM software). The call drilled the Class-3 **key-to-meter / jackpot-limit** behaviour and uncovered concrete scope gaps. [[raw/meter_sas_function-june-10-2025.md]]

- **The educational baseline (Juan, 30 yrs gaming ops).** A **jackpot limit** (typical US **$1,200**, an **IRS/federal tax mandate** on wins ≥$1,200) triggers a **hand-pay lockup**. Three ways it resolves: (1) **standard hand-pay** — attendant turns the reset key, the value is wiped from the machine and paid by the casino system (no voucher in system-validation; player waits); (2) **allow-hand-pay-receipt** — the key prints a jackpot ticket and instantly returns the machine to play (Seminole practice); (3) **key-to-credit / key-to-meter** — the win is transferred to the **credit meter** (via a physical key turn, or, on advanced systems like **Bally SDS**, a player self-transfer on the tracking display). In **Georgia it can only be key-to-meter** (money must return to the credit meter for the hands calculation). [[raw/meter_sas_function-june-10-2025.md]]
- **Scope gap #1 — key-to-meter can't be set higher than the jackpot limit.** Juan established that **key-to-credit is a local casino operations choice (risk/AML), separate from the federal tax jackpot limit**, and can be set **higher** than the jackpot limit (Hard Rock uses ~$1,200 main-floor + $10,000–$50,000 high-limit). JVL had **implemented the key-to-meter limit as forced-lower-than the jackpot limit** — Irina defended it as correct, Val/Juan ruled it a **scope gap**: the two must be **independent values**. [[raw/meter_sas_function-june-10-2025.md]]
- **Scope gap #2 — no partial transfer against a key-to-credit limit.** The key-to-credit limit is a **threshold** (born from AML; kiosk single-transactions capped ~$3,000): a win above it should transfer **only up to the limit** to the credit meter, leaving the remainder as hand-pay. JVL doesn't do this **partial transfer** today. [[raw/meter_sas_function-june-10-2025.md]]
- **Scope gap #3 — no lab test for the mechanical key-to-meter case, and a possible double-count.** JVL has **no test case** where a win above the jackpot limit is sent to the credit meter by a mechanical key turn, and the team is unsure whether that path **double-posts** (jackpot meter + hand-pay meter + credit meter) — which would make machines look like they **overpaid / are losing**. Because **Seminole machines have used key-to-credit for ~2 years**, this "could be affecting our numbers." [[raw/meter_sas_function-june-10-2025.md]]
- **Testing-rig limitation (Vlad).** JVL can only exercise this through a **~20-year-old IGT "SAS Tool" emulator** (a SAS *tool*, not a full SAS *host*), with truncated functionality and no player-tracking screen — so many host-side flows can't be reproduced. Val's counter: the mechanical key-to-meter path is a **mechanical process needing no SAS command**, so JVL should be able to bench-test it directly. [[raw/meter_sas_function-june-10-2025.md]]
- **Action.** Team to write up all the options, build a **flow diagram**, verify the behaviour and implement (Zhenya to implement the independent/higher key-to-meter + partial transfer). **Class 3 must be made correct first** (Georgia layered on per the [[wiki/decisions/2025-05-28-class3-meters-constitution-georgia-parallel-logic.md|constitution/legislation ruling]]). Next meeting **Friday 10:30**. [[raw/meter_sas_function-june-10-2025.md]]

## Jackpot / key-to-meter flowchart review (June 12 2025)

The next-day continuation of the June-10 scope-gap call ([[wiki/meetings/2025-06-12-jackpot-meter-flowchart-review.md]]; Val, [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan]], SAS/meter expert [[wiki/people/vladislav-gurin.md|Vlad]], [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]] (owner-confirmed 2026-07-18: meter/QA → Shishko)). Vlad walked the **future-state flowchart** of Class-3 jackpot / key-to-meter behaviour that he built as the June-10 undertaking. (Nebraska was asked to delay their call so this could run on.) [[raw/jp_meters-june-12-2025.md]]

- **The variants walked.** (1) No key-to-meter limit, jackpot limit $1,200 → any win above the limit starts the **jackpot hand-paid dialogue**; turn the key, all money goes to hand-pay. (2) key-to-meter limit **equal to** the jackpot limit — "how it works today," but Juan called it **unrealistic** (an operator would never set key-to-credit exactly equal to the jackpot limit). (3) jackpot limit $1,200, **key-to-meter $25,000**: a $5,000 win shows the **same existing jackpot/hand-pay dialogue** ("congratulations… please call attendant" — Val/Juan ruled **do not build a new dialogue**, reuse the one players/staff already know), then on the audit-key turn the $5,000 transfers to the **credit meter / balance** ("key-to-meter" / "win to balance"); there is **no individual meter** tracking it. (4) hard case — a **$30,000** win above both limits: **$25,000 (the key-to-meter limit) transfers to the credit meter** on the first key turn, the remaining **$5,000 stays hand-pay** on a second key turn; the **dialogue shows twice** (both need casino intervention). [[raw/jp_meters-june-12-2025.md]]
- **Ticket vs lockup / tax.** Under **secure-enhanced validation** the second key turn **prints a jackpot ticket** and releases the machine; under **system-validation only** there is no ticket, so the machine stays **locked until the attendant returns with cash**. Tax is on the **original $30,000** (the $1,200 is exempt), so **$28,800 taxable at 25%** (28% "foreign tax" for non-US citizens). [[raw/jp_meters-june-12-2025.md]]
- **The win-calculation double-posting worry (Juan).** In the "win to balance" flow the transaction posts to **both the coin-out meter and jackpot credits**, so Juan wants the **net-win formula** confirmed (coin-in − coin-out − jackpots = net win) to be sure JVL isn't "double dipping." The intended handling: the $25,000 posts to **both coin-in and coin-out (zeroing out)** because it's addressed via the jackpot meter, while the full **$30,000** posts to the **jackpot meter**. Because there has been "**only one jackpot in the last 18 months**," any error is invisible to operators — but JVL may have **reported a machine as losing more than it did**. Juan to send a sheet illustrating the formula. [[raw/jp_meters-june-12-2025.md]]
- **Total-jackpot-credit meter gap.** Today the **Total Jackpot Credits** meter reflects only the **attendant-paid** portion (what's requested over SAS), not the **key-to-meter balance** amounts — so a $30,000 win shows less than $30,000. Val wants the total-jackpot meter to reflect the **full** jackpot value. [[raw/jp_meters-june-12-2025.md]]
- **The blocking undertaking — a block diagram of every meter.** The team stalled ("we're all staring at each other… a vacuum") over which meter increments in the key-to-meter case — there is no agreed **credit-in meter** name. Val made it an undertaking: **Vlad to produce a block diagram of every single meter JVL has (maximum, proper names, whether used or not)** — "we cannot move forward without it" — for a **Tuesday 11 am** meeting (~June 17; Vlad to send invites). [[raw/jp_meters-june-12-2025.md]]

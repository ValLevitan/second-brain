# BDC (Business Development Bank of Canada)

BDC is the Canadian government-owned development bank JVL has borrowed from before. It sits at the head of the export-financing chain JVL has used: Val described it (≈May 2024) as "a government company, called BDC … So they first pre-approve you, then they recommend you to **EDC**, then **EDC brings Elevate**." See [[wiki/organizations/edc.md]] and [[wiki/organizations/elevate.md]]. This page is the org itself; the May-20 2025 financing-intro meeting is stubbed at [[wiki/meetings/2025-05-20-bdc-flexport-ga-coam-financing.md]].

## May 20 2025 — financing-intro call for the Georgia COAM financial product

Val and a JVL colleague met [[wiki/people/baba-fakohora.md|Baba Fakohora]], BDC's senior account manager (tech group) and JVL's new relationship manager, to explore financing JVL's Georgia-market rollout of its payment hardware (the FlexPort/GA-COAM financial product — see [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]]). [[raw/bdc_intro_to_flex-port_ga-coam-may-20-2025.md]]

- **JVL's ask — a different model than Elevate/EDC.** Under the existing [[wiki/organizations/elevate.md|Elevate]] chain the lender approves *JVL's customer* (the operator) for a five-year term; that works for a centralized distributor but is "impractical" across Georgia's many small, disaggregated Master License Holders in the half-year rollout window. So JVL wants BDC to instead **finance JVL itself** ("maybe we can get approved and get terms") so JVL as manufacturer can extend terms to its own customers. [[raw/bdc_intro_to_flex-port_ga-coam-may-20-2025.md]]
- **What BDC offers / does not.** Baba was explicit: **BDC does not offer revolvers or lines of credit** — it provides **growth capital** (a term facility given up front). Repayment can be flexible: interest-only for the term, or a **balloon payment tied to revenue milestones**, a "quasi-equity-based" structure. The catch: **once repaid, the funds are not re-accessible** — a fresh application is required for more capital. [[raw/bdc_intro_to_flex-port_ga-coam-may-20-2025.md]]
- **Growth and Transition Capital (GTC) team.** BDC's GTC team writes larger checks than Baba's group — **over $1M** (the last deal Baba cited was ~**$5M**) — and can **finance intangibles**, suited to an upfront growth project where the capital must be patient and flexible. Baba judged "$6 to $9 million to generate $45 million in revenue is a good ROI." [[raw/bdc_intro_to_flex-port_ga-coam-may-20-2025.md]]
- **Capital ask (business economics).** Val sized JVL's need at ~**$2,000 per location** × 6,000 Georgia locations = **~$12M** to capture 100% of the market; at a realistic ~**50% share ≈ $6M**, generating ~**$45M** of project revenue over ~three years (≈$15M/yr on top of JVL's current ~$8M existing-equipment revenue). Funds needed by **Jan 1 2026**. See [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]] for the market picture. [[raw/bdc_intro_to_flex-port_ga-coam-may-20-2025.md]]
- **Val's improvised structure idea:** borrow from BDC and use that sum as **collateral for JVL's suppliers**, having suppliers advance a proportional line of credit against it — because JVL's bill-of-material-to-sales-price ratio is large, so cash comes in faster than the three-year customer terms go out. Baba noted BDC cannot guarantee a revolver, but agreed the secured capital could back supplier credit and extend runway. [[raw/bdc_intro_to_flex-port_ga-coam-may-20-2025.md]]
- **Next steps:** Baba to email JVL a detailed information request (extensive cash-flow projections + qualitative business/project detail) and open a **deal virtual room** for uploads; then a GTC-team conversation. JVL is **not raising equity** — it "always operate[s] with our own capital." [[raw/bdc_intro_to_flex-port_ga-coam-may-20-2025.md]]

# Incom (payment processor)

Incom is the third-party **payment processor** JVL is integrating with to activate and read the Georgia **COAM prize-redemption cards** through JVL's pay-kiosk. It supplies the card-activation/balance-check API and the test/production credentials; JVL's FlexPay/FlexPort kiosk is the client. Spelling appears as "Incom" in the meeting body (and "income" for the card VIN) and "incomm" in the source filename; kept both plus "InComm" as aliases pending confirmation (see Owner Decisions). [[raw/incomm_commissioning_plan_february-27-2025.md]]

Earlier appearances: on the Feb 11 and Feb 18 embedded standups Incom was named as the payment processor supplying the **test server**, with a **production contract awaiting Val's signature** and card-provider fields to fill in the FlexPay activation sequence. See [[wiki/topics/flexpay-mobile-app-and-service-advocates.md]] and [[wiki/topics/jvl-machine-lineup.md]]. [[raw/Embedded_Group_alignment_and_update_2025-02-11.md]]

### Test vs production environments
- JVL's current credential is a **development credential** that works **only in the test environment**; testing uses a **"flight" test card**. [[raw/incomm_commissioning_plan_february-27-2025.md]]
- Path to production: JVL submits Incom's **merchant setup form** → Billy + contract admin (said to take "a couple of weeks") → Incom assigns a **unique merchant ID for JVL** (transcribed "JBL") → Incom issues a production **user ID and secret** that work only in production, using a different endpoint → JVL configures its machines with the production credential and activates a **production Georgia Core card** (not the flight test card). [[raw/incomm_commissioning_plan_february-27-2025.md]]
- Incom wants to work with JVL **in the shop** on the first machine to confirm it is configured with the production credential and can activate a production card; after that, per-install testing can be streamlined, but the technician should still send a **test transaction from the location** so Incom can verify the machine is configured correctly. [[raw/incomm_commissioning_plan_february-27-2025.md]]
- Why per-location verification matters: Incom described a kiosk operator who shipped machines misconfigured with the wrong location's store ID, so all activations came in under the wrong location and the billing was wrong — Incom only knows the store ID it is told. [[raw/incomm_commissioning_plan_february-27-2025.md]]

### Balance-check API
- Balance check is currently **out of scope** for JVL's kiosk because it can't swipe cards yet, but Incom and JVL agreed to **test and certify the balance-check API now** by feeding a **PAN number** (Incom supplies test PANs) rather than a physical swipe; the test card already carries PAN data on track one plus the Incom VIN. A number "549345" was referenced for the test. [[raw/incomm_commissioning_plan_february-27-2025.md]]
- A standard **MAG card reader** (not EMV) is sufficient for the card. Test cards can be reset with a **reversal** or a **deactivation** transaction so the same test card is reusable. [[raw/incomm_commissioning_plan_february-27-2025.md]]

### The COAM / debit-card legal state (per Incom)
- The industry read Incom gave: using a **credit card** is prohibited, using a **debit card** is unknown, and using a **debit card issued as a prize redemption** is allowed — "the state of the market today." At least two operators are already doing this; one restricts to **COAM cards** only, the other may accept any debit card. Whether a regulator (the "GLC" — Georgia Lottery Corporation) would treat a Walgreens open-loop gift card the same is unclear. [[raw/incomm_commissioning_plan_february-27-2025.md]]

## Mar 19 2025 — card dispensing/activation user-cases walkthrough (Alan)

Alan took the JVL team step-by-step through issuing a prepaid card from the kiosk ([[wiki/meetings/2025-03-19-incomm-flexpay-card-user-cases.md]]; a **Susanne** on the Incom side handed him the sample card): [[raw/incomm_flex-pay_user_cases.md]]

- **Card data:** the test cards carry identical data on the **magnetic stripe** and a **30-digit activation barcode** — first ~11 digits are the product **UPC**, the last 19 digits the **VAN (Virtual Account Number)** (track-one number beginning "605812"/"605823" — both cited on the call), printed under the barcode. Incom recommends a **barcode reader** so the card can be read **while still in the dispenser** (less mechanism, no need to dispense before reading); if magstripe-only, the UPC must be configured on the kiosk since it isn't on the stripe. The kiosk should support **UPC configuration per product** (Pavel asked to write the requirement; Alan framed it as supporting both MagStripe and barcode). [[raw/incomm_flex-pay_user_cases.md]]
- **Two-step activation:** first a **pre-authorization** (non-financial — verifies connectivity, kiosk setup, card number and loadable amount), then — after redeeming the winning ticket — an **Activate** call with near-identical fields but a new transaction ID. The card must **never be handed to the consumer before activation completes**; failed cards go to an internal **escrow bin/tray** for troubleshooting, never to the player. [[raw/incomm_flex-pay_user_cases.md]]
- **IDs and reconciliation:** requests carry access ID (test credential "ICT test," lowercase i), **merchant ID** (assigned by Incom), **store ID** (assigned by the Incom merchant so their activation reports reconcile — not invented by JVL), and **terminal ID** (JVL's own kiosk identifier); a **machine setup form** per deployment tells Incom/the merchant who and where (MLH/LLH names, address). All fields must be kiosk-configurable. [[raw/incomm_flex-pay_user_cases.md]]
- **Fees and limits:** consumer pays a **$2 fee** (e.g. redeem $22 to load $20 — only the load amount is sent to Incom); the fee is revenue-shared down the chain location→merchant→Incom (Alan's split figures were explicitly "a sample value"). Card max is **$1,000**; the minimum he needed to re-check with Adam ("either $10, $20 or $1"). [[raw/incomm_flex-pay_user_cases.md]]
- **Downstream use:** at merchant POS only the **magstripe/track-two** matters — "MasterCard does not know anything about" the VAN or barcode, which exist solely for activation between JVL and Incom; the mobile number/email capture is **optional**, only for sending Ts&Cs ("no validation code required to use the card"). A returning player's card **cannot be deducted** by the kiosk — "there is nothing you can do with that card" (whether the card could fund more play is a question Alan would take to John). Incom doesn't care how kiosks reach JVL's gateway — only the gateway talks to Incom. [[raw/incomm_flex-pay_user_cases.md]]
- The product's UPC context: the card discussed is the "**COEM card**" (as transcribed — consistent with the Georgia COAM redemption card this integration serves). [[raw/incomm_flex-pay_user_cases.md]]

## COAM gift-card solution meeting — "JVL Payment Options" (John Houseal)

The market/solution meeting Val committed to on the Apr 2 SMIB call — owner Val, "Timeline: Today, tomorrow, or Monday," purpose "Discuss market dynamics and understand operator strategies" [[raw/SMIB_FlexPay_Action_Items_April_2_2025.md]]. The substance was first captured from a derived notes document (see [[wiki/meetings/2025-04-03-incomm-coam-gift-card-solutions.md]]); the **actual Teams recording** ("JVL Payment Options," ~36 min, **John Houseal** / **Allen Lee** InComm-side, with Val and Juan) is a fuller, speaker-labelled transcript of the same meeting and confirms every point below plus the detail in the next subsection. **Date discrepancy (see Open contradictions):** the recording header dates the meeting **April 3, 2025, 5:42 PM**, while the derived notes are headed **April 7** — recorded, not resolved. **John Houseal** (the Incom lead) and Val worked the merchant problem that **gift cards can't be used to play at COAM machines**, so merchants fear losing in-store spend (feedback "from multiple operators"; use case: a $100 win loaded onto a MasterCard can't fund further COAM play). [[raw/2025-04-07-COAM_gift_card_solution_incomm_notes.md]] [[raw/incomm_transcript_gacoam_payment_options-april-4-2025.md]]

- **Three solution options (John):** (1) player asks the **clerk to swipe the card for cash** (no system changes); (2) contract a **merchant acquirer** to put card readers on kiosks/machines — rejected as unattractive: **$50–$200/month maintenance + 3% transaction fee** (a $100 transfer nets the merchant $97); (3) **barcode on the card** processed **directly with Incom** — no merchant acquirer, no 3% fee, avoids **PCI compliance** ("the barcode is not considered payment card information"), only a modest **~$20–25/month** accounting/settlement fee. [[raw/2025-04-07-COAM_gift_card_solution_incomm_notes.md]]
- **Barcode security:** protected by a **PIN set on first use** (first PIN entered becomes the card's PIN); changeable anytime via **IVR or the website**. [[raw/2025-04-07-COAM_gift_card_solution_incomm_notes.md]]
- **Role model:** location houses the machines; machine owner manages them; **JVL supplies the machines/kiosks**; **Incom is the program manager and technology provider** (gift-card product + APIs, money movement, compliance) — Incom **does not build kiosks**; operators design their own kiosk configurations (card readers, digital wallets, etc.). [[raw/2025-04-07-COAM_gift_card_solution_incomm_notes.md]]
- **Incom's expansion plans:** targeting **Texas, Florida, Pennsylvania, Nebraska and Iowa** "to improve the player experience and reduce dependence on cash," with per-market product adaptations — ATM-usable cards in Pennsylvania, large-win payouts in Nebraska — plus a **print-on-receipt** product for merchandise markets (player picks among gift-card providers). [[raw/2025-04-07-COAM_gift_card_solution_incomm_notes.md]]
- **Follow-up:** Juan + Val to "regroup and think of a proper business model" for the discussed solution. The card-scanning prospect surfaced the same day in the FlexPort scanner ruling — see [[wiki/decisions/2025-04-07-flexsmib-modern-flexport-legacy.md]]. [[raw/2025-04-07-COAM_gift_card_solution_incomm_notes.md]]

### Detail the recording adds

- **How the money actually moves:** funds sit on a gift card held in an account at **Sutton Bank** that InComm manages. When a player scans/authorises a purchase, InComm confirms the balance and the merchant gives the player play-credit *immediately*; InComm then **wires the merchant whole (~a day later)** — the merchant extends credit on the belief InComm will pay, because InComm holds the card balance. InComm must now process not just gift-card activations but **redemptions/spend**, routing money back to the **7,000 merchants** in "Co-In." [[raw/incomm_transcript_gacoam_payment_options-april-4-2025.md]]
- **PIN mechanics:** the barcode balance-check can't be done today (Jared wants PIN-based for security, since the barcode is exposed and "roommates can take photos of it"); PIN is **set on first use** (whatever four digits are first entered), one PIN per barcode, changeable via IVR or website. **Cards are not reloadable** — a player gets a **new card each cash-out**; "those are the rules until 2026." Making cards rechargeable would require **KYC** on the player, "which I don't think anybody wants to do." [[raw/incomm_transcript_gacoam_payment_options-april-4-2025.md]]
- **PCI:** option 2 (mag-stripe reader) is in PCI scope because it reads real card data (16-digit number, expiry, CVV) sent to MasterCard; option 3 (barcode) is **outside PCI scope** because the barcode "means nothing to MasterCard… only to InComm" — InComm makes it payment information only inside its own wall, insulating the industry. [[raw/incomm_transcript_gacoam_payment_options-april-4-2025.md]]
- **The four participants (Val's framing):** the **location** (houses machines), the **master** (owns/routes the machines), **JVL** (supplies machines/kiosks), and **InComm** = "the **program manager** for the gift-card product and the **technology provider** for the APIs" and money movement (all regulated in the US) — InComm **does not build equipment** ("that will not happen"). Val questioned the point of a kiosk at all; John: most people want to limit clerk interaction and keep the gaming environment self-sufficient. [[raw/incomm_transcript_gacoam_payment_options-april-4-2025.md]]
- **Operators design their own flow** — John's examples: **Excel** (owns **Bulldog Entertainment** in Georgia) puts a kiosk in every location, using TITO + a digital wallet; **Intermax at Kroger** wouldn't pay **~$15,000** per kiosk so they hung cards and put optical readers on the machines (ran into problems, now looking at kiosks); **United** (~1,300 locations) is deploying handheld devices to associates rather than $10–15k kiosks. [[raw/incomm_transcript_gacoam_payment_options-april-4-2025.md]]
- **Juan's opening framing (from the pre-recording audio):** kiosk-product rollout is slow — "maybe three to 400 with thousands to go" — and someone at the GAMOA meeting asked "how does anyone expect us to do this in one year's time"; masters/locations are "savvy… used to skating around the letter of the law," hence the gift cards; Juan's worry is that an automated kiosk lets a player walk away, whereas a window redemption keeps the location owner able to upsell. [[raw/incomm_meeting—april-3-2025-1.md]]
- **Fannin County detail:** **Pace-O-Matic** sued the sheriff's office in **Fannin County, Texas** and a judge ruled its machines were **skill machines, not gambling machines** — "the first time a court in the state of Texas has ever issued an opinion on these devices" — driving InComm's Texas interest. [[raw/incomm_transcript_gacoam_payment_options-april-4-2025.md]]

## June 23 2025 — Nebraska/Georgia alignment; SkillPay, PrizePay, the card-back-into-machine flow

A weekly alignment call ([[wiki/meetings/2025-06-23-incomm-jvl-nebraska-georgia-alignment.md]]; [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] + [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel]] with InComm's **John** (Houseal), **Alan** (= Allen Lee) and **Adam**) covering the differences between Nebraska and Georgia and the still-unfinalized products. [[raw/incomm_jvl_alignment-june-23-2025.md]]

- **Product line (as described on this call).** In **Georgia** today there is one product — the **Georgia COAM PrizePay card** (orange or black wrap; **$2 activation fee**, single-load, up to **$1,000**, **no cash access** — Georgia bars a product with direct cash access from COAM winnings). The new product is **SkillPay**: **$3 activation fee**, **has cash access**, and a **unique kiosk-only activation** (the multi-step process below). InComm chose the name "SkillPay" for Georgia because the regulator lacks "the mental bandwidth to separate the two products" from PrizePay. [[raw/incomm_jvl_alignment-june-23-2025.md]]
- **SkillPay's two-step legal process.** To give the card cash access, InComm first redeems the COAM winnings to a **compliant product**, then uses that product to **purchase** the SkillPay card — so the direct lottery payout is the *third* step, not the initial load. The player experiences **one step**; legally it is two. This is why activation must be done **by the kiosk/system**, not by "an $11-an-hour employee." It took InComm "~12 months and a quarter-million dollars in legal fees" to get regulator sign-off (they signed an off-network agreement with MasterCard; final signature still pending), and the **secondary purchase API** JVL would call for the in-between step is **not yet finalized** ("we haven't finalized the product… we're still working that out"). [[raw/incomm_jvl_alignment-june-23-2025.md]]
- **Nebraska = a cash state → the original PrizePay.** For Nebraska (different from Georgia) InComm offers the **original PrizePay product**, used in **Pennsylvania** route gaming (e.g. Circle K), loadable up to **$2,500**. Two versions: a **no-activation-fee** version (free) and one with a **$3 activation fee** (cash access). Fee model: the card is free, purchases are free, grocery **cash-back** is free (buy a $2 Coke, pull $200 free) — only an **ATM withdrawal costs $2.50**, which is how InComm makes money ("we're gambling on the fact that you're going to use the ATM"). Nebraska's largest operator (JVL's long relationship) + one other control ~**70%** of the market and today pay out via **ATMs**; InComm supports a few Nebraska locations (M3T). A win too large for an on-site ATM (jackpots $2,000–$5,000) is a stated pain point the card solves. An optional companion app can send funds to a **bank account** (like Venmo: instant 1% or free ACH in 2–3 days). [[raw/incomm_jvl_alignment-june-23-2025.md]]
- **Not reloadable.** These cards are **not reloadable**; John's read is that "nobody actually wants a reloadable card" (operators/legislators talk about it, but no player has asked) — players care most about **privacy**. [[raw/incomm_jvl_alignment-june-23-2025.md]]

## Aug 31 2026 — the broker's view: InComm's daily file, the two shut-off thresholds, and a two-day cash cycle

The [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-31-arc-gaming-transactional-demo-class-a.md|Aug-31 ARC Gaming demo]] is the corpus's clearest account of what it is like to sit **between** InComm and the locations. [[wiki/organizations/arc-gaming.md|ARC Gaming]] is its own card broker, and its COO [[wiki/people/georgios-zazanis.md|George Zazanis]] walked [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] through the whole loop. Full operational detail on [[wiki/organizations/arc-gaming.md]]; what belongs here is the InComm-side mechanics. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

- **InComm sends the broker a daily transaction file.** "[InComm] sends a big file with all the Excel transactions. We know which location and how much they are liable for. Let's say location A, two hundred dollars." Val believed the file lags — "I think [InComm] sends the file two days later" — and Zazanis disagreed: "Don't quote me. I think it's a daily file. **It's a hundred percent daily file.**" Not settled in the source; both readings recorded. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **There are two independent shut-off thresholds, and InComm owns one of them.** "There is a threshold generated by [InComm]. So if [InComm] is back to back, **they will shut down the location.** And then it's the threshold that we set up as a broker." The broker's own cap: "It doesn't go more than 10k… **Our exposure is 10k per day.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **The broker, not the location, is on the hook to InComm.** Val stated the chain and got agreement: "the location is exposed to the card processor, **but the card processor is me**… you make a dollar for every card. Let's say a card is five hundred dollars… **you make a dollar. It's nothing, but you're on the hook for five hundred dollars.**" The **$1-per-card** broker margin is Val's figure, stated as an example rather than quoted from an ARC rate card. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **Recovery is manual and two days behind.** ARC's accounting reads the previous day's file, sets up an ACH swipe of the location's bank account for the next day, then re-checks the bank and reconciles by hand — "weekends… we do on Monday." Val's summary, agreed to: "So now it's two days… you get the money into your bank account. You need to go back to your bank account, check if you got the money, and reconfirm it with your accounting system." Asked how InComm gets paid its side today: "**Manually, daily. With operators daily.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **JVL's pitch is to automate the whole loop** — reconcile the InComm report automatically, swipe by ACH before the second day, post into the accounting system with "no human intervention," move the money same-day, and disable machines automatically when an account is short. This is the commercial case for the cloud-processing service; architecture on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **And the regulator is now checking the other end of the same pipe.** ARC reports the GLC writing to locations demanding data, and comparing machine winnings against cards actually issued — "some of them they're zero." Same regulatory turn as the Aug-27 Georgia Lottery notice above, from a second operator. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

## Sept 2 2026 — the sealed-packaging rule forces barcode activation on Flex-IQ

Reported by [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] on the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-02-flex-iq-pre-pilot-updates-after-the-show.md|Sept-2 Flex-IQ pre-pilot call]] (Russian; no InComm staff present) as JVL's own restatement of InComm's requirement — not a fresh InComm statement. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_IQ_Barcode_Receipt_Ewallet_Strategy-September-2-2026.md]]

- **The rule, as Andrei stated it:** cards for non-kiosk devices ship sealed and "никто, Кроме финального получателя игрока, не имеет права распаковывать её. Соответственно наружу в этой карточке торчит только баркот" — nobody but the final recipient, the player, may unwrap the pack, so only the barcode is exposed. Activation must therefore run off the barcode. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_IQ_Barcode_Receipt_Ewallet_Strategy-September-2-2026.md]]
- **Kiosks are unaffected** — kiosk cards load unwrapped and keep today's activation flow: "в киоск карточке загружаются уже распакованные и то, как они у нас сейчас активируются, это пускай так и остаётся." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_IQ_Barcode_Receipt_Ewallet_Strategy-September-2-2026.md]]
- **The card and its envelope carry the same barcode** — Andrei: "на карточке физически находится баркот ровно такой же, который находится на конверте" — which is what lets JVL still reconcile a record to a card after activation without the 16-digit number. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_IQ_Barcode_Receipt_Ewallet_Strategy-September-2-2026.md]]
- **Why it appeared now:** "изменились требования у инкома, они теперь… Поставляют их в вот в таком виде, и если это не киоск, он должен работать с такими карточками, упакованными." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_IQ_Barcode_Receipt_Ewallet_Strategy-September-2-2026.md]]
- **On a failed card, the player calls InComm and the location is not involved** — Andrei, from experience: "И локации до этого никакого дела. Единственное, что я могу сделать, это звонить напрямую инкому и жаловаться, что [не] провели транзакцию." Val added that the card is InComm's issue and "мы вообще к этому ничего общего не имеем." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_IQ_Barcode_Receipt_Ewallet_Strategy-September-2-2026.md]]
- **Multiple card providers restated as a JVL requirement:** "сейчас у нас только инк[омм], но нам надо уметь поддерживать всех, кто есть на рынке" (Andrei), endorsed by Val. See [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-27-flex-iq-multiple-clearinghouses-selectable-per-device.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_IQ_Barcode_Receipt_Ewallet_Strategy-September-2-2026.md]]
- **Andrei will re-verify barcode activation with InComm** by activating a couple more cards before the pilot: "Мы проверим это ещё раз с инкомом, сделаем ещё активацию карточки парочки." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_IQ_Barcode_Receipt_Ewallet_Strategy-September-2-2026.md]]

## Sept 2 2026 (lunch) — the issuing chain explained, the ATM network named, and the PAX question answered with a bounty

Later the same day, a **two-hour working lunch** near Lawrenceville ([[wiki/meetings/2026-09-02-incomm-machine-payments-and-recycler-standoff.md]]) put three InComm people across the table from [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] and [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]]. **None of the three is named in either source**, so nothing here is attributed to a person. The two rulings out of it are [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-card-to-machine-launches-with-the-location-as-merchant-of-record.md]] and [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-hold-the-recycler-back-but-sign-no-undertaking.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].

### The issuing chain, stated end to end for the first time

- **Sutton Bank issues; Mastercard is the network; Pulse is the mandated backup.** "Six years ago, when we started building out the original product, we **issued through Sutton Bank out of Ohio**… **Mastercard was the network that we landed on** six years ago, and we've stuck with it so far." The second rail is not a choice: "the secondary networks on there **by the regulators** are **Pulse**, the secondary backup in debit network" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Every product change needs a triple approval, which is why everything takes so long.** "There's a **symbiotic relationship between Mastercard and Sutton**. So everything Sutton has to do, Mastercard has to ultimately approve because it's on their network. So **how we classify the funds moving onto the card has to be approved by Sutton, which also has to be approved by Mastercard. So it's like a tripled approval** up chain from us, **which is why it takes so long to build some of these products**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].
- **They had to teach Mastercard what the Georgia market is.** "we have to educate not only our issuing bank, but we also have to **sit down with Mastercard and educate them**. Because the COAM market, **as large as it is in Georgia**, [is] relatively unknown to almost anyone who doesn't work directly with [it]" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Black card vs blue card: a funding-structure difference the consumer never sees.** "the only real difference from a consumer perspective is… [from a] **regulatory standpoint, the funding structure is different on the back end**, and that's something the consumer will never know or see. Kind of the invisible hand." Same network either way — "It's the **same network rails** for redemption." This is the product pair settled on Aug 26 above (black = Prize Pay, blue = Skill Pay) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].

### The ATM network behind the card

- **MoneyPass, chosen for its convenience-store footprint.** "If you use it at a **MoneyPass** ATM, it's only the **two dollars and fifty cents** transaction fee, and they could pull out **up to six hundred dollars** at a time… we've contracted with MoneyPass and said, when people use this card on your ATM network, **we don't want them to have to pay an ATM surcharge**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Off-network, InComm controls nothing.** "If you take it to another ATM that's not on this network… we have the two dollars fifty cent fee, which helps cover our transaction costs… **but we can't guarantee that there's not a surcharge on top**. They may have a **six dollar service fee** on top of the two dollar fee… and on top of that, they may have a two hundred dollar [limit]" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Why MoneyPass and not AllPoint.** "**MoneyPass and AllPoint I think are the top two. AllPoint you see in C[V]S and Target**… You don't typically see them in convenience locations… **MoneyPass is mainly — that's why we went with MoneyPass — is because they have a very large convenience footprint.**" The choice dates to the original PrizePay launch: "This is something we did six years ago when we launched the original **PrizePay** card for true lottery **outside of the state of Georgia**. MoneyPass had the largest footprint for Seven Eleven and a lot of other large convenience [chains]" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].
- **InComm does not operate ATMs and does not regulate them.** "**We're not in the ATM space. We don't operate ATMs. We engage ATMs as business partners**… when I say we're not in the ATM business, I mean **we don't engage ATM regulation and certification**. We can advise and say you have to be a **certified ATM operator**." This is consistent with the [[wiki/meetings/2026-07-09-bill-recycler-atm-registration.md|Jul-9 2026]] answer that InComm has no ATM registrations to sponsor JVL with [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].
- **And InComm knows the recycling idea is not unique.** "I know in the ATM space there's some solutions [a vendor is] working on to **recycle cash in a very similar way**. So I know the problem you're talking about. I get it." *(The vendor's name is rendered "EOSon" and is not identifiable from this source; not asserted.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]]

### Two things JVL's ports can do that InComm had not considered

- **A port can emulate a validator where there is only one SAS port.** "our ports can replace, can **mimic a validator**… We remove that plug. We put our port. We plug the validator into our port… **So we emulate a validator**… pretend that we are counting pulses." Where there are two: "**majority of the market today, 90% of the market has two SAS ports**" — the port then sits on SAS 2 [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].
- **On SAS 2 the port behaves like a casino player-tracking system — including promo credits.** "our device behaves like a **player tracking system in a class three casino**, which has enormous amount of capabilities. As one of the capabilities… **we can easily on SAS two port issue promo credit**. So we can send the promo credit to your phone number and say, go in this location between 3 p.m. and 4 p.m. And here's a validation QR code. Scan it at our port, and here's $20." Val raised the regulatory edge himself: "**Lottery is loud and clear that we're not allowed to give any incentives. This [is] not an incentive. This [is] a free play, and it registers on a promo credit meter, which does not go into a counter**" — prefaced with "maybe this is gonna freak out lottery corporation again" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].

### Why locations do not upgrade away from MudaGoose, in Val's account

Asked why so many keep it: "**it belongs to a location, not really to an operator**. Kind of system agnostic. If they change machines, another operator comes in. **Location's used to it. There's no learning curve. Plug and play.** But if you cannibalize this system… tomorrow you wake up in front of the fact that **no more RS485 protocol. No more wires. They're not yours anymore. It's a different reality**." And his diagnosis of the confusion PAX creates: "they're confused in the situation where **[MudaGoose] remains on the location. That throws everybody off**"; "**you came in. You didn't change anything. You just made the situation awkward. Now there's this system and the other system**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].

### The same afternoon, JVL answered its own accusation — and the answer is bad for card volumes

Three hours after the lunch above, walking out of the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-02-continental-bifurcated-operations-and-vendor-lock-in.md|Continental visit]], [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] and [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] worked out where they think the missing money goes. Val's read of the operator's replay request: it is "**a wonderful way for location to keep on giving cash and skirting everything under**" — cash keeps being paid out by hand, and the replay feature makes the Intralot report look right. Andrei agreed and tied it straight to InComm's own number: "**That's why such a minimal amount of cards has been issued in the three months. Probably that's a loophole**… **It's an opportunity for any replay solution. They would like to have it just to have an excuse for** [Intralot]. **But it doesn't mean that more cards will be** [issued]." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Vendor_Lock_In_Dead_End-September-2-2026.md]]

That is the first dated statement in the corpus of **why the 1.5-cards-a-day figure is low**, and it cuts against the assumption that better replay technology lifts card issuance. Andrei hedged it himself — "**It's not a prediction. It's not a forecast. Just thinking out loud**" — and it is recorded on that basis, as JVL's internal reasoning rather than an established market fact. The fuller passage is on [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]] (Sept 2, Continental). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Vendor_Lock_In_Dead_End-September-2-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Continental_Vendor_Lock_In_Dead_End-September-2-2026.md]]

## Sept 2 2026 (evening) — InComm seen from a competing processor's side

Hours after the lunch above, a rival payments company sat in the JVL Georgia office and described InComm from the outside ([[wiki/meetings/2026-09-02-billy-liu-playpay-partnership-deep-dive.md]]). [[wiki/people/billy-liu.md|Billy Liu]] of [[wiki/organizations/play-payments.md|Play Payments]] competes with InComm on the card network — "**We compete against [InComm] because we offer a wallet that is a Visa wallet**," against InComm's Mastercard prepaid product — and what he said is worth keeping because it is the corpus's only account of InComm's commercial manner from a company it tried to recruit. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]

- **InComm approached him to partner and he refused, expecting to be cut out.** "**They want to partner up, and I said, 'Partner up with you. I'm gonna get fucking kicked to the curb.' They're very difficult people to deal with.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]
- **He confirmed the 1.5-cards-a-day figure** when Val put it to him — "**Average. Exactly. It's horrible**" — the same number InComm gave at lunch and the basis of Val's missing-$900-a-day accusation. *(Billy's own first attempt at the number is garbled; the figure is Val's, confirmed rather than independently supplied.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]
- **On who gets the fees: master licence holders, not stores.** "**Except [InComm] doesn't share fees to the store**… **They share with the MLHs.**" Val agreed as to the stores and added that his own share comes to him directly: "**[InComm] gives me 0.5 percent of the processing fee.**" That 0.5% matches the "fifty basis points" InComm's own people named at lunch — see [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]] (Sept 2, evening) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].
- **He put the $1,000 load cap down to InComm, not the lottery.** "**We're just following the thousand dollar cap rule that [InComm] imposes.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]
- **Thirty years of physical cards is the position Visa pushed him away from.** "[InComm has] been doing this for thirty years, offering physical cards," where Visa told PlayPay "**we don't want to go backwards and offer a physical card.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]
- **And an unsourced rumour about Skill Pay, recorded as rumour:** "I heard that **skill pay isn't coming or isn't gonna come easily. I don't know all the details.**" Nothing corroborates it; the Skill Pay timetable InComm itself gave is above [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

**The exclusivity he then asked JVL for — no InComm on JVL's devices, no physical card dispensing — is the counterweight to InComm's own demand that same day**, and is held on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-val-proposes-fifty-percent-of-play-payments.md]] and [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-hold-the-recycler-back-but-sign-no-undertaking.md]], deliberately not duplicated here.

## Sept 8 2026 — a master licensee says InComm carries the broker's risk, which contradicts what InComm told Val

At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-08-dixie-amusement-bundle-offer-and-card-economics.md|Sept-8 Dixie visit]] [[wiki/people/salim-khambawala.md|Salim Khambawala]] made a claim about InComm that [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] had never heard and that cuts against his own prior enquiry. The full economics are on [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]] (Sept 8 2026); what belongs here is what is asserted about **InComm**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]

- **The claim.** Asked what happens when a location cannot pay for the cards it loaded, Salim: "**Currently, they take the risk and they take it**" — i.e. InComm absorbs the broker's loss. Val: "[InComm] does not take a risk for the brokers." Salim: "**They do.**" Val: "How do you know that?" Salim: "**For sure. Currently, [InComm] is the market leader. They don't want competition.**"
- **Val's own information, twice stated, says the opposite.** In the room: "**so I never heard that [InComm] takes the risk, but I will speak to [InComm] and say, take the risk.**" And in the debrief minutes later: "**I asked [InComm] two years ago if they would take a risk, and they told me no. But things may change. I don't know. I genuinely don't know.**" **Recorded, not resolved** — one is a second-hand operator report, the other a two-year-old answer from InComm itself, and neither source addresses the other.
- **Salim's caveat on whether JVL would get it:** "**They will. But if they look at you as a competition, then they won't because they don't want competition. They want acquisition. Market acquisition is their goal.**"
- **InComm has offered Salim the broker role and he refuses it.** "**[InComm] is offering that to us, and we don't want to get involved in that. It's just a headache.**" His reasons are on [[wiki/people/salim-khambawala.md]].
- **Structural correction: InComm is a program manager, not the provider.** "**that's where [InComm] is just a program manager. [InComm] is not the provider. Provider is one of the bank entities.**" And on scale against the competition: Fiery Pay's backer is **Fiserv** — "**U.S. largest financial provider**" in Salim's words — "**But [InComm] is bigger.**"
- **A dated explanation of InComm's pre-July-1 numbers.** Salim accepts InComm's **1.5 cards/day** figure as true before gift cards became legal, and attributes it to the pilot period and a market-share push: "**They did the pilot; they were running it till July first. Nobody was in a rush**… they were all about **market acquisition**… **In May, June, July. I think they gave away free equipment also.**" His own post-July-1 rate is **3.5–4 cards a day**. That dates the 5,000-free-device giveaway already recorded on [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]].
- **Who restocks the cards, and how badly it works.** "**[InComm] says it's never been at least three days and four days**" without stock at the location. Val's product answer — ship on a low-stock signal rather than on an order — is on [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]].
- **And InComm is named as the only party holding the data JVL needs to price default risk:** "**[InComm] for sure will have that data because they did the pilot for the lottery for the first three or four years.**"

*(The commitment to go back to InComm and ask them to carry the broker's risk is held once, on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-08-the-bundle-put-to-dixie-with-installation-explicitly-excluded.md]].)*

At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-09-rao-machine-performance-and-exclusivity-offer.md|Sept-9 Rao meeting]] the Georgia distributor who installs the competing **Access** system gave his read of InComm's position, and [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] gave a figure for the installed base. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]]

- **5,500 locations on PAX with InComm, "all of it needs to be replaced"** — Val, in the room, using it as the reason to move now. *(A different quantity from the penetration figures already on [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]]; none of them is reconciled.)*

They want somebody like us to come in and put our equipment. They don't want to. They want to clear cards. They don't want to bother with equipment.**" That matches what InComm has said about itself since June 2025 — "makes no money on kiosks," not in the kiosk/game-card-system business — recorded above.

You need to get it.'" Both recorded; **neither is tested in the source**. *(Reading "Absad" as the "Afshad"/"Avshad" of the Aug-12 and Sept-9 records is an **inference** from context.)*
- **And on InComm's size as an argument about its behaviour:** "**Income don't care. They are not going to worry. They're going to do whatever they want because it's a billion dollar company.**" Rao's own characterisation, unsupported elsewhere in this source.
- **A claim about Skill Pay that conflicts with InComm's own account** — Rao describing "SkillPay" as a crypto company issuing the ATM-access card — is recorded, with his own disclaimer that it is an assumption from hearsay, on [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]] (Sept 9). **The corpus's direct InComm testimony on the blue Skill Pay card stands unchanged.**

## Sept 9 2026 (evening) — JVL's intent to displace InComm is said out loud, to a customer

**This is the first source in the corpus in which JVL's plan to replace InComm on the back end is stated to anyone outside JVL.** At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-09-arc-gaming-backend-displacement-negotiation.md|4:35 PM ARC meeting]] [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] told [[wiki/people/david-sloan.md|David Sloan]], ARC's CEO: "On the back end, **we're looking to replace [InComm]**," and then, with the confidentiality frame attached, "I'm buying the business and the hope that **I can replace [InComm]. This is highly confidential. We don't have a confidentiality agreement**." Earlier in the same meeting he put it as the sharp half of a question to the same man: "**Aside from these kiosks, I'm looking at taking the business from [InComm].**" Full ruling and context on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-val-discloses-the-incomm-displacement-intent-to-arcs-ceo.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

- **It is not framed as a hardware play.** The free kiosk, point-of-sale and five ports are the means — "we're **virtually converting the entire location to us**" — and the back end is the object.
- **The route is undecided.** "If I want to go with somebody else, **the solution is available to me now. But I don't know if that somebody else is the right solution**"; "**If I have to do it myself, then half a year.**" The partner is not named by either man and **is not identified here**.
- **Note the tension with Val's own position the same morning**, recorded in the Rao section above: that InComm "**will happily**" have JVL put its equipment in because it only wants to clear cards. Wanting JVL's hardware in the field and being displaced from the back end are different things, and **no source in the corpus reconciles them or records InComm being told anything about the second.**
- *(The transcript renders InComm as "income" throughout — four occurrences, all flagged in the derived notes, and the notes record that the correction had **not** been applied to the raw file.)* [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Backend_Displacement_Negotiation-September-9-2026.md]]

# Narayani Amusements

> **Status note (Sept 1 2026).** Six days after the meeting below, the two managers who came to JVL's office on this account told Val the company had been **renamed Akshar Gaming** the previous week after a change of ownership, and that its owner is **not** Vishal Patel — Vishal's company being **Ananta Amusement**. Whether that is this same route renamed or a sister company is **not settled**; see `## Open contradictions` below and [[wiki/organizations/akshar-gaming.md]]. Narayani, Legends, Continental?"). **Inference, labelled as such:** "Mariani Amusement" is the derived notes' rendering of Narayani, not a second company. ## The operation (as stated by Narayani's side, Aug 26 2026)

- **Scale: "around nine hundred machines on field… Should be more than nine hundred now"**, across "**About four hundred and ninety locations altogether**." Asked whether 490 locations for 900 machines was low, the answer was "That's the industry average." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]
- **Geography:** "Greater Atlanta area" plus locations in **Valdosta, Augusta, and Savannah**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]
- **Financing:** "We have our own money that we use, and then of course, we take finance from manufacturers." The most recent example given was a deal "I just made with IGD [IGT] right in that show… a fifteen month deal." A colleague detailed the manufacturer terms on offer — "a nine-month payment plan, some will have a three-month no-payment plan and then a six-month plan… ten days of no payments and then nine months of payment… maximum was twelve months of payments… Twelve months of payments plus three months of no payments. So that's again fifteen months." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]
- **Financing is not the lever.** "Not really. We can use our own money as well if we have the right deal. And if that's what the location is asking for if we can get the equipment at a good price." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]
- **Location threshold:** "I don't do any locations that performs less than 20-25,000 hours a month" — the transcript says "hours"; the derived notes read the same passage as his **20–25k/month locations**, i.e. dollars. Recorded with the discrepancy visible. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]

## Who dictates the purchase

Narayani's stated buying rule is that it does not choose the games — the location does, and behind the location, the player:

- "at the end of the day, It's the location who who asks we have to give them what they ask for." Val accepted the framing immediately — "I fully understand you. Customer dictates." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]
- "The end decision maker is the player because if a player asks for a jibia [JVL], The customers are location will tell us. I need a jibia, and then you should find out which option can be done." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]
- What locations are asking for: "every location that we have they ask for aristocrats, like not even that many lightning wonders. But aristocrats cash eruptions and maybe one hold the battle." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]

## The game mix in a Narayani location

A six-machine location was walked through: "one Arista Cloud [Aristocrat], one Light and Wonder" then "There will be like two IGS:, a Photon box or a Prizm Sight or Prizm… Both of them would be a Prizm Sight depending on what actual location is." The sixth decision now goes to "**Bad Dog**" — "It's been performing really good. Percentages are good for us as well. And it attracts the customers too" — because "**Fusion** has been not been that great again." Val did not recognise the latter: "Fusion? I'm sorry, I don't know who Fusion is." **The brand names in this passage are transcript renderings and are not resolved here** ("Arista Cloud", "IGS", "Photon box", "Prizm Sight", "Bad Dog", "Fusion"); none is matched to a corpus entity. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]

## JVL install base at Narayani

- **Still in play:** "We do have some JVLs, initially we also have those JVLs in our site. I guess we still have **40 or 50 or 60 JVLs** currently." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]
- **Idle in the warehouse:** roughly **25 old JVL D27 cabinets** — "We have what twenty five, I would have to just count an exact number, but it should be somewhere there" — bought at **$13,200** each ("You Bought it for thirteen two"), sitting unused: "those machines are sitting here for me." These are the trade-in stock. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]
- **Performance complaint:** "I had one location which was where JVL was doing very well, and I don't know what happened to them either. You know now they're not performing that either… So nobody's really asking for [JVL] right now." His own diagnosis is payout-driven: Aristocrat and Light & Wonder win locations "by setting a very high payout center. You know, their payouts are like ninety one ninety two percent," which he says he dislikes — "But it's the name of the game… the flow is going that way." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]

## Card payments in Narayani's locations (Aug 26 2026)

- **Most locations run a gift-card program from a competitor.** "If they go with a gift card program right now, everybody's doing NCOM [InComm]… which is fifty dollars for the POS a month… Fifty dollars a month is their subscription." The derived notes read the same passage as locations "mostly use InComm via **PAX** at $50/month subscription"; the transcript's own rendering of the second vendor is garbled ("Edistro practice is doing it the five pay") and is **not** resolved here. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]
- **Hardware on top of the subscription.** Val: "plus you have to buy hardware… They're hardware, three to five hundred dollars. Plus each machine has a piece of hardware." A JVL voice added that for one competing product "you have to pay around **seven hundred and fifty dollars for the device**, and then if you want it to be replaying, you need to buy something else"; Narayani's answer was that the in-machine connector is "like **four hundred dollars** in change", and the JVL voice totalled it "So in total it goes up like fifteen —" (the sentence is cut off mid-figure). The product name in this exchange is transcribed "try and pay" and is **unidentified**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]
- **The big locations do not want automation.** "my big locations bigger house they don't even want, They like to just stick with the old system where they have to do manual replay. All they want is how this gift cards are being issued… They have they have ATM access to it." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]

## Competitive politics — the "syndicate" idea

Narayani's read of the big manufacturers: "Right now, they're trying to monopolize the market so badly, right? I was when we were in the mall, I was getting all the big masters together. I'm like, Let's make a syndicate together and show these guys that they cannot dominate the market." Val's answer was a warning — "When they do that, this is when you get a real pickle. And I've seen it over and over again" — followed by his reason JVL deserves the business: "none of the light and wonder, IGT aristocrat decision makers came here and asked you for business. Not one ever, and never will. And I did." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]

## Open questions

- **What happens to the three-year data contract when a machine is sold with a route?** A JVL voice raised it directly — "a lot of upgrades I've done for machines are with machines that belong to you now… You buy and sell routes as everyone does, right? This three year contract." Narayani's reply is garbled ("You can't. We cannot remove that. If you want to make a twenty five machine deal at this price?") and the point was dropped without resolution. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]
- **Will Narayani install JVL transactional equipment across its locations?** Val made this the condition of the full 25-machine swap; the answer was "Look, I would want it if it accommodates my customer, which is the location. I would push for it… Let me come look at it first." **Still unresolved after the Sept-1 demo** — the equipment was shown, the men who saw it cannot decide, and the answer went back to Vishal with them. Val's own prediction of what they would carry back was minimal: "**they will say that JVL has this thing that they could install in the machine, and you could move from machine to machine. That's all they remember.**" Andrei was bleaker still — "**Maybe they will tell him that there is a game machine.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

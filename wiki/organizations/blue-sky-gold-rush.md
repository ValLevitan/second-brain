# Blue Sky / Gold Rush

A Georgia operator group, new to the industry, that came to the JVL office on **Oct 15 2026** for a transactional demo and asked to buy JVL's refurbished trade-in machines — [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-15-blue-sky-gold-rush-refurbished-machines-offer-and-transactional-demo.md]]. The two names are two companies run by the same team. Operator economics only on this page; no named person's pay (§15.8).

## Who they are (as they told it, Oct 15 2026)

- **Blue Sky** has "about 100 locations". A second group of "about 30 locations" is "coming" — "new mega hit places" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]]. The notes read the 30 as **Gold Rush** [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]].
- **Gold Rush** is older: "They've been longer than me actually, but they never did anything because Chirag was the owner before, and he was also president of the HRE." HRE was described as "an Indian association. All the gas stations, retail association. He got about six thousand stores under him", which runs trade shows. Chirag is "still a partner"; "He got another partners, more partners, so they can run the company, and I'm helping them out." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]] (What "HRE" stands for is not said — unclear.)
- **New owners.** "We just bought that agency"; "I'm new in the industry, so nobody's looking outside the box, and I'm looking outside the box" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]].
- **People named on the tape:** **Hiran**, who introduced himself to Val mid-meeting, and **Raj**, whom Felipe addressed at the end. The Plaud labels mix people, so which of them made which statement below is not known [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]]. *(A "Raj" also came up on the Oct-14 sales call — a distributor contact sent two people, "one with Raj". Whether it is the same Raj is unclear; nothing links them.)*
- **Other states.** "We're now going into North Carolina. We're dabbling a little bit in Virginia"; Texas "we're just now looking at it"; a two-week look at Chicago ended with "It's not our market" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]].

## How they run the route

- **Bad Dog machines lead.** "We're doing very good with the bad dogs… Bad dog for us right now is number one outside of any major company; it brings in the best amount of revenue." They refurbished old machines "putting in a whole new screen and everything. We paid fifteen hundred dollars to get it completely brand new." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]]
- **Moving to casino style, with variety.** "most of our locations have at least five or six machines. We don't want to give them those older games anymore. We want to go towards the casino styles… IGT at one location, this game at one location". They are also buying adjustable stools [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]].
- **Payments today.** They pay Fiery Pay "sixty-nine point ninety-nine" a month — "That's everything with TITO". TITO is possible but they chose not to use printers: "the printer is an extra expense and the printer goes down… people also lose the paper… there's a chance of counterfeiting". "We've already switched three companies… These are the three that we're going to go"; InComm's terminal is free and "the other two are charging the monthly charge" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]].
- **Fees go to the locations.** On who pays a monthly fee: "they're all charging the locations… That's why we're letting everybody choose… when you do your contract, you're doing it with them. Because the draft has come out of their account." They send flyers and videos to their locations and let them "pick one" of two or three options [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]]. Compare [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-17-the-ninety-dollar-fee-is-drafted-from-the-location-not-paid-by-the-master.md]] (Oct-15 note).
- **New sites.** "We just installed six new sites"; locations "at Carlton" and "down south by Griffin" are being fitted [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]].

## Their view of the market (Oct 15 2026)

- "The whole market is down, thirty, forty. Everybody I talk to, every master". Two causes named: the gift-card change ("This gift card system is taking so long for people to know and figure out") and online gambling ("Online gambling is killing us") [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]].
- Costs rise as they fight for revenue: "Overhead is going up, and just because we're fighting to keep that revenue." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]]
- More on the Georgia picture: [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]] (Oct 15).

## The deal under discussion (Oct 15 2026)

- **The refurbished machines.** JVL has 20 trade-in machines, refurbished. The operator asked for all 20 at "Around the nine thousand mark" over "twelve months" — "this is going to be a trial period. Once I know it's a hundred percent… I need to buy the new ones up." Val: "I don't think that I can meet 9,000", and offered to think "in the direction of maybe fourteen months or sixteen months payment" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]]. The rules Val set are on [[wiki/decisions/2026-10-15-refurbished-trade-in-machines-are-sold-apart-from-the-show-special-and-show-special-machines-are-not-financed.md]].
- **Their cash position (deal context).** "my cash flow is very tight"; "that's only because I'm spending 25% of my budget"; "I'm gonna make sure the next six months I got that money coming in no matter what", partly "because I don't know what's gonna happen in March with the change" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]].
- **2027 volume.** "among all my locations, we're upgrading 65 machines… Half are getting discontinued… 65 machines. Machines… Not locations." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]]
- **Credit.** Felipe: "if we do the deal, we would have to run a credit check." Val: the company as guarantor, and "We need your bank reference." The operator: "The credit won't be a problem." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Blue_Sky_Gold_Rush_Refurbished_Machines_Offer_and_Transactional_Demo-October-15-2026.md]]
- **The new machine and the show special** were also offered — [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-free-ports-and-pos-with-every-machine-bought.md]] (Oct 15).

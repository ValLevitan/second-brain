# NTS (Nebraska Technical Services)

**NTS — Nebraska Technical Services** is the **largest gaming route operator in the Omaha, Nebraska market** and JVL's most promising prospective customer/distributor for the [[wiki/projects/nebraska-skill-market.md|Nebraska skill-gaming entry]]. It is a **family-owned business** run by president/co-owner **[[wiki/people/ryan-cruz.md|Ryan Cruz]]** and his sister **Sarah**. [[wiki/people/denis-desmarais.md|Denis Desmarais]] met them in Omaha and reported to [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] on May 29 2025. (Val referred to the company once as "NTC"; the full name given on the call is Nebraska Technical Services / "NTS.") [[raw/denis_ntc_nebraska-may-29-2025.md]]

## What is known (May 29 2025)

- **~80–85% market share in Omaha** — Denis "saw their name" at most locations he visited. They cover the **eastern** part of the state directly; the **western** market past Lincoln they cover **in partnership with [[wiki/organizations/jj-operator.md|J&J]]**. [[raw/denis_ntc_nebraska-may-29-2025.md]]
- Already familiar with JVL: they **used to buy JVL countertop video games** in the past. [[raw/denis_ntc_nebraska-may-29-2025.md]]
- Their current product mix includes **Vanilla** and **Bank Shot**, but they are **dropping Bank Shot** ("not even a nice game"), and are welcoming/excited about a new JVL product. [[raw/denis_ntc_nebraska-may-29-2025.md]]
- **Interested in the ~300 refurbished machines from South America** that Val has available (Denis to relay the two models + what's included). [[raw/denis_ntc_nebraska-may-29-2025.md]]
- In the **Lincoln** market (where Ryan lives) NTS grew from **0% to ~35% in the last two years** and is expanding aggressively. [[raw/denis_ntc_nebraska-may-29-2025.md]]

# Double Down Gaming

A **small, deliberately high-volatility Georgia COAM route** run by [[wiki/people/ryan-odonnell.md|Ryan O'Donnell]], who also sits on the [[wiki/organizations/gamoa.md|GAMOA]] board. JVL met the company for the first time at the Lawrenceville office on **September 1 2026** — see [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-01-double-down-gaming-visit.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]

*(Observation, not a sourced fact: on the location counts each operator gave in his own meeting, this is the smallest of the five Georgia routes JVL visited across Aug 26 – Sept 1 2026, and the only one whose owner described his strategy as deliberately raising bet levels and jackpot ceilings rather than spreading risk.)*

## Size and shape of the route

- **The route shrank on purpose.** "We had about **25 locations last year**… We ended up **selling some of our locations to a partner** that we've done work with in the past. So right now we're **down to 12 locations**. We're trying to build that back up to **20 in the next six to** [eight months]." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **Revenue is concentrated in a handful of sites.** "**five or six of our locations make up 60% of our revenue, 70% of our revenue.** We got a couple middle ones and a bunch of bottom ones." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **The two biggest locations are partner-run, so they are off-limits for testing.** "**my two largest locations are run by my partners, so I don't really have control over it. We're just along for the ride.**" This is what blocked Val's free swap-test offer. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **Placement is hard and he says so plainly.** Asked how hard it is to get locations placed: "**I think it's hard. Locations are tough.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **New locations start at six machines, never eight or nine.** "We did **six machines** to start, but we want **eight**, so I just like to get them in. **I don't like to go eight or nine right out of the gate. I usually like to baby step it.**" And the kiosk pressure rises with volume: "Put six machines in. Now they want these kiosks. It's tough. **The ones that are typically the higher volume place, the more they want kiosks.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]

## The high-roller locations

- **Best sites are Vietnamese cafes.** "some of my best locations, which are **Vietnamese cafes**, still run silver bar on a lot of machines. Their game is [Primero]." *("silver bar" is unclear and recorded as heard; "Premiero" is a garble of **Primero**, the manufacturer the corpus already carries.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **Bets: $1 minimum, $10 maximum.** Val asked both: minimum "**It is a dollar**," maximum "**Ten dollars.**" Some machines cap lower and cannot be changed — "Five. Some of them are harder. Hardcode it, so **IGT doesn't allow you to set it.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **Jackpots run at $12,000, and at two sites they are not progressive at all.** "We set those to ten, and we set the **jackpots to twelve thousand. Starting at twelve thousand.**" Then: "So it's not progressive. **We have two locations. One location we set it at ten, ten, ten thousand one dollar, and it runs to twelve. The other place is just twelve. Minimum twelve, maximum twelve.**" *(Transcribed as spoken; the repeated figures are garbled and are not smoothed here.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **The historical baseline he came from.** "**Forever, we always had these [Primeros] and the [Banillas]**, and it was just **always paying back seventy percent to the player, sixty percent to the player**." *("primroses"/"vanillas" are garbles of Primero and Banilla, both carried elsewhere in this wiki.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]

## What he buys

- **Brand ranking, in his words:** "We have a handful of **Light and Wonders**. They've performed well over the last year. **The [JVLs] are kind of like our next level down. Bad Dog.** I like Bad Dog for a couple of reasons, but mostly **people tend to like that game**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **He has already bought JVL machines.** Asked whether JVL machines had ever been tested for him, the answer in the room was "He has. We recently purchased **six [Diamond Link]** for you" — six Diamond Link units, recently. *(Rendered "six diamond rings"; read as **Diamond Link** because Val's very next line is "Can I switch **diamond links** to this machine for free…". The turn is mis-split between the JVL and operator sides in the transcript.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **Old cabinets can take the new software.** He asked directly — "is it possible to buy that software for the old cabinets?" — and was told "**Absolutely.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]

## Kiosks and redemption

- **Kiosks come from IFS.** "I had a couple **kiosks already from IFS**, so I'm using IFS one." The card side runs through a contact he names as "**Bob at Perez**" — see [[wiki/people/bob.md]] for why that name matters. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **His cards are not ATM-access cards yet.** Asked "is it ATM access card?" — "**Not yet. I'm waiting in the dust.**" Andrei asked to be kept posted. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **For higher-volume locations he names two things, one of them unclear.** "for the locations that were higher volume, we use **access** our customers. Typically, we are using **easy win** for the first time. **My next install is going to be easy win.**" *("access" is plausibly the Georgia competitor **Access** already tracked on [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]]; "easy win" is read by the derived notes as **EasyLink** — a TITO supplier the corpus carries as EasyLynx/Easy Links. **Neither reading is asserted here**; both renderings are recorded as spoken because the source does not spell either name out.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]

## Where JVL got to

No purchase. Ryan passed for this visit and pointed at one upcoming location: "**Now I'm gonna pass right now. I've just got one place coming up**… But I always want to stay abreast." He would put "at least one, maybe two" JVL machines there "depending on how many machines they end up with," and wants numbers first: "**once I get this place with some numbers, I'll see how they do.**" The full pitch he heard is on [[wiki/topics/slot-game-design-principles.md]] and [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]].

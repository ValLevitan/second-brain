# RBC (Royal Bank of Canada)

RBC is one of JVL's Canadian banks. The relationship surfaced on a **June 4 2025** service-review call ([[wiki/meetings/2025-06-04-rbc-services-review.md]]) that [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] and [[wiki/people/monica.md|Monica]] used to escalate deep dissatisfaction with RBC's service; an RBC wealth-management/commercial team pledged to "reset" the relationship. RBC holds the group's holding-company banking (**JBL Labs Inc** / JVL Labs); by contrast JVL's US subsidiary banks with [[wiki/organizations/cibc.md|CIBC]] and Bank of America. [[raw/RBC_services_review-june-4-2025.md]]

## The June-4 service-review call

- **Val's blunt framing:** "we are absolutely flabbergasted… dealing with RBC. It is… a nightmare. All of the banks are shit… But RBC is on top" — a chain of hand-offs ("one guy pins you to the next guy") where "nobody knows anything, cannot get shit done." [[raw/RBC_services_review-june-4-2025.md]]
- **The RBC diagnosis (Tim):** JVL was placed in the **wrong part of the bank** — small-business/retail coverage with unsophisticated managers — rather than **wealth management / private banking** appropriate to a company at JVL's revenue. The fix offered was a **full reset** of who covers the account, led personally by the team on the call. [[raw/RBC_services_review-june-4-2025.md]]

### Concrete pain points raised
- **Euro account (JBL Labs Inc):** Monica cannot get access to the company's **Euro bank account**, cannot grant access to the office banking administrators, and cannot connect the Euro account to the regular account (they are on **different platforms**, requiring a secret email + secret password + separate browser). The previous business account manager **Crystal Tang** went on leave ~a month prior; the unnamed replacement rep does not know the account. [[raw/RBC_services_review-june-4-2025.md]]
- **GIC / "bonds" deposit:** it took ~**five calls just to deposit $3 million** in GICs; Val/Monica hold access cash they want to park in short-term (1-, 2-, 3-month) GICs on a rotating basis for maximum safe yield — current holdings ~**$1.1M USD + ~$119k CAD** (Val cited ~$1.4–1.6M), with GICs **expiring June 5** (the next day). (Val used "bonds" out of US habit; corrected to GIC in Canada.) [[raw/RBC_services_review-june-4-2025.md]]
- **Physical check / e-banking:** depositing a physical check electronically failed because the business lacked the "check pro" feature, and Monica couldn't reach anyone for the e-banking steps — she ended up going to the branch in person. [[raw/RBC_services_review-june-4-2025.md]]
- Contrast Val drew with **CIBC**: there he has one individual acting as a "personal concierge" — he emails "please transfer money… here's an invoice" and it's paid, no secret-password steps. [[raw/RBC_services_review-june-4-2025.md]]

### JVL economics disclosed (context for coverage tier)
- The RBC entity's **annual revenue ~$10M USD, growing ~30%/year** — the basis for RBC saying JVL "should not be with a retail banking platform." (Business/banking economics — permitted under §15.8.) [[raw/RBC_services_review-june-4-2025.md]]

## July-9 2025 follow-up — commercial move + the US-banking question

A follow-up video call ([[wiki/meetings/2025-07-09-rbc-us-banking-followup.md]]) after the June-4 reset. Sequence: **June 4** (escalation/reset promised) → **July 9** (RBC brings in a commercial relationship manager and Val discloses the US move). [[raw/meeting_RBC-july-9-2025.md]]

- **Progress since June 4:** the **GICs were renewed**; Monica's accounting team had no material issues with that. The **Euro account** is still unresolved — foreign-currency accounts (Euro/GBP) sit on a **separate portal** from RBC Express, and only **Val** can operate the Euro account (it needs a code + a verification code), so day-to-day staff can't. RBC's forthcoming **"RBC Edge"** product (rolling out this year) is meant to fold foreign-currency accounts into the main portal, but isn't released yet. [[raw/meeting_RBC-july-9-2025.md]]
- **The proposed move:** RBC wants to transition JVL off the current business-account setup onto the **commercial team**, led personally by **Saad** — a "much more personalized" relationship model. Sean framed this as the right fix regardless of JVL's future plans. [[raw/meeting_RBC-july-9-2025.md]]
- **Val's US-move disclosure (why this matters for RBC).** Val told RBC JVL is **moving production to the US** (it already has a US entity, registered years ago, previously just a sales entity; **tariffs + uncertainty** drove the manufacturing decision). JVL's immediate US coverage is **Georgia, South Dakota, Nebraska, Texas and Florida**. The Canadian entity stays open for now, but Val predicts JVL may **close it within three-to-five years** unless Canada's economy changes. See [[wiki/decisions/2025-06-24-move-manufacturing-from-canada-to-georgia.md]]. [[raw/meeting_RBC-july-9-2025.md]]
- **The banking decision Val put to RBC.** JVL's processing is tied to RBC via an **RBC banking API** integration that took ~half a year to build, so Val is reluctant to switch — but if RBC **can't service JVL in the US**, that must be raised "sooner than later," because JVL would then migrate to another platform (JVL has already opened **CIBC USA** accounts, not yet triggered; CIBC serves it out of Chicago). Val said he'd rather be transparent than let RBC spend time/money "nourishing" a Canadian relationship with no future. RBC said it has **US affiliate banks** and would come back with US options. See [[wiki/organizations/cibc.md]]. [[raw/meeting_RBC-july-9-2025.md]]
- **Real-estate context Val gave:** JVL bought a **building in Austin, Texas** three years ago (funded by **CIBC**, not RBC), **refinanced June 2025** for another four years; JVL has an **option on an industrial freestanding building near Atlanta, Georgia**. (Business/banking economics — §15.8.) [[raw/meeting_RBC-july-9-2025.md]]
- RBC would reconvene internally the next day and revert to Monica (Val away from Saturday, back the 22nd). [[raw/meeting_RBC-july-9-2025.md]]

## Aug 27 2026 — RBC activity is to "hit a sunset"

On the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-27-bank-of-america-treasury-needs-review.md|Aug-27 Bank of America treasury call]], Val said JVL is "transferring our company from Canada to the U S. We're closing Canadian accounts, We're moving to the U S accounts because we use Royal Bank of Canada as well… So the Royal Bank of Canada transactions will hit a sunset, and Bank of America accounts will increase." This is Val's plan as stated to another bank; no source records the RBC accounts actually closing. Details on [[wiki/organizations/bank-of-america.md]] (History — Aug 27 2026).

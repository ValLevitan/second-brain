# J&J (gaming route operator)

J&J is a privately-owned US gaming **route operator** (not a manufacturer) that JVL first spoke to on May 7 2025 as a prospective customer for its cabinets and FlexPay payment solution. [[raw/intro_jj_jvl-may-7-2025.md]]

## What they are

- A route operator that places gaming machines with locations; based in **Central Illinois** (Scott Todd said he is originally from Illinois). [[raw/intro_jj_jvl-may-7-2025.md]]
- **Over 25,000 machines "on the street"** as of the call, and roughly **1,400–1,500 employees** (they had just completed an acquisition). [[raw/intro_jj_jvl-may-7-2025.md]]
- Footprint: **Illinois, Georgia, Nebraska, Montana, Nevada**, and growing in **Pennsylvania**; expanding into **Nebraska**; eyeing a small route in **South Dakota** (a customer is asking) but not there yet. [[raw/intro_jj_jvl-may-7-2025.md]]
- Familiar with JVL's history — they had "run a lot of JVL countertops over the years" and later JVL's Cash Code validators. [[raw/intro_jj_jvl-may-7-2025.md]]

## What Val pitched

- JVL manufactures full cabinets/games (vertical, dual-screen, toppers), **Class III skill games** for Nebraska and Georgia; main competitor is **IGT**. JVL is taking its Nebraska games through **GLI**, expecting approval within ~30 days, and is opening a Nebraska office. [[raw/intro_jj_jvl-may-7-2025.md]]
- JVL's Georgia presence: an office and **over 2,500 games installed** across ~40–50 locations, with most operators. [[raw/intro_jj_jvl-may-7-2025.md]]
- JVL is introducing its **payment solution** (FlexPay) in Georgia and will expand it to Nebraska. See [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]]. [[raw/intro_jj_jvl-may-7-2025.md]]

## June 16 2026 — Omaha site visit: ownership, history, and Nebraska facility

Val visited J&J's new Omaha office and Rick Rees — J&J's **Nebraska Operations Manager** — gave a full facility tour (warehouse, gaming floor, cash-handling vault, collection staging). [[raw/Meeting_Notes_JJ_Gaming_Nebraska_Site_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]

- **Ownership and history.** J&J is owned by the **Jansen family** (Kay Jansen, Jay Jansen) out of Illinois. It started **100+ years ago in vending** and pivoted to gaming when **Illinois legalized in 2009**, setting its first machines in **2012**. It bought **Midwest Amusements** (rebranded **Trestle**) to acquire its Nebraska route, and separately bought **Epic**, a game-development company, for vertical integration. J&J runs its own **24/7 surveillance camera systems** at all locations. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_JJ_Gaming_Nebraska_Site_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **Nebraska facility.** J&J is moving into a **~24,000 sq ft** building in Omaha (leased from Trestle/formerly Midwest Amusements), up from **1,300 sq ft** — with about **70 employees on the Nebraska side**. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_JJ_Gaming_Nebraska_Site_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **Route size.** ~**500 locations in Nebraska**, ~**100 in Iowa**, ~**600 total**; plus a small presence in **Alabama** (<100 machines). Nebraska recently allowed a **5th machine per location**. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_JJ_Gaming_Nebraska_Site_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]

## J&J Nebraska cash operations and back-office systems (June 16 2026)

- **Cash operations.** A **14-person collection team** runs **7 two-man routes per day**, covering ~**70 locations daily**. Teams carry pre-strapped cash from J&J's own **vault**, fill terminals, collect machine proceeds, and return cash for counting and recycling. **No armed security**; only one vehicle break-in since Rick arrived. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_JJ_Gaming_Nebraska_Site_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **Back-office systems.** All machines connect via the **Easy Link** system (Nebraska requires dual SaaS). Terminals run **Express RT** software (by Clint Lerman) at **$35/day per site**. Each machine has a **SMIB (Slot Machine Interface Board)** module connecting to the terminal and central server. **James Jansen** (in Illinois) handles all analytics and purchasing decisions; **Jeff Harwick** oversees technical. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_JJ_Gaming_Nebraska_Site_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]

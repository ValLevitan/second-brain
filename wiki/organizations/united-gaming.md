# United Gaming (Georgia's largest master operator)

**United is the largest master operator in the Georgia COAM market**, and the account JVL has never been able to reach above working level. It has appeared across the corpus since January 2026 as a market fact — the #1 master, a consolidator buying up other people's routes, and a company whose purchasing decisions sit above anyone JVL talks to. Those earlier appearances are on [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]] (Jan-27, Feb-18, Aug-11, Aug-21, Aug-26, Aug-31), [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md]] and [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md]]. This page exists because the **Sept-2 2026 InComm lunch** ([[wiki/meetings/2026-09-02-incomm-machine-payments-and-recycler-standoff.md]]) put a former United executive across the table from Val and produced the first account of **who now decides what United buys** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].

## Who decides what United buys (Sept 2 2026)

Val asked directly — "who is the purchasing decision man there?" — and pressed twice more when the first answer was general [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]]:

- **The lenders set the budget; the CFO and COO execute it.** "Even when I was there, we shifted from a very informal decisioning process to a more formal **quarterly capex approved budget**. And so ultimately, **the lenders are determining what the capex budget is. The CFO and the COO, Flurry and Marsha Hayward, facilitate what that means.**" Asked whether the CEO is involved: "Yeah, but I think ultimately it's **managed by the CFO and the COO**."
- **Jason Flurry, CFO** — "been in the Georgia market a long time. He came over to United in an acquisition deal, and so he's [a] pretty savvy CFO. He's been there a while, knows the state, knows the business, **very involved in their gaming strategy**."
- **Marsha Hayward, COO** — "She worked for [Scientific] Games for a long time… And then she came over to the United **right before I left**." Asked if she is loyal to her old employer: "No, they're interested in making money." She is hard to reach: "**Marsha's pretty hard to track down. Also, she doesn't engage the market**… She's pretty private."
- **What they are buying across:** "between kiosks and card systems, and TITO systems, and games, they've got to spend that budget in a way that makes sense and makes money."
- **The advice Val was given:** "you've got to **get some time with Jason Flurry and Marsha Hayward** for United."

*(Marsha Hayward's former employer is rendered "Psy Games" in the transcript; read as **Scientific Games** — an inference from role and context, not a corrected quote. See [[wiki/organizations/scientific-games.md]].)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]]

## The debt, and why the lenders now hold the pen

- **The original line of credit was about $860 million to $1 billion, drawn in 2023 and spent on acquisitions.** "**MGG**… **private equity**… they funded the original **billion-dollar line of credit for United [in] 2023**," and later, more precisely: "that first billion, maybe it was like **eight hundred and sixty million dollars in credit**. Largely, that was used to **acquire competition and acquire routes**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].
- **MGG facilitated a refinance and new lenders came in.** "MGG facilitated the **refinance** of that debt, and other partners came in at that point. **Fortress** was involved." The consequence: "he did his refinance deal, and naturally, I think that changes the game for United. You know, **now you got investors with a lot more power in the company**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].
- **On the bankruptcy rumour.** Val: "I'm hearing that the financial problems are coming." The answer separated the company from the man: "I mean I hear people are saying **Bunnies going bankrupt**… I understand that **he's fine. I'm not worried about him**… I don't have any other inside information that anybody else doesn't have." *(The owner's name is rendered "Bunnies" here; the corpus has carried United's owner as **Bunty** since Feb 2026 — treated as the same person, an inference from role and context, consistent with the "Bandi/Bundy" garble recorded on [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]] for Aug-31.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]]
- **The owner remains the centre of gravity whatever happens.** "if you see headlines about new leadership or something — that's what everybody's kind of watching for… But **whatever happens, Bunty will be the spine**." And on his manner: "He's pretty private, I would say… Even back in the day, **he didn't engage a lot of people directly**." Val's own position: "You know Bunty well? **Never met him. Don't know him at all**… even I don't know anything about any of the top management" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].

**This is compatible with, and sharper than, the Aug-31 account** [[wiki/people/georgios-zazanis.md|George Zazanis]] gave of United "going down" and top locations moving to other masters — see [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]] (Aug 31). The Sept-2 account adds the mechanism (a refinance transferring control to lenders) and denies the bankruptcy of the man; it does **not** contradict the earlier rumour and is not treated as resolving it.

## The exodus to InComm — and a name collision worth flagging

Val opened the lunch with "what is this exodus from United?" The InComm side described a chain: one of them left United first, **John** took him to lunch, and a few months later he brought the next one across. *(The transcript renders the surname "Housewell"; read as **John Houseal**, InComm's route-gaming lead already in the corpus — see [[wiki/people/john-houseal.md]]. An inference from the near-identical name and the matching role, not a corrected quote.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]]

**Two of the three InComm people are ex-United, and they describe different paths out** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]]:

- **The commercial lead** — "a little over three years" at United, where "we ran operations and their gift cards, and that's pretty much a little bit of everything over there." Before that, **ten years in Atlanta at Home Depot**, mostly "investigations and asset protection," where "we were losing **over a billion dollars a year in stolen merchandise**." He was hired after **Bunty personally interviewed him** for a posted position: "Believe it or not, it was a **smaller company** at that point… it was him and **Paul Oland**."
- **A second, earlier leaver** — "I left," after which "**John** found out I left… reached out and said, 'Let's go to lunch.' And then a few months later, I said, '**Jason**, I'm gonna go to lunch'" — i.e. he was recruited first and then brought the next person across. His own United role: "helped roll out the **gift card program for United Gaming**. Worked closely with **John Housewell** at In[Comm], and that's how I ended up here."

*(Neither is named in the source, and the exchange is the answer to Val's opening question, "what is this exodus from United?")*

**None of the InComm attendees is named in either Sept-2 source**, and the derived notes deliberately describe them by role only [[raw/Meeting_Notes_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]]. But there is a collision the corpus should not lose:

- The wiki carries **"Jason Hall"** twice — as **United's director of operations** (Feb-18 2026, the only United contact [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]] could reach) and as **InComm's Georgia COAM lead** who "joined InComm ~5 months earlier" as of Jul-27 2026, i.e. around February 2026. Both are on [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]] and [[wiki/organizations/incom.md]].
- The commercial lead's self-description — three years running United's operations and gift cards, then recruited into InComm — fits that pattern on its face.
- **The one bare "Jason" in the transcript does not settle it, and arguably cuts the other way.** It appears in the second leaver's line "and then a few months later, I said, 'Jason, I'm gonna go to lunch'" — which could address the colleague across the table, **or** refer to **Jason Flurry**, United's CFO, who is named twice later in the same transcript. Ambiguous; no reading is adopted.
- **An earlier-dated source now says it outright (back-filled Aug 27 2026).** In office talk recorded after the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-27-bank-of-america-treasury-needs-review.md|Aug-27 Bank of America call]], [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]] said that before his current United contact "it was Jason Hall, who is in now with helping Andre at income [InComm]. He… was the person. I had contact with." Val: "Jason Hall, was with United?" Felipe: "Yeah, he was… my contact for placing orders and everything." Val then phoned Jason and asked, "I also understand that you uh worked for United, am I correct?" (Jason's reply is not on the tape), and afterwards said "That changes a lot of things that Jason worked for, income… Worked, sorry, worked for United." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_BofA_Treasury_MS_Dynamics_Integration-August-27-2026.md]] So a source now states that InComm's Jason Hall is the former United contact. Reading "Andre" as [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] (who works with InComm) is an inference. No pages exist to merge; the identity is recorded here and queued for owner confirmation.

## Market position (from the Sept-2 lunch)

- **United is named as one of the larger operations, alongside others with very different strategies:** "you have [GAMOA], and then you have the larger operations: Bolag, Equus, **United**, Ultra… Operating in the same market, very different strategies and outlooks." *(Two of the names are garbles the source does not disambiguate; recorded as spoken.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]]
- **United has been on InComm's cards from the start.** Asked "is United on In[Comm] cards now?" — "**From the beginning.**" And on hardware: "Primarily… I think all of the operators are adding solutions and testing. So **United has a number of different kiosks and systems**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].
- **What United would never do, from the inside.** "In my experience at United, you always have a handful of games that I would say were at like **tier two**, not the top tier earners… And what I could never get us doing over there was **moving games like you would on a casino floor**, where you tinker with it all the time and try to optimize. I think a lot of these operators just want to **set it and forget it**." The illustration: two JVL machines together were "the top performing games in the whole area," the same pairing failed at another location, "and we would say… one yes, one no. It's not enough to make a decision." The wider argument this belongs to is on [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]] and [[wiki/topics/machine-data-repurchase-program.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].

He's going to say, well, you need to go talk to Ashish", and "Ashish is going to go talk to Bunchy's dad". Rich did not name United here; linking this to United rests on the wiki's Feb-18 2026 record of Bunty as United's owner and Ashish as its buyer (inference). The figure is his estimate ("I don't know… probably").

# Akshar Gaming

A **large amusement-machine route** — "**almost more than one… one fifty**" locations and "**close to a thousand machines**," with more locations being added. Val's reaction in the room: "One five zero. That's a pretty big company." *(Neither Sept-1 source names the state. That the route is a **Georgia COAM** operation is an **inference** from the account's Aug-26 history, the meeting's location at JVL's Georgia office, and the Georgia-specific systems discussed — MudaGoose, EasyLink, InComm gift cards, the GLC.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

**The name.** Asked "what's the name of the company?", [[wiki/people/neel.md|Neel]] answered "**now we changed to Akshar Gaming**," and [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]] spelled it out in the room — "**A K S H A R**" — which Val repeated back. It changed "**just last week**," i.e. the last week of August 2026, and the reason given was "**we just had a change of ownership** since it was for five years." *(Later in the same exchange the transcript renders the company as "For Oxia"; read as a garble of Akshar — an inference, recorded as an alias, not asserted.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

**The second company.** "we have one more with Vishal's name… It's **Ananta**… **Ananta amusement**," with "**four hundred**" machines. Asked "Vishal is the owner of both," the answer was "**Not Vishal.** For [Akshar], it's a different person, and that's Vishal" — garbled, and the derived notes read it as: Ananta is [[wiki/people/vishal-patel.md|Vishal Patel]]'s, Akshar has a different owner. **The owner of Akshar is never named.** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

## Who decides

- **Neel runs operations, Jai runs service and orders**, and the two of them make the technical and purchasing calls for **both** companies — "So you make purchasing decisions for both companies." "**Yes.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]
- **But not without Vishal.** "Most of the time, **Vishal will know what's going on**… Me and Jai will decide, and then we will," and on closing the trade-in: "**Since [Vishal] isn't here, we need to go back and explain everything to him.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]
- **Neel is not related to Vishal by blood** — "Not directly. **Same city**"; "Not that. Just came to know." Val's summary, unchallenged: "Cultural relationship." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]
- **Service calls are outsourced to Angie**, who used to work for them: "You might know **Angie. She used to work with us.** Since she started her own service call team… **She handles all the service calls**… It's an **outsourced job**." Angie is the same administrator [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]] described on Aug 26 as JVL's day-to-day contact on this account — see [[wiki/people/vishal-patel.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]
- **The location dictates, within limits.** "**it still, we decide if he gets the game or not.** But of course, **if a tier one or a tier two location comes and says, hey, I need this game, I will have to give them those.**" Val's own read of locations, offered to them: "The location never knows what they're… They scream and say I want, but in actuality they have no idea… **They always point out and ask for suggestions.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

## The card system they already run — the most detailed operator account in the corpus

Neel described their existing in-location card system from the inside. **The system is not reliably identified:** the transcript renders it "**access**" and "**the X's**", and the derived notes call it an "**Acres-style card system**." No name is asserted here. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

- **Price: about $5,000 one-time for six machines**, and it scales. "it's a **one time installation fee** that comes with the POS and all that… It comes around **five thousand something**… **six machines**… **It actually goes up as you add.**" What that buys: "**They install their current card machines, card readers, the POS, and the player cards.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]
- **Then $13.30 per machine per month.** Val guessed high — "I thought it was fourteen ninety five" — and was corrected: "**It's thirteen thirty.**" Val's reaction: "**You have a super deal there.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]
- **MudaGoose is removed completely and the vendor rewires the location**, for a mechanical reason, not a commercial one: "**They completely remove** [MudaGoose] **and they run their own wires**… because **if the MudaGoose is connected, then once the customer hits collect on it, the money goes to the card as well, and it prints a ticket at the goose too. So it confuses the location where to pay.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]
- **The player flow, walked step by step with Val.** A plastic player card is taken empty from the cashier, cash goes in at the machine with the card inserted, the balance follows the player from machine to machine, and at the end it is split at the counter — part to gift cards, part left on the card. **The balance persists between visits:** "It stays on your card. You take it back… **The next day you come back again, you still have fifty dollars.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]
- **It does not issue gift cards.** "**The X's does not issue gift card themselves. You still need to have** [InComm] **or some other form of gift card.**" Those gift cards are not reloadable — "once they give them 100 dollars, they use it. It's just throwing it" — and locations reorder: one took "around **fifty cards**" and after three months "**I am out of the card**, so that's why he ordered new." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

## EasyLink — the play-through limitation that annoys players

They run **two EasyLink locations and are removing one**: "you only have one location with EasyLink. **We just have two of them, and we are now removing EasyLink from one.**" The complaint is the redemption rule: a printed ticket must be played through enough hands before the next ticket can be redeemed. "if I want to, **I have no option to just play fifty dollars** because once I have… one hundred dollars of voucher, **I need to have enough hand counts to redeem the next ticket**." And from a player: "**I don't want to play a full five hundred.** A four[hundred] or smaller ticket is okay, but if I have a five hundred dollar ticket… I just want to get the dollars. I cannot." The card system does not have this problem — "**with access system, you can do that**… I have two hundred dollars on the card, and now I just want to have fifty dollars." Cost: "**EasyLink was very expensive** since we had a lot of older games. **We added a lot of printers**, so it cost almost a hundred more." *(The cost figure is garbled — "almost a hundred more" — and no total is asserted here.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

**Printers are a standing cost even when they come free.** "most of the games come with printers. **Aristocrat comes with a printer, Lightning comes with a printer**" — but "**you need to service it. We need to provide the paper and all of this**," and "because we have a lot of games… **we have to rent those printers**." Val's point, twice: "It comes with the printer, **but you pay for the printer**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

**Kiosks: they deliberately did not buy one.** "We don't have any other kiosks. They just have a single kiosk… **We didn't opt for a kiosk.**" *(What the "single kiosk" belongs to is not recoverable from the source. The same passage names two things the locations use — transcribed "PayPay" and "pay in Super" — neither of which is resolved here.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

## The InComm credit ceiling, raised as a live constraint

Neel raised location credit limits as the thing that pushes operators toward a second provider. The derived notes read it as locations getting only **$3,000–$6,000 per week** against locations that pay out **$50,000**, so "they need something with a higher payout." The transcript passage is garbled — "[InComm] gives them a bare minimum of three thousand or six hundred dollars per week. And some location payouts, like fifty thousand dollars just once a year" — and the figures are recorded here as the **notes'** reading, with the garble visible. See [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]] and [[wiki/organizations/incom.md]]. ## Open contradictions

**Is Akshar the same route as [[wiki/organizations/narayani-amusements.md|Narayani Amusements]]?** Two sources six days apart describe what may be one company and may be two. Recorded, not resolved; no page has been merged or renamed.

- **Aug 26 2026 (Narayani's own office):** Vishal Patel answers for a route of "**around nine hundred machines on field**" across "**about four hundred and ninety locations altogether**," and the derived notes name the company **Narayani Amusements**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Trade_In_Negotiation-August-26-2026.md]]
- **Sept 1 2026 (JVL's office):** Neel answers for a route of "**close to a thousand machines**" across "**one fifty**" locations, says the company was renamed **Akshar Gaming** last week after a change of ownership, and says its owner is **not Vishal** — Vishal's company is **Ananta Amusement**, with **400** machines. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]
- The machine counts are close (≈900 → ≈1,000); the **location counts are not** (490 vs 150). Neither source reconciles them, neither names Narayani and Akshar in the same breath, and the derived Sept-1 notes themselves flag the problem: "The vault account name stays 'Narayani Amusements' for continuity… but the company now trades as **Akshar Gaming**; worth deciding whether to rename the account." [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

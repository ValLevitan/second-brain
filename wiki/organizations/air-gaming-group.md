# Air Gaming Group

A **multi-state amusement-machine operator** JVL has sold to before, which came to the JVL Georgia office on **September 1 2026** for a walkthrough of the transactional range — see [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-01-air-gaming-group-transactional-walkthrough.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Air_Gaming_Group_Transactional_Walkthrough-September-1-2026.md]]

**Karim is the sole owner and the only decision maker.** Val asked in the first two minutes and got a clean answer: "Who makes the decision? **Karim.** … And Karim is the only owner." — "**He has partners in different companies, but he remains the decision.**" The man who came said so himself: "**I'm here for training and to see the console system.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Air_Gaming_Group_Transactional_Walkthrough-September-1-2026.md]]

**The JVL history.** Val opened with it — "**We were neighbours at one point. And we still have our machines.**" [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]] gave the fuller version on the earlier recording that afternoon, naming the account as the next appointment: "**Air Gaming**… The ones that I told you were neighbours at" — Val: "**North Cross. That's fantastic. They bought a lot of machines from us**" — Felipe: "**Especially Altera. They recently…**" *("North Cross" is a place name and is not resolved; the sentence about the recent purchase is cut off.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Air_Gaming_Group_Transactional_Walkthrough-September-1-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

## Texas — the footprint, and the reason JVL wants it

- **About 100 Texas locations.** Val: "How many locations do you have in Texas? **We're very interested in Texas.**" — "**A hundred locations.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Air_Gaming_Group_Transactional_Walkthrough-September-1-2026.md]]
- **Machines per location: the derived notes read ~25; the transcript does not settle it.** The exchange runs "What's the average? **Twenty-five machines per location**. We don't. We're doing [vend]ing over there. So I don't get **average five per location**." Both figures sit in the same garbled block. The notes assert ~25; recorded as the notes' reading, with the "five per location" clause visible rather than dropped. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Air_Gaming_Group_Transactional_Walkthrough-September-1-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Air_Gaming_Group_Transactional_Walkthrough-September-1-2026.md]]
- **They revenue-share in Texas rather than own outright.** Val: "What do you mean, [vend]ing?… **You're sharing revenue.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Air_Gaming_Group_Transactional_Walkthrough-September-1-2026.md]]
- **Their Texas kiosks are Big Daddy, and they are being hacked.** "the thing is that **we use Big Daddy kiosks over there, that's getting hacked** or whatever, a couple of [players] are getting the things." The fraud is ticket copying — the customer's own words: "We have a machine in Texas. So now over there, **if they're doing fraud, they're getting a ticket.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Air_Gaming_Group_Transactional_Walkthrough-September-1-2026.md]]
- **They also run Primero equipment, on revenue share rather than a monthly fee.** Asked whether Primero has a cloud server — "**No. We're not paying anything monthly.**" Val's explanation: "you don't have to pay monthly for the kiosk because **you pay shareholder shares for the machine. They collect profit participation.**" And on why Primero's kiosk refuses a copied ticket: "**Primero only validates their own.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Air_Gaming_Group_Transactional_Walkthrough-September-1-2026.md]]

**Why Texas is the opening, in Val's words:** "**For Texas, we have a wonderful solution. For Texas, dispensing is legal**… In Texas, they copy the ticket. So **in Texas, we are going to be installing our system very rapidly because that's a huge problem.**" He offered a written guarantee — "**I can actually give you a guarantee that people cannot copy tickets**… **I guarantee to you, no duplication. I can sign my name on** [it]" — and proposed a trial: "**I will do an experiment in Texas with you if you want.**" The architecture behind the guarantee is on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]] (Sept 1 2026); the market thread is on [[wiki/projects/texas-used-machine-distribution.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Air_Gaming_Group_Transactional_Walkthrough-September-1-2026.md]]

## What they came for, and what worried them

- **Kiosks, and a starting position of none.** "We try to do the [kiosk] thing"; Val: "The main point for you is [kiosks]." The visitor's cheapest-thing question — "**give me the cheapest solution. I just want to be compliant**" — got Val's answer that for bare Georgia compliance "**you don't need any of these units**," followed immediately by the argument for buying them anyway: a player who can move between machines keeps playing. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Air_Gaming_Group_Transactional_Walkthrough-September-1-2026.md]]
- **Whether the cards can be dispensed, and when.** "**The card dispenser is approved. We can do it in a matter of a week or two**," against "**ATM dispensing has not yet been approved**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Air_Gaming_Group_Transactional_Walkthrough-September-1-2026.md]]
- **Updates and warranty.** Updates are "**automatic**"; warranty is "**standard warranties… I think it's ninety days, but we can extend.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Air_Gaming_Group_Transactional_Walkthrough-September-1-2026.md]]
- **Data privacy — their sharpest objection.** "another concern is **the data being released, like the vendors having access to the location information**… if they have information about which location is doing well and which one is doing poorly." Val's answer was that the horse has bolted — "**We have this information. [InComm] has this information. GLC has this information**… **The moment your machines got hooked up to GLC, it's done**" — plus "**data in the cloud is encrypted. Nobody can use it. You have to worry about GLC, not about us.**" He made the point with a crude sexual analogy, and Andrei registered in the room that it had landed badly: "**He didn't like it in terms of security.**" The derived notes flag it for Val ahead of the office meeting. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Air_Gaming_Group_Transactional_Walkthrough-September-1-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Air_Gaming_Group_Transactional_Walkthrough-September-1-2026.md]]
- **Onboarding a location:** "**They spend one day filling in their information, and it takes twenty-four hours for approval. Then we are ready to go live**… Everything happens on the portal." Val added that JVL stays out of the master's location relationships by default — "**many customers don't want us to be involved with locations**… We communicate with you, and we have a technician. We can send the technician." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Air_Gaming_Group_Transactional_Walkthrough-September-1-2026.md]]

## What Val recommended, against his own product interest

Asked which configuration he would choose, Val named a **standalone self-service kiosk** that takes the money, issues the e-wallet and exchanges it for a card — "**this is the piece of equipment that can grow with you, and this does not require an attendant, and this stands next to the machine**" — while stressing "**it's not up to me.**" And he told them to wait on the ATM: the same advice he gave two other operators the same week. The ruling context is on [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-31-transactional-equipment-only-with-location-exclusivity.md]] and the catalogue on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Air_Gaming_Group_Transactional_Walkthrough-September-1-2026.md]]

## The follow-up

Val pushed for the real meeting: "**Let's make a deal. I'm here next week. Let's set up a meeting in your office.**" On timing: "**I'm back on Tuesday and leaving again on Friday. Let's set up something between Wednesday or Thursday**" — agreed, "**Wednesday, Thursday. That sounds good.**" The visitor asked "**When can I give you a call?**" and was told he was out that day.

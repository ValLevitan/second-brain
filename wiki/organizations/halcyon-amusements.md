# Halcyon Amusements

A **family route operator running in both Georgia and Texas**, run by [[wiki/people/yash-patel.md|Yash Patel]], which visited the JVL office on **August 27 2026** — see [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-27-halcyon-amusements-recycler-pitch.md]]. The company name comes from the calendar entry "Halcyon Amusements LLC - JVL" that [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] **manually confirmed as the match for the recording on 2026-09-05**; the transcript itself names neither the company nor the person. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Halcyon_Amusements_Recycler_Pitch_and_Lottery_Notice-August-27-2026.md]]

## The route

- **Texas: "I've got four hundred locations in Texas."** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Halcyon_Amusements_Recycler_Pitch_and_Lottery_Notice-August-27-2026.md]]
- **Georgia: locations too**, including one in **Lilburn** offered up as a Flex-IQ pilot site — "I've got a location in Lilburn." Its machine cap is nine ("Nine's the limit"). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Halcyon_Amusements_Recycler_Pitch_and_Lottery_Notice-August-27-2026.md]]
- **He buys games in Georgia at about $15,000** and contrasts that with Illinois, where "you buy a bank of games, and in La Moya, they're charging you about hundred thousand dollars a game… It's the same game I buy here for fifteen thousand dollars. There, it's ten times the price." *(The Illinois place name is garbled, and his own "ten times" does not square with $100,000 against $15,000 — both recorded as spoken, neither corrected.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Halcyon_Amusements_Recycler_Pitch_and_Lottery_Notice-August-27-2026.md]]

## Cash handling today — and why Val pitched the recycler at it

- **Texas cash management is PTI plus a kiosk.** "Yes, I use a **PTI**, and then a **PTI Sentry** system with the kiosk." The dispenser is automatic — "They get a ticket, they scan it, and it dispenses out" — but the cash behind it is not: "**We preload the ATM**," with "physical people" doing the refill. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Halcyon_Amusements_Recycler_Pitch_and_Lottery_Notice-August-27-2026.md]]
- **Val's attack on the pre-load model:** "You take the money from the machine. You go in the back room. You sort it… There's probably some shrinkage in between… You take the pre-sorted money. You now have to go and fit the money into each bin. And that's expensive. Each cassette." The operator conceded the shrinkage — "A little bit always happens" — and Val: "Money is sticky." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Halcyon_Amusements_Recycler_Pitch_and_Lottery_Notice-August-27-2026.md]]
- **The recycler answer:** the player's own buy-in cash funds the payouts, so "you never preloaded… you're not preloading or sorting… There's no shrinkage because the player is using their own cash," and staff touch only a lockable drop cassette — "even that comes lockable, so they could swap it instantly. A different cassette." Device thread on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Halcyon_Amusements_Recycler_Pitch_and_Lottery_Notice-August-27-2026.md]]
- **His response was to ask for pricing:** "So give me some numbers on this. It's a good idea. I've got four hundred locations in Texas." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Halcyon_Amusements_Recycler_Pitch_and_Lottery_Notice-August-27-2026.md]]

- In the room: Val — "So why don't you try that?" / "I'll give you a call. Can we line up a location with you?"; the operator — "Sure, no problem. Let's do a test program and see how it goes. … I have to go to Texas next week." Val's closing shape: "One machine in October, one in November." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Halcyon_Amusements_Recycler_Pitch_and_Lottery_Notice-August-27-2026.md]]

## A Flex-IQ pilot offer that was made but not closed

An unnamed JVL voice offered a **free three-month Flex-IQ pilot** running against **MudaGoose**: "we can start with standard I.Q. and annotation with [MudaGoose]. Then, in a matter of months, we can add sports and make this whole system work without even [MudaGoose]… are we ready to run this pilot release of I.Q. working in [MudaGoose] for three months as a pilot release? When I say pilot, I mean **no charge**. If you have a location somewhere nearby which perfectly fits this purpose, we can install one in a matter of a couple of weeks." The operator offered **Lilburn** ("Let's try it there. It's Lilburn, right here. That's perfect"). The transcript then moves on — **no install date and no confirmation are recorded**, and the derived notes do not carry this item at all. *(MudaGoose is rendered "Montegoos" and "Nootegoo" in the transcript.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Halcyon_Amusements_Recycler_Pitch_and_Lottery_Notice-August-27-2026.md]]

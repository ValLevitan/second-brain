# Renova (Georgia repair depot)

**A repair depot in Gwinnett County, Georgia, that fixes gaming and other electronic equipment.** [[wiki/people/christos-tzoumaras.md|Christos Tzoumaras]] of [[wiki/organizations/intralot.md|Intralot]] wants it as the repair partner in a group that would run routes for lotteries, next to Intralot and JVL [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Intralot_Christos_Tzoumaras_Dinner_Route_Partnership_and_Repair_Depot-October-13-2026.md]].

**Name note.** The name "Renova" is said only once, on the morning of Oct 13 2026: "**Have you met Louis with Renova?**" Val: "**I never heard the name.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Intralot_Machine_Lockouts_Test_Lab_and_Route_Partnership-October-13-2026.md]]. At the dinner that evening the company name is not said, and neither is Louis's surname [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Intralot_Christos_Tzoumaras_Dinner_Route_Partnership_and_Repair_Depot-October-13-2026.md]]. Reading the dinner's Louis as the morning's "Louis with Renova" is an **inference** from context: Christos said in the morning that the repair man "**is coming tonight**", and on the morning call he addressed someone as "**Luigi**" about the dinner time [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Intralot_Machine_Lockouts_Test_Lab_and_Route_Partnership-October-13-2026.md]]. Whether "Renova" is the exact spelling is **unclear**.

## The business (as Louis described it, Oct 13 2026)

All from the dinner [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Intralot_Christos_Tzoumaras_Dinner_Route_Partnership_and_Repair_Depot-October-13-2026.md]]; full meeting: [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-13-intralot-christos-tzoumaras-dinner-route-partnership-and-repair-depot.md]].

- **Depot only, no field service.** "**So we don't do any field service. The model is strictly like depot**". Inside the depot: "**The advanced exchange, so keeping some products on the shelf**", "**some light configuration**", "**some light inventory management**", and logistics ("**we do logistics, but it's all depot, no field right now**").
- **Gaming customers.** "**we've been working with Intralot, Scientific Games, Aristocrat**", and "**today we have Aristocrat and Light and Wonder and the big players as their North American repair depot here in Georgia**". It does not configure those makers' machines: "**We don't configure those machines.**"
- **Printers and bill acceptors.** "**in the gaming market, we repair all the printers**"; asked if they repair in house: "**Yes, JCM, MEI, Transact.**" Bill acceptors: "**we do a lot of those.**"
- **Price model.** A flat rate per unit; "**if we can't repair it**" there is no charge; "**Ninety days standard**" warranty; "**All new parts**". For a printer, "**anywhere between a hundred fifty bucks to three hundred bucks, depending on the the model**"; bill acceptors "**Same ballpark**".
- **Val's comparison.** A Montreal company "**charges one hundred twenty dollars Canadian**", which Val put at "**roughly eighty dollars**" US. Val: "**Expensive.**"
- **Size.** "**a forty thousand square foot facility here in Gwinnett. Sixty employees, of which maybe thirty are technicians.**" Louis said the company grew from twenty to sixty staff and that he has "**tripled the company**" since coming out of the pandemic; sales are "**coming up at eight**" (Val had asked "nine million, ten million") — read as about $8 million, **inference**.
- **Plans.** Space "**across the street, which would put us at sixty thousand square feet**", and "**a facility in Nevada for 2028**". They "**just bought a big ERP system**" — "**NetSuite**".
- **Most volume is now outside gaming.** "**mostly now it's been surveillance equipment. So cameras, DVRs, access control**" — "**probably like twenty thousand units a month from our facility on one shift**". "**Lately, it's been a lot of Israeli companies.**"
- **Where.** "**We're off Oak Grove Parkway, off Jimmy Carter.**"
- **Open to new states.** Asked about Wyoming and Nebraska: "**It's not opposed to it. If the business makes sense**".

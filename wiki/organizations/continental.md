# Continental (Salim Punjani)

A COAM operator run by [[wiki/people/salim-punjani.md|Salim Punjani]] as **two companies** — Continental, which came first, and a second one Val's question rendered as "northeastern" — with a Georgia estate and a **larger and faster-growing estate outside Georgia**, in **Texas primarily** plus **Virginia**. It is the clearest example in the corpus of an operator whose business is **bifurcated by regulation**: the two halves run different machines, different payout methods and different reporting, because Georgia asks for a report and Texas does not. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]]

## Scale — what was actually said

- **Locations across the two companies.** Asked "how many machines or locations together do you have?", Salim answered "**Together, between both of them, hundred fifty**." The next block, labelled to Val, reads "**Hundred ten locations. So it's closer to a thousand. A four-machine location.**" The derived notes take the figure as **~150 locations** and about **a thousand machines in Georgia**. Recorded with the divergence visible: the transcript carries both "hundred fifty" and "Hundred ten," and the machine count is **Val's arithmetic in the room** (locations × machines-per-location), not a figure Salim stated. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]]
- **Outside Georgia: about 1,500 machines.** "**About 1,500 machines outside**… of Georgia." — "**Texas primarily.**" *(Later in the same segment, asked how many machines he has in Texas, he answered "In Texas, I believe I've pushed **one eight hundred nine hundred**" — garbled and not resolvable to a number. Both are recorded; neither is reconciled.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]]
- **The out-of-state business is new.** "That's the Virginia and Texas part. **Pretty big. We just started scratching the surface**… because **we just started a year and a half ago**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Vendor_Lock_In_Dead_End-September-2-2026.md]]
- **And still expanding.** The one man running it "**is preparing… about another 20 machines in Texas. So he's going to prepare them next week.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Vendor_Lock_In_Dead_End-September-2-2026.md]]

## The two halves of the business

### Outside Georgia — no ticket system, manual payout, different machines

- **No ticket system at all.** Val asked how tickets are validated: "**We don't have ticket systems. It's still MudaGoose.**" The stack is MudaGoose "**connected to printer**" plus "**our own SAS system**" for reporting — "**We don't have any fancy system for** [Texas], **because we don't need them. For our own reporting, we are own.**" *(The transcript writes "staff reporting system" where the derived notes read "SAS reporting system"; both renderings appear in the same source.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]]
- **Payout is manual, at a cash register — not by dispenser.** Val: "how do you pay out? You have a dispenser." — "**This starts a cash register**"… "dispenser or manual? No manual." — "**Manual**… **Special cash register because there is no regulation that you can do.**" The cashier flow: "there's a cashier in the back. There's an amount… **Everyone is connected to one machine**… if they come in and say it was machine number six… **Then they look at the printout and say, 'I got your printout.'**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]]
- **Nobody asks about player movement between machines,** because there is no reporting: "**Not in those places, because over there is no reporting.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]]
- **The machines themselves differ by state.** "**The machines are a little different. They have in Texas. We have win your money back, and in Virginia we have a free. In Texas, we win your money back.** In a sense, you know, **if you put in a dollar and you play a bet, if you lose lose your bet, it gives you an option to win the dollar back.**" And Virginia: "**Previously, before you play your bet, you can see if you're going to win or not.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]]
- **Virginia runs the same transactional stack as Texas.** Val: "which transactional equipment do you have in Virginia? Same MudaGoose?" — "**Same MudaGoose.**" *(This answer carries the Speaker-3 label — a third voice appears in segment 1 as well, most plausibly Andrei, but the labels are demonstrably mis-split — so the attribution is flagged rather than asserted — see the speaker-label warning on [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-02-continental-bifurcated-operations-and-vendor-lock-in.md]].)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]]

### Georgia — Access, not TITO

- **Access covers about three-quarters of the estate, and has for seven or eight years.** "**In Georgia, we use** [Access]. **We don't use TITO. So seventy-five percent of our patients are** [Access]. **We started with that** [Access] **almost seven or eight years ago**" — when, in Val's words, "there was no in the market." *(The transcript renders the vendor "Axis" and "access"; the derived notes write "**Axcess**". The corpus has carried this Georgia card competitor as **Access** since 2025 — see [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]] — so "Access" is used here, with both other renderings kept as evidence. "our patients" is transcribed as written; the derived notes read the same phrase as his **estate**. No substitute word is asserted here.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]]
- **He is moving every non-Access location to Novomatic, and Novomatic is free to him.** "**all my locations that they don't have access to, I push them to Novomatics.**" The reason he is not ripping Access out is insurance, not dissatisfaction: asked why he wants a replacement, "**In town, it's exactly the same thing. Novomatic has much better user interface.**" And the fallback is explicit: "**if that goes bad, we'll just go back to access.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Vendor_Lock_In_Dead_End-September-2-2026.md]]
- **In Georgia he buys machines only from Aristocrat** — the numbers and the finance terms are on [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]] (Sept 2) and [[wiki/people/salim-punjani.md]].
- **Georgia purchasing is closed for now.** "**Now we're not buying anymore.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]]

## The JVL relationship

- **The route in is Felipe, on a fortnightly cadence.** "**Filipe is always in touch with me every 15 days. He's like, Hey, Salim, do you need something? Can I get you? Great guy. He's the one… who got JVL back in the market.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Vendor_Lock_In_Dead_End-September-2-2026.md]]
- **Two JVL verticals came back from his locations shortly before the visit**, to be replaced with Aristocrat machines — "**They're just two, but they came back because they want to say what they want.**" See the open question below. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Vendor_Lock_In_Dead_End-September-2-2026.md]]
- **What JVL was quoted, and why.** Val priced the transactional suite at **$1,000 per machine** plus **$1,000 for the POS** plus **$90 per month per location**, and then said plainly why the number was high: "**Access gave you a wholesale price. I'm giving you a retail price. I'm giving you retail because you're not my customer.** But of course, when push comes to shove, and you would like to buy, we can sit down and talk." Full pricing comparison on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]] (Sept 2, Continental). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Vendor_Lock_In_Dead_End-September-2-2026.md]]
- **The live opportunity is refurbished machines for Texas**, not Georgia — see [[wiki/projects/texas-used-machine-distribution.md]] (Sept 2).

## Open contradictions

**How big Continental is.** Two dated characterisations three weeks apart, not reconciled by any source. Recorded, not resolved.
- **Aug 11 2026 — Felipe, planning the trial wave:** "**Continental, it's a small operator, but yes.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GA_COAM_Pipeline_Trial_Forecast-August-11-2026.md]]
- **Sept 2 2026 — Salim, in his own facility:** about **150 locations** across the two companies and about **1,500 machines outside Georgia**, Texas primarily, plus Virginia. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Continental_Bifurcated_Operations_Texas_and_Virginia-September-2-2026.md]]
- Felipe's judgement is second-hand and qualitative and may be about the *Georgia* estate only, which is the estate he sells into; Salim's figures are first-hand and cover both companies and all states. Neither source addresses the other, so no side is picked here.

# GAMOA — the Georgia coin-operated amusement machine operators' association

The trade association of Georgia's COAM master licensees — the non-profit that runs the state's annual trade show, carries the industry's political and legal representation, and is the body JVL keeps trying to get closer to. JVL exhibits at its show — "you stopped our booth, right?" — and has done for several years. Two of the operators JVL met in the last week of August and the first of September 2026 hold seats on it: [[wiki/people/salim-khambawala.md|Salim Khambawala]] of [[wiki/organizations/dixie-amusement.md|Dixie Amusement]] as vice president, and [[wiki/people/ryan-odonnell.md|Ryan O'Donnell]] of [[wiki/organizations/double-down-gaming.md|Double Down Gaming]] as a board member. Salim's description of it: "**GAMOA is not for profit. We wear [the] GAMOA head because we do this for our passion and the industry we live. Nobody pays us.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]

**Naming (owner correction).** The canonical spelling is **GAMOA**. Transcripts render it *GOAM, GMUA, Gamua, Gamboa, Gomoa* and *Gomorrah*; those are garbles and are recorded as aliases only. The wiki writes GAMOA in its own voice. Note that **neither September-1 2026 transcript speaks the name at all** — the body is referred to throughout only as "the association," "the state association" or "the board"; it is the derived notes that supply the name, writing "Association (GAMOA) intel." So the identification of the body Ryan describes as GAMOA rests on the derived notes plus role context, not on the transcript. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]

## Origins — the gift-card lobbying, about fifteen years back

The only account in the corpus of where the association came from, given on **September 2 2026** by [[wiki/people/billy-liu.md|Billy Liu]] of [[wiki/organizations/play-payments.md|Play Payments]] — an outsider to the operator trade who says he helped fund it ([[wiki/meetings/2026-09-02-billy-liu-playpay-partnership-deep-dive.md]]): [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]

> "In the early days with **this lobbying of gift cards**, I was part of it. **I donated money to get GAMOA going, and I even did some lobbying on my own. That dates back 15 years.** Georgia became what it became."

- **It dates the association's founding push to the gift-card question**, not to the machines — which is consistent with the legislative-intent account [[wiki/people/ryan-odonnell.md|Ryan O'Donnell]] gave on Sept 1 (gift cards exist so locations do not pay cash; see [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]]). **Neither source cites the other and no founding date is asserted here** — "about fifteen years" is the speaker's own rounding.
- **The source also fixes a recent meeting by it:** a JVL colleague, dating Billy's banking licence, refers to "**the Davis GAMOA meeting about a year ago**." *("Davis" is transcribed as spoken; no meeting or person of that name appears anywhere else in the corpus and nothing is inferred from it.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]
- **GAMOA is spelled correctly in this source**, unusually — see the naming note above.

## How it is funded and staffed

From [[wiki/people/ryan-odonnell.md|Ryan O'Donnell]], a sitting board member, at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-01-double-down-gaming-visit.md|Sept 1 2026 Double Down Gaming visit]]:

- **The budget is split evenly between membership dues and the show.** "For the association, like annual budget… It's a **fifty-fifty between dues and the show**. The show's a good revenue stream, and then dues are [the rest]." Asked whether anything else comes in — "the association lives off of that budget. There's no other money coming in" — the answer did not name another source. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **The work is volunteer except one paid administrator.** "**Nobody gets paid. Nobody gets paid.**" The single exception is the administrative role — "The admin… She's a salary employee" — confirmed as "the only one that gets paid. Which is part of your budget." The derived notes identify the association's administrator by first name, **Christina**; she is named separately in the transcript as the person who would send Val the board list, not in the pay exchange. **No figure is stated anywhere and none is recorded here (§15.8).** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **Membership is about 190.** From [[wiki/people/salim-khambawala.md|Salim Khambawala]] the day before: "**Total one hundred and ninety.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]

Twelve" — the derived notes take **twelve**, and the spread is left visible here rather than averaged. The board **is elected**, and "**They're three-year terms.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]

He's not into the machine or the [coin] business.**" *("corn" transcribed as written; read as *coin*, an inference.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **How a manufacturer representative gets elected is not known even to the board.** "**I'm not 100 sure. It's a great question.** He was on the board when I got there. He's been there for a long time." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]

Val approved of the candidate — "He's a very eloquent young man… comes across very gentlemanlike. **I think it's a great choice**" — but **the candidate is never named in the source**; the surrounding sentence ("He's taking BJ to Mars. ## The political and legal apparatus

Val worked through this systematically — "**You gotta be working with politicians. All day. So who's your political arm here?**" The answers, in order: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]

- **Political champion: Representative Alan Powell**, described as "**the chair of the oversight committee**." The association's own framing is plural — "We have a couple of champions" — but only Powell is named.
- **Lobbyist: Joe Butler, of Butler Avenue.** Asked "Who is the lobbyist from?" — "**Butler Avenue, Joe Butler.**"
- **Two law firms.** The first is "**Les Snyder**," an Atlanta/Georgia firm that "**has been representing the association probably from inception**." The second is rendered "**Chris and Milowitz**," also a speaker at association meetings and the expo. *(Both are recorded as transcribed; neither firm name is corroborated elsewhere in the corpus and no spelling is corrected.)*
- **The association does not donate to campaigns; members pool per event.** "**As an association, we don't typically donate to campaigns.** We just go around and say… 'This guy's got an event. **We need twenty grand. We bring four, and this guy brings one, and this guy brings nine.** We're going to go to this meeting.'" Val's read on the cost of all of it: "**supporting all of this is very expensive. We're talking about multiple millions**" — the reply, "We[,] our budget," breaks off and never confirms or denies the figure.
- **Local political involvement is the board's stated top ask of the membership.** "**That's one of the biggest things we need from the group: to be politically involved locally.**"

## The annual show

The association's show is half its income and, in 2026, the source of its main operational problem.

- **Record attendance: 900, against an expectation of 600–700.** "So **900. We were expecting six to seven hundred**" — "**It's the largest ever**" — and it has grown "**like two or three years in a row. So it just keeps coming up.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Venue_Selection-September-1-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Double_Down_Gaming_Venue_Selection-September-1-2026.md]]
- **The problem is location, not space.** "**It's not really the larger space. It's the convenient location.**" The current venue suits in-state members — "The location is super convenient… For people that live in state, it's fine too" — but "**we've gotten a little bit of pushback**… we have **a lot of exhibitors now from out of town**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Venue_Selection-September-1-2026.md]]
- **What the current venue does right is the hotel.** "**The hotel and having the center there all together really fits our needs**"; "**That hotel element is great.**" The options floated were vague — a venue near the airport, or something "more centrally located for travel," ideally still with an attached hotel. Asked "Is there a venue by the airport?" the answer was "**There's venues in the venue everywhere. I don't think we're quite at the downtown**" — transcribed as spoken and not interpretable further. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Venue_Selection-September-1-2026.md]]
- **No decision in that conversation.** "The problem is, if we double attendance in the next few years, it will work. But we'll see. **It's just a thought. We just keep it around.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Venue_Selection-September-1-2026.md]]
- **Charity and the auction.** "**Charity was a big thing for us. We got to spread this around.** We were talking about even splitting and having a couple… **it's nice to change it each year.**" On the auction, the association "got a lot of feedback"; the argument made in the room was to auction higher-priced items donated at cost — "**if you donate this, it's a ten thousand dollar machine. Maybe cost you guys five grand, and then somebody goes buy [it for] twelve. That's a good deal. You're giving five. The person's giving twelve.**" A separate complaint was about floor pressure to solicit donations — "as soon as that shit starts, I'm out, because you feel awkward." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **JVL's exhibitor verdict on the 2026 show was the best it has given.** From the JVL side of the table: "**I think this was the best show that you put out so far**… I think it was **the easiest, the most organized**… For us, it was **extremely smooth. There was no hassle. Very attentive.** For us, and this is our six… year… for us, this was the easiest. **So you're in the right track somewhere.**" *(The exhibitor's count of years is garbled — "six, six, six, six year. Maybe ten, ten. Whatever it is" — and no year count is asserted here.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]

On Sept 1 he went at it a different way — he asked Ryan directly for the **list of board members**, and then asked for it to be sent to him **personally**: "Can you ask her to send it to me personally?" ## Sept 8 2026 — the association's posture on transactional equipment, and the tax history behind it

From [[wiki/people/salim-khambawala.md|Salim Khambawala]], the sitting vice president, at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-08-dixie-amusement-bundle-offer-and-card-economics.md|Sept-8 Dixie visit]] — the clearest statement in the corpus of **why the association wants JVL's product category to arrive slowly**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]

- **Glad it is happening; wants it slow-walked and timed.** Asked "What's the association's take on the ATMs and the transactional equipment?" — "**[The association] is happy it's happening, but I think we have to slow walk and time it**… some of the politicians or somebody getting mad and **putting a piece of legislation out in the next election and putting more taxes on our industry or putting restrictions is not a good idea**… But I think it's an excellent way to do things the right way than to do it the wrong way."
- **The reason is the calendar, not the product.** "**July first twenty-six gift card became legally allowed to be in the market**; before it was pilot. **You cannot go to a legislator in August and say, 'Hey, by the way, the gift card… could go into an ATM and get cash.'**" His read of the legislature: "**Some don't care. A couple of politicians or legislators said, 'You know, [COAMs] are not the only thing we talk about at the capital.' And some are involved, and some are naive. But all of them do matter.**"
- **The tax history is the argument.** "**it started at five. Then six. Then seven. Then eight. Then nine. Then ten. We kept it. Then 2024, when we wanted this gift card, they jumped up to 30. Now you go and ask for something else; they'll jump to 20.**" And the current rate: "**And now the tax is thirty percent. Now it's thirteen percent.**" *(The sequence is spoken quickly and the 30 → 13 → "they'll jump to 20" ordering is not internally tidy; recorded as spoken. The derived notes read it as: 5 % climbing to 10 %, a jump to **30 %** in 2024 when the industry asked for the gift card, now **13 %**.)* Val's own position on it: "**The taxes will go up. We know that. There's nothing we can do about it.**" Salim's: "**We understand it's going to go up, but how fast do you want that to go up?… So higher the tax, lesser money.**" [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Dixie_Amusement_Georgia_Transactional_Equipment_and_Bundle_Economics-September-8-2026.md]]
- **Two structural rules he cited in the same breath**, both of which bound what any vendor can sell into the state: **Georgia regulates three tiers only** — "**In Georgia, the regulation is only on three sectors. One is a distributor, JVL. One is a master, and one is a location**" — and **a location may not take more than half its gross from COAM**: "**in the law it says you cannot [derive] more than fifty percent of your gross revenue of the store from these [COAM]**… **This has to be a part of your business, but not your whole business. You cannot open a game room and put 50 games in there.**" *(The transcript renders "derive" as "deprive" and COAM as "coins"; the notes flag both.)*
- **On Class A he would not speak for the association at all** — "**Association is not like that**" — and gave a personal forecast instead. See [[wiki/topics/georgia-class-a-machines.md]] (Sept 8 2026). This matches the Sept-1 pattern above, where a board member answered on Class A with "It's my view" and "We don't have a specific thing."

## Sept 9 2026 — Val names a collective operator boycott as the one outcome he would accept

Discussing a **September-2 letter** worrying that players now reach cash too quickly (full passage on [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]]), [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] was asked at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-09-rao-machine-performance-and-exclusivity-offer.md|Sept-9 Rao meeting]] whether JVL should change how it enters the market. He said no — and then named the one thing that could actually stop him, which is an association decision rather than a regulatory one [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]]:

> "what could happen is that **operators, all of the operators, could get together, and the operators could say, we collectively decide not to promote the system in our locations, and we collectively, as the operators of [the] Coin Operat[ed] Machines Association, we are not going to buy it**… **But I'm fine with that. I understand. What does it have to do with the technology?**"

- **The body is not named GAMOA in the source.** Val says "Coin Coin Operators Machines Association," which is a description of the Georgia COAM operators' association rather than its name; **identifying it as GAMOA is an inference** from role and market, consistent with the naming note above (the transcripts render the name many ways and sometimes not at all).
- **What it adds:** every other source on this page treats the association as a channel to be courted. This is the first to treat it as a **veto** — and Val's stated position is that he would accept the veto without changing the product, because "it has nothing to do with the technology." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Rao_Machine_Performance_and_Exclusivity_Offer-September-9-2026.md]]

## Sept 17 2026 — a sitting board member's criticism of his own association, and his 70/30 count on SkillPay

[[wiki/people/yash-patel.md|Yash Patel]] of [[wiki/organizations/halcyon-amusements.md|Halcyon Amusements]], over breakfast with Val ([[wiki/meetings/2026-09-17-halcyon-amusements-georgia-class-a-and-texas-route-partnership.md|Sept-17, 8:49–10:19 AM ET]]). **He is a board member, and he says so before criticising** — "**I'm a member of [GAMOA]. I'm on the board, right? So this is nothing against them. It's not vindication.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Halcyon_Amusements_Georgia_Class_A_and_Texas_Route_Partnership-September-17-2026.md]]

- **His charge: compulsory membership, enforced socially.** The association "**has made it to where they want to force every other retailer and master licensee and operator, manufacturer, to be a member… And it's like if you're not a member, if you're not part of this club, then we don't like you. You're ostracized.**" *(This is the demand-side view of the "every part of the trade in one body" structure recorded above.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Halcyon_Amusements_Georgia_Class_A_and_Texas_Route_Partnership-September-17-2026.md]]
- **His count on the SkillPay split: about 70/30 in favour, not 50/50.** Val put the market as "split more or less in the middle"; Yash: "**I'd say seventy thirty, honestly**", reasoning from having sat on both the retail and the operator side. His evidence is specific and is about **non-members**: "**All the other operators that I know, I'm friends with, that are not [GAMOA] members, they all want skill pay. All the retailers I know, they all want skill pay. There's nobody out there who says no.**" And within the association itself: "**the [GAMOA] members — half of them, probably majority, wanted the skill pay**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Halcyon_Amusements_Georgia_Class_A_and_Texas_Route_Partnership-September-17-2026.md]]
- **His governing rule, and Val's agreement:** "**the minority should just get absorbed into the majority… If it's good for ninety percent of the people, the ten percent needs to deal with that and move in one direction. Because indecision is the worst decision.**" Val's own version is about **splitting**, not direction: "**The split of the market is more dangerous than everybody going in one direction. I almost prefer everybody going in whatever direction they pick. Just pick a… direction and go in that direction.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Halcyon_Amusements_Georgia_Class_A_and_Texas_Route_Partnership-September-17-2026.md]]
- **On lobbying, he tells his own masters to stop.** "some of these guys are like, oh, we're going to lobby to the state to get this law changed. I'm like… **save your fucking money.** Because now you're lobbying for one thing, then you get another master… [who] wants it done a different way. That's more beneficial to him than the industry… **Now you're bringing more attention to this wound. It's festering now. Just leave it alone. That's my strategy: do nothing.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Halcyon_Amusements_Georgia_Class_A_and_Texas_Route_Partnership-September-17-2026.md]]
- **On Class A, he accepts "one hundred percent against" as the association's line** and gives its reason (Class A cutting Class B revenue, and therefore state scholarships), while disagreeing with it himself. Full passage on [[wiki/topics/georgia-class-a-machines.md]] (Sept 17 2026). **This does not settle whether GAMOA holds a formal position** — the Sept-1 board member said "we don't have a specific thing" — and both stand. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Halcyon_Amusements_Georgia_Class_A_and_Texas_Route_Partnership-September-17-2026.md]]

*(The recording renders GAMOA as "**Demola**" throughout, with "**Gamboa**", "**Gamow**" and "**Gamal**" as further variants in the same passage; the derived notes read all of them as GAMOA and flag "Demola" as a probable new glossary entry. The identification rests on context — "I'm a member of Demola. I'm on the board" from a Georgia COAM operator — and is consistent with the many renderings this page already carries.)* [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Halcyon_Amusements_Georgia_Class_A_and_Texas_Route_Partnership-September-17-2026.md]]

**§15.8:** clear — association budget structure, membership and board composition, show economics and political-spending mechanics only. The one pay-related statement in the source (that a single administrator is salaried while the board volunteers) carries **no figure**, and none is recorded.


## History — May 18 2026: the president declines to endorse InComm's ATM-card pilot (as relayed)

On the [[wiki/meetings/2026-05-18-ga-trip-recap-kiosk-intel.md|May-18 GA trip recap]] [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]] reported that, after [[wiki/people/john-houseal.md|John Houseal]] presented InComm's gift-card-to-ATM pilot at the meeting they attended, "Mike Patel then took the stage and they say, 'Listen, we are not endorsing this product.'"

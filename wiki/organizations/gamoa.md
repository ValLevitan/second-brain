# GAMOA — the Georgia coin-operated amusement machine operators' association

The trade association of Georgia's COAM master licensees — the non-profit that runs the state's annual trade show, carries the industry's political and legal representation, and is the body JVL keeps trying to get closer to. JVL exhibits at its show — "you stopped our booth, right?" — and has done for several years. Two of the operators JVL met in the last week of August and the first of September 2026 hold seats on it: [[wiki/people/salim-khambawala.md|Salim Khambawala]] of [[wiki/organizations/dixie-amusement.md|Dixie Amusement]] as vice president, and [[wiki/people/ryan-odonnell.md|Ryan O'Donnell]] of [[wiki/organizations/double-down-gaming.md|Double Down Gaming]] as a board member. Salim's description of it: "**GAMOA is not for profit. We wear [the] GAMOA head because we do this for our passion and the industry we live. Nobody pays us.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]

**Naming (owner correction).** The canonical spelling is **GAMOA**. Transcripts render it *GOAM, GMUA, Gamua, Gamboa, Gomoa* and *Gomorrah*; those are garbles and are recorded as aliases only. The wiki writes GAMOA in its own voice. Note that **neither September-1 2026 transcript speaks the name at all** — the body is referred to throughout only as "the association," "the state association" or "the board"; it is the derived notes that supply the name, writing "Association (GAMOA) intel." So the identification of the body Ryan describes as GAMOA rests on the derived notes plus role context, not on the transcript. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]

## Origins — the gift-card lobbying, about fifteen years back

The only account in the corpus of where the association came from, given on **September 2 2026** by [[wiki/people/billy-liu.md|Billy Liu]] of [[wiki/organizations/play-payments.md|Play Payments]] — an outsider to the operator trade who says he helped fund it ([[wiki/meetings/2026-09-02-billy-liu-playpay-partnership-deep-dive.md]]): [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]

> "In the early days with **this lobbying of gift cards**, I was part of it. **I donated money to get GAMOA going, and I even did some lobbying on my own. That dates back 15 years.** Georgia became what it became."

- **It dates the association's founding push to the gift-card question**, not to the machines — which is consistent with the legislative-intent account [[wiki/people/ryan-odonnell.md|Ryan O'Donnell]] gave on Sept 1 (gift cards exist so locations do not pay cash; see [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]]). **Neither source cites the other and no founding date is asserted here** — "about fifteen years" is the speaker's own rounding.
- **The source also fixes a recent meeting by it:** a JVL colleague, dating Billy's banking licence, refers to "**the Davis GAMOA meeting about a year ago**." *("Davis" is transcribed as spoken; no meeting or person of that name appears anywhere else in the corpus and nothing is inferred from it.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]
- **GAMOA is spelled correctly in this source**, unusually — see the naming note above.

## How it is funded and staffed

From [[wiki/people/ryan-odonnell.md|Ryan O'Donnell]], a sitting board member, at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-01-double-down-gaming-visit.md|Sept 1 2026 Double Down Gaming visit]]:

- **The budget is split evenly between membership dues and the show.** "For the association, like annual budget… It's a **fifty-fifty between dues and the show**. The show's a good revenue stream, and then dues are [the rest]." Asked whether anything else comes in — "the association lives off of that budget. There's no other money coming in" — the answer did not name another source. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **The work is volunteer except one paid administrator.** "**Nobody gets paid. Nobody gets paid.**" The single exception is the administrative role — "The admin… She's a salary employee" — confirmed as "the only one that gets paid. Which is part of your budget." The derived notes identify the association's administrator by first name, **Christina**; she is named separately in the transcript as the person who would send Val the board list, not in the pay exchange. **No figure is stated anywhere and none is recorded here (§15.8).** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **Membership is about 190.** From [[wiki/people/salim-khambawala.md|Salim Khambawala]] the day before: "**Total one hundred and ninety.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]

## The board

- **Twelve elected members on three-year terms.** Asked "How big is the board now?" the answer in the room ran "Fifteen men. Twelve" — the derived notes take **twelve**, and the spread is left visible here rather than averaged. The board **is elected**, and "**They're three-year terms.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **One seat is a manufacturer representative — currently Robert Berry.** Asked "who is the manufacturer that is on the board right now?" — "**Robert Berry.**" A JVL voice supplied what he does: "**He sells parts for this region. He's not into the machine or the [coin] business.**" *("corn" transcribed as written; read as *coin*, an inference.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **How a manufacturer representative gets elected is not known even to the board.** "**I'm not 100 sure. It's a great question.** He was on the board when I got there. He's been there for a long time." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **A new manufacturer-representative nomination was being voted on that week.** "He was **nominated, and we're voting on it now**. We had a **meeting last week** about it." Val approved of the candidate — "He's a very eloquent young man… comes across very gentlemanlike. **I think it's a great choice**" — but **the candidate is never named in the source**; the surrounding sentence ("He's taking BJ to Mars. Rotation.") is garbled beyond recovery and no name is inferred from it. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **Seats are unwanted, and vacancies get filled by whoever will take them.** "**A lot of people don't want to do this**"; "somebody can volunteer, but typically what happens is…" — the mechanism trails off, and a fourth voice adds that a replacement serves "the remaining amount of time." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **Officers, from the Aug-31 Dixie visit:** Salim Khambawala is **vice president**; the sitting president is **Mike Patel**, then in his second year. See [[wiki/people/salim-khambawala.md]] for the full officer picture, including the unreconciled question of whether Salim still holds the office. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]

## The political and legal apparatus

Val worked through this systematically — "**You gotta be working with politicians. All day. So who's your political arm here?**" The answers, in order: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]

- **Political champion: Representative Alan Powell**, described as "**the chair of the oversight committee**." The association's own framing is plural — "We have a couple of champions" — but only Powell is named.
- **Lobbyist: Joe Butler, of Butler Avenue.** Asked "Who is the lobbyist from?" — "**Butler Avenue, Joe Butler.**"
- **Two law firms.** The first is "**Les Snyder**," an Atlanta/Georgia firm that "**has been representing the association probably from inception**." The second is rendered "**Chris and Milowitz**," also a speaker at association meetings and the expo. *(Both are recorded as transcribed; neither firm name is corroborated elsewhere in the corpus and no spelling is corrected.)*
- **The association does not donate to campaigns; members pool per event.** "**As an association, we don't typically donate to campaigns.** We just go around and say… 'This guy's got an event. **We need twenty grand. We bring four, and this guy brings one, and this guy brings nine.** We're going to go to this meeting.'" Val's read on the cost of all of it: "**supporting all of this is very expensive. We're talking about multiple millions**" — the reply, "We[,] our budget," breaks off and never confirms or denies the figure.
- **Local political involvement is the board's stated top ask of the membership.** "**That's one of the biggest things we need from the group: to be politically involved locally.**"

## The annual show

The association's show is half its income and, in 2026, the source of its main operational problem.

- **Record attendance: 900, against an expectation of 600–700.** "So **900. We were expecting six to seven hundred**" — "**It's the largest ever**" — and it has grown "**like two or three years in a row. So it just keeps coming up.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Venue_Selection-September-1-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Double_Down_Gaming_Venue_Selection-September-1-2026.md]]
- **The problem is location, not space.** "**It's not really the larger space. It's the convenient location.**" The current venue suits in-state members — "The location is super convenient… For people that live in state, it's fine too" — but "**we've gotten a little bit of pushback**… we have **a lot of exhibitors now from out of town**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Venue_Selection-September-1-2026.md]]
- **What the current venue does right is the hotel.** "**The hotel and having the center there all together really fits our needs**"; "**That hotel element is great.**" The options floated were vague — a venue near the airport, or something "more centrally located for travel," ideally still with an attached hotel. Asked "Is there a venue by the airport?" the answer was "**There's venues in the venue everywhere. I don't think we're quite at the downtown**" — transcribed as spoken and not interpretable further. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Venue_Selection-September-1-2026.md]]
- **No decision in that conversation.** "The problem is, if we double attendance in the next few years, it will work. But we'll see. **It's just a thought. We just keep it around.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Venue_Selection-September-1-2026.md]]
- **Charity and the auction.** "**Charity was a big thing for us. We got to spread this around.** We were talking about even splitting and having a couple… **it's nice to change it each year.**" On the auction, the association "got a lot of feedback"; the argument made in the room was to auction higher-priced items donated at cost — "**if you donate this, it's a ten thousand dollar machine. Maybe cost you guys five grand, and then somebody goes buy [it for] twelve. That's a good deal. You're giving five. The person's giving twelve.**" A separate complaint was about floor pressure to solicit donations — "as soon as that shit starts, I'm out, because you feel awkward." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **JVL's exhibitor verdict on the 2026 show was the best it has given.** From the JVL side of the table: "**I think this was the best show that you put out so far**… I think it was **the easiest, the most organized**… For us, it was **extremely smooth. There was no hassle. Very attentive.** For us, and this is our six… year… for us, this was the easiest. **So you're in the right track somewhere.**" *(The exhibitor's count of years is garbled — "six, six, six, six year. Maybe ten, ten. Whatever it is" — and no year count is asserted here.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]

## Why JVL cares

- **Access to the association is JVL's standing Georgia problem.** Val asked Salim on Aug 31 how to get in front of his 30–40-strong buying group inside the ~190-member association, and concluded the only route that works is a personal ask from a member; the detail is on [[wiki/people/salim-khambawala.md]] and [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]]. On Sept 1 he went at it a different way — he asked Ryan directly for the **list of board members**, and then asked for it to be sent to him **personally**: "Can you ask her to send it to me personally?" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Operations_Review-September-1-2026.md]]
- **The board is also where Class A gets decided politically.** Ryan's account of the board's posture — "We're not in favor of them" — and his own reasoning are on [[wiki/topics/georgia-class-a-machines.md]]. The Class A threat first reached JVL through a GAMOA board meeting in February 2026 ([[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]]).

## Open contradictions

**Is the show's venue changing, or is the question still open? (Aug 31 vs Sept 1 2026.)** Two association board members, one day apart, describe it differently. Recorded, not resolved.

- **Aug 31 — Salim Khambawala (vice president):** the venue is changing and getting bigger, "We are also going to change venues… **Too big. So now, Georgia convention**," with next year's planning starting immediately. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dixie_Amusement_Kiosk_Wait_and_See_and_Market_Economics-August-31-2026.md]]
- **Sept 1 — the Double Down conversation:** no decision, the current venue "would work for the next year or two," and the alternative is "**just a thought. We just keep it around.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Double_Down_Gaming_Venue_Selection-September-1-2026.md]]
- Neither source mentions the other, and neither is a written association record.

*(The two commitments arising here — sending Val the board-member summary and the show/association information — are owed by [[wiki/people/ryan-odonnell.md|Ryan O'Donnell]] and are held once on his page, deliberately not duplicated or re-counted on this one.)*

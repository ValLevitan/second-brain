# MG Oil

The **largest video-lottery-terminal (VLT) route operator in South Dakota**, owned by [[wiki/people/troy-erikson.md|Troy Erikson]], and JVL's target anchor customer for the [[wiki/projects/south-dakota-vlt-market.md|South Dakota VLT market entry]]. JVL decided to pursue the MG Oil opportunity on the **Jan 7 2025** call ([[wiki/decisions/2025-01-07-pursue-mg-oil-south-dakota.md]]) pending an introduction from a contact named "Maurice"; that introduction happened on **May 13 2025** when Troy Erikson met [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]]. [[raw/jira_value_stream_distribution_infrastructure_january_7_2025.md]] [[raw/mg_oil_south_dakota_intro-may-13-2025.md]]

("NG Oil" appears in the May-13 transcript as a mis-hearing of the name; "MG Oil" is the form used from Jan 7 onward.)

## What the company is (May 13)

- **Started as an oil company in the 1970s** and grew into a diversified operator: oil distribution, fuel semis, about **35 convenience stores**, **five or six truck stops**, roughly **100 casinos**, and the **VLT route business**. [[raw/mg_oil_south_dakota_intro-may-13-2025.md]]
- Runs **just under 7,000 machines out of the ~9,000** in the South Dakota market — "you're virtually the only player." The next-largest operator has ~1,000; the rest are small (30–100 machines each). MG Oil is **two or three times the size of everyone else**. [[raw/mg_oil_south_dakota_intro-may-13-2025.md]]
- About **600 route locations** (bars, bowling alleys, restaurants) in addition to company-owned locations; a location typically has an establishment licence for **10 machines**. [[raw/mg_oil_south_dakota_intro-may-13-2025.md]]
- **South Dakota Lottery operator number 378** — the licence number the state uses to identify the operator. [[raw/mg_oil_south_dakota_intro-may-13-2025.md]] The **May-2025 SD Lottery operators list** confirms #378 as "**M.G. Oil Company, 1180 Creek Dr., Rapid City, SD 57703**, 605-342-0527, contact **Erickson, Troy**, established 1992-03-23" (surname spelled "Erickson" there). See [[wiki/projects/south-dakota-vlt-market.md]]. [[raw/sd_operators_list-may-2025.md]]
- Fleet composition: roughly **1,900 old VLCs (~20% of the route)**, ~**150 Grover** machines, and the rest split among **AGT / Grand Vision** and **IGT**. (South Dakota market intel — competitors, replacement rate — is on [[wiki/projects/south-dakota-vlt-market.md]].) [[raw/mg_oil_south_dakota_intro-may-13-2025.md]]

## Relationship with JVL

- MG Oil offered to be JVL's **beta-test site** and, if the games play well, to order **10–100 machines** — the concrete offer is detailed on [[wiki/people/troy-erikson.md]]. Contact chain: Troy Erikson (owner) → **Dana** (general manager, oversees gaming) ↔ **[[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]]** (JVL product manager). [[raw/mg_oil_south_dakota_intro-may-13-2025.md]]
- On the Sept-23 2025 pricing meeting Val used MG Oil as the worked example of the **CFO-to-CFO credit** mechanism: the invoice would state the full retail price (~$18,000) while a discount credit is routed directly CFO-to-CFO, "bypassing everything else," to preserve list-price integrity. See [[wiki/topics/jvl-machine-pricing-strategy.md]]. [[raw/pricing_strategy_meeting-september-23-2025.md]]

## The company in Dana Giesey's telling (June 18 2026)

From the [[wiki/meetings/2026-06-18-mg-oil-dana-giesey-rapid-city-visit.md|June-18 2026 Rapid City visit]], where [[wiki/people/dana-giesey.md|Dana Giesey]] gave Val the company's history on request. This is the fullest account of MG Oil in the corpus; it fills in ownership and origins the May-2025 profile above did not have. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]

- **Origins.** The company started with [[wiki/people/troy-erikson.md|Troy Erickson]]'s father **Martin**, "**a rep for Standard Oil**", who "**ended up buying a small oil job here in Rapid City and started hauling fuel for people and selling oil**", then bought up old service stations with service bays and converted them into convenience stores. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **How the gaming route grew.** MG Oil noticed the lottery machines another vendor had placed in its Flying J truck stop, took a few years to break in, then started buying its own machines for its own locations; from there it placed machines in the convenience stores it already delivered fuel to, and finally **bought out other operators as they retired**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **What it runs today (Dana's figures).** "probably **fifty or sixty semis** on the road that deliver fuel" across **South Dakota, part of North Dakota, a little of Wyoming and Nebraska**; "**forty, around forty convenience stores**"; truck stops; **a couple of restaurants in Rapid City** plus more elsewhere (the count is garbled and is not given a number here); **a lot of rental properties and strip malls**, bought mainly because MG Oil already rented standalone casino space inside them and the landlords eventually sold; and "**several hundred employees**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **The dependency, stated flatly:** "**we wouldn't be in the business if it weren't for the lottery.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **Wholly private, no partners, never wanted any.** "**Completely private company, just family owned.**" They have had many partnership offers; the only partner ever was one Martin had in three or four convenience stores at the very start, bought out years ago. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **Other offices named:** **Wagner, South Dakota** — "just south of Mitchell about an hour away" — which runs the Nebraska business, and **Yankton**, "about an hour away from our Nebraska office." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]

### Ownership and succession (June 18 2026)

- **Troy is buying the company from his father.** Martin "**is not real active in the company**". Troy's **brother** was bought out years ago and is not involved. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **The next generation.** **Mackenzie Erickson** "**has some interest in the company**" and "**has been working in different areas of the company trying to learn that stuff**". Dana on the difficulty: "**We're so diversified; there is a lot to learn in this company** … especially on the fuel side." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **The exposure Dana raised himself:** "**One of the things is that we're all getting older**" — "**a lot of our management team across our whole company is about in that same age group.**" Many are long-tenured, including staff inherited when MG Oil absorbed other oil companies, "**Most of the employees of the companies that we purchased have stayed on.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]

### The Nebraska arm: T&T / TNT out of Wagner

- **MG Oil's Nebraska business trades as "T and T" out of the Wagner office.** It arrived by acquisition: T&T had itself bought the small company that ran the Nebraska route, and MG Oil then bought T&T. The man who had owned that small company still works there and "**knows everybody down there**." Dana: "**We're not in danger of losing our customers; we'll take care of them, but we didn't really get in on the big** [opportunity]." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **The two people who run it are Robert "Whitey" Tolliver and Mike Kotab**, both working out of Wagner, South Dakota; the man on the ground in Nebraska reports to them. Decision-making on Nebraska sits with **Troy plus Whitey and Mike**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **Dana thinks Troy would sell it.** "I know he's had some people talking to him about buying that down there" and "**I think he would sell that in Nebraska.**" Nebraska context on [[wiki/projects/nebraska-skill-market.md]] (Jun-18). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **June 22 2026 — Val meets Whitey and Mike by video; the arm is called "TNT Enterprises".** The recording of that call is titled "TNT Enterprises (Robert "Whitey" Tolliver, Michael "Miko" Kotab)". TNT had no open complaints (Dennis had fixed the small things); they said the **state** — Nebraska's 1099 rules and "wager system" — hurts their players more than any manufacturer, and they run with **hand pays turned off**. Val left three product ideas with them. Detail on [[wiki/projects/nebraska-skill-market.md]] and [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]] (both Jun-22); stub [[wiki/meetings/2026-06-22-ntn-operator-introduction.md]]. *(An earlier notes-only file called this operator "NTN"; that was a garble of TNT.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_TNT_Enterprises_Nebraska_Intro_and_Product_Ideas-June-22-2026.md]]

### The January 2026 ransomware attack

MG Oil's accounting was still behind in June because of a **ransomware attack in January** that encrypted everything on its servers. Dana: "We had a big ransomware attack on the system here **back in January** … **we lost everything on our servers**… **So I had to start over and recreate everything.**" Their IT people shut systems down once they realised something was present, "**but before they could get it shut down, they just encrypted everything**." Recovery has run through "**this company in California**" — "**They've been able to recover quite a bit of our data, but it's taken a long time and has been expensive too.**" Dana did not answer Val's question about whether they paid the ransom. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]

*(Val's comparison, for contrast: JVL was hit "**two years ago**", caught the intrusion on a system alarm, shut everything off, had backups, and restored in **two days** with small damage — and has since migrated fully to the cloud. That belongs to JVL's own history; see [[wiki/topics/infosec-and-server-infrastructure.md]].)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]

## Open contradictions

- **How many truck stops MG Oil runs.** May 13 2025 (Troy's own account): "**five or six truck stops**". June 18 2026 (Dana): "**two truck stops at least in the Sioux Falls area we have**" — a sentence that is itself ambiguous and may be two clauses run together, so a plain "two" is not asserted. The two counts are not reconciled and neither is preferred. [[raw/mg_oil_south_dakota_intro-may-13-2025.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]

## Open questions

- **How many convenience stores.** May 13 2025 gives "about 35"; June 18 2026 gives "forty, around forty." Consistent with ordinary growth over thirteen months, so recorded as evolution rather than conflict — but neither figure is precise. [[raw/mg_oil_south_dakota_intro-may-13-2025.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_MG_Oil_Dana_Giesey_South_Dakota_Visit-June-18-2026.md]]
- **When MG Oil actually started.** The May-2025 profile says "started as an oil company in the **1970s**"; the state operator list gives MG Oil's operator licence as established **1992-03-23**; Dana's June-18 account gives the sequence (Standard Oil rep → oil jobbership → convenience stores) but no dates. No single founding date is asserted.

# JVL Systems, Inc.

JVL Systems, Inc. is the group's **US operating company**, a Florida corporation, **100% owned by [[wiki/organizations/jvl-labs.md|JVL Labs Inc.]]**. [[raw/jvl_corporate_structure_2025-04.md]] It is the entity Val chose to open the group's first CIBC US bank account for. [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]] Part of the umbrella [[wiki/organizations/jvl.md|JVL group]].

## California franchise tax

- California taxes the corporation because it has an **employee present in California** — not because of sales. The filing requirement began with the **2024** tax year. With no sales into California, the **$800 statutory minimum** is the only tax owed. [[raw/jvl_systems_corporate_identity_tax_ids_2026-06-04.md]]
- **2024 year:** an FTB bill was paid on 2026-03-18 by Inna — $993.49 plus a $22.85 processing fee, $1,016.34 total; the underlying tax is $800, the rest late-payment penalty/interest. [[raw/jvl_systems_corporate_identity_tax_ids_2026-06-04.md]]
- **2025 year:** 2025 California sales were USD 11,496.53 to a single customer (RU Hungry LLC, Visalia), well below sales/economic-nexus thresholds, so the tax stays at the $800 employee-presence minimum. [[raw/jvl_systems_corporate_identity_tax_ids_2026-06-04.md]]

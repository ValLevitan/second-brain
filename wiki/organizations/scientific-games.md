# Scientific Games

Scientific Games is an incumbent gaming vendor JVL encounters in the **Puerto Rico** market. On the Mar 24 2025 markets call, Val reported (from his Puerto Rico tour) that Scientific Games holds the **exclusive contract for Puerto Rico's off-track-betting (OTB) network** — thousands of small OTB outlets across the island with upwards of **5,000 machines**, a mix of Scientific Games VLTs and generic third-party VLTs, all managed by Scientific Games. Per the route operators he spoke to, that OTB market is currently **tax-free**. [[raw/games_markets-march-24-2025.md]]

(Transcribed variously "Scientific Games" and "Cygames"/"Sci games"; recorded as one company.) See [[wiki/projects/puerto-rico-expansion.md]].

# Bad Dog / Zephyr (machine maker)

A gaming-machine maker that competes with JVL in Georgia. It was called **Bad Dog** and is now called **Zephyr**. Many earlier pages name "Bad Dog" as a competitor without saying who they are; this page gathers what its sales lead, [[wiki/people/david-villari.md|David Villari]], told Val and Felipe on **Oct 16 2026** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]. Everything below is David's own account, not checked against any other source.

## The name

- **Why it changed.** At a show the logo sat at the back of the booth with the dog blacked out: "bad is red, dog is black, and games is white. So all you could see was Bad Games… it just wasn't professional enough." A partner that works in Class III casinos also did not like the name, "so we changed the name". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]
- **Two new names are heard.** David first agrees the new name is "Zephyr", then says "we changed the name, Sanford Gaming". The notes give Zephyr and list "Sanford Gaming" as also heard [[raw/Meeting_Notes_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]. Which is right is **unclear**; this page uses Zephyr.
- **A show neighbour.** "we were right next to you guys at" a show (which show is not clear in the transcript). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]

## Who runs it

- **Owner: Brian**, "a Texas guy… Fort Worth area", the only owner ("Single point ownership"), and "hands on every day". David could not recall his last name. David says Brian built a restaurant point-of-sale system called **Aloha** in the early 2000s, and "NCR acquired it back in 2012". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]
- **Chief product officer: Justin Harris.** "He created the first Grover Banilla game ever and Figure Eight" (sweepstakes software). He left Banilla/Grover in 2018, later worked as a consultant, and was hired full time "back in July". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]
- **Sales: David Villari** — "I run the sales for them." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]
- **Size.** "In total, twenty-five, including the development", with "only two sales reps". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]
- **A minority owner tied to Fiery Pay.** David says the Fiery Pay owner (heard "Offshot") is "one of the minority owners of Bad Dog". The same sentence goes on "so he's our CEO", which does not fit "Brian, the owner" and is **unclear**. The corpus elsewhere names the Fiery Pay principal as Afshad (see [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]]); that this is the same man is an **inference**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]

## What it sells and where

- **Georgia.** Boards are sold only in Georgia: "The only place that Zephyr sells boards is Georgia. Sell a lot of them." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]] Earlier pages already record Bad Dog's board-only conversions in Georgia (about $2,700–2,800, and refurbished cabinets for $5–6K) — see [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]].
- **No grey markets.** "No gray. Unfortunately, although that's where the money's at, no gray." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]
- **Puerto Rico, its first Class III market.** Zephyr had used "Nick Farley with Clips Gaming" for Georgia and had never used GLI; it got GLI certification for Puerto Rico, which "Kickstarted the class three for us". ("Clips Gaming" with Nick Farley reads as [[wiki/organizations/eclipse-gaming-lab.md|Eclipse]] — **inference**.) For Puerto Rico it stripped a machine down — "took anything fancy off of it. It's literally just the cabinet" — and sells it for about "eight grand". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]
- **How it sells into Puerto Rico.** FOB: machines go to "Miami. Fort Lauderdale, wherever the cheapest boat is"; the distributor arranges truck, shipping, "all the duties, all the fees"; Zephyr gives its distributor "six month terms"; "we ship 72 at a time". Its distributor there is **Christian**, who has "three licenses" and is partnered with a large local property owner. Detail on [[wiki/projects/puerto-rico-expansion.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]
- **Next markets named:** "We're going to do pull tab market. We're going to do plastic market" ("plastic" as heard; meaning **unclear**). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]
- **Game hold.** "Our games are sixteen percent… If you want to do RTP, it's about ninety four." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]

## Partners

- **Gaming Capital Group** (also heard "Gaming Group CGC"). "They're partners with us. They don't own any part of us", working toward casinos. Talks began "back in probably April or May this year". No machines bought yet: "No commitment yet." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]
- **Fiery Pay.** "We did build the product, the backend… all the development was done by Bad Dog", together with [[wiki/organizations/rks.md|RKS]] ("Jim Beck over at RKS, who makes the MudaGoose"). Bad Dog will help with later fixes and updates, and "We're gonna sell the kiosks for Fiery Pay, not the point of sale, but we're just selling the hardware." David then said "I don't know too much about the fire stuff." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]] RKS's own account (Aug 18 2026) is that it did the Fiery Pay hardware except the POS — see [[wiki/organizations/rks.md]]. The two accounts can both be true (one did the back end, one the hardware) — **inference**.

## How JVL sees it (from other pages)

Operators in Georgia have rated Bad Dog as a leading low-cost maker — for example Blue Sky / Gold Rush called it "number one outside of any major company" ([[wiki/organizations/blue-sky-gold-rush.md]]) — and JVL prices against it ([[wiki/topics/jvl-machine-pricing-strategy.md]]). On Sept 9 2026 Val asked for a direct line to its sales lead; the Oct-16 breakfast is that meeting (**inference**; see [[wiki/people/david-villari.md]]).

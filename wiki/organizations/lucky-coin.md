# Lucky Coin (Georgia COAM master operator)

A **third-generation family-owned Georgia COAM route** — roughly **50 locations and 250 machines**, spread "all over" the state rather than concentrated in the north [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]]. It is run day to day by [[wiki/people/kyle-shepherd.md|Kyle Shepherd]] and his younger brother **Curtis**; the derived notes give the legal name as **Lucky Coin Inc** and place the office in **Jefferson, Georgia** [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].

It is an existing JVL customer, and — as of the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-02-lucky-coin-service-and-upgrade-offer.md|Sept-2 2026 office visit]] — the customer who told Val most bluntly why JVL is not his primary supplier.

*(Name note: the wiki carried this account as "Kyle Shepard / Lucky Coin" from [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]] (Jun-24 and Jul-6 2026) before it had a page. The Sept-2 derived notes spell the principal **Shepherd** and cite the calendar invite; the transcript renders him "Kyle Shepard". Both are kept as aliases — see [[wiki/people/kyle-shepherd.md]].)*

## Ownership and history

- **Founded in the early 1990s by Kyle's maternal grandfather, surname Jones**, from the **Tucker / Scottdale** area just outside Atlanta.  letting him keep "one foot in that kind of shady lifestyle, and then one foot on a little cleaner path." He had dropped out of school in the eighth grade to take care of his family [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **The origin moment was a card table at the American Legion in Tucker.** The grandfather was a member only because "he just needed a place to hang out"; **Butch Fochet** had machines in the hall, and back then "they didn't have stackers on the bill acceptors — all the cash fell into these big plastic bins inside the cabinets." Fochet came in and dumped that cash on the table where the grandfather was playing poker. Val's summary of the reaction, which Kyle did not correct: "this cash is passing by me" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **The first machines were eight-liners** — rendered in the transcript as "old J Masters, eight liners" — and the route later ran "a lot of the old **Cadillac Jack** stuff" and countertop cabinets [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Kyle's father joined a few years later.** He ran his own **forklift-motor repair business** until the grandfather "begged him for a couple years" to come across. Born in 1962, he is still in the warehouse most days in a more passive role and is still consulted [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **The company was never in the grandfather's name.** It was held by Kyle's grandmother — "Nothing was in his name" — and when she died (as of Sept 2026, "December will be a year") it passed to Kyle's **mother**, who owns it but has nothing to do with running it. The grandfather died in **2021** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Kyle and Curtis run it as partners**; Kyle is the decision-maker on purchasing — "it's me. We're still a family business, so I run the business" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Val believes he sold to the grandfather personally.** "There's no question in my mind that we did business together. One million percent" — through **JVL** and **Cash Code** (bill acceptors, eight-liners, horse-racing machines, countertops). Kyle could not confirm it and said the route did not go into horse racing on purpose. Recorded as Val's claim, not as a documented trading history [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].

## How it buys

- **Two criteria, in Kyle's order.** First, "the revenue that it throws, and really, it's more of a **cash in** than the revenue. I want people to play it." Second, "**service**, service issues. How reliable the machines?" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]]
- **The fleet is mixed and partly legacy:** "a handful of the old **Primero** boards" (since updated), some **Banilla** equipment — "I haven't bought anything from them in a while because they haven't come out with anything new in a long time" — around **15 to 20 JVL Flex cabinets** (the figure is [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]]'s estimate, not Kyle's), and a handful of **Altair** units from JVL's earlier generation [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]]. *(The transcript renders Banilla as "Bandila" in one place and "Banilla" in another.)*
- **Redemption stack: EasyLink plus GBT kiosks, bought and installed by Lucky Coin itself.** Asked what works, Kyle: "For us, it's been **EasyLink and GBT kiosk**. That's been the best combo that we found." Every location can issue a gift card; the ones without a kiosk use "the little **PAX**, the handheld credit card terminal"; **30–40% of locations have kiosks** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Class A: not in it, and the reason was distrust of the promoters.** When the category appeared, "I wanted to see how they would compete with the class B," and his read of **Jinko** and **Primero**, the two big early movers, was that they were "very self-motivated… I didn't feel like they were looking out in interest of you. I thought they were more just trying to make a few bucks while they could before these things were eventually found to be illegal." He now calls his opinion "evolving" — "I think they're **more complementary than competitive** at this point" — and would "probably" place some in his locations. See [[wiki/topics/georgia-class-a-machines.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Earlier in the corpus (Jul 6 2026): the local-area-progressive install.** Felipe described "a special version… made for the progressive local area progressive settings of **three machines in different locations**" on **Software 1919**, built for a few masters including Lucky Bucks, still running with "toppers and everything looking good"; JVL agreed to leave it un-upgraded because the customer does not need dual SAS and "they want to keep it as is." Felipe named the customer in the same breath — "This is one of the customers that will be picking up one of the verticals, which is **Kyle Shepard**." Full account on [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]] (Jul-6) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Call_with_Felipe_Miranda_Giorgi-July-6-2026.md]].
- **He believes his location relationships are unusually close**, and uses them as a reporting channel: "My locations have my phone number. They can all call me directly… they know when my location birthdays are… I very regularly am kept in the loop on my location licensees" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].

## Why JVL is not the primary supplier — the service account, in the customer's words

Asked directly — "what is holding you back from buying JVL as a primary supplier? Tell me the truth" — Kyle answered: "**Truthfully, yeah, we have more service issues out of our JVL games than we do most others.**" He had raised it at his own monthly internal "board meeting" the day before, specifically because he knew he was coming [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].

The specifics, as given:

- **A menu button that forces a re-flash.** "There's a button in that menu somewhere that, if you press it, it somehow throws everything so out of whack that you have to flash it to get it back where it needs to be." A route man — "He's not a tech. He's a route guy" — pressed it while pulling numbers at a bar in **Peachtree City**, and a technician had to decommission, flash and recommission the game [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **The decommission/recommission cycle is the actual cost.** [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]] stated the rule: "for the lottery pipelines, if you're doing a hard reset for any reason, you must decommission before you do the hard reset, and after it's done, recommission — even if it's the same version." Kyle's arithmetic: "when you flash it, it can take a **15-minute job and turn it into a two-hour job**," and "what would be a quick, simple fix on most any other manufacturer's game… turns into a headache." His technicians' experience of support is that "most of the time, any error that this thing throws… what they're told when they call tech support is **flash**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **The Peachtree City location is the sore one.** Between the Altair and the trial machine JVL had placed there, "we were down there at least every other day… almost every day for two weeks" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **No accountability on support calls.** "It's very rarely does anyone here take accountability for the issue. It's always something that you've never heard of, or something that doesn't happen, or something that we've done" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **JVL refuses to help integrate third-party equipment, and a competitor does not.** "We call and say, 'Can you guys help us configure this with your equipment?'… The answer we always get is, 'We don't do that. You have to figure it out.'… If I call **Banilla**… they will **bend over backwards** to help me make that work with their stuff." Kyle allowed it was JVL's prerogative — "but that's not the answer I get when I talk to other manufacturers." In the post-meeting debrief neither Val nor Felipe could identify who had said it; Val speculated aloud that a JVL person the transcript renders "**Adam**" may have, and told Felipe to ask him. **Unresolved — no JVL "Adam" is identified in this source** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **A misunderstanding that turned out not to be a fault at all.** A technician scanned the QR code on the trial game to pull its numbers and believed the scan had failed because the figures "flash on the screen and go off quicker than he can get a picture." Val's explanation: the scan had worked; JVL has "**one day delay in processing**" before the report is readable, which is why Felipe could see the numbers only afterwards. "This is a big misunderstanding" — and it is solved by the modems JVL is now installing, which remove the need to scan at all [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **A rudeness complaint neither party settled, involving a technician named Aaron.** Felipe raised it in the debrief — "I talked to Aaron, and I was very surprised how he spoke to him. He was very rude" — and described the QR-code call as "a very disrespectful conversation on his part, not on my part. I have text messages." Kyle defended the man without excusing him: "he is a top three technician in the state… I would put him up against anybody… He would rather have his head in the machine in the warehouse and nobody talk to him all day." **Whose employee Aaron is is not stated**: Kyle's defence reads as a defence of his own man, while the derived notes list the episode among the complaints about JVL support. Recorded both ways; **not** linked to [[wiki/people/aaron.md]], who is JVL's engineering-process and AI-tooling lead and does not fit this context (§4 — resolve by context, leave unresolved rather than force a match) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].

**Val's answer** — that machines should not break at all, that he finally understands the flash burden and will fix it without committing to a date, and what he offered in the meantime — is on [[wiki/topics/jvl-operating-principles.md]] (Sept 2 2026). The commercial offer that followed is [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-free-ports-and-pos-with-every-machine-bought.md]].

## Where this account stands after Sept 2

- **Kyle has not pulled the JVL machines off the route and does not want to.** "We haven't pulled the JVLs off our route yet. I'm not… I don't want to. I'm happy to work with you guys any way that I can" — conditional on seeing "a real attempt to make it better," after which "hopefully [we] get to the point where we can buy them a bunch at a time" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Private numbers were exchanged**, with Val's standing instruction: "If it happens, call me. Call me personally if we don't react to the extent that you wish" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Kyle is willing to be the transactional pilot, but not in his best locations.** "It has bugs. I'm sure it has bugs. That's going to stop me from putting it in my very best locations to start" — Val agreed and asked for "a **tolerant location**," promising JVL would "wire the first location. We will **babysit** your first location, literally babysit… overnight" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **The ATM feature is the thing he actually wants.** Lucky Coin was "moving forward with GBT Easy Links until this ATM debit card" came up, and on cash dispensing: "**If it could spit out cash, I'd throw that in every one of my locations.**" His own caution about the competing product: he thinks "their solution and the AT[M]" will "create a real problem with legislators in Georgia." See [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-hold-the-recycler-back-but-sign-no-undertaking.md]] for why JVL cannot sell him that today [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Val's read of the prize, given to Felipe after Kyle left:** "I see 50 locations, of which **30 could be exclusively our equipment from the start, and you could buy 30 machines from us**. This is what I see" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_and_Upgrade_Offer-September-2-2026.md]].

### The single worst thing: decommission and recommission — the same complaint, now from the hands that do it

- **The headline, unprompted:** "the amount of times I end up having to **decommission a game and [fla]sh it just to fix what I would consider as a relatively simple error that shouldn't require a pull**… So I try to **avoid having to do that as much as I can**." And why it costs beyond the time: "it's a pain with **Interlot** now, **it wipes the game's history out and it's back at zero**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]]
- **The worked example — a brand-new game disabled by the lottery after ten days.** "it was in the field for **ten days**, and after a week and a half **the lottery disabled it** because they said that it had started **reporting the wrong signature** to the lottery. **The signature that the game reported when I commissioned it was not the one that the game was sending to the site controller anymore.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]]
- **And the support answer he got, put to Val's face:** "when I called you and told you about it, **you told me that that didn't happen with the new games. When I'm sitting there… with it in front of me, like it's happening now.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]]
- **The JVL answer, both the workaround and the walk-back.** "we go to **status**, and then it's **locked by [the] accounting system**, and then we **unlock it manually**, and then it comes back up. So what I said that it cannot happen is that **it cannot be the wrong version. Not that it was not happening to you.**" The technician: "I did that **two or three times**, and it **worked for a minute, and then it went back**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]]
- **Then the volume-menu button — the same fault Kyle reported on Sept 2, hit again while fixing something else.** "I went in to **turn the volume down**, and when I turned the volume down, **I pressed the button in the volume menu that breaks the whole game**, and the solution was **to flash it**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026.md]]

### The honest verdict: above-average attention, but the games earn

- **Service load, asked and answered plainly.** "**Probably above average**… We have a **relatively small number of them on our route, and we spend proportionately a greater amount of time on them** than we do on a lot of other games." And on the per-call comparison: "**a little bit more time. Usually, more phone calls** — and that's **partly because I'm not the most familiar with the JVL system** and how to fix them."

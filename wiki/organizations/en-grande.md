# En Grande (Colombian Ruta operation)

**En Grande** is the Colombian **Ruta** business (slot machines in street/small venues) in **Medellín** that [[wiki/people/julian-puerta.md|Julian Puerta]] was responsible for as of mid-2024 — Val: "You have a responsibility in Medellin for Engrande … It's an ongoing responsibility." It runs JVL **Echo** machines that Val sold "to Eliana, En Grande" — a deal on which Val says he made no money and took only a "stupid 10 percent … chiseled … to eight percent," done "because I wanted you to succeed." [[raw/julian_puerta_jvl_mexico.md]]

## State as of ≈May 2024 (date inferred; pre-window source — History-era snapshot)

- **Not making money.** Val: by Julian's own calculations the machines need to make **$12/day** to break even "and I don't see how it's going to make $12 a day." [[raw/julian_puerta_jvl_mexico.md]]
- **Biggest operational blocker:** "**Colquegos** [as transcribed — the Colombian gaming regulator] not being able to process **change orders** quickly," which prevents switching under-performing locations fast. Julian's counter: the target locations previously ran illegal slots, so the revenue budget exists — "we just have to figure it out how to put it into our slot machines." [[raw/julian_puerta_jvl_mexico.md]]
- Product improvement was expected to lift performance only "by 30 percent marginally … it cannot triple the profit" (Val); a **WAP (wide-area progressive) with a life-changing jackpot** was the intended headline feature for Colombia, where per-terminal bet/prize limits are low. [[raw/julian_puerta_jvl_mexico.md]]
- Funding: [[wiki/people/eliana.md|Eliana]]'s ~$2.2–2.3M commitment behind the machines "runs out in October" (2024) and was not expected to be renewed. [[raw/julian_puerta_jvl_mexico.md]]
- Money collection was already largely **virtual** — Julian collects from shop owners via a payment link (banks/virtual wallets), with one person reconciling, and a bank-API/POS integration in the works; a casino-side feature to load money to a slot terminal by scanning it from an app was "ready for next month" (allowed in casinos, where customer data can be tracked — not yet permitted for Ruta). [[raw/julian_puerta_jvl_mexico.md]]

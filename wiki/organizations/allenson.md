# Allenson

**Allenson** is a Georgia COAM route run by **Malik**, who calls himself "a master licensee" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Allenson_Malik_Market_Review_and_Data_Partnership-February-16-2026.md]]. He is a long-time JVL customer who bought his JVL machines through [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]] ("I bought a lot of them from in couple years ago… Felipe") [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Allenson_Malik_Market_Review_and_Data_Partnership-February-16-2026.md]].

**Name note (§4).** The company name is never spoken in the Feb-16 transcript; Malik is addressed by first name only. The link Malik = Allenson rests on the derived notes' calendar pairing [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Allenson_Malik_Market_Review_and_Data_Partnership-February-16-2026.md]] and on the May-4 2026 notes, which call the same account both "Allenson" and "Malik's best location" [[raw/Meeting_Notes_July_Trial_Rollout_Plan-May-4-2026.md]]. The Feb-18 2026 to-do call spells it "Allenson", "Allinson" and "Allison" in the same recording, and calls it one of the smaller masters ("AKS and Allison are a bit smaller") [[raw/To-Do_Georgia_meetings-February-18-2026.md]]. Malik has no person page. A speaker label "Siddiqui" at the start of one of Felipe's turns may be Malik's surname; it is **unclear** and not used here [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Allenson_Malik_Market_Review_and_Data_Partnership-February-16-2026.md]].

## JVL at Allenson

- **40 to 50 JVL machines across the route.** "I have JVL P 1 P 2 place. I have I think forty to fifty JVLs. In my route, I think more so it's placed every single locations." ("P 1 P 2" and "pitch one" are read as JVL's Peach 1 / Peach 2 by context.) His locations range from one that "makes thirty grand" to one that "makes five grand" (period not stated) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Allenson_Malik_Market_Review_and_Data_Partnership-February-16-2026.md]].
- **Why his JVL buying slowed.** Felipe said Malik was "perhaps one of our biggest Altera fans" but his buying "slowed down with the Flex". Malik's answer was price, and one past deal: when Val visited his office, JVL offered a three-machine progressive set that had to be bought whole ("you have to buy the whole thing for fifty thousand dollars") and asked for "commitment letters or a guaranteed"; "then we went to IGT. And then we signed it with IGT." He also said he bought "two sets like this" from JVL. Val did not remember the deal; Felipe recalled "around thirteen thousand dollars" (what that figure covered is not stated) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Allenson_Malik_Market_Review_and_Data_Partnership-February-16-2026.md]].
- **A JVL trial was already running with him** on Feb 16 ("We have a trial going with you now") [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Allenson_Malik_Market_Review_and_Data_Partnership-February-16-2026.md]].
- **He plans to expand out of state.** "we are planning to expand"; he had met Light & Wonder "and somebody else also to get the right price range for the machines to get into that area" (the out-of-state markets under discussion) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Allenson_Malik_Market_Review_and_Data_Partnership-February-16-2026.md]].

His full market read, the data agreement and the product preview are on [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]] (Feb 16 2026) and [[wiki/topics/machine-data-repurchase-program.md]] (History). Meeting stub: [[wiki/meetings/2026-02-16-allenson-malik-market-review-and-data-partnership.md]].

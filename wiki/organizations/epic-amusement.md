# Epic Amusement (Georgia master licensee — Kenny Patel)

A large Georgia COAM **master licensee**, owned and run by [[wiki/people/kenny-patel.md|Kenny Patel]], and a long-standing but hard-to-close JVL buyer. In February 2026 [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]] ranked it the **#2 master** in the state with **520 locations**, and said it had been "promising an order for the since November". [[raw/To-Do_Georgia_meetings-February-18-2026.md]] Val met Kenny in person for the first time on **August 25 2026** — see [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-25-epic-amusement-kenny-patel-visit.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]

## Aug 25 2026 — the visit: what Epic told JVL

The account below is from the recording. The derived notes differ in several places (a trade-in, a machine commitment, "good" test results); those claims are not in the recording and are not used — see the meeting stub. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]

**Who was there for Epic.** Kenny, and a former commercial banker who says he has worked for him "five and a half" years. He joined when "Epic was looking to get a loan", and now acts as Kenny's liaison with lenders and attorneys — "he's my eyes and mouth" (Kenny). A technician, **Dustin**, is named as the person who works on the machines. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]

**How JVL sits in Epic's fleet.**
- **JVL ranks "the third" in bringing Epic revenue** (Kenny; "out of how many" is not clear in the transcript). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]
- **JVL out-earns Bad Dog, but Bad Dog is cheaper.** JVL makes about "$40–45" more win per day per unit than Bad Dog, but Bad Dog sells Epic a **board only**, "like twenty seven hundred, twenty eight hundred bucks", to convert an old cabinet. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]
- **Volume.** About "six seven hundred games" a year on average (the transcript merges this answer into Val's line, so the speaker is not certain). Val: "the only stumbling block are the terms." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]
- **Region.** "GVL does good when you leave Atlanta" — callers from Savannah or Macon ask for JVL; in Atlanta the demand is for Light & Wonder (Kenny). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]

**Kenny's conditions.**
- **No wire-cutting for Access.** "your game has to be able to cut the wires. That's the one thing I pissed off about is uh access." Felipe's answer: upgrade the games to the latest version and they connect to Access through **dual SAS**, with no line cut; the upgrade is free. Kenny said only **five** of Epic's JVL games had been upgraded so far. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]
- **Financing, not cash.** "Cash is never going to be… possible" for Epic, and "everybody's offering zero percent interest rate." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]
- **Data deal needs the lenders.** Before a data-sharing contract, Epic must make sure "our lenders are okay with signing it". Terms on [[wiki/topics/machine-data-repurchase-program.md]] (Aug 25). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]

**The test machine.** Epic had **one** of JVL's new 43-inch verticals on test at one of its "top tier" locations (Sugar Hill). Over 48 days the location took in about **$246,000** across **six** machines, of which the JVL machine took about **$14,000**. A per-day figure spoken as "seventy seven sixty four" is unclear. Kenny: "your new game is worse than your old game." On his third-party reports JVL's fleet runs at **$147 a day** over the last five weeks and **$162** overall; Val said JVL's own reports show **$140–240 a day** on new machines. Val and Felipe both said one location is not a fair test; Kenny wants the full game set in a test. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]

**Kenny on the market.** Georgia numbers are "down like twenty five thirty percent" January–May, and "only way the new equipment will get sold is financing." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]] Epic is already weighing the competitor **Access** system at the counter; his description of it and Val's answer are on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]] (Aug 25, Epic). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]

**How it ended.** No order. Kenny agreed to visit JVL's office for a longer look at the equipment — first "next Tuesday… eleven thirty", then moved to a Thursday, 11 AM to 1 PM, with lunch. The banker's closing line: "Your overall solution thing is definitely where the market's at." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]

**Withheld (§15 #8).** Kenny described lawsuits between Epic and two other named Georgia companies. The names and allegations are held for an owner ruling and not recorded here. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]

# CIBC

CIBC is the bank JVL is working with to open US business accounts. (The transcript garbles the name as "CABC.")

## Relationship with JVL

- CIBC's US cross-border team (Michelle Tumby, then Taylor) is setting up US-dollar business accounts for the three JVL companies, one at a time, starting with JVL Systems Inc. [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]]
- Tony is JVL's relationship contact and is copied on all account matters. [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]]

## Key facts about CIBC's US banking

- In the US, CIBC only offers US-dollar accounts (no Canadian-dollar accounts). [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]]
- Money can only move between the US and Canada by wire transfer or cheque. [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]]
- The account offered is a "simplified business account": $10/month (waived with a $2,500 balance), $100 to open, under 500 transactions/month. Online banking links all three accounts and supports transfers and ACH. [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]]
- Add-on fees: wire service $15/month flat plus per-transfer ($10 domestic, $15 to Canada); ACH push service $25/month for 50 payments (receiving ACH is free); one-off wires $30 domestic / $40 international. [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]]
- CIBC does not issue its own US credit card; it partners with Elan Financial. A US credit card needs a US tax ID and a US address for the business and guarantor. [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]]
- CIBC US cannot wire funds to prohibited countries: Syria, Iran, North Korea, Cuba, and Crimea ("Crema" in the transcript). [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]]
- CIBC's Canadian-side online platform is "cash management online," which interfaces with QuickBooks, and (per the banker) SmartBooks and Xero. [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]]

## July 2 2026 — account structure to receive aggregator payments + Business Central integration

On a ~22-minute [[wiki/meetings/2026-07-02-cibc-business-central-integration.md|July-2 11:07 AM ET CIBC call]] ([[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] with CIBC's Michael Hill and Sanjay Vishwanathan; a third voice, "Speaker 3", asks many of the questions — who this is is unclear: the transcript line "So so, wait, Sanjay" hints it may be Sanjay, while the older derived notes call this person an unnamed advisor) the topic was how to set up CIBC accounts to receive third-party ("aggregator") payments for JVL. The transcript is now the source of record; the earlier derived notes are kept as a lead. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_CIBC_ACH_Account_Architecture-July-2-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_CIBC_Business_Central_Integration-July-2-2026.md]]

- **Val's original design — a zero-balance receiving account plus a transfer on command.** One account that always sits at zero. When money arrives, JVL makes an API call ("How much money do I have on this account now?"), then orders that exact amount moved to a second CIBC account. Val's example: $105 comes in, is date-stamped, moves, and the first account is back at zero. The second account is an "accumulation account" — "that's a transactional account already". The goal is no mixing of funds and "absolute purity" between what is sent and what is received. Val stressed he wanted no automatic transfer: "We want force transfer… trigger that transfer based on a command." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_CIBC_ACH_Account_Architecture-July-2-2026.md]]
- **CIBC's answer — the zero-balance account sweeps at end of day, not on command.** Michael explained CIBC's zero-balance account moves the day's deposits out automatically at the end of the day. The only other option is a manual transfer. "We don't have it where you communicate to us transfer, and it's done automatically." Val said manual transfers would be "a tremendous… overhead expense", as he expects frequent transfers. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_CIBC_ACH_Account_Architecture-July-2-2026.md]]
- **Account type and fees.** JVL's accounts were opened as small-business / business-banking accounts, not analysis accounts. To make the receiving account a zero-balance account CIBC would have to convert the account type, and Michael warned "that's going to change your fee structure." Michael recalled this path had been discussed earlier, pending JVL's plans. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_CIBC_ACH_Account_Architecture-July-2-2026.md]]
- **The challenge to the design.** Speaker 3 asked why Val needs an API reading at all: each deposit is visible in the account as it lands, the accountants can compare the credits with the aggregator's report during the day, and any mismatch is raised with the aggregator. Val: "This was a mental architecture we had in our minds… I don't have the answer to this." He said the aggregator side holds the detailed report of many small transactions. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_CIBC_ACH_Account_Architecture-July-2-2026.md]]
- **Operating account — a checking account, not money-market.** Speaker 3 presumed the second account should be a money-market account to earn a return. Val: "No, no, that's an operational account"; Speaker 3: "So… just checking account again." *(Correction: the earlier derived notes said a money-market account was "agreed". The transcript shows the opposite, and this section now follows the transcript.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_CIBC_ACH_Account_Architecture-July-2-2026.md]]
- **Val reconsidered — one account first.** Val confirmed his currently open account can receive ACH and connect to Business Central. When Speaker 3 said "Monday morning is when you will get the account paperwork to sign for the second account under JVL", Val said "Don't don't do it yet… I'm going to go with your uh suggestion, Michael… trying to work through one account", adding "maybe there was an error in our architectural thinking." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_CIBC_ACH_Account_Architecture-July-2-2026.md]]
- **Contact protocol.** Val asked how to reach Michael; Michael: "You go through Sanjay." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_CIBC_ACH_Account_Architecture-July-2-2026.md]]

**§15.8:** clear — banking / account-structure / aggregator-payment economics only; no named-person compensation. **§15 #9:** no account, routing or other identifying numbers appear in the transcript. The Business Central side of this call is on [[wiki/topics/business-central-erp-implementation.md]] (July-2). *(The account structure was later reworked; per §1 the follow-up Outlook correspondence is out of scope.

# JVL Labs Inc.

JVL Labs Inc. is the **Canadian parent (holding) company** of the JVL group. It owns most of the operating entities; the group's non-Canadian companies report up to it. See the umbrella page [[wiki/organizations/jvl.md|JVL (group of companies)]].

## Ownership & directors

- **Sole director:** Inna Levitan. [[raw/jvl_corporate_structure_2025-04.md]]
- **Shareholders:** 91% Inna Levitan, 4.5% Peter Guterres, 4.5% Boris Itskov. [[raw/jvl_corporate_structure_2025-04.md]]
- Val Levitan describes Labs as "the Canadian" company of the group. [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]]

## What JVL Labs owns

Per the April 2025 corporate structure chart: [[raw/jvl_corporate_structure_2025-04.md]]

- **JVL Systems, Inc.** — 100% owned by JVL Labs. See [[wiki/organizations/jvl-systems.md]].
- **JVL Gameworks Corp.** — 100% owned by JVL Labs. See [[wiki/organizations/jvl-gameworks.md]].
- **JVL Colombia SAS** — 100% owned by JVL Labs.
- **JVL Gaming Peru S.A.C.** — 95% owned by JVL Labs, Inc.; 5% owned by Carlos Alberto Fonseca Sarmiento.
- **2574038 Ontario Inc.** — 100% owned by JVL Labs.

**JVL Mobi, Inc.** is a **sibling** holding company, **not** owned by JVL Labs: it has the same director (Inna Levitan) and the same shareholder split (91% Inna Levitan / 4.5% Peter Guterres / 4.5% Boris Itskov). [[raw/jvl_corporate_structure_2025-04.md]] JVL Mobi Mexico S.A. de C.V. is held by JVL Mobi Inc. together with 2574038 Ontario Inc. [[raw/jvl_corporate_structure_2025-04.md]]

## CCPC status (Sept 2026) — and what depends on it

Labs is a **CCPC**, a **Canadian-Controlled Private Corporation**, and on the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-07-departure-tax-trust-and-bad-debt.md|Sept-7 2026 call]] cross-border tax lawyer [[wiki/people/vitaly-timokhov.md|Vitaly Timokhov]] put keeping it that way at the top of his corporate workstream: "this corporation has to remain CCPC." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]

- **Ownership confirmed on that call.** Vitaly assumed [[wiki/people/inna-levitan.md|Inna]] was a *director* and Val a *shareholder*; Val corrected him — "**Inna is the owner**." This corroborates the April-2025 structure chart above (Inna sole director, 91% shareholder). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]] [[raw/jvl_corporate_structure_2025-04.md]]
- **The exposure.** If Labs' controlling shareholder stops being Canadian-resident, **Labs ceases to be a CCPC**, and the [[wiki/topics/sred-tax-credit.md|SR&ED]] benefit largely goes with it — the provincial portion becomes unavailable and the federal portion only offsets profits Labs does not have. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]
- **The proposed fix is on hold.** A Canadian-resident trust holding the Labs shares: [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-07-jvl-labs-ccpc-trust-held-pending-the-gaar-ruling.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]
- **Loss pool.** Vitaly read about **$18 million** of losses at JVL off the 2025 financial statements — the capacity available to absorb the income inclusion from the 680 bad-debt play. See [[wiki/topics/jvl-corporate-loans-and-shareholder-financing.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]

To be worked out with Mark at BSP. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Gilmore_GameWorks_US_Tax_and_Labs_Filing-June-30-2026.md]]

## July 8 2026 — a development company now; 2026 is the cutoff year for Canadian manufacturing

On the [[wiki/meetings/2026-07-08-gilmore-bsp-intercompany-software-licensing-and-florida-nexus.md|July-8 2026 accountants' call]] the June-30 question (how Labs charges Systems with no machine sales) got its answer: software-licensing fees, sourced to Florida — see [[wiki/decisions/2026-07-08-jvl-labs-charges-jvl-systems-software-licensing-fees-sourced-to-florida.md]]. On Labs itself: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Gilmore_Cross_Border_Tax_Strategy-July-8-2026.md]]

- **Its role (Val).** "JVL Labs has become a development company, content development company." Labs owns the software; it was developed there (Sean asked, Val confirmed). The Minsk company works for it as a subcontractor — see [[wiki/organizations/jvl.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Gilmore_Cross_Border_Tax_Strategy-July-8-2026.md]]
- **When manufacturing stopped (Val).** The shift "started at the end of twenty twenty four" and happened gradually. For 2026: "Twenty twenty six will be zero"; "It's, a cutoff year for Canadian manufacturing." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Gilmore_Cross_Border_Tax_Strategy-July-8-2026.md]]
- **But some 2026 invoices exist.** Val had "found out recently" that Labs did some invoicing in 2026 and suggested it can be reversed. Igor: "I am sure the only invoices in twenty twenty six from labs is only for service. Is nothing there about the machines or parts" — he would double-check. Inna recalled at least two shipments from the Canadian office to the US in 2026; Val said the trucks carried shelves and "There was nothing sold." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Gilmore_Cross_Border_Tax_Strategy-July-8-2026.md]]
- **US state returns to be marked final (Mark).** If Labs has no 2026 sales of goods, its 2025 **Georgia** return and a blank **Pennsylvania** return would be marked final, and Labs would move to just the treaty-based federal return ("the eleven twenty F with a treaty"). Georgia final or not waits on Igor's confirmation; one more small Georgia return is "not a big deal either way." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Gilmore_Cross_Border_Tax_Strategy-July-8-2026.md]]
- **The 2025 Canadian return (Sean).** Already filed "showing sale of goods", so 2025 stays the old way; the change applies from the 2026 fiscal year. Mark said Ashley now had what she needed to finish the Labs US work. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Gilmore_Cross_Border_Tax_Strategy-July-8-2026.md]]
- **Losses stay useful.** Sean: Canada will take the income side, "but we've got the losses and everything up here." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Gilmore_Cross_Border_Tax_Strategy-July-8-2026.md]]

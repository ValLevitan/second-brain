# GLI (Gaming Laboratories International)

GLI is the independent testing lab that certifies JVL's gaming machines and games so they can be sold into regulated markets. JVL sees the cost and slowness of GLI approval as a key bottleneck to scaling up game production.

## Relationship with JVL

- GLI has been in business about 40 years. [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]]
- JVL's account contacts at GLI: Deb (client-facing account manager, fairly new), [[wiki/people/patrick-gli.md|Patrick]] (now senior director, keeps oversight of JVL), and technical/testing people Dan, Jason, Joseph, and Joe. [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]] The Jan 28 2026 transcript gives two full names: Deb is taken as [[wiki/people/deb-gli.md|Deborah Oxnem]] and Dan as [[wiki/people/dan-lewis.md|Dan Lewis]] (both by context). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]
- As an independent lab, GLI must stay neutral — it will not train or coach a client on how to test so as to pass, because that would risk its accreditation. This is what "proprietary" tends to mean in their conversations. [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]]

## Certification facts

- JVL's amusement product does not need to be tested. [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]]
- Class 3 approval is broken into stages; math testing is the most labour-intensive block, which Dan measures at close to 20 hours. [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]]
- Example GLI billing quoted: about 20 hours at $170/hour for a submission. [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]]
- Markets can require the GLI-11 standard, or GLI-11 plus additional state rules, or their own rules instead. A game already approved to GLI-11 can move to another market as a cheaper "transfer" with only the extra local testing (for online, the relevant standard is "19"). [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]]
- Live JVL approvals in progress: two games for South Dakota, two games for Florida, plus a separate 40-game online transfer proposal for about three states (Michigan, Pennsylvania, and another). [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]]

Account contact [[wiki/people/deb-gli.md|Deb]] (client-facing account manager). 

## Jan 28 2026 — the Omni platform certification kickoff

JVL opened GLI certification of its new Omni platform at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-01-28-gli-omni-platform-certification-kickoff.md|Jan-28 kickoff]] (JVL: Val, Pavel Frolov, Juan Martinez, Efim Podvoiski; GLI: Deborah Oxnem, Dan Lewis). All from the transcript unless marked. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]

- **What JVL is submitting.** Val's summary: GLI has so far approved JVL products on the AMD V1000 processor with the Rage framework. The new product is a Unity-based framework with Pixi inside and an internal database, on the new FP7 hardware, in the cabinet GLI already approved (code name rendered "Dash Super"). The sample game for testing is Calle Ocho: "We're not putting it in the market… it doesn't need to function but it… provides a tie-in for you guys to test". Detail on [[wiki/topics/omni-core-advancement-and-game-templating.md]] (Jan 28 2026). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]
- **Timing, stated as estimates.** Asked for the lab-entry date, Pavel said "probably in May, mid-May." Val: "accept those as estimates". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]
- **RNG.** Pavel: the mask plus RNG used for online will be packaged "into a container" for land-based, "very close" to the previous platform. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]
- **How the two sides will work.** JVL writes white papers, GLI reviews them; GLI's commercial proposal waits until then; questions go through Val and Juan. See [[wiki/decisions/2026-01-28-gli-omni-certification-runs-on-jvl-white-papers.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]
- **What JVL owes GLI** (none has a date):
  1. A list of all peripherals, marking those GLI already approved, so all are tested in one pass. Juan: "We'll list them all and I'll put a note by previously approved." (The transcript renders "peripherals" once as "employees".)
  2. A white paper on how the math and RNG are built in the new framework — given to Efim: "So, Yefim, this is an undertaking for you".
  3. A white paper on how GLI can plug into JVL's ecosystem and get an output — taken by Val as "JVL's undertaking"; no person named.
  4. A white paper on testing the setup screens, since "we're changing practically every single screen". Val offered automation: "we could probably provide you with a lot of automation."
  [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]
- **GLI's testing approach (Dan).** Tools that compare screen images with a reference image, and a wish to run tests from JVL's own ecosystem so any deviation stands out. See [[wiki/people/dan-lewis.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_GLI_Omni_Platform_Certification_Kickoff-January-28-2026.md]]

## Sept 11 2026 — GLI as a paid regulatory adviser, not just a certifier

A new use of the relationship, ruled by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-11-nova-game-change-regulatory-procedure.md|Sept-11 9:30 AM call]]: JVL cannot answer its own question about what counts as a lawful "change" when an operator swaps, adds, enables, disables or bug-fixes a game on a machine already in the field — so it will buy the answer.

- **Why GLI and not the other lab.** "**I would probably start with GLI. They're the most savvy and they have the most resources to explain things to us**", and "**I think [Interlock] somewhat adheres to GLI's regulations.**" Val declined to put the second lab on the line "at this stage" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Game_Change_Regulatory_Procedure-September-11-2026.md]]. *("Interlock" is the transcript's rendering of the second lab; the derived notes read it as Intralot and the Sept-10 Georgia Lottery transcript produced the same garble for Intralot. Recorded, not asserted.)* [[raw/Meeting_Notes_NOVA_Game_Change_Regulatory_Procedure-September-11-2026.md]]
- **Val's terms for the session.** GLI must send someone who knows the subject: "**GLI needs to bring somebody on the subject who understands it. It's not a, it's not an administrative issue**", and prepped properly "**then in one meeting would probably iron it out**". He also expects to pay: "**they will ask for a payment. They don't want to consult for free… So let's make it a productive one.**" And he wants to be in the room himself, "**otherwise, which is going to be burning hours on it and nothing's going to come out**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Game_Change_Regulatory_Procedure-September-11-2026.md]].
- **Target: the week of September 14 2026** — "**Sometimes for next week**" — with [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]] owning the setup and the question list [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Game_Change_Regulatory_Procedure-September-11-2026.md]].

**This is an advisory engagement, not a submission.** It carries no certification date for any product, and in particular none for NOVA — see [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-11-the-game-change-question-goes-to-gli-as-a-paid-consultation.md]] and [[wiki/topics/nova-platform-architecture.md]].

## Oct 16 2026 — GLI as the door to Puerto Rico

At the [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-16-david-villari-zephyr-puerto-rico-distribution-and-beta-test.md|Oct-16 breakfast]], [[wiki/people/david-villari.md|David Villari]] of [[wiki/organizations/bad-dog-zephyr.md|Bad Dog / Zephyr]] described the Puerto Rico route. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]

- **The step.** "you got to get the certification, send it to the Puerto Rico Gaming Board, and then you're fine." Val: "You need certification from GLI. Yeah, it's a light certificate. It's an easy certificate." David: "If you're already class three certified, which you guys are". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]
- **A competitor's first GLI work.** Zephyr had "never been Gli certified before" (it used another lab for Georgia), got GLI certification for Puerto Rico, and says that "Kickstarted the class three for us". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_David_Villari_Zephyr_Puerto_Rico_Distribution_and_Beta_Test-October-16-2026.md]]
- **No date or product** for a JVL submission was set. See [[wiki/projects/puerto-rico-expansion.md]].

# GLI (Gaming Laboratories International)

Independent gaming test/certification lab. JVL must have its games approved by GLI (and hold the required licenses) before games can ship to a floor.

## Facts

- A GLI representative visited JVL in person on 2025-01-07 (met with Val and Juan) [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]].
- GLI charges by testing time — the example given was ~$170 per hour, e.g. "20 hours at $170" to approve a game [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]].
- JVL views GLI approval as a **bottleneck**: as JVL scales up game production, slow/expensive certification limits how fast games reach the floor [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]].
- Standards are layered: a base standard (e.g. "GLI 11") plus per-jurisdiction requirements (e.g. Georgia vs. Arkansas) [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]].
- Named GLI contacts: **Patrick**, **Dan**, and **Jason**. Dan identified the **math** portion as the most expensive block of GLI testing time [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]].

*The visiting GLI representative's name is unclear from the transcript (she came from Vegas, previously worked at Valley/Bally Corporate). Flagged rather than guessed.*

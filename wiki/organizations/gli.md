# GLI (Gaming Laboratories International)

GLI is the independent testing lab that certifies JVL's gaming machines and games so they can be sold into regulated markets. JVL sees the cost and slowness of GLI approval as a key bottleneck to scaling up game production.

## Relationship with JVL

- GLI has been in business about 40 years. [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]]
- JVL's account contacts at GLI: Deb (client-facing account manager, fairly new), [[wiki/people/patrick-gli.md|Patrick]] (now senior director, keeps oversight of JVL), and technical/testing people Dan, Jason, Joseph, and Joe. [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]]
- As an independent lab, GLI must stay neutral — it will not train or coach a client on how to test so as to pass, because that would risk its accreditation. This is what "proprietary" tends to mean in their conversations. [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]]

## Certification facts

- JVL's amusement product does not need to be tested. [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]]
- Class 3 approval is broken into stages; math testing is the most labour-intensive block, which Dan measures at close to 20 hours. [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]]
- Example GLI billing quoted: about 20 hours at $170/hour for a submission. [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]]
- Markets can require the GLI-11 standard, or GLI-11 plus additional state rules, or their own rules instead. A game already approved to GLI-11 can move to another market as a cheaper "transfer" with only the extra local testing (for online, the relevant standard is "19"). [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]]
- Live JVL approvals in progress: two games for South Dakota, two games for Florida, plus a separate 40-game online transfer proposal for about three states (Michigan, Pennsylvania, and another). [[raw/gli_visit-jan-7-2025.md]]

Account contact [[wiki/people/deb-gli.md|Deb]] (client-facing account manager). 

## Sept 11 2026 — GLI as a paid regulatory adviser, not just a certifier

A new use of the relationship, ruled by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-11-nova-game-change-regulatory-procedure.md|Sept-11 9:30 AM call]]: JVL cannot answer its own question about what counts as a lawful "change" when an operator swaps, adds, enables, disables or bug-fixes a game on a machine already in the field — so it will buy the answer.

- **Why GLI and not the other lab.** "**I would probably start with GLI. They're the most savvy and they have the most resources to explain things to us**", and "**I think [Interlock] somewhat adheres to GLI's regulations.**" Val declined to put the second lab on the line "at this stage" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Game_Change_Regulatory_Procedure-September-11-2026.md]]. *("Interlock" is the transcript's rendering of the second lab; the derived notes read it as Intralot and the Sept-10 Georgia Lottery transcript produced the same garble for Intralot. Recorded, not asserted.)* [[raw/Meeting_Notes_NOVA_Game_Change_Regulatory_Procedure-September-11-2026.md]]
- **Val's terms for the session.** GLI must send someone who knows the subject: "**GLI needs to bring somebody on the subject who understands it. It's not a, it's not an administrative issue**", and prepped properly "**then in one meeting would probably iron it out**". He also expects to pay: "**they will ask for a payment. They don't want to consult for free… So let's make it a productive one.**" And he wants to be in the room himself, "**otherwise, which is going to be burning hours on it and nothing's going to come out**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Game_Change_Regulatory_Procedure-September-11-2026.md]].
- **Target: the week of September 14 2026** — "**Sometimes for next week**" — with [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]] owning the setup and the question list [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_NOVA_Game_Change_Regulatory_Procedure-September-11-2026.md]].

**This is an advisory engagement, not a submission.** It carries no certification date for any product, and in particular none for NOVA — see [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-11-the-game-change-question-goes-to-gli-as-a-paid-consultation.md]] and [[wiki/topics/nova-platform-architecture.md]].

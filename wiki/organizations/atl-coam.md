# ATL COAM

**ATL COAM** is a Georgia COAM master licensee run by **Arpit**. He came to the JVL office in Lawrenceville on Oct 14 2026 for a demo of JVL's transactional equipment — [[wiki/meetings/2026-10-14-atl-coam-transactional-demo-show-special-and-daily-caps.md]]. The calendar invitee is "ATL COAM LLC" [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ATL_COAM_Transactional_Demo_Show_Special_and_Daily_Caps-October-14-2026.md]]. Arpit has no person page; he is described here.

## Who he is and what he runs (as he told it, Oct 14 2026)

- **On his own since 2022.** Val asked "Do you have any partners in the company? No, sir. By yourself? Yeah. When did you start it?" — "2022." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ATL_COAM_Transactional_Demo_Show_Special_and_Daily_Caps-October-14-2026.md]]
- **About fifty locations,** "Mostly in Metro Atlanta, but I do have a few locations in South Georgia." He does not operate in other states: "I thought about other states… But not yet. Too busy." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ATL_COAM_Transactional_Demo_Show_Special_and_Daily_Caps-October-14-2026.md]]
- **High-volume sites by choice.** "I would say eighty percent of them are high volumes. High volume, as in twenty plus, twenty thousand plus per day." He does not chase numbers of small shops: "I don't care about like generating like hundred locations, two hundred locations, as long as I have good size locations." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ATL_COAM_Transactional_Demo_Show_Special_and_Daily_Caps-October-14-2026.md]]
- **About five machines a location.** Another voice said six; he said "I would say five machines." "Aristocrat is dominating because of their games… And IGT is slowing down a little." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ATL_COAM_Transactional_Demo_Show_Special_and_Daily_Caps-October-14-2026.md]]
- **Idle and older equipment.** Close to sixty Banilla machines — "Right now, it's just sitting. I am using here and there. It's not completely dead." Eight or ten old IGTs, about six of them in locations. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ATL_COAM_Transactional_Demo_Show_Special_and_Daily_Caps-October-14-2026.md]]
- **JVL machines today.** "We have less than ten JVLs. Probably a couple at the warehouse"; some went with locations sold to a different master; "actively, I probably have four three games", and they are horizontal. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ATL_COAM_Transactional_Demo_Show_Special_and_Daily_Caps-October-14-2026.md]]
- **He reads game data.** "We work with a player profile or location, and we do analyze like how the games are performing." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ATL_COAM_Transactional_Demo_Show_Special_and_Daily_Caps-October-14-2026.md]]

## His pain points

- **Banilla's TITO system.** Asked for his biggest pain: "I would say TITO, Banilla's TITO system" — "the user experience", and "especially when the locations are not in metro area, it's pain when there's communication errors". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ATL_COAM_Transactional_Demo_Show_Special_and_Daily_Caps-October-14-2026.md]]
- **Daily caps from the card processors — the bigger one.** "these processing companies like InComm and them, they are for high volume locations. They have certain limits… a lot of stores are out by two p." One company "They've given a lot of my locations ten thousand per day. That includes Friday, Saturday, Sunday, Monday". Some locations "have to have multiple providers just for the gift cards". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ATL_COAM_Transactional_Demo_Show_Special_and_Daily_Caps-October-14-2026.md]] Val's answer is on [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]] (Oct 14) and [[wiki/organizations/incom.md]] (Oct 14, 2:26 PM).
- **His locations pay.** "They always keep a positive balance. They are always in good standing… None of them have ever had any issues with EFTs or anything." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ATL_COAM_Transactional_Demo_Show_Special_and_Daily_Caps-October-14-2026.md]]
- **Fees go to the locations soon.** "I'm paying some now, but pretty soon I'm starting to pass the fees along to my locations". Val: "We could collect from anybody as long as they pay." See [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-free-ports-and-pos-with-every-machine-bought.md]] (Oct 14, 2:26 PM). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ATL_COAM_Transactional_Demo_Show_Special_and_Daily_Caps-October-14-2026.md]]

## What JVL offered and where it stands

- **The offer:** the show special with free transactional equipment and $90 a month per location — [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-free-ports-and-pos-with-every-machine-bought.md]] (Oct 14, 2:26 PM) and [[wiki/topics/jvl-machine-pricing-strategy.md]] (Oct 14).
- **The ask.** Felipe wants a commitment on how many locations to test, because prices change after January 1: "it will not be under the current price." He also said JVL need not wait until November 1: "We'll sign an agreement, so we can take the game ahead of time and start testing it." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ATL_COAM_Transactional_Demo_Show_Special_and_Daily_Caps-October-14-2026.md]]
- **Val: one location first.** "Start with one location. Don't do any more. Start with one. Live with the issues… and then when you feel comfortable, roll out more." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ATL_COAM_Transactional_Demo_Show_Special_and_Daily_Caps-October-14-2026.md]]
- **No commitment yet.** The notes record no formal decision: "Offer terms were stated; ATL COAM did not commit." [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ATL_COAM_Transactional_Demo_Show_Special_and_Daily_Caps-October-14-2026.md]]
- **How JVL felt.** After he left, Val: "I feel very, very positive about the whole thing." Felipe agreed. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ATL_COAM_Transactional_Demo_Show_Special_and_Daily_Caps-October-14-2026.md]]

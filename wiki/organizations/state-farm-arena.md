# State Farm Arena (Atlanta Hawks premium seating)

State Farm Arena is the downtown Atlanta arena of the Atlanta Hawks. It also hosts concerts and shows. JVL's contact is its premium-hospitality seller, who introduced herself as "Caitlin from the State Farm Arena and the Atlanta Hawks" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_State_Farm_Arena_Suite_Options_Intro_Call-September-23-2026.md]]. The notes spell her "Caitlyn Tu", from the calendar invite; the surname is not spoken on the recording [[raw/Meeting_Notes_State_Farm_Arena_Suite_Options_Intro_Call-September-23-2026.md]].

JVL is a possible **customer**, not a partner: Val wants a suite to host clients in the Atlanta area. First call: [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-23-state-farm-arena-suite-options-intro-call.md]].

## What the arena offers (as quoted Sept 23 2026)

All figures are the seller's, spoken on the call [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_State_Farm_Arena_Suite_Options_Intro_Call-September-23-2026.md]]:

- **Per-event suite rental.** Price depends on the game or show and the head count — "some suite rentals that cost you know around three thousand dollars, and then some that cost twenty five thousand". A **$500** deposit gives priority to purchase and goes toward the total; the rest is due in full.
- **Premium Reserve.** A prepaid bank of money, **$25,000 minimum**, drawn down on suites or club seats as you choose. It is "a year-over-year commitment"; the fiscal year runs July 1 to June 30, so this year's funds last to June 30 2027. Unused money is not kept from you — you lose the priority to purchase.
- **Full-season suite lease.** From an 8-person suite up; "start looking at like two hundred and ten thousand. And then it goes all the way up to, you know, five hundred thousand". "Typically three or five year commitments", covering games and shows.
- **Loge box.** A 4-person version of the lease. Val said no: he needs 8 to 12.
- **Food and drink.** Bundled with the cost: "beer wine food water and soda are included with our suite leases"; liquor is not, and can be discussed.
- **Events.** "About fifty to sixty shows during a fiscal year", beside the Hawks season.

**The number Val asked for.** Four or five winter events, an 8–12 suite, with catering: "You could be looking at thirty to forty thousand", as individual rentals, not a lease. This season's suite rentals are already on sale, so inventory "might be a little bit different", but options remain [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_State_Farm_Arena_Suite_Options_Intro_Call-September-23-2026.md]].

*Inference, not a ruling:* Val's stated wish (five or six chosen games) fits per-event rentals, not the lease or the Premium Reserve. No purchase was made or agreed.

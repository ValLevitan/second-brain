# Elevate

JVL customer that buys JVL video lottery terminals, financed through EDC (Export Development Canada).

## Facts

- Had an EDC financing approval covering ~700 machines (originally quoted as video lottery terminals ~two years earlier) [[raw/james_reble_elevate_january_14_2025.md]].
- Paying for machines in tranches tied to shipments; the December shipment was the third 56-machine voucher, with three more shipments expected end of January / early February 2025 [[raw/james_reble_elevate_january_14_2025.md]].
- Contacts: **James Reble** and his CFO **Tim** [[raw/james_reble_elevate_january_14_2025.md]].

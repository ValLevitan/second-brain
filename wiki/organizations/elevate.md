# Elevate

Elevate is a **specialty lender / financier** (owner-confirmed). It made a loan to a gaming operator to buy JVL's machines and took a **pledge on those machines** as collateral. It is **not** a JVL customer — the machines are bought by the borrower, and Elevate finances the purchase. [[wiki/people/james-reble.md|James Reble]] is JVL's day-to-day contact there; **Scott Rambeau** is its CFO. [[raw/elevate_convo_w_scott_rambeau_january_15_2025.md]]

## Relationship with JVL (current — from the Scott Rambeau call, Jan 15)

- Elevate's CFO is **Scott Rambeau** (transcribed "Scott Rambo"), there "since day one." [[raw/elevate_convo_w_scott_rambeau_january_15_2025.md]]
- Elevate made a loan (a "second loan") secured by a **pledge on all the video terminals** being purchased; the collateral was originally described using an old May 11, 2022 quote (item "C10-VLOT-TERM-RU"). Scott called Val to confirm item numbers so he can amend the pledge agreement to match the current Flex SD27 / V43 machines. [[raw/elevate_convo_w_scott_rambeau_january_15_2025.md]]
- Elevate finances gaming operators (here, a borrower in Colombia) that Canadian and local Colombian banks will not underwrite; Scott's view is defaults are near-impossible because the borrower's gaming license is at stake. [[raw/elevate_convo_w_scott_rambeau_january_15_2025.md]]
- **Christine** is another Elevate-side correspondent, depending on the topic. Val was introduced to Elevate via "BDC" (likely Business Development Bank of Canada — inferred) and heard about Scott from Julian Porte. [[raw/elevate_convo_w_scott_rambeau_january_15_2025.md]]
- Funding was scheduled for the day after the call; last shipments end of February 2025; a "fourth advance" invoice exists; the last advance was revised/split because it "was in court." [[raw/elevate_convo_w_scott_rambeau_january_15_2025.md]]

## Deal logistics

- The financed quantity is about **700 machines**, originally described on the securing quote as "video lottery terminals"; the invoices show the current Flex model names and different unit prices, which is why the pledge agreement needs amending. [[raw/elevate_convo_w_scott_rambeau_january_15_2025.md]] [[raw/james_reble_elevate_january_14_2025.md]]
- The machines ship in batches ("vouchers"/advances) of 56; the December shipment was the third; three more were expected around end of January / early February. Payment is released per shipment (the final tranche once [[wiki/people/mary.md|Mary]] shared the paperwork). [[raw/james_reble_elevate_january_14_2025.md]]

## History

- **≈May 2024 (date inferred; pre-window source):** Val described the approval chain to Julian Puerta: exporting machines out of Canada is a precondition ("if you do not export machines out of Canada, then you're not going to get Elevate to finance"), and "we have another company, which is called a government company, called **BDC, Business Development Canada**. So they first pre-approve you, then they recommend you to **EDC**, then **EDC brings Elevate**. It's a big, big, big chain" — with requirements like employing people, audited financial statements and years in business. He also noted Julian "already know[s] Elevate finance … how they work," consistent with the later note that Val heard about Scott Rambeau through Julian Porte. The plan discussed then: invoice machines at ~$8,000 (≈$4,500 imported hardware + ≈$3,500 software) with Elevate financing the whole amount over five years. [[raw/julian_puerta_jvl_mexico.md]]
- **The Jan 14 call (James Reble) initially framed Elevate as a JVL *customer* that buys the machines and finances them through *EDC*, paying JVL per shipment.** [[raw/james_reble_elevate_january_14_2025.md]] **Owner ruling (2026-07-13):** this was the Jan-14 meeting's own early misunderstanding, corrected by the Jan-15 Scott Rambeau call — Elevate is a **lender** that took a pledge on the machines, not a customer, and the "financed through EDC" detail belongs to that superseded framing. Current state is the lender description above.

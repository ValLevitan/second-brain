# Elevate

Elevate is the finance company involved in the JVL machine sale. James Reble is JVL's day-to-day contact there and Scott Rambeau is its CFO. Its exact role — customer vs. lender — is described differently across two sources (see Open contradictions). [[raw/james_reble_elevate_january_14_2025.md]] [[raw/elevate_convo_w_scott_rambeau_january_15_2025.md]]

## Relationship with JVL

- Elevate's EDC financing approval covered about 700 machines, originally described as "video lottery terminals." Elevate wanted JVL's actual invoices (which show different unit prices and Flex model names) to line up with that original approval. [[raw/james_reble_elevate_january_14_2025.md]]
- The order is being shipped in batches ("vouchers") of 56 machines. The December shipment was the third 56-machine voucher; three more shipments were expected, targeting end of January / early February. [[raw/james_reble_elevate_january_14_2025.md]]
- Elevate pays JVL per shipment; the final tranche was to be released once Mary shared the paperwork. [[raw/james_reble_elevate_january_14_2025.md]]
- Elevate has a CFO (unnamed in the Jan 14 transcript) in addition to James Reble. Elevate uses Zoom; James mentioned wanting to migrate to Teams. [[raw/james_reble_elevate_january_14_2025.md]]

## From the Scott Rambeau call (Jan 15)

- Elevate's CFO is **Scott Rambeau** (transcribed "Scott Rambo"), there "since day one." [[raw/elevate_convo_w_scott_rambeau_january_15_2025.md]]
- Elevate made a loan (a "second loan") secured by a **pledge on all the video terminals** being purchased; the collateral was originally described using an old May 11, 2022 quote (item "C10-VLOT-TERM-RU"). Scott called Val to confirm item numbers so he can amend the pledge agreement to match the current Flex SD27 / V43 machines. [[raw/elevate_convo_w_scott_rambeau_january_15_2025.md]]
- Elevate finances gaming operators (here, a borrower in Colombia) that Canadian and local Colombian banks will not underwrite; Scott's view is defaults are near-impossible because the borrower's gaming license is at stake. [[raw/elevate_convo_w_scott_rambeau_january_15_2025.md]]
- **Christine** is another Elevate-side correspondent, depending on the topic. Val was introduced to Elevate via "BDC" (likely Business Development Bank of Canada — inferred) and heard about Scott from Julian Porte. [[raw/elevate_convo_w_scott_rambeau_january_15_2025.md]]
- Funding was scheduled for the day after the call; last shipments end of February 2025; a "fourth advance" invoice exists; the last advance was revised/split because it "was in court." [[raw/elevate_convo_w_scott_rambeau_january_15_2025.md]]

## Open contradictions

- **What Elevate is — customer or lender.** The Jan 14 call (James Reble) is recorded as Elevate being *a JVL customer that buys the machines and finances them through EDC*, paying JVL per shipment. [[raw/james_reble_elevate_january_14_2025.md]] The Jan 15 call (Scott Rambeau) describes Elevate as *a specialty lender/financier* that loaned to a Colombian gaming operator to buy the JVL machines and took a pledge on those machines as collateral — with no mention of EDC, but a "BDC" introduction. Both descriptions are recorded; not resolved. [[raw/elevate_convo_w_scott_rambeau_january_15_2025.md]]

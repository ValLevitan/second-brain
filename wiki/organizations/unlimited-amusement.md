# Unlimited Amusement

A **Georgia COAM operator** and an **existing JVL customer** ("Maybe brother, you already know. **We already buy machines**") that took the Mega Hit Blaze V3 + transactional-bundle pitch at the JVL Georgia office on **September 15 2026**, 2:24–3:35 PM ET ([[wiki/meetings/2026-09-15-unlimited-amusement-mega-hit-blaze-v3-and-bundle-pitch.md]]). It sits in the Aug–Sept 2026 Georgia operator-visit run ([[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]]), and is unusual in that run for how much of the market teaching comes from the buying side rather than from JVL. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Unlimited_Amusement_Mega_Hit_Blaze_V3_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pitch-September-15-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Unlimited_Amusement_Mega_Hit_Blaze_V3_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pitch-September-15-2026.md]]

The name first appears in the corpus one day earlier, on **Sept 14 2026**, as a colleague's "**2:00 PM Tuesday meeting with unlimited amusement**" — at which point it was a bare mention and too thin for a page ([[wiki/meetings/2026-09-14-competitor-field-reconnaissance-and-class-a-visits.md]]). The Outlook invite for this session is titled "Unlimited Amusement - JVL transactional", 2:00–3:25 PM ET, organised by [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda Giorgi]]; the recording runs 2:24–3:35 PM. ## What they told JVL about the Georgia market

This is the substantive value of the visit: an operator's own read of who plays, where, and what breaks.

**Who plays, by headcount and by spend.** The exchange is muddled and the two men talk over each other, so it is recorded as spoken rather than tidied:

- **By number of players**, the operator: "**mostly African-American community.** The first, second is like **Spanish**… Fourth, you can say **Chinese**." Indian-Pakistani players he put at "maybe **ten percent or fifteen percent**." His illustration of the ratio, as spoken: "if you have like **six thousand** mutation, maybe **five hundred, five thousand, five hundred** mutation black people" *(the self-correcting number series and the word "mutation" are both left exactly as transcribed; **no ratio is extracted from them**)*.
- **By dollars per head**, [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] proposed and the operator agreed a different order: "On a **per dollar basis, Chinese probably number one, Spanish number two**" — operator: "**Blacks number three. Chinese number one.**"
- **Chinese-community rooms are a different tier.** "their bed starts from most of the location. **Bed maximum is like five thousand, six thousand. Over there, fifteen**" — and on the bet size, "I think **five dollar, ten dollar, twenty**. I don't know which one." *(Whether "bed" is bet or cash-out ceiling is not stated; left as spoken.)* "But only few in like whole Atlanta." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Unlimited_Amusement_Mega_Hit_Blaze_V3_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pitch-September-15-2026.md]]

**Where they play — the operator's geography of metro Atlanta.** "They **divide the area**… If you go to **Singleton** area, this is all Spanish… Same thing there in **Lawrenceville**, all Spanish. **Swanee** is mostly Chinese. **Gainesville** is a Spanish area." White population: "**Johns Creek** and this area… If you go **eighty-five towards north, Hartwell, Commerce**, they are white people," and "as soon as you cross **Buford and Jefferson**, it's mostly a white population because they have **big farms**… **Only 30 acre farms**." On Augusta: "a mixed population with white people. But if you go **85 towards South Carolina**, they are all white people." *(One place name is rendered "Jimmy Carter in North Carolina is very Hispanic" and is **not** interpreted; the place-name spellings above are the transcript's own.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Unlimited_Amusement_Mega_Hit_Blaze_V3_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pitch-September-15-2026.md]]

**The regulated-vs-grey framing, from both sides.** The operator: "in Georgia, I think in the **last fifteen years**, the black market made normal people do one thing or the other" — and, on why: "**Georgia, loti never allowed to pay cash**, and before everyone paying cash" *("loti" is left as transcribed; the Georgia Lottery is the obvious reading and is **not** written into the quote)*. Val reframed it: "that's **not black money. That's actually legitimate money**… what you're talking about is **the evolution from unregulated to regulated**," and put the risk on the location rather than the operator — "what we don't want and what you don't want, **black market, cheap location, where location circumvents the card and takes it**." His conclusion: "**Everybody who became billionaires**… from grass roots to driving Mercedes now. **That business will disappear if you do not convert into normal payout.** And in order to convert to normal payouts, **these companies will make money.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Unlimited_Amusement_Mega_Hit_Blaze_V3_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pitch-September-15-2026.md]]

## Their problems, in their own words

- **Replay is the number-one complaint, and it is about the card, not the machine.** "Only the problem we are right now is **not about the machine; it's about that system**… we **cannot transfer from one machine to the** [other]." And: "Once they put it in, they cash it out. They need to do the card, then **they cannot replay**… So how can the customer have cash to play that? **That's the biggest issue. My numbers are going down**… **The biggest problem is the replay right now.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Unlimited_Amusement_Mega_Hit_Blaze_V3_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pitch-September-15-2026.md]]
- **They have already bought a credit-transfer system that is not approved.** "there's **black box** which is like from one machine to other machine you can transfer the credit"… "almost like **ten plus location** is still [waiting] because **they promise very soon**, from one machine to other machine is coming. **It's still not approved yet.** It's still with Georgia or TC process, but **we already bought the system**." Their ask of JVL follows directly from it: "we just wanna make sure if we are getting **everything is ready to go and ready to use from very first day**." *(Who supplies the "black box" is not stated; "TC process" is garbled and not interpreted.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Unlimited_Amusement_Mega_Hit_Blaze_V3_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pitch-September-15-2026.md]]
- **Every vendor has told them the same thing.** "One company exit some… They say, okay, do our. **This is the best thing.** Then **easy pay**. Final **InComm**… Then **IGBT MT3** is coming. So **each one have say, our system is the best**. Rest is we got. In our **four five locations, it's like so much problems**." *(The competitor names here — "exit one", "easy pay", "IGBT MT3" — are speech-to-text renderings and are **not** matched to any real company. The exception: **InComm** is the standing correction ruled by Val on 2026-09-16, see below.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Unlimited_Amusement_Mega_Hit_Blaze_V3_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pitch-September-15-2026.md]]
- **Card fees anger the players more than losses do.** Fees as they described them: "the fees range from **two to three dollars**… **three dollars for installation activation fees**. After this, anytime if I use an ATM [they charge]." The player reaction: "If I win seven thousand dollars, I can pay you five hundred dollars as a tip. **But if you see the card companies charging me to withdraw one dollar, I'm mad.**" And on limits: "They say **five in thousand dollar, why I pay each time? And they have limit.**" Their own workaround: "if you have one customer who puts **seven or eight thousand a month** in your machine, **we don't charge ATM fees. Just pay ATM fees from your pocket.**" See [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Unlimited_Amusement_Mega_Hit_Blaze_V3_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pitch-September-15-2026.md]]
- **Locations run out of cards, and the fix today is to borrow from the shop next door.** "if they have **ten cards left**, they try to call us"; if nobody answers, "they just call other store. They say, **can you bring the card?**… take them **ten twenty card**. As soon as your card come, you can get back." Val pressed the failure case — "**But what if they don't?**" — and the answer was "**Nobody has that situation yet. But they will.**" One provider is credited with solving it by automation: "they **automatic notification**… they send you **200 cards**, and their software issue like **170 cards**[, then the] automatic system sends the notification" and more cards arrive on time — "**This is the only one. If they don't, you're out of business.**" *(That provider is rendered "**Ferry Bay**", "**ferry pay**" and "**PayPay**" across three lines. JVL's known Georgia competitor of that name is **Fiery Pay** ([[wiki/meetings/2026-08-12-fiery-pay-competitive-analysis.md]]) — a plausible match and **recorded here as an inference only**, not asserted, since the source never spells it.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Unlimited_Amusement_Mega_Hit_Blaze_V3_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pitch-September-15-2026.md]]
- **They pay for service at their good locations.** Against Val's "**I've been in the business for 40 years. I've never seen a location pay for it**", the operator: "**here mostly all they charge to locations**" — and "of course, **if it's good, good location, and they ask us, we pay**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Unlimited_Amusement_Mega_Hit_Blaze_V3_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pitch-September-15-2026.md]]
- **Most but not all of their players have a smartphone** — put at "out of a hundred, maybe **eighty people have good phones**" when Val offered phone-delivered tickets. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Unlimited_Amusement_Mega_Hit_Blaze_V3_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pitch-September-15-2026.md]]

## What JVL offered them

The machine and the free-hardware bundle, in the same shape being put to every Georgia master through August–September 2026 — composition and prices on [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-free-ports-and-pos-with-every-machine-bought.md]] and [[wiki/topics/jvl-machine-pricing-strategy.md]]; the game itself on [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]]; the e-wallet demo on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]].

Two things said here that bear on the relationship specifically:

- **Val told them JVL's own regulator and card partner do not yet know how the market will work** — "I met with **Georgia Lottery Corporation**. They are not clear on how everything will function. The lawyer for the association, **[[wiki/people/joe-kim.md|Joe Kim]]**… He is not sure how everything will function. **InComm, the card provider, not sure which card they're gonna end up with**" — and used that uncertainty as the argument for buying from a local technology supplier that will "**grow with you**" rather than from a fixed product. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Unlimited_Amusement_Mega_Hit_Blaze_V3_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pitch-September-15-2026.md]]
- **The customer's card provider is undecided and stayed undecided.** The derived notes record it as a decision of the meeting: "**No decision on the card provider. Left open.**" [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Unlimited_Amusement_Mega_Hit_Blaze_V3_and_Transactional_Bundle_Pitch-September-15-2026.md]]

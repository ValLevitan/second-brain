# Burger King (Playlands machine customer)

A legacy JVL customer: "по их словам, 200 машин когда-то было куплено" — about **200 JVL amusement machines bought at some point** for Burger King **Playlands**. The account resurfaced in April 2025 through [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] ([[wiki/meetings/2025-04-15-klimovich-update-burger-king-online.md]]). Contact name given: **Gary Giger** ("Гэри Гигер"; whether he or Burger King was on the original invoice was being checked). [[raw/andrei _klimovich_update-apr-15-2025.md]]

## State of the account (Apr 15 2025)

- **Broken units:** 10 tabletop machines and 6 "Волей" (rendered "Volley"-type; older model) not working. The "Волей" model is unrestorable — "ни деталей, ничего невозможно… Эта тема закрыта" (roughly 7–10 years old; no parts, no restoration plans). [[raw/andrei _klimovich_update-apr-15-2025.md]]
- **Expansion claim:** Burger King "якобы расширяются и хотят вернуться обратно к Playlands"; Klimovich found no confirmation — public signals point the other way (BK "активно закрывал эти детские площадки"). Recorded as their claim, unverified. [[raw/andrei _klimovich_update-apr-15-2025.md]]
- **Repair path ruled by Val:** no remote "Repair Kit" — BK should **ship the 10 machines (to Canada)**, JVL opens them, gives an estimate, repairs what's approved or returns/parts-out the rest ("клиенту мозги дурить вообще не надо. Пусть он пришлет эти 10 машин"). Distance-repair is impractical: parts substitutions and refactoring over the years, no proper change documentation, possible non-original customer repairs. [[raw/andrei _klimovich_update-apr-15-2025.md]]
- **"Repair Kit" is not a JVL concept:** Val — the term "never in [its] life circulated in JVL … это называется oxymoron"; the history behind the misconception: pre-May-2023 "ЭКО" builds (as transcribed) used the old CPU + VGA-connector monitor, so a monitor failure forced replacing CPU + harness + monitor together, which Felipe's circle had loosely called a repair kit (~$1,500 in that telling); there was also one COVID-era defect-driven replacement wave. [[raw/andrei _klimovich_update-apr-15-2025.md]]
- **Pricing rules for the account (Val):** spare parts are quoted at **MSRP** (distributors get a 25% discount ≈ "30% маркап"; direct clients get no discount); machines — current **distributor price $3,050**, retail "$4,000 … Посмотрите на Амазоне, вот 3,99[9]"; BK gets the distributor price **only if they ask** for a discount (they have a good record and are treated like a distributor). Tariff urgency is a legitimate sales lever — "IGT выслало всем письмо, где они открыто говорят о тарифах… Всем операторам в Джорджии получили эти письма"; future inventory will carry tariff costs. [[raw/andrei _klimovich_update-apr-15-2025.md]]
- Side note from the CPU story: replacing the old CPU incidentally improved responsiveness — "это unintentional changes, над которыми мы не работали специально." Arkady (identity still unresolved — see Owner Decisions) reported decommissioned old Georgia machines with the same CPU as BK's old units, a possible parts source. [[raw/andrei _klimovich_update-apr-15-2025.md]]

## The negotiation ladder (Apr 18 2025)

On the Apr 18 Klimovich update ([[wiki/meetings/2025-04-18-klimovich-update-orders-tariffs-burger-king.md]]) Val laid out a staged pricing plan for the account (client has ~**200 old machines** and states an intent to buy new ones): [[raw/andrei_klimovich_update-apr-18-2025.md]]

- **Step 1 — quote the Repair Kit as-is: $2,500** (plus ~20% tariff), while repeating the goodwill offer to have BK ship the broken units in for a cheaper in-house diagnosis/repair. Val's eyeball estimate of the real monitor+touchscreen repair is ~**$600**; the full monitor "sandwich" MSRP alone is ~$1,500. Expect a negative reaction. [[raw/andrei_klimovich_update-apr-18-2025.md]]
- **Step 2 — discount the Repair Kit to ~$2,100** (~15%, near distributor cost), framed as loyal-partner goodwill; "less than 15% we can't." [[raw/andrei_klimovich_update-apr-18-2025.md]]
- **Step 3 — discount new machines: $750 off the $4,000 retail = 18% → $3,240/machine** (still above the **$3,050** distributor price / bundle floor). Tariffs (~20%) sit on top of everything. [[raw/andrei_klimovich_update-apr-18-2025.md]]
- **Separate option — trade-in:** take the old machines and replace them with new ones at the 18% price; but the old machines yield only ~**$150** of reusable parts (per [[wiki/people/arkady-batson.md|Arkady]]) — effectively **just the cabinets**, which could be re-outfitted for reselling used **ECO** slot machines to **Latin America** (disassembly is man-hours). Val's rule: **do not combine trade-in with the discounts** (buyers with selective memory will try to stack them). [[raw/andrei_klimovich_update-apr-18-2025.md]]
- **The "kit" origin (Val, again):** an unqualified predecessor (Val fired him and his manager) coined it — a CPU failure led to shipping a set of parts, then the new CPU wouldn't mate the old monitor, so the monitor got added, and the bundle became "the kit." [[raw/andrei_klimovich_update-apr-18-2025.md]]

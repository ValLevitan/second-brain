# Play Payments (PlayPay)

A ten-year-old Atlanta payments company that holds a **banking licence** and issues a **reloadable virtual Visa debit card** out of a player wallet. It entered the corpus on **September 2 2026**, when its founder [[wiki/people/billy-liu.md|Billy Liu]] spent an evening at the JVL Georgia office demonstrating it ([[wiki/meetings/2026-09-02-billy-liu-playpay-partnership-deep-dive.md|the partnership deep-dive]]) and [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] ended the meeting by proposing to buy half the company ([[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-val-proposes-fifty-percent-of-play-payments.md]]) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

**Naming.** The system is **PlayPay**; the app is **PayApp**. Billy states both plainly: "Our system is called PlayPay… **Our app is called PayApp**." The company is **Play Payments**; the derived notes give the pairing in the attendee line as "Billy Liu (Play Payments / PlayPay)" [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]. The transcript also renders the wallet "PlayPlay wallet" and "Play Pay wallet" in places — the same product [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

## The banking licence — the thing that changed everything

- **Ten years as a side project, then the licence arrived.** "Play Payments is the company, and **it's ten years old**. I've been working behind the scenes, doing things, and then magic happened. **We received the banking license**… I had it as a hobby. But **when the banking license arrived, it was kind of like an epiphany**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].
- **When it arrived is stated vaguely and never pinned down.** Asked directly "When did it happen?", Billy answered "It happened late" and trailed off; minutes later he said "It arrived in **twenty three, twenty four**… I forgot. Time passes," and a JVL colleague put it at "**It's been two years. I remember the Davis GAMOA meeting about a year ago.**" Billy accepted that — "You told me exactly. So I lost a little track of time there." **No single date is stated**, and none is asserted here [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].
- **A gaming licence disqualifies you from a banking licence — the structural moat.** "**If you have a gaming license, you won't get a banking license**… The **treasury department** will check on you, and make sure that you know gaming isn't. Because **traditionally they're polar opposites**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].
- **The process took ten years and about ten interviews, four of them lost to COVID.** "They interviewed me like **ten times in the whole process in ten years**. It didn't just come. It took a while"; "**doing COVID, which took away four years**." He nearly quit: "I was getting ready to give up during COVID because nothing was happening. **I was paying fees out the wazoo**… You need partners to do the whole money movement" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].
- **The stated next ambition:** "**The next thing I want to do is buy a bank**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

## How a player gets in — six fields, no documents

- **Registration is a handful of fields and no ID.** Billy says "**six pieces of information**," but the items he actually names are four: "**First name, last name. You create an access code. It can be the last four of your social**… and then your **handle** that needs to be **at least eight characters long**." *(The count and the list do not match in the source; both are recorded as spoken and nothing is invented to fill the gap.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]
- **No documents at all.** Val: "Which documents are you looking for? Passport." Billy: "**No. Nothing. No social security, no passport.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]
- **The handle is the address.** "It's not a phone number. **It's a handle**… It's how we send to each other," carried as a QR code: "**I have a QR code to represent my handle.** If this thing has a scanner, scan, then the money goes to here." Billy's own handle predates the eight-character rule — "I'm special because **all the new signups have to have eight characters**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Refusal is effectively gone once the account exists.** Asked "Can you refuse somebody?" — "**Not anymore. He has an account. We could turn someone off if we suspect fraud**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

## The card itself

- **Virtual first, reloadable, and it goes into the phone wallet.** "It's prepaid, but **it's reloadable**. It's **virtual first**"; "Now it's going to Visa, and it'll display with the **Visa logo**"; "**I can add it to whichever phone this is. Apple Pay. Now it's legal tender. I can tap anywhere I shop.** If you have an Android phone, same thing. Google" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].
- **One card, reloaded, FDIC secured, up to $100,000.** "This is a reloadable card. **I can reload as many times as I want. Just one card**… **Up to a hundred thousand dollars. This card is FDIC secured. It's bankable.**" Not in one go, though: "**Not all at once. There are some breaks**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].
- **It is a debit card, and it does not touch the player's credit file.** "It's debit. It's not credit… The difference is one is prepaid, one is postpaid"; and on credit rating, "**Debit cards do not get rated**… This is a what's called a **non-bank**… a non-bank is a **prepaid limiting card**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Money leaves four ways.** "I can send this money to a **debit card**… to **PayPal**, to **Venmo**, or **push it back to my bank account**. This is legal tender" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].
- **The card must be funded before it exists.** "**He has to load the first amount in order to get a card**"; the sequence is "Add a card. Create your virtual card. Then it says initial amount. Put an amount, and it'll create it." So the wallet holds the winnings first, and the player then chooses how much to convert: "the player decides how much money he wants to convert, all hundred five dollars, or only twenty" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Visa will not supply blank plastic — and that is Visa's decision, not PlayPay's.** "**we don't provide a blank one. Visa won't either.** If they ask, they told us they don't want to go backwards"; Visa's words as Billy reports them: "'**If you want to do this, we'll support you. We'll give you the best technology, but we don't want to go backwards and offer a physical card.**'" What survives is a player-initiated order: "**The player him or herself can order a physical card**" once the account exists. So a JVL kiosk cannot dispense a PlayPay card — "It's only a digital wallet, a digital virtual card" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

## The fee stack

Extracted by Val over several passes, and internally inconsistent in places. Everything below is recorded as stated.

| Item | As stated | Source line |
|---|---|---|
| Load onto the Visa card | **$2 per $1,000 tranche** | "It costs two dollars to put the entire sum up to one thousand dollars… **it'll be another two dollars for the next tranche of a thousand**" |
| Maximum per load | **$1,000** | "**A thousand is maximum. A thousand is the so-called Georgia thing**" |
| Maximum per person per day | **$2,500**, "**because of money laundering rules**" | "We allow up to twenty-five hundred per day per person" |
| Cost of that daily maximum | **$6** = three loads | "Six dollars… Three loads… **But you load it one time. It just charges you six on it**" |
| Push back out (to a machine or location) | **$2 per push** | "**Two dollars per push. Per thousand of the push. It could be more than that**" |
| Wallet-to-wallet, before conversion to Visa | **"virtually no fees"** | "There's virtually no fees except when they convert… **Hundred dollars will come here as hundred dollars**" |
| Back-end revenue share | **~half a percent**, tiered | "Half a percent, more or less, because **they're tiers**" |

[[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]

- **The caps do not reconcile inside the source.** Alongside the $1,000 / $2,500-per-day figures Billy also says "**we can allow four thousand dollar tranches**" and, separately, "We can allow the load higher. **In fact, we allowed up to ten thousand dollars.**" All three are recorded; the source never reconciles them, and no single limit is asserted here [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Where the $1,000 cap comes from, in Billy's account.** Not the lottery: "**It's not lottery. It's more about the gaming. Since we don't have a gaming commission here in Georgia**, as well as I do, **in the jurisdictions that do have, they have a twelve hundred dollar upper limit. But the big beautiful raised it to two thousand**"; JVL's working rule is InComm's — "**We're just following the thousand dollar cap rule that [InComm] imposes.**" *("the big beautiful" is transcribed as spoken and is not expanded here.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]
- **The push fee is described as going to the location, not to PlayPay.** "There is an **unload fee, which rewards the location**." That matters because it is the one fee in the stack that already flows the way Val argues all front fees should — see [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-val-proposes-fifty-percent-of-play-payments.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Val identified the weakness immediately: it costs the player money to go back into a machine.** "If you want to load ten, **it'll cost you twelve dollars**." Billy's explanation: "Because you're doing an unload. **It's the same thing.**" The workaround Billy uses himself is to leave money in the wallet rather than convert it: "Now you understand **why I keep money. So that I can play**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Where the real revenue is, and Billy could not size it.** Asked flatly "if you transacted a billion dollars, how much money would you make?", Billy answered "**I don't know the answer because I have to calculate**" and then "**I don't know the exact answer because there is** [tiers]." His description of the mechanism: "A Visa card, when they go and spend money, **Visa has a program where we share**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

## How it reaches a player today — and why the store still matters

- **At the point of redemption the player picks card or wallet.** Billy's description of the flow through a partner's kiosk: "He says, '**Hey, you want this in a physical gift card or [PlayPay] wallet?**' They say, 'What is that?' Well, right here. **Scan the QR code**" — and if the player already has an account, "**I can send that money directly to your wallet from him. Our API just needs his QR code**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].
- **The store is not bypassed — settlement runs through it.** Asked whether the wallet cuts the location out: "**Settlement involves the store. We have that. Play payments has to have an ACH transactional settlement with the store in order for this to work. It goes into the machine from the player's wallet**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].
- **The phone is meant to be the ticket.** "It's self-service… **your phone is your TITO ticket. You upload credits, download credits, go to the next machine**" — which Val immediately qualified: "**Only if the hardware permits. On the machine.**" That hardware gap is the whole reason the two companies were talking [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].
- **Nothing works for a player who has never heard of PlayPay.** Val pushed this point for most of an hour — "The player has nothing. Doesn't know about you" — and Billy's answer was that there is no product in that case: "**Then why would the player want to use his credits out of his wallet? There's no point**"; "He could, but he doesn't. **If he doesn't, then he can't ask for the wallet**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

## Where it already sits in the Georgia market

- **EasyLink built PlayPay in as its digital redemption option.** "**Easy Links came to us and asked us, hey, would you like to provide a digital solution for us? I said yeah. We built an API for them**"; and at the show, "they announced that they will use **PlayPay as a digital solution**." At redemption "you can choose **PlayPay digital wallet or gift card, physical gift card**. So that thing… **sends via our API… the money straight to the wallet, and they're off**." *(The source renders the partner "Easy Links" / "Easy Link"; the corpus carries this competitor as **EasyLink**. Billy also gestures at the kiosk half of that stack — "give and take. The German company, that the kiosk guys… **Kevate kiosk**" — which matches the **Gewete** kiosk maker already on [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]]; read as Gewete from context, an inference, not a corrected quote. A third name, "**Batten and EasyLanes**," is offered by a JVL voice and is not identified.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]
- **It competes with [[wiki/organizations/incom.md|InComm]] on the card network.** "**We compete against [InComm] because we offer a wallet that is a Visa wallet**" — against InComm's Mastercard prepaid product. Billy's read on InComm's Skill Pay: "I heard that **skill pay isn't coming or isn't gonna come easily**. I don't know all the details" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].
- **It sells into locations that have nothing else.** "**we've been successful with locations that don't have shit. MudaGoose is all they have.**" Val's response was that this is not a strategy: "**Billy, location does not give a fuck about your problem. Your product needs to be pushed and pushed**… As long as I comply with that stupid [GLC] regulation, I don't give a fuck what you put in here. **That's all they care**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

## The legal theory the product rests on

Billy's account of why pushing money to a location is lawful where handing over cash is not — the "two-step" reading of the Georgia Lottery Corporation's rule: "**When I push the location, this is the same thing as giving them cash**… **As long as there's a two-step process, as long as you just don't hand the cash over, you're not breaking the law.**" A JVL voice supplied the rule he was invoking: "**Redemption stops at the moment that a gift card is created**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]].

This is the same two-step doctrine the corpus already carries from the InComm side — see [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-26-no-cash-from-coam-redemption-device-for-skill-pay.md]] and [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]] — stated here by a competing processor rather than by InComm.

- **What are the real load limits?** $1,000 per load, $2,500 per person per day, $4,000 tranches and "up to ten thousand dollars" are all stated in the same conversation and never reconciled — [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]
- **Does the exclusivity Billy asked for extend to the EasyLink relationship he already has?** He asked JVL not to offer InComm and not to dispense physical cards, while PlayPay itself sits inside a competitor's kiosk stack alongside a physical gift card at the same redemption screen. Not raised by either side — [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Partnership_Deep_Dive-September-2-2026.md]]

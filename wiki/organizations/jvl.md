# JVL (group of companies)

JVL is a **gaming technology company** — it builds and operates gaming machines (EGMs), develops software platforms, runs a distributed international workforce, and holds commercial real estate as a secondary activity. (source: JVL Constitution — ~/Projects/Constitution/) It operates as a group of related entities sharing leadership, standards, and operating principles. Val Levitan, President, holds final decision authority across all entities; **Inna Levitan** is the majority owner of the group's holding companies. See the governing document at [[wiki/topics/jvl-operating-principles.md]].

## What JVL does

- Val describes JVL to the bank as "an engineering company; we have engineering product and amusement industry." [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]]
- The 2025 strategy positions JVL as a small, fast-moving competitor against larger gaming-technology companies, competing on speed to market and lean operations rather than head-to-head development scale. [[raw/jvl_strategic_plan_and_vision_2025_2025-01-08.md]]
- JVL is deliberately run as a small company; every process, document, and decision is judged against "is this necessary?" (source: JVL Constitution — ~/Projects/Constitution/)

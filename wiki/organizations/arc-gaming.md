# ARC Gaming

A **Georgia COAM master operator** that is also its own **transactional broker** — it issues the gift cards its players redeem into, and carries the cash exposure for doing so. It runs **"more than 200 locations"** in Georgia and self-ranks **"number two or three, give or take"** among the state's masters, though its COO qualified the ranking in the same breath: "**We don't compare the amount of locations. We compare the comparison between**…" Asked whether he meant decals or game revenue, his answer is garbled — "I don't know the amount of revenue you make. This is what makes you. **This is how the GLC says we stop.** Obviously, the more locations you are top there" — so the basis of the #2–3 claim is **not** clearly established. (Val had opened by guessing "with 170 locations, are you number three or four?") [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

Its COO is [[wiki/people/georgios-zazanis.md|Georgios "Zaza" Zazanis]]; its CEO is **David Sloan**. The main record is the [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-31-arc-gaming-transactional-demo-class-a.md|Aug 31 2026 transactional demo]]. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

**Name note (§4).** "Arc" has appeared in this corpus before **in senses that are not confirmed to be this company**: a competitor kiosk vendor ("Arc — Dean's company", with Mark Martinez pitching a MudaGoose + back-end-TITO dual-print integration, and later "Mark Martinez, ex-Arc, selling a $10K kiosk"), and "Lucky Bucks / Arc Luck" in Felipe's Feb-2026 master map. Those mentions are left where they are on [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]] and **nothing has been merged into this page**. What *is* well-supported is the customer thread: the Aug-21 2026 pipeline records "**ARC** wants to buy the **5 machines currently on trial** and place an order for **15 more**, delivering from the first week of September," which lines up with Zazanis's own closing item on Aug 31 about "the remaining five" — an **inference**, labelled as such. ## How ARC's money actually moves (Aug 31 2026)

This is the clearest account in the corpus of a Georgia master running its own card brokerage, and it is the setup for everything JVL pitched.

- **ARC is the broker, and therefore the one exposed.** "We are the broker." Val pushed until the liability was named: "the location is exposed to the card processor, but the card processor is me… you're on the hook for five hundred dollars." Zazanis accepted it — "That's what I'm saying. My exposure." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **Two thresholds, and a hard cap of $10K per day.** "there are two different thresholds. There is a threshold generated by [InComm]. So if [InComm] is back to back, they will shut down the location. And then it's the threshold that we set up as a broker, and it goes based on the tier or the relationship that exists. **It doesn't go more than 10k. The biggest one we might have today is 10k. Our exposure is 10k per day.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **The daily cycle is manual.** "[InComm] sends a big file with all the Excel transactions. We know which location and how much they are liable for." Then: "every morning… We are supposed to go and check the data for the previous day, and we go to their bank account and take this amount of money, **except during the weekends that we do on Monday**." The mechanism is **ACH** — "I swipe the account every day" — and the right to do it comes from the location agreement: "the parts of this agreement then give us all the bank information we want." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **How current the InComm file is — the two men disagree, mildly.** Val: "I think [InComm] sends the file two days later." Zazanis: "Don't quote me. I think it's a daily file. **It's a hundred percent daily file.**" Val then re-framed the point as elapsed time to money-in-bank rather than file age — "So now it's two days… you get the money into your bank account. You need to go back to your bank account, check if you got the money, and reconfirm it with your accounting system" — and Zazanis agreed with that description ("Correct"). Recorded as spoken; the underlying fact is not settled in this source. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **The sanction of last resort is a kill switch, and ARC hates using it.** "I have the ability to shut down the location completely… From the kiosks… **I disabled the bill acceptors for all the machines**… So nobody can play essentially." And why it is not a real option: "We're screwed because he loses money, I lose money. So it's a very difficult decision to make. Now because we have cases like this, we have locations that are not paying us." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

## What JVL pitched against it

- **Full cloud processing, no human in the loop.** Val: "I can do it all automatically… I swipe the account before the second day. [InComm's] report. When [InComm's] report comes in, I reconcile it automatically… And I do **automatic posting in the accounting system**, automatically, no human intervention. And I'm not going to tell you how because it's my secret." Plus same-day money movement — "I will put money into your account automatically" — and remote enforcement: "We will disable the machines automatically… [if] the account of the user does not have enough funds." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **Val corrected ARC's own idea of doing this from the kiosk.** Zazanis floated having the kiosks generate the file — "It's an EST file. Needs to generate an EST file with all the account information." Val: "the kiosks will not generate it… you need for the bank to accept that. Set up ACH. You need a third party transaction to bring [an ACH] in and to put it into your account." (The file format is transcribed "EST"; no expansion is given in either source.) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **Val would sell the cloud service on its own.** "I will happily deal with you just for cloud services. This is what you want. No problems. My goal is totally different. I actually **put very little emphasis on selling kiosks**." Device architecture on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **Zazanis's reaction was warm but bounded.** "But I like the idea." And, about a pilot: "If nothing prevents us, saying, 'Oh, let's try this and pilot it and test it and check it,' in a few locations, regardless of what we…" (the turn is labelled Speaker 4, a JVL voice, and reads as JVL proposing the pilot rather than ARC — attribution unclear, recorded as such). His summary of his own position was bleaker: "There is a good technology that helps you as an operator so much. Yet you cannot use it for any reason because **you start with a product that you cannot get out of it**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **Where he thought it *would* work: greenfield.** "how you get new locations? Especially **bars**, where there is no previous experience… nobody knows how it was before… If you go to a certain location to a gas station… and you try to fix this, they will kick you out… But new locations, bars, restaurants, where they have no previous experience… this the best case scenario for everybody. Whereas there's no attendance… There's nobody to supervise anything. Everything works by itself." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

## The kiosk lock-in

ARC's binding constraint, stated by its COO without being asked twice.

- **A previous ARC CEO bought from himself, for twenty years.** "the previous CEO of Arc… made an agreement with his other company. He owned another company. So the CEO said to himself, 'Look, man, how many years you want? **Twenty**.' Signed the agreement, and he signed and he bought from himself. He quit from the CEO because of all what happened." The agreement survived him: "We already bought it. **There is no way around it.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **The vendor.** "The kiosk provider we have belongs to **EXIO**. I know why I say this. There are some relationships built, but it's beyond my reach and my capabilities. One thing that will never happen is that we will never get out of what we have currently today. That is the key. So whatever we do, we have to build on top of it." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **ARC is still paying, and paying twice.** "We pay them, and on top of that, we pay more. We pay for the extra equipment that needs to go inside the machine. You pay for this. You pay for the cables. We pay." Asked whether the counterparty also takes recurring revenue: "In a monthly basis, there is something, but **I do not know how much**." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **The rate ARC quoted for the in-machine equipment: "We're paying thirteen ninety nine dollars per month. Fourteen dollars per month per machine."** Val's arithmetic: "So if they have nine machines, this is multiplied." *(The derived notes join this figure to the ninety PAX locations below — "a free swap for the ~90 PAX locations paying $13.99/month/machine". In the transcript the two are stated as separate points; the join is the notes' reading, not a quoted statement.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **"Bill" and why the proposal cannot go to him.** Val knows the counterparty — "I know him for thirty years… He was a president and CEO of a company called G. P. With an office in Long Island" — and offered to call him directly. Zazanis refused the route flat: "I'm afraid that if he [sees] the idea, he will say no and go and do it… **Has to come from David. David has to tell Bill.** Bill, you need to work with[them] on this solution." Val also read the politics correctly: "when you say it is what it is, I understand what it means. When your CEO tells you, George, it is what it is." Ruling on [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-31-arc-kiosk-board-swap-routed-through-the-ceo.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **David Sloan is friends with Bill.** Val: "The Sloan… Is friends with Bill. For years." Zazanis: "Maybe. Bill friends." And on the blocker being the friendship, not the technology: "The part is the relationship that he has with the CEO. So if I say this sucks, the other guy says George. **It is what it is. Find the solution.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

## The printer problem (ARC's own #1 service cost)

- **What the kiosks can and cannot do today:** "It doesn't accept money. It only issues vouchers. It accepts the vouchers and dispenses the card." Val restated it and got agreement: "the capability of your kiosk, is to accept a presented voucher and to dispense a card. **That's all.**" Every game machine, by contrast, is "equipped with a bill validator and a TITO printer" — though "not everybody, some machines like some old ICTs, we struggle with some older manufacturers, so not every machine has." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **The headache.** "No exaggeration. **The highest volume we have is the printers jammed** paper from vouchers, either from the TITO printer or from the [kiosk]." Val: "So the machine remains out of service." Asked what TITO actually costs ARC to run, Zazanis had never measured it: "So what does it cost? We haven't. I never done the math." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **Zazanis got to the answer himself** before JVL finished the pitch: "we can eliminate the printer from the kiosks and replace them with a **barcode reader**" — but balked at the bigger number: "We cannot go around to sell the printers because it's massive. That's the biggest concern because there are **many more than the kiosk printers**." Val's version reverses it: strip the per-machine printers ("We removed six printers, so who cares?") and put **one** printer into the kiosk. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

## What ARC's locations actually do with the cards

An unusually candid description of Georgia redemption practice, from the operator's side.

- **Locations pay players cash and keep the vouchers.** "Locations they pay cash… if you are a player, you win a hundred dollars. I give you a hundred dollars. You give me the voucher. Now I'm the owner. I have the voucher. I go to the kiosk and I get a gift card for myself… **the owner will absorb the fee** of the transaction." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **And often they do not even do that.** "We have locations. They have **fifty thousand dollars in vouchers**. They didn't redeem them. They never redeem them… You see piles of vouchers and cards and whatever, everything." Asked what the cards get spent on: "We do not know. It's not in the system, so we don't know what they're doing. **We are not asking any questions** because they are paying cash. No questions asked." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **The GLC has started asking.** "we receive a letter. Drop a letter at many locations requesting data… if GLC now… sees that you have a machine that has produced **ten thousand dollars of winnings**, they expect to see a similar amount [of] card issues, and some of them they're zero. **It's a spaghetti mix. There is no standard.**" This is the operator-side view of the same regulatory turn Halcyon showed Val four days earlier — see [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]] (Aug 27 2026) and [[wiki/organizations/incom.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **Val's prediction:** "Eventually they'll start issuing cards because the lottery will come down on that." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

## Two location types, and the ninety-location swap

ARC splits its estate: "There are two types of locations: **the kiosk location** and **the PAX device**." The PAX locations are the small ones — "It's a gas station. Little guy… they care less, but still they want the solution to be compliant. So we went to a Pax device." ARC does not buy the PAX units: "There is an agreement there. Very nice agreement. I made before I came, just when I joined the company." Val's opening was to replace them for free; Zazanis's reply names the size of the prize and the internal cost of taking it: "we're going to have a **free swap**, and we don't have to do anything, **in ninety locations**" — but it "comes to other factions, like going to say to the accounting, we're going to spend so much money, put all the guys in place, the fights." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

## Class A

ARC is the operator side of Georgia's Class A expansion, partnered with **Primero**. Full treatment on [[wiki/topics/georgia-class-a-machines.md]]; the JVL commitment is [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-31-class-a-deal-with-arc-within-three-months.md]]. Two ARC-specific facts:

- **The commercial formula.** ARC does not buy the Class A machines — "We don't buy them… profit share." ARC supplies the cabinet: "We put our cabinet." Val's reading of the split — "Primero puts the game and you go fifty" — drew "Much less" from Zazanis, so the **50/50 figure is Val's characterisation and was corrected in the room without a replacement number being given**. Recorded unresolved. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **ARC is not exclusive to Primero.** "we partnered with Primero for any reason, good or bad. I have no idea, by the way, why it was before me. We work together now, and **we're not exclusive** with them. We just happen to be the only one doing it." And he wants more suppliers: "We cannot launch the same six games for the next six years. It's a recipe for failure. So we need new content, new games… More manufacturers come. It's a plus." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

## Washington DC

ARC operates a second, much smaller market that it wants JVL to enter.

- **Scale: "I think we are twenty-seven now"** locations. Val: "It's a tiny market." Zazanis did not dispute it — "It's a small market, tiny market" — but argued the effort transfers. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **One manufacturer, and ARC is unhappy with it.** "We are only with one manufacturer, and it's **as bad as it can be**." Named: "**Omega**, do you know Omega? There are as bad as they can be. On the area, there's nobody else approved there." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **ARC is actively recruiting manufacturers into DC.** "We convinced **Premiere** because of the Class A franchise. So Premiere has already started their license process. We convinced **Light and Wonder** for them also to go in [DC]." ("Premiere" is the transcript's rendering of **Primero**.) The games in question are **Class B**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **The barrier is the DC lottery, not the rules.** "The problem there is the lottery… The lottery might take you three or four months. **You need to find somebody there with political connections.** They go very far there. They're very slow." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]

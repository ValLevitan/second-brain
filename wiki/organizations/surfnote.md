# SurfNote

The **other** of [[wiki/people/billy-liu.md|Billy Liu]]'s two companies, and the older one. It came into the corpus on **September 15 2026**, when Billy opened the commercial half of the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-15-billy-liu-playpay-nda-and-georgia-float-economics.md|Sept-15 evening session]] by separating it from [[wiki/organizations/play-payments.md|Play Payments]] — a distinction no earlier source in the corpus makes [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]].

## What it is, in his own words

- **It is the licensed entity, not the payments one.** "**I'm licensed with the Georgia Lottery as a [SurfNote]. Play payments is the payments company. The [SurfNote] is the… Licensed with the [lottery].**" The derived notes put the licence date at **2014** — "licensed with the Georgia Lottery since 2014" — and the transcript supports the year: "**In the very beginning, in 2014**", and "I've been selling these units **since twenty fourteen**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]].
- **Two companies, two ownership structures.** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]]: "So those are two separate companies. [SurfNote] and Play payments, but **they both belong to you one hundred percent**." Billy: "**Play payments has my brother involved. The two of us. [SurfNote], my company… one hundred percent.**" *(The transcript renders the last phrase "Surf notes, my company, Mark one hundred percent"; "Mark" is not interpretable and no name is asserted.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]]
- **PlayPayments came afterwards.** "**PlayPayments is separate because I built PlayPayments afterwards.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]]
- **It makes SMIB boards and has a POS.** "We started selling these boards, these **SMIBs**"; and today "**We have a POS**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]].

## The 2014 launch-party story, and the market share that came out of it

Billy's account of how SurfNote got its start — told as the origin of everything since [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]]:

> "Back in **twenty fourteen, [Intralot] won the bid to become the central monitoring system**… **Debbie Alford** was the [lottery] director at the time, so I knew her. We were all at the party listening to her tell a story… **I interrupted her by raising my hand. She said, 'Billy, can you wait till the end. Don't interrupt me.'** I said, '**Debbie, I just want to tell you that whatever it is that you guys want to do, [SurfNote] can do it. I have the board. I have everything.**'"

He describes the reaction as hostile — "**Pissed off the whole business. Everybody was like, 'God damn it! What you know? Why couldn't you wait?'**" — and the outcome as the opposite: "**Lo and behold, we started selling these boards, these SMIBs. We sold, if not ten thousand of them, we sold maybe nine, eight, nine thousand.**"

- **The size of the market at connection.** "**In the very beginning, there was only twenty thousand. Not even. Eighteen thousand connected in the very first week of connections… Georgia started with eighteen thousand machines. And half of those were using [SurfNote]s.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]]
- **He both beat and partnered with the CMS winner.** "**[Intralot]'s piece of shit didn't work, so we kind of took their glory away. But we also worked with [Intralot] because they are the… partner… I partnered with the partner to sell that many boards.**" *(The transcript renders Intralot as "Intellicom" and "Interloch" in this passage; the correct spelling also appears in it, and the derived notes record the two garbles as corrected.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]]

## Where it stands in September 2026 — small, and losing the connection race

- **About 500 locations.** "**I have maybe five hundred locations connected.**" That is against the ~9,000 boards sold a decade earlier [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]].
- **Why, in his account: the incumbents are easier.** "**Axis is pushing it hard. They have several contract men. They contracted some technical groups that are constantly out there connecting**"; store owners "**go with the simplest, easiest route**." What is left is loyalty: "**I still have some loyal people that want… to get connected, but they don't want to use [Axis] or EasyLinks.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]]
- **And it is the question he put to Val to open the working half of the evening:** "**What's your take? How do you see yourself winning this?**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]]

### His competitor numbers do not add up inside the source

Given in consecutive sentences: "**[Axis] is strong right now in Georgia. But they're not taking the whole state either. It's not quite there. I think they may have thirty-four thousand locations connected. Easy Links is closing on a thousand.**" — then, immediately: "**Those two together, you got five thousand.**"

**Thirty-four thousand and "five thousand together" cannot both be right**, and the source never reconciles them. Recorded exactly as spoken; **no figure for Axis is asserted here.** (A reading of "thirty-four thousand" as "three, four thousand" would make the two sentences agree, but that is a guess and is not adopted.) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]]

## How it relates to Play Payments and to JVL

SurfNote is the half of Billy's business that **touches the machine**; [[wiki/organizations/play-payments.md|Play Payments]] is the half that touches the money. That split is why the Sept-15 conversation turned on control: Billy told Val that on an Axis location "**I'm fully digital. I can't influence the machines**", and that his leverage over a non-paying location runs through his own AFT, kiosk and POS rather than the machine. The full commercial argument is on [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]].

Nothing in the source says which of the two companies would be the counterparty to any JVL deal, and Val's [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-02-val-proposes-fifty-percent-of-play-payments.md|Sept-2 proposal to take half the company]] was made before this distinction was drawn.

# Wells Fargo

Wells Fargo is a large US bank that JVL has tried to bring on for its payments and banking since late 2025. **As of April 23 2026, Wells Fargo had declined JVL's operating business.** The stated reason was cross-border payment risk, not the company's reputation. A contact, Ilya, took over the relationship and planned to approach the bank again. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Ilya_Wells_Fargo_Operating_Business_Readout-April-23-2026.md]]

## Apr 23 2026 — why the operating business was declined, and who re-engages

A six-minute call between [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] and **Ilya** ([[wiki/meetings/2026-04-23-ilya-wells-fargo-operating-business-readout.md]], 11:10 AM ET). Ilya had spoken to **Brad**, the Wells Fargo banker who had looked at JVL. All from the transcript [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Ilya_Wells_Fargo_Operating_Business_Readout-April-23-2026.md]]:

- **Who Ilya is.** Ilya told Brad that Val "reached out to me because you know me from Scotia days". He "checked the internal system" and speaks of Wells Fargo lending as "we". *(Inference: Ilya works at Wells Fargo now. The transcript does not state his title.)*
- **Not a credibility problem.** Brad's words, as Ilya passed them on: "from a credibility perspective, management team. From like, you know, zero to ten credibility. Ten he said like there is no issue." Ilya: "What you thought, for example, could have been in the Bible- nothing was there" (garbled). Later: "No, nothing like that happened in your name." Val: "that's actually a wonderful news."
- **The real reason: money crossing the border.** Wells Fargo is "very good when it comes to. U. S. and money being moved within the U. S. but the minute all of these electronic payments start moving. Globally… it's a little bit challenging for us to uh to absorb that". Brad said the bank is "very big when it comes to like the gaming side", but "they only know it in America. And when the money starts to move. Across the border, that's when they become a little bit nervous." Ilya: "maybe just appetite in terms of. The operating business, the transaction themselves."
- **Val disputes the premise.** "I never asked to move anything globally, like never." Ilya suggested it may have come from "your. Um, statement analysis"; Val: "Could be through my statement analysis".
- **The relationship moves to Ilya.** Brad said "you can take over the name, so he he'll transfer it over to me so I can actually. Then work with you on this."
- **The plan: present JVL as a US company.** Ilya will "circle back with him next week" and position that "because you're moving now to US… You… Be primarily U. S. company", then "reengage with some of the folks he was talking to".
- **Val wants the bank.** He offered to "Change around" whatever would make them comfortable, and said: "I'd like to engage with wealth [Wells] because they have that's an incredible bank. I'd like to deal with them."
- **Real-estate lending, touched and parked.** Ilya also offered help with mortgages ("I know you have CIBC"); a minimum loan size came up, and Ilya said "there is no rush on that front". This concerns Val's buildings, not JVL's operations, and is not developed here.

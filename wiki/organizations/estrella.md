# Estrella (Puerto Rico law firm)

Estrella is a **Puerto Rico-based law firm** JVL interviewed on Apr 10 2025 for the Puerto Rico market entry ([[wiki/meetings/2025-04-10-estrella-law-firm-carlos-infante.md]]) — one of the law firms from the Gaming-Commission referral round (see [[wiki/projects/puerto-rico-expansion.md]]). Val valued that they are "actually located in Puerto Rico … Many law firms have a representative in Puerto Rico, but really not in Puerto Rico," and that they speak English. [[raw/estrella_law_carlos_infante_puerto_rico-april-10-2025.md]]

## People and standing

- **[[wiki/people/carlos-infante.md|Carlos Infante]]** — the lead lawyer on the call; has a working relationship with the PR Gaming Commission and has obtained **temporary licences before, in the sports-betting context** ("not in the context of the slot machines, but it's the same people"). [[raw/estrella_law_carlos_infante_puerto_rico-april-10-2025.md]]
- **Jose** (surname not stated) — the market-entry/corporate partner; laid out the three-phase approach. A **Monica** on their side is *not* on this call — the Monica present is JVL's in-house counsel. [[raw/estrella_law_carlos_infante_puerto_rico-april-10-2025.md]]
- Part of "an actual international network of law firms," with resources in US jurisdictions. [[raw/estrella_law_carlos_infante_puerto_rico-april-10-2025.md]]

## Their proposal shape (Apr 10)

- **Three phases:** (1) pre-work / market assessment — no PR entity needed, "a couple of weeks, maybe a month," JVL staff can visit PR without doing business formally; (2) formalize + pursue the **temporary licence** (either authorize the US entity in PR or create a local entity — "mainly an issue of taxes"); (3) full licence and scale-up. Engagement letter with fixed-fee parts plus hourly parts **capped for the first stage** ("so you don't have a surprise invoice at the end of the month"). Engagement is e-signed via their **NINTEX** platform. [[raw/estrella_law_carlos_infante_puerto_rico-april-10-2025.md]]
- The **temporary-licence fee was deliberately not fixed** in the proposal — it requires selling the concept to the Commission; estimate + expected timeline promised to Val **by Monday**. Val: "I looked at your quote … It doesn't look unreasonable." [[raw/estrella_law_carlos_infante_puerto_rico-april-10-2025.md]]

## Engagement-agreement negotiation (May 5 2025 — [[wiki/meetings/2025-05-05-estrella-engagement-agreement-negotiation.md]])

- Estrella had sent a draft engagement agreement (its "standard agreement," adjusted for the Apr-10 discussion) "a couple of weeks ago." It restructured the discount tiers as **percentages instead of fixed quantities** after changing its accounting systems. [[raw/estrella_engagement_agreement_negotiations-may-5-2025.md]]
- The firm proposed a **$5,000 fee to incorporate a new company** and, in the earlier draft, a recurring **$5,000 trust-deposit** with a "fulfilment" top-up mechanism, plus a **14-day** termination clause, a late-payment fee, "no work until deposit," and an automatic annual hourly-rate increase — all of which Val pushed back on (detail on [[wiki/projects/puerto-rico-expansion.md]]). [[raw/estrella_engagement_agreement_negotiations-may-5-2025.md]]
- Estrella uses **NINTEX** for e-signature; Val asked to switch to **DocuSign** for execution. Carlos confirmed a **flat fee** for licence-obtainment services, **hourly** for other work, and a **flat fee** for the corporate-structure creation. [[raw/estrella_engagement_agreement_negotiations-may-5-2025.md]]
- **Jose** (the corporate/market-entry partner) said Estrella has represented some Puerto Rico companies "from their beginning 30, 35 years ago" and still counsels them — echoing Val's long-relationship pitch. Estrella found most of Val's ~20 requests reasonable/standard and will counter after **Monica** grooms the marked-up document. Estrella's client-service norm is the **"sundown rule"** (respond before the next sunrise). [[raw/estrella_engagement_agreement_negotiations-may-5-2025.md]]

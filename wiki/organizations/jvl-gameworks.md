# JVL Gameworks Corp.

JVL Gameworks Corp. is a **US operating company**, a Florida corporation, **100% owned by [[wiki/organizations/jvl-labs.md|JVL Labs Inc.]]**. [[raw/jvl_corporate_structure_2025-04.md]] It is the entity JVL uses for its **Amazon sales channel** (the ECHO home device). [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]] Part of the umbrella [[wiki/organizations/jvl.md|JVL group]].

## Corporate identity

- **Legal name:** **JVL Gameworks Corp.** — confirmed by Monica Levitan (Director of Corporate Compliance), 2026-07-13. The "JVL Gameworks, Inc." form seen on an insurance endorsement is a variant, not the legal name. [[raw/jvl_gameworks_corporate_identity_tax_ids_2026-07-13.md]]
- **State of incorporation:** Florida. [[raw/jvl_gameworks_corporate_identity_tax_ids_2026-07-13.md]]
- **Incorporated:** April 12, 2024. [[raw/jvl_gameworks_corporate_identity_tax_ids_2026-07-13.md]]
- **FEIN:** ***REMOVED*** — per the IRS FEIN letter dated 2024-07-12, on file. [[raw/jvl_gameworks_corporate_identity_tax_ids_2026-07-13.md]]
- **Business / mailing address:** Industrious (coworking), **19505 Biscayne Blvd, Suite 2350, Aventura, FL 33180 USA** (owner FINAL ruling 2026-07-14). The Georgia warehouse (6500 McDonough Dr) was only a mailing address, not the place of formation. [[raw/jvl_gameworks_corporate_identity_tax_ids_2026-07-13.md]]
- **Address contradiction RESOLVED — explained (owner, 2026-07-14):** the address of record for **all JVL group companies** is **19505 Biscayne Blvd, Suite 2350, Aventura, FL 33180** (Industrious). The other street number on file is not a typo but the **old address**: JVL Gameworks was registered while the group sat in the **Chase Bank building (Suite 407)** one door down — so the IRS EIN letter carries that then-current address, and the Industrious lease application repeats it because the form asked for the "current" address at move-in. Superseded by the move; Form 8822-B (Monica Levitan, 2026-07-13) updates the IRS record. **19505 is final.** [[raw/jvl_gameworks_corporate_identity_tax_ids_2026-07-13.md]] (Explanation: owner statement, 2026-07-14.)

## Tax, financials & banking

- **US income & franchise returns:** prepared by **BSP CPA** (Mark Kransler / Ashlee Hohman) as part of the consolidated JVL group. [[raw/jvl_gameworks_corporate_identity_tax_ids_2026-07-13.md]]
- **Financials:** compiled by **Gilmore & Company LLP ("GCO LLP")**, Mississauga, under Canadian standard CSRS 4200 (the same firm that does JVL Labs' Canadian work). 2025 figures: revenue USD 131,549; net income USD 40,264; income taxes payable USD 10,703. [[raw/jvl_gameworks_corporate_identity_tax_ids_2026-07-13.md]]
- **Banking:** Bank of America account (account name "JVL Gameworks Corp."), opened ~August 2024, business address 6500 McDonough Dr. [[raw/jvl_gameworks_corporate_identity_tax_ids_2026-07-13.md]]
- **Insurance:** added as an **additional named insured** on JVL Systems' general-liability policy (endorsement #1). [[raw/jvl_gameworks_corporate_identity_tax_ids_2026-07-13.md]]

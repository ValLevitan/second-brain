# JVL Gameworks Corp.

JVL Gameworks Corp. is a **US operating company**, a Florida corporation, **100% owned by [[wiki/organizations/jvl-labs.md|JVL Labs Inc.]]**. [[raw/jvl_corporate_structure_2025-04.md]] It is the entity JVL uses for its **Amazon sales channel** (the ECHO home device). [[raw/cibc_account_the_january_6_2025.md]] Part of the umbrella [[wiki/organizations/jvl.md|JVL group]].

## Corporate identity

- **Legal name:** **JVL Gameworks Corp.** — confirmed by Monica Levitan (Director of Corporate Compliance), 2026-07-13. The "JVL Gameworks, Inc." form seen on an insurance endorsement is a variant, not the legal name. (source: Corporate Registry — JVL Gameworks Identity & Tax IDs)
- **State of incorporation:** Florida. (source: Corporate Registry — JVL Gameworks Identity & Tax IDs)
- **Incorporated:** April 12, 2024. (source: Corporate Registry — JVL Gameworks Identity & Tax IDs)
- **FEIN:** [held in Corporate Registry] — per the IRS FEIN letter dated 2024-07-12, on file. (source: Corporate Registry — JVL Gameworks Identity & Tax IDs)
- **Business / mailing address:** Industrious (coworking), **19505 Biscayne Blvd, Suite 2350, Aventura, FL 33180 USA** (owner FINAL ruling 2026-07-14). The Georgia warehouse (6500 McDonough Dr) was only a mailing address, not the place of formation. (source: Corporate Registry — JVL Gameworks Identity & Tax IDs)
- **Address contradiction RESOLVED — explained (owner, 2026-07-14):** the address of record for **all JVL group companies** is **19505 Biscayne Blvd, Suite 2350, Aventura, FL 33180** (Industrious). The other street number on file is not a typo but the **old address**: JVL Gameworks was registered while the group sat in the **Chase Bank building (Suite 407)** one door down — so the IRS EIN letter carries that then-current address, and the Industrious lease application repeats it because the form asked for the "current" address at move-in. Superseded by the move; Form 8822-B (Monica Levitan, 2026-07-13) updates the IRS record. **19505 is final.** (source: Corporate Registry — JVL Gameworks Identity & Tax IDs) (Explanation: owner statement, 2026-07-14.)

## June 30 2026 — why GameWorks exists, and its 2025 US tax

On the [[wiki/meetings/2026-06-30-gilmore-gameworks-us-tax-and-labs-filing.md|Jun-30 2026 call]] with the Gilmore accountants, the accountants asked why sales had been put in GameWorks. Val's answer: [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Gilmore_GameWorks_US_Tax_and_Labs_Filing-June-30-2026.md]]

- **Purpose: keep home-unit risk away from the gaming company.** "The US is very litigious. We wanted to separate the gaming company with assets… and a potential litigation of any sort for home units." Val: "JVL GameWorks only sells through Amazon in the US and only home units." *(One line reads "we sold two homes in the US in various states"; in context this is most likely "home units", a transcript garble — unclear.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Gilmore_GameWorks_US_Tax_and_Labs_Filing-June-30-2026.md]]
- **Where the units come from.** Sean (Speaker 2 — inference): "these would have been units that GameWorks would have had to get from JVL Labs, then correct." Val did not dispute it. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Gilmore_GameWorks_US_Tax_and_Labs_Filing-June-30-2026.md]]
- **2025 US tax.** The US return was due June 15 and had already been signed and filed through BSP (Ashley). Nick read from it: US tax of **$10,703** plus interest — about **$11.5K** in all. This matches the income taxes payable of USD 10,703 in the Corporate Registry above. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Gilmore_GameWorks_US_Tax_and_Labs_Filing-June-30-2026.md]]
- **The plan to wipe the profit.** Book more revenue in JVL Labs on the machines Labs sold to GameWorks, so GameWorks shows no profit. If BSP agrees to amend the filed US return, do it now; if not, pay now and create a loss next year to carry back. Detail on [[wiki/topics/jvl-finance-back-office.md]] (Jun-30). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Gilmore_GameWorks_US_Tax_and_Labs_Filing-June-30-2026.md]]
- **Amazon-entity note.** Val's line that GameWorks "only sells through Amazon" is one more data point for the open Amazon-commerce-entity question on [[wiki/topics/echo-home-gaming-device.md]]. It is recorded there, not treated as settling it. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Gilmore_GameWorks_US_Tax_and_Labs_Filing-June-30-2026.md]]

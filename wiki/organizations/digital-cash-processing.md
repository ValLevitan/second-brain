# Digital Cash Processing / ICP Inc.

A US payment-processing group that walked into the Georgia COAM market off the back of the August 2026 trade show and asked [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] whether its card product is legal there. First and so far only contact: the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-11-digital-cash-processing-card-solution-and-cash-float-partnership.md|Sept 11 2026 11:07 AM Google Meet]], an exploratory call organised by their side [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]].

**Two names, one group, relationship not stated.** The meeting is titled "JVL/DCP"; the derived notes place [[wiki/people/andrew-cashin.md|Andrew Cashin]] and Linda Markert at **ICP Inc.** and [[wiki/people/rick-beer.md|Richard "Rick" Beer]] at **Digital Cash Processing**, from the calendar invite. On the recording Cashin speaks of all three as one company ("she is a project manager and salesperson **for us**"). **Nothing in either source says how ICP Inc. and Digital Cash Processing relate** — one page, both names, and the question left open [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]].

## Who they are

- **Rick Beer is the president and started the company.** "**Rick Beer is the president of the company, and he started this company. I'm going to say in 1996**, okay, and it's gone through different iterations or whatever… **Basically, been doing this payment processing business for a long time.**" The same introduction ends "**So, and he resides up in Minnesota**" — *the "he" has no explicit antecedent in the sentence; the derived notes read it as Rick Beer, and that is recorded as the notes' reading* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]].
- **Linda Markert is a project manager and salesperson** — "**On the phone is Linda Markert, and she is a project manager and salesperson for us.**" She organised the meeting and says almost nothing on the recording; she has no page of her own in this wiki [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]].
- **Andrew Cashin describes himself as "an outside guy"** and did nearly all the talking. His own history runs through **Cash Systems** → **Global Cash Access** → **Everi** → **IGT**. Full profile: [[wiki/people/andrew-cashin.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]].
- **What the business is, in Cashin's summary:** "**we've been doing cash… effectively putting driving cash to the floor in casinos for years… speaking to all ATMs and things, and then also the banking… side with all the interfaces running… selling services for banks throughout the Caribbean**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]].

## What they actually hold — stated by Rick Beer, and it is the most useful thing in the meeting

> "we're a registered **P2PAC** in the U. S. and we have **our own issuing party and switch**. So we do a lot of customization. We work with a lot of financial institutions, mostly internationally in the **East Caribbean**. But we **drive ATMs in the U. S. We process ATM transactions**… in the U.S. and we **board submergents** on our **PPA** payment facilitation. So we can do… not only your ATMs and card issuance…, but we can do **payment processing in the U.S.** as well."
> [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]

*(The transcript renders the licence as "P2PAC", "PPA" and later "PEC", and "sub-merchants" as "submergents". The derived notes read the whole as **registered payment facilitator** boarding **sub-merchants**; that is the notes' reading and **no acronym is asserted here**.)* [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]

**Why it matters to JVL:** this is the second counterparty in the corpus to hold its own processing rails rather than resell someone else's, after [[wiki/organizations/play-payments.md|Play Payments]]. Where PlayPay's moat is a banking licence and a Visa wallet, this group's is **ATM driving plus its own issuing side**, which is precisely the half of the chain [[wiki/organizations/incom.md|InComm]] does not let JVL near.

## What they built for the Georgia show, and why they do not know if it is legal

- **A card product bolted into an off-the-shelf ATM, in six to eight weeks.** "**six or seven or I don't know maybe eight weeks before the show… somebody convinced us that we should show up and do a card thing product in our Gen Mega ATM machine, an off the shelf Gen Mega ATM out of Texas**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]].
- **Built with "Bill Nader at Gaming Solutions Corporation"**, a relationship Cashin dates to 1995 and **United Coin** in Las Vegas [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]. *(This may be the surname of the surname-less "Bill" the corpus already carries as ARC Gaming's kiosk vendor. The matching evidence and the reasons it is **not asserted** are on [[wiki/people/bill.md]].)*
- **The card is generic magstripe, and it was already certified by accident.** "**we had our kiosk at GLI because we're rolling out… that same car[d]**… **we have a card-based software approval test, except it's in that base code.**" See [[wiki/organizations/gli.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]].
- **What the card does:** load it at the ATM, put a PIN on it, then "**you can either a take it down the street. Use it in an ATM today that takes Max Stripes** [magstripes], **or B, you could put it right back in our ATM machine and get cash out**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]].
- **Then they saw the incumbent's card at the show.** "**I watched all these videos… about this card, you know, what InComm has. Then they rolled out this… absorbent card at the show, right? The three-dollar thing.**" *(The word before "card" is garbled; the $3 activation matches the InComm Skill Pay card already on [[wiki/organizations/incom.md]] — recorded as the figure spoken.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]
- **And nobody can tell them whether the logo matters.** "**I truly… today don't know what the big difference is between putting a little logo on it and not having a logo**… **In this one, they say… you can't do cash for it, but they can be converted over. This one you can, so I don't know how what the difference is legally on that one.**" Bill Nader's position was the same: "**guys, I don't know which card you want to put in it**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]].

## How the float works on their side — the mechanics Val was actually after

Asked by Val what can be earned on holding the money, Rick would not give a number but gave the plumbing. This is the clearest description of prize-card float mechanics from an operator of the rails anywhere in the corpus; the commercial reading of it is on [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]] (Sept 11). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]

- **The money sits in an FBO account they control** at their sponsor bank — "**That would be in a bank account, an FBO account that we have… control**".
- **It would be invested, conservatively.** Val: "So you probably put it in a very safe investment." Rick: "**Probably safe, safe investment that can be easily, you know, converted to liquid.**"
- **The release event is the cash draw.** Val: "when a customer with the card then requests cash through an ATM network… you get the fee, and you release the money from the bank." Rick: "**Correct, correct. And if we're driving the ATM… we wouldn't have to pay any additional fees. So there would be ATM interchange… that would be bypassed.**"
- **The binding constraint is banking law, not appetite.** "**have to hold money for so long. Then… we have to determine… can we even in the FBO that we have with our sponsor bank… And if so, for how long? And then after so much time, then we**… **regulations that are basically a depository institution, so we have to consider all that. It just undercuts a lot of it.**"
- **And how the incumbent earns it:** "**That's the card issuer. They're going to make interchange. So if the customer uses it at [a] place, the issuer is going to make interchange… and then… depending on what they do with the funds and how they invest it, obviously you can make money there.**"

## What they want, and what Val told them

Their model is the opposite of JVL's on hardware and the same as JVL's on processing, which is why the call ended without a deal:

- **They do not want to sell hardware.** "**we're not really in the business of trying to make money on the hardware. I got to tell you that.**" And: "**We want to offer a solution to just share revenue on the ATM two dollars side or something like that. That's all. We don't want to be a hardware guy. We just want to be a processor side, and people distribute our stuff.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]
- **Val called that competing on price** — "**I don't understand. Why do you want to compete on pricing?**" — and refused the ATM fee to them on principle, because it belongs to the master operator. Full ruling: [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-11-market-access-traded-for-a-share-of-the-back-end-and-the-atm-fee-belongs-to-the-master-operator.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]].
- **They would need JVL's ATM certified on their network** — Val: "need to certify my ATM with an ATM network"; Cashin: "**Exactly. It'd be us.**" That is the one concrete technical dependency named on the call [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]].
- **They had never seen JVL's hardware** — "**I don't even know what your hardware looks like**" — and Val offered a look while refusing to sell it to them: "**you don't need to purchase my hardware. My hardware is purchased by masters**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]].

**It ended amicably and with nothing agreed.** "**let's just keep following up on these different opportunities, different jurisdictions. Again, Georgia is not the end of it. There's lots of [route] markets out here.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Digital_Cash_Processing_Card_Solution_and_Cash_Float_Partnership-September-11-2026.md]]

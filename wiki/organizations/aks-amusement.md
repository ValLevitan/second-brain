# AKS Amusement

**AKS Amusement LLC** is a **medium–large Georgia coin-operated-amusement-machine (COAM) master/terminal operator** and a JVL customer in the Georgia land-based market. It is led by CEO **Zahir**, with **Karim** as its technical leader / operations manager. Per the JVL operator briefing (2026), AKS **appears to have originated from — or been closely associated with — the smaller-master ecosystem connected to Lucky Bucks** (alongside groups such as Dynamic Gaming); the briefing states this as market context, not confirmed fact. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_AKS.md]]

Zahir is a GAMOA member but is not active in GAMOA leadership and is generally hands-off with day-to-day location operations, so execution on the account depends on who owns reporting internally. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_AKS.md]]

## Scale and market position

- **2026 decals assigned: 510.** [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_AKS.md]]
- **Estimated locations: ~102** (briefing's estimate, based on the ~5-machines-per-location typical Georgia configuration). [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_AKS.md]]
- **Relative market position: medium–large, approximately #18 in decals statewide.** [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_AKS.md]]

## Relationship with JVL

AKS has an established history with JVL through both **direct Flex purchases** and **Altera-R exposure via other masters**. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_AKS.md]]

- The **primary operational point of contact is Karim**, who has historical familiarity with JVL from earlier deployments tied to Lucky Bucks / Imran. Karim is technically and operationally capable but may not be the person responsible for performance reporting and analysis. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_AKS.md]]
- Long-running service relationship: **constant call / FaceTime help** setting machines up and troubleshooting. The briefing estimates **~70–90 inbound service calls between 2025 and 2026**. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_AKS.md]]

## Purchase history with JVL

Direct purchases: **29 Flex D27 cabinets** and **9 Flex V43 cabinets** (the most recent V43 purchase was associated with a **trade-in transaction**). [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_AKS.md]]

AKS has also acquired **Altera-R units and additional Flex cabinets indirectly**, through cabinet transfers and split-order distribution from other masters — for example, **Dynamic Games LLC** historically placed cabinet orders that were then distributed across AKS and Dynamic (a shared purchasing flow). [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_AKS.md]]

## Commercial / strategic notes (2026)

Following the trade-in purchase, [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]] discussed the newest software package with Zahir, including the **newly designed Nudge feature** and other changes intended to improve player engagement and profitability through faster, more intuitive play. (The Nudge/RTP thread is tracked on [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]].) [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_AKS.md]]

**Agreement in principle:** Felipe negotiated a **no-charge software upgrade for AKS cabinets, conditional on AKS providing location-level performance numbers for all machines via Georgia Lottery reports** — an "upgrade-for-data" give/get framework. If AKS complies with full location reporting, the briefing frames this account as a valuable reference set for identifying reporting inconsistencies and informing broader corrective strategy. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_AKS.md]]

The briefing's suggested meeting objectives were to confirm the upgrade scope and timing, secure a commitment to complete location-level Lottery reports (cadence, format, owner), validate performance and reconcile discrepancies, and align responsibilities across Zahir, Karim, and whoever owns reporting internally. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_AKS.md]]

See [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]]; related Georgia masters [[wiki/organizations/diamond-amusement.md|Diamond Amusement]] and [[wiki/organizations/inamax-ultra.md|Inamax / Ultra]].

## Name note (§4) — the other Karim

On **September 1 2026** an operator called **Air Gaming Group** visited the JVL Georgia office, and its decision maker was identified in the room as "**Karim**… **the only owner**," who "has partners in different companies, but he remains the decision." That is a different man from AKS's Karim, who is AKS's technical/operations lead under CEO Zahir. No source connects the two, neither has a person page, and nothing has been merged — resolve by context. See [[wiki/organizations/air-gaming-group.md]].

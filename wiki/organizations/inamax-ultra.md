# Inamax (Powered by Ultra)

**Inamax (Powered by Ultra)** is one of the **most established, respected, and operationally sophisticated master-operator groups in the Georgia coin-operated-amusement-machine (COAM) market**, and a JVL customer. It was founded by CEO **Nick Damani** and his wife **Nadiya**, and has a long track record of innovation across Georgia and other jurisdictions — the briefing credits the group with introducing **one of Georgia's earliest redemption systems in 1996** and with operating the **Titan redemption platform** (the system deployed at Kroger locations, per prior field demonstrations). [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]

Because of its reputation and scale, Inamax has **outsized influence**: what it standardizes on tends to shape what smaller operators adopt, making it a **"reference operator"** in Georgia. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]

Leadership and key contacts (2026 briefing): CEO **Nick Damani**; CFO **Ray Cosentino**; head of operations **David Sherman**; operations / warehouse manager **Ashley Gunther**; lead technician **Thomas Acosta**. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]

## Scale and market position

- **2026 decals assigned: 1,140.** [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]
- **Estimated locations: ~228** (briefing's estimate, based on the ~5-machines-per-location typical Georgia configuration). [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]
- **Relative market position: large, approximately #5 in decals statewide.** [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]

## Relationship with JVL

JVL has a positive and stable relationship with Inamax across technical and operational teams. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]

- JVL held **several informal training sessions with Inamax's technicians in 2024**. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]
- Inamax has experienced **little to no technical friction with JVL games** to date. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]
- **Ashley Gunther is a key internal stakeholder** — highly knowledgeable, operationally disciplined, and closely aligned with Ray Cosentino. She is not a technician by role but is highly influential in standardizing products: if she supports a product, adoption tends to follow (a **gating factor** for JVL). [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]
- Decision influence runs through **executive + operations alignment (Ray Cosentino + Ashley Gunther)**, with technicians acting as validators rather than primary drivers. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]
- Service history: **~10 service calls between 2024 and 2025, mostly about hard resets** — a low support burden. There is no history of Ultra bringing equipment in for repair, though JVL sent its technician **Andres** to their warehouse twice in 2024 to help fix CPU-board problems. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]

## Purchase history with JVL

Direct purchases: **26 Flex D27 cabinets**, **8 Flex V43 cabinets**, and **1 Echo COAM cabinet**. Inamax does **not appear to hold a significant number of additional JVL units via third-party acquisitions or route purchases** (the briefing flags this as unconfirmed and worth validating). [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]

## Key observation — limited volume vs footprint (2026)

Despite Inamax's size and generally positive technical experience, its **JVL purchasing volume has remained limited relative to its footprint**. The briefing's working assumption is that this is **not** driven primarily by performance complaints or service issues. The open executive-level question is **what the real constraint is** — commercial terms, standardization preferences, strategic partnerships, internal product-approval workflow, competitive benchmarks, etc. — best addressed directly with Ray Cosentino, with awareness that Ashley Gunther's operational approval is a gating factor. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]

The briefing's suggested meeting objectives were to confirm what is required for Inamax to expand its JVL footprint (decision criteria, approvals, benchmarks), align executive-to-executive expectations ([[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] + Ray), identify the next practical step (pilot placement, standardization review, or a defined action plan), and keep the Nebraska trial (below) moving in parallel through [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan]]. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]

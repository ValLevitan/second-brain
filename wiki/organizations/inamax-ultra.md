# Inamax (Powered by Ultra)

**Inamax (Powered by Ultra)** is one of the **most established, respected, and operationally sophisticated master-operator groups in the Georgia coin-operated-amusement-machine (COAM) market**, and a JVL customer. It was founded by CEO **Nick Damani** and his wife **Nadiya**, and has a long track record of innovation across Georgia and other jurisdictions — the briefing credits the group with introducing **one of Georgia's earliest redemption systems in 1996** and with operating the **Titan redemption platform** (the system deployed at Kroger locations, per prior field demonstrations). [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]

Because of its reputation and scale, Inamax has **outsized influence**: what it standardizes on tends to shape what smaller operators adopt, making it a **"reference operator"** in Georgia. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]

Leadership and key contacts (2026 briefing): CEO **Nick Damani**; CFO **Ray Cosentino**; head of operations **David Sherman**; operations / warehouse manager **Ashley Gunther**; lead technician **Thomas Acosta**. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]

## Scale and market position

- **2026 decals assigned: 1,140.** [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]
- **Estimated locations: ~228** (briefing's estimate, based on the ~5-machines-per-location typical Georgia configuration). [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]
- **Relative market position: large, approximately #5 in decals statewide.** [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]

## Relationship with JVL

JVL has a positive and stable relationship with Inamax across technical and operational teams. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]

- JVL held **several informal training sessions with Inamax's technicians in 2024**. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]
- Inamax has experienced **little to no technical friction with JVL games** to date. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]
- **Ashley Gunther is a key internal stakeholder** — highly knowledgeable, operationally disciplined, and closely aligned with Ray Cosentino. She is not a technician by role but is highly influential in standardizing products: if she supports a product, adoption tends to follow (a **gating factor** for JVL). [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]
- Decision influence runs through **executive + operations alignment (Ray Cosentino + Ashley Gunther)**, with technicians acting as validators rather than primary drivers. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]
- Service history: **~10 service calls between 2024 and 2025, mostly about hard resets** — a low support burden. There is no history of Ultra bringing equipment in for repair, though JVL sent its technician **Andres** to their warehouse twice in 2024 to help fix CPU-board problems. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]

## Purchase history with JVL

Direct purchases: **26 Flex D27 cabinets**, **8 Flex V43 cabinets**, and **1 Echo COAM cabinet**. Inamax does **not appear to hold a significant number of additional JVL units via third-party acquisitions or route purchases** (the briefing flags this as unconfirmed and worth validating). [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]

## Key observation — limited volume vs footprint (2026)

Despite Inamax's size and generally positive technical experience, its **JVL purchasing volume has remained limited relative to its footprint**. The briefing's working assumption is that this is **not** driven primarily by performance complaints or service issues. The open executive-level question is **what the real constraint is** — commercial terms, standardization preferences, strategic partnerships, internal product-approval workflow, competitive benchmarks, etc. — best addressed directly with Ray Cosentino, with awareness that Ashley Gunther's operational approval is a gating factor. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]

The briefing's suggested meeting objectives were to confirm what is required for Inamax to expand its JVL footprint (decision criteria, approvals, benchmarks), align executive-to-executive expectations ([[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] + Ray), identify the next practical step (pilot placement, standardization review, or a defined action plan), and keep the Nebraska trial (below) moving in parallel through [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan]]. [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]

## Feb 11 2026 — the executive call: Ray's market read, JVL's place at Inamax, and the trial ask

On the [[wiki/meetings/2026-02-11-inamax-ray-cosentino-georgia-market-trial.md|Feb-11 call]] [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]], [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan]], [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]] and [[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael]] met [[wiki/people/ray-cosentino.md|Ray]] of Inamax. Juan set the goal: JVL, as a mid-sized maker, only gets Intralot reports and cannot see which game in a multi-game box earns, so it wanted Ray's view to shape a product "we introduce later this year". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]

**The market, as Ray sees it.**
- **Size.** "We were twenty five thousand ish units in Georgia in twenty seventeen, and we're pushing… close to forty five, fifty thousand at this point." Convenience stores are "kind of saturated", but redemption for a "clerkless" solution opens new verticals. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]
- **Kroger.** "We hold the Kroger account. So we've started installing games in grocery across all of Georgia." Asked by Michael who plays there, Ray said the players are "primarily women aged fifty to seventy". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]
- **Gift card and July.** Ray mentioned the move to gift cards and "the July twenty sixth rules, or you know, legislation coming into play" (the date is as transcribed and unclear). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]
- **Same games, different operators.** "At the end of the day, the games are the same. We all offer the same games… It's what's approved." Operators set themselves apart by their own development. Inamax has its own player-tracking panel and a point of sale at every location, and needs makers to integrate with it. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]
- **IGT relationship.** Inamax works "really closely with IGT"; IGT brings games and Inamax lets players from its locations try them before release, as a case study. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]
- **Top games at Inamax.** "Our top five games here in Georgia are legends. Light and Wonder game package two… number four is the curve the IGT curve… number five is the Light and Wonder game set one. From there, number six is Peach three." Number three is not stated in the transcript. Ray's explanation: the big makers win on well-known legacy themes (Sun and Moon, Midnight Train, Price is Right). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]
- **Player behaviour.** Georgia "makes no sense to me": players skip a Banilla game "in the ninety ninety five percentile" and sit at a Light & Wonder where "the redemption is fifty percent", maybe for comfort with the theme. Aristocrat is "on the higher end" of RTP, Light & Wonder low. Players do not chase progressives much; with redemption Inamax likes "to stay around the ten thousand or below". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]
- **Other makers.** Novomatic: price seems high, "the numbers haven't really proven themselves", weak support on the ground, slow to help with the player-tracking panel — "if you don't want my business, then I have other options." Konami: "just haven't really pushed"; Ray did not know who its distributor is; a trial "did okay". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]
- **Games per machine.** Most machines carry about six games; makers price by game count, "so, we still stick around the six because I just say, hey, give me the six highest performing games." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]

**JVL at Inamax.**
- "We have twenty- one JVL terminals active today." Ray shared a summary of all JVL games in the chat (NTI = net terminal income); the figures are not in the transcript. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]] *(The 2026 briefing above counts 35 cabinets bought directly. "Active today" and "bought" are different measures, so this is not treated as a contradiction — an inference.)*
- **Where JVL sits.** "JVL is doing well." JVL "does better in the Hispanic market and the Hispanic areas". Asked whether more Latino-themed games would help, Ray said "it helps", but it came from location requests: stores hear that "John down the street has this peach game". The Peach 1, 2 and 3 machines are mostly in one area. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]
- **Price bucket.** Aristocrat and the IGT Curve are "over twenty thousand dollars", so Ray keeps them for his best locations. Light & Wonder, Bad Dog and JVL are "all kind of in… the same bucket" on price. For a brand-new location, "historically I'll put something like a like a light and wonder an aristocrat, and then probably a JVL and a bad dog" — "a few middle of the road games… a few top tier games, and then see what happens." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]
- **Route acquisitions — asked, not answered.** Felipe said his own system shows one Peach 3 at Inamax and asked whether Inamax had bought JVL machines through other route acquisitions. No answer is recorded. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]

**Fleet refresh and trade-ins.**
- Inamax has been removing older Primero and Banilla machines (transcribed "Romeros and Vanillas") to freshen its offer. Many it brought back and rebuilt: a "thousand dollar monitor conversion" to a 43-inch screen plus a "three thousand dollar four thousand dollar" Bad Dog board; Val put it at five to six thousand, and Ray said "tops". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]
- Inamax is also holding many old Banilla and IGT Prodigy View machines. The Prodigy Views still perform "through the roof", and Ray has bought used ones from the Caribbean and Chicago for parts. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]
- A trade-in is "definitely a possibility". Ray would want a discount on newer or higher-performing JVL cabinets, "some of your peach themed cabinets", and "if we could get maybe a few thousand out of them, that would be enticing." The decision is his. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]
- Val reminded Ray of JVL's trade-in offer around Christmas, said "That offer is finished", and offered to extend it for Inamax. Felipe said he had texted and emailed the offer. Ray said he would talk separately and "see if there's some number of cabinets, I can I can get rid of"; Felipe suggested lunch the next week. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]

**The trial ask.** Val asked Inamax to "place us against your top performers in your top locations, and we'll give you machines for free", with JVL allowed to "actually come in and take the actual numbers" rather than go through any system, paying for technicians' time if needed. Ray: "I would be okay with that. I mean, I'll definitely put two or three games." He offered either to send someone to extract the data or to be told exactly which figures JVL needs. Val then asked for "three machines to try? Three different locations?" and said Felipe would make the final arrangements. Ray's reply to the three-location ask is not captured. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]

**Nebraska.** Ray asked on his own whether JVL has a Nebraska offering: the market "has grown really, really quickly" and "I can only stick so many Light, Wonders and Bad Dogs in the location." Juan said JVL is "now live with the Nebraska skill product in about six locations", started "a week and a half ago", and promised to reply "right away". See [[wiki/projects/nebraska-skill-market.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]

**The owner, Nick.** Ray said Nick "started off forty years ago in the payphone business", moved into ATMs, has been in Georgia "since the late eighties" after living in the Chicago area, helped "get the skill legislation passed for Georgia", and was "one of the very first operators here". Val said "I know him, and he knows me." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]

**Engineering.** Inamax has "thirteen to sixteen- ish" engineers, "primarily software", reporting half to Ray and half to Nick. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]

## Open contradictions

**Who leads Inamax / Ultra (2026 briefing vs Aug 31 2026).** Two sources describe the leadership differently. Recorded, not resolved.

- **The 2026 operator briefing:** CEO **Nick Damani**, who founded the group with his wife **Nadiya**; CFO **Ray Cosentino** (a man, throughout the briefing and the Nebraska thread above). [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_Inamax_Ultra.md]]
- **Aug 31 2026, George Zazanis:** "**Ray Constantino. He's the CEO.**" — and separately that the CFO "**is a lady**" who "used to work on the Kroger CFO", and that the company is really run by **the owner's wife**, whose name he could not recall. The derived notes of the same meeting record it the same way — "Ultra (CEO Ray Constantino) — the owner's wife actually runs it; meet at their office." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Gaming_Transactional_Demo_and_Class_A-August-31-2026.md]]
- **What is and is not in tension.** "Ray Constantino" and "Ray Cosentino" are very likely the same person (an **inference** from the shared first name and the shared Ultra context) — but the two sources put him in **different offices**. The "owner's wife runs it" claim is compatible with the briefing's co-founder Nadiya; the "CFO is a lady" claim is not compatible with Ray Cosentino being CFO. Neither source is a corporate record. Nothing has been rewritten on the strength of the later one.
- **Feb 11 2026 data point (adds, resolves nothing).** Ray himself called Nick "the owner of our company" and said engineering reports half to each of them. His own title is not stated in that transcript. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Inamax_Ray_Cosentino_Georgia_Market_and_Trial_Agreement-February-11-2026.md]]
- **Also unresolved in the source itself:** the nationality of the owner. Val asked "the owner is Italian?" and got "Yes. The owner is Italian. Yes." — immediately followed by Zazanis saying "Indian guy." The transcript does not settle it and neither does this page.

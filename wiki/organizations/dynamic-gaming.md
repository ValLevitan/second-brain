# Dynamic Gaming

**Dynamic Gaming** is a **Georgia COAM master licensee** and an existing JVL customer. A JVL operator briefing places it in the smaller-master group linked to Lucky Bucks, as market context only — see [[wiki/organizations/aks-amusement.md]] [[raw/georgia_operator_briefing_AKS.md]]. It first appears as a counterparty at the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-17-dynamic-gaming-georgia-master-licensee-and-cashless-pitch.md|Sept 17 2026 meeting]].

## Identity (unresolved)

The derived notes say the Outlook invite names **Eshan Huddani – DYNAMIC GAMING LLC** and that the name is never spoken [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Dynamic_Gaming_Georgia_Master_Licensee_and_Cashless_Pitch-September-17-2026.md]]. In the recording the visitor is greeted "**Michael**", Val once corrects himself to "**Ishar**", and "**Shah**" and "**Asif**" come up when the receivable is discussed. The visitor names his company in passing: "**Not my dynamic, my gaming**". He says he has a **partner** who decides on selling old machines, and that he was "**a location manager before I became**" a master. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dynamic_Gaming_Georgia_Master_Licensee_and_Cashless_Pitch-September-17-2026.md]]

## Size and fleet (as he stated it, Sept 17 2026)

- **About 130–150 locations.** He: "**I cannot be paying for hundred and thirty, hundred and fifty locations**"; Andrei: "**a beautiful capacity of 130 locations**". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dynamic_Gaming_Georgia_Master_Licensee_and_Cashless_Pitch-September-17-2026.md]]
- **He has seven-year contracts with his locations** — his answer when Val warned him about competitors paying fees: "**Then I have seven-year contract.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dynamic_Gaming_Georgia_Master_Licensee_and_Cashless_Pitch-September-17-2026.md]]
- **Aristocrat:** "**I just bought 65 machines**", because "**every location is asking for**" Aristocrat One, Two and Three, "**And if I don't give them, I lose that location. They threaten me.**" Aristocrat is raising prices and discounting only now, on "**Only three months term**" — "**you have to buy right now on gun point.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dynamic_Gaming_Georgia_Master_Licensee_and_Cashless_Pitch-September-17-2026.md]]
- **IGT's Prices Right:** he says he was first to report a payout bug — "**I lost 150, 000 in five locations**" — was told by IGT "**You are the only one complaining**", then got five other masters to check; IGT pulled the game, fixed it and re-released it, and "**Now I have eight versions**" in the field. Of **127** Prices Right machines, "**Only thirty-seven make money**"; the 37 on the latest version average "**two hundred and thirty-eight dollars**", while "**AristaCraft is making four hundred**". He said he would confirm the numbers. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dynamic_Gaming_Georgia_Master_Licensee_and_Cashless_Pitch-September-17-2026.md]]
- **Fort Knox:** "**I have sixty machines. I am going to pull that out.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dynamic_Gaming_Georgia_Master_Licensee_and_Cashless_Pitch-September-17-2026.md]]
- **JVL cabinets in his warehouse:** about twenty dual-screen JVL machines that locations removed for Aristocrats — "**those twenty javas that I have**… **They remove this, put aristocrats**". Later he speaks of "**thirty, forty**", and in the debrief Val says "**my 40 machines**". The count is not fixed. Plans for them are on [[wiki/projects/texas-used-machine-distribution.md]] (Sept 17, afternoon). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dynamic_Gaming_Georgia_Master_Licensee_and_Cashless_Pitch-September-17-2026.md]]

## Cashless today: Fiery Pay, as a stopgap

- **He runs Fiery Pay** (rendered "Fireplace", "Afshar's", "Offshot"). Felipe's summary: a "**complete solution at two of the ninety locations where he has their POS**". He is on it because it is finished, not because it is right: "**It's not a permanent solution. It's a bandage for right now for the store owners that are bothering me too much.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dynamic_Gaming_Georgia_Master_Licensee_and_Cashless_Pitch-September-17-2026.md]]
- **He says others followed him to it:** "**I told people I'm going with Fiery Pay. Everybody started doing Fiery Pay… We reached I think nine or eleven percent of market share in a very short period. We were getting about fifty, sixty applications a day.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dynamic_Gaming_Georgia_Master_Licensee_and_Cashless_Pitch-September-17-2026.md]]
- **He tried Novomatic's system first** — his location was "**the first ever location in Georgia that they tried**" — and "**it failed miserably**". Prices for both systems are on [[wiki/topics/georgia-prize-card-processor-economics.md]] (Sept 17). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dynamic_Gaming_Georgia_Master_Licensee_and_Cashless_Pitch-September-17-2026.md]]
- **Loyalty:** asked if he would stay with Fiery Pay, "**I'm loyal to my money.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dynamic_Gaming_Georgia_Master_Licensee_and_Cashless_Pitch-September-17-2026.md]]

## What he says a working product must do

Stated repeatedly; JVL's answers are on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]] (Sept 17). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dynamic_Gaming_Georgia_Master_Licensee_and_Cashless_Pitch-September-17-2026.md]]

- **Redemption behind the counter.** "**the location owner likes to keep the control**", and players "**like to talk to the cashier**". A gas-station player "**is waiting for my sandwich**" and will not press many buttons; the same man "**will do it in a casino. He will not do it at the gas station.**"
- **One printer behind the register:** "**I don't want six printers or nine printers at a location that needs to refill for nine different times.**"
- **Split transaction:** "**If I won five hundred dollars… hundred dollars back on machine number one… hundred dollars in lottery… hundred dollars in other gift cards**" — with one generic button: "**You just need to have a button for other gift card.**" He says TITO cannot do this and "**your system is better**".
- **A finished product:** "**It has to be a finished product. It has to work.**" And his complaint that he was brought in late: "**We would have won the game if you guys would have involved me earlier.**"

## Confidentiality and contract

He asked what JVL would do with his location data — "**you will be knowing where my good locations are, where my bad locations**" — and whether JVL would sell it "**to a casino or any other big master like Bulldog**". Val offered to sign a non-disclosure agreement and to destroy the data on request. Asked about a contract for the equipment, Val: "**No contract.**" See [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-17-the-ninety-dollar-fee-is-drafted-from-the-location-not-paid-by-the-master.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dynamic_Gaming_Georgia_Master_Licensee_and_Cashless_Pitch-September-17-2026.md]]

## The receivable

He asked for relief on an old balance. Felipe: "**just over ten thousand dollars, and some of them are past two hundred sixty**" (no unit is spoken; the notes read it as days), for repair parts ("**These are just repair belts**", as transcribed), and "**Last time I received half a payment**". Val refused to discount it: "**I can't discount a year-old account… I don't mind working with you on new deals and giving you breaks on new deals**". *Inference, labelled:* it sits beside the day-earlier ruling [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-16-do-not-discount-hold-price-extend-terms-add-value.md]]; no source links the two. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Dynamic_Gaming_Georgia_Master_Licensee_and_Cashless_Pitch-September-17-2026.md]]

# EDS (Colombian gaming operation)

**EDS** (as transcribed) is a gaming operation in **Colombia** that runs JVL machines and has a standing business relationship with JVL. Known through [[wiki/people/julian-puerta.md|Julian Puerta]], who speaks of it as "our operation." [[raw/julian _puerta_nebraska-march-29-2025.md]]

## What is known (Mar 29 2025)

- JVL's machines are **the best performers on the EDS operation** — 15-year-old hardware and 10-year-old software still making **$70 a day** per machine, out-earning "even… brand new cabinets in casinos." [[raw/julian _puerta_nebraska-march-29-2025.md]]
- Industry visitors come to inspect "**EDS, PAPS operations**" (the term "PAPS" is as transcribed — meaning unclear); the **CEO of ACE World** visited "last week," brought by **Miguel Cuadros**. [[raw/julian _puerta_nebraska-march-29-2025.md]]
- Market backdrop: most companies are struggling with sales in Colombia because of US-dollar exchange-rate uncertainty, and are shifting toward **revenue-share deals**. [[raw/julian _puerta_nebraska-march-29-2025.md]]
- **Eliana** — "the best money maker of the whole operation" (role otherwise unstated) — may approach Val about **changing the terms of the agreement** with JVL; Julian said "the right step for her is to convert the machines in ECO" (verbatim; meaning unclear). [[raw/julian _puerta_nebraska-march-29-2025.md]]
- Julian regards every aspect of the **EDS–JVL business** as covered by existing (partly verbal) agreements, and committed to call **Marta** on Monday to check everything is properly written. [[raw/julian _puerta_nebraska-march-29-2025.md]]

# EDS (Colombian gaming operation)

**EDS** is a **Colombian company** and gaming operation that runs JVL machines and has a standing business relationship with JVL; the decision-maker on its side is **[[wiki/people/eliana.md|Eliana]]**, and [[wiki/people/julian-puerta.md|Julian Puerta]] — who speaks of it as "our operation" — is retained by it as its day-to-day operations consultant (retainer figure held §15.8). Val treats "EDS" as the company's actual name, dictating proposal language that begins "EDS is a Colombian company, it already has relationship with JVL." [[raw/julian _puerta_nebraska-march-29-2025.md]] [[raw/meeting_with_julian_puerta_texas_eds.md]]

## The Texas distributorship role (≈Mar 22 2025)

Val's plan makes EDS the **distributor/importer of record for ~400 refurbished JVL machines into Texas**: EDS symbolically buys the machines from JVL, imports them (probably via a new **"EDS USA"** entity with a US bank account, e.g. registered in Florida), keeps ~20% of sale proceeds (split 50-50 Julian/Eliana), and pays the ~80% balance to JVL against parts/services receivables. Val also values the EDS channel as a possible future **financing line** and a legal platform for cross-border fees (e.g. "tell EDS Colombia to pay JVL Belarus a development fee"). If Eliana declines, [[wiki/people/marta.md|Marta]]'s Automated Technologies is the fallback distributor. Full terms: [[wiki/projects/texas-used-machine-distribution.md]]. [[raw/meeting_with_julian_puerta_texas_eds.md]]

## What is known (Mar 29 2025)

- JVL's machines are **the best performers on the EDS operation** — 15-year-old hardware and 10-year-old software still making **$70 a day** per machine, out-earning "even… brand new cabinets in casinos." [[raw/julian _puerta_nebraska-march-29-2025.md]]
- Industry visitors come to inspect "**EDS, PAPS operations**" (the term "PAPS" is as transcribed — meaning unclear); the **CEO of ACE World** visited "last week," brought by **Miguel Cuadros**. [[raw/julian _puerta_nebraska-march-29-2025.md]]
- Market backdrop: most companies are struggling with sales in Colombia because of US-dollar exchange-rate uncertainty, and are shifting toward **revenue-share deals**. [[raw/julian _puerta_nebraska-march-29-2025.md]]
- **Eliana** — "the best money maker of the whole operation" — may approach Val about **changing the terms of the agreement** with JVL; Julian said "the right step for her is to convert the machines in ECO" (verbatim; meaning unclear). See [[wiki/people/eliana.md]]. [[raw/julian _puerta_nebraska-march-29-2025.md]]
- Julian regards every aspect of the **EDS–JVL business** as covered by existing (partly verbal) agreements, and committed to call **[[wiki/people/marta.md|Marta]]** on Monday to check everything is properly written. [[raw/julian _puerta_nebraska-march-29-2025.md]]

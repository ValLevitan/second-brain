# Funambulist Gaming

A **Nebraska gaming route operator** and an existing JVL machine customer, visited by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] at its **Omaha office on June 16 2026** during his Nebraska field trip ([[wiki/meetings/2026-06-16-funambulist-gaming-omaha-visit.md]]). It is one of the larger Nebraska routes JVL is trying to grow into — see [[wiki/projects/nebraska-skill-market.md]] (Jun-16 2026). [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]

**Name spelling.** The speech-to-text transcript renders the company three different ways in the same conversation — **"Fanambliss Gaming"**, **"Finambulist"** and **"Phenomenalist"** — and the business-development contact as **"Scott Wine"**. The derived meeting notes state that the correct forms are **Funambulist Gaming** and **Scott Twine**, confirmed against email addresses captured in the visit note held in Val's vault. The wiki uses the corrected forms; the garbles are kept as aliases and inside verbatim quotes only (§4). [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]

## What the company is (as described by Heather Ackerman, Jun 16 2026)

- **Nebraska-only footprint, statewide.** "**Phenomenalist** [Funambulist] is all over Nebraska. We run from here all the way to the other side of the state and up and down." Asked where else they operate: "**Finambulist** [Funambulist] is Nebraska." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **About 300 locations.** "We have probably about three hundred locations… We've got a lot out on the streets." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **Corporate office in Virginia; owned by Winners Marketing.** "Our corporate office is in Virginia. And we're owned by **Winners Marketing**." Asked who Winners Marketing is, Heather answered **hedged**: "Winners Marketing is the manufacturing arm, **if I'm not mistaken**." Recorded as her uncertain statement, not as an established fact. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **Two owners.** Val asked "how many owners are there?" and was told "**Two**." He then asked whether he could fly to Virginia to meet them; Heather did not answer and routed it to Scott Twine, so **where the owners actually sit is not established** in this source. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **Family-style, not corporate.** "I'm glad we're getting to meet each other because we're a family. Kind of a family business. **We may not be super corporate structured, but we do have a very healthy book.**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **Omaha site.** The derived notes record that warehouse and operations run out of the Omaha site. In the transcript this is **Val's own summary put to Heather** — "You guys have your warehouse here. The whole operation works out of here" — which she did not separately confirm beyond agreeing that Funambulist has "a very healthy presence in Nebraska." [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **Already a JVL customer.** The exchange "You guys purchase some machines from us. **We did.**" sits inside a single merged speaker block, so the confirmation is not separately attributable in the transcript — but the derived notes state plainly that Funambulist is already a JVL customer, and Heather immediately supplied the rep's name herself: **"Dennis"** — [[wiki/people/denis-desmarais.md|Denis Desmarais]] — adding "**Dennis has been here many times**." No machine count, model or location was given. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]

## People named

- **Scott Twine** (transcript "Scott Wine") — **Director of Business Development**. Heather's wording: "Scott Wine is our business development director or director of business development." He is **Virginia-based but spends about half his time in Nebraska**, is **not an owner but is a decision-maker**, and is **covering Andy Suydan's work** while Andy is out. He was due in Nebraska **the week after** the visit — after Val had left the state. Heather routed every question about reaching the owners to him: asked whether Val could fly to Virginia to meet the owners, she answered "that would be something to connect with Scott on." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **Andy Suydan** — **Val's scheduled contact for the visit, who was absent**, and nobody in the office knew about Val's appointment. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **Heather Ackerman** — Val's actual host. She **works directly for the owners**, has "been around for a long time," and describes herself as having moved **out of the sales and business side**: "I do a lot of kind of dabble in everything… I do more party planning and travel." She was careful about the limits of what she knew — "I don't want to give you incorrect information. That's why I stopped" — and placed herself in the same bracket as JVL's own field rep: "**like me, I'm like Dennis. I cannot — but I know who to get.**" She scanned Val's QR business card, took his details and committed to forward them to Scott. (Recorded in prose rather than as a person page — one substantive appearance so far, the same treatment given J&J's Scott Todd and Jeff Pryor.) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **Chris** — met Val at the front office and did not know about the meeting: "I didn't know anything about your meeting. It was kind of a last minute thing." Brief appearance only. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]

## What Val told them (Jun 16 2026)

- **His standard opening.** He owns JVL, is **second generation**; the business started "**forty two years ago**". Val: "I graduated from being educated in **digital electronics** at that time and joined them." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **His differentiator, verbatim:** "I'm looking forward to establish special relationships in every market. What does it mean? It means that **I listen to the decision makers, I pinpoint three pain points for them, and I address it. And I make all the decisions in the company, so I can address it. Dennis, for example, cannot.** So that's why I'm here and that's why I meet with the ownership." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **The rep change, stated plainly to a customer.** "We're **replacing Dennis** here, so **Dennis will be gone**. We're bringing somebody else." [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **The office move, as stated on the day.** "And we had an office in **South Dakota**; we're going to **move it to Nebraska**. So yeah… And **we like your market**. I mean this is a great region for us, so we're hoping to grow here." *(As-of Jun 16 2026. A later source — the Aug-26 2026 record on [[wiki/projects/south-dakota-vlt-market.md]] — has the South Dakota office **closed** with its Light-&-Wonder test bench shipped to Georgia, so the Jun-16 "move it to Nebraska" framing is not the wiki's current state.)* [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]
- **No return trip.** "I usually don't make frequent trips. We have a lot of clients, and I do meet with every ownership all the time. So it would not be natural for me to come back here unless they're here or we meet in Virginia with the ownership." He was flying out the next morning. Val asked for a warehouse tour; Heather was willing but had other commitments, and **no next meeting was set in the room**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Funambulist_Gaming_Omaha_Visit-June-16-2026.md]]

# EDC (Export Development Canada)

EDC (Export Development Canada) is the financing body a JVL customer, Elevate, uses to fund its purchase of JVL machines.

## Relationship with JVL

- Elevate's approval with EDC covered about 700 machines, described in the original quote as video lottery terminals. JVL's task was to make sure its invoicing matched what EDC approved. [[raw/james_reble_elevate_january_14_2025.md]]
- The quote to EDC was made about two years earlier; unit prices and model descriptions have since changed with hardware/software configuration. [[raw/james_reble_elevate_january_14_2025.md]]

Note: the transcript garbles the name as "EDS" in one spot; recorded here as EDC (Export Development Canada). See [[wiki/organizations/elevate.md]].

# EDC (Export Development Canada)

EDC (Export Development Canada) was named in the Jan 14 2025 call as the financing body behind the Elevate/JVL machine deal. NOTE: that call framed Elevate as a JVL *customer financed through EDC*, which the owner later corrected (2026-07-13) — Elevate is a **lender** that took a pledge on the machines — so EDC's exact role in this deal is now **uncertain** and single-source. See [[wiki/organizations/elevate.md]].

## Relationship with JVL

- Elevate's approval with EDC covered about 700 machines, described in the original quote as video lottery terminals. JVL's task was to make sure its invoicing matched what EDC approved. [[raw/james_reble_elevate_january_14_2025.md]]
- The quote to EDC was made about two years earlier; unit prices and model descriptions have since changed with hardware/software configuration. [[raw/james_reble_elevate_january_14_2025.md]]

Note: the transcript garbles the name as "EDS" in one spot; recorded here as EDC (Export Development Canada). See [[wiki/organizations/elevate.md]]. Not to be confused with **EDS**, the Colombian gaming operation running JVL machines — [[wiki/organizations/eds-colombia.md]].

## History

- **≈May 2024 (date inferred; pre-window source):** Val's own description of the chain places EDC squarely in the middle of the export-financing pipeline: "**BDC** … first pre-approve[s] you, then they recommend you to **EDC**, then **EDC brings Elevate**" — and only for goods actually exported out of Canada (software-only export "they're not going to do it"). [[raw/julian_puerta_jvl_mexico.md]]

## May 20 2025 — reconfirmed in the BDC financing intro

On the [[wiki/meetings/2025-05-20-bdc-flexport-ga-coam-financing.md|BDC call]] Val restated the chain from the borrower's end: **[[wiki/organizations/elevate.md|Elevate]] is a Canadian entity approved by EDC** (transcribed "Expert Development Canada" — a garble of Export Development Canada) that acts as a financial bridge where the **purchase approval falls on the purchaser (the operator), not on JVL**, giving an approved customer a **five-year term**. JVL's problem — Elevate is slow to approve and suits a centralized distributor, not Georgia's many small operators — is why JVL now seeks **BDC** to finance JVL directly instead (see [[wiki/organizations/bdc.md]]). [[raw/bdc_intro_to_flex-port_ga-coam-may-20-2025.md]]

## June 8 2026 — Val looks for an EDC equivalent in the US

Asking [[wiki/organizations/bank-of-america.md|Bank of America]] how JVL could offer its customers terms, Val described EDC as "a crown corporation in Canada" that insures banks, so a bank does basic due diligence on JVL's customers and offers them a payment plan: "We sell them equipment, we get paid. The bank keeps the margin… our customers get to enjoy three year credit." The bank's credit officer knew of no US program like it in twenty-eight years. [[wiki/meetings/2026-06-08-bank-of-america-banking-review-and-georgia-financing.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_BofA_Lenin_Hernandez_Banking_Review_and_Georgia_Financing-June-8-2026.md]]

# Year-end statements — reclassification + Peru loss + accrued interest (Jun 30 2025)

A short (~7-minute) [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] ↔ [[wiki/people/alka-sood.md|Alka]] call reviewing the interim financial statements the external accountant (**Sean**, [[wiki/people/sean-riley.md|Sean Riley]]) had prepared: he included both the **ScrapNeck accrued-interest** restatement (FY2023/2024 → retained earnings) and the estimated **Peruvian loss** (its own line).

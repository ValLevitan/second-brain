## Notes on the sources

- **§12:** new meeting. No page cites either file; the only earlier Billy stubs are Sept 2 and Sept 15, with different content. One notes + transcript pair = one stub.
- **Speaker labels are unreliable.** Four speaker numbers for a two-person call; Val is mostly Speaker 1 and Billy mostly Speaker 3, but Speakers 2 and 4 carry both men. Attribution on the substance pages is by content.
- **Where the notes and the transcript part ways (transcript followed):**
  - **Which card is the "path to cash".** The notes say the **black** card gives ATM or cash-back access. In the transcript Val's line opens "**And what do the blue cards offer? Path to cash.**", then a stray fragment "**The black cards.**", then "**ATM access or cashback access**". The wiki's own record has the **blue** card as Skill Pay, the one with ATM access ([[wiki/organizations/incom.md]], Aug 26). The pages here attribute the path to cash to the blue card and note the fragment.
  - **The plastic card was not dropped.** The notes record, as a decision, that the pay tag is preferred "not a dispensed barcode card" and that Val "stopped pressing the plastic-card idea". In the transcript Val agrees a card is "**costly and inefficient**" but says "**My only question is whether the player will embrace it**", then proposes the $5 promo incentive; Billy sums up: "**So you actually want to test the idea of printing a barcode on a physical card**", and both expect to "**dispense those cards in the very beginning**".
  - **Card cost, left out of the notes.** A voice says "**It's gonna cost us five dollars to print that card and dispense it**"; Val answers "**it's not gonna cost us five dollars… Forty forty nine cents or whatever**". The $5 is the promo credit, not the card cost.
  - **Who sent the notice to stores** is lost in the transcript ("**Send out a notice to all of their stores that the Skill Pay card is coming**"). The notes supply InComm; that reading is kept as an inference.
  - **"Decisions Made"** in the notes are conversation positions between two companies with no agreement. No decision page is created.
- **Terms left as transcribed:** "**keto card**" (Billy's card-scan feature) and "**Bob**" in the closing line. The notes say Val did not know either; not guessed. The transcript's "**LLA**" is read as LLH (location licence holder), which Billy defines two minutes later.

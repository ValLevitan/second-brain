# Georgia state-tax follow-up — the PL 86-272 exemption path (Jun 3 2025)

A follow-on Georgia-tax call with [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]], chartered accountant [[wiki/people/sean-riley.md|Sean Riley]] and US tax advisor [[wiki/people/mark-tax-advisor.md|Mark]]. Val pressed *why* JVL Labs (a Canadian company) is taxed in Georgia based on its association with the US company, and the advisors laid out the **Public Law 86-272** exemption path: if JVL Labs ships only a tangible good via a common carrier with **no attached services (no management fees)**, it should owe **no Georgia income tax** — just a disclosure filing.

# JVL Mexico tax-litigation strategy call — Humberto Sarkis (May 29 2025)

Strategy call on JVL Mexico's tax matter (part of the [[wiki/decisions/2025-03-24-close-jvl-mexico-operation.md|Mexico wind-down]]) with [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]], Mexican gaming lawyer [[wiki/people/humberto-sarkis.md|Humberto Sarkis]], his tax colleague **Mauricio**, and [[wiki/people/monica.md|Monica]]. The plan discussed: take the write-off, remain visible, fight each collection attempt, and rely on the **5-year expiry** of the tax liability; Val pressed for **court precedent** (he cited **Casa Mayor** using three years) and challenged the team on the technical defensibility of the case.

This is a **meeting transcript** and is in scope; it is distinct from the out-of-scope email corpus "Legal/Mexican Tax Litigation" (§1). Substance lives on [[wiki/people/humberto-sarkis.md]] and [[wiki/topics/jvl-finance-back-office.md]]. (Transcript internally duplicates — §12, facts taken once.)

# Game-design review — Jackpot Rush (JPRE) any-bet + denomination scheduler (May 30 2025)

Review with [[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael Gottlieb]], [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] and [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]]. Michael's premise: the **max-bet-required** progressive eligibility on **Jackpot Rush** (JPRE) is too rich (~$3 / 480 credits) versus average bets of ~$1.20–$1.87, so most players see a static credit tower instead of running progressive meters. Two decisions came out of it — **remove the max-bet requirement** (progressives eligible at any bet) and **build a denomination-scheduler capability** across games — captured on [[wiki/decisions/2025-05-30-jackpot-rush-any-bet-and-denomination-scheduler.md]]. Design detail on [[wiki/topics/slot-game-design-principles.md]].

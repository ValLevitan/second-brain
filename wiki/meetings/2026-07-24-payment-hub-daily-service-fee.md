# Payment Hub Daily Service Fee (July 24 2026)

**Date:** July 24, 2026, 10:00–10:22 AM Eastern (~13 min)
**Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/nathan-carver.md|Nathan Carver]], [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]], [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]].

A short call in which Val asked the group to explain the **"daily fee"** so he could close a knowledge gap. Nathan explained it is a **configuration in Payment Hub** assigned to a customer location — whatever fee is set there flows onto the invoice when billing runs — and Juan added it was envisioned as an upcharge for extra services. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Payment_Hub_Daily_Service_Fee-July-24-2026.md]] The group agreed a **consolidated daily invoice should be emailed for as long as an account is open, even when every line is zero**, and that the fee field should **default to zero on new locations**, activated only when a fee applies.

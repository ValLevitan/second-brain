Then Bosik's network-and-security document was walked line by line and split between MVP 1 and MVP 2 [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Transactional_MVP1_SkillPay_Multi_Product_and_Device_Network_Security-September-17-2026.md]].

**Where the derived notes and the transcript diverge.** More than on any previous day in this series — ten places, all flagged on the pages that carry them. Four are substantive omissions, one is a speaker inversion, and two are assertions firmer than the source:

1. **Speaker inversion.** The notes attribute the worked $1,500 / $1,000 / $1,200 example to Chudinov; **Klimovich** posed it and Chudinov answered it.
2. **Firmer than the source.** The notes state flatly that capability 144 "moves to MVP2". The transcript supports that only for the remote-firmware-update content; after the capability was thinned, Bosik said of the remainder "это идёт в MVP один" and Klimovich assented. Recorded as unclear.
3. **Firmer than the source.** The notes place requirement 1410 "in MVP1 under capability 144". Klimovich took it for the **portal dashboard** he is building, and in the same passage said the *device*-side display can wait — "для MVP один переживём, если там их не будет показываться". The notes conflate the two surfaces.
4. **Omission — the reason the firewall is unavoidable.** Bosik's CloudFront/dynamic-address explanation of why the SIM carrier cannot restrict the devices to JVL's own hosts is absent from the notes entirely. It is the technical ground for the whole MVP 1 security decision.
5. **Omission — requirement 1556.** The notes never give the requirement ID for the firewall / NAT / WAN-LAN item; Irina reads it on screen as "15, 56".
6. **Omission — capability 141.** Irina believed the whole of it had been moved to the next release the previous day; Chudinov corrected her — "**Капс[тура] 141 не может уехать [в MVP] 2[:] там уже часть физически закрыта или практически закрыта. Там одна фича осталась за мной**". Not in the notes.
7. **Omission — requirement 1411** (buffering Flex-Port events when there is no signal, and Flex-Port telemetry into Grafana), and Chudinov's statement that he has no picture yet of what the Flex-Port side logs.
8. **Omission — what requirement 1421 is.** The notes list it as a capability-less orphan; the transcript shows it is about replacing the Flex IQ gateway at a location, with every issued-but-unredeemed ticket lost, and Klimovich accepting that risk into MVP 2.
9. **Omission — the whole fractional-amount exchange** (cents added to Flex-Port because ACH can carry any amount; Flex IQ redemption in whole dollars; Georgia sums in multiples of five; manual wallet creation floor). Carried on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]].
10. **Omission — the MVP 2 parking capability's number.** Irina reads it off screen as "a capability, something in the 170s"; the notes carry no number at all, and the corpus still does **not** establish 177.

Otherwise the notes track the transcript: no back-imported prior-day material, and Klimovich's mid-sentence self-correction ("2 products — no, not 2 — multi-product") survives in substance.

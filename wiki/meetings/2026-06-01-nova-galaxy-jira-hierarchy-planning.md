# NOVA / Galaxy JIRA Hierarchy Planning (June 1 2026)

**June 1, 2026, 3:34 PM ET · ~1h 17m.** Attendees: [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]], [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]].

Planning the JIRA hierarchy for a large machine release organised under **three top-level umbrellas** — **FP7** (new hardware/processor board), **NOVA** (the on-cabinet platform), and **Galaxy** (the web-services tier, split into **Transaction Galaxy** and **Gaming Galaxy**) — before touching the live hierarchy. The group worked out a proposed **Product → Capability → Feature** model (requirements *linked*, not nested) to prototype in a sandbox the next day. §11 header time. §15.8: platform/JIRA-tooling economics only, no named-person compensation.

Durable substance is split across two topics: the FP7/NOVA/Galaxy three-umbrella structure and the "Galaxy = the renamed JVL Web Services tier" naming update live on [[wiki/topics/nova-platform-architecture.md]] (June-1); the JIRA-hierarchy restructuring (Product→Capability→Feature, top streams, sandbox-first) continues the May-7 restructuring thread on [[wiki/topics/github-release-management-pipeline.md]] (June-1).

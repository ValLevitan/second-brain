# Intro call with J&J operator (Scott Todd, Jeff Pryor) (May 7 2025)

A first sales/intro call between [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] and two executives of the gaming route operator **J&J** — Scott Todd (Chief Revenue Officer) and Jeff Pryor (Chief Operating Officer). Val pitched JVL's cabinets, Nebraska/Georgia skill games, and the FlexPay payment solution; the two sides agreed to arrange game viewings in Georgia and Nebraska.

Substance lives on [[wiki/organizations/jj-operator.md]] (who J&J is, their footprint, and the next steps). The call opened on Val's birthday ("today is my birthday").

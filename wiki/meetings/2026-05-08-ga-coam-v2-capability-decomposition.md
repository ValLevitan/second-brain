# GA-COAM v2 Capability Decomposition (May 8 2026)

**Date:** 8 May 2026, 1:11pm Eastern (§11 recording header time); ~44m. Bilingual (English + Russian; Teams garbled the middle stretches).
**Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]], [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim Podvoiski]], [[wiki/people/igor-metelski.md|Igor Metelski]], [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]].

The morning of two back-to-back May-8 alignment sessions on the **V2 / V43 GA-COAM v2 "3-JP-Progressive"** Georgia release — the purpose being to pair market-facing **capabilities** with developer-facing **features** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ga_coam_v2_capability_decomposition-May-8-2026.md]]. The consequential outcome is structural: Juan's single bloated **Capability 9** ("deliver a new jackpot experience to replace the Georgia peach progressive bonus … 3 levels of progressive … match-3 symbol concept … compatible with all legacy line-and-ways games") was decomposed into **three distinct capabilities** — (1) support three different *families* of games in one package (Lines & Ways + Jackpot Rush/JPRE-Georgia + Fiery Ice Link Georgia — never done before in a single product), (2) three unrelated ("non-symbiotic") progressives coexisting in the same package, and (3) the new match-3 progressive replacing the legacy peach bonus [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ga_coam_v2_capability_decomposition-May-8-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ga_coam_v2_capability_decomposition-May-8-2026.md]]. Efim surfaced the implicit foundational capability (the three-families combination) that was "so obvious to Juan he didn't think it worth mentioning" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ga_coam_v2_capability_decomposition-May-8-2026.md]]. Val's working principle carried the meeting: a **capability is executed once (all-encapsulating); a requirement is durable, singular, reusable** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ga_coam_v2_capability_decomposition-May-8-2026.md]]. Also settled: the liquid-glass / new-screen interface stays under its own pre-existing capability (not one of the three); progressive-controller and data-centre pieces are **features, not capabilities** (market view vs implementation); [[wiki/people/igor-metelski.md|Igor]] writes framework-side features while the data-centre features fall to Sergey / a "Dima" / [[wiki/people/evgeny-kharetski.md|Kharetski]] in a later dedicated session (Val declined to add more people to this line — "if each person says 3 sentences, that's 60 sentences") [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ga_coam_v2_capability_decomposition-May-8-2026.md]]. Val recessed the meeting one hour so he and Juan could formalise the capabilities and draft requirements before reconvening (the [[wiki/meetings/2026-05-08-ga-coam-v2-requirements-workflow-personas.md|3:00pm requirements-workflow session]]) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ga_coam_v2_capability_decomposition-May-8-2026.md]].

Substance and citations live on [[wiki/topics/persona-driven-design-and-orchestration-platform.md]] (May-8 2026 section — capability/requirement/feature/persona workflow) and [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]] (May-8 2026 — GA-COAM v2 capability decomposition). §15.8: GA-COAM / 3-JP-progressive / capability economics only; no named-person compensation.

# Backend-architecture kickoff — CMS, transactional products, HHR & Omni (Aug 14 2025)

**Date:** 2025-08-14 (12:04 PM) · **Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]], [[wiki/people/aaron.md|Aaron Bockelie]], [[wiki/people/will-jones.md|Will Jones]], [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]], [[wiki/people/nathan-carver.md|Nathan Carver]], [[wiki/people/dmitri-chudinov.md|Dmitri Chudinov]] (transcribed "Dzmitry Chudzinau"). [[raw/backend_architecture-august-14-2025.md]]

Val's cross-cutting architecture kickoff to bring four threads together — the legacy **CMS**, the **transactional products** (calendar priority for the July-1-2026 Georgia deadline), **HHR**, and the **Omni** framework everything is now built on. The group agreed to first produce an **overall block-diagram architecture of *what* the system does (not *how*)**, then carve MVPs, use cases, and the detailed "how" from it; a phased plan and owners were set. [[raw/backend_architecture-august-14-2025.md]]

The ruling and phased plan are on [[wiki/decisions/2025-08-14-backend-architecture-block-diagram-first-mvp.md]]. Substance is split across [[wiki/topics/persona-driven-design-and-orchestration-platform.md]] (CMS modernization / observability-orchestration platform), [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]] (transactional-products architecture) and [[wiki/topics/hhr-historical-horse-racing.md]] (HHR).

# Escrow Model and Owned-Network Vision — July 21 2026

**Date:** July 21 2026, 7:05 PM Eastern (recording header); ~43 minutes, in Russian. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Escrow_Model_and_Owned_Network_Vision-July-21-2026.md]]

**Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] and [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]]. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Escrow_Model_and_Owned_Network_Vision-July-21-2026.md]]

A continuation of an earlier discussion — the narrow case (raised by "Dmitry") of **what to do with local transactions stored on a machine when a device is unbound**. Val argues JVL should hold such in-flight transactions in **escrow** rather than store them, because JVL does not own the local network; and he lays out his **owned-network product vision** (SMIB/FlexPort + recycler + skill-pay on JVL's own network, without MuthaGoose). They agreed to bring in Pavel and Dmitry the next day to confirm whether MuthaGoose supports the escrow mode. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Escrow_Model_and_Owned_Network_Vision-July-21-2026.md]]

Durable substance (the escrow model for other-network transactions, the IQ-as-pocket / Payment-Hub-to-INCOM flow, the print-ticket-then-validate fallback, and the owned-network vision — SMIB, FlexPort, recycler, skill-pay card, market-share plan) → [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]]. Same escrow / transaction-flow theme as the [[wiki/meetings/2026-07-17-iq-down-printer-bypass.md|July-17 IQ-down printer-bypass]] design; relates to a separate July-21 "Unbind vs Freeze / offline transactions" discussion and continues the June IQ-binding work ([[wiki/meetings/2026-06-12-port-iq-pairing-for-texas-market.md|Jun-12 Port-IQ pairing]]).

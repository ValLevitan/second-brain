# Flex Recycler Slim — a narrow between-machines payout kiosk (Val + Genadi, Mar 11 2026)

**Date:** 2026-03-11 (recording header "11 March 2026, 08:31pm") · **Duration:** ~28m51s · **Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] (started transcription), [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi Peari]]. Russian, heavily garbled transcript.

**Purpose:** a hardware/industrial-design one-on-one working a new **slim, narrow payout kiosk — the "Flex Recycler Slim"** — meant to stand *between machines* on a gaming floor. Continues the payout-device catalog thread from the [[wiki/meetings/2026-02-25-genadi-packaging-texas-flexport-market-strategy.md|Feb-25 packaging/Texas call]] (where the bill recycler was deferred) and the [[wiki/meetings/2026-02-12-genadi-flex-station-flexbill-texas.md|Feb-12 FlexBill-for-Texas call]] — and revives the "between-machines dispensing tower" concept from the Apr 2025 FlexPay-Evolution work.

**§15.8:** clear — market/BOM economics only, no named-person compensation.

Substance (the ≤1-foot / ~11-inch width rule; the competitor "Silver Kiosk" critique; perimeter lighting, ≤15-inch symmetric screen, smooth vandal-resistant gapless front + Gorilla-Glass touchscreen, front-access service, layout printer→scanner→dispenser; the cheap-BOM / recurring-revenue economics ~$1,500–$2,000 build → ~$5,000 sell, ARM + cheap chip; the reuse-JVL-board-vs-simplify debate; the low-power door-monitoring mode; two-size metal pedestal, anti-theft mounting; plastic-prototype status + ~7–8-week tooling) lives on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]] (Mar-11), with hardware progress on [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md]] (Mar-11) and the go-to-market framing on [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md]] (Mar-11).

A competitor kiosk — Val/Genadi call it the **"Silver Kiosk,"** attributed to a maker transcribed "Данила / Danilo" — is critiqued as the design foil but has no wiki page (competitor product, identity unconfirmed). A pricing/fabrication vendor transcribed "Quaternion / кватернион" and a metal/enclosure supplier rendered "Andersen" are named in passing (garbled, unconfirmed).

# InComm working lunch — card-to-machine payments, and the cash-recycler standoff

**Date/time:** September 2 2026, **~11:55 AM – 2:02 PM Eastern** (~2h 07m), a working lunch — from the derived notes' header [[raw/Meeting_Notes_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].
**Location:** a Lebanese / West Indian restaurant near Lawrenceville, Georgia; the host introduces it in the transcript and names his grandmother as the restaurant's namesake [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].

**Calendar:** none found on val@jvl.ca; the notes record the meeting as filed on content alone [[raw/Meeting_Notes_InComm_Machine_Payments_and_ATM_Recycler_Standoff-September-2-2026.md]].

**Purpose:** two halves that pulled in opposite directions — a briefing on InComm's forthcoming **card-to-machine** product, which JVL wants, and a confrontation in which InComm told Val that his **cash-recycler ATM** would put the partnership at risk.

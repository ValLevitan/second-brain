# Nebraska game design + planning — the six Golden Husk games (June 23 2025)

**Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]], [[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael]] (Gottlieb), [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan]], [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena]], [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim]] ("Afim").

Working session locking the design + production plan for the six Nebraska skill games under the **Golden Husk** bonus (Spicy Fruits, Jem Brilliante, Dutch Mill, Clover Fortune, Crazy Spins, Midnight Money). The team walked the **primary power/skill meter** behaviour (30-second/on-touch timer, win-modifier text, ceiling-rounding) and the **secondary pattern-match (Simon-style) game** (25 rounds → 104%, loading screens, low-contrast text), all captured in Jira design + production tasks; design finishes end of June, production starts early July. The durable game-design mechanics are on [[wiki/topics/slot-game-design-principles.md]]; market/schedule facts on [[wiki/projects/nebraska-skill-market.md]].

Durable points (single source):

- **Cool Cat Keno UI review (the "Liquid Glass" dashboard).** Val's feedback: the **service/money buttons are too pale** — the green on **draw/play** must be brighter and more intense (the colour of money), and win should read as **gold**. Lena explained the button taxonomy on the Liquid Glass dashboard (yellow meters; blue/green service buttons like "more games"/question/quick-peek/clear/speed; **draw and play always green**) and that the static screens understate it — the finished dashboard is animated with gradients/reflections. She agreed to make the **draw button bright, intense green**. The "total draws 27" info readout struck Val as **detached** from the draw button; left as Lena's call. Cats will be **animated**; the game references competitor **Squish Kino** for the free-game layout.
- **Schedule accepted.** Val reviewed the schedule that morning, had **no comments** (all prior comments addressed; Lena/Michael had reviewed it in detail the day before); Juan also had none. Accepted as-is and moved on.
- **La Rosa** restated as **Lion's Riches with the La Rosa art** (consistent with Nov-6's "clone of Lion's Riches"), in progress.
- **Buy-a-bonus $1,000-max-cap-win feature** → the candidate theme to exploit it is **Jungle Jaguar Extreme**; Michael + Lena will do a quality pass on it. Michael flagged that parts of the **JVL art library are getting dated** and must be modernised ("it is 2025… we're a forward-looking organization") — every game should look cutting-edge.
- **Texas / South Dakota game idea.** For markets 30–90 days out (**Nebraska** deploying, **South Dakota**), Michael floated a **Texas-flavoured** game (Western/rustic — e.g. reworking **Rodeo Queen**) that would also play in South Dakota. Juan cautioned against the name **"Lone Star"** because it implies a Texas-designed product ("it could just get weird") — lean **Americana** instead. Anastasia is performing well on her feedback-heavy concept work.

# FlexPay TITO architecture settled — FlexSMIB + FlexPort + Georgia shipment (Apr 7 2025)

A ~33-minute English meeting "dedicated to SMIB and FlexPay integration" that settled the two-device TITO architecture and the **FlexSMIB** name (see [[wiki/decisions/2025-04-07-flexsmib-modern-flexport-legacy.md]]), set the TITO beta target of **April 30**, and turned into a shipping order: **4 FlexPays + 16 FlexPorts to Georgia, 1 FlexPay + 2 FlexPorts to the Florida office, shipping tomorrow**.

# Asimex call — importing Flex machines into Mexico (~Mar 19 2025, date inferred)

An English-language call between [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]], **Hector** (JVL's Mexico-side finance/coordination contact — see [[wiki/people/hector.md]]; attribution is an inference, the transcript gives no surname), and **Alejandro**, JVL's contact at the Mexican freight/customs firm **ASIMEX** (see [[wiki/organizations/shipping-vendors.md]]), about how to import JVL's **Flex machines from Canada into Mexico** now that Mexico has banned importing complete slot machines. [[raw/Asimex - Importation of Flex machines_2.md]]

**Date is inferred, not stated:** the transcript itself is undated; the earliest of the three transcription runs of this audio is timestamped 2025-03-19, so the meeting happened on or before that date. The decree it discusses was "publicated on past October" (Alejandro dates it October 18), i.e. October 2024, referenced as recent. [[raw/Asimex - Importation of Flex machines_2.md]]

Substance lives on [[wiki/organizations/shipping-vendors.md]] (the import-ban workaround, costs, credit terms, NOM certificates, and Asimex's Mexico warehouse) and [[wiki/people/hector.md]].

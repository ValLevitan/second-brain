# Epic Amusement visit — Kenny Patel (Aug 25 2026)

**Date:** August 25 2026, ~11:11 AM Eastern, on site at Epic's office in Georgia. One Plaud recording of ~4 h 41 min (English). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]] The derived notes pair it with an Outlook event "Customer visit - Epic Amusement Kenny Patel", 11:00 AM ET. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]

**Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda]]; for Epic, [[wiki/people/kenny-patel.md|Kenny Patel]] (transcript "Speaker 3"; identified because a player in his own story says "hey, uh, Kenny") and an unnamed former commercial banker who works for him ("Speaker 4"). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]] Kenny's surname comes from the notes and calendar title only. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]

**Purpose:** Val's first face-to-face with Epic's owner — "I wanted to meet you… face to face" — to win machine orders and introduce JVL's transactional products. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]

**Outcome:** no order. Val: "I would like to walk away with an order"; Kenny: "I'll see what I can do and I'll get back with you." A follow-up at JVL's office was agreed. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]]

**One recording, several conversations (§12).** The file runs past the Epic meeting. By timestamp: 0:00–1:54 the Epic office meeting; ~1:54–2:10 a walk to a nearby gas station to look at competitor machines; ~2:23–3:10 lunch, Val and Felipe only; ~3:57 a voicemail Val left for a Texas operator "Joe"; ~4:23–4:41 (about 3:34–3:52 PM) arrival at the shop of the Mutha Goose maker — the speaker says "they had one of our MudaGoose systems". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]] One stub is kept: only the Epic part is a meeting in its own right here. The last segment is the start of the same-day Really Killer Systems visit — **confirmed on content**: it ends with the maker saying "I guess I better bring Tony in" for Val's who-is-who question, and the separate RKS recording opens on that same question ("Tony… He wants to know who which operators are doing what around here now"). That visit has its own stub: [[wiki/meetings/2026-08-25-really-killer-systems-operator-vetting.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Really_Killer_Systems_Operator_Vetting-August-25-2026.md]]

**Notes vs transcript (transcript followed).** The derived notes carry claims that are **not in the recording**: a one-to-one fleet trade-in at ~$6,000 a cabinet, "no questions asked"; an Epic commitment of "20 units + ~15 more" from September 1, with 50–63 more to swap; V3 games "shipping September 1"; "good early location test results"; and Val's 1984 / Latin-America / AMD-processor story. In the recording Kenny commits to nothing, and says the new game on test does worse than JVL's old games. The notes also reverse the order of the transactional pitch (the recording gives the "final destination" first, and the minimum-entry system only after Kenny pushes back), and list a "Chris" among the attendees whom the recording does not identify. The notes omit the $14,500 price, the 10% VIP discount, the test-machine numbers and the lunch items. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Epic_Amusement_Kenny_Patel_Visit-August-25-2026.md]] **Where those claims come from (back-fill finding, inference):** the $6,000 one-to-one swap "no questions asked", the "twenty… plus extra fifteen" from September 1, the fifty-to-sixty-three cabinets, V3 on September 1, the good location tests and Val's 1984 / Latin-America / AMD story are all in the recording of the **Aug-27 ARC coffee** ([[wiki/meetings/2026-08-27-arc-coffee-fleet-swap-and-transactional-pitch.md]]). The Epic notes appear to have borrowed them from that meeting. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Coffee_Fleet_Swap_and_Transactional_Pitch-August-27-2026.md]]

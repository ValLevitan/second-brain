**When:** September 1 2026, **~1:05 – 2:44 PM Eastern** (~1 hour 39 minutes), per the recording header on the derived notes — a demo of roughly an hour, then ~35 minutes of internal coaching after the customer left. **Where:** the JVL Georgia office. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

**Who:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda Giorgi]], [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] (product), and from the customer side [[wiki/people/neel.md|Neel]] (operations) and [[wiki/people/jai.md|Jai]] (service and orders) of [[wiki/organizations/akshar-gaming.md|Akshar Gaming]] / Ananta Amusement. **[[wiki/people/vishal-patel.md|Vishal Patel]] did not attend** — the notes record that the export filename said "Vishal Patel product demo" and that the file was renamed because he was not present. The decision still routes through him. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

**Purpose:** the demo promised on Aug 26 — show the **Mega Hit Games** cabinet, walk the transactional range, and settle the D27 trade-in. It did not close, because the people who came were the operations and service managers rather than the owner. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

**Capture:** a **Plaud** recording (offline), routed via `Plaud-Inbox/JVL-Customer-Visits`. Notes + transcript of the same recording = one derived family, one stub (§12). [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Narayani_Amusements_Mega_Hit_Demo_and_Trade_In-September-1-2026.md]]

Durable points (single source):

- **Sales.** No Amazon sales this week; **one distributor sale to Game Room Guys** (one "cold version"), shipped out of Amazon. Distributor price **$3,080 + $125 flat Amazon shipping = $3,200**. Game Room Guys asked to ship on **their FedEx** (~$40 vs $125); Andrei declined (fulfillment already moved to Amazon, no leverage). Val's steer: **don't lower the price** — move them to **order themselves via Amazon Prime Business** (the **5% cashback** covers the FedEx gap); target self-order price **$3,300**; Game Room Guys to be the **first distributor to self-order via Amazon**. [[raw/echo_distribution_marketing_update-september-26-2025.md]]
- **Newsletter leads.** The **RePlay email-newsletter banner** drew **3 reseller leads that week for $750** (≈$250/lead); 2 are close to ordering. [[raw/echo_distribution_marketing_update-september-26-2025.md]]
- **Firearms media buys.** Agreement with **American Handgunner** (~**160,000 unique visitors/mo**; covers 15 premium handgun brands priced $3k+): banners for the **whole of October, starting Mon Sept 29 → Oct 31**, **$750 for 60,000 impressions** (~$11.5 per 1,000). Two more magazines in play: **AR-15** (~**4M unique visitors/mo** — "one of the biggest," call next week) and **Athlon Outdoors** (A-T-H-L-O-N; ~**600,000 unique visitors/mo**, hunting-brand hub). Two or three of these should suffice to test the firearms hypothesis. [[raw/echo_distribution_marketing_update-september-26-2025.md]]
- **Influencers.** Firearms: **3 influencers** in slow contact, one a **huge premium-firearms collector** (crazy money, high-end guns, safes, shooting ranges — a showing-off collector persona, not a professional), plus another collector-area influencer (dead YouTube, active Instagram). Arcades: agreement with **Justin**, one of the biggest Instagram arcade-game collectors — **$1,200** for a video review on YouTube + Instagram + stories, with a **Linktree** redirect for ~2 weeks; **65% of his audience is 45+**, mostly homeowners/collectors ("hit the bullseye"). One more smaller arcade influencer to add. [[raw/echo_distribution_marketing_update-september-26-2025.md]]
- **Website — B2B/amusement version released.** The web team released the **B2B / amusement version**; Andrei to populate it with images. Structure: one main **B2C landing page** (home version) with blogs/links that **redirect resellers/distributors/small-business owners to a supporting B2B page** (B2B price ≠ retail; always-in-stock, support terms, volume-based discounts). Val pushed on **how you qualify a visitor as a reseller** — Andrei: nothing prohibits anyone visiting; no private info is exposed; the call-to-action is "get in touch to discuss details." Val: "you're not hitting an excitement note for me." [[raw/echo_distribution_marketing_update-september-26-2025.md]]
- **Flex page still not done.** The Flex page is **not implemented or documented** (Val had asked ~3 weeks back); Andrei didn't treat it as high priority. Val: it's **not** high priority — don't shift priorities; finish the week's plan. **Google Analytics is up**; an analytics + SEO contractor is on board (docs signed). [[raw/echo_distribution_marketing_update-september-26-2025.md]]
- **Amazon instability + Prime Day.** Amazon ad **impressions were dead all week** (support bounced him around; recovered overnight, unexplained) — Andrei worries Amazon "is not stable enough" and floated adding a **Shopify e-commerce module** to the website to sell direct; **Val: don't go there** (complicates shipping). **Prime Day (Oct 6–7):** JVL not officially selected, so it runs its **own** discount + Meta traffic + internal Amazon ads, **extended the whole week Mon–Fri** (more cart-decision time; ~**40 machines currently in carts**). Discount ~**$400 (10%)**; Val open to going more aggressive, "discount it down to $3k." [[raw/echo_distribution_marketing_update-september-26-2025.md]]
- **Monika blockers.** Need **Monika's** approval on the **Google Ads contract**, and her **interview for the documentary** (the film is otherwise ready — only black-screen blocks where her interview goes; no replies yet). Val to push her. [[raw/echo_distribution_marketing_update-september-26-2025.md]]
- **Val's "cruising framework" — building ECHO to run without Andrei.** The session's teaching core: Val wants Andrei to build a **repeatable annual marketing plan/calendar** so ECHO runs autonomously (he wants to **remove Andrei's "indispensability"**: "I don't like indispensable people… business is run" so it survives a person being gone 3 months). Method: **map the EXISTING process first** (good or bad — do not invent a new one), make it **version 1**, run next year on it, then improve to **version 2 each June**. Build it by **reverse-engineering from a realistic sales plan** (e.g. ~1 machine/week Amazon + ~2/distributors ≈ **3/month**) → traffic needed → sources → **artifacts** (physical + digital) → media plan → creative brief → advertising schedule → budget → operational plan (e.g. "when stock drops to 5, ship automatically"). Val's imagery: a **cork board with stickies and thread**, one **fully-focused week** with all interruptions shut off — and the plan "will be wrong, which is okay; that's what a plan is." He said the April→now period was legitimately a **setting-up / learning phase** (~another month or two to finish experimenting), after which the calendar for next year should be locked. [[raw/echo_distribution_marketing_update-september-26-2025.md]]
- **Andrei's travel.** Away **Oct 9–10 (Thu/Fri)**; proposed a **fully-dedicated planning week Oct 13–17** (Val: don't start next week — "simmer on it," prep the people who report to you first). A **longer trip** follows; he offered to work part-time remotely. Val: "let me think about it." [[raw/echo_distribution_marketing_update-september-26-2025.md]]

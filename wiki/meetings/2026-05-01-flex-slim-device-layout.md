# Flex-Slim Device Layout (May 1 2026)

**Date:** 1 May 2026, 2:01pm Eastern (§11 recording header time); ~43m.
**Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi Peari]] (industrial design), [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] (ergonomics).

A notes-only working session (no transcript) to finalise the front-panel device layout of the **Flex-Slim paystation** — a ~11.5"-wide single-touchscreen slim variant of JVL's paystation, with scanner, printer and bill validator/recycler stacked vertically on the front. The group locked the stack order (scanner top → printer middle → bill validator/recycler raised as high as possible) on ergonomic first principles, ruled the slim single-dispenser-only, chose to reuse the existing paystation screen-angle molds to save ~2 months to market, kept the field-replaceable bezel-glass sub-assembly, and left liquid-drainage and MEI/GSM recycler-clearance as open Genadi actions. Substance and citations live on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]] (May 1 2026 section). §15.8: device/component/market economics only; no named-person compensation.

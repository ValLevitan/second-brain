# RGS API integration strategy (Val, Efim, Aaron)

~74-min discussion (file named "Aaron_Val") on how to make JVL's RGS easy for operators to integrate, and on the marketplace vision. Substance on [[wiki/topics/rgs-architecture-and-integration.md]].

- Attendees: Val, [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim Podvoiski]] (RGS author, does most of the explaining), [[wiki/people/aaron.md|Aaron]].
- Purpose (Val's stated objective): make it easy for anyone to integrate JVL's RGS into their framework. Covered: what the RGS is (math container + wallet + game), the ~4-call API, reverse vs. direct integration, Postman/OpenAPI, moving RGS off SVN to Git, and Val's give-away-the-RGS / "installer-magic" idea. The exchange was tense (Val vs. Efim). Aaron: JVL's API "could use a little love" before offering outside integration.

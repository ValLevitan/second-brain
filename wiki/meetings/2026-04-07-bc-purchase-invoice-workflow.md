# BC Purchase Invoice Workflow — Val / Lev Rudky (Apr 7 2026)

**Date:** April 7 2026, 5:25 PM Eastern (recorded ~15-minute portion of a ~2-hour session; recording header time per §11)
**Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/lev-rudky.md|Lev Rudky]] (BC/ERP consultant)

A working session on the **Accounts Payable payment-scheduling workflow in Microsoft Business Central** — how Purchase Orders flow to Purchase Invoices and why payment terms weren't carrying through automatically on the payables side. After sustained questioning by Val, the root cause emerged: the **wrong AP Commerce add-on** was being used (the A/R "Installment Payment" module instead of the A/P "Progress Payment" module), and Lev committed to installing the correct one.

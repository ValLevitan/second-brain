# Developing games faster — Val & Michael Gottlieb (Mar 12 2025)

A long strategy conversation between [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] and [[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael Gottlieb]] (Senior VP of Game Design) about producing games far faster. Michael gave a blunt assessment of why the online-games market is unwinnable; Val laid out his goal to compress production time and the parallel-tracks / disjoint-animation approach to get there. Nothing was locked ("I'm going to think about it… we'll have another conversation").

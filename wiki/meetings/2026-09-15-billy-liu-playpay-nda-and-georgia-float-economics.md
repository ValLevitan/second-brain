## Notes on the sources

**§12 — one meeting, not two.** Four files: two derived notes and two transcripts, one pair per Plaud segment. The segments **touch exactly at 7:08 PM**, which is the pattern that can mean either a recording break inside one meeting or two genuinely distinct sessions. Decided **on content** as **one meeting**:

- **No goodbye and no greeting.** Segment 1 ends mid-task, with Billy coaching Val through saving the NDA as a PDF. Segment 2 opens mid-sentence, with no re-introduction and no re-stating of who is present.
- **Nobody joins or leaves.** The same three voices run through both files.
- **Topic continuity across the join, and an explicit back-reference.** Segment 1 ends with the NDA being filled in; two hours into segment 2 Billy treats it as concluded within the same conversation — "**We signed an NDA. We're going to keep it**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]]. Segment 2's only "last time" reference points **backwards to Sept 2**, not to segment 1: "**Last time you said that you wanted to give away the equipment**" [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]].
- **The derived notes themselves say so.** Both transcripts are headed "**segment 1 of 2**" / "**segment 2 of 2**"; the second note names the first as its "**Companion session**", calls itself "the second Plaud session of the same evening, starting minutes after the NDA session ended", and files both against **one** Outlook entry [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Georgia_Payment_Ecosystem_and_Float_Economics-September-15-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Billy_Liu_PlayPay_Mutual_NDA_Renegotiation-September-15-2026.md]].

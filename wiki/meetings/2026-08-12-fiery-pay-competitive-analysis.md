# Fiery Pay competitive analysis (GA-COAM)

**Date:** August 12, 2026, 3:31–4:02 PM Eastern (~31 min)
**Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]], [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda Giorgi]] (Andrei Klimovich invited, not present)

Review of a competitor payment system, **Fiery Pay** (run by "Afshad," linked with Aristocrat), showing up in Georgia COAM locations: how it converts machine credits to a gift card and back to player points, why the team sees it as a fraud-exposed "legal-ish" workaround, and JVL's answer — a **recycler + flex port** built around the "replay" temporary wallet and InComm's Skill Pay card.

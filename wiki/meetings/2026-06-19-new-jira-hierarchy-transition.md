# New JIRA Hierarchy transition — standard review (June 19 2026)

**June 19, 2026, 11:01 AM ET · ~1h26m · Russian.** Attendees: [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/dmitri-kovalchuk.md|Dmitri Kovalchuk]], [[wiki/people/andrey-bosik.md|Andrei Bosik]], [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]], [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]].

The team reviewed [[wiki/people/dmitri-kovalchuk.md|Dmitri]]'s draft standard for JIRA hierarchy and terminology — the transition/rollout of the JIRA-hierarchy standard **locked** on [[wiki/meetings/2026-06-16-jira-hierarchy-standard.md|June 16]]. Five documents were shared ahead (two versions of the standard, one from Andrei, one from Irina, and an HTML linking releases to the hierarchy). Durable substance — the glossary (Capability / Requirement / Initiative), ADRs made situational (not per feature), "platform" dropped for "product," one feature = one project = one repository, ADRs living in their repos (duplicated, not central), and "semver" written out in full — lives on [[wiki/topics/github-release-management-pipeline.md]] (Jun-19 2026).

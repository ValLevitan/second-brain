# Flex-Recycler readiness for the Georgia show — can it demo on Aug 18?

**Date:** July 31, 2026, 1:30 PM ET (~15 min). Teams meeting "Recycler_state_of_ready_alignment." Held in Russian.
**Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]], [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi Peari]].

Right after the [[wiki/meetings/2026-07-31-georgia-expo-product-demo-plan.md|noon expo-planning meeting]], Val took the **recycler readiness** question to engineering: can the Flex-Recycler be ready to demo at the **Georgia show (Aug 18)**? Pavel judged the ideal flows possible but not realistic on the timeline and proposed an intermediate "digital-card-issuance screen" before cash payout; the hardware, the missing card dispenser, an untested Flex dispenser, and a CPI/JCM denomination-logic constraint were the main blockers.

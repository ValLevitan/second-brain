## Reading warning — the notes are a gloss, not the source

Both derived notes are English summaries of Russian speech that is heavily garbled in places. Four points where this stub's pages depart from the notes, each argued on the page concerned:

- Val asks for an "**air шок**"; the notes write "gas shocks". The **gas** wording enters the call only from Genadi, later.
- The notes read "**пока мы работаем с этими not sow mach**" as "the current suppliers". No supplier is named in the source; the phrase is not interpretable.
- The notes compress "**Одно радует, что это все в такой ситуации, но это не так радует**" into "the size of it is not a happy thing", dropping the everyone-is-in-it-too half.
- The notes state "The component price is about $46" as a separate fact; in the source that number exists only inside the "$46 as it should be, $60 as it actually costs" comparison, and the figure Genadi first quotes is garbled.

Two of Genadi's short lines in segment 1 — "**Вот и цвет**" and "**А я думал[,] что это серьёзно?**" — do not appear in the notes at all. They are recorded on the lid decision page and no ruling is read into either.

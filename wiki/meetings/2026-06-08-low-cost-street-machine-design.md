# Low-cost street machine design — build the whole device under $2,000 (June 8 2026)

**June 8, 2026, 9:48 PM ET · ~30 min (mostly Russian).** Attendees: [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi Peari]].

Val wants a stripped-down "street" gaming machine because street prices have dropped, targeting a whole device built for **under $2,000 including the bill acceptor** (~$1,200 for the machine, ~$800 the bill acceptor). The pair worked through the cost levers — a single 43-inch monitor, one door only, a move to a Rockchip main board with several boards removed, and a self-built bill-path "nose"/bezel with a built-in QR reader — plus the recurring-fee business model that pairs with the low price. Val meets Piratka and Pavel the next morning (June 9, 8:00) to test whether the direction is feasible; this starts the June street-machine hardware thread.

Durable substance — the sub-$2,000 device target and cost-stripping direction (Rockchip board, single monitor, one door, self-built QR nose replacing the player-tracking port) — lives on [[wiki/decisions/2025-02-27-flex-street-cabinet-design-direction.md]] (Jun-8, the street-machine design-direction thread). The sell-cheap-plus-recurring-fee business model and device economics live on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]] (Jun-8). This meeting opens the multi-meeting street-machine program — see [[wiki/topics/low-cost-street-machine.md]].

# Flex-IQ MVP Cashier & Player Flow Demo (May 26 2026)

**Date:** 26 May 2026, 3:02 PM (recording start, §11 header time); ~1h 9m; Russian.
**Attendees:** [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]], [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]], [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]], [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] — with [[wiki/people/alexei-khamin.md|Alexey "Lesha" Khamin]] and [[wiki/people/ivan.md|Ivan "Vanya" Shpak]] assisting on-site in Minsk with the hardware and test cards.

A live end-to-end demo of the **Flex-IQ MVP** cashier + player flow — Pavel and Irina walked Andrei and Val through installing a device, setting up a customer and location, funding it through the Payment Hub, and running a cashier payout, then showed the assembled hardware on camera. This is the direct continuation of the [[wiki/meetings/2026-05-22-transactional-mvp-strategy.md|May-22 Transactional MVP Strategy]] (which set the Flex-IQ mechanics at the business level) and the [[wiki/meetings/2026-05-25-transactional-products-review-flexiq.md|May-25 Flex-IQ product walkthrough]]. Andrei's overall read: the system is ready to enter MVP subject to a short fix list, though he has not yet seen the hardware integrated on a real location.

The substance — the install/registration/InComm/funding/payout walkthrough, the card-not-issuing blocker, the Keycloak lock-out, the Mutha-Goose in-line splice and the counter-to-Mutha-Goose cable-run problem, the placeholder terms-and-conditions, and the "proceed to MVP" / "no abbreviations, ever" / "defer UX nice-to-haves" decisions — lives on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]] (May-26 2026 section). §15.8: transactional-product/service economics only; no named-person compensation.

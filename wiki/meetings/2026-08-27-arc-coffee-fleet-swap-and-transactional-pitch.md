**Purpose:** Val asked for ARC's business on two fronts — to win back the machine business he lost there, and to use ARC "as a springboard into transactional equipment". [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Coffee_Fleet_Swap_and_Transactional_Pitch-August-27-2026.md]]

**Outcome:** ARC restated a one-to-one trade-in of its old JVL cabinets at $6,000 and a staged order from September 1; Val committed to take all of them. ARC is holding off on any kiosk until the lottery's general counsel issues his updated paper. A demo of the entry-level product was set for Monday at JVL's office. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Coffee_Fleet_Swap_and_Transactional_Pitch-August-27-2026.md]] The notes point to the Aug-31 session as the follow-up. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Coffee_Fleet_Swap_and_Transactional_Pitch-August-27-2026.md]]

**Notes vs transcript (transcript followed).** [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ARC_Coffee_Fleet_Swap_and_Transactional_Pitch-August-27-2026.md]]
- The notes say ARC is "formerly Lucky Bucks". The recording does not say this; ARC says only that "we took over in twenty twenty three" and that Val had visited "twice with the previous ownership". (The former-name link rests on Val's ruling of 2026-09-10, recorded on the ARC page.)
- The notes say ARC "already runs a kiosk + Fiery Pay in most locations". The recording has "we have a kiosk now in most of our cases" and, separately, "we're discussing another solution… the fire pay". Fiery Pay is under discussion, not stated as running.
- The notes name the counsel "Joe Kim". The recording says only "the lottery's general counsel" and "Joe".
- The notes leave out ARC's own voice on the swap numbers (5 in the market, "twenty… plus extra fifteen", "at least fifty in the warehouse", Val's "sixty three"), ARC's yes to the data modem, the $49 monthly fee, InComm's dollar, the NFC critique, the single-SAS pulse fix and the demo date.

**The pattern check (ARC retellings).** This is the earliest recorded meeting with ARC in the corpus (ARC refers to an earlier talk "the other day", and to Val's time with them at the Expo). Here the order talk comes from **ARC's own mouth** in the room ("we commit for twenty… plus extra fifteen"), and it matches the Aug-21 internal pipeline line ("buy the 5 machines… and place an order for 15 more"). So this source does **not** show an internal retelling running ahead of the room. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ARC_Coffee_Fleet_Swap_and_Transactional_Pitch-August-27-2026.md]]

# MG Oil introduction — Val ↔ Troy Erikson

**May 13 2025** (~22 min; audio transcribed 2025-05-13). The long-awaited introduction (from the Jan-7 MG Oil decision, [[wiki/decisions/2025-01-07-pursue-mg-oil-south-dakota.md]]) between [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] and [[wiki/people/troy-erikson.md|Troy Erikson]], owner of [[wiki/organizations/mg-oil.md|MG Oil]] — South Dakota's largest VLT route operator. Troy offered MG Oil as JVL's beta-test site and a 10–100-machine order to follow; Val agreed to ship six machines and named [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]] as the coordinating product manager.

Substance on [[wiki/people/troy-erikson.md]], [[wiki/organizations/mg-oil.md]] and the May-13 section of [[wiki/projects/south-dakota-vlt-market.md]].

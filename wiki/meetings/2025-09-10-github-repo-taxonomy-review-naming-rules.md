# GitHub repo taxonomy review and naming rules (Sept 10 2025)

**Sept 10 2025, 1:34pm.** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/aaron.md|Aaron Bockelie]], [[wiki/people/andrey-bosik.md|Andrei Bosik]], [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]].

Fast review refining the naming spreadsheet — [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] playing "dictator" to force decisions. Collapsed local-gaming-server + its GUI, treated the taxonomy as a **filter for valid names (not a definition of every repo)**, and Aaron wrote out a short set of **naming rules** (fewest kebabs left-to-right; if a repo doesn't fit, justify it or merge it; merging usually improves clarity, splitting sometimes does; defer complex merges with a deadline). Named the CMS-replacement / orchestration-observation platform **"platform flex back end,"** and defined the planning-tab columns (target org, validated name, move date, planned action, target repo, responsible person, dependencies) that become Jira issues. This is session 2 of the Sept 9→16 git-repo-structure arc — substance and dated sequence live on [[wiki/decisions/2025-02-11-github-org-naming-and-structure.md]].

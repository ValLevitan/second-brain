# JVL $1M cash injection — approaching warrant holders (Alan Litwack call, Jul 7 2026)

A short (~2.5-minute) call in which [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] sought strategy advice from **Alan Litwack** (advisor; caught him mid-holiday). Val's framing: **JVL is short on cash and he is seeking an additional ~$1M injection**, and wants to approach the company's **warrant holders** to raise it. *(The transcript renders "JVL" as "JBL" — ASR mangle.)*

- **The question.** Can Val approach the warrant holders for the injection, and how? → [[wiki/topics/jvl-corporate-loans-and-shareholder-financing.md]] (Jul-7 2026).
- **Litwack's advice.** You can't *force* warrant holders, but you can *ask* — the **terms have to be attractive enough** (security, yield, equity) for them to participate.
- **The fallback is Val himself.** If the warrant holders decline and the company needs the money, **Val can and will put the $1M in himself** ("I can, and I will").
- **Structuring principle.** Structure the injection on terms **very favorable to Val** (security / yield / "free equity"), but **offer the warrant holders the *same* terms** — that way they can't complain Val did too well for himself while still giving Val a strong position if they pass.
- **Follow-up.** A **10:00 AM next-day call** booked to work through the structure.

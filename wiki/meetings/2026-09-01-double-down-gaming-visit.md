# Double Down Gaming at JVL — the association, an operator profile, and the triple-progressive plus modular-cashless pitch (Sept 1 2026)

Thin stub.

**When:** September 1 2026, **10:25–10:29 AM Eastern** (recording 1, ~4 minutes) and **10:33–11:53 AM Eastern** (recording 2, ~1h 20m), per the recording headers on the two derived note files. **Purpose:** a first meeting with a Georgia master JVL had not sold much to. It ran four ways — the **trade show and the association**'s finances, board and political apparatus; Ryan's **operator profile** (route size, high-roller locations, what he buys and why); Val's pitch for the **vertical machine with three progressive styles**; and Andrei's walkthrough of the **modular cashless stack** (card activation by barcode, Flex ports, the recycler as a closed-loop ATM). It closed on Class A and the ATM rules — "the two big things we need to get settled."

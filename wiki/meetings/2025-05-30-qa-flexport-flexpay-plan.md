QA / rollout-planning meeting (Russian) with [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]], [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]] and QA lead [[wiki/people/irina-makarych.md|Irina]] ("Ира"; transcribed surname ambiguity with [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Shishko]] — Tier A, not reconciled), continuing the [[wiki/meetings/2025-05-20-flexpay-flexport-test-plan.md|May-20 test-plan strategy]] and the [[wiki/decisions/2025-05-27-release-ewallet-mvp-first.md|e-Wallet-MVP-first ruling]].

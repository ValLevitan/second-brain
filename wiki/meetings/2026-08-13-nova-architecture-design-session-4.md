# NOVA architecture design — session 4 (Aug 13)

**Date:** August 13, 2026, ~5:13–6:45 AM Eastern (~1h31m; held in Russian)
**Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]], [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]], [[wiki/people/alexander-biratka.md|Alexander Biratko]], [[wiki/people/evgeny-kharetski.md|Evgeny Kharetski]], [[wiki/people/alexei-khamin.md|Alexey Khamin]], [[wiki/people/anton-seregin.md|Anton Seregin]], [[wiki/people/vladimir-samusenko.md|Vladimir Samusenko]]

Fourth NOVA design session ("session 4 of 5"; session 3 is not in this batch — referenced, not ingested).

# Vertical Roadmap and Rage→Omni Transition (May 7 2026)

**Date:** 7 May 2026, 6:02pm Eastern (§11 recording header time; the `_140226` filename stamp is a different zone, not the meeting time); ~21m.
**Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael Gottlieb]], [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]], [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena Zhukova]], [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim Podvoiski]].

Game-development planning call sequencing the post-Georgia **vertical product** roadmap (South Dakota, Nebraska, Texas) against the strategic **Rage Studio → Omni** platform transition. The working consensus: all three vertical releases stay on **Rage** through end-2026 (a Q4 Omni release is "absolutely impossible" / "no way in hell" — SD needs only one net-new asset, Solar King, on Rage), the **first Omni product lands Q1 2027 in skill markets** (not the regulated SD market), and Omni V1 is a "massive" launch of three new cabinets on the FP7 board (D27 + D43). The call also staggered content release (2 Jackpot Rush + 2 Fiery Ice per drop, not 20 games at once), deferred the Wolfpack-vs-Twin-Bison ordering until Georgia data lands, and discounted the inconclusive Nebraska 9-day performance data. Platform/transition substance and citations live on [[wiki/topics/omni-core-advancement-and-game-templating.md]] (May 7 2026 section); the SD/NE/TX vertical-market substance on [[wiki/projects/south-dakota-vlt-market.md]] (May 7 2026 section), cross-linking [[wiki/projects/nebraska-skill-market.md]]. §12: transcript + derived notes = one meeting (notes stamp "Game_Development (20260507_140226)") → ONE stub, source-count 2. §15.8: market/platform/machine economics only (cabinet/game-count/board decisions); no named-person compensation.

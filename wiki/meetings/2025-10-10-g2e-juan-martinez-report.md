# G2E post-show status report (Juan Martinez) — Oct 10 2025

**Type:** a short **written status report** by [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]] after the **G2E (Global Gaming Expo)** trade show — **not a meeting transcript**. Captured per §3 as a thin report stub; the durable market milestones are folded into the relevant project pages ([[wiki/projects/south-dakota-vlt-market.md]], [[wiki/projects/nebraska-skill-market.md]], [[wiki/projects/texas-used-machine-distribution.md]]). Florida items have no dedicated project page, so they are recorded here.

- **South Dakota — JCM iVizion firmware approved (dated milestone).** The **JCM iVizion BV** (bill validator) was **approved — specifically firmware v2.84-44**; the **beta-test approval letter is expected Oct 14**, and **other-location placements can start Oct 15**. Juan and **[[wiki/people/denis-desmarais.md|Denis]]** discussed **another technician-training session around Nov 15**. **Keno game design has started.** (Folded into [[wiki/projects/south-dakota-vlt-market.md]]; the Keno work is the [[wiki/meetings/2025-10-06-game-design-scheduling-jira-grooming.md|Oct-6]] Black Hills/Solar/Liberty pack.) [[raw/G2E_juan_martinez_report-october-10-2025.md]]
- **Florida — Fiery Ice Link submitted to GLI.** The **Fiery Ice Link (Lower Volatility)** build has been **submitted to GLI**. A meeting with **Royal Caribbean Cruises** is planned for **late October**. (The Fiery Ice progressive/nudge history is on [[wiki/topics/slot-game-design-principles.md]] and [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]]; the Royal Caribbean dock-ship operations contact was first noted on the [[wiki/meetings/2025-09-23-pricing-strategy.md|Sept-23 pricing]] call.) [[raw/G2E_juan_martinez_report-october-10-2025.md]]
- **Nebraska — attorney accepted the video; software to Eclipse.** The **attorney has accepted the demo video**, and the **Nebraska software has been submitted to Eclipse for testing** — advancing the [[wiki/meetings/2025-09-18-games-concepts-markets-review.md|Sept-18]] "video → Dept of Revenue / lawyer → green light to Eclipse" sequence. (Folded into [[wiki/projects/nebraska-skill-market.md]].) [[raw/G2E_juan_martinez_report-october-10-2025.md]]
- **Texas — two G2E operator prospects.** Met **Rich Winley** at G2E — will **test three machines in two locations**; he uses **SAS via Count-R kiosk hardware** and "could be open to" a **digital wallet or gift card**. Met **Salim Punjani** with **Continental** (a **Georgia** operator with some **routes in Texas**) who wants to try the machine; Continental **uses Pulses like legacy GCOAM, not SAS**. (Folded into [[wiki/projects/texas-used-machine-distribution.md]].) [[raw/G2E_juan_martinez_report-october-10-2025.md]]
- **G2E contacts summary.** Met **three Miami casino managers** who want to try JVL's games; **Royal Caribbean Cruises**; **Rich Winley** (Texas); and **Salim Punjani** (Georgia, re the Texas opportunity). [[raw/G2E_juan_martinez_report-october-10-2025.md]]

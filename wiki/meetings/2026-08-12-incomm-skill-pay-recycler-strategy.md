# InComm — Skill Pay card & recycling-ATM strategy (Jason Hall)

**Date:** August 12, 2026 (Eastern; ~17 min; transcript has no speaker labels)
**Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], **Jason Hall** (InComm)

Informal one-on-one. Jason walked Val through how InComm's Georgia card program runs today (~4,000–5,000 locations, ~50,000 cards/week), his read on the Fiery Pay threat (balance sheet, not features), the Skill Pay card (mid-September, ATM-usable), and whether JVL should **sell** its recycling ATM to operators rather than operate cash routes (Jason leaned sell). Val confirmed JVL stays with InComm and declined Fiery Pay's integration request. Substance on [[wiki/organizations/incom.md]] (Aug 12).

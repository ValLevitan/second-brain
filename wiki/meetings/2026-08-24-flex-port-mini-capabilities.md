# Flex-Port-Mini capabilities (Aug 24 2026)

**When:** August 24 2026, recorded in two segments — a ~4-minute warm-up at **~10:02–10:06 AM Eastern**, then the ~1h27m main session at **~10:06–11:34 AM Eastern** [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Preamble-August-24-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Design-August-24-2026.md]].

**Who:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] and [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi Peari]] for the warm-up, with [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]] joining at its end ("Добрый день" at 03:47) and present throughout the main session [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Preamble-August-24-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Design-August-24-2026.md]].

**Purpose:** design a stripped-down, low-cost port — **Flex-Port-Mini** — that connects to a gaming machine only through the machine's second SAS port, and the give-away-the-hardware / monthly-fee business model to sell it [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Flex_Port_Mini_Capabilities_Design-August-24-2026.md]].

**Language:** both segments are in Russian.

Substance lives on:
- [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-24-flex-port-mini-via-second-sas-only.md]] — the product ruling: 2nd-SAS-only connection, ~$45 build budget for the Mini and ~$250 for the full Flex-Port, a minimal non-touch display, wired CAT5/RS-485 as stage-1 networking.
- [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-24-free-hardware-fixed-monthly-fee-model.md]] — the commercial model: all hardware free, a fixed ~$60–90/month fee, the per-transaction dollar handed back to the operator.
- [[wiki/decisions/2026-08-24-drop-pin-from-evoucher-flow.md]] — dropping the eVoucher PIN.
- [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]] — the device design, the eVoucher/QR player flow, the local-network problem, and the two-stage rollout.
- [[wiki/topics/ai-assisted-development.md]] — Genadi's AI-agent adoption and Val's "AI is built to flatter" caution (warm-up segment).

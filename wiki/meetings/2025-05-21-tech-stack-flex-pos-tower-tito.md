# Flex POS / Flex Tower / Flex TITO tech-stack selection (May 21 2025)

A Russian-language architecture call chaired by [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] with [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi Peari]] (industrial design) and [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]], weighing which hardware/software stack to build **Flex POS** and **Flex Tower** on: the existing **embedded (STM32) "sandwich"** used for FlexPort, versus the **industrial PC board Genadi found, running Linux**. The §3 continuation of the May-1/2/6/15/20 flex-tower thread ([[wiki/meetings/2025-05-20-flex-tower-industrial-design.md]]).

**No ruling** — Val deferred the decision to a Monday (~May 26) follow-up so Genadi can price out monitor/touchscreen driver options overnight with the Chinese suppliers; Val meets Pavel the next day to prepare. Substance (the embedded-vs-industrial trade-off, cost math, browser-GUI/programmer-stack argument, TITO-server-on-Linux, and Val's late flip toward embedded) is on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]].

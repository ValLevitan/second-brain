# E-wallet architecture release-planning meeting (May 27 2025)

A focused ~31-min session [[raw/release_e-wallet_flex-pay_flex-port-may-27-2025.md]] on how to release the **e-wallet** architecture (FlexPort + FlexPay) as an MVP for the Georgia QAM market, with [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]], [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]], [[wiki/people/dmitri-chudinov.md|Dmitri Chudinov]], [[wiki/people/eugine-yavorski.md|Eugine Yavorski]], [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi Peari]] and QA's [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]]. Held the same day as the derailed dev review [[wiki/meetings/2025-05-27-flexpay-flexport-dev-review.md]], which it references as "the previous meeting." (Dating note, Tier B: Val had promised a "Thursday" e-wallet session at the end of the dev review, but this release meeting's audio was transcribed on May 27 alongside the dev review — so it is dated May 27, not the following Thursday. [[raw/release_e-wallet_flex-pay_flex-port-may-27-2025.md]])

The rulings — e-wallet MVP first, freeze/rename version 17b, JCM UBA only, Bitbucket→GitHub in ~1 week, twice-weekly meetings, the November 2025 deadline, the Incom in-house full-cycle test, and the Georgia hardware list — are on [[wiki/decisions/2025-05-27-release-ewallet-mvp-first.md]]. A derived meeting summary [[raw/e-wallet_release_meeting_summary-may-27-2025.md]] (§12 family — same meeting) tabulates the hardware quantities and action items.

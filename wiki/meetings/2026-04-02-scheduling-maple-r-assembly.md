# Scheduling Maple-R Assembly (Apr 2 2026)

Working session (~1h46m) — [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] with [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]], [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]] and [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi Peari]] — building out the Jira schedule for the **Maple-R** cabinet retrofit destined for Texas (~80 units), working live in Jira Plans. "Maple-R" is the assembly line for the Texas refit first sketched on [[wiki/meetings/2026-03-26-initiative-priority-schedule.md|Mar-26]] (the "Maple" Texas refit of former Colombia/Mexico touchscreen machines). Substance on [[wiki/projects/texas-used-machine-distribution.md]] (Apr-2). [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Scheduling_Maple-R_Assembly-April-2-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Scheduling_Maple-R_Assembly-April-2-2026.md]]

Key items worked: linking the software track (**IP-10-33**) to the electromechanical track (**IP-10-47**) as a dependency; creating **HFM-482** ([[wiki/people/elizabeth.md|Elizabeth]] to assemble 2 prototype cabinets, starting Apr 2) → **HFM-483** (alpha-software integration test, linked to software story ST-56-45); compressing the printer-integration story **HFM-478** to one day with 5 sub-tasks; an initial component order of **20 sets** (not the full 80); and a target of **Apr 21** for the first 10 production machines.

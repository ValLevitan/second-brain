# Flex Tower / Flex POS foundational platform decision — STM32 vs ARM-Linux (June 4 2025)

A Russian-language architecture call with [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]], [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]], [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi Peari]] and the embedded developers ([[wiki/people/evgeniy-alekseev.md|Evgeniy Alekseev]] and the FlexPort-integration "Zhenya" / [[wiki/people/eugine-yavorski.md|Eugine Yavorski]]) — a formal, category-by-category pros/cons walkthrough of the two candidate platforms for the Flex Tower and Flex POS payout devices: a cheap embedded **STM32** firmware board vs a cheap **ARM-based embedded-Linux** board. The meeting confirmed the direction first taken on May 26 — **Linux wins** (Pavel: "Я за Linux") — and set the next steps (order sample boards, bring up Linux, write the capabilities list + business architecture, hold per-device user-experience meetings). The decision-grade substance is on [[wiki/decisions/2025-05-26-arm-linux-platform-flex-devices.md]]; the design-thread context is on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]].

# Bank of America banking review and Georgia financing (June 8 2026)

**June 8, 2026, 4:00 PM Eastern (header), about 70 minutes, in person.** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_BofA_Lenin_Hernandez_Banking_Review_and_Georgia_Financing-June-8-2026.md]] Held at JVL's office in the Industrious building; Inna said JVL had moved there from its own office across the street, in the Chase building. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_BofA_Lenin_Hernandez_Banking_Review_and_Georgia_Financing-June-8-2026.md]]

**Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/inna-levitan.md|Inna Levitan]], and for Bank of America **Lenin Hernandez** (commercial business banking; JVL's point of contact) in the room, with **Stephanie Six** (lending/credit) on speakerphone for the middle part. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_BofA_Lenin_Hernandez_Banking_Review_and_Georgia_Financing-June-8-2026.md]] Lenin's surname is from the derived notes; the transcript says "Lenin" and, once, "Hernandez". [[raw/Meeting_Notes_BofA_Lenin_Hernandez_Banking_Review_and_Georgia_Financing-June-8-2026.md]]

**Purpose:** a first working session with JVL's new Bank of America banker: signature cards for three accounts, financing to buy the leased Georgia building, an operating line of credit for JVL Systems, and an automatic bank-to-ERP feed (CashPro treasury).

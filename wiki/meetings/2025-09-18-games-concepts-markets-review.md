# Games-concepts and markets review — "follow the dollar" (Sept 18 2025)

**Date:** 2025-09-18 (transcribed 3:42 PM; the file carries no recording-header date line and opens "We currently don't get enough handle…"). **Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] and [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]] (the product owner running installs/operators). [[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael Gottlieb]] and [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena/Elena]] referenced. English.

A working review that grooms the JVL game pipeline by market. Val's frame: "let's follow the dollar sign… where is our money coming from today? **Georgia**… **South Dakota**… **Nebraska**… **Texas**," with **zero forecast from any Class 3 games** but the market kept as potent. Market-by-market substance folds into the four market project pages; the cross-game design thinking (the "nine-bonus" rule, the fever/one-cent bonus) folds into [[wiki/topics/slot-game-design-principles.md]]. The Jira product-plan grooming (remove-from-plan / archive) ties to [[wiki/decisions/2025-04-03-jira-capability-level-and-github-mapping.md]].

Durable points (single source):

- **South Dakota priority order — Keno #1, one-cent bonus #2, progressive #3.** A 60–90-day holding pattern gathering data from the six installs; the **92% cap can't be exceeded** (they'd hoped to). Add back a **progressive** (even a single-level), a **Keno** option (25¢ minimum bet, $8,000 max — "just to be competitive… players being able to sit down and be presented with a Keno option"), and the **one-cent bonus**. See [[wiki/projects/south-dakota-vlt-market.md]].
- **Georgia — combat Ultimate Firelink with Calle Ocho; revise the home-screen look.** MG Oil agreed higher bets (min 40 credits on three games, 50 on the other three), deploying Monday via [[wiki/people/denis-desmarais.md|Dennis]]. Average drop was **$2,700**; at 8% hold ≈ **$240–250/day**, ~2.5× the state average. Light & Wonder's **Ultimate Firelink** is a four-level bonus that matches JVL's **Calle Ocho** four-level bonus, so Val wants Calle Ocho for Georgia; **Jackpot Rush Vertical (At-Any-Bet)** is "practically ready" and goes next to Eclipse. Val wants the home-screen icons restyled closer to Light & Wonder / Aristocrat (thinner or no border, larger icons) so players stop recognizing a JVL game "at a distance" — applied to Calle Ocho and JPRE and any new game. See [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]].
- **Nebraska — video in progress, nothing needed for game concept.** [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena]] is producing the video → Department of Revenue / lawyer review → Eclipse → **Nov/Dec deployment**. See [[wiki/projects/nebraska-skill-market.md]].
- **Texas — scale the San Antonio rollout down to 2–3 machines.** Scheduling with **Vinny Bale** (prep down from 5 to 2–3 machines); the **ICT printer** model was confirmed to [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Gennady]] (asked whether to start with the existing JCM printer); [[wiki/people/mary.md|Mary]] is building the operator-outreach script, filtering which operators actually offer skill today (two websites are defunct/bankrupt); **Alex** has gone silent ~30 days on the CPU kits (Juan suspects he wants exclusivity/margin, or the machines may not even be installed). See [[wiki/projects/texas-used-machine-distribution.md]].
- **The "nine-bonus" rule for Class 3 on Omni.** Val's ruling: **all JVL games going forward should have a minimum of six bonuses, aiming for nine.** Rationale — "people want to discover… it dies quickly because they discover it," so give players longer to discover; "all the new games… have way more bonuses than we do… that's the new methodology being deployed from everybody." He deliberately names it "**nine bonuses**" so the team (RT, path-of-least-resistance) doesn't settle at seven. Class 3 gets built on **Omni** (more powerful graphics processor, more visual presentation) as a big redesign from a base already roughly working. Targets: **Calle Ocho 9-bonus, Fortune Fupa 9-bonus, Cash Kittens 9-bonus** (Cash Kittens = a variation/second release of Fortune Fupa). See [[wiki/topics/slot-game-design-principles.md]].
- **Fortune Fupa concept kept, math replaced.** Fortune Fupa was, per Michael and the Themes analysis, more volatile than Lightning Link / Dragon Link / Firedice; keep it graphically/audio/conceptually but **replace its par sheet/map with a Light & Wonder-style par sheet** that has persistent pots but is not volatile. [[wiki/topics/slot-game-design-principles.md]]
- **The one-cent "fever bonus."** Named **fever bonus** (IGT's term) because you **cannot call it a free spin and then charge one cent** — GLI likely wouldn't approve — so game symbols using the "free spin" wording on the reel strips must be reworked. Val wants it **branded** (can't copyright, but can trademark and drive as JVL's own, reusable in other jurisdictions). "Mostly a production type of work" — [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim]] is free and Lena's department can queue it. [[wiki/topics/slot-game-design-principles.md]]
- **Jira product-plan grooming.** Remove the **HHR** game-concept tab ("we're not there yet"); freeze/ archive everything sitting in Game Design (Fortune Fupa, Cash Kittens, La Rosa / Paraíso Cubano) — Val: "I prefer freezing things if we're not going to bring it to the market… I don't see any productive output unless we get nine bonuses." Val explained the **plan is a Jira "gather-up" / presentation** — "remove from plan" pulls an item off the view but **nothing is deleted from Jira** (the GD numbers, comments, history all stay); he told Juan to create an **archive** and move items there, and to confirm with [[wiki/people/aaron.md|Aaron]]. La Rosa is the one Latina game Val still wants eventually ("to complete the full Fiery Ice lineup"). See [[wiki/decisions/2025-04-03-jira-capability-level-and-github-mapping.md]].
- **Production finishes ~mid-October; then game-concept/design for the new Class-3-on-Omni work.** Everything scheduled in production (JPRE practically finished; the lower-volatility Fiery Ice revisions for Hialeah) will be done by mid-October and continue its lifecycle through GLI lab into locations; Nebraska/Texas/South Dakota/Georgia stay on the **old operating system**, Class 3 goes to **Omni**. The Fiery Ice volatility rework is being done because JVL cannot currently sell those machines; if the adjustment yields consistent win-per-unit, Val will "pull my last favor" to convert operators from daily-fee to sales.

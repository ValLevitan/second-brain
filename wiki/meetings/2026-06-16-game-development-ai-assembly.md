# Game Development — AI game-assembly from contracts and capabilities (June 16 2026)

**June 16, 2026, 4:02 PM ET · ~41m.** Attendees: [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/evgeny-kharetski.md|Evgeny Kharetski]], [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]], [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]].

Val framed the purpose: explore whether AI can **assemble games from structured designer input**, using **contracts and capabilities** instead of per-game custom programming. The afternoon follow-on to the [[wiki/meetings/2026-06-03-game-manifest-definition.md|June-3 manifest]] and [[wiki/meetings/2026-06-03-white-label-experiment.md|June-3 white-label]] threads — this session standardized the vocabulary (the June-3 "manifest" is retired in favor of data contracts + repos), fixed the one-game-one-repo architecture, and launched a confidential Calle Ocho sandbox experiment.

Durable substance lives on:
- [[wiki/topics/slot-game-design-principles.md]] (Jun-16) — the standardized game-design vocabulary (contract / capabilities / game mechanics / artifact) and the reskin-via-capabilities concept.
- [[wiki/topics/ai-assisted-development.md]] (Jun-16) — AI assembling games from pre-built components like a website constructor, and the Calle Ocho reverse-engineering sandbox experiment.

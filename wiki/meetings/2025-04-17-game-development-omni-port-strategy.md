# Game development update — Omni port strategy & philosophy (Apr 17 2025)

A ~69-minute conversation between [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]], [[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael Gottlieb]], [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim]], [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena]] and [[wiki/people/dmitriy-khandov.md|Dima]] about *why* JVL is porting Kai-Ocho to Omni now and how the team should think about the framework. Internally duplicated transcript (§12) — facts taken once.

The substance lives on:
- [[wiki/topics/omni-core-advancement-and-game-templating.md]] — the new Omni hardware board (single half-size AMD board that drops into existing cabinets), the "ever-evolving framework / never reach optimal" doctrine, the "throw-away code is acceptable to launch the engine" trade-off, and Val's resource reprioritisation (pull Metelski off online, Irina to help port).
- [[wiki/decisions/2025-04-14-kai-ocho-first-omni-framework-game.md]] — the naming ruling (can't reuse "Calle Ocho"; ship to GLI as "Kayo Ocho Omni" with a hyphen).
- [[wiki/topics/game-production-throughput-strategy.md]] — Val's management lesson on exhaustion/perfection ("companies get exhausted from perfection… very dangerous to exhaust people") and the deadline-with-a-contractor escape valve.

Also: continue deploying the **current Coyote** (once the bill-validator bug is fixed) while building the new one; the 4-game viewing (Crescent City Carnival "gorgeous"; Dragons of Rain lightning "kick-ass"; **Rodeo Queen** critiqued — horse "plasticky/porcelain," girl too small, "too much purple for an American theme"); Michael's Sega Dreamcast 1995 dual-SH2 anecdote (ship on one processor, add the second later) as precedent for shipping imperfect-but-good; Timur finishing a tool/"instrument" (~hours, capped at 16).

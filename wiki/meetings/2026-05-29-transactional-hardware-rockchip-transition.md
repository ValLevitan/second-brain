# Transactional Hardware Rockchip Transition (May 29 2026)

**Date:** 29 May 2026, 11:33 AM Eastern (§11 recording-header time); ~9m.
**Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi Peari]], [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]].

A short alignment settling which compute platform the transactional devices (recycler, dispenser, paystation) run on. They were first built on the aging **V-1000** (AMD-based) platform; the whole transactional category now moves to **Rockchip**. A hardware-platform decision.

Substance recorded on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]] (May 29 2026 — transactional category moves V-1000 → Rockchip). This extends the platform choice on [[wiki/decisions/2025-05-26-arm-linux-platform-flex-devices.md]] (which adopted Rockchip/ARM-Linux for the Flex Tower / POS / TITO devices) to the transactional-device category — see that decision's May-29-2026 update.

# Low-cost street cabinet — compute-platform feasibility (RK3568 → RK3588) (June 9 2026)

**June 9, 2026, 12:07 PM ET · ~22 min (mostly Russian).** Attendees: [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi Peari]], [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]], [[wiki/people/alexander-biratka.md|Alexander Biratko]].

The feasibility read Val wanted from Biratko and Pavel on the low-cost "street" slot machine — a stripped, cheap cabinet to compete in price-sensitive markets like Texas where the Flex cabinet is too expensive. The group worked the compute board (RK3568 too small at 2 GB → step up to RK3588 for ~$10 more), memory sizing (cap ~6 games, order 8 GB samples and measure), ordering proper samples from China rather than a local Orange Pi 5, and a test path porting Calle Ocho onto the RK board. Continues the [[wiki/meetings/2026-06-08-low-cost-street-machine-design.md|June-8 low-cost street-machine design]] opener.

Durable substance — the board choice, memory sizing, component strip, and the sub-$2,000 build target — lives on [[wiki/topics/low-cost-street-machine.md]].

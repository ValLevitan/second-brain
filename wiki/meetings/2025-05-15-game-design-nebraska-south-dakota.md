# Game-design review — Nebraska nudge ruled out, South Dakota V43 (2025-05-15)

A ~105-minute game-design review (English; the transcript internally duplicates — §12, facts taken once) with [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan]] (reporting from his Nebraska visit), [[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael Gottlieb]], [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim]], [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena]] and [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]].

Headline: the Nebraska Department of Revenue **attorney** (the boss of JVL's contact [[wiki/people/brittany-walker-bell.md|Brittany]]) ruled that JVL's **nudge does not qualify as skill** — reversing the May-8 "enhanced nudge adopted" position and killing nudge entirely for Nebraska. The market-entry detail (nudge dropped; replace with a Vanilla-style power-slider skill overlay; start from Georgia Peach games, flatten pay tables, feed top-wins into a Swift bonus) is on [[wiki/projects/nebraska-skill-market.md]]; the two skill-mechanic designs (Lucky Rainbow / Bullseye) and the Bonilla-Games/Vanilla market read are on [[wiki/topics/slot-game-design-principles.md]]; the South Dakota vertical-V43 submission is on [[wiki/projects/south-dakota-vlt-market.md]].

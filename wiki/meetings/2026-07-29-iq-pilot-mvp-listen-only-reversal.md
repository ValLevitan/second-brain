# IQ Pilot MVP scope lock — the Mutha Goose connection reverts to listen-only

**Date:** July 29, 2026, 9:30–11:00 AM ET (recording header 9:33–10:04 AM, ~31 min). Held in Russian.
**Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]], [[wiki/people/dmitri-chudinov.md|Dzmitry Chudzinau]] (Dima), [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]], [[wiki/people/sergey-tsymanov.md|Sergey Tsimanov]], [[wiki/people/ivan.md|Ivan Shpak]].

Pavel pulled the team together to **lock what goes into the nearest Flex-IQ MVP** (Ivan was there to scope what needs re-testing; Sergey as the main implementer). The headline outcome was a **reversal to "listen-only"** — Val declared the earlier decision to sit IQ "in break" between the Mutha Goose board and printer wrong, and ruled that IQ must have zero influence on the Mutha Goose install: the MG printer reconnects directly to the MG system, IQ no longer prints MG tickets or its own receipts, and only listens on the side for a validation signal. Val also insisted the Flex-IQ work be restructured under the capability model rather than mapped as one-to-one product features, flagged **remote software update** as the top MVP priority, and imposed schedule discipline ("hurry slowly" — no Friday release, no install before Tuesday Aug 4). This continues the IQ pilot thread from the [[wiki/meetings/2026-07-28-iq-pilot-release-priorities.md|Jul-28 pilot release priorities]], [[wiki/meetings/2026-07-22-iq-process-flow-review.md|Jul-22 process-flow review]] and [[wiki/meetings/2026-07-23-iq-process-flow-part-2.md|Jul-23 process-flow part 2]] meetings.

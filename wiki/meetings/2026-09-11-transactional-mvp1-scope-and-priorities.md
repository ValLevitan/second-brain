# Transactional MVP 1 scope and priorities — Sept 11 2026, 8:04–9:29 AM ET

Titled **"Transactional: CAPs, REQs - WIP, Deliveries priorities (MVP1-Oct 1st, MVP2-Nov 1st, FUTURE)"**. Most of the session was the capability list on screen, gone through item by item.

**This is the Friday schedule meeting Val booked on Sept 9.** It satisfies the standing commitment that Val and Pavel meet on Friday to work the October-1 schedule through — see [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-09-first-transactional-installation-planned-for-october-1.md]].

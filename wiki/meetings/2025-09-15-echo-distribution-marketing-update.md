Durable points (single source):

- **Meta (Instagram/Facebook).** The website is up; Meta drives some traffic to the website (to collect visitor data for later targeted/lookalike ads) and some **directly to Amazon** (advertising the **installment offer — $166/month × 24 months, no interest**). Displaying the price **$3,990** up front yields higher-quality traffic (fewer "trash" clicks). Not yet enough visitors to build a **lookalike** audience (~2 more weeks expected). Instagram gets subscribers (**~220 now, goal ~2,000** to look reliable); **Facebook is "almost dead"** (no reaction) — Andrei is considering cutting Facebook spend or a different approach. Meta has run ~**3–3.5 months**; still **no attributable sales through Meta**.
- **Amazon status.** **3 reviews, all 5-star** — unlocking promo tools (discounts/coupons) and an application to **Amazon Prime sales**. **Return rate too high:** short-term **11%**, long-term **10%**, vs Amazon's required **≤5%** (2 machines returned) — hurts organic traffic. Mitigations on outbound machines: batteries replaced, software updated to latest (checked with Arkady), a **USB stick** included in the box. Inventory: **home version — 7 available + 6 processing + 6 shipped last Friday**; **coin-op — 4 left + 6 processing + 6 shipped last week** (Arcadia preparing another 6+6). Priority shifting to the **home version** (expects Amazon B2C to grow).
- **Shipping bottleneck = Amazon itself.** Carrier must book an **appointment** to deliver to the Amazon warehouse — delayed up to 2–3 weeks. Amazon's preferred carriers work only **inside the US** (not from Canada); plan to try shipping into the US by JVL's carrier, then to Amazon. Amazon **lost 1 of 12 machines** in a shipment; reimbursement is a separate process with no price field in the interface (open question of what declared price to claim).
- **Influencer plan (Instagram).** Budget approved at **$2,000–2,500/month** for Instagram influencers; ~**50** shortlisted, filtered by fit + **engagement rate >1%** (reactions/views over the last month) + an on-camera "appealing persona" who uses the product. Pricing examples: a **100k-subscriber arcade collector (6% engagement) asks $1,000 per reel**; others **$400 / $325**. Audiences targeted: luxury sports (golf/tennis/sailing/horse riding), RV living, hunting/gun enthusiasts, and arcade collectors. Val's timing steer: engage now — from today to **Dec 22** is the peak selling window.
- **Trade media.** A **RePlay** magazine article is prepared for **October** plus a full-size printed ad; a **newsletter placement** is live (every ~2 days through Oct–Nov, banner refreshed every 2–3 weeks). **Arcade Heroes** (arcade review site, ~25k unique users/month, B2B) to be contacted (Andrei has the editor-in-chief's contact). A distributor, **Scott Bright** of **All American Amusement Company** (old-school; possibly Michigan), inquired about the amusement version via the newsletter ad.
- **Guns media (two hubs).** **Crowley Media** (main brand **Athlon Outdoors**) — Instagram/YouTube/newsletters/portal; would push a 3-month package (price TBD). **American Handgunner** (also owns Guns Magazine + Shooting Industry brands), ~**115k unique visitors/month**; Andrei leans to placing **ads** (not an article) at ~**$1,000–1,500/month** for ~65k–70k appearances. Val wants a **12-month** commitment eventually but agreed to trial first.
- **Val's premium-targeting coaching.** Val redirected away from average-gun-owner media toward the **premium collector** ("drives a Maserati… carries guns in Loro Piana pouches… $50,000 of guns and doesn't flinch") — the buyer who'll buy an ECHO as a man-cave conversation piece. He walked Andrei through prompting an AI (ChatGPT-5) to list **premium handgun custom shops** — named **Staccato** and **Wilson Combat** at **$2,000+** (and $5,000+ for semi-automatics) — to find the right premium influencers/media. He killed the **RV / home-repair** directions ("wrong direction") except a premium home-renovation segment; **guns and horses** kept as premium buckets.
- **Google not yet running.** Waiting on the contract, which is "in **Monica's** court"; close to signing.

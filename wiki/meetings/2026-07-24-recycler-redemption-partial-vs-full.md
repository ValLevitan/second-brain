# Recycler Redemption — Partial vs Full-Only Decision (July 24 2026)

**Date:** July 24, 2026, 8:07–8:55 AM Eastern (~48 min)
**Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]], [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]], [[wiki/people/alexander-biratka.md|Alexander Biratko]].

The call settled Alexander's development question — does the Flex-Recycler support **partial** redemption, or only full? The answer splits by stage, scoped to the **Georgia recycler** under the working assumption that cash payout becomes permitted there. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Recycler_Redemption_Partial_vs_Full-July-24-2026.md]] The core rule: the printed **JVL temporary wallet** is always redeemed 100% at the recycler (no split; all funds move to the card), but **partial cash-out from the resulting Skill Play card is allowed** — the player inputs an amount, the recycler checks it against the bills it holds and offers the nearest feasible amounts, with no denomination choice.

# Fast-pacing general-alignment stand-up — session 3 (Sept 24 2025)

**Date:** 2025-09-24 (2:33 PM) · **Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]], [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]]. Russian.

**Session 3** of the recurring "fast-pacing / general-alignment stand-up" series — sequenced to the [[wiki/meetings/2025-09-10-fast-pacing-alignment-standup.md|Sept-10 opener]] (session 1) and the [[wiki/meetings/2025-09-17-fast-pacing-alignment-standup-spec-prototyping.md|Sept-17 spec-prototyping]] (session 2, which booked "Sept 24, 10:30" — this one ran 2:33 PM). What advanced since Sept-17: the "reverse-Omni into a spec" interview plan moved into execution (interviewees/interviewers assigned, transcription tooling debated), and Val worked a clean **game-pipeline model** to settle Irina's portfolio confusion. Spec/testing substance ties to [[wiki/topics/test-automation-strategy.md]] and the reverse-Omni thread on [[wiki/decisions/2025-04-03-jira-capability-level-and-github-mapping.md]] (Sept-10→24 section); the nine-bonus concept ties to [[wiki/meetings/2025-09-18-games-concepts-markets-review.md|the Sept-18 games/markets review]] (slot-game-design).

Durable points (single source):

- **Portfolio confusion → the game-pipeline model.** [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina]] opened that she can't tell from the portfolio what is being released, or when, or what to QA — release information reaches her as scattered "leaks" (Val's word for it: noise, not information) from [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena]] (products) and [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim]] (Jackpot Rush). Val's ruling: everyone must "look at the same one thing" — the portfolio. He laid out the pipeline as **three separate stages, each with its own life-cycle that do NOT auto-synchronize:** **Game Concept** (a game in concept — owned by [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan]] by context, transcribed "Wan"; Juan decides when a concept promotes to design), **Game Design** ([[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael]] as producer designs the game), and **Game Production/Development** ([[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena]] physically builds it — and [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel]] noted production "always" then requires further programmer rework). Not everything in concept reaches design; not everything designed reaches production. [[raw/fast-pacing-general-alignment_stand-up-september-24-2025.md]]
- **Kill sunk-cost design.** Val: where a design in progress is no longer needed, **kill it now** and accept losing the money already spent on design rather than push it into production — "I want to kill the mentality that because we already invested in the design, let's do the production." [[raw/fast-pacing-general-alignment_stand-up-september-24-2025.md]]
- **Two new South-Dakota requirements arrived; don't build them ad hoc.** (1) From [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim]] — a completely different free-spins approach so **each free spin enters the played-game history at a price of one**; (2) an **electronic/"soft" emitters-to-be-saved** requirement. Val's process ruling: **first collect ALL requirements ("reclamations"), group them, decide who builds them and when, schedule it** — then work; "right now I require nothing from you while the requirement-collection is going on." [[raw/fast-pacing-general-alignment_stand-up-september-24-2025.md]]
- **Old framework vs Omni.** Val won't touch the games on the **old framework** ("the 100 framework") — he doesn't want to break/rebuild them, and would let them run their own way — and instead wants to **implement new challenges/requirements on Omni** ("умней"/Omni). But Omni must first be "caught up" to a baseline level with an initial spec/requirement set he can keep appending to; JVL is working on Omni "this week and next." [[raw/fast-pacing-general-alignment_stand-up-september-24-2025.md]]
- **The nine-bonus game concept.** Val wants to push a **game design carrying nine bonuses** — for machines sold at "$300 / 400 / 500 thousand a game," operators want more entertainment, branching and other bonus elements that JVL lacks today but the competition has. He will **not** insert these new features into the old framework — they go on **Omni**. (Continues the Sept-18 "nine-bonus rule" — Kaiocho / FFP / Cash-Kittens on Omni — see [[wiki/meetings/2025-09-18-games-concepts-markets-review.md]].) [[raw/fast-pacing-general-alignment_stand-up-september-24-2025.md]]
- **Omni-spec progress + transcription tooling.** The Omni specification effort is "at the beginning of the path"; three meetings were held Sept-24 and are being transcribed. **Teams transcribes Russian poorly**, so [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel]] is transcribing with a **local offline Whisper** app, then feeding AI **two transcriptions** (one with speaker/time labels, one with better text) to merge — Monday's came out well; he may need an **NVIDIA GPU** (talk to [[wiki/people/andrey-bosik.md|Andrei]]) to speed it. **Val's recommendation:** switch from Teams to **Google Meet** for meeting transcription (needs a paid Google One / Workspace account), then push it to **Gemini** for English translation — likely a cheaper, better path. [[raw/fast-pacing-general-alignment_stand-up-september-24-2025.md]]
- **Reverse-Omni interviews now assigned.** Today's most useful meeting: **"Zhenya Koretski"** ([[wiki/people/evgeny-kharetski.md|Evgeny Kharetski]] — resolved by the LGS/data-center/backend context + the registry's Koretski garble-alias; Tier-A queued) asked many questions and wants to **dig into the code himself with AI**, build his own document, then reconcile it with the meeting record — skeptical that AI alone will "do it all beautifully," but sees value in using AI as his own tool. Val praised this strongly and told Pavel to give him ~**2 hrs/day** for it. Interview assignments: the new hire **[[wiki/people/alexander-tumanov.md|Alexander Tumanov]]** was brought in to **interview Zhenya (Kharetski) about the data-center setup** (booked Mon/Tue next week); [[wiki/people/irina-makarych.md|Irina Makarych]] is being pulled onto the meetings to absorb the path; [[wiki/people/igor-metelski.md|Igor Metelski]] was interviewed the prior day (Metelski, who already knows the material, asks fewer questions than a from-scratch learner like Zhenya, so his knowledge must be actively extracted when he sits with Makarych or [[wiki/people/alexander-biratka.md|Sasha]]). An **Anton** (transcribed "Tyshkevich" — surname unclear, not in the §4 Anton cluster; **queued**, not force-matched) had a session with Sasha ~a week ago and will join later once a mis-set morning meeting is replanned. [[raw/fast-pacing-general-alignment_stand-up-september-24-2025.md]]

Val put it on the agenda — discuss with them how the identifiers are generated (the Flex setup already generates identifiers, "maybe automatically") and pull Hrybko into the effort. [[raw/fast-pacing-general-alignment_stand-up-september-24-2025.md]]

Continuing. (Ties to the Sept 9→16 git-structure arc on [[wiki/decisions/2025-02-11-github-org-naming-and-structure.md]].) [[raw/fast-pacing-general-alignment_stand-up-september-24-2025.md]]

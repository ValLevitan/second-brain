# Galaxy / Nova Platform Strategy

**July 16, 2026, 10:00–11:00 AM Eastern** (notes + transcript = one meeting; ~58m). Attendees: [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]], [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]], [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]].

A strategy session on how to move forward with the **Galaxy** transition, the **SVN → GitHub** move, the **Nova vs. Rage** platform question, and where the **Miranda** design tool fits. (The meeting was mostly in Russian and the transcript garbles product names — Rage as "рейч/речь", GitHub as "гетхаб/кидхап", Biratko as "пиратка/биратка"; "Koretsky/Корецкий" = [[wiki/people/evgeny-kharetski.md|Evgeny (Zhenya) Kharetski]], "Dima Khantov" = [[wiki/people/dmitriy-khandov.md|Dmitriy Khandov]], "Chudinov" = [[wiki/people/dmitri-chudinov.md|Dzmitry Chudzinau]], Sasha = [[wiki/people/alexander-biratka.md|Alexander Biratko]].) **"Miranda" here is the design tool** (the Unity/C# scene-authoring tool), not [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda]].

Substance lives on:
- [[wiki/topics/nova-platform-architecture.md]] (Jul-16) — the Rage-is-a-dead-end reasoning (Ogre engine, locked/unlicensed packages), the rejected Miranda-for-Rage idea, the preferred Nova-plus-game-conversion path, Val's three-way decomposition of Rage (engine → PixiJS, dev ecosystem → Miranda, ~50–100 game scene rebuilds), and Pavel's timelines (machine ~2 months; Galaxy 6+ months).
- [[wiki/topics/github-release-management-pipeline.md]] (Jul-16) — the DataCenter SVN-to-GitHub migration assigned to Kharetski and the GitHub-workflow rationale.

Links back to the [[wiki/meetings/2026-06-30-nova-migration-and-autonomous-coding.md|June-30 Nova-migration meeting]]. A focused follow-up with Kharetski and Biratko is booked for July 17.

# Player Behavior Simulator — commissioning meeting (Aug 7 2025)

**Date:** 7 August 2025, 4:00pm EST (recording header). **Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael Gottlieb]], [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]], [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim Podvoiski]], [[wiki/people/anton-leshkevich.md|Anton Leshkevich]] ([[wiki/people/aaron.md|Aaron Bockelie]] joined but dropped mid-call). [[raw/Player_behavior_simulator.md]]

**Purpose:** after ~two months of looping over why the **Fiery Ice Link** ("Gayocho") game posts excessive negative-win days at three Florida casinos, Val cut the loop and commissioned a **Player Behavior Simulator** — a tool to emulate typical player sessions (not test math accuracy) so JVL can judge whether the game's *volatility* (not its RTP) is driving players away. [[raw/Player_behavior_simulator.md]]

The substance — the Fiery Ice performance data, the volatility-vs-RTP reframing, Michael's game-design dissertation ($20 test / lottery-ticket philosophy / hold-and-respin / 70-30 base-bonus split), Efim's Python-simulator design, Anton's code-side progressive check, and Val's "our own school of game design" — lives on [[wiki/topics/slot-game-design-principles.md]]. [[raw/Player_behavior_simulator.md]]

**Follow-up agenda:** in file 1 Juan committed to re-collect progressive-controller data "next Monday" (~Aug 11) for a follow-up review. [[raw/Player_behavior_simulator.md]] Val subsequently circulated an agenda email to Michael and Juan for a **Monday 2 PM, 1.5-hour** follow-up meeting, summarizing "a discussion I had with Efim and Anton" (the [[wiki/meetings/2025-08-08-player-simulator-engineering-discussion.md|~Aug-8 engineering discussion]]) and asking the two to come prepared with the player-behavior scenarios, reports and metrics they most want to simulate. [[raw/player_behavior.md]] That Monday (~Aug 11) follow-up meeting itself is not yet in the corpus.

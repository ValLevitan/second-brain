# Flex Recycler first cycle test & MEI rework decision

**Date:** August 14, 2026, 12:00 PM ET (~9 min)
**Attendees:** [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]], [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]], [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda Giorgi]], [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]], [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]]

Andrei reported the first full Flex Recycler cash cycle worked end to end (deposit → TITO-style ticket → play across a flex-port machine → cash out → real cash). Val directed a re-run traversing both machines with mixed-bill dispensing, and decided to scrap the current MEI recycler (wrong construction; MEI won't supply) and build the correct multi-cassette recycler in Minsk, targeting a mid-September beta timed to InComm's Skill Pay card launch.

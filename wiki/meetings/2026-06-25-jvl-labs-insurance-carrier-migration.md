# JVL Labs Insurance Carrier Migration (June 25 2026)

**June 25 2026, morning ET (header), ~13m.** Attendees: [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]], [[wiki/people/inna-levitan.md|Inna Levitan]], and **Cyndi Anderson** of **HUB International** (JVL's external insurance broker — recorded in prose, no person page).

A short broker call resolving JVL Labs' mismatched insurance: HUB broker Cyndi Anderson explained that **Everest Insurance has a $25,000 minimum-premium floor**, so removing inapplicable coverages could never bring the premium below that number — Everest is structurally the wrong carrier for what JVL Labs has become. The fix is to migrate carriers (to Economical, a Canadian preferred insurer). Val agreed; the Everest policy (on extension) was to lapse and the new policy be bound after Val's review.

Substance recorded on:
- [[wiki/organizations/jvl.md]] — the insurance-carrier-migration as a JVL corporate-admin fact (Everest $25K floor → Economical, crime coverage dropped, IP insurance handled separately). A June-29 "JVL Labs Insurance Policy Review" follow-up is expected.

# Electro-mechanical alignment — POS board, core-control firmware, warehouse (Sept 22 2025)

**Date:** 2025-09-22 (4:28 PM) · **Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]] and [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi Peari]]. Russian.

A one-on-one on the transactional-hardware arc — POS/FlexPort board bring-up, the core-control firmware, warehouse consolidation, and the Georgia service-desk staffing. Hardware substance folds into [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]] (Sept-22 section); this stub keeps the durable decisions and open items. Sequenced to the [[wiki/meetings/2025-09-09-pos-spatial-hardware-board-planning.md|Sept-9 POS board planning]] and [[wiki/meetings/2025-09-17-flexport-smib-completion-scope.md|Sept-17 FlexPort-completion scope]].

Durable points (single source):

- **POS RockChip board — no simple USB image.** The cheap Chinese "RockChip" (rock-chip) processor board can't be flashed JVL's usual way (plug a USB stick and load the image); the vendor's procedure needs proprietary PC software and a physical connection, and JVL cannot get the source files from the Chinese vendor. Genadi is pursuing three paths in parallel: extract the softload source files from the vendor, find a better-supported Chinese vendor with the same ARM industrial platform, or engage **[[wiki/people/dima-pikulev.md|Dima Pikulev]]** to rebuild the Linux so a USB image works. Genadi's view: an **ARM industrial platform is the right direction** for embedded (history/memory support); the RockChip is cheap but poorly supported.
- **Val reluctant to use Dima Pikulev.** Val: "мне нравится дима пикулев, но если б я мог его не привлекать" — he only gives a small amount of time, is effectively semi-retired ("he doesn't work because he needs to… he doesn't even want to work"), and hasn't finished the **"forward"/firmware for the core-control system** JVL is already waiting on for Flex. See [[wiki/people/dima-pikulev.md]].
- **Core-control system firmware (Flex / Flex Top).** The core-control board firmware was originally for **Flex Top**, then consolidated so **one board replaces three** on Flex; still unfinished. A **bigger flash chip** (more memory) was added recently because the firmware didn't fit, so [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel]] (Pavel Frolov, by Minsk-engineering context) must re-solder one at his end; files to be handed to Pavel by end of week for Minsk. [[raw/alignment_elctro-mechanical-september-22-2025.md]]
- **Alekseev's universal interface-board plan.** [[wiki/people/evgeniy-alekseev.md|Evgeniy Alekseev]] is busy in Jira and will open **33 tasks**; he is building the **monitor interface board** first, then a single **main interface board** universal to any ARM platform JVL later drops in — carrying a **USB hub/multiplier** ("no processor, no firmware, purely electronic"), communicating over one wire to avoid confusion. Val questioned USB reliability ("how will we know USB stopped communicating?"); Genadi: it adds no new bus lines, it just multiplies the same USB (a converter, like on the monitor). See [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]].
- **FlexPort board waits on the mechanical form.** Genadi wants to finalize the FlexPort mechanical/plastic form first because **"Evgeny" (Евгений) will have to adjust the board file** to whatever they decide (mount, size, perimeter lighting, scanner reach) — "we must be first, he comes after us." Per the updated §4 registry ruling, hardware-adjacent board work on **Genadi Peari's projects** resolves to [[wiki/people/evgeniy-alekseev.md|Evgeniy Alekseev]] (there is no fourth "Zhenya"); this is the board-file side of Alekseev's FlexPort/interface-board work below. [[raw/alignment_elctro-mechanical-september-22-2025.md]]
- **Georgia service desk — Albertini now first line; Elizabeth being onboarded.** [[wiki/people/albertini.md|Albertini]] now takes **60–70% of support calls** (previously routed through [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]]) — technically capable, polite, "European style," not very driven; Val is moving Felipe to escalation/last line and Albertini onto more quotas via the ticketing system he wants to implement ("roses" — a CRM/ticketing tool, name garbled), which JVL already runs and works well. [[wiki/people/elizabeth.md|Elizabeth]] is being trained on the **service/reference material** for the **South Dakota** machines (10 machines, sealing/labeling tasks); English is her hurdle, so the team chat with instant translation lets her follow the technical group. Val coached Genadi on management: she isn't ignoring him, she's "thinking / working on something" and will ask when she has a real question — "you've developed management you didn't have three years ago." Genadi to screen-share and walk her (and Albertini, and optionally Felipe) through the material. See [[wiki/people/elizabeth.md]] and [[wiki/people/albertini.md]].
- **Denis meeting recap — amusement / rental-type products.** Genadi prepped **5 topics** for the [[wiki/people/denis-desmarais.md|Denis]] meeting, walked production and the Flex line, and gathered JVL's old **Flex-E casino developments** (Carnival, Fortune, matrices, prototypes) as reference so Denis can build an "arena/rental-type" offering "like last year, more/less." He can reuse the contractors and tooling used then (Dima, Igor, various artists; the plastics/prototype tooling paid for on Flex-E). It came out to **five (Genadi wrote ~6, likely 5) purely-mechanical / electro-mechanical "claims"** (transcribed "файф клеймс" — the exact meaning of "claims" is unclear in the source; likely patent or product claims, kept verbatim, not interpreted). [[raw/alignment_elctro-mechanical-september-22-2025.md]]
- **Warehouse consolidation — add a cheap "overflow warehouse."** The current warehouse is ~75% full, but ~15–20% is old **Altera/Maple** machines JVL doesn't use and must move out. Val wants a cheap **overflow warehouse** ~1–2 hours from the Atlanta house to hold them, and to **order proper new boxes/packaging** (new labels, maybe a different colour) so JVL-origin machines/parts don't get mixed with the units [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe]] returns from America (which otherwise have to be re-sorted). New sequencing: first clear the **Canadian warehouse** and clean out **Georgia**, freeing shelving + a lift to ship to Georgia (buy only a small local forklift for the narrow aisles), *then* set up overflow storage. Val is unsure whether to keep the current well-priced warehouse (~$7.40/sq-ft) or take a taller new one (~$13/sq-ft) — no decision yet. Genadi's rule: if a prototype/sample is untouched for **2 years, throw it out**; Val agrees. [[raw/alignment_elctro-mechanical-september-22-2025.md]]

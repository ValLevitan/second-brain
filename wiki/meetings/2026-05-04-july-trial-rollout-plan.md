# July Trial Rollout Plan (May 4 2026)

**Date:** 4 May 2026, 10:15pm Eastern (§11 recording header time); ~42m.
**Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda Giorgi]], [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]]. (§4: the recording title "Call with Felipe and 1 other" — the "1 other" is Juan Martinez, the only other speaker on the transcript.)

A tactical plan for a **July Georgia trial rollout**: seed ~10 free complete V43 machines into hand-picked top-tier operator locations ahead of the Aug 15 Georgia gaming show, so JVL can quote real field performance rather than renders. Operators keep all revenue for a 3–6 month trial, then buy; JVL chooses the locations (only the top 5–10% qualify — average locations produce misleading data). Val's heuristic: a list of 10 mid-size operators (50+ locations each) yields ~20 candidate top locations, of which ~10 machines could be placed with ≥8 operators [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_july_trial_rollout_plan-May-4-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_July_Trial_Rollout_Plan-May-4-2026.md]]. Primary offer is an incremental free machine; a software upgrade is the fallback for operators who can't take a second unit [[raw/Meeting_Notes_July_Trial_Rollout_Plan-May-4-2026.md]]. Also covered: the Aug 15 show booth (215, 20x20) and product mix (V43 + D27, e-wallet/Tito/FlexPay/POS/recycler/dispenser), and holding the Texas Maples pitch a few days pending a new QR-code-reader integration [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_july_trial_rollout_plan-May-4-2026.md]]. The notes carry a May 5 post-meeting update: Felipe delivered the 10-priority/5-contingency operator list, and Val moved the target ship date from July 1 to the **last week of June 2026** (the July 4 long weekend would compress the pre-show window) [[raw/Meeting_Notes_July_Trial_Rollout_Plan-May-4-2026.md]]. Substance and citations live on [[wiki/projects/georgia-market-rollout.md]] (May 4 2026 section). §15.8: market/machine/booth economics only; no named-person compensation.

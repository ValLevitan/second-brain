- **§12:** no wiki page cited either file, and no page carried this content (grepped for the PO amounts, "Everglory", "Gen Touch", "Bashida", "springs", "708-261"). - **Notes vs transcript — where the wiki follows the transcript:**
  - **Dongguan Bashida.** The notes say "Val: pay in full." The transcript does not say that. Val asks "Тонал пей, ты говоришь?" and Mikhail answers "Да, они ничего не спрашивают, сумма небольшая" — the vendor is not asking and the sum is small. "Тонал пей" most likely means "do not pay" (an inference). The wiki records the outcome as **unclear, probably wait**.
  - **The $6,960 line and PO 370.** The notes list them as two items and say Mikhail "corrected himself to parts." In the transcript he first says molds, then parts, then corrects back: "Это не детали… Аня это прессформа да там 30% можно заплатить." The wiki treats the $6,960 and PO 370 as probably **one mold order** — $6,960 is exactly 60% of $11,600 (an inference, not stated).
  - **Guangdong Hi Pray.** The notes say it is held "on Mikhail's judgement." In the transcript **Val** proposes waiting ("Это можно подождать") and Mikhail agrees and gives the reason.
  - **Yangzhou springs.** The notes say Val said stock covers 80 machines. In the transcript **Mikhail** says "У нас их, у нас их на 80 машин"; Val replies that will not be enough.
  - **Everglory's terms.** The notes write "40% for parts, 100% for parts." The transcript line is garbled: "40% за детали 100% за детей." The wiki quotes it as spoken.
  - **Hong Kong Wanlin.** The notes say "going by sea." The transcript says "это эрма отправляем" — the mode is unclear.
  - **The earlier alignment call.** The notes say this is the meeting Val tried to arrange in a morning call about a Texas Flex-Slim release. The transcript does not mention that. *(Update, later ingest: that morning call is now stubbed at [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-23-texas-flex-slim-release-street-cabinet-and-bom-source-of-truth.md]]; in its transcript Val says he is trying to meet Mikhail that day about the signed orders. That this 1:02 PM call is that meeting remains an inference.)*
- **Garbled passages not used:** an English-rendered stretch at about 02:08–03:08 ("Julia, you know the place down there", "Tigorish postponed it", "they were sorting for vendor pay") is too broken to use. Val's "Крис 7 405 дальше" may be Val noting the figure for Cris (A/P); unclear.

# Flex-Recycler pre-May4 REQ Daily Sync — Personas & e-Wallet (Apr 29 2026)

**Date:** 29 April 2026, 7:01pm Eastern (§11 recording header time; the `_150151` filename stamp is a different zone, not the meeting time); ~1h 46m.
**Attendees:** [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] (production requirements lead, driving the doc), [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]] (product / Texas market), [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]].

A daily-sync working session in the "pre-May4 REQ" push — a requirements sprint toward a **May 4** milestone — reviewing Andrei's draft Personas document live in SharePoint, then starting the Player use-cases. The persona list was cut from five to **four locked personas** (Player; Location Owner / Site Attendant merged; Technician; Route Operator), with the Player prioritised first. The bulk of the call became Val's tutorial on **why the Recycler architecture exists** — the float/TITO/casino-infrastructure problem the Recycler eliminates — and the ruling that credit transfer is framed as an **e-wallet umbrella** (QR, barcode, PIN, plastic card, phone photo, SMS-PIN — "no standard, no rule"), with voucher-seizure-on-entry semantics, QR+PIN auth, and **no co-installation** with machine-side bill acceptors or third-party redemption kiosks (JVL-only ecosystem). Substance, decisions, and citations live on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]] (Apr 29 2026 section). Part of the Apr-28→30 Flex-Recycler REQ series — cf. [[wiki/meetings/2026-04-28-recycler-architecture-planning.md|Apr 28 architecture planning]], the same-day [[wiki/meetings/2026-04-29-flex-recycler-development-planning.md|Apr 29 IP-1059 dev planning]], and the [[wiki/meetings/2026-04-30-flex-recycler-req-prep.md|Apr 30 REQ prep]]. §15.8: product/market/machine economics only; no named-person compensation.

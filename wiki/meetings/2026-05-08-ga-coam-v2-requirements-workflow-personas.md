# GA-COAM v2 Requirements Workflow and Personas (May 8 2026)

**Date:** 8 May 2026, 3:00pm Eastern (§11 recording header time); ~33m. Bilingual (English + Russian; Teams garbled the Russian stretches).
**Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]], [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim Podvoiski]], [[wiki/people/igor-metelski.md|Igor Metelski]], [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]].

The reconvened afternoon session following the [[wiki/meetings/2026-05-08-ga-coam-v2-capability-decomposition.md|1:11pm capability-decomposition meeting]] — Val and Juan paired during the recess and returned to walk requirements against the three new capabilities [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ga_coam_v2_requirements_workflow_and_personas-May-8-2026.md]]. Two durable outcomes. First, the **workflow rules** (intended JVL-wide, not just GA-COAM): acceptance criteria live in **requirements, not capabilities**; **features link to capabilities, not requirements** (a capability is *done/closed* when all its features merge to `main`; requirements stay durable and reusable); one JIRA feature = one GitHub feature branch (~5 workdays each), max 2–3 concurrent branches per developer, each repo carries a **Claude Code reviewer**, and feature descriptions must be technical enough to hand directly to Claude Code as the implementation source of truth (business acceptance criteria in requirements, technical acceptance criteria in features) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ga_coam_v2_requirements_workflow_and_personas-May-8-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ga_coam_v2_requirements_workflow_and_personas-May-8-2026.md]]. Second, a **third persona — JVL Product Manager** — was formalised alongside the existing operator/master and player personas (an individual inside JVL who dictates how the product behaves in the market; Val corrected Efim's "operations manager" to "Product Manager: manages product") [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ga_coam_v2_requirements_workflow_and_personas-May-8-2026.md]]. First PM-persona requirement: **progressive settings cannot be changed on the fly** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ga_coam_v2_requirements_workflow_and_personas-May-8-2026.md]]. Also: the attract-mode display fix (all three progressives shown only in attract mode, not during play — Juan to correct an over-broad requirement) [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ga_coam_v2_requirements_workflow_and_personas-May-8-2026.md]]; Juan holds [[wiki/people/igor-metelski.md|Igor]] off feature-drafting until Juan meets [[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael]] to design the match-3 game flow and finishes the requirement/persona edits (all today, May 8) [[raw/Meeting_Notes_ga_coam_v2_requirements_workflow_and_personas-May-8-2026.md]]; when a feature is unclear, Igor **must clarify with Juan (the business representative), not Efim** [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_ga_coam_v2_requirements_workflow_and_personas-May-8-2026.md]].

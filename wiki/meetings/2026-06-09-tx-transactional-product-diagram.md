# TX transactional product diagram — Flex-IQ + FlexPort player flow for Texas (June 9 2026)

**June 9, 2026, 6:17 PM ET · ~1h42m.** Attendees: [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]], [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]], [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]].

[[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] walked a **diagram of the Texas transactional product family / player flow** — an ecosystem where **Flex IQ** is installed at the location and every machine is upgraded with a **FlexPort**, built out as swim-lanes tracing the player from cash-in through QR-ticket redemption. The group worked the "kill-the-QR-on-scan" reconciliation model (mirror a TITO ticket, no partial cash-in), the host/ticket-server requirement, wiring the location vs. a wireless FlexPort↔IQ link, and Val's pre-validated encrypted-QR "nozzle" idea to avoid wiring; plus the Texas market realities (fully-manual game rooms as the low-hanging fruit, the recycler-vs-dispenser choice, and flat-fee reporting as the JVL revenue model). No decisions locked — Andrei to write a short two-language brief; Val to take it to the engineers. Extends the Texas transactional push from the [[wiki/meetings/2026-06-05-amot-texas-market-scouting.md|June-5 AMOT scouting]] report-back.

Durable substance — the Flex-IQ / FlexPort transactional flow and the wireless/encrypted-nozzle direction — lives on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]] (Jun-9); the Texas-market framing sits alongside [[wiki/projects/texas-used-machine-distribution.md]].

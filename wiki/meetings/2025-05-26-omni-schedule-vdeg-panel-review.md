# Omni schedule / V-Deck panel review — is FRAM 476 a feature? (May 26 2025)

A Russian-language Omni task/schedule review with [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]], [[wiki/people/alexander-biratka.md|Alexander Biratka]] (Sasha) and [[wiki/people/dmitriy-khandov.md|Dmitriy Khandov]] (Dima); [[wiki/people/igor-metelski.md|Igor Metelski]] and [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena]] referenced. The §3 continuation of the [[wiki/meetings/2025-05-14-omni-schedule-and-test-review.md|May-14]] and [[wiki/meetings/2025-05-19-omni-schedule-review.md|May-19]] schedule reviews (a distinct day, distinct content — the V-Deck feature-decomposition question).

The core of the call: whether the V-Deck stories under Sasha move to Igor Metelski, and Val's realisation that the "**V-Deck Panel for Omni**" (FRAM 476) is mis-modelled as a single feature because it depends on the whole game (including the un-started bonus) and so cannot close. Val teaches feature decomposition but declines to restructure in-flight work. Substance is on [[wiki/topics/omni-core-advancement-and-game-templating.md]].

# Feature/capability Jira structure — defining + reflection (2025-05-06)

Two short recordings from the same May-6 continuation of the [[wiki/meetings/2025-05-05-feature-granularity-aaron.md|May-5 feature-granularity session]] ([[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] ↔ [[wiki/people/aaron.md|Aaron]]) — Val opens the "reflection" one with "if I understood correctly what we discussed yesterday."

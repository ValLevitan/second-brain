# JIRA Hierarchy Restructuring (May 7 2026)

**Date:** 7 May 2026 — a two-part same-day session: Part 1 at **1:31pm Eastern** (engineering 1:1, ~1h 10m) and Part 2 at **3:01pm Eastern** (broader group review, ~1h 34m). §11 recording-header times (the `_093156` / `_110154` filename stamps are a different zone, not the meeting time).
**Attendees:** Part 1 — [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/dmitri-kovalchuk.md|Dmitri Kovalchuk]]. Part 2 adds [[wiki/people/dmitri-chudinov.md|Dmitri Chudinov]] (transcribed "Dzmitry Chudzinau"), [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]], [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena Zhukova]].

Working sessions to actually rebuild JVL's Jira issue hierarchy (the Initiative→Product→Capability→Feature levels that the May-4 Flex-Recycler milestone locked) — Kovalchuk is doing it in a Jira **sandbox**, breaking all the parent-child links and re-laddering value-stream / platform / capability / feature / product so that **Capabilities sit above Features** and the whole thing renders correctly in **Jira Plans** (the only view that gives full company-wide alignment). Val's non-negotiable is that Features attach to Capabilities. The 1:1 also explored, but did not commit to, **abandoning Jira for GitHub** as an alternative planning surface (Kovalchuk: GitHub could pilot a project non-destructively, but a generated plan would be a static "snapshot", not a movable board).

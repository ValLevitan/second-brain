## Notes vs transcript

The transcript is the source of record. The derived notes differ in three places, and the wiki follows the transcript:
- The notes list a "**standing rule reconfirmed**: US profits get moved to Canadian losses via intercompany charges." In the transcript this is Val's wish, and Sean pushes back: no management fees from Labs to Systems; it must be "machine sales as opposed to admin or licensing fees"; and 2026 needs a meeting with the US accountants. No rule was confirmed. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Gilmore_GameWorks_US_Tax_and_Labs_Filing-June-30-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Gilmore_GameWorks_US_Tax_and_Labs_Filing-June-30-2026.md]]
- The notes leave out the whole 2026 problem (no machine sales next year, bigger Systems profits, losses stuck in Labs, the follow-up meeting with Mark at BSP). The wiki records it from the transcript. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Gilmore_GameWorks_US_Tax_and_Labs_Filing-June-30-2026.md]]
- The notes write "SR&ED and taxes". The transcript says "**Shreds** and **JAX**". "Shreds" is JVL's usual word for SR&ED; "JAX" is kept as spoken (unclear). [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Gilmore_GameWorks_US_Tax_and_Labs_Filing-June-30-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Gilmore_GameWorks_US_Tax_and_Labs_Filing-June-30-2026.md]]

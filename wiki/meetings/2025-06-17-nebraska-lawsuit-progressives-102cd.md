# Nebraska — American Amusements lawsuit clears the progressive/jackpot restrictions (Jun 17 2025)

A short JVL call reporting that a lawsuit filed by **American Amusements** was won by the plaintiff, so JVL's attorney instructed the team to **disregard Nebraska regulation sections 102C and 102D** — removing the restrictions on look-alike lottery games and, crucially, on **progressive/linked jackpots**. On the call with [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]], [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim]] ("Yefim") and others (a "Bill" and a "Lena" are referenced); the reporter travels to Nebraska the next day to write up a report.

Substance lives on [[wiki/projects/nebraska-skill-market.md]] (see the Jun-17 section).

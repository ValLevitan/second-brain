# ADP payroll review — report methodology & payroll-reduction benchmarking (May 29 2026)

A ~13-minute [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] ↔ [[wiki/people/monica.md|Monica Levitan]] review (recording header 3:49pm ET) in which Monica walked Val through a revised **ADP payroll report** format. The record here captures only the durable **reporting-methodology, aggregate-payroll, and vendor** facts.

> **§15.8 exclusion.** The report itself lists every active ADP employee with position, employment status, and annual compensation. **No individual's compensation is recorded** — per §15.8 the named-person pay data is deliberately excluded. Only company-level payroll trend, report methodology, and vendor economics are captured below.

- **Report methodology (durable).** The revised report is keyed to the **effective date a payroll was run** (the sample shown was the Apr 17 2026 run). The correct way to annualize is the **bi-weekly salary column × 26**, *not* the "amount paid this payroll" column — the latter folds in one-time items (severance, vacation payouts) that distort the annual figure. (Monica explained; Val agreed.) → [[wiki/topics/jvl-finance-back-office.md]] (May-29).

- **Payroll-reduction benchmarking (company-level).** Val wants to **quantify the payroll cuts he began ~two years ago** by comparing **May 2024 → May 2025 → May 2026** (same month across the board, three-point comparison). This is a total-payroll trend for JVL, not any individual's pay. Monica to pull the ADP reports for the last May payroll in each of those years. → [[wiki/topics/jvl-finance-back-office.md]] (May-29).

- **Vendors / systems.** Val asked **how much JVL pays ADP** under the contract; Monica didn't recall the figure and will find out (open question). US payroll runs on **Paylocity** (a separate system from ADP); Monica to pull the US-side salary data from Paylocity for the same benchmarking. *(The ADP contract cost is a vendor-economics fact, allowed; the Paylocity US pay figures are named-person comp and are not recorded here.)* → [[wiki/topics/jvl-finance-back-office.md]] (May-29).

# Klimovich update — home orders, tariffs/sales tax, Burger King, website (Apr 18 2025)

A ~159-minute [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] update with [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]]. The raw transcription is badly corrupted with hallucinated foreign-language loops (Japanese recipe/train text) interleaved with the real dialogue (§12 artifact); the durable facts below were read past the garbled passages.

The substance lives on:
- [[wiki/topics/echo-home-gaming-device.md]] — three revived distributor orders (GameRoomGuys ×2, Birmingham Vending Company ×1, dormant since 2023); the fourth Amazon ECHO sale via ecosystem-wide targeted ads (static renders, no voiceover); the US-stock plan (put ~10 home units + 6 amusement units into the US, sell from there, +$300 framed as "price adjustment due to tariffs, not a price increase"); JVL absorbs tariffs only on its own Amazon retail sales.
- [[wiki/organizations/burger-king.md]] — the repair/replacement negotiation ladder Val coached (Repair Kit $2,500 as-is → discount to $2,100 → new machines at 18% off = $3,240; +~20% tariffs; bundle floor $3,050) and the separate trade-in option (old machines worth ~$150 parts; only cabinets reusable, for used-ECO resale to Latin America).
- [[wiki/topics/online-games-rgs-distribution.md]] — Val's verdict that the classic online account-manager model "does not work" (no operator/player loyalty, everyone makes the same games), the account-manager candidate falling through, and the still-unproven **Marketplace** as the one untested model.
- [[wiki/projects/jvl-corporate-website.md]] — CMS/back-end/front-end ready for first release; Ivan to test (synced with Irina for priority); About-Us uses Val's copy; ECHO page possibly by end of May.
- [[wiki/people/arkady-batson.md]] / [[wiki/people/lev-rudky.md|Lev]] — the invoice/shipping pipeline (Canada sales via Arkady; US sales via Lev until BC is installed) and the customs-broker error; the cross-border tariff/inter-company-transfer and per-state sales-tax mechanics (Avalara-style add-on configured for 4 states incl. Georgia; unconfigured states googled/added by hand); open corporate UPS/FedEx accounts (JVL Systems).

Credit-card vs ACH: Birmingham asked to pay by card without the 3% fee; Val's line — hold 3% (try to negotiate the processor to 2%) or push the buyer to **ACH** (Val's preferred method).

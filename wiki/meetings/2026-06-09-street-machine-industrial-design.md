# Street machine industrial design — single-screen vertical 43" cabinet (June 9 2026)

**June 9, 2026, 1:29 PM ET · ~51 min (Russian).** Attendees: [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi Peari]].

Val walked what's known and still open for the street machine: a self-contained single-screen unit with a **vertical 43" HD display** (good black level, no topper), a full-screen touchscreen, a monolithic sheet-metal body with the screen built in, a hidden TITO printer and back-load bill validator, one perimeter light line, no amplifier, and a cloud progressive linking machines in a formation. He stressed that **service is the core of the business** — front service access plus two-way feedback on every peripheral is what makes the recurring remote services sellable. Next step: Val writes the industrial-design brief and Genadi briefs the designer Igor Starovoytov. Follows the [[wiki/meetings/2026-06-08-low-cost-street-machine-design.md|June-8 low-cost street-machine design]] opener.

Durable substance — the cabinet design, peripherals, service/feedback model, and progressive — lives on [[wiki/topics/low-cost-street-machine.md]].

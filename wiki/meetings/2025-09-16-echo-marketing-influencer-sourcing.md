# ECHO distribution & marketing update (Sept 16 2025)

**Date:** 2025-09-16 · **Attendees:** [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]], [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]]. Russian.

**Series sequencing:** this is **session 2** of the Sept-15/16/17/26 ECHO-marketing series — it follows the [[wiki/meetings/2025-09-15-echo-distribution-marketing-update.md|Sept-15 opener]] (Sept-17/26 come in later batches). §12 corpus-wide cross-check: distinct content from the Sept-15 opener and the ~18 prior Klimovich/ECHO meetings — a new session, not a re-transcription. Substance folds into [[wiki/topics/echo-home-gaming-device.md]] (Sept-16 section).

Durable points (single source):

- **Influencer sourcing method (guns).** Four brand categories — gun brands, premium gun-safe makers, custom-holster makers, gun-storage-room makers — top ~5 US brands each; find influencers who collaborated with **2–3** of those brands (audience match), prioritised by number of collaborations. [[wiki/people/valeria.md|Valeria]] runs the same method on the **horses** vertical (~4 sub-groups). Sailing dropped ("not rich enough").
- **First influencer responses.** Two arcade-collector influencers replied; one asks **$750 for one Instagram Reel + 2 stories over 2 weeks** with redirect to JVL's channel (Andrei calls it a good standard package); negotiating to sign and ship a machine for review.
- **Amazon ad shift (call with [[wiki/people/fedor.md|Fedor]]).** Stop **visual/display** ads inside Amazon — cost-per-cart runs ~**2× more expensive** than **search/query** ads; reallocate the ~**$20/day** visual budget to search. Rationale: search is capped by keyword relevance (Amazon won't serve irrelevant queries), so visual becomes useful only once search traffic is exhausted (JVL is far from that ceiling). "Gaming equipment" is the current best "locomotive" keyword (most relevant traffic + carts); a new **$100/day** budget was set but Amazon's algorithm "broke" since last Friday and served no traffic — support case open.
- **Time tracking.** Last week: **1h10** on company review + syncing with Fedor; **2h20** on app setup / shipments / debugging with Amazon support. Not yet automatable — "a very live, dynamic environment."
- **Pricing experiments.** Current **$600 discount** live; can go more aggressive to ~**$2,990** for **Prime Day**. Val asked whether dropping price ~$1,600 would sell ~80 machines/month; Andrei non-committal. Amazon keeps price history internally but shoppers see only the current price + current discount, so experiments don't hurt.
- **June-2026 mental deadline.** Andrei needs ~**3 months per external channel** (Google/X-Twitter/Meta/influencers) to run at real volume; can't sign the Google contract yet. Val's framing: by **~June 2026** JVL should know definitively whether the product sells at a viable price or must reposition / re-price. Distributors sell **4–5 machines/month at ~$4,000** (resellers Batson, Game Room Guys, Birmingham, Wendling → operators; ~50/50 home vs operator placement); JVL plans to **intercept distributor web traffic** via Google (analyse their inbound search terms).
- **Website walk-through + costs.** ECHO page (home version): 3D-model viewer, **AR/VR "place it in your room,"** installment message (**$166/month, 0% APR**), machine-evolution timeline, lifestyle photos (dissatisfied with AI-generated people — wants stock photos), Travel-ready/RV, selling points (compact footprint, "29-mm" touchscreen [transcribed verbatim; likely 29-inch — unclear], high-quality audio, 1/2-player + tournaments/leaderboards), games block (gameplay reels per category; Adult blurred), support block, specs, footer. Costs stated: full ECHO page design+dev **$4,500**, visual content ~**$100/image**, all-in **$6,000**. A temporary **Flex** landing page (built for Felipe's expo): video + per-machine brochures + Georgia office contact — page **$638**, video ~**$1,500**. Blog module + online-games client area are built but unfilled (online paused); an incoming **SEO/blog manager** (unnamed) will populate the blog.
- **Roadmap.** Update the ECHO page in 1–2 weeks (home + amusement + a B2B message to recruit distributors); then a full **Flex** page (no Flex prices yet; aim to ship something by end-October — a customizer/quote generator without a Unity scene, but reuse the AR "place-in-interior" feature for Flex). A **FlexPay** ("transactional") page once its product info is complete. All slot models are **Flex** (D and V versions; a possible cranked variant to be added as a new model when it appears).
- **Influencer tooling (parked).** Sourcing influencers manually takes 2–4 hours per batch; platforms optimise it. Best tool: **Abfluence** (Andrei first said "U-Platform," corrected to Abfluence) — a database refreshed every ~4 days with engagement/fake-follower metrics, searchable by brand. A **48-hour trial** was granted; Valeria is checking its accuracy. Subscription negotiated to **$660/month**. Decision: **too early to subscribe** at current volume; revisit when JVL runs 10–15 collaborations/month (sensible once the monthly influencer budget approaches ~$10k vs today's $2–2.5k).

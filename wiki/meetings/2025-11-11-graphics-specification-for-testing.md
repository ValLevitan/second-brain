# Graphics specification for testing (and Val's automated-game-generation vision) (Nov 11 2025)

**Date:** 2025-11-11 (5:20 PM recording header) · **Duration:** 37m 11s · **Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]], [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]]. Russian.

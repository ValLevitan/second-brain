# MRP and Sales Watch Demo (July 14 2026)

**Date:** July 14, 2026, 1:03 PM ET (~1 h 5 min)
**Attendees:** [[wiki/people/nathan-carver.md|Nathan Carver]], [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/lev-rudky.md|Lev Rudky]], [[wiki/people/mikhail-perelman.md|Mikhail Perelman]].

Nathan demoed two Business-Central tools — **Sales Watch** (fast sales-number comparisons) and the **MRP** purchasing/planning tool — after moving the calculations **into code so the AI agent only orchestrates rather than computes** (faster, more accurate responses). Val cut the sales portion short to focus on MRP; the room then worked through the MRP output columns (stale-PO 180-day indicator, on-order vs order-quantity), the save-and-resume-work-in-progress requirement, the empty-default adjusted-quantity column, the refresh-against-changed-BC-data problem, listing all parts not only shortages, and bill-of-materials traversal guardrails.

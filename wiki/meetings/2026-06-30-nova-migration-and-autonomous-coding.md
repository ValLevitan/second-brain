# Nova Migration and Autonomous Coding

**June 30, 2026, 12:33 PM Eastern** (notes-only; ~1h33m). Attendees: [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]], [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel Frolov]], [[wiki/people/dmitri-chudinov.md|Dzmitry Chudzinau]], [[wiki/people/alexander-biratka.md|Alexander Biratko]], [[wiki/people/evgeny-kharetski.md|Evgeny Kharetski]], [[wiki/people/dmitri-kovalchuk.md|Dmitri Kovalchuk]], [[wiki/people/andrey-bosik.md|Andrei Bosik]], [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]].

Val called the team together — an hour after Pavel's [[wiki/meetings/2026-06-30-claude-code-centric-e2e-release-approach.md|11:02am E2E demo]] — to agree **how** to move JVL to autonomous coding (Claude Code), automated testing, and CI/CD, and how to roll out the new JIRA hierarchy and the NOVA/Galaxy platforms **without freezing product releases**. Many separate experiments had all come back positive; Val's question was the transition path and what to push next.

Substance lives on:
- [[wiki/topics/nova-platform-architecture.md]] (Jun-30) — the migration decision (no release freeze; two feasibility spikes), RAID Studio kept for South Dakota vs Georgia releases targeting NOVA, and the NOVA/Galaxy platform taxonomy.
- [[wiki/topics/ai-assisted-development.md]] (Jun-30) — the Capability→Feature→ADR→Claude workflow, informal ADRs (no formal architect), reverse-engineering Requirements from code, and dev-environment independence.
- [[wiki/topics/github-release-management-pipeline.md]] (Jun-30) — the JIRA-hierarchy standard adopted (in Kanban), the CI/CD standard in progress, and the "every feature ties to a Capability" go-ahead.

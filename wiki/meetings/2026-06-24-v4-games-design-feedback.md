# V4 Games Design Feedback (June 24 2026)

**Date:** June 24, 2026, 5:12 PM ET (notes-only, ~4 min)
**Attendees:** [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val Levitan]], [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda]] (Giorgi)

Val asked Felipe for his impressions of the **V4 software** (the Georgia software version), setting aside known bugs. Felipe liked the new look — the game display and main-screen design — and praised the Fiery Ice Link progressive; he raised two concerns (both previously reported to Juan): missing idle attraction videos, and a repetitive center-reel nudge across the 25- and 50-line games.

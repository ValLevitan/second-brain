# FlexPay TITO scope-gap meeting (Mar 28)

A mostly-Russian scope/gap meeting to define the business requirement and use cases for making **FlexPay issue and validate TITO (ticket-in/ticket-out) tickets** at locations that have **no JVL machines**, and to agree an MVP technical approach. Attendees: [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] (the "least technical" one, driving the use-case questions), [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel]] (Pasha — designs the SAS-server/proxy), [[wiki/people/dmitri-chudinov.md|Dima Chudinov]] (backend), and [[wiki/people/eugine-yavorski.md|Eugine Yavorski]] (Evgeny — to implement the WSS protocol); [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi]] attended as optional "moral support."

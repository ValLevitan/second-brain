# Minsk staff roster

Canonical proper names of the JVL **Minsk** payroll, absorbed per owner approval (2026-07-13) from the Minsk salary registers. **Names only** — no compensation figures are recorded here or in `raw/` (§15.8). This roster exists to anchor entity resolution: many transcript "Dima / Zhenya / Anton / Igor" mentions resolve to the full names below. [[raw/minsk_staff_roster_2025_names-only.md]]

~26 people are on these registers. This is one payroll set and does **not** include the graphics team (Lena Zhukova, Yulia, Anastasia, the Khandovs), the hardware lead (Genadi Peari), the RGS author (Efim Podvoiski), the game-design VP (Michael Gottlieb), procurement (Mikhail Perelman), the online-commercial lead (Andrei Klimovich), or Canada-based staff (Eugine Yavorski) — those appear elsewhere and are tracked on their own pages.

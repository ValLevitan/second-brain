## May 27 2025 — JVL Labs Georgia state-tax review (2023/2024) with US advisors

The "Georgia tax filings" meeting the external accountants had asked for (flagged on the May-7 call). US tax advisors **Mark** ([[wiki/people/mark-tax-advisor.md]]) and **Ashley** ([[wiki/people/ashley-tax-advisor.md]]) reviewed JVL Labs' Georgia exposure with [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]], [[wiki/people/inna-levitan.md|Inna]] ("Ina") and [[wiki/people/alka-sood.md|Alka]] ("Elka"). ([[wiki/meetings/2025-05-27-georgia-tax-review-jvl-labs.md]]) [[raw/finance_tax_review_for_2023_2024-on_may-27-2025.md]]

- **Why JVL Labs (a Canadian company) owes Georgia tax at all.** JVL Labs files a **treaty-based US federal return** that just discloses wages — no US federal income tax, "it's all in Canada per the treaty." But Georgia uses **market-based income sourcing**: because JVL Labs sells to Georgia-based [[wiki/organizations/jvl-systems.md|JVL Systems]] (its subsidiary, which already files in Georgia), those sales create **nexus** in Georgia even though JVL Labs has no physical presence there. Georgia then **apportions** JVL Labs' federal net income by the fraction of revenue sourced to Georgia and taxes that portion. [[raw/finance_tax_review_for_2023_2024-on_may-27-2025.md]]
- **Voluntary Disclosure (VDA):** covers a **three-year** lookback; **2022 had zero Georgia sales** (no impact), so real exposure is only 2023 and 2024. A VDA **waives late penalties** but **not interest**. Nothing had been filed for Georgia in prior years (JVL Systems files there; JVL Labs never had). The prior-year question had been raised with "Daniel," who then "went missing," so it was never resolved. [[raw/finance_tax_review_for_2023_2024-on_may-27-2025.md]]
- **The numbers (corporate tax economics).** JVL Labs' net income was ~**$3.4M / CAD 3.776M** for 2023 (≈ **USD 3.438M**) and ~**USD 2.377–2.547M** for 2024 — roughly **$3.3M/year CAD**. About **90%** of last year's income was attributable to Georgia. Mark's back-of-the-envelope Georgia tax: an effective rate of ~**5.19%** giving ~**$160k for 2023**, and a "similar, same amount" for 2024 — "a significant tax… because we haven't paid taxes yet for a long time." [[raw/finance_tax_review_for_2023_2024-on_may-27-2025.md]]
- **Why Georgia and not other states.** Georgia's corporate rate (~**7–8%**) is lower than **Florida's ~9%** (Florida excludes the first $50k of corporate income but has no personal income tax), and JVL Systems is already in Georgia — so Georgia is the favorable place to regularize. For a trivial exposure in another state (e.g. $2,000 of Illinois income) Mark would **not** recommend filing (compliance cost > tax). [[raw/finance_tax_review_for_2023_2024-on_may-27-2025.md]]
- **The offset — foreign tax credit.** Any Georgia tax paid becomes a **foreign tax credit** in Canada, "pretty close to a dollar-for-dollar credit" per Mark (who defers Canadian advice to [[wiki/people/sean-riley.md|Sean Riley]]). ## June 3 2025 — Georgia PL 86-272 exemption path (Mark + Sean)

A third Georgia-tax call ([[wiki/meetings/2025-06-03-finance-georgia-taxation.md]]) with [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]], chartered accountant [[wiki/people/sean-riley.md|Sean Riley]] and US advisor [[wiki/people/mark-tax-advisor.md|Mark]] — the follow-up to the [[#may-27-2025--jvl-labs-georgia-state-tax-review-20232024-with-us-advisors|May-27]]/[[#may-30-2025--georgia-foreign-tax-credit-call-with-sean-riley-the-canadian-side|May-30]] reviews. It **materially refines** the earlier "significant tax owed" expectation: with the right structure, JVL Labs may owe **no Georgia income tax at all**. [[raw/finance_georgia_taxation-june-3-2025.md]]

- **Public Law 86-272 is the exemption.** Mark's key teaching: if JVL Labs ships a **tangible good via a common carrier with no services attached**, PL 86-272 bars a state from charging income tax on it. Georgia's market-based sourcing still creates a **disclosure/reporting** requirement (JVL Labs must file), plus possibly a small **net-worth tax** (~$1,000, maybe covered by a minimum) — but **no income tax**. (Contrast: Texas's gross-receipts tax is **not** exempt under PL 86-272; California has an $800 minimum.) This refines the May-27 "~$160k/year Georgia tax" and May-30 "~$225k combined" framing — evolution, not contradiction: the earlier figures assumed the exemption did **not** apply. [[raw/finance_georgia_taxation-june-3-2025.md]]
- **What broke the exemption: management fees.** The reason JVL had exposure is that past years booked a **management fee** from JVL Systems to JVL Labs (~**$500k** in 2023, essentially to zero out Systems' ~$500k profit) plus **~$53k of "other revenues"** on Labs (Sean suspected tax credits). A service fee attached to the product **invalidates PL 86-272**. Fix: **stop doing management fees** and instead **raise the transfer price** JVL Labs charges JVL Systems for the tangible goods, so Systems isn't reselling at a big profit — "add to the sales price, remove the service." The Canadian government "will love it even more" (more income in Canada, where losses shelter it); the IRS is fine because Labs files a treaty-based federal return. [[raw/finance_georgia_taxation-june-3-2025.md]]
- **Machines ship without software.** A load-bearing fact for the exemption: machines are **shipped without software**, and the **software is applied by the Florida entity** — so from Canada it is purely a tangible good moving via common carrier (no service performed in Georgia). [[raw/finance_georgia_taxation-june-3-2025.md]]
- **Entity geography clarified.** JVL Systems is a **US entity now based in Florida** (moved from **Delaware ~6 months ago**), with warehouse/physical presence in **both Georgia and Florida**, growing into **South Dakota and Nebraska**. The **Peru/Mexico/Colombia** offices sit in the **Canadian** entity, not the US one. Florida is a **cost-of-performance** state (~5.3%, first $50k excluded); Georgia is **market-based** (~5.5%). A third-party/logistics warehouse (not a JVL Systems warehouse) was floated to avoid Georgia nexus, but a third-party warehouse still gives nexus in most states. [[raw/finance_georgia_taxation-june-3-2025.md]]
- **The game plan + undertaking.** Get away from management fees, fix the Labs↔Systems pricing, then Labs owes no Georgia income tax (disclosure filing only); prior years may need **amended federal + Pennsylvania** returns (Pennsylvania exempt under PL, so reporting-only), with any late-filing penalty small (a few hundred dollars). **Undertaking:** Sean to walk Alka through the plan and supervise the alignment of the figures. Val: "tax optimization, not tax evasion." No §15.8 material — corporate/tax economics only. [[raw/finance_georgia_taxation-june-3-2025.md]] See the transfer-pricing tension note on [[wiki/topics/tariffs-and-customs-compliance.md]].

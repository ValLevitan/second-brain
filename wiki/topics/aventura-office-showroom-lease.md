# Aventura office / showroom lease ("White Tower")

A lease [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] is negotiating for a **JVL office + slot-machine showroom** in a mixed-use project in **Aventura, Florida** (South Florida, the Miami area). This is **not** the Georgia/Louisville manufacturing warehouse (a separate track — see [[wiki/decisions/2025-06-24-move-manufacturing-from-canada-to-georgia.md]]); it is a small ground-floor office suite that doubles as a showroom for up to **15 slot machines** on display. As of the July-8 2025 review the lease was still in negotiation, not signed. [[raw/white_tower_lease_review-july-8-2025.md]]

## The space and the deal

- **~2,294 sq ft**, ground floor (right off the lobby, "the easiest one to access for your visitors"), in a project the brokers say they have leased for **five-plus years**. The project includes a **"new tower"** with shared amenities — a multi-purpose meeting room, a pool and a gym (visitors can't use the pool/gym; liability). [[raw/white_tower_lease_review-july-8-2025.md]]
- **Use:** an office plus a **showroom for up to 15 slot machines**, plugged in and operational "for show purposes only" (not a casino). The permitted-use clause is being expanded to allow this explicitly. The 15 machines draw ~**3 amps each** (peak), so the electrician must split them across several breakers (~5 machines/breaker on 15-amp breakers). [[raw/white_tower_lease_review-july-8-2025.md]]
- **Val's current office is a sublease at One Turnberry**; this would be a direct deal with a landlord. [[raw/white_tower_lease_review-july-8-2025.md]]
- **Landlord profile:** owns buildings in **Mexico** with similar (gaming-industry) tenants, so it "knows the business very well"; described as excited about the deal and providing a **below-market rate** — brokers call it the **cheapest new deal in the project in five/six years**. [[raw/white_tower_lease_review-july-8-2025.md]]
- **Brokers:** **Jack** (lead) and **Juan** (a broker — **distinct from [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan Martinez]]**, JVL's field lead; recorded as a name collision, not the same person). The landlord's attorney redlined the lease off-call. [[raw/white_tower_lease_review-july-8-2025.md]]

## Economics (lease terms — §15.8 business economics, allowed)

- **Base rent: $49/sq ft × 2,294 sq ft**; a small error in the draft was corrected to **$9,367** (the transcript's corrected figure). [[raw/white_tower_lease_review-july-8-2025.md]]
- **Security deposit: 2 months** (reduced from more; described as the South-Florida minimum for an excellent-credit tenant). Val argued insurance already covers damage; the landlord held that the deposit protects the landlord specifically. [[raw/white_tower_lease_review-july-8-2025.md]]
- **Parking:** start with **4 spaces**, flexibility to **6**. [[raw/white_tower_lease_review-july-8-2025.md]]
- **Operating-expense cap 5%**; **proportionate share 2%** (the suite's sq ft ÷ the building's); **default interest 12% max**; ~**4-year** term. The landlord will supply 2023/2024 actual operating expenses and an estimated 2025 figure. Pandemic-expense language is being struck. [[raw/white_tower_lease_review-july-8-2025.md]]
- **Total rent ≈ $600,000** over the lease — the figure Val cited as what "aches" him to pay a landlord (see the buy-vs-lease frame below). [[raw/white_tower_lease_review-july-8-2025.md]]
- **Build-out / timing:** the GC will do the interior work **without a permit** (no construction drawings/CDs, which would add 2–2.5 months) — built "per code" (sprinklers, HVAC exhaust, outlets). Work takes **4–6 weeks** after lease signing. Target: sign by ~**July 15**, deliver by ~**November 15 2025**; the landlord offered a day of rent abatement per day of delay. [[raw/white_tower_lease_review-july-8-2025.md]]

## Val's durable negotiating positions

These recur as Val's standing posture on any lease (he said he uses them "everywhere," including Canada/Europe leases):

- **No indemnification of the landlord.** Val will not indemnify the landlord for anything — because his **gaming licenses require disclosing any litigation** ("are you currently in court… declared bankruptcy… any of your directors been sued?"), and being pulled into court for the landlord's disputes jeopardizes license applications "countrywide." He will accept being sued for his own default ("if I break the law, take me to court") but not indemnify. Brokers said they'd never seen this comment before and would have their attorney explain the clause. [[raw/white_tower_lease_review-july-8-2025.md]]
- **Arbitration, not court.** Val wants a fast arbitration path and to "short-fuse" any litigation — framed as *giving the landlord an automatic win* to avoid years of court. [[raw/white_tower_lease_review-july-8-2025.md]]
- **Recording-consent by notice.** Florida is a **two-party-consent** state; Val will post a sign that entering the premises / phoning consents to recording, so recordings are usable to settle disputes quickly (contrasting Ontario/most-US one-party consent). [[raw/white_tower_lease_review-july-8-2025.md]]
- **A hard delivery deadline with a cancellation right** — if the landlord doesn't deliver the space by a set date, Val can cancel ("pick an end… my life needs to be restored at some point"). [[raw/white_tower_lease_review-july-8-2025.md]]
- **Recapture only on sublease** (Val confirmed he has zero interest in subleasing, so he's protected); **corporate change = notify, not approve** (landlord can't block a change of ownership if the lease is in good standing); **JVL's own cleaning staff allowed** on top of building janitorial; kitchen to be upscaled; terrace plantings; **relocation rights kept** but only if the landlord pays all costs and provides a better space. [[raw/white_tower_lease_review-july-8-2025.md]]

## Strategic frame — Val would rather buy than lease

Val told the brokers his "heart aches" at paying a landlord ~**$600,000** over the lease: "I would much rather, just like I did right now with Georgia — in Georgia I bought a building — buy a building in Florida and move my company and pay the $600,000 to myself." He has no trouble raising money for a building but **can't find one to buy** (the brokers agreed good buildings aren't available and offered to help him find one). This ties the lease to the broader US-move / buy-real-estate thread — the Austin building already owned, the **Atlanta-area industrial building** under option, and the Miami building search (see [[wiki/organizations/rbc.md]] July-9 and [[wiki/organizations/cibc.md]]). [[raw/white_tower_lease_review-july-8-2025.md]]

# SR&ED Tax Credit — JVL's Canadian R&D claim

How JVL claims the **SR&ED** credit — Canada's federal **Scientific Research & Experimental Development** program, a refundable R&D tax credit — and how the annual claim is put together. The team colloquially calls the refunds "shreds." This is a **different** credit from the [[wiki/topics/ontario-media-tax-credit.md|Ontario Media Tax Credit]] JVL also claims on game-production labour; the two are not conflated. [[raw/SREDs_progress_review-March-20-2026.md]]

## What the credit is worth to JVL (a recurring cash inflow)

- SR&ED produces real **cash refunds** for JVL. In the year-end 2023/2024 finance review, Val cited a recent **~$267,000 CRA refund plus interest** (that [[wiki/people/hector.md|Hector]] had emailed about) as his example of a tax credit that "comes back" as actual cash — contrasting it with the foreign-tax-credit question he wanted [[wiki/people/sean-riley.md|Sean Riley]] to confirm. Val framed the whole posture as "tax optimization, not tax evasion… moving money from one bucket to the next and it comes back." [[raw/finance_tax_review_for_2023_2024-on_may-27-2025.md]]
- The cash-forecast model to end-2026 books SR&ED as a modelled inflow of **$150,000** (labelled "shreds"), alongside a separate **$600,000** media tax credit. [[raw/cash_forecast_till_2027-september-16-2025.md]]
- The **GST/SR&ED filings** are part of [[wiki/people/alka-sood.md|Alka Sood]]'s finance workload, tracked for their cash-flow impact. [[raw/finance_update_alka-july-3-2025.md]]
- **Location-independent.** When JVL decided to move manufacturing from Canada to Georgia, it was explicit that the **SR&ED / media tax credit has "absolutely zero influence" from where manufacturing physically sits** — so the credits did not tie JVL to Canada. *(The SR&ED claim is on R&D labour, which is performed by the development team regardless of where machines are assembled — inference from the source's framing.)* [[raw/Alignment_procurement_manufacturing_transfer-june-26-2025.md]]

## How the claim is prepared (Mar-20 2026 working session)

On the [[wiki/meetings/2026-03-20-sred-progress-review.md|Mar-20 2026 SR&ED progress review]] ([[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] with the R&D leads [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi Peari]] (hardware), [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim Podvoiski]] (RGS) and [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena Zhukova]] (design)) the team began assembling the **2025** claim. [[raw/SREDs_progress_review-March-20-2026.md]]

- **Method — list first, allocate second.** Val's "black and white" approach: first **write down every project** JVL worked on, then **distribute the R&D labour across those projects** and decide what to claim. [[raw/SREDs_progress_review-March-20-2026.md]]
- **Attribute labour to eligible projects.** The eligibility work is deciding which people's time attaches to which R&D project. Genadi said he already had his list and did not need the maximum spread "because I don't have many people to attach." *(Attribution is of labour/hours to projects; no individual pay figures are recorded — §15.8.)* [[raw/SREDs_progress_review-March-20-2026.md]]
- **What to defer to next year.** Genadi flagged a couple of **2025** items with little work actually done — a platform effort, and some year-end activity around a "RockChip"-type chip — that are probably **better added to the 2026 claim** where the main activity will fall, rather than forced into 2025. [[raw/SREDs_progress_review-March-20-2026.md]]
- **Next step.** Val took the bulk of the write-up on himself, aiming to have the project list drafted **by Wednesday**, when the group reconvenes (~15–30 min) to advance it and set a work schedule for the remaining steps. [[raw/SREDs_progress_review-March-20-2026.md]]
- **Careful wording (Val's recurring discipline).** As with the media-tax-credit coaching, Val stressed that he **deliberately uses precise, correct wording** on these claims and asked the team to use the same word choice. [[raw/SREDs_progress_review-March-20-2026.md]]

## Mar-31 2026 claim-review session — ownership assignments + new claims

The [[wiki/meetings/2026-03-31-sred-claim-review.md|Mar-31 2026 claim-review session]] ([[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] with [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi Peari]], [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim Podvoiski]] and [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena Zhukova]]) worked the "list first, allocate second" method (above) into a consolidated candidate list. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_SREDs_claim_review-March-31-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_SREDs_claim_review-March-31-2026.md]]

- **From 22 candidates to a 14-claim V1.7 list.** Of Val's original **22** candidate projects (the "V1.6" list), **10 were kept with an owner assigned** and **12 left "undecided"** — some flagged as possibly not SR&ED-eligible (e.g. a consolation/pattern-match game). **Four new claims** outside the original 22 were added — **3 from Genadi, 1 from Efim** — giving a **14-claim V1.7** consolidated list. Efim undertook to restructure the list using Claude (drop the 12 undecided, keep the 10, add the 4). [[raw/Meeting_Notes_SREDs_claim_review-March-31-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_SREDs_claim_review-March-31-2026.md]]
- **Ownership = attribution of R&D labour to claims (not pay — §15.8).** Assigning a claim to a person means whose R&D labour attaches to that project and who writes the narrative. Assignments recorded: the Flex-Port / Flex-POS / payment-hub / firmware claims to the contractors [[wiki/people/eugine-yavorski.md|Evgeny Yavorski]] and [[wiki/people/evgeniy-alekseev.md|Evgeny Alekseev]] (with Genadi on Flex-Port hardware); the Keno math engine and the volatility-modeling claim to [[wiki/people/efim-podvoiski.md|Efim]]; the manifest asset-reconstruction and game-art claims to [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena]] with [[wiki/people/andrew-khandov.md|Andrei Handov]] and [[wiki/people/dmitriy-khandov.md|Dimo Handov]]. No individual salary figures appear or were ingested. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_SREDs_claim_review-March-31-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_SREDs_claim_review-March-31-2026.md]]
- **Genadi's 3 new claims — Flex S hardware R&D.** (1) **Flex S concept-evolution design** — redesigning the monitors and cabinet for a modern look, improved audio, and configurability with more/new graphical elements; the difficulty was combining all the features into one cohesive product. (2) **43-inch curved monitor for Flex S** — a "smart plastic" design with lights around the monitor to *visually* increase the perceived curvature, avoiding expensive chemically-aged curved LCD panels; difficulties in the plastic-and-lighting design, using a curved TFT alternative, and a curved touch screen. (3) **New audio system for Flex S** — low frequencies **without a large subwoofer**, with sound bled under the monitors so there are **no visible speaker grills**; work included acoustic simulations, prototypes, and specially-developed speakers, freeing cabinet space for other devices. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_SREDs_claim_review-March-31-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_SREDs_claim_review-March-31-2026.md]]
- **Efim's new claim — a volatility & financial-risk modeling framework.** For slot-based casino games, replacing per-spin Monte-Carlo simulation (milliseconds/spin × millions of spins) with a **distribution-based stochastic model**: sample 100M+ spins once to build an empirical outcome distribution, then draw from that distribution for session/day simulation — several orders of magnitude faster. It adds "inverse" modeling: defining a **target outcome distribution before the game design is finalized**. Motivated by a Florida operator whose concern was **how often the casino loses money in a day** — a heavy-tailed / skewed reality that standard-deviation metrics don't capture. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_SREDs_claim_review-March-31-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_SREDs_claim_review-March-31-2026.md]]
- **Write-up method.** Val will generate the SR&ED-format claim narratives from the meeting transcript plus Claude — explicitly using Genadi's spoken descriptions (captured because the meeting was transcribed) rather than requiring each engineer to write their own. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_SREDs_claim_review-March-31-2026.md]]

## Sept 7 2026 — the whole credit is at risk if JVL Labs stops being a CCPC

On the [[wiki/meetings/2026-09-07-departure-tax-trust-and-bad-debt.md|Sept-7 2026 call]] with cross-border tax lawyer [[wiki/people/vitaly-timokhov.md|Vitaly Timokhov]], the SR&ED credit turned from a preparation exercise into an existential one. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]

- **The trigger is corporate status, not the claims.** [[wiki/organizations/jvl-labs.md|JVL Labs]] must remain a **CCPC** (Canadian-Controlled Private Corporation). [[wiki/people/inna-levitan.md|Inna Levitan]] is Labs' sole director and 91% shareholder; if control of Labs passes outside Canada, **Labs ceases to be a CCPC**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]
- **What non-CCPC status costs.** Vitaly: "the provincial portion of [SR&ED] is not available, and the federal portion is available only to the extent to offset the profits, **which you don't have**." In other words the refundable cash benefit — the thing this page is about — largely disappears, because the remaining federal credit is non-refundable and JVL has no profits to apply it against. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]
- **The number he is working from — and the caveat on it.** Vitaly used "**about $1,000,000 worth of tax credits back… it's a major cash flow**," and later said losing it would be "quite disastrous." He was explicit that the figure came from **what Val had told him** and that he had **not looked at the tax returns**. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]
- **It does not obviously square with the figures above** — a **$150,000** modelled SR&ED inflow in the cash forecast [[raw/cash_forecast_till_2027-september-16-2025.md]] and a separate **$600,000** media tax credit, or the **~$267,000** CRA refund Val cited in 2025 [[raw/finance_tax_review_for_2023_2024-on_may-27-2025.md]]. Whether ~$1M is SR&ED alone, SR&ED plus the [[wiki/topics/ontario-media-tax-credit.md|Ontario media credit]], or a different basis entirely is **not stated in any source** — recorded as an open question, not resolved. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]
- **The proposed fix is blocked for now.** Vitaly's answer — a Canadian-resident trust holding the Labs shares — is on hold pending a Tax Court ruling on the General Anti-Avoidance Rule: [[wiki/decisions/2026-09-07-jvl-labs-ccpc-trust-held-pending-the-gaar-ruling.md]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]

*(Worth reading against the June-2025 ruling already on this page that the credits are **location-independent** and so did not tie JVL to Canada. That was about where machines are **manufactured**. This is about who **controls the corporation** — a different lever, and one that does bite.)* [[raw/Alignment_procurement_manufacturing_transfer-june-26-2025.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Departure_Tax_Trust_and_Bad_Debt-September-7-2026.md]]

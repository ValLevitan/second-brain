# FlexPay mobile app and service advocates

A JVL discussion (Jan 13 2025) about a planned FlexPay mobile app and a new user type — the "service advocate" — that the app should serve. The call was framed as building a persona profile so the product can target this role.

**Earlier origin (Nov 24 2024).** An older source (staged 2025-03-29) shows this app track began earlier: [[wiki/people/andrei-klimovich.md|Andrei Klimovich]] had **selected an external contractor** (Poland legal entity + Belarus dev team) for a **~$8,000 FlexPay discovery phase** (spec + mockups → a later fixed-price build). Whether that contractor was actually engaged or was later superseded by JVL's in-house work is unconfirmed — see [[wiki/decisions/2024-11-24-engage-flexpay-discovery-contractor.md]] and Owner Decisions. [[raw/andrei_klimovich_update_report_online_strategy1_2025-03-29.md]]

## What a service advocate is

- A service company (or an employee of one) hired to look after a master's machines across all brands: solve technical issues, source parts from manufacturers, and pass locations' needs and wants back to the master. [[raw/mobile_app_advocate_persona_profile_january_13_2025.md]]
- They don't make final purchase decisions, but they strongly influence what a master buys — advising what's "hot," what locations request, and which machines cause trouble. They can champion a machine or damage it (e.g. complaining about too many service calls or poor support from a manufacturer). [[raw/mobile_app_advocate_persona_profile_january_13_2025.md]]
- "What's hot" comes from what they hear at locations (e.g. "that machine is always full"), not from GFC reports. [[raw/mobile_app_advocate_persona_profile_january_13_2025.md]]

## Reporting and money flow

- Chain: a master hires a service company; the service company employs the advocates/techs who go to locations. Advocates report to the master via their operations manager. [[raw/mobile_app_advocate_persona_profile_january_13_2025.md]]
- Advocates generally do **not** buy parts under their own name or handle invoicing — JVL invoices the master directly. A rare exception: a company with its own LLC can be invoiced directly (e.g. "Diamond Services"), maybe one or two at most. There is no markup by advocates. [[raw/mobile_app_advocate_persona_profile_january_13_2025.md]]
- Example used in the call: "I got an invoice from Felipe for a CPU for $400" — [[wiki/people/felipe-miranda.md|Felipe Miranda]] is named on the JVL side that issues a parts invoice. The point was whether a service advocate could mark that up when charging the master (answer: no — advocates don't handle invoicing). Felipe's exact role/last name is not stated. [[raw/mobile_app_advocate_persona_profile_january_13_2025.md]]

## Product significance

- Service advocates were judged as important, or even more important, than masters for upselling the FlexPay mobile app and cross-selling generally. So the app is being designed with the service-advocate persona as a target user. [[raw/mobile_app_advocate_persona_profile_january_13_2025.md]]
- The speaker will draft/update the mobile document to include the service-advocate role and update "whale" (a new-user-profile tool or app, name unclear) with this persona. [[raw/mobile_app_advocate_persona_profile_january_13_2025.md]]
- No urgency: nobody will need a mobile app for a year or more; the work is subcontracted (no salaries), so JVL will move at a comfortable pace. South Dakota and Puerto Rico are the stated priorities instead. [[raw/mobile_app_advocate_persona_profile_january_13_2025.md]]

Unclear: "whale" (appears as "whale"/"wial") — a tool or profile system JVL uses; and whether the "Igor" it was "too early" to involve on the UI is the same Igor who handles Puerto Rico setup. Flagged.

## Note: FlexPay the payment product vs. the mobile app

The **manufacturing** of the FlexPay kiosks (tooling, cabinet material, assembly throughput, and the Georgia market-sizing) is covered separately on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-kiosk-production-and-tooling.md]] (Mar 27). [[raw/flex_pay_production-march-27-2025.md]]

The **FlexPay** payment product itself is confirmed progressing — the Jan 24 2025 call put the related **FlexPort** device's beta ~3 weeks out and described FlexPay validating tickets. That hardware is documented in [[wiki/topics/jvl-machine-lineup.md]]. However, the **mobile app** and the **service-advocate persona** that this page is about remain **single-source** (Jan 13) and stay FLAGGED until a second source confirms them. [[raw/flex_port_hardware_up-jan-24-2025.md]]

- **FlexPort ↔ FlexPay integration progressing (Feb 11):** the Feb 11 embedded standup advanced the FlexPort-module-to-FlexPay-kiosk connection (WebSocket, device registration before accepting money, PIN/ticket validation), with the payment processor **Incom** supplying the test server and a production contract awaiting Val's signature; **Felipe** ("Filippo") to host a multi-machine production test in his office. Details on [[wiki/topics/jvl-machine-lineup.md]] and [[wiki/meetings/2025-02-11-embedded-group-flexport-flexpay.md]]. This is the payment product/hardware, still not the mobile app. (Whether "Felipe"/"Filippo" is the same Felipe who issues JVL parts invoices is unclear — see Owner Decisions.) [[raw/Embedded_Group_alignment_and_update_2025-02-11.md]]

- **Embedded group progress (Feb 18):** the FlexPort↔FlexPay integration is being brought up on a real machine by [[wiki/people/eugine-yavorski.md|Evgeny]] ("Zhenya") for the first field locations. A "Dima" (software; not attributed — see Owner Decisions) is delivering a new FlexPay build that **bypasses the card dispenser**, and adding **server-side log hooks** so an internet-connected FlexPay's logs can be pulled directly for fast feedback. A gap was found: the protocol has **no command for FlexPort to signal it is in an error state** to FlexPay — to be added. The FlexPay **activation sequence** was documented: (0) switch the factory QR scanner from Serial/HID to CDC mode; (1) **registration** (scan a QR in setup → bind the device serial number in JVL's device-management system); (2) create a **license** on the server ("Лиза"/"license") and apply it via a QR round-trip (FlexPay need not be online); then fill the **Incom** card-provider fields (manually or via a QR). Val has an **Incom** contract in its final stage and must arrange a production-server test (the test environment worked; production couldn't connect). Timeline: at least another week; Val pushed for a two-week (~March 5) buffer. See [[wiki/meetings/2025-02-18-embedded-group-flexport-flexpay.md]] and [[wiki/topics/jvl-machine-lineup.md]]. [[raw/Embedded_Group_alignment_and_update_2025-02-18.md]]

- **Embedded group (Feb 25):** debugging continued. The FlexPort↔FlexPay **WebSocket connection drops even though [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel]]'s keepalive ping-pong runs every second** and is received — cause unknown; pings weren't appearing in the logs. Two durable outcomes: (1) Val ruled on the **abandoned-cash-out** case ([[wiki/people/eugine-yavorski.md|Evgeny]]'s question) — if a player cashes out and leaves without scanning a ticket / entering a PIN, cancel the ticket as **"not claimed,"** pull the credits off the machine, report it to FlexPay, and restore the money on paper via FlexPay during audit if the player returns; this needs a **new informational protocol command** for abnormal states (device failure, not-claimed funds). (2) Val directed that **detailed comms logging become a formal requirement** — an enable-able log mode like Bally/Konami AFT provide. Ship config: ~4 machines per FlexPay; 4 FlexPay + ~15 ports; FlexPort installed in JVL's own machines too (Val's uniformity requirement), working across all software including old versions. Target: config + working protocol by Thursday. See [[wiki/meetings/2025-02-25-embedded-group-flexport-flexpay.md]]. [[raw/Embedded_Group_alignment_and_update_2025-02-25.md]]

- **Embedded group (Mar 4):** [[wiki/people/eugine-yavorski.md|Evgeny]] ("Женя") reported the FlexPort↔FlexPay data exchange working at a **minimal level** — data flowing both ways. He traced the earlier disconnect problem to needing to **ping with text messages**. He can read a **barcode ticket** and the ping-code verifies; still needs a validator to confirm credits are transferred onto the gaming machine. He is adding a **watchdog** (to auto-restart on hangs) and a **log file**, and the group agreed on **event-based state reporting** to FlexPay after registration — report "all clear," "in error," or "recovery needed," and specifically flag **unclaimed money** (the abandoned-cash-out case) — rather than periodic polling. The **firmware ("repair") update** protocol is not yet started; the group decided machines can ship for experiments **without** it because units can update from a **flash drive**. A **magnetic-card-reader** request was raised (Val heard "all the pays want it"): deferred — not this release, no task set. Open sub-question: support only the **Incom** card protocol (relatively easy) or **any** payment card (needs a full payment-terminal/kiosk tied to a money-handling system); needs an **investigation** into whether an Incom API can debit part of a card's balance. [[raw/Embedded_Group_alignment_and_update_2025-03-04.md]]
- **Embedded group (Mar 11):** Evgeny reported the integration **nearly feature-complete**, finishing the last stage — **cash-out** (credit withdrawal from the EGM to FlexPay); all protocol commands worked out but "very raw." **Firmware update** still unimplemented (~1 week); currently over serial port, but a **USB-stick** update already works and is preferred. Testing is blocked on getting a **bill acceptor** that can be wired in — JVL's bill acceptors (GCM / "High Vision") use different cables than the standard, so [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi]] is to supply the pinout to re-solder a cable; alternatively a **stub/plug** lets FlexPort **emulate** the bill acceptor (ticket-only), making the bill acceptor **non-mandatory**. Plan for the **log file**: FlexPort logs the whole FlexPay↔FlexPort protocol exchange; pull it **once a day** via a new protocol command, with an option to dump to a **USB stick** if the network fails (encryption deemed unnecessary — the log is just numbers, read-only). Code merge: the integration dev is merging his branch into the **main branch owned by [[wiki/people/evgeniy-alekseev.md|Zhenya Alekseev]]** (commit ~175); the **watchdog** still isn't firing in the main branch. Next-day plan: bring FlexPorts to the **Toronto office** to run **two machines on one bus/station** together before shipping — the Toronto hardware is tested (one JVL machine + one older machine); a **machine emulator** on a laptop can stand in for a real machine. [[raw/Embedded_Group_alignment_and_update_2025-03-11.md]]

- **Embedded group (Mar 17 — one meeting, two transcripts):** [[wiki/people/eugine-yavorski.md|Eugine Yavorski]] (reporting "Евгений"/Zhenya) reported one game machine passing almost all functionality **except cash-out** (which requires intercepting the signal from the machine's electromechanical meters and is new work), with **FlexPay working** at both workstations and a **new printer** installed and printing tickets. Software versions were clarified: **version 3** runs with a "**заглушка**" (stub) — **no machine and no validator**; **version 4** runs fully as intended (machine connected to a validator + bill acceptor); and a new build that runs **without a validator** (FlexPort **emulates** the bill-acceptor responses) was promised for **Thursday** to Minsk — confirming FlexPorts can be installed on machines with or without a bill acceptor, taking money via tickets. Minsk is blocked on having **no UBA bill acceptor** and couldn't get a "cash-code" working (sees a signal out of the machine but no FlexPort output). The code (**bootloader, root/prod-loader, Python project** + docs) is being placed on JVL's **GitHub** this week (master branch; a stray "main" noted). Two design points: (1) **dual-language** — after a session the language must **return to a default set in Setup / Remote Initial Setup** (English default; Spanish in Peru), and FlexPort should take its default from **FlexPay** (which has a language concept); language is not yet handled (deferred post-MVP as the product was built for Georgia first). (2) The **two-phase transaction protocol** — money transfer in both directions must be **request + confirmation** (reject/commit); FlexPay **freezes** funds awaiting confirmation and releases them if none comes; the risk case is a machine **rejecting** a ticket-funded credit after FlexPay already debited, needing a refund. It currently runs in **one phase** and must be reworked to two; Yavorski to verify whether money is debited at phase one. Cash-out work needs cooperation with **[[wiki/people/evgeniy-alekseev.md|Evgeniy Alekseev]]**. FlexPort also intermittently **hangs in the "registering" state**; reliability (cable-pull, watchdog) and **download-over-Ethernet** (shippable without it) are the week's remaining work; the team tests **against written requirements** in a Minsk test environment. Val stressed the **MVP window (6 months) is long past** and took full responsibility for an earlier date error (he had feared a June 2025 law). See [[wiki/meetings/2025-03-17-embedded-group-flexport-flexpay.md]]. [[raw/Flex-port-update-March-17-2025.md]] [[raw/Embedded_Group_alignment_and_update_2025-03-18.md]]

- **Embedded group (Mar 25):** the FlexPort↔FlexPay bring-up continued with two durable symptoms. (1) **Cash-out works only once** per ticket: on the setup [[wiki/people/eugine-yavorski.md|Yavorski]] runs, money is removed from the gaming machine but is **not re-credited to the ticket** on a second attempt (the machine debits, but FlexPay never sees it). (2) **FlexPort still hangs** — [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel]] suspects a **collision when two async messages** (e.g. a ping and a new command like a barcode/ticket check) are sent at nearly the same time; his fix idea is to **queue outgoing messages in a pool** so they go one-by-one, and to hold one back if two arrive together. The prior "hangs during the night" freeze is reported **mostly fixed** — progress. A **QR-scanner motion-detection** quirk was raised by [[wiki/people/genadi-peari.md|Genadi]] (it only scans when it detects movement, sometimes going unresponsive after sitting idle). **Process:** with the April expo approaching, Val insisted on **live working meetings** (Yavorski now full-time, pairing daily with the back-end "Dima"/[[wiki/people/dmitri-chudinov.md|Chudinov]] on fresh logs) rather than only async chat. See [[wiki/meetings/2025-03-25-embedded-group-flexport-flexpay.md]]. [[raw/Embedded_Group_alignment_and_update_2025-03-25.md]]

- **TITO for non-JVL locations — scope gap (Mar 28):** a new requirement extends FlexPay beyond JVL's own machines: **issue and validate TITO tickets** at locations with **no JVL machines** (e.g. five non-JVL machines running on TITO). The MVP puts **FlexPort inside each machine as a SMIB** (SAS port ↔ CAT6/router ↔ FlexPay) and adds a **"Flex SAS server"** proxy split between FlexPort and FlexPay, converting **SAS ↔ the existing WSS/VCC protocol** with at least one new "issue ticket" command. Blocker on the cheaper adapter route: SAS needs **9-bit** serial and most Ethernet-to-RS232 adapters do only 8-bit. Legal limit reaffirmed: **only winnings** (not inserted "Money In") convert to a card, though on a TITO ticket all money through the machine is redeemable. [[wiki/people/pavel.md|Pavel]] designed it, [[wiki/people/dmitri-chudinov.md|Dima Chudinov]] to supply the SAS command list, [[wiki/people/eugine-yavorski.md|Eugine Yavorski]] to implement the WSS side (~2 weeks). Full detail on [[wiki/decisions/2025-03-28-flexpay-support-tito-non-jvl-locations.md]] and [[wiki/meetings/2025-03-28-flexpay-tito-scope-gap.md]]. [[raw/scope_gap_tito_flex_pay-march-28-2025.md]]

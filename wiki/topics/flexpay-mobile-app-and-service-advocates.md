# FlexPay mobile app & service advocates

Planning for JVL's FlexPay mobile app, and the "service advocate" as a user/audience for it.

## Service advocates — who they are

- A service advocate is a service company (or its employee) hired by a "master" to maintain many machines of all brands, source parts, fix technical issues, and relay what locations want back to the master [[raw/mobile_app_advocate_persona_profile_january_13_2025.md]].
- They don't make final purchase decisions but strongly influence them ("what's hot out there") — and can also hurt a brand if service is poor [[raw/mobile_app_advocate_persona_profile_january_13_2025.md]].
- On parts/billing: advocates almost never buy in their own name; JVL invoices the master directly (e.g. Premiere Games). A rare exception is an advocate with its own LLC (e.g. Diamond Services). No markup runs through them [[raw/mobile_app_advocate_persona_profile_january_13_2025.md]].

## Why they matter to the app

- Service advocates are seen as as-important-or-more in upselling FlexPay's mobile app to masters, so they are being treated as a distinct user profile in the app design [[raw/mobile_app_advocate_persona_profile_january_13_2025.md]].

## Pace

- Not urgent — no one will need the mobile app for a year or more; it's built by a subcontractor, so there's runway to move at a proper pace. South Dakota and Puerto Rico are the stated priorities instead [[raw/mobile_app_advocate_persona_profile_january_13_2025.md]].

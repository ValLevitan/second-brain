# Ontario Media Tax Credit — JVL's game-production claim

How JVL claims an Ontario **Media Tax Credit** on the labour that goes into producing its slot games, and the recurring examiner reviews that test whether each game qualifies. Built from the July-28 2025 team-coaching call where [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] briefed [[wiki/people/michael-gottlieb.md|Michael Gottlieb]] and [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena]] on how to answer an Ontario Creates examiner's questions. [[raw/media_tax_credit_team_coaching-july-28-2025.md]]

## What the credit is, and how JVL qualified

- The **Media Tax Credit** is a program "designed for companies in the media business," and by definition the content must have **an element of skill**. [[raw/media_tax_credit_team_coaching-july-28-2025.md]]
- JVL applied for the program for a number of years and at first had a **very difficult time qualifying** — it "failed to comply until it came to more or less arbitration." JVL contended it is an Ontario company whose games have an element of skill (a player *interaction*, however small). After that arbitration an **official release** made JVL a qualifiable, legitimate, **eligible producer of content**, and it has received the Media Tax Credit for many years since. [[raw/media_tax_credit_team_coaching-july-28-2025.md]]
- The credit is administered through **Ontario Creates**; the reviewing officer on the July-28 matter is an Ontario Creates examiner who is "on our side" but diligent and wants a clean report. He referred to eligibility "for the purposes of **OID**." *(Inference: "OID" is likely the Ontario Interactive Digital Media Tax Credit; the transcript says only "OID" — recorded verbatim, not expanded as fact.)* [[raw/media_tax_credit_team_coaching-july-28-2025.md]]

## What JVL claims, and the program economics

- JVL claims **labour** that goes into producing a game — "everything from A to Z": time for design, graphic-design, mathematician, and every aspect [[wiki/people/lena.md|Lena]] counts as production, plus the creative labour of creating content. [[raw/media_tax_credit_team_coaching-july-28-2025.md]]
- **Per-game economics (corporate program figures):** for each deployed game the **claim is $100,000**, and JVL receives **about 40% (~$40,000) per game**. Claims go strictly by **deployment** — JVL "can't claim something that has not been deployed" / is not visible; if no customer ever activated a feature, there is nothing to show and nothing to claim. [[raw/media_tax_credit_team_coaching-july-28-2025.md]]

## The "claim the labour twice" argument (same-branded games, different streams)

- A game like **Clover Fortune** can exist for **Mega Hit (MH1)** *and* for **Georgia Qualm / Georgia Peach** — same marketing/branding name, but **two entirely different games**: different math, different production, different creative. JVL therefore claims the production labour on each stream in parallel (Val's framing: not literally 100% twice — more like ~90% and ~70%, but the majority is claimed in repetition/parallel). [[raw/media_tax_credit_team_coaching-july-28-2025.md]]
- The branding name is marketing, not the game (Val's analogy: the same game sold as "JVL" in one jurisdiction and "ABC Electronics" in another). Simply being in a different jurisdiction does **not** by itself make two eligible products — the *production* must differ. [[raw/media_tax_credit_team_coaching-july-28-2025.md]]

## The July-28 examiner inquiry — two things to answer

The Ontario Creates officer was not satisfied with the earlier explanation (drafted in part by "Juliana") and asked JVL to be specific on two points: [[raw/media_tax_credit_team_coaching-july-28-2025.md]]

**(1) How the G-Comb (Georgia Peach) product differs from Mega Hit in production.** Michael's comparison (for the **2022** claim year): [[raw/media_tax_credit_team_coaching-july-28-2025.md]]
- Mega Hit uses **RNG-based math**; Georgia has **unique math for the Georgia market** (fundamentally different math always).
- Mega Hit shows the **pay table** on the second screen; Georgia shows the **game logo/graphics** on the second screen.
- Georgia has a **skill-based nudge mechanic** that does not exist in Mega Hit.
- Mega Hit often uses more complex **5×3** reel layouts; many Georgia games use a simpler **3×3** layout.
- Mega Hit audio is **aggressive/impactful for a casino**; Georgia audio is **compatible with bar/restaurant** environments.
- Mega Hit has additional **sign panels / large signage** and **multiple bolt-on progressives**; Georgia has a custom **Georgia Peach progressive** and support for a **mechanical button deck**.
- A game may share a name with the 3×3 layout version but is an **entirely different game**. (Note: by 2024 JVL made more Georgia-only custom content — e.g. **Liberty Spins** with no Class-3 equivalent — but the 2022 games under review "basically share names.")

**(2) Demonstrate the interactivity / skill element of the listed online games.** [[raw/media_tax_credit_team_coaching-july-28-2025.md]]

## The online-games interactivity gap (surfaced July 28)

- JVL applied for **13 online games** as having interactive features. On review, **three do not qualify** — **China Emperor, China Temple, Golden China** (the "China" series) — because their **Red Envelope** feature is passive (a preview of the prize), not real interaction. JVL "made a mistake" including them; the root cause was an intended redesign of the Red Envelope bonus to be touchable that ended up automatic. [[raw/media_tax_credit_team_coaching-july-28-2025.md]]
- The other online games do have their own interactive bonus: **Aztec Star, Lion's Reach, Midnight Money, Dragon Dreams, Keops Wild, Battle of the Pool** (pick-a-symbol / free-spin-scenario bonuses), **Winter Princess** (PPO/PQ Fortune interaction), and **Mythic Tiger** and **Three Stars** (the **Golden Coin / Jackpot Link** bonus, where the player touches the screen to reveal each new golden coin). The examiner still questioned Mythic Tiger and Three Stars; Michael was to reword the interactivity description and resend the video link. [[raw/media_tax_credit_team_coaching-july-28-2025.md]]
- **The larger discovery (a surprise to Val):** the special **golden-frame interactive bonus** JVL builds into land-based Mega Hit games was **never implemented in the online games** — it was designed and its math was done, but no customer ever enabled it, so it was never deployed. Val reacted strongly ("this is a complete surprise… a black mark on us… a decision made bypassing me"). Confirmed with [[wiki/people/irina-shishko.md|Irina Shishko]] and with a former team member ("Valentin," no longer at JVL). Because claims go by deployment, the undeployed bonus cannot be claimed — so games applied for on the strength of that feature were at risk. [[raw/media_tax_credit_team_coaching-july-28-2025.md]]

## Val's coaching principles for the examiner response

- **No lies, no bullshit, no invention** — state clearly and truthfully what is involved in each production; the goal is to explain interactivity (not to "prove skill"), and the appraiser wants to give the money to Canadian companies. [[raw/media_tax_credit_team_coaching-july-28-2025.md]]
- **Stay out of the jargon "rabbit hole."** Michael's point (agreed by Val): US-style examiners are unfamiliar with slot machines, so avoid terms like "reel stops" or "volatility" that trigger more questions — write for the layman ("this audio is more impactful, this audio is more compatible"). "Sometimes less is more." [[raw/media_tax_credit_team_coaching-july-28-2025.md]]
- **Submit in writing** to avoid triggering a call. Michael was tasked to put both answers (product differences + online interactivity) plus the video links into **one concise document** (~within the hour) that Val can submit directly. [[raw/media_tax_credit_team_coaching-july-28-2025.md]]

## Forward-looking rule for game design

- Every game JVL designs **needs an interactive feature** — otherwise it loses the ~$40,000/game claim. The upcoming **Fiery Ice stream / Omni Coyote** games do not need the special golden-frame bonus added, because they already carry interaction (the **PQ Fortune** bonus, and the interaction inside **Jackpot Rush**). [[raw/media_tax_credit_team_coaching-july-28-2025.md]]

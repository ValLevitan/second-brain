# JVL corporate loans and shareholder financing

How JVL's **related-party (shareholder/investor) loans** and its **institutional loans** are recorded, reconciled and (in some cases) converted to equity. Worked through on the **June 11 2025** loan-review call between [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] and JVL's finance controller, then in detail on **June 13 2025** with the controller and external chartered accountant [[wiki/people/sean-riley.md|Sean Riley]]. The controller — **unnamed** on the June-11 transcript — is **resolved to [[wiki/people/alka-sood.md|Alka Sood]]** by the June-13 sources (Alka describes the same deep loan-schedule review she and Val had done, and Sean addresses her by name — Tier A resolution, previously queued). The working file was "**JVL loan schedule as of May 31 2025.xlsx**." This is **loan/corporate economics, not labour compensation** — recorded per §15.8. ## Related-party (shareholder / investor) loans

- **[[wiki/people/doug-skrepnek.md|Doug Skrepnek]]'s lending group — "Skrepnik Capital."** Doug lends to JVL through two related companies, **Romper** and **2032968 Ontario Inc.**; the summary document he prepared is titled "**Skrepnik Capital — Summary of loans and accrued interest as of December 31 2024**" (dated/prepared January 20). Accrued interest owing is **roughly slightly over $1.4 million each** for Romper and 2032968, totalling **close to $2.9 million**. Val questioned the figure and asked for the supporting record; the calculation traces interest **from 2018 onward by loan**, reduced by payment tranches along the way. Val instructed the controller to **book the ~$2.9M accrued interest for Skrepnik + its associated company** into the financial records. [[raw/finance_loans_review-june-11-2025.md]]
- **Val's own loan to JVL — via 840914 Ontario Inc. (Val's company).** The main schedule was **missing all of the 840914 calculations** (principal, interest rates, accrued interest). Per Val's "loan verified" sheet, JVL owes him ~**$2.4 million principal** (excluding interest) plus ~**$1.9 million accrued interest** — total loan balance ~**$4.3 million**. Val told the controller to **integrate the 840914 repayment + summary** into the master control sheet but **NOT to book his accrued interest yet** — he may **convert it into equity** and wants to consult chartered accountant **Sean** ([[wiki/people/sean-riley.md|Sean Riley]]) first. [[raw/finance_loans_review-june-11-2025.md]]
- **Boris and Peter — $150,000 each, converted to equity.** Two ~$150k loans (**$300,000 total**) were, by adjudication, ruled no longer owed and **converted into shares**; Val wants this **solidified** on the books (currently still sitting as a $300k loan). Val told the controller his message is sufficient evidence internally ("we work together… I trust what you're telling me"), and to provide documentation only if the external accountants ask. [[raw/finance_loans_review-june-11-2025.md]]
- **The off-books problem (the driver of the review).** The accrued interest on these related-party loans had only ever been kept as an **offline record** — no expense and no liability captured in the accounting system (NavVision). Val: "these offline records cannot continue." A detailed schedule had been prepared back in **February** (estimating interest from 2018) but never posted to the financial records. [[raw/finance_loans_review-june-11-2025.md]]

### Romper / ScrapNeck (Doug Skrepnek's group) — exact interest and booking method

- Doug's **Romper** and **2032968 Ontario Inc.** were folded into one entity, **ScrapNeck Capital**. All **principal was repaid in February** (total lent **$4.5 million**, total repaid **$4.5 million**). Val flagged the booking reduces the ~$3M gain in JVL Labs (interest expense), so timing matters. [[raw/finance_loans_review_sean_alka-june-13-2025.md]] [[raw/finance_loan_summary_detailed_review_alka-june-13-2025.md]]

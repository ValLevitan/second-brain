# Fortune Fu Paws (game)

A flagship JVL game series for 2025, built on the Omni platform. (Transcripts garble the name as "future full pause" / "FuPos"; the strategic plan spells it **Fortune Fu Paws**.)

## Facts

- A key 2025 games deliverable, using modular design to spawn market-specific variants [[raw/jvl_strategic_plan_and_vision_2025_2025-01-08.md]] [[raw/jira_value_stream_distribution_infrastructure_january_7_2025.md]].
- Target timeline: **in the lab by June 2025, in the market by September 2025** [[raw/fp-7_performance_analysis_january_13_2025.md]].
- Aimed at the Class 3 Florida market; must run within the FP7 board's video limits (see [[wiki/topics/fp7-hardware-platform.md]]) [[raw/fp-7_performance_analysis_january_13_2025.md]].
- Lena's team develops it, balancing spine animation and video to stay under the performance threshold [[raw/fp-7_performance_analysis_january_13_2025.md]].

# JVL machine pricing and commercial strategy

How JVL prices its new gaming machines (the [[wiki/topics/jvl-machine-lineup.md|Flex]] lineup) into the US class-3 / skill markets — the standard retail number, the per-market premium/discount, the discount-and-quoting rules, and the cost pressures pushing the price. Machine retail and market economics only; no named-person compensation (§15.8). Set on the **Sept-23 2025 pricing meeting** ([[wiki/meetings/2025-09-23-pricing-strategy.md]], [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] + [[wiki/people/juan-martinez.md|Juan]]) and captured in a written "Pricing Strategy" policy summary of that meeting.

## The standard retail number

- JVL's **bill of material** for a machine lands around **$15,000-$18,000**; retail is set **between $16,000 and $18,000**, with deals "cut from there." [[raw/pricing_strategy_meeting-september-23-2025.md]]
- Val wants a **single number that everyone talks about** — a standard price JVL publishes on a "digital medium" and quotes consistently. [[raw/pricing_strategy_meeting-september-23-2025.md]]
- The written policy summary settles the machines as **"premium gaming machines," retail $16,000-$18,000**, all now shipping **with printers**. [[raw/Pricing_strategy-september-23-2025.md]]

## Market-specific pricing

Val worked the per-market numbers through live; the written policy summary codified them: [[raw/Pricing_strategy-september-23-2025.md]]

| Market | Price | vs baseline | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nebraska** | $16,000 | baseline | standard reference [[raw/pricing_strategy_meeting-september-23-2025.md]] |
| **Texas** | $16,500 | premium, no SD add-on | premium-brand positioning; the SD regulatory premium does not apply [[raw/pricing_strategy_meeting-september-23-2025.md]] |
| **South Dakota** | $17,200 | +regulatory premium | GLI soft-cost burden (below) [[raw/pricing_strategy_meeting-september-23-2025.md]] |

- **South Dakota GLI premium math:** the SD market is very small and the **GLI (lab-certification) burden very high** — Val expects to spend **~$15,000-$20,000/year extra on GLI just for South Dakota**. Normalized over ~100 machines/year that is **~$200/machine of burden**; on a **×4 market-entry multiplier** that is a **~$800 premium per machine**. So a machine sold to Nebraska at $16,000 becomes **~$16,800** in South Dakota. Val then rounded the standard SD/TX pair to **$17,200 / $16,500**. Stable SD sales are expected to top out at **~100-150 machines/year** (Val: can't raise the price once machines are stable). [[raw/pricing_strategy_meeting-september-23-2025.md]]
- Earlier in the meeting Val floated a flat **$500** GLI premium before computing the $200×4 = $800 figure — recorded as him reasoning toward the $800 number, not a separate price. [[raw/pricing_strategy_meeting-september-23-2025.md]]

## Quoting and discount rules (Val's Sept-23 policy)

- **"Dennis" (sales) may quote ONE number only** — no other number, no matrix. Val: "I don't like to have matrix circulating because somehow it escapes my digital box." Any actual deal is cut by **Val or Juan directly with the operator, bypassing Dennis**; Dennis holds only the single list number in his head. [[raw/pricing_strategy_meeting-september-23-2025.md]]
- Dennis had reportedly been quoting **$15,000 with a printer / $14,500 without**; the plan is to raise the standard number, then bring specific deals back down (e.g. "you opted for the topper, an $800 item, so it's $15,000"). [[raw/pricing_strategy_meeting-september-23-2025.md]]
- **CFO-to-CFO credit mechanism:** for a key account like [[wiki/organizations/mg-oil.md|MG Oil]], the **invoice states the full retail price ($18,000)** but the company receives a **credit applied separately, routed directly from JVL's CFO to the buyer's CFO, "bypassing everything else"** — preserving list-price integrity while still discounting. [[raw/pricing_strategy_meeting-september-23-2025.md]] [[raw/Pricing_strategy-september-23-2025.md]]
- **Volume + terms:** discounts key off units bought and terms; "people that can afford always want terms." [[raw/pricing_strategy_meeting-september-23-2025.md]]

## Competitive positioning

- At **$16,000-$17,000, JVL is lower than almost everybody in the class-3 casino space.** The **IGT** casino box for sale "has to be $18,000 or higher." **Ainsworth's** electronics sit inside an old **Grand Vision** cabinet ("at most a $5,000 cabinet, old as hell"); **Spilo** machines are old; **VLCs** are 20-year-old. Val's read: the new-machine suppliers in this space reduce to **JVL, IGT, and a new "Epic."** In Texas, Val sees "no issue positioning ourselves as a premium brand." [[raw/pricing_strategy_meeting-september-23-2025.md]]

### The Texas price reality (Juan↔Jay call, same day)

On the separate Sept-23 Texas market call ([[wiki/meetings/2025-09-23-texas-skill-game-strategy-jay-call.md]]), Texas consultant **Jay** told Juan that JVL's **~$17,000 list price is too high to enter Texas** and "you're going to have to really rethink what your price is." The Texas competitive floor: [[raw/Texas_skill_game_strategy-september-23-2025.md]]

- **Vanilla** big steel-cabinet skill game runs **~$8,000-$10,000** all-in (shipping + tax); its **boards** run **~$5,000-$9,000, generally ~$5,000** (a customer supplies the cabinet). JVL could self-build from a board + cabinet + printer + bill acceptor for **~$7,500**. [[raw/Texas_skill_game_strategy-september-23-2025.md]]
- **Pace** (distribution rights held by **Matt Paschal / E2**) **gives the game away free or ~$900** on a **30-35% revenue-share** model where "Matt's group" also takes an estimated **~5% override** on all action running through their games; Pace's **TRT terminal** was **$9,500** and is now given away too. [[raw/Texas_skill_game_strategy-september-23-2025.md]]
- Market share order per Jay: **Vanilla #1, Pace #2, Aurora #3** (plus "Paramount"). A $17k premium machine faces a hard entry against ~$8-10k incumbents on rev-share terms. [[raw/Texas_skill_game_strategy-september-23-2025.md]]

## Cost pressures pushing the price up

- **Tariffs:** a known **10-15% uplift** on JVL's bill of material is coming; the biggest driver is **South Korea (all of JVL's panels come from there)**, not China. "We cannot really avoid this." (Broader tariff context: [[wiki/topics/tariffs-and-customs-compliance.md]].) [[raw/pricing_strategy_meeting-september-23-2025.md]]
- **Printers:** every machine now ships **with a printer**, adding **~$1,000+** of retail uplift; printer cost is roughly **~$500** ("a brand-new Transact was ~$800 retail"). Refurbished JCMs may or may not still be usable — **unclear**. [[raw/pricing_strategy_meeting-september-23-2025.md]] [[raw/Pricing_strategy-september-23-2025.md]]

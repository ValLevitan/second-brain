## The conceptual framework

- A conceptual diagram of a casino **payment system** connects **personas and use cases** (a player inserting cash, collecting winnings) to **capabilities** — player capabilities (send/retrieve funds easily, view credits) and machine capabilities (handle multiple payment formats, display credits) — grounded in **user-centred design** (ergonomic interfaces; the player "never needs to leave their chair"). Fund types/payment flows named: **Cash, AFT, TITO, Voucher**. The document's stated point: technical requirements stay grounded in first-person user statements (e.g. "As a player I want clarity on the status of my funds"). [[raw/casino-systems-product-management.md]] This is the same personas→capabilities model applied to FlexPay on [[wiki/topics/flexpay-business-architecture-payout-devices.md]].

## The Gaming Systems Platform Product Manager job profile

- A **technically-minded Product Manager** leading integrated casino gaming-systems platforms — an engineering mindset to architect holistic solutions from player interfaces and payment processing to game logic and operator dashboards, translating technical requirements into coherent systems while keeping focus on player satisfaction and operational efficiency. Key responsibilities include system-architecture views, capability models/specs, cross-functional discovery, and competitive analysis. Qualifications: **5+ years** product management for complex technical systems, an engineering/CS background, experience with architecture diagrams and capability frameworks; preferred: gaming-technology / casino-management-system experience and embedded/hardware integration. [[raw/casino-systems-product-management.md]] Ties to the **Miami PM hire** thread. [[raw/casino-systems-product-management.md]]

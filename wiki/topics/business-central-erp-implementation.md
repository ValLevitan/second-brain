# Business Central ERP implementation (JVL's Dynamics 365 rollout)

Microsoft **Dynamics 365 Business Central (BC)** is JVL's ERP/accounting system, rolled out company-by-company to replace the legacy **Navision** installation. Worked through in depth on an Apr 17 2025 status call between [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] and [[wiki/people/lev-rudky.md|Lev]] (Rudky, JVL's Sr. MS Business Central MIS specialist) so Val could "put a picture together" from Lev's messages. This is the *system* side; how the finance *people* and tasks are organized sits on [[wiki/topics/jvl-finance-back-office.md]]. [[raw/business_central_implementation_update-apr-17-2025.md]]

## Status by company (Apr 17 2025)

- **JVL Mexico** — being closed ([[wiki/decisions/2025-03-24-close-jvl-mexico-operation.md]]), so it "is going to stop being an issue" and stop using BC. [[raw/business_central_implementation_update-apr-17-2025.md]]
- **JVL Systems (the US company)** — running BC in production: day-to-day accounting and **accounts receivable** work correctly, the **installment-payment "scheduler" add-on** works, and the tax program works. The company can **manufacture, sell, purchase and manage inventory** in BC — though manufacturing is set up only for **select items** so far (each item takes a large number of parameters to configure; "it's a time-consuming process… but it's not a showstopper"), and any production request can still be executed by prioritizing the missing item's setup. [[raw/business_central_implementation_update-apr-17-2025.md]]
- **JVL Labs (the Canadian company; where manufacturing currently is)** — BC implementation **on hold**; Labs still runs on **Navision**. Val's decision: **stay on Navision in Labs until it is certain where manufacturing lands** (Canada vs Georgia). **(Resolved June 26 2025: manufacturing moves to Georgia — see the June-26 section below; Labs stays on Navision, assembly is set up on the JVL Systems BC.)** [[raw/business_central_implementation_update-apr-17-2025.md]] [[raw/Alignment_procurement_manufacturing_transfer-june-26-2025.md]]

## What implementing Labs in BC would take

- **Copy from two sources** — the BC Systems setup **plus** Navision-Labs (items/inventory are copied from Navision Labs, not Systems) — then **adapt to Canada** (taxes are "not the only thing"), and implement the **full production module including the financial side**. [[raw/business_central_implementation_update-apr-17-2025.md]]
- Separately, **reporting**, which includes **material requirements generation (MRP)**: start from a forecast ("we need to manufacture that many machines for such-and-such configurations"), then generate a report of what's required, what's in stock, what's in the pipe, and what must be ordered. This would **replace the Excel forecasting sheet [[wiki/people/mikhail-perelman.md|Michael Perelman]] uses today** (Val feeds him a forecast; he inputs part/machine numbers; it measures current stock and lists components to order). [[raw/business_central_implementation_update-apr-17-2025.md]]
- It's a large man-hour job mainly because of **bill-of-materials redundancies** — many small items across BOM levels. In Navision JVL built **exception lists** ("what not to take into account"); engineering changes / in-development parts add further complication. [[raw/business_central_implementation_update-apr-17-2025.md]]

## The licensing / single-database question (the crux)

- **Where manufacturing lands drives the design.** If manufacturing stays in Canada, Labs needs a fully-blown (expensive) manufacturing BC license; if it moves to **Georgia**, Labs "can be implemented in BC without fully-blown manufacturing" and could sit on the **same database as Systems**, cutting license count. Committing to full Canadian manufacturing and then finding it irrelevant would be "a major waste of resources." [[raw/business_central_implementation_update-apr-17-2025.md]]
- **License rule (Lev):** a company **without** manufacturing can be put on the same database as one **with** manufacturing, **but not the other way around** — the manufacturing license is "wider… with more capabilities" and a cheaper-license company won't carry it. So you can add a **cheap-license** company onto an **expensive (manufacturing) license** database, not vice versa. [[raw/business_central_implementation_update-apr-17-2025.md]]
- **The US-tax obstacle to merging:** Systems has **complex US taxation** via an add-on Lev called **"Alara"** (as transcribed) that pulls **external American data sources** state-by-state / zip-code-by-zip-code; Canadian taxation "doesn't require any add-ons." Lev's worry: putting the American tax add-on on a shared database with a Canadian company could trigger compatibility problems on Canadian sales transactions (the add-on processes only American jurisdictions). Unresolved without testing/consulting. [[raw/business_central_implementation_update-apr-17-2025.md]]
- **License cost of splitting:** each user on each system needs a license; Systems has ~**7 users** in the States, so keeping two separate license types would be roughly a **$10,000/year difference** (Canadian manufacturing licenses are pricier and carry more users) — Val's initial "couple of thousand dollars" guess was corrected upward by Lev. [[raw/business_central_implementation_update-apr-17-2025.md]]

## BC's three entity levels (Lev's explainer)

- **Tenant** — what you buy a license for (top level).
- **Environment** — one live + three test environments allowed.
- **Company** — multiple companies can live within one environment.

Two companies can share a tenant, and can be put into the **same environment** — which is where Lev has "concerns regarding finances." He believes he can find a solution but "need[s] to test… and consult." [[raw/business_central_implementation_update-apr-17-2025.md]]

## May 24 2025 — cleaning the chart of accounts (GL) before Labs converts

A [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] / [[wiki/people/alka-sood.md|Alka Sood]] / [[wiki/people/lev-rudky.md|Lev Rudky]] / [[wiki/people/hector.md|Hector]] working call ([[wiki/meetings/2025-05-24-finance-gl-account-chart-of-accounts.md]]) reviewing the **US chart of accounts (GL)** on screen. The **chart of accounts is "the backbone on the finance side"** — [[wiki/people/lev-rudky.md|Lev]] cannot start the JVL Labs BC conversion until it is cleaned (after the COA comes the **posting setup**). The transcript internally duplicates its opening — facts taken once. [[raw/finance_meeting_gl_account-may-24-2025.md]]

- **Two charts, aligned but not identical.** The **US ("American") GL** was built largely **from scratch** by **Daniel** (using the Canadian GL only as a base — "**most of it created from scratch. The numbering is different**"). Main-category titles will be **1:1 across both entities**, but subsidiary accounts differ (e.g. US bank accounts vs Canadian RBC accounts). Val wants **different number ranges** so a reader can tell at a glance whether an account is US, Canadian, or JVL Systems. The **BOFA (Bank of America) / BOFA credit-card** accounts must be replaced with the Canadian **RBC** accounts. [[raw/finance_meeting_gl_account-may-24-2025.md]]
- **Dimensions = tags on ledger entries.** A **dimension** is an extra parameter picked up by the GL / customer / vendor ledgers so reports can be organised by it; **important for consolidation**. **US has dimensions; the Canadian (Navision/"InVision") system has no active dimensions** — the plan is to add the same tags to the Canadian side. Val: "so certain things have tags." [[raw/finance_meeting_gl_account-may-24-2025.md]]
- **Posting setups differ between systems** — the Canadian entity (InVision/Navision) and the new BC entity have different functionalities, groupings and additional parameters, "**so it's not a good match**"; posting setup directs certain accounts to other functionalities that write back into the GL. [[raw/finance_meeting_gl_account-may-24-2025.md]]
- **Renaming an account is safe.** Each visible account number has a hidden **internal ID**; changing the visible account number/name does **not** break the system's formulas or logic ("**it'll treat it the same way… just going to call it something different**"). Val wants to **preserve Daniel's work** as much as possible. [[raw/finance_meeting_gl_account-may-24-2025.md]]
- **Val's AI-first naming doctrine for the GL (and all lists).** Because JVL will "**use artificial intelligence extensively**" and "**a machine needs to read files**," descriptions must avoid **restricted/reserved characters** (slashes, spaces, asterisks, square/curly brackets, commas, periods); machines are **case-sensitive** ("capital means capital"), so use **all lowercase, underscores (or hyphens), no spaces, no abbreviations** (BOFA → "bank of america"; no "chk" for checking), **no plurals**. Apply the same cleansing to **item descriptions, part numbers, and all other lists** (inventory, customer, vendor, dimensions), not just the GL. Val: "**For me, the most important thing is AI. There is nothing more important for me than AI… I want to fully satisfy AI.**" He suggests ChatGPT can generate the reformatting prompt and reformat the Excel automatically (even to CSV). [[raw/finance_meeting_gl_account-may-24-2025.md]]
- **Native-BC fields vs AI-consumed data.** Some columns/values are **native to Business Central's internal reporting** and an AI won't read them: the **"Totaling" column** uses BC's own range syntax with two periods (e.g. "1131_1..1131_19") to total an account range; **summary/posting accounts** are shown capitalised with a **FALSE** flag (nothing can be posted to a summary account, only to a sub-account). These stay as-is for BC's native reporting; the naming cleanup targets the descriptions/data the AI will read. [[raw/finance_meeting_gl_account-may-24-2025.md]]

## June 17 2025 — the GL structure walkthrough (the flat file for the BC upload)

The June-17 continuation of the [[#may-24-2025--cleaning-the-chart-of-accounts-gl-before-labs-converts|May-24 chart-of-accounts cleanup]] ([[wiki/meetings/2025-06-17-finance-gl-chart-of-accounts-structure.md]]). [[wiki/people/alka-sood.md|Alka]] walked [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]], [[wiki/people/hector.md|Hector]] and [[wiki/people/lev-rudky.md|Lev]] through a document with **three tabs — Proposed / Systems chart of accounts / Labs trial balance** — the last being the raw NaVision data by account code with 2023 and 2024 balances. The transcript internally duplicates its content (twice) — facts taken once. [[raw/finance_gl_accounts_structure-june-17-2025.md]]

- **The design goal (restated).** One account structure common to both **internal reporting** and the **external accountants'** published financial statements, so BC can print a system-generated P&L "at the press of a button" — no back-office manipulation, no lead sheets — such that "a brand-new person walking in without any understanding of the business" could pull a report and understand it. This deliberately removes work [[wiki/people/hector.md|Hector]] does building lead sheets. [[raw/finance_gl_accounts_structure-june-17-2025.md]]
- **Only two years of data brought over (decision, locked on the call).** NaVision is very old ("from 2003… almost the company year"); many accounts have zero balances and haven't been used in 10+ years. Val's ruling: **pull the trial balance for two years (2024 + 2023), not five** — "I'll go with two." Rationale: the business is "radically changing," fewer accounts is better, and re-adding a standalone account later "is easy," while the old NaVision mapping is retained as a 20-year reference so "nothing is lost." Lev's supporting view: "the fewer accounts, the better," each account carrying maintenance/setup/reconciliation cost. [[raw/finance_gl_accounts_structure-june-17-2025.md]]
- **Account count cut roughly in half.** Alka reduced the GL from **~700 accounts to ~450** ("taken away 300… taken away half"); the NaVision Labs GL runs "close to 700… 800 lines," many with zero balances. The **Systems** GL was a BC template adapted by **Daniel**; it has a stray "year" error to fix later — treated as a **separate initiative**. Labs will get a structure "unique to JVL Labs" that is also adaptable to Systems, rather than being modeled on Systems. [[raw/finance_gl_accounts_structure-june-17-2025.md]]
- **Simplifications proposed.** A single **intercompany receivables** account (the subsidiary is the customer) instead of one per entity; **delete** machines/parts/demo inventory and **work-in-process inventory** ("we don't do work-in-process accounting here"); **keep** interim clearing (a safeguarding account), bank accounts, credit-card accounts, and investments-in-subsidiaries (kept separate). Alka/Hector to independently mark accounts they think are useless. [[raw/finance_gl_accounts_structure-june-17-2025.md]]
- **Fixed assets — stop carrying fully-amortized assets.** Example: **$115,000 spent on software, fully amortized (zero net book value)**; a ~$249 hardware remnant to be written off. Limit to **four or five categories** (land, building, equipment, tools) — but keep separate the classes that carry **different tax treatment / amortization rules** (tooling, samples — three-year), and add a **Systems** category for **machines on lease** (fully amortized after three years) so accountants can map to trial balance for tax/CRA filings without reclassification. Val: he has never in 30 years "referred to amortized assets." [[raw/finance_gl_accounts_structure-june-17-2025.md]]
- **The flat-file / upload question.** Alka wants to deliver the accounts in the **structure and format (a flat file)** Lev needs to load into BC "seamlessly," but is nervous: a prior ERP chart-of-accounts install was **not one flat file** — it was a **series of files** (load the hierarchy first, then summary/posting accounts, then posting accounts, then align). They agreed to an **offline call to check BC's upload procedure**; Lev believes he can upload the chart. [[raw/finance_gl_accounts_structure-june-17-2025.md]]
- **Account number vs description (Daniel's embedded codes are meaningless to the system).** In BC the **account number is column A** and the **description is column B — a free text field** "connected to the account." Daniel's long embedded reference numbers (e.g. `1112-220.11-1703031`, the repeated `03031`, a stray `1700`) "do not mean anything" for system performance — references Daniel made for himself; there is some hierarchy (levels of the same entity split into detail) but the repeated segment is unexplained, and Val may not use it. [[raw/finance_gl_accounts_structure-june-17-2025.md]]
- **AI-first naming rule (reaffirms May-24).** Val: "an AI agent, which I anticipate on using a lot, **cannot use periods, spaces, or commas**" — use **underscores or dashes, no slashes**. The **"Totaling" column** stays as BC's native range syntax for totals/subtotals. [[raw/finance_gl_accounts_structure-june-17-2025.md]]
- **Sub-ledger clarifications (Lev).** BC has **one ledger per entity** (customer / vendor / inventory), filterable by parameters (customer type, vendor type, inventory type, location) — there is **no sub-ledger-to-a-sub-ledger** as a separate entity. The **inventory sub-ledger** (item ledger) records every transaction and rolls up; e.g. **$1.7M inventory main** is produced by filtering the item ledger by location. The AR (invoicing/collections), AP, and inventory sub-ledgers each **feed the GL**. Subsidiaries are booked as **customers with a different classification** than direct customers; reports select by classification. Val's wish for **two separate AR GLs** (direct-customer AR vs subsidiary AR) was **deferred** ("I'll come back to it later… check with Hector"). Control accounts named: **bank accounts** (reconcile in BC in 15–30 min/month, not Excel), **receivables**, **payables**, **inventory**. [[raw/finance_gl_accounts_structure-june-17-2025.md]]
- **Strategic direction — finish BC, then replace it.** Val restated that BC is "shooting birds with a cannon" for JVL's business, that he and Lev already decided **not** to do the **manufacturing component**, and that after implementing/running BC he will "**ask Lev to find a simple software… and move away from Business Central**" — because SaaS user interfaces "will be replaced by AI" within ~3 years. Full ruling on [[wiki/decisions/2025-06-17-implement-then-replace-business-central.md]]. [[raw/finance_gl_accounts_structure-june-17-2025.md]]
- **Schedule.** Alka to send a revision that night; Hector to review by Monday/Tuesday; Alka to send Lev the updated version by end of next week (Lev "held for 10 days"). Alka flagged she has over-committed to JVL and must return to her other clients. **No §15.8 material** — corporate/vendor economics only. [[raw/finance_gl_accounts_structure-june-17-2025.md]]

- **Progress payment (event-driven, A/P) vs installment payment (time-driven, A/R) — why both add-ons are needed.** Installment payments (sales/customer side) are **time-driven**: fixed calendar intervals (e.g. every 30 days for 9 payments), predictable and auto-calculated. Progress payments (purchase/vendor side) are **event-driven**: triggered by milestones (PO issuance, shipment, receipt of goods) that a calendar cannot predict. This is why the A/R installment add-on could not be repurposed for vendor-side progress payments as Lev originally hoped — hence two separate AP Commerce add-ons. Both add-ons attach to and extend BC's standard **payment terms** table (the "Due Date Calculation" column), adding a further level of detail. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lev_Rudky_BC_progress_payments_payment_terms-1-April-15-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Lev_Rudky_BC_progress_payments_payment_terms-April-15-2026.md]]
- **Every purchase-payment event requires a posted purchase invoice — "purchase invoice" means the BC posting process, not a vendor document.** Each movement tied to an order (PO issued, prepayment, receipt) must post to the **vendor ledger** and **general ledger**; those entries are created by posting a purchase invoice. When a vendor doesn't issue a separate document per milestone, JVL **emulates it internally** by posting a purchase invoice against the PO for each payment tranche. Val's realization on the call: the term "purchase invoice" here refers to the posting process in BC, not necessarily a document received from the vendor. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lev_Rudky_BC_progress_payments_payment_terms-1-April-15-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Lev_Rudky_BC_progress_payments_payment_terms-April-15-2026.md]]
- **Payment-terms table needs cleanup + a standard, and carries a calc error.** The terms table (shared by A/R and A/P — the left-hand columns are BC-standard) is confusingly named: codes use mixed capitalization and spacing. Lev added new, correctly-configured terms assigned to specific customers/vendors but left the old (possibly wrong) terms in place; the old/unused ones must be **deleted** so only the correct terms remain. On a "zero down, 2-month grace, 6 equal payments" term, the first payment was set at **60 days instead of 90 days** and the percentage divided by 6 rather than reflecting the schedule after the grace period — Val flagged it as mis-set; Lev to fix. Val also wants a documented **standard table** describing how term codes are interpreted (as exists for customers), so the correct term can be applied to each vendor. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lev_Rudky_BC_progress_payments_payment_terms-1-April-15-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Lev_Rudky_BC_progress_payments_payment_terms-April-15-2026.md]]

BC licenses are **annual commitments** — reassignable between users but not cancellable mid-term; a ~6-month option exists but isn't much cheaper. Lev hit a one-off issue where a license reassignment took **~2.5 days** to sync (normally 15–30 minutes) — a Microsoft ticket was opened but it eventually resolved on its own. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_Lev_Rudky_BC_progress_payments_payment_terms-2-April-15-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_Lev_Rudky_BC_progress_payments_payment_terms-April-15-2026.md]]

Lev is attempting this with **permission sets + security filters** in BC, but it doesn't work: higher-level ("super") permission sets override the field-level security filters, nullifying the restriction. ## May 21 2026 — the three-source cost hierarchy for the MCP, and bootstrapping 2026 cost data from Navision

The follow-up [[wiki/people/val-levitan.md|Val]] / [[wiki/people/lev-rudky.md|Lev Rudky]] call ([[wiki/meetings/2026-05-21-bom-cost-extraction-msbc-mcp.md]]) pinned **where the material-requirements MCP gets per-part cost** and how to populate it given the Jan-1-2026 Navision→BC cutover. Continues the [[#may-11-2026--material-requirements-report-bridged-via-the-bc-mcp-layer|May-11 MCP bridge]]. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_bom_cost_extraction_msbc_mcp-May-21-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_bom_cost_extraction_msbc_mcp-May-21-2026.md]]

- **BC exposes three distinct cost sources; only the MCP path can use all three.** (1) **Last paid invoice price** — the price actually paid on a received-and-invoiced item; (2) **open purchase-order price** — the price on an active PO not yet received/invoiced; (3) the **Vendor Card / Item Vendor Catalog** — manually-entered per-part-per-vendor prices independent of any PO or invoice. Lev's key distinction: BC's *standard* reports use only source (1), whereas the MCP layer can pull from all three — which is why the source-selection logic belongs in the MCP, not in BC. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_bom_cost_extraction_msbc_mcp-May-21-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_bom_cost_extraction_msbc_mcp-May-21-2026.md]]
- **Val's priority order (decision).** Priority 1 last paid invoice → Priority 2 open PO → Priority 3 Vendor Catalog; if all three are absent, output an explicit **zero / "no data"** flagged for human analysis. Val: a zero on a washer or nut doesn't matter, but a zero on a new board does. Lev noted counter-arguments to any fixed order (a paid price can be inflated on a small one-off buy; an open PO may be a different fast-delivery vendor; catalog prices go stale) — Val accepted the order as judgment-plus-simplicity, with low-confidence rows flagged rather than optimised. [[raw/Meeting_Notes_bom_cost_extraction_msbc_mcp-May-21-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_bom_cost_extraction_msbc_mcp-May-21-2026.md]]
- **The bootstrap problem — 2026 has almost no BC cost data.** Because JVL "mostly hasn't been buying anything in 2026," sources (1) and (2) are sparse, so the **Vendor Catalog (source 3) must be populated** with historical cost carried over from Navision to make the MCP useful from day one. Lev's caution — dumping old Navision prices over BC risks overwriting with stale figures — was accepted by Val as a low risk in JVL's case (prices "haven't really changed" on the mature parts in scope); a threshold-age flag on catalog-sourced rows was floated in the notes but not required. [[raw/Meeting_Transcript_bom_cost_extraction_msbc_mcp-May-21-2026.md]] [[raw/Meeting_Notes_bom_cost_extraction_msbc_mcp-May-21-2026.md]]

Mechanism: open Toli's BC account, describe the scope; if small he may just execute, else negotiate.

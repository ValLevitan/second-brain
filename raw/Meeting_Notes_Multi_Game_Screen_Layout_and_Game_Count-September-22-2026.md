## Summary

**How many games the selection screen shows.** Val started by saying the product must ship with 15 games, not 10, because he needs a price increase to go with it. Michael pushed back. His view, formed on his recent field trip, is that the 10-game release was right. Adding more titles inside the same families brings back the problem the team spent a year fixing — Felipe's criticism that all the Jackpot Rush games felt the same. Michael said Jackpot Rush has about two real mechanics, not four, and Fiery Ice Link also has two. If a player picks three Jackpot Rush games, or three Fiery Ice games that play alike, they conclude every game in the box is the same. His rule: no more than two titles per family. Lena pointed out the product does hold 15 games — only 10 are shown on the screen. By the end of the call Val agreed to leave it at 10 games in three categories, with 15 games available to work with. Val said he still does not know how he will square that with the price increase he wants.

**Layout and progressives.** Michael's design puts a progressive value on each game icon. That was never meant for this release — he planned it for V4. Val asked whether the new layout could still go into V3. Lena said redoing the whole layout would take at least two weeks, so Val moved it to the next release. Michael noted a downside of the new layout: it drops the progressive promotion text, and promoting the progressives is what they should be doing.

**Release philosophy (not settled).** Michael described Juan's proposed approach — instead of discrete, all-new releases, carry the top earners from V3 into V4 and add new titles around them. Shipping 15 brand-new games is aggressive, expensive and slow. Val said he has no philosophy of his own on this yet and needs to be led to one. He did accept the underlying idea of chasing the winners.

**Whether operators will ever swap games.** Michael doubts it. From his conversations with Felipe, field software upgrades are hard and operators set it and forget it, so extra titles may never be seen. Val answered that the new machine has a built-in modem that can enable and disable games remotely. Michael asked who would do that. Val said JVL would do it for the customer — there is no interface for the customer to manage it.

**Idle screen.** Val asked why frame zero of the idle screen shows Fiery Ice Link with Calle Ocho. Lena said frame zero actually shows Mega Hit Blaze, but the current layout is broken so she cannot display it. She will send it to Val once it is fixed.

**Nebraska settings and the product-owner gap.** Michael needs guidance on progressive levels and bet levels for Nebraska. Efim is waiting on answers. Michael would have preferred suggested progressive levels from Juan but cannot get them, so he will start making the calls himself rather than let Efim spin his wheels. He said the line count (40 or 50 lines on a couple of games) he can work out; the progressive values are the real question. Val said there is no product manager. He is setting up an intercompany transfer to move someone from Minsk to the US and will make a product owner of them — a diligent chaser, not at Juan's level — but it will take time. Val said he cannot offer a better solution right now. He also told Michael the new person going to Nebraska will be sent to Georgia first, at least until January.

**Status.** Val said the product was submitted today and he expects approval by Friday. He called the modem a likely game changer.

## Decisions Made

- **Ship 10 games in three categories for now, with 15 games available in the product.** Michael's position, accepted by Val at the end of the call after he had opened by insisting on 15. Lena confirmed the product holds 15 games and shows 10. (Decided by Val Levitan, with Michael Gottlieb)
- **Cap each game family at two titles.** Jackpot Rush and the Link games have about two distinct mechanics each, so more titles per family makes the games feel identical to players. (Michael Gottlieb; Val Levitan said he can subscribe to the logic)
- **Progressive values on the game icons move to the next release, not V3.** Lena said reworking the whole layout takes at least two weeks. Val called it V4 or "V3 and a half"; the number was left open. (Decided by Val Levitan)
- **Michael will set the Nebraska progressive levels and bet levels himself.** Juan's input is not available and Efim is blocked waiting. Val did not object and said he cannot offer a better solution now. (Decided by Michael Gottlieb, accepted by Val Levitan)
- **The Friday October 2 meeting moves to Thursday.** Michael is out that day and Val is on a flight. (Decided by Val Levitan)
- Considered and superseded: shipping 15 games on the selection screen to support a price increase — Val opened with this, then agreed to 10 shown in three categories by the end of the call.

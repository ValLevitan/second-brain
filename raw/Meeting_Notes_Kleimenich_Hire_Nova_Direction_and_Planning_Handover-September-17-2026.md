## Summary

A short call straight after the Game_Conversion_progress meeting. Val used it to settle three things: hiring Sergey Kleimenich, dropping Rage in favour of Nova, and taking planning work away from Pavel.

**Sergey Kleimenich.** Pavel reported what Sergey told him: he doubts his own expertise is enough for this kind of task, he sees himself more as an executor, and he leaned heavily on Lena's opinion in the past. Pavel added that if everyone accepts the result may not be perfect, the experiment can still be run — nobody plans to build twenty games and then throw them away. Val agreed, saying only taxes and death are certain and everything else is a matter of probability.

Val called Sergey a strong candidate. Pavel relayed Sergey's question: may he go to Lena with questions during the work, or is this fully secret? Val's answer was no — absolutely not. Val was clear that this is not a judgement on Lena's ability; his point is that her view is fixed and cannot be changed, and he does not want to consult her on this.

Irina said she does not think Sergey actually needs anything; he is simply being careful. Val agreed and described him as a capable, reliable and talented person who is fishing for reassurance. Pavel explained the background: Sergey once held a lead developer role on a project that failed, and that experience now stops him from aiming higher. Val said that suits him — Sergey would not make a good manager, and Val will direct the work himself.

Val's instruction: if the team is comfortable with the direction, hire Kleimenich starting Monday, put him on the Texas and Class A track, and keep the work completely secret.

**Rage is dropped; everything goes to Nova.** Pavel pointed out that the current experiment converts Rage horizontal into Rage vertical, and that later they would probably need to convert the same Rage games into Nova as well. Val cut that short: build no more games on Rage at all, switch straight to Nova, and do Texas on Nova. He took the silence as agreement and said there is no time for debate.

**Planning comes off Pavel.** Val told Pavel plainly that planning must be taken away from him, because he dislikes it and does not do it well. His proposal is to bring in Dima Kovalchuk as an administrator — an application administrator, as Val put it when Irina asked. The working method: Pavel and Irina sit with Kovalchuk, record the conversation, talk through the plans, and Kovalchuk then administers them. They meet on a regular cadence, he reports the plans back, they make corrections, it is all recorded from the meeting, and he carries the administration forward.

Val's reasoning: JVL gains an extra pair of hands and a good administrator, the team gets full transparency, Pavel stops doing work he hates, and Pavel can come to Atlanta and take on something serious. Pavel and Irina both agreed and the call ended.

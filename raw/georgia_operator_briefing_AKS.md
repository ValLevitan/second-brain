Operator Briefing — AKS Amusement LLC

Company Overview

Company: AKS Amusement LLC
CEO: Zahir
Tech Leader / Operations Manager: Karim

AKS Amusement is a medium–large Georgia COAM master operator. Based on market
context, AKS appears to have originated from (or been closely associated with) the smaller-
master ecosystem connected to Lucky Bucks (alongside groups such as Dynamic Gaming).
Zahir is a GAMOA member but is not actively involved in GAMOA leadership and is generally
more hands-off with day-to-day location operations.



Relationship with JVL

AKS has an established history with JVL through both direct Flex purchases and Altera-R
exposure via other masters.

   •   Primary operational point of contact is Karim, who has historical familiarity with JVL
       from earlier deployments tied to Lucky Bucks/Imran.

   •   Karim is technically/operationally capable, but may not be the person responsible
       for performance reporting and analysis. For the meeting, it would be ideal to include
       their performance/data owner to ensure the discussion results in commitments
       and usable data.



Company Size & Market Presence

   •   2026 Decals Assigned: 510

   •   Estimated Locations: ~102 (based on ~5 machines/location typical GA

   •   Relative Market Position: Medium–Large operator (approximately #18 in decals
       statewide)

Commercial / Strategic Notes

Following the trade-in purchase, Felipe discussed the newest software package with Zahir,
including the newly designed Nudge feature and other changes intended to improve player
engagement and profitability through faster, more intuitive play.

Agreement in principle: Felipe negotiated a no-charge software upgrade for AKS cabinets
conditional on AKS providing location-level performance numbers for all machines, via
Georgia Lottery reports.



Technical / Operational Notes

   •   Karim is the key operational stakeholder and is familiar with JVL systems.

   •   Because Zahir is less involved in location-level detail, execution will depend on who
       attends and who owns reporting internally.

Meeting requirement: ask Zahir to include whoever owns:

   •   Lottery report retrieval

   •   location performance tracking / analytics

   •   decisions tied to placements based on performance



Strategic Considerations for Meeting

   •   AKS is large enough to matter and has meaningful JVL footprint (direct + acquired
       inventory).

   •   The upgrade-for-data structure creates a clear “give/get” framework and a strong
       reason to align at leadership level.
   •   If AKS complies with full location reporting, this account can become a valuable
       reference set for identifying reporting inconsistencies and informing broader
       corrective strategy.



Suggested Meeting Objective

   •   Confirm upgrade scope and timing.

   •   Secure commitment to provide complete location-level Lottery reports (define
       cadence, format, and owner).

   •   Establish a practical plan to validate performance and reconcile any reporting
       discrepancies.

   •   Align on next steps and responsibilities (Zahir vs Karim vs performance owner).

# Meeting Summary with Andrei Klimovich

### Overview:
Andrei Klimovich reviewed three potential candidates for the Account Manager position: Sam Mamedov, Egor, and Charlotte.

## Candidates:

### 1. Sam Mamedov:
- **Location:** Ukraine
- **Experience:** Approximately 3 years in slot game marketing and business development, minimal direct account management experience.
- **Skills:** Digital marketing, brand promotion, business development.
- **Expectations:** $4,500/month including bonuses.
- **Notes:**
  - Has experience promoting studios and creating relationships from scratch.
  - Less experienced in managing existing accounts for growth.
  - Recommended for future digital marketing consulting rather than current account management role.

### 2. Egor:
- **Location:** Ukraine (cannot currently leave the country due to military restrictions)
- **Experience:** 6 years total, including affiliate traffic management and three recent years with Platipus Games (online game provider).
- **Skills:** Strong industry knowledge, excellent English, good with numbers and analytics.
- **Expectations:** $2,500 base + bonuses up to approximately $3,000 monthly net.
- **Notes:**
  - Highly motivated, actively job-seeking.
  - Limited risk due to Ukraine military situation.
  - Potential concern about military draft eligibility in the future.
  - Has experience with regulated markets (Europe, Latin America, Canada).

### 3. Charlotte:
- **Location:** Currently in Latvia, originally from Colombia
- **Experience:** Extensive experience with Endorphina Gaming (large online game provider), previous role at Vegangster platform.
- **Skills:** Account management (70 accounts total, 30 very active), fluent in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French.
- **Expectations:** $3,200 base + bonuses reaching approximately $5,000 monthly.
- **Notes:**
  - Distinctive, memorable appearance (tattoos, piercings) advantageous for networking.
  - Strong professional and communicative skills.
  - Potentially brings in new accounts from Baltic, USA, Canada, Latin America, and UK.

## Recommendations:
- Exclude Sam from the immediate account management position but maintain contact for potential digital marketing consulting.
- Choose between Egor and Charlotte based on budget and immediate needs:
  - Egor is cost-effective, motivated, and immediately available.
  - Charlotte is more expensive but brings extensive experience and a diverse portfolio.

## Strategic Considerations:
- Current top 20 accounts generate approximately $733,000 turnover monthly, resulting in $38,000 GGR and approximately $2,000 revenue to the company.
- To justify Egor’s salary expectations, revenue from these accounts needs significant growth (~150% increase).
- Potential bonus structure tied to turnover growth or account revenue performance:
  - Suggested monthly revenue targets of approximately $200 per account as a baseline performance metric.

## Verification and Hiring Recommendations:
- Implement thorough verification of candidate’s claims (salary, account numbers) through documentation or alternative strategies.
- Consider hiring two candidates temporarily to mitigate risks of candidate underperformance or other unforeseen circumstances.
- Maintain ongoing candidate search and consider re-evaluating after three months.

## Next Steps:
- Prepare formal employment offers clearly outlining terms, salary, and conditions.
- Verify candidate honesty and professionalism through requested documentation (e.g., tax records).
- Structure clear, attainable bonus targets based on account growth and performance.
- Continue evaluating candidate fit based on ongoing interactions and documentation provided.

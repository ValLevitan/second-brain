## The Texas question

Val opened with a warning rather than a pitch: Texas is volatile and his soundings give wildly inconsistent numbers — **some say $250/day/machine net win, others say $25/day**. He said plainly that it does not add up.

What attracts him is that in Texas you can apparently open your own locations rather than placing machines in someone else's, and that a clean operation seems to be left alone. He told a long story about a Colombian operator who built a route, bought up the locations as leases expired, kept the real estate, and sold 50% of the business to BlackRock in 2010 — that is the model in his head: buy or lease, build one by one, run clean.

## What Val actually offered

He was blunt about the shape of it, and this is the part that matters:

> "We will not be equal partners… I'm looking for somebody to be a front man to open up locations. I will not be splitting money fifty-fifty because it will be mainly my investment."

He also said he would **manufacture an inexpensive machine specifically for Texas**. And he gave an unvarnished description of himself as a business partner — "I am a wolf… I will absolutely kill you if you fuck around" — framed as full disclosure before anyone commits.

She was equally direct back: she has always worked for herself, is not corporate and doubts she could be, keeps her own books with her son, and would not want a partner who expects otherwise. She wants to be in the field setting up locations.

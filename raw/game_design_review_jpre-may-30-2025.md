## Key Decisions and Action Items

### Decisions Made:
1. **Remove max bet requirement** for Jackpot Rush progressive eligibility
2. **Implement denomination scheduler** to adjust bet levels based on time of day/week
3. **Design games with scheduler capability** across the board, not just specific titles

### Action Items:
1. **Michael**: Draft requirements for scheduler-enabled games
2. **Val**: Have legal team research patent issues around denomination scheduling
3. **Juan**: Provide market analysis on optimal bet levels for different time periods
4. **Team**: Consider Georgia's "any bet" progressive model for adaptation to Florida market

### Key Insights:
- Average bet on Jackpot Rush is $1.20-$1.40, well below the $3 max bet required for progressives
- Players not betting max are getting a suboptimal experience (static credit tower vs. dynamic progressive meters)
- Market has two distinct player segments: value players (Mon-Thu) and higher-volume players (Fri-Sun)
- First button bet level is critical for player engagement (40¢ vs 60¢ can mean 50% revenue difference)
- Industry trend moving toward "progressives at any bet" eligibility

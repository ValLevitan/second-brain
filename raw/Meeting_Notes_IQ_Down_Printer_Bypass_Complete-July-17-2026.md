## Summary

*Meeting held in Russian. Setup: the Flex IQ device sits between the Mutha Goose game controller and the ticket printer at a location. Launch on Mutha Goose locations is August 1, 2026 — 5 devices at 5 locations, about two weeks away.*

**Final flow decision (second half).** The long dispute ended with agreement on one flow. Default path: every cashout transaction goes to card issuance, no alternatives offered. Only if card issuance fails does the cashier get two options: retry, or press a button that prints a ticket. The printed ticket is the standard Mutha Goose ticket — identical to what Mutha Goose itself would send to the printer — and the location then settles it the old way; that is no longer JVL's concern. The "Void" button is renamed (e.g. "validation failed — try again or print ticket"). Transaction statuses become pending / redeemed / ticket. Andrei recapped it at ~01:55:29; Pavel committed: "no problem, we'll do it by August 1" (~01:59:08). Val stressed this is only for the August 1 launch; capability expansions come later, once the market is understood (~01:59:11).

**Resolution of the deferred-redemption proposal.** The first-hour proposal (print a bearer ticket with a voucher number, player returns later, cashier finds the pending transaction and retries card activation) was dropped. Val's counter-model — behave like PAX: transaction failed, print a ticket, hand back to the old system — won. The turning point was Irina's player-perspective question ("I walk up, card can't be issued — what do I get?") at ~01:20:05. Val then allowed the missing step to be implemented: if the transaction failed, give the cashier "try again" or "print" (~01:21:30). Andrei: "Hooray, I'm glad we met here" (~01:21:50).

**Resolution of the card-vs-lottery-tickets requirements dispute.** Val held his position: the product was designed to emulate PAX with small improvements; there was never a designed choice for the player between a card and lottery tickets; "void" only ever meant cancel. Pavel and Irina initially saw no difference between Val's "mark as failed" and the existing Void button (~01:15:11); Val insisted the difference is huge — no fork is presented, the message is simply "transaction failed" (~01:15:45). Pavel then formulated the agreed outcome: "we abandon the fork; there is always one path, through the card; only if the card fails do we allow a return to the regular ticket" (~01:53:11). Val framed the whole disagreement as a "drift" in interpretation of what the device should do, which happened because he was absent from earlier meetings (~01:22:41). He said his goal is that JVL prospers, not to point fingers (~01:23:02, ~01:30:40).

**Resolution of "either us or Mutha Goose" — the 25-ticket history.** Pavel disclosed that the requirement to ingest Mutha Goose's 25 stored tickets came from him, out of reverse-engineering: IQ had to parse that command anyway, and ingesting the tickets looked like a benefit (issue cards for them, earn the activation percentage) (~01:34:14). Val called that specific decision a critical mistake, practically and strategically (~01:37:06, ~01:38:49): those 25 tickets are already cashed by the time IQ reconnects — players run to the cashier immediately (~01:37:41) — so no money can be made on them; and building on Mutha Goose's memory helps the Mutha Goose company push JVL out of the market later (~01:29:07). Agreed: IQ processes only real-time transactions; everything historical from Mutha Goose is ignored and nothing is stored; the only history offered to a location is what sits in JVL's own hub (~01:49:10, ~01:59:38–01:59:56). Pavel said the ingestion is a small part and easy to switch off (~01:39:28). Val's strategic alternative: give locations free SMIBs to expand capabilities through JVL's own hub (~01:28:47, ~02:00:19). Val asked Andrei directly whether he agrees; Andrei: "Absolutely" (~02:00:48). Dropping the history also makes behavior uniform whether 1, 20, or 1000 tickets were missed (Pavel, ~01:39:34), which removes the reprint double-payout path flagged in the first hour.

**Dead-IQ (power-off) case, revisited.** If IQ fully dies, Mutha Goose starts beeping within seconds ("printer lost"). The cashier unplugs IQ and connects the printer directly. Pavel clarified an important detail: Mutha Goose caches unprinted tickets and prints all of them once the printer is reconnected — cashouts made during the gap are not lost (~01:46:05, ~01:47:00). Irina noted the first player arriving at the counter makes the problem visible right away, so there won't be 30 people waiting (~01:45:29). Val: practically this is not a scary thing (~01:45:04). Val rejected Andrei's plan to instruct location staff on the wire swap: "even if we had the possibility, I wouldn't do it" — no monitor manual tells you to plug the laptop back in if the screen breaks (~01:48:14). A replacement device starts from zero; no history is pulled from Mutha Goose (~01:27:02). Val floated a parallel wiring harness instead of cutting the chain; Pavel explained the break-the-chain design was an early project decision — it is what lets IQ print its own receipts (~01:41:41–01:42:48).

**Boundary amounts (new topic, end of meeting).** Irina raised the two limits where a card cannot be issued (~02:03:06). Under ~$10: e.g. a $5 cashout minus the $2 activation fee leaves $3, which cannot go on a card; Andrei proposed handling it automatically as the print-ticket scenario, with an on-screen message that the amount cannot be issued to a card; Val liked it but wants Juan consulted (~02:04:41–02:07:03). Over $1000: INCOMM's market limit is $1000 per card; a jackpot could exceed it, and the case is currently unhandled (~02:03:24). Splitting into several transactions/cards tied to the same voucher number is technically easy (Andrei, ~02:08:07, ~02:10:49), but Val flagged legality, know-your-customer and tax obligations (wins above $1200 are taxable; Georgia law says gambling is illegal, so what tax applies?) (~02:07:50–02:09:56). Irina questioned whether Georgia even has a jackpot limit or that is a Florida rule (~02:10:28). All of it goes to Juan, who is to discuss multiple-card issuance one-on-one with INCOMM (~02:04:15, ~02:10:35).

**Offline IQ loose end.** Irina asked what happens when a transaction reached IQ but the internet is down — the tilt may block the screen so neither card nor ticket is possible. Andrei will test the offline case today; if pending transactions are locked, at minimum the print-ticket path must be opened (~02:01:34–02:02:40).

**Val's market view.** Val personally does not believe the market will accept card issuance: players want cash, a black market will continue (cards bought for cash on the spot), and some locations may keep working with PAX or the bare printer — possibly penalizable by the lottery, he doesn't know. Until a cash-out card exists, he thinks the business system (not IQ itself) won't work. Andrei: that part is not our concern (~01:53:32–01:58:31).

**Carried from the first hour (unchanged):** failure-visibility work (tilt screen made red and blinking), Dima and Sergei's extended terminal statuses for remote monitoring, the expired-license test, and the deferred silent broken-wire/burnt-port edge case. Andrei still intends to label the location cables (~01:47:47). Since July 1 locations may not pay out in merchandise; lottery tickets and gift cards remain allowed (first hour).

## Decisions Made
- **Single default flow: card issuance only, no player-facing choice; on card failure the cashier can retry or print a standard Mutha Goose ticket; the Void button is renamed; statuses become pending / redeemed / ticket** — the product was designed to emulate PAX, not to offer a fork; a printed standard ticket returns settlement to the old system. (Decided by Val Levitan; accepted by Andrei Klimovich, Pavel Frolov, Irina Shishko; ~01:21:30–01:59:05)
- **Deferred-redemption bearer-ticket proposal is dropped** — replaced by the single flow above. (Val Levitan with Andrei Klimovich, Pavel Frolov; ~01:21:30–01:53:21)
- **IQ stops ingesting Mutha Goose's 25 stored historical tickets; real-time transactions only; nothing historical is stored** — those tickets are already cashed, no money is made on them, and relying on Mutha Goose memory is strategically dangerous; also makes behavior uniform for any outage length. (Val Levitan, agreed by Pavel Frolov and Andrei Klimovich; ~01:49:10, ~01:59:38)
- **JVL will not instruct location staff on the unplug-IQ/reconnect-printer fallback** — Val would not do it even if access existed. (Val Levitan; ~01:48:14)
- **Cashouts below the card minimum (under ~$10 after the $2 fee) are auto-routed to print-ticket with an on-screen message** — the amount physically cannot go on a card; Val likes the solution, pending Juan's opinion. (Andrei Klimovich, endorsed by Val Levitan; ~02:04:41–02:06:52)
- **The over-$1000 cashout problem (INCOMM $1000/card limit, taxes, legality of multiple cards) is delegated to Juan** — he knows the policy landscape and will talk to INCOMM one-on-one. (Val Levitan, Andrei Klimovich; ~02:04:02–02:10:40)
- **Tilt/out-of-service screen to be made red and blinking/pulsing** — carried from the first hour. (Andrei Klimovich, Pavel Frolov; ~00:19:21)

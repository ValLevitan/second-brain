## Summary

**Nebraska dual box rule.** Nebraska build is up and playing. About 10 machines are deployed there, so changing the mechanic is not a risk (Val). Michael and Efim move the skill (dual box) choice to the outcome of the spin. New rule: on a line win, do not rack; the dual box comes up, player picks, then rack. On a match 3 Progressive or free spins trigger, the box text says "to access bonus". Lena says Igor asked for written requirements for the box behavior. Michael: the same approach will be used for Class A.

**Aaron's Pub (art).** Lena showed the design. Two women symbols; the redhead is worth more and triggers the bonus. Beer is golden, not Guinness, because Guinness looks too dark. Topper and second screen together form the pub front. Val suggested making the redhead more dominant (arms crossed, no beer) and said beer on both women is repetitive; he said these are optional comments. Michael prefers an inviting look. Val liked the three-segment use of the screens and wants to reuse it in other games; Lena says it does not suit every theme (e.g. Wildfire Ranch). Aaron's Pub goes to the next Nebraska release.

**Wildfire Ranch.** Art shown. Michael: "aspirational wealth" for Nebraska / South Dakota players (ranch, expensive horse, mansion, local dog breed). Bonus always shows on the Topper.

**Schedule.** IP-531 / 1164: all South Dakota D27 NOVA concepts are done. Math is not done for all games; Aaron's Pub, Wildfire Ranch and two new Keno games (Solar Keno, Queen of Luxor Keno) need builds and play testing. Rodeo Queen for SD is the dual-screen version: same art style, different math, some backgrounds redrawn. Wild Colt with match 3: nothing started. Final designs exist for some SD games. South Dakota will have 4 Keno games in total (Black Hills Keno Deluxe, Cool Cat Keno, Solar Keno, Queen of Luxor Keno). No date yet for South Dakota.

**Priorities.** Team works Class A in NOVA before South Dakota D27. Val confirmed: no change. Class A concepts (Lions Riches done as test bed) should be complete by end of next week. Class A will use a new Liquid Glass shader with semi-transparent dialog boxes; Nebraska keeps the larger dual box, which Michael says is right for that market. Lena suggested maybe a new Georgia release (curved monitor, August) before South Dakota; not decided. Game production: Nebraska is the only project in production, schedule shows December 21.

**Class 3 and Colombia.** Val raised going back to Class 3 for Colombia in NOVA. Michael: regular Class 3 (casinos) is "suicide" — casinos have no capital, many games fail; regional/street gaming will keep growing. Val agreed on regular Class 3. On Colombia: JVL has an untouched installed base run by a reliable person; Val says without something new by end of 2027 that business will be lost. Machines are JVL-owned (participation), first-version Flex, with VDACs; plan is to upgrade them on NOVA, as Rage is being decommissioned. Val rejected reusing an existing Class 3 game as a start; first ask what is making money in Colombia now. Michael will write questions; Val will open the dialogue with the Colombia contact and arrange a call later.

**Churn.** Efim explained: churn = total amount bet ÷ money brought in (e.g. $50 bet on $20 = 2.5). It depends mostly on RTP; higher volatility gives lower churn. In the field it is measured roughly as total bet ÷ money in.

## Decisions Made

- **Nebraska dual box: rack only after the dual box choice; on match 3 Progressive / free spins the text reads "to access bonus."** — more consistent behavior. (Michael, Efim)
- **Class A in NOVA stays ahead of South Dakota D27.** — Val confirmed priorities unchanged. (Val)
- **Colombia: start from what is making money there now, not from an existing Class 3 game.** (Val)

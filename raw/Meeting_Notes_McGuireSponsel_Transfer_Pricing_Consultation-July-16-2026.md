## Summary

Intro call with McGuireSponsel (referred by Mark Kransler at BS&P) to scope a transfer-pricing study documenting the intercompany price JVL Labs (Canada) charges JVL Systems (US). Val's goal is a defensible file on record in case the IRS ever challenges the pricing — he stressed it is not urgent.

**How McGuireSponsel works.** They send a questionnaire; Val fills it out; they benchmark against comparables and produce a study covering the ten IRS requirements, so the position holds up under audit. Val raised his main reservation — JVL is a non-standard, niche industry, so he questioned how they would find comparables. Greg said this is common with smaller taxpayers in niche industries: they find the closest available comparables, which may not be directly on point but are supportive enough to defend under audit.

**The deliverable.** A 50–75 page PowerPoint document plus a backup benchmarking file. It addresses ten specific requirements set out in the US Treasury Regulations (Greg cited IRC sections — transcribed as "section 42" and "6662"; these are almost certainly §482 (transfer pricing) and §6662 (penalty protection)). The study also references the OECD standard, which the US ten requirements are said to fully cover. About 70% is written from scratch and specific to JVL — industry write-up (Val will need to help), company write-up, org chart, description of the covered transactions, benchmarking, and conclusion. Only a ~10-page regulatory section is templated/repeated across their studies. Citations for the ten requirements can be provided up front if Val wants them.

**Who does the work.** Greg Lambrecht leads their global business services practice — ~30 years in international tax, former EY managing director; he is not the transfer-pricing specialist and reviews the overall product. The transfer-pricing practice is led by a CPA manager (name transcribed as "John Baudour" — verify spelling) who has done 100+ studies and handles benchmarking and questionnaires; he is out until the following week. Benchmarking is done by a newly hired analyst from Deloitte (2 years' benchmarking experience), reviewed by a manager.

**Price and timing.** Fixed fee, not hourly — about **$13,000 for a single transaction/report**. Turnaround is **4–6 weeks** after the completed questionnaire is returned and the engagement letter is signed.

**Next step.** McGuireSponsel will send the questionnaire; Val will review it to gauge how much information he must provide and the direction of the study. A follow-up call with the transfer-pricing lead can be scheduled the following week. No engagement committed yet.

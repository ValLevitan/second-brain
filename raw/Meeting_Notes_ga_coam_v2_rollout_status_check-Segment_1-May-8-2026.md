# Meeting Summary — GA-COAM V2 Rollout Status Check (Segment 1: Georgia V2 Rollout Strategy)
**Date:** May 8, 2026, 3:44 PM Eastern
**Duration:** 16m 21s
**Segment:** 1 of 2
**Attendees:** Val Levitan, Juan Martinez, Felipe Miranda Giorgi

## Summary

Val opened with a tight, declarative summary of the **Georgia V2 / V43 rollout plan** ahead of the August 18 industry show. The session was largely Val's read-out with Felipe confirming and pushing back on a few specific points; Juan participated lightly.

**Product scope.** Version 2 / V43, a vertical product housing **eight games**, with the headline feature being **three non-symbiotic progressive mechanics** running concurrently in the same machine. Val refined his own phrasing mid-flow: *"non-symbiotic, probably a better way to describe it — three different progressive mechanics in the same machine."* The opening package of 8 games breaks down as:

- **4 traditional Lines & Ways games**
- **2 Jackpot Rush–type games**
- **2 Fiery Ice–type progressive games**

**Two premium games held in reserve for the show.** The team will continue developing two additional premium titles between the end-of-June rollout and the August 18 show. These will be added *only if needed*: if the June rollout performs well, Val explicitly *does not* want to burn the premium titles at the show — keep them for a later push. If the June numbers are merely "slightly above average — good but nothing impressive," then add the two premium titles to push performance over the edge.

**Operator placement plan.** Felipe has circulated a list — friends-of-JVL operators (term used loosely) with sizable operations (50–250 locations each). Approximately **10 primary operators + 5 backup** if any of the primary don't move. Target placement: ~10 machines, fluid range of **5 to 15 machines**. Fewer than 5 wouldn't generate meaningful data; more than 15 would produce "absurd numbers" they can't manage cleanly.

**Placement terms — non-negotiable conditions:**
- **Top-performing location only.** Not average, not second-best. The single best-performing location of each operator. *"This should be a non-negotiable condition of placement."*
- **~90-day test period.** Operator keeps all revenue during the test.
- In exchange, JVL requires:
  - **QR scans.**
  - **COL-MID** (column ID — the machine number assigned by lottery at installation). Felipe supplied the name when Val couldn't recall it.
  - **Monthly lottery performance reports** — added by Felipe as a non-optional ask. Felipe was emphatic: *"otherwise we're going blind. We need that, because we might get some numbers that look either great or terrible to us, but we don't know what we're standing up against."* This is the report where the master scratches off the location name on the printed copy — Juan and Val both know it. Juan: "100% agree."
- Permission to publicise the operator's hopefully-good results.

**Timeline.**
- **June 7** (end of first week of June) is the current target product-delivery date.
- By **mid-to-late May** (week of May 18 onward) the team should know if June 7 is realistic. At that confirmation point, Felipe begins **concrete operator-by-operator discussions**.
- **~2 weeks** for Felipe to close 10–15 operator commitments. Val: a couple of operators per day is a realistic pace for Felipe.
- **End of June** = production rollout into the field.
- **August 18** = industry show. By then JVL expects ~6+ weeks of field collection data to use as a testimonial at the show.

**Felipe's question on changing the game mix: rejected.** Felipe asked whether the game-package composition could be shifted — for example, 1 Line + 3 Ways instead of 4 Lines & Ways, or 3 Jackpot Rush + 3 Fiery Ice instead of 2+2. Val shut it down hard: *"the game selection is set. It's engraved in stone. It's untouchable. We are operating beyond a tight schedule and a lot of people are nervous and unhappy. So we're not touching that."* Felipe: understood.

**Pre-show anticipation through Gamoa newsletter.** Felipe asked about creating anticipation in the trade press — "new games coming from JVL, we're cooking something" — without giving full disclosure, just enough to drive interest into the August show. Val: yes, do it. Juan confirmed the venue is the Gamoa newsletter. Val: the marketing message should specifically emphasise *the three non-symbiotic progressive mechanics in one machine*. Felipe asked for help translating "non-symbiotic" into marketing-friendly language. Juan accepted that as his undertaking; Val noted the meeting transcript itself can be fed to Claude to draft marketing copy.

**Meeting close.** Val: I'll circulate the meeting notes. Felipe: clear on the task, masters lined up.

## Decisions Made

- **Georgia V2 / V43 game package = 8 games: 4 Lines & Ways + 2 Jackpot Rush + 2 Fiery Ice.** Engraved in stone — no further changes. (Val, accepted by Felipe.)
- **Two additional premium titles held in reserve.** Added to the August 18 show only if June rollout performance is merely average, not if it's already strong. (Val.)
- **Field placement: 5–15 machines, target ~10, only in top-performing operator locations.** (Val, Felipe.)
- **90-day operator test; operator keeps revenue; JVL requires QR + Col-MID + monthly lottery performance reports.** Monthly lottery reports added by Felipe as a non-optional requirement. (Felipe, accepted by Val.)
- **Confirm-realistic point for June 7 delivery: week of May 18 onward.** Felipe begins concrete operator discussions at that point. (Val, Felipe agreed.)
- **2-week negotiation window for Felipe to close 10–15 operators.** (Val, Felipe agreed.)
- **Pre-show anticipation via Gamoa newsletter** — marketing line led by "three non-symbiotic progressive mechanics in one machine." (Val, Felipe, Juan agreed.)
- **Juan to draft marketing language** translating "non-symbiotic" into accessible copy, using the meeting transcript + Claude. (Juan.)

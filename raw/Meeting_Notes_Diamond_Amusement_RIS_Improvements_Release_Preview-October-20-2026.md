## Summary

Val and Felipe visited Diamond Amusements to present the RIS Improvements release. Val reminded them that at his last visit he promised to put other work on hold and make the changes they asked for. He handed over the printed release notes and asked them to read and approve. **Tentative delivery: end of November.**

Felipe went through the points:

- **Battery change:** no more hard reset; the machine will not go into critical error.
- **Software upgrade:** if the cabinet keeps the same games, peripherals, bet buttons and denominations, the upgrade reloads the previous settings. No settings file from Felipe is needed.
- **Printer (TITO):** a simple on/off switch in the settings menu. Diamond can do it themselves; no password or new settings file. A Diamond person asked about swapping a bad printer for the same type; Felipe: in theory yes, if the new printer has the right firmware.
- **Lease mismatch:** Felipe mentioned a call yesterday from a Diamond tech (transcribed "Kowalski") whose machine's lease had expired. After the release the machine keeps the previous lease, warns that an upgrade is needed, and does not shut off.
- **Peripheral replacement** (printer, bill acceptor, monitor): no more QR code / passport picture at the next hard reset. It registers at the next lease scan. Val: it is transparent to them.
- **Machines waiting for an upgrade:** the machine gets a temporary lease renewal and shows "upgrade pending" but keeps running. Val: JVL can set a reasonable period, for example 30 to 60 days.
- **Critical error:** the yellow screen (for example error 700) will name the cause, so a tech can fix it from a photo instead of extra trips.
- **Lease renewal:** one scan instead of three to five.
- **Cash-out tab:** a dedicated tab for non-TITO cash-outs (for example MudaGoose), so records are not overwritten in the log. The TITO cash-out tab already exists.

**Rollout:** new builds for P1, P2 and P3 first (Diamond has the most of these), end of November, then the rest. This first upgrade needs a new software install with a decommission and recommission, **only this time**. After that, settings are preloaded when nothing changes. Val also said JVL is adding cell modems to the machines and JVL pays for the data.

Diamond reacted well: this makes life easier for their techs in the field. Val asked them to tell JVL anything else they need: "I don't read minds." Val confirmed the trial of the two machines is extended and their software is being changed. Val expects a 10 to 30 percent improvement in net win from the new software, new games and tweaks. A Diamond person said one JVL machine is about to go out to a tier-one location. Someone at Diamond had a birthday.

## Decisions Made

- **Trial of the two machines is extended, with new software on both.** (Val)
- **First rollout goes to P1, P2 and P3 cabinets, end of November; one-time decommission and recommission for this upgrade.** (Val, Felipe)

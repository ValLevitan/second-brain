# Meeting Summary — South Dakota Vertical Game Lineup

**Date:** 14 May 2026, 7:57pm Eastern
**Duration:** ~25 minutes
**Segment:** 2 of 2 (lineup discussion — followed the Jira schedule review)
**Attendees:** Val Levitan, Michael Gottlieb, Juan Martinez, Lena Zhukova, Efim Podvoiski

## Summary

Working session on the South Dakota Vertical (VLT) game lineup and how to lay out three game categories on a vertical screen. Michael had already updated the rack (with Juan, day prior) and posted the latest draft during the call.

**Proposed lineup — up to 20 games.**
- 3 Keno products: **Black Hills Keno**, **Keno Deluxe**, **Cool Cat Keno**, plus **Solar** (4 Keno titles in the rack — Solar Keno added as the next entry).
- 6 Lines & Ways games: **Lions Riches**, **Wild Cult**, **Dragon Pays**, **Midnight Money**, **Aaron's Pub**, and a planned **Dakota Bison** (clone of Lions Riches reskinned in Twin Bison art, extending Lena's existing Twin Bison treatment).
- 5 Jackpot Rush titles + 6 Fiery Ice Link titles (these were posted under question marks — adds 11 to reach 20 if everything is enabled).

Nebraska gets the same lineup minus Keno.

**Jackpot Rush / Fiery Ice — Val's "no" was a misread.** Juan said the team was under the impression Val had said no to Jackpot Rush and Fiery Ice on South Dakota Vertical. Val pushed back firmly: *"I will never say no to something that you should be saying yes or no. It's out of my character. I just don't do things like this, ever."* Decision authority on the lineup remains with Juan/Michael.

**Active-games constraint.** Juan does not want more than 10 active games on screen at one time, via RIS — although all 20 can sit in the pack. This drove the rest of the screen-layout debate.

**Screen layout problem — fitting 3 categories on a vertical cabinet.**
- Lena pushed back against stacking 6 Lines & Ways buttons in a single row on vertical: at that pixel resolution buttons get too small, logos become unreadable, and you can't show seam-of-game during attract.
- Michael agreed; at that resolution the buttons reduce to game names (no Cleopatra-next-to-Queen-of-Luxor flourishes possible).
- Michael floated a "pyramid" — 4 Lines & Ways across the bottom with 3 Keno above, but the question is whether the top row becomes a reach.
- Juan argued Keno should be in the lower-right corner — that's what operators and players keep asking for — but balancing 3 Keno icons there is awkward.
- Michael suggested thinning category bars, dropping the yellow tag lines under each category, reducing gaps between buttons and category bars to free vertical space.

**Layered / expanding menus discussion.**
- Efim proposed a model: all 4 category bars always visible; touching one expands its row of games while collapsing the previously-expanded one.
- Michael initially heard this as a "layered menu" with go-back navigation (which he dislikes — needs prominent exit, attract-mode timeout to avoid players thinking the cabinet is Keno-only). After clarification he warmed to the *expanding-row* concept.
- Lena objected: asymmetric open/closed categories will look ugly; complex to develop correctly; not intuitive for the player.
- Michael flagged the question both ways: how do competitors with verticals handle this? If the leading vertical cabinet uses a layered menu, players already have familiarity and JVL isn't doing anything weird. Juan took the action to get Denis to pull competitor home-screen photos.

**Val's "liquid glass" suggestion.** Val proposed leaning into the liquid-glass aesthetic: keep the background hero image (rooster/lion/etc.) large and dominant, then overlay more-transparent buttons on top. Background provides contrast and "the size you want" while transparent overlays give the second-pane volume. Michael's concern: legibility when icons sit on top of background text/logos. Val countered with iPhone as a precedent — "iPhone does it quite successfully." Juan added that the multi-category banner labeling ("Keno") would compensate even if the icons themselves are hard to read. Michael said the icons would be readable; the issue is overlaying icons on existing logo art (e.g., the Calle Ocho logo) — he'd reformat the logo first. Val agreed the logo should move up.

**Two-banners-on-one-row variant.** Michael proposed: shift Lines & Ways to the left half of one banner, put Keno on the right half with a dividing bar; touching each side reveals only that category's games. Juan saw the elegance but noted it caps Lines & Ways at 4 games at a time, which forces him back to "give them the proven performers." Lena added that switching between the two halves would essentially be another carousel, which both Michael and Juan dislike ("There's nothing that's complicated that you can't make more complicated.")

**Pay-table / SAS concern.** Juan flagged a past experience with Novomatic where >20 games on a box overloaded the system, constantly sending pay-table IDs via SAS. Efim said he's aware there are limits but isn't deep on this — they need to consult the people who work directly on this. Efim suggested 20 games "may hit it easily." Michael's hedge: if there's a hard limit, choose Jackpot Rush *or* Fiery Ice as the third category for the first release, then add the other in a subsequent vertical release (or swap).

**Where they landed (working assumption).** Michael's verbal summary at the end: *6 Lines & Ways, 3 Keno, 2 Jackpot Rush, 2 Fiery Ice Link* — "and a partridge in a pear tree." Not a final design decision — Michael acknowledged "it's not a decision we have to make tomorrow. There'll be something elegant we come up with." Juan: 3 proven performers + 3 candidates (Bison, Aaron's Pub, others) is needed to learn whether the unproven ones have legs.

**Dakota Bison rationale.** Michael explained the move to clone Lions Riches as Dakota Bison: South Dakota's two lowest performers (Dutch Mill, Liberty Spins) are one-liners. He's wary of doing more one-liners for the state. Counterpoint that Georgia loves a one-line product (Crazy Spins is a top performer there) and prefers 3-by-3 grids — markets evolve differently. He cited proximity to Las Vegas as a possible reason South Dakota players want a casino-style experience. *"The data is the data. Ignore the data at your own peril."* Reusing Lions Riches art (extending Lena's Twin Bison work) is the efficient path.

**Closing logistics — vacations.**
- Efim: vacation **22 June – 5 July 2026**.
- Michael: in **Australia 18 June – 8 July 2026** to see his son and grandson (already previously mentioned).
- Val volunteered he will be working through that window: "I'm here, I'm working, so don't worry."

**Off-topic banter.** Lena is taking a sick day tomorrow (May 15), and Monday May 18 is Victoria Day (Canadian holiday). Val joked about Advil + Tylenol next to the computer. Brief riff about working with Claude — Efim: working with Claude doesn't relieve effort, it forces more decision-making (thinking) instead of mechanical work. Val: "This was Claude. Claude was my Trojan horse."

## Decisions Made

- **South Dakota Vertical pack = up to 20 games** containing: 3 Keno, 6 Lines & Ways (incl. Dakota Bison), 5 Jackpot Rush, 6 Fiery Ice Link. (Michael, Juan)
- **Active-on-screen cap = 10 games.** (Juan)
- **Dakota Bison added to the pipeline** — clone of Lions Riches with Twin Bison art treatment by Lena. (Michael)
- **Lineup decision authority sits with Juan/Michael, not Val** — explicitly reaffirmed. (Val)
- **Working layout (non-final): 6 Lines & Ways + 3 Keno + 2 Jackpot Rush + 2 Fiery Ice Link active.** Layout method itself (pyramid / expanding row / split-banner / liquid glass overlay) still unresolved.

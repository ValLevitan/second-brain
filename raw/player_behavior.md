Hi Michael and Juan,
I wanted to bring you both up to speed on a discussion I had with Efim and Anton about developing a Player Behavior Simulator, which will be the focus of our Monday meeting at 2 PM.
Meeting Objective:
I proposed creating a tool that would allow us (non-technical stakeholders) to simulate and analyze player behavior patterns on our games - not to test mathematical accuracy, but to understand how real players interact with our games.
Key Concept:
The simulator would emulate thousands of player sessions with configurable parameters such as:

Starting bankroll (e.g., player arrives with $200)
Target win goals (e.g., wants to leave with $500)
Session time limits
Betting pattern variations

Why This Matters:
While our games may be mathematically sound, we need to understand:

What percentage of players leave satisfied vs. frustrated
Whether our game mechanics create the intended player experience

Currently, we operate somewhat "blind" - we see revenue results but don't fully understand the player journey that creates those results.
For Monday's Discussion:
Please come prepared to discuss:

What player behavior scenarios you'd find most valuable to simulate
What reports and metrics would help you make better product decisions
Your vision for how this tool could improve our game design process

I believe this tool will help us develop our own "school of thought" for game design, moving beyond just copying competitors to truly understanding what creates engaging player experiences.
The meeting is scheduled for 1.5 hours. Looking forward to your input on shaping this simulator to meet our needs.

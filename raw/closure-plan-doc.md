# Canadian Manufacturing Operations Closure Plan
**Meeting Date:** July 29, 2024

## Executive Summary

This document outlines the comprehensive plan for closing Canadian manufacturing operations and transitioning all production to Georgia by December 31, 2024. The closure involves systematic transfer of inventory, equipment refurbishment, personnel transitions, and establishment of new warehouse facilities in Georgia.

## Meeting Participants

- **Val Levitan** - Meeting Leader
- **Mikhail Perelman** - Director of Procurement and Logistics
- **Genadi Peari** - Director of Electro-Mechanical Department

## Strategic Decision

Complete closure of all Canadian manufacturing operations with full dissolution of Canadian corporate presence by December 31, 2024.

## Personnel Transitions

### Canadian Team
- **Arkadiy** - Canadian operations manager to manage and support the transition
- **Andrei** - Will complete Echo assembly before transition

### Georgia Team
- **Anibal** - New operations manager arriving August 23, 2024

## Product Transfer Priorities

### Priority 1: Flex Products
Ship 100 complete Flex sets to Georgia for immediate production continuity.

### Priority 2: Echo Machines
Assemble and ship 40 Echo units:
- 20 units for amusement applications
- 20 units for home use applications

### Priority 3: Parts Inventory
Transfer approximately 500 sets of parts, consisting of:
- Plastic components
- Metal components
- Limited quantities of monitors, printed circuit boards, and touch screens

### Priority 4: Flex Machine Refurbishment
Rework and update 14 Flex machines:
- Units originally manufactured for Puerto Rico
- Units returned from Peru operations
- All units to be brought to current specifications

### Priority 5: Complete Machine Refurbishment
Refurbish all returned machines from Peru and Mexico operations using available parts inventory. These units will be shipped to auxiliary storage in Georgia.

## Warehouse Strategy

### Current Facility
- Toronto warehouse to be completely emptied and lease terminated by December 31, 2024

### Georgia Facilities
- **Primary Warehouse**: Existing operational facility
- **Auxiliary Storage**: New 5,000 square foot warehouse
  - Estimated cost: $8 per square foot
  - Location: Within close proximity to primary facility
  - Purpose: Storage of refurbished equipment and overflow inventory

## Financial Considerations

- All existing purchase orders to be cancelled
- No payments to be made on current orders
- New procurement to be handled through alternative channels

## Key Performance Indicators

- Maintain 130+ machines in operational inventory
- Successfully transfer 100 Flex sets
- Complete assembly and shipment of 40 Echo units
- Transfer all salvageable parts inventory
- Refurbish and ship 14 Flex machines
- Complete refurbishment of all Peru/Mexico returns
- Achieve zero inventory in Toronto facility
- Secure and operationalize 5,000 sq ft auxiliary warehouse

## Risk Mitigation

- Two-week buffer for initial shipping arrangements
- 30-day timeline for Echo assembly allows for delays
- Overlapping personnel ensures knowledge transfer
- Auxiliary warehouse provides overflow capacity
- Phased approach prevents operational disruption

## Conclusion

This plan ensures continuous manufacturing operations while executing a complete transition from Canadian to Georgia-based production. The systematic approach minimizes disruption to customers while maximizing asset recovery and personnel support during the transition period.

# South Dakota VLT performance story: October 2026

**South Dakota: October looks better, but the gain is luck. Play per machine fell again.**

A year ago we had 6 machines. In October 2025 each one made about $173 a day, twice the state average. Since then we grew to 56 machines. Total net machine income went up more than 4 times, from $32K to $139K a month.

But each new machine made less than the ones before. By April we were at the state average. Since June we are below it in every month except July.

**October was better on paper, but people play less:**
- $80 a day per machine, up from $74 in September. The state average is $83, so we are 4% below (9% below in September).
- Total net machine income was $139K, up 13% from September and 5% below the July peak of $147K.
- The gain is luck, not play. The machines kept 10.0% of the money played; the normal hold is 9.0%. At the normal hold, October would have made about $125K, or $72 a day per machine, the lowest ever.
- Each machine played 707 games a day. That is the lowest ever (754 in September, 883 in July).
- The average bet was $1.13, the highest ever. Fewer games, bigger bets.
- Wild Colt and Lion Riches bring in 46% of the money, about $18 a day each. Dutch Mill ($8) and Liberty Spins ($9) are still the weakest. Liberty Spins paid out only 88% in October, so its small gain is luck too.
- Fleet: 56 machines, one more than September.

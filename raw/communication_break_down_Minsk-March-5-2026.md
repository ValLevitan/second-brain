Pavel:
If we look at it objectively — we do have feedback loops, and quite a few of them. But they are fragmented by level: one loop operates at the executive level, others exist between developers, QA, and me or Irina. Each segment communicates within itself, but signals between segments travel poorly.
In essence, this is a classic hierarchical model: manager — subordinates. Information moves vertically and gets filtered at each level. The alternative is a flat organization, where feedback is not tied to the chain of command and anyone can directly share context with anyone else. The question is whether we are ready for such a model — and what would need to change to get there.

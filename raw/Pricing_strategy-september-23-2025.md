# Pricing Strategy
*Established: September 23, 2025*

## Core Pricing Structure

### Premium Gaming Machines
**Retail Range**: $16,000 - $18,000
- All machines include printers (~$1,000 value)
- Bill of materials: $15,000 - $18,000
- Target margins built into pricing

### Market-Specific Pricing
| Market | Price | Premium/Discount | Rationale |
|--------|-------|------------------|-----------|
| **South Dakota** | $17,200 | +$800 | GLI regulatory costs ($20K/year ÷ 100 units × 4) |
| **Texas** | $16,500 | Premium | Premium brand positioning |
| **Nebraska** | $16,000 | Baseline | Standard pricing reference |

## Pricing Philosophy

### Single Price Communication
**Critical Rule**: Sales team (Dennis) quotes ONE price only
- No matrix circulation
- No negotiation at sales level
- Executive-level discounts only

### Discount Structure
**CFO-to-CFO Credits**:
- Invoice shows full retail price ($18,000)
- Credits applied separately at CFO level
- "Bypassing everything else"
- Maintains price integrity

## Cost Pressures

### Tariff Impact
- **10-15% increase** on materials expected
- Primary source: South Korea (panels)
- Not primarily China-related
- "Cannot really avoid this"

### Component Costs
- Printers: ~$500 cost ($800 retail)
- Potential for refurbished JCMs
- All machines now include printers

## Competitive Positioning

### Premium Justification
- Below IGT ($18,000+)
- New technology vs 20-year-old VLCs
- Full feature set included
- "Lower than almost everybody in Class 3 casino space"

## Summary

Val and Mikhail went down a file of open purchase orders, vendor by vendor, and decided for each one whether to pay now, pay part, or wait. Genadi joined and spoke a few times. This is the meeting Val said he was trying to arrange with Mikhail in the alignment call two hours earlier — see [[Meeting_Notes_Texas_Flex_Slim_Release_Street_Cabinet_and_BOM_Source_of_Truth-September-23-2026]].

**Val stated his purpose plainly, twice.** *"I am trying to make less work for myself. My goal right now is not to find where accounting is wrong. I am trying to find less work for myself."* Mikhail: *"I want the same."* The session was therefore a triage, not an audit.

**A note on the figures below.** The recording is a spoken walkthrough of a spreadsheet in Russian, and the transcription of numbers and of Chinese vendor names is unreliable in places. The amounts and PO numbers are recorded as they were spoken. Where the audio is clearly garbled it is marked.

### Vendor by vendor

**Dongguan Bashida Import & Export** — seven invoices, small amounts, the vendor is not pressing. Val: pay in full.

**Dongguan Hi Yong Mall** *(name as spoken)* — seven invoices and the large ones. Val asked whether he has to pay everything. Mikhail said nearly all are due, and that for the molds they ordered the final payment splits into multiple payments — a breakdown he had already given to the team.

- **Mold order, $28,000 total.** $12,000 already paid. **$7,405 is due now** — this was the second payment, owed in June. It is now the end of September and it has not been made. Val: pay $7,405.
- **$200, $100 and $475** — Val: not a problem, no discussion needed.
- **$6,960** — a May order (Mikhail first said molds, then corrected himself to parts), which should have been paid by the end of August. A prepayment was made; the final payment is outstanding.
- **PO 370** — Mikhail checked: this one is a mold, so **30% can be paid**. On roughly **$11,600, that is about $3,480**.
- **PO 464** — manufacture of parts that must be paid before shipment. These are Flex parts, mostly for the **V43**. Val asked whether they can wait. Mikhail: some stock exists but it will not last to year end, and the timing is against them — it is the end of September, they ship, then a month and a half in transit, so **early November** at best.
- **PO 501** — DDP delivery covering the other POs. Val asked whether paying the other invoices would make the vendor relax this. Mikhail: no — and it is not only this vendor. He had contact with **Everglory** the same day, demanding the same: **40% for parts, 100% for parts.**
- **PO 440, $200** — modification of a mold. It is a form, so nothing needs shipping.
- **PO 456 / 356** *(number unclear in the audio)* — 20 sets, two parts for the CT-23, 20 pieces, **$130 for both parts**. Needs air freight.
- **PO 485, $475** — one missing part for Flex, part number **708-261**. 100 pieces already arrived by air; since it is a cheap part and they had to cast a batch anyway, 500 were made, and the remaining 400 will travel in the general shipment.
- **PO 313, $28,000** — a mold, **30% payable now**. This is the second 30%; the remaining 30% is spread over **six monthly payments**.

**Gen Touch — PO 446, $6,700.** One hundred 21" touchscreens at $67 each. **There is no stock.** Mikhail said the PO has been hanging unsigned for a very long time and some of it will probably have to go by air. The three of them could not agree on the weight: Mikhail guessed 3–4 kg, Genadi said about 400 grams, Val said about half a kilo. Mikhail's point stands regardless — glass can only ship crated on skids, because it is not something to risk, and **the packaging can weigh more than the goods.**

**Guangdong Hi Pray Technology** *(name as spoken)* — can wait. Mikhail was blunt about why: *"They did not perform well in the spring, so we are delaying the next release."* They held up one release by three months. This is the motherboard for **Echo**; they could not source the processor, which is made to special order.

**Hong Jing / Huanzhou** *(name as spoken)* — several small payments, parts for new projects. Must be paid, because one of them is needed urgently.
- **PO 453** — hardware for the 43". Sea freight only.
- The other two are for new projects.
- **Stock check on part 346: 87 pieces on hand.** Mikhail: that finishes September and October, and then there is nothing.

**Hong Kong Wanlin** — harnesses, small, going by sea.

**Jindao** *(name as spoken)* — a small order, already delivered, and the vendor is not asking for anything. Can wait.

**Shenzhen Everglory — very urgent.** Small orders, around $200 each. They have to ship urgently — by air and by sea express — **touchscreens for the V43**. Mikhail said that payment is not in this file yet and would come to Val for signature today; he had asked for a change to the transfer. Val thought he had already signed everything today. Two other Everglory POs: one for samples that were bought and shipped (Minsk, Kiev), and one for touchscreen board components — some had been lost and **Elizabeth asked for 15 sets**. That invoice was issued in **July**. Mikhail: better to pay now so it stops rolling over.

**Guangzhou / Hangzhou** *(name as spoken)* — payment can be delayed about two weeks.

**StarTech** — the company JVL buys televisions from. Payment can wait. Mikhail had ordered **70 extra sets of small televisions** on top of 30 already in stock, to reach 100.

**Yangzhou — springs, $3,000, 500 pieces.** Mikhail preferred sea, since they are not light and JVL buys in bulk to cover a long period. Val said the current stock only covers about **80 machines**, which will not be enough, and asked to send part by air. **Agreed: about 100 pieces by air, the rest by sea.**

**Genadi's closing point on air freight.** He argued they can ask for an air shipment at any time and the vendor will send within a week, so why pay the premium now. Mikhail's answer: the Chinese vendor says a week, but **JVL's own payment also takes a week** — so from the moment he starts today, that is already the fast scenario.

## Decisions

- **Pay Dongguan Bashida in full** — seven small invoices, vendor not pressing. *(Val Levitan)*
- **Pay $7,405** — the overdue second mold payment at Dongguan Hi Yong Mall, owed since June. *(Val Levitan)*
- **Pay 30% of the ~$11,600 mold on PO 370 — about $3,480.** *(Val Levitan, on Mikhail Perelman's confirmation that it is a mold)*
- **Pay 30% on PO 313** ($28,000 mold); the remaining 30% stays on a six-month monthly schedule. *(Val Levitan)*
- **The small POs — $200, $100, $475 — are not a problem and go through.** *(Val Levitan)*
- **Shenzhen Everglory is urgent and gets paid**, including the July invoice for the 15 touchscreen-board sets Elizabeth requested. A separate Everglory payment reaches Val for signature today. *(Val Levitan and Mikhail Perelman)*
- **Hong Jing gets paid** — PO 453 plus the two new-project POs; one is needed absolutely urgently. *(Val Levitan)*
- **Guangdong Hi Pray is held**, on Mikhail's judgement, because they delayed a release by three months in the spring. *(Mikhail Perelman, accepted by Val Levitan)*
- **Jindao waits** — already delivered, no pressure from the vendor. *(Mikhail Perelman, accepted by Val Levitan)*
- **Guangzhou / Hangzhou can be delayed about two weeks.** *(Mikhail Perelman)*
- **StarTech waits.** *(Mikhail Perelman)*
- **Yangzhou springs: about 100 pieces by air, the balance by sea** — against Mikhail's initial preference for all-sea, because current stock only covers 80 machines. *(Val Levitan)*
- **Prepayment before shipment is the standing vendor posture and will not be waived by paying other invoices.** Everglory demanded 40% / 100% for parts the same day. *(Stated by Mikhail Perelman)*

- **What a 21" touchscreen actually weighs.** Three different answers were given in the room (3–4 kg, ~400 g, ~half a kilo). It matters, because it decides whether air freight is affordable. Unresolved.
- **The V43 parts timing.** Ordering now means early November arrival, and current stock does not reach year end. Not explicitly decided whether that is accepted.
- **Part 346 runs out after October** — 87 pieces on hand. Flagged by Mikhail, no reorder decision recorded.
- **Whether Val's belief that he had already signed everything today matches the Everglory item Mikhail is sending.** The two statements did not reconcile in the call.

Business architecture and the website design for online - November 6,
2024

**Summary:**

Here's a summary of the main points from the meeting regarding the new
website development, product priorities, and the next steps in other
initiatives:

### **Meeting Goal**

-   **Purpose**: To align on the deliverables for the first release of
    the new website, agree on project expectations, and obtain a
    fixed-price project estimate from the contractor.

### **Key Points Discussed**

1.  **First Release Deliverables**:

    a.  **Objective**: Obtain art direction and initial concept (art
        vision) for the new website.

    b.  **Feedback Provision**: Up to three rounds of feedback on the
        art vision are allowed before confirming the design.

    c.  **Fallback Plan**: If the vision is unsatisfactory, the
        collaboration can be terminated, with payment for the work
        completed on art direction.

2.  **Revised Project Priorities**:

    a.  **New Priority**:

        i.  **First**: Emphasis on Amazon-supported "eco-amusement"
            products (previously online games were prioritized).

        ii. **Second**: Online gaming products.

        iii. **Third**: Land-based games.

    b.  **Rationale**: The Amazon segment is considered a substantial
        profit center, meriting higher priority.

3.  **Project Approach**:

    a.  **Parallel Development**: Online gaming sections to be worked on
        while financial forecasts for online gaming are prepared.

    b.  **Amazon Brand Registry**: Consider using Amazon\'s tools to set
        up brand content without creating a separate website initially.

4.  **Website Goals & KPIs**:

    a.  **Goals**: Boost brand recognition and credibility, drive
        organic traffic, and grow social media engagement.

    b.  **KPI Targets**: Increase organic traffic by 30% and social
        media audience by 20% within the first year of full site
        operation.

5.  **Self-Service Capability**:

    a.  **Long-term Goal**: Create a self-service model where clients
        can directly access game information, review standard contract
        templates, and execute contracts through DocuSign.

    b.  **Short-term Goal**: Begin with a minimal version of the website
        focusing on game access and client communication.

6.  **Modular Development Phases (Website Releases)**:

    a.  **Initial Release**: Design the landing page, game collection,
        individual game profiles, and essential contact forms.

    b.  **Second Release**: Blog module, latest news, and partner page
        for SEO and marketing.

    c.  **Third Release**: Closed client module to reduce administrative
        overhead by centralizing documentation and resources for
        operators.

    d.  **Future Phases**: Add "eco-amusement" support, followed by
        e-commerce integration and multi-language support as the site
        matures.

### **Timeline & Action Items**

-   **First Website Phase Deadline**: Aiming for completion by March 10.

-   **Next Actions**:

    -   **Send Document for Contractor Evaluation**: Refine vision with
        additions, such as self-service and specific goals.

    -   **Follow-Up Meeting on Flex-Pay Mobile Application**: Scheduled
        for November 20 at 10:30 a.m.

### **Additional Project Notes**

-   **International Expansion**: Explore selling eco-amusement machines
    through Amazon and similar Russian platforms (e.g., Ozon).

-   **Content Creation Strategy**: Focus on brand storytelling and game
    information to support both B2B and B2C engagement.

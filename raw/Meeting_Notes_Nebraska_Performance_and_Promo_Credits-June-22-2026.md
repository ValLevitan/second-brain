## Summary

Georgia V3 submission is complete. Next concept work shifts to Nebraska D27, pausing the vertical Dakota concepts Lena had started.

Nebraska performance is a concern. JVL is #1 or #2 in average bet but last or second-last in net win over the past 19 days. Val visited locations and found machines placed without strategy in drugstores — crookedly set up, varying heights, unattended. Light and Wonder has dual-screen with topper and leads performance; JVL has dual-screen without topper and trails. A key factor may be JVL's 25-cent minimum bet vs competitors' 40–50 cents. Michael argued the low bet lets one player camp on the machine at 25 cents, blocking higher-value play. Val agreed to raise minimum bet to match competitors.

The team confirmed the Nebraska strategy: continue with D27 for the next release, then follow with a vertical release. Michael noted that switching to vertical now would require re-jigging since D27 titles were already selected. Val wants to first improve D27 performance to #2 or #3 before pivoting.

Val endorsed the game rotation concept (Juan's idea): load many games, enable fewer, rotate based on popularity via modem. Operators are receptive. Lena flagged that Irina and Pavel need requirements for Georgia V3's game selection setup urgently — how games appear on the home screen when more games exist than display slots.

Promo credits emerged as a major initiative. Val proposed "Insert $20, get $2 free in promo credits." He cited a historical precedent from the 1980s where a similar tactic eliminated competition. The promo credits would hit a separate SAS meter, be non-cashable (restricted credits), but wins pay normally. Liability is just the theoretical win. Val wants this machine-centric — local, not dependent on cloud or kiosk. Juan will check Nebraska regulations.

Val wants toppers included free of charge on all machines (Nebraska and South Dakota). List price stays the same. The first 10 SD machines already came with toppers sitting in the office.

For South Dakota, vertical cabinets are necessary and Keno is a must — competitors have 7–9 fast Keno games. Michael confirmed JVL's Keno speed settings already match.

No player loyalty to specific game titles. Buffalo/bison is the one recognizable theme that stands out. Wheels are popular but will only be built in the new framework. Val wants an elegant new-game notification when games rotate in.

For street machines (in design), Val would add a VDEC with 18-inch screen and go to 45 or 47-inch single vertical main screen. Juan's concern was price; Val acknowledged cutting profit but said a compromised machine won't get played.

Friday meeting at 9 AM confirmed. Lena may be late (hospital appointment) and will message the team.

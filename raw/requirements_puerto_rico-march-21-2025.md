## Product Requirements Document
### Puerto Rico Product Rollout - 2025

### 1. Megahit Game Compatibility with Flex Machines
- The Megahit software must support Flex machines configured with physical button panels.
- Ensure Megahit compatibility in a two-monitor configuration (reduced from the standard multi-monitor setup).
- Implement necessary software framework adjustments to manage correct button inputs and monitor setups.

### 2. HG6 Software Button Panel Compatibility
- The HG6 software shall simultaneously support both legacy (old) and current (new) button panel hardware.
- Update and clearly document button panel configurations within the XML configuration files, mapping both old and new button panel key codes to avoid manual selection errors.

### 3. Final Approval of Button Panel Design
- Button panel designs for Altera V and Maple cabinets require formal approval from Juan Martinez.
- Approval must explicitly define:
  - Final panel configuration (number and arrangement of buttons).
  - Game selection and configuration limitations specific to the Puerto Rico market.

### 4. Regulatory and Certification Requirements
- All product configurations and hardware-software combinations must achieve certification approval from Gaming Laboratories International (GLI) for deployment in Puerto Rico.

### 5. Standardized Product Configuration Matrix
- Develop and maintain a detailed product configuration table, clearly defining:
  - Cabinet categories:
    - Retrofit Cabinets
    - CPU Kits
    - Flex Machines (including V43 vertical and D27 horizontal models)
  - Panel configurations (physical buttons or VDEC touch controllers)
  - CPU boards utilized:
    - Cabinet board
    - V1000 board
    - ARC-2000 board (as potential alternative)
  - Software packages:
    - HG6
    - Megahit
  - Game compatibility clearly defined for vertical and horizontal screen orientations.

### 6. Flex Machine Registration and Configuration
- Megahit software on Flex machines must recognize and adapt to fewer monitors and reduced touchscreen configurations as dictated by Puerto Rico’s regulatory and registration requirements.

### 7. ARC-2000 Board Evaluation
- Evaluate the feasibility and compatibility of integrating the ARC-2000 low-power mobile platform as a cost-effective hardware alternative.
- Clearly document performance and compatibility impacts before broader adoption.

### Next Steps:
- Technical and product management teams to verify technical compatibility and complete required software modifications.
- Juan Martinez to review and finalize button panel and game configurations.
- Initiate GLI certification processes for all outlined products and configurations.

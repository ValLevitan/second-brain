**About JVL**:

JVL is a prominent player in the slot machine manufacturing industry,
particularly distinguished in the state of Georgia as a leading supplier
to local operators. With an expert team of 80 professionals, JVL is
dedicated to crafting engaging casino products, living by its slogan,
\"We Take Our Games Seriously.\" This commitment is evident in every
aspect of its operation. At the heart of JVL\'s success is its lean
organizational structure, transforming clients\' needs into JVL's
innovations. This setup enables direct and seamless access to top
decision-makers for customers, and a slew of in-house specialist,
ensuring that JVL\'s products are bespoke solutions tailored to the
market. This advantage is particularly noticeable when contrasted with
larger competitors\' slower, more cumbersome processes. Beyond JVL's
significant presence in Georgia, JVL has ventured into select casinos in
Florida and is steadily increasing its influence in Latin America,
focusing on Peru, Colombia, and Mexico. These markets predominantly
favour a profit-participation model and align well with JVL\'s
expertise. JVL\'s foray into the online realm marks a strategic pivot,
demonstrating its adaptability and foresight. By integrating its
high-quality gaming content with various online platforms, they are
proactively responding to the evolving demands of the gaming industry.
Despite competition from larger industry players, JVL\'s steadfast
dedication to quality, customerization, and a deep library of slot games
underlines the ethos for its products, \"We Take Our Games Seriously.\"
This philosophy has helped JVL build a reputable name in the casino
gaming world and continues to open doors to new opportunities in both
physical and online gaming markets.

***JVL Insight*** : JVL's AI-Driven Knowledge Base.\
\
In applying JVL's efforts on implementing AI-driven applications, JVL\'s
objective is to create a Company-Wide Knowledge Base that can be
accessed from anywhere with the company's credentials. With the
development and implementation of ***JVL Insights***, JVL anticipates
significant improvements in knowledge sharing, decision-making, game
designing, work-flow, efficiency and overall productivity. Through these
tools, our employees will have access to a centralized and intelligent
system that leverages AI technologies in aggregating, categorizing, and
sharing the relevant tribal knowledge (something that the experts have
in their heads but is not necessarily documented) and information from
various sources in real time. ***JVL Insights*** will enable our teams
to quickly find accurate and up-to-date information, reduce duplicate
efforts, spend less time in meetings and make informed decisions more
efficiently. In other words, JVL Insights aims to break down the
corporate silos and offer the tribal knowledge in an organized fashion.

We aim to foster a culture of learning and sharing where employees can
easily contribute their expertise, learn from one another, and drive the
company\'s success.

Implementing ***JVL Insights*** aligns with our strategic goals to
optimize operational processes, enhance employee productivity, and
maintain our goal of "**ONE-JVL - ONE SOURCE OF TRUTH!**"

The ***JVL Insights*** initiative is designed to enhance knowledge
sharing and decision-making across various levels of the organization.
Several personas within JVL would particularly benefit from this tool:

**Product Developers and Designers**: These professionals need
up-to-date information on market trends, customer preferences, and
technological advancements to create engaging and competitive casino
products. ***JVL Insights*** would provide them with real-time data and
insights for their design choices.

**Marketing and Sales Teams**: These teams require detailed knowledge of
different markets, especially as JVL expands into new regions. The tool
would offer them tailored information on regional preferences, enabling
them to effectively target their marketing and sales strategies.

**Customer Support Staff**: With direct access to a comprehensive
knowledge base, customer support staff can more efficiently resolve
client queries and issues, ensuring a higher level of customer
satisfaction.

**Management and Executive Team**: For decision-makers, ***JVL
Insights*** offers a centralized source of real-time information,
facilitating informed decision-making and strategy development.

**IT and Technical Support Staff**: As they work on integrating and
maintaining various systems, having a tool that offers quick access to
technical documentation, troubleshooting guides, and best practices
would be invaluable.

**Human Resources Professionals**: They can use the tool to access and
share information about company policies, training programs, and
employee development resources, enhancing their ability to support the
workforce.

**Finance and Operations Teams**: These teams need up-to-date
information on company performance, market trends, and operational
efficiencies. JVL Insights can provide them with real-time analytics and
reports to aid in budgeting, forecasting, and operational management.

**Research and Development (R&D) Personnel**: Access to the latest
industry research, technological advancements, and competitor
information would be crucial for these individuals to innovate and
develop new products.

**Compliance and Legal Teams**: As JVL operates in different regions
with varying regulatory requirements, these teams can use JVL Insights
to stay updated on legal changes and ensure company compliance.

**Remote and Field Employees**: For those working outside the
traditional office setting, especially in expanding markets like Latin
America, having access to a centralized knowledge base ensures they
remain connected and informed.

**New Employees**: For onboarding and training purposes, JVL Insights
can provide new hires with essential company information, helping them
to quickly acclimate to their roles and the company culture.

**International Teams and Partners**: As JVL expands globally,
international teams and partners would benefit from a tool that offers
insights into local market trends, regulatory requirements, and cultural
nuances.

By catering to the unique information needs of these diverse personas,
***JVL Insights*** would significantly contribute to enhancing overall
efficiency, collaboration, and effectiveness within the company.

To design and implement ***JVL Insights***, a comprehensive AI-driven
knowledge base for JVL, it\'s essential to define both functional and
non-functional requirements. These requirements will help ensure that
the tool meets the specific needs of JVL and its various teams.

**Functional Requirements**

**Data Aggregation and Categorization**: Ability to aggregate and
categorize information from various sources within JVL, including market
trends, customer feedback, and internal documents.

**Search Functionality**: A robust search engine that allows users to
quickly find relevant information using keywords, phrases, or questions.

**Real-Time Data Access**: Provide real-time access to up-to-date
information and insights for all users.

**User-Specific Access and Customization**: Different access levels and
custom views based on user roles (e.g., Product Developers, Marketing,
Customer Support) to provide relevant information tailored to their
specific needs.

**Collaborative Tools**: Features for users to contribute, share, and
collaborate on knowledge within the platform.

**Integration with Existing Systems**: Seamless integration with other
JVL systems and databases for a unified user experience. Adobe, MS
Business Central, Atlassian suite of products (JIRA, Confluence, Xray)

**Training and Onboarding Resources**: Inclusion of training materials
and guides for new employees. Integration with ADP Payroll Application.

**Reporting and Analytics**: Capability to generate real-time analytics
and reports for decision-making and strategy development. Integration
with OLAP, Tableau and Excel.

**Mobile Accessibility**: A mobile-friendly interface or app to allow
access for remote and field employees.

**Non-Functional Requirements:**

**Performance**: High responsiveness and minimal downtime, ensuring
quick access to information.

**Scalability**: Ability to scale the system as the company grows and
expands into new markets.

**Security**: Robust security measures to protect sensitive company
data, including access controls and encryption.

**User-Friendly Interface**: An intuitive and easy-to-navigate interface
suitable for a diverse range of users with different technical skills.

**Reliability**: Consistent and dependable performance with minimal
errors or bugs.

**Compliance**: Adherence to legal and regulatory standards in all
operating regions, especially concerning data handling and privacy.

**Interoperability**: Compatibility with various operating systems and
platforms, ensuring seamless integration with other business tools.

**Data Backup and Recovery**: Reliable data backup and recovery
mechanisms to prevent data loss.

**Multilingual Support**: Support for multiple languages to cater to
international teams and partners.

These requirements will guide the development of ***JVL Insights,***
ensuring it effectively meets the diverse needs of JVL\'s teams and
supports the company\'s strategic objectives.

## Summary

**Nebraska: add precognitions and anticipations.** Michael played the Nebraska build; the games (especially Fiery Ice) feel flat. The first Nebraska release was rushed. He wants to add the Class 3 effects: precognition (an effect before something good happens) and anticipation (wind-up before the third trigger symbol). Most assets exist; Wildfire Ranch and Lions Riches need new ones. Lena: about two weeks of work. Val: Nebraska is not performing; he is glad. Michael is surprised how poorly the games perform; the newer match-3 progressive should also help.

**Why no precognition in Georgia.** Georgia uses nudge: the trigger is a stack on reel 3, made by a nudge, so Class 3 anticipations do not fit. Future 5-reel Georgia games will use dual box and get Class 3 presentation (precogs, anticipations). 3-reel Georgia games keep nudge — competitors use it and players expect it. Michael will confirm with Felipe when the decision is due.

**Class A math.** Efim and Michael reviewed Rodeo Spins (its re-spin must feel different from Party Time; Lena may change Party Time colors for this game) and Queen of Luxor (base game, pick bonus, free spins, Party Time; some event about 1 in 60 spins; volatile, acceptable for an Egyptian theme). Bayou Riches will be softer. Efim: first round of math for all six games by end of next week.

**Class A timeline.** No Eclipse approval needed — machines can go to locations right away. Production starts mid-January, after Nebraska, and will be built in NOVA, not Rage (no more framework changes in Rage). Storyboards for Party Time and dialogs are done. Lena's high-level estimate for six games: about one month for artists if Georgia V3 assets are reused; about three months if assets are redesigned — both assume NOVA and its tools are ready.

**Georgia V4.** Lena estimated April–May, maybe later: two complex new games — Fly Me to the Moon (a take on Aristocrat's Midnight Train, Georgia math) and a Colombian carnival game (a take on a firecracker game, 5×3 reels with firecracker strands). Val wants a few months in locations before the August trade show, so no later than June 1. Val: "let's try for June."

**Texas footprint and promo credits.** Val described Texas: two machines with a Flex Pay Slim recycler between them (vertical 14-inch screen, QR/barcode scanner, TITO-type printer, bill-to-bill recycler), and a Flex-Port AFT in each machine. The player inserts cash, gets a printed ticket that works as a temporary e-wallet, and scans it at the machine (optional PIN). Val wants to add loyalty and promo credits, and the same capability in Georgia and Nebraska (Georgia: card dispenser, no recycler).

Discussion: Efim asked how loyalty works with a temporary wallet; Val: short-term loyalty while the player keeps the same ticket, or a card. Val separated the two: promo = up-front credit that cannot be cashed out (played first, wins go to the cash meter); loyalty = kickback that can be cashed out. Efim and Michael doubt that $1 per $20 changes behaviour; to give $5 per $20 the base RTP must drop to about 75%, and then players may spend less time on the machine (Efim's estimate, not calculated). Michael: promotions can train players to wait for them. Efim and Michael prefer loyalty by amount bet, with clear thresholds, and the reusable ticket as a longer-term wallet. Val shared that extra credit per $20 worked on street machines in the 1980s. Val will think more. No decision.

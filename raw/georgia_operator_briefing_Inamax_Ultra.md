Operator Briefing — Inamax (Powered by Ultra)

Company Overview

Company: Inamax (Powered by Ultra)
CEO: Nick Damani
CFO: Ray Cosentino
Head of Operations: David Sherman
Operations/Warehouse Manager: Ashley Gunther
Lead Technician: Thomas Acosta

Inamax/Ultra is one of the most established, respected, and operationally sophisticated
master-operator groups in Georgia. Founded by Nick Damani and his wife Nadiya, the
organization has a long track record of innovation across Georgia and other jurisdictions.
They are credited with introducing one of Georgia’s earliest redemption systems in 1996
and continue to operate the Titan redemption platform (the system currently deployed at
Kroger locations, per prior field demonstrations).

Inamax’s reputation and scale give them outsized influence; what they standardize on
tends to shape what smaller operators adopt.



Relationship with JVL

JVL has a positive and stable relationship with Inamax across technical and operational
teams.

   •   JVL held several informal training sessions with their technicians in 2024.

   •   Inamax has experienced little to no technical friction with JVL games (based on your
       current history).

   •   Ashley Gunther is a key internal stakeholder: highly knowledgeable, operationally
       disciplined, and closely aligned with Ray Cosentino. She is not a technician by role,
       but is highly influential in standardizing products—if Ashley supports a product,
       adoption tends to follow.

Key Observations / Open Questions

volume has remained limited relative to their footprint.

Current working assumption:

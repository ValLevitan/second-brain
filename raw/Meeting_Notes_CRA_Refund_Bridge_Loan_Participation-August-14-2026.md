## Summary

Jacob called to understand when the CRA money actually arrives, because that timing is what makes the refund real security for a bridge loan. Val walked him through the status and then made clear he does not want outside money.

**Refund timing.** The certificate has been issued. The CRA website shows the assessment being issued **August 31, 2026**. Val called CRA twice and got no reply. Jacob's concern: in the ~16–17 days before then, nothing stops CRA from opening a review and holding the money. Val agreed delay is possible but said he is not worried the money is coming.

**Amounts as discussed on the call.** The federal portion at risk of review is **$350,000**; that still leaves **$1.35 million approved**, but Val accepted that if the federal portion goes to review the $1.35M gets delayed with it. Val said CRA has no jurisdiction to challenge the media tax credit portion.

**Why Val thinks review risk is low.** JVL is a clean, simple company with no internal maneuvering and all taxes filed. SR&ED returns have been filed for more than 20 years; the media tax credit for roughly 7–10 years. The media credit requires proof that JVL's games contain an element of skill or human manipulation — JVL went through that review, audit and challenge three to five years ago, got a favourable decision that is on file, and has not been challenged since. Jacob agreed a first review exists to validate the technology against the mandate.

**Service complaint route.** Jacob suggested filing a CRA service complaint, which he has had success with. Val said he already knows the tool — he filed one complaint last year after about a year of delay and was paid immediately. But a complaint is not available here because **there is no assessment yet**. Val expects a grace period after the assessment letter to pay the amount and was unsure whether it is 30 or 60 days.

**Val's $600,000 advance.** Val has already advanced **$600,000**, which after the processing fee is **$576,000 cash** to the company. Terms follow Doug's guidance: **4% fee plus 10% annual interest**. Val said the company could not wait for a loan, so he advanced. He is issuing a notice **today** inviting all warrant holders to accede to that same loan on the same terms, at each shareholder's discretion, **within 30 days**, and he does not mind extending. He said he asked Doug for guidance specifically so the terms would not be ungodly for the company, that this is not about cash or making money, and that the point is the timing of a lifeline.

**Evidence dispute (unresolved).** Jacob asked to validate the August 31 target — ideally by speaking to a CRA agent, or with a screenshot from Val's CRA online account. Val pulled it up, could not share externally, and emailed Jacob a CRA screenshot. Jacob said it appears to be for the **2025** tax year, not the **2024** return that carries the SR&ED claims, and asked for the equivalent screenshot for the 2024 T2. Val said the media tax credit claim covers 2023 and 2024 because he has 18 months to go, and that 2025 has not been filed at all; Jacob's point was that you can go back two years, so a 2024 T2 should show something similar. The two did not reconcile this; Val said he did not follow what Jacob was describing.

**Val refuses outside capital.** Jacob said he has a group that could raise the capital. Val said flatly he does not want to deal with a group and does not want anybody else in his business — if Doug wants to put money in, fine; if not, fine. Jacob clarified Val would deal only with Doug, who would assemble the group. Val still declined: he is working 16-hour days with no weekends and having another person to report to is the last thing he wants. He kept the door open anyway — Jacob can contribute on the last day, even the day the money arrives, and Val will still give him his share of the proceeds.

**Val's motivation.** Two reasons for continuing: protecting everybody's investment, and defending his life's work, his name on the company, and his standing in the industry. He acknowledged that everyone has a cash crunch, that nobody wants to park money in a company that has promised recovery for ten years, and that he may be blinded by his own attachment to JVL.

Jacob closed by saying he will take it back, do some digging, and talk to Doug.

He described it as $600,000 advanced, "which minus the processing fee is equal to five hundred seventy-six thousand dollars cash." He did not say aloud that the $24,000 gap is the 4% fee, though the arithmetic matches. (Decided by Val; already executed before the call)

- **A notice goes out today inviting all warrant holders to accede to Val's loan on the same terms, 30-day window, extendable at Val's discretion** — Val must open the same terms to everybody. (Decided by Val)
- **No outside group capital** — Val will not take money from a group or add anyone he has to report to. Participation stays open on Val's terms to the individuals themselves: Doug directly if he wants in, and Jacob personally, right up to the last day ("you could contribute on the last day, I will still give you the proceeds"). (Decided by Val)
- **No CRA service complaint for now** — the tool does not apply before an assessment exists. (Val; Jacob had proposed it)

## Escalation — added 2026-08-14 after the call, from the screenshot itself

The screenshot Val sent Jacob does not evidence the refund, and reading it closely makes an existing worry worse rather than better.

**Attribution, so the record is honest about who said what.** Jacob's concern on the call was *timing* — when CRA processes and issues the notice, and whether a review could hold the money. He treated the 2024 return as filed ("I know this is for a twenty twenty four filing"; "the tax return that included that filing refund… was submitted") and asked for the 2024 tracker only so his timing evidence would be about the right return. **The doubt over whether the amended 2024 T2 was ever transmitted is not Jacob's — it is drawn after the call from the missing 2024 tracker plus the pre-existing open item in the handover note.** Both questions happen to need the same artifact.

1. **The August 31 target is the FY2025 initial assessment, and it is the wrong refund.** The tracker covers the 2025-12-31 T2, filed 2026-06-29 and received 2026-06-30 per [[CRA_corptax_T2_return_received_JVL_Labs-June-30-2026]]. That return is the integrated SR&ED plus T2 package and does carry a claim, but the expected refund on it is roughly **$370,000**, due around September 2026. It is not the **$1,693,589** on the amended 2024 return, which is what secures the bridge. So August 31 is a real date attached to about a fifth of the money. (Corrected 2026-08-14: an earlier version of this section said no credit claim rides on the FY2025 return. That was wrong.)
2. **There is still no proof the amended 2024 T2 was transmitted.** [[Skrepnek_bridge_tax_credit_refund_handover-August-14-2026]] already records this: Inna signed the electronic filing authorization on 2026-07-21 and Nick Gardner (`ngardner@gcollp.ca`, Gilmore & Company LLP) said he would submit, but there is no transmission confirmation and no File Status Update for the 2024 year. Note that CRA does send those automatically and the vault holds the one for the FY2025 receipt, so the absence of a 2024 equivalent, and of a 2024 progress tracker beside the 2025 one, is real evidence and not just a gap in filing.
3. **Val's statement on the call that 2025 has not been filed is incorrect.** It was filed 2026-06-29. What is still outstanding for 2025 is the OIDMTC claim, which was deliberately held back (Ontario Creates deadline June 30, 2027), not the return itself.
4. **This touches the warrant-holder notice.** [[Warrant_Holder_Notice_and_Participation_Procedure-August-14-2026]] describes the security as the 2024 refund. If the 2024 return is not filed, the notice misdescribes the security to every holder, and the sent email that told Jacob the return went in during late July needs correcting.
5. **It also touches what was said to Jacob and Doug on the calls.** August 31 has been used as the date the money becomes real. On this evidence it is the date for roughly $370,000, not for the $1,693,589 the bridge is secured on.

**Next step, ahead of anything else:** get Nick Gardner to confirm in writing whether and when the amended 2024 T2 was transmitted, and pull the CRA progress tracker for the **2024** tax year from the same My Business Account screen.

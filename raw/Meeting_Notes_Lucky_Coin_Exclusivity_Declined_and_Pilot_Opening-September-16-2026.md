## Summary

This is Kyle's answer to the exclusive-partnership offer Val put to him at the September 9 shop visit ([[Meeting_Notes_Lucky_Coin_Service_Burden_and_Exclusive_Partnership-September-9-2026]]). The answer was due Tuesday September 15; the call came Wednesday.

**Kyle said no to exclusivity, and only to exclusivity.** His words: *"as much as I appreciate your offer, the exclusivity... I just don't know that I want to put myself in a box like that. As fast as the technology is changing in this state."* He was explicit that nothing else about the offer bothers him.

**He offered two things instead.** More JVL machines worked into his route, and a trial of the kiosk in a location or two.

**Kyle explained why the answer took a week.** Lucky Coin is a family business. Most decisions he makes alone, but an offer of that size he wanted to talk over with Curtis and Barry first. He said plainly that Val was not too aggressive and that nothing in the way they have spoken so far put him off: *"I feel like we have a little chemistry, so we'll keep working together and we'll make something go."*

**Val apologised for the pressure and then stepped back deliberately.** He said his ask was a lot, that he is fast and works on handshake commitments, and that it bothers people. Then he parked the pursuit: *"I'm not gonna pursue this business. I'm gonna leave it in your court... Let it sit, and you do things at your pace. I'm here for you."* Kyle said he would let Val know once he has the details.

**The state of the account.** Not a loss. Exclusivity is dead for now; a non-exclusive route expansion plus a kiosk pilot is live, and the next move belongs to Kyle by Val's own choice.

## Summary

**Who he is.** Arpit is a master licensee who started in 2022, alone, with no partners. He has about 50 locations, mostly in Metro Atlanta and a few in South Georgia. He says 80% are high volume (over $20,000 a day). Locations average about 5 machines. Aristocrat leads; IGT is slowing. He has about 60 Banilla machines, mostly sitting idle, 8–10 old IGTs, and fewer than 10 JVLs (3–4 active, horizontal). He does not operate in other states.

**Demo (Andrei).** Flex-IQ on the counter: each cashier logs in with a personal PIN; the cashier creates a wallet; the player loads money, plays any machine with a Flexport, scans the ticket back to the wallet and moves to another machine. Flexport works on any dual-SAS cabinet; single-SAS works by pulses. One printer prints all tickets, so no TITO printer per machine. A designed printer enclosure arrives this Friday. The kiosk is a card dispenser in Georgia and can be a cash dispenser later. The master sees all locations in a live cloud interface; locations see only their own data. An AI assistant for game performance is planned. Lost tickets can be reprinted from the history.

**His pain points.** Banilla's TITO system (user experience, communication errors outside metro areas). The bigger one: prize-card processors cap high-volume locations, and stores run out by about 2 PM. One provider gives his best locations $10,000 a day.

**Val on daily caps.** JVL is a broker between the location and InComm, so JVL sets the limit, not InComm. If the location keeps a positive balance, there is no interruption. JVL checks the bank balance automatically several times a day. Weekends are the risk (Friday night to Monday). Val's proposal: a guarantor account or a credit card on file; JVL pre-authorizes the card, takes what the bank account has, and releases the card. JVL can text the location when the balance is low. Arpit says his locations always keep a positive balance.

**Sign-up.** Locations fill a short web form (address, license numbers, bank account). JVL pre-authorizes and sends the location to InComm, which approves within 48 hours. The counter can offer several card providers (e.g. Fiery Pay); a kiosk is limited to one card type per dispenser.

**Install.** Arpit sends the machine list per location; JVL prepares a bracket for each machine with the port pre-installed. Three cables; 10–15 minutes per machine. A wireless, self-configuring controller is due "in a couple of months"; large-scale Wi-Fi roughly from 2027. Machines without an AFT opening get a fully enclosed surface-mount Flexport. All equipment has LTE modems; JVL pays the data.

**Split entry.** The system can record part of a win as spent on lottery tickets. Andrei said JVL was not advertising this feature.

**Show special and fee.** Felipe: the machine (10 games visible + 5 in the bank) is $3,750 without the topper; Val: about $800 more with the topper. With it come Flex-IQ, FlexPay/Slim, a printer and 6 Flexports free. Val put it as "all of it for under sixteen thousand dollars." Prices change after January 1. Monthly fee: **$90 per location, not per device**, from November 1, collected by ACH; the equipment stops if the fee is not paid. Val said competitors charge $1–1.50 per device per day. Extra games are about $250 each.

**Games.** The "Diamond" family (name as heard) has linked progressives in three categories (bet level, high roller, mystery). New hardware reports game popularity by cell modem; JVL may not switch games remotely, only advise.

**Next step.** Felipe: a game can be placed before November 1 under a signed agreement. Val: start with one location, learn, then roll out ("hurry slowly"). Business cards to be exchanged; a follow-up call with the game list was offered for tomorrow morning if he has not replied.

**After he left.** Felipe and Val felt very positive; Felipe said this was the first time he saw the full flow working on SAS. Andrei said he is concerned about how much work remains before production and asked to sit down today on what is missing (see [[Meeting_Notes_Transactional_MVP1_Launch_Plan_Brackets_Daily_Caps_and_MVP2_Scope-October-14-2026]]).

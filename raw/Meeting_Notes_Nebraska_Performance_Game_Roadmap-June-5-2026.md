# Meeting Summary — Game Development (Nebraska Performance & Game Roadmap)
**Date:** June 5, 2026, 5:58 PM Eastern
**Duration:** ~46 minutes
**Attendees:** Val Levitan, Juan Martinez, Michael Gottlieb, Lena Zhukova, Efim Podvoiski

## Summary

**Nebraska is the main worry.** Val opened with "what's next for us" and went straight at why JVL isn't performing in Nebraska. There are only 3 customers and ~11–12 machines (all upgraded). One operator (Ambleside) bought outright and has 120 locations, but has sent no real data yet. The other two (NTS, TNT) are hit-and-miss with missing days. Win-per-unit runs as high as $200–240 but some spots are $40–60. The one clear fact: Light & Wonder is outperforming everybody. They got approved in February, so their machines are less than 45 days old.

Val does not believe the easy explanation that Nebraska players are loyal to L&W's brands. He thinks something structural is going on that JVL isn't seeing — the same mistake they made in Georgia, where they leaned on famous brands and missed that L&W had changed how skill-stop works. He also blames the field rep (Denis), is replacing him, and is going to Nebraska himself. The replacement is a formally trained mathematician — logical and systematic.

A big data problem: Nebraska runs the intralot system (like Georgia) but has not enabled manufacturer reports, so there's no game-level data. South Dakota gives detailed reports; Nebraska gives nothing.

**Plan for Nebraska: pump out games and see what sticks.** Since approval in Nebraska is simple and JVL has a deep library, the emergency move is to push more titles into the market while they're flying blind. Two top performers (Lion's Riches, Wild Colt) aren't even in Nebraska yet. The current Nebraska release leans on 3x3 games (Clover Fortune, Gem Brilliante, Dutch Mill), and South Dakota data shows players don't like 3x3s.

**Texas branding — reversed.** Until now JVL deliberately hid that Texas machines were Nebraska carryovers (nothing said "Texas"). Juan argued they should brand Texas games as Texas (Lone Star, Yellow Rose of Texas, Rodeo Queen) since they're legally skill games anyway. Val agreed. This is symbols and names only — not the progressive math. Michael will think through Texas-specific designs.

**Vertical machines.** Val wants vertical introduced in Nebraska (and South Dakota) to "salt the pot." Juan wants to talk to the two big customers directly (without Denis) before committing — if they won't buy the existing D27 dual-screen games, JVL goes straight to vertical.

**Design review — Solar (Keno game).** Lena showed the new main screen with "liquid glass" borders over the Keno board. Val liked the border a lot. It will animate: planets light up, the border fills and grows, then transitions into free games with a fire theme (orange/yellow numbers, fireballs as extra draws). Val wants the motion to look intentional, not broken.

**Queen of Luxor.** Lena flagged this Egyptian game (built ~2015 off IGT Cleopatra) as looking dated and proposed redressing it for Georgia V3. Juan cautioned: wait for game-level data from the first Georgia Vertical install (via Felipe, ~August) — it might just get swapped out, not rebuilt. Michael agreed to hold; defended keeping an Egyptian title since there's still demand. Efim had only old, thin vertical data (~$300K total coin-in on a few machines); one title was running ~20% of play on a six-game machine, which Michael called solid. (Transcript was unclear whether that 20% was Queen of Luxor itself.)

**Roadmap order:** finish Georgia V2 → Georgia V3 (planned through August) → South Dakota vertical (SD horizontal still needed too). A bigger framework switch is coming because of AI, but "not tomorrow."

**Logistics:** Michael in Australia June 18 – July 8 (will ask to move some meetings). Lena on vacation July 10–21. Efim away the same stretch as Michael. Next Friday's call (June 12) may move to Thursday June 11 — depends on Val's travel; Val to decide. Val closed by stressing he lives by the JIRA schedule and wants tasks moved/updated as work happens, meeting or not.

## Decisions Made

- **Replace the Nebraska/SD field rep (Denis); Val goes to Nebraska himself.** Reason: poor performance and no usable data from the ground. (Decided by Val)
- **Emergency tactic: keep pushing library titles into Nebraska to see what sticks.** Reason: scarce data, simple approval, deep library. (Val, endorsed by Michael)
- **Juan to write an REQ for a second Nebraska release, sequenced after South Dakota vertical.** (Val set the order; Juan to execute)
- **Allow Texas-specific branding (names/symbols only, not progressive math).** Reason: games are legally skill games; no need to hide it. (Juan, agreed by Val)
- **Hold off on the Queen of Luxor redesign until game-level data arrives (~August).** Reason: data may show it should be swapped, not rebuilt. (Michael, Lena, with Juan's caveat)
- **All new releases use the dual box skill for now.** (Michael/Juan)

## Summary

Introductory call with Herring Bank, arranged through John at InComm. Campbell is a principal of the bank (family name on the building, ~127 years old, "a fintech masquerading as a financial institution"). Maya is Val's business liaison there.

Context: JVL makes slot machines sold in Georgia, Nebraska and South Dakota. In Georgia JVL is introducing transactional products, one with ATM-style cash dispensing. Val asked whether such a machine must be registered with an ATM network.

Campbell's answer: if a network card initiates a cash dispense, the card activity is what requires network sponsorship. Flow: card triggers the transaction → Herring authorizes it → the machine dispenses → settlement follows. Herring is a principal network member bank with its own APIs; it can hold balances, transmit money, and support cash dispensing or receiving.

InComm's Prize Play card (John's product) is the driver. Use cases discussed: a player cashing out from the card at a machine, and machines accepting the card directly. Val has not yet decided whether machines should accept all network cards or only loaded cards; he will follow market requirements.

Business structure: JVL is not the operator, and Herring wants the economic agreement with whoever owns the cash in the machine. Val will instead consolidate the operators under a single agreement with himself and route them all to Herring one way, which Campbell welcomed. Herring would hold a non-economic support agreement with JVL for the machine and software.

Rough costs from Campbell (explicitly loose): cost of cash in the machine around prime + 1%; per-transaction fee roughly 2–10 cents. Real pricing needs use cases and volumes.

Herring can also provide general banking in the three states, and ATM cash replenishment through their armored service — Maya can set that up for Val or his customers.

The contract counterparty will be Herring Bank. Ricky Rodriguez runs Herring's product side; the working group will be Val, Maya and Ricky. Herring wants a JVL machine in its lab for testing. Maya will attend the Georgia Co-op Expo for a day; JVL has a booth there and Val will show the equipment.

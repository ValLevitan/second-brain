# Meeting Summary — Alignment: Echo Marketing & Sales Update
**Date:** March 27, 2026, 9:20 PM Eastern

**Segment:** Segment 1 of 2
**Attendees:** Val Levitan, Andrei Klimovich

## Summary

Andrei provided a comprehensive update on the Echo product's marketing and sales performance across multiple advertising platforms, website optimization efforts, and sales pipeline activity.

**Amazon & Advertising Performance**

Andrei reported working with an Amazon manager on moving the Echo product to a different category within the Amazon catalog, which is expected to boost organic traffic. New advertising campaigns have been designed and will launch within the next week alongside updated creatives. One order came through Amazon this week. A 15% Easter/Spring discount campaign is currently running — no direct sales from it yet, but 23 carts have accumulated over the week, and Andrei expects some to convert into orders soon.

No distributor orders this week, but two new contacts are in the pipeline: one reseller and one operator who wants to purchase machines for installation in bars. Both have asked questions and Andrei is waiting to see how they develop.

**Ad Cost Optimization**

Working with the ad specialist, Andrei reduced the Meta (Facebook/Instagram) cost per cart from $20 to $11 — nearly a 50% decrease. Cost per click on Meta is now 20 cents, down from an initial 50-60 cents. Andrei noted that as ads run longer they tend to exhaust the primary audience and costs rise, so they plan to regularly refresh creatives to maintain performance.

**Google Ads Issues**

Google partially blocked the account this week with another gambling suspicion strike. The team spent the week proving they are not a gambling operation. The campaign is only now starting to run again, but CPC has spiked to $1 (should be 15-20 cents). Andrei is monitoring through the weekend — if the price doesn't normalize, they will pause the campaign and investigate the root cause.

**Bing & Twitter Assessment**

Bing advertising is stable in pricing but produces zero carts. Audience research and customer surveys confirm the target audience does not use Bing. Andrei plans to let the Easter campaign finish and then shut down Bing advertising entirely.

Twitter is extremely expensive at $2.50 per click versus 20 cents on Meta — a roughly 12x cost difference. It generates some traffic but no carts. Customer surveys confirm Twitter ranks 5th or 6th among platforms used by the target audience. Andrei consulted with multiple ad buyers who confirmed Twitter is generally 3-4x more expensive than Meta. His recommendation: stop Twitter after the Easter campaign and potentially engage a Twitter-specialist freelancer with a limited budget to test whether a domain expert can achieve better results.

**Website & SEO**

The Google trust index for the website grew from 11 to 13 points this week. Reaching 20 points would mark the site as reliable in Google's eyes, and Andrei knows what steps are needed to get there. The team is optimizing the site to grow organic (free) traffic. Importantly, they are planning to move away from the external web development agency and transition all website management to Claude (AI tool), enabling any team member to make changes and additions without developer dependency.

**Knowledge Base**

Andrei built a unified knowledge base synthesized from customer research, accumulated product knowledge, and market data. All team members — designers, copywriters, and the Amazon specialist — now operate from this shared resource. Andrei is already seeing results: fewer questions about copy and ad targeting. The goal is zero ad-hoc questions, with the knowledge base refreshed quarterly.

**AI Tooling Discussion**

Val suggested Andrei adopt Claude Code desktop as a tool for managing files and building skills-based workflows. Val explained how Claude can accumulate its own knowledge base through "skills," manage files on the computer, and produce high-quality outputs including transcripts — handling Russian-English code-switching and jargon through a lexicon table. Val noted that while today's general AI won't fully autonomously manage marketing, the technology is advancing rapidly toward "superintelligence" within the next year or two, and starting to use Claude now will yield compounding returns. Andrei expressed enthusiasm and prefers to learn by doing — he plans to start using it immediately.

**Seasonal Sales Context**

Andrei reviewed last year's data: March had only one sale as well. Helium 10 historical data on Amazon competitors confirms that January-March are consistently low months, with sales starting to pick up from April onward.

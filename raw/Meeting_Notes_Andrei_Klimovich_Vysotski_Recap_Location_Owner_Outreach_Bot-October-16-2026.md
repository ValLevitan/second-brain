## Summary

**Owner data found so far.** Andrei said public business-registration databases show which business runs a given location (for example, a gas station). Some contacts were found through those businesses, but not all are current; some data may be from 2019. The businesses were ranked by number of registered locations, giving a top list of businesses that may own many locations. For about 70% of locations no owner-level contact was found. There are a few more ideas for where AI could look; otherwise someone must call the location itself and ask which business runs it.

**The "Sirius" path.** Val asked why not go the way he proposed, through "Sirius" (name as transcribed). Andrei said Sirius is part of the conference trip next week. Sergei passed on all the information and will handle it. Val said this is the most promising path.

**How to get consent to contact owners.** No final decision. The current idea is an individual who reaches out on behalf of clients interested in retail-marketing contacts. Andrei said at this first stage the company name should not appear, and maybe the machines should not be mentioned either. Val said the story is simple: Sergei collects a database and has clients who want to sell to these owners directly — nothing to worry about.

**Voice bot.** Val estimated that of about 7,000 accounts, maybe half (about 3,000) can be made reliable, and asked how they will be contacted physically. Answer: a bought voice-bot service, not one JVL builds. It must be found, set up and trained, and then it calls and talks to people by voice. Andrei said Sergei Vysotski can choose and set it up when the time comes. There is no second plan yet.

**Call center as plan B.** Andrei said a call center is a separate task with no decision. It would need people who match the owners' background — owners are expected to be mostly Indian or Pakistani, with a few Korean. The cover story would need even more care.

**Sergei must cut his time.** Val said that in the past week things changed: Sergei must greatly reduce his load on this project because his own business is growing. Andrei did not know the details. Sergei once said he has a group of people he could hand the work to. Andrei said this guarantees nothing — the work is specific — but at minimum an introduction will be made.

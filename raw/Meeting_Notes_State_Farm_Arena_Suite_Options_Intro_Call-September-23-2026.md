## Summary

First call after Val's website inquiry. Caitlyn sells premium hospitality at State Farm Arena, home of the Atlanta Hawks. She wanted to understand what JVL is trying to do; Val wanted to know what a suite costs.

**What Val wants.** Hosting partners and customers in the Atlanta area is a new thing for JVL. The purpose is brand building and staying top of mind with existing clients. Val wants a suite for **8 to 12 people with food catering**, about **4 to 5 events a year**, and he will host CEO-level guests himself. He is only interested in Hawks basketball games, not concerts. Fall, winter and spring suit him; summer is hard because people in his industry travel. He has never been to the arena. He lives in Miami and the JVL business is in Lawrenceville, so getting downtown is a consideration for him.

**What the arena offers.** Caitlyn laid out four paths:
- **Per-event suite rental** — price depends entirely on the game or show, roughly **$3,000 to $25,000** each. A **$500 deposit** buys priority to purchase and is applied to the total; the balance is due in full.
- **Premium Reserve** — a prepaid bank of money, **$25,000 minimum**, drawn down against suites or club seats as you choose. It is a year-over-year commitment on a July 1 – June 30 fiscal year, so this year's funds run to June 30, 2027. Unused money is not forfeited, but the priority-to-purchase status lapses.
- **Full-season suite lease** — from an 8-person suite upward, roughly **$210,000 to $500,000**, typically a **3 or 5 year commitment**, covering every game and every show.
- **Loge box** — a smaller 4-person version of the lease.

**Food is included.** With suite leases, beer, wine, food, water and soda are bundled into the price. Liquor is not, and would need a separate conversation.

**The number Val asked for.** For five games a year in an 8–12 person suite with catering, Caitlyn said **$30,000 to $40,000**, bought as individual rentals rather than a lease. Suites for this season are already on sale, so inventory on the best games may be thinner, but options remain.

**Season shape.** NBA regular season runs October to April. The arena also hosts 50–60 concerts and shows per fiscal year — Caitlyn named Kacey Musgraves next week, Jonas Brothers, Journey in October, Olivia Rodrigo around the holidays, and WWE earlier this month.

**Next step is a site visit.** Val is in Atlanta the week of October 12 and arrives Tuesday October 13. The arena is only free on Monday October 12 and Wednesday October 14 — Tuesday, Thursday and Friday are blocked for concert load-ins, and Val cannot be there on the 12th. Nothing was booked. They agreed Caitlyn calls Val on October 12 to see how his week looks, targeting a tour on **Wednesday October 14**, with Val's own fallback that he will probably be back the following week anyway.

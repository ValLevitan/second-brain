## Summary

Held in Russian. Continues from [[Meeting_Notes_Hardware_Repo_BOM_Source_of_Truth-September-24-2026|Segment 1]]. The transcription is rough in places.

**Monitor, panel and scanner.** Val described a competitor design ("Lighting Wonder" as heard in the transcript): the TV sits like ours, with a step-down and a panel on an arm below it. Genadi said the scanner must then be mounted higher, about 10 cm from the player. Val said the scanner can sit under the monitor.

**Player tracking panel stays.** Genadi understood from the last meeting that Val wanted the TV lowered to drop the player tracking panel. Val said no, he does not propose that. The street machine keeps our port, as on the Flex.

**Vertical monitor tilt.** Val wants the same tilt as now. Genadi said the tilt causes problems: when the monitor opens it can swing back and hit the technician unless it is spring-loaded. Val thought offset hinges could solve this. Genadi prefers a different layout: open the door, pull out a shelf above it with all the electronics in front, and never touch the monitor. He said this is cheap and simple.

**Hanging the TV.** Genadi said the monitor is heavy (three people to hang it); today one person hooks it on. Val said he can agree to hanging the TV. He thinks the mistake was using hooks with pins to hit, instead of a crossbar to hang on. Genadi said there are two flat ribs, and it is easy. Val said he can be persuaded on the shelf idea, but he does not yet see how everything fits.

**Video deck and panel.** Genadi asked about an elongated touchscreen video deck (about $100) versus the mainstream button panel. Val said a square deck is out; the deck must be the width of the machine. The panel is shallow because the machine is tight and players sit on a high chair. On one side a bump for the slam button, on the other a cup holder; no room for a plate. The panel should slope about 10 degrees so water drains. The same size is kept for buttons or the video deck. Genadi will look for a long monitor, even if expensive, since the main configuration is buttons and the video deck is an upsell. Val said $1,000 is still the golden mark and he thinks they will fit. Val: the look is more important than the price.

**Printer and bill acceptor.** Genadi plans the printer in the middle, under the video deck, so the cabinet stays shallow. The bill acceptor goes in the usual place, on the right above the panel, and supports front load (the transcript also says back load; unclear). Val agreed.

**FlexPort revision (separate topic, not for the street machine).** Val reminded Genadi that FlexPort needs a revision: fully enclosed in a plastic box, with thick posts that fix to the machine's front panel and hold the port about an inch away ("floating" design), because many machines have a non-standard cutout. The posts are both mount and wiring conduit. Genadi said the current external plastic base is almost there; it needs a back cover, and the tubes can be moulded into that cover with four holes. Val is not sure of the distance, since the TV may overhang; it may need two inches.

**Igor.** Genadi may talk to Igor (surname not stated) this weekend, but can only describe things verbally. Val said Igor needs time to think, so plan several conversations. Genadi will try to reach him.

Genadi ended the call tired; he has to work with China tonight.

## Decisions Made

- **The player tracking panel stays; the TV is not lowered to remove it.** (Val)
- **The video deck, if used, spans the full width of the machine; a square deck is ruled out.** (Val)
- **The play panel is shallow with a slam-button bump on one side and a cup holder on the other, sloped about 10 degrees.** (Val)
- **The look of the video deck matters more than its price; $1,000 stays the target.** (Val)
- **Printer in the middle under the video deck; bill acceptor on the right above the panel.** (Genadi proposed; Val agreed)
- **FlexPort gets a revision: fully enclosed plastic box on posts that mount to the front panel and carry the wiring.** (Val)

Not decided: hanging the TV on hooks plus a pull-out electronics shelf. Val said he "can be persuaded" but is not yet convinced.

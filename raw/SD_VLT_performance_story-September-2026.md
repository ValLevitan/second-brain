# South Dakota VLT performance story: September 2026

**South Dakota: we sell more machines, but each machine makes less money.**

A year ago we had 6 machines. Each one made about $164 a day, twice the state average. Since then we grew to 55 machines. Total net machine income went up 4 times, from $29K to $123K a month.

But each new machine made less than the ones before. By April we were at the state average. Since June we are mostly below it.

**September was our weakest month for each machine:**
- $74 a day per machine. The state average is $82, so we are 9% below.
- Total net machine income was $123K, down 8% from August and down 16% from the July peak.
- It is not bad luck. The machines kept their normal 8.9% hold.
- The real problem is less play. Each machine played 754 games a day. That is the lowest ever (883 in July).
- People who do play bet more: the average bet was $1.11, the highest ever.
- Wild Colt and Lion Riches bring in half the money. Liberty Spins and Dutch Mill are weak, at about $7 a day each.

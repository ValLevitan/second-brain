Operator Briefing — Diamond Amusement LLC

Company Overview

Company: Diamond Amusement LLC
CEO: Shawn Fellows
COO: Terry Spivey
Head Warehouse Technician: Terra Horton

Diamond Amusement has been active in the Georgia COAM market for over 30 years and is
considered one of the more influential operators in the state. Shawn Fellows previously
served two terms as President of GAMOA and remains highly involved in industry advocacy
through AMOA. Terry Spivey is also very active in Georgia industry matters, particularly
through GAMOA initiatives.

Overall, Diamond is viewed as a mature, well-organized operator with strong technical
capabilities and significant market influence.



Relationship with JVL

JVL maintains a positive working relationship with Diamond at multiple levels:

   •   Friendly relationship with Shawn Fellows

   •   Operational alignment with COO Terry Spivey

   •   Active technical collaboration with Head Technician Terra Horton

Diamond has upgraded several cabinets to the latest Dual SAS configuration, and we have
worked closely with their technical team to accelerate field deployment and minimize
downtime.

As part of strengthening technical alignment, JVL conducted a dedicated technician
training session at Diamond’s office on January 28, 2026, focused on improving
troubleshooting capabilities in the field.

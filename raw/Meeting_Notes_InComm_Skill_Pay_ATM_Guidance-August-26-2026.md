## Summary

This was the follow-up call after the Georgia expo between JVL and InComm about the card products JVL will use in the Georgia COAM market. Most of the call was about what InComm will and will not allow around cash-out, plus the steps JVL needs to complete before going live.

**Missing invoices.** Andrei said JVL has not received the invoices that InComm was supposed to send to two approved email addresses. Allen Lee said he is usually copied on the report but not the invoice, and he will check with InComm's accounting team. The tested transaction ran in production about two weeks ago.

**The two cards.** There are two cards. The black card is "Prize Pay" with a $2 activation fee. The blue card is "Skill Pay" with a $3 activation fee. Both are a single, direct redemption (no two-step transaction — that older two-step idea was dropped after InComm spoke with the lottery and regulators). Both can be spent anywhere Mastercard is accepted, including buying lottery tickets. Skill Pay also has ATM/cash access as one feature. InComm expects Skill Pay to eventually replace almost all Prize Pay use, though both stay available. Skill Pay is non-reloadable and InComm has no plans to make it reloadable (reloadable would require KYC and monthly load limits, which InComm thinks players don't actually want).

**Rollout timing.** From about September 15, JVL will be able to issue a Skill Pay card directly without issuing a gift card first — once inventory ships to the stores and the contract is amended. Kiosk cards ship first (early September); the wrapped POS cards come later (late September). Because Skill Pay is not yet ready for POS, JVL will launch its first real customer location (in about a week) using POS with the black Prize Pay card.

**ATM / cash-out guidance (the main point).** Jason Hall's clear guidance: InComm does not want any cash coming out of a COAM redemption device for a Skill Pay card. InComm will not be comfortable with the same entity (JVL) operating both a COAM redemption kiosk and an ATM as a coordinated, paired product. A player physically receiving a Skill Pay card and then converting it to cash at a separate, independent ATM is allowable. This posture follows concerns raised by Joe Kim, the Georgia Lottery general counsel who regulates COAM, after he saw expo demos of machines dispensing cash directly and of ATM-only redemption devices. InComm believes its own product is fine because ATM access is just one feature (with daily withdrawal limits), not the only use. The posture may loosen in a few years once the lottery gives clearer guidance.

**JVL's products and ATM ambitions.** Val confirmed JVL is only certifying two products with InComm for the next 12 months: Flex IQ (the tabletop POS) and Flex Port (mounted on the outside of a machine). JVL does want to become an ATM manufacturer/operator eventually, but Val was firm that this cannot happen unless JVL is licensed as an ATM operator (which needs a bank — JVL has contacted Harris Bank but nothing has moved past the first conversation). Val stressed JVL will not break the law and will not risk 43 years of work over the Georgia market.

**Certification process.** For each product, JVL sends InComm UX screenshots and a video of the full operation; InComm reviews and certifies the UX against its requirements. Andrei will send the videos. Any POS interface changes JVL makes should follow the same review process (video + test receipt + test card).

**Commission.** The commission structure stays the same moving from Prize Pay to Skill Pay (InComm is recovering first-year setup costs). Before JVL can activate Skill Pay, the reseller agreement needs a product amendment. The activation fee must stay static, but JVL is free to share or give up its own commission to the location. Juan flagged that giving commission to a location could look like inducement under GLC rules — those rules apply to master/location license holders; it is unclear whether they touch a manufacturer of transactional equipment. Juan will clarify.

**Game credits via barcode.** Both cards carry a barcode on the back that can be scanned to convert to game credits. This is still in internal testing. Jason will send a deck on it, and the parties will hold a separate call to discuss.

## Decisions Made

- **JVL will certify only two products with InComm for the next 12 months — Flex IQ and Flex Port.** InComm asked JVL to clarify all products coming to Georgia in the next 6–12 months. (Val Levitan)
- **JVL's first live location launches with POS + the black Prize Pay card**, because Skill Pay is not yet available for POS. (Andrei Klimovich / Val Levitan)
- **No cash may be dispensed from a COAM redemption device for a Skill Pay card**, and InComm will not accept the same entity operating a COAM redemption kiosk and an ATM as a coordinated pair. A separate, independent ATM converting a physically-issued card to cash is allowable. (Jason Hall / Adam Harlow)
- **Commission structure carries over unchanged from Prize Pay to Skill Pay, and a product amendment to JVL's reseller agreement is required before Skill Pay can be activated.** (Jason Hall)
- **The activation fee stays static ($3 Skill Pay / $2 Prize Pay), but JVL may share its own commission with the location as it sees fit.** (Jason Hall)

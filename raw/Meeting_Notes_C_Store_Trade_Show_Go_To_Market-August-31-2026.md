## Summary

A brief hallway-level conversation that produced a real go-to-market idea: reach Georgia locations through **convenience-store trade shows** rather than only through the gaming trade shows.

Two events were identified:

- **C-Store Connect Show — September 17, in Georgia.** The concern is that booths may already be sold out. Val has a friend who is putting up a booth at that show and offered to ask whether JVL could take a small piece of it.
- **GACS Southern Convenience Store & Petroleum Show — October 22.** Later, so there should still be time to get in.

Val was unreserved about the direction — "Definitely. Good thinking. Totally with you on this." He also noted explicitly that this is **not a COAM show**, which is the point: these reach the store owners directly rather than the operators.

One further detail from the same conversation: the machines currently under test are **performing exceptionally well**, with a figure of $295 mentioned, and one machine is being moved for a C-store trade show.

A note in the show material mentioned the convention returns to Georgia next year and will include a tabletop vendor section, which the colleague thought would suit JVL.

## Decisions Made

- **Pursue convenience-store trade shows as a channel** — a deliberate move outside the COAM show circuit to reach store owners directly.
- **Go for the October 22 GACS show**, where there is still time to secure space.

## Summary

Val and Felipe reviewed the Georgia COAM sales pipeline and set a trial plan for the coming months.

**August trials (second wave).** Felipe forecast 13 trial cabinets. Val asked to raise Continental from one cabinet to two, so the wave became 14 cabinets across five operators: United Gaming (5), Legends Amusement (3), Continental (2), Mariani Amusement (2), and Jaya Game Amusements (2). Val's reason for two instead of one: if a single machine fails that is a 100% failure, but with two you still have something to stand on. Of these, only Legends and Mariani are firmly discussed; Continental and Jaya want to see the game at the show first, and United is not confirmed.

**August sales.** Felipe forecast 12 sales: 5 to Diamond Amusement (trading in 5 Altera cabinets at a $1,500 credit each, strictly one-for-one), 5 to Arc (Arc is meeting its top executives this week and should reply within a day; possibly 5 more in September), 1 to DGKL Amusements (quote sent for a new cabinet), and 1 already closed to Allenson (the old $5,000 credit; payment expected this or next week).

**Val rejected loose show estimates.** Felipe estimated ~20 cabinets from show conversations in September and 15–20 in October. Val dismissed these as guesses built on nothing and told Felipe to work operator by operator instead — how many machines each has, the strength of the relationship, and what deal it would take to convert each one ("point of tilt").

**Scaling the trials.** Val wants far more than 14 on trial. He framed the plan as waves: wave 1 (done), wave 2 (the current 14, on current software), and wave 3 in September at about 50 machines. The math: from Felipe's list of ~80 operators (90–95% with payment history), narrow to a reliable list of about 60–75, then run roughly 25 operators × 2 machines ≈ 50 per month — 25 for September, the next 25 for October, the next 25 for November. No trials in December.

**V3 and the modem.** The show (mid-August) features V3 — the same games Felipe has now plus two premium games (Twin Bison / "fiery link" and Lions Riches) — bundled with the modem. Val expects V3-plus-modem approval around September 1 (Eclipse typically approves in ~3 days with a letter ~2 days later). The modem replaces the QR/barcode method for data collection; operators sign a waiver consenting to data collection and, in exchange, receive the discount Val already put in the price list. Val stressed that discount is a framework/guideline for structuring deals, not a fixed per-customer rate (the written version was specific to Arc; VIP discount and trade-in terms only apply where they fit).

**Bugs / crashing game.** The current 14 cabinets are showing a crash tied to one game in the new family. The team has reproduced it, which Val called a positive sign. Priorities: immediately disable the problematic game if it can be isolated, then fix the bug and push the fix to machines already in the field; the second wave must ship with known bugs fixed. Removing the game is only a last resort. This is where the call got heated — Felipe questioned rushing version 1 out with a known crash, and Val pushed back hard, stressing he would never knowingly ship crashing games, that he is burning roughly $1 million a month and fighting for the company's survival, so machines need to move now (upgrades come free once the fix is ready). Val took a five-minute pause to cool down.

**Selling approach Val coached.** Position the trial in the operator's interest: while JVL is still flexible about free trials, lock the deal in now, start putting "free money" in the operator's pocket, and upgrade the software automatically when the new version ships — so the operator loses nothing and JVL captures the momentum.

**Production.** Val asked how Elizabeth is holding up against a 50-machine-a-month target. Felipe reported production is improving: temporary help is handling pre-assembly, work is organized into teams (Anibal, Andres training others, Adam on repairs), and pre-assembly for Echo and Videx is moving well, so final assembly is much simpler.

## Decisions Made

- **August second-wave trial fixed at 14 cabinets** — United Gaming 5, Legends 3, Continental 2, Mariani 2, Jaya 2; Continental raised from 1 to 2. (Decided by Val)
- **Scale trials to ~50 machines/month starting September (wave 3)** — drawn from a narrowed list of ~60–75 reliable operators, ~25 operators × 2 machines per wave, for September, October, and November; none in December. (Decided by Val)
- **Show/September product is V3 + modem** — existing games plus Twin Bison and Lions Riches, bundled with the modem; target approval ~September 1; September trials ship as V3+modem. (Decided by Val)
- **Bug response: isolate and disable the crashing game, fix the bug, and push the fix to fielded and second-wave machines** — removing the game only as a last resort. (Decided by Val)
- **Data collection moves to the modem, not QR** — operators sign a data-collection waiver and receive the price-list discount in return; the written discount is a framework, applied per deal. (Decided by Val)

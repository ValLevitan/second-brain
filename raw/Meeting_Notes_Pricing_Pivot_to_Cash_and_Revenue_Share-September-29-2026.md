## Summary

**Problem C.** Speaker 1 raised a new problem: if JVL invoices $12,000, receives $4,000 in cash and finances $8,000, JVL declares $12,000 of revenue and pays income tax on money it has not received. Val agreed it is a real problem.

**Domicile.** Speaker 1 had asked where JVL is based, thinking it might be offshore. Val said it makes no difference unless JVL were offshore, and moving JVL is impossible or would take months or years. Val asked how leases and railcar financing handle this; Speaker 1 said they declare full revenue and pay the taxes.

**Simpler deal.** Speaker 1 said the simplest answer is: his side pays **$4,000 per machine**, JVL invoices **$4,000 per machine**, **no note**, and everything is split **50/50** as planned. He loses the extra depreciation above $4,000, but every other problem goes away. Getting money out of JVL can still be solved creatively later. He must run it by John and Eddie again.

Val agreed it is much easier. Val asked him first to decide for himself, calmly, whether he is still interested in the deal before he talks to anyone. Speaker 1 agreed.

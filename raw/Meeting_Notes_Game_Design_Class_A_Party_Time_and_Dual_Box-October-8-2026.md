## Summary

**Class A requirements (IP-1190 / IP-1220).** Michael is writing the full overview of what Class A needs. No legislative requirements were found; he reverse-engineered Primero's approach, with one change. "Party time" wins pay $50 each and are the main draw. Val: a payout can be less than $50 but not more; for example 125 could be paid as 50, 50 and 25. Michael: valid point, they will check it in the math. Free spins are capped at $50, so they are weak. Michael and Efim's plan: after free spins end, the next base-game spin (with a new wager) may trigger party time. They found nothing in the rules against it. Primero's help screens are vague. Lions Riches (IP-1193) is the test bed. The Class A requirements show "ready by October 16"; Michael: "we'll see" — it is concept level and will evolve, mostly the math. The document is in Confluence, linked from IP-1220. It notes a possible new cabinet with a smaller VDeck showing bet up / bet down icons instead of bet levels. Once Class A Lions Riches plays, they refine it there, then carry it to the other games. One game is the definitive target.

**Storyboards.** Lena showed GD-979 (under IP-556 Class A V43 Nova design, IP-1198 Lions Riches, GD-945 Nova Lions Riches adaptation). About 16 detailed screens with real values for programmers. Example: changing the bet level disables party time; going back to the previous level enables it. At the Class A kickoff, Efim will explain it to the developers in Russian.

**South Dakota D27 03 Nova (IP-1164).** Adds Solar Keno and Queen of Luxor Keno (4 Keno games in total), brings Fiery Ice and Jackpot Rush, and two new games: Aaron's Pub and Wild Fire Ranch. Val asked if this was unchanged from two weeks ago. Michael: abstracts are moving — Sapphire Star Oct 6, Sparkle Sevens Oct 7, Calle Ocho today, Rodeo Queen tomorrow. Queen of Luxor Keno is a new design, about 1–1.5 weeks out. Aaron's Pub design is done and closed. Lena is now on Solar Keno.

**Georgia COAM V43 V4 (placeholder IP-222 under IP-535).** No schedule yet. Preliminary list: Aaron's Pub, Wild Fire Ranch, Wolf Pack (wolf variant of Twin Bison), Disco Spins, Fly Me to the Moon, maybe Treasure Island (perceived persistence). Fiery Ice: La Rosa and Dragons of Rain. Class A stays the priority.

**Nebraska D27 production (IP-1143).** Lions Riches, Wild Fire Ranch, Match 3 bonus for lines and ways games, Sapphire Star as first horizontal dual-screen game, home screen; Dragon Pays starting. New task IP-1221 links to a Confluence configuration page (buttons, progressive reset levels, Jackpot Rush and Fiery Ice link settings) — one source of truth, done for South Dakota and now Nebraska. Efim's review found some items to fix. Progressive reset levels were debated without competitor data; rule is "do no harm"; lighter on minis because small amounts do not move players.

**Select-a-coin and dual box.** Efim suggested making the wrong coin's touch area smaller. Michael rejected it: too close to the edge, risk of a YouTube video, and players already know how to play. Michael credited Val's idea to place the dual box lower, right above the button panel, for comfort. Nebraska keeps its current panel; the lower small panel is for Class A. Dual box goes to both Nebraska and Class A. Georgia will also get dual box; open choice is dual box everywhere versus dual box for 5-reel and nudge for 3-reel (competitors still use nudge on 3-reel). 5-reel will be dual box. Val favors dual box; Michael agrees.

At the end Michael asked about a service for Juan; the recording stops there.

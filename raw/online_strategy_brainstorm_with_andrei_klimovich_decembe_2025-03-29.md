### **Meeting Summary:**

**Participants discussed the following key points:**

1.  **Business Strategy and Website Integration:**

    a.  The team emphasized aligning website updates with evolving
        business strategies.

    b.  Identified the current phase as design discovery, allowing
        flexibility to adapt the strategy before implementation.

    c.  Discussed the need to reflect any sales or marketing tactics
        changes on the website.

    a.  Explored moving from revenue-sharing models to fixed-price sales
        for games, potentially using a digital storefront with tiered
        pricing and discounts.

    b.  Considered options like trial periods or revenue-share
        conversion to fixed-price purchases.

    c.  Emphasized building a streamlined, automated process for
        integrating operators into their system.

3.  **Market Research Proposal:**

    a.  Suggested assigning a partnership manager to investigate the
        market for custom slot developers in Eastern Europe and other
        cost-effective regions.

    b.  Goal: gather data on pricing and operating models to refine
        their strategy.

4.  **Operator Engagement and Integration Challenges:**

    a.  Concerns about operators\' reliance on third-party platforms for
        integration and technical support.

    b.  Discussed the feasibility of enabling operators to directly
        integrate games via API without third-party platforms.

    c.  Acknowledged that only larger operators with internal technical
        teams might adopt direct integration.

5.  **Building a Digital Storefront:**

    a.  Brainstormed the concept of an online \"game store\" allowing
        operators to browse, purchase, and integrate games autonomously.

    b.  Highlighted the need for robust API infrastructure and
        user-friendly contract signing within the store.

6.  **Competitor Analysis and Market Positioning:**

    a.  Debated the viability of competing with smaller outsourcing
        studios on price and efficiency.

    b.  Discussed shifting focus to regulated markets where their
        licensing and history provide a competitive edge.

    c.  Noted the importance of quality and the scalability of game
        production to stand out in the market.

7.  **Operational Constraints and Next Steps:**

    a.  Proposed testing the new fixed-price sales model with selected
        operators to gauge interest.

    b.  Highlighted internal restructuring efforts to optimize
        development processes and reduce game production times.

        tactics.

    a.  Assign a manager to research pricing models and operational

3.  **Pilot Test New Revenue Model:**

    a.  Develop a sample pricing list for games, including fixed prices,
        revenue-sharing options, and discounts.

    b.  Approach operators with this offer and collect feedback.

4.  **API and Storefront Development:**

    b.  Begin planning a digital storefront for autonomous game

5.  **Quality and Process Improvements:**

    a.  Finalize the restructuring of internal workflows to reduce game

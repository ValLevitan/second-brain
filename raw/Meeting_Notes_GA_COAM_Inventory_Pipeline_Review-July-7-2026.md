**Date:** July 7, 2026, 5:03 PM Eastern

## Summary

**Market — what competitors are selling.** Felipe says the buzz in Georgia is all about "Access" solutions: a loyalty/players card that converts to a gift card, run off a small monitor mounted on the machine (like a Kroger POS). Pricing is about $100/month per location of 6 machines — roughly $15/machine/month — with the equipment nearly free. It used to cost more when Tyson was the only distributor selling Access to masters in Georgia. Now there are three main sellers: Genesis, Raul, and Tyson. Raul is the best-known and cheapest, and is selling a lot. Felipe will call Raul today to get exact installation and device pricing.

**Income solution.** Some operators took the "income" solution to meet the deadline, but it's seen as a fallback when nothing better is available. Not much demand.

**Operator sentiment.** Felipe spoke to Salim (formerly VP at Gomoa, now with Dixie). Operators are unhappy with current solutions, especially kiosks — everyone is moving away from kiosks toward something simpler. Payline / AppShots is gaining ground because it needs only one printer off the Mother Goose and sends credits by pulse — similar to JVL's approach. Still, many prefer Access for its transaction security and its ability to connect both SaaS and non-SaaS machines.

**Epic trial.** Epic picked up a second machine for the trial, likely installed tomorrow. Owner Kenny is hard to reach, but his right-hand man Dustin approved it and will provide the call ID and QR code. The QR code matters — without it JVL doesn't get paid per game. Felipe made a tutorial telling operators to scan the code and also send a photo as a backup (the photo carries the serial number needed to connect).

**Sales / forecast (Georgia only).** June actuals in Georgia: 9 machines. (Felipe also cited 10 in South Dakota / MG Oil and 1 in Texas added on the 30th, but Val is tracking Georgia only.) July forecast: 15 to 20 games, of which 15 are sold and going on test. This is separate from the 14 V43 units — roughly 29 games in total.

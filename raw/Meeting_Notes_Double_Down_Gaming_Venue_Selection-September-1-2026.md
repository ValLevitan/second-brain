## Summary

Opening minutes of the Double Down Gaming visit. Val welcomed Ryan O'Donnell and framed the agenda: JVL wants to show its progress with transactional products.

The conversation then turned to the trade event's record attendance — 900 against an expectation of 600–700, described as the largest ever after two or three record years in a row. That success is forcing a venue question. The current venue's integrated hotel-plus-event-center format fits well and could carry the event for another year or two, but out-of-town exhibitors push back on the location, which is not convenient for travel. Ryan's read: the issue is not space, it is location. Options discussed loosely — somewhere near the airport or more central, ideally still with an attached hotel. No decision; Val's position was to keep the thought around and see, especially if attendance doubles in the coming years.

Ryan took a short phone call ("Sean") during the segment, then told Val he is in town every week, considers this market important, and asked for recurring face-to-face time with industry people he can learn from.

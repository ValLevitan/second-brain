# Meeting Summary — JVL Labs Insurance Carrier Migration

**Date:** June 25, 2026, morning (Eastern)
**Duration:** ~13 minutes
**Attendees:** Val (JVL), Inna (JVL), Cyndi Anderson (HUB International)
**Full negotiation record:** [[JVL_Labs_Insurance_Negotiation_Record-June-2026]]

## Background

JVL Labs' insurance renewed June 21 2026 with Everest Insurance Company of Canada through HUB International (broker: Cassidy Doherty). The renewal binders totaled $36,480/year — rated for a manufacturer/distributor profile that no longer reflects Labs' operations. Val pushed back with two detailed emails (June 22 and 23) itemizing every inapplicable coverage and describing Labs' actual operations. Everest's best offer came back at $25,000 CGL + $750 crime = $25,750 + tax, with the admission that removing the challenged coverages "does NOT change the premium." Val escalated on June 24, demanding a full policy review including termination. Cassidy escalated to Cyndi Anderson. See the full negotiation record for the complete email-by-email exchange.

## Summary

Cyndi cut to the structural issue in one sentence: **Everest Insurance has a $25,000 minimum premium floor.** No amount of coverage removal or operational explanation would bring the premium below that number. Everest is the wrong carrier for what JVL Labs has become.

Cyndi obtained a quote from **Economical** (a Canadian preferred insurer at HUB) at **$6,000/year** — an 84% reduction from the $36,480 Everest renewal and 77% below Everest's best offer of $25,750. Other carriers declined due to JVL's high US revenue stream; Economical was the one that quoted.

The new policy is a basic general liability policy with no property component. This means crime coverage cannot be attached (crime requires a property limit). Val confirmed crime is not a concern — Labs doesn't write checks or handle physical assets.

Inna's original concern about "crime" was actually about intellectual property theft, not physical theft or employee dishonesty. Cyndi confirmed IP protection requires a separate, specialized policy — it is never covered under general liability. HUB has several IP policies available. This can be addressed anytime.

Cyndi is transferring the JVL account to herself for the remaining 8-10 months she will be at HUB. The Everest policy (on extension) expires June 26; Cyndi will notify Everest to let it lapse and bind the Economical policy once Val reviews.

## Decisions Made

- **Switch from Everest to Economical** — Everest's $25K minimum makes it structurally wrong for Labs' reduced operation. $6,000/year vs. $25,750 minimum. (Cyndi's recommendation, Val agreed)
- **Let the Everest policy lapse** — expires June 26; no renewal. (Cyndi)
- **Crime coverage not required** — no physical assets, no check-writing, no premises. (Val confirmed)
- **IP insurance to be handled separately** — standalone policy, not bundled with general liability. Not urgent. (Cyndi and Val agreed)
- **Account transferred to Cyndi Anderson** — she will manage JVL for her remaining tenure at HUB. (Cyndi)

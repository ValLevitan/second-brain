## Summary

Val introduced himself as JVL's second generation and asked directly what it would take for Vishal to buy on a regular basis. Vishal's operation is large: ~900 machines across ~490 locations (greater Atlanta plus Valdosta, Augusta, Savannah), self-financed with manufacturer terms on top (IGT deal at the show, up to ~15 months). His blunt feedback: locations ask for Aristocrat and Light & Wonder; nobody asks for JVLs right now, and he ties that to payout levels (competitors at 91–92% RTP with higher minimum bets).

Val pushed back with the manufacturer's math: JVL pays out MORE (94%) at a LOWER minimum bet (25 cents), which is exactly why the operator's net win suffers — the player benefits instead of the house. The new games are configured head-to-head with Aristocrat/L&W (same payouts, same minimum bets) at roughly 40% lower machine price, so return on investment should be far better.

The negotiation: Vishal has ~25 old JVL D27s idle in his warehouse. Val opened at $2,000 trade-in credit; Vishal asked $7,000; they landed on a **pilot of 3 machines at $5,000 trade-in each** — structured as $4,000 per cabinet plus $1,000 for a 3-year data contract ($337/year/machine, confidential, popularity/service/gameplay data only, no financials, no resale). New machine price $14,500 (D43). Vishal offered to swap all 25 at that price and install in his 20-25k/month locations as marketing; Val conditioned the full 25-machine deal on Vishal installing JVL transactional equipment (Flex ports + POS, ~$1,000 per unit value, included in the deal) across his locations — after Vishal sees it working. On competing payment providers, Vishal reported locations mostly use InComm via PAX at $50/month subscription; his big locations prefer manual replay plus gift-card issuance with ATM access.

A demo visit at the JVL office was agreed for **Tuesday, September 1 at 1:00 PM** (this became [[Meeting_Notes_Double_Down_Gaming_Venue_Selection-September-1-2026|the Sep 1 visit day]]'s "Product Demo and System Integration Meeting" slot). Cell numbers were exchanged.

## Decisions Made

- **3-machine pilot swap:** D27 trade-in at $5,000 each ($4,000 + $1,000 data contract) against new D43s at $14,500, machines equipped with cellular modems.
- **3-year data purchase contract** at $337/year/machine accepted by Vishal ("Fine").
- **Full 25-machine swap parked** until Vishal sees the transactional equipment and agrees to installs; demo set for Tue Sep 1, 1:00 PM.

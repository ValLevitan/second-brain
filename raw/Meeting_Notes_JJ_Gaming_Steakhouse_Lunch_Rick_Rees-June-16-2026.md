## Summary

Val invited Rick to lunch at the end of the site visit — his standing move after a first meeting. Most of the 78 minutes is relationship-building, not business, and that is the point of the record: this is the raw material for how Val works a new account, and it is the only place Rick is described as a person rather than a job title.

**The business part.** Val ran a single, direct play. Rick had confirmed he is responsible for the Nebraska bottom line — profit, hiring, and what happens locally — so Val put the pitch in exactly those terms: switch to JVL equipment, reduce expenses, improve local profit, and *"you'll shine. It's your achievement."* Rick's reply set the real obstacle: *"You got a lot of people to impress, not just me. I'm step one."* And then the harder one — *"But especially for a system that you have nowhere else. So how are you testing it?"* Val answered honestly: **"This will be my first test."** Rick came back twice with the same caution, unhurried: *"being the first is not always the best."* Val did not argue the point away — *"It's not. I'm not saying that it is... That's on me, I know what I'm doing."* Nothing was closed. Val's own closing framing was that nothing happens overnight, JVL will keep experimenting, and the immediate work is to change a set of games and restart. Rick repeated his commitment to get Val to the right people and named them: **James and Jeff** — James Jansen (analytics and purchasing) and Jeff Harwick (technical) — with an email to circulate. Val: *"I look forward to the emails. I assume you will circulate it?"* Rick: *"Absolutely."*

**Rick Rees, for the account record.** Started at J&J in Illinois in 2013 as a technician; opened and ran a new territory office for about three years, went back to Springfield (where he is from) and ran that office for about five years, then took the Nebraska opportunity and came out in September 2025. We're not just numbers to them."* And: *"I can call the CEO of the company."* Val's read on that, stated aloud, was that Rick has a direct line to the decision-maker, which is the thing that makes him useful.

## Decisions Made

- **Rick will circulate an introduction email to James Jansen and Jeff Harwick** — restated and confirmed at lunch after being agreed in the morning session.
- **Val will not push for a first-mover commitment from Rick.** Rick is step one, not the buyer, and has stated the objection JVL has to answer for everyone above him: there is no existing installation of the cash-recycling system anywhere in the US.

Online vision - Efim, Anton, Igor, Andrei - November 12, 2024

The meeting transcript primarily highlights the vision and requirements
for expanding the online gaming integration platform. Here's a summary:

1.  **Vision for Expansion**:

    a.  Release one online game weekly.

    b.  Enable direct integration with a thousand casinos.

    c.  Create an Amazon-like storefront where casinos can sign
        contracts, download APIs, access integration guides, and use the
        RGS (Remote Gaming Server) independently.

2.  **Current Challenges**:

    a.  The current RGS requires manual interaction with each new casino
        integration, limiting scalability.

    b.  Architectural adjustments are necessary to support a higher
        number of direct integrations, aiming for a more automated,
        minimal-interaction model.

3.  **Platform Architecture**:

    a.  The discussion includes the need for a more modular, flexible
        RGS to reduce dependencies on external platforms, making the RGS
        an independent framework.

    b.  Considerations were given to creating a system where casinos
        could set up and manage integrations with minimal support,
        potentially through a container-based model similar to
        EveryMatrix.

4.  **Regulatory Requirements**:

    a.  Some countries require platform approval, especially when
        regulations allow online operations only by land-based casinos.

5.  **Automated Integration and Support**:

    a.  Develop tools, documentation, and video tutorials to minimize
        manual support for casinos.

    b.  A custom portal may be needed for casinos to manage their
        integrations and data within the RGS system.

6.  **Roadmap and Milestones**:

    a.  Break down the development process into manageable monthly
        milestones.

    b.  Set clear goals for each milestone, focusing on automation and
        scaling up integrations.

    c.  Targeting a timeline to have around 1,000 casino accounts
        directly integrated.

7.  **Next Steps**:

    a.  Schedule the next meeting on December 10 to explore the
        architecture changes, automation levels, and possible structural
        redesigns needed to support large-scale integrations.

This summary reflects a clear roadmap towards achieving an autonomous,
scalable, and user-friendly RGS that minimizes dependency on external
platforms and manual intervention, while aiming for rapid deployment of
games and support for a high volume of casino accounts.

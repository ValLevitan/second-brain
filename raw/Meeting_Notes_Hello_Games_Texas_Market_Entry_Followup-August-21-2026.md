## Summary

Val opened with his standard line — "How do we make money together?" Joe has Steve Camillo booked to inspect the game in depth (~Sept 20). Val's pitch widened well beyond the one skill game:

- **JVL footprint.** In Georgia, South Dakota (VLT), Nebraska; also Class III; also Mexico, Peru, Colombia. Just released **15 skill games in Georgia** (operators pick 6–8 — you don't inundate a player). Machines carry an **LTE data modem**: games can be swapped, and settings/collections/game-popularity monitored, remotely (rotate out the bottom game every 60–90 days).
- **The real pitch — transactional equipment, not just games.** Val positioned JVL's **SMIB/ports, point-of-sale/dispensers, and a recycling ATM** as the way off the operators' pain points. Today they use "pyramid" tech + a printer per machine: a player cashes out, walks to the counter, and the game ends. JVL's ports let a player **move machine-to-machine without cashing out** (keeping money in play), remove mechanical devices and bill acceptors, and use one counter printer with barcodes/QR/phone. The **recycling ATM** (stand-alone, ships January) takes bills into recycling cassettes and pays out from the same cassettes — no more sweeping/counting/refilling. Val's pedigree: former owner of **Cash Code** (bill acceptors, sold to CPI in 2006, tech now behind CPI/MEI); non-compete expired, so he is restarting the transactional company on that technology.
- **The Texas reality (Joe & Jordan's honest read).** Market is ~**80% Banilla**; Pace is here with lobbyists prepping for the **January 3–4 legislative session**. Out-of-state operators (Pennsylvania etc.) flooding in and Pace's high profile have drawn heat; State Sen. **Bob Hall** has made it his mission to shut down skill/cash-paying games. Skill's survival is "50/50"; operators are cautious and even eyeing non-skill alternatives. Their advice: **do not invest heavily in Texas until after the session** (~Feb, once the direction is clear); the state is effectively on pause.
- **Where it landed.** Val agreed to park heavy investment but keep the relationship alive. Both liked the transactional equipment. Plan: Val sends a technician + product manager to **install the transactional equipment on JVL's machine at a location the first week of October** (after Camillo is back), bringing a POS device and a recycler as a **functionality demo** (form/fit to be tailored for Texas later — today's build is Georgia's prepaid-card model, needs adapting). One location, **free, no obligation** — try it, keep it or remove it. Val also showed his **Aircall countertop platform** (149 games) as a possible Texas-legal sweepstakes vehicle if a market exists. A four-way email (Val, Joe, Jordan, Camillo) will start the equipment thread.

## Decisions Made

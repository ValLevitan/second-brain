## Summary

First executive alignment with AKS Amusements — one of the Felipe-organised operator series (Diamond Feb 9, Inamax Feb 11, Allenson Feb 16). Val's framing: JVL wants direct, top-level relationships with Georgia operators so its machines make more money for them; Juan ran the substance.

**AKS's operation (Jay's numbers).** A self-described "pretty small" Georgia operator: 100+ locations, ~560 active machines across Banilla (180+, largest by unit count), IGT (~150), Genta(?), Primero, Aristocrat, JVL (sixth, 19 active now, ~30–32 including disabled/deleted), Bad Dog. By revenue Aristocrat dominates: **~7% of machines producing ~24% of cash-in** over the trailing three months. Jay pulls Intralot machine-meter reports weekly into his own database/dashboards. JVL's showing: 19 active machines, ~$500K cash-in / $393K out / ~$132K net over ~90 days → ~$7K net per machine per quarter, ~$80/day — and when the filter includes all 30 machines the per-unit net drops ~45% (Juan flagged it; Jay cautioned it is not apples-to-apples since inactive machines had partial windows). Top 20–25 locations produce 70–80% of AKS's net; top locations are gas-station convenience, geographically scattered (Rome, Cleveland, Newton) — Jay attributes performance more to the location operator than geography. About four JVL machines sit head-to-head with Aristocrat in top locations; in one six-machine A-location the ranking is IGT (aggregate of two), Aristocrat, JVL, Banilla. Aristocrat placement is partly demand-pull: locations ask for it by name, and the $22–24K price means operators protect it with better placements. Business is steady (±3–8% weekly), weather dips only; AKS turns away unknown locations and grows by referral. Players skew heavily male, evenings, Friday–Saturday peaks. EasyLink: ~12 locations by next week, real-time data via site controller, kiosk redemption already at 75–85% versus the teller window; pre/post-TITO impact analysis coming once EasyLink lands in strong locations with history.

**July 1 (gift-card mandate).** Jay's read: the market expects a pushback of the date; AKS is "behind the eight ball," looking at cost-effective options that integrate with MudaGoose (transcribed "mother deuce") without buying kiosks for weaker locations.

**The JVL asks and offers.**
1. *Granular data:* Intralot shows neither side which of the six games in a box earns — Jay wishes he had it himself. JVL's SAS-level game menus hold it: JVL asked permission to scan the QR/game-history screens in-location (or have AKS staff do it) — Val: money-in/money-out is useful "but has much less value than a QR scan." Jay agreed ("a win for all of us"), pointing to Kareem for recurring-location scans.
2. *In exchange:* free trial games, complimentary software (latest dual-SAS support for the EasyLink rollout), upgrade kits, local tech training sessions, extended warranties — whatever suits AKS, subject to Zaire's approval (Felipe had already broached upgrades with Zaire).
3. *Anonymized report:* Jay will produce a cleaned report of top-tier JVL locations versus the number-one machine in each, pending approval.

**Transactional products pitch (Val).** Coming ~May: TITO or e-wallet, operator's choice; POS counter device for locations that won't buy a kiosk; e-wallet can eliminate printers and bill acceptors — ~$2,000/machine hardware saved plus the service burden of paper and cash collection. Jay wants a demo when ready and prefers JVL go **through AKS**, not direct to locations ("we tend to have a pretty strong relationship with location owners… there's a learning curve") with JVL marketing materials to help present it.

**Roadmap teaser (Juan, screen share).** Next-generation JVL software: persistent games (IGT Cash Eruption-like), a multi-level progressive reminiscent of classic Bally Quick Hit, the classic Peach-style games, in an "Apple liquid glass" visual treatment — data-sharing partners get it first.

Val closed calling it a door-opening session and asked for recurring meetings.

## Decisions Made

- **AKS agrees in principle to the data partnership** — anonymized location report plus in-location QR/game-menu scans via Kareem — pending Zaire's sign-off. (Jay)
- **Transactional product introductions go through AKS, not direct to locations.** (Jay's preference, accepted)
- **JVL to support the AKS EasyLink rollout with dual-SAS software and training as part of the exchange.** (Juan's offer)

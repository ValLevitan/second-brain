## 6. Twin Bison Concept Art Review

*Very positive reception from all attendees. Val described it as the strongest game so far.*

**Visual Design**
- Symbol hierarchy: Bison (oversized) → Free Spins (oversized) → Animals (mid-tier) → Royals (desaturated, small, no circle borders).
- Pay table: Higher values on left, diminishing to right (most important first).
- Silver/gold Twin Bison text reflects lighting effect and free spins white bison mechanic.
- Left side of bison has a white, wintery atmospheric effect — praised by team.

**Game Mechanics**
- **Free Spins:** Winter scene. White bisons run across bottom of screen with snow effect; reels freeze with ice/snowflake effect; wild reel appears.
- **Bison Lock:** In free spins, bison symbol locks on reel (watery/icy → frozen effect) until a winning pay line, then releases.
- **Precog/Anticipation:** Should correlate with winning outcomes to avoid disappointing players. Frequency to be evaluated.
- **Free Spins Transition:** Use Indian pattern from the free spin symbol.
- **Nudge Effect:** Same liquid glass triangle arrows used across all games.
- 10 paylines — payline arrows needed on concept.

## Summary

**Rear cover and connectors.** Genadi said the rear cover must sit far back to leave room for connectors. Val agreed and asked that the unit be easy to install. Genadi said changing a cable means taking the cover off. Val said that is acceptable. This mounting style is only an exception, for machines where you cannot cut into the panel the way casinos do. Val's method: a template, holes cut with a hole saw, wires passed through, and fasteners placed at the sides so they do not stick out. Genadi agreed the current layout is wrong.

**Dashboard and printer paper.** The dashboard cannot slide down, so there is no access to the bill acceptor and printer, and no way to load printer paper. Val said the dashboard should come off after the service panel is removed. The only real problem he sees is paper loading. His fix: make the lower lip part of the panel so it moves down with the printer. Genadi suggested placing the devices at the very bottom, like the Flex today (gate style, top surface for player tracking and speakers, everything folds). Val agreed and asked Genadi to use the same construction: rectangular is fine, just lower the two devices onto the panel, so the space opens when the panel goes down.

**Bill acceptor tunnel and door.** Genadi said the bill acceptor needs a closed tunnel on top, or old bills can slip into the gap; that is why it cannot slide down. A hinged door would remove the problem. Val said a hinged door with all its hardware is heavy. Genadi said a small plastic door would be light. Val said he proposes making the whole construction from plastic. He does not want to add metal to reinforce a door; he wants plastic and floating parts.

**Looks and layout.** Genadi said the side view looks empty. Val said nobody sees it, because machines stand side by side. Genadi said the street machine will probably never have a printer (only the private machine, for tickets); the space can hold an electronics drawer behind the door, and Igor can style it. Val likes a panel that folds out, especially with electronics inside. On a cup holder: Val said there is no cup if a printer is there; Genadi said then the printer goes lower. Cabinet width is the same as the Flex, the monitor slightly bigger, the panel the same size as the Flex (about 27 inches). Val said all fine.

**New idea from Val.** Val asked why a street machine needs the Flexport at all. It may need only one device (word unclear in transcript), with the rest of the interface moved to the machine's own touch screen. Genadi asked whether player tracking from others will ever be needed. Val said he thinks that is not possible. Val said he will think about it more.

**Igor.** Genadi proposed giving the model to Igor to work on angles and styling, if the concept stays. Val said only if no metal is needed on the panel, and then said it is too early.

At the end Val asked if Genadi will deal with Alekseev tomorrow. Genadi said Alekseev already called twice and he will call him back now.

## Decisions Made

- **Do not give the cabinet model to Igor yet.** — Val said it is too early; he wants to think about dropping the Flexport on the street machine first. (Decided by Val)
- **Use the Flex-style construction with the two devices lowered onto the panel, in plastic, without metal reinforcement.** — Val's position; Genadi raised the hinged-door option but did not insist. (Decided by Val)

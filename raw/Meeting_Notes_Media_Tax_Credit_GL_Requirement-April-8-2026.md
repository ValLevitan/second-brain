## Summary

Val asked Igor to explain what Inna Levitan has requested, what data he needs, where it comes from, and why he cannot pull it directly from the GL.

Igor explained that Inna has asked him to update the supporting documentation for the **media tax credit** claim. She sent him the prior-year format to update for the **2023–2024 period**. The work covers several categories of expense:

1. **Payments to Minsk** — direct payments made to the Minsk office during the period.
2. **Marketing expenses** — payments to various vendors booked under marketing GL accounts.
3. **Research & Development expenses** — payments to vendors booked under R&D GL accounts.
4. **Travel expenses** — hotel, car/vehicle rentals, airfare, and other travel-related costs.
5. **Contractor payments** — certain contractor invoices that fall within the marketing, R&D, and other accounts.

The core challenge is that the media tax credit requires proof that invoices were **both provisioned and paid** within the requested period — provisioned-only invoices are not accepted. Each GL account (yield account) contains expenses from multiple vendors. For each vendor, Igor must:

- Identify which vendor provided services under that account
- Determine which invoices that vendor submitted during the period
- Verify which of those invoices were actually **paid** (not just accrued) within the 2023–2024 period

This vendor-by-vendor, invoice-by-invoice verification is what makes the work time-consuming — it cannot be pulled as a simple GL report because the GL shows postings, not payment status per vendor per invoice.

Igor also flagged that the biggest issue is with **JVL Labs (the Canadian entity)** — for the requested 2023–2024 period, Labs does not have these types of expenses recorded in the GL, yet the media tax credit claim requires them.

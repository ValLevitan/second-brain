## Summary

Val briefed Genadi on a concrete Texas job: convert an existing 25-machine location (mostly Banilla) to **one Flex Recycler + 25 Flex-Ports** (not 25 recyclers). Use case: a customer feeds cash into the recycler, gets a QR code, moves freely between machines, and cashes out at the recycler. He also wants the Flex-Port connected to the machine's **second SAS**.

The core problem: **the current recycler is too small** for a 25-machine Texas location, and JVL's present product is not configurable — the vendor assigns the largest recycling cassette to the smallest denomination and won't reconfigure it. Val wants a **larger-capacity, stackable recycler** (e.g. 4/6/8 cassettes, ~50–60 mils vs 25–30). He met **CPI's marketing person at the Georgia show**, who said CPI has a product that fits (CPI positioned it for vending / large retail checkout — Walmart/Kroger). Val wants the CPI product info + SDK (JVL already has agreements signed with CPI, so no obstacle), and to keep working with **JCM** in parallel; also mentioned an "IT" cradle-port product Genadi dislikes as too flimsy. Genadi pushed back that "more cassettes" may not help (cassettes fill up) and that the real need may be a **larger cash box** (~900–1,200 bills max); Val accepted the limits but still wants CPI's stackable option evaluated. Bill acceptors at the location are unknown; Genadi will contact Emmanuel.

Val also relayed **Andrei's alternative topology** for locations without a recycler: Flex-IQ at the counter + Flex-Ports on each machine + a standalone slim TITO printer near the machines. Val talked himself out of it on the call — the shared-printer cash-out invites theft/disputes ("I'll get the living crap out of you… somebody grabs your ticket"); printing at the cashier via IQ means leaving the machine to add money. **The recycler-as-ATM satisfies every angle**, so the alternative was parked.

## Decisions Made
- Prepare the Texas install as **one (possibly two) larger recycler + 25 Flex-Ports**, converting an existing Banilla location. (Val)
- **Pursue the CPI stackable recycler** (get product info + SDK) while continuing with JCM. (Val)
- Parked Andrei's counter-printer topology (theft/dispute and convenience problems). (Val)

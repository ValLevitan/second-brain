## Summary (business content only)

A relationship dinner with the maker of the **Mutha Goose (MudaGoose)** system — a device built ~25 years ago simply to get printers out of gaming machines (one printer at a counter/kiosk instead of one per machine, before barcodes/QR existed). Useful competitive and ecosystem intelligence:

- **Pricing models.** MudaGoose is a **one-time purchase** (the unit plus dongles), **no recurring fee**. By contrast, **Fiery Pay charges ~$50 per location per month**, and accounting-system vendors want steep monthly per-machine fees — operators feel "bled dry" every time the lottery pushes something new.
- **Fiery Pay mechanics.** Fiery Pay uses its **card as the equivalent of a QR code**, not mobile money and not a gift card per se — a player collects on one machine and carries the card to another to keep playing (all value on the card). Their stated next step: a **QR code on every card** so a card can be loaded/issued in seconds. (Val had earlier understood Fiery Pay as "an Indian thing.")
- **Backend / data.** The MudaGoose unit goes online and supplies **data packets** to a customer's cloud server; the maker does **not** build backends himself — third parties do (e.g. Platinum Pet Services in Florida, plus a local operator). ~**6 machines per location** on average.
- **What the maker is building now:** a **SAS-to-network-protocol wedge** so operators adding "secondary SAS" to machines can push money onto the secondary SAS while keeping the legacy MudaGoose system running — operators want to preserve what they already paid for rather than rip-and-replace.
- **Bill-acceptor protocol color.** JCM is tight-lipped about its ID-003 editions (the maker's last official copy came from Paceomatic under NDA), whereas **MEI and Incredible Technologies publish their protocols openly** — echoing JVL's own frustration sourcing JCM connectors and docs.

No decisions or commitments; this was relationship-building and intelligence-gathering.

## Key Facts
- Mutha Goose (MudaGoose): one-time-purchase counter/kiosk printer-consolidation + data system; supplies data packets to third-party backends; building a SAS-to-network wedge for secondary-SAS operators.
- Fiery Pay: ~$50/location/month; card = QR-code equivalent; moving to per-card QR.

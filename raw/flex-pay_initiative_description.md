The Flex-Pay Initiative for GA-COAM Market

  Overview

  The Flex-Pay initiative is JVL's comprehensive solution
  for automating payment and redemption processes in the
  Georgia Coin Operated Amusement Machine (GA-COAM) market.
   It consists of both hardware (kiosks) and software
  components designed to comply with Georgia's specific
  gaming regulations while modernizing the player
  experience.

  Core Components

  1. Flex-Pay Kiosk System
  - Automated kiosk for dispensing and loading prepaid
  MasterCard debit cards
  - Players redeem COAM winning tickets and receive value
  loaded onto prepaid cards
  - Eliminates manual cash handling and improves
  transaction efficiency
  - Integrates with Incomm's payment processing
  infrastructure

  2. Flex-Port Hardware
  - Companion device that enables TITO (Ticket In, Ticket
  Out) functionality
  - Connects to gaming machines via SAS protocol
  - Supports both modern machines (with dual SAS ports) and
   legacy machines
  - Enables cashless gaming by accepting prepaid cards at
  the machines

  Key Features

  Payment Flow:
  1. Player wins on COAM machine and receives a ticket
  2. Player goes to Flex-Pay kiosk to redeem ticket
  3. Kiosk dispenses a prepaid MasterCard and loads the
  winning value
  4. Card can be used for:
    - Playing additional COAM machines (via Flex-Port
  readers)
    - Purchasing lottery tickets
    - General retail purchases anywhere MasterCard is
  accepted

  Technical Specifications:
  - SAS 6.03 protocol compliance for GA-COAM requirements
  - Support for hand pay conditions and proper meter
  tracking
  - Remote firmware update capabilities
  - WSS protocol for network communication
  - Barcode scanning for card activation

  Regulatory Compliance

  The system is specifically designed to meet Georgia COAM
  regulations:
  - Proper separation between gaming transactions and
  payment processing
  - Compliance with cash-out limits and hand pay
  requirements
  - Support for required SAS events (51 and 52 for hand pay
   conditions)
  - MCC code classification as "skill-based games" for
  payment processing

  Business Model

  Three Implementation Options:
  1. Clerk-Assisted: Players ask clerks to swipe cards for
  cash
  2. Merchant Acquirer: Install card readers with
  traditional payment processing (3% fees)
  3. Barcode Solution: Direct processing with Incomm using
  barcodes (preferred - lower fees)

  Current Status (as of May 2025)

  - Hardware development in progress with units being
  tested in Minsk and Toronto
  - Firmware updates ongoing for both Flex-Port and SMIB
  components
  - Working on proper meter incrementing for GA-COAM
  compliance
  - Test cards ordered from Incomm for multiple locations
  - Target deployment includes Georgia, with expansion
  plans for other states

  Market Impact

  The Flex-Pay initiative addresses several market needs:
  - Reduces cash handling for operators
  - Provides players with flexible payment options
  - Enables merchants to retain in-store spending
  - Modernizes the COAM gaming experience while maintaining
   compliance
  - Creates a seamless ecosystem between gaming and retail
  transactions

  This initiative represents JVL's strategic move to become
   a comprehensive payment solution provider in the
  regulated gaming market, leveraging partnerships with
  Incomm and adhering to strict regulatory requirements
  while improving the player and operator experience.

## Summary

A long relationship-and-sales visit at Epic Amusement. Val delivered his full origin story (second generation, father started 1984; owned the transactional-equipment company sold to CPI/Crane National Vendors in 2006; sat on the GSA board; back in business 2008 across Mexico/Colombia/Peru; pulled out of Latin America ~2018 over currency risk; refocused on Georgia). He was candid about JVL's Georgia stumble: a new **AMD processor was incompatible with the Linux OS**, so machines looked alive to attendants but weren't earning — misdiagnosed as bad content, cost real market share before the hardware root cause was found and fixed.

**The offer to Epic:**
- **Games/fleet.** New V3 games shipping **September 1** (free upgrade), a great cabinet, good early location test results. New titles include a remade "Lion's Riches" (JVL's most popular, beautiful on vertical), a first-ever bison game, and a new triple-progressive ("Fiery Eyes" / Jackpot Rush / third). Regional demand: JVL is always wanted in Savannah/Macon (south); Atlanta demands Aristocrat/Light & Wonder.
- **Fleet trade-in.** One-to-one swap of Epic's old cabinets, **no questions asked**, ~**$6,000** value each, whatever condition. Epic committed to **20 units + ~15 more** progressively from Sept 1, with **50–60+ (Kenny said 63)** more in the warehouse to swap over the next 3–4 months.
- **Modem / data-sharing.** New machines carry a **cellular modem (OptConnect)**; Val wants a **data-sharing agreement** (popularity reports only, kept confidential) to improve software, and will **knock ~$1,000 off** a machine if Epic keeps the modem connected for **three years**. Epic already pulls grouped data from Intralot; JVL wants the game-level detail.
- **Transactional "final destination."** Val walked Kenny through the recycler-as-ATM end state and the InComm background conversions (win → prepaid Mastercard → prize card → Skill Pay card → present Skill Pay to the recycler acting as a **licensed ATM**, bank behind it). Explained the **minimum-entry system** first (Flex-IQ on the counter, connects to the existing Modbus and even to two MudaGooses, **no rewiring**, issues redemption cards, gives per-machine/monthly win and issuance reports). Framed the whole thing around satisfying **master (ROI), location (players never bother the attendant, never cash in), and player (move between machines freely and privately)**. Post-July-1 Georgia law requires converting winnings to a prepaid Mastercard, which the Flex-IQ entry device handles.
- **The ATM profit-center point.** Owning the kiosk/ATM and collecting the redemption fee is the profit center; if a master puts an ATM in a store, it can **take an income stream from the location** — so the natural owner of the ATM business is an operator, not the location (a location could appoint a related small operator). Recycler cash economics walked through (cassettes by denomination, EFT split lottery/master/store, open the machine and take exactly the profit out).

**Networking / logistics.** Mike Patel reached out about dinner; Val wants to line up **Salim "Sam" (GAMOA)** to assemble a **masters buying group** (WhatsApp group committing to cabinet volumes at a special price), as Salim did when Val first entered. G2E booth in Las Vegas in March already booked. Warehouse tour and organization (a helper to keep parts organized for assemblers).

## Summary

**Why Jason called.** Jason wants to set up JVL's product with InComm and get it into locations. He is worried about the "unknowns" in Val's email. He does not want to discuss who did what in the past. He wants to agree what they will do, what they will not do, and move on. Val agreed to park the history.

**Val's position: JVL is a hardware provider, not a clearinghouse.** JVL does not clear transactions. It provides whatever the market sets up: debit cards, or ATM cards if the market offers them. In the last 30 to 60 days the market (InComm) moved to debit-card only, with no ATM transactions. So JVL's device cannot dispense cash, because InComm's card gives no path for it. Jason agreed: "It's impossible. I think that's right."

**Cash dispense is the sensitive point for InComm.** Jason said JVL's solutions are good, but he cannot let a device that dispenses cash be associated with InComm cards, at least for now. Val said Andrei already replied to Jason that JVL does not provide this feature, and Val told Chris at the GLC that JVL is removing it. Later Val said: the dispenser capability is removed now. He removed it to comply with the current legal interpretation, not to comply with InComm.

**Next step is Prize Play.** Jason will reply to Andrei and work through the normal steps to certify and deploy the product with Prize Play. Skill Pay with ATM access is not settled. Jason said the lottery will introduce a rule (he said "tomorrow, Wednesday", and later "Thursday"). InComm gave the GLC its opinion. InComm hopes the rule lets Skill Pay with ATM access come back in some form.

**Disagreement on control of the market.** Val said: the moment the Skill Pay card is back, JVL will add the feature, and InComm cannot restrict JVL from the market. Jason said InComm can pull its card from a solution it thinks is not compliant, and InComm must keep the right to certify a solution. Val said he will follow any legal or regulatory limit, but will fight any limit that comes only from another private company. Val also said he believes InComm is creating obstacles to approval of JVL's COAM ATM, then said this may be unfair. Jason said he is not doing anything, and that the clarity must come from the regulator. Each company will have its own legal reading of the rule; they agreed to talk it through when the rule comes out. Val said he does not see any disagreement so far.

**ATM certification.** Jason asked if JVL is seeking ATM certification. Val: if it is required, yes. He prefers certification over litigation. Val does not know who closed that path, InComm or Joe Kim. Jason said Joe Kim saw the solution and focused on a COAM device dispensing cash. Jason has seen the video of the cash recycler that JVL sent to InComm, and the devices on the show floor in August. For a future Skill Pay talk, Jason asked Val to write down the actual steps and transactions, and to send them to him directly. Val said he is happy to be transparent.

**Val's other items.**
- Val said John (InComm) talked about InComm guaranteeing unpaid transactions, and about InComm paying JVL half a percent of all money held in accounts. Jason: "I know about that last one." Val said the guarantee is time sensitive because JVL will clear many transactions.
- Val has a couple of private family offices lined up to "buy the market". JVL plans to rent equipment to operators at a low price, with no money up front for them. Jason: "That would be great."
- Val wants to take over the about 5,500 accounts InComm is unhappy with. Jason asked if that means buying existing brokers. Val: none of them make money. Jason: brokers have their own location agreements; to do business there JVL would need to acquire the contract, otherwise there is a 12-month cooling period. Val said Joe Kim told him InComm did not plan to fight those contracts, and proposed to set aside what Joe Kim said and start fresh with Jason.

**The agreement.** Jason wants to go back to the written agreement and add any new items there to negotiate. He pointed to the $300,000 daily cap: JVL must wire InComm when $300,000 or more is outstanding. With many locations JVL would hit that fast, so Val should think about it against his settlement timing with locations.

**Reset.** Val: "let's press a reset button on our relationship and move forward." Jason agreed. Val added that if JVL succeeds and the Skill Pay card comes into effect, InComm could buy JVL. Jason asked to finish Prize Play and stay in touch as the rules develop. Val asked for the agreement so both work from the same template; Jason said he will send it right away.
